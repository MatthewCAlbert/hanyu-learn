## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 疒 "a person lying on a bed, sweating — illness" gives the
meaning; 丙 *bǐng* gives the sound (for *bìng*). Shuowen: 疾加也 "an illness
grown severe" — 病 was the graver word, 疾 the lighter.

## Mnemonic
Laid up in the sickbed 疒: ill.

## Notes
疒 "sickness" also heads 疼, 痛, 疲, 症, 瘦. The 丙 phonetic also writes 柄
*bǐng*, 炳 *bǐng*. Senses: illness (生病, 看病), a fault / defect (毛病, 语病).

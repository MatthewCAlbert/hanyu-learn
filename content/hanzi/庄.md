## Etymology
The traditional 莊 is pictophonetic (艸 + 壯 for sound) and means dignified, solemn. The simplified 庄 is a different, older popular graph: 广 (building) over 土 (earth), used for farmstead or village. The simplification merged the two, so the dignified sense (庄严) and the village sense (村庄) now sit under one character. 土 is not a sound clue.

## Mnemonic
A house 广 on the earth 土 — a farmstead.

## Notes
村庄 "village", 庄稼 "crops", 庄园 "estate", 庄严 "solemn", 庄重 "dignified". The 壯 sound hint is gone, so 庄 is sound-opaque.

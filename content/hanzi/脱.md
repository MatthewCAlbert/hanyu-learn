## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (肉, flesh/body) supplies the meaning, and 兑 *duì* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's original definition is specific: 脫 meant flesh
wasting away, becoming gaunt — losing bodily substance.

"To lose flesh/substance" generalized to "to shed, to come off" (脱皮,
skin peeling; 脱衣服, to take off clothes) and, figuratively, "to escape,
to get free of" (脱离, 逃脱) — all variations on something falling away
from what it was attached to.

## Mnemonic
The body 肉 exchanging 兑 its outer layer for nothing: shedding it, "脱."

## Notes
脱 as a resultative complement (甩脱, 摆脱) consistently adds the sense of
successfully getting free of something — a burden, a pursuer, a habit.

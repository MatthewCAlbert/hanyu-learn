## Etymology
亻 "person" + 牛 "ox" — Shuowen: 分也 "to divide," with the note that "the ox
is a large thing, so it can be divided" (a person portioning up an ox). From
"a portion, a piece" the word became a classifier for discrete things: matters,
garments, pieces of luggage.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 cutting up an ox 牛 into portions: individual pieces.

## Notes
Measure word: 一件事, 一件衣服, 一件行李, 一件作品. As a noun: 文件
"document," 零件 "spare part," 条件 "condition," 案件 "case."

## Etymology
扑 is an old variant of 攴 / 撲, "to strike lightly", with 卜 *bǔ* as sound (攴 = 又 + 卜). In simplification 扑 took over the work of the more complex 撲, whose phonetic 業 was lost. So 扑 shows meaning (手 = action) and a plausible sound part (卜).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 patting the ground and divining 卜 where to pounce.

## Notes
卜 is sound only; its series is small (扑 *pū*, 朴 *pǔ*, 讣 *fù*). 扑克 "poker" (sound loan, 扑克牌), 扑灭 "put out", 扑面而来 "strike one's face", 扑通 (sound word).

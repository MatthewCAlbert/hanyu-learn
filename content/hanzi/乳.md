## Etymology
Shuowen explains 乳 as 孚 (to hatch, to nurse) plus 乚 (the swallow, a bird linked with childbirth). Oracle-bone forms instead show a woman holding a child at her breast, which most modern scholars prefer. The senses "milk, breast" and "to give birth to" (乳 as a verb) are all old. The two explanations disagree on the right-hand part.

## Mnemonic
A child 子 at the breast, held by a hand: nursing.

## Notes
乳制品 "dairy products", 牛乳 "cow's milk", 乳房 "breast", 乳酸 "lactic acid", 乳牙 "milk tooth". Compare 孚 in 浮 and 孵.

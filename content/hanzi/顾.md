## Etymology
Simplified from 顧. 页 (頁) "head" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 還視也 "to
turn round and look." The phonetic 雇 *gù* is replaced in the simplified form
by 厄, which no longer carries the sound.

## Mnemonic
Turning the head 页 to look back: to heed.

## Notes
Senses: turn and look (回顾, 环顾, 左顾右盼, 义无反顾), attend to / take care
of (照顾, 顾全, 奋不顾身, 后顾之忧), patronize a shop (光顾, 惠顾, 主顾),
and the literary adversative "yet, but." Surname *Gù*.

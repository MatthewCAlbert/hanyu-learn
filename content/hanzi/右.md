## Etymology
又 "a right hand" over 口 "a mouth." Shuowen: 助也 "to assist" — the hand and
the voice together helping. 右 was the word "to aid" (later written 佑), and
from "the aiding hand" came "the right side."

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 and a mouth 口 lending a hand: the right.

## Notes
Mirror of 左 (a left hand + 工). 右 was often the side of honour in classical
ranking (无出其右 "no one surpasses him"); "the Right" in politics. 右边,
右手, 向右转.

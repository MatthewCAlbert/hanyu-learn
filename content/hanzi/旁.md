## Etymology
The old graph 㫄 carries 方 *fāng* as its phonetic (for *páng*), with a
further element Shuowen marks as unexplained. The gloss is 溥也 "broad, wide."
The everyday sense is "the side, beside," and "other" (旁人).

## Mnemonic
Standing 立 off to one direction 方: at the side.

## Notes
The 方 phonetic also (loosely) writes 傍 *bàng* "to be next to," 磅 *bàng*.
旁边 "beside"; 旁听 "to sit in on"; 一旁 "to one side."

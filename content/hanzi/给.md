## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (糸) "thread" gives the meaning; 合 *hé* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 相足也 "to make up what is lacking, to supply" — keeping someone
stocked with thread and cloth. From "to provide" came "to give" (*gěi*, a
colloquial reading) and "to supply / provisions" (*jǐ*, 供给, 自给自足).

## Mnemonic
Handing over 合 a supply of thread 纟: to give.

## Notes
Two readings: *gěi* (everyday "give," and the coverb "for / to": 给我买),
*jǐ* (formal "supply"). The 合 phonetic also writes 洽 *qià*, 拾 *shí*,
答 *dá*, 塔 *tǎ*.

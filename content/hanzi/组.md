## Etymology
Simplified from 組. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "thread" gives the meaning; 且
*qiě* gives the sound (for *zǔ*, via 祖 / 助). Shuowen: 綬屬 "a woven silk
ribbon / seal-cord." From the making of braided bands grew "to put together,
to organise" and "a group, a set."

## Mnemonic
Threads 纟 braided together: a set — and, as a verb, to assemble one.

## Notes
The 且 phonetic also writes 祖 *zǔ*, 助 *zhù*, 租 *zū*, 姐 *jiě*, 粗 *cū*.
组织 "to organise / an organisation"; 小组 "a small group"; 组长 "group
leader."

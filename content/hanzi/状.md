## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 犬 (dog) supplies the meaning; 丬 (simplified 爿) supplies
the sound. Shuowen's original sense, 犬形也, is startlingly literal: "the
shape/form of a dog" — the general sense "shape, form, condition" is a
straightforward broadening from that one specific animal's outline to
"shape" in general.

## Mnemonic
A dog's 犬 outline, propped up like a shape carved from a plank 丬: form,
appearance.

## Notes
状 covers "shape, condition" (形状, 状况, 状态) and, separately, "a
formal document describing a condition" (告状 "to file a complaint,"
状元 "the top scorer" — one whose achievement is formally recorded) —
both senses trace to "a description of how something looks."

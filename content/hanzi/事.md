## Etymology
事 shares its origin with 史 "scribe" and 吏 "official": a hand (又) gripping a
tool — a writing brush in its holder, or a hunting-standard. The three words
split apart from one graph. 事 settled on "an affair, a matter, a task, a
job." Shuowen: 職也 "an office, a duty."

## Mnemonic
A hand holding the implement of office: business to attend to.

## Notes
Senses: matter / affair (事情), incident (出事), work (做事, 事业), trouble
(没事儿 "it's nothing / I'm free"). Compare 史, 吏.

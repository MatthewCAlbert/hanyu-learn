## Etymology
一 "one" over 白 *bái* for sound — "one hundred" (十十 "ten tens"). The
oracle-bone graph is 白 with a horizontal stroke through or above it, marking
the round number.

## Mnemonic
白 lent for sound, capped with 一: one hundred.

## Notes
百 stands for "all / every / a great many" in idiom: 百姓 "the common people
(the hundred surnames)," 百货 "general merchandise," 百闻不如一见. 百分之…
"per cent."

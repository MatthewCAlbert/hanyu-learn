## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning and 甫 *fǔ* supplies the sound. In Shuowen 鋪 is the metal ring on a door; "to spread out" (*pū*) and "shop, bed" (*pù*) are later uses of the same graph — the latter from a "post station" or a place where goods are spread out.

## Mnemonic
A metal 钅 sheet spread out by 甫 workmen.

## Notes
甫 is sound only. Readings: *pū* "to spread" (铺路, 铺床), *pù* "shop; bed" (卧铺 "sleeper berth", 铺子, 店铺). Series: 捕 *bǔ*, 铺 *pū/pù*, 浦, 辅.

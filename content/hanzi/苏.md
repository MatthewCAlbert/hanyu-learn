## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional 蘇: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning — perilla, a herb — and 穌 *sū* supplies the sound. Simplification replaced 穌 with 办, a shape-substitute that provides no sound clue. The sense "to revive" (苏醒) comes from the plant's use as a reviving herb and from the related 穌.

## Mnemonic
A herb 艹 that does 办 the job of waking you up.

## Notes
办 *bàn* gives no help with the reading. 苏醒 "regain consciousness", 复苏 "revive", 苏州, 江苏 "Jiangsu", 苏联 "the USSR", 苏丹 "Sudan; sultan".

## Etymology
Ideographic. 衤 (clothing) and 刀 (knife) together — Shuowen's gloss makes
the logic explicit: making a garment ultimately needs a needle, but
cutting the cloth with a knife is the very first step, so "the beginning
of tailoring" stands for "beginning" in general.

## Mnemonic
The first cut 刀 of cloth 衤, before the garment exists: the very start.

## Notes
初 as a prefix marks "the first stage of" across many domains — 初一
"the first day [of the lunar month]," 初中 "junior high (the first
level)," 最初 "at the very beginning" — all descended from that first
cut of fabric.

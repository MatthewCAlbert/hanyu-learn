## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning — to question — and 卂 *xùn* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as to ask. "News, information" follows from what you learn by asking.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 flying 卂 fast: news.

## Notes
卂 is sound only (it means "fast flight", archaic). 通讯 "communication", 资讯 "information", 讯息, 审讯 "interrogate", 问讯 "inquire". 迅 *xùn* shares it.

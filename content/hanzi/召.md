## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning "to call out to," and 刀
*dāo* supplies the sound.

"To call, to summon" (召开, "to convene [a meeting]"; 号召, "to call
upon/rally") is a formal, often authoritative summons — distinct from the
more everyday beckoning of 招 (covered elsewhere in this app), which
shares this same phonetic and a closely related sense.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 calling out with the sharp, cutting authority of a blade 刀:
"召," to summon.

## Notes
召 and 招 (covered elsewhere in this app) are close cousins in both sound
and sense — 召 leans formal/authoritative (summoning a meeting), while 招
leans everyday (beckoning, recruiting).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, 尊 *zūn* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged — and 尊's own sense "to honour, to venerate"
also fits: following a path with the respect due to it. Shuowen's gloss
循也 "to follow along" matches "to comply with, to abide by" directly.

## Mnemonic
Walking 辶 the path with the same respect 尊 owed to an honoured guide.

## Notes
遵 anchors 遵守 "to abide by/observe [rules]," 遵循 "to follow [a
principle]," and 遵命 "to obey an order [formal/deferential]" — always
about following rules or guidance with due respect.

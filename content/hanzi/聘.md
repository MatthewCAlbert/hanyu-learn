## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 耳 (ear) supplies the meaning — to ask after, to hear news of — and 甹 *pīng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as visiting to inquire; "to send gifts and invite a person" (engage, hire, betroth) follows.

## Mnemonic
Lend an ear 耳 to a candidate, then send a gift to hire them.

## Notes
甹 is sound only. 聘请 "to hire", 招聘 "recruit", 聘用, 聘礼 "betrothal gifts".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning and an abbreviated 徹 *chè* supplies the sound, so the right half 育攵 is what remains of 徹. Shuowen glosses it as to manage; "to withdraw, to remove" is the usual sense. The right half is a broken-up sound element, so it is not declared separately.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pulls the troops out — a retreat.

## Notes
撤退 "retreat", 撤销 "revoke", 撤离 "evacuate", 撤职 "dismiss". Compare 彻 *chè* (徹): the same sound.

## Etymology
矮 is a post-classical character, absent from Shuowen, and this entry
cannot confirm a settled account of why 矢 (arrow) contributes the
semantic side of "short (in height)" — an arrow's own length is not an
obvious source for measuring human stature, and this entry does not
invent a bridge it cannot source. 委 *wěi* supplies an approximate sound.
Given both gaps, confidence stays low.

## Mnemonic
Something so short it barely reaches the length of an arrow 矢, tossed
aside 委: "矮," short.

## Notes
矮 describes height specifically (矮个子, "a short person") — for
describing distance, quantity, or other kinds of "small," reach for 小 or
少 instead.

## Etymology
Traditional 廚: 厂/广 (a building/shelter) supplies the meaning, paired
with 豆 (a stemmed sacrificial food vessel) and 寸 (a hand) — a building
where food is prepared and handled. Not in Shuowen with this exact
analysis; the overall picture, a dedicated food-preparation building, is
uncontested regardless.

## Mnemonic
A shelter 厂 where food-vessels 豆 are handled by a steady hand 寸 — the
kitchen.

## Notes
厨 anchors 厨房 "kitchen," 厨师 "chef, cook" — nearly always appearing in
these two compounds rather than standing alone.

## Etymology
太 is a differentiated form of 大 "big" — the same graph with an extra stroke
added to force an emphatic reading: "extremely big, greatest, highest." In
early texts the word was also written 大 or 泰. From "the utmost" come the
titles 太阳 (the "greatest yang" — the sun), 太子 (crown prince), 太太
(the senior lady), and the adverb "too, excessively" (太贵了).

## Mnemonic
大 "big" with one more mark: bigger still — too big.

## Notes
太 is the phonetic in simplified 态 *tài* "manner" (traditional 態 uses 能).
泰 is built on 大, not 太. The adverb 太…了 "too …" is its commonest use.

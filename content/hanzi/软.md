## Etymology
Traditional 輭/軟: 车 (cart) supplies the meaning and 耎 *ruǎn* — itself
already meaning "weak, pliant, soft" — supplies both sound and an echo of
meaning, describing a cart with give in its suspension or wheels rather than
a rigid one. So this is a phono-semantic compound rather than a pure
pictophonetic split. The modern simplified right side 欠 is a graphic
substitute for 耎 and should not be read as its own meaningful or phonetic
unit.

## Mnemonic
A cart 车 with just enough give 欠 in its wheels to ride smoothly.

## Notes
软 is the standard antonym of 硬: 软件/硬件 "software/hardware," 软硬兼施 "to
use both a soft and a hard approach." Figuratively also covers a weakened
resolve — 心软 "soft-hearted," 腿软 "legs going weak."

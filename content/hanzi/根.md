## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (tree) supplies the meaning; 艮 *gèn* supplies the sound.
The original, concrete sense — 木株也, the stump/root-base of a tree — is
unchanged in the modern word.

## Mnemonic
A tree's 木 stubborn 艮 grip on the earth: its root.

## Notes
"A tree's root" extends smoothly into "basis, origin" in the abstract
(根本, 根据, 根源) — the same metaphor "root cause" uses in English.

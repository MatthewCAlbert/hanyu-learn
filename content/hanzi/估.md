## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 古 *gǔ* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The original sense named a class of
person — a merchant/trader — from which "to estimate, to guess" grew,
plausibly through the idea of a merchant appraising goods before a deal.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 weighing up an old 古 item's worth before naming a price.

## Notes
估 today is almost entirely the "estimate" verb: 估计 "to estimate/reckon,"
估价 "to appraise/price," 低估/高估 "to underestimate/overestimate." The
"merchant" sense survives only in the compound 估衣 (secondhand clothing
dealer), now rare.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, 敫 *jiǎo* supplies the
sound, shifted to *yāo*. Not in Shuowen. The oldest attested sense is close
to "to intercept, to meet on the way" — going out to meet someone —
which softened into the modern, friendlier "to invite."

## Mnemonic
Moving 辶 out to intercept someone on the road — and asking them to join
you.

## Notes
邀 is the everyday verb for invitations: 邀请 "to invite" (formal), 应邀 "at
[someone's] invitation." Distinct from 请, which covers a broader range of
"please/to ask" senses.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 口 *kǒu* the sound. Shuowen's gloss is reining in a horse; "fasten, button, deduct" grew from "hold back".

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pressed to the mouth 口 of the bottle: sealing it.

## Notes
口 is sound only (*kǒu/kòu*, a near match). 纽扣 "button", 扣除 "deduct", 扣押 "detain", 回扣 "kickback".

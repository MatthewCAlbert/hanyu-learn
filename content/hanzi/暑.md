## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 日 (sun) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition is
simply "hot" — and 者 *zhě* supplies the sound.

暑 names summer's heat specifically, as opposed to 热 (hot in general) —
the sun's own seasonal intensity, not just any warmth.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 blazing down on someone 者 caught out in it: "暑," summer
heat.

## Notes
暑假 ("summer vacation," lit. "summer-heat holiday," paired with 寒假,
covered elsewhere in this app — "winter-cold holiday") is the character's
single most common everyday compound.

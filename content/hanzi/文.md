## Etymology
Pictographic — crossing lines forming a pattern. The oracle-bone graph often
shows the marks on the chest of a standing figure: a tattoo or body-pattern.
Shuowen: 錯畫也 "interlaced strokes." From "a pattern / mark" grew "writing"
(the earliest characters being 文 "simple graphs"), then "a text," "language,"
"culture," and "civil / literary" (as against 武 "martial").

## Mnemonic
Crossed lines making a pattern: markings — and, from markings, writing.

## Notes
文 is Kangxi radical 67. 文字 pairs it with 字 (compound graphs). Senses:
language (中文, 英文), a text / essay (课文, 作文), culture (文化), a surname.

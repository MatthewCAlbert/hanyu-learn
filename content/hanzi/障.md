## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (mound, wall) supplies the meaning — a barrier — and 章 *zhāng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as to separate, to block.

## Mnemonic
A mound 阝 piled up as a chapter 章 against the flood.

## Notes
章 is sound only. Series: 彰 *zhāng*, 樟 *zhāng*, 漳 *zhāng*, 障 *zhàng*. 障碍 "obstacle", 故障 "malfunction", 保障 "guarantee", 屏障 "screen".

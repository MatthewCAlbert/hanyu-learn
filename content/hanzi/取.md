## Etymology
Associative: 又 "a hand" seizing 耳 "an ear." Shuowen cites the Zhou practice
of cutting off the left ear of a slain or captured enemy as a tally. From "to
seize" grew the everyday "to take, to fetch, to get."

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 closing on the ear 耳: taking hold.

## Notes
Senses: take / fetch (取钱, 取书, 领取), obtain (取得, 获取, 争取), select /
adopt (取消 cancel, 录取 admit, 采取, 取名). 取笑 "to make fun of";
自取其辱 "to bring shame on oneself."

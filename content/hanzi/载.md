## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 车 (cart, vehicle) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition is directly "to ride in/be carried by" — and 𢦏 *zāi* (the
same phonetic root behind 哉, an exclamatory classical particle) supplies
the sound.

"To be carried in a cart" generalized to "to carry, to load" anything
(装载, 载重) and, separately (*zǎi* reading), to "a year" — one full
cycle "carried" through from start to end (千载难逢, "a once-in-a-thousand-
years opportunity").

## Mnemonic
A cart 车 loaded up and carrying its burden onward: "载," to carry.

## Notes
Two readings split by sense: *zài* is the everyday "to carry/load"
(下载, "to download," lit. "to load down"); *zǎi* survives in the more
literary "a year" (三年五载, "several years").

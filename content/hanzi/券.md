## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 刀 (knife) supplies the meaning and the top element 龹 (a worn-down form of 𢍁) supplies the sound *juàn* (→ *quàn*). Shuowen glosses it as a tally or contract: a bamboo or wooden slip notched with a knife and split in two, each party keeping one half. A ticket or voucher is the modern descendant.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刀 splitting the contract in two, so each side holds a half.

## Notes
入场券 "admission ticket", 债券 "bond", 优惠券 "coupon", 证券 "securities", 礼券. 券 *quàn* and 卷 *juǎn* are related by shape only.

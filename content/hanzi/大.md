## Etymology
Pictographic — a person standing front-on with arms outstretched and legs
apart, filling space. Shuowen: "heaven is great, earth is great, and the human
too is great, so 大 takes the shape of a person." Meaning: big, great.

## Mnemonic
A person stretched out as wide as they can: big.

## Notes
大 is Kangxi radical 37 and the base of 天 (a head above 大), 太, 夫, 央. Two
readings: *dà* (common), *dài* in 大夫 "doctor," 大王 (in opera).

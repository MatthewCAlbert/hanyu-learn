## Etymology
Ideographic in the traditional form 掃: 扌 (hand) holding 帚 (a pictograph
of a broom, bristles bound to a handle) — a hand sweeping with a broom.
The modern right-hand component 彐 is a graphic reduction of that broom
shape, not a fresh, unrelated component — the same recognizable bristle
shape reduced to fewer strokes, so the whole picture (hand plus broom)
survives even after simplification.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 sweeping a reduced broom-shape 彐 back and forth: to "扫,"
sweep.

## Notes
Two readings split by grammar: *sǎo* is the everyday verb "to sweep"
(打扫, 扫地); *sào* survives narrowly in 扫帚 itself, the noun "broom."

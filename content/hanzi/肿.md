## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh) supplies the meaning — a swelling — and the traditional 重 *zhòng* supplies the sound (*zhòng* → *zhǒng*). Simplification replaced 重 with 中 *zhōng*, a nearer sound neighbour, so the sound clue still works.

## Mnemonic
A swelling in the middle 中 of the flesh ⺼.

## Notes
中 is sound only. 肿瘤 "tumour", 肿胀 "swelling", 浮肿 "oedema", 红肿 "red and swollen".

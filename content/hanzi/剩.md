## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 刂 (knife — here signalling "to cut off, to divide") supplies
the meaning, 乘 *chéng* supplies the sound, shifted to *shèng*. The core
sense "to remain, leftover" fits a cutting/dividing image: what remains
after a portion has been cut away.

## Mnemonic
After riding 乘 off with most of it, a knife-cut 刂 sliver remains.

## Notes
剩 is the everyday word for "leftover": 剩下 "to remain," 剩饭 "leftover
food," 剩女 (a modern, contested slang term for an unmarried woman past a
socially expected marrying age). Shares 乘 with 乘 itself and 胜 shèng (to
win) — a phonetic series with drifted, unrelated meanings.

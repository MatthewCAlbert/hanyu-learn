## Etymology
Simplified from 場. 土 "earth / ground" gives the meaning; 昜 *yáng* gave the
sound — an open, level, sun-caught stretch of ground: a threshing floor, then
a field, an arena, a venue.

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** 昜 is replaced by 勿, which
is neither semantic nor a recoverable phonetic. Learn 场 as sound-opaque: 土
tells you it is a piece of ground, nothing tells you it is *chǎng*.

## Mnemonic
A flat piece of open ground 土 — read the sound off by heart.

## Notes
One of eight HSK 1–2 characters whose phonetic was lost in simplification —
compare 过 (咼), 商 (章), 边 (臱). Two readings: *chǎng* "arena / place"
(操场, 广场, 机场, 商场), *cháng* "an open yard" and a measure word for
weather / bouts (一场雨, 一场病).

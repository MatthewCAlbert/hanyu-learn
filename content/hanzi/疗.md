## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 療: 疒 (the sickness radical,
depicting a person lying on a sickbed — seen also in 病, 痛) supplied the
meaning, and 尞 *liáo* supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 尞 with the unrelated 了** (*le/liǎo*), a close
but not inherited sound match — a shape substitution rather than a
preserved phonetic.

## Mnemonic
Illness 疒 finally finished 了 off, treated and resolved: "疗," to treat
(medically).

## Notes
治疗 and 医疗 (both covered elsewhere in this app) build directly on this
character; 疗 rarely stands alone outside such compounds.

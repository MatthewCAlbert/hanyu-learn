## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh, body) supplies the meaning — the flank, the ribs — and 劦 *xié* (three 力 working together) supplies the sound. Simplification replaced 劦 with 办, a shape substitute that gives no sound clue. "To threaten, to coerce" extends from seizing someone by the sides.

## Mnemonic
Flesh ⺼ gripped from both sides: a threat.

## Notes
威胁 "threaten", 胁迫 "coerce", 胁从 "accomplice", 两胁 "both flanks". The 劦 family: 协 *xié*, 胁 *xié*, 恊.

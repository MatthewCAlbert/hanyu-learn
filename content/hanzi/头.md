## Etymology
Simplified from 頭. The full graph is 页 (頁) "head" for meaning + 豆 *dòu*
for sound. Shuowen: 首也 "the head." The simplified 头 is a cursive
abstraction — neither the head-radical nor the phonetic survives in it.

## Mnemonic
Learn 头 by its meaning — the head-radical it came from is gone.

## Notes
Senses: head (头发, 点头), the top / end (山头, 街头, 从头), a chief (头目,
头头), first (头等), and the toneless noun suffix -头 (石头, 里头, 木头).

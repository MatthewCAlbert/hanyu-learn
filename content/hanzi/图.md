## Etymology
Simplified from 圖. 囗 "an enclosed area, a border" gives the meaning; the
inside was 啚 *bǐ* (a granary / rough terrain), Shuowen glossing the whole as
"to plan out a difficult undertaking" — mapping the bounds of a district.
Simplification replaces 啚 with 冬.

## Mnemonic
Marking out what lies inside the border 囗: a map.

## Notes
Senses: a picture / chart / map (地图, 图书, 图片), and "to plan / seek"
(图谋, 企图, 贪图 "covet"). 图书馆 "library" (a hall of pictures and writings).

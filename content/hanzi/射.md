## Etymology
Ideographic. The oracle-bone graph pictures a hand drawing back a bow with an
arrow — a clean pictorial scene of archery. By the time of the small-seal
script used in Shuowen, the bow-and-arrow picture had already been replaced
by 身 (body) beside 矢/寸, and Shuowen reads the result as "a bow-and-crossbow
shot leaving the body and striking something far away," analysing it as 矢
(arrow) + 身 — treating 身 as a stand-in for the archer's body rather than its
usual "body" meaning. Because the visible components in the modern form
(身 + 寸) no longer match either the oracle-bone picture or Shuowen's own
matter reading, the composition is agreed to be old but its present-day parts
should not be over-interpreted.

## Mnemonic
A bow 身-shape drawn back by a hand 寸, arrow about to fly.

## Notes
Extends metaphorically to any "sending out and hitting a target": 反射 "to
reflect," 注射 "to inject," 射击 "to shoot" (sport/military). The 寸-looking
component here is unrelated to the "inch/hand" 寸 seen in 村, 对, 寺.

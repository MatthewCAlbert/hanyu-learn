## Etymology
雨 "falling weather" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 凝雨 "frozen rain." The
lower element is a worn 彗 "broom" (*huì*), which once supplied the sound;
it now looks like a bare hand 彐. The "wipe clean / avenge" sense grew from
snow's whiteness and the idea of sweeping.

## Mnemonic
Frozen rain 雨, swept down out of the sky: snow.

## Notes
Senses: snow, to snow (下雪, 雪花, 大雪), snow-white (雪白, 雪亮), and to
wipe out a wrong (雪耻, 雪恨, 昭雪, 洗雪). 雪上加霜 "frost on top of snow"
— one misfortune after another.

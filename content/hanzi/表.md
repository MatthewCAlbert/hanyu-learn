## Etymology
Associative: 毛 "fur" set within 衣 "clothing" — the ancient fur coat worn
with the hair turned outward. Shuowen: 上衣也 "an outer garment." From "the
side that faces out" grew "surface, exterior" and "to show, to make outward."

## Mnemonic
The furry 毛 outside of the coat 衣: the face it shows the world.

## Notes
Senses: outside / surface (表面, 外表, 表皮), show / express (表示, 表现,
表达, 发表), a model (表率, 为人师表), a chart or form (表格, 时间表,
填表), a watch (手表, 钟表 — also written 錶), and a maternal-line cousin
(表哥, 表姐, 姑表).

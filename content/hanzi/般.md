## Etymology
舟 "boat" + 殳 "a hand wielding a pole" — Shuowen: 象舟之旋 "a picture of a
boat being turned about," 辟也 "to wheel round." From "to turn / move" the word
drifted to "a manner, a way, a sort" (一般, 这般, 百般).

## Mnemonic
Poling 殳 a boat 舟 round and round: a manner of doing things.

## Notes
Chiefly in fixed frames now: 一般 "generally / ordinary," 像…一般 "just
like…," 万般无奈 "utterly helpless." 搬 (扌 + 般) "to move / shift" keeps the
motion.

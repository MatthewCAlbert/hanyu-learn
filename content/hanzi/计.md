## Etymology
Simplified from 計. Associative: 讠 (言) "speech — counting aloud" beside 十
"ten," a complete tally. Shuowen: 會也，算也 "to total up, to reckon."

## Mnemonic
Counting off 十 out loud 讠: doing the sums.

## Notes
Senses: count / calculate (计算, 计数, 计时, 数以万计), a plan or trick
(计划, 计策, 中计, 缓兵之计), a gauge or meter (温度计, 血压计, 计价器),
and to mind / bother about (计较, 不计前嫌, 斤斤计较). 会计 "accounting."

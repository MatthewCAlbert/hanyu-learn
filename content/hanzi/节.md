## Etymology
Simplified from 節. 竹 "bamboo" (written 艹 in the simplified form) gives the
meaning — Shuowen: 竹約也 "the binding-point of a bamboo stalk," its joint.
The phonetic 即 *jí* is worn down to 卩 and no longer readable.

## Mnemonic
The knotted joint that divides one length of bamboo 艹 from the next: a node.

## Notes
Senses: joint / node (关节, 竹节, 骨节), a section / segment (章节, 音节,
两节车厢), a festival (春节, 节日, 过节), to economize (节约, 节省, 节能),
moral firmness (气节, 节操), and rhythm (节奏, 节拍). *jiē* only in 节骨眼
and 节子.

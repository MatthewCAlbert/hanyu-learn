## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 遞: 辶 (movement) supplied the
meaning "to pass along," and 虒 *sī* supplied a looser sound match for
the modern reading *dì*.

**Simplification replaced 虒 with 弟** (*dì*), which happens to give an
exact match to this character's modern reading — like 担's swap to 旦, a
rare case of simplification landing on a fresh, cleaner phonetic rather
than losing the sound clue.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 passing hand to hand, like something handed down from an
elder to a younger brother 弟: "递," to hand over.

## Notes
快递 ("express delivery," lit. "fast-pass") and 传递 ("to transmit, to
pass along," pairing 递 with 传) both build on this character's plain
"to pass from one to another" core.

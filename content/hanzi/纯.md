## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (silk thread) supplies the meaning, and 屯 *tún/zhūn*
supplies the sound. Shuowen's original definition is specific: 純 simply
meant "raw silk" — unbleached, undyed, unmixed with anything else.

"Pure, unmixed" generalizes that raw-silk sense to anything unadulterated
— 纯洁 (morally pure), 纯粹 (pure and simple), 单纯 (simple,
uncomplicated).

## Mnemonic
Silk thread 纟 straight from its cocoon, still bundled 屯 and untouched by
dye: "纯," pure.

## Notes
纯 and 单纯 overlap in the "simple, uncomplicated" sense, but 纯 alone
leans more toward "unmixed/undiluted" (纯牛奶, "pure/whole milk"), while
单纯 leans toward "naive, uncomplicated in outlook."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning — to swallow — and 天 *tiān* supplies the sound (*tiān* → *tūn*, an old shared sound). Shuowen glosses it as swallowing.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 big enough to swallow the sky 天.

## Notes
天 is sound only (not "heaven"). 吞咽 "swallow", 吞没 "engulf", 吞吞吐吐 "hem and haw", 狼吞虎咽 "wolf down", 吞并 "annex".

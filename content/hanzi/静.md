## Etymology
Simplified from 靜. Pictophonetic: 青 *qīng* gives the sound (for *jìng*);
争 (爭) "to grasp, to contend" is the other element — Shuowen glosses the
whole 審 "to examine carefully," a settled, unhurried attention.

## Mnemonic
When the contending 争 stops: stillness.

## Notes
The 青 phonetic also writes 清 *qīng*, 情 *qíng*, 请 *qǐng*, 晴 *qíng*.
Senses: quiet / silent (安静, 寂静, 静悄悄), calm / unruffled (平静, 冷静,
镇静, 心静), and motionless (静止, 静脉, 树欲静而风不止).

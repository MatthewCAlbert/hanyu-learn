## Etymology
Simplified from 靜. Pictophonetic: 青 "blue-green, untroubled" gives the
meaning; 争 (爭) *zhēng* gives the sound (for *jìng*). Shuowen glosses the
whole 審 "to examine carefully," a settled, unhurried attention.

## Mnemonic
When the contending 争 stops: stillness.

## Notes
青 is the phonetic in 清 *qīng*, 情 *qíng*, 请 *qǐng*, 晴 *qíng* — but in 静
itself Shuowen makes 争 (爭) the sound. Senses: quiet / silent (安静, 寂静,
静悄悄), calm / unruffled (平静, 冷静, 镇静, 心静), and motionless (静止,
静脉, 树欲静而风不止).

## Etymology
斤 "axe" with 戶 (户) *hù* for sound. Shuowen: 伐木聲也 "the sound of chopping
wood." The graph was borrowed for "a place" (场所, 住所, 派出所), and for the
classical nominalising particle 所 "that which" (所以, 所有, 所说的).

## Mnemonic
The ring of an axe 斤 against a door-plank 户: a spot — and, borrowed,
"that which."

## Notes
所 + verb makes a noun phrase: 所见所闻 "what one has seen and heard,"
所长 "one's strong point." Also a measure word for buildings: 一所学校.

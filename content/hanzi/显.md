## Etymology
The traditional 顯 was pictophonetic: 頁 (head) supplied the meaning; 㬎
*xiǎn* (itself built from 日 "sun" over 絲 "silk," picturing sunlight
glinting off silk) supplied the sound. The original sense, 頭明飾也, was
a bright head-ornament — a crown, an ear ornament, something that catches
the light and draws the eye.

Simplification dropped 頁 (head) entirely, keeping only a simplified 㬎
(日 over 业, a regularisation of 絲) to carry the whole word — the same
pattern as 总, 亲 and 产 elsewhere in this dataset, where the
meaning-bearing half is cut and the sound-bearing half survives alone.

## Mnemonic
Sunlight 日 catching a thread of silk 业: unmistakably visible.

## Notes
"A glinting head-ornament, easily seen" generalised fully into "visible,
obvious, prominent" (明显, 显然, 显示) — the ornament is long gone, but
the idea of something that stands out remains.

## Etymology
Traditional 飾: 巾 (cloth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's gloss 㕞也 is
close to "to wipe/polish clean" — combined with 人 (a person) and 食 *shí*
for sound, shifted to *shì*. A cloth used by a person to wipe something
clean and presentable generalised to "to decorate, to adorn" more
broadly.

## Mnemonic
A cloth 巾, wielded by a person 人 to polish something food-worthy 食 of
display.

## Notes
饰 anchors 装饰 "to decorate," 首饰 "jewellery [worn on the head/body],"
and 掩饰 "to cover up, to disguise" — decoration and concealment both
built on "changing how something appears on the surface."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning and 朿 *cì* the sound. A 策 was a bamboo horse-whip (Shuowen); "to whip on, urge" is the verb. Bamboo slips (策) were also the books of the time, so "document, plan, policy" (政策) comes by that route.

## Mnemonic
A bamboo ⺮ stick with thorns 朿 — a whip to drive the plan forward.

## Notes
朿 is sound only here (it depicts thorns); the series 刺 *cì*, 策 *cè*. 政策, 对策, 策略, 决策, 策划.

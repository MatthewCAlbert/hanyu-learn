## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning; 官 *guān* supplies the
sound. The original meaning is concrete: a six-holed bamboo wind instrument
like the 篪, not yet "to manage" or "tube."

## Mnemonic
An official 官 who plays the bamboo 竹 pipe to keep everyone in line —
"to manage."

## Notes
"Tube-shaped instrument" → "any tube, pipe" (血管, 吸管) and, separately,
"to manage, be in charge of" both descend from the same root, the second
sense likely via "to pipe/direct," though the semantic link is looser than
the phonetic one.

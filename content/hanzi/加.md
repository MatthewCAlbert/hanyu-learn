## Etymology
力 "strength / effort" + 口 "mouth" — Shuowen: 語相增加也 "to heap words on
one another, to exaggerate." From "to pile on" grew the plain "to add, to
increase, to put in," and the arithmetic "plus."

## Mnemonic
Words 口 laid on with force 力: piling it on — adding more.

## Notes
Senses: add (增加, 加水), plus (二加二), impose (加压, 施加), and the
light-verb "-ify" (加大, 加深, 加快). 加油 "come on!" (add fuel).

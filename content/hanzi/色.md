## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen analyses 人 "person" over 卪 "a kneeling
figure" and glosses it 顏氣 "the air of the face," i.e. complexion and
expression; the early graph is disputed. From "the look on a face" grew
"colour" and "appearance, kind."

## Mnemonic
The colour that rises in a person's face: their expression — and colour
itself.

## Notes
*sè*: colour (颜色, 红色, 色彩), expression (脸色, 神色, 喜形于色), kind /
sort (各色人等, 音色, 特色), scenery (景色, 春色), lust (好色, 色情), and
the purity of precious metal (成色); *shǎi* (colloquial): 色子 "dice,"
掉色 "to fade."

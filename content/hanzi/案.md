## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 几
屬, means "a piece of low-table furniture" — and 安 *ān* supplies the
sound.

From "a wooden table/desk," 案 extended to whatever is laid out on one
for review: "a case, a file, a record" (答案, covered elsewhere in this
app; 案件, "a [legal] case") — the desk standing in for the documents
spread across it.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 table, steady and secure 安, with a case file spread across
it: "案," a case.

## Notes
方案 ("a plan, a proposal") and 答案 ("answer," covered elsewhere in this
app) both extend 案 from a physical desk to the abstract documents and
decisions associated with one.

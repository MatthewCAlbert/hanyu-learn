## Etymology
Shuowen's own gloss is unexpected and hard to reconcile with the modern
sense: 所以驚人也, "that which startles people" — built from 大 (a person)
and an obscure component 𢆉. Confidence is low because this reading does
not obviously connect to "fortune, luck," and the app's decomposition
data (土 over 丷 over 干) does not match Shuowen's own analysis at all,
suggesting a long, undocumented graphic drift between the seal-script
form and the modern shape.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土, a lucky sign 丷, a dry spell broken 干: things turning out well.

## Notes
幸 keeps "fortunate, lucky" as its clear, stable modern sense — 幸福,
幸运, 不幸 "unfortunate" — whatever "startling" meant in the earliest
attested form has left no visible trace.

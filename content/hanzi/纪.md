## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (silk thread) supplies the meaning; 己 *jǐ* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 絲別也, "the separating-out of silk threads,"
pictures sorting tangled strands into distinct, ordered lines — the root
of "order, discipline, a record."

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟, sorted out strand by strand, each one its own 己: order kept,
a record made.

## Notes
纪 covers "discipline, order" (纪律), "a record" (纪录 "record," 纪念 "to
commemorate"), and "an era" (世纪 "century," 年纪 "age") — all variations
on threads sorted into a clear, orderly sequence.

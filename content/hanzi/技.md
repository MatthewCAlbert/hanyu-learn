## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 支 *zhī* (recall from
earlier in this dataset: a hand holding half a bamboo stalk) supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 巧也, "skill, dexterity," is unchanged in the
modern word.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌, skilled enough to hold and shape a bamboo branch 支 into
anything: a technique.

## Notes
技 pairs almost universally with 术 in modern compounds — 技术
"technology/skill," 科技 "science and technology," 技巧 "technique" —
two near-synonyms for "skill" combined for emphasis and precision.

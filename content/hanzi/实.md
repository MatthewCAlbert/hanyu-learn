## Etymology
Simplified from 實. 宀 "roof" over 貫 "strung cowries" — a house filled with
stored wealth. Shuowen: 富也 "rich, full." From "full, solid" grew "real,
true, actual" and "fruit / seed" (a plant's "solid" part). Simplification
replaces 貫 with 头.

## Mnemonic
A house 宀 packed solid with wealth: full — and so, real.

## Notes
Senses: solid / full (充实, 结实), real / true (真实, 事实, 实际), fruit
(果实), honest (老实, 实在). Opposite 虚 "empty / false."

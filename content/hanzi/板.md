## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 "wood" gives the meaning; 反 *fǎn* gives the sound (for
*bǎn*). Also written 版 for the "printing block / edition" sense.

## Mnemonic
A flat piece of wood 木: a board.

## Notes
The 反 phonetic also writes 版 *bǎn*, 饭 / 飯 *fàn*, 返 *fǎn*, 贩 / 販
*fàn*. Senses: board / plank / sheet (木板, 黑板, 玻璃板), stiff / inflexible
(呆板, 死板, 古板), the beat in opera (一板一眼), the boss (老板), and "to
put on a stern face" (板着脸).

## Etymology
Pictographic — a figure kneeling with the hands folded or crossed in front,
the conventional early depiction of a woman. Shuowen: 婦人也 "a woman."

## Mnemonic
A figure seated with hands folded: a woman.

## Notes
女 is Kangxi radical 38 and the semantic in a large family: 妈, 姐, 妹, 好,
她, 姓, 婚. Two readings: *nǚ* (common), *rǔ* = classical "you" (used for 汝).

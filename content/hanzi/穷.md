## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 窮: 穴 (a cave, a hole — the
semantic component in 空, 突) supplies the meaning "an extremity, the
farthest point one can reach," and 躬 *gōng* ("one's own body, bent low")
supplied the sound.

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** Simplification
replaced 躬 with the unrelated 力 ("strength, effort"), which is neither
semantic nor phonetic here — a shape substitution only. So 穷 keeps its
"cave/extremity" meaning component but gives no hint toward *qióng*.

"Reaching the very end/extremity" (of a cave, of resources) generalized
to both "exhausted, at the limit" (穷尽) and, specifically, "poor,
lacking resources" (贫穷) — someone at the very end of what they have.

## Mnemonic
A cave 穴 so deep that no amount of effort 力 can find its end: "穷," poor
— or exhausted.

## Notes
穷 shows the same simplification pattern as 权, 宝, and 奖: a real
semantic component survives while the phonetic is swapped for an
unrelated look-alike.

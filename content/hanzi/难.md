## Etymology
Simplified from 難. The old graph is a bird character: 隹 "short-tailed bird"
with 堇 *jǐn* for sound — it named a kind of bird. It was borrowed for
"difficult" and the sense stuck. Simplification replaces 堇 with 又, a bare
shape-sign, so the modern 难 keeps only the bird 隹 and no sound clue.

## Mnemonic
Grasping at a bird 隹 with one hand 又: hard to do.

## Notes
Two readings: *nán* "difficult" (困难, 难题, 难看), *nàn* "calamity, distress"
(灾难, 难民, 遇难). 难 + verb = "hard to —": 难看, 难听, 难受, 难过.

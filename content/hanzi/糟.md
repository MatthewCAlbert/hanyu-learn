## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 米 (rice/grain) supplies the meaning, 曹 *cáo* supplies the
sound, shifted to *zāo*. Shuowen's gloss 酒滓也 is "wine lees/dregs" — the
solid grain residue left after brewing. "Rotten, ruined, in a mess" is a
direct extension: leftover dregs are spoiled, useless, and a wreck of what
they came from.

## Mnemonic
The gritty grain 米 leftovers at the bottom of the wine vat — a mess.

## Notes
糟 anchors the everyday complaint 糟糕 "terrible, what a mess" and 乱七八糟
"in complete disarray." 糟蹋 "to waste/ruin" applies the same "spoiled dregs"
image to squandered resources, opportunities, or people.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
薄味, means "a thin, weak taste," the way water dilutes a flavor — and 炎
*yán* supplies the sound.

"Weak in taste" (清淡, light/bland food) extends to "faint, pale" (of
color, 淡蓝色, "pale blue") and, figuratively, to "indifferent, cool"
(冷淡, 淡忘 "to fade from memory") — all variations on something diluted
or weakened.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 poured in until even fire's 炎 intensity is washed out and weak:
"淡," bland or faint.

## Notes
清淡 (light, bland — of food) and 冷淡 (cold, indifferent — of an
attitude) both extend 淡's "diluted, weakened" core into very different
domains: taste and temperament.

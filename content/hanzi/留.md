## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen analyses it as 田 with a phonetic 丣, but the
modern shape (卯 over 田) does not transparently carry that sound, and the
early graphs are debated. The stable meaning is "to stop in place, to stay."

## Mnemonic
Something held down on the field 田 and not carried off: left behind.

## Notes
Senses: stay / remain (留下, 停留, 留学), keep / reserve (保留, 留座), leave
behind (留言, 留胡子), detain (扣留, 拘留). 留意 "to pay attention."

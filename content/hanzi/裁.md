## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 衣 (clothing) supplies the meaning, 㦱 *zāi* (itself built
from 戈 and 十, reduced in the modern shape) supplies the sound. Shuowen's
gloss 制衣也 "to cut and make clothing" is exactly what a tailor does —
cutting cloth to a pattern — which generalised into "to cut, to trim, to
reduce" (裁员 "to lay off staff," literally "cut staff") and "to judge,
decide" (裁判 "referee/verdict" — deciding where the cut falls).

## Mnemonic
Cloth 衣 cut by a blade 戈, measured to the inch 十.

## Notes
裁 spans tailoring (裁缝 "tailor"), reduction (裁员, 裁减), and judgement
(裁判 "referee," 裁定 "to rule/adjudicate") — all variations on "deciding
exactly where to cut."

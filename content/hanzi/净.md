## Etymology
Simplified from 淨. The semantic is 氵 "water" (in the simplified form written
冫, an "ice" shape) — washing clean; 争 *zhēng* gives the sound (for *jìng*).
Shuowen's entry is a place-name; the "clean" sense is later. From "clean" grew
"only, nothing but" (净说些废话) and "net" of amounts (净重).

## Mnemonic
Water 冫 that has carried everything off: clean.

## Notes
The 争 phonetic also writes 静 *jìng*, 睁 / 睜 *zhēng*, 挣 *zhèng* / *zhēng*.
干净 "clean" (*gānjìng*); 净化 "to purify"; 净是… "it's all just …".

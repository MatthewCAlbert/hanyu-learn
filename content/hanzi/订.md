## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 丁 *dīng* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 平議也, "to discuss and settle through fair
debate," is a group reaching agreement through talk — the direct ancestor
of the modern "to arrange, to order, to conclude [an agreement]."

## Mnemonic
Words 讠, nailed down 丁 firmly: an agreement settled.

## Notes
订 covers both "to arrange, to book" (订票, 预订) and "to draw up
[a formal document]" (制订, 签订) — both are, at root, talking something
through until it is fixed and settled.

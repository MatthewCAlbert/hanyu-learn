## Etymology
Ideographic. 臤 (itself built from 臣 "a bent, watchful eye" and 又
"hand," together meaning "firm, capable, worthy" — the ancestor of 賢
"virtuous") combined with 土 (earth) — ground that is firm, unyielding,
hard-packed. Shuowen's gloss, 剛也, "hard, unyielding," is that quality
applied generally. Confidence is medium because the app's decomposition
data cannot fully resolve the top component, matching the fact that 臤
itself is a compound most learners will not recognise on sight.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 packed so firm 又 that nothing can move it: solid, resolute.

## Notes
坚 shares its "firm, worthy" root 臤 with 贤 "virtuous, capable" and 紧
"tight" — a small family all built around the idea of something held
firm and unyielding.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺼ (肉) "body" gives the meaning; 北 *běi* gives the sound (for
*bèi*) — and 北, a drawing of two people standing back to back, chimes with
the sense. Shuowen: 脊也 "the spine, the back."

## Mnemonic
The side of the body ⺼ that two people turn to each other 北: the back.

## Notes
*bèi*: the back (背部, 后背), the reverse (背面, 手背), turn away from / recite
(背对, 背书, 背台词), go against (背叛, 违背), out of the way / hard of
hearing (背静, 耳背), unlucky (背运); *bēi*: carry on the back (背包, 背债,
背黑锅).

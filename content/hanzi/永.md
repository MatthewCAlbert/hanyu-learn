## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph shows a person swimming in a stream, or a long, branching
watercourse. Shuowen: 長也。象水巠理之長 "long; a picture of the long channel of
a river." From "a long watercourse" came "long-lasting, far-reaching, eternal."
The swimming sense passed to 泳.

## Mnemonic
A river running on and on: forever.

## Notes
永 is Kangxi radical 85's cousin. 泳 (氵 + 永) "to swim" is the differentiated
graph. 永远 "forever," 永久 "permanent," 永恒 "eternal."

## Etymology
Ideographic. 犬 (dog) inside 穴 (a burrow/hole) — Shuowen's gloss is a
vivid little scene: 犬从穴中暫出也, "a dog suddenly bursting out from its
burrow." That unexpected, abrupt emergence is the direct source of the
modern "sudden, abrupt."

## Mnemonic
A dog 犬 bursting out of its den 穴 without any warning: sudden.

## Notes
突 covers "sudden" (突然, 突如其来) and, from the same "bursting forth"
image, "to break through" (突出 "to stand out," 突破 "breakthrough," 冲突
"conflict" — forces bursting against each other).

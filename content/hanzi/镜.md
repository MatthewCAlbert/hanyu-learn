## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — ancient mirrors were
discs of polished bronze, not glass — and 竟 (covered elsewhere in this
app) supplies the sound, an exact match.

## Mnemonic
Polished metal 钅, finished 竟 to a perfect reflective shine: "镜," a
mirror.

## Notes
眼镜 ("glasses," lit. "eye-mirrors") and 镜子 ("mirror," the everyday
noun) both build on this character's core "polished reflective surface"
sense, even now that mirrors and lenses are made of glass rather than
metal.

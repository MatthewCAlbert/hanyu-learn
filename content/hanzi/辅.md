## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 輔: 车 (cart) supplies the meaning, 甫 *fǔ*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss describes it as
the cheekbone, likened to a cart's side-rails — the classical idiom 辅车相
依 "cheek and jaw depend on each other, like a cart on its side-boards"
captures mutual support. "To assist" grew directly from that image of two
parts holding each other up.

## Mnemonic
A cart's 车 side-rail, holding steady 甫 whatever rests on it.

## Notes
辅 anchors 辅导 "to tutor/coach," 辅助 "to assist," and 辅音 "consonant"
(literally "supporting sound," as opposed to the vowel it accompanies) —
always a supporting role to a main one.

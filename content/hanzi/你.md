## Etymology
亻 "person" + 尔 (the simplified 爾) *ěr*. 尔 was itself the old second-person
pronoun; 你 is a Tang-era vernacular respelling with 亻 added and the vowel
shifted to *nǐ*. So 尔 gives both the sound and the original pronoun sense.

## Mnemonic
The person 亻 I am addressing as 尔: you.

## Notes
您 "you (polite)" is 你 with 心 "heart" added. 尔 also writes 弥 / 彌 *mí*,
迩 / 邇 *ěr*. 你们 pluralises with 们.

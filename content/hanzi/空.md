## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 穴 "cave, hollow" gives the meaning; 工 *gōng* gives the sound
(for *kōng*). Shuowen: 竅也 "an opening, a hollow."

## Mnemonic
The hollow inside the cave 穴: empty.

## Notes
The 工 phonetic also writes 红 *hóng*, 江 *jiāng*, 功 *gōng*, 攻 *gōng*.
*kōng*: empty (空瓶, 空手), sky / air (天空, 空气, 航空), hollow / vain
(空话, 空想, 空前); *kòng*: leave blank (空格, 空出), free time (有空,
抽空, 空闲), a vacancy or gap (填空, 空缺, 空子).

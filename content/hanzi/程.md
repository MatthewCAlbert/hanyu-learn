## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning; 呈 *chéng* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 品也, "a graded measure," is spelled out
precisely: originally a tiny unit in an ancient system for measuring
grain and length — ten "hairs" made one 程, ten 程 made a 分, and so on.
A fixed, graduated step in a larger measuring scale.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾, weighed out and presented 呈 in exact, graduated amounts: a
measured step, a fixed process.

## Notes
"A fixed graduated step" generalised into "a course, a process, a
schedule" — 过程 "process," 课程 "course [of study]," 工程 "engineering
[project]," 程序 "procedure" — all built from small, ordered steps
toward a larger goal.

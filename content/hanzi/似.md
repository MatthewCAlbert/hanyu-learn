## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 以 *yǐ* supplies the
sound. Shuowen records this character in its older written form, 佀 (人
+ 以), with the same phonetic relationship; the meaning, "to resemble,
to be like," has stayed consistent across the graphic variation.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻, taken 以 to be someone else at a glance: they look alike.

## Notes
似 covers both plain resemblance (相似, 类似) and the hedging "seemingly,
as if" (似乎, 好似) — a likeness that might, on closer look, turn out not
to hold.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
names the cords that hold a carriage's canopy in place, keeping it from
collapsing — and 隹 *zhuī* supplies the sound.

The load-bearing cords that hold a structure together generalized to "to
maintain, to hold together" (维持, 维护) and, in modern technical use, "a
dimension" (维度, 三维, "three-dimensional") — an independent axis
supporting the whole structure of a space, the same logic applied
mathematically.

## Mnemonic
Cords 纟 holding a canopy steady overhead like a bird 隹 perched
motionless: "维," to maintain.

## Notes
维持 ("to maintain, to sustain") and 维度 ("dimension," a modern
scientific coinage) both extend this character's core "supporting
structure" image, one concretely and one mathematically.

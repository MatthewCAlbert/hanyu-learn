## Etymology
Ideographic. 木 (a bundle of sticks/wood) encircled by 囗 (a binding
loop) — Shuowen's gloss, 縛也 "to bind," is exactly this picture: sticks
tied together into a single, manageable bundle.

## Mnemonic
Sticks 木, wrapped tight in a loop 口: a bundle, bound and controlled.

## Notes
"A bound bundle" generalised into the abstract "to restrain, to control"
(约束, 拘束) — a bundle of sticks and a person under restraint share the
same underlying image of freedom of movement cut off.

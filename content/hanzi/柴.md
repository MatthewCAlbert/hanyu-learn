## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning, 此 *cǐ* supplies the sound,
shifted to *chái*. Shuowen's gloss 小木散材 "small scattered pieces of
wood" matches the modern "firewood" directly — kindling, not structural
timber.

## Mnemonic
Small scattered scraps of wood 木, gathered up right here 此 for the fire.

## Notes
柴 anchors 柴火 "firewood," 火柴 "matches" (literally "fire-wood," the
small sticks that carry a flame), and the set phrase 柴米油盐 "life's daily
necessities" (firewood, rice, oil, salt) — the bare essentials of running
a household.

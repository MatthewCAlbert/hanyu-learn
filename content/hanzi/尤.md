## Etymology
Not securely attested. The graph is close to 尢 (a component associated
with lameness/deformity in other characters, such as 尴) with an added
mark; the exact logic connecting that image to "outstanding" or "to blame"
— its two main classical senses — is not clearly documented, and the two
senses may in fact be separate words sharing one shape rather than one
meaning that branched in two directions.

## Mnemonic
A small mark added to an ordinary shape — enough to make it stand out
above the rest.

## Notes
尤 splits cleanly into "especially, particularly" (尤其, 尤为) — the
dominant modern use — and "to blame, fault" surviving only in 怨天尤人 "to
blame heaven and others [rather than oneself]."

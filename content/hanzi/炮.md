## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 火 (fire) supplies the meaning and 包 *bāo* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as roasting meat with the hair still on. The "cannon" sense arrived with gunpowder: before that, a stone-throwing machine was written 砲 (石 + 包); with the new weapon, 炮 (fire) displaced it.

## Mnemonic
A fire 火 wrapped 包 in a shell: a cannon.

## Notes
Readings: *pào* (大炮, 鞭炮 "firecracker"), *páo* (炮制 "prepare herbal medicine"), *bāo* "to sauté". 包 is sound only.

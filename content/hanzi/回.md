## Etymology
Pictographic — a spiral or eddy, in the oracle bone a single coiling line, now
regularised into a square within a square. Shuowen: 轉也 "to revolve." From
"to turn / circle" grew "to return / go back," "to reply," and "a time / an
occurrence."

## Mnemonic
Water curling round on itself: to turn back.

## Notes
Senses: return (回家, 回国), reply (回答, 回信), a time (一回, 上回), turn back
(回头). Also 回族 the Hui (Chinese Muslim) people.

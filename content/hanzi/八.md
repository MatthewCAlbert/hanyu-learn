## Etymology
Indicative — two strokes curving away from each other, meaning "to divide, to
split apart." Shuowen: 別也 "to separate; a picture of two things back to
back." The number "eight" is a phonetic borrowing of this graph; the original
"divide" sense passed to 分 (八 + 刀).

## Mnemonic
Two strokes pulling apart: split — borrowed for "eight."

## Notes
八 is Kangxi radical 12 and still means "divide" as a component in 分, 半, 公.
八 is a lucky number (発 *fā* "to prosper" rhymes in Cantonese).

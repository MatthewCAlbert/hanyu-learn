## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen glosses 互 as a tool "for winding rope onto" — a
reel or spool, pictured with rope crossing back and forth across it as
it winds. That crisscrossing, back-and-forth motion is the source of the
modern "mutually, reciprocally": each side doing the same thing to the
other, back and forth, the way rope winds across a spool.

## Mnemonic
Rope, wound back and forth across a spool: each side crossing the other.

## Notes
互 never stands alone as a full word — it is always a bound prefix:
互相 "mutually," 互联网 "the internet" (literally "the mutually-connected
net"), 互动 "interactive" — always marking an action that goes both ways.

## Etymology
A late character, first common in Song vernacular. 亻 "person" gives the
meaning; 故 *gù* gives the sound (a loose fit for *zuò*) and a hint of sense
(故 "to cause"). It split off from 作 *zuò* "to do, to make," with which it
still largely overlaps. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 making things happen 故.

## Notes
Rough division of labour: 做 for concrete making and doing (做饭, 做作业,
做朋友), 作 for abstract or bound uses (作业, 作用, 工作, 写作). Many pairs
allow either.

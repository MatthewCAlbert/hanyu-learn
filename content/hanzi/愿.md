## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 "heart" gives the meaning; 原 *yuán* gives the sound (for
*yuàn*). It absorbed the traditional 願 — 頁 "head" for meaning, 原 for
sound — whose "wish" sense took over, displacing the older 愿 "honest,
sincere."

## Mnemonic
What the heart 心 leans toward: a wish.

## Notes
Senses: be willing / want to (愿意, 情愿, 宁愿), a wish or vow (愿望, 心愿,
如愿以偿, 许愿 / 还愿 to make / redeem a vow). 甘愿 "to do gladly";
自愿 "of one's own accord."

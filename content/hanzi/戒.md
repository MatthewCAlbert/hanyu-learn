## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 戒 as 警也, "to be on guard," and describes the
graph directly: 廾 (two hands) gripping 戈 (a dagger-axe) — hands raised,
weapon ready, guarding against the unexpected. "To warn," "to give up [a
habit]," and the Buddhist "precept" all extend from that watchful, guarded
stance.

## Mnemonic
Two hands 廾 gripping a weapon 戈, standing watch.

## Notes
戒 covers vigilance (警戒 "to be on alert"), abstinence (戒烟 "to quit
smoking," 戒酒 "to give up alcohol"), and the physical ring worn as a
reminder or symbol (戒指 "ring" — literally "a warning finger-band").

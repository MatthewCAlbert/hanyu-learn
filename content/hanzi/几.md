## Etymology
Two characters share this shape.

**几 *jī*** is a pictograph of a small low table or armrest (Shuowen: 踞几也
"a table to lean on when squatting"). It survives in 茶几 "tea table."

**几 *jǐ* "how many; a few"** is simplified from 幾 (幺幺 "small threads" + 戍),
whose senses run from "minute, subtle" to "nearly" to "how many."

## Mnemonic
A little low stool — and, borrowed, "a few."

## Notes
几 *jǐ* asks about a small number and needs a measure word: 几个人, 十几.
It also means "several" in statements: 好几次.

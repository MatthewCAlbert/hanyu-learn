## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 (mouth, marking a sound/speech-related character, used
here purely for a transliteration syllable) supplies a general
"spoken/foreign-word" association; 加 *jiā* supplies the sound. 咖 was
coined specifically to write the first syllable of 咖啡 "coffee," a 20th
century loanword — far too late for any Shuowen attestation, so
confidence rests on this being a purely phonetic construction with no
older semantic story to uncover.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 that just wants one more 加 cup: coffee.

## Notes
咖 has no independent meaning outside 咖啡 "coffee" and 咖喱 "curry" —
both loanwords, both borrowing the character purely for sound.

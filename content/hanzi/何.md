## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 可 *kě* supplies the
sound. The original, concrete sense, 儋也 "to carry, to bear on the
shoulder" (the oracle-bone form shows a person carrying a pole or
weapon), was entirely displaced by a phonetic loan: 何 was borrowed
purely for its sound to write the interrogative "what, why, how," and
that borrowed sense is the only one that survives today.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻, asking "why must I 可 carry this?": what, and why.

## Notes
The original "to carry" sense was reassigned to a new character, 荷
(艹 added on top) — 荷载 "load-bearing" — leaving 何 free to serve
full-time as an interrogative, with no trace of carrying left in it.

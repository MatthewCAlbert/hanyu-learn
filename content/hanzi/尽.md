## Etymology
Ideographic in its oldest form: a hand holding a brush, scrubbing out an
empty food vessel (皿) — the food is gone, and what's left is cleaning up
the empty container. That gives the core sense, "used up, exhausted,
finished." Shuowen, working from a later, already-abstracted seal-script
form, instead reads it as pictophonetic (皿 for meaning, with a phonetic
element).

Confidence is medium because the modern simplified 尽 (尺 over ⺀) does not
transparently preserve either the brush-and-vessel picture or Shuowen's
phonetic reading — it is a further graphic simplification layered on top
of an already-disputed earlier form.

## Mnemonic
Scraping the very last of it 尺 out of the bowl, down to the final drop
⺀: nothing left.

## Notes
尽 covers both "to exhaust, use up completely" (尽力, 用尽) and, as an
adverb, "to the utmost, entirely" (尽快, 尽量) — using everything you have
and pushing to the limit are the same underlying idea.

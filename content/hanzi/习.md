## Etymology
Simplified from 習. The full form is 羽 "wings, feathers" over 白 (in the
oracle-bone graph, 日 "sun"): a young bird flapping its wings again and again
in the daylight — "to practise, to do repeatedly," hence "habit" and "to
study." Shuowen: 數飛也 "to fly repeatedly."

The simplification keeps only half of the 羽 element, so the "wings" image is
no longer legible on the card.

## Mnemonic
Half a wing, beating over and over: practice.

## Notes
Doubled as 习习 "rustling" in classical verse. The full 羽 survives in 翅 (wing),
翻 (to flip). 学习 pairs "acquire" with this "drill."

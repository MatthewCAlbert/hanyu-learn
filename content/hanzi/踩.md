## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to tread on — and 采 *cǎi* supplies the sound. A late graph, absent from Shuowen. 采 is itself a hand 爪 picking from a tree 木, but here it supplies only the sound.

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 picking 采 where it steps.

## Notes
采 is sound only. Series: 彩 *cǎi*, 菜 *cài*, 踩 *cǎi*, 睬 *cǎi*. 踩踏 "trample", 踩油门 "step on the gas", 踩雷 "step on a landmine / strike a bad choice".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 屚 *lòu* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged — 屚 itself is built from 尸 (a roof/shelter) over 水,
picturing water dripping through a roof, so the sound component carries a
visual echo of the very thing being named. Shuowen's original sense is
specific: a water clock, "a bronze vessel that receives water, marked in a
hundred graduations across day and night." "To leak, to divulge, to miss by
mistake" all generalise from water escaping where it shouldn't.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 dripping through a hole in the roof 屚, one drop timing the night
away.

## Notes
漏 covers physical leaks (漏水 "to leak water," 屋顶漏雨 "the roof leaks") and
informational ones (泄漏/透露-adjacent 漏 in 漏嘴 "to let something slip," 遗漏
"to omit by oversight").

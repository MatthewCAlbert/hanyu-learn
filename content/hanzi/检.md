## Etymology
Simplified from 檢. 木 "wood" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 書署也 "the wooden
tablet sealed over a document"; 佥 (僉) *qiān* gives the sound (for *jiǎn*).
From "a seal that keeps a thing in check" grew "to restrain" and "to check, to
examine, to inspect."

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 seal keeping things in order: to check.

## Notes
The 佥 phonetic also writes 脸 / 臉 *liǎn*, 险 / 險 *xiǎn*, 捡 / 撿 *jiǎn*,
俭 / 儉 *jiǎn*, 签 / 簽 *qiān*. 检查, 检验, 检讨 "self-criticism."

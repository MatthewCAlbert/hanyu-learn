## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 足 "foot" gives the meaning; 艮 *gèn* gives the sound. Shuowen:
足歱也 "the heel." From "heel" grew "to follow at someone's heels," and from
there the coverb "with / and" (跟朋友一起, 跟…一样).

## Mnemonic
Right on someone's heel 足: following along with them.

## Notes
The 艮 phonetic also writes 根 *gēn*, 很 *hěn*, 恨 *hèn*, 眼 *yǎn*, 银 / 銀
*yín*. 跟 "with" overlaps 和 / 跟 / 同 in the spoken language.

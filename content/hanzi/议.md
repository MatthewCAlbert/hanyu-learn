## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 义 (simplified 義)
supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss is simply 語也, "speech, to speak" —
already general enough to cover "to discuss, to deliberate" without much
extension needed.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 spoken with a sense of what's right 义: a proposal, a debate.

## Notes
议 covers both the neutral "to discuss" (讨论, 议论) and the more formal
"a proposal, a motion" (建议, 议题) — talk that moves toward a decision
rather than talk for its own sake.

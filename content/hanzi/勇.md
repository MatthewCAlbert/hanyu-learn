## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 力 (strength) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
气, means "spirit, vital energy" — and 甬 *yǒng* supplies the sound, an
exact match.

Vital energy channeled into strength is exactly "courage, bravery" — the
inner spirit that lets someone act despite fear (勇气, 勇敢).

## Mnemonic
Strength 力 flowing through like a bell's clear ring 甬 (甬 itself once
pictured a bell): "勇," brave.

## Notes
勇气 ("courage") and 勇敢 ("brave") are the two most common everyday
compounds, both keeping this character's core "spirited strength" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 覽: 见 (to see) supplies the meaning; the
traditional phonetic was 監 *jiān* ("to observe, to inspect" — itself a
picture of a person looking into a basin of water as an early mirror).
Simplification reduced that component substantially, so no working
`phonetic` is declared for the modern shape — treat 览's top portion as a
graphic remnant rather than a live sound clue.

## Mnemonic
An eye 见 taking in everything, the way one gazes into a mirror to
observe closely.

## Notes
览 is almost always bound: 浏览 "to browse," 展览 "exhibition," 一览
"an overview." A verb of taking in a broad view, rather than close,
detailed inspection.

## Etymology
Traditional 牽 is pictophonetic: 牛 (ox) with a rope drawn across it (冖) for meaning, "to lead forward", and 玄 *xuán* for sound. Simplification replaced 玄 with 大, so the sound clue is lost and the shape is now misleading — 大 does not mean "big" or "person" here.

## Mnemonic
A big 大 farmer leading the ox 牛 by the rope 冖.

## Notes
牵手 "hold hands", 牵挂 "worry about", 牵涉 "involve", 牵强 "far-fetched". The other 玄 sound: 弦, 眩, 炫.

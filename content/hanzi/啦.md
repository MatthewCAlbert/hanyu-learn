## Etymology
A late, spoken-language character: 口 (mouth) marks a sound word and 拉 *lā* supplies the sound. 啦 is most often a contraction of the particles 了 + 啊 (*le a* → *la*), and also stands for cheers and singing (*lālā*). It has no classical etymology.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 pulling 拉 a happy sound out: la!

## Notes
拉 is sound only. 好啦 "all right", 走啦 "off we go", 啦啦队 "cheerleaders", 啦啦 (lalala). Sentence-final: a livelier alternative to 了.

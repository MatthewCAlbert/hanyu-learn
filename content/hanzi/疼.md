## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 疒 "ailment" gives the meaning; 冬 *dōng* gives the sound (an
approximate fit for *téng*). A late character, not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
An ache 疒 that comes on like the bite of winter 冬: it hurts.

## Notes
Senses: ache / be sore (头疼, 肚子疼, 腰酸背疼), and — from feeling another's
pain — to dote on, to cherish (疼爱, 心疼, 疼孩子). 疼痛 "pain."

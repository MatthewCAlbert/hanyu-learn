## Etymology
Simplified from 變. The full graph is 攴 "a hand acting on something" with 䜌
*luán* for sound. Shuowen: 更也 "to alter." Simplification reduces the whole
top to 亦 and keeps only 又, so neither the acting hand nor the phonetic is
legible.

## Mnemonic
Learn 变 by its meaning — "to change" — not by 亦 and 又.

## Notes
Senses: change / transform (变化, 改变, 变成), an unexpected turn (变故,
政变), and after a verb "-into" (翻译变…, though 成 is commoner there).

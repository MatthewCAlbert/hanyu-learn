## Etymology
Ideographic. 斤 (an axe) supplies the meaning directly — cutting with a
tool. Shuowen pairs it with 𠔍 (an ancient form of 絕 "to sever," itself a
picture of cut threads), giving "axe" plus "severed thread" for "to cut
through, to sever." The modern simplified right-hand piece, compressed to
米, is a further graphic reduction of that severed-thread image, no longer
separable as its own checkable component.

## Mnemonic
An axe 斤 coming down on a tangle of thread 米: cut clean through.

## Notes
断 covers physical severing (打断, 切断) and, by extension, an abrupt
break in continuity of any kind — 判断 "to judge" (to cut a decision out
of uncertainty), 不断 "unceasingly" (literally "without a break").

## Etymology
Shuowen writes 幻 as the reverse of 予 ("to give"), glossing "deceiving, confusing". The idea is that giving is turned backwards — what is offered is not what it seems. The reading is Shuowen's own and it is hard to test, since 予 itself is a contested graph. In modern script 幺 plus the reversed part is what remains.

## Mnemonic
A gift 予 turned inside out: it was never real.

## Notes
幻想, 幻觉, 梦幻, 变幻. In Buddhist and novel contexts "illusion" (幻灭).

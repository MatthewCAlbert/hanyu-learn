## Etymology
Not securely attested in its modern shape. Shuowen describes a walled market
(市有垣) and analyses the graph as 冂 with 乛, the sound coming from a reduced
之 — a marketplace. The graph was later reshaped and the analysis no longer
fits the strokes 亠 + 巾.

## Mnemonic
A fenced ground everyone heads toward to buy and sell: the market.

## Notes
Senses: market / fair (菜市, 夜市, 上市), trade (市场, 股市, 市价), a city or
municipality (城市, 北京市, 市长). 市民 "city resident"; 超市 "supermarket".

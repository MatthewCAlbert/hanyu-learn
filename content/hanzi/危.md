## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen's gloss is precise: 在高而懼也, "to be high up and
afraid" — the picture is a person perched on a height (厃), with 卪 (a
kneeling, self-restraining figure) added to show them holding themselves
back from the edge out of fear. Confidence is medium because the app's
decomposition data groups these pieces slightly differently, and the
exact reading of each stroke is a matter of scholarly reconstruction
rather than a simple, uncontested picture.

## Mnemonic
Standing high up 厃, drawing back 㔾 from the edge: danger.

## Notes
危 covers both the concrete "high and precarious" (literally, in older
usage) and the now-dominant abstract "dangerous" (危险, 危害, 危机) — the
fear of a physical height generalising fully into fear of any threat.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 瓦 "fired clay, earthenware" gives the meaning; 并 (幷)
*bìng* gives the sound (for *píng*) — Shuowen's 幵聲 is generally taken as a
graphic corruption of 并. Shuowen writes it 缾 with 缶 "jar" for
the same sense.

## Mnemonic
A vessel of fired clay 瓦: a bottle.

## Notes
Senses: bottle / jar / vase / flask (酒瓶, 花瓶, 热水瓶, 保温瓶), and a
measure word for bottled contents (一瓶牛奶, 两瓶啤酒). 瓶颈 "bottleneck."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 擋: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 当 (traditional
當, "to face, to confront") supplies the sound, and here also an echo of
meaning — to block is to put a hand up against whatever is coming at you.
Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 raised right at the moment 当 something comes at you — blocked.

## Notes
挡 covers physical blocking (挡路 "to block the way," 遮挡 "to shield/block")
and the mechanical sense "gear" (一挡 "first gear"), from a lever position
that "blocks" the engine at a given ratio.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 宀 (roof, building) supplies the meaning — the eaves — and 于 *yú* supplies the sound (*yú* → *yǔ*). Shuowen glosses it as the edge of a roof. In philosophical use it came to mean "all space" (the Huainanzi pairs 宇 "space" with 宙 "time").

## Mnemonic
Under the roof 宀 of the entire sky.

## Notes
于 is sound only. 宇宙 "universe", 宇航员 "astronaut", 眉宇 "brows", 气宇 "bearing". 宙 *zhòu* is "time", 宇 "space".

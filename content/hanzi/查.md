## Etymology
Originally a variant of 楂 — 木 "wood" with 且 (worn to 旦) — naming a wooden
raft, and also the hawthorn 山楂. It was borrowed for the unrelated verb "to
check, to look into," which is now its main use.

## Mnemonic
Poling a wooden 木 raft along, looking things over: to inspect.

## Notes
Senses: examine / inspect (检查, 调查, 查看), look up (查字典, 查资料),
find out (查明, 查获, 追查). Read *Zhā* as a surname and in place names
(查济). 查询 "to make inquiries."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 鴨: 鸟 (bird) supplies the meaning, 甲 *jiǎ*
supplies the sound, shifted to *yā*. Not in Shuowen with this exact
character, though ducks themselves are ancient; the character's structure
is uncontested regardless.

## Mnemonic
A bird 鸟, first 甲 into the water every time — a duck.

## Notes
鸭 anchors 鸭子 "duck" and 烤鸭 "roast duck" (most famously 北京烤鸭 "Peking
duck"). Distinguish visually from the phonetically similar 甲 and 押,
which share the 甲 phonetic but not the meaning.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 王 (here 玉, jade/precious-stone material) supplies the
meaning, 离 *lí* supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen.
璃 exists almost entirely to form 玻璃 "glass," and like its partner 玻,
carries no independent meaning of its own in modern use — its ultimate
origin may lie in a borrowed or transliterated term, but that is not
securely documented here.

## Mnemonic
A jade-like material 王, sound-spelled with 离 — glass.

## Notes
璃 never stands alone in ordinary Mandarin. 玻璃 is the everyday word;
琉璃 "coloured glaze/glass" is a separate, more decorative-arts register
using the same character.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning, and 宿 *sù* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's own definition is somewhat removed from the modern
sense — "disorder" or "to trample" — and the exact path from a tangled
thread to the modern "to shrink, to contract" is not something this
entry can trace with full confidence, hence medium rather than high.

The everyday modern sense is straightforward regardless: thread pulled
in on itself contracts — 缩小 ("to shrink"), 收缩 ("to contract"), 缩短
("to shorten").

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟 pulled in tight overnight, like something curling up to
sleep 宿: "缩," to shrink.

## Notes
缩短 ("to shorten," of time or length) and 缩小 ("to shrink," of size) both
build directly on this character's plain "to contract, become smaller"
sense.

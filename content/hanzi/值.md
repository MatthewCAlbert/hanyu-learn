## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 直 *zhí* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original sense, 措也 "to place, to set" — interchangeable
in classical texts with 置 — is about positioning something, not yet
"worth" or "value."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻, placed exactly where they belong, straight and true 直: in
their rightful position.

## Notes
"To be placed [at a post]" gives 值班 "to be on duty," while a separate
extension, "to be placed [against a price]," gives 价值 "value" and 值得
"to be worth [it]" — one root, two modern jobs.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional 鑑/鑒: 金 (metal) supplies the meaning — a bronze basin of water, then a bronze mirror — and 監 *jiān* supplies the sound; the earliest mirror was a basin of water (see 监). The simplified 鉴 compresses 監 on top, so the phonetic is only partly visible and is not declared here.

## Mnemonic
A metal 金 mirror held up to see yourself and judge.

## Notes
鉴定 "appraise", 借鉴 "draw lessons from", 鉴别, 鉴赏 "appreciate (art)". The "mirror/example to learn from" sense gives 借鉴.

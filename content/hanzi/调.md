## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 周 *zhōu* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 龢也 "harmony," describes bringing things — voices,
opinions, ingredients — into agreement through active adjustment.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 going all the way around 周 until everyone agrees: to adjust,
to harmonise.

## Notes
Two readings split by direction of action: *tiáo* "to adjust, harmonise"
(调整, 空调) versus *diào* "a tune; to transfer, to investigate" (调查,
调动) — bringing something into tune versus moving it into position.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 可 *kě* gives the sound (for
*hé*). Shuowen records 河 as the proper name of the Yellow River; the general
sense "river" is the later use.

## Mnemonic
A body of water 氵, its sound from 可: a river.

## Notes
The 可 phonetic also writes 何 *hé*, 荷 *hé*, 哥 *gē*, 歌 *gē*, 苛 *kē*.
Senses: river (河流, 运河, 河边), the Yellow River specifically (河西,
河套, 江河), and the Milky Way (银河, 天河). 河马 "hippopotamus."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 射 *shè* gives the sound
(for *xiè*). Shuowen: 辭去也 "to take one's leave" — the original sense was
declining or excusing oneself in words. From "to decline politely" grew both
"to thank" and, of flowers, "to wither / fall" (凋谢).

## Mnemonic
The parting words 讠 you say on your way out: thanks.

## Notes
The three senses — thank (谢谢), decline (谢绝), wither (花谢) — all descend
from "words of leave-taking." Also the surname 谢.

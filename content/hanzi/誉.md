## Etymology
Traditional 譽 is pictophonetic: 言 (speech) supplies the meaning — to praise, speak well of — and 與 *yǔ* supplies the sound. Simplification replaced 與 with 兴, a shape that is neither semantic nor phonetic here, so the sound clue is lost. Do not read 兴 "rise" into it.

## Mnemonic
Words 言 that make a person rise 兴 in reputation.

## Notes
荣誉, 名誉, 声誉, 誉为 "praised as". The same reduced 與 shape (兴) appears in 举.

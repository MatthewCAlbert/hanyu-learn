## Etymology
Oracle-bone 因 shows 大 "a person" lying spread out on 囗, a mat or bedding —
the original word for "mat," now written 茵. The graph was borrowed early for
the abstract senses "to rely on, to follow from," and hence "cause, reason."

## Mnemonic
A person 大 resting on the mat 囗 that supports them: what a thing rests on —
its cause.

## Notes
茵 "cushion, mat" carries the pictured sense. Modern 因: cause / reason
(因为, 原因, 因素), on the basis of (因此, 因地制宜).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning, 隹 *zhuī* supplies the sound,
shifted to *duī*. Not in Shuowen. The core image — earth piled up — is
stable across every extended use, from a literal mound to any pile or mass
of things heaped together.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 piled high enough that a bird 隹 could perch on top.

## Notes
堆 doubles as noun and classifier: 一堆 (a pile of), 土堆 "mound of earth,"
垃圾堆 "rubbish heap." Shares 隹 with 谁, 推, 推, 唯 — a phonetic that carries
sound loosely across several unrelated finals.

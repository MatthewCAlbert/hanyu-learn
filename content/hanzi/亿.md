## Etymology
Simplified from 億. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; the traditional phonetic
意 *yì* is replaced in the simplified form by 乙 *yǐ*, a rough phonetic
substitute for *yì*. The number
sense — a very large count — is old (億 also once meant "to be content,
settled").

## Mnemonic
More people 亻 than can be counted: a hundred million.

## Notes
Senses: one hundred million (一亿, 亿万, 十几亿人口), and, in classical
usage, simply "a countless number" (亿兆). Note the scale: 万 (10⁴), 亿
(10⁸), 万亿 (10¹²).

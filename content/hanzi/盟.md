## Etymology
Shuowen's 盟 is an oath ritual: 囧 (a lit window, "bright") over 血 (blood), the blood of a sacrificed animal. In the modern graph 血 became 皿 (a vessel) and 囧 became 明, which keeps both the "bright, witnessed" idea and the sound *míng* → *méng*. So 明 is both the sound and a meaning clue.

## Mnemonic
An oath sworn over a bowl 皿 of blood in the bright 明 sight of the gods.

## Notes
联盟 "alliance", 同盟, 加盟 "join (a league)", 盟友 "ally", 盟约 "oath". 盟 and 明 differ in tone and the nasal initial only.

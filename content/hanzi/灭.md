## Etymology
Traditional 滅 is pictophonetic: 氵 (water) for meaning, 烕 for sound; the older 烕 is itself "fire + axe". Simplified 灭 is a popular variant that replaces the whole thing by 一 over 火, which as a picture reads as "a covering put over fire". That reading works for the shape but is not shown to be why the variant arose.

## Mnemonic
Cover 一 the flames 火 completely to put them out.

## Notes
消灭, 毁灭, 灭火, 灭亡, 灭绝. Not a pictophonetic in its simplified form.

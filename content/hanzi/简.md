## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning; 间 (simplified 閒)
supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 牒也, is "a writing tablet" — bamboo
slips were the standard writing surface before paper, bound together
into books. Each individual slip carried only a little text, which is
the root of "brief, simple."

## Mnemonic
A single bamboo slip ⺮, with just enough space 间 for a few words:
brief, to the point.

## Notes
"A single, brief bamboo slip" generalised into "simple, concise" (简单,
简直 "simply, just") and, separately, into "a brief written note" (简历
"résumé" — literally "a brief record") — brevity by necessity becoming
brevity by style.

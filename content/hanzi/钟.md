## Etymology
钟 merges two traditional characters: 鐘 "bell, clock" (金 + 童 *tóng* for
sound) and 鍾 "a wine vessel; to gather" (金 + 重 *zhòng* for sound). The
simplified form writes both with 钅 "metal" and 中 *zhōng* for the sound.

## Mnemonic
A cast-metal 钅 bell — and the clock that took over its job of marking time.

## Notes
Senses: a bell (钟声, 警钟, 编钟), a clock (钟表, 闹钟), clock time (五点钟,
十分钟, 一刻钟), and — from 鍾 — to concentrate feeling (钟情, 钟爱,
一见钟情). 钟头 "an hour."

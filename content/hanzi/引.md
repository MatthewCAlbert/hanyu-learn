## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 弓 (a bow) with 丨 (a straight stroke marking the
drawn bowstring, pulled taut) — "to draw a bow open." The whole character
is that single vivid action.

"To draw, to lead, to attract" (吸引, 引起) all generalize the same
pulling-toward motion from a bowstring to anything drawn along a similar
line: attention, a crowd, a conclusion.

## Mnemonic
A bow 弓 pulled back into a taut straight line 丨: to "引," draw or lead.

## Notes
引 recurs as the first half of both 引起 (to give rise to) and 吸引 (to
attract) — worth learning as a shared root, since both extend the same
"drawing toward" image in different directions.

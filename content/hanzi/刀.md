## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen already calls it 象形, "a picture": the oracle-bone
and bronze forms show a single-edged blade with its handle, the curved
stroke tracing the spine of the knife and the short diagonal marking the
cutting edge.

## Mnemonic
A blade with its handle, seen from the side: a knife.

## Notes
刀 is a small, closed radical — as 刂, its compressed left-side form, it
reliably marks cutting actions: 切, 割, 刻, 判 all group under it.

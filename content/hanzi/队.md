## Etymology
Simplified from 隊. 阝 is 阜 "a slope," giving the meaning; 㒸 gave the sound.
Shuowen: 从高隊也 "to fall from a height" — this was the original of 坠 "to
drop." The graph was borrowed for "a line / column of people," and so "a team,
a squad, a group"; 坠 (土 added) took back "to fall." Simplification writes the
right side as 人.

## Mnemonic
People 人 filed along the slope 阝: a column, a team.

## Notes
Senses: a line / queue (排队), a team / squad (球队, 队伍, 军队), the Young
Pioneers (少先队). Measure word for people in formation: 一队士兵.

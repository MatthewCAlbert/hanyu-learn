## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 青 *qīng* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 澄水之皃 "the look of water settled clear."

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 left to settle until you can see through it: clear.

## Notes
The 青 phonetic also writes 情 *qíng*, 请 / 請 *qǐng*, 晴 *qíng*, 睛 *jīng*,
精 *jīng*. Senses: clear / clean / pure (清水, 清洁, 清晰), quiet (清静,
冷清), settle up (清理, 结清, 清账), distinct (清楚, 分清). 清朝
"the Qing dynasty."

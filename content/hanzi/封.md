## Etymology
The early graph shows a hand 寸 planting a tree or heaping soil (the doubled
土 now written 圭) to raise a boundary mound — the act of granting a lord his
land. From "to mark off and close a territory" grew "to seal shut" and "an
envelope."

## Mnemonic
A hand 寸 banking up earth 圭 to close off a border: to seal.

## Notes
Senses: seal / close up (封口, 密封, 封存), an envelope and its classifier
(信封, 一封信), confer a fief or title (封王, 分封, 加官进封), blockade
(封锁, 封路, 查封), and "feudal" (封建). 封面 "front cover."

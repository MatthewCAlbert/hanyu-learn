## Etymology
Shuowen's account of the traditional form 嚴 describes urgent commands
being called out — 吅 (two mouths, insistent calling) with 厰 supplying
the sound. The modern simplified shape 严 (亚 over 厂) compresses this so
heavily that neither of Shuowen's original components is cleanly
recoverable in it, so this entry does not assert a specific modern
decomposition and keeps confidence low.

"Urgent, insistent commands" generalized to "strict, severe" (严格, 严重)
— the tone of someone issuing orders that must be obeyed without
argument.

## Mnemonic
Orders barked out so sharply they demand obedience: "严," strict.

## Notes
严格 ("strict") and 严重 (covered elsewhere in this app — "serious,
grave") both carry this character's core sense of intensity, applied to a
person's manner in one case and to a situation's gravity in the other.

## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 尸 (a reclining body shape) with 毛 (hair, fur)
drawn behind it — literally hair trailing behind a body: a tail. Shuowen
also notes the character's sound echoes 微 ("tiny"), fitting for the
slender, tapering shape of a tail.

## Mnemonic
Hair 毛 trailing out behind a body 尸: "尾," a tail.

## Notes
尾巴 (tail, pairing 尾 with the sound-only suffix 巴, covered elsewhere in
this app) and 结尾 ("the ending," lit. "tied-off tail") both extend this
character from the literal animal part to "the trailing end" of anything.

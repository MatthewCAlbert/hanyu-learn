## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 般 *bān* supplies the
sound. The meaning, 移也 "to move, to relocate," is unchanged from its
earliest attestations to the modern word.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 carrying a whole household 般 to a new place.

## Notes
搬 covers moving both people (搬家 "to move house") and objects (搬东西
"to carry/move things") without distinguishing them — the hand doing the
carrying is the same either way.

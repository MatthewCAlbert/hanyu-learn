## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 (woman) supplies the meaning; 台 *tái/yí* supplies the
sound — recall from earlier in this dataset that 台's own early sense was
tied to 胎 "fetus, to be pregnant." Shuowen's gloss, 女之初也, "a woman's
beginning," together with that phonetic link, points toward pregnancy
and new life as the root image behind "beginning, to start."

## Mnemonic
A woman 女, at the very first stage 台 of something new: the beginning.

## Notes
始 pairs with 终 "end" as the classical beginning/end couplet — 开始 "to
begin," 自始至终 "from start to finish," 原始 "original, primitive"
(literally "at the very first source").

## Etymology
Shuowen analyses 司 as 后 "sovereign" written in reverse — the minister who
carries out affairs on the ruler's behalf, "away outside." From "to manage on
another's authority" grew "to take charge of, to attend to" and "a bureau, a
department."

## Mnemonic
A mirror image of the ruler 后: the one who runs things for him.

## Notes
Senses: to take charge of (司机 "one in charge of the machine — a driver," 各司
其职), a department (司法 "the judiciary," 外交部…司), and 公司 "a company."

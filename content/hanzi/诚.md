## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (from 言, speech) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition is simply "trustworthy" — and 成 *chéng* supplies the sound.

Words that can be relied upon generalize to the person who speaks them:
"sincere, honest" (诚实, 真诚) — speech and character both held to the
same standard of trustworthiness.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 that always comes true 成, exactly as promised: "诚," sincere.

## Notes
诚实 ("honest") and 真诚 ("sincere, genuine") both build on this
character's core "trustworthy speech/character" sense; 诚 also survives
in the formal closing 诚挚 ("sincerely," in letters).

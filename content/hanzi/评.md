## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 平 *píng* supplies the
sound and, fittingly, also contributes a sense of "level, fair" — to
评 something is to weigh it and pass fair, level-headed judgment through
speech.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠, weighed out level and fair 平: a judgment, a review.

## Notes
评 covers both formal assessment (评估, 评选, 评定) and everyday
commentary (评论, 好评/差评 "positive/negative reviews") — always
speech that renders a judgment on something.

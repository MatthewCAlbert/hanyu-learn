## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 白 "white, bright" gives the meaning; 勺 *sháo* gives the sound.
The original word is *dì* "bright, clear," and by extension "a bullseye" — the
bright centre of a target. That survives in 目的 "aim / target," 的确
"definitely," 一语中的 "hit the mark."

The everyday particle *de* (possessive and attributive: 我的, 红的) is a much
later borrowing: this ready-made graph was assigned to an unstressed
grammatical syllable it has no semantic connection to.

## Mnemonic
The bright white 白 spot at the centre of a target.

## Notes
Three readings: *de* (particle), *dì* (目的), *dí* (的确). The particle use is
one of the highest-frequency characters in the language and purely
grammatical.

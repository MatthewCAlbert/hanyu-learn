## Etymology
A late character (not in Shuowen), read as 大 "big" over 長 (镸) "long" — a
big, long wrapper that slips over something. From "a cover / sheath" grew "to
slip over, to harness," "a set" (a group of matched things), and figuratively
"a routine, a convention" (客套, 老一套).

## Mnemonic
A long 长 loose wrap thrown over the whole thing 大: a cover — a set.

## Notes
Senses: cover / case (手套 glove, 信封套, 避孕套), slip on / harness (套上,
套车), a set (一套房子, 一套餐具, 成套), formula / trick (圈套, 套话, 套近乎).
Measure word for matched sets.

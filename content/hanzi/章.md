## Etymology
Ideographic. 音 (music, sound) with 十 — Shuowen explains 十 here as "the
end of counting," standing in for any endpoint: when a piece of music
reaches its natural stopping point, that unit is called one 章, "a
chapter, a movement." Confidence is medium because reading 十 as an
abstract symbol for "completion" is a somewhat indirect gloss rather than
a directly pictured idea.

## Mnemonic
Music 音, played through to its natural end 十: one complete chapter.

## Notes
"A complete section of music" generalised into "a section of writing"
(文章, 章节) and, separately, into "an official seal/stamp" (印章) — a
mark that certifies something as complete and authorised.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 建 *jiàn* "to set up, to build"
gives the sound and a hint of sense — a well-built person. Shuowen: 伉也
"strong, sturdy."

## Mnemonic
A solidly built 建 person 亻: robust.

## Notes
The 建 phonetic also writes 键 / 鍵 *jiàn* (key), 毽 *jiàn*. Senses: healthy /
strong (健康, 健身), vigorous (健步, 稳健), good at (健谈 "a good talker").

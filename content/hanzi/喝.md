## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 曷 *hé* gives the sound. Shuowen's
gloss points to "hoarse" — the original sense was *hè* "to shout oneself
hoarse" (喝彩, 吆喝). "To drink" (*hē*) is a later colloquial use of the same
mouth-graph.

## Mnemonic
The mouth 口 at work — shouting, or drinking.

## Notes
The 曷 phonetic also writes 渴 *kě* (thirsty), 歇 *xiē* (rest), 竭 *jié*
(exhaust), 揭 *jiē* (lift off). Two readings: *hē* "drink," *hè* "shout."

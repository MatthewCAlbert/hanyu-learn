## Etymology
口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 乞 *qǐ* gives the sound (a loose fit for *chī*).
Shuowen's 吃 meant "to stammer" — 口吃 still keeps that. The "to eat" sense
belonged to a separate character 喫; in the simplified standard 吃 absorbed it
and 喫 fell out of use.

## Mnemonic
Working the mouth 口 — over food.

## Notes
Idioms: 吃苦 "endure hardship," 吃惊 "be startled," 吃亏 "come off worse,"
吃力 "strenuous." 好吃 "tasty" (*hǎochī*) vs "gluttonous" (*hàochī*).

## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: the character represents a hand or forearm
used as a measuring reference — the human body was the original
measuring instrument, with 寸 marking a small unit near the wrist pulse
and 尺 marking a longer span (ten 寸) up the forearm. As an atomic
pictograph, it has no separate semantic or phonetic component to name.

## Mnemonic
A forearm held out flat, its own length serving as the measure: "尺," a
Chinese foot.

## Notes
尺 survives as both the traditional unit of length (about a third of a
meter) and the everyday word for a ruler (尺子) — the tool named directly
after the body part it once was.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree/wood) supplies the meaning, 亥 *hài* supplies the
sound, shifted to *hé*. Not in Shuowen. The original object is a fruit
pit/stone — the hard woody centre of a fruit — which extended metaphorically
to "core, nucleus" (核心) and, in the 20th century, to the atomic nucleus
(核 "nuclear"), and separately to "to check, to verify" (核实, 审核) via the
idea of getting to the hard, verified centre of a matter.

## Mnemonic
The hard woody 木 pit at the very centre of a peach.

## Notes
Two readings: *hé* is standard (果核 "fruit pit," 核心 "core," 核电站
"nuclear power plant"); *hú* survives in a few colloquial words like 煤核儿
"unburned coal lump." The "verify" sense (核对, 核实) and the "pit/nucleus"
sense are the same character doing very different jobs.

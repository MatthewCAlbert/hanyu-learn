## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen reads both halves as abbreviations: 聿 stands in for
律 "law, statute," and 廴 stands in for 廷 "the royal court." Put together,
"to establish the court's law" — 立朝律也 — the founding act of setting a
government's rules in place.

## Mnemonic
A brush 聿, extending 廴 its authority across the land: to establish.

## Notes
"To establish laws for a court" broadened completely to "to found, to set
up" anything — 建立, 建设, 建筑 — with no trace of the original legal,
governmental sense left in ordinary use.

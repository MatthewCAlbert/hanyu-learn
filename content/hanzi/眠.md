## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 目 (eye) supplies the meaning, 民 *mín* supplies the sound,
shifted to *mián*. The character is closely related to 瞑 (eyes closing in
sleep or dizziness); 眠 fixed onto the plain "to sleep" sense while 瞑 kept
the more figurative "dazed, dizzy" uses.

## Mnemonic
An eye 目 closing for the whole of the people's 民 rest.

## Notes
眠 is more literary/formal than 睡: 睡眠 "sleep [noun, clinical/formal],"
失眠 "insomnia," 冬眠 "hibernation." Everyday speech favours 睡觉 for the
act of sleeping itself.

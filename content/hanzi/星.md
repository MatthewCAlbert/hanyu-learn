## Etymology
Pictophonetic. The old form has 晶 — three suns, standing for points of light
twinkling — as the semantic, with 生 *shēng* for the sound. The 晶 was reduced
to a single 日. Meaning: a star, a point of light.

## Mnemonic
A speck of daylight 日 born 生 into the night sky.

## Notes
The 生 phonetic also writes 姓 *xìng*, 性 *xìng*, 牲 *shēng*. 星期 "week"
(lit. "star period") is named for the seven classical luminaries.

## Etymology
Simplified from 動. 力 "strength, effort" gives the meaning; 重 *zhòng* gave
the sound. Shuowen: 作也 "to act, to set in motion." Simplification replaces
重 with 云, a bare shape-sign, so the sound clue is lost.

## Mnemonic
Putting force 力 behind something to get it going: to move.

## Notes
The lost phonetic 重 also writes 董 *dǒng*, 懂 *dǒng*. Senses: move (动作,
运动), touch / stir (感动, 动人), start to (动身, 动手), "at every turn"
(动不动就…).

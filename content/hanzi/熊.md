## Etymology
Pictophonetic. Shuowen describes 熊 as a bear-like beast that lives in the
mountains and hibernates in winter, and analyses it as built on 能 (itself
originally a picture of a bear, later borrowed for the abstract sense
"ability") with 炎 reduced to 灬 for sound. The bottom 灬 is therefore a
worn-down sound component from 炎, not literal "footprints" or "fire," a
folk reading that is common but not what Shuowen records.

## Mnemonic
The bear-shape 能 with its four paw-prints 灬 left behind in the snow.

## Notes
能 kept the "ability, capability" sense in modern Mandarin (可能, 能力) while
熊 preserves the original "bear" meaning with 灬 added to distinguish them —
a rare case of a phonetic addition splitting one graph's senses into two
characters.

## Etymology
The traditional 雜 was pictophonetic: 衣 (clothing/cloth) supplied the
meaning; 集 supplied the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 五彩相會, "the five
colours coming together," pictures dyed fabric with many colours mixed
into one piece — the direct ancestor of "mixed, miscellaneous."

Simplification replaced both components with the unrelated 九 and kept
木, producing a shape with no recoverable link to cloth or colour.
Confidence is low, and no component is declared, because the modern 杂
cannot be read back into either half of the traditional character.

## Mnemonic
Nine 九 different kinds of wood 木, all thrown together: a mixed bag.

## Notes
"Many colours mixed into one cloth" generalised completely into "mixed,
miscellaneous" of any kind — 复杂 "complicated," 杂志 "magazine"
(literally a "miscellany" of articles), 杂技 "acrobatics" (a mixed
variety show).

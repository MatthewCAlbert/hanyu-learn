## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning and 失 *shī* supplies the sound (*shī* → *diē*). Shuowen glosses it as to stumble. "To fall" and "(prices) to drop" (下跌) follow. 失 also happens to mean "to lose", which fits the "losing one's footing" picture, but formally it is the phonetic.

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 that loses 失 its ground: a tumble.

## Notes
跌倒 "fall over", 跌价 "drop in price", 下跌 "fall", 跌宕 "ups and downs", 跌打损伤 "bruises and sprains".

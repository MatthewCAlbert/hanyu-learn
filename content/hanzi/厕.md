## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 厂 (a building or shelter, standing in for 广) supplies the meaning and 則 (simplified 则) *zé* supplies the sound (*zé* → *cè*). Shuowen glosses 廁 as a "clean" place — the side room for privy use. "By the side of" (as in 厕身) is a related early sense.

## Mnemonic
A building 厂 at the side, ruled by custom 则.

## Notes
则 is sound only. 厕所 "toilet", 公厕 "public toilet", 如厕 "go to the toilet" (polite), 茅厕. Series: 侧 *cè*, 测 *cè*, 厕 *cè*.

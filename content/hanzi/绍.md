## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (糸) "silk thread" gives the meaning; 召 *zhào* gives the
sound (for *shào*). Shuowen: 繼也 "to join on, to continue" — knotting one
thread to the next. From "to link" comes the sense in 介绍 "to introduce"
(to link two parties).

## Mnemonic
Tying a new thread 纟 onto the end of the old one: carrying on, linking up.

## Notes
The 召 phonetic also writes 招 *zhāo*, 照 *zhào*, 超 *chāo*, 昭 *zhāo*. 绍 now
appears almost only in 介绍 and the place-name 绍兴.

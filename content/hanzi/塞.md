## Etymology
Shuowen lists 塞 as "to block, separate", written with 土 (earth) and an abbreviated upper element related to 寒. That 土 is the meaning component fits "to plug with earth", but the identity and role of the upper element is disputed, and the readings (*sāi*, *sè*, *sài*) point to older sound drift. Not securely attested beyond 土.

## Mnemonic
Stuffing earth 土 into a gap under a roof 宀 to block it.

## Notes
Three readings: *sāi* "to stuff" (塞子, 堵塞 is *dǔsè*), *sè* "to block" in written compounds (阻塞), and *sài* "fortress, frontier" (边塞). Appears in many place names in transliteration (塞尔维亚).

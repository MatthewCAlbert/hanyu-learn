## Etymology
Traditional 隱 is pictophonetic: 阝/阜 (a mound or hill, a place to hide behind) for meaning, 㥯 *yǐn* for sound. Simplification replaced 㥯 with the unrelated 急 *jí*, so the sound clue is lost; 急 "urgent" means nothing here.

## Mnemonic
Hide behind a hill 阝 when something is urgent 急.

## Notes
隐藏, 隐私, 隐患, 隐蔽. 稳 *wěn* (穩) keeps a different part of the same series. 阝 on the left is the "mound" 阜 (not the right-hand 邑).

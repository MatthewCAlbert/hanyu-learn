## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 擇: 扌 (hand) supplied the meaning
— to pick out by hand — and 睪 *yì* supplied the sound.

**Simplification reduced 睪 to an unrecoverable fragment**, the same
reduction seen in 译 (covered elsewhere in this app), leaving no reliable
sound clue toward *zé* in the modern shape — this entry declares only
the surviving semantic 扌 and keeps confidence at medium.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 reaching in to pick out just the right one: "择," to choose.

## Notes
选择 (to choose, covered elsewhere in this app) pairs 择 with the
near-synonym 选 — both halves independently mean "to select," reinforcing
each other into the standard modern word for choosing.

## Etymology
攵 (攴) "a hand with a stick — to act on" gives the meaning; 己 *jǐ* stands
next to it and carries the sound (for *gǎi*). Shuowen: 更也 "to alter." Some
read 己 as a kneeling child being disciplined into new behaviour.

## Mnemonic
A corrective hand 攵 laid on 己: things get changed.

## Notes
Senses: change / alter (改变, 修改, 改名), correct (改正, 改错, 批改), reform
(改革, 改造, 改进), redo (改天, 改口). 改善 "to improve"; 改善 vs 改良.

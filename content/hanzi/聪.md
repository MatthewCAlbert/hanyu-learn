## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 聰: 耳 (ear) supplies the meaning, 悤 *cōng*
supplied the sound in the traditional form. Shuowen's gloss 察也 "to
perceive keenly" ties sharp hearing to sharp perception generally.
Simplification replaced 悤 with 总, so the modern right-hand side is a
graphic stand-in rather than a live sound clue.

## Mnemonic
Ears 耳 that catch everything 总, all at once — sharp perception.

## Notes
聪 anchors 聪明 "intelligent, clever" — one of the most common
compliments in Mandarin — and 失聪 "to lose one's hearing," which keeps
the character's original, literal "hearing" sense alive alongside the
figurative "clever."

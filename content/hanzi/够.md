## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 多 "much, many" gives the meaning; 句 *gōu* gives the sound
(for *gòu*). Also written 夠 with the parts reversed. Meaning: to be enough,
to suffice.

## Mnemonic
Enough 多 of it, and then some: 够.

## Notes
Senses: sufficient (足够, 够用, 不够), reach / stretch to (够得着, 够不到),
and the colloquial intensifier "quite, really" (够累的, 够朋友, 够呛).
天气够冷的 "it's plenty cold."

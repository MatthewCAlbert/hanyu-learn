## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen explicitly calls it 象形, "a picture" — of a rabbit
crouched back on its haunches with its short tail visible behind. The long
ears, round body, and stub tail are all recognisable in the older seal
forms.

## Mnemonic
A rabbit crouched low, ears back, tail a small dot 丶.

## Notes
兔 names the zodiac's Rabbit year (兔年) and, in idiom, stands for speed and
timidity: 兔子 "rabbit," 龟兔赛跑 "the tortoise and the hare," 狡兔三窟 "a
cunning rabbit has three burrows" (always keep a backup plan).

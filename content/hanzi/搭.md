## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to hang over, to attach — and 荅 *dā* supplies the sound. A late graph, in the additions to Shuowen. The extended senses "to take (a ride), to match, to put up (a structure)" come from "to place on top of".

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 hooking a cloth over a pole to answer 荅 the need.

## Notes
荅 is sound only. 搭档 "partner", 搭配 "match", 搭乘 "board (a ride)", 搭建 "set up", 搭讪 "strike up a conversation".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen defines it exactly
as "drawing breath inward" — and 及 *jí* supplies the sound.

"To inhale" (呼吸, breathing) extends naturally to "to draw in, to absorb"
more generally: 吸引 (to attract, lit. "to draw in and pull"), 吸收 (to
absorb).

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 reaching 及 to draw air all the way in: "吸," to inhale.

## Notes
呼吸 (to breathe) pairs 吸 with its opposite 呼 (to exhale/call out) — a
classic paired-opposite compound naming the whole cycle by naming both
halves.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 邏: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, 罗
(traditional 羅, "net") supplies the sound, shifted to *luó* — and 罗's own
sense "to cast a net over an area" echoes meaning: patrolling casts a net
of watchfulness over a territory. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
Moving 辶 back and forth, casting a watchful net 罗 over the whole area.

## Notes
逻 anchors 巡逻 "to patrol" and, in a modern coinage, 逻辑 "logic" — a
20th-century phonetic loan from the English word, reusing 逻 purely for
its sound and unrelated to the patrol sense.

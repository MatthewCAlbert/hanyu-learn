## Etymology
Ideographic. 乂 (crossed lines, marking an open, sparse weave) over 布/巾
(cloth) — the picture is fabric woven loosely, with gaps between the
threads. That "sparse, few and far between" sense is preserved directly
in 稀 (禾 + 希, "sparse crops"), which uses 希 purely as a phonetic and
confirms the reading. From "rare, uncommon" the meaning extended to "to
hope for" — wanting something precisely because it is scarce.

## Mnemonic
Cloth 布, woven with gaps 乂 you can see clean through: sparse, and so,
worth hoping for.

## Notes
希 split its two senses across two characters: 稀 kept "sparse, rare" in
the literal, concrete register (稀少, 稀疏), while 希 itself specialised
almost entirely into "to hope, to wish" (希望).

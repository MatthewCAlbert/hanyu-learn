## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (from 阜, a mound or hill — the semantic component in
陆, 阴, 阳) supplies the meaning "to go down (a slope)," and 夅 supplies
the sound, itself a picture of feet descending.

"To descend" (下降, 降落) extends naturally to "to surrender" (投降, xiáng
reading) — laying down arms is a figurative descent from a position of
resistance.

## Mnemonic
Feet 夅 stepping down a mound 阝: "降," to descend — or to lay down arms and
surrender.

## Notes
Two readings split by sense: *jiàng* for the everyday "to descend/lower"
(降低, 降雨); *xiáng* only in the specific sense "to surrender/subdue"
(投降, 降服).

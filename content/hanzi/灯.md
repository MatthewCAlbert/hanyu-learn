## Etymology
Simplified from 燈. 火 "fire" gives the meaning; the traditional phonetic is
登 *dēng*, which the simplified form replaces with the simpler 丁 *dīng* (a
near match for *dēng*).

## Mnemonic
A small fire 火 kept for its light: a lamp.

## Notes
Senses: lamp / light (电灯, 台灯, 路灯, 红绿灯), and light-emitting devices
generally (灯泡 bulb, 灯笼 lantern, 探照灯 searchlight). 灯火通明
"ablaze with lights."

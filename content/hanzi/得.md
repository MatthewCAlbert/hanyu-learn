## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph shows a hand (又) picking up a cowrie shell (貝), often
with 彳 "road" added: finding money on the way — "to get, to obtain." Shuowen:
行有所得也 "to gain something as one goes." The modern middle element 旦 is a
worn-down 貝.

## Mnemonic
A hand 寸 on the road 彳 closing over a shell: to get.

## Notes
Three readings: *dé* "to get / gain" (得到, 得奖), *děi* "must / have to"
(得走了), and toneless *de*, the structural particle before a complement
(跑得快, 好得很).

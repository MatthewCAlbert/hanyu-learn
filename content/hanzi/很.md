## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 彳 "to step, to go" gives the meaning; 艮 *gèn* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 不聽从也 "to defy, to go against" — the original sense, still visible
in 狠 "ruthless" and 恨 "to hate." The everyday degree-adverb use ("very") is a
bleached, grammaticalised sense with the "defiant" flavour worn away.

## Mnemonic
Going 彳 stubbornly against 艮 the current — pushed to an extreme: "very."

## Notes
The 艮 phonetic also writes 恨 *hèn*, 狠 *hěn*, 痕 *hén*, 跟 *gēn*, 根 *gēn*.
As a bare degree adverb, 很 often carries little real emphasis — it is largely
a grammatical filler before a predicate adjective.

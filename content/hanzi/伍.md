## Etymology
Shuowen: 伍 is 亻 (person) with 五 (five), a squad of five soldiers — the basic unit of the ancient Chinese army. 五 is also the sound. From a squad come "ranks, troops" and, in compounds, the numeral five in finance (the form 伍 is the formal numeral five on cheques).

## Mnemonic
Five 五 people 亻 in a squad.

## Notes
队伍 "team, troops", 入伍 "enlist", 退伍 "be demobilised", 落伍 "fall behind", 伍 as the financial numeral.

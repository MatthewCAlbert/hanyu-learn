## Etymology
A person (大) with the region above the head marked by a line (一, in the
oracle bone a full circle). Shuowen: 顛也 "the crown of the head; the highest,
with nothing above it." From "the space overhead" come "sky," "heaven /
nature," and "a day."

## Mnemonic
The line drawn right above a person's head 大: the sky.

## Notes
Senses: sky (天空), day (今天, 三天), season (夏天), weather / nature (天气,
老天). 天 caps 大 the way 立 caps a standing figure.

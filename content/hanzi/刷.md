## Etymology
Ideographic, and a character absent from Shuowen: 尸 (a flat, body-like
shape, here likely standing for a surface) with 巾 (cloth) and 刂 (a
blade/scraping tool) — cloth and blade together scraping a surface clean.
Because the exact contribution of 尸 is not confidently attested, this
entry keeps confidence low and declares only 刂 as a real, if only
loosely supporting, component.

"To brush, to scrub" (刷牙, to brush teeth) is the base sense; "to swipe"
(刷卡, to swipe a card) is a modern extension built on the same quick,
repeated scraping motion.

## Mnemonic
A cloth 巾 dragged by a blade-edge tool 刂 across a surface: to "刷,"
brush or scrub.

## Notes
刷卡 (to swipe a card) and 刷牙 (to brush teeth) look unrelated in English
but share the same Chinese image: a quick, repeated back-and-forth motion
across a surface.

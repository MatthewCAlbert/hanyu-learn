## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (心) "heart" gives the meaning; 夬 *guài* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 喜也 "pleased, gratified" — the original sense, kept in 快乐 "happy"
and 痛快 "satisfying." From "a heart at ease" came "quick, prompt" and "sharp"
(刀很快).

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 unclenched 夬: pleased — and light on its feet.

## Notes
The 夬 phonetic also writes 决 / 決 *jué*, 缺 *quē*, 诀 / 訣 *jué*. Senses of
快: fast (快车), soon (快要…了), sharp (刀快), pleased (快乐).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (cowrie shell — money, valuables in the archaic economy;
the same semantic component as in 财, 购, 贵) supplies the meaning, and 化
*huà* supplies the sound.

"Goods, merchandise" is a direct extension of "wealth, valuables" — the
things money can be converted (化) into and traded for.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 that has changed 化 form into something you can carry away: "货,"
goods.

## Notes
货 pairs with measure-word-like uses in 一批货 (a batch of goods) and appears
in the very productive compound 货物 (merchandise) and 售货 (to sell goods).

## Etymology
Two 人 on 土 "the ground" — two people seated facing each other on the earth.
The bronze graph shows exactly this. From "to sit" grew "to ride / travel by"
(坐车, 坐飞机 — you sit in the vehicle) and the classical "on account of"
(坐罪 "to be punished for").

## Mnemonic
Two people 人 settled on the ground 土: sitting.

## Notes
坐 "sit" vs 座 "a seat / stand" (坐 + 广): 请坐 but 座位. As a resultative,
坐下 "sit down."

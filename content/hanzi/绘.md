## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (silk, thread) supplies the meaning — embroidered, multicoloured cloth — and 會 (simplified 会) *huì* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as multicoloured embroidery. The extension is "to paint, to draw".

## Mnemonic
Colours on silk 纟 which come together 会.

## Notes
会 is both a good sound and a suggestive meaning (gathering colours), but the formal analysis is phonetic. 绘画 "painting", 绘制 "draw up", 描绘 "depict", 绘声绘色 "vividly".

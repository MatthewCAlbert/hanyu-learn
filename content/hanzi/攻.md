## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 攵 (a hand with a stick, to strike) supplies the meaning and 工 *gōng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as to strike. "To attack" and then "to work hard on, to study" (攻读) follow.

## Mnemonic
A stick-hand 攵 working 工 at the wall: an assault.

## Notes
工 is sound only. Series: 功 *gōng*, 攻 *gōng*, 红 *hóng*, 江 *jiāng*, 空 *kōng*. 攻击 "attack", 进攻, 攻读 "study for a degree", 攻关 "tackle a key problem".

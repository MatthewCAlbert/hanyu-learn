## Etymology
Pictographic — a net: a frame with the mesh drawn as crossing threads inside.
Shuowen credits its invention to Fu Xi, "who knotted cords to fish with."

## Mnemonic
A mesh strung inside a frame: a net.

## Notes
网 is Kangxi radical 122; as a top component it flattens to 罒 (罗, 罚, 置,
罪). Modern senses: a net (鱼网, 球网), a network, and — the everyday one —
the internet (上网, 网上, 网站, 网友).

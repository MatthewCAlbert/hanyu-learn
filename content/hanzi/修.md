## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 彡 (a component marking ornamentation, bristles, fine
lines) supplies the meaning; 攸 *yōu* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss,
飾也 "to adorn, to decorate," describes removing dirt and adding
ornamental colour — grooming and polishing something into good order.

## Mnemonic
Fine brushstrokes 彡, applied leisurely 攸, until everything looks
polished and cared for.

## Notes
"To groom, to decorate" broadened into "to repair, to build, to study" —
修理, 修建, 修养 — the common thread being deliberate, careful
improvement of something over time.

## Etymology
The traditional 產 was pictophonetic: 生 (to be born, to grow — itself a
pictograph of a sprouting plant) supplied the meaning; an abbreviated form
of 彥 supplied the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 生也, "to give birth, to grow,"
is unchanged in the modern word.

Simplification cut the semantic 生 entirely, keeping only the phonetic
portion (regularised to 亠丷厂). Confidence is medium, and no component is
declared, because — like 总 and 亲 elsewhere in this dataset — what
remains in the modern shape is the sound-carrying half, not the
meaning-carrying one; declaring 生 as semantic here would describe a
character that is not actually on the page.

## Mnemonic
New growth 丷, breaking through the ground 厂: something is born,
something is produced.

## Notes
产 joins 总 and 亲 as a third case in this dataset of the *meaning*-side
component being the one simplification cut, the reverse of the more
familiar pattern (过, 边) where the *sound*-side is lost instead.

## Etymology
Traditional 艷 is built on 豐 ("abundant, full") — beauty here begins as a
kind of richness or fullness rather than a specific feature. The
simplified 艳 reinterprets the top as 丰 ("lush, abundant" — a close
relative of 豐) over 色 ("colour, appearance"), which reads naturally as
"abundant colour/appearance" even though it is a simplification-era
recombination rather than Shuowen's own account of the traditional
structure.

## Mnemonic
Abundant colour 丰色 — beauty so rich it can't be missed.

## Notes
艳 anchors 鲜艳 "brightly coloured, vivid," 艳丽 "gorgeous, resplendent,"
and, in a more loaded register, 艳遇 "a chance romantic encounter" — colour
and allure sharing one character throughout.

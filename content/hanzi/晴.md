## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 日 "sun" gives the meaning; 青 *qīng* gives the sound (for
*qíng*). The earlier graph 夝 wrote it with 夕 "evening" ("the rain stops and
the stars come out"); the sun form is later.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 back out after the rain: clear.

## Notes
The 青 phonetic also writes 清 *qīng*, 情 *qíng*, 请 *qǐng*, 睛 *jīng*, 精
*jīng*. Senses: fine / clear / cloudless (晴天, 晴朗, 天晴了, 放晴). 晴雨表
"a barometer"; 晴空万里 "a vast clear sky."

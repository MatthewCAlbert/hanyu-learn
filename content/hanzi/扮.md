## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 分 *fēn* ("to divide, a
portion/role") supplies the sound and here also an echo of meaning — to
play a part is to take on an allotted portion, a role divided out among a
cast. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 dressing you into the part 分 you've been given to play.

## Notes
扮 anchors 打扮 "to dress up," 扮演 "to play a role," 装扮 "to disguise
oneself as" — all built on "taking on an appearance/role by hand," whether
in theatre, costume, or everyday self-presentation.

## Etymology
A vertical line passing through the middle of an enclosed shape. The most
common reading is a banner or standard planted at the centre of a gathering,
with the box marking the field it commands. Shuowen: 內也 "the inside," "a
line passing up and down through" the centre. From "the centre" come "middle,
within," "China" (中国 "the central state"), and — read *zhòng* — "to hit the
centre, to attain" (中奖, 打中).

## Mnemonic
A pole driven through the dead centre of the field: the middle.

## Notes
Two readings: *zhōng* "middle / within" (中间, 中午), *zhòng* "to hit / be hit
by" (中毒, 考中). 中 abbreviates 中国 in 中文, 中餐, 中医.

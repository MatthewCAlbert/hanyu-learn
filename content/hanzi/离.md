## Etymology
离 was an ancient graph for a bird or beast caught in a snare (Shuowen even
calls it a mountain spirit). It served as the phonetic in 離 "oriole," which
was then borrowed for "to leave, to part." The simplified form drops the
bird 隹 and reuses 离 alone.

## Mnemonic
A creature tangled in the net at the top, twisting to get free: to break
away.

## Notes
Senses: leave / depart (离开, 离别, 分离), be distant from (离这儿很远, 离
目标不远), without (离不开). Also the trigram ☲ (离卦). Surname Lí.

## Etymology
Simplified from 適. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; the traditional phonetic
啇 *dí* is written 舌 in the simplified form (not a sound match — the clue is
lost). Shuowen: 之也 "to go toward." From "to go to (the right place)" grew
"to fit, suitable, agreeable" and "just, exactly" (适逢, 适才).

## Mnemonic
Arriving 辶 just where you should be: a good fit.

## Notes
An unrelated old 适 *kuò* (a personal name) is a homograph. Senses: suitable
(适合, 合适, 适应), just / opportunely (适逢其会), comfortable (舒适).

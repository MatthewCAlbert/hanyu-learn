## Etymology
𠔼 "a covering" (the top, not 凡) over 口. Shuowen: 合會也 "to bring together" —
things gathered under one cover, or voices speaking as one. From "together"
grew "the same, alike, identical," and the "fellow-" prefix (同学, 同事,
同乡).

## Mnemonic
Everything brought under one lid 𠔼: the same.

## Notes
Two readings: *tóng* (common), *tòng* only in 胡同 "a lane / alley." Opposite
不同, 相同 aside.

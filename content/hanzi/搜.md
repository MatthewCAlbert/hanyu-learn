## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 叟 *sǒu* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. 搜 is a later standardisation of the search/gathering
sense once carried by 蒐 (originally a plant name tied to spring hunting
expeditions that involved searching the fields); 搜 fixes that sense onto
the hand radical for a clean, general "to search."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 groping around like an old man 叟 patting down his pockets.

## Notes
搜 dominates the modern vocabulary of searching, especially digital: 搜索
"to search," 搜索引擎 "search engine," 搜查 "to search (a person/place,
often police)."

## Etymology
Simplified from 筆. Associative: 竹 "bamboo" over 毛 "hair" — a writing brush
is exactly that, a bamboo shaft tipped with a tuft of hair. (The traditional
筆 instead writes 聿, a hand gripping the brush.)

## Mnemonic
Bamboo 竹 shaft, hair 毛 tip: a brush.

## Notes
Senses: any writing instrument (毛笔, 钢笔, 铅笔), a stroke of a character
(笔画, 一笔一画), the act or style of writing (笔试, 笔名, 文笔), and a
measure word for sums and transactions (一笔钱, 一笔生意, 一笔账).

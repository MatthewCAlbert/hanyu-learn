## Etymology
Pictographic — a workman's tool. It is usually taken as a carpenter's set
square, or a pounder / ram. Shuowen: "resembles a person holding a measuring
square." From the tool comes "work, labour" and "a worker."

## Mnemonic
A carpenter's square: the mark of work.

## Notes
工 is Kangxi radical 48. Phonetic in 功 *gōng* (merit), 攻 *gōng* (attack),
红 / 紅 *hóng* (red), 江 *jiāng* (river), 空 *kōng* (empty).

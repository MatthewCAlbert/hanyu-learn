## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen reads it as a hand (又) raising a rod or staff (the
picture behind 舉杖) — the household head, disciplining and instructing.
矩也, "a standard, a rule," frames the father as the family's living
measure of correct conduct.

## Mnemonic
A hand 八, raising a rod 乂: the household head, teaching by example and
by discipline.

## Notes
父 is almost always bound in modern compounds — 父亲, 父母, 祖父
"grandfather," 继父 "stepfather" — rarely standing fully alone the way
the more colloquial 爸 does.

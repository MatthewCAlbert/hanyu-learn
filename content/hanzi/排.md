## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 非 *fēi* gives the sound (for
*pái*). Shuowen: 擠也 "to shove, to push aside." From pushing things into
place grew "to arrange in a line" and "a row."

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 nudging people shoulder to shoulder: a row.

## Notes
The 非 phonetic also writes 徘 *pái*, 俳 *pái*, 辈 / 輩 *bèi*. Senses: line up
/ arrange (排队, 排列, 安排), a row (前排, 排球 volleyball), rehearse (排练,
排演), a platoon (排长), reject / drain (排斥, 排水, 排除), and a raft
(木排, 竹排). 排骨 "spare ribs."

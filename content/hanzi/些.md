## Etymology
Not securely attested. 些 (此 "this" over 二) is a late graph. In the classical
《楚辭》 it is a sentence-final particle; the modern use — a vague-plural
quantifier "some, a few, a small amount" — developed separately in the
vernacular. The parts (此, 二) do not build the meaning in any clear way.

## Mnemonic
"This much, that much" — an unspecified handful.

## Notes
些 needs a demonstrative or 一 before it: 这些, 那些, 哪些, 一些, 有些. It
already carries plurality, so no measure word follows (这些书, not 这些本书).

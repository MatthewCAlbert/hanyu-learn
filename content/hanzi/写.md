## Etymology
Simplified from 寫. 宀 "roof" gave the meaning and 舄 *xì* the sound. Shuowen:
置物也 "to set an object down" — originally "to transfer / lay out," and from
"to lay out (marks on a surface)" came "to copy out, to write." Simplification
reduces 宀 to 冖 and 舄 to 与.

## Mnemonic
Setting marks down in order under one roof 冖: writing.

## Notes
写 also "to depict / describe" (写景, 描写) and "to compose" (写信, 写作).
听写 "dictation" (write what you hear).

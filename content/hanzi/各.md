## Etymology
Ideographic, but the two traditional readings disagree on the picture.
Shuowen reads 夂 (a foot moving) with 口 (mouth) as "someone advancing while
another calls a halt, each side not listening to the other" — the source of
"differing, separate" (異辭也). Modern scholars reading the oracle-bone form
instead see a foot (夂) arriving at 凵, a semi-underground dwelling —
"a person coming home from outside" — with 口 a later graphic variant of
that dwelling shape, not literally "mouth."

Confidence is medium because both stories are attested and neither can be
ruled out from the character alone.

## Mnemonic
A foot 夂 stops at each doorway 口 in turn: to each, their own.

## Notes
"Each, respectively" (各位, 各种, 各自) is the sense that survives; nothing
in ordinary use points back to either "arguing" or "arriving home."

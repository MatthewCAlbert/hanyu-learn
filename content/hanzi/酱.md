## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 酉 (a fermenting vessel) supplies the meaning — fermented pastes — and 爿 *qiáng* (here 丬) supplies the sound (*qiáng* → *jiàng*). In Shuowen the graph also includes 肉 (meat), since the first sauces were meat-based; in the modern graph that part is reduced to 夕, an abbreviation of 肉/⺼ and not "evening".

## Mnemonic
A jar 酉 of fermenting paste, sealed up like a bed 丬.

## Notes
丬 is sound only. 酱油 "soy sauce", 果酱 "jam", 甜面酱 "sweet bean paste", 酱菜 "pickles". 将 *jiāng* and 浆 *jiāng* share the sound part.

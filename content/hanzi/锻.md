## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
小冶, means "small-scale metalworking" — and 段 *duàn* supplies the sound.

"To forge metal" extends figuratively to the body: 锻炼 (covered
elsewhere in this app's note on 炼 — "to exercise," lit. "to forge and
refine") — training the body the way a smith hammers metal into
strength.

## Mnemonic
Metal 钅 hammered piece by piece 段: "锻," to forge.

## Notes
锻炼 ("to exercise, to work out") is overwhelmingly the character's most
common modern compound — the literal metalworking sense survives mostly
in this etymology.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 攵 (a hand with a tool/stick, marking a forceful action)
supplies the meaning; 求 *qiú* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 止也
"to stop, to halt," is the older sense: to actively intervene and stop
something bad from happening — the seed of "to rescue."

## Mnemonic
Reaching out 攵 to what you seek 求 to save.

## Notes
"To stop [something bad]" narrowed into the specific, urgent "to rescue,
to save" — 救命, 抢救, 急救 — while the more general "to halt" sense
faded from ordinary use.

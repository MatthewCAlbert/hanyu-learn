## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning, 肖 *xiào* supplies the sound,
shifted to *shāo*. Shuowen's gloss 出物有漸也 is "things emerging gradually" —
originally describing grain ripening or being distributed bit by bit. "A
little, slightly" (稍微, 稍稍) directly continues that "small increment"
sense.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾 that resembles 肖 the last harvest, but only slightly different —
a small margin.

## Notes
稍微 "slightly," 稍等 "wait a moment," 略微 (near-synonym) all share this
"small degree" register. Two readings: *shāo* is the common modern reading;
*shào* survives only in a few place names and archaic usages.

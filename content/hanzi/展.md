## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 尸 (a reclining human figure, used generally for the body
at rest or in repose) supplies the meaning; an abbreviated form of 𧝑
supplied the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 轉也, "to turn, to roll over," is a
body-in-motion sense — turning over, stretching out — the root of the
modern "to unfold, to spread out, to exhibit."

## Mnemonic
A body 尸, stretching out 衣 to its full length: to unfold, to display.

## Notes
展 covers both "to develop, to expand" (发展, 展开) and "an exhibition,
a display" (展览, 展示) — something folded or contained, opened out for
all to see.

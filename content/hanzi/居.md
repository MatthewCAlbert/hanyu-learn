## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 尸 (a reclining/seated body shape) supplies the meaning —
Shuowen's definition, 蹲, means "to squat, to settle down" — and 古 *gǔ*
supplies the sound.

"To settle down, to squat in place" generalized to "to reside, to dwell"
(居住, 家居) — settling into a place long enough to call it home.

## Mnemonic
A body 尸 settling down, as old and unhurried 古 as the ground it sits
on: "居," to reside.

## Notes
居住 ("to reside") and 邻居 ("neighbor," lit. "nearby-dwelling") both build
on this character's core "settled in place" sense.

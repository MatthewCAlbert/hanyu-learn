## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 鄰: 阝 (here 邑, settlement/town) supplies the
meaning. Shuowen's gloss is precise: 五家爲鄰, "five households make one
鄰" — a specific administrative grouping in ancient local organisation,
which generalised to "neighbour" broadly. The traditional phonetic was 粦
*lín*; simplification replaced it with 令, so today's visible right side
is a graphic stand-in rather than the original sound clue.

## Mnemonic
Five households 阝, close enough to share one命令 令 — neighbours.

## Notes
邻 anchors 邻居 "neighbour," 邻近 "nearby, adjacent," and 邻国 "neighbouring
country" — the old administrative-unit sense has faded, leaving only the
general "nearby, adjacent" meaning.

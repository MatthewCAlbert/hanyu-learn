## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 炎 *yán* supplies the
sound. Xu Shen adds a folk-etymological flourish linking 談 to 淡 "bland,
mild" — talk that is 和懌而悅, agreeable and easygoing — but the core
composition is a plain phonetic pairing, not a picture of fire or heat.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 flaring up 炎 like a fire: an animated conversation.

## Notes
炎 as a phonetic recurs in 淡 dàn "bland" and 谈 tán "to talk" — two very
different senses sharing one sound component, a small reminder not to read
"fire, heat" into either.

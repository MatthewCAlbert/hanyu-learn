## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — liquid, fluid — and 夜 *yè* supplies the sound; Shuowen glosses it as a body fluid.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 that appears only at night 夜: dew, the liquid in the dark.

## Notes
夜 is sound only (the match is exact). 血液, 液体, 液晶 "liquid crystal", 唾液, 输液 "IV drip".

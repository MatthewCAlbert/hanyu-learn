## Etymology
Pictographic: oracle-bone forms show a field divided into plots by paths and ditches, which the modern form keeps — a square cut by cross-paths. Shuowen reads the outer box and the crossed lines as a plot of land crossed by field paths. It is the base of many land words.

## Mnemonic
Rice paddies seen from above, divided by the banks.

## Notes
田径 "track and field", 田野 "fields", 农田, 稻田, 田 as surname Tián. Radical in 男, 留, 界, 略, 畅.

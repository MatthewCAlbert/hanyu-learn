## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, and 农 (農) *nóng* supplies
the sound, an exact match. 浓 is closely related to the family of
characters built on this same phonetic with a "thick, abundant" sense
(醲, thick wine; 脓, thick pus) — this entry keeps confidence medium since
it cannot cite a Shuowen entry for 濃 itself.

"Thick, concentrated" applies to liquids (浓茶, strong tea), color (浓
绿, deep green), and abstractly to feeling (浓厚的兴趣, "keen interest," lit.
"thick/concentrated interest").

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 thickened as densely as a field of crops 农 (agriculture):
"浓," thick, concentrated.

## Notes
浓 and its family members 脓 (pus) and 醲 (thick wine, now rare) all share
the same phonetic 农 and the same "thick, dense" core sense, applied to
different substances.

## Etymology
A late graph absent from Shuowen, usually read as two things (two 口) pierced by a vertical stroke 丨: items on a skewer or string. It is related in shape and sense to the older 毌 "to pierce through, string together" (the root of 贯, 患). The reading as a picture is the standard one but not documented in a text. Not securely attested beyond the shape.

## Mnemonic
Two beads 口 threaded on a rod 丨.

## Notes
串 is the measure word for strings or bunches of things (一串葡萄, 一串钥匙) and a verb "to string together" (串联, 串门 "drop in on neighbours"). 烤串 "kebab" is the everyday use.

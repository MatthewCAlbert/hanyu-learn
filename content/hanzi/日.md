## Etymology
Pictographic — the sun: a disc with a mark in the centre (in the oracle bone a
dot or short line, distinguishing it from a plain circle). Shuowen: 太陽之精不
虧 "the essence of the sun, never waning." From "sun" comes "day."

## Mnemonic
A circle with a dot: the sun.

## Notes
日 is Kangxi radical 72 and the semantic in a huge family: 明, 早, 时, 星,
晚, 是, 春. Senses: sun (日出), day / date (生日, 日期), daily (日报). Japan is
日本.

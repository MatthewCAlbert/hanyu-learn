## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 耒 (plough, farm work) supplies the meaning and 毛 *máo* supplies the sound (*máo* → *hào*). The graph is a variant of the Shuowen 秏, a kind of rice, with 耒 replacing 禾. The sense "to use up, to waste" is a later borrowing. The details of the replacement are not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A plough 耒 drags through the hair 毛 of the field: expensive work, using up effort.

## Notes
毛 is sound only. 消耗 "consume", 耗费 "expend", 耗时 "time-consuming", 能耗 "energy consumption", 噩耗 "bad news" (耗 here means "news" by sound).

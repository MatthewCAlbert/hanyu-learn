## Etymology
Simplified from 車 — a pictograph of a chariot seen from above: the box in the
middle, the axle running across, and (in the fuller oracle-bone forms) a wheel
at each end. Shuowen: "the general name for the body and wheels of a vehicle."

## Mnemonic
An axle 十 through a wheel: a cart.

## Notes
车 is Kangxi radical 159, in 轮 / 輪 (wheel), 转 / 轉 (turn), 较 / 較. Two
readings: *chē* "vehicle" (汽车, 火车), *jū* the chariot piece in Chinese
chess.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 戀: 心 (heart) supplies the meaning; the
traditional phonetic was 䜌 *luán* (a tangle of thread being sorted, the
same root seen in 變/变 "to change"). Simplification replaced 䜌 with 亦, so
today's visible top is a graphic stand-in, not a working sound clue. Not
in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心, tangled up too 亦 in someone to let go.

## Notes
恋 anchors 恋爱 "to be in love, romance," 恋人 "lover, sweetheart," and
figuratively 恋家 "attached to home," 依恋 "reluctant to part with" — the
same "tangled attachment" image applied beyond romance.

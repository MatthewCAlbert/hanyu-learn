## Etymology
Pictographic — a sprout just pushing up through the line of the ground, not yet
in leaf. Shuowen: 艸木之初也 "the beginning of a plant." From "a thing with
potential" grew "ability, talent" (人才, 天才), and — grammaticalised — "only
then, just, not until" (三点才来) and "merely" (才十岁).

## Mnemonic
A seedling barely up: raw potential.

## Notes
Distinguish 才 (extra hook, "talent / only then") from 寸 and from the 扌
"hand" radical it resembles. 刚才 "just now" (a noun); 才 as an adverb sits
before the verb.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 勸: 力 (effort, strength) supplies the meaning,
雚 *guàn* supplies the sound, shifted to *quàn*. Shuowen's gloss 勉也 is "to
exert oneself, to encourage effort" — the sense narrowed to "to urge, to
persuade [someone else] to make an effort." Simplification replaced the
elaborate 雚 with 又, so the modern 劝 no longer visibly carries a real
phonetic — the popular reading of 又 as "a hand beside a throne" is a modern
mnemonic, not the historical structure.

## Mnemonic
A firm hand 又 lending its strength 力 to push you toward the right choice.

## Notes
劝 is the everyday verb for persuading or advising: 劝说 "to persuade," 劝告
"to advise," 劝架 "to mediate a quarrel" (literally "urge apart the
fighting").

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in Shuowen: 辵 (here shown as 彳 and a foot) supplies the meaning — to go on foot — and 土 *tǔ* supplies the sound. "On foot" gave "infantry, followers", then "disciples, apprentices", and "bare, empty, merely" (徒然, 徒手). In the modern graph 土 and the foot fuse into 走 and the sound element is no longer recognisable.

## Mnemonic
Walking 彳 in someone's steps, you are a follower.

## Notes
徒步 "on foot", 徒弟 "apprentice", 歹徒 "criminal", 信徒 "believer", 徒劳 "in vain". The 土 sound element is hidden inside 走; 走 itself is a different character here.

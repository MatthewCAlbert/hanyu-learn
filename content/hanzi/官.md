## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 宀 (a roof, building) over 𠂤 (a mound — used here,
per Shuowen's own note, the same way it is in 師/师, to mean a gathered
group of people) — a roofed place where a body of people serves the ruler.
That building sense shifted metonymically onto the people themselves: an
"official" is, by this account, named after the government hall they work
in, the same shift seen in 政府.

Confidence is medium because Shuowen's own explanation of 𠂤 as "a crowd"
here is an interpretive gloss rather than a settled pictographic reading.

## Mnemonic
A roof 宀 sheltering a gathered crowd serving the ruler: "官," an official.

## Notes
官 is the bound morpheme in 军官 (military officer), 法官 (judge), 警官
(police officer) — always the title-holder, never the institution itself
(that job goes to 局, 部, 院).

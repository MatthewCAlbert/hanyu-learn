## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 酉 (wine jar — the semantic component in every alcohol-related
character: 酒, 醉, 酌) supplies the meaning, and 星 *xīng* supplies the sound.

The original, narrower sense was "to sober up" after drinking — coming back
to clear-headedness from wine's fog. That sense generalized to "to wake up"
from sleep and, further, to "become aware, realize" (醒悟) — all three senses
share the same core image of clouded awareness clearing.

## Mnemonic
A wine jar 酉 under the stars 星: sobering up as the stars come into clear
focus overhead — "醒," to wake.

## Notes
Compare 酉's other family members 酒 (jiǔ, alcohol), 酌 (zhuó, to pour a
drink), 醉 (zuì, drunk) — 醒 is the one member of the set that describes
coming *out* of alcohol's effect rather than into it.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 走 (to run, to hasten — the same semantic root as this
app's entry for 趋) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition is simply
"swift" — and 取 *qǔ* supplies the sound.

The everyday modern sense "interest, fun" (兴趣, covered elsewhere in
this app; 有趣, "interesting") is generally understood as a later
development from "the direction one hastens toward" — what one is drawn
to pursue — though this entry cannot confirm that bridge with full
certainty, hence medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
Feet running 走 to take hold of 取 whatever draws them onward: "趣,"
interest — what one hastens toward.

## Notes
兴趣 (interest, covered elsewhere in this app) and 有趣 ("interesting,
fun") both use 趣 in its fully modern sense; the "swift" sense survives
only as etymological history.

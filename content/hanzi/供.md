## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning "to set out, to provide,"
and 共 *gòng* supplies the sound — and, unusually, also nudges the meaning,
since 共 independently means "together, jointly": a person setting
something out *together* for others' use.

The reading split tracks the grammar: *gōng* is the everyday verb "to
supply" (供应, 提供), while *gòng* is the noun/legal sense "a statement,
testimony" (口供) — someone "supplying" an account of events under
questioning.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 setting something out for everyone in common 共: to "供,"
provide.

## Notes
供 and 共 share the same phonetic root and a family resemblance in meaning
("jointly providing/sharing") even though 共 alone never takes the "to
supply" verb sense on its own.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 勵: 力 (strength, effort) supplies the meaning,
厉 (traditional 厲, "severe, to sharpen") supplies the sound, shifted to
*lì*. Not in Shuowen. The image of sharpening one's effort — whetting
oneself the way a blade is whetted — carries directly into "to encourage,
to spur on."

## Mnemonic
Effort 力 sharpened 厉 to a fine edge.

## Notes
励 is almost always paired: 鼓励 "to encourage," 激励 "to motivate/inspire,"
奖励 "to reward" — rarely standing alone, always doing the work of spurring
someone toward more effort.

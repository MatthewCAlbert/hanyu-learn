## Etymology
Associative: 斗 "a measuring scoop" beside 禾 "grain" — measuring out grain to
grade it. Shuowen: 程也 "a standard, a measure." From "a graded measure" grew
"a category, a class," and hence a branch of study or a department.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾 measured by the scoop 斗 and sorted into classes: a category.

## Notes
Senses: a branch of study (科学, 学科, 文科, 科目), a department or section
(内科, 外科, 科室, 科长), a family in biology (猫科动物), a legal clause
(作奸犯科, 金科玉律), and stage business in classical drama (科白).

## Etymology
Indicative, and the exact mirror of 上: a short stroke placed below a longer
baseline — "below, down." Shuowen: 底也 "the underside," marked 指事 "an
indicative graph."

## Mnemonic
A mark hanging under the line: down.

## Notes
Pairs with 上 everywhere: 上下, 上课 / 下课, 下边. Verb senses: get off
(下车), fall (下雨), issue (下命令). As a complement: settle / remain (坐下,
留下). Also the verbal measure "a go" (等一下).

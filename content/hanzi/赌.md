## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (money) supplies the meaning — to wager — and 者 *zhě* supplies the sound (*zhě* → *dǔ*). A late graph (in the additions to Shuowen).

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 put down by someone 者 who has hopes.

## Notes
者 is sound only. 赌博 "gamble", 赌场 "casino", 赌注 "stake", 打赌 "make a bet", 赌气 "act out of pique". Series: 赌, 都, 堵 *dǔ*, 睹 *dǔ*.

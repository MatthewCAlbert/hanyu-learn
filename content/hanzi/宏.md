## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 宀 (roof, building) supplies the meaning — a hall resonant with a deep echo — and 厷 *gōng* supplies the sound. The extension "vast, grand" follows from a large resonant room. 厷 is the old word for the upper arm (now 肱).

## Mnemonic
A building 宀 with a vast echo, big as an arm span 厷.

## Notes
厷 is sound only. 宏大, 宏伟 "magnificent", 宏观 "macro", 宏图 "grand plan". Series: 肱 *gōng*, 宏 *hóng*.

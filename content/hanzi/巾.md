## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: a piece of cloth hanging down, worn at the
waist as a sash or carried as a cloth. The frame 冂 and the vertical
stroke 丨 together trace the cloth's outline, standing in for the
appearance of woven thread (糸).

## Mnemonic
A length of cloth hanging straight down: "巾," a piece of cloth.

## Notes
巾 is the semantic component behind a large family of cloth-related
characters — 帽 (hat), 布 (cloth), 帮 (to help, originally group work done
"shoulder to shoulder" with cloth banners) — worth watching for as a
recurring radical.

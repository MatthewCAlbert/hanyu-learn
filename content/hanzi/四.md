## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph for "four" was 亖 — four stacked lines, the tally
continued from 一二三. Because four lines were hard to tell from three, the
number was rewritten with 四, a graph borrowed from a word for "nostrils /
breath" (the original of 泗). So the modern shape is a phonetic replacement,
not a picture of four of anything.

## Mnemonic
Four was once four lines; now it borrows a different graph. Just memorise.

## Notes
Numbers above three all abandon the tally. 四 is homophonous with 死 "death,"
so it is avoided in some contexts (floor numbers, phone numbers).

## Etymology
Pictographic — a fur garment with the pelt and tufts shown. Shuowen notes 求
as the old form of 裘 "a fur coat." It was borrowed for "to seek, to request,
to beg, to strive for," and 裘 (衣 added) took back "fur coat."

## Mnemonic
A shaggy fur coat — the graph lent out to "seek."

## Notes
求 is the phonetic in 球 *qiú* (jade / ball), 救 *jiù* (to rescue). Senses:
seek (追求, 寻求), request (请求, 要求), demand (供求, 需求).

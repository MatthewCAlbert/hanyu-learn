## Etymology
Two figures side by side, both facing the same way and close together.
Shuowen: 密也 "near, packed together," and notes the graphic relation to 从
(one behind another) — 比 is the two facing the other way. From "side by side"
grew "to compare, to match against."

## Mnemonic
Two people standing shoulder to shoulder to be measured against each other.

## Notes
比 is Kangxi radical 81. As a coverb it sets up comparison: A 比 B 高 "A is
taller than B." Also "ratio" (比例), "than" (比较).

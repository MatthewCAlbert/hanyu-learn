## Etymology
⺮ "bamboo" over 具 "to set out with both hands" — laying out bamboo counting
rods to do a sum. Shuowen: 數也 "to count." From literal reckoning grew "to
calculate, to plan," "to regard as / count as" (算是, 就算), and "to count as
valid" (说话算数).

## Mnemonic
Bamboo rods ⺮ laid out 具 on the table: doing the sum.

## Notes
Senses: calculate (计算, 算账, 心算), plan / scheme (打算, 算计), reckon as
(算了, 总算, 算不上). 算命 "to tell fortunes."

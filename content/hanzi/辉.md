## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 光 (light) supplies the meaning and 軍 (simplified 军) *jūn* supplies the sound (*jūn* → *huī*). Shuowen glosses it as brightness.

## Mnemonic
Light 光 flashing off the armour of the army 军.

## Notes
军 is sound only. Series: 挥 *huī*, 晕 *yūn*, 运 *yùn*, 辉 *huī*. 光辉 "radiance", 辉煌 "splendid", 辉映 "shine on each other".

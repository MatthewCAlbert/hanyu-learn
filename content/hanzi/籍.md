## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺮ (bamboo — bamboo slips were an early writing surface)
supplies the meaning, 耤 *jí* supplies the sound and reads unchanged.
Shuowen's gloss 簿書也 "a register, a record book" matches the modern
sense directly — a written record, originally kept on bound bamboo
slips.

## Mnemonic
Bamboo 竹 strips, bound into the record once ploughed 耤 into memory.

## Notes
籍 anchors 户籍 "household registration," 国籍 "nationality," 书籍 "books"
(the broader written-record sense), and 籍贯 "one's ancestral hometown" —
all variations on "a formal, written registration of who or what belongs
where."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning, 旦 *dàn* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 安也 "settled, stable" fits
level ground — flat earth that offers no obstacle. "Candid, open" is a
figurative extension: a mind as level and unobstructed as flat land.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 as level and open as the first light of dawn 旦.

## Notes
坦 spans terrain (平坦 "flat, level") and character (坦白 "to confess
openly," 坦率 "candid, straightforward") — the same "no hidden bumps"
image applied to ground and to honesty alike.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic — and 然 supplies both sound and meaning here, having once
been the very word for "to burn" (然's own bottom component 灬 is itself
a row of flames, the same fire-radical seen in 热, 烈). Once 然
grammaticalized away into the abstract "so, thus, -ly" (its role in
突然, 竟然, 既然, 依然, all covered elsewhere in this app), 火 was added
back to spin off 燃, keeping the original "burn" meaning in its own
dedicated character — exactly the same split seen in 源/原.

## Mnemonic
Fire 火 doing what its own root 然 once meant on its own: to "燃," burn.

## Notes
This explains why so many grammatical suffix words (突然, 竟然, 既然,
依然) all end in 然 with no independent meaning left in modern use — 然
itself drifted from "burning" all the way to a bare grammatical particle,
and 燃 is where its old, literal sense went to live on.

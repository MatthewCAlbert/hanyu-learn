## Etymology
The fuller forms are 箇 (⺮ "bamboo" + 固 *gù* for sound) and 個 (亻 + 固),
glossed by Shuowen as 竹枚也 "a bamboo stalk" — bamboo canes being the
archetypal thing you count one by one. 个 is an old abbreviated graph, a single
angled stroke echoing half of 竹, adopted as the everyday form.

## Mnemonic
One cane cut from the clump: a single item.

## Notes
个 is the default measure word, used when no more specific classifier is
called for (一个人, 三个问题). Also 个子 "stature," 个人 "individual."

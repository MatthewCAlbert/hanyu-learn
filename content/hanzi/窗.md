## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 穴 (a hole, an opening) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition is directly "an opening for ventilation" — and 囱 *cōng*
(itself originally a pictograph of a chimney/smoke-vent) supplies the
sound, doubling as a closely related image: both a chimney-hole and a
window are openings that let air and light through a wall.

## Mnemonic
A hole 穴 cut through the wall like a chimney vent 囱, letting light and
air pass through: "窗," a window.

## Notes
窗户 (window, pairing 窗 with 户, covered elsewhere in this app — "a
single door-leaf") and 窗口 ("a window/counter," also used figuratively
for a service counter) both build on this character's plain "an opening
in a wall" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 冫 "ice" (two frozen strokes) gives the meaning; 令 *lìng* gives
the sound (for *lěng*). Shuowen: 寒也 "cold."

## Mnemonic
Ice 冫 setting in: cold.

## Notes
The 令 phonetic also writes 领 / 領 *lǐng*, 龄 / 齡 *líng*, 铃 / 鈴 *líng*,
零 *líng*, 岭 / 嶺 *lǐng*. 冫 "ice" also heads 冰, 冻, 凉, 准.

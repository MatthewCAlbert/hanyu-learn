## Etymology
Simplified from 藍. Pictophonetic: 艹 "plant" gives the meaning — Shuowen:
染青艸也 "the herb that dyes things blue," i.e. indigo; 监 (監) *jiān* gives
the sound (for *lán*).

## Mnemonic
The plant 艹 you crush for blue dye: 蓝.

## Notes
The 监 phonetic also writes 篮 / 籃 *lán*, 滥 / 濫 *làn*, 鉴 / 鑒 *jiàn*,
槛 / 檻 *kǎn*. Senses: blue (蓝色, 蔚蓝, 蓝天), the indigo plant (青出于蓝),
and blueprint (蓝图, 蓝本).

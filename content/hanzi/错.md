## Etymology
Simplified from 錯. 钅 (金) "metal" gives the meaning; 昔 *xī* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 金涂也 "to inlay / plate with metal." From patterns of inlaid metal
came "interlaced, criss-crossing" (交错, 错综), and from "things crossed up"
came "wrong, a mistake" (错误, 认错).

## Mnemonic
Threads of metal 钅 crossing every which way: tangled — and so, wrong.

## Notes
The 昔 phonetic also writes 借 *jiè*, 惜 *xī*, 醋 *cù*, 蜡 / 蠟 *là*. 不错
"not bad," 没错 "that's right."

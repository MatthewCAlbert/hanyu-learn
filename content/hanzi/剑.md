## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 刂 (blade) supplies the meaning and 僉 (simplified 佥) *qiān* supplies the sound (*qiān* → *jiàn*). Shuowen glosses it as a weapon worn at the side.

## Mnemonic
A blade 刂 all 佥 gather around: a sword.

## Notes
佥 is sound only here (it is a rare word for "all"). Series: 剑 *jiàn*, 检 *jiǎn*, 验 *yàn*, 险 *xiǎn*, 签 *qiān*. 剑客 "swordsman", 宝剑, 刻舟求剑 "stick to old ways".

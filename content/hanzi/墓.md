## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — a mound — and 莫 *mò* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as a mound (the grave mound).

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 piled where there is nothing 莫 more to see: a grave.

## Notes
莫 is the old word for "sunset" (the base of 暮) and sound-only here. Series: 募 *mù*, 幕 *mù*, 慕 *mù*, 暮 *mù*, 墓 *mù*. 坟墓, 墓地, 公墓.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺮ "bamboo" gives the meaning; 快 *kuài* gives the sound. The
older word was 箸 *zhù*, but 箸 sounded like 住 "to stop / stay" — unlucky for
boatmen, who wanted a fast trip — so they said 快 "fast" instead, and the ⺮
top was added to make 筷.

## Mnemonic
Bamboo ⺮ sticks, renamed for "fast" 快 to dodge an unlucky homophone.

## Notes
A rare case where a character was swapped for a taboo. Measure word 双:
一双筷子.

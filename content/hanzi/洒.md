## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 灑: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning. Shuowen's
gloss 汛也 is "to sprinkle, to splash" — water scattered in drops. The
traditional phonetic was 麗 *lì*; simplification replaced it with 西, so
today's visible right-hand side is a graphic stand-in rather than a working
sound clue for *sǎ*.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 flicked out toward the west 西, scattering in every direction.

## Notes
洒 covers literal sprinkling (洒水 "to sprinkle water") and the figurative
"carefree, unrestrained" register in 洒脱 "free and easy" — a separate,
later extension of "scattered, loose" onto personal manner.

## Etymology
An associative compound: 夕 "dusk" + 口 "mouth." Shuowen explains it directly —
after dark you cannot make each other out, so you call your name aloud with
your mouth. From "to name" grew "a name," "reputation / fame" (有名), and the
classifier for people (三名学生).

## Mnemonic
Calling out 口 who you are in the dark 夕: your name.

## Notes
名字 pairs this with 字 (originally the courtesy name). 名 also "famous"
(名牌, 名人) and a measure word for persons.

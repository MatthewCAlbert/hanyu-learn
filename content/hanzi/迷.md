## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 (movement, the road) supplies the meaning; 米 *mǐ*
supplies the sound and, per Shuowen, nothing more — 米 does not mean
"rice" here. The original sense, 惑也 "confusion, bewilderment," gave rise
to the modern "to lose one's way" (迷路) and, by extension, "to be lost
in [an enthusiasm]" (球迷, 歌迷 — a fan, someone "lost" in devotion).

## Mnemonic
Wandering 辶 through a field of rice 米, losing all sense of direction.

## Notes
米 is a clean pictophonetic example: it contributes sound only, never a
"grain of rice" meaning, in every character it appears in as phonetic
(迷, 谜, 类's simplified shape included) — the meaning is carried entirely
by the other component.

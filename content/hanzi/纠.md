## Etymology
Traditional 糾 is 糸 (thread) + 丩, a graph for intertwined cords that is also the sound *jiū*. Shuowen glosses it as three strands plaited into a rope. "To twist, to entangle" gives "to correct, to investigate" (纠正) and "to get tangled up" (纠纷).

## Mnemonic
Threads 纟 twisted 丩 together in a knot.

## Notes
丩 is both sound and picture. 纠正 "correct", 纠纷 "dispute", 纠缠 "pester", 纠结 "tangled". Same 丩 in 收 *shōu* and 叫 *jiào*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 田 (field, land) supplies the meaning and 各 *gè* the sound. Shuowen's gloss is to survey and mark out land; "outline, sketch" and "strategy" (战略) follow from laying out the main lines, and "omit" (省略) from leaving out detail.

## Mnemonic
Roughly sketching out a field 田 for each 各 farmer — only the outline.

## Notes
Two pronunciations are listed in dictionaries: *lüè* is standard. 各 is sound only; see also 格, 路, 洛, 客. 策略, 忽略, 简略.

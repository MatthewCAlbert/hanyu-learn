## Etymology
Listed in the later additions to Shuowen as "fine, subtle", and built from 女 (woman) and 少. 少 supplies the sound — compare 眇 and 渺, both *miǎo*, where 少 has the same m- reading. Why 女 was chosen (delicate beauty? a finer, slighter thing?) is not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A subtle woman 女 with just a little 少 of something special.

## Notes
少 is sound only here, which is confusing because 少 alone is *shǎo/shào*. 妙 mostly appears in 奇妙, 巧妙, 美妙, 莫名其妙.

## Etymology
口 "mouth" marks it as a spoken interrogative; 那 *nà* gives the sound. 哪 is a
late graph deliberately split off from 那 "that" — the same word-family, with
口 added to mark the question form "which."

## Mnemonic
"That" 那 turned into a question with a spoken 口: which?

## Notes
Readings: *nǎ* "which" (哪个, 哪里), toneless *na* as a form of the particle 啊
after -n (好难啊 → 好难哪), *něi* the colloquial contraction of 哪一.

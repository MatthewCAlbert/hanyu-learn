## Etymology
Pictographic — three small specks, standing for grains of sand or tiny
particles. Shuowen: 物之微也 "the minuteness of things." The exact opposite of
大.

## Mnemonic
Three tiny specks: small.

## Notes
小 is Kangxi radical 42; 少 is 小 with one more stroke. As a prefix, 小- can be
literal (小学) or a familiar diminutive before a surname (小王). 小心 "be
careful" (lit. "make the heart small").

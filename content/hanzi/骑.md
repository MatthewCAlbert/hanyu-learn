## Etymology
Simplified from 騎. Pictophonetic: 马 (馬) "horse" gives the meaning; 奇 *qí*
gives the sound. Shuowen: 跨馬也 "to sit astride a horse."

## Mnemonic
Legs spread over the horse's 马 back: to ride.

## Notes
The 奇 phonetic also writes 寄 *jì*, 崎 *qí*, 椅 *yǐ*, 绮 *qǐ*. Senses:
ride astride (骑马, 骑车, 骑自行车), sit astride generally (骑墙 to fence-sit),
and — read *jì* — a cavalryman or mounted force (铁骑, 轻骑, 骠骑).

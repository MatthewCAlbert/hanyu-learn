## Etymology
Pictophonetic, and Shuowen notes explicitly that both halves carry
meaning as well as sound: 扌 (hand, the giving hand) and 受 *shòu* ("to
receive," itself the receiving hand accepting an object passed across) —
受 supplies the sound and, doubly, completes the picture: one hand
giving, the other receiving.

"To give, to bestow" (授予, "to confer/award") extends to "to teach" (教
授, covered elsewhere in this app) — bestowing knowledge the same way one
bestows an object.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 handing something across to another hand that receives 受 it:
"授," to bestow — or to teach.

## Notes
教授 (professor, covered elsewhere in this app) and 授予 ("to confer/
award," as in awarding a degree or medal) both build on this character's
core "hand-to-hand bestowal" image.

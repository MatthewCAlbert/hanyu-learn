## Etymology
Pictophonetic — and, unusually, 要 supplies both the sound *and* the
original meaning here: 要's own oldest form was a pictograph of a person
with hands set on their hips, marking the waist. Once 要 was repurposed
for its now-dominant senses "important" and "to want," a semantic
determiner ⺼ (肉, flesh/body) was added to spin off a dedicated character
for the body part it originally drew.

## Mnemonic
The body 肉 at its most essential 要 point — where hands rest on hips: the
"腰," waist.

## Notes
This is the same pattern as 胸/匈: a character's original body-part
meaning was inherited by an unrelated abstract word, and 肉/⺼ was added
afterward to give the body part its own distinct written form.

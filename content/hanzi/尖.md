## Etymology
Ideographic: 小 (small) sits on top of 大 (large): a narrow tip broadening below, which is the shape of a point. The character is a late creation, not in the original Shuowen, so its explanation is the graph's own shape rather than an old text.

## Mnemonic
Small 小 on top, big 大 below — a spear's sharp tip.

## Notes
尖锐 "sharp", 尖端 "cutting-edge", 顶尖 "top-notch", 尖叫 "scream", 笔尖 "nib". Useful as a visual cue: it reads like a pointed arrow.

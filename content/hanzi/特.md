## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 牛 "ox" gives the meaning; 寺 *sì* gives the sound (for *tè*).
Shuowen: 牛父也 "a bull, a male ox" — a single prize animal. From "one
outstanding beast" grew "singular, special, particular, extraordinary."

## Mnemonic
The one bull 牛 that stands out from the herd: special.

## Notes
The 寺 phonetic also writes 时 / 時 *shí*, 持 *chí*, 待 *dài*, 诗 / 詩 *shī*,
等 *děng*. Senses: special (特别, 特点, 特色), specially (特意, 特地),
secret agent (特工, 特务).

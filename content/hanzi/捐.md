## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 肙 *yuān* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss is "to discard"; "to give up (money) for a cause", hence "donate", follows.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 gives up a bit of the pot 肙 — a donation.

## Notes
肙 is sound only. Series: 娟 *juān*, 鹃 *juān*, 涓 *juān*, 绢 *juàn*, 捐 *juān*. 捐款, 捐赠, 捐献, 捐助.

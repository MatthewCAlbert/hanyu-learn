## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 彳 (a road, movement) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 均布, means "spread out evenly" — applying the same standard
uniformly across every case, exactly what a law does — and 聿 *yù* (a
hand holding a writing brush, the same component seen in 笔/筆) supplies
the sound, fittingly for something written down and codified.

## Mnemonic
A road 彳 kept perfectly even by rules set down with a brush 聿: "律,"
law.

## Notes
法律 (law, covered elsewhere in this app) and 律师 (lawyer, also covered
elsewhere) both build on this character's core "an evenly, universally
applied written rule" sense; 律 also names musical pitch-standards
(旋律, melody) via the same "evenly regulated" idea.

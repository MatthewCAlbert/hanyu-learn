## Etymology
厂 "cliff, slope" over 泉 "a spring" — water welling out at the foot of a
cliff. Shuowen: 水泉本也 "the source of a spring." This is the original of 源
"source"; 原 itself was borrowed for "original, primary" and "a plain."

## Mnemonic
The spring 泉 at the cliff's foot 厂 where the water starts: the origin.

## Notes
源 carries the water sense now. 原: original / former (原来, 原因, 原文,
原谅 to pardon), raw (原料, 原油), and open level land (草原, 平原, 高原).
原则 "principle."

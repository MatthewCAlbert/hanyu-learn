## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning; 相 *xiāng* supplies the
sound. The original sense is the woven bamboo compartment of a large
cart — 大車牝服也 — where people rode or goods were stowed; the material
generalised so that 箱 now covers any box or case, not just bamboo ones.

## Mnemonic
Bamboo ⺮ woven into a box, face to face 相 with whatever it holds inside.

## Notes
"A cart's storage compartment" generalised completely to any container —
冰箱 "refrigerator" (literally "ice box"), 邮箱 "mailbox," 箱子
"suitcase, box" — the bamboo is long gone from the material, only "box"
remains.

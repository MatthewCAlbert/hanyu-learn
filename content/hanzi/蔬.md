## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning, 疏 *shū* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged — and 疏's own sense "spread out, sparse" may
carry an echo of vegetables planted in spread-out garden rows, though its
formal role here is phonetic. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
Garden plants 艹, spread out 疏 in neat rows — vegetables.

## Notes
蔬 is almost always bound in 蔬菜 "vegetables," rarely standing alone.
Distinct from 菜, the broader everyday word that also covers cooked
dishes generally, not only raw vegetables.

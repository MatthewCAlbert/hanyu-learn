## Etymology
攵 (攴) "a hand with a stick — to strike, to correct" beside 子 "a child," with
爻 (interlaced rods, learning) above the child in the old form. Shuowen: "what
those above set out and those below imitate." A child being drilled, rod in
hand. The modern left side is written 孝, but historically it is 爻 + 子.

## Mnemonic
Guiding a child 子 along, switch 攵 in hand: to teach.

## Notes
Two readings: *jiào* "to teach / education / a religion" (教育, 教师, 佛教),
*jiāo* "to teach (a skill, someone)" (教书, 教我做饭).

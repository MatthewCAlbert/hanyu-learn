## Etymology
Traditional 喪 is 哭 (to wail) over 亡 (to die, to lose), with 亡 marked as "also sound". In the simplified 丧 the two 口 of 哭 are reduced, so the mourning picture is no longer visible. Two readings: *sàng* "mourning, to lose" and *sāng* in 丧事 funeral matters.

## Mnemonic
Wailing for the lost 亡.

## Notes
丧失 "lose", 沮丧 "dispirited", 丧生 "die", 丧事. The related 哭 and 器 keep their mouths.

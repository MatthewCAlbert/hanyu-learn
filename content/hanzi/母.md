## Etymology
Pictographic. The oracle-bone form is 女 (a kneeling woman) with two dots
added at the chest — breasts, marking her specifically as a nursing
mother. Shuowen's gloss plays on the sound: 母 is glossed as 牧 "to tend,
to raise" (as one tends livestock), and separately describes the shape as
either a pregnant woman or a nursing one — sources agree on the picture,
less so on which exact moment it depicts.

## Mnemonic
A woman 女, with two dots marking where she nurses her child: mother.

## Notes
The two dots survive into the modern shape as the two short horizontal
strokes inside 母 — one of the few pictographic details from oracle bone
script still visible in a simplified character today.

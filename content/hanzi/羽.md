## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen glosses 羽 as 鳥長毛也, "a bird's long feathers," and
marks the graph 象形, "a picture" — two feathers, or a single feather's
paired vanes, side by side.

## Mnemonic
Two feathers, side by side, ready for flight.

## Notes
羽 is Kangxi radical 124 and anchors 羽毛 "feather/plumage," 羽绒 "down
[filling]," and 羽毛球 "badminton" (literally "feather-ball," from the
shuttlecock's feathered construction).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 目 "eye" gives the meaning; 青 *qīng* gives the sound (for
*jīng*). Meaning: the eyeball, the pupil.

## Mnemonic
The dark centre 青 of the eye 目.

## Notes
The 青 phonetic also writes 请 / 請 *qǐng*, 情 *qíng*, 清 *qīng*, 晴 *qíng*,
精 *jīng*. 睛 barely stands alone — it needs 眼 (眼睛). 画龙点睛 "add the
pupil to the dragon" (the finishing touch).

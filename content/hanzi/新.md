## Etymology
斤 "axe" gives the meaning; 亲 *qīn* gave the sound (Shuowen: 𣂪聲 — 亲 itself
contains 辛 *xīn*, the deeper sound source). Shuowen: 取木也 "to cut firewood" —
this is the original of 薪 "firewood." The word was borrowed for "new, fresh"
(a freshly cut thing), and 薪 was coined to carry "firewood" again.

## Mnemonic
A tree freshly felled by the axe 斤: new-cut.

## Notes
薪 (⺿ + 新) is the descendant that kept "firewood / wages." 新 now: new
(新年, 新闻), newly (新买的), a person new to something.

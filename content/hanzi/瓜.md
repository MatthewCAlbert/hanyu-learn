## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: the character draws a melon or gourd hanging
from its curling vine, the outer strokes tracing the vine and the
enclosed shape the fruit itself. As an atomic pictograph, it has no
separate semantic or phonetic component to name.

## Mnemonic
A round fruit hanging heavy from a curling vine: "瓜," a melon.

## Notes
西瓜 (watermelon, lit. "western melon") and 黄瓜 (cucumber, lit. "yellow
melon") both build on this character as the general word for any
melon-family vine fruit.

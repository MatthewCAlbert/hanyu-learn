## Etymology
Ideographic. 又 (a hand) against 厂 (a cliff/overhang) — the oracle-bone
picture is a hand reaching up to scale or grip a cliff face, the hand
turning as it climbs. Shuowen's gloss, 覆也 "to turn over, to overturn," is
the sense that generalised from that turning motion.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 scrabbling against a cliff face 厂, flipping this way and that:
to turn over, to oppose.

## Notes
"To turn over" extends into both directions of the modern word: literal
reversal (反面, 相反) and, separately, active opposition (反对) — turning
something over and turning against it are the same root idea.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning; 圣 *kù* (an obsolete
reading, unrelated to the modern character 圣 "sacred," which is a
different, later-simplified character sharing this shape) supplies the
sound. The original sense, 異也 "different, strange," is unchanged today.

## Mnemonic
A feeling 忄 in your gut that something here 圣 is off: strange.

## Notes
Simplified 怪's right-hand component looks identical to simplified 圣
("sacred," itself the simplified form of the unrelated 聖) — a pure
graphic coincidence from two different simplification decisions, not a
shared origin.

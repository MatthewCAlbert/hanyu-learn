## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 工 (a carpenter's square/tool, standing for craft and
skilled work) supplies the meaning; 丂 *kǎo* supplies the sound. The
original sense, 技也 "skill, craft," is unchanged — the word has always
belonged to artisans first.

## Mnemonic
A craftsman's tool 工, used just so 丂: real skill.

## Notes
巧 splits into "skilful" (技巧, 巧妙) and, via "a skilfully-timed
coincidence," "as it happens, opportunely" (凑巧, 碰巧, 恰巧) — a skilled
hand and a lucky break share the same word because both feel, from the
outside, like something landing exactly right.

## Etymology
亻 "person" beside 更 "to alter." Shuowen reads it as an associative graph:
"when a person is not at ease, one changes things" — hence "convenient,
suited." From there grew the adverb "then, in that case" and the noun
"bodily waste" (a polite euphemism).

## Mnemonic
Adjusting 更 things to suit a person 亻: convenient.

## Notes
Two readings: *biàn* — convenient (方便, 便利, 便条), then / as soon as (说完
便走), ordinary (便饭, 便服, 随便), excrement (大便, 小便); *pián* — only in
便宜 *piányi* "cheap" and 便便 "portly."

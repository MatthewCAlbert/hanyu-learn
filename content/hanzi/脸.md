## Etymology
Simplified from 臉. Pictophonetic: ⺼ (肉) "flesh" gives the meaning; 佥 (僉)
*qiān* gives the sound (for *liǎn*). A relatively late word — early Chinese
used 面 for "face."

## Mnemonic
The flesh ⺼ of the face.

## Notes
The 佥 phonetic also writes 检 / 檢 *jiǎn*, 险 / 險 *xiǎn*, 捡 / 撿 *jiǎn*,
俭 / 儉 *jiǎn*, 签 / 簽 *qiān*. 脸 also = "face / dignity" (丢脸, 要脸,
不要脸).

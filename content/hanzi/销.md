## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 鑠
金, means "to melt metal" — and 肖 *xiāo* supplies the sound, an exact
match.

"To melt away" generalizes to "to dispose of, to cancel, to eliminate"
(销毁, "to destroy"; 撤销, "to revoke") and, in commerce, "to sell off
(stock)" (销售, covered elsewhere in this app) — melting metal down being
one very old, very physical way of getting rid of it.

## Mnemonic
Metal 钅 melted down until it resembles 肖 (to resemble) nothing of its
former shape: "销," to melt away — or to sell off.

## Notes
销售 ("to sell," lit. "to melt away and sell") and 取消 ("to cancel,"
using a different second character) both extend 销's "to dispose of" core
in commercial and administrative directions respectively.

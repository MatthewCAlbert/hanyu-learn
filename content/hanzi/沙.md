## Etymology
Ideographic. 氵 (water) with 少 (few, little) — Shuowen's gloss is a
precise little scene: 水散石也...水少沙見, "scattered stone in water...
when the water runs low, the sand shows through." Sand is what is left
visible once the water retreats.

## Mnemonic
Where the water 氵 runs low 少, the sand shows through underneath.

## Notes
沙 covers "sand" literally (沙子, 沙漠 "desert") and, via a loanword
association, furniture: 沙发 "sofa" is a pure sound-transliteration of
English "sofa," with 沙 contributing nothing but its syllable.

## Etymology
Simplified from 會. The old graph shows a lid (亼) fitted over a vessel with
its contents — things brought together and made to match. Shuowen: 合也 "to
join, to fit." From "to come together" grew "a meeting / gathering," and from
"to fit / be able to match" grew the modal "can, know how to" and the future
"will."

The simplified 会 (人 over 云) is a schematic reduction, not a new picture.

## Mnemonic
A lid closing neatly onto its pot: things coming together.

## Notes
Two readings: *huì* (开会, 会说, 一会儿), *kuài* only in 会计 "accounting." The
modal 会 marks learned ability and likelihood, against 能 (capacity) and
可以 (permission).

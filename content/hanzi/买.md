## Etymology
Simplified from 買. The old graph is 网 (罒 "a net") over 貝 "a cowrie shell,
money" — casting a net over goods with cash, "to buy." Shuowen: 市也 "to
trade." Simplification wears 网 down to 乛 and 貝 to 头, so neither part is
now legible.

## Mnemonic
A net thrown over the goods, paid for with shells 贝: to buy.

## Notes
卖 / 賣 "to sell" is 买 with an extra top stroke (出 "put out" + 買). Antonyms
that look almost the same: 买 / 卖.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 皿 (vessel) supplies the meaning, 分 *fēn* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 盎也 is "a wide-mouthed basin," matching
the modern object directly — a basin, and by extension the flowerpot (花盆)
and any basin-shaped container.

## Mnemonic
A wide vessel 皿 for dividing 分 water or soil among many uses.

## Notes
盆 also survives as an old volume unit in Shuowen-era texts (roughly 128
litres), long obsolete. Modern use is entirely concrete: 脸盆 "washbasin,"
花盆 "flowerpot," 盆地 "basin" (a geographic bowl of land).

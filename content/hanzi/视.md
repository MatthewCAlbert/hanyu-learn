## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 见 "to see" gives the meaning; 礻 (the component form of 示
*shì*) gives the sound. Shuowen: 瞻也 "to look at," analysing it as 从見示.

## Mnemonic
Deliberately turning the eye 见 toward something: to look at it.

## Notes
礻 here is 示 "altar / to show," not 衤 "clothing" — a constant confusion; 示
also writes 神, 礼 / 禮, 祝. 视 leans toward attentive looking: 电视, 重视,
视力, 忽视.

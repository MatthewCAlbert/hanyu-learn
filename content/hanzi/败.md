## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 贝 (cowrie-shell money, valuables) struck by 攵
(a hand wielding a tool) — smashing valuables, "to ruin, to destroy."

"To ruin" extended to both directions of a contest at once: "to be
defeated" (失败, 打败仗) and, less commonly, "to defeat" (打败对手, "to
defeat an opponent") — the same verb can describe either side depending
on how it is used with an object.

## Mnemonic
A hand 攵 smashing valuables 贝 to pieces: "败," ruin — or defeat.

## Notes
打败 is genuinely ambiguous in isolation: 打败了对手 means "defeated the
opponent," while simply saying someone 打败了 (with no object) usually
means they themselves were the one defeated — context and word order
carry real weight here.

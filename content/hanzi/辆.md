## Etymology
Simplified from 輛. 车 (車) "cart" gives the meaning; 两 (兩) *liǎng* gives the
sound (for *liàng*) and chimes with the paired wheels of a cart. It is the
measure word for wheeled vehicles.

## Mnemonic
A cart 车 counted by its pair 两 of wheels: one 辆.

## Notes
Used only as a classifier: 一辆车, 三辆自行车, 两辆卡车.

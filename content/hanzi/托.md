## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 乇 *tuō* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The core sense is a hand supporting
something from below — a tray, a palm held flat — which extends to "to
entrust, to rely on" (托付 "to entrust," 托福 — the TOEFL exam's Chinese
name, borrowed for its sound) the way one entrusted matter rests supported
in someone else's hands.

## Mnemonic
A flat palm 扌 holding something steady, propped up 乇 and safe.

## Notes
托 and the near-homophone 拖 tuō (to drag/pull) are unrelated and easy to
confuse in writing — 托 has no 木/⺡ inside it, only 乇. 委托 "to commission,"
托儿所 "nursery/daycare" are common compounds.

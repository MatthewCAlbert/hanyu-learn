## Etymology
Simplified from 輕. 车 (車) "vehicle" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 輕車也 "a
light fast chariot." The phonetic 巠 *jīng* is written 圣 (又 over 土) in the
simplified form and no longer legible as a sound.

## Mnemonic
The swift bare war-chariot 车, stripped of everything heavy: light.

## Notes
Senses: light in weight (轻重, 减轻, 轻装), small in degree (轻伤, 轻微,
年轻), gentle / soft (轻声, 轻轻地, 手脚轻), rash / frivolous (轻率, 轻浮,
轻举妄动), and to make light of (轻视, 轻敌, 轻工业).

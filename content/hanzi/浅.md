## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition is
simply "not deep" — and 戋 (戔) *jiān* supplies the sound.

"Shallow" (of water) extends to "light" (of color, 浅蓝色, "pale blue")
and, figuratively, to "superficial, lacking depth" (肤浅, 浅薄) — a shallow
pool and a shallow understanding share the same core image.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 so thin and small 戋 (a reduced, diminished shape) it barely
covers the ground: "浅," shallow.

## Notes
浅 shares its phonetic 戋 with 钱 (money), 残 (broken/remnant), and 贱
(cheap/lowly) — a productive phonetic family worth learning together for
the sound, not the meaning, which differs across all four.

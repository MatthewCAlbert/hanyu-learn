## Etymology
Simplified from 風. The old graph has 凡 *fán* for sound over 虫 "insect /
serpent" — Shuowen records the belief that "when the wind blows, creatures are
born." Simplification keeps the 凡 frame and reduces 虫 to 乂.

## Mnemonic
Something stirring 乂 inside the moving air: wind.

## Notes
凡 also writes 帆 *fān* (sail), 汎 / 泛 *fàn*. Senses of 风: wind (刮风, 台风),
custom (风俗, 民风), manner / style (风格, 作风), scene (风景), word going
round (风声, 听到风).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; Shuowen names 弋 *yì* as
the phonetic. Confidence is medium because the Song-dynasty commentator
Xu Xuan already flagged this pairing as a loose match, suggesting the
sound may instead trace to 忒 — a reminder that even Shuowen's own
tradition sometimes debated its calls. The core meaning, 更也 "to change,
to replace," is not in question.

## Mnemonic
One person 亻 standing in 弋 for another: to substitute, to replace.

## Notes
"To replace one thing with another" extends naturally into "generation"
(一代人 "a generation") and "era, dynasty" (古代, 现代, 年代) — each
generation or age being, in a sense, what replaces the one before it.

## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 敬 as 肅也, "solemn, reverent," analysing it as
攴 (a hand with a rod, associated with discipline and correction) alongside
苟 (a component tied to urgency/carefulness, not the "dog" 犭-adjacent
shapes it superficially resembles). The overall picture is disciplined,
careful attentiveness directed at another person — the root of "respect."

## Mnemonic
A hand ready with a rod 攴, holding itself back out of careful respect 苟.

## Notes
敬 is the core of the polite register: 尊敬 "to respect," 敬礦/敬酒 "to
offer a toast," 敬请 "please [formal request]." Distinct from 静 jìng
"quiet," a common confusion for learners because of the shared reading.

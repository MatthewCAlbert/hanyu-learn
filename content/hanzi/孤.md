## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 子 (child) supplies the meaning — a child without a father, an orphan — and 瓜 *guā* supplies the sound. The extension is "alone, solitary".

## Mnemonic
A lonely melon 瓜 on the vine with no parent child 子 beside it.

## Notes
瓜 is sound only. 孤独, 孤单, 孤儿, 孤立. Compare 孤 and 弧 *hú*: both carry 瓜.

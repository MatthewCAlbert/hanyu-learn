## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (grass/plant) supplies the meaning; 洛 *luò* supplies the
sound. Shuowen draws a fine distinction now lost in everyday use: grass
withering is 零, while a tree shedding (its leaves) is specifically 落.
Duan Yucai notes 落 uses the grass radical anyway because a tree's twigs
and branches are, structurally, no different from grass stems.

## Mnemonic
Leaves falling from the grass 艹 by the river Luo 洛.

## Notes
"Leaves falling" generalised into "anything descending, declining, or
settling" — 降落 "to land," 落后 "to fall behind," 段落 "a paragraph"
(a natural resting/settling point in a text).

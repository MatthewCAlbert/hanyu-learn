## Etymology
Traditional 徑 is pictophonetic: 彳 (step, road) supplies the meaning — a footpath — and 巠 *jīng* supplies the sound. Simplification rewrote 巠 as the reduced shape 圣, which no longer indicates the sound. Shuowen glosses it as a pedestrian path, a short-cut.

## Mnemonic
Walk 彳 the path that your hand 又 and work 工 made.

## Notes
直径 "diameter", 途径 "route", 田径 "track and field", 口径 "calibre". The 巠 family: 经 *jīng*, 茎 *jīng*, 轻 *qīng*, 劲 *jìn*.

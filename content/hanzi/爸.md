## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 父 "father" gives the meaning; 巴 *bā* gives the sound. A
colloquial word for one's father, resting on the near-universal nursery
syllable *ba*, recorded since the 《廣雅》 (3rd c.).

## Mnemonic
父 "father," spoken as a baby's first *ba* 巴.

## Notes
The 巴 phonetic also writes 把 *bǎ*, 吧 *ba*, 疤 *bā*, 芭 *bā*. 父 "father" as
a top component also caps 爷, 爹. 爸爸 doubled, second syllable toneless.

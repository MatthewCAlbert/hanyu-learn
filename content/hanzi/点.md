## Etymology
Simplified from 點. 黑 "black" gives the meaning; 占 *zhān* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 小黑也 "a small black mark" — a dot, a blot. Simplification keeps 占
and reduces 黑 to its bottom four dots 灬 (which elsewhere mean "fire," but
here are just the remnant of 黑).

## Mnemonic
A small black speck 灬 marked 占 on the page.

## Notes
The 占 phonetic also writes 站 *zhàn*, 战 / 戰 *zhàn*, 沾 *zhān*, 粘 *zhān*.
Senses: dot / point (一点儿), o'clock (三点), to order food (点菜), to nod
(点头), to light (点火).

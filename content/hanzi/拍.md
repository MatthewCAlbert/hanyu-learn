## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; the sound component has
drifted slightly. Shuowen's original character (written 𢫦) used 百 *bǎi*
"hundred" for sound; the modern standard form uses the graphically similar
白 *bái* "white" instead. Either way the job is the same — 拍 pāi, 迫 pò,
珀 pò, and 伯 bǎi all share this phonetic, so the sound family is intact
even though the specific glyph shifted.

## Mnemonic
A white 白 gloved hand 扌 clapping.

## Notes
"To pat, strike" (拍打) extended naturally to "to take a photo/shoot a
film" (拍照, 拍摄) — capturing an image being a kind of quick, precise
strike — and to the beat of music (节拍).

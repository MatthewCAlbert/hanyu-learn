## Etymology
The old graph shows 庚 (a winnowing tool or a pestle) with grains of husk
scattered below — threshing and winnowing grain. Shuowen reads 康 as an early
form of 穅 / 糠 "chaff." From the image of a full harvest being processed grew
"abundance, ease, well-being," and from "at ease and well" came "health"
(健康). The modern 广 + 隶 shape is a corruption.

## Mnemonic
Grain being winnowed clean, husks flying: plenty — and so, well-being.

## Notes
糠 (米 + 康) kept "chaff." Senses of 康: healthy (健康), at peace / prosperous
(小康, 康乐, 安康). Also the surname Kang.

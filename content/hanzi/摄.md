## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 攝: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 聂
(traditional 聶, itself three 耳 "ears" together, "to whisper") supplies
the sound, shifted to *shè*. Shuowen's gloss 引持也 is "to draw in and
hold" — matching "to absorb, to take in" directly, and extending to
"to photograph" (capturing an image) and "to act on behalf of" (摄政, to
hold power in another's stead).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 drawing something in close, quiet as a whisper 聂.

## Notes
摄 spans nutrition (摄取 "to absorb/ingest [nutrients]"), imaging (摄影
"photography," 摄像头 "camera/webcam"), and regency (摄政 "to act as regent")
— all variations on "taking hold of and drawing in."

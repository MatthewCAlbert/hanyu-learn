## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 欠 (open mouth, breath) supplies the meaning, and 其 *qí* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as deceive, defraud.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 欠 that says "this one 其 is yours" to cheat you.

## Notes
其 is sound only. 欺负 "bully", 欺骗 "deceive", 欺诈 "fraud", 欺压. 其 appears in 期, 旗, 棋, 骑, 基.

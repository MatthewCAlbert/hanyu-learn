## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 石 (stone) supplies the meaning — a grinding stone, a millstone — and 麻 *má* supplies the sound. The verb "to grind, to polish" grows from the tool.

## Mnemonic
Hemp 麻 ground on a stone 石 until it is smooth.

## Notes
麻 is sound only. Series: 摩 *mó*, 魔 *mó*, 磨 *mó*. Two readings: *mó* (磨刀, 磨损, 折磨) and *mò* "millstone, to mill" (石磨, 磨面). 磨合 "run in; to adapt to each other".

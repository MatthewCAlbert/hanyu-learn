## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 贝 (money/valuables) supplies the meaning; 次 *cì*
supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 貨也, "goods, merchandise," is
close to the modern "capital, resources" — tradeable wealth of any kind.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝, set aside next 次 in line for the right use: capital, funds.

## Notes
资 covers "capital, funds" (资金, 投资 "to invest") and "qualification,
seniority" (资格, 资历) — money set aside for the future and standing
earned over time share the same underlying sense of accumulated,
usable resource.

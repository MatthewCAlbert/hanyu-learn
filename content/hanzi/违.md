## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 違: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, 韦
(traditional 韋) supplies the sound, shifted to *wéi*. Shuowen's gloss 離
也 "to depart from, to separate" matches "to violate, to disobey" — moving
away from what was agreed or required.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 straying away from the rule, tanned leather-tough 韦 and set
on its own path.

## Notes
违 anchors 违反 "to violate," 违法 "to break the law," and 违背 "to go
against [a promise/principle]" — always about departing from an
established rule or expectation.

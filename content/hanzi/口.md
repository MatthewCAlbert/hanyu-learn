## Etymology
Pictographic — an open mouth. Shuowen: 人所以言食也 "what a person speaks and
eats with." From "mouth" comes "an opening / entrance" (门口, 出口, 路口) and
the classifier for family members (三口人) and things with mouths (一口井).

## Mnemonic
An open mouth: 口.

## Notes
口 is Kangxi radical 30 — one of the busiest, both as a real "mouth"
(吃, 喝, 唱, 叫) and as a bare box shape (回, 国, 图). Also "spoken" (口语,
口音) and "a taste for" (可口, 胃口).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 巾 (cloth, covered elsewhere in this app) supplies the
meaning, and 冒 *mào* supplies the sound — and doubles as meaning too:
冒's own oldest sense was "a head covered over," before it shifted to its
now-dominant senses "to risk, to brave" (冒险). 巾 was added once 冒 had
drifted away from its literal "covered head" meaning, to give that sense
a dedicated character.

## Mnemonic
Cloth 巾 covering the head as fully as 冒 once did on its own: "帽," a
hat.

## Notes
帽子 (hat, the everyday noun) and 冒 (now mainly "to risk/brave," as in
冒险) are historically the same word, split apart once 冒's meaning moved
on and left the literal "hat" sense to this newer character.

## Etymology
Phonetic loan. 吨 is a 20th-century coinage built purely to transliterate
the English unit "ton" — 口 marks it as a sound-transcription character
(the same role 口 plays in 咖, 啡, 吗), and 屯 *tún* supplies an
approximation of the foreign sound. Neither component contributes meaning;
there is no attested semantic story here beyond "this character exists to
spell a borrowed word."

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 sounding out a foreign word, one ton 屯 at a time.

## Notes
吨 is purely a unit of measure (公吨 "metric ton," 吨位 "tonnage") with no
figurative extensions — one of the cleaner examples in the set of a
character built solely for phonetic transcription rather than native
meaning.

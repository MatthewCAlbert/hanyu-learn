## Etymology
Simplified from 慣. Pictophonetic: 忄 (心) "heart, disposition" gives the
meaning; 贯 (貫) *guàn* gives the sound — and 贯 "to string through" suits a
habit worn smooth by repetition.

## Mnemonic
A groove worn in the heart 忄 by doing the same thing again and again: a
habit.

## Notes
The 贯 phonetic also writes 惯 itself and 掼 *guàn*. Senses: be used to
(习惯, 惯例, 惯常, 司空见惯), and to let a child have its way, to spoil
(惯坏, 娇惯, 别把他惯着了). 惯犯 "a habitual offender."

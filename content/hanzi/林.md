## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: two 木 (tree) side by side — "level ground with
clustered trees is called 林." Doubling the tree pictograph is a direct,
transparent way of showing many trees together: a grove or forest.

## Mnemonic
Two trees 木木 standing together — enough to call it a "林," a grove.

## Notes
森林 ("forest," pairing 林 with 森, which itself triples 木 for an even
denser image) shows the same doubling logic taken one step further —
three trees for a full forest, two for a smaller grove.

## Etymology
Ideographic, with a phonetic-shaped top that no longer functions as a live
sound clue. Shuowen glosses 惠 as 仁也, "kindness, benevolence," analysing
it as 心 (heart) below 叀 — a spindle, associated elsewhere with careful,
considerate handling. Simplification has obscured 叀's outline further, so
no `phonetic` is declared here — the top is best read as a fixed shape
rather than decomposed.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 that handles others as carefully as thread on a spindle.

## Notes
惠 anchors 优惠 "discount, preferential [treatment]," 恩惠 "favour,
kindness," and 惠顾 "your patronage" (a shopkeeper's polite address to
customers) — kindness specifically framed as a benefit given to someone
else.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺼ (肉) "body / flesh" gives the meaning; 却 (traditional 卻)
*què* gives the sound (for *jiǎo*). Shuowen: 脛也 "the lower leg" — the sense
narrowed later to "foot."

## Mnemonic
The part of the body ⺼ you stand on: the foot.

## Notes
Extended senses: the base of something (山脚, 墙脚, 桌脚), leftover dregs
(下脚料), and — via 脚色 — a stage role (now written 角色). 脚步 "footstep";
拳打脚踢 "to punch and kick."

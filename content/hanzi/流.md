## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 㐬 *liú* gives the sound — it
pictures a newborn slipping out head-first amid fluid, hence "to gush, to
flow out." Shuowen: 水行也 "water on the move."

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 rushing out and away 㐬: to flow.

## Notes
The 㐬 element also writes 硫 *liú*, 琉 *liú*, 疏 *shū*. Senses: flow (流水,
流泪, 流血), a current / stream (河流, 电流, 气流), spread / circulate
(流传, 流行, 流通), drift / rove (流浪, 流亡, 流放), and a rank or class
(一流, 三教九流, 上流社会).

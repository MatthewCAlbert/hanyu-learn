## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning — voices answering one another —
and 禾 *hé* "standing grain" gives the sound. Shuowen: 相譍也 "to respond in
concord." From "harmony of voices" came "harmony, peace," then the everyday
conjunction "and / with."

## Mnemonic
Voices 口 in tune, like a field of grain 禾 swaying as one.

## Notes
Many readings: *hé* (和平, and), *huò* (和面 "mix dough"), *huó* (和泥),
*hè* (和诗 "compose a matching poem"), *hú* (和牌 in mahjong). The 禾
phonetic also writes 私, 科.

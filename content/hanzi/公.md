## Etymology
Associative: 八 "to split / turn away from" over 厶 "the private self" (the
original of 私). Shuowen, citing Han Feizi: 背厶為公 "to turn one's back on
self-interest is 公." Meaning: public, common, fair.

## Mnemonic
Turning away 八 from what is private 厶: for the common good.

## Notes
Senses: public / common (公共, 公园, 公司), fair / impartial (公平, 公正,
大公无私), official business (办公, 因公, 公文), male animal (公鸡, 公牛),
and a respectful title or "grandfather" (外公, 老公公, 主人公). 公里
"kilometre"; 公斤 "kilogram."

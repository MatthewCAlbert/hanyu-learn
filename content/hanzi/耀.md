## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 光 (light) supplies the meaning — to shine — and 翟 supplies the sound (*dí/zhái* → *yào*). The older 燿 has 火 (fire) in the same place. Shuowen glosses it as to illuminate.

## Mnemonic
Light 光 spreading like the long tail feathers 翟 of a pheasant.

## Notes
翟 is sound only (the pheasant's tail plumes). 照耀 "shine upon", 炫耀 "show off", 耀眼 "dazzling", 光耀, 荣耀 "glory".

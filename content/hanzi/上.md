## Etymology
Indicative. A short stroke placed above a longer baseline — "above, up." The
mirror image is 下 "below." Shuowen names it explicitly: 指事也 "an indicative
graph." One of the small set of characters that point rather than picture.

## Mnemonic
A mark sitting on top of the line: up.

## Notes
Pairs with 下 throughout: 上下, 上课 / 下课, 上边 / 下边. Verb senses: get on
(上车), go to (上班), previous (上次). Two readings: *shàng* (common), *shǎng*
in 上声 "the third tone."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, and 狂 *kuáng*
("wild, unrestrained," as in 疯狂) supplies the sound. 逛 is a
post-classical, colloquial character absent from Shuowen — its history is
less firmly documented than the ancient pictophonetic core, hence medium
confidence.

Movement with no fixed destination — wandering — is exactly "to stroll,
to go window-shopping" (逛街, 逛商场).

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 as free and unrestrained 狂 as a wild wander with nowhere in
particular to be: "逛," to stroll.

## Notes
逛街 ("to stroll the streets/go shopping") is by far the most common
compound — the destination-less, leisurely quality of 逛 is exactly what
distinguishes it from 走 or 去, which imply purposeful movement toward
somewhere.

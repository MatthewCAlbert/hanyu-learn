## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 勁: 力 (strength) supplied the
meaning, and 巠 *jīng* supplied the sound.

**Simplification reduced 巠 to an unrecoverable left-hand fragment**,
leaving no reliable sound clue toward *jìn/jìng* in the modern shape —
this entry declares only the surviving semantic 力 and keeps confidence
at medium.

## Mnemonic
Strength 力 channeled into one focused burst: "劲," energy or force.

## Notes
Two readings split by grammar: *jìn* is the everyday noun "energy,
strength, vigor" (有劲, 使劲); *jìng* is the adjective "strong, tough" (劲
敌, "a formidable opponent").

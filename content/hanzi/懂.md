## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (心) "heart / mind" gives the meaning; 董 *dǒng* gives the
sound. A late character, not in Shuowen. Meaning: to understand, to grasp.

## Mnemonic
When it settles in the mind 忄: you understand.

## Notes
懂事 "sensible, mature (of a child)"; 懂行 *dǒngháng* "to know the trade";
听不懂 "can't understand (what is heard)"; 看得懂 "can read / follow."

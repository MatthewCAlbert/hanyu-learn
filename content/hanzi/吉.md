## Etymology
Shuowen reads 吉 as 士 plus 口, glossing it "good". Oracle-bone and bronze forms suggest 士 is really an axe or tool head placed over a 口, a container, but whether it depicts a sealed vessel, a blessing or something else is disputed. The popular "words of a scholar are good" is a Shuowen-style reading, not demonstrated. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
Lucky words from a scholar's 士 mouth 口.

## Notes
吉利, 吉祥, 吉他 (guitar, sound loan), 吉普 (jeep, sound loan). Also supplies the sound in 洁 *jié* and 结 *jié*.

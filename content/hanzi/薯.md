## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning and 署 *shǔ* supplies the sound. 薯 is a variant of the older 藷 (used in 薯蕷 "yam") and was extended to tubers generally; the potato came from the Americas much later and took the name by extension (马铃薯).

## Mnemonic
A plant 艹 with an office 署 underground: a tuber.

## Notes
署 is sound only (not "office"). 薯条 "chips/fries", 薯片 "crisps", 红薯 "sweet potato", 马铃薯 "potato", 山药 is the yam proper.

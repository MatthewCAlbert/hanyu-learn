## Etymology
Shuowen: 一舉而二也 "two at one go," derived from 冓 (interlocking timbers)
abbreviated — a stack of two. From "twice / a second time" grew "again, once
more, further."

## Mnemonic
One layer laid, then a second: again.

## Notes
再 = "again" pointing forward in time (再见, 再说, 再来一个); 又 = "again"
pointing back (又来了, 又下雨了). 不再 "no longer."

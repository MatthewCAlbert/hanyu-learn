## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 礙: 石 (stone) supplies the meaning, 㝵 *ài*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The sense "to
block, to hinder" traces back to a literal stone obstacle in one's path.

## Mnemonic
A stone 石 blocking the way, right where you 㝵 least expect it.

## Notes
碍 is almost always bound: 妨碍 "to hinder," 障碍 "obstacle," 不碍事 "no
problem, doesn't matter." Rarely appears standing alone as a single-
character word.

## Etymology
Simplified from 餃. Pictophonetic: 饣 (食) "food" gives the meaning; 交 *jiāo*
gives the sound (for *jiǎo*). A dumpling; older names were 角子 (for the
crescent shape) and 交子 (for the joining of the old year and the new). See
the word entry 饺子.

## Mnemonic
A food 饣 folded and joined 交 shut: a dumpling.

## Notes
The 交 phonetic also writes 校 *xiào* / *jiào*, 较 / 較 *jiào*, 郊 *jiāo*,
效 *xiào*. 水饺 boiled, 蒸饺 steamed, 煎饺 pan-fried.

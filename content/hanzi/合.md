## Etymology
亼, an inverted "mouth" or a lid, sits over 口 "a vessel's mouth" — a cover
brought down to meet its container. Shuowen: 合口也 "the mouths meet." From
"to close together" grew "to join, to combine, to suit."

## Mnemonic
A lid 亼 dropped onto the pot 口: closed, joined.

## Notes
*hé*: close (合眼, 合上书), join / combine (合作, 合并, 联合), suit / agree
(合适, 合理, 符合, 合得来), whole (合家, 合村), add up to (一米合三尺);
*gě*: a unit of volume, one-tenth of a 升.

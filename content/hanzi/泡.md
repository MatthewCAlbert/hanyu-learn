## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning and 包 *bāo* supplies the sound (*bāo* → *pào*). In Shuowen 泡 is a river's name; "bubble" (a water-wrapped pocket of air) and "to soak" are later senses.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 wrapped 包 around air: a bubble.

## Notes
包 is sound only. 泡沫 "foam", 泡泡 "bubbles", 灯泡 "light bulb", 浸泡 "soak", 泡茶 "steep tea", 泡面 "instant noodles". Readings: *pào*, and *pāo* "puffy" (豆腐泡).

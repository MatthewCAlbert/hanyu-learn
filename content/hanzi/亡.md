## Etymology
Shuowen reads 亡 as 入 over 乚 "hidden": someone going into hiding, hence "to flee, to be lost". The early forms are less clear — some scholars connect them to an edge or point (the same word as 芒), others to a hidden place. The popular "a person in a coffin" story is not attested. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
Someone slipping out of sight: gone.

## Notes
死亡, 灭亡, 逃亡, 伤亡. 亡 is also in 忙 *máng* and 忘 *wàng* as a sound part, and in 盲 *máng* and 芒 *máng*.

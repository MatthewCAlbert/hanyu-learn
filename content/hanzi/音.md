## Etymology
Derived from 言 "speech" by adding a stroke inside the mouth — a modulated
sound as distinct from plain talk. Shuowen: "a sound that arises from the
heart and is measured on the outside." Meaning: sound, tone, note,
pronunciation.

## Mnemonic
Speech 言 with a note held in the mouth: a tone.

## Notes
音 is Kangxi radical 180 and part of 意 (音 + 心), 章, 韵 / 韻. Senses: sound
(声音, 噪音), musical note / music (音乐, 音符), pronunciation (发音, 读音,
口音).

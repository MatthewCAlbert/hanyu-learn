## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 彳 (walk, road) supplies the meaning — to go along, follow — and 盾 *dùn* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as walking in order.

## Mnemonic
Walking 彳 behind a shield 盾 and following the leader's line.

## Notes
盾 is sound only (not "shield"). Series: 盾 *dùn*, 循 *xún*, 遁 *dùn*, 钝 *dùn*. 循环 "cycle", 遵循 "follow", 循序渐进 "step by step", 循规蹈矩.

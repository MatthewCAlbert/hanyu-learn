## Etymology
Pictographic — a snake, its body curled and its tail hanging. Shuowen: 它,
虫也。象冤曲垂尾形 "a serpent; a picture of the coiled body and drooping tail."
This is the original of 蛇. The graph was repurposed in the early 20th century
as the inanimate third-person pronoun "it," split off from 他.

## Mnemonic
A coiled snake — the shape kept, the meaning replaced with "it."

## Notes
蛇 (虫 + 它) is the descendant that carries "snake." 它 / 他 / 她 are all *tā*;
它们 for a group of things or animals.

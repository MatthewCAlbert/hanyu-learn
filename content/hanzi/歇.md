## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 欠 (breath escaping — the same "yawn" component seen in 欠
itself) supplies the meaning, 曷 *hé* supplies the sound, shifted to *xiē*.
Shuowen's gloss 息也 "to rest, to breathe" matches the modern "to rest, to
stop" closely.

## Mnemonic
Breath 欠 pausing to ask 曷 "why keep going?" — time for a break.

## Notes
歇 is the everyday verb for a short rest or pause: 歇一歇 "take a short
break," 歇会儿 "rest a bit," 歇业 "to close [a business] temporarily." More
casual in register than 休息.

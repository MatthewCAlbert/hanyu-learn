## Etymology
The traditional 舊 named a kind of owl (鴟舊): 萑 (a horned-owl pictograph)
for meaning, 臼 *jiù* for sound. "Old, former" is a phonetic loan (假借)
onto this bird name, with no obvious semantic bridge from "owl" to "old."

Simplification discarded the owl picture entirely, replacing it with the
unrelated shapes 丨 and 日. Confidence is low, and no component is
declared, because the modern shape has no recoverable link to either the
owl or to "age."

## Mnemonic
A single line 丨 marking one more day 日 gone by: getting old.

## Notes
旧 is the plain opposite of 新 "new" in virtually every register — 旧的
vs 新的, 怀旧 "nostalgic," 依旧 "still, as before" — the owl origin has
left no trace in ordinary meaning.

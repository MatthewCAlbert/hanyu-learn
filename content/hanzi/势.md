## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 力 (strength/force) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 埶 *yì*. This entry, like several others in this dataset
(剧, 赶), is a later Shuowen addendum rather than part of Xu Shen's
original text; the gloss, 盛力權也, is "abundant force and authority" —
raw power, and the position it earns.

The modern top component, 执, is a simplification of 埶 that keeps a
similar shape while being a distinct, easier-to-write character in its
own right. Confidence is medium because the phonetic relationship
between 执 and *shì* is looser than most pairs in this dataset.

## Mnemonic
Force 力, firmly grasped and held 执: power, and the position it wins
you.

## Notes
势 covers raw power (权势, 势力) and, by extension, "a tendency, a
trend" (趋势, 形势) — power in the moment naturally implying the
direction things are heading.

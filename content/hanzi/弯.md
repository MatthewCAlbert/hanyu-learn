## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 彎: 弓 (a bow — already curved by
nature) supplies the meaning, and 䜌 *luán* supplied the sound.

**Simplification reduced 䜌 to a small fragment resembling 亦**, which
supplies no reliable sound clue toward *wān* on its own — a stroke
reduction rather than a preserved phonetic, which is why this entry
declares only the surviving semantic 弓.

## Mnemonic
A bow 弓, curved by its very nature, bent even further: "弯," bent,
curved.

## Notes
拐弯 ("to turn a corner," lit. "to bend at a turn") and 弯曲 ("curved,
winding") both build directly on this character's plain "bent, curved"
core sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 艱: Shuowen's gloss 土難治也 is "hard-to-work
soil" — 堇 (a component tied to hard clay) carried the meaning, and 艮
*gèn* supplied the sound. Simplification replaced 堇 with 又, so the
modern 艰's left side is a graphic stand-in rather than the original
semantic component — 艮, kept from the traditional form, no longer
functions as a live sound clue either once 堇 was removed. Best treated as
a largely fixed simplified shape.

## Mnemonic
Land too tough to till by hand 又, no matter how you turn 艮 the plough.

## Notes
艰 anchors 艰难 "difficult, hard," 艰苦 "arduous, hard [conditions]," and
艰巨 "formidable [task]" — always about difficulty requiring sustained
effort, close in register to 难 but more literary.

## Etymology
Simplified from 詞. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 司
*sī* gives the sound (for *cí*). Shuowen: 意內而言外也 "meaning within,
uttered without."

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 broken into its units: words.

## Notes
The 司 phonetic also writes 饲 / 飼 *sì*, 伺 *sì*, 祠 *cí*. Senses: a word or
term (单词, 生词, 词典), diction / wording (措词, 词不达意), lines / lyrics
(歌词, 台词, 致辞 — also 辞), a part of speech (名词, 动词), and the sung
verse form of the Song (宋词, 词牌).

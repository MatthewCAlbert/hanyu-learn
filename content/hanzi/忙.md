## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (心) "heart" gives the meaning; 亡 *wáng* gives the sound
(for *máng*). Shuowen: 心迫也 "the heart pressed / hard-driven" — hurried,
harried.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 under pressure 亡: too busy.

## Notes
The 亡 phonetic also writes 忘 *wàng* (forget), 芒 *máng*, 盲 *máng* (blind),
茫 *máng*. 帮忙 "to help" (lit. "help with a busy matter"); 忙着做… "busy
doing …".

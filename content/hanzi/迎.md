## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; 卬 *áng* gives the sound (for
*yíng*). Shuowen: 逢也 "to meet, to encounter" — going out toward someone.

## Mnemonic
Setting out 辶 toward the arriving guest: to welcome.

## Notes
The 卬 phonetic also writes 仰 *yǎng*, 昂 *áng*, 抑 *yì*. Senses: welcome /
greet (欢迎, 迎接, 迎宾), go to meet (迎候, 迎亲), move toward / face into
(迎风, 迎面, 迎难而上, 迎刃而解), and cater to (迎合, 逢迎).

## Etymology
戈 "a halberd" guarding 口 "a settlement" along 一 "a border" — Shuowen: 邦也
"a state." This is the original of 國 / 国. When 或 was borrowed for the
grammatical senses "some," "perhaps," and "or," the graph 國 (囗 wrapped
around 或) was made for "country."

## Mnemonic
A weapon 戈 holding a border 一 round a town 口 — the first "state," lent out
to "or."

## Notes
Senses: or (或者, 或…或…), some / a certain one (或人, 或曰), perhaps (或许).
惑 (或 + 心) "to be confused"; 域 (土 + 或) "a region."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning; 生 *shēng* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss is doctrinal rather than neutral — it defines 性
through the Han-dynasty view that human nature's yang-qi is inherently
good — so treat "human nature, disposition" as the meaning and the
philosophical framing as period colour, not as settled fact about the
character.

## Mnemonic
The heart 忄 you were born 生 with: your nature.

## Notes
性 as a suffix turns a verb or adjective abstract — 积极 "positive" becomes
积极性 "initiative," 可能 "possible" becomes 可能性 "possibility" — a
grammatical function distinct from, though descended from, "inborn nature."

## Etymology
Simplified from 學. The old graph shows two hands (𦥑) handling 爻 (interlaced
counting-rods) above 冖 "a building," with 子 "a child" beneath — a child in a
schoolhouse being taught to reckon. Shuowen: 覺悟也 "to come to understand."
Simplification collapses the hands and rods into ⺍.

## Mnemonic
A child 子 under a roof 冖, learning to work with the rods ⺍.

## Notes
学 is the semantic in 觉 / 覺 *jué* (its near-mirror). Senses: study (学习,
学生), a field of knowledge (数学, 科学), a school (小学, 大学).

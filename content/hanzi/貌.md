## Etymology
Ideographic/pictophonetic hybrid: 皃 was the original character for "a
person's face, appearance" (itself a picture of a face over a kneeling
body); 貌 adds 豸 (an animal radical, used loosely for "form, bodily
shape" in some compounds) while 皃 continues to supply the sound and the
core "appearance" meaning. The 豸 addition does not indicate anything
bestial about the meaning.

## Mnemonic
A form 豸-shape marked out by its face 皃 — one's overall appearance.

## Notes
貌 anchors 外貌 "outward appearance," 面貌 "look, aspect [of a place/
situation]," and 貌似 "seemingly, ostensibly" — always about the visible
surface, sometimes explicitly contrasted with a different underlying
reality.

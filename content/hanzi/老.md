## Etymology
Pictographic — a stooped figure with long, thin hair, leaning on a stick.
Shuowen: 考也。七十曰老 "aged; at seventy one is 老," reading the parts as a
person, hair, and a bent form.

## Mnemonic
An old person, hunched over a cane, hair straggling.

## Notes
老 is Kangxi radical 125 (component form 耂: 考, 者, 孝). As a prefix it can
mark respect and familiarity (老师, 老王), long standing (老朋友, 老顾客), or
just be a fixed part of a name (老虎, 老鼠).

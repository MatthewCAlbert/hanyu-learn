## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 齐 (simplified 齊)
supplies the sound. Shuowen's entry is, like 浪 elsewhere in this
dataset, a proper river name rather than the modern sense — the 濟 river,
one of the "Four Great Rivers" of ancient Chinese geography. "To aid, to
help, to ferry across" is a later generalisation, plausibly from the
concrete act of ferrying someone across that actual river.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵, crossed all together, evenly 齐: to help each other over.

## Notes
济 covers "to aid, to relieve" (救济, 接济) and, via "to cross
successfully," economics: 经济 "economy" (literally close to "managing
and aiding," from a classical phrase about governing for the people's
benefit).

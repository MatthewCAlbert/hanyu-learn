## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 石 (stone) supplies the meaning — minerals and ore are dug
from stone — and 广 (from 廣/黄-adjacent forms) supplies an approximate
sound toward *kuàng*.

"Mineral, ore" (矿物) extends to "a mine," the place where it is dug
(矿山, 煤矿 "coal mine") — the substance and its extraction site sharing
one word, the way many resource words do.

## Mnemonic
Stone 石 hollowed out under a wide, open shelter 广: a "矿," a mine.

## Notes
矿泉水 ("mineral water," lit. "mine-spring water") is probably the single
most common everyday compound built on this character.

## Etymology
Traditional 貧 is 貝 (shell money, wealth) with 分 *fēn*, which Shuowen marks as "also sound": wealth that has been divided up until little remains. 分 is therefore both the sound clue and a meaning clue.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 split into pieces 分 until it is gone: poor.

## Notes
贫穷 "poor", 贫困 "poverty-stricken", 贫富 "rich and poor", 贫血 "anaemia". 分 *fēn* → 贫 *pín*: the initial changed, a long-standing sound drift.

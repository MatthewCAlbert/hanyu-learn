## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 立 *lì* gives the sound (for
*lā*). Shuowen's old gloss is 摧也 "to break, to snap"; the everyday sense is
"to pull, to drag, to draw."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 hauling something toward you.

## Notes
Senses: pull / drag (拉车, 拉门), draw out / stretch (拉长), play a bowed
instrument (拉小提琴), chat (拉家常), and — after a verb — the toneless
particle in some dialects. 拉肚子 "to have diarrhoea."

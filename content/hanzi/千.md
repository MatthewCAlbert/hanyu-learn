## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is 人 "a person" with one horizontal stroke drawn
across the body — a tally convention in which a marked figure stood for a
thousand (two strokes for two thousand, and so on). Shuowen: 十百也 "ten
hundreds."

## Mnemonic
A single bar struck through the standing figure: one thousand.

## Notes
Senses: a thousand (一千, 千米), and "a great many" in fixed expressions
(千方百计, 千辛万苦, 成千上万, 千篇一律). 秋千 "a swing" (an unrelated
loan use). Surname *Qiān*.

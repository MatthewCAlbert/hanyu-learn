## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 "female" gives the meaning; 且 *qiě* gives the sound (for
*jiě*, via the 祖 / 组 *zǔ* series). Shuowen records it as a Sichuan word for
"mother"; the standard meaning became "elder sister."

## Mnemonic
The female 女 senior to you: elder sister.

## Notes
The 且 phonetic also writes 组 / 組 *zǔ*, 租 *zū*, 祖 *zǔ*, 助 *zhù*, 粗 *cū*.
姐姐 doubled, second syllable toneless; 小姐 "Miss," 大姐 a friendly address.

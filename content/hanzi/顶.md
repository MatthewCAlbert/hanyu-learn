## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 页 (from 頁, the head-radical seen in 项, 顺, 题) supplies the
meaning "the top of the head," and 丁 *dīng* supplies the sound.

"Top of the head" generalized to "top" of anything (山顶, mountaintop; 屋顶,
roof) and, as an adverb, to "extremely, the very top degree of" (顶好,
"the very best").

## Mnemonic
The head 页, capped at a single point like a nail 丁 driven straight down:
"顶," the very top.

## Notes
As a verb, 顶 also means "to prop up, to withstand" (顶住) — the top of the
head is exactly what bears weight when someone carries something balanced
overhead.

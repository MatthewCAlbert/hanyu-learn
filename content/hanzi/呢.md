## Etymology
口 "mouth" marks it as a spoken particle; 尼 *ní* supplies the sound. 呢 is a
purpose-made graph for the sentence-final particle — carrying a question along
(你呢？), marking a state in progress (下着雨呢), or softening a statement. It
pictures nothing.

A separate 呢 *ní* means "woollen cloth" (呢子, 呢绒).

## Mnemonic
A trailing note 口 that leaves a question hanging.

## Notes
The 尼 phonetic also writes 泥 *ní*, 昵 *nì*. Compare the particles 吗
(yes/no question), 吧 (suggestion), 啊 (exclamation).

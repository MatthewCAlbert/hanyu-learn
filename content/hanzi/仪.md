## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — conduct, bearing, rules for behaviour — and 義 (simplified 义) *yì* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as measure, standard. The "instrument, apparatus" sense is a later extension to the thing that measures.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 acting with righteousness 义 follows etiquette.

## Notes
义 is sound only. 仪式 "ceremony", 礼仪 "etiquette", 仪器 "instrument", 仪表 "appearance". The same 义: 议 *yì*, 蚁 *yǐ*, 仪 *yí*.

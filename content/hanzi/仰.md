## Etymology
Shuowen writes 仰 from 亻 and 卬. 卬 itself is an older graph for "to look up" (oracle forms show one figure looking up at a kneeling one), also read *áng*, so 卬 is the sound and the earlier word. 亻 was added to make "look up to (a person)" distinct. This sense (look up, admire, rely on) is well attested.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 raising their face to the one 卬 above.

## Notes
信仰 "belief", 仰望 "look up to", 仰慕 "admire", 久仰 "pleased to meet you". 昂 *áng* belongs to the same 卬 family.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 方 (the flag radical 㫃, a banner on a pole) supplies the meaning and 其 *qí* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as a banner with five streamers, used to gather the troops.

## Mnemonic
A flag 方 hoisted over the basket 其 of every rally.

## Notes
其 is sound only. 国旗 "national flag", 旗帜 "banner", 旗袍 "qipao" (the Manchu banner-people's dress), 红旗, 锦旗 "presentation pennant".

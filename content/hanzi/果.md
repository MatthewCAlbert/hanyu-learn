## Etymology
Pictographic. A tree 木 with a round fruit drawn at the top. Shuowen:
木實也 "the fruit of a tree; a tree with the fruit-shape shown above it." The
top element is the fruit, not the "field" 田 it now resembles. From "fruit"
come "result, outcome" (结果) and "in fact, indeed" (果然).

## Mnemonic
Round fruit sitting in the crown of a tree 木.

## Notes
Phonetic in 课 / 課 *kè*, 棵 *kē*, 颗 / 顆 *kē*, 裹 *guǒ*. The "result" sense
drives 结果, 如果, 后果, 效果.

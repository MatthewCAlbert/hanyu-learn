## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 川 *chuān* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 說教也, "to instruct, to teach through
speech," is unchanged in the modern word.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 that flow steadily, like a river 川, shaping a person over time:
training, instruction.

## Notes
训 covers formal instruction (培训, 训练) and, in a sterner register,
admonishment (教训 "a lesson [learned the hard way]," 训斥 "to scold") —
teaching and reprimanding both flow from the same authority to correct.

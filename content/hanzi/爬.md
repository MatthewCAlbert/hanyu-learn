## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 爪 "clutching claw / hand" gives the meaning; 巴 *bā* gives
the sound (for *pá*). Not in Shuowen; the older word for "to crawl" was 匍匐.

## Mnemonic
Down on all fours, clawing 爪 forward: to crawl.

## Notes
The 巴 phonetic also writes 爸 *bà*, 把 *bǎ*, 吧 *ba*, 疤 *bā*, 芭 *bā*.
Senses: crawl / creep (爬行, 小孩会爬了, 爬虫), climb / clamber (爬山,
爬树, 爬楼梯, 往上爬). 爬格子 "to write for a living."

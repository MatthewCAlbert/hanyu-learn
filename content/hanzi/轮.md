## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 车 (cart/vehicle) supplies the meaning "wheel," and 仑 *lún*
(from 侪/侢-adjacent phonetic series, simplified to 仑) supplies the sound.

The extension from "wheel" to "to take turns, to rotate" (轮流, 轮到) is a
direct metaphor: a wheel's whole point is that each spoke comes back around
to the same position in sequence.

## Mnemonic
A cart 车 rolling on a round rim 仑: the "轮," wheel — and things that come
round again, taking their turn.

## Notes
Same phonetic 仑 recurs in 论 (lùn, discuss) and 伦 (lún, human relations) —
all descend from a "logically ordered, sequential" sound-and-sense family,
even though 轮's own semantic side (车) is unrelated to theirs.

## Etymology
Pictographic — a tuft of hair, fur or feathers, the strands curling off a
central shaft. Shuowen: "hair of the brow and head, and the fur of beasts."

## Mnemonic
A curling wisp of fur: 毛.

## Notes
毛 is Kangxi radical 82. Senses: hair / fur / feather / down (羽毛, 毛衣
"woollen jumper"), a dime (colloquial: 五毛), gross / careless (毛手毛脚,
毛病 "a fault"). Also the surname Mao.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
平徧, means "level and evenly spread throughout" — and 匀 *yún* supplies
the sound.

"Level, evenly spread" (of land) generalizes to "equal, even" of anything
distributed fairly (均匀, 平均, "average" — a value spread evenly across a
set).

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 leveled evenly 匀 across the whole plot: "均," equal, even.

## Notes
平均 ("average," lit. "level-even") and 均匀 ("evenly distributed") both
build on this character's core "spread level and fair" sense.

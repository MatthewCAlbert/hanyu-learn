## Etymology
Shuowen's 刮 is pictophonetic: 刂 (knife) supplies the meaning — to scrape — and 𠯑 *guā* supplies the sound. In the modern graph 𠯑 is written as 舌 (tongue), which looks like the "tongue" component but is a corrupted form of the phonetic, so reading 舌 as "tongue" is misleading. The phonetic is not declared here for that reason.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 scraping a tongue 舌 (don't try this at home).

## Notes
刮风 "the wind blows", 刮胡子 "shave", 刮脸, 刮目相看 "look at in a new light". 括 *kuò* and 刮 *guā* have the same corrupted 舌 as a sound part.

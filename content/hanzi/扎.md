## Etymology
Shuowen glosses 扎 (*zhá*) as pulling up, from 扌 (hand) with an element 乚 (a sound or form element, the identity is not secure). The senses "to tie, to bind" (*zā*) and "to pierce, to prick" (*zhā*) belong to 紮 / 紥 and 刺, which simplification merged into 扎. So 扎 has three readings and, historically, at least two source characters. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 hooking a string 乚 around a bundle to tie it up.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — a horizontal bar across a gate — and 黃 (simplified 黄) *huáng* supplies the sound. From a bar laid across come "horizontal" and "across". A second sense *hèng* "unreasonable, violent" is also listed.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 bar painted yellow 黄 across the door.

## Notes
黄 is sound only. 横 *héng* "horizontal" (横向, 横线), 横 *hèng* "perverse" (蛮横). Series: 黄, 簧 *huáng*, 璜, 潢, 蟥.

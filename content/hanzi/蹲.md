## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to squat — and 尊 *zūn* supplies the sound (*zūn* → *dūn*). Shuowen glosses it as to squat.

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 lowering in honour 尊 of the ground.

## Notes
尊 is sound only (not "honour"). 蹲下 "squat down", 蹲点 "be stationed on site", 蹲坑 "squat toilet", 蹲守. Series: 尊 *zūn*, 遵 *zūn*, 樽 *zūn*, 蹲 *dūn*.

## Etymology
Indicative: 木 "tree" with a long stroke laid across the top branches to
point at them. Shuowen: 木上曰末 "the top of a tree is called 末." It is the
mirror of 本 "root," where the mark sits at the foot.

## Mnemonic
The bar rides high on the tree 木: the tip, the far end.

## Notes
Senses: tip / end / last part (末端, 末尾, 期末, 周末, 世纪末), minor and
non-essential (本末倒置, 舍本逐末, 细枝末节), and powder or crumbs (粉末,
药末, 茶叶末). 末了 "finally"; 末班车 "the last bus."

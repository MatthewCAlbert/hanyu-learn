## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 贈: 贝 (shell — the ancient currency/valuables
radical) supplies the meaning, 曾 *céng* supplies the sound, shifted to
*zèng*. Shuowen's gloss 玩好相送也 is "to give a treasured object as a
gift," matching the modern sense directly.

## Mnemonic
A valued item 贝, given freely, once again 曾 and without expecting
anything back.

## Notes
赠 anchors 赠送 "to give as a gift," 赠品 "a free gift/bonus item [with a
purchase]," 捐赠 "to donate." Distinct from 送, which covers giving more
generally without the emphasis on a treasured or ceremonial gift.

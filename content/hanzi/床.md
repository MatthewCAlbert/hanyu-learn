## Etymology
The older graph is 牀 — 木 "wood" with 爿 *pán* for sound, glossed by Shuowen
as "the thing one sits or lies on to rest the body." 床 (广 "building" + 木) is
a popular variant that became standard; in it the sound clue 爿 is gone.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 frame to lie on: a bed.

## Notes
Senses beyond "bed" (起床, 床上): a stand or frame (车床 "lathe," 河床
"riverbed," 苗床 "seedbed"). Measure word 张: 一张床.

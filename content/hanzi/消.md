## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 肖 *xiào* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 盡也 "to be used up, to run out," pictures
water evaporating or draining away until nothing is left.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵, resembling 肖 less and less of itself as it evaporates away:
gone.

## Notes
消 covers "to disappear, to dwindle" (消失, 消化 "to digest" — food
disappearing into the body) and "to consume, to use up" (消费, 消耗) —
both descend from the same image of something steadily running out.

## Etymology
Simplified from 餓. Pictophonetic: 饣 (食) "food" gives the meaning; 我 *wǒ*
gives the sound (for *è*). Shuowen: 飢也 "hungry, starving."

## Mnemonic
Thinking of food 饣 all day 我: famished.

## Notes
我 as a phonetic *è* also writes 娥, 峨, 蛾, 鹅 / 鵝 — the sound shifted from
*wǒ* along this whole series. 饿 also transitive: 别饿着孩子 "don't let the
child go hungry."

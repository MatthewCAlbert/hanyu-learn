## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning — words in agreement — and 皆 *jiē* supplies the sound (*jiē* → *xié*). Shuowen glosses it as harmony. "Joke" is a later sense from words that "go together" in a punning way (谐音 "pun").

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 that all 皆 agree: harmony.

## Notes
皆 is sound only here, though "all" fits. 和谐 "harmonious", 谐音 "homophone/pun", 诙谐 "humorous", 谐调.

## Etymology
Simplified from 腦. ⺼ (肉) "flesh" gives the meaning — soft matter of the body;
Shuowen glosses it 頭髓也 "the marrow of the head." The right-hand element in
the old form depicts hair over a skull; simplification schematises it to
亠 + 凶, which no longer reads as anything.

## Mnemonic
The soft flesh ⺼ packed inside the skull.

## Notes
The same schematised right element appears in 恼 / 惱 *nǎo* "vexed" — the one
sound clue the simplified shape preserves. Senses: brain (大脑), mind (头脑),
"computer" in 电脑.

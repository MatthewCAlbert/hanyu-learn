## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth, clay) supplies the meaning — sculpting was
traditionally done in clay — and 朔 *shuò* supplies an approximate sound.
This entry cannot cite a Shuowen entry for 塑, so it keeps confidence at
medium.

"To mold, to sculpt (in clay)" (塑造, 塑像, "a sculpted statue")
generalized in the modern era to "plastic" (塑料) — a different moldable
material inheriting the same "shaped by molding" word.

## Mnemonic
Clay 土 shaped anew each month, like the moon returning to its first
phase 朔: "塑," to mold.

## Notes
塑造 ("to mold/shape," both literally a sculpture and figuratively a
character or image) and 塑料 ("plastic," lit. "moldable material") both
extend this character's core "shaped by molding" sense.

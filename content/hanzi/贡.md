## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (shell money, valuables) supplies the meaning — goods presented to a superior — and 工 *gōng* supplies the sound (*gōng* → *gòng*). Shuowen glosses it as to present as a merit.

## Mnemonic
Valuables 贝 delivered by labourers 工 as tribute.

## Notes
工 is sound only (although "work" suits). 贡献 "contribute", 进贡 "pay tribute", 贡品 "tribute", 贡献奖.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh) supplies the meaning and 包 *bāo* supplies the sound. 包 is itself an old picture of a fetus wrapped in the womb, so it is the earlier word for "womb"; 胞 added ⺼ to make it explicit. Shuowen glosses it as the membrane that wraps a newborn. From the shared womb comes "compatriot" (同胞); from the sac, "cell" (细胞).

## Mnemonic
Flesh ⺼ in a wrapper 包: a womb.

## Notes
同胞 "fellow countryman", 细胞 "cell", 双胞胎 "twins", 胞衣. 包 is both sound and the older word.

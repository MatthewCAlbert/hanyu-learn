## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning and 尤 *yóu* supplies the sound. This is the old, separate graph for "worry, grief", later used as the simplification of the more complex 憂. Shuowen attests the sense of anxiety. 尤 contributes sound only, not "especially".

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 that is especially 尤 troubled.

## Notes
担忧, 忧虑, 忧愁, 忧郁, 无忧无虑. 尤 is sound-only here; compare 犹 and 优 (both *yóu/yōu*).

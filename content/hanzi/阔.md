## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 门 (gate) supplies the meaning — a wide opening — and 活 supplies the sound (*huó* vs *kuò*, a very old h-/k- alternation). Shuowen glosses it as sparse, spaced out.

## Mnemonic
A gate 门 so wide it is full of life 活.

## Notes
活 is sound only; compare 括 *kuò*, 阔 *kuò*, 栝 — all *-uò*. 广阔, 宽阔, 开阔, 阔绰 "lavish", 辽阔 "vast".

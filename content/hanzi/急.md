## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 "heart" gives the meaning; the top is a worn 及 *jí*, which
supplied the sound. Shuowen: 褊也 "narrow, tight" — a tightness in the chest.

## Mnemonic
The heart 心 pulled tight: anxious, pressed for time.

## Notes
Senses: urgent / pressing (急事, 紧急, 急件), anxious / worried (着急, 焦急,
急得团团转), quick-tempered (性急, 急脾气), fast and turbulent (急流, 急促,
急转弯), and an emergency (救急, 急诊, 当务之急).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 吏 *lì* "an official" gives the
sound (for *shǐ*, via 史 *shǐ*) and a hint of sense — an official dispatched
on a mission. From "to send (someone)" grew "to cause, to make (someone do)"
and "to use / employ," and the noun "an envoy."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 sent out on the office's 吏 business: dispatched — made to go.

## Notes
使 / 史 / 吏 / 事 are a related graphic family (a hand holding an implement of
record). Senses: cause / make (使人高兴), use (使用), an envoy (大使, 使馆),
"even if" (即使, 假使).

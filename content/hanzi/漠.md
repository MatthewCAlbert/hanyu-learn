## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 莫 *mò* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 北方流沙也 is "the shifting sands of
the north" — the character named the Gobi-type desert specifically, using
the water radical for the sand's flowing, water-like drift. "Aloof,
indifferent" (冷漠, 漠不关心) is a figurative extension: a manner as dry and
empty as desert sand.

## Mnemonic
Sand flowing like water 氵, silent 莫 and endless — the desert.

## Notes
漠 anchors 沙漠 "desert" and, separately, the emotional register: 冷漠
"cold and indifferent," 漠视 "to disregard/ignore" — both readings of
"empty, without warmth."

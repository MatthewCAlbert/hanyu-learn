## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — a pole, shaft — and 干 *gān* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as a tree-name; the modern sense of "pole, rod, lever" is later. Two readings: *gān* "pole" (旗杆) and *gǎn* "shaft" (笔杆, 枪杆).

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 stick used to do 干 the job: a pole.

## Notes
干 is sound only. 栏杆 "railing", 旗杆 "flagpole", 杠杆 "lever", 笔杆 "pen shaft". Compare 秆 *gǎn* "stalk" and 竿 *gān* "bamboo pole".

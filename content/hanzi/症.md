## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 疒 (sickness) supplies the meaning and 正 *zhèng* supplies the sound. 症 is a later graph, not in the original Shuowen, built for the "symptom" sense that earlier writing carried with 證 (evidence, sign). 正 therefore gives the sound without adding "correct".

## Mnemonic
A sickness 疒 that is plainly 正 correct to the doctor: the exact symptom.

## Notes
疒 is the "sickness" radical in 病, 疼, 瘦, 疯. 症状 "symptom", 癌症 "cancer", 后遗症 "after-effects", 对症下药 "prescribe for the exact illness".

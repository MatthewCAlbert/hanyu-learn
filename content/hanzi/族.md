## Etymology
Ideographic. 㫃 (a banner streaming from a pole — 方 in the modern shape
is what remains of that banner) together with 矢 (an arrow) — Shuowen's
gloss, 矢鋒也，束之族族也, describes a bundle of arrowheads gathered
tightly under one banner: a military unit rallied together. That "banded
together under one standard" sense generalised into "clan, ethnic group."

## Mnemonic
Arrows 矢, gathered under one banner 方: a clan, bound together.

## Notes
族's "arrowhead" sense split off entirely onto 鏃 (with the metal
radical) once 族 was needed full-time for "clan, ethnicity" — 民族,
家族, 种族 — group identity replacing the original weapon.

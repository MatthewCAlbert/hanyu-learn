## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (from 言, speech) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 報, means "to respond, to address" — and 胃 *wèi* supplies
the sound.

"To address, to speak of" generalizes to "to call, to refer to as" (称
谓, "a form of address") and "to mean" (所谓, "so-called, what is meant
by").

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 that reaches all the way to the stomach 胃 of the matter,
naming exactly what is meant: "谓," to call — or to mean.

## Notes
所谓 ("so-called," often signaling skepticism about the label that
follows) and 称谓 ("a title, a form of address") both build on this
character's core "to name/refer to" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 良 *liáng* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's entry is, surprisingly, a proper noun — the name of the
Canglang river, flowing south into the Yangtze — with no mention of
"wave" at all. The now-dominant sense, "wave, billow," is a later
generalisation first recorded in the Yupian dictionary.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 at its finest 良, rolling and cresting: a wave.

## Notes
"Wave" extended figuratively into "unrestrained, freewheeling" (浪漫
"romantic," 流浪 "to wander/drift") — a wave's lack of a fixed path
becoming the image for a person who lives without one.

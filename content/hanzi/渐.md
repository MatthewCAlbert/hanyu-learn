## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, and 斩 *zhǎn* supplies the
sound. Like 江 and 治 (both covered elsewhere in this app), Shuowen's
original definition names a specific river before the word broadened —
here, to the everyday adverb "gradually, little by little" (逐渐, 渐渐),
plausibly following the same slow, incremental image as a river's steady
flow.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 cutting 斩 its way forward so slowly the change is barely
visible from one moment to the next: "渐," gradually.

## Notes
渐渐 (reduplicated, "gradually," the everyday adverb) and 逐渐 ("gradually,
step by step," pairing 渐 with 逐 "to follow in sequence") both keep this
character's core "slow, incremental change" sense.

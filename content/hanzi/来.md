## Etymology
Pictographic — a wheat or barley plant with drooping ears and roots. Shuowen
explains that this grain was regarded as a gift that "came" from heaven, so
the graph was borrowed for "to come." The agricultural word passed to 麦 / 麥;
来 kept only "come."

## Mnemonic
A stalk of wheat, the crop that "came" as a gift: to come.

## Notes
麦 / 麥 (来 with 夂 added) now carries "wheat." 来 is a directional complement
meaning "toward the speaker" (回来, 进来, 起来), against 去 "away."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 昜 *yáng* (the original
form of 阳 "sun, yang") supplies the sound and echoes the meaning too —
water made hot by the sun. The original sense, 熱水也 "hot water," widened
into "broth, soup" — water that has had something simmered in it.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 warmed under the sun 昜: hot water, then soup.

## Notes
汤's "hot water" sense survives mainly in place names and set phrases
(赴汤蹈火 "to brave fire and water"); in everyday speech 汤 almost always
means "soup."

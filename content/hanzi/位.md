## Etymology
An associative compound: 亻 "person" + 立 "to stand." Shuowen: "the places to
the left and right where people stand in the courtyard" — one's assigned spot
by rank at court. From "the place where one stands" grew "position, seat,
rank," "a digit's place," and the respectful classifier for people.

## Mnemonic
Where a person 亻 is set to stand 立: their position.

## Notes
Measure word for people, used politely: 三位老师, 各位. Senses: seat / place
(座位, 位置), rank / status (地位, 职位), a digit (个位, 十位).

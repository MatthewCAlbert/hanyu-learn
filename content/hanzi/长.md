## Etymology
Simplified from 長 — an old person with long, unbound hair, leaning on a
stick. The oracle-bone graph shows the streaming hair clearly. Shuowen: 久遠也
"long, far-reaching." From "long" grew "long in time / seniority," and — read
*zhǎng* — "to grow" and "an elder, a chief."

## Mnemonic
An elder with hair grown long: length, and years.

## Notes
Two readings: *cháng* "long (in space or time)" (长短, 长期, 长城), *zhǎng*
"to grow / senior / head" (长大, 成长, 校长, 队长, 长子).

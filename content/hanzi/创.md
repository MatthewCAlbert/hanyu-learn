## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 刂 (knife) supplies the meaning; 仓 (simplified 倉) *cāng*
supplies the sound. The original character, 刅, was a simple picture of a
blade plus a wound-mark; 創 (with 倉 added for sound) was recorded by
Shuowen as a variant, and it is that variant which survived as the modern
standard.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 cutting into stores kept in a granary 仓: the first, painful
cut that starts something new.

## Notes
创 splits its two original senses by pronunciation: *chuāng* keeps the
literal "wound" (创伤 "trauma"), while *chuàng* carries the figurative
"to start, to found" (创造, 创新, 创业) — the idea being that anything
truly new starts by breaking open what came before.

## Etymology
Ideographic. Traditional 區 is 品 (many small objects, stacked in a
triangle) placed inside 匸 (a hiding place/container) — "to store away,
conceal." Shuowen's gloss is exactly this: 藏隱也.

Simplification replaced 品 with the simple mark 乂, purely as a stroke-count
reduction — 乂 does not depict "many objects" and carries no meaning of its
own here. Confidence is medium because the semantic core (匸, an enclosure)
still holds, but the modern right-hand side is a graphic stand-in rather
than a meaningful component.

## Mnemonic
Objects 乂 sealed away 匸: a storage area, a district.

## Notes
"A place where things are kept" broadened to "an area, a district, a zone" —
地区, 山区, 郊区 — with no trace of the original "concealment" sense left
in ordinary use.

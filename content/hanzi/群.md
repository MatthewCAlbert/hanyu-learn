## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 羊 (sheep) supplies the meaning; 君 *jūn* supplies the
sound. Xu Xuan's note explains the choice of 羊 directly: 羊性好羣，故从羊
— "sheep by nature like to flock together, hence the sheep radical." The
Shuowen even contrasts it explicitly with 犬部 "dog radical," noting sheep
form groups where dogs are solitary.

## Mnemonic
A ruler 君 leading a flock of sheep 羊: a group, a crowd.

## Notes
群 generalised from "a flock of sheep" to any group at all — 人群 "crowd
of people," 群众 "the masses," 群 as the modern word for a chat/social
group — the animal imagery is long gone from ordinary use.

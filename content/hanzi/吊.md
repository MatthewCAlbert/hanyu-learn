## Etymology
吊 is the simplified form of 弔 "to condole, to mourn" (Shuowen: a person 人 holding a bow 弓, to drive off birds from the corpse), merged with an older vernacular 吊 "to hang". The shape 口 over 巾 has no secure etymology; it is a popular graph. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A cloth 巾 hanging from a hook with a mouth 口 beneath.

## Notes
吊 diào "to hang" (吊灯, 吊桥) and "to condole" (吊唁). 吊销 "revoke (a licence)", 提心吊胆 "on tenterhooks". The merger explains why one character covers hanging and mourning.

## Etymology
The old graph shows sparks flying up from a fire (the bottom, now written 示,
is a corrupted 火; the top 覀 is a corruption of 𡆠 "rising"). Shuowen: 火飛也
"fire flying up." The core sense "to float up,
light" passed to 飘 / 飄; 票 was left with a borrowed meaning — "a light slip
of paper": a note, a warrant, a ticket, a ballot.

## Mnemonic
A light scrap carried up on the heat: a slip of paper.

## Notes
票 is the phonetic in 漂 *piāo* / *piào*, 飘 / 飄 *piāo*, 标 / 標 *biāo*.
Senses: ticket (车票, 机票, 门票), a vote (投票, 选票), a banknote (钞票).

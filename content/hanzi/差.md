## Etymology
The old graph pairs 來 (a wheat plant) with 左 (a left hand) — rubbing or
grinding grain by hand, an uneven, imprecise action. Shuowen: 貳也，差不相值也
"a discrepancy; things not tallying." From "uneven, at variance" grew "to
differ," "a difference," "to fall short / be poor," and "to send on an errand."

## Mnemonic
Grain rubbed unevenly by hand: things that don't quite match up.

## Notes
Four readings: *chà* "poor / lacking / wrong" (差不多, 还差一个), *chā*
"difference" (差别, 时差), *chāi* "to dispatch / an errand" (出差, 差事),
*cī* "uneven" (参差不齐).

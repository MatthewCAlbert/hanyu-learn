## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 "wood" gives the meaning — early cups were turned from wood —
and 不 gives the sound, via 否 *fǒu* (Shuowen's old form is 桮, with 否 as the
phonetic). Also written 盃 with 皿 "vessel."

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 vessel to drink from: a cup.

## Notes
Measure word for drinks: 一杯水, 三杯咖啡. Also "a cup / trophy": 世界杯,
干杯 "bottoms up."

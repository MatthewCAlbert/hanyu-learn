## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — a wooden fence or rail — and 兰 supplies the sound *lán*. The traditional 欄 uses 闌; simplification replaced it with 兰, which reads *lán* too, so the sound clue survives.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 railing around the orchids 兰.

## Notes
兰 is sound only. 栏杆 "railing", 栏目 "column / section", 专栏 "special column", 栏位 "field (of a form)". The newspaper-column sense comes from vertical dividing rules between columns.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 走 (to run, to walk briskly) supplies the meaning, and 刍
*chú* supplies the sound.

"To hasten toward" generalizes from a literal hurried walk to an abstract
direction of change: "to tend toward, a trend" (趋势, "a trend," lit.
"the direction things are hastening toward").

## Mnemonic
Feet running 走 to gather fresh-cut fodder 刍 before it's gone: "趋," to
hasten — or to trend toward.

## Notes
趋势 ("a trend") is by far the most common modern compound — the literal
"to hurry on foot" sense has mostly given way to this abstract,
directional one.

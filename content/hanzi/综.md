## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
names the heddle threads on a loom, the mechanism that lifts and
separates warp threads so the weft can pass through — and 宗 *zōng*
supplies the sound.

The loom mechanism that draws many separate threads together into one
woven whole generalizes to "to synthesize, to combine" (综合) —
gathering separate strands into a single, organized result.

## Mnemonic
Threads 纟 lifted and sorted by the loom's guiding ancestor-mechanism 宗
(宗, "ancestral," here only sound), woven together into one whole:
"综," to synthesize.

## Notes
综合 ("comprehensive, to synthesize") is the character's overwhelmingly
dominant modern compound — its literal weaving-mechanism sense survives
only as this etymology.

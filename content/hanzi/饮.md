## Etymology
Traditional 飲: 饣 (food/eating) supplies the meaning. The traditional
phonetic was 酓 *yǎn*; the modern simplified 饮 shows 欠 (an open mouth,
breath escaping) on the right, which reads naturally as "mouth open to
drink" even though it no longer supplies a working sound clue for *yǐn* —
best treated as a graphic simplification rather than a live phonetic.

## Mnemonic
Food 饣, taken in through an open mouth 欠 — to drink.

## Notes
饮 anchors 饮料 "beverage," 饮食 "diet, food and drink," and 饮水 "to drink
water" — a more formal/written register than the everyday verb 喝.

## Etymology
Shuowen reads 奏 as hands 廾 offering up a plant or offering, with 夲 (rushing forward) — "to present, to advance something to a superior". The sense "memorial to the throne" (奏折) follows, as does "to perform music" (演奏) from presenting a performance. The modern shape is badly worn. Not securely attested beyond the "present" core.

## Mnemonic
Raising a piece up to the emperor 天 — to submit it, or play it.

## Notes
演奏, 伴奏, 节奏 "rhythm", 奏效 "take effect", 奏折 "memorial". 凑 *còu* carries 奏 as its sound part.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 马 (horse) supplies the meaning — a tall, spirited horse six *chi* high — and 喬 (simplified 乔) *qiáo* supplies the sound (*qiáo* → *jiāo*). "Proud, haughty" is a metaphor from a high-strung horse.

## Mnemonic
A spirited horse 马 holding its head high like a tall 乔 tree.

## Notes
乔 is sound only (also a surname). 骄傲 "proud" (both senses), 骄横 "arrogant", 骄阳 "scorching sun", 娇骄. Series: 桥 *qiáo*, 侨, 娇 *jiāo*, 轿 *jiào*, 骄.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (wood) supplies the meaning; 乔 *qiáo* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 水梁也, "a beam over water," is exactly a bridge —
the wooden beam-crossing sense generalised into today's more general
"bridge," including modern ones with no wood in them at all.

## Mnemonic
Wood 木, arched up tall 乔, spanning a river: a bridge.

## Notes
桥 keeps its literal sense almost unchanged — 桥梁, 天桥, 立交桥 — one of
the more etymologically transparent characters in this batch.

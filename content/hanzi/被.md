## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning; 皮 *pí* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's original sense is concrete: a sleeping-garment "one and a half body
lengths long" — a quilt or coverlet, not yet the passive-voice marker.

## Mnemonic
A blanket 衤 that covers your skin 皮.

## Notes
The passive-voice use (被他打了 "was hit by him") is a later grammaticalisation
of "to cover" → "to be covered by, subjected to." The concrete "quilt" sense
survives in 被子.

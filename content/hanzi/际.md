## Etymology
Simplified from 際. The left 阝 is 阜 "a wall / mound," giving the meaning; the
traditional phonetic is 祭 *jì*, reduced to 示 in the simplified form.
Shuowen: 壁會也 "where two walls meet" — a seam, an edge, a boundary. From
there "the point where two things join, a juncture" (国际, 之际, 交际).

## Mnemonic
The line where two walls 阝 meet: a border.

## Notes
Senses: edge / boundary (边际, 一望无际), between / interpersonal (国际, 人际),
a juncture in time (春秋之际, 危急之际).

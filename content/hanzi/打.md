## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 丁 *dīng* gives the sound — though
the reading drifted a long way to *dǎ*, a shift that is well documented from
the Tang on. Shuowen: 擊也 "to strike."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 coming down hard on a nail 丁: to hit.

## Notes
丁 also writes 订 / 訂 *dìng*, 顶 / 頂 *dǐng*, 钉 / 釘 *dīng*. 打 has become an
all-purpose light verb: 打电话, 打车, 打开, 打球, 打工, 打算.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to tread — and 戔 (simplified 戋) *jiān* supplies the sound (*jiān* → *jiàn*). Shuowen glosses it as to step on. "To carry out, to put into practice" (实践) is an extension from walking a path.

## Mnemonic
Treading 足 in small 戋 steps along a path.

## Notes
戋 is sound only. 实践 "practice", 践踏 "trample", 践行 "put into practice", 履践. See also 浅, 钱, 线, 残, 贱 for the same sound part.

## Etymology
Ideographic. 冂 (a boundary, a bounded space) with 入 (to enter) inside it:
entering from outside into an enclosed area. Shuowen's gloss is exactly
this — 自外而入也, "to enter from without."

The bottom stroke-shape has since been regularised to look like 人 (person)
rather than 入 (enter); it is a graphic convergence, not a change of
meaning. Read it as "enter," not "person."

## Mnemonic
Stepping 入 through a doorway 冂: inside.

## Notes
内 and 外 (outside) are the basic spatial pair — 国内 vs 国外, 内部 vs
外部 — and 内 alone already means "inner, within" without needing 部 or
其他 attached.

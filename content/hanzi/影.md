## Etymology
Pictophonetic, and a late character. 景 *jǐng* supplies the sound (and, since
景 originally meant "sunlight" and covered "shadow" too, some of the sense);
彡 "streaks, markings" is the semantic, standing for the play of light and
shade. When 景 specialised to "scenery," 影 was coined to carry "shadow, image,
reflection."

## Mnemonic
The streaks 彡 of light and dark that a bright scene 景 throws down: a shadow.

## Notes
彡 "pattern strokes" also in 形 (form), 彩 (colour), 须 / 鬚 (beard). Senses:
shadow (影子), image / film (电影, 影片), reflection (倒影).

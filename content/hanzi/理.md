## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 王 (玉) "jade" gives the meaning; 里 *lǐ* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 治玉也 "to work jade" — cutting it along its natural veins. From the
grain of the stone grew "pattern, texture," then "the order in things,
principle, reason," then "to put in order, to manage."

## Mnemonic
Following the veins 里 in the jade 王: the grain of things — their logic.

## Notes
The 里 phonetic also writes 鲤 / 鯉 *lǐ*, 埋 *mái*, 厘 *lí*. Senses: texture
/ grain (纹理, 木理, 肌理), natural law / reason (道理, 理由, 合理, 真理),
manage / tidy (管理, 整理, 处理, 理财), and pay heed to (理睬, 理会,
不理不睬). 理论 "theory"; 物理 "physics."

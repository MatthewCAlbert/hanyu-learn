## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 攵 (攴) "a hand with a stick — action, to drive" gives the
meaning; 方 *fāng* gives the sound. Shuowen: 逐也 "to banish, to send away."
From "to drive off" grew "to release, to let go, to put down, to set free."

## Mnemonic
Driving 攵 something out and away: to release.

## Notes
The 方 phonetic also writes 房 *fáng*, 访 / 訪 *fǎng*, 芳 *fāng*, 防 *fáng*.
Senses: put (放在桌上), let out (放假, 放学), release (放心 "let the heart
rest"), set off (放炮).

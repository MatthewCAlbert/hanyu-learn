## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree) supplies the meaning, 利 *lì* supplies the sound,
shifted to *lí*. Shuowen's gloss 果名 "the name of a fruit" is as direct as
it gets — the character simply names the pear tree and its fruit.

## Mnemonic
A fruit tree 木 whose fruit cuts as cleanly as a blade 利.

## Notes
梨 is almost entirely concrete: 梨子 "pear," 梨花 "pear blossom." Shares 利
with 犁 lí (plough) and 痢 lì (dysentery) — three unrelated senses off one
loosely shared sound.

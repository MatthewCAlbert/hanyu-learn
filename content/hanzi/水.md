## Etymology
Pictographic — a stream running down the middle with droplets or eddies to
either side. Shuowen: 象眾水並流 "a picture of many waters flowing together."
Compare 川 "a river" (three unbroken lines).

## Mnemonic
A current down the centre, spray on both banks: water.

## Notes
水 is Kangxi radical 85; as a left-side component it becomes 氵 (河, 海, 洗,
没), and at the foot sometimes 氺 (泰, 黎). Senses spread to "liquid" (汽水,
药水) and "level / standard" (水平).

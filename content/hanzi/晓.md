## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 日 (sun) supplies the meaning — dawn, brightness — and 堯 (simplified 尧) *yáo* supplies the sound (*yáo* → *xiǎo*). From dawn it extends to "clear, understood" (知晓, 晓得).

## Mnemonic
Sun 日 rising over a tall 尧 hill: the break of dawn.

## Notes
尧 is sound only (it is also the name of the legendary Emperor Yao). 晓得 "know", 揭晓 "announce", 家喻户晓 "known to all", 拂晓 "daybreak". 尧 appears in 烧, 浇, 绕, 挠, 晓.

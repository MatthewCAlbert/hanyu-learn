## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 日 "day" gives the meaning; 乍 *zhà* gives the sound (for
*zuó*). Shuowen's gloss 壘日也 points to "the day just stacked behind this one"
— yesterday, and by extension the recent past.

## Mnemonic
The day 日 that just 乍 slipped by: yesterday.

## Notes
The 乍 phonetic also writes 作 *zuò*, 炸 *zhà*, 诈 / 詐 *zhà*, 怎 *zěn*
(loosely). 昨 needs 天 or 日 (昨天, 昨日) — it does not stand alone.

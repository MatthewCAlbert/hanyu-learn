## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辛 (the radical for pungent, bitter, or harsh sensations —
also seen in 辛苦, 辛辣) supplies the meaning, and 束 *shù* supplies an
approximate sound.

"Spicy hot" (辣椒, chili pepper) extends figuratively to a sharp,
merciless character or method (毒辣, ruthless; 心狠手辣, cruel and
ruthless) — the same "harsh sensation" carried from the tongue to a
personality.

## Mnemonic
A bundle 束 of pungent 辛 chili peppers, tied tight: "辣," spicy hot.

## Notes
辛辣 pairs 辣 with its own semantic component 辛 as a near-synonym compound
— useful for remembering that 辛 alone already carries a "harsh, pungent"
sense outside of literal spiciness.

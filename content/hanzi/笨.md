## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning, and 本 *běn* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original definition is concrete: 笨 named the coarse,
unrefined pith inside a bamboo stalk — the rough part left over once the
useful outer fiber is stripped away.

"Coarse, unrefined" (of bamboo pith) extended metaphorically to "coarse,
slow-witted" — a person's mind, like unrefined pith, lacking the fine
workmanship of something sharper.

## Mnemonic
The rough, unrefined core 本 (root/base) hidden inside a bamboo stalk 竹:
"笨," clumsy or dull.

## Notes
笨 covers both intellectual clumsiness (笨蛋, "idiot") and physical
awkwardness (笨手笨脚, "clumsy-handed and clumsy-footed") — the same
"unrefined, ungainly" core applied to mind and body alike.

## Etymology
Simplified from 飛 — a pictograph of a bird in flight, wings beating above the
body and tail. Shuowen: 鳥翥也 "a bird rising in flight." The simplification
keeps one wing and the body-line.

## Mnemonic
A single beating wing: to fly.

## Notes
飞 is Kangxi radical 183. 飞机 "aeroplane" (flying machine), 起飞 "take off,"
飞快 "extremely fast." Also "to flit / dart" figuratively (飞奔).

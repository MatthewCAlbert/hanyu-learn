## Etymology
Ideographic. The original character 譱 combined 誩 (two 言 "speech,"
picturing debate or dialogue) with 羊 (sheep, an auspicious animal in
early ritual life) — "speaking auspiciously, speaking well of." The
modern shape keeps only 羊 clearly, with the lower portion a worn-down,
now barely legible remnant of 誩.

## Mnemonic
Words spoken as gently as a sheep 羊: kind, good.

## Notes
善 covers both moral goodness (善良, 慈善 "charitable") and practical
skill (善于 "to be good at") — the same character does double duty for
"virtuous" and "capable" the way "good" does in English ("a good person"
vs. "good at chess").

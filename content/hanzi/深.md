## Etymology

sound. Shuowen's entry is oddly narrow for such a common word — it defines
深 only as the proper name of a river flowing from Guiyang into Yingdao,
with no mention of "deep" at all.

character became common, it absorbed 罙's job as well, and 罙 dropped out
of independent use. Confidence is medium because the modern, universal
sense of the character is a borrowing from a word the character did not
originally mean.

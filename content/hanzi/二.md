## Etymology
Two horizontal strokes for the number two — the tally continued from 一, before
三. Shuowen assigns it cosmic weight ("the number of earth"), but the sign is
simply "two."

## Mnemonic
Two lines: two.

## Notes
二 is Kangxi radical 7. Division of labour with 两: 二 for counting, ordinals
and fractions (第二, 二分之一), 两 for "two of something" before a measure word
(两个). 二 colloquially also "stupid / rash" (二百五, 你太二了).

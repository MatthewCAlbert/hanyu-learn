## Etymology
Ideographic in the traditional form 孫: 子 (child) with 系 (thread,
lineage — Shuowen glosses it directly as 續, "continuation") — "a child's
child," the line of descent continuing on, thread-like, from one
generation to the next.

**Simplification replaced 系 with the unrelated 小** ("small"), which is
neither semantic nor phonetic here — a shape substitution that drops the
"continuing thread of lineage" image the traditional form carried.

## Mnemonic
A child 子, small 小 but carrying the family line onward: "孙,"
grandchild.

## Notes
孙子 (grandson) and 子孙 ("descendants," lit. "children and
grandchildren," a collective term) both build on this character's core
"the next generation down the line" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen: 刂 (knife) supplies the meaning "to divide, to
cut apart," and 歹 (𣦵, bone fragments/remains — the same component seen in
死, 残) supplies the sound *liè*, while its own "cut-apart fragments"
meaning happens to reinforce the picture: dividing something produces
discrete pieces.

From "divided into discrete pieces," 列 generalized to those pieces set out
"in a row, in order" — a list (列表), a rank (队列), or a train of connected
cars (列车, whose 列 is literally its measure word: 一列火车, "a train," lit.
"a row of train-cars").

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 cutting remains 歹 into separate, evenly spaced pieces: a "列,"
a row or list.

## Notes
列车 (train) uses 列 as its own dedicated measure word — one train is 一列
火车, not 一个火车, because the whole vehicle is conceived as a row of linked
cars.

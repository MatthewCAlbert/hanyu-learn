## Etymology
Simplified from 級. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "silk" gives the meaning — Shuowen:
絲次弟也 "the graded ordering of silk threads"; 及 *jí* gives the sound.

## Mnemonic
Silk threads 纟 sorted by quality, one grade after another: a level.

## Notes
The 及 phonetic also writes 极 / 極 *jí*, 吸 *xī*, 汲 *jí*. Senses: rank /
grade / level (年级, 高级, 级别, 上级), a step (石级, 台级), and a measure
word for steps and grades (十几级台阶, 七级风).

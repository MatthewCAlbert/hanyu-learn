## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 詳: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning, 羊 *yáng*
supplies the sound, shifted to *xiáng*. Shuowen's gloss 審議也 "to
deliberate carefully" matches the modern "detailed, thorough" directly —
a matter discussed in full, leaving nothing out.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 laid out fully, one after another like a flock of sheep 羊 —
nothing left uncounted.

## Notes
详 anchors 详细 "detailed," 详情 "full details," and 不详 "unclear,
unspecified" — the negative use flagging exactly what 详 promises but
fails to deliver.

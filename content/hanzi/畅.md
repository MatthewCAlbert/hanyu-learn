## Etymology
Traditional 暢 is built from 申 (to stretch out, extend) and 昜 *yáng*, which supplies the sound. The "stretch out unhindered" sense gives "smooth, free-flowing, unobstructed". Shuowen's own gloss ("not growing") is odd, and the exact logic of 申 here is read from the later senses, so treat the meaning side as interpretation.

## Mnemonic
Stretch 申 out in the sunshine 昜, completely unobstructed.

## Notes
昜 is sound only; its series is large: 汤 *tāng*, 扬 *yáng*, 场 *chǎng*, 肠 *cháng*, 荡 *dàng*, 畅 *chàng*. Compounds: 畅通, 畅销 "sell well", 畅谈 "talk freely".

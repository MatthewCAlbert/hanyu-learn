## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 至 "to arrive" gives the meaning; 刂 (刀) *dāo* gives the sound
(for *dào*). Shuowen: 至也。从至刀聲 — a doubled-up "arrive," 至 itself being a
pictograph of an arrow reaching the ground.

## Mnemonic
An arrow 至 lands, sharp as a blade 刂: it arrives.

## Notes
到 is the standard resultative complement for "successfully reach a target" —
看到, 听到, 找到, 想到. As a preposition: "to / until" (从…到…).

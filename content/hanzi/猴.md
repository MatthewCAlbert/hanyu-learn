## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 犭 (animal, specifically the dog/beast radical) supplies the
meaning, 侯 *hóu* supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not securely
attested in Shuowen under this exact form; the animal-radical-plus-sound
structure is uncontested regardless.

## Mnemonic
A quick, clever beast 犭 as sharp-eyed as a feudal lord 侯.

## Notes
猴 anchors the zodiac's Monkey year (猴年) and a family of "clever, mischievous"
idioms: 猴子 "monkey," 猴急 "impatient, antsy," 尖嘴猴腮 "sharp-mouthed and
gaunt" (an unflattering description).

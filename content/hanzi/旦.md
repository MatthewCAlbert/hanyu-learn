## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 旦 as 明也, "bright, daybreak," and explains
the graph directly: 日 (sun) appearing above 一 (a line standing for the
ground/horizon) — the sun's disk rising just above the earth's edge.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 lifting just above the flat line 一 of the horizon — dawn.

## Notes
旦 anchors 元旦 "New Year's Day" (literally "the first dawn"), 一旦 "once,
if ever" (a conditional marker built on "in a single dawn"), and the
classical opera role 旦角 for female characters — an unrelated later loan
use of the shape.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning; 勺 *sháo* supplies the
sound. The original, concrete sense is "to bind, to tie up" — 纏束也 —
before it extended to the abstract "binding agreement" (contract, promise)
and then to "restrict, be economical with" (节约).

## Mnemonic
A thread 纟 tied around a ladle 勺: a binding agreement.

## Notes
"To bind with thread" → "a binding agreement" (合约, 条约) and, separately,
→ "approximate, roughly" (大约) — an agreement fixes things loosely enough
to leave room, unlike an exact measurement.

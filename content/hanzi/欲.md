## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 欠 (open mouth, wanting) supplies the meaning — craving — and 谷 *gǔ/yù* supplies the sound (谷 has an old reading *yù*). Shuowen glosses it as covetous desire.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 欠 hungry as an empty valley 谷.

## Notes
谷 is sound only here. 欲望 "desire", 食欲 "appetite", 随心所欲 "as one pleases", 欲速则不达 "haste makes waste", 欲 as "about to" (摇摇欲坠). Series: 浴 *yù*, 欲 *yù*, 裕 *yù*, 俗 *sú*.

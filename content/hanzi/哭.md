## Etymology
The two 口 "mouths" (吅) depict the sound of wailing and are the reliable
part. The lower 犬 is disputed: Shuowen takes it as a reduced 獄 supplying the
sound, while others read a howling dog or a mourner. Meaning: to cry, to weep.

## Mnemonic
Two mouths 口 open wide, crying out: to weep.

## Notes
Senses: weep aloud (哭泣, 大哭, 痛哭), mourn (哭丧, 哭灵). Contrast 泣 "to sob
silently" and 号 "to howl." 哭穷 "to plead poverty"; 哭笑不得 "not knowing
whether to laugh or cry."

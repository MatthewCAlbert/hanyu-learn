## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (silk thread) supplies the meaning; 冬 *dōng* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's original sense, 絿絲也, "twisted-off silk thread,"
is fairly narrow and technical; the now-dominant sense "end, final" is a
phonetic loan onto this word, helped along by 冬 itself meaning "winter"
— the natural end of the year. Confidence is medium because the bridge
from "twisted thread" to "the end" is not a transparent semantic step.

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟, spun out all the way to winter 冬: the very end of the year,
the very end of anything.

## Notes
终 pairs with 始 as the classical end/beginning couplet — 自始至终
"from start to finish," 始终 "throughout," 终于 "finally, at last" —
终点 "the finish line" is where a journey's thread runs out.

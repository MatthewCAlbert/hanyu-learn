## Etymology
食 "food, to eat" gives the meaning; the upper element (歺 with 又) carried
the sound. Shuowen: 吞也 "to swallow." From "to eat" grew "a meal."

## Mnemonic
Bringing food 食 to the mouth: a meal.

## Notes
Senses: eat (餐风露宿, 饱餐一顿), a meal (早餐, 午餐, 晚餐, 聚餐), food /
cuisine (西餐, 中餐, 快餐, 自助餐), and a measure word for meals (一日三餐).
餐厅 "dining hall / restaurant"; 餐具 "tableware."

## Etymology
玨 (two pieces of jade) with 刂 "knife" between them — Shuowen: 分瑞玉 "to
divide a ceremonial jade." Splitting a jade token to share it out gave "to
distribute, to apportion," and from there "a group formed by division" — a
class, a squad, a work-shift, a scheduled run (航班).

## Mnemonic
A jade disc cut 刂 in two 王王: a share, a group.

## Notes
Senses: a class of students (班级, 同班), a work shift (上班, 加班), a squad
(班长), a scheduled service (班车, 末班车). Also the surname Ban.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 永 *yǒng* (itself a
pictograph of a long, branching river, meaning "long, forever") supplies
the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 潛行水中也, "to move submerged through
water," is unchanged in the modern word.

## Mnemonic
Moving through a river 氵 that stretches on forever 永: to swim.

## Notes
泳 is a bound form, never used alone the way 游 "to swim, to roam" can
be — it only appears in 游泳 "to swim" and derived compounds like 游泳池
"swimming pool," always paired with 游.

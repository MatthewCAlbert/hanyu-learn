## Etymology
Simplified from 開. The full graph has 門 "gate" around 幵 / 廾 — two hands
lifting the bar (一) off a doorway. Shuowen: 張也 "to spread open." The
simplification discards the gate and keeps the bar and the pair of hands.

## Mnemonic
Two hands 廾 lifting the bar 一: it opens.

## Notes
开 heads a broad family of senses: open (开门), switch on / start up (开灯,
开车, 开会), begin (开始), bloom (花开), and the light verb in 开玩笑.
Opposite 关.

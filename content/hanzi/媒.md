## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 (woman) supplies the meaning; 某 *mǒu* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss plays on the sound of the word itself: 謀也，謀合
二姓, "to scheme; to scheme to join two family lines together" —
a matchmaker's role was traditionally understood as a woman's, and as an
act of careful planning.

## Mnemonic
A certain 某 woman 女 who knows exactly how to bring two families
together: a matchmaker, an intermediary.

## Notes
"A matchmaker between two families" generalised into "any intermediary,
any go-between" — 媒体 "media" (an intermediary carrying information),
传媒 "mass media" — the modern, dominant sense has nothing to do with
marriage at all.

## Etymology
The traditional character 類 was pictophonetic: 犬 (dog) supplied the
meaning, on the logic that dog breeds show family resemblance especially
clearly (種類相似，唯犬爲甚 — "things of a kind resemble each other, and
dogs show this most of all"); 頪 *lèi* supplied the sound.

The simplified 类 keeps neither piece: 犬 was dropped, and 頪 was replaced
by the unrelated shapes 米 and 大. Confidence is low because no source
consulted documents the intermediate steps of that particular
simplification — it is a wholesale graphic replacement, not a recoverable
semantic or phonetic swap, so no component is declared.

## Mnemonic
Rice 米 grains are all one kind, however big 大 or small: a category.

## Notes
A useful check on the "every component is a picture" temptation: nothing
about 米 or 大 relates to "category" — the modern shape is simply not
decomposable into meaning-bearing parts.

## Etymology
Simplified from 時. 日 "sun" gives the meaning — time reckoned by the sun —
and 寺 *sì* gave the sound. Shuowen: 四時也 "the four seasons." Simplification
keeps only 寸, the bottom of 寺, so the sound clue is much reduced.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 marking off the hours.

## Notes
Senses: time (时间), hour / o'clock (三时), season (四时), a period (盛唐时),
"when" (…时). The full phonetic 寺 also writes 等, 特, 持, 待.

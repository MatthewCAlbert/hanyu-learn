## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen reads it as breath let out smoothly and evenly
(从亏 "exhalation," 从八 "divided equally"); others see a balance beam or a
still water surface with the level marked. The meaning "flat, even" is old and
stable.

## Mnemonic
A level bar with the load balanced equally on each side: flat.

## Notes
Senses: flat / level / even (平地, 平坦, 水平), calm (平静, 心平气和,
风平浪静), equal / fair (平等, 公平, 平分), ordinary (平常, 平凡, 平淡),
put down (平乱, 平反), draw a game (打平, 平局), and the level tone in
poetry (平仄, 平声).

## Etymology
Simplified from 兩. The bronze graph shows a matched, symmetrical pair of
fittings — usually taken as the twin yoke-pieces of a carriage, or the two
pans of a balance. From "a matched pair" came "two (of a set)" and the weight
unit 兩 "tael."

## Mnemonic
Two identical halves hung side by side: a pair.

## Notes
两 vs 二: 两 for "two of something" before a measure word (两个, 两天) and in
large numbers (两百); 二 for bare counting, ordinals and fractions (第二, 二分
之一). Also the weight unit (二两茶叶).

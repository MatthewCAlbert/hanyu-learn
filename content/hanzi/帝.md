## Etymology
Shuowen analyses 帝 as 上 plus 朿 for sound and glosses it "the title of one who rules all under heaven". Oracle-bone and bronze forms are quite different and hotly debated: a flower calyx (the origin of 蒂 "stem-end"), a bundle of sticks for sacrifice, or a ritual structure have all been proposed. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
An emperor enthroned above everyone at the top.

## Notes
帝 is the sound part of 蒂 *dì*, 谛 *dì*, 缔 *dì*, 啼 *tí*. 皇帝, 上帝 "God", 帝国, 帝王. Contrast 皇 and 王.

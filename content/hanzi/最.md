## Etymology
The top element is 冃 "a cover, a helmet," not 日 "sun"; below is 取 "to
seize." Shuowen: 犯而取也 "to seize by force" — a military term for pressing in
under cover and carrying off the spoils or the enemy's head. From "the topmost
take, the prize" grew the superlative marker "most, -est."

## Mnemonic
Grabbing 取 the very top prize: the most.

## Notes
最 + adjective is the standard superlative: 最大, 最好, 最后, 最近. The
misleading 日-looking top is 冃.

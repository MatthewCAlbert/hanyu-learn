## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 癶 (two feet, pointing outward/upward — the same
component seen in 发) over 豆 — and Shuowen specifically notes that here 豆
is not the "bean" it usually is, but a shape depicting a step or block
used to mount a carriage. Two feet climbing up onto that mounting-step is
"to ascend."

"To publish, to record" (登记, 刊登) is a later extension — an entry
formally "mounted" or "entered" into a register, the same climbing-up
image applied to a document instead of a person.

## Mnemonic
Feet 癶 stepping up onto a mounting-block 豆 (not a bean here!) to climb
aboard: "登," to ascend.

## Notes
Do not read the bottom of 登 as "bean" — per Shuowen it specifically
depicts a mounting-step, one more case where a component's everyday
reading is not what it means inside this particular character.

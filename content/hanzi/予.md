## Etymology
Shuowen glosses 予 as handing over and says it is a picture of two things passed to each other. Some modern scholars read the oracle form as a weaving shuttle, but that is not agreed. The sense "I" for the pronoun is a loan for the same sound. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
One hand passes a bundle to another — to give.

## Notes
Common in 给予, 赋予, 授予, 予以. In the grammar frame 予以 + abstract noun (予以批评) it is formal. 野 and 序 contain 予 as a sound part. 幻 is described as the reverse of 予.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning; the traditional phonetic
was 責 *zé*. Shuowen's gloss, 聚也 "to gather, to pile up," names
harvested grain stacked into stores — a concrete, agricultural image of
accumulation.

Simplification replaced 責 with the much simpler 只 *zhǐ*. Confidence is
medium because that substitution is a looser sound match than most pairs
in this dataset.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾, gathered up until it's all 只 you have: a growing stockpile.

## Notes
"Grain piled up in storage" generalised fully into "to accumulate" of
any kind — 积累, 积极 (literally "piled-up positivity," i.e. proactive)
— and, mathematically, into "a product" (面积 "area," 体积 "volume") —
a quantity built up by multiplication.

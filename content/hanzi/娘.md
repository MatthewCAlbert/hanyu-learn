## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 (woman) supplies the meaning; 良 *liáng* supplies the
sound. Confidence is medium for an unusual reason: 娘 postdates Shuowen
Jiezi entirely, so there is no Han-dynasty analysis to draw on — the
earliest attestation is the 6th-century Yupian, which glosses it simply
as "a term for a young girl."

## Mnemonic
A good 良 woman 女: a young lady, and — once you have one of your own —
mother.

## Notes
娘 shifted from "young girl" to "mother" over the following centuries, a
generalisation still visible in the coexistence of 姑娘 "young lady" and
娘 alone meaning "mum" in many regional varieties of Chinese.

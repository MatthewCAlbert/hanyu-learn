## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 宀 "roof" gives the meaning — someone lodged under one's roof;
各 *gè* / *kè* gives the sound. Shuowen: 寄也。从宀各聲 "to lodge; roof for
meaning, 各 for sound." (各 "to arrive" also chimes faintly with the idea of an
arrival being received.)

## Mnemonic
An arrival 各 taken in under your roof 宀: a guest.

## Notes
The 各 phonetic also writes 格 *gé*, 路 *lù*, 落 *luò*, 略 *lüè*. Senses: guest
(客人), traveller (旅客), customer (顾客), the objective (客观).

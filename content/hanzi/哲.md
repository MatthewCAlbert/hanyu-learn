## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning — wise speech — and 折 *zhé* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as to know. A wise person's words are a "哲" word; it later names philosophers.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 that decides things with a sharp break 折: wisdom.

## Notes
折 is sound only. 哲学 "philosophy", 哲人 "sage", 哲理 "philosophical principle", 先哲 "ancient sages".

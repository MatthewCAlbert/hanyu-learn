## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 同 *tóng* supplies the sound,
shifted to *dòng*. Shuowen's original sense 疾流也 is "a fast-flowing
current" — water rushing through a channel. "Cave, hole, hollow" is a later
sense built on the same idea of an opening a current runs through, and is
now the dominant meaning.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 boring the same 同 hollow shape again and again until it opens a
cave.

## Notes
洞 also serves as the unambiguous spoken digit for "zero" when reading
numbers aloud one digit at a time (电话号码 read as 洞 instead of 零), because
it is impossible to confuse with any other digit's sound.

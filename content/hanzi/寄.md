## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 宀 (roof, house) supplies the meaning, and 奇 *qí* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's original sense is "to entrust" — literally, to leave
something at someone else's house for safekeeping.

That "entrusting" idea splits into two modern senses: "to mail" (寄信, 寄
包裹 — entrusting something to the postal system) and "to lodge
temporarily" (寄宿, 寄居 — entrusting oneself to someone else's roof).

## Mnemonic
A strange 奇 parcel left under someone else's roof 宀 for safekeeping:
to "寄," send or entrust.

## Notes
寄生 ("parasitic," lit. "to live entrusted [on another]") extends the same
"entrusting oneself to another" image into biology.

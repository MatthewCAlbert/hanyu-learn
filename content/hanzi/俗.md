## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
習, means "habit, established practice" — and 谷 *gǔ* supplies the sound;
the modern readings *sú* and *gǔ* have drifted apart over time, a common
outcome after two millennia of sound change even where Shuowen itself
attests the pairing.

"What people habitually do" generalizes to both "custom, popular
practice" (风俗, 习俗) and, by contrast with refined/elite taste, "vulgar,
unrefined" (俗气, "tacky").

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 following the well-worn path of the valley 谷 everyone else
takes: "俗," custom — the ordinary way, for better or worse.

## Notes
风俗 ("custom, folkway") is neutral/positive, while 俗气 ("tacky, vulgar")
is negative — the same "commonly done" core sense splits into opposite
value judgments depending on the compound.

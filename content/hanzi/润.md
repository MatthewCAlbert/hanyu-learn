## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 潤: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 闰
(traditional 閏, "intercalary [leap day/month]") supplies the sound. Shuowen
ties 潤 to water's tendency to seep and moisten downward, giving the
straightforward modern senses "moist, to moisten" and, figuratively, "to
embellish" (润色 "to polish [a text]," literally "moisten the colour").

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 seeping in gradually, like the extra day 闰 slipped into a leap
year.

## Notes
A 2021-era internet-slang sense has attached itself to 润: borrowed
phonetically from English "run," 润 now means "to emigrate [to escape
adverse conditions]" — a striking case of a stable, ancient character
picking up a brand-new, unrelated meaning purely through sound.

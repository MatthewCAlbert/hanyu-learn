## Etymology
Simplified from 應. The full graph is 心 "heart" with the phonetic 䧹 (a
falcon on a wrist, *yīng*). Shuowen: 當也 "to be fitting, ought." The
simplified 应 keeps only the top of 䧹 under 广 and drops both the heart and
the bird.

## Mnemonic
Learn 应 by its senses — the falcon and the heart it once held are gone.

## Notes
*yīng*: ought / should (应该, 应当, 理应), to answer a call (应声, 应征);
*yìng*: respond / answer (答应, 反应, 呼应), comply / cope (应付, 应对,
适应), meet / suit (应邀, 得心应手, 应有尽有). 应酬 "social engagement."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 斿 *yóu* — a pennant 㫃 with 子
below, streaming in the wind — gives the sound. In the simplified script 游
also absorbs 遊 "to travel, to roam."

## Mnemonic
Moving through the water 氵 as freely as a banner 斿 flies: to swim.

## Notes
Senses: swim (游泳, 游水, 游到对岸), roam / tour (游览, 旅游, 游客, 游行
demonstration), a stretch of a river (上游, 下游, 中游), roving / mobile
(游击, 游牧, 游移). 游说 *yóushuì* "to lobby."

## Etymology
Simplified from 錢. 钅 (金) "metal" gives the meaning; 戋 (戔) *jiān* gives the
sound. Shuowen records it first as a farm tool (a kind of spade), "also: money"
— early bronze coins were cast in the shape of spades and knives.

## Mnemonic
A spade-shaped piece of metal 钅: coin.

## Notes
The 戋 phonetic also writes 浅 / 淺 *qiǎn*, 线 / 線 *xiàn*, 贱 / 賤 *jiàn*,
践 / 踐 *jiàn*. 钱 also a surname, and a weight unit (一钱 = 5 g).

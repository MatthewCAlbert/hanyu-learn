## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 宀 (house) supplies the meaning and 妟 *yàn* supplies the sound; 妟 (a woman 女 and sun 日) is itself a word for "peaceful, at ease", so it adds sense too. Shuowen glosses 宴 as "at ease, at rest"; "banquet" grows from leisurely feasting at home. The common story of "a woman cooking for a banquet" is a reading of the shape.

## Mnemonic
At home 宀 with a woman 女 and the sun 日, at ease and feasting.

## Notes
宴会 "banquet", 宴请 "host a dinner", 酒宴, 婚宴 "wedding banquet", 国宴 "state banquet".

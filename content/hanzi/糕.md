## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 米 (rice/grain) supplies the meaning, 羔 *gāo* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The straightforward sense —
a rice- or flour-based cake — has stayed concrete since the character's
earliest recorded use.

## Mnemonic
Rice 米, pressed and steamed as soft as a lamb's 羔 wool — a cake.

## Notes
糕 anchors 蛋糕 "cake" (a modern coinage for Western-style cake), 年糕
"New Year's rice cake," and 糟糕 "terrible, what a mess" — the last an
unrelated idiom pairing 糕 with 糟 purely for its sound.

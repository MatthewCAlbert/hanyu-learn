## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 山 (mountain) supplies the meaning — towering height — and 宗 *zōng* supplies the sound (*zōng* → *chóng*). The sense "lofty" grows into "to esteem, to revere".

## Mnemonic
A mountain 山 shrine to the ancestors 宗: reverence.

## Notes
宗 is sound only here, although it also means "ancestor". 崇拜 "worship", 崇高 "lofty", 崇敬 "revere", 推崇, 崇尚 "uphold". Series: 综 *zōng*, 棕 *zōng*, 踪 *zōng*, 崇 *chóng*.

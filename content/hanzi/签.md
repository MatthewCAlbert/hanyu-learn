## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 籤: ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning, 佥 *qiān*
(traditional 僉) supplies the sound and reads unchanged. The original object
was a thin bamboo stick or slip — used for drawing lots, marking pages, or
writing a brief note — and "to sign one's name" grew out of marking a
document with such a slip or stroke.

## Mnemonic
A thin bamboo ⺮ slip everyone 佥 draws from, or signs their mark on.

## Notes
签 covers both the object (牙签 "toothpick," 抽签 "to draw lots") and the act
(签名/签字 "to sign," 签证 "visa" — literally "a signed permit"). 佥 as a
phonetic is rare outside this character and 检/验-adjacent forms in
traditional script.

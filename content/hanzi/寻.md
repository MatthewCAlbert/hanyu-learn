## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen, and built from several old measuring/handling
components: 工 and 口 together suggested something tangled or disordered,
while 又 (a hand) and 寸 (a hand-span measure) show a hand sorting that
tangle out, piece by piece. "To sort out a tangle" extended naturally to
"to search for" something — searching being exactly that same
methodical, hand-over-hand sorting through disorder.

Simplification reduced all of this to 寻 (彐 plus 寸), keeping only a
fragment of the original hand-and-measure imagery — this entry keeps
confidence medium given how much of the original picture was compressed
away.

## Mnemonic
A hand 寸 sorting through a tangle, piece by piece, until it turns up what
it was looking for: "寻," to search.

## Notes
寻找 (to seek, pairing 寻 with the everyday 找) and 寻求 (to seek/pursue,
more abstract) both keep this character's core "searching through" sense
alive in modern compounds.

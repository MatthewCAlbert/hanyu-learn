## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning; 君 *jūn* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 下裳也, "the lower garment," names the skirt-like
piece worn below the waist, as distinct from the upper garment (衣/衫).

## Mnemonic
Cloth 衤, draped as befits a ruler 君, from waist to ankle: a skirt.

## Notes
裙 today covers "skirt" broadly (裙子) and, in compounds, extends to
anything skirt-shaped or skirt-adjacent (围裙 "apron," literally
"a skirt worn wrapped around").

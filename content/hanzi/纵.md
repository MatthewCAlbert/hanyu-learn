## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 糸 (thread, rope) supplies the meaning and 從 (simplified 从) *cóng* the sound. A slack rope is a loosened one; from that come "to let go, to give free rein" and "vertical", as opposed to horizontal (横).

## Mnemonic
Loosen the thread 纟 and let the followers 从 go as they wish.

## Notes
Two senses to learn: "to let loose" (放纵, 纵容) and "vertical, lengthwise" (纵向, 纵横). In 操纵 "to manipulate", the sense is "release and pull".

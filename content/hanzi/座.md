## Etymology
广 "building, shelter" over 坐 "to sit" — the place inside a building where
one sits. 坐 (*zuò*) also carries the sound. A late written differentiation
from 坐: the verb stayed 坐, the noun became 座.

## Mnemonic
Under the roof 广, the spot where you 坐 sit: a seat.

## Notes
Senses: seat / place (座位, 让座, 满座), stand / base (底座, 钟座), and a
measure word for large fixed things (一座山, 一座桥, 一座城市). 座右铭
"motto." Keep 座 (noun) apart from 坐 (verb).

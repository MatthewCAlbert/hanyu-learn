## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "liquid" gives the meaning; 由 *yóu* gives the sound.
Shuowen records 油 only as a river name; the sense "oil, fat, grease" is the
later general use of the same graph.

## Mnemonic
A liquid 氵, its sound from 由: oil.

## Notes
The 由 phonetic also writes 邮 / 郵 *yóu*, 抽 *chōu*, 袖 *xiù*, 宙 *zhòu*,
届 *jiè*. Senses: oil / fat (石油, 花生油, 汽油), oily / greasy (油腻,
满脸油), to oil (油漆 to paint), glib (油腔滑调, 油嘴滑舌, 老油条).

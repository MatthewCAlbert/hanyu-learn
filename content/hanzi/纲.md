## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 綱: 纟 (thread/cord) supplies the meaning, 冈
(traditional 岡, "ridge") supplies the sound, shifted to *gāng*. Shuowen's
gloss 网紘也 is "the main cord that holds a net together" — the single
strong rope a whole net's structure depends on. "Guiding principle,
outline" is a direct metaphor: the one main thread everything else hangs
from.

## Mnemonic
The one strong cord 纟, running like a mountain ridge 冈, that holds the
whole net together.

## Notes
纲 anchors 大纲 "outline, syllabus," 纲要 "a summary of key points," and
提纲 "an outline [for a talk/essay]" — always the load-bearing main thread
of an argument or plan, not the details hanging off it.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 心 (heart, mind) supplies the meaning — quick intelligence — and 彗 *huì* supplies the sound. 彗 also means "broom" (and a comet), but it contributes only its sound here. Shuowen glosses it as clever, quick-witted.

## Mnemonic
A mind 心 that sweeps 彗 clean of confusion: wisdom.

## Notes
智慧 "wisdom", 慧眼 "keen eye", 聪慧 "bright", 慧根 (Buddhist "wisdom root"). 彗 itself: 彗星 "comet".

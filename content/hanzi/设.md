## Etymology
Ideographic. 讠 (speech) with 殳 (a hand holding a tool/staff, marking
"to make someone act") — Shuowen glosses the pair as 以言使人也, "to use
words to direct people," since any act of arranging or setting something
up (施陳) requires directing people to carry it out.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 backed by force 殳: an order given, and so a plan put in place.

## Notes
"To direct people to arrange something" generalised into the modern "to
set up, to establish" — 设计 "to design," 设备 "equipment," 建设 "to
construct" — arranging things by directing people toward a plan.

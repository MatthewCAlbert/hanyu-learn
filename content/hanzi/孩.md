## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 子 "child" supplies the meaning; 亥 *hài* supplies the sound.
Shuowen records 孩 as the old form of 咳 "an infant's laugh" (口 + 亥), written
here with 子 instead — the sense narrowing to "small child."

## Mnemonic
The child 子 you rock at hour 亥, late at night.

## Notes
The 亥 phonetic also writes 该 *gāi*, 刻 *kè*, 核 *hé*, 骸 *hái*. 孩 rarely
stands alone — it needs 子 (孩子) or 儿 (小孩儿).

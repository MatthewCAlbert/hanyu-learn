## Etymology
殳 "a hand wielding a mallet" on the right; the left element depicts a cliff or
slabs of stone being struck. Shuowen: 椎物也 "to hammer a thing" — beating
metal or stone into shape (the sense kept in 锻 "to forge"). From "a worked
length / piece" grew "a section, a stretch, a paragraph, a stage."

## Mnemonic
Hammer 殳 striking the rock face: knocking off a section.

## Notes
段 is now mostly the abstract measure word — 一段路 "a stretch of road,"
一段时间 "a period," 第三段 "the third paragraph." Also a surname. 锻 / 鍛
"to forge" keeps the literal sense.

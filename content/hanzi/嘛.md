## Etymology
A late, spoken-language character: 口 (mouth) marks a sound word and 麻 *má* supplies the sound. 嘛 is a sentence-final particle for obviousness or insistence ("of course!"), and appears in transliteration (喇嘛 "lama", 嘛呢). No classical etymology.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 that adds a drawn-out "ma" of hemp 麻: of course!

## Notes
麻 is sound only. 不是嘛 "isn't it", 说了嘛 "I told you", 来嘛 "come on", 嘛 as a casual softener. Not the question particle 吗 *ma*.

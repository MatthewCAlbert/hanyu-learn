## Etymology
Pictographic — whiskers hanging from the chin. Shuowen: 頰毛也 "the hair of
the cheeks." The graph was taken over entirely for grammatical use — the
classical conjunction "and, but, yet, and so" — with the "beard" sense long
gone (that is now 胡子 / 鬍).

## Mnemonic
A beard trailing off the chin: kept only as "and."

## Notes
而 is Kangxi radical 126. Modern uses are mostly literary or fixed: 而且
"moreover," 然而 "however," 而已 "that's all," 从而 "thereby," 反而 "on the
contrary."

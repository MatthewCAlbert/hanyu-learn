## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 協: Shuowen glosses it 眾之同和也, "many acting
together in harmony," and analyses the graph as 劦 (three 力 "strength"
signs together, i.e. combined force) under 十 (ten, standing for "many").
Simplification replaced 劦 with 办, so today's visible right-hand side is a
graphic stand-in for that original triple-strength picture, not an
independent meaning-bearing component.

## Mnemonic
Many hands 十 pooling their strength 办 into one combined effort.

## Notes
协 anchors 协助 "to assist," 协调 "to coordinate," 协议 "agreement" — always
about combined, cooperative action rather than solo effort.

## Etymology
Simplified from 紅. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "silk" gives the meaning — Shuowen
defines it as a pink-red dyed silk; 工 *gōng* gives the sound (for *hóng*).

## Mnemonic
Dyed silk 纟, its sound borrowed from 工: red.

## Notes
The 工 phonetic also writes 江 *jiāng*, 空 *kōng*, 功 *gōng*, 攻 *gōng*, 贡 /
貢 *gòng*. Extended senses: revolutionary (红军, 又红又专), popular / in favour
(走红, 红人, 网红), bonus / dividend (红利, 分红). 红包 "red envelope."

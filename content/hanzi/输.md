## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 车 (vehicle) supplies the meaning; 俞 *yú* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 委輸, is "to transport goods by cart" — moving
something from one place to another so that the destination gains and the
origin loses.

## Mnemonic
A cart 车 carrying something away 俞: transported, and — from the losing
end of that transfer — lost.

## Notes
"To transfer" splits into two live modern senses: 输入/输出 "input/output"
(the neutral, technical transfer) and 输 "to lose [a game, a bet]" — the
Notes in the Shuowen commentary explain the second directly: what is
transferred away is what the loser gives up.

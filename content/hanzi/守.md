## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 宀 (a roof/building — an official residence) with
寸 underneath, which Shuowen glosses abstractly as "法度," a measure or
standard of law — an official post kept within a building, bound by rule.
That reading of 寸 as "law/measure" (rather than its other common role as
"an inch, a small unit") is an interpretive gloss rather than a settled
pictographic fact, hence medium confidence.

"To guard, to keep watch over" is the natural extension: an official post
bound to law is one that must be faithfully kept and defended.

## Mnemonic
A roof 宀 with a fixed rule 寸 posted beneath it: someone must "守," guard
and keep it.

## Notes
守 covers both active guarding (守门, to guard a goal/gate) and abstract
faithfulness to a standard (遵守, to abide by rules; 保守, conservative — to
"keep and guard" the old ways).

## Etymology
Simplified from 陰. 阝 (阜) "hill" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 山之北也 "the
north side of a hill," which lies in shadow. The traditional phonetic 侌
*yīn* is replaced in the simplified form by 月 "moon," the yin counterpart of
the yang sun (阳 / 陽).

## Mnemonic
The side of the hill 阝 the moon 月 rules, never the sun: the shade.

## Notes
Senses: shade / the shady side (阴凉, 树阴, 背阴), overcast (阴天, 阴云),
the moon (太阴历), the yin principle (阴阳, 阴电), hidden or sinister
(阴谋, 阴险, 阳奉阴违), the netherworld (阴间, 阴曹), and the sunk (intaglio)
carving of a seal (阴文).

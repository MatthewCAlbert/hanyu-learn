## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 賀: 贝 (shell/money — a gift of value) supplies
the meaning, 加 *jiā* supplies the sound, shifted to *hè*. Shuowen's gloss
以禮相奉慶也 is "to honour someone's good fortune with a ceremonial gift" —
matching the modern "to congratulate" directly, and consistent with 加's
own sense of "adding to" someone's fortune with a gift.

## Mnemonic
A gift 贝 added 加 on top of someone's good fortune — congratulations.

## Etymology
Ideographic. 广 (a roofed building) over 林/𣏟 (hemp fibres) — Shuowen's
own commentary explains the logic directly: hemp fibre is processed
indoors, under a roof, which is why the character uses 广. 枲 names the
raw, unprocessed plant; 麻 specifically names it once it has been
worked into usable fibre.

## Mnemonic
Hemp fibres 林 laid out to dry under a roof 广: flax, ready to be spun.

## Notes
麻 covers the plant and material (芝麻 "sesame," 麻烦 "trouble" —
literally "tangled hemp fibres") and, separately, the sensation "numb,
tingling" (麻木, 发麻) — a limb gone numb feeling, to the person
experiencing it, oddly like a knot of tangled fibre.

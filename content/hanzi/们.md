## Etymology
Simplified from 們. Pictophonetic: 亻 "person" gives the meaning — 们 only
pluralises words for people — and 门 (門) *mén* gives the sound. A late graph,
common from the Song, for the plural suffix.

## Mnemonic
People 亻 by the gateful 门: more than one.

## Notes
们 attaches to pronouns (我们, 你们, 他们) and to human nouns (老师们,
朋友们), but never after a number: 三个老师, not 三个老师们.

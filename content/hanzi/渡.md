## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — to cross a body of water — and 度 *dù* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as to cross, to ferry. "To pass through (a time or a difficulty)" is an extension.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 measured 度 out and crossed.

## Notes
度 is sound only here. 渡过 "cross; get through", 过渡 "transition", 渡口 "ferry crossing", 渡船. Series: 渡 *dù*, 镀 *dù*, 度 *dù*.

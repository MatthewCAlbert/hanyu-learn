## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 彈: 弓 (bow) supplies the meaning, 单 (traditional
單) supplies the sound, shifted to *tán*/*dàn*. Shuowen's gloss 行丸也 is "to
send pellets flying" — a bow used to shoot small round shot, the ancestor
of both "to flick/pluck" (弹 *tán*, plucking a string the way a bow snaps a
pellet away) and "bullet, projectile" (弹 *dàn*, the pellet itself).

## Mnemonic
A bow 弓 launching a single 单 pellet — flick, and it's gone.

## Notes
Two readings by function: *tán* is the verb (弹钢琴 "to play piano," 弹跳 "to
spring/bounce"); *dàn* is the projectile noun (子弹 "bullet," 炸弹 "bomb").
Both trace back to the same bow-and-pellet image.

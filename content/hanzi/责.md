## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 贝 (money/valuables) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 朿 *cì* "a thorn." Shuowen's gloss, 求也, "to demand,
to seek payment," names a creditor pressing for what is owed — the root
of both "responsibility" (what you owe, in duty) and "to blame" (to
press someone for an account of their failure).

The modern top component, 龶, is a regularised remnant of 朿. Confidence
is medium because that remnant is not independently recognisable as a
sound clue in ordinary reading.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 owed, pressed for like a thorn 龶 that won't stop poking: a
debt, a duty, a demand.

## Notes
责 splits into "duty, responsibility" (责任, 负责) and "to blame, to
reproach" (指责, 责怪) — both are the same underlying pressure, applied
either to oneself (a duty to fulfil) or to someone else (a failure to
answer for).

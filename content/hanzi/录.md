## Etymology
彔 (the modern 录's ancestor) is an old, independent ideograph: notches cut
into wood, 刻木彔彔也 — a picture of marking, tallying. Traditional 錄
added 金 (metal) on top of this as a semantic layer, originally naming a
metallic colour (金色也, "the colour of metal," between blue and yellow);
that "colour" sense was later overtaken entirely by the borrowed meaning
"to record, to write down."

Modern simplification dropped 金 and reverted to the bare ancient 彔/录 —
reusing the original root rather than reducing the traditional character
piece by piece. Confidence is medium because what today's 录 shows is that
original notched-wood ideograph, not a semantic-plus-phonetic pair built
for "recording."

## Mnemonic
Notches 彐 cut into a stream of wood 氺: a mark left, a record kept.

## Notes
录 joins 极, 证, 胜 as another case of simplification resurrecting a small,
ancient character rather than trimming the traditional one component by
component.

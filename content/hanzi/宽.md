## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 宀 (roof, house) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's own
definition is specifically "a house that is spacious" — and 莧 (simplified
to 苋) supplied the sound.

"Spacious" (a house) generalized to "wide" of anything (宽的路, a wide
road) and, figuratively, to "lenient, tolerant" (宽容, 宽大) — a spacious
house making room for others is a natural metaphor for a spacious,
accommodating temperament.

## Mnemonic
A roof 宀 stretched wide enough to hold a whole patch of purslane 苋
underneath it: "宽," spacious.

## Notes
宽 and 严 (strict) form a natural antonym pair in describing temperament
(宽严并济, "combining leniency with strictness") as well as in their
literal spatial/measurement senses.

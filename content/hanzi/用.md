## Etymology
Not securely attested. The oracle-bone graph is variously read as a bucket, a
bell, or a panel of a wooden fence; Shuowen's analysis (卜 "divination" + 中)
is a reading of the later shape. Whatever it depicted, the character was fixed
early on "to use, to employ," and by extension "expenditure" (费用) and the
auxiliary "need" (不用).

## Mnemonic
A tool taken up and put to work.

## Notes
用 is Kangxi radical 101. It heads 甩 "to fling," 甫, and appears in 备 / 備.
The negative 不用 "need not" is very common; the affirmative uses 要 or 得.

## Etymology
Ideographic: 木 (tree) below, with a rounded shape above depicting a
cluster hanging from a branch — a flower head, a fruit, or a cloud-like
mass. Not in Shuowen. The core image, something round and clustered
attached above a stem, is why 朵 became the standard classifier for flowers
and clouds (一朵花, 一朵云).

## Mnemonic
A round bloom 几 sitting atop its branch 木.

## Notes
朵 is almost entirely a classifier in modern use (一朵花 "a flower," 一朵云 "a
cloud") and rarely stands alone as a noun. Also appears in 耳朵 "ear" — an
unrelated, purely phonetic use with no floral connection.

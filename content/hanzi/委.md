## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 委 as 委随也, "to yield, to follow along
compliantly," and analyses the graph as 女 (woman) under 禾 (grain,
bending pliantly in the wind) — the character reflects an old-fashioned
gender association between women and yielding compliance, which is worth
naming plainly rather than passing over. "To entrust, to appoint" (委托,
任命) grew from that "yielding, following" sense: to entrust someone is to
let a matter bend to their handling.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾 bending pliant in the wind, entrusted to bend where it must.

## Notes
委 anchors 委托 "to entrust," 委员会 "committee," and 委屈 "to feel wronged/
put out" — the last a modern extension of "yielding" toward the sense of
having had to bend under unfair treatment.

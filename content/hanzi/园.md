## Etymology
Simplified from 園. 囗 "an enclosure" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 所以樹果也
"the walled plot where fruit trees are grown." The traditional phonetic 袁
*yuán* is replaced in the simplified form by the simpler 元 *yuán*.

## Mnemonic
A fenced 囗 plot of ground for growing things: a garden.

## Notes
Senses: a garden or orchard for cultivation (花园, 果园, 菜园, 园丁), and a
laid-out place for the public (公园, 动物园, 游乐园, 校园). 园地
"a field / sphere (of activity)."

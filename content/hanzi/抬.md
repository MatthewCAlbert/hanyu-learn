## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 台 *tái* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The sense "to lift, to raise" — often
with more than one pair of hands, as in carrying something jointly — has
been stable since the character's earliest attestations.

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 lifting something up onto a platform 台.

## Notes
抬 is the everyday verb for raising (抬头 "to raise one's head," 抬起来 "lift
it up") and for joint carrying (两个人抬 "two people carrying it together").
Figuratively, 抬高 "to raise" (prices, status) and 抬杠 "to bicker/argue for
the sake of arguing."

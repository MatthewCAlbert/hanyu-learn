## Etymology
An associative compound: 京 "a high structure / mound" + 尤 "outstanding,
surpassing." Shuowen: 就高也 "to go up to the high place" — to move toward and
reach the highest point. From "to approach, to attain" grew "to accomplish"
(成就, 就业) and the grammatical adverb "then / precisely / only" (就走, 就是).

## Mnemonic
Heading straight for the highest tower 京 and getting there.

## Notes
京 "capital / high building" also in 景, 鲸 / 鯨, and simplified 惊
(traditional 驚 uses 敬). The grammatical 就 —
immediacy, emphasis, restriction — is one of the busiest function words in
Mandarin.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 犬 (dog) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's oddly specific
definition describes a dog suddenly, silently chasing after someone —
and 黑 *hēi* ("black," here carrying only sound) supplies the reading.
The exact leap from "a dog's sudden silent pursuit" to the general
adjective "silent, quiet" is not something this entry can trace with
full confidence, hence medium.

## Mnemonic
A black 黑 dog 犬 stalking silently, giving nothing away: "默," silent.

## Notes
沉默 ("silent, taciturn," covered elsewhere in this app via 沉) and 默默
(reduplicated, "silently, without a word") both build on this
character's core "quiet, unspoken" sense.

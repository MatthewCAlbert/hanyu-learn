## Etymology
Ideographic rather than purely pictophonetic, despite the surface
resemblance to a sound-component structure: Shuowen glosses 悉 as 詳盡也,
"thorough, exhaustive," and analyses it as 心 (mind) combined with 釆 —
itself a pictograph of an animal's paw print, used elsewhere for "to
distinguish, to discern in detail" (as in 释, 采). A mind that discerns
every detail is exactly "to know thoroughly."

## Mnemonic
A mind 心 that tracks every detail 釆, missing nothing.

## Notes
悉 anchors 熟悉 "familiar with," 知悉 "to be informed of" (formal), and
获悉 "to learn [that]" (news register) — always about complete, detailed
knowledge rather than a passing awareness.

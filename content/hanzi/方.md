## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen reads the graph as two boats lashed side by
side; others see a plough, or a knife hafted on a handle. The oracle-bone form
does not settle it. The meanings are wide — "square," "direction / region"
(东方, 地方), "method / a prescription" (方法, 药方), and "just now" (方才).

## Mnemonic
A squared-off shape, borrowed for "square," "side," and "way."

## Notes
方 is Kangxi radical 70 and the phonetic in 房 *fáng*, 放 *fàng*, 访 / 訪
*fǎng*, 芳 *fāng*, 防 *fáng*. As a left component it can also be 㫃 (旅, 族,
旗).

## Etymology
Associative: 夂, a knotted cord-end meaning "to finish" (the original of 終),
over 冫 "ice." Shuowen: 四時盡也 "when the four seasons come to their end" —
the icebound close of the year.

## Mnemonic
The year tied off 夂 and frozen 冫: winter.

## Notes
终 "to end" took over the "finish" sense; 冬 kept the season. Senses: winter
(冬天, 冬季, 隆冬, 过冬), and in 冬冬 / 咚咚 the boom of a drum. 冬眠
"hibernation"; 冬至 "the winter solstice."

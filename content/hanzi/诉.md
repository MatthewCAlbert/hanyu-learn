## Etymology
Simplified from 訴. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; the right element gives
the sound. Shuowen's old form has 屰 *nì* as phonetic; the received character
uses 斥 *chì*, a loose phonetic for *sù*. Meaning: to tell, to state one's
case, to complain — hence 告诉, 起诉, 诉苦.

## Mnemonic
Laying out your grievance 斥 in words 讠.

## Notes
The 斥 phonetic fit is imperfect (*chì* vs *sù*), a common situation with
言-side words. Compare the near-synonym 告.

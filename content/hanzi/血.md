## Etymology
Pictographic. 皿 (a ritual vessel) with a mark above it representing a drop
of blood — Shuowen glosses it as sacrificial animal blood offered up in a
vessel, 祭所薦牲血也. The oracle-bone form shows the drop as a small circle
inside the vessel's rim; later scripts flattened it to the short stroke
seen today.

## Mnemonic
A drop 丿 caught in a bowl 皿: blood.

## Notes
Colloquially read *xiě* in isolated everyday words (流血, 输血) but *xuè*
in compounds and set phrases (血液, 血管, 高血压) — both are the same
character, split by register rather than meaning.

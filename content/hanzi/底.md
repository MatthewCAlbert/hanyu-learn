## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 广 (a building/shelter, used generally for architectural and
spatial senses) supplies the meaning; 氐 *dǐ* supplies the sound. Shuowen
gives two senses side by side — "a dwelling in the mountains" and, more
usefully for the modern word, simply 下也 "below, underneath."

## Mnemonic
Underneath a roof 广, right at the very base 氐: the bottom.

## Notes
"The bottom" extends naturally to "the end" in time — 年底 "year-end," 月底
"end of the month" — the bottom of a container standing in for the bottom
of a period.

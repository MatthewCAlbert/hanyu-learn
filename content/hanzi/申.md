## Etymology
A pictograph of lightning — the same bent, forking source shape behind
电/電 (electricity/lightning) and 雷 (thunder, both covered elsewhere in
this app). Lightning's sudden, far-reaching extension across the sky
gave the extended sense "to extend, to state at length, to explain" (申
请, covered elsewhere in this app) — a claim or request stretched out and
made explicit, the way a lightning bolt stretches across the sky. 申 is
also, separately, the ninth of the twelve Earthly Branches, an unrelated
calendrical use riding on the same graph.

## Mnemonic
A bolt of lightning splitting the sky wide open, extending its reach in
an instant: "申," to extend or state.

## Notes
申请 (to apply, covered elsewhere in this app) and 电/雷 (electricity and
thunder) all trace back to the same ancient lightning pictograph — three
very different modern words sharing one deep root.

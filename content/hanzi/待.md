## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 彳 (a component marking movement/the road) supplies the
meaning; 寺 *sì* supplies the sound — *dài* and *sì* diverged over time,
but the pairing is Shuowen-attested. The original sense, 竢也 "to wait,"
is unchanged today.

## Mnemonic
Standing at the roadside 彳, waiting outside the temple 寺.

## Notes
"To wait" extends to "to treat, to deal with" (对待, 招待) — how you wait
on someone becomes how you treat them — and separately to "about to,
going to" (待要) in more literary registers.

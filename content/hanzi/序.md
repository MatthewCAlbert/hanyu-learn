## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 广 (a shelter, building) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
original definition is startlingly architectural, "the east-west walls
[of a hall]" — and 予 *yú* supplies the sound.

From naming a hall's structural walls, 序 generalized to "order,
sequence" (顺序, 次序) — the walls of a building establishing its
structure and layout — and, separately, to "a preface" (序言), the
introductory space one passes through before entering the main hall/text.

## Mnemonic
The walls 广 of a hall, giving structure and order 予 to everything they
enclose: "序," order — or the preface that stands before the main room.

## Notes
顺序 ("sequence, order") and 序言 ("preface") look unrelated in English but
share this one character's core "structuring walls" image, applied to
arrangement and to a text's entryway respectively.

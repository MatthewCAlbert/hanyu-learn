## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 力 "strength" gives the meaning; 奴 *nú* gives the sound (for
*nǔ*). Meaning: to put out one's strength, to strive.

## Mnemonic
Straining every muscle 力: making an effort.

## Notes
The 奴 phonetic also writes 怒 *nù* (anger). 努力 "to make an effort" (verb +
object; see the word entry); 努嘴 "to jut the lips as a signal."

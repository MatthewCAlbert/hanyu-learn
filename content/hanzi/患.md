## Etymology
Shuowen reads 患 as 心 with something piercing through it from above — worry that "pierces" the heart — plus a sound element. The modern upper part 串 is the same piercing shape (a string passing through two objects). The exact analysis of the upper part is debated. The sense "worry, misfortune, disease" all stem from trouble in the heart.

## Mnemonic
A skewer 串 through the heart 心: calamity.

## Notes
患者 "patient", 患病, 后患, 隐患 "hidden danger", 祸患. 串 *chuàn* is also the everyday word "to string together".

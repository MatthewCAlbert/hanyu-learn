## Etymology
舟 "boat" gives the meaning; the right side 㕣 (几 over 口) carried the sound
— Shuowen reads it as a reduced 鉛 *yán*. 舟 is the older, more literary word;
船 is the everyday one.

## Mnemonic
A 舟 boat, written large for the everyday word: a ship.

## Notes
Senses: boat / ship / vessel of any size (小船, 轮船, 帆船, 船只). 船舱
"cabin / hold"; 船员 "crew member"; 上船 / 下船 "to board / disembark".

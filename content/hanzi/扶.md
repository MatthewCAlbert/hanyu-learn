## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 夫 *fū* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 佐也 "to assist" matches the modern
sense directly: to support someone with your hand, literally or
figuratively.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 steadying a grown man 夫 who's stumbled.

## Notes
扶 covers physical support (扶老人 "to help an elderly person [up/along]," 扶
着墙 "to steady oneself against the wall") and figurative aid (扶贫 "poverty
relief," 扶持 "to support/nurture" a person or cause).

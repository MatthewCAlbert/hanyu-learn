## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 厭: 厂 (a cliff/press) supplies the meaning —
Shuowen's gloss 笮也 is "to press down" — while 猒 *yàn* supplied the sound
in the traditional form. 猒 itself independently meant "sated, having had
enough [of food]," and that sense of being pressed to the point of excess
is very likely what carried into "to detest, to be sick of." Simplification
replaced 猒 with 犬, so the modern 厌 no longer visibly carries a working
phonetic — 犬 here is a graphic stand-in, not "dog" as a meaningful
component.

## Mnemonic

and "rotten" — the two are unrelated.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree) supplies the meaning, 兆 *zhào* supplies the sound,
shifted to *táo*. Shuowen's gloss 果也 "a fruit" is as plain as it gets — the
character simply names the peach tree and its fruit.

## Mnemonic
A fruit tree 木 whose blossoms are the first sign 兆 that spring has come.

## Notes
桃 carries strong cultural symbolism beyond the fruit itself: 桃花 "peach
blossom" (spring, romance), 世外桃源 "a hidden utopia" (from the Peach
Blossom Spring legend), and 桃子 as the everyday word for the fruit.

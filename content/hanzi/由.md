## Etymology
Not securely attested. The graph has been read as a sprouting seed with a
shoot pushing out of the top, or as a jar / a lamp; Shuowen has no settled
entry. The meanings are grammatical and abstract: "from, by, via," "a cause /
reason" (理由, 缘由), "to follow," and "up to (someone)" (由你决定).

## Mnemonic
Something emerging from a source — "coming from, by way of."

## Notes
As a coverb: 由他负责 "he is responsible for it," 由北京出发 "set off from
Beijing." 由于 "owing to"; 自由 "free" (self-determined); 不由得 "can't help
but."

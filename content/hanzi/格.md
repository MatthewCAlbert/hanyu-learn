## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (wood) supplies the meaning; 各 *gè* supplies the sound.
The original, concrete sense, 木長皃 "the appearance of long branches,"
generalised through "a long straight line" into "a grid, a rule, a
standard" — the abstract senses that dominate the modern word.

## Mnemonic
Each 各 wooden 木 slat, laid out in a grid: a pattern, a standard.

## Notes
格 covers a wide grammatical/structural range without straying from
"a fixed frame": 表格 "a form/table," 性格 "personality" (one's fixed
pattern of behaviour), 及格 "to pass [a standard]," 格外 "especially"
(outside the usual frame).

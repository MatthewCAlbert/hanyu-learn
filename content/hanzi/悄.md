## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning, 肖 *xiào* supplies the
sound, shifted to *qiāo*. Not in Shuowen with this exact reading, though a
related classical sense ("anxious, worried") is recorded. The dominant
modern sense "quiet, silent" is a later development from a mind gone
still.

## Mnemonic
A mind 忄 gone as still and unremarked as a shadow 肖.

## Notes
悄悄 "quietly, stealthily" and 静悄悄 "hushed and silent" are the everyday
forms; the reduplicated 悄悄 is far more common in speech than 悄 standing
alone.

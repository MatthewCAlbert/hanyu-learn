## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 欠 (breath escaping — the "yawn/exhale" component) supplies
the meaning, 斤 *jīn* supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's
gloss 笑喜也 "to laugh with joy" matches the modern "delighted, happy"
directly — joy expressed as an audible breath or laugh.

## Mnemonic
A breath 欠 let out light as an axe-blow 斤, in pure delight.

## Notes
欣 is largely literary/formal today: 欣赏 "to appreciate/enjoy," 欣慰
"gratified," 欢欣 "joyful" — a more elevated register than everyday 高兴.

## Etymology
Shuowen: 罪 originally meant a bamboo fish-trap net, from 网 (net, here written 罒) with 非 as the sound element. The word for "crime" was 辠 (自 over 辛); Shuowen reports that the First Emperor of Qin disliked 辠 because it resembled 皇, and had 罪 used for the crime word instead. So "net" and "crime" were joined by decree, not by logic.

## Mnemonic
Caught in a net 罒 of wrongdoing 非 — it is a crime.

## Notes
犯罪, 罪犯, 罪恶, 得罪 "offend". 非 is not a meaning element here. 辠 is long obsolete.

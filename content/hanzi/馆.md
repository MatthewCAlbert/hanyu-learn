## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 饣 (食) "food" gives the meaning; 官 *guān* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 客舍也 "a lodging house for guests" — a place where travellers were
put up and fed. From "hostel" the word broadened to any public building of a
set function.

## Mnemonic
A house where guests are fed 饣, run by an official 官.

## Notes
The 官 phonetic also writes 管 *guǎn*, 棺 *guān*. Modern senses: 饭馆
(restaurant), 宾馆 (hotel), 图书馆 (library), 博物馆 (museum), 大使馆
(embassy).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 "heart / mind" gives the meaning; 相 *xiāng* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 冀思也 "to long for, to think about."

## Mnemonic
The mind 心 turning something over, as if looking 相 straight at it.

## Notes
The 相 phonetic also writes 箱 *xiāng*, 湘 *xiāng*, 霜 *shuāng*. Senses: think
(想问题), want to (想去), miss (想家), suppose (我想是的).

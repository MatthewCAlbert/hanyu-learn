## Etymology
Simplified from 遠. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; the traditional phonetic
was 袁 *yuán*. Simplification swaps it for 元 *yuán* — a near-identical sound,
so the clue for *yuǎn* survives the change. Shuowen: 遼也 "far off."

## Mnemonic
A long way 辶 to travel: far.

## Notes
The 元 phonetic also writes 完 *wán*, 院 *yuàn*, 玩 *wán*. Opposite 近. Senses:
distant in space or time (远方, 远古), distantly related (远亲), by far
(远远超过).

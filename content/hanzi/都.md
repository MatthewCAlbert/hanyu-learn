## Etymology
Pictophonetic. The right-hand 阝 is 邑 "settlement, city," giving the meaning;
者 *zhě* gives the sound. Shuowen: a walled town holding an ancestral temple
— a capital or metropolis (*dū*). The adverb "all, both, entirely" (*dōu*) is
a grammaticalised extension: everything gathered into one place.

## Mnemonic
The city 阝 where everything is gathered.

## Notes
Right-side 阝 is 邑 "city" (那, 邮, 部); left-side 阝 is 阜 "mound" (阴, 阳,
院). The 者 phonetic also writes 著 *zhù*, 猪 / 豬 *zhū*, 暑 *shǔ*, 署 *shǔ*.

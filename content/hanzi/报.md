## Etymology
Simplified from 報. The full graph shows 㚔 "shackles" with a hand 又 pressing
down a kneeling figure 卩 — passing sentence on a criminal. Shuowen: 當罪人也
"to deal with an offender." From "to requite" grew "to repay," "to report,"
and hence "a report; a newspaper." Simplification writes the shackles as 扌.

## Mnemonic
A hand holding a wrongdoer to account: giving what is due — reporting back.

## Notes
Senses: repay / requite (报答, 报恩, 报仇), report / announce (报告, 报名,
汇报), news sheet (报纸, 日报, 晚报), telegram (电报). 报销 "to claim
expenses."

## Etymology
Ideographic: 又 (a hand) grasping or crossed by 丶 (a small mark standing
for an object held or a crossing point). Not in Shuowen; the picture —
something crossing or gripped between prongs — is straightforward and
uncontested even without a classical source.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 gripping something 丶 crosswise — a fork's prongs, or an "X."

## Notes
叉 covers the utensil (叉子 "fork") and the crossing shape generally: 打叉
"to mark with an X" (as opposed to 打钩 "to check/tick"), 叉路口 "a fork in
the road." Three readings by use — *chā* (fork/cross), *chá* (to be
stuck/jammed), *chǎ* (to spread legs apart) — all sharing the one "crossing
apart" image.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 毛 (hair, fur) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 稀毛长, means "sparse, long, fine hair," the kind used for a
writing brush tip — and a reduced form of 高 *gāo* supplies the sound,
compressed in the modern shape into a fragment that is not cleanly
recoverable, which is why this entry keeps confidence at medium.

The finest possible hair generalized naturally to "the tiniest amount"
(丝毫, "not the slightest bit"; 毫无, "utterly without") and, as a unit, to
a very small fraction (毫米, millimeter; 毫升, milliliter).

## Mnemonic
A single fine hair 毛, so thin it barely registers: "毫," the tiniest
amount.

## Notes
毫米 (millimeter) and 丝毫 ("not in the slightest," a fixed emphatic
negative expression) both build on this character's core "vanishingly
small" sense.

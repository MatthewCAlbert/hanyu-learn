## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 彡 (fine lines/decorative markings) supplies the meaning;
采 *cǎi* (itself "to pick/gather," seen earlier in this dataset) supplies
the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 文章也, is "patterned ornamentation" —
decorative markings of the kind produced by dye or paint.

## Mnemonic
Fine brushstrokes 彡, applied by a hand still gathering 采 more hues:
colour, and decoration.

## Notes
彩 covers both plain "colour" (彩色, 色彩) and, by association with
decorative brilliance, "splendid, wonderful" (精彩) and "a prize/lottery
win" (彩票, literally a "coloured ticket") — colour as a marker of
something special.

## Etymology
Pictographic — a right hand, drawn with three fingers (Shuowen notes that
hands are usually simplified to three). It was borrowed for the adverb
"again, also, moreover," and the "hand" sense went to 手.

## Mnemonic
A three-fingered hand: a hand — borrowed for "again."

## Notes
又 is Kangxi radical 29 and a live "hand" in 取, 受, 友, 反, 双. As an adverb,
又 = "again" pointing to the past (又来了), against 再 = "again" pointing
forward (再见). 又…又… "both … and …".

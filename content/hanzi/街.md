## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 行 "crossroads, thoroughfare" gives the meaning — it wraps
around the phonetic 圭 *guī* (for *jiē*). Shuowen: 四通道也 "a road open in
four directions."

## Mnemonic
A way 行 through the town, built up on both sides: a street.

## Notes
The 圭 phonetic also writes 鞋 *xié*, 佳 *jiā*, 挂 / 掛 *guà*, 娃 *wá*,
蛙 *wā*. Senses: street (大街, 街道, 逛街), and by extension a market
(街市) or neighbourhood (街坊). 街头 "street corner."

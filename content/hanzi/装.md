## Etymology
Simplified from 裝. Pictophonetic: 衣 "clothing" gives the meaning; 壮 (壯)
*zhuàng* gives the sound. Shuowen: 裹也 "to wrap, to bundle." From "to dress /
outfit" grew "to load / pack" (装车), "to install / fit" (安装), and "to put
on an act, to feign" (装病, 假装).

## Mnemonic
Wrapping the body in 衣 clothing: to dress up — and so to load, and to
pretend.

## Notes
The 壮 phonetic also writes 状 / 狀 *zhuàng*, 庄 / 莊 *zhuāng*, 桩 / 樁
*zhuāng*. Senses: outfit / costume (服装, 西装, 化装), load / fill (装满,
集装箱), install (装修, 安装), feign (装傻, 装模作样).

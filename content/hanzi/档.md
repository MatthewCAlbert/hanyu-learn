## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — a wooden frame, shelf or rail — and 當 (simplified 当) *dāng* supplies the sound (*dāng* → *dàng*). A late graph. From a shelf for filing, it extended to "files, records" (档案) and to "grade, class" (高档, 低档).

## Mnemonic
Wooden 木 shelves where records meet 当 their place.

## Notes
当 is sound only. 档案 "file, archive", 档次 "grade", 搭档 "partner", 存档 "save a file", 档期 "schedule". Readings: *dàng*, and *dǎng* "to block" in 档 as a variant of 挡.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 昷 (𥁕) *wēn* gives the sound.
Shuowen records 溫 as a river name; the everyday sense "warm, lukewarm" is an
extension, from there "mild, gentle" (温和, 温柔) and "to review / reheat"
(温习, 温故知新).

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 at a gentle heat 昷: warm.

## Notes
The 昷 phonetic also writes 瘟 *wēn* (pestilence), 蕴 / 蘊 *yùn*. 温度
"temperature," 气温 "air temperature," 体温 "body temperature."

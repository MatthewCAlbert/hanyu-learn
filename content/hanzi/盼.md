## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 目 (eye) supplies the meaning and 分 *fēn* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as the clear contrast of white and black in the eye, quoting the Odes: "the lovely eye so clear". 分 ("to divide") happens to fit the contrast, but is formally the sound. "To look towards, to hope for" is a later sense.

## Mnemonic
An eye 目 looking out and dividing 分 the horizon, waiting.

## Notes
盼望 "long for", 期盼 "look forward to", 盼头 "something to hope for", 左顾右盼 "look around".

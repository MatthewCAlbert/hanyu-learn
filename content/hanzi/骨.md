## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 冎 (a pictograph of a bone joint) with 肉/⺼
(flesh) — Shuowen's definition, 覈, means "the hard core" — the bone as
the solid core wrapped in flesh.

## Mnemonic
A joint 冎 wrapped in flesh 肉: "骨," bone — the hard core beneath the
soft.

## Notes
骨头 (bone, the everyday noun) and 骨折 ("a bone fracture," covered
elsewhere in this app's entry for 折) both build on this character's
plain, literal "bone" sense.

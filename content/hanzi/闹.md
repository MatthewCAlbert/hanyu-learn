## Etymology
Ideographic, and a post-classical character (not in Shuowen): 门 (gate,
door) framing 市 (market) — the bustle and noise of a marketplace glimpsed
through a gateway. That crowded, loud image gives both "noisy" (热闹,
lively/bustling; 闹市, a busy market street) and "to make a disturbance, to
cause trouble" (闹事, 吵闹).

## Mnemonic
A market 市 crowding right up against the gate 门: loud, crowded — "闹,"
noisy.

## Notes
热闹 ("bustling, lively") is usually positive — a crowded, energetic scene —
while 闹 alone or in 吵闹/捣蛋 skews negative, toward disruptive noise or
trouble-making. Context and the surrounding compound decide the valence.

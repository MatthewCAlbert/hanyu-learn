## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 工 (a carpenter's square/tool, standing generally for
"rule, standard") supplies the meaning; 弋 *yì* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's gloss, 法也, "law, model, standard," is close to the modern
"pattern, form, style."

## Mnemonic
A tool 工 marked with a peg 弋 to keep every piece to the same standard.

## Notes
式 keeps its "standard, pattern" sense across very different registers —
方式 "method," 正式 "formal/official," 公式 "formula" — all variations on
"the fixed way something is done."

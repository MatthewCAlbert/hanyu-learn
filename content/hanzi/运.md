## Etymology
Simplified from 運. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 移徙也 "to
shift, to move." The traditional phonetic is 军 (軍) *jūn*; the simplified
form swaps in 云 *yún*, a closer match for *yùn*, whose "cloud" sense also
suggests drifting motion.

## Mnemonic
Things on the move 辶, carried along like clouds 云: to transport — and the
turning of fortune.

## Notes
Senses: transport / carry (运输, 搬运, 货运), revolve / turn (运转, 运行),
wield / apply (运用, 运筹), fortune / luck (运气, 命运, 好运), and a
movement or campaign (运动, 奥运会).

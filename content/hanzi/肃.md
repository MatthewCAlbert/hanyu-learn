## Etymology
Not clearly decomposable for a modern reader even with Shuowen's own
gloss in hand. Shuowen ties 肅 to "handling affairs with trembling
respect," describing the graph as a hand-tool component positioned over a
mark associated with a deep chasm — evoking the caution of standing at
the edge of a precipice. The image is vivid in Shuowen's own words but not
independently verifiable component by component, so confidence stays low.

## Mnemonic
A hand steadying itself, trembling with respect, at the edge of a chasm —
solemn caution.

## Notes
肃 today is almost entirely formal/literary: 严肃 "serious, solemn," 肃静
"silence, order [in a courtroom]," 肃立 "to stand solemnly at attention."

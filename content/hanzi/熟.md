## Etymology
孰 was the original word for "cooked" (a hand tending food at a vessel); when
孰 was taken over for "which / who," 灬 (火) "fire" was added below to keep
the cooking sense. So 灬 gives the meaning and 孰 *shú* the sound.

## Mnemonic
Fire 灬 under the pot until the food 孰 is done: cooked.

## Notes
Senses: cooked / done (煮熟, 熟食, 半生不熟), ripe / mature (成熟, 瓜熟蒂落),
familiar (熟人, 熟悉, 眼熟, 耳熟), practised / fluent (熟练, 熟能生巧,
驾轻就熟), and deep — of sleep (睡熟了). Colloquial reading *shóu*.

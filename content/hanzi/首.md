## Etymology
Pictographic. The oracle-bone and bronze forms show a head in profile with
eye and hair clearly drawn; Shuowen already reads the top curls (巛) as hair
(髪). The modern top component, regularised to 丷, is a stylised remnant of
that hair, and the lower part is a stylised face. The app's decomposition
data records the lower piece as 自 (a shape historically tied to "nose,"
itself once a stand-in for "self/oneself") — a case of two originally
distinct head-related pictographs converging in outline over time.

## Mnemonic
Hair 丷 on top of a face 自: the head.

## Notes
From "head" the meaning extended to "chief, first" (首都, 首先) the same
way "head" does in English ("head of state," "first and foremost") — the
body part becomes the metaphor for priority and leadership.

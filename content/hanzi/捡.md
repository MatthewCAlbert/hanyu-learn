## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to pick up — and 僉 (simplified 佥) *qiān* supplies the sound (*qiān* → *jiǎn*). A late graph, absent from the original Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 picking from the whole 佥 pile.

## Notes
佥 is sound only. Series: 剑 *jiàn*, 检 *jiǎn*, 验 *yàn*, 险 *xiǎn*, 签 *qiān*. 捡起 "pick up", 捡漏 "find a bargain", 捡便宜, 捡到.

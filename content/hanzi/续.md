## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 賣 *mài*. Shuowen's gloss, 連也 "to link, to connect," and
its noted ancient variant 賡 (庚+貝, an associative character for things
handed on in succession) both point to the same core idea: unbroken
continuity, whether of thread or of a task.

Simplification replaced 賣 with the already-simplified 卖. Confidence is
medium because 卖/賣's own phonetic value here is a somewhat loose match
for *xù*.

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟, sold on 卖 and picked up again by the next hand: continued
without a break.

## Notes
续 pairs constantly with 继 and 持 in this dataset's "continuity" family
— 继续 "to continue," 持续 "to persist," 陆续 "one after another" — all
variations on an unbroken thread.

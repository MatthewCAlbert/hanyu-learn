## Etymology
Simplified from 試. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning — a test
was originally an oral examination — and 式 *shì* gives the sound. Shuowen:
用也 "to put to use, to try out."

## Mnemonic
Putting someone to the question 讠 by a set standard 式: a test.

## Notes
The 式 phonetic also writes 拭 *shì* (wipe), 轼 / 軾 *shì*. Senses: try
(试一试, 尝试), test / exam (考试, 面试), tentative (试用, 试验).

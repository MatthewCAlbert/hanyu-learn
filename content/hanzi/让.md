## Etymology
Simplified from 讓. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; the traditional
phonetic 襄 *xiāng* is written 上 in the simplified form, so the sound clue is
lost. Shuowen's old gloss is "to reproach in words"; the classical sense "to
yield, to defer" (谦让, 让位) became central, and from it "to let / allow"
(让我看看) and the passive marker (让雨淋了).

## Mnemonic
Stepping back in words 讠 and giving way.

## Notes
Senses: yield / give way (让路, 让座), let / allow (让他去), tell someone to
(妈让我回家), be … by (让人骗了). 让 also "to transfer / sell on" (转让).

## Etymology
Associative: 刂 "knife" beside 禾 "standing grain" — a blade keen enough to
reap. Shuowen: 銛也 "sharp." From "a sharp edge" grew "smooth-going,
favourable," and from the harvest it cuts, "gain, profit, advantage."

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 through the grain 禾 in one clean stroke: sharp — and what it
yields, profit.

## Notes
Senses: sharp (锋利, 利刃), smooth / favourable (顺利, 不利, 利于), benefit /
gain (利益, 利润, 有利), interest on money (利息, 本利, 高利贷). 利用
"to make use of"; 势利 "snobbish."

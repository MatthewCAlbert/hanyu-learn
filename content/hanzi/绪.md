## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (silk) supplies the meaning — the loose end of a silk thread, where you start unwinding — and 者 *zhě* supplies the sound (*zhě* → *xù*). From the thread end: beginning, a clue, and then a train of thought, a mood.

## Mnemonic
The end of the thread 纟, found by a person 者 who knows where to start.

## Notes
者 is sound only. 情绪 "mood", 思绪 "train of thought", 头绪 "clues", 绪论 "introduction". Series: 绪 *xù*, 煮 *zhǔ*, 著, 诸, 猪.

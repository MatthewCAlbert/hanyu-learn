## Etymology
Simplified from 顏. 页 (頁) "head" gives the meaning; 彦 (彥) *yàn* gives the
sound (for *yán*). Shuowen: 眉目之閒也 "the space between the brows" — the
forehead, then the face and its expression, then "countenance / colour"
(颜色, 和颜悦色).

## Mnemonic
The front of the head 页: the face.

## Notes
彦 "a man of talent" (才彦) is the phonetic here and little else. 颜色
"colour" — see the word entry. Also the surname Yán (as in Yan Hui,
Confucius's disciple).

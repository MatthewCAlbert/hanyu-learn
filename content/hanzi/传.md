## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 专 *zhuān* supplies the
sound — the same phonetic series as 转 and 团. Shuowen's original sense,
遽也 "urgent, by relay," names the imperial post-relay system: riders and
horses stationed along a route, each carrying a message onward to the
next.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻, entrusted 专 with a message, passing it along.

## Notes
From "to relay a message" the meaning split into two readings: *chuán* "to
transmit, spread" (传播, 宣传) and *zhuàn* "a written account passed down"
(自传 "autobiography," 水浒传 as a title) — one verbal, one nominal.

## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is a horizontal line crossed by a short vertical — a
cutting stroke. It was originally the word "to cut" (the source of 切). Borrowed
for the number seven, and when that use fixed, 切 (七 + 刀) was coined to carry
"cut." Shuowen's cosmological gloss is later.

## Mnemonic
A slicing cross-stroke, borrowed to write "seven."

## Notes
The lower stroke of 七 hooks up, distinguishing it from 十 (straight). 七 is
homophone-adjacent to 妻 "wife"; 七上八下 "all in a flutter."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 火 (fire) supplies the meaning and 乍 *zhà* supplies the sound. A late graph, absent from Shuowen. 乍 means "suddenly" in other contexts, which suits the explosive sense, but in the formal analysis it is the phonetic. Two readings share the graph: *zhà* "explode" and *zhá* "deep-fry".

## Mnemonic
A sudden 乍 fire 火: boom.

## Notes
乍 is a good sound clue and a convenient meaning. 爆炸 "explode", 炸弹 "bomb", 炸鸡 "fried chicken", 炸药 "explosive". Series: 诈 *zhà*, 榨 *zhà*, 炸 *zhà/zhá*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (心) "heart" gives the meaning; 白 *bái* gives the sound.
Shuowen glosses it *bó* "still, without striving" — the modern reading *pà*
and the sense "to fear" are a later development on the same graph.

## Mnemonic
The heart 忄 that goes pale 白: afraid.

## Notes
The 白 phonetic also writes 拍 *pāi*, 泊 *bó*, 帕 *pà*, 伯 *bó*, 迫 *pò*.
Senses: fear (害怕, 怕黑, 可怕), be unable to stand (怕冷, 怕水), and the
hedging "I'm afraid / probably" (恐怕, 怕是来不及了).

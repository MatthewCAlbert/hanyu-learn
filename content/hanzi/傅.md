## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 尃 *fū* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 相也 is "to assist" — someone
who helps another grow or govern, the root of "tutor" (师傅, 太傅) as an
assisting, guiding figure rather than a mere instructor.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 spreading 尃 knowledge to those in their care.

## Notes
傅 survives mainly in 师傅 "master craftsman/skilled worker" (also a
general polite address for a tradesperson) and historical titles like 太傅
"Grand Tutor." Distinct from 付 fù (to pay/hand over), an unrelated
character with a similar shape.

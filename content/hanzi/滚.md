## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 滾: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 衮 *gǔn*
(originally a ceremonial dragon robe) supplies only the sound. The core
meaning — water tumbling and boiling — is the basis for both extended senses:
"to roll" (things tumble the way boiling water churns) and the blunt
imperative 滚! "get lost, roll away."

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 churning and rolling like a robe 衮 tumbling in the wash.

## Notes
滚水/开水 "boiling water" is the literal, everyday sense; 滚开! and 滚蛋! are
common rude imperatives built on "roll away from here" — worth flagging as
impolite before a learner tries them out.

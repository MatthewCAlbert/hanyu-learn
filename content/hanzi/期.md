## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 月 "moon" gives the meaning — periods of time are reckoned by
the moon — and 其 *qí* gives the sound (for *qī*). Shuowen: 會也 "an appointed
meeting (time)."

## Mnemonic
A span marked off by the moon 月: a period, a term.

## Notes
The 其 phonetic also writes 欺 *qī*, 棋 *qí*, 旗 *qí*, 淇 *qí*. Senses: a term
/ phase (学期, 时期), a scheduled date (到期, 如期), to hope / expect (期望,
期待).

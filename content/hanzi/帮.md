## Etymology
Simplified from 幫 (帛 "silk cloth" semantic, 封 *fēng* phonetic). The
simplified 帮 re-forms it as 邦 *bāng* phonetic over 巾 "cloth." The earliest
sense is concrete — the upper of
a shoe, or the side panel of something (船帮, 鞋帮). "To help, to assist" and
"a band / gang" (帮会, 一帮人) are later extensions.

## Mnemonic
The cloth 巾 side-piece that holds a thing together — lending support.

## Notes
The 邦 phonetic also writes 绑 / 綁 *bǎng*, 梆 *bāng*. 帮忙 (verb-object) vs
帮助 (transitive) — see the word entries.

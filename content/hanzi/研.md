## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 石 (stone) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
䃺, means "to grind fine" (with a stone tool, as one would grind ink or
pigment) — and 开 *kāi* supplies the sound.

"To grind fine" generalized figuratively to "to study closely, to
research" (研究, covered elsewhere in this app) — refining a question
down to its essence the way a stone grinds a substance down to powder.

## Mnemonic
A stone 石 grinding something open 开, refining it down to its finest
essence: "研," to grind — or to research.

## Notes
研究 (to research, covered elsewhere in this app) is the character's
overwhelmingly dominant modern compound — its literal "grinding" sense
survives mostly as this etymology, not living usage.

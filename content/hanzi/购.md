## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 購: 贝 (cowrie-shell money) supplied
the meaning "to acquire with money," and 冓 *gòu* supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 冓 with the visually similar 勾** (*gōu*), which
is close in sound but not the character's inherited phonetic — a shape
substitution rather than a preserved sound clue, which is why this entry
does not declare it as an attested phonetic and keeps confidence at
medium.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 exchanged with a hooked 勾 handshake sealing the deal: "购," to
purchase.

## Notes
购买 (to purchase, pairing 购 with 买) and 购物 (to shop, lit. "to purchase
goods") are the two most common everyday compounds built on this
character.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 走 (walk) supplies the meaning and 尚 *shàng* supplies the sound (*shàng* → *tàng/tāng*). A late graph. The sense "to wade" (*tāng*) is the older and "a trip" (*tàng*, a measure word) the later.

## Mnemonic
A walk 走 still held up 尚 by habit: a round trip.

## Notes
尚 is sound only. 一趟 "one trip", 跑一趟 "make a trip", 趟 *tāng* in 趟水 "wade through water". Series: 堂 *táng*, 棠, 躺 *tǎng*, 趟.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 里 (land, a village) supplies the meaning — open land outside the settlement — and 予 *yǔ* supplies the sound. Shuowen also records an old form 埜 (林 + 土, trees on earth). The sound has drifted (予 *yǔ*, 野 *yě*).

## Mnemonic
Beyond the village 里 where you give 予 yourself to nature: the wilds.

## Notes
野生, 视野, 野餐, 野心. Contrast 郊 "suburbs". 予 is sound only here.

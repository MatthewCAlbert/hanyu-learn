## Etymology
The traditional form 蓋 was pictophonetic: 艹 (grass — the original lids
here were woven of reed or straw) supplied the meaning, and 盍 *gě/hé*
supplied the sound. 盍 itself was built from a vessel 皿 being covered.

**The simplified form's top no longer reads as grass or as 盍.** The
top of 盖 resembles 羊 (sheep) purely as a shape simplification — it carries
no meaning ("sheep" is not attested anywhere in this character's history)
and no reliable sound clue either. Only 皿 (vessel) at the bottom survives
as a real, meaningful component: something with a lid needs a vessel to sit
on. Given how much has been lost or obscured, confidence here is low.

## Mnemonic
A lid pressed down over a vessel 皿: "盖," to cover.

## Notes
Do not read the top of 盖 as "羊" (sheep) — it is a graphic remnant of the
lost phonetic 盍, not a real component with its own meaning.

## Etymology
Simplified from 廣. The traditional graph is 广 "a roof built against a
hillside" with 黃 *huáng* for the sound. Simplification keeps only 广 and
drops the phonetic entirely, so the modern character is just the bare
radical.

## Mnemonic
A roofline thrown wide against the slope 广: broad.

## Notes
As a radical (read *yǎn*) 广 heads 店 库 座 床 庭 廊. As a word, *guǎng*:
wide / vast (广大, 宽广, 地广人稀), spread / extend (推广, 广播, 广而告之),
numerous (大庭广众). Short for Guangdong / Guangzhou (广东, 两广). Surname
*Guǎng*.

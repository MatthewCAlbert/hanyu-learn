## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 溝: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning. Shuowen's
gloss 水瀆也 is "a water channel/ditch." The traditional phonetic was 冓
*gòu*; simplification replaced it with 勾, so the modern right side is a
graphic substitute rather than a live sound clue for *gōu*.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 channeled along a hooked 勾 ditch.

## Notes
沟 covers literal ditches and gullies (山沟 "mountain gully," 沟渠 "irrigation
channel") and figuratively "a gap, a divide" — 代沟 "generation gap"
extends the same image of a channel separating two sides.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning, 每 *měi* supplies the
sound, shifted to *huǐ*. Shuowen's gloss 恨也 groups it with regret and
self-directed resentment — matching the modern "to regret, to repent"
closely.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 replaying every 每 mistake, wishing it undone.

## Notes
悔 anchors 后悔 "to regret," 悔恨 "to feel remorseful," 悔改 "to repent and
reform." Distinct from 恨, which directs blame outward; 悔 turns it
inward, at oneself.

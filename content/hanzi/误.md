## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 吴 *wú* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 謬也 "mistaken, erroneous," was already
general — a wrong statement, and by extension any kind of mistake.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 spoken carelessly, like idle chatter from the state of Wu 吴:
an error slips in.

## Notes
误 covers both the noun "a mistake" (错误, 失误) and the verb "to delay,
to hold up" (耽误) — a mistake in speech and a mistake that costs time
are treated as the same underlying idea of something going wrong.

## Etymology
Simplified from 關. The full graph is 門 "gate" with a bar drawn across the
opening — Shuowen: 以木橫持門戶也 "a length of wood held crosswise to fasten a
door." From "to bar / shut" grew "a mountain pass / a barrier / a customs
post," and "to concern, to involve" (关系, 相关). The simplified 关 keeps only
the bar-and-loops and drops the gate.

## Mnemonic
The bolt drawn across the doorway: shut — and, by extension, what bears on
what.

## Notes
Senses to keep apart: 关 "to close / switch off" (关门, 关机, opposite 开);
关 "a pass / checkpoint" (海关, 过关); 关心 / 关于 "to be concerned with / about."

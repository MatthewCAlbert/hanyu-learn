## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning, and 多 *duō* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's own definition is a specific, vivid image: grain stalks
leaning against each other and swaying/shifting in the wind.

That gentle, gradual shifting extended to the general verb "to move, to
shift" (移动, 转移) — motion that happens by degrees rather than all at
once.

## Mnemonic
Many 多 stalks of grain 禾 leaning and swaying together: to "移," shift.

## Notes
移民 ("to migrate/immigrate," lit. "shift the people") and 转移 ("to
transfer, to shift") both build on this same gradual-shifting image rather
than on any sense of sudden relocation.

## Etymology
Ideographic: two 火 (fire) stacked: a flame that is hot and rising, Shuowen's "fire light going up". The doubled form means blazing heat. Medically, 发炎 "inflammation" extends the same image to a swelling that feels hot.

## Mnemonic
One fire 火 on top of another: hot, hot.

## Notes
炎热 "scorching", 炎症 "inflammation", 肺炎 "pneumonia", 发炎. 炎 is a sound part in 谈, 淡, 痰, 毯 *tán/tǎn*, which are not about fire.

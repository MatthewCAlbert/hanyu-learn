## Etymology
Simplified from 態. 心 "heart / mind" gives the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 能 *néng*. Simplification swaps it for 太 *tài* — a much closer
sound. Shuowen: 意也 "the disposition of the mind" — one's bearing, manner,
state.

## Mnemonic
What the heart 心 is set to at a given moment: one's manner.

## Notes
Senses: attitude / manner (态度, 表态), state / form (状态, 液态, 生态),
posture (姿态). The word entry 态度 has more.

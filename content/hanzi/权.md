## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 權: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning, and
雚 *guàn* supplied the sound. Shuowen's own gloss is startlingly concrete —
權 originally just named a species of tree with yellow flowers. It reached
"authority, power, right" the way many measuring tools do: 權 was also the
word for the sliding counterweight on a beam scale, and whoever controls the
weight controls the balance — control over the scale became control in
general.

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** Simplification replaced
雚 — a complex bird-and-plant compound — with the unrelated 又 (hand), which
is neither semantic nor phonetic here; it is a stroke-count reduction only.
So 权 keeps its "wood" meaning component but gives no hint at all toward
*quán*.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 lever held in the hand 又: whoever's hand is on the lever holds
the "权," the power.

## Notes
Compare 观 (guān), 欢 (huān), 劝 (quàn) — all lost the same 雚 phonetic to the
same 又 substitution, so none of them hint at their own pronunciation either.

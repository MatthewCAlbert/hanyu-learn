## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 攴 (a hand with a stick or rod — the "strike" radical seen in
放, 政, 教) supplies the meaning, 高 *gāo* supplies the sound and reads
unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 橫擿也 is close to "to strike sideways," matching
the modern "to knock, to tap, to rap" directly.

## Mnemonic
A hand with a rod 攴 rapping high 高 up on the door.

## Notes
敲 covers literal knocking (敲门 "to knock on the door") and figurative
extraction of value: 敲诈 "to extort," 挨敲 "to get ripped off" — a metaphor
of hammering value out of someone the way one hammers a nail.

## Etymology
Associative: 女 "a woman" seated under 宀 "a roof." Shuowen: 靜也 "quiet, at
rest" — a person settled safely indoors. From "at peace" grew "safe," "to
settle / place," and "to install."

## Mnemonic
Someone seated quietly within four walls 宀: at ease.

## Notes
Senses: peaceful / calm (安静, 安心, 平安), safe (安全, 治安, 转危为安),
settle / place (安家, 安排, 安置), install / fit (安装, 安上), content with
(安于现状), and the literary "how / where" (安能, 安知). 安慰 "to comfort."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, and Shuowen notes explicitly that both halves carry
meaning as well as sound: 攵 (a hand wielding a tool, "to act upon") and
正 *zhèng* ("correct, upright") — 正 supplies the sound and, doubly, the
meaning: Shuowen defines the whole character simply as "正," to govern
*is* to make correct, to set things right by active enforcement.

## Mnemonic
A hand 攵 actively enforcing what is correct 正: "政," to govern.

## Notes
政府 and 政治 (both covered elsewhere in this app) build directly on this
character; the "correct/upright" sense of its own phonetic 正 is worth
remembering as the conceptual heart of the whole word family.

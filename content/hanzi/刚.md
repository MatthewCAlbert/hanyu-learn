## Etymology
Simplified from 剛. 刂 (刀) "knife" gives the meaning; 冈 (岡) *gāng* gives the
sound. Shuowen: 彊斷也 "to cut through with force" — hard, strong, unyielding.
From "hard / firm" grew the temporal "just now, only just, barely" (刚才,
刚到).

## Mnemonic
A blade 刂 hard enough to cut clean through: firm — and, of time, "only just."

## Notes
The 冈 phonetic also writes 钢 / 鋼 *gāng* (steel), 纲 / 綱 *gāng* (guiding
rope / outline). Senses: hard (刚强, 刚硬), just now (刚刚, 刚才), barely
(刚够).

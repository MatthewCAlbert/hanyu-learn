## Etymology
Pictographic. The oracle-bone form is a footprint: three toes shown
(following the same artistic shorthand used for hands drawn with three
fingers), the original character for "foot" — later written 趾 once 止
itself had been pulled toward an entirely different job.

## Mnemonic
A single footprint, planted and unmoving: to stop, right here.

## Notes
"Foot" is now written 趾; 止 itself kept only the extended, abstract sense
"to stop, to halt" (停止, 禁止, 阻止) — a clean example of one character
splitting duties with a newer, more specific relative.

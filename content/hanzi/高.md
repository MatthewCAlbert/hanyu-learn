## Etymology
Pictographic — a tall building: a raised structure with an upper storey or a
gate-tower, sitting on a base. Shuowen: 崇也。象臺觀高之形 "lofty; a picture of
a high terrace-tower."

## Mnemonic
A tower rising storey on storey: high.

## Notes
高 is Kangxi radical 189 and part of 京, 亭, 亮. Senses: tall / high (高山),
loud (高声), advanced (高级, 高中), a surname. Opposite 低 or 矮.

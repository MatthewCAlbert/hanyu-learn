## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 貿: 贝 (shell/money) supplies the meaning, 卯
*mǎo* supplies the sound, shifted to *mào*. Shuowen's gloss 易財也 "to
exchange goods/wealth" matches the modern "trade, commerce" directly.

## Mnemonic
Goods 贝 exchanged, timed like the fourth hour 卯 of an old market day.

## Notes
贸 is almost always bound: 贸易 "trade," 外贸 "foreign trade," 商贸
"commerce and trade." Rarely stands alone as a single-character word.

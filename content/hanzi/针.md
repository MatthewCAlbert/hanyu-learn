## Etymology
钅 (from 金, metal — the semantic component in every metal-related
character: 铁, 铜, 钢) supplies the meaning "a metal implement." The
right-hand 十 does not correspond, in either sound or sense, to the
phonetic used in the older variant 鍼 (which used 咸 *xián* instead) —
this entry cannot confirm what, if anything, 十 is meant to contribute
here, so no phonetic is declared and confidence stays low.

## Mnemonic
A sliver of metal 钅 sharpened down to a point: a "针," needle.

## Notes
针 covers both the sewing needle (针线) and the medical injection needle
(打针) — one character for the whole category of thin, pointed metal
tools used to pierce.

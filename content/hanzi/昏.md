## Etymology
Shuowen: 日 (sun) with an abbreviated 氐 *dǐ* meaning "low": the sun going down, dusk. The modern top 氏 is that abbreviation, not the word 氏 "clan", so a "village at sunset" story misreads it. Dusk then gives "dim, dark" (昏暗), "dizzy" (昏迷) and "muddled". The word 婚 "marriage" is derived from 昏 because weddings were held at dusk.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 sinking below the horizon: dusk.

## Notes
黄昏 "dusk", 昏迷 "coma", 昏暗 "dim", 昏头. 婚 *hūn* is formed from 昏 + 女 (a wedding at dusk).

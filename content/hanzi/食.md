## Etymology
Pictographic — a footed bowl heaped with grain (皀), with a lid or a mouth
bending over it (亼). Shuowen reads it as a vessel of rice. Meaning: food; to
eat.

## Mnemonic
A lid lifting off a bowl of rice: a meal.

## Notes
食 is Kangxi radical 184; as a left-side component it compresses to 饣 (饭,
饿, 馆, 饺). Two readings: *shí* "to eat / food" (食物, 零食, 食堂), *sì*
"to feed" (classical, 食马者). 日食 / 月食 "eclipse" (the sun/moon "eaten").

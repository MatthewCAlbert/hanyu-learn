## Etymology
氵 "water" gives the meaning; the phonetic in Shuowen's old form is 𠯑
*guō* / *huó* (now reshaped to 舌). Shuowen: 水流聲 "the sound of running
water." From "flowing, moving water" — the opposite of stagnant — grew
"living, alive," "to live," and "lively, flexible" (灵活, 活跃).

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 in motion, not stagnant: alive.

## Notes
Senses: alive / to live (活着, 生活), lively / flexible (活泼, 活动), work / a
job (干活儿, 累活), and — after an adjective — "extremely" (笑得活像…). 活该
"serves one right."

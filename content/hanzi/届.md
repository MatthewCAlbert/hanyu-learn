## Etymology
Not securely attested. Traditional 屆 combines 尸 (a reclining body/person
shape, common in measure-of-extent characters like 尺, 局) with 凷/由 below;
sources disagree on the exact logic connecting "body" to "to arrive at [a
time/place]" or "a period, term." Best treated as a grammatical/measure-word
character whose picture is not securely recoverable.

## Mnemonic
A figure 尸 that keeps arriving, right on schedule, one term 由 after
another.

## Notes
届 is almost entirely a classifier and time-word in modern use: 第一届 "the
first [session/term]," 本届 "this term/session," 应届 "of the current
graduating class" — always about a recurring, numbered period or event.

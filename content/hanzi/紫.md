## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 糸 (silk/thread) supplies the meaning, 此 *cǐ* supplies the
sound, shifted to *zǐ*. Shuowen's gloss 帛靑赤色 is "a blue-red colour of
silk" — the dye colour purple, named through the textile it was most
associated with, the way many colour characters in Chinese trace back to
dyed silk rather than an abstract idea of colour.

## Mnemonic
Silk 糸 dyed this 此 particular shade — purple.

## Notes
紫 sits among the "textile" colour characters (红, 绿, 紧 share 糸-family
roots), and carries a strong cultural association with imperial rank —
紫禁城 "the Forbidden City," literally "the Purple Forbidden City."

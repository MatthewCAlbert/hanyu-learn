## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's original,
oddly specific definition is "a cover/wrapping for a horse's tail," to
keep it from becoming tangled — and 分 *fēn* supplies the sound.

The image of many loose threads generalized to "numerous, in a jumble,
disorderly" (纷纷, "one after another, in succession"; 纷乱, "chaotic") —
the tangled-thread mess the cover was meant to prevent.

## Mnemonic
Threads divided 分 in every direction, tangled and numerous: "纷," in
profusion.

## Notes
纷纷 (reduplicated, "one after another, in a flurry") is the character's
most common everyday use — many separate things happening in quick,
scattered succession.

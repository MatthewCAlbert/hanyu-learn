## Etymology
Simplified from 歲. The full graph is 步 "to pace" with 戉 (an axe) for sound.
Its earliest sense is the planet Jupiter (歲星), whose circuit of the sky takes
about twelve years — hence the word for "a year," and then "a year of age" and
"the harvest."

The simplified 岁 (山 over 夕) is a bare shape reduction and shows none of
this.

## Mnemonic
Learn it by its job — a year, a year of age — not by 山 and 夕.

## Notes
几岁 / 多大 "how old"; 岁数 "age (in years)"; 过年 "see out the 岁."

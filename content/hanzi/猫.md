## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 犭 (犬) "animal" gives the meaning; 苗 *miáo* gives the sound
(for *māo*), close enough to the cat's own cry to feel apt.

## Mnemonic
The animal 犭 that says something like 苗: the cat.

## Notes
The traditional radical is 豸 (貓); the mainland form uses 犭. 猫 also means
"to hide, to lie low" colloquially (猫在家里, 猫冬). 熊猫 "panda"; 猫头鹰
"owl" (lit. "cat-head hawk").

## Etymology
A pictograph of a standing person with the legs crossed. Shuowen: 交脛也
"crossed shanks; it depicts the crossing." From "to cross / intersect" grew
"to hand over, to exchange, to deliver," "to associate with," and "to pay in"
(交费).

## Mnemonic
Two legs crossed at the knee: things brought together, handed across.

## Notes
Senses: hand over / submit (交给, 上交, 交作业), exchange / deal (交换, 交易,
交往), intersect (交叉, 交点), friendship (交情, 结交). 交通 "traffic";
成交 "to close a deal."

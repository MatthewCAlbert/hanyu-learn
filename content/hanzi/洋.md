## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning and 羊 *yáng* supplies the sound. In Shuowen 洋 is the name of a river in the state of Qi; the senses "vast, wide" and then "ocean" are later (洋洋 "vast"), and "foreign" (洋人) comes from overseas, over the ocean.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 as vast as a flock of sheep 羊 on a hillside.

## Notes
羊 is sound only. 海洋 "ocean", 太平洋, 西洋 "the West", 洋葱 "onion" (foreign onion), 洋气. Series: 样, 详, 祥, 洋 — all *-yáng/-xiáng*.

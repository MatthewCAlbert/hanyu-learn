## Etymology
划 now covers two words. 劃 *huà* was 刂 "knife" with 畫 *huà* for the sound
— to cut, to mark off. 划 *huá* "to paddle, to scratch" is a separate graph.
The simplified form writes both as 戈 + 刂, so the old phonetic is gone.

## Mnemonic
A blade 刂 drawn across to score a line: marking off.

## Notes
*huà*: mark off / divide (划分, 划界, 区划), transfer funds (划账, 划拨),
plan (计划, 筹划, 谋划); *huá*: row / paddle (划船, 划桨), scratch or slit
(划火柴, 手划破了), and "to be worth it" (划算, 划得来, 不划算).

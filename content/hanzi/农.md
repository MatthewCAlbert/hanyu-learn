## Etymology
The traditional 農 combined 晨 (early morning — itself 辰, a farming
tool, plus 日/hand) with a phonetic element — Shuowen's gloss, 耕也，種也
"to plow, to plant," names the earliest and most fundamental of human
labours, done at first light. Earlier bronze-script forms show 林
(bushes/wilderness) and 辰 together: clearing land with a stone or shell
tool to bring it under cultivation.

Simplification cut this down to 冖 over an unresolved lower piece,
leaving neither the tool nor the field visibly intact. Confidence is
medium: the agricultural sense is certain and unbroken, but the modern
shape's own components are not individually checkable against it.

## Mnemonic
Rising before dawn, under a covering roof 冖, to work the fields:
farming.

## Notes
农 anchors an entire semantic field around rural, agricultural life —
农村 "village," 农民 "farmer," 农业 "agriculture," 农历 "the lunar
[farming] calendar" — the character that names the work names the whole
world built around it.

## Etymology
穴 "a hole, a cave" with 牙 "a tooth" inside it. Shuowen: 通也。从牙在穴中 "to
pass through; a tooth in a hole" — gnawing or boring an opening. From "to
pierce / bore through" grew "to pass through" (穿过) and "to put (the body)
through a garment" — to wear (穿衣服).

## Mnemonic
A tooth 牙 boring a hole 穴 clean through.

## Notes
Senses: pierce (穿孔, 看穿), pass through (穿过马路), wear / put on (穿鞋).
The garment sense pairs with 脱 "take off."

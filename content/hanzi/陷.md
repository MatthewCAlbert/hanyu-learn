## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (the 阜 mound radical) supplies the meaning, and 臽 *xiàn* — a person falling into a pit — is both the sound and the earlier picture of the idea. Shuowen glosses it as a fall from high ground. From falling into a pit: to sink, to be trapped, to frame someone.

## Mnemonic
A hill 阝 that drops away into a pit 臽.

## Notes
陷入 "fall into", 陷阱 "trap", 缺陷 "defect", 陷害 "frame (someone)", 沦陷 "fall (to the enemy)". 阝 on the left is the "mound" 阜, not the right-hand 邑 "city".

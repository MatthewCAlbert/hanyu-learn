## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 餅: 饣 (food) supplies the meaning, 并
(traditional 並) supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss
麪餈也 is "a wheat-flour cake" — a flat, round baked or cooked food, which
covers everything from flatbread to mooncakes to modern crackers.

## Mnemonic
Food 饣 pressed flat and joined together 并 into one round cake.

## Notes
饼 spans savoury (烧饼 "baked flatbread," 饼干 "biscuit/cracker" — literally
"dry cake") and festive foods (月饼 "mooncake" for Mid-Autumn Festival), plus
a modern tech-slang sense: 画饼 "to make empty promises" (literally "to
draw a cake" — a picture of food that can't be eaten).

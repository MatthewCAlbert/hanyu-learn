## Etymology
Simplified from 帶 — a pictograph of a belt: the sash across the top, with
ornaments and tasselled ends hanging below over a cloth 巾. Shuowen: 象繫佩之形
"a picture of the girdle and its pendants." From "a belt" grew "a band, a
strip, a zone" and, from wearing something on the belt, "to carry, to bring,
to lead."

## Mnemonic
A sash with pendants hanging from it: a belt — and what you carry on it.

## Notes
Senses: belt / strap / tape (皮带, 磁带), a zone (热带, 地带), to carry / bring
(带钱, 带来), to lead / bring along (带路, 带孩子), to bear a trace of (带笑).

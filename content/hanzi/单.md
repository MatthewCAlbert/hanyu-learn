## Etymology
Simplified from 單. The oracle-bone graph shows a Y-shaped hunting weapon or
sling with two weights at the fork. That picture gave nothing usable to later
readers, and 單 was borrowed wholesale for "single, sole" and, later, "a
list, a bill."

## Mnemonic
One forked stick carried alone: single.

## Notes
Senses: single / sole (单身, 单独, 单数 odd number), thin / weak (单薄,
单衣), a sheet / list / bill (名单, 账单, 菜单, 传单). Surname read *Shàn*;
in 单于 *chányú* (a Xiongnu title) read *chán*.

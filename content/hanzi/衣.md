## Etymology
Pictographic. A robe seen from the front: a collar at the top and two
overlapping lapels below. Shuowen: 依也。上曰衣，下曰裳 "clothing; the upper
garment is 衣, the lower is 裳," and reads the graph as a covering shape.

## Mnemonic
A collar and two crossed lapels: a garment.

## Notes
衣 is Kangxi radical 145; as a left-side component it becomes 衤 (衬, 袖, 被,
裤), which is one dot different from 礻 (示). The bottom-split form 裏 / 裹
wraps another element inside it.

## Etymology
脏 stands for two words. 臟 *zàng* "internal organ" was ⺼ "body" + 藏
"to store" (organs store the vital essences). 髒 *zāng* "dirty" was 骨 +
葬. The simplified form writes both as ⺼ + 庄, with 庄 *zhuāng* as a rough
phonetic.

## Mnemonic
For the organ sense: what the body ⺼ keeps stored inside.

## Notes
*zàng*: an internal organ (心脏, 肝脏, 内脏, 五脏六腑); *zāng*: dirty,
filthy (脏话, 弄脏, 脏乱差, 又脏又累).

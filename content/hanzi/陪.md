## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (here 阜, "mound, earthwork") supplies the meaning, 咅 *pǒu*
supplies the sound (shifted to *péi*). Shuowen's original sense is 重土也, "a
doubled earth mound" — an extra rampart built up beside a main one. "To
accompany, to attend alongside" is a later extension: standing beside, the way
a secondary mound stands beside the main one.

## Mnemonic
A second mound 阝 raised right alongside the first — company by your side.

## Notes
Shares the 咅 phonetic with 培 péi (to cultivate, also "mound of earth around
a plant's roots") and 赔 péi (to compensate) — all three read *péi*, all three
trace back to the same "beside/alongside" mound imagery in different domains
(earthworks, farming, debt).

## Etymology
口 "mouth" marks it as a spoken particle; 马 *mǎ* supplies the sound. 吗 is a
purpose-built graph for the yes/no question particle placed at the end of a
statement (你好吗？). It pictures nothing.

## Mnemonic
A questioning note 口 tacked on: "…right?"

## Notes
The 马 phonetic also writes 妈 *mā*, 骂 / 罵 *mà*, 码 / 碼 *mǎ* — four unrelated
meanings, so 马 here carries sound and nothing else. Compare the particles
吧, 呢, 啊.

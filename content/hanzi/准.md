## Etymology
Two characters now share this shape.

**准 *zhǔn* "to permit, to grant"** (批准, 准许) is an old graph — 冫 + 隹 —
whose origin is obscure.

**准 "accurate; a standard; in accordance with"** is simplified from 準 — 氵
"water" + 隼 for sound. Shuowen: 平也 "level" — water finds its own level, the
image behind "a standard, exact, accurate" (标准, 准确, 瞄准).

## Mnemonic
Water settling perfectly level: the standard to measure against.

## Notes
准 also = "quasi- / would-be" (准新娘 "bride-to-be") and "punctual" (准时).
说不准 "can't say for sure."

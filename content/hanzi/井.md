## Etymology
Pictographic: the oracle-bone form is a well-head frame, the crossed timbers laid at the top of a well. Shuowen explains the shape as the frame structure and a bucket; the "eight families, one well" is the classical field-system (井田) idea. The simplified shape is still the crossed frame.

## Mnemonic
Four logs crossed over a hole in the ground: a well.

## Notes
井 also names a neat, square layout: 井然有序 "in good order", 井田. 油井 "oil well", 水井, 天井 "skylight / ceiling", 井底之蛙 "frog in the well". Surname Jǐng.

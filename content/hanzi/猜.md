## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 犭 (dog) supplies the meaning, 青 *qīng* supplies the sound,
shifted to *cāi*. Shuowen's original gloss 恨賊也 is "to resent, to be
suspicious/hostile" — a dog's wary, distrustful watchfulness. The modern
sense "to guess" is a later development from that suspicion/uncertainty, and
the exact path between the two is not fully documented, so the connection is
plausible rather than certain.

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭 eyeing something green 青 and unfamiliar, unsure what to make of it —
guessing.

## Notes
猜 is the everyday verb for guessing games and riddles: 猜谜语 "to solve a
riddle," 猜测 "to speculate," 你猜 "guess!" It keeps 犭 even though no animal
is involved in the modern meaning — a case where the radical outlived its
literal sense.

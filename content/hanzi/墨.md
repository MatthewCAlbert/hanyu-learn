## Etymology
Shuowen: 墨 is 黑 (black) over 土 (earth), with 黑 marked as "also sound" (*hēi* → *mò*): ink made from soot, pressed into a solid lump. 土 is the lump, black the colour. A single graph also names a Chinese school of thought and a family (Mozi) by the same sound.

## Mnemonic
Black 黑 pressed into a lump of earth 土: an ink stick.

## Notes
墨水 "ink", 墨汁 "liquid ink", 笔墨 "brush and ink", 墨镜 "sunglasses", 墨西哥 "Mexico" (transliteration). 黑 is both sound and meaning here.

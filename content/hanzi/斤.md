## Etymology
A pictograph of an axe or adze — a transverse blade lashed to a haft.
Shuowen: 斫木也 "for chopping wood; it depicts the form." The graph was
borrowed as a unit of weight and now rarely means "axe" on its own.

## Mnemonic
A hafted blade set crosswise: the woodsman's axe.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 69), in 斧 析 斯 断 新. As a phonetic it writes
近 *jìn*, 欣 *xīn*, 芹 *qín*. As a word: a catty, ≈ 500 g (一斤苹果,
半斤八两), and 斤斤计较 "to haggle over every ounce."

## Etymology
Simplified from 鳥, a pictograph of a long-tailed bird — head and beak on
top, body, wing, and the tail and feet below. Shuowen: 長尾禽總名也 "the
general name for long-tailed fowl." The simplified form keeps the outline and
levels the four tail-dots to one stroke.

## Mnemonic
Beak, eye, body, tail, claws: a bird in profile.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 196), heading 鸡 鸭 鹅 鸽 鹰 and most bird names.
Shuowen paired it with 隹, the "short-tailed bird" radical. 鸟瞰
"a bird's-eye view."

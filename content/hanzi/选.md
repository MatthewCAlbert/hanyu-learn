## Etymology
Simplified from 選. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning — picking some out and
sending them on; the traditional phonetic 巽 *xùn* is replaced by 先 *xiān* in
the simplified form (a rough match for *xuǎn*). Shuowen: 選擇也 "to choose."

## Mnemonic
Going through 辶 and picking out the ones to send first 先: to select.

## Notes
Senses: choose / pick (选择, 挑选), elect (选举, 竞选), an anthology / the
chosen (文选, 入选, 选手).

## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 门 (door) with 才 inside representing the bolt or
bar (歫門, "that which blocks the door") — a door with its bar drawn across
it. Shuowen's gloss of 才 here as specifically a door-bolt is an
interpretive reading of an otherwise simple vertical-stroke shape, which is
why this entry sits at medium rather than high confidence.

## Mnemonic
A door 门 with its bolt 才 slid firmly across: "闭," closed.

## Notes
闭 combines with 关 in 关闭 ("to shut down/close," redundant near-synonym
pairing) and stands alone in the fixed expression 闭嘴 ("shut your mouth,"
lit. "close mouth").

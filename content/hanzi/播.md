## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 番 *fān* supplies the
sound and, per Shuowen's own note, also echoes "to scatter, to disperse."
The original, agricultural sense, 種也 "to sow," is the direct ancestor
of the modern "to broadcast" — scattering seed and scattering a signal
are the same underlying image.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 scattering seed, over and over 番, across the whole field.

## Notes
"To sow seed broadly" is the exact metaphor behind "to broadcast" (广播,
播放, 直播) — the same word for agriculture and mass media because both
are about spreading something as widely as possible.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 石 "stone" gives the meaning — the knock of one hard thing on
another; 並 (并) *bìng* gives the sound (for *pèng*). A late character, also
written 掽 and 踫.

## Mnemonic
One stone 石 struck against another: a bump.

## Notes
Senses: bump / knock into (碰头, 碰壁, 别碰杯子), run into by chance
(碰见, 碰上, 碰巧), try one's luck (碰运气, 碰碰看), and to clink glasses
(碰杯). 碰钉子 "to be rebuffed."

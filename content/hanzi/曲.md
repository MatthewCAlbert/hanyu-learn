## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen: 象器曲受物之形, "pictures the shape of a curved vessel
that holds things" — a bent container, basket, or trough. "Bent, crooked" is
the direct sense; "wrong, unjust" (是非曲直 "right and wrong") and "a song,
melody" (歌曲, read *qǔ*) are both later extensions — a melody's bends and
turns, or moral crookedness.

## Mnemonic
A curved trough, bent to hold what's poured into it.

## Notes
Two readings split the senses cleanly: *qū* "bent, wrong" (弯曲, 曲折,
曲解); *qǔ* "song, tune" (歌曲, 戏曲, 作曲). 曲 is Kangxi radical 73 but has
few members under it in the modern set.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 广 (building) for meaning and 發 (simplified 发) for sound. Shuowen's sense is a building falling down; that gives "abolished, abandoned, wasted". The sound has drifted — 发 is *fā* but 废 is *fèi* — so the phonetic is a loose hint at best.

## Mnemonic
A building 广 that has been sent away 发 — abandoned.

## Notes
废除 "abolish", 报废 "scrap", 半途而废 "give up halfway", 废话 "nonsense". Same phonetic as 拨 *bō* and 泼 *pō* — the 発/发 series covers fā/bō/pō/fèi.

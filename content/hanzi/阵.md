## Etymology
Ideographic in the traditional form 陣: 阝 (a mound — high ground used for
a military encampment) with 车 (chariots) arranged upon it — chariots
drawn up in battle formation on defensible high ground.

"Battle formation" (阵地, 阵营) extended figuratively to "a burst, a spell"
of anything transient and self-contained (一阵雨, "a burst of rain"; 一阵
笑声, "a burst of laughter") — a formation as a bounded, momentary
arrangement, whether of troops or of weather.

## Mnemonic
Chariots 车 drawn up on a defensible mound 阝: an "阵," a battle
formation — or any burst of activity that comes and goes as a unit.

## Notes
阵 is a common measure word for anything that arrives, holds briefly, and
passes: 一阵风 (a gust of wind), 一阵疼痛 (a spell of pain) — always
something transient, never a lasting state.

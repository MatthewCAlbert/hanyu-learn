## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen calls bamboo "a plant that stays green through
winter," and marks the graph 象形, "a picture" — two bamboo stalks side by
side, each with drooping leaves at the top, exactly as the character still
shows today.

## Mnemonic
Two bamboo stalks standing side by side, leaves drooping at the top.

## Notes
竹 is Kangxi radical 118 and heads a large family of bamboo-object and
bamboo-material characters: 笔 (brush/pen), 筷 (chopsticks), 篮 (basket),
签 (slip/sign), 答 (answer — originally a bamboo tally). 竹 alone also
names the flute in some classical contexts (丝竹 "string and bamboo
[instruments]," i.e. music in general).

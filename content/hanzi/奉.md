## Etymology
Shuowen: 奉 is hands 廾 and 手 holding something up, with 丰 *fēng* as the sound — "to hold up respectfully with both hands". The senses "to offer, to serve, to receive from above" (奉命) all follow. The lower part in the modern graph has been simplified and merged, so not every stroke is separately recoverable.

## Mnemonic
Offering a gift up with both hands.

## Notes
奉献 "dedicate", 供奉, 奉命 "by order", 奉承 "flatter", 信奉 "worship". The 丰 phonetic also appears in 捧 *pěng*, 棒 *bàng*, 俸 *fèng*.

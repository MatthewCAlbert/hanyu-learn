## Etymology
Not securely attested in its concrete picture. Traditional 虧 combines 雐
(a bird name) with 亏 (breath escaping unevenly, related to 气) — the exact
logic connecting a bird-name component to "deficiency, loss" is not clearly
documented. The drastically simplified 亏 preserves none of that visible
structure, so it is best treated as a fixed shape rather than decomposed.

## Mnemonic
A breath 亏 that comes out short — something's missing.

## Notes
亏 covers loss (吃亏 "to suffer a loss/disadvantage," 亏本 "to lose money on
a deal") and, colloquially, ironic relief: 亏你想得出来! "how did you even
think of that!" and 多亏 "thanks to [something fortunate]" both flip the
"deficiency" sense toward "luckily."

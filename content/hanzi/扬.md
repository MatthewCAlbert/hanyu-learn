## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 飛
舉, means "to fly up, to raise" — and 昜 *yáng* supplies the sound. 昜 is
also the root behind 阳/陽 (sun, bright), so the sound-donor here shares a
family resemblance with brightness and rising.

"To raise up" (of a hand, of dust) extends to "to make known, to
publicize" (表扬, "to praise publicly"; 宣扬, "to spread word of") — raising
something up so everyone can see it.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 raising something up as high and bright as the sun 昜: "扬," to
raise.

## Notes
表扬 ("to praise") and 发扬 ("to carry forward, to develop [a tradition]")
both extend this character's "raising up for all to see" core sense into
recognition and continuation.

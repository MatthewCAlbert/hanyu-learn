## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 灬 (fire) supplies the meaning — to boil, to cook in water — and 者 *zhě* supplies the sound (*zhě* → *zhǔ*). Shuowen glosses it as to boil; its old shape used a cauldron 鬲.

## Mnemonic
A person 者 keeps the fire 灬 going under the pot.

## Notes
者 is sound only. 煮饭 "cook rice", 煮饺子 "boil dumplings", 煮沸 "bring to the boil", 煮熟 "cooked through", 煮酒. Series: 煮, 著, 诸, 猪, 绪.

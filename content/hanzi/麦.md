## Etymology
Shuowen: 麥 is 來 over 夊. Surprisingly, 來 was originally the word for wheat itself (a pictograph of a wheat plant), and was later borrowed for "come"; 麥 added 夊, a foot pointing down, which Shuowen links to autumn sowing "buried deep" (the foot treads the seed in). The simplified 麦 compresses 來 into a form that no longer looks like a plant.

## Mnemonic
A wheat plant with a foot on it, tamped down at planting.

## Notes
小麦 "wheat", 麦子, 麦片 "oatmeal", 大麦 "barley", 麦克风 "microphone" (sound loan). 来 is the older word for wheat; the reading 麦 *mài* is not related to 来 *lái* by sound.

## Etymology
自 "nose" over 心 "heart" — breath rising from the chest through the nose.
Shuowen: 喘也。从心从自 "panting; from heart and nose," adding that 自 is close
to the sound. From "breath" branch out "to rest / cease" (休息, 停息),
"news / word" (消息, 信息), "interest on money" (利息), and "offspring"
(子息).

## Mnemonic
Breath drawn from the heart 心 up through the nose 自.

## Notes
自 depicts a nose and is Kangxi radical 132 (its "self" meaning is a loan).
The many senses of 息 all radiate from "breath."

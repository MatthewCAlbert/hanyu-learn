## Etymology
Simplified from 隨. The character is 辶 "movement" — going after someone —
plus 阝 (阜) and the phonetic 隋 *suí*. The simplified form compresses the
right side to 有 + 辶, so the phonetic is no longer legible on its own.
Shuowen: 从也 "to follow."

## Mnemonic
Falling in behind and moving 辶 with another: to follow.

## Notes
Senses: follow (跟随, 随行, 随从), comply with (随和, 随大流), along with /
as (随着时间), as one pleases (随便, 随意, 随时). 随身 "on one's person."

## Etymology
Simplified from 畫. The full graph is 聿 "a hand holding a brush" above 田
and its boundary lines — Shuowen: 界也 "to mark out borders," a field seen
from above with its four edges drawn. The simplified 画 keeps the enclosure
and 田 and drops the brush.

## Mnemonic
Ruling lines around a field 田: to draw.

## Notes
Senses: draw / paint (画画, 绘画, 画像), a picture (国画, 油画, 壁画), a
stroke of a character (笔画, "王" 字四画). 画蛇添足 "to add legs to a
snake" — to overdo it.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 每 *měi* gives the sound (for
*hǎi*). Shuowen: 天池也，以納百川者 "the heavenly pool that takes in the
hundred rivers."

## Mnemonic
The water 氵 that every 每 river runs to: the sea.

## Notes
The 每 phonetic also writes 梅 *méi*, 悔 *huǐ*, 敏 *mǐn*, 侮 *wǔ*. Extended
senses: a great inland lake (青海, 里海), a huge quantity (人山人海, 火海,
海量), and "overseas / foreign" (海外, 海归). 海关 "customs."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. The original semantic component was 火 (fire); the modern
standard character replaces it with 日 (sun), a small script reform that
better matches the everyday source of warmth — sunlight rather than a
hearth fire. 爰 *yuán* supplies the sound throughout.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日, reached for 爰 on a cold day: warmth.

## Notes
暖 covers physical warmth (温暖, 暖气) and, by extension, emotional
warmth (暖心 "heartwarming") — the same shift "warm" makes in English
between a temperature and a personality.

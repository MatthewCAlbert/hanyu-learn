## Etymology
Traditional 嘆 and 歎 are pictophonetic, with 口 or 欠 (open mouth) for the exhale and an element related to 難/𦰡 for sound. Simplification replaced the complex sound part with 又, the same generic substitute seen in 难, 欢 and 观. So 叹 shows meaning (口 = the mouth sighing) and no sound clue; 又 "again, hand" has no meaning here.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 letting out one more 又 sigh.

## Notes
叹气 "to sigh", 感叹, 惊叹, 赞叹. 又 here marks 又-simplified forms: 难, 欢, 观, 戏, 邓. Do not read 又 as "again".

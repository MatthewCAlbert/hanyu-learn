## Etymology
Simplified from 滿. 氵 "water" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 盈溢也 "brim-full,
overflowing." The phonetic was 㒼 *mán*; simplification rewrites it as 艹 over
两, which no longer carries the sound, so 满 now reads as a whole.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 risen right to the brim: full.

## Notes
Senses: full / filled (满座, 装满, 客满), completely (满不在乎, 满口答应),
reach a limit (期满, 年满十八, 假期未满), satisfied (满意, 满足, 不满),
proud / complacent (自满, 骄傲自满). 满族 "the Manchu people" (a separate
proper-noun use).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺮ "bamboo" gives the meaning; 寺 *sì* gives the sound. Shuowen:
齊簡也 "to make bamboo slips even" — trimming a bundle of writing-slips to
equal length. From "made equal" grew "rank, grade, class" (等级), "and so on"
(等等), and the bleached "to wait" (等一下).

## Mnemonic
Bamboo slips ⺮ squared off level: of one rank; waiting one's turn.

## Etymology
Simplified from 漢. 氵 "water" is the semantic — 漢 was the name of a river, a
major tributary of the Yangtze — and 𦰩 supplied the sound. The Han dynasty
took its name from the river; "Han" as the name of the people and language
followed.

Simplification replaces 𦰩 with 又, a bare shape-sign, so 汉 keeps the water
radical and loses the sound clue.

## Mnemonic
The great river 氵 that gave a dynasty, a people and a language their name.

## Notes
The same 堇-family phonetic is reduced to 又 in 难 / 難, 叹 / 嘆, 艰 / 艱,
仅 / 僅.

## Etymology
A pictograph of a face or figure above 灬 "fire," marked with soot. Shuowen:
火所熏之色也 "the colour smoke leaves." Some read the top as a branded /
tattooed face. From "soot-black" grew "dark," "secret / illicit" (黑市, 黑钱)
and "wicked" (黑心).

## Mnemonic
A face smudged by the fire 灬 below: black.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 203), heading 墨 "ink," 默 "silent," 黯 "dim."
Senses: black (黑色, 漆黑), dark (天黑, 黑夜), illicit (黑车, 黑社会).

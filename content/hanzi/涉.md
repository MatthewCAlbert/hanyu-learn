## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen writes 涉 with 步 (a step, two footprints) between two streams 沝 — someone walking through water. The modern form kept only one 氵. From "to wade across" come "to pass through, to be involved in" (涉及, 干涉).

## Mnemonic
Step 步 by step through the stream 氵.

## Notes
涉及 "involve", 交涉 "negotiate", 干涉 "interfere", 涉外, 跋涉 "trek". 步 is a meaning component here, not the sound.

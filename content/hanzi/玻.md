## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 王 (here 玉, jade — the "precious stone-like material"
radical) supplies the meaning, 皮 *pí* supplies the sound, shifted to *bō*.
Not in Shuowen; glass and its dedicated vocabulary postdate the classical
corpus. 玻 essentially never appears alone — it exists almost entirely to
form 玻璃 "glass."

## Mnemonic
A stone-like material 王, its surface as smooth as skin 皮 — glass.

## Notes
玻璃 "glass" is a disyllabic word; neither 玻 nor 璃 carries independent
meaning outside it in modern use, similar to how 沙发 (sofa) works as one
inseparable unit.

## Etymology
An associative compound: 音 "sound, speech" over 心 "heart." Shuowen spells out
the logic — 察言而知意 "one perceives the intent by examining the speech" — the
heart's thought made audible. Meaning: thought, idea, intention, meaning.

## Mnemonic
The heart 心 given voice 音: what one means.

## Notes
Senses: meaning (意思, 意义), intention / wish (愿意, 故意, 同意), a hint
(意味, 春意). 注意 "pay attention," 意见 "opinion," 意外 "unexpected."

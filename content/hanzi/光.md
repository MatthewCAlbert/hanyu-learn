## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen's own reading of the seal-script form is exactly the
picture: 火 (fire) positioned above 人 (a person) — fire held above a
person, so that person radiates light. The oracle-bone form is even more
specific: a kneeling figure with a torch or flame blazing over the head.

By small-seal script the kneeling person had already flattened toward 儿,
and the modern top component, ⺌, is what remains of the fire.

## Mnemonic
A flame ⺌ burning bright over a kneeling figure 兀: light.

## Notes
"Light, brightness" extended to "merely, only" (光是, 只光) and to "used
up, nothing left" (花光, 用光了) — light as the last thing visible once
everything else is gone.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning; 告 *gào* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 就也, is "to arrive at, to accomplish" — earlier
bronze-script forms instead show 宀 (a roof) and 舟 (a boat) together with
告, and scholars disagree on whether the original picture is "travelling
by boat" or "building a dwelling" (both being things people "make").
Confidence is medium because which of those two readings is primary is
not settled.

## Mnemonic
Setting out 辶 to announce 告 what you've just finished making.

## Notes
"To arrive at, to accomplish" narrowed into the modern, very general "to
make, to build, to create" — 制造, 创造, 建造 — with 辶 now read as
"bringing something into being" rather than literal travel.

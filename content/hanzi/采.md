## Etymology
Ideographic. 爫 (a hand, reaching down) above 木 (a tree) — the oracle-bone
and bronze forms show a hand plucking fruit or leaves directly from a
tree. Shuowen's gloss, 捋取也, "to strip off, to pick," is exactly this
picture.

## Mnemonic
A hand 爫 reaching up into a tree 木 to pick its fruit.

## Notes
The traditional variant 採 adds a second, redundant hand radical (扌) to
the same picture; the modern simplified standard 采 is actually closer to
the original ancient form, not a later reduction of it.

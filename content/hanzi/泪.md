## Etymology
Ideographic: 氵 (water) plus 目 (eye) — water from the eye, plainly and
directly: a tear. This vulgar/folk-simplified form is actually very old,
attested well before modern simplification, alongside the more classical
pictophonetic form 淚 (水 semantic + 戾 lì phonetic) — 泪 simply won out as
the standard when simplification codified one variant per character.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 straight from the eye 目: "泪," a tear. About as transparent as a
character gets.

## Notes
眼泪 (tears, pairing 泪 with 眼 "eye" for emphasis) and 流泪 ("to shed
tears," lit. "to flow tears") are the two most common everyday compounds.

## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: three horizontal strokes represent jade discs,
and the vertical stroke through them is the cord they are strung on — a
string of jade pieces. The dot in the modern shape was added later
specifically to keep 玉 visually distinct from the near-identical 王
(king), which lacks it.

## Mnemonic
Three jade discs 三 strung on a cord 丨, with one extra drop 丶 marking
this as precious "玉," jade, and not the king 王 it would otherwise look
just like.

## Notes
玉 and 王 are the single most confusable pair in the basic script — the
whole distinction between "king" and "jade" rests on that one small dot,
worth drilling in isolation.

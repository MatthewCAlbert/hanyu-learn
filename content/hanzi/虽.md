## Etymology
Simplified from 雖. The graph originally named a large lizard-like creature —
虫 "reptile / insect" with 唯 *wéi* for the sound. It was borrowed wholesale
for the concessive conjunction "although," and the simplified form keeps just
口 + 虫.

## Mnemonic
Learn 虽 by its grammar — the lizard it once named is long gone.

## Notes
Senses: although, even though (虽然, 虽说, 虽死犹荣), and the paired
虽…但是… / 虽…可是… "although … yet …". 虽败犹荣 "honoured even in defeat."

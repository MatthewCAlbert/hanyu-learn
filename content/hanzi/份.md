## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 分 (*fēn* / *fèn*) gives the
sound and the sense — a share allotted to a person. A late differentiation
from 分.

## Mnemonic
The portion 分 that falls to a person 亻: a share.

## Notes
Senses: a portion / share (一份饭, 股份), a copy (一份报纸, 复印一份), a named
division (年份, 月份, 省份). Also 身份 "identity, status." Measure word for
documents, meals, gifts, jobs.

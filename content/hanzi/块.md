## Etymology
Simplified from 塊. 土 "earth" gives the meaning; 夬 *guài* gives the sound
(for *kuài*). Shuowen (under the old form 凷): 墣也 "a clod, a lump of earth."
From "a lump" grew "a piece / chunk" and the measure word for lump-like
things.

## Mnemonic
A clod of earth 土 broken 夬 off: a lump.

## Notes
The 夬 phonetic also writes 快 *kuài*, 决 / 決 *jué*, 缺 *quē*. Measure word:
一块肉, 一块钱 (colloquial for 一元), 一块儿 "together" (in one lump).

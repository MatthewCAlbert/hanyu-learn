## Etymology
Per Shuowen, 施 originally described the flowing, rippling appearance of
a banner streaming in the wind — 方/㫃 (a flag on a pole, the semantic
core) with a phonetic element supplying the sound. This entry keeps
confidence medium since the modern right-hand component is not cleanly
recoverable as that original phonetic.

A banner unfurling and spreading out generalized to "to spread out, to
carry out, to implement" (实施, 施行) — an action, like a flag, unfurled
for all to see.

## Mnemonic
A banner streaming out from its pole, spreading wide for everyone to
see: "施," to carry out.

## Notes
措施 (a measure, covered elsewhere in this app) and 实施 ("to implement,"
lit. "to carry out for real") both build on this character's core
"spreading out into action" sense.

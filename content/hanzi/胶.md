## Etymology
Traditional 膠: ⺼ (flesh/hide) supplies the meaning — the original
substance was glue rendered from boiled animal hide, not, as a popular
modern gloss has it, "tendons connecting muscles." "Sticky, to glue" comes
directly from that hide-glue's adhesive property, later generalising to
rubber (橡胶) and any sticky or elastic material.

## Mnemonic
Hide and flesh ⺼, boiled down until it bonds ⺼交 things together.

## Notes
胶 spans traditional adhesive (胶水 "glue") and modern materials science:
橡胶 "rubber," 塑胶 "plastic," 胶带 "adhesive tape" — the ancient
hide-glue image stretched to cover a whole family of sticky, mouldable
substances.

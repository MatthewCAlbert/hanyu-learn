## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
倚, means "to lean on" — and 衣 *yī* supplies the sound, an exact match.

"To lean on" extends to "to rely on" (依靠, 依赖) and "in accordance with"
(依照, 依据) — leaning on a person, a resource, or a rule are all the
same "depending on" image.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 wrapped snugly in clothing 衣, leaning on it for support and
warmth: "依," to rely on.

## Notes
依然 ("still, as before," lit. "leaning on it, unchanged") and 依旧 (a
close synonym) both extend "leaning on/relying on" into "remaining
unchanged over time."

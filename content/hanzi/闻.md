## Etymology
Simplified from 聞. 耳 "ear" gives the meaning; 门 (門) *mén* gives the sound
(for *wén*). Shuowen: 知聞也 "to come to know by hearing."

## Mnemonic
An ear 耳 at the door 门, catching what passes: to hear.

## Notes
Senses: hear (听闻, 闻讯, 耳闻目睹), news / what one hears (新闻, 见闻,
奇闻, 传闻), reputation (闻名, 默默无闻), and — a Chinese peculiarity — to
smell (闻一闻, 闻到香味). Surname *Wén*.

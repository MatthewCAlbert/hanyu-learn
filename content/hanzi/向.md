## Etymology
Pictographic — a window (口) set in the wall of a house (宀). Shuowen: 北出牖也
"a window opening to the north." From "a window that faces a direction" grew
"to face toward, toward," "to side with" (偏向), and "all along, formerly"
(向来, 一向).

## Mnemonic
A window in the wall, facing out: which way it points.

## Notes
As a coverb, 向 marks the direction or target of an action: 向前走, 向他学习,
向东. 方向 "direction"; 向导 "a guide."

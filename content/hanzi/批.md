## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; the modern phonetic is 比
*bǐ*. The oldest attested form of this word, written 𢱧, used a different,
now-obsolete phonetic component and meant literally "to strike with the
back of the hand" — a sharp, decisive physical gesture. Confidence is
medium because the exact graphic path from that older form to today's
批/比 pairing is not fully documented.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 striking down decisively, comparing 比 one thing against
another before passing judgment.

## Notes
"A sharp strike, a decisive gesture" softened into "to comment on, to
mark up [a document]" (批改, 批注) and, separately, into "to approve
officially" (批准) — both still carry a sense of decisive, hands-on
judgment.

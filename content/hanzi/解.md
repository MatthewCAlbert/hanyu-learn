## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen's gloss, 判也，从刀判牛角, is a butcher's picture:
using a knife 刀 to divide an ox 牛 at its horns 角. The oracle-bone form
shows two hands pulling apart an ox's horns directly; by the Warring
States period one hand had been replaced by 刀, giving the modern
three-part 角/刀/牛 structure.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刀 dividing an ox 牛 exactly at the horns 角: taking something
apart, piece by piece.

## Notes
"To take apart physically" extended cleanly to "to take apart mentally" —
理解 "to understand" (to break a problem down), 解释 "to explain," 解决
"to resolve" — dissection as the root metaphor for comprehension.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 膚: ⺼ (flesh) supplies the meaning; the
traditional phonetic was 胥 *xū*. Simplification replaced 胥 with 夫, so
the modern right-hand side is a graphic stand-in rather than a working
sound clue for *fū*. The core sense, the body's outer skin, has stayed
stable.

## Mnemonic
Flesh ⺼, its outer layer standing guard like a grown man 夫 at the gate.

## Notes
肤 anchors 皮肤 "skin," 肤色 "skin tone," and the figurative 肤浅 "shallow,
superficial" — a reading that goes no deeper than the surface skin.

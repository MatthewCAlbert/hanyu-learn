## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 皿 (vessel, container) supplies the meaning, 合 *hé* supplies
the sound and reads unchanged. 合 also means "to close, to fit together" —
exactly what a box's lid does — so while its formal role here is phonetic,
it is one of the cases where the sound component happens to echo the
object's function.

## Mnemonic
A container 皿 with a lid that closes 合 tight.

## Notes
盒 is the everyday classifier and noun for boxed items: 一盒巧克力 "a box of
chocolates," 饭盒 "lunch box," 盒子 "box" generically. Distinguish from 箱
xiāng, which is for larger cases and trunks.

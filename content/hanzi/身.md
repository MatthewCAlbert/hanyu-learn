## Etymology
Pictographic. A person in profile with the trunk — and especially the belly —
drawn full and rounded. Shuowen: 躬也。象人之身 "the trunk; a picture of a
person's body." The swollen midsection is why some read the graph as a
pregnant woman; either way it depicts the torso.

## Mnemonic
A standing figure seen side-on, the body filled out.

## Notes
身 is Kangxi radical 158. Senses: body (身体), oneself / in person (亲身),
a life (终身), and the classifier for suits of clothes (一身衣服).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; 甬 *yǒng* gives the sound
(for *tōng*). Shuowen: 達也 "to get through, to reach."

## Mnemonic
A road 辶 you can travel end to end with nothing in the way: open — it goes
through.

## Notes
The 甬 phonetic also writes 桶 *tǒng*, 痛 *tòng*, 涌 *yǒng*, 勇 *yǒng*, 踊
*yǒng*. Senses: pass through / unobstructed (通过, 通车, 想不通), connect
(通信, 交通, 沟通), thorough / expert (精通, 通晓), general (通常, 普通,
通用), and a measure word for letters and calls (一通电话).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning, 厶 *sī* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's entry is unusually specific: 私
originally named a variety of grain, and by a northern regional custom the
owner of a grain field was addressed as 私主人, "the private owner." That
possessive, individual-ownership sense is very likely the seed of the
modern "personal, private."

## Mnemonic
A stalk of grain 禾, marked out as belonging privately 厶 to one owner.

## Notes
私 spans neutral privacy (私人 "personal/private," 私立 "privately run
[school]") and the more critical "selfish" (自私 "selfish," 私心 "selfish
motives") — the same root idea of "set apart for oneself" read two ways.

## Etymology
Simplified from 論. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 仑 (侖)
*lún* gives the sound — and 侖 "to sort into order" fits the sense of ordered
argument. Shuowen: 議也 "to discuss, to deliberate."

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 marshalled into order 仑: an argument.

## Notes
The 仑 phonetic also writes 轮 / 輪 *lún*, 伦 / 倫 *lún*, 沦 / 淪 *lún*. Two
readings: *lùn* "to discuss / a theory / regardless" (讨论, 理论, 无论), *lún*
only in 论语 "the Analects."

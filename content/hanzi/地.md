## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 土 "earth, soil" gives the meaning; 也 *yě* gives the sound
(for *dì*).

## Mnemonic
The ground 土 you stand on.

## Notes
The 也 phonetic also writes 他 *tā*, 池 *chí*, 施 *shī*. Two readings: *dì*
"earth / ground / place / land" (地方, 土地, 地图), and toneless *de*, the
adverbial particle after a manner word (慢慢地走).

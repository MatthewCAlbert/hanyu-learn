## Etymology
Oracle and bronze forms of 監 show a person bending over a basin 皿 of water, eyes toward the surface — the earliest "mirror". "To look down at" then gave "to supervise, to inspect" (监督, 监测). The later 鑑/鉴 added 金 for the bronze mirror. Simplified 监 compresses the figure on top, so it no longer reads as a person.

## Mnemonic
An overseer leaning over a bowl 皿 to watch what is inside — supervising.

## Notes
Two readings: *jiān* (监督, 监狱 "prison") and *jiàn* in 太监 "palace eunuch". 蓝 and 篮 contain the same 監 part.

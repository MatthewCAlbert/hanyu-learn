## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional 島: 山 (mountain) supplies the meaning, 鳥 the sound. Shuowen defines an island as a hill in the sea where one can rest. 鸟 is an older *diǎo* in some readings, so the sound clue is accurate.

## Mnemonic
A mountain 山 in the sea where the birds 鸟 rest.

## Notes
Here the bird is a sound element, which also happens to be a good picture. 岛屿, 半岛, 岛国, 海岛.

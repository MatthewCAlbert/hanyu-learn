## Etymology
Simplified from 壞. 土 "earth" gives the meaning — a wall or structure crumbling
to dust; 褱 *huái* gave the sound. Shuowen: 敗也 "to go to ruin." Simplification
replaces 褱 with 不, a bare shape-sign, so the sound clue is lost. (An
unrelated old 坏 *pī* "unfired brick" happens to share the shape.)

## Mnemonic
The ground / the structure 土 falling apart: ruined, bad.

## Notes
Senses: bad / evil (坏人, 坏事), broken / gone off (坏了, 东西坏了), and, after
a verb, "terribly" (气坏了, 累坏了). Opposite 好.

## Etymology
宀 "roof" over 且, a stand piled with cuts of meat — the oracle-bone graph
shows an offering set out on its stand under a roof. Shuowen: 所安也 "what is
settled / fitting," noting 多 as an abbreviated phonetic. From "properly set
out" grew "suitable, fitting, appropriate."

## Mnemonic
The offering laid out just right under the roof 宀: as it should be.

## Notes
Senses: fitting / proper (适宜, 相宜, 事不宜迟), and "cheap / advantageous" in
便宜 (see that word entry). 不宜 "not advisable."

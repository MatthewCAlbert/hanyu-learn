## Etymology
Shuowen's gloss is abstract: "when something divided out is further
increased, that is called 戴" — 異 (to divide, to differentiate) combined
with a phonetic element related to 𢦏/戈. The modern components (a
top piece over 戈, a weapon/tool) do not map cleanly onto a clear
semantic + phonetic split in the current simplified shape, and the exact
mechanism of the classical gloss is not something this entry can confirm
beyond quoting Shuowen — hence low confidence.

The modern, narrower sense "to wear (on the head or face)" — 戴帽子 (to wear
a hat), 戴眼镜 (to wear glasses) — is a later specialization, distinct from
Shuowen's much more abstract "to add to what is already divided out."

## Mnemonic
Something balanced carefully on top: "戴," to wear (something on your head).

## Notes
戴 is specifically for things worn on the head, face, or hands (帽子,
眼镜, 手表, 戒指) — clothing on the body uses 穿, not 戴.

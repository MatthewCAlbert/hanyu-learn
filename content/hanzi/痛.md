## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 疒 (illness, the sickbed radical) supplies the meaning; 甬
*yǒng* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss is the general 病也 "illness,"
with the specific sense "pain, ache" understood from context and later
usage rather than spelled out in the definition itself.

## Mnemonic
Lying sick in bed 疒, feeling a passage 甬 of sharp pain run through you.

## Notes
Physical pain (疼痛, 头痛) extends naturally to emotional pain (悲痛,
痛苦) and, in the adverb 痛快, flips to intensity without the negative
charge — "thoroughly, to a satisfying degree."

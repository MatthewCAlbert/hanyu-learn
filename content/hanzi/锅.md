## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 鍋: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning, 呙
(traditional 咼) supplies the sound, shifted to *guō*. Not in Shuowen — pots
of this kind postdate the classical corpus. The straightforward "metal
cooking vessel" sense has held steady since.

## Mnemonic
A metal 钅 vessel, curved 呙 to hold whatever's cooking inside.

## Notes
锅 is the everyday word for cookware: 锅子/火锅 "hot pot," 炒锅 "wok," 锅碗瓢盆
"pots and pans" (a set phrase for kitchenware in general). Shares 呙 with 过,
whose own phonetic was lost in simplification — a reminder that not every
character keeps its sound clue equally well.

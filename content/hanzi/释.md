## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 釋: 釆 (a claw-print pictograph, the
semantic component behind "to distinguish, to sort apart" — the same
root behind 番, "to take turns") supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition is simply "to untangle, to resolve" — and 睪 *yì* supplies the
sound.

**Simplification reduced 睪 to an unrecoverable fragment**, the same
reduction seen in 译 and 择 (both covered elsewhere in this app), leaving
no reliable sound clue toward *shì* in the modern shape.

## Mnemonic
Sorting things apart 釆 until every tangle comes undone: "释," to
resolve or release.

## Notes
解释 (to explain, covered elsewhere in this app) and 释放 ("to release,"
of a person or a substance) both build on this character's core "to
untangle and set free" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (艸) "plant" gives the meaning; 化 *huà* gives the sound (for
*huā*). 花 is a later graph for a word earlier written 華 / 华 "blossom, to
flower." From "flower" grew "pattern / florid" (花样, 花纹), "blurred"
(眼花, 花白), and "to spend" money or effort (花钱, 花时间).

## Mnemonic
A plant 艹 transformed 化 into bloom: a flower.

## Notes
The 化 phonetic also writes 华 / 華 *huá*, 货 / 貨 *huò*. The "spend" sense is
very common: 花了不少钱.

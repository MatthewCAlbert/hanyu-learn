## Etymology
An associative compound: 田 "field" + 力 (a picture of a plough, or of a
straining arm). Shuowen spells out the logic: 男用力於田 "the man puts his
strength into the field." Meaning: male, a man.

## Mnemonic
The one who puts muscle 力 into the field 田: a man.

## Notes
男 pairs with 女. 力 "strength / effort" is Kangxi radical 19, also in 动, 加,
努, 劳. Senses: male (男人, 男生), son (长男).

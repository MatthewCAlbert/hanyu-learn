## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 目 "eye" gives the meaning; 垂 *chuí* "to hang down, to droop"
gives the sound and, aptly, the image — eyelids drooping. Shuowen: 坐寐也 "to
doze while sitting."

## Mnemonic
The eyes 目 drooping shut 垂: falling asleep.

## Notes
The 垂 phonetic also writes 邮 / 郵 *yóu*, 唾 *tuò*, 锤 / 錘 *chuí*. 睡觉
"to sleep" (verb + cognate object); 睡着 *shuìzháo* "to fall asleep."

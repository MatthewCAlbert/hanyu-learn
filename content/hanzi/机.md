## Etymology
Simplified from 機 — 木 "wood" for meaning, 幾 *jī* for sound — whose senses
run from "the trigger of a crossbow" to "a loom," "a mechanism," and "a
pivotal moment." Simplification abbreviates 幾 to 几 *jǐ*, a shortened
phonetic.

(A separate old character 机, "a small low table," already existed and
happened to share the shape; the modern character subsumes it.)

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 contraption with a trigger poised to spring.

## Notes
The 几 phonetic also (loosely) writes 讥 / 譏 *jī*, 饥 / 飢 *jī*, 肌 *jī*.
Senses: machine (机器), aircraft (飞机), opportunity (机会), the crux (机密).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (from 阜, a mound or wall — the barrier-forming
component also seen in 阵) supplies the meaning "to block off, to
separate," and 鬲 *lì* supplies the sound.

"To separate" (隔开, 相隔) extends naturally to "at an interval of" (隔壁,
next door, lit. "separated by one wall"; 隔一天, "every other day, at a
day's interval").

## Mnemonic
A wall 阝 standing between, with an ancient tripod vessel 鬲 sealed off on
the far side: "隔," to separate.

## Notes
隔壁 ("next door," lit. "separated by a wall") and 间隔 ("an interval, a
gap") both build directly on this "separated by a barrier" core sense.

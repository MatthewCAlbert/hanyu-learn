## Etymology
Shuowen classifies 投 as ideographic — 扌 (hand) and 殳 (*shū*, a
staff-like weapon or tool held in the hand) both contributing meaning: the
hand-motion of hurling a held object. Some modern phonologists instead treat
殳 as doing double duty, since Old Chinese *doːl (殳) and *doːɡ (投) are close
enough to be a plausible phonetic pair — but Shuowen itself gives no 聲
reading, so that phonetic role is not securely attested and this entry
follows Shuowen's own ideographic account.

Either way, the sense — a hand casting something away from itself — is why
投 covers everything from "to throw" a ball to "to cast" a vote to "to throw
oneself into" a river or a cause (投河, 投入).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 hurling a staff 殳 end over end: to "投," throw.

## Notes
投 is unusually productive in figurative compounds because "throwing" maps
onto "committing" so cleanly: 投资 (invest, lit. "throw funds"), 投入 (throw
oneself in), 投票 (cast a vote).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, with the phonetic also carrying meaning. 女 (woman)
supplies the general meaning; 昏 *hūn* "dusk, twilight" supplies the
sound and, per Shuowen, the whole ritual logic too: a bride was
traditionally fetched at dusk, because women were associated with yin
(the dark, receptive principle) — hence 婚, built on "twilight."

## Mnemonic
A woman 女 married at dusk 昏, as tradition required: a wedding.

## Notes
婚 pairs with the closely related 姻 (which names the relationship
between the two families joined by marriage) in the compound 婚姻
"marriage" — 婚 emphasises the ceremony and the bride's role, 姻 the
resulting bond between households.

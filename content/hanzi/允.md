## Etymology
Shuowen reads 允 as 㠯 (an old form of 以, "to take, to use") above 儿 (a person): "to be trustworthy, faithful". Modern scholars have proposed a nodding person, others a person with a pledge. None of these is secure, and the 厶 in the modern shape is the remains of 㠯. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A person 儿 who keeps their word: "I allow it".

## Notes
允许 "allow", 允诺 "promise", 默允 "tacitly permit", 公允 "fair". 允 is also a sound part in 吮 *shǔn* and 狁.

## Etymology
Ideographic. 大 (a person, arms and legs spread — the same figure as in
立) with an added horizontal stroke across the head — Shuowen reads that
stroke as 簪, a hairpin: the mark of an adult man's formal coming-of-age
headdress. Xu Shen adds the etymological pun behind 丈夫 "husband,
grown man": under Zhou-dynasty measure, an adult male stood 八尺 "eight
feet" tall, i.e. one 丈 (a fathom) — "a fathom of a man."

## Mnemonic
A person 大, crowned with a hairpin 一 marking them fully grown: a man.

## Notes
夫 splits into the honorific "husband" (丈夫, 夫妻) and, in a separate
old reading, a general term for "a man, a labourer" (农夫 "farmer," 车夫
"cart-driver") — adulthood and occupation both marked by the same
coming-of-age pin.

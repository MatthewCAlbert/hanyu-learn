## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 片 (a split-off slice, the "half of 木" component) supplies the
meaning, 反 *fǎn* supplies the sound, shifted to *bǎn*. Shuowen's gloss 判也
"divided" reflects the object's origin: a wooden board split off for
carving printing blocks or writing on. "Edition," "version," and "page" all
descend from that single physical printing board.

## Mnemonic
A wooden slab 片 flipped over 反 to press ink onto paper.

## Notes
版 anchors a large modern vocabulary of media and publishing: 出版 "to
publish," 版本 "version," 正版/盗版 "genuine/pirated," 网页 uses a different
character for "page" (页) — worth distinguishing since both mean "page" in
English but never substitute for each other.

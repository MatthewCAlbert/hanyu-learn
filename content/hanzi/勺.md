## Etymology
Pictographic: a scoop 勹 holding something (the dot 丶 stands for the contents), as Shuowen notes: "a picture; something in the middle". The same idea as 包, which wraps a thing. This is a standard reading, not independently proven.

## Mnemonic
A ladle 勹 with soup 丶 in it.

## Notes
勺子 "spoon", 汤勺 "ladle", 饭勺, 勺 as a former unit of volume. 勺 is also a sound part in 约, 钓, 灼, 酌, 杓.

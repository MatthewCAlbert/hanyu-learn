## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 衣 (clothing, cloth) supplies the meaning — early bags were
made of woven cloth — and 代 *dài* supplies the sound. 袋 is a
post-classical character, so its sound history is less firmly attested
than the ancient pictophonetic core, which keeps this at medium rather
than high confidence.

袋 also serves as a measure word for anything bagged: 一袋米 ("a bag of
rice"), the container standing in for a counted unit of its contents.

## Mnemonic
Cloth 衣 folded to take the place 代 of a container: a "袋," bag.

## Notes
口袋 ("pocket," lit. "mouth-bag") and 塑料袋 ("plastic bag") both build
directly on this character's core "bag" sense.

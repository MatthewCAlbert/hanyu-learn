## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 辶 (movement) with 豕 (a pig, here standing for
a fleeing hunted animal, reduced from 豚) — chasing down a fleeing pig,
the archetypal hunted creature. "To chase" generalized to "to pursue"
anything and, separately, to "one by one, in sequence" (逐渐, covered
elsewhere in this app; 逐个, "one at a time") — each item pursued and
dealt with in its turn.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 hot on the heels of a fleeing pig 豕: "逐," to chase.

## Notes
逐渐 ("gradually," covered elsewhere in this app) and 追逐 ("to chase
after," pairing 逐 with the near-synonym 追) both build on this
character's core "pursuing in sequence" sense.

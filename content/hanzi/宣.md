## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 宀 (roof/building) supplies the meaning. Shuowen's
original sense is a specific place: 天子宣室也, "the emperor's Xuan Hall,"
a chamber for making pronouncements. Xu Xuan's added commentary links the
phonetic to 回 "to circle back," describing how wind circulates through
and out of a space — the image behind "to proclaim, to spread abroad."
Confidence is medium because that circulation-based reading of the
phonetic is Xu Xuan's own interpretive gloss rather than a directly
pictured idea.

## Mnemonic
Beneath one roof 宀, a message circles round and round 亘 until the whole
world has heard it: to proclaim.

## Notes
"The hall where the emperor makes announcements" generalised into "to
announce, to proclaim" broadly — 宣布, 宣传 "to publicise," 宣誓 "to take
an oath [aloud]" — every sense keeping the idea of something spoken to be
widely known.

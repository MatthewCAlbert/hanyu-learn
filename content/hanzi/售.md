## Etymology
Per Shuowen, 售 combines 口 (mouth — the calling-out of a vendor) with a
reduced form of 雔 *chóu* (a pair of birds, here supplying only sound) to
mean "goods sold away from the hand" — completing a sale. Because the
modern shape's top component (隹, a single bird) is only a fragment of
Shuowen's original 雔 (a pair), this entry keeps confidence at medium
rather than declaring a clean phonetic.

"To sell" narrowed from Shuowen's fuller "to successfully sell/dispose
of goods" into the ordinary modern verb.

## Mnemonic
A vendor's voice 口 calling out over the goods laid out for sale: "售,"
to sell.

## Notes
出售 ("to sell, to offer for sale," pairing 售 with 出) and 销售 (covered
elsewhere in this app) both keep 售's core "to sell" sense, in more
formal/written registers than plain 卖.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (tree) supplies the meaning; 子 *zǐ* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 果也, "fruit," names the plum tree specifically —
one of the earliest and most widely cultivated fruit trees in ancient
China, common enough to become one of the most frequent surnames.

## Mnemonic
A tree 木 bearing its children 子, round and ripe: plums.

## Notes
李 lives a double life in modern Chinese: as the plum itself (though now
mostly displaced in everyday speech by 李子), and, far more visibly, as
one of the most common Chinese surnames — 李 is a leading contender for
"most common surname in the world."

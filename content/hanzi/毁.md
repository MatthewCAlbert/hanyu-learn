## Etymology
Shuowen's 毀 is pictophonetic: 土 (earth) for meaning "to break, to chip away" with 毇 (an abbreviated form meaning to pound hulled rice) for sound. The modern 毁 has 工 where 土 stood, so the earth element is not visible. The "man striking a vessel" explanation is a reading of the later shape, not an attested etymology.

## Mnemonic
A tool 工 hammering 殳 at a mortar 臼 until it breaks.

## Notes
毁灭, 摧毁, 毁坏, 烧毁. Two shapes, 毁 and 毀, both appear; 毁 is the standard simplified form. Sound part is 毇 *huǐ*, which has no independent role today.

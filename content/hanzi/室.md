## Etymology
宀 "roof" gives the meaning; 至 *zhì* gives the sound and, meaning "to
arrive," adds the idea of the place one comes home to. Shuowen: 至，所止也
"where one stops."

## Mnemonic
Under the roof 宀, the place you finally arrive 至 at day's end: your room.

## Notes
The 至 phonetic also writes 到 *dào*, 屋 *wū*, 侄 *zhí*. Senses: a room
(卧室, 教室, 浴室), an office or unit (办公室, 研究室, 科室), a household or
family line (皇室, 宗室), and — in classical usage — a wife (室人, 正室).

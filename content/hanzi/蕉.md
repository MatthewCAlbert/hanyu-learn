## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning; 焦 *jiāo* supplies the
sound. 蕉 names the banana/plantain plant family — a straightforward
plant-naming construction, with no deeper story beyond "a plant, sounding
like *jiāo*."

## Mnemonic
A plant 艹, its leaves scorched 焦 brown-gold by the tropical sun: the
banana plant.

## Notes
蕉 is a bound form, appearing almost only in 香蕉 "banana" (literally
"fragrant banana-plant [fruit]") — it does not stand alone as an
everyday word the way 米 or 果 do.

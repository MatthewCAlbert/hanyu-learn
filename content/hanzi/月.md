## Etymology
Pictographic — a crescent moon, drawn with an inner stroke to distinguish it
from 夕 "evening." Shuowen: 闕也。太陰之精 "the waning one; the essence of the
lesser yin." From "moon" comes "month" (one lunar cycle).

## Mnemonic
A crescent hanging in the sky: the moon.

## Notes
As a side component, 月 is very often 肉 "flesh," not the moon — compare 明,
朋, 期 (moon) with 肚, 脸, 脑, 胖 (flesh). Time words: 月份, 上个月, 月亮.

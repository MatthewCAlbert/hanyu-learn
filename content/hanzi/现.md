## Etymology
Pictophonetic. The 王 on the left is 玉 "jade" (the radical loses its lower
dot), giving the meaning; 见 *jiàn* "to see" gives the sound (for *xiàn*) and
a hint of sense — the lustre that shows on polished jade. From "to become
visible" come "to appear / manifest" and "present, current, now."

## Mnemonic
The sheen that comes into view 见 on a piece of jade 王: it appears.

## Notes
王 as a left component almost always means 玉 "jade" (玩, 玲, 球, 理), not
"king." The 见 phonetic also writes 视 / 視, 观 / 觀. Senses: now (现在),
cash (现金), ready-made (现成).

## Etymology
A mouth 口 with something protruding upward — usually read as a tongue, or a
wind instrument (a pipe / flute) held to the lips. Speech issuing from the
mouth. Shuowen distinguishes 言 "to state plainly" from 語 "to debate."

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 with the tongue out, mid-word: speech.

## Notes
言 is Kangxi radical 149; as a left component it compresses to 讠 (说, 话,
语, 请, 谢). Senses: words / speech (语言, 名言), to say (言之有理), a word /
character (七言诗, 一言不发).

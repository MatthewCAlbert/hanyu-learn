## Etymology
Not securely attested. 已 is a differentiated form of 己 — the same coiled
shape with the top loop left more open. Its origin as a picture is unclear; it
is a grammatical word: "already," "to stop / cease," and the literary "too,
excessively" (已甚).

## Mnemonic
己 with the loop broken open — "that's done already."

## Notes
Three near-identical graphs: 己 *jǐ* "self" (loop half open), 已 *yǐ*
"already" (loop more open), 巳 *sì* "the sixth branch" (loop closed). 已经
"already"; 而已 "that's all"; 不得已 "have no choice."

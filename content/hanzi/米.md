## Etymology
Pictographic — grains of husked rice or millet, shown as dots around a central
dividing line (the ear of grain, or the frame of a sieve). Shuowen: 粟實也 "the
kernel of the grain."

## Mnemonic
Loose grains scattered around the stalk: rice.

## Notes
米 is Kangxi radical 119 (粉, 粥, 精, 糖). Two lives in modern Chinese: the
grain (大米, 米饭), and the metric metre (公里 "kilometre," 米 alone for
"metre" — a sound borrowing from French *mètre*).

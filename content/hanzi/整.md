## Etymology
Ideographic, with the bottom component also carrying the sound (亦聲).
Shuowen reads it as three parts working together: 束 (a bundle, tied up),
攴 (a hand with a stick, tapping/striking), and 正 (straight, correct) —
"bundle it, tap it into shape, until it is straight and orderly." 正 both
means "orderly" and supplies the reading *zhěng*.

The app's decomposition data groups the top two pieces (束 and 攴) into
one compressed leaf, 敕, which is itself a separate character (an
imperial edict) that happens to share this exact shape — a coincidental
graphic overlap, not an etymological link to "orderly."

## Mnemonic
Tap 攴 a bundle 束 until it sits straight 正: everything in order.

## Notes
整 covers both "whole, complete" (整个, 整天) and "to tidy, to fix"
(整理, 整齐) — the state of being in order and the act of putting things
in order share one character, as they do in English "to set right."

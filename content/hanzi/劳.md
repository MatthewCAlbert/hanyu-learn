## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 勞: Shuowen glosses it 劇也, "strenuous, hard," and
analyses the graph as 力 (effort) under a reduced form of 熋/熒 (blazing
fire/light) — working as hard, and as relentlessly, as a fire burns. The
simplified 劳 keeps 力 at the base with 艹/冖 above as a graphic trace of
that reduced fire component, not a picture of "grass" in its own right.

## Mnemonic
Effort 力 that burns on and on, tireless as a fire that won't go out.

## Notes
劳 anchors 劳动 "labour, to work," 劳累 "worn out from work," 辛劳 "toil,
hard work," and the polite 劳驾 "excuse me, may I trouble you" — asking
someone to expend a little effort on your behalf.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 過: 辶 (movement) for meaning, 咼 *guō*
for sound.

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** Simplification replaced 咼
with 寸, which is neither semantic nor phonetic — it is a shape substitution.
So 过 gives you the meaning (movement, passing) and no pronunciation hint at
all. Do not try to read 寸 *cùn* into it.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 passing by.

## Notes
One of eight characters in HSK 1–2 whose phonetic was lost in simplification;
the app flags these, and they are worth learning as sound-opaque from the
start. Compare 边 (臱 lost) and 举 (舉 lost).

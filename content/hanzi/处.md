## Etymology
Simplified from 處. The old graph has 夂 "a foot" coming to a halt at 几 "a
stool," under 虍 "tiger" for sound — Shuowen: 止也 "to stop and rest." From "to
settle / stay" grew "to be in (a situation)," "to get along with," "to handle /
manage," and "to punish" (*chǔ*); and as a noun, "a place" (*chù*).

## Mnemonic
A foot 夂 stopping at the stool 卜: to settle down somewhere.

## Notes
Two readings: *chǔ* "to dwell / deal with / punish" (处理, 相处, 处分),
*chù* "a place / point" (到处, 好处, 长处, 办事处).

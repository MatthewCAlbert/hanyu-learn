## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 法,
means "a standard, a pattern to follow" — a wooden template or mold used
to shape copies — and 莫 *mò* supplies the sound.

"A pattern to copy from" gives both "a model, an example" (模范, 模特,
"a fashion model") and, physically, "a mold" (mú reading, 模具, "a
mold/die").

## Mnemonic
Wood 木 carved into a template, hidden 莫 (莫's own old sense, "sun set
into the grass," here only sound) beneath every copy made from it:
"模," a model or mold.

## Notes
Two readings split by sense: *mó* is the everyday "model, pattern"
(模范, 模型); *mú* is specifically "a mold" for casting (模具, 模子) —
the same character, a concrete-versus-abstract split.

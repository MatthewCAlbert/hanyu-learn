## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition is
vivid: water obstructed, breaking off at an angle in rapid, agitated
waves, exactly what happens when a current hits a rock — and 敫 *jiǎo*
supplies the sound.

Turbulent, provoked water generalizes directly to emotional provocation:
"to stir up, to excite" (激动, 刺激, both covered elsewhere in this app).

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 thrown into agitated waves the moment it meets an obstacle:
"激," to stir up.

## Notes
激动 (excited/moved) and 刺激 (to stimulate/provoke, both covered
elsewhere in this app) both descend directly from this character's vivid
"turbulent water" image, applied to emotion instead of a river.

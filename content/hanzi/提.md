## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 是 *shì* gives the sound (for
*tí*). Shuowen: 挈也 "to carry by the handle, holding it down at one's side."

## Mnemonic
Something swinging from the hand 扌: carried — and, by extension, raised for
attention.

## Notes
The 是 phonetic also writes 题 / 題 *tí*, 堤 *dī*, 匙 *chí*. Senses: carry
(提水, 手提包), lift / raise (提高, 提升), bring up (提出, 提到, 提议),
move forward (提前), draw out (提取, 提炼). *dī* only in 提防 "to guard
against."

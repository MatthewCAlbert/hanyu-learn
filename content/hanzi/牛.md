## Etymology
Pictographic — an ox's head seen head-on: two curved horns at the top, the
ears, and the snout. Shuowen reads the strokes as the horns, head and back.

## Mnemonic
Two horns over a broad head: an ox.

## Notes
牛 is Kangxi radical 93 (component form 牜: 物, 特, 牧). Distinguish from 午
"noon" (crossed top) and 生 "to grow." Slang 牛 / 牛× "impressive, badass."

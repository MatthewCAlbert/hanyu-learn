## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 足 "foot" gives the meaning; 各 *gè* gives the sound (for *lù*).
Shuowen: 道也 "a road."

## Mnemonic
The way 各 your feet 足 take: a road.

## Notes
The 各 phonetic also writes 格 *gé*, 客 *kè*, 落 *luò*, 略 *lüè*, 各 *gè*.
Senses: road (马路, 公路), route / line (三路车), way / means (出路), a class
(这路人).

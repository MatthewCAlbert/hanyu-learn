## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen: 门 (a gate) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition describes tallying and inspecting things as they pass through
a gate, an administrative checkpoint review — and 兑 *duì* supplies the
sound.

"To review things passing through a checkpoint" generalized to "to read
through, to peruse" (阅读, 阅览) — text passing before the eyes the way
goods once passed before an inspector at the gate.

## Mnemonic
Goods tallied and reviewed as they pass through a gate 门, exchanged 兑
for a mark of approval: "阅," to review — or to read.

## Notes
阅读 ("to read") and 检阅 ("to review/inspect," as of troops) both keep
this character's core "to examine as it passes before you" sense, one
applied to text, one to a formal inspection.

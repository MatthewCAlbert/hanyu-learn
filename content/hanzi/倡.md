## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 昌 *chāng* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 樂也 ties the original sense to
music and performance — 倡 once named a singer or performer (倡优). "To
initiate, to advocate" (提倡, 倡议) is a later extension: the performer who
leads a song is the one who "starts things off."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 leading the way, radiant 昌 and out in front.

## Notes
倡 today is almost entirely the "advocate, propose" sense: 提倡 "to
advocate," 倡议 "to propose an initiative." The older "performer" sense
survives only in the historical compound 倡优, now archaic.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (艸) "plant" gives the meaning; 采 *cǎi* "to pick" gives the
sound (for *cài*) and a hint of sense — vegetables are the plants you gather to
eat. Shuowen: 艸之可食者 "the edible plants."

## Mnemonic
The plants 艹 you go out and pick 采: greens.

## Notes
The 采 phonetic also writes 彩 *cǎi*, 踩 *cǎi*, 睬 *cǎi*. Senses: vegetable
(青菜), a dish of food (点菜, 川菜), and slang "weak, a noob" (菜鸟, 太菜了).

## Etymology
Pictographic — two people standing back to back. Shuowen: 乖也。从二人相背
"to turn away; two people with their backs to each other." This was the
original of 背 "the back." It was borrowed for the direction "north" — the
cold, shaded side that a south-facing house turns its back on — and 背
(北 + ⺼) was coined for "the back."

## Mnemonic
Two figures turned back to back — facing away, toward the cold: north.

## Notes
败北 "to be defeated" keeps the "turn one's back (and flee)" sense. Directions
order 东/西 then 南/北: 东北, 西北. 北方 "the North"; 北京 "northern capital."

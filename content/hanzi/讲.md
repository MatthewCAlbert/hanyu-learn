## Etymology
Simplified from 講. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; the traditional
phonetic 冓 *gòu* is replaced by 井 *jǐng* in the simplified form — a closer
match for *jiǎng*. Shuowen: 和解也 "to reconcile through talk." From "to talk
over" grew "to explain, to lecture, to tell," and "to be particular about"
(讲究, 讲卫生).

## Mnemonic
Setting a matter out in words 讠: to explain.

## Notes
Senses: speak / lecture (讲话, 讲课, 演讲), tell (讲故事), explain (讲道理),
insist on / value (讲信用, 讲面子), negotiate (讲价).

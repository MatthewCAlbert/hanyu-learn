## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 宀 (roof/house) supplies the meaning; 畐 *fú* (a pictograph
of a full, round wine vessel or belly) supplies the sound and, as in 福,
carries a strong echo of "fullness" too. A household under one roof, with
everything full and complete — 備也, "complete, provided for" — is the
picture behind "wealthy."

## Mnemonic
A house 宀, filled to the brim 畐 with everything you could need: rich.

## Notes
富 and 福 share the same phonetic 畐 and a closely related sense of
"fullness" — material wealth (富) and good fortune (福) both trace back
to the same image of a container filled to capacity.

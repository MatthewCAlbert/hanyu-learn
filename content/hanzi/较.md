## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 车 (vehicle) supplies the meaning; 交 *jiāo* supplies the
sound. The original, concrete sense names a specific part of a horse-drawn
carriage — the curved bronze handrail along the side of the carriage box,
something a passenger could grip and steady themselves against.

## Mnemonic
Two carriages 车, passing 交 each other on the road, close enough to
measure one against the other.

## Notes
"A handrail, something to steady yourself by" extended, via "to measure
oneself against," into "to compare" (比较) and, separately, "relatively"
(较为, 较少) — comparison as a kind of steadying reference point.

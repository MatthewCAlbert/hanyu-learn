## Etymology
日 "sun" over an element read as 正 / 止. Shuowen: 直也。从日正 "straight; from
sun and upright" — taking the sun as the measure of what is straight and
correct. The bronze forms support 日 + 止, but the exact logic is
interpretation. From "correct, right" (实事求是) came "this / these" and then
the copula "to be."

## Mnemonic
Measured against the sun 日 and found upright 正: that's right — that's it.

## Notes
The path from "correct" to "this" to "to be" is well documented across early
texts. 是的 "yes," 是不是 "is it or isn't it," 于是 "thereupon."

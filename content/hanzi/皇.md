## Etymology
Shuowen reads 皇 as 自 (self; the old "beginning") over 王, "the first king" (始皇). Modern scholars instead read the bronze form as a crown or ceremonial plume-topped ornament above a base, and the later 白 as a distortion of the crown. The two readings disagree and neither is certain. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A king 王 with a shining crown 白 on his head.

## Notes
皇帝 "emperor", 皇后 "empress", 皇宫 "palace", 皇上 "His Majesty". 煌 and 凰 and 蝗 have 皇 as sound.

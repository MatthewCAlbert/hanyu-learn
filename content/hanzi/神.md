## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 礻 (altar/ritual) supplies the meaning; 申 *shēn* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's gloss, "the heavenly spirit that draws forth the ten
thousand things," plays on 申's own sense of "to extend, stretch out" —
申 also depicts lightning in the oldest forms, another apt image for a
sky-god.

## Mnemonic
At the altar 礻, extend 申 your prayers to the gods.

## Notes
From "deity" the meaning generalised to "spirit, mind, expression" (精神,
神情) — a shift from "the god above" to "the animating spirit within,"
paralleled in many languages.

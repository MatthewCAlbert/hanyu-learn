## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 噴: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning, 贲
(traditional 賁) supplies the sound, shifted to *pēn*. Shuowen's gloss 吒也
is close to "to shout/expel forcefully from the mouth" — the sense
generalised from an angry exhalation to any forceful spray or spurt (喷水
"to spray water," 喷嚷 "to spurt out").

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 that suddenly bursts 贲 open, spraying everything in front of it.

## Notes
Two readings: *pēn* "to spray, spurt" is the everyday verb (喷泉 "fountain,"
喷嚏 "sneeze"); *pèn* survives in 喷香 "fragrant" and a few seasonal-produce
idioms.

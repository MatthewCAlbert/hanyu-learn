## Etymology
Ideographic. 页 (head) with 火 (fire, standing for heat/fever) — Shuowen's
gloss is literal and medical: 熱頭痛也, "a fever headache." The character
names a physical symptom before it names an emotional state.

## Mnemonic
A head 页 burning up 火 with fever: irritable, on edge.

## Notes
"A feverish headache" generalised entirely into the psychological register
— 麻烦 "trouble," 烦恼 "vexation," 不耐烦 "impatient" — with essentially
no trace of the original physical-illness sense left in modern use.

## Etymology
Associative: 廾 "two hands" holding 玉 "a jade" up to turn it over and admire
it. Shuowen: 玩也 "to play with." From "to finger, to toy with" grew the very
broad "to do, to make, to fix, to get, to mess with."

## Mnemonic
Two hands 廾 turning a jade 玉 this way and that: fiddling with it.

## Notes
*nòng*: do / make (弄饭, 弄坏, 弄清楚), play with / fool with (玩弄, 弄权,
戏弄); *lòng*: a lane / alley (弄堂, in Shanghai usage). 摆弄 "to fiddle
with"; 卖弄 "to show off."

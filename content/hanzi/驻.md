## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 马 (horse) supplies the meaning — a horse that stands still — and 主 *zhǔ* supplies the sound (*zhǔ* → *zhù*). Shuowen glosses it as a horse standing. "To be stationed, to stay" (of troops, diplomats) follows.

## Mnemonic
A horse 马 that stays where its master 主 stops.

## Notes
主 is sound only. Series: 住 *zhù*, 注 *zhù*, 柱 *zhù*, 驻 *zhù*. 驻扎 "be stationed", 驻外 "posted abroad", 驻华大使 "ambassador to China", 驻足 "stop in one's tracks".

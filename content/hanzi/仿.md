## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 方 *fāng* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 相似也 "to resemble" matches the
modern "to imitate, to copy" — one person modelled to resemble another.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 shaped 方 to look just like the original.

## Notes
仿 anchors 模仿 "to imitate," 仿照 "to follow [a model/pattern]," and 仿佛
"as if, seemingly" (a fixed disyllabic word, not decomposable into "imitate
+ Buddha"). 仿制品 "counterfeit/replica" is the everyday noun for a copied
object.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 衣 (cloth, garment) supplies the meaning and 列 *liè* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as leftover cloth after cutting; "to split, to tear" follows from cutting fabric.

## Mnemonic
A garment 衣 laid in rows 列 and ripped along the line.

## Notes
列 is itself sound-only here although "row/split" is fitting. 分裂, 破裂, 裂缝 "crack", 裂痕. 烈, 例, 列, 裂 are one sound family.

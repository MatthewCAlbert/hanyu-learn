## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 县 supplies the sound *xiàn* → *xuán* and is also the older word for "to hang" (the character 縣 originally meant "hung up", before it became "county"). 心 was added later to separate "to suspend" from "county" — and also gives "to be suspended in worry". Shuowen marks 縣 as "also sound".

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 left hanging in the county 县 square — suspense.

## Notes
县 as "county" and 悬 as "hang" were one word; the split is a famous example of a character losing its original meaning. 悬崖, 悬挂, 悬念 "suspense", 悬殊 "great disparity".

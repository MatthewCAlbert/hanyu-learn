## Etymology
Simplified from 經. 纟 (糸) "silk thread" gives the meaning — the warp threads
stretched lengthwise on a loom, which the weft crosses. The phonetic 巠 *jīng*
is written 𢀖 in the simplified form. From "the fixed warp" grew "a constant
standard, a canonical text," and "to pass along / through."

## Mnemonic
The lengthwise warp threads 纟 that everything else is woven across: the
through-line.

## Notes
Senses: warp (经纬, 经线), a classic / scripture (经典, 圣经, 佛经), pass
through / undergo (经过, 经历, 曾经), manage (经营, 经理, 经手), and the
body's channels (经络) and menstruation (月经). 经济 "economy."

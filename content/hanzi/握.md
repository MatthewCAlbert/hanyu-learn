## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 屋 *wū* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 搤持也 "to grip and hold firmly," is unchanged
in the modern word — a tighter, more forceful grip than the more general
持 or 拿.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 closing around something the way walls 屋 close around a room:
a firm grip.

## Notes
握 covers both the literal handshake (握手) and the abstract "to have a
firm grip on" — 把握 "to grasp, to be sure of," 掌握 "to master" — a
tight physical hold as the model for command over a skill or situation.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 擠: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 齐 (traditional
齊, "even, aligned") supplies the sound, shifted to *jǐ*. Not in Shuowen. The
sense "to squeeze, to crowd" plausibly echoes 齐's "pressed level/together"
image, though its formal role in the character is sound rather than meaning.

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 pushing everyone level and even 齐 into one crowded space.

## Notes
挤 is the everyday word for crowding: 挤公交 "to squeeze onto the bus," 拥挤
"crowded," and figuratively 挤时间 "to squeeze out time" from a busy
schedule.

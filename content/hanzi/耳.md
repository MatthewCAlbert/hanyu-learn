## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: the character traces the outer shape of a
human ear — its outline and inner folds simplified into a small set of
strokes. Shuowen's definition is direct: "the organ in charge of
hearing."

## Mnemonic
The curved outline of an ear, drawn straight from life: "耳," ear.

## Notes
耳朵 (ear, the everyday noun, adding the suffix 朵) and 耳机 ("headphones,"
lit. "ear-machine") both build on this character's plain, pictographic
"ear" sense.

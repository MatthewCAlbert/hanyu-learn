## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen: 山 (mountain) supplies the meaning — the
original definition names a specific mountain shape, "one like a
hall/chamber" — and 宓 *mì* supplies the sound. 宓 independently means
"quiet, still, tucked away," and it is this sense of 宓, rather than
Shuowen's mountain-shape definition, that clearly underlies the modern
senses "dense, close together" (密集) and "secret, hidden away" (秘密,
保密).

Given that the modern meaning tracks the phonetic's own sense more
closely than Shuowen's literal definition of the whole character, this
entry keeps confidence medium.

## Mnemonic
A mountain 山 hiding a still, silent 宓 chamber deep inside: "密," dense —
or secret.

## Notes
秘密 pairs 密 with 秘 (mysterious) as a near-synonym compound — see this
app's entry for that word for how the two halves divide the "hidden" idea
between them.

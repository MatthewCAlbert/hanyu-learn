## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: multiple hand-shapes (手, 𠂇, and a kneeling
figure 卩) together depict someone receiving and holding up an object
with both hands, respectfully — "to receive and support," Shuowen's own
definition, 奉.

"To receive and hold up" extends to "to bear (a responsibility)" (承担,
"to bear/undertake") and "to inherit, to carry on" (继承, 承认's own
"to accept" sense, covered elsewhere in this app).

## Mnemonic
Many hands together, lifting up and receiving what is passed to them:
"承," to bear or accept.

## Notes
承认 (to admit/acknowledge, covered elsewhere in this app), 承担 (to bear
responsibility), and 继承 (to inherit) all extend this character's core
"to receive and take up" image in different directions.

## Etymology
Per Shuowen, the character depicts a hand holding a carpenter's square
(規巨, a tool for measuring right angles) — a large tool for making
precise, large-scale measurements. The leap from "a measuring tool" to
the modern everyday sense "huge, giant" (巨大, covered elsewhere in this
app) is generally treated as a phonetic-loan-like extension — big tools
for big jobs, in effect — that this entry cannot confirm with full
certainty, hence medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
A hand gripping an oversized carpenter's square, built to measure
something enormous: "巨," huge.

## Notes
巨大 (huge, covered elsewhere in this app) and 巨人 ("giant," a person of
enormous size) both keep this character's everyday "immense" sense, far
removed from its original measuring-tool meaning.

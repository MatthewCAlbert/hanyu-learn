## Etymology
Built around 彳 (movement, a road), the rest of the character's history
is genuinely unsettled — the components combined with it in the modern
shape do not cleanly resolve into an attested semantic-plus-phonetic
pair, and this entry does not invent a bridge it cannot source. The core
sense, "tiny, minute, barely perceptible" (微小, 微笑 "a faint smile"), is
well attested even where the exact pictorial history is not.

## Mnemonic
Something moving 彳 so faintly it can barely be perceived: "微," minute.

## Notes
微笑 ("a faint smile") and 微信 (WeChat, lit. "micro-message") both keep
this character's core "small, subtle" sense — one describing a gentle
expression, the other borrowed for a brand name evoking lightness and
immediacy.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning — sincerity, trust — and 京 *jīng* supplies the sound (*jīng* → *liàng*). Shuowen glosses it as trustworthy; "to believe the best of someone, to forgive" follows.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 from the capital 京 that is fair-minded: understanding.

## Notes
京 is sound only here. Series: 凉 *liáng*, 晾 *liàng*, 掠 *lüè*, 惊 *jīng*, 景 *jǐng*. 原谅 "forgive", 谅解 "understand", 体谅 "show consideration".

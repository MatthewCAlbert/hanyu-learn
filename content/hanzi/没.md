## Etymology
氵 "water" plus 𠬛 (a hand reaching into a whirlpool, now corrupted to 殳).
Shuowen: 沈也 "to sink, to go under." From "to submerge / disappear" grew "to
be gone, to not exist," and from there the everyday negative *méi* "not have,
there is not," which also negates completed action (没去过).

## Mnemonic
Sinking 氵 out of sight — gone, none.

## Notes
Two readings: *méi* "not have / not" (没有, 没关系), *mò* "to sink, to
confiscate, to submerge" (沉没, 没收, 淹没). 没 negates 有 and the past;
不 negates everything else.

## Etymology
目 "eye" with a straight vertical stroke above it — Shuowen: 正見也 "to see
straight," a level gaze sighting along a true line. From "straight" grew
"upright, frank" and the adverb "directly, straight through."

## Mnemonic
The eye 目 sighting dead level along a line: straight.

## Notes
直 is the phonetic in 值 *zhí* "value" and 植 *zhí* "to plant." Senses:
straight (直线, 笔直, 直发), vertical (直立, 直升机), upright / candid
(正直, 直率, 直言), direct (直接, 直达), continuously (一直, 直到).

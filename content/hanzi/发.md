## Etymology
Simplified from two characters. **發** — 弓 "bow" + 癹 for sound — Shuowen:
"to shoot an arrow," from which "to send out, to issue, to develop, to break
out." **髮** — 髟 "hair" + 犮 for sound — "hair of the head." Simplification
merged both into 发, whose parts are no longer legible.

## Mnemonic
An arrow loosed from the bow — sent out; and, by merger, "hair."

## Notes
Two readings: *fā* "to send out / develop / happen" (发现, 发生, 出发, 发展),
*fà* "hair" (头发, 发型, 理发 "haircut").

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺮ (bamboo) supplies the meaning — arrows were made from bamboo shafts — and 前 *qián* supplies the sound (*qián* → *jiàn*). Shuowen glosses it as arrow.

## Mnemonic
A bamboo ⺮ stick flying ahead 前.

## Notes
前 is sound only (though the picture fits). 火箭 "rocket", 弓箭 "bow and arrow", 箭头 "arrowhead / arrow sign", 一箭双雕 "kill two birds with one stone".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 王 (here 玉, jade) supplies the meaning, 㐱 *zhěn* supplied
the sound in the classical form (now written 彡 in the modern shape, a
graphic simplification of that phonetic rather than an independently
meaningful stroke set). Shuowen's gloss 寶也 "treasure" matches the modern
"precious, treasured" directly.

## Mnemonic
Jade 王, treated with care 彡 — a treasure.

## Notes
珍 anchors 珍惜 "to treasure/cherish," 珍贵 "precious, valuable," and 珍珠
"pearl" (literally "treasure-bead," pairing with 珠 below).

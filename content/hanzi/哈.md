## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning; 合 *hé* supplies the sound.
Confidence is medium rather than high for a different reason than usual —
not contested interpretation, but late attestation: 哈 postdates Shuowen
Jiezi entirely, so there is no Han-dynasty analysis to cite, only the
character's plain, still-legible modern construction.

## Mnemonic
An open 口 mouth that comes together 合 in a burst of laughter: "ha!"

## Notes
Mostly onomatopoeic (哈哈 "ha ha") and interjectional (哈欠 "yawn"), unlike
most 口-semantic characters in this level, which name actions of the mouth
rather than imitate a sound directly.

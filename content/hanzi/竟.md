## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 音 (sound, music) with 儿 (a standing person)
beneath it — "when a piece of music is finished, that is called 竟": a
person standing at the point where the sound comes to an end. The base
sense is "to finish, to come to completion" (as still seen in 毕竟, 究竟,
where "in the end" comes from the same root).

From "reaching the end," 竟 grammaticalized into an adverb marking
surprise at an outcome — "actually, unexpectedly, to the point of" (竟然)
— the sense that the end result was not what was expected along the way.

## Mnemonic
A person 儿 standing still as the last note of the music 音 fades: "竟,"
finished — and, surprisingly, that's how it ended.

## Notes
竟然 (unexpectedly) and 毕竟/究竟 (after all, when all is said and done)
all draw on the same "reaching the end" core, just pointed in slightly
different rhetorical directions.

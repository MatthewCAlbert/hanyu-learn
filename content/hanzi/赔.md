## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 賠: 贝 (shell — the ancient currency, and the
root of the whole money vocabulary: 财, 购, 货) supplies the meaning, 咅
*pǒu* supplies the sound, shifted to *péi*. Not in Shuowen; a later
character built once cowrie-shell money had long since become a fixed
metaphor for value rather than actual currency.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 handed over, right beside 咅 the person you wronged.

## Notes
赔 covers financial compensation (赔钱 "to pay compensation/to lose money,"
赔偿 "to indemnify") and the social apology sense (赔礻不是 "to apologise" —
literally "to compensate for being in the wrong"). Shares 咅 with 陪 and 培,
all read *péi*.

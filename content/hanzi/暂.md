## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 暫: 日 (sun/day) supplies the meaning, 斩
(traditional 斬, "to cut off") supplies the sound, shifted to *zàn*.
Shuowen's gloss 不久也 "not lasting long" matches the modern "temporary"
directly — a stretch of time cut short.

## Mnemonic
A day 日 cut short 斩 — over before you know it.

## Notes
暂 is the everyday word for temporariness: 暂时 "temporary, for now," 暂停
"to pause/suspend," 暂且 "for the time being." Distinct from 短 duǎn
"short," which describes length rather than provisional status.

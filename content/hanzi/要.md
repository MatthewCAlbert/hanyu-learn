## Etymology
Pictographic. The graph shows a person with both hands at the waist. Shuowen:
身中也 "the middle of the body" — this is the original of 腰 "waist." From
"the waist / the central, essential part" grew "the main point, essential,"
and from there "to demand" and "to want / need / will."

## Mnemonic
Hands planted on the hips 女: what you insist on having.

## Notes
腰 (⺼ added) now carries "waist." Two readings: *yào* "want / need / must"
(要求, 不要), *yāo* "to demand / coerce" (要挟) and in 要求 as a verb.

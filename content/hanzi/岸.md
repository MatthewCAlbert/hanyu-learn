## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 山/厂 (mountain/cliff — Shuowen actually roots the semantic
side in 屵, a steep bank, closely related to 山) supplies the meaning, 干
*gān* supplies the sound, shifted to *àn*. Shuowen's gloss 水厓而高者 is "a
high bank at the water's edge" — matching the modern "shore, bank" exactly.

## Mnemonic
A steep cliff 山/厂, dry 干 above the waterline.

## Notes
岸 anchors 海岸 "coast," 河岸 "riverbank," 上岸 "to go ashore" — always the
solid land bordering water, as opposed to 滩 tān, which leans toward a flat
beach or sandbar.

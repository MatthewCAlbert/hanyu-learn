## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — to keep in reserve — and 諸 (simplified 诸) *zhū* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as storing up. The older sense "crown prince (储君)" is the heir kept in reserve.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 saving all 诸 the goods for later.

## Notes
诸 is sound only. 储存, 储备, 储蓄 "savings", 储藏, 储量. Series: 储 *chǔ*, 著/煮/猪/诸 *zhū/zhǔ*.

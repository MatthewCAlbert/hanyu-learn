## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 日 "sun" gives the meaning; 辰 *chén* gives the sound — and 辰,
which also means "an early hour," reinforces it. Meaning: dawn, early morning.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 at hour 辰: daybreak.

## Notes
The 辰 phonetic also writes 震 *zhèn*, 唇 *chún*, 娠 *shēn*, 振 *zhèn*. 早晨
"early morning," 清晨 "the small hours of the morning," 晨练 "morning
exercise."

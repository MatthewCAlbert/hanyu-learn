## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen glosses 夕 as 莫也, "dusk," and describes the graph
directly: 月 (moon) shown only half-visible — the crescent moon just
appearing as day fades into evening. A clean, concrete picture with little
room for dispute.

## Mnemonic
The moon 月, only half seen as evening falls.

## Notes
夕 is Kangxi radical 36 and anchors 除夕 "New Year's Eve" (literally "the
eve that removes [the old year]") and 夕阳 "the setting sun." 朝夕 "morning
and evening" pairs it with 朝 to mean "constantly, day and night."

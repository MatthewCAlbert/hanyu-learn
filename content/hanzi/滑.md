## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 骨 *gǔ* supplies the sound,
shifted to *huá*. Shuowen's gloss 利也 is close to "smooth, slick" — water
makes a surface slide freely, and that image carries directly into the
modern "slippery, smooth" as well as the figurative "slick, not to be
trusted" (滑头).

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 over bone 骨 — slick enough that nothing gets a grip.

## Notes
滑 spans literal slipperiness (滑冰 "to ice-skate," 滑倒 "to slip and fall")
and character judgement (圆滑 "smooth/slick in dealing with people," 狡猾
"cunning" — where 猾 replaces 氵 with 犭, reusing the same 骨 phonetic in an
animal-behaviour context).

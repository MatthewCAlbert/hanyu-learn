## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 皮 *pí* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged — and, fittingly, 皮 also means "skin/hide," so the
sound component here carries a natural echo of draping an animal hide or
cloth over the shoulders, even though its formal role is phonetic. Shuowen's
gloss 从旁持曰披 is "to hold from the side," matching the modern "to drape
over."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 draping a hide 皮 over the shoulders against the cold.

## Notes
披 covers draping clothing (披上外套 "to throw on a coat") and, figuratively,
"to spread open" (披露 "to disclose/reveal," literally "to unveil/spread
open") — information laid bare the way a garment is unfolded.

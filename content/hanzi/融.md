## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 鬲 (a tripod cooking vessel) supplies the meaning — steam rising from a cauldron — and 虫, an abbreviated 蟲 *chóng*, supplies the sound. The modern sense "to melt, to blend" develops from steam and heat.

## Mnemonic
A cauldron 鬲 with a bug 虫 steaming inside — melting together.

## Notes
虫 is sound only here (not "insect"). 金融 "finance", 融合 "merge", 融化 "melt", 融洽 "harmonious". 融资 "financing".

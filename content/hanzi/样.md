## Etymology
Simplified from 樣. 木 "wood" gives the meaning — 樣 first named a kind of oak
(= 橡) — and the phonetic was 羕 *yàng*, simplified to 羊 *yáng*. From "a
specimen (of wood)" grew "a sample, a model, a pattern, a shape, a kind."

## Mnemonic
A sample cut from the tree 木: a model to copy.

## Notes
The 羊 phonetic also writes 洋 *yáng*, 详 / 詳 *xiáng*, 祥 *xiáng*. Senses:
kind / sort (一样, 各样), appearance / manner (样子, 模样), a sample (样品).

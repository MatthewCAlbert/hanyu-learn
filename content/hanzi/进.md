## Etymology
Simplified from 進. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; the traditional form had
隹 "bird" as (part of) the phonetic, with an old aside that birds only hop
forward. Simplification swaps 隹 for 井 *jǐng* — a rare case where the change
actually sharpens the sound clue for *jìn*.

## Mnemonic
Moving 辶 forward, drawn on like water to a well 井.

## Notes
进 is the directional complement "in / forward" (走进, 拿进来). Senses: enter
(进入, 进门), advance (进步, 前进), take in (进口 "import"). Opposite 出 or 退.

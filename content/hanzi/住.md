## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 主 *zhǔ* gives the sound (for
*zhù*) — and, as "host / master of a house," chimes with the sense. A late
character; not in Shuowen. Meaning: to dwell, to stay, to stop.

## Mnemonic
The person 亻 who is master 主 of the house lives there.

## Notes
The 主 phonetic also writes 注 *zhù*, 柱 *zhù*, 驻 / 駐 *zhù*. (往 *wǎng* only
looks as if it contains 主 — its right side is a corrupted 㞷, phonetic 王, not
the 主 phonetic.) As a resultative, 住 means "and it held fast": 记住, 站住,
抓住, 忍不住.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 戰: 戈 (a dagger-axe, the weapon
radical seen in 我, 或) supplied the meaning, and 單/单 *dān* supplied the
sound.

**Simplification replaced 单 with 占** (*zhàn*), which happens to give an
exact match to this character's modern reading — like 担's swap to 旦
and 递's swap to 弟, a rare case where a simplification substitution
landed on a cleaner phonetic than the one it replaced.

## Mnemonic
A weapon 戈 seized to occupy 占 contested ground: "战," war.

## Notes
战争 and 战斗 (both covered elsewhere in this app) build directly on this
character; 挑战 (to challenge, also covered elsewhere) extends "battle"
into any contest, literal or figurative.

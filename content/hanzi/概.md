## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (wood) supplies the meaning; 既 *jì* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's own entry is unusually thin — it only notes how the
character is read, without stating its meaning directly. The
better-attested traditional account, found in later dictionaries, is
that 概/槩 named a wooden strickle: a straight stick drawn across the top
of a grain measure to scrape off the excess and level it flush.
Confidence is low because that fuller account rests on post-Shuowen
sources rather than the Shuowen entry itself.

## Mnemonic
A wooden stick 木, drawn across the top to level everything off exactly
既 flush: an overall, approximate measure.

## Notes
"A stick that levels grain roughly flush" is a fitting root for the
modern sense "approximately, roughly, in general" — 大概, 概况, 一概而论
"to lump everything together" — precision traded for a good-enough
overall measure.

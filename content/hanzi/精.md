## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 米 (rice/grain) supplies the meaning; 青 *qīng* supplies
the sound. The original, concrete sense, 擇也 "to select, to sort," names
rice that has been carefully cleaned and sifted — Duan Yucai's commentary
glosses it directly as 簡米, "picked-over rice."

## Mnemonic
Rice 米, sorted so carefully it gleams blue-green 青: the very finest of
the batch.

## Notes
"Carefully sorted, refined rice" generalised into "essence, the finest
part of anything" — 精华, 精彩, 精美 — and further into "vital energy,
spirit" (精神, 精力), the refined "essence" of a person rather than of
grain.

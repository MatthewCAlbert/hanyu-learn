## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning — to consult by speaking — and 次 *cì* supplies the sound (*cì* → *zī*). Shuowen glosses it as taking counsel.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 asking for the next 次 piece of advice.

## Notes
次 is sound only. Series: 姿 *zī*, 资 *zī*, 咨 *zī*, 恣 *zì*. 咨询 "consult", 咨询师 "counsellor", 咨文 "a diplomatic note".

## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 女 (woman) with 又 (a hand), glossed as "holding
[the tools of] her role" — Shuowen frames 妻 explicitly as "a woman equal
in standing to her husband," managing her own share of household
affairs. The exact identity of the remaining middle component in the
modern shape is not something this entry can pin down cleanly, hence
medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
A woman 女 with her hand 又 firmly on her own share of the household's
affairs, equal partner to her husband: "妻," wife.

## Notes
妻子 (wife, covered elsewhere in this app) and 夫妻 (husband and wife, a
paired term naming both roles) both build on this character's core
"equal marriage partner" sense.

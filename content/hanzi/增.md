## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 土 (earth) supplies the meaning; 曾 *céng/zēng* supplies
the sound — piling up earth being a direct, physical picture of "adding
more." The original sense, 益也 "to add to, to benefit," generalised
smoothly to the fully abstract "to increase" used today.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土, piled on again 曾 and again: more and more.

## Notes
增 pairs with 加 in most modern compounds (增加, 增长) — both mean "to
add, to increase," with 增 keeping a slightly more formal, measured
register than the everyday 加.

## Etymology
Shuowen: 宗 is 宀 (roof, building) over 示 (altar, spirits): the ancestral temple where the forefathers are honoured. This is a well-attested ideograph. Extended senses: the lineage or clan (宗族), the main line, and by extension a school or sect (宗教, 正宗).

## Mnemonic
A house 宀 with an altar 示 inside it — the family shrine.

## Notes
宗教 "religion", 宗旨 "aim", 祖宗, 正宗 "authentic". 崇 and 综 (綜) also contain 宗 as a sound part (*chóng*, *zōng*).

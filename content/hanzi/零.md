## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 雨 "rain" gives the meaning; 令 *lìng* gives the sound (for
*líng*). Shuowen: 餘雨也 "the last drops of rain after a shower." From "a few
straggling drops" grew "a remnant, a fraction, odd bits" (零钱, 零食), and the
modern "zero."

## Mnemonic
The last stray raindrops 雨: what's left over — down to nothing.

## Notes
The 令 phonetic also writes 冷 *lěng*, 领 / 領 *lǐng*, 龄 / 齡 *líng*, 铃 /
鈴 *líng*. 零下 "below zero"; 一百零一 "a hundred and one" (零 fills the gap).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 豕 (boar/pig) supplies the meaning, 高 *gāo* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen describes a specific wild boar with
bristles stiff as brush-shafts — a fierce, formidable animal. "Brave,
heroic, bold" is a direct extension of that animal's ferocity onto human
character.

## Mnemonic
A wild boar 豕, bristles standing tall 高 — bold and fearsome.

## Notes
豪 anchors 豪杰 "hero, outstanding figure," 自豪 "proud [of oneself/one's
achievements]," and 豪华 "luxurious, grand" — boldness extending from
personal courage to extravagant display.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 陸: 阝 (a mound) supplies the
meaning — Shuowen's definition is "high, level ground," i.e. dry land —
and 坴 *lù* supplies the sound.

**Simplification replaced 坴 with the unrelated 击** ("to strike"), which
is neither semantic nor phonetic here — a shape substitution that leaves
no sound clue toward *lù* in the modern form.

## Mnemonic
High ground 阝 struck 击 firm and dry: "陆," land.

## Notes
大陆 ("continent, mainland") and 陆地 ("dry land," as opposed to sea)
both build directly on this character's plain "dry, elevated ground"
core sense.

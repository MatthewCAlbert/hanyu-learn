## Etymology
Pictophonetic in form: 艹 (plants) is conventionally read as the meaning component, 臧 *zāng* as the sound. 藏 is a late character, usually counted among the words Xu Xuan added to the Shuowen; 臧 itself (Shuowen: 善也 "good") was earlier borrowed for "to store", and 艹 was added to make the "store, hide" word distinct. Why 艹 was chosen (hiding under vegetation? a granary store?) is not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
Something stashed under a pile of grass 艹 so a minister 臧 won't find it.

## Notes
Two words share the graph: *cáng* "to hide, store" (收藏, 隐藏) and *zàng* "storehouse; Tibet" (宝藏, 西藏). 臧 carries sound only; the *zāng/cáng/zàng* spread is ordinary sound drift.

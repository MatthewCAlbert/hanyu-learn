## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 亭 *tíng* gives the sound — and
a roadside pavilion 亭 is just where a traveller halts. Shuowen: 止也 "to
stop."

## Mnemonic
A traveller 亻 pulling up at the wayside pavilion 亭: to stop.

## Notes
The 亭 phonetic also writes 婷 *tíng*, 亭 itself, 葶 *tíng*. Senses: stop /
halt (停止, 停下, 停电), park (停车, 停放), be stationed (停留, 停泊),
suspend (停职, 停课).

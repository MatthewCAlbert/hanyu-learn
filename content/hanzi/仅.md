## Etymology
The traditional form 僅 was pictophonetic: 亻 (person) for meaning, with a
phonetic component on the right (historically 堇 *jǐn*, unrelated to the
modern simplified shape). Simplification replaced that phonetic entirely
with 又, a common simplification stand-in used across many characters
(汉, 仅, 对 among them) purely to cut strokes — 又 here has no meaning of
"hand" and no sound relationship to *jǐn*.

Confidence is medium: 亻 is a genuine, checkable semantic component, but no
phonetic is declared because 又 in 仅 is graphic shorthand, not a real sound
clue.

## Mnemonic
One person 亻, and only that 又 — barely, merely.

## Notes
仅 "merely, only" and 仅仅 (the doubled, more emphatic form) both work as
adverbs narrowing a claim down to the bare minimum — 不仅 "not only" flips
that into "more than just."

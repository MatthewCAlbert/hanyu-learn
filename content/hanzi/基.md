## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 土 (earth) supplies the meaning; 其 *qí* supplies the
sound. The original, concrete sense, 牆始也 "the starting point of a
wall," is a foundation course of packed earth laid before any wall goes
up — exactly the modern sense of "foundation, base."

## Mnemonic
Earth 土, packed down first, before anything 其 else can be built: the
foundation.

## Notes
基 generalised from "a wall's foundation" to "the basis of anything" —
基本 "basic," 基础 "foundation," 基金 "a fund" (capital set aside as a
base) — always the thing everything else is built on top of.

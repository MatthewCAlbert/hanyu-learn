## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 又 (a hand) holding 中 — which Shuowen glosses as
"correct, impartial" — a hand holding an impartial record: "one who
records events," the ancient court scribe/historian. The word "history"
grew directly out of this role: what the scribe recorded became the
record of the past itself.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 holding an impartial tally 中, recording events exactly as they
happened: "史," history.

## Notes
历史 (history, covered elsewhere in this app) pairs 史 with 历 ("to pass
through/experience") — 史 itself survives mostly bound in compounds
(史书, 史料) rather than standing alone.

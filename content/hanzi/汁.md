## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water/liquid) supplies the meaning; 十 *shí* supplies
the sound. Some sources read 十 here as also hinting "many, varied" —
a rich broth made from many ingredients — but the core, checkable
composition Shuowen gives is simply liquid plus sound.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵, ten 十 fruits squeezed into it: juice.

## Notes
汁 is a bound form — it appears in compounds (果汁, 橙汁) but is rarely
used alone the way 水 "water" is, always implying a liquid extracted or
pressed from something else.

## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen (no 聲 marker given, so 巴 is presented as
meaning-bearing, not purely phonetic): ⺼ (肉, flesh/body) plus 巴 — Shuowen
does not gloss exactly what 巴 contributes beyond appearing alongside 肉,
so the "从肉从巴" pairing is treated here as an attested but not fully
explained ideographic compound, hence medium rather than high confidence.

"Having much flesh" is the base sense — fat, plump — extended to rich soil
(肥料, fertilizer; 肥沃, fertile) via the same idea of abundance.

## Mnemonic
A body 肉 grown round as a coiled shape 巴: "肥," fat, plump — or richly
fertile.

## Notes
肥料 (fertilizer) and 减肥 (to lose weight, lit. "reduce fat") show the two
main directions 肥 branches into: agricultural abundance, and the human
body.

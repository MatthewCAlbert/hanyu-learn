## Etymology
Ideographic. 冃 (an ancient pictograph of a hat) placed over 目 (eye) —
Shuowen's gloss, 蒙而前也, "covered, yet advancing," pictures someone
pushing forward despite their eyes being obscured: reckless advance in
the face of danger. 冒's very first, most literal sense was simply "a
hat," before the "reckless advance" sense took over.

Confidence is medium because the modern top component has converged in
shape with the unrelated 日 "sun" — a graphic coincidence, not a second
appearance of "sun" in the story.

## Mnemonic
A hat pulled low 日 over the eyes 目, pushing forward blind: to brave it,
to risk it.

## Notes
冒 covers both "to brave, to risk" (冒险, 冒犯) and "to emit, to give off"
(冒烟, 冒汗) — something pushing its way out despite an obstruction, the
same underlying image either way.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 親 *qīn*. Simplification replaced it with the much simpler
寸 *cùn*. The original sense is "an inner garment, a lining" — something
worn close against the body, beneath an outer layer — the direct
ancestor of "to set off, to contrast" (something underneath that shapes
how the outer layer looks).

## Mnemonic
Cloth 衤, measured out 寸 precisely to fit close beneath the outer layer:
a lining.

## Notes
衬 splits into the concrete "underlining" (衬衫 "shirt," 衬衣
"undergarment") and the figurative "to set off by contrast" (衬托) — an
inner layer shaping how the outer one is perceived.

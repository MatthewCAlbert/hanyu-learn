## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (money) supplies the meaning and 兼 *jiān* supplies the sound (*jiān* → *zhuàn/zuàn*). A late graph, in the additions to Shuowen, with an early sense of overpaying or of profiting at someone's expense. "To earn, to make a profit" is the sense that survived.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 doubled 兼: profit.

## Notes
兼 is sound only. 赚钱 "make money", 赚取 "earn", 赚头 "profit margin", 赚钱不易. Series: 赚, 嫌, 谦, 廉. Two readings: *zhuàn* is standard, *zuàn* is regional.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 阝 (terrain, the "mound/hill" form of 阜) supplies the
meaning; 佥 (simplified 僉) *qiān* supplies the sound — a looser tonal
match than most pairs in this dataset, hence medium confidence. The
original sense, 阻，難也, is "obstructed, difficult [terrain]" — ground so
uneven it is hard to cross, the direct ancestor of "dangerous."

## Mnemonic
A mountain slope 阝, treacherous for everyone 佥 who tries to cross it:
danger.

## Notes
"Difficult, obstructed terrain" generalised fully into "danger" of any
kind — 危险, 保险 (literally "guaranteeing against danger," i.e.
insurance), 冒险 "to take a risk."

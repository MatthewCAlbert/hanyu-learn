## Etymology
An associative compound: 八 "to split" over 牛 "ox." Shuowen: 物中分也 "to
divide a thing down the middle" — the ox being chosen "because it is a large
thing that can be divided." Meaning: half.

## Mnemonic
An ox 牛 split 八 in two: half.

## Notes
半 goes directly before a time unit (半年, 半天, 半小时) but needs 个 before a
countable noun (半个苹果). 一半 "a half" (of a whole); 半夜 "midnight."

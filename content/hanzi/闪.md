## Etymology
Ideographic, and a character not found in Shuowen: 门 (door, gate) with 人
(a person) glimpsed inside it — someone flashing past a gap in a doorway,
there and gone in an instant. That vivid, brief glimpse became the general
word for "a flash" (闪电, lightning) and, verbally, "to dodge, to duck out
of the way" (闪开, 躲闪) — the same quick, evasive motion.

## Mnemonic
A person 人 flashing past a gate 门, gone before you can look twice: "闪,"
a flash — or to dodge.

## Notes
闪电 (lightning, lit. "flash-electricity") and 闪光 (a flash of light) both
build directly on the "brief flash" sense; 闪开 and 躲闪 build on the
"dodge" sense instead.

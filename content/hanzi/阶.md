## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (a mound — the natural rise a staircase climbs, also
seen in 阵, 限) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 陛, means
"steps, a stairway" — and the phonetic (皆 in the traditional form,
represented by 介 in the modern simplified shape, a close sound match)
supplies the sound.

"Physical steps" generalizes to "a stage, a level, a rank" (阶段, covered
elsewhere in this app; 阶级, "class/rank") — a staircase's discrete steps
as a natural metaphor for discrete levels of anything.

## Mnemonic
A mound 阝 cut into steps, each one marking 介 (to lie between) the level
before and after: "阶," a step.

## Notes
阶段 (stage, covered elsewhere in this app) and 台阶 ("a flight of steps,"
pairing 阶 with 台 "platform") both build on this character's core
"discrete steps, physical or figurative" sense.

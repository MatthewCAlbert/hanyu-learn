## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 尊 directly as 酒器也, "a wine vessel," and
explains the graph as 酋 (a mature, fermented wine jar) held up by 寸 (a
hand), offered with both care and ceremony. "To honour, to venerate" grew
from the reverence shown in that ritual act of presenting wine.

## Mnemonic
A wine jar 酋 lifted with careful hands 寸 — offered with respect.

## Notes
尊 anchors 尊敬 "to respect," 尊重 "to respect/value," and the classifier
use for statues and vessels (一尊佛像 "a Buddha statue") — a direct
continuation of its original life as a name for a ceremonial vessel.

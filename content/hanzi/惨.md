## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning — cruelty, distress — and 參 (simplified 参) *cān* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as cruel, bitter. The sound is *cǎn*.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 mixed in 参 to a tragedy: miserable.

## Notes
参 is sound only; 参 itself has readings *cān*, *shēn*, *cēn*. 悲惨, 惨痛, 惨白 "pale", 惨重 "heavy (losses)".

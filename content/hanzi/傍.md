## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning and 旁 *páng* supplies the sound (*páng* → *bàng*). Shuowen glosses it as near, at the side. "To lean on" and "to depend on" follow.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 sticking to the side 旁 of another.

## Notes
旁 is sound only (though it also means "side"). 傍晚 "toward evening", 依傍 "lean on", 傍边 "beside", 傍大款 "cling to the rich".

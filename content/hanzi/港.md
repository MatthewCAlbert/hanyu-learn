## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning and 巷 *xiàng* supplies the sound (*xiàng* → *gǎng*). A late graph: it meant a branch channel off a river before it became the word for a harbour or port. The sound relation is loose.

## Mnemonic
A water 氵 lane 巷 where boats come in: a harbour.

## Notes
巷 is sound only. 港口 "harbour", 香港 "Hong Kong" (Fragrant Harbour), 港湾 "harbour", 港币. 港 can also abbreviate Hong Kong (港人, 港片).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, but the semantic side is a fossil rather than a clean
component. The oracle-bone form shows 肉 (meat) held by 又 (a hand) beside a
table-like shape that supplies the sound — the whole picture is "presenting
meat," an offering. By bronze script the hand doubled and the table shape
settled into 爿 *qiáng/pán*, later regularised as the phonetic 丬.

In the modern character, the meat 肉 and hand 又 have eroded into 夕 and 寸 —
shapes that no longer look like meat or a hand at all, so the app cannot
declare a semantic component here without pointing at pieces that no longer
carry that meaning visibly. Only the phonetic 丬 is still a real, checkable
piece of the character.

## Mnemonic
A general 丬 at evening 夕, measuring 寸 out his troops: "about to" advance.

## Notes
Confidence is medium because the "meat offering" reading is a scholarly
reconstruction from oracle-bone and bronze forms, not something visible in
the modern shape. The grammatical senses ("will," "about to," and the

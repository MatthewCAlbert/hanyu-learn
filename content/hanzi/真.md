## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen's gloss — "an immortal transformed, ascending
to heaven" — is a Daoist reading of the later shape, not the origin. The
bronze graphs are variously analysed: a ladle 匕 over a cauldron 鼎 (a vessel
filled with real substance), or 貞 *zhēn* as a phonetic. No account is settled.

The meaning is firm: "true, real, genuine" — the opposite of 假.

## Mnemonic
A vessel filled right to the brim with the real thing: true.

## Notes
真 also writes the phonetic in 镇 / 鎮 *zhèn*, 慎 *shèn*, 填 *tián*. Adverbial
真 "really" needs no 的 before an adjective (真好); predicative 真的.

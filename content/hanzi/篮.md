## Etymology
Simplified from 籃. ⺮ "bamboo" gives the meaning; 监 (監) *jiān* gives the
sound (for *lán*). Shuowen: 大篝也 "a large basket."

## Mnemonic
A woven bamboo ⺮ container: a basket.

## Notes
The 监 phonetic also writes 蓝 / 藍 *lán*, 滥 / 濫 *làn*, 鉴 / 鑒 *jiàn*, 槛 /
檻 *jiàn*. 篮球 "basketball" (named for the peach-basket goal); 摇篮 "cradle."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 奂 *huàn* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 易也 "to exchange, to swap."

## Mnemonic
One hand 扌 giving, taking back something else: to swap.

## Notes
The 奂 phonetic also writes 唤 / 喚 *huàn* (to call), 涣 / 渙 *huàn*, 焕 / 煥
*huàn*. Senses: exchange (交换, 换钱), replace (换衣服, 更换), change / switch
(换车, 换工作).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 青 *qīng* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 謁也 "to seek an audience, to make a request in words."

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 offered up in request.

## Notes
The 青 phonetic also writes 清 *qīng*, 情 *qíng*, 晴 *qíng*, 精 *jīng*, 睛
*jīng*. Senses: please (请坐), invite / treat (请客), ask (请问), request
leave (请假).

## Etymology
日 "sun" above an element Shuowen reads as 甲 "the top / first" — the sun just
cleared above something, i.e. dawn. The lower part is now written 十, which is
neither "ten" nor meaningful; it is a worn-down 甲 (or 屮 "a sprout"). Meaning:
early, morning.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 just up over the treetops: early.

## Notes
早上 / 早晨 "morning," 早饭 "breakfast," 早就 "long ago," 早点儿 "hurry up a
bit." Greeting: 早！

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 戈 (a weapon, spear-axe) supplies the meaning — to cut off — and 雀 *què* (a small bird, written 隹 with 十 on top in the modern form) supplies the sound. The common story of "a bird cut off from its nest by a weapon" is a reading of the modern shape, not an etymology. The phonetic is not declared here since 雀 has been broken apart in the graph.

## Mnemonic
A weapon 戈 slicing across a sparrow's 隹 path: cut short.

## Notes
截止 "cut off (a deadline)", 截至 "up to", 截然 "sharply", 截图 "screenshot", 拦截 "intercept". Not the same 隹 as the "short-tailed bird" role in 焦.

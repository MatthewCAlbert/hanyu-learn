## Etymology
Simplified from 養. The full graph is 食 "food" with 羊 *yáng* for the sound
(for *yǎng*). Shuowen: 供養也 "to provide for, to nourish." The simplified
form keeps 羊 on top and shrinks 食 to a few strokes below.

## Mnemonic
Putting food on the table for the household: to provide.

## Notes
Senses: raise / keep (养鸡, 养花, 养家), bring up (养育, 抚养, 养父 foster
father), form / cultivate (养成习惯, 培养), rest and recover (养病, 养伤,
休养), maintain (养路, 保养). 营养 "nutrition"; 养老 "to provide for the
aged."

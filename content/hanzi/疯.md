## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 瘋: 疒 (sickness — the "bed with a person leaning
against it" radical seen in 病, 疼, 痛) supplies the meaning, 风 *fēng* (wind)
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Traditional Chinese medicine held
that many nervous and mental ailments were caused by wind entering the body,
so 风 here is not purely arbitrary sound — it likely also carries a trace of
that older medical theory, though its job in the character is still
classified as phonetic rather than semantic.

## Mnemonic
An illness 疒 that blows in like the wind 风 and scrambles your thinking.

## Notes
疯 gave rise to a large colloquial family: 疯子 "madman," 疯狂 "crazy/frenzied,"
发疯 "to go mad," 疯了 "to have lost it" — nearly all of them intensifiers
rather than clinical terms in everyday speech.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in 殘: 歹 (bone, death, harm) supplies the meaning, 戔 (simplified 戋) *jiān* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss is "to injure, to destroy"; "remaining, leftover" and "cruel" both follow from damage.

## Mnemonic
A bit of bone 歹 spears 戋 left over — what remains.

## Notes
戋 is sound only here; its series shows 浅 *qiǎn*, 钱 *qián*, 线 *xiàn*, 贱 *jiàn*, 栈 *zhàn*. 残疾 "disability", 残酷 "cruel", 残留 "remain".

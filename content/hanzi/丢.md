## Etymology
Not securely attested. 丢 does not appear in Shuowen; it is a later colloquial
character. The popular reading of its top as 王/丰 and bottom as 厶 "private,
personal" — as if depicting a treasured object slipping from a private
holding — is a modern mnemonic rather than a sourced derivation, so it is not
presented here as fact.

## Mnemonic
Something precious 王 slips out of a cupped, private hand 厶 — gone.

## Notes
丢 covers both "to lose" (丢了钥匙 "lost the keys") and "to discard" (丢垃圾
"throw out the trash"), plus the figurative 丢脸 "to lose face." Distinguish
from 掉 diào, which overlaps in "to drop/lose" but leans more on the physical
falling-away sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning, 隹 *zhuī* (short-tailed bird)
supplies only the sound. Shuowen's original sense 諮也 is close to "yes, I
hear you" — a spoken assent. The modern restrictive sense "only, alone" is a
later grammaticalisation, common enough that it now dominates over the
original "yes" meaning in standard Mandarin.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 with a single bird's 隹 chirp of agreement — "yes, only that."

## Notes
唯一 "the only one," 唯独 "only, alone" are the everyday forms. As phonetic
隹 also appears in 谁 shéi/shuí (who) and 推 tuī (to push) — three different
finals from one phonetic, a reminder that 隹 gives only a rough sound clue,
not an exact reading.

## Etymology
Traditional 遷 is pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) for meaning, a complex element for sound. Simplification replaced the sound element with 千 *qiān*, which happens to match the reading, so the new phonetic works as a sound clue. The sense is to go up, to move away, to relocate.

## Mnemonic
Walking 辶 a thousand 千 miles to a new home.

## Notes
千 supplies sound only in this simplified form. 搬迁, 变迁, 拆迁 "demolish and relocate", 迁就 "to accommodate".

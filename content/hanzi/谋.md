## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning — to deliberate, plan — and 某 *mǒu* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as thinking through a difficulty.

## Mnemonic
Talking 讠 about "so-and-so" 某 behind their back — a scheme.

## Notes
某 is sound only. Series: 媒 *méi*, 煤 *méi*, 谋 *móu*. 阴谋, 参谋, 谋生 "make a living", 谋求.

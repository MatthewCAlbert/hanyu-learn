## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 心 (heart) supplies the meaning, 非 *fēi* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 痛也 "pain, anguish" matches the
modern "sorrow, grief" directly.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 aching at what is wrong 非 in the world.

## Notes
悲 anchors 悲伤 "sorrowful," 悲剧 "tragedy," 悲观 "pessimistic" (literally
"seeing sorrowfully") — its antonym 喜 pairs with it in the classical
四字 phrase 悲喜交加 "a mix of joy and sorrow."

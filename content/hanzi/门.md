## Etymology
Simplified from 門 — a pictograph of a double-leaf gate, two 戶 "door-panels"
side by side, often with a lintel across the top. Shuowen glosses it by a
sound-play, 聞也, but the graph is plainly the gate itself.

## Mnemonic
Two door-leaves hung side by side: a gate.

## Notes
门 is Kangxi radical 169 and the frame of 问, 间, 闻, 闹, 闭. Senses: door /
gate (大门, 门口), a family or school (名门, 一门课), and a measure word for
courses and artillery (三门课, 一门炮).

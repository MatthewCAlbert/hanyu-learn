## Etymology
The mirror image of 即: where 即 shows a kneeling figure approaching a food
vessel, 既 shows a figure (旡, drawn with the head turned back) already
turning *away* from it — someone finished eating. "Already, having
completed" is the base sense.

That "already done" idea generalizes to the conjunction use — 既...又...
("both... and...") frames two things as jointly, already-simultaneously
true. Shuowen marks 旡 as the phonetic (从旡聲), so this character may in
fact be pictophonetic rather than purely ideographic; medium confidence
reflects that the vessel component on the left is not cleanly recoverable
in the modern shape (as with its pair 即).

## Mnemonic
A figure turning its head away 旡 from the empty table: "既," already done.

## Notes
既然 ("since, given that") and 既...又... ("both...and...") are the two
grammatical constructions that carry 既's everyday use; the plain "already"
sense (as opposed to 已经) survives mostly in formal writing.

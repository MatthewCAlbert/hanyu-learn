## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 也 *yě* supplies the
sound, shifted to *chí*. Shuowen's gloss 陂也 "a pool, a reservoir" matches
the modern "pool, pond" directly.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 gathered and held, also 也 useful for keeping fish or for
defence.

## Notes
池 anchors 游泳池 "swimming pool," 池塘 "pond," and historically 护城池
"moat" (a defensive water-filled ditch) — always still, contained water
rather than a flowing river.

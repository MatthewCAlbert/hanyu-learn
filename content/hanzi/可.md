## Etymology
口 "mouth" with 丂 (breath forced out) — Shuowen: 肯也 "to assent." An
approving sound. From "to approve, to permit" grew "can, may, -able" (可以,
可能, 认可), and, as an emphatic / adversative, "but, yet, really" (可是,
这可怎么办).

## Mnemonic
An "aye" 丂 from the mouth 口: it may be done.

## Notes
可 + verb makes adjectives: 可爱, 可怕, 可惜, 可靠. Two readings: *kě* (common),
*kè* only in 可汗 "khan." The emphatic 可 (可好了 "it's really nice") is
purely a tone-of-voice marker.

## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 扇 as 扉也, "a door-leaf," and analyses the
graph as 户 (a single door/door-leaf) with 翄 (wings) — a door swings on its
hinge the way a wing beats. That shared "swinging/flapping" motion is why
the same character later named the hand fan (扇子), and why it also serves
as the classifier for doors and windows (一扇门, 一扇窗).

## Mnemonic
A door 户 that flaps open and shut like a bird's wing 羽.

## Notes
Two readings: *shàn* is the everyday noun (扇子 "fan," 电风扇 "electric fan")
and classifier; *shān* is the verb "to fan" (扇风 "to fan air," 扇耳光 "to
slap" — a swift flapping motion of the hand).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 女 (woman) supplies the meaning and 圭 *guī* supplies the sound (*guī* → *wá*). Shuowen reports 娃 as a Wu-Chu regional word for "pretty" (a pretty girl), and in northern dialects it came to mean "baby, child". The 圭 jade tablet is not a pictorial element.

## Mnemonic
A girl 女 treasured like a jade tablet 圭.

## Notes
圭 is sound only. 娃娃 "baby, doll", 洋娃娃 "doll", 娃哈哈 (brand). Series: 哇 *wā*, 蛙 *wā*, 洼 *wā*, 娃 *wá*.

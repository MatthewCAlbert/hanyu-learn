## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 页 (head — the same component seen in 头, 顶) supplies the
meaning; 屯 *tún* supplies the sound. The original sense, 下首也 "to lower
the head," describes a kowtow: striking the forehead to the ground as a
gesture of deep respect.

## Mnemonic
A head 页 stopped 屯 mid-motion, bowed down: a pause, a beat.

## Notes
"To bring the head sharply down" generalised into "to stop, to pause"
(停顿) and into a classifier for a single, complete bout of something —
一顿饭 "a meal," 一顿打 "a beating" — one clean stop-start unit.

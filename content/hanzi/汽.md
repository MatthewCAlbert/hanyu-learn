## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 气 *qì* gives the sound — and,
since 气 itself means "vapour, air," also the sense. Water turned to vapour:
steam. (Shuowen's older gloss, 水涸也 "water drying up," is a related earlier
use.)

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 becoming air 气: steam.

## Notes
气 "air / breath / qi" is Kangxi radical 84 and the phonetic in 氣 (its own
full form). Senses of 汽: steam (汽车 "steam car," now just "car"), 汽水
"fizzy drink," 汽油 "petrol."

## Etymology
Simplified from 禮. 礻 (示) "altar, the spirits" gives the meaning — Shuowen:
所以事神致福也 "the means of serving the spirits and drawing down blessing."
The phonetic 豊 (a ritual vessel heaped with offerings) is reduced to a bare
乚 in the simplified form.

## Mnemonic
What is done at the altar 礻 to set things right with the spirits: rite.

## Notes
Senses: rite / ceremony (礼仪, 婚礼, 葬礼, 典礼), propriety and manners
(礼貌, 礼节, 有礼, 礼让), and a gift (礼物, 送礼, 礼品). 礼拜 "worship / a
week"; 礼堂 "auditorium." Surname *Lǐ*.

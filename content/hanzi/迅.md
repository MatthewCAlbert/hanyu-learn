## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
is simply "swift" — and 卂 *xùn* (itself an old pictograph of an insect
or bird darting quickly) supplies the sound, reinforcing the sense of
rapid motion.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 as fast as a darting insect 卂: "迅," swift.

## Notes
迅速 ("rapid, swift," pairing 迅 with the near-synonym 速) is the standard
modern compound — 迅 rarely stands entirely alone outside it.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. The left 阝 is 阜 "a mound / an earthen wall," giving the
meaning; 完 *wán* gives the sound (for *yuàn*). Shuowen: 一曰周垣也 "a wall
running all around" — a walled yard. From "courtyard" grew "an institution
housed in a walled compound" (医院, 学院, 法院).

## Mnemonic
A wall 阝 run right round 完 a space: a courtyard.

## Notes
Left-side 阝 is 阜 "mound" (阴, 阳, 陈, 除); right-side 阝 is 邑 "city" (那,
都, 邮). The 完 phonetic also writes 玩 *wán*, 远 / 遠 *yuǎn*.

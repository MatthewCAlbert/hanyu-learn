## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth/land) supplies the meaning, 或 *huò* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. 域 is essentially a clarified variant of 或
itself — 或 originally meant "a state, a bounded territory" (a shape
showing a settlement 囗 defended by a weapon 戈) before it was borrowed for
the grammatical sense "or, perhaps"; 域 adds 土 to keep the "territory"
meaning unambiguous once 或 drifted away from it.

## Mnemonic
Land 土 marked out, a boundary that 或 once meant on its own before it
wandered off to mean "or."

## Notes
域 anchors 区域 "region, area," 领域 "field, domain [of knowledge/activity],"
and computing's 域名 "domain name" — a striking case of a very old
territorial word finding new work in internet infrastructure.

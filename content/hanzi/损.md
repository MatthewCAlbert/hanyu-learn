## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 損: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 员
(traditional 員) supplies the sound, shifted to *sǔn*. Shuowen's gloss 減也
"to reduce, to diminish" matches the modern sense directly — a hand-caused
decrease, extended to damage and harm generally.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 chipping away at a member's 员 share, bit by bit.

## Notes
损 covers loss and damage across registers: 损失 "loss," 损坏 "to damage,"
and the colder, sarcastic sense 损人 "to mock/deride someone" — cutting
someone down with words the way 损 cuts down a quantity.

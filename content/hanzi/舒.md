## Etymology
舍 "to set down, to let go" beside 予 "to hand over" — Shuowen: 伸也 "to
stretch out, to unfold," noting 予 *yǔ* as also carrying the sound. From "to
extend / let out" grew "to relax, to be at ease" (舒服, 舒适, 舒展).

## Mnemonic
Setting down 舍 the load and letting the limbs out 予: relaxed.

## Notes
The 予 phonetic also writes 抒 *shū* (to express), 序 *xù* (order). Also the
surname Shū. 舒 rarely stands alone — see the word entry 舒服.

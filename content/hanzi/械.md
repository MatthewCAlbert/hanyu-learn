## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — wooden restraints, shackles — and 戒 *jiè* supplies the sound (*jiè* → *xiè*). Shuowen glosses it as stocks and shackles. The sense widened to "implements, apparatus" and then "weapon".

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 tool built to guard 戒 against escape.

## Notes
戒 is sound only. 机械 "machinery", 器械 "apparatus", 械斗 "armed brawl", 军械 "ordnance".

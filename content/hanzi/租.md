## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 禾 "grain" gives the meaning; 且 *qiě* gives the sound (for
*zū*). Shuowen: 田賦也 "the levy on farmland," paid in grain.

## Mnemonic
The grain 禾 handed over each season for the use of the land: rent.

## Notes
The 且 phonetic also writes 组 / 組 *zǔ*, 祖 *zǔ*, 助 *zhù*, 阻 *zǔ*,
诅 / 詛 *zǔ*. Senses: rent / hire (租房, 出租, 租车), lease out (把房子租给
别人), and rent or land tax as a charge (房租, 地租, 租金). 出租车 "taxi."

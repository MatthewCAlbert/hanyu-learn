## Etymology
Simplified from 紙. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "silk / fibre" gives the meaning —
the earliest paper was a mat of pounded silk and plant fibre; 氏 *shì* gives
the sound (for *zhǐ*).

## Mnemonic
Fibres 纟 beaten flat and dried into a sheet: paper.

## Notes
Senses: paper (白纸, 报纸, 卫生纸, 纸巾), and a classifier for documents in
set phrases (一纸空文, 一纸命令). 纸张 "paper (as a material)"; 纸币
"banknote."

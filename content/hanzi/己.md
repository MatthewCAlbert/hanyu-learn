## Etymology
Not securely attested. The oracle-bone graph is a bent or coiling line —
often read as a length of cord, or the warp-thread of a loom (making it a
source of 纪 / 紀 "to order threads → a record"). It was borrowed for "self /
oneself" and for the sixth heavenly stem; the picture and the meanings have no
clear link.

## Mnemonic
A curled thread, borrowed to point back at oneself.

## Notes
Distinguish 己 *jǐ* "self" from 已 *yǐ* "already" (the top gap closes further)
and 巳 *sì* "the sixth branch" (the top is fully closed). 自己, 知己,
利己主义.

## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 憑/凴: a person leaning on 任/冯-type support over
几 (a low table/armrest). The core image is physically leaning on something
for support, which extends naturally to "to rely on" and "on the basis of" —
relying on evidence the way one leans on a table.

## Mnemonic
Leaning your weight 任 on a small table 几 — relying on it to hold you up.

## Notes
凭 is common in the fixed pattern 凭 X 也 / 无论 X, meaning "no matter what" —
凭你怎么说 "no matter what you say" — an extension of "based on/regardless of
the grounds given." Also the everyday noun in 凭证 "proof, voucher."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 "heart" gives the meaning; 咸 *xián* gives the sound (for
*gǎn*). Shuowen: 動人心也 "to move a person's heart."

## Mnemonic
Something that stirs 咸 the heart 心: to feel it.

## Notes
The 咸 phonetic also writes 减 / 減 *jiǎn*, 喊 *hǎn*, 憾 *hàn*, 撼 *hàn*,
咸 itself *xián*. Senses: feel / sense (感觉, 感到, 感冒 catch cold), be
moved (感动, 感激), a sense (责任感, 幽默感, 手感), gratitude (感谢, 感恩).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 阝 (mound/terrain, the "earthworks" form of 阜) supplies
the meaning; 余 *yú* supplies the sound. The original sense, 殿陛也, is
"palace steps" — climbing a staircase means leaving one level behind for
the next, which is the root of the modern "to remove, to get rid of."

## Mnemonic
Climbing the palace steps 阝, leaving the one below 余 behind: to remove,
to move past.

## Notes
"Stepping past a level" extends into both "to remove, eliminate" (除了,
消除) and the mathematical "to divide" — dividing being, in a sense,
removing a quantity a fixed number of times.

## Etymology
Traditional 賓: 宀 (roof) over a component built on 貝 (shell/valuables) —
a guest arriving at one's home bearing a gift, "the one who is honoured
[with valuables offered in welcome]." Simplification replaced the visible
貝 with 兵, so the modern 宾's lower half is a graphic stand-in and should
not be read as "soldier," despite resembling that character — a folk
reading with no source behind it.

## Mnemonic
A guest arriving under your roof 宀, bringing something valued in exchange
for your welcome.

## Notes
宾 anchors 宾馆 "hotel" (literally "guest hall"), 来宾 "visiting guest,"
贵宾 "VIP guest" (literally "honoured guest"). 主宾 "host and guest" is the
classic pairing this character belongs to.

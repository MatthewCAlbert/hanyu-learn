## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 日 (sun, light) supplies the meaning — the ring of haze around the sun or moon (a halo) — and 軍 (simplified 军) *jūn* supplies the sound. "Dizzy" is the later sense: spinning, ringed vision.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 swimming in circles around the army 军 — dizzy.

## Notes
军 is sound only. Two readings: *yūn* (头晕, 晕倒) and *yùn* (晕车 "car sick", 晕船). Same sound part: 运 *yùn*, 军 *jūn*, 挥 *huī*, 辉 *huī*.

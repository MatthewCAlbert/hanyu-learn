## Etymology
Simplified from 貴. 贝 (貝) "cowrie shell, money" gives the meaning — it is a
word about worth and price; the top element carried the sound. Shuowen:
物不賤也 "a thing that is not cheap." From "costly" grew "precious, valued,"
"of high rank / noble" (贵族), and the polite "your" (贵姓, 贵国).

## Mnemonic
Something that costs a lot of shells 贝: expensive; prized.

## Notes
贝 "money" also heads 买, 卖, 财, 账, 费, 贷. Opposite 贱 / 賤 "cheap; lowly."
太贵了 "too expensive"; 名贵 "rare and valuable."

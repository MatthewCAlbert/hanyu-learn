## Etymology
Simplified from 歡. The old form is 欠 "a person exhaling, mouth agape" — the
element for words about breath, desire and voice — with 雚 *guàn* for sound.
Shuowen: 喜樂也 "joy, delight."

Simplification swaps 雚 for 又, a bare shape-sign with no sound value, so the
modern 欢 keeps only the meaning-bearing 欠.

## Mnemonic
A wide-open mouth 欠 crying out for joy.

## Notes
欠 "to gape / owe" also heads 歌 (song), 歇 (rest), 欲 (desire), 吹 (blow).
The same 又-for-雚 substitution is in 观 / 觀, 劝 / 勸.

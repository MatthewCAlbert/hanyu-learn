## Etymology
Pictophonetic in Shuowen: 寸 (hand, rule) supplies the meaning and 之 *zhī* supplies the sound; the oldest sense is a government office. In the Han dynasty, foreign monks were housed in a government office (the Honglu Si), and 寺 came to mean a Buddhist temple. Modern 土 is the worn-down 之.

## Mnemonic
Clasped hands 寸 on the earth 土 in prayer at the temple.

## Notes
The modern 土 is a distorted 之, not "earth"; 寺 is also a sound part (诗, 持, 特, 时, 等, 待, 侍, 恃 all carry 寺). 寺庙, 清真寺 "mosque", 少林寺, 寺院.

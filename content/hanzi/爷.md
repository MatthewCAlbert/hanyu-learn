## Etymology
Simplified from 爺. The full form is 父 "father" for meaning, with 耶 *yé* for
sound — a colloquial word for one's father recorded from the Six Dynasties on
(《木蘭詩》: 阿爺無大兒).

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** 耶 is reduced to 卩, which
is neither semantic nor a recoverable phonetic. Learn 爷 as sound-opaque: 父
tells you it is a term for a male elder, and nothing tells you it is *yé*.

## Mnemonic
A father-figure 父 — read it off by heart, not off the shape.

## Notes
One of eight HSK 1–2 characters whose phonetic was lost in simplification —
compare 过 (咼), 商 (章), 边 (臱). Senses: grandfather (爷爷), old master
(老爷), a deity (老天爷).

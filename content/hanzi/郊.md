## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (here 邑, settlement) supplies the meaning, 交 *jiāo*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 距國百里為郊 is
precise: "the area 100 *li* from the capital is called 郊" — a specific
administrative ring of countryside just outside the city proper, which
generalised to "suburbs, outskirts" broadly.

## Mnemonic
The ring of land where city and countryside cross 交, just outside the
settlement 阝.

## Notes
郊 anchors 郊区 "suburbs," 近郊 "the near suburbs," and 郊游 "an outing to
the countryside" — always the transitional zone between city and open
country.

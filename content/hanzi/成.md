## Etymology
戊 "a broad-bladed halberd" with 丁 *dīng* for sound. Shuowen: 就也 "to bring
to completion." From "to accomplish" grew "to become, to turn into," "to
succeed / OK" (成不成), and "a tenth" (八成 "80% / most likely").

## Mnemonic
The weapon 戊 laid down, the work done: accomplished.

## Notes
The 丁 phonetic also writes 城 *chéng* (city), 诚 / 誠 *chéng*, 盛 *chéng* /
*shèng*. Senses: complete (完成), become (成为, 变成), a result (成绩, 成果),
adult (成人, 成年), OK (行, 成).

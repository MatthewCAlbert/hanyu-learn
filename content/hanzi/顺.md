## Etymology
Simplified from 順. 页 (頁) "head" gives the meaning; 川 "a flowing stream"
carries the sound (for *shùn*) and its own image of water running downhill.
Shuowen: 理也 "in accord, along the grain."

## Mnemonic
Turning the head 页 to face the way the stream 川 runs: going with it.

## Notes
Senses: follow / go along with (顺从, 顺应, 顺其自然), smooth / favourable
(顺利, 通顺, 一帆风顺), in the direction of / on the way (顺路, 顺手, 顺时针
clockwise), obedient / agreeable (孝顺, 温顺), and to put straight (理顺,
顺一顺头发).

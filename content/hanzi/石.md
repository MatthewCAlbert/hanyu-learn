## Etymology
Ideographic-and-pictographic. 厂 (a cliff overhang) with 口 (a rounded
lump) beneath it — Shuowen's gloss, 山石也, "mountain stone," pictures
exactly this: a boulder or rock lying at the base of a cliff face.

## Mnemonic
A boulder 口, resting where the cliff face 厂 meets the ground: stone.

## Notes
石 as a radical marks minerals and stone-related objects broadly — 码,
破, 硬, 磁 and dozens more — one of the more visually stable radicals,
still recognisably "cliff plus rock" in its modern form.

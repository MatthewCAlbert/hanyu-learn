## Etymology
Simplified from 練. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "silk" gives the meaning; 柬
*jiǎn* gives the sound (for *liàn*). Shuowen: 湅繒也 "to boil raw silk soft
and white." From that repeated working grew "to drill, to practise" and
"seasoned, skilled."

## Mnemonic
Working the raw silk 纟 over and over until it is supple: training.

## Notes
The 柬 phonetic also writes 炼 / 煉 *liàn*, 拣 / 揀 *jiǎn*. Senses: practise /
drill (练习, 训练, 练字, 排练), and experienced / polished (老练, 熟练,
干练, 精练). 教练 "coach"; 练兵 "to train troops."

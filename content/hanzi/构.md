## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 構: 木 (wood) supplied the meaning
— building with timber — and 冓 *gòu* supplied the sound, an exact
match.

**Simplification replaced 冓 with the visually similar 勾** (*gōu*), the
same shape-substitution pattern seen in 购 (covered elsewhere in this
app) — close in sound but not an inherited phonetic.

## Mnemonic
Timber 木 hooked 勾 together, piece by piece, into a frame: "构," to
construct.

## Notes
构成 ("to constitute, to make up") and 机构 (institution, covered
elsewhere in this app) both build on this character's core "to build up
a structure" sense.

## Etymology
爫 "a hand reaching down" and 又 "a hand reaching up," with 冖 (originally 舟)
"a thing" between them — one hand passing something, the other taking it.
Shuowen: 相付也 "to hand over to each other." The graph did double duty for
"give" and "receive"; 授 (扌 added) was later coined for "give," leaving 受
with "receive, accept, bear, suffer."

## Mnemonic
One hand 爫 lowering a thing, another 又 taking it: to receive.

## Notes
Senses: receive / accept (接受, 受到), bear / undergo (受苦, 受伤, 忍受),
be popular / welcome (受欢迎). 难受 "hard to bear."

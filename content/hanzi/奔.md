## Etymology
Shuowen reads 奔 as 夭 (a person with swinging arms) with an abbreviated 賁 for the sound. Bronze forms show the same person above three feet 止, a vivid "running" picture; the three feet were later garbled into 卉, so the field-of-grass image is a misreading. The sound and meaning of the modern graph fit the early form, but the Shuowen sound reading and the bronze "three feet" picture are two different explanations of the lower part.

## Mnemonic
A person 大 sprinting through the grass 卉 — as fast as three feet!

## Notes
Two readings: *bēn* (奔跑, 奔波, 奔走) and *bèn* "to head for" (投奔, 奔向). Related: 贲 *bēn*, 喷, 愤.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree) supplies the meaning — the plum or apricot tree — and 每 *měi* supplies the sound (*měi* → *méi*). The tree is classically a symbol of winter fortitude, but that is a cultural association and not part of the graph.

## Mnemonic
Every 每 spring the tree 木 blossoms: plum.

## Notes
每 is sound only. 梅花 "plum blossom", 梅雨 "plum rains (the early summer rainy season)", 青梅, 杨梅 "bayberry", 梅 also surname.

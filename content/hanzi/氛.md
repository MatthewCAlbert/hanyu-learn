## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 气 (vapour, air) supplies the meaning — mist, vapours — and 分 *fēn* supplies the sound. Shuowen lists it as an auspicious vapour; "atmosphere, mood" follows.

## Mnemonic
Air 气 divided 分 into currents of mood.

## Notes
分 is sound only. 气氛 "atmosphere", 氛围 "ambience", 战争气氛. 雰 *fēn* is the older variant with 雨.

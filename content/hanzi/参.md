## Etymology
Simplified from 參. The old graph pictures the three bright stars of Orion's
belt (the constellation 參) shining over a kneeling figure. From "three / a
triad" grew "to join with, to take part in, to consult" (*cān*), and — as
separate readings — "ginseng" (*shēn*, from a homophone) and "uneven,
staggered" (*cēn*, 参差).

## Mnemonic
Three stars 厶 over a person 大, sparkling 彡: joining the array.

## Notes
Three readings: *cān* "to participate / consult / refer to" (参加, 参观,
参考), *shēn* "ginseng" (人参), *cēn* "uneven" (参差不齐).

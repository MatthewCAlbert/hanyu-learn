## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning "to rub, to wipe," and 察
*chá* supplies an approximate sound. Like several other late hand-verbs
(躺, 摆), 擦 postdates Shuowen, so its sound history is less firmly
documented than the ancient pictophonetic core of the script; medium
confidence reflects a plausible but not classically-sourced sound pairing.

"To wipe" (擦桌子) and "to graze/brush past" (擦过) are both direct readings
of a hand or surface moving lightly against another.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 inspecting 察 a surface closely — and wiping it clean while it's
at it: "擦."

## Notes
擦 also names the friction sound/sensation itself, as in 摩擦 (friction) —
worth learning alongside this entry since both describe surfaces moving
against each other.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 擺: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning —
"to move with the hand, to set out, to swing" — and 罷 *bà* supplied an
approximate sound (*bǎi* and *bà* are not a close phonetic match by modern
readings, though the pairing is old enough that the original Old Chinese
sounds may have been closer; not confirmed here). Simplification carried
罷→罢 through unchanged, so the same loose sound relationship holds in the
modern form.

The senses split along a physical/social line: "to lay out, to arrange" (摆
好, 摆放) and "to swing, to sway" (摆动, 钟摆 "pendulum") are both hand/motion
senses; "to show off, to put on airs" (摆架子) extends "to lay out for
display" figuratively.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 setting things out, then giving up 罢 and just letting them swing
loose: to "摆," arrange — or sway.

## Notes
钟摆 (pendulum) and 摆动 (to swing/oscillate) both foreground the "swaying"
sense; 摆放/摆设 (to arrange, to display) foreground "to lay out" instead —
worth learning as two related but distinct clusters.

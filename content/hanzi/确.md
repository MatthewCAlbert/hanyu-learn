## Etymology
Simplified from 確. 石 "stone" gives the meaning — hard as rock, and so
unyielding, sure; the traditional phonetic 隺 *hè* is written 角 in the
simplified form. Shuowen: 堅也 "firm, solid." From "rock-solid" grew "certain,
definite, true."

## Mnemonic
As settled as stone 石: certain.

## Notes
Senses: firm / solid (确实, 确定), accurate (准确, 明确), definitely (的确,
确信). 正确 "correct."

## Etymology
Associative: 爪 "a hand" (on the left) pressing down 卩 "a kneeling figure /
a seal." Shuowen: 執政所持信也 "the token of authority an official holds."
From pressing a seal grew "to print" and "a mark left by pressure."

## Mnemonic
A hand 爪 bearing down on the seal 卩: to stamp.

## Notes
Senses: a seal / chop (印章, 盖印, 公印), a mark or imprint (脚印, 手印,
烙印, 印象 impression), to print (印刷, 复印, 排印), and to tally / bear out
(印证, 心心相印). 印度 "India" (sound transcription); surname *Yìn*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 塞,
means "to stop up, to fill in a gap" — and 真 *zhēn* supplies the sound.

"To fill in (with earth)" generalizes to "to fill in" any blank —
填空, "to fill in the blank"; 填表, "to fill out a form."

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 packed in to make something whole and true 真: to "填," fill in.

## Notes
填空 (fill-in-the-blank) and 填表 (to fill out a form) are the two most
common everyday compounds, both extending "filling a gap with earth" to
filling a gap with information.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning, 交 *jiāo* supplies the sound,
shifted to *yǎo*. Not in Shuowen, but the structure is uncontested and the
sense "to bite" has been stable since the character's earliest attestations.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 clamping down where two things cross 交 — a bite.

## Notes
咬 covers both animal bites (狗咬人 "the dog bit someone") and the
figurative 咬牙 "to grit one's teeth" and 咬文嚼字 "to nitpick over wording"
(literally "biting at words, chewing over characters").

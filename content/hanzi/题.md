## Etymology
Simplified from 題. Pictophonetic: 页 (頁) "head" gives the meaning — Shuowen:
額也 "the forehead"; 是 *shì* gives the sound (for *tí*). From "the front /
top" grew "a heading, a title, a subject."

## Mnemonic
The forehead 页 of a text — the words at its top: the title.

## Notes
The 是 phonetic also writes 提 *tí*, 堤 *dī*, 匙 *chí*. Senses: a title /
heading (标题, 题目, 命题), a topic or subject (话题, 主题, 离题), a
question or exercise (问题, 考题, 难题, 答题), and to inscribe (题字, 题词,
题名).

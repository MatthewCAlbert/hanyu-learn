## Etymology
Simplified from 賣. The full graph is 出 "to put out" over 買 "to buy" —
putting goods out for buyers to take. Shuowen: 衒也 "to hawk, to cry wares."
Simplification writes 出 as 十 and 買 as its own simplified form 买, so 卖
sits right next to 买 with just one extra stroke.

## Mnemonic
Goods put out 出 for the buyer 买: to sell.

## Notes
买 / 卖 are a near-identical antonym pair — mind the top stroke. Senses: sell
(卖东西, 出卖), show off (卖弄, 卖乖), give one's all (卖力). 外卖
"takeaway."

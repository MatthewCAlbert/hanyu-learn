## Etymology
The tally of strokes stops after 四 (whose tally 亖 is attested); 五 used the
crossing-strokes graph from the oracle-bone period. The graph is two
horizontal lines (heaven and earth) with an X crossing between them — Shuowen
reads the X as yin and yang meeting. It was adopted for the number by phonetic
borrowing; it is not a picture of five things.

## Mnemonic
Two lines with a cross between: the borrowed sign for five.

## Notes
The tally series ends at 四 (亖); 五 had the crossing graph from the start, and
六 onward were distinct graphs. 五 also in 五行 "the Five Phases," 五官 "the
features of the face."

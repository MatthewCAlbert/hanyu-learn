## Etymology
Like 四, "five" abandoned the tally of five strokes early on. The replacement
graph is two horizontal lines (heaven and earth) with an X crossing between
them — Shuowen reads the X as yin and yang meeting. It was adopted for the
number by phonetic borrowing; it is not a picture of five things.

## Mnemonic
Two lines with a cross between: the borrowed sign for five.

## Notes
四 and 五 both replaced their tallies; 六 onward were distinct graphs from the
start. 五 also in 五行 "the Five Phases," 五官 "the features of the face."

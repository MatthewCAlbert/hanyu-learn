## Etymology
Simplified from 護. The traditional graph is 言 "speech" with 蒦 *huò* for
the sound — to speak up for and shield someone. The simplified 护 rebuilds it
as 扌 "hand" for the meaning with 户 *hù* carrying the sound.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 held over the doorway 户 to keep it safe: to protect.

## Notes
Senses: protect / guard (保护, 护理, 爱护, 守护), escort (护送, 护航),
nurse (护士, 护工), and to shield someone's faults (护短, 袒护, 官官相护).
护照 "passport."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 吴 *wú* supplies the sound (*wú* → *yú*); 女 is the nominal meaning component. Shuowen glosses it as amusement. Why 女 was chosen (a pleasing companion? an entertainer?) is not securely attested, and it should not be read as a gendered meaning.

## Mnemonic
Having a ball in the Wu 吴 style: fun.

## Notes
吴 is sound only; also the name of an ancient state and a surname. 娱乐 "entertainment", 文娱, 娱乐圈 "show business", 自娱自乐.

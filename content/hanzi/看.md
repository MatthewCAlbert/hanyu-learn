## Etymology
An associative compound: 手 "hand" over 目 "eye" — raising a hand to the brow
to shade the eyes and peer into the distance. Shuowen: 睎也。从手下目 "to gaze;
a hand above the eye." Both parts carry meaning; there is no phonetic.

## Mnemonic
A hand 手 shading the eye 目 to look far off.

## Notes
看 spans "look at" (看书), "watch" (看电视), "read," "visit" (看朋友, 看病),
"regard as" (看作), and "it depends" (看情况). Two readings: *kàn* (common),
*kān* "to guard / mind" (看门, 看孩子).

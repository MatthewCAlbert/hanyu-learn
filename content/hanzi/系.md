## Etymology
系 is a hand grasping the top of a hank of silk threads (糸) — "to tie
together, to link." In the simplified standard it also absorbs 係 and 繫 "to
bind; to be." From "to connect" grew "a connected set" — a system, a lineage,
a university department.

## Mnemonic
A hand gathering the loose threads 糸 into one: tying them together.

## Notes
Two readings: *xì* "system / department / relation; to be" (系统, 中文系,
关系, 确系), *jì* "to fasten / tie" (系鞋带, 系安全带).

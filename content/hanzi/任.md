## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 壬 *rén* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 符也 "a tally, a matching token," names something
two parties hold that proves mutual trust — the root of "to entrust
someone with a responsibility."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 you would trust with the matching half 壬 of a tally: someone
reliable, someone appointed.

## Notes
From "a token of mutual trust" the sense spread to "responsibility,
appointment, office" (任务, 责任, 主任) — the modern words all keep the
core idea of a duty entrusted to a specific, trusted person.

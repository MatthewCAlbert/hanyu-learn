## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 斗 (a measuring ladle) with 米 (rice/grain)
pictured inside it — the act of measuring out grain by the ladleful. "A
measured-out portion" generalized to "material, ingredient" (材料, 原料) —
whatever has been measured and set aside for use.

The separate verb sense "to expect, to guess, to reckon" (料想, 预料,
不出所料) extends the same "measuring" idea into the abstract: to gauge or
estimate an outcome in advance.

## Mnemonic
Grain 米 poured into a measuring ladle 斗: a "料," a measured portion or
ingredient.

## Notes
料 splits cleanly into a concrete-noun family (材料, 原料, 燃料 "fuel," all
"material") and an abstract-verb family (预料, 料想, 意料之外 "beyond
expectation") — both built on the same core image of measuring something
out.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen — but with a startling original meaning: 罒
(from 网, a net) supplies the sense, and 直 *zhí* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's actual definition is "to pardon, to release" — releasing
someone from a net, the opposite of capture.

The dominant modern sense, "to place, to set up" (放置, 设置), does not
follow obviously from "to release" — dictionaries generally treat this as
either a separate strand of the same character or a merger with the
similar-sounding 寘 *zhì* ("to place"), and this entry does not assert a
settled bridge between the two, hence medium rather than high confidence.

## Mnemonic
Something straight 直 lifted clear of the net 罒 that once held it — set
free, and set down: "置," to place.

## Notes
位置 ("position, location") and 设置 ("to set up") both use the modern
"place, set" sense exclusively — the Shuowen-era "to release" meaning has
no living trace in everyday Mandarin.

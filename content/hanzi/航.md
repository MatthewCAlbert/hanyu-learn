## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 舟 (boat) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 方
舟, describes two boats lashed side by side for stability — and 亢 *kàng*
supplies the sound.

From "boats bound together for a crossing," 航 generalized to "to
navigate, to voyage" by water and, in the modern era, by extension to
travel through the air as well (航空, "aviation"; 航天, "spaceflight").

## Mnemonic
Boats 舟 lashed together, riding high 亢 and steady across open water:
"航," to navigate.

## Notes
航空 ("aviation") and 航天 ("spaceflight") both extend this character's
"navigating a crossing" core sense from water into the sky, keeping the
same 航 even though boats are long out of the picture.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread, silk) supplies the meaning, and 扁 *biǎn* supplies
the sound (*piān* is 扁's other reading, in 扁舟). Shuowen's own definition is
wonderfully concrete: 編 originally meant threading bamboo writing-slips
together in order with cord — literally how early books were bound.

"To compile, to edit" (编书, 编辑) is a direct descendant of that
book-binding image; "to weave, to knit" (编织) reapplies the same
thread-and-order action to cloth or baskets instead of bamboo slips.

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟 binding flat strips 扁 together in order: to "编," compile or
weave.

## Notes
编 and 织 both mean "to weave," but 编 implies arranging discrete strands or
units *in sequence* (hence its extension to editing/compiling), while 织
is the more general weaving-of-fabric verb.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 女 (woman) supplies the meaning, and 波 *bō* supplies an
approximate sound toward *pó*. This entry cannot cite a Shuowen entry
matching the modern sense, so it keeps confidence at medium.

"Old woman" (老婆婆, a term for an elderly woman) narrows in specific
kinship terms to "grandmother" (奶奶's alternative, 婆婆 — also
specifically "mother-in-law" from the husband's side) and, in the
casual 老婆 (covered elsewhere in this app), colloquially to "wife."

## Mnemonic
A woman 女 as weathered and steady as ocean waves 波 over many years:
"婆," an old woman.

## Notes
婆婆 specifically means "husband's mother" (mother-in-law from the wife's
perspective) — a distinct, more specific kinship role than the general
"old woman" sense the character also carries.

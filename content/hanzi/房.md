## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 户 "door, household" gives the meaning; 方 *fāng* gives the
sound. Shuowen: 室在旁也 "a chamber off to the side" — originally a side room
flanking the main hall, later any room or building.

## Mnemonic
A door 户 opening off the side: a room.

## Notes
The 方 phonetic also writes 放 *fàng*, 访 / 訪 *fǎng*, 芳 *fāng*, 防 *fáng*.
Senses: room (房间), building (楼房), house (房子), branch of a family (大房).

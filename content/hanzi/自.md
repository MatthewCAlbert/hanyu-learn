## Etymology
Pictographic — a nose, seen from the front (the original of 鼻). Shuowen: 鼻也
"the nose." Chinese speakers point to their own nose to mean "me," so the graph
came to mean "self, oneself," and from there "from, since"; 鼻 (自 + 畀) took
back "nose."

## Mnemonic
A nose — pointed at to mean "myself."

## Notes
自 is Kangxi radical 132 and part of 息 (自 + 心 — breath through the nose),
臭 (自 + 犬 — a dog's nose → smell), 首. Senses: self (自己, 亲自), from / since
(来自, 自古), naturally (自然), of one's own accord (自动, 自愿).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 舟 (boat) supplies the meaning — a warship — and 監 *jiān* supplies the sound. Simplification replaced 監 with 见 *jiàn*, which has the same reading as 舰, so 见 works as a sound clue (and does not mean "to see").

## Mnemonic
A boat 舟 that keeps a lookout 见 for the enemy.

## Notes
见 is sound only. 军舰 "warship", 舰队 "fleet", 航空母舰 "aircraft carrier", 舰艇 "naval vessel".

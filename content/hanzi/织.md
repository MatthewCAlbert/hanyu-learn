## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 織: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning. Shuowen's
gloss 作布帛之總名也 is "the general term for making cloth" — 戠 *zhī*
supplied the sound in the traditional form. Simplification replaced 戠 with
只, so today's visible right-hand component is a graphic stand-in rather
than the original phonetic — a sound clue that has drifted, not a live one.

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟, over and over 只, building up into cloth.

## Notes
织 anchors 织布 "to weave cloth," 编织 "to knit/weave," and figuratively 组织
"to organize" (literally "to weave together into a structure") — the same
image of many threads forming one coherent whole.

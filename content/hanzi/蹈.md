## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to stamp, tread — and 舀 *yǎo* supplies the sound (*yǎo* → *dǎo*). Shuowen glosses it as to step on; "dance" comes from rhythmic stamping.

## Mnemonic
Feet 足 scooping 舀 the floor in a dance.

## Notes
舀 is sound only (it means "to ladle"). 舞蹈 "dance", 手舞足蹈 "dance with joy", 循规蹈矩 "stick to the rules", 重蹈覆辙 "repeat a mistake". Series: 滔 *tāo*, 稻 *dào*, 蹈 *dǎo*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 土 "earth" gives the meaning — a hall raised on a rammed-earth
terrace; 尚 *shàng* gives the sound (for *táng*). Shuowen: 殿也 "a hall."

## Mnemonic
The great room built up on its earthen 土 platform: the hall.

## Notes
The 尚 phonetic also writes 常 *cháng*, 尝 / 嘗 *cháng*, 掌 *zhǎng*, 赏 / 賞
*shǎng*, 党 / 黨 *dǎng*. Senses: a main hall (礼堂, 教堂, 澡堂), a hall for
some function (食堂, 课堂, 会堂), the same-surname paternal line (堂兄, 堂姐,
堂房), a court session (过堂, 对簿公堂), and a measure word for lessons and
matched furniture (一堂课, 一堂家具). 堂堂正正 "upright and dignified."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 皿 (vessel) supplies the meaning, 成 *chéng* the sound. Shuowen defines it as grain filling a ritual vessel. That gives *chéng* "to hold, to ladle", and the extension "full, abundant" is read *shèng* (昌盛, 盛行).

## Mnemonic
A bowl 皿 filled to the point of completion 成.

## Notes
Two readings, two senses: *chéng* "to fill/hold" (盛饭), *shèng* "abundant, grand" (丰盛, 盛大, 茂盛). 成 is sound only.

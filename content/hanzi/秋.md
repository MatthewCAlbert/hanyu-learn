## Etymology
禾 "grain" beside 火 "fire" — the season the crops ripen and the stubble is
burned off. Shuowen: 禾穀熟也 "when the grain is ripe." (The oracle-bone
graph drew a cricket, the insect of autumn; the grain-and-fire form is
later.)

## Mnemonic
Ripe grain 禾 and the burning 火 of the fields: autumn.

## Notes
Senses: autumn (秋天, 秋季, 深秋, 中秋), harvest time (麦秋, 秋收), a year
(千秋万代, 一日三秋), and a critical juncture (多事之秋, 危急存亡之秋).
秋千 "a swing." Surname *Qiū*.

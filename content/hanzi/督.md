## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 目 (eye) supplies the meaning — to watch over, inspect — and 叔 *shū* supplies the sound (*shū* → *dū*). Shuowen's gloss is "to examine".

## Mnemonic
An uncle 叔's eye 目 watching you do your homework.

## Notes
叔 is sound only here. 监督 "supervise", 督促 "urge", 基督 "Christ" (sound loan), 督察.

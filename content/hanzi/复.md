## Etymology
简化 form covering several characters. **復** — 彳 "step" + 复 (a foot 夊
returning to a dwelling) for sound — Shuowen: 往來也 "to go and come back,"
giving "to return, to
reply, again, to repeat." **複** "double, compound" (复杂, 复数) and part of
**覆** "to cover / overturn" were also folded into 复.

## Mnemonic
Going out 彳 and coming back the same way: again.

## Notes
Senses: reply (回复, 答复), recover (恢复, 复原), review (复习), again
(复活, 复发), and "compound / complex" (复杂, 复印).

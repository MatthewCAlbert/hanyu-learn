## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 欠 (open mouth, lack) supplies the meaning, and 兼 *jiān* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as not having enough to eat; "deficient" then "short in the duty owed, sorry, apologetic" follows.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 欠 that couldn't even say a whole 兼 apology.

## Notes
兼 is sound only. 抱歉 "sorry", 道歉 "apologise", 歉意 "regret", 歉收 "poor harvest".

## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: the character originally drew a specific
venomous snake (蝮, a viper), its coiled resting shape traced directly.
Shuowen goes on to explain that this same graph became the root radical
for the whole broad category of small creatures — crawling, flying,
furred, shelled, or scaled — with 虫 generalizing from "snake" to
"insect, bug" as separate characters (蛇, for instance) took over the
specific "snake" meaning.

## Mnemonic
A small creature coiled up, resting — origin of a whole radical for
every small crawling or flying thing since: "虫," insect.

## Notes
虫 is the semantic component behind a huge family of small-creature
characters (蚊, mosquito; 蛇, snake; 蝴蝶, butterfly) — worth recognizing
as a radical even where the individual character is unfamiliar.

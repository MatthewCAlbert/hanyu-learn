## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 并 *bìng* supplies the sound,
shifted to *pīn*. Not in Shuowen. The core sense is "to join pieces together
by hand," which branches into three everyday uses: assembling parts (拼图
"jigsaw puzzle"), spelling sounds together (拼音 "pinyin" — literally "to
piece sounds together"), and risking everything at once (拼命 "to go all
out," literally "to stake one's life").

## Mnemonic
Two hands 扌 pushing pieces together side by side 并 until they fit.

## Notes
拼音 pīnyīn, the Latin-alphabet transcription system this app's readings use,
is this same character — "spelled sound," built by piecing together
individual letter-sounds rather than writing a character whole.

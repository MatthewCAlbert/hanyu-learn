## Etymology
A late compound graph, read as 上 "up" over 下 "down" fused — something held
fast between the two, neither rising nor falling. Hence *qiǎ* "to be wedged,
to jam; a checkpoint" (关卡). The reading *kǎ* is a sound-loan for foreign
"card" and "calorie," now the commoner one.

## Mnemonic
上 and 下 clamped together with nothing between: stuck.

## Notes
Two readings: *qiǎ* — jam / get stuck (卡住), a checkpoint (关卡, 哨卡);
*kǎ* — card (卡片, 银行卡, 信用卡), calorie (卡路里), and 卡车 "truck" (from
"car"). Not related to the older 卡 in any Shuowen entry.

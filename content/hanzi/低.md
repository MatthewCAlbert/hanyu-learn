## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 氐 *dī* gives the sound — and
氐 itself means "the base, low down," so it carries a hint of sense too. Not
in Shuowen; the word was earlier written 氐.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 stooping down to the base 氐: low.

## Notes
The 氐 phonetic also writes 底 *dǐ*, 抵 *dǐ*, 邸 *dǐ*, 骶 *dǐ*. Senses: low
in height / position / degree (低头, 低价, 低温, 降低), to let hang down
(低垂), beneath (低于平均).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 凍: 冫 (ice — a reduced form of 仌/氷) supplies the
meaning, 东 (traditional 東) supplies the sound, shifted to *dòng*. Shuowen's
gloss 仌也 "ice" is direct: the character simply names the state of being
frozen, and every modern sense (冷冻 "to freeze," 冻死 "freezing cold," 果冻
"jelly," a wobbly frozen-textured dessert) extends from that.

## Mnemonic
Ice 冫 forming in the east 东 wind's cold.

## Notes
冻 pairs with 冰 (ice as a noun/substance) — 冻 is more often the verb/state
("to freeze, frozen") while 冰 is the thing itself. 冻死了! is a common
colloquial complaint about being cold, not literal danger.

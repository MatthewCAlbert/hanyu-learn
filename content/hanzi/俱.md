## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 具 *jù* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 偕也 "together, alongside" matches the
modern "all, together" directly.

## Mnemonic
People 亻 fully equipped 具 and moving together, all at once.

## Notes
俱 is largely literary/formal today: 俱乐部 "club" (a 20th-century phonetic
loan for English "club," reusing 俱's sound rather than its meaning), 与生
俱来 "innate, born with," 面面俱到 "to attend to every aspect."

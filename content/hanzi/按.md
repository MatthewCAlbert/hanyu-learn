## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 安 *ān* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's gloss, 下也, is exactly "to press down."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pressing something down until it's calm and settled 安.

## Notes
"To press" extends to "to press pause on, to hold off" (按下不表) and,
separately, to "according to" (按照, 按时) — following a plan the way a
finger presses along a fixed line.

## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph shows a foot (止) stepping up and away from 凵, a pit or
the mouth of a semi-underground dwelling — "to go out." Shuowen instead reads
the later shape as a plant pushing up out of the ground; this is a
reinterpretation, not the origin.

## Mnemonic
A foot crossing the threshold and out.

## Notes
Directional complement "out" (拿出, 出来, 出去). Senses fan out: emerge
(出现), produce (出口, 出书), exceed (出色), leave (出门, 出国).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 巾 "cloth" gives the meaning; 尚 *shàng* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 下帬也 "a lower garment, a skirt" — 常 was originally the word for a
skirt (later written 裳). It was borrowed for "constant, regular, ordinary,
often," and 裳 was left to carry "skirt."

## Mnemonic
The everyday cloth 巾 you always have on: usual.

## Notes
The 尚 phonetic also writes 掌 *zhǎng*, 堂 *táng*, 赏 / 賞 *shǎng*, 尝 / 嘗
*cháng*. Senses: often (常常, 经常), ordinary (平常, 正常), a constant
(反常).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 攵 (攴) "action, to make happen" gives the meaning; 古 *gǔ*
gives the sound (for *gù*). Shuowen: 使為之也 "to bring something about; a
cause."

## Mnemonic
What made 攵 a thing happen: its cause.

## Notes
The 古 phonetic also writes 姑 *gū*, 估 *gū*, 咕 *gū*, 枯 *kū*, 固 *gù*.
Senses: cause / reason (缘故, 原故, 无缘无故), on purpose (故意, 明知故犯),
an incident (事故, 变故), old / former (故乡, 故人, 故居, 依然如故),
therefore (故此, 是故), and to pass away (病故, 已故, 亡故). 故事 "a story";
故障 "a fault, a breakdown."

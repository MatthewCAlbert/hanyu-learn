## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻(person) supplies the meaning, and 咅 *pǒu* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original meaning is "反" — to turn one's back on, to oppose
— a person turning away, closely related to 背 *bèi* (back; to turn one's
back on), which shares both the sound and the "turning away" sense.

The modern meaning "-fold, times (a multiplier)" is a separate development,
not a direct extension of "to oppose" — most dictionaries treat it as an
early semantic drift or loan use whose exact bridge is not clearly attested,
so this jump is best learned as a fact about the modern word rather than a
story that follows logically from the etymology.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 saying "咅!" (a dismissive sound) and doubling back: turn away
once, or turn away twice — "倍," -fold.

## Notes
增加了一倍 means "increased *by* 100%" (i.e., doubled), not "increased to
100%" — a common trap, since English "one-fold increase" phrasing does not
map cleanly onto 倍's counting convention.

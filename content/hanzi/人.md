## Etymology
Pictographic. A side view of a standing person, the two strokes being the legs
and trunk. One of the oldest and most stable graphs in the script — the oracle
bone form is recognisably the modern one.

## Mnemonic
Two legs, walking.

## Notes
As a left-hand component it compresses to 亻 (人字旁), which is why 你, 他, 们,
住 and 作 all group under Kangxi radical 9 even though 人 is not visible in them.

## Etymology
A late colloquial graph (Song–Yuan). 口 "speech" gives the meaning — it is a
spoken pronoun; 自 "self" supplies both a rough sound and the sense. Not in
Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
The word out of one's own 自 mouth 口 for "us": 咱.

## Notes
Senses: we / us — the inclusive first person, taking in the listener (咱们,
咱俩), and in northern speech simply "I / me" (咱不懂). Contrast 我们, which
may or may not include the listener.

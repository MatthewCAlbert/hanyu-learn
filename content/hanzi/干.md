## Etymology
Three characters now share this shape.

**干 *gān*** is a pictograph of a forked weapon or shield — Shuowen: 犯也 "to
offend against." This survives in 干涉 "to interfere," 相干 "to be relevant."

**干 *gān* "dry"** is simplified from 乾 (the 乾 of 乾坤 keeps *qián*).

**干 *gàn* "to do; a trunk"** is simplified from 幹 — 树干 "tree trunk," 干活儿
"to work," 能干 "capable."

## Mnemonic
A shield raised against something — and, by merger, "dry" and "to do."

## Notes
Two readings to keep apart: *gān* (dry, to concern) and *gàn* (to do,
trunk, capable). 干净 "clean" is *gānjìng*.

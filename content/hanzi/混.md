## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning and 昆 *kūn* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss is an abundant, welling flow; the sense of muddied, mixed, confused follows from churning water.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 churned by the swarming insects 昆 — all mixed together.

## Notes
昆 is sound only: 棍 *gùn*, 滚 *gǔn*, 混 *hùn*, 昆 *kūn*. 混合, 混乱, 混淆, 混凝土 "concrete". Two readings: *hùn* (to mix), *hún* in 混浊 "muddy".

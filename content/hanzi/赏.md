## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (money, valuables) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition is precisely "to bestow [wealth] upon those with merit" — and
尚 *shàng* supplies the sound.

"To bestow a reward" extended to "to appreciate, to enjoy" (欣赏, 观赏, "to
view and enjoy scenery") — rewarding merit and taking pleasure in
something both share the idea of recognizing value.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 esteemed 尚 and bestowed on the deserving: "赏," to reward — and,
by extension, to admire.

## Notes
欣赏 ("to appreciate, to enjoy") and 奖赏 ("a reward," pairing 赏 with 奖)
both branch off this character's core "recognizing and honoring value"
sense.

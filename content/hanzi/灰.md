## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen: 死火餘蕈也 "what remains of a dead fire," analysed as
火 (fire) with 又 (hand) above — once a fire has gone out, its remains are
cool enough to be picked up by hand. "Ash," and by extension the colour
"gray," both come straight from that dead-fire residue.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 finally safe to reach into a fire 火 that has burned all the way
down to ash.

## Notes
灰 covers the literal (烟灰 "cigarette ash," 灰尘 "dust") and the
figurative — 灰心 "discouraged, downhearted," building on ash as the
lifeless leftover of something that was once burning bright.

## Etymology
Not securely settled. The oracle-bone graph is usually taken as an ancestral
tablet or offering-stand — the original of 祖 "ancestor" — though Guo Moruo
read it as a phallus, emblem of the male ancestor. It was borrowed early for
the grammatical
senses "moreover" and "for now."

## Mnemonic
A tiered stand set up for offerings, borrowed to mean "and what's more."

## Notes
Senses: and / moreover (而且, 并且, 况且), for the time being (暂且, 姑且,
得过且过), just / simply (你且听我说), and "even" in classical 尚且 / 且…
何况. As phonetic in 组 祖 租 助 姐 粗.

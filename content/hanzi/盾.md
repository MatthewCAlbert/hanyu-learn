## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen glosses 盾 as a body-shield, explicitly noting its
purpose — 所以扞身蔽目, "to guard the body and block the eyes/line of
sight" — and marks the graph 象形, "a picture," of that shield shape with
an eye 目 visible peering out from behind it.

## Mnemonic
An eye 目 peering out cautiously from behind a raised shield 厂.

## Notes
盾 covers the literal object (盾牌 "shield") and, unrelatedly, the currency
name for the Dutch guilder — a phonetic loan reusing the character for
sound alone, unconnected to its shield meaning.

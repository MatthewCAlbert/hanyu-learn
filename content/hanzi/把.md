## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 巴 *bā* supplies the sound —
*bǎ* and *bā* differ only in tone. Shuowen glosses it simply as 握 "to grip."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 gripping a snake 巴 by the tail.

## Notes
The grammatical 把-construction (把 + object + verb) grew out of this literal
"to hold/take" sense — 把书放在桌上 is, historically, "take the book, put [it]
on the table."

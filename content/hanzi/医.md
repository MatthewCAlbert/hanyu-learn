## Etymology
Simplified from 醫. The full character has 酉 "wine jar" at the bottom — for
the medicinal alcohol used in early treatment — under 殹, which supplies the
sound. Shuowen: 治病工也 "one whose work is treating illness."

医 by itself is a different old graph: 匸 "a container" enclosing 矢 "arrow" —
a quiver (read *yì*). Simplification kept this top-left piece and dropped the
酉. So the arrow in 医 is a leftover from "quiver," not a comment on medicine.

## Mnemonic
An arrow 矢 pulled from the body and set in its case: healing.

## Notes
Simplification by keeping one component and discarding the phonetic-bearing
rest — compare 声 (from 聲), 网 (from 網).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 償: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 尝
(traditional 償's phonetic component, related to 賞/尝) supplies the sound,
shifted to *cháng*. Not in Shuowen. The stable core sense — a person
repaying or making good on something owed — has held steady since the
character's earliest attestations.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 giving something back, tasting 尝 the fairness of the exchange.

## Notes
偿 anchors 偿还 "to repay [a debt]," 赔偿 "to compensate," 得不偿失 "the
losses outweigh the gains" (literally "what's gained doesn't repay what's
lost") — always about restoring balance after a loss or debt.

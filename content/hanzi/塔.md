## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning and 荅 *dá* supplies the sound (*dá* → *tǎ*). 塔 is a later graph (in the additions to Shuowen) made to write the Sanskrit *stūpa* in its Chinese form (窣堵波, 塔婆), and the pagoda; the character is a transliteration of the loanword, so the components say little about the building itself.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 stacked level on level into a tower.

## Notes
荅 is sound only. 塔 is also used for any tower: 金字塔 "pyramid", 铁塔, 灯塔 "lighthouse", 电视塔.

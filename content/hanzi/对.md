## Etymology
Simplified from 對. The old graph has 寸 "hand" beside 丵, a bristly or
branched shape (often taken as a candelabrum or a planting tool). Shuowen
glosses it 譍無方也 "to respond without hesitation" — a hand raised in answer.
From "to respond / face" come "correct" (对不对), "a pair" (一对), and "toward /
opposite" (对面).

The simplified 对 swaps 丵 for 又, another "hand" shape, keeping the
hand-in-response idea.

## Mnemonic
A hand 寸 raised straight back in answer: right.

## Notes
Senses to keep apart: 对 "correct" (你说得对), 对 "toward / to" (对我说),
对 "a pair," 对 "opposite" (对门).

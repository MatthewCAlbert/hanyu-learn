## Etymology
异 is the simplified form of 異 *yì*. Shuowen explains 異 as 分也 "to separate", from 廾 (two hands) and 畀; oracle-bone forms instead look like a person with raised arms and a large head, which some scholars read as a masked figure. The origin of the "different, strange" sense is not securely attested. The simplified 异 is an old variant that was adopted wholesale, so its parts (巳, 廾) should not be read as a story.

## Mnemonic
Two hands 廾 holding up a snake-like 巳 — surely not a normal thing, hence "strange".

## Notes
Frequent in 差异, 异常, 惊异. 异 here is a single graph for 異 only; 翼 and 冀 keep the older 異 shape in traditional script.

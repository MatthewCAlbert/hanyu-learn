## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen reads 生 "growth" over 丹 "a cinnabar well"
and calls it "the colour of the east," of growing plants — but the early
graph and this analysis are both uncertain. The modern shape 龶 + 月 is worn.

## Mnemonic
The colour of things that grow: blue-green.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 174) and the phonetic in 清 请 情 晴 睛 精.
Senses: blue / green / the dark colour of nature (青山, 青天, 青草), young /
youthful (青年, 青春, 青涩), and black (青丝 black hair, 青布). 青蛙 "frog";
青菜 "leafy greens." Surname *Qīng*.

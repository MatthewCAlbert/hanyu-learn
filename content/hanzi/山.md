## Etymology
Pictographic — three peaks rising side by side, the middle one tallest.
Shuowen: 有石而高 "stony and high."

## Mnemonic
Three peaks: a mountain.

## Notes
山 is Kangxi radical 46 and the semantic in 岛 / 島 (island), 岸 (shore), 峰
(peak), 岭 / 嶺. 爬山 "to hike"; 山水 "landscape / scenery."

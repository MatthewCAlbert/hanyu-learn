## Etymology
Shuowen reads 害 as pictophonetic: 宀 (house/roof) and 口 (mouth) for
meaning, 丯 *jiè* for sound — with a moralising aside from Xu Shen
himself: 祸尝起於家，生於忽微，故害从宀, "disaster often starts at home,
born from the smallest carelessness, which is why 害 uses the roof
radical." The modern component shown where 丯 was, 丰, is a later graphic
convergence with the unrelated character 丰 "abundant"; confidence is
medium because 丯 no longer survives as a separately checkable piece.

## Mnemonic
Careless words 口 spoken under one's own roof 宀 can do the deepest harm.

## Notes
害 covers both the abstract "harm, to injure" (害怕 "to be afraid,"
危害 "to endanger") and the concrete "a calamity, a pest" (灾害, 虫害) —
one word for both the act and the thing that causes it.

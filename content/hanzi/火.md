## Etymology
Pictographic — flames rising to a point, with sparks flying off to the sides.
Shuowen: 炎而上 "blazing and rising."

## Mnemonic
Tongues of flame licking upward: fire.

## Notes
火 is Kangxi radical 86; at the foot of a character it becomes 灬 (煮, 蒸, 热).
Senses: fire (火车 "fire-cart" = train), anger (发火, 火气), popular / hot
(火爆, 这个很火).

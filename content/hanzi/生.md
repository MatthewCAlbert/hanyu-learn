## Etymology
Pictographic — a sprout (屮) pushing up above the line of the earth (土).
Shuowen: 進也。象艸木生出土上 "to advance; a picture of a plant coming up out of
the ground." From "to grow" spread "to be born, to give birth, to produce," and
by metaphor "raw / unfamiliar" (生肉, 生词) and "life."

## Mnemonic
A shoot breaking the surface of the soil: it grows.

## Notes
生 is Kangxi radical 100 and the phonetic in 星, 姓, 性, 牲. Senses fan out
widely: 生活, 生日, 学生, 医生, 陌生, 生气.

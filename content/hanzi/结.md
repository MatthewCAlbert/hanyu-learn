## Etymology
Simplified from 結. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "cord" gives the meaning; 吉 *jí*
gives the sound (for *jié*). Shuowen: 締也 "to tie, to fasten."

## Mnemonic
A cord 纟 pulled tight into a knot: 结.

## Notes
The 吉 phonetic also writes 洁 / 潔 *jié*, 桔 *jié*, 颉 *jié*. *jié*: tie /
knot (结绳, 打结, 蝴蝶结), form / build (结合, 结婚, 结交, 结盟), settle up
(结账, 结算, 结案), congeal (结冰, 结晶), a lump (疙瘩, 心结); *jiē*: bear
fruit (结果, 树上结满了桃), and 结实 *jiēshi* "sturdy, durable."

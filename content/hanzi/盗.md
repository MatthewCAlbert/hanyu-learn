## Etymology
Traditional 盜 is 次 over 皿. Shuowen explains 次 here as 涎, a person with watering mouth, desiring what is in the vessel 皿: coveting and then taking what is not yours. The simplified 盗 keeps the structure. 次 has no connection to its common meaning "next, order" in this character.

## Mnemonic
Drooling over a bowl 皿 of someone else's food — and then taking it.

## Notes
盗窃, 强盗, 盗版 "pirated edition", 防盗. The same 次 "drooling" element appears in 羡 *xiàn* "to envy".

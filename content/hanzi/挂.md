## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 圭 *guī* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's original sense is "to draw, to mark out" (畫也) — a hand making a
distinguishing mark — not yet "to hang." Confidence is medium because the
modern, dominant sense ("to hang, suspend") is a later colloquial shift
that Duan Yucai's commentary itself flags as a departure from the classical
meaning, rather than a documented step-by-step semantic path.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 hanging a jade tablet 圭 on the wall.

## Notes
挂号 "to register" (at a hospital, post office) preserves a trace of the
older "to mark, to record" sense, sitting oddly next to the now-dominant
"to hang" (挂钩, 悬挂) — both are legitimate, just from different eras of
the word's history.

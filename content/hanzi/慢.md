## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (心) "heart" gives the meaning; 曼 *màn* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 惰也 "slack, negligent." From "a slack attitude" grew "rude,
offhand" (傲慢, 怠慢) and "slow" (慢车, 慢慢来).

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 in no hurry 曼: taking it slow.

## Notes
The 曼 phonetic also writes 漫 *màn*, 馒 / 饅 *mán*, 蔓 *màn* / *wàn*. 慢慢
(doubled) "slowly, gradually"; 慢点儿 "(a bit) slower / careful."

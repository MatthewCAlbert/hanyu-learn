## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen, though with an origin more specific than most
learners expect: 鮮 was originally the name of a particular fish from the
"貉" region, built from 魚 (fish) with a reduced form of 羴 (the smell of
mutton, itself built from three 羊) supplying the sound.

The word's broadening to "fresh, delicious" generally is often explained
by fish and mutton both being classic examples of prized, perishable
food — a plausible and widely repeated account, but one this entry treats
as a later folk rationalization of the sound-loan rather than the
settled original mechanism, hence medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
Fish 鱼 and mutton 羊, the two archetypal fresh delicacies: "鲜," fresh.

## Notes
Two readings split by sense: *xiān* is the everyday "fresh" (新鲜, 鲜
花); *xiǎn* survives in the more literary "rare, few" (鲜为人知, "rarely
known to people").

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to kick — and 易 *yì* supplies the sound (*yì* → *tī*). A later graph, in the additions to Shuowen. 易 contributes only sound, not "easy" or "change".

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 that makes things change 易 places: a kick.

## Notes
易 is sound only. 踢球 "play football", 踢足球, 踢馆 "challenge a rival", 踢开 "kick away". Series: 剔 *tī*, 惕 *tì*, 赐 *cì*, 锡 *xī*, 踢 *tī*.

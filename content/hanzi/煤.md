## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 火 (fire) supplies the meaning, 某 *mǒu* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen; coal as a fuel and its dedicated
character are both later than the classical corpus. The "fire" radical
marks it plainly as fuel/combustible material.

## Mnemonic
Fire's 火 fuel, dug up from somewhere 某 underground.

## Notes
煤 anchors 煤炭 "coal," 煤气 "coal gas / gas [for cooking]," 煤矿 "coal mine" —
a small, tightly concrete vocabulary cluster with no notable figurative
extensions.

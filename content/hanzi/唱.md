## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 昌 *chāng* gives the sound (for
*chàng*). Shuowen: 導也 "to lead" — originally "to lead off a song, to be the
first to raise the voice," hence "to sing" and "to call out."

## Mnemonic
The mouth 口 raised, bright 昌 with song.

## Notes
The 昌 phonetic also writes 娼 *chāng*, 猖 *chāng*, 倡 *chàng*. 唱歌 "to sing a
song"; 唱票 "to read out ballots"; 唱反调 "to sing a different tune."

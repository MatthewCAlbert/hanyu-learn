## Etymology
The structure is 隹 (a short-tailed bird) over 灬 (fire): a bird over the flames, scorched. Shuowen defines it as burned, scorched. Whether 隹 is a meaning element (roasting a bird) or a sound element is a matter of scholarly debate; the picture reading is the common one but not certain. The extension "anxious, worried" is "burning with worry".

## Mnemonic
A little bird 隹 left on the flame 灬 — burnt, scorched.

## Notes
焦点 "focus", 焦急, 焦虑 "anxiety", 焦距 "focal length". Related: 蕉 *jiāo* (banana), 礁 *jiāo* (reef), 樵 — 焦 as sound.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — the legs and knees, to kneel — and 危 *wēi* supplies the sound (*wēi* → *guì*, an old initial alternation). Shuowen glosses it as to kneel in homage.

## Mnemonic
Feet 足 folded under danger 危: down on your knees.

## Notes
危 is sound only. 跪下 "kneel", 下跪, 跪拜 "kowtow", 跪地, 罚跪. Compare 脆 *cuì* and 诡 *guǐ* for the 危 sound family.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 冫 (water) supplies the meaning; 兄 *xiōng* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original sense, 寒水也 "cold water," has nothing to do
with the modern "situation, circumstance" — that later sense is a
phonetic loan (假借) that fully displaced the original, concrete meaning.

## Mnemonic
Cold water 冫, poured on an older brother's 兄 plans: "but consider the
situation..."

## Notes
况 covers "circumstances, conditions" (情况, 状况) and, as an adverb,
"moreover, let alone" (何况, 况且) — both senses trace to the same
borrowed word, with no trace of "cold water" left in ordinary use.

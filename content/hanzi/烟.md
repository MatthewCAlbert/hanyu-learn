## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 火 (fire) supplies the meaning; the phonetic varies by
form. Shuowen's main entry uses the complex 垔 *yīn*, but explicitly notes
an alternative form using the simpler 因 *yīn* instead — 烟，或从因 —
which is exactly the variant that became the modern standard character.

## Mnemonic
Fire 火, its cause 因 rising up in a visible trail: smoke.

## Notes
烟 covers both "smoke" generally (烟囱 "chimney") and, by metonymy,
"tobacco" specifically (香烟, 抽烟, 戒烟) — the substance named for the
smoke it produces.

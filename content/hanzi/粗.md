## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 米 (rice, grain) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 疏, means "coarse, unrefined" — and 且 *qiě* supplies the
sound. The original sense was literally coarse, unhulled grain.

"Coarse" (of grain) generalized to "thick" (of a physical object, 粗的
绳子, "a thick rope") and, figuratively, to "rough, crude" (粗糙, 粗心
"careless," lit. "coarse-hearted").

## Mnemonic
Grain 米 left unrefined, still coarse and heavy in the hand: "粗," rough
or thick.

## Notes
粗心 ("careless," lit. "coarse-hearted") and 仔细 (careful, its close
antonym) form a natural contrast pair worth learning together.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 彳 (movement/step) supplies the meaning, 皮 *pí* supplies
the sound, shifted to *bǐ*. Shuowen ties the original sense to movement
toward a further point — "going on, further along" — which is exactly why
彼 came to mean "that, over there," the far point relative to 此 "this,
here."

## Mnemonic
A step 彳 taken toward that far shore 皮 over there.

## Notes
彼 is largely literary/formal today: 彼此 "each other," 顾此失彼 "to
attend to one thing at the cost of another." Colloquial Mandarin uses 那
for "that" in almost every everyday context instead.

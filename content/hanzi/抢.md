## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 仓 (traditional 倉) supplies the
sound — *cāng* and *qiǎng* share the same phonetic series though the finals
have drifted apart over time. The oldest attested sense of traditional 搶 is
"to bump against, to collide," which slid into "to rush at" and then "to
snatch, to grab" in modern use. Not in Shuowen; the character postdates it.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 darting into the storehouse 仓 before anyone else gets there.

## Notes
Two readings split by sense: *qiǎng* "to grab/rob" (抢劫, 抢购) is the
everyday reading; *qiāng* survives in 呼天抢地 "to wail to heaven," an old
"knock, bump" sense. Shares 仓 with 沧 cāng (vast, of water) and 创 chuàng
(to create/wound) — three unrelated senses on one shared sound.

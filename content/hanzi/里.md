## Etymology
田 "field" over 土 "soil." Shuowen: 居也 "a dwelling place" — where there is
farmland and ground, people settle. From "a settlement / village" came the
land-distance unit 里 (about 500 m today).

The "inside" sense (里边, 家里, 心里) is not original: it comes from
simplification merging 裏 / 裡 "lining, interior" into 里.

## Mnemonic
Fields 田 and soil 土 together: a village — and the walk between villages.

## Notes
里 is Kangxi radical 166 and the phonetic in 理 *lǐ*, 鲤 / 鯉 *lǐ*. (童's
bottom half is 里, but its sound comes from 重.) As a bare locative suffix
("in / inside") it is toneless: 屋里, 手里.

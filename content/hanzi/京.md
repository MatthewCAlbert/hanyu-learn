## Etymology
Pictographic. The graph shows a tall building raised on a mound or pillars —
a lookout tower or ceremonial hall on high ground. Shuowen: 人所為絕高丘也
"an artificially made, exceedingly high mound." From "great raised structure"
it came to mean the fortified seat of a ruler — a capital.

## Mnemonic
A tall hall standing on a raised platform: the capital.

## Notes
Phonetic in 景 *jǐng* (scenery), 惊 / 驚 (to startle), 鲸 / 鯨 (whale). The
"capital" sense is fixed in 北京, 南京, 京城.

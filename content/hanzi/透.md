## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning "to pass through, to
penetrate," and 秀 *xiù* supplies an approximate sound. 透 is a
post-classical character (not in Shuowen), so its sound history is less
firmly attested than the ancient pictophonetic core; medium confidence
reflects that gap.

"To penetrate" extends both to light/liquid passing through a barrier
(透明, transparent; 透水, to let water through) and, adverbially, to doing
something "thoroughly, all the way through" (说透, to explain thoroughly;
看透, to see through someone).

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 that pushes all the way through, coming out the other side as
cleanly as a fine crop 秀: "透," to penetrate.

## Notes
透 as a resultative complement after another verb (说透, 想透, 看透)
consistently adds the sense "all the way, completely, thoroughly" — a
productive pattern worth learning as a unit.

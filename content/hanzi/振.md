## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 辰 *chén* supplies the
sound, shifted to *zhèn*. Shuowen's gloss 舉救也 is "to raise up and
rescue" — a hand lifting something to safety, which generalised to "to
shake, to rouse, to arouse [spirits]."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 shaking someone awake at first light 辰.

## Notes
振 anchors 振动 "to vibrate," 振奋 "to invigorate/uplift [spirits]," and
振兴 "to revitalise [an industry, a nation]" — always about lifting
something from a low or still state into vigorous motion.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning — respectful speech — and 兼 *jiān* supplies the sound (*jiān* → *qiān*). Shuowen glosses it as deference.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 that holds back and doubles up 兼 on modesty.

## Notes
兼 is sound only. Series: 嫌 *xián*, 廉 *lián*, 歉 *qiàn*, 谦 *qiān*. 谦虚 "modest", 谦逊 "humble", 自谦, 谦让 "yield politely".

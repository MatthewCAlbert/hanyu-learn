## Etymology
Shuowen writes 暴 from 日 (sun), 出, 廾 (hands) and 米 (grain) — hands bringing grain out into the sun. That is the original "to dry in the sun", still seen in the later extra-radical form 曝 *pù*. The senses "sudden, violent, cruel" were borrowed for the same sound; Shuowen's own gloss is "rushing". The modern graph has distorted 米 into 水, so reading a story from 共 and 水 would be reading the corruption.

## Mnemonic
A storm that dries and cracks everything: the sun 日 beating down hard.

## Notes
暴力, 暴雨, 暴风雨, 暴躁. 暴露 is "expose to the sun" literally, then "expose" generally. Pairs with 曝光 "exposure".

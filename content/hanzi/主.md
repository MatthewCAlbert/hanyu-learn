## Etymology
A pictograph of a lampstand with the flame 丶 burning at its tip — the
original word for the lamp-wick, later written 炷. It was borrowed for "the
one in charge": host, master, owner, lord; hence "main, primary" and "to
preside over."

## Mnemonic
The flame that holds steady at the centre of the lamp: the one in charge.

## Notes
炷 "wick, a stick of incense" keeps the pictured sense. Senses: owner / master
(主人, 房主, 雇主), host (主办, 东道主), main (主要, 主力, 主食), advocate
(主张, 民主), the Lord (天主, 救世主).

## Etymology
The traditional 緊 combined 糸 (thread) with 臤 *qiān* (itself 臣+又,
"firm, taut") above it — "thread pulled taut and bound." 臤 supplied both
a echo of "firmness" and, loosely, the sound.

Simplification compressed 臤 down to a bare 又, leaving only a fragment of
the original upper component. Confidence is medium: 糸 (thread) is still a
genuine, checkable semantic piece, but no phonetic is declared, since what
remains of 臤 is a graphic remnant rather than a intact sound clue.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 pulling thread 糸 taut: tight.

## Notes
"Tight, taut" extends smoothly to "urgent, pressing" (紧急, 抓紧) — the
same shift "tension" makes in English between a literal pulled cord and a
stressful situation.

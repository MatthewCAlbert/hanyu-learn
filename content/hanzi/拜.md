## Etymology
Ideographic: two hand-shapes joined in a ceremonial gesture — a bow with
hands pressed together, the traditional posture of respect and greeting.
The exact classical source text for this reading is less precisely
documented than for many other characters here, but the visible picture
of doubled hands is uncontested.

## Mnemonic
Two hands 手手 pressed together, bowing low in respect.

## Notes
拜 covers ceremony (拜年 "to pay a New Year visit," 拜佛 "to worship
Buddha") and everyday politeness: 拜托 "please, I'm counting on you," 拜访
"to pay a visit [formal]."

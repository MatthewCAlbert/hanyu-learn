## Etymology
Simplified from 邊. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; 臱 *mián* gave the sound.
Shuowen: 行垂崖也 "to travel along the edge of a cliff" — the margin, the
border, the side.

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** 臱 is replaced by 力,
which is neither semantic nor a recoverable phonetic. Learn 边 as sound-opaque:
辶 tells you it has to do with a boundary you move along, nothing tells you it
is *biān*.

## Mnemonic
The edge you walk 辶 along — read the sound off by heart.

## Notes
One of eight HSK 1–2 characters whose phonetic was lost in simplification —
the playbook names it alongside 过 (咼) and 举 (舉). As a locative suffix
("-side"): 上边, 里边, 旁边, 前边.

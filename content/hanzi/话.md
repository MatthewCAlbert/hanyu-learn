## Etymology
Simplified from 話. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning. Shuowen's old form has
昏 as the phonetic; the received character writes 舌 "tongue" instead — an
element that also fits the sense (words come off the tongue). Meaning: speech,
words, talk.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 rolling off the tongue 舌.

## Notes
Senses: speech / what is said (说话, 讲话), a remark (一句话), a language or
dialect (北京话, 普通话). 电话 "telephone" = "electric speech."

## Etymology
Shuowen explains 若 as hand 右 picking plants 艸, "choosing vegetables". Oracle-bone forms instead show a kneeling figure with raised hands smoothing the hair, read by some as "compliant" (the sense of 诺 "assent"). The common meanings "if, as, like" are all borrowings; the origin is not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A person gently smoothing the hair: agreeable, "if so".

## Notes
若干 "a few", 倘若 "if", 若是, 若无其事 "as if nothing happened", 般若. 若 is the sound part of 诺 *nuò* and 惹 *rě*.

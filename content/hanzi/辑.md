## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 輯: 车 (cart) supplies the meaning, 咠 *jí*
supplies the sound, shifted to *jí*. Shuowen's gloss 車和輯也 describes a
cart running smoothly, all its parts working harmoniously together. "To
gather, to compile, to edit" extends from that "many parts brought into
smooth harmony" image to assembling many pieces of writing or media into
one coherent whole.

## Mnemonic
A cart's 车 many parts, brought together in one harmonious 咠 whole.

## Notes
辑 anchors 编辑 "to edit," 剪辑 "to edit [film/video]," and 专辑 "album,
special collection" — always about assembling separate pieces into one
finished, coherent product.

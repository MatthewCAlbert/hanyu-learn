## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 石 "stone / hard ware" gives the meaning; 宛 *wǎn* gives the
sound — and 宛 "curved, bent" suits a rounded vessel. Also written 椀 (wood)
and 盌 (dish).

## Mnemonic
A hard little hollow 石, curved 宛 to hold food: a bowl.

## Notes
The 宛 phonetic also writes 婉 *wǎn*, 腕 *wàn*, 豌 *wān* (豌豆 pea), 蜿
*wān*. Senses: a bowl (饭碗, 茶碗, 大碗), a bowl-shaped part (轴碗), and a
measure word (一碗汤, 两碗米饭). 铁饭碗 "an iron rice bowl — a secure job."

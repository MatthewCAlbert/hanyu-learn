## Etymology
Simplified from 條. 木 "wood" gives the meaning; 攸 *yōu* gave the sound.
Shuowen: 小枝也 "a slender twig." From "a thin branch" grew "a strip, a long
thin thing," "an item / clause" (条件, 条款), and the measure word for long
things. Simplification reduces 攸 to 夂, dropping the sound clue.

## Mnemonic
A slim twig off the tree 木: a strip.

## Notes
Measure word for long, narrow things: 一条路, 一条鱼, 一条腿, 一条河. Also
条子 "a note / slip," 面条 "noodles."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — lead, bluish-grey metal — and 㕣 *yǎn* supplies the sound (*yǎn* → *qiān*), which in the modern graph appears as 几 over 口 and is no longer recognisable. The same 㕣 appears in 沿 and 船.

## Mnemonic
The soft metal 钅 that marks the page: the lead in a pencil.

## Notes
铅笔 "pencil", 铅球 "shot put / lead ball", 铅字 "type", 铅中毒 "lead poisoning". Pencil 'lead' is actually graphite, but the name stuck.

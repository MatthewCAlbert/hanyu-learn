## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning and 旁 *páng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses 榜 as a board that straightens bows. The sense "posted board; notice; list of names" came later, from a board that was nailed up for announcement.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 board stuck on the side 旁 of a wall for everyone to read.

## Notes
旁 is sound only. 榜单 "ranking list", 排行榜 "chart", 榜样 "role model", 金榜题名. A second reading *bàng* "oar" is rare.

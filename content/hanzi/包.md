## Etymology
Pictographic. 勹 is a body curled round, enclosing 巳 "an unformed foetus" —
Shuowen reads it as a womb wrapping a child. From "to enclose, to wrap" grew
"a bundle, a bag," "to include / contain" (包括, 包含), and "to undertake / take
charge of" (包办).

## Mnemonic
A body 勹 curled protectively around what is inside 巳: to wrap.

## Notes
包 is the phonetic in 抱 *bào*, 饱 / 飽 *bǎo*, 泡 *pào*, 炮 *pào*, 跑 *pǎo*.
Senses: bag (书包, 钱包), a steamed bun (包子), a swelling / lump.

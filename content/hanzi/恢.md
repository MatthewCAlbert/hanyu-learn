## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning, 灰 *huī* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. The classical sense is "great, vast, expansive"
— a spirit or scope that is broad and generous — from which "to restore"
(恢复, restoring something to its full, original scope) later developed.

## Mnemonic
A mind 忄 as vast and unbounded as drifting ash 灰 on the wind.

## Notes
恢复 "to restore, to recover" is by far the dominant modern use — 恢复健康
"to recover one's health," 恢复正常 "to return to normal." The older "vast"
sense survives only in the literary 恢弘 "grand, magnificent in scope."

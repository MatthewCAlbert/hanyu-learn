## Etymology
Shuowen: a horizontal wooden bar (大木) fastened across an ox's horns 角 to keep it from goring — the origin of 衡 as "crossbar". 行 *xíng* supplies the sound (*xíng* → *héng*). From a cross-beam it became the beam of a balance, hence "to weigh" and "balance".

## Mnemonic
A cross-bar tied on the horns 角 so that the ox walks 行 in balance.

## Notes
行 is sound only here. 平衡 "balance", 均衡, 衡量 "weigh up", 抗衡 "counter", 度量衡 "weights and measures".

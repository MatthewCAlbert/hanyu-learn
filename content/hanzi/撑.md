## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to prop up — and 掌 *zhǎng* supplies the sound as a rough match (*zhǎng* → *chēng*). The older 撐 has 牚 *chèng* as the sound element; 掌 is a replacement variant, so the sound clue is approximate.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 supporting the palm 掌 of the person above.

## Notes
掌 is sound only here. 支撑 "support", 撑伞 "put up an umbrella", 撑船 "pole a boat", 撑腰 "back up", 撑不住 "can't hold out".

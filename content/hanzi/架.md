## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (wood) supplies the meaning; 加 *jiā* supplies the sound.
The original sense, 杙也，所以舉物 — "a stake, used for holding things up" —
is the direct ancestor of the modern "rack, frame, shelf."

## Mnemonic
Wood 木 added 加 to wood, built up into a frame.

## Notes
架 as a classifier (一架飞机 "a plane," 一架钢琴 "a piano") extends "a
supporting frame" to any large object that visibly rests on or is built
around a structural frame.

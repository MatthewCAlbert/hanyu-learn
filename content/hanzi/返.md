## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, 反 *fǎn* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged — and 反's own sense "to turn back, to reverse"
also echoes meaning. Shuowen's gloss 還也 "to return" matches the modern
sense directly.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 turned back 反 the way it came — returning.

## Notes
返 anchors 返回 "to return," 返程 "the return leg [of a trip]," and 返老还
童 "to regain one's youth" (a classical idiom, literally "return from old
age to childhood").

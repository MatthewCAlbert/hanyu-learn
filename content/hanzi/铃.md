## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 鈴: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning, 令 *lìng*
supplies the sound, shifted to *líng*. Shuowen's gloss describes a small
metal bell that rings out — 令丁, an onomatopoeic-flavoured name for its
sound — matching today's meaning directly.

## Mnemonic
Metal 钅 shaped to answer every command 令 with a ring.

## Notes
铃 names the object across contexts old and new: 门铃 "doorbell," 铃声
"ringtone," 风铃 "wind chime." Shares 令 with 领, 冷, 龄 — a productive
phonetic series across several finals.

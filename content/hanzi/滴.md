## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — to pour in drops — and 啇 *dì* supplies the sound (*dì* → *dī*). Shuowen glosses it as water dripping.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 falling from a quiet 啇 source, one drop at a time.

## Notes
啇 is sound only. Series: 敌 *dí*, 嫡 *dí*, 滴 *dī*, 摘 *zhāi*, 适 *shì*. 滴水 "dripping water", 一滴 "a drop", 水滴, 点滴 "bit by bit; an IV drip".

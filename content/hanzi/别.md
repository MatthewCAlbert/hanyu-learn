## Etymology
刂 (刀) "knife" gives the meaning; the left element was 冎 "bone" (now worn to
另). Shuowen: 分解也 "to cut apart" — separating flesh from bone. From "to
divide / part" grew "to take one's leave" (告别), "other, another" (别的,
别人), "to distinguish" (区别), and the prohibitive "don't" (别走).

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 parting flesh from bone: to separate; hence "another," and "don't."

## Notes
Two readings: *bié* (common), *biè* in 别扭 "awkward, contrary." The
prohibitive 别 is a contraction of 不要.

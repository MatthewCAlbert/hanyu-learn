## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: the character draws an ancient stemmed
serving vessel used for holding meat at ritual meals — a shape not
unlike a modern goblet, with a wide bowl on a tall foot.

**The "bean" sense is a phonetic loan.** The original word for "bean"
was 菽/尗 (the same 尗 seen in this app's entry for 叔); 豆 was borrowed
for its sound once the ritual vessel's own name faded from daily use,
possibly helped along by the vessel's rounded shape resembling a bean
pod.

## Mnemonic
A stemmed ritual dish, borrowed by sound alone to also mean "豆," bean.

## Notes
豆腐 (tofu, covered in this app's entry for 腐) and 土豆 ("potato," lit.
"earth-bean") both use 豆 in its modern borrowed sense — its original
"ritual vessel" meaning survives only in historical/archaeological
contexts.

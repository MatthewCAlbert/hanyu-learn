## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
回, means "to turn back, to turn away" — and 辟 *bì* supplies the sound.

"To turn away from" generalizes directly to "to avoid" (避免, 躲避) — the
same physical turning-away image applied to danger, a topic, or a
responsibility.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 that turns sharply away from the ruler's edict 辟 (辟, law/
decree): "避," to avoid.

## Notes
逃避 ("to escape/evade," pairing 避 with 逃 "to flee") and 避免 ("to
avoid," covered elsewhere in this app via 免) both build directly on this
character's "to turn away from" core.

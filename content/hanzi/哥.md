## Etymology
Two 可 stacked. Shuowen: 聲也。从二可 — a doubled 可, and originally the word
"to sing" (it is the source graph of 歌). The "elder brother" sense is a
separate, later borrowing: a northern kinship word for "father / brother"
entered Chinese in the Tang and was written with this ready graph, displacing
the classical 兄.

## Mnemonic
A voice raised twice over 可可 — borrowed to name the elder brother.

## Notes
兄 survives in compounds (兄弟, 兄长). 哥哥 (doubled, toneless second syllable)
is the address term; friendly 大哥, 帅哥.

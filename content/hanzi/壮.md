## Etymology
Pictophonetic in 壯: 士 (a grown man) supplies the meaning, 爿 *qiáng* the sound. Simplification wrote 爿 as 丬, which is the same part. The sound has drifted from *qiáng* to *zhuàng*.

## Mnemonic
A strong man 士 with a bed frame 丬 slung on his back.

## Notes
强壮, 壮观, 壮烈. Also the Zhuang people (壮族), by transliteration. 爿 is the left half of 牀/床 "bed"; 状 and 壮 both use the 丬 sound part.

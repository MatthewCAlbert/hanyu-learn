## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, and 兆 *zhào* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original sense, "to stir, to disturb" (撓), underlies the
reading *tiǎo*: to provoke or stir someone up (挑逗, 挑战, "to pick a
fight/issue a challenge").

The other reading, *tiāo*, covers a related but distinct hand action: "to
carry balanced on a shoulder pole" and, from sorting through a load bit by
bit, "to pick, to select" (挑选) — a hand sifting through options one at a
time.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 stirring things up, a sign 兆 (omen) of trouble to come: "挑," to
provoke — or, patiently, to pick through and select.

## Notes
Two readings split cleanly by sense: *tiǎo* for provoking/challenging
(挑战, 挑逗), *tiāo* for carrying/selecting (挑东西, 挑食 "to be a picky
eater").

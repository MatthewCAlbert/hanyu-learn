## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is a field marked into plots and thick with dots —
usually read as a densely planted field, or a finely carved surface. Shuowen:
密也 "dense, close-set." From "packed / thorough" grew "all around, a
circuit," "a week," "thorough / considerate" (周到), and the Zhou dynasty.

## Mnemonic
A field packed full to every corner: complete, all the way round.

## Notes
Senses: a circuit / lap (周长, 圆周), a week (周末, 上周, 周年), thorough
(周密, 周全), to relieve (周济). Also the surname Zhou.

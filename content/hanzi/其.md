## Etymology
Pictographic — a winnowing basket, the original of 箕. It was borrowed for the
classical pronoun "his / her / its / their," and for "that / the said," and
kept only those grammatical uses; 箕 (⺮ added) carries "basket / dustpan."

## Mnemonic
A winnowing basket — the graph handed over to "its, that."

## Notes
Modern uses are largely fixed: 其他 / 其它 "the others," 其中 "among them,"
其实 "in fact," 尤其 "especially," 与其 "rather than," 各得其所.

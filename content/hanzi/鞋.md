## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 革 "tanned leather" gives the meaning; 圭 *guī* gives the
sound (for *xié*). The older word was 屨; 鞋 (also written 鞵) took over.

## Mnemonic
Leather 革 shaped to the foot: a shoe.

## Notes
The 圭 phonetic also writes 街 *jiē*, 佳 *jiā*, 挂 *guà*, 娃 *wá*. Senses:
shoe (皮鞋, 布鞋, 拖鞋 slippers, 运动鞋). 鞋带 "shoelace"; 鞋垫 "insole".

## Etymology
Simplified from 誰. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning — it is a
question asked aloud — and 隹 *zhuī* "short-tailed bird" gives the sound
(a loose fit for *shuí*). Shuowen: 何也 "which one, who."

## Mnemonic
Asking in words 讠 which one: who?

## Notes
Two readings for the same word: literary *shuí*, colloquial *shéi*. The 隹
phonetic also writes 推 *tuī*, 堆 *duī*, 椎 *zhuī*, and (with sound worn away)
唯, 惟.

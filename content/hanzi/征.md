## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 彳 (a road, movement — the semantic component in 往, 得)
supplies the meaning, and 正 *zhèng* supplies the sound, an exact
family match (征/正 share the same root sound). This character's own
early sense was "to march, to go on a military campaign" (征战, 出征).

**Simplification merged in a second, originally distinct character.**
Traditional 徵 — a more complex character meaning "to summon, to levy, a
symptom/sign" (征兆, 特征) — was also simplified to this same shape,
despite having no etymological connection to the marching sense. Modern
征 therefore carries two separate ancestries at once, which is why this
entry keeps confidence at medium.

## Mnemonic
A road 彳 marched down in strict, correct 正 formation: "征," to campaign
— and, from an unrelated merged character, a sign or symptom summoned
into notice.

## Notes
征服 ("to conquer") and 特征 ("a distinguishing feature/characteristic")
look like they should share one etymology but do not — one descends from
the marching-campaign 征, the other from the merged-in 徵.

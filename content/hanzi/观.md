## Etymology
Simplified from 觀. 见 "to see" gives the meaning; the traditional phonetic is
雚 *guàn*, replaced in the simplified form by 又. Shuowen: 諦視也 "to look
carefully at, to contemplate." From "to observe" grew "a view, an outlook"
(观点, 世界观) and — read *guàn* — "a Daoist temple" (道观).

## Mnemonic
Taking a considered look 见 at something.

## Notes
The same 雚 → 又 shortcut is in 欢 / 歡, 劝 / 勸. Senses: watch / view
(参观, 观察, 观众), an outlook (乐观, 观念), a sight (奇观, 壮观).

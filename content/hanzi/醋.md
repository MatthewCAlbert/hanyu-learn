## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 酉 (wine vessel) supplies the meaning and 昔 *xī* supplies the sound (*xī* → *cù*). Shuowen defines 醋 as the guest pouring a drink back to the host — a return toast — and lists 酢 as the word for the sour liquid. The two later swapped places: today 醋 is vinegar, and 酢 survives mainly in the sense of "a toast back" (酬酢).

## Mnemonic
Wine 酉 aged for a long time ago 昔 until sour: vinegar.

## Notes
昔 is sound only. 醋 *cù* "vinegar", 吃醋 "be jealous" (literally "eat vinegar"), 醋意 "jealousy", 醋溜, 山西老陈醋. Series: 错 *cuò*, 措 *cuò*, 惜 *xī*, 借 *jiè*.

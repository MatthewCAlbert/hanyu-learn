## Etymology
Traditional 躍 is pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to leap — and 翟 *dí/yuè* supplies the sound. Simplification replaced 翟 with 夭 *yāo*, a closer-sounding and simpler part, so the new phonetic still works as a rough sound clue.

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 kicking up in a little leap 夭.

## Notes
夭 is sound only here. 跳跃 "jump", 活跃 "active", 飞跃 "leap", 踊跃 "eager". 跃 and 耀 are in the same 翟 family in the traditional forms.

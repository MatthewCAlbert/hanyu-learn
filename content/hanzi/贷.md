## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 貸: 贝 (shell/money) supplies the meaning, 代
*dài* supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 施也 "to
give, to extend" matches the modern "to lend/borrow" directly — money
extended from one party to another, to be returned in turn (代, "in
place of/instead," echoing the exchange).

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 handed over, to be returned in its place 代 later.

## Notes
贷 anchors 贷款 "loan," 借贷 "to borrow/lend," and 高利贷 "usury, predatory
lending" — the everyday vocabulary of formal borrowing and lending.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 心 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning, 夗 *yuàn* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 恚也 "resentment, anger" matches
the modern sense closely — a stable character with little drift.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 curled up 夗 tight around an old grudge.

## Notes
怨 pairs with 恨 as near-synonyms for resentment, though 怨 leans toward
"to blame, to complain" (抱怨 "to complain," 埋怨 "to grumble at") more than
outright hatred — a milder, more everyday register than 恨.

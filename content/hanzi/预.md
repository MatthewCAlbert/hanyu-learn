## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 页 (head) supplies the meaning; 予 *yǔ* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original gloss, 安也, "at ease, at peace," is close to
an unrelated, far more common character, 豫 (also "at ease," and
"beforehand, in advance") — Shuowen notes classical texts commonly used
豫 for this sense instead. The modern "in advance, to prepare ahead of
time" descends from that closely related 豫, not cleanly from 預's own
narrow entry.

## Mnemonic
A head 页, given 予 the chance to think ahead before anything happens:
to prepare in advance.

## Notes
预 is almost entirely a bound prefix in modern Chinese — 预习 "to
preview [a lesson]," 预报 "a forecast," 预防 "to prevent," 预订 "to book
in advance" — always marking that something is done ahead of the event
itself.

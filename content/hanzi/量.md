## Etymology
The early graph shows a sack or bundle with 日 above — weighing a load of
grain. The modern shape (旦 over 里) is worn and no longer readable as its
parts. Shuowen: 稱輕重也 "to weigh heavy against light."

## Mnemonic
A filled sack hoisted to judge its weight: measuring.

## Notes
*liàng*: quantity / amount (数量, 重量, 产量), capacity or tolerance (气量,
胆量, 酒量), and a measure word category (量词); *liáng*: to measure
(量体温, 量身高, 丈量, 打量 to size up). 力量 "strength"; 商量 "to discuss."

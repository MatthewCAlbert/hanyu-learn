## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 穴 (a cave, hole — the same semantic component covered in
this app's entry for 穷, where it likewise marks "an extremity") supplies
the meaning — Shuowen defines it using that very character, 窮, "to reach
the very bottom/end" — and 九 *jiǔ* supplies the sound.

"To reach the deepest point of a cave" generalizes directly to "to
investigate thoroughly, to get to the bottom of" (研究, 究竟, both
covered elsewhere in this app).

## Mnemonic
A cave 穴 explored all the way to its ninth 九 (i.e. furthest) chamber:
"究," to investigate thoroughly.

## Notes
究 shares its semantic root with 穷 (poor, exhausted) — both picture
reaching the absolute end of a cave, one applied to knowledge, the other
to resources.

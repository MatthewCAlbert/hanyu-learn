## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 乃 *nǎi* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's gloss, 因也, is "to follow on from, to continue on the basis of" —
one action following from a previous one, which is the root of the modern
"still, yet."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 who, after all this time 乃, is still here.

## Notes
仍然 and 仍旧 both mean "still" and are close to interchangeable — 旧 adds
a note of "as before, unchanged," while 然 is the more general continuity
marker also seen in 突然, 当然.

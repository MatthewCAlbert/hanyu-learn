## Etymology
The cooking word is attested in Shuowen as 煼, "to fry", pictophonetic with 火 (fire). 炒 is the later, simpler replacement graph, with 少 as a rough sound substitute. 少 here carries no sense of "few". The extended sense "to speculate, to hype" (炒股, 炒作) is a modern metaphor from frying and stirring.

## Mnemonic
A little 少 fire 火 under the wok.

## Notes
少 is a weak sound clue (*shǎo/chǎo*). 炒菜 "stir-fry dishes", 炒饭 "fried rice", 炒股 "speculate in stocks", 炒作 "hype", 炒鱿鱼 "fire (someone)".

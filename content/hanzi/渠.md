## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — a channel where water is held — and 榘 supplies the sound, abbreviated in the modern graph to 巨 over 木 (the abbreviated 榘 *jǔ* → 渠 *qú*). So the parts 巨 and 木 together are a sound element, not "a wooden ditch".

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 running through a gutter marked out by a big 巨 tree 木.

## Notes
水渠 "irrigation channel", 渠道 "channel", 沟渠 "ditches", 渠 as a classical "he/she" pronoun. Series: 渠 *qú*, 距 *jù*, 矩 *jǔ*.

## Etymology
Pictographic — three drifting lines, representing rising vapour or cloud.
Shuowen: 雲气也 "cloud-vapour." From "vapour / air" spread "breath," "the qi
(vital energy)," "smell," "weather" (天气), "temperament / anger" (脾气,
生气), and "airs / manner" (气派).

## Mnemonic
Three wisps rising: vapour, air, breath.

## Notes
气 is Kangxi radical 84 and the phonetic in 氣 (its own full form) and 汽
*qì* "steam." The "anger" sense: 生气, 气人, 别气我.

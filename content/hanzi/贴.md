## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (money, valuables) supplies the meaning, and 占 *zhàn*
supplies an approximate sound (*tiē* and *zhàn* diverge more than most
phonetic pairs in the modern reading; not classically sourced here, since
贴 postdates Shuowen). The original sense was financial — "to pawn, to
mortgage" — before shifting to the now-dominant "to stick, to paste" (贴
邮票, to stick on a stamp) and "to subsidize, to be close to" (补贴,
体贴).

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 staked on a claim 占: pawned, and stuck to that claim — "贴," to
stick.

## Notes
体贴 ("considerate, thoughtful," lit. "body-close") shows the more abstract
"close to, attentive to" branch of 贴's meaning, distinct from the literal
"to stick/paste" branch most learners meet first.

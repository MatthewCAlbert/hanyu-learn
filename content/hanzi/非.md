## Etymology
Pictographic — two wings, or two facing sides, turned away from each other.
Shuowen: 違也 "to go against; from the two wings of 飛, taken as facing
opposite ways." From "opposed / contrary" grew the negative "not, is not,"
and "wrong / a fault" (是非).

## Mnemonic
Two halves turned back to back: not this, not so.

## Notes
非 is Kangxi radical 175 and the phonetic in 悲 *bēi*, 排 *pái*, 罪 *zuì*,
啡 *fēi*. 非常 "extraordinary → very"; 除非 "unless"; 非…不可 "must".

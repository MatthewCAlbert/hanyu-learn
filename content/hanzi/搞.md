## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — 搞 is a hand-verb, "to do, to
handle." 高 *gāo* supplies only the sound (*gǎo* vs *gāo* differ in tone); there
is no "tall" in the idea of doing something.

搞 does not appear in Shuowen Jiezi — it is a comparatively late character that
rose from northern colloquial speech into standard Mandarin, which is also why
its meaning is so broad and resists a single gloss.

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 reaching up high 高 to sort something out — whatever it takes, get it
done.

## Notes
One of the most semantically bleached hand-verbs in modern Mandarin — 搞什么
"what's going on," 搞清楚 "figure out," 搞好 "get right," 搞砸 "screw up" — closer
to English "do/handle" than any single translation. Compare 抢, another 扌 verb
where the phonetic carries sound only.

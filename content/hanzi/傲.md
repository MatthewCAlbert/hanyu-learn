## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — haughty, arrogant — and 敖 *áo* supplies the sound.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 who roams about 敖 with nose in the air.

## Notes
敖 is sound only; compare 熬 *áo*, 遨 *áo*, 嗷 *áo*. 骄傲 "proud/arrogant" (positive or negative), 傲慢 "arrogant", 傲气, 傲人 "impressive".

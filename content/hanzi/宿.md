## Etymology
Ideographic/pictophonetic. Shuowen glosses 宿 as 止也, "to stop, to rest,"
and analyses it as 宀 (roof) over 㐬 — itself a picture of a person lying on
a mat — giving "a person resting on a mat under a roof." The modern shape's
lower portion is a graphic evolution of that mat-and-person picture, not
literally "100" as it might superficially resemble.

## Mnemonic
A person resting on a mat, sheltered under a roof 宀 for the night.

## Notes
Two readings by sense: *sù* is everyday ("to stay overnight," 住宿
"lodging," 宿舍 "dormitory"); *xiù* names a constellation-lodge in the
classical 28 lunar mansions (星宿) — the night sky's own resting-places for
the moon's monthly journey.

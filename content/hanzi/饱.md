## Etymology
Simplified from 飽. Pictophonetic: 饣 (食) "food" gives the meaning; 包 *bāo*
gives the sound (for *bǎo*) — and a full belly is a wrapped, packed one.
Shuowen: 猒也 "sated."

## Mnemonic
Food 饣 packed in 包 until there is no more room: full.

## Notes
The 包 phonetic also writes 抱 *bào*, 泡 *pào*, 跑 *pǎo*, 炮 *pào*, 饱 itself.
Senses: full / sated (吃饱了, 温饱, 饱餐), plump and full (饱满, 谷粒饱满),
and "to the full" (饱览, 饱经风霜, 大饱眼福).

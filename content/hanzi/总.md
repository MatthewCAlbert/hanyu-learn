## Etymology
The traditional form 總 was pictophonetic: 糸 (silk thread) supplied the
meaning "to gather and bind together" (聚束也); 悤 *cōng* supplied the sound.

Simplification cut the 糸 semantic component entirely, leaving only a
simplified 悤 to stand for the whole character. That is the reverse of the
usual pattern in this dataset (where simplification strips the *phonetic*
and keeps the meaning side, as in 过): here the *meaning-bearing* 糸 is gone,
and what is left, 丷 over 口 over 心, is really just old 悤 with the dot
regularised away. Declaring a semantic or phonetic component on today's
shape would misdescribe it, since neither remaining piece was ever the
"binding thread" that gave 總 its sense.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 that keeps count of everything: the "overall, in total."

## Notes
From "to gather into a bundle" the meaning extended to "overall, general"
(总的来说) and then to "always, invariably" (总是) — gathering many
instances into one summary judgment.

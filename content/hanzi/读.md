## Etymology
Simplified from 讀. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning — reading was reading
aloud. Shuowen: 誦書也 "to recite a text." The phonetic was 𧶠 *yù* (the
element behind 续 / 續, 牍 / 牘, 犊 / 犢); simplification writes it as 卖, which
looks like 賣 *mài* but is not the same element, and the sound clue is
obscured.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 sounded out: reading aloud.

## Notes
Two readings: *dú* "to read / study / attend school" (读书, 阅读), *dòu* "a
pause in a sentence" (句读). Do not read the right side as 卖 "to sell."

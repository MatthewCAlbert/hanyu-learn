## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart, mind) supplies the meaning and 合 *hé* the sound. Shuowen's gloss is "using the mind"; "exactly, appropriately" is the later development. 合 and 恰 differ in initial (h- vs q-), a long-standing sound drift.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 that fits 合 exactly, no more nor less.

## Notes
合 is sound only; the series: 洽 *qià*, 恰 *qià*, 给, 拾. Compounds: 恰当, 恰好, 恰巧, 恰恰 "precisely".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 隹 *zhuī* gives the sound (for
*tuī*). Shuowen: 排也 "to push."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 flat against something, shoving: to push.

## Notes
Senses: push (推门, 推车), push forward / promote (推动, 推广, 推行),
infer / work out (推理, 推测, 类推), nominate (推举, 推选, 推荐),
decline / put off (推辞, 推脱, 推迟). 推敲 "to weigh one's words."

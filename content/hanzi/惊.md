## Etymology
The traditional form 驚 was pictophonetic with a very different semantic:
马 (馬, horse) supplied the meaning — horses startle easily, and "a
startled horse" was the base image — while 敬 *jìng* supplied the sound.

**Simplification changed the semantic entirely.** 马 was dropped in favor
of 忄 (heart), reframing fright as an emotional/inner-state matter rather
than specifically an animal's reaction, and 敬 was reduced to just its 京
fragment, which no longer functions as a full phonetic component. Because
neither surviving piece is a real inherited semantic-plus-phonetic pair
from the traditional character, this entry declares only the new semantic
京's neighbor 忄, and keeps confidence medium given how much changed.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 leaping up as if it just glimpsed the capital 京 unexpectedly:
"惊," startled.

## Notes
惊 shows the same kind of simplification-driven semantic shift as 权 and
奖 show phonetic loss — here the change is to *which* domain (animal
behavior vs. inner feeling) the character is grounded in, not just to its
sound clue.

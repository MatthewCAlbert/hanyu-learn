## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, and 余 (covered
elsewhere in this app) supplies an approximate sound toward *tú*. This
entry cannot cite a Shuowen entry matching the modern character
precisely, so it keeps confidence at medium.

"A road, a route" (道路) extends figuratively to "a path in life,
prospects ahead" (前途, "one's future/prospects," lit. "the road
ahead").

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 following the road, with whatever remains 余 of the journey
still ahead: "途," a route.

## Notes
前途 ("prospects, future," lit. "the road ahead") and 用途 ("use,
purpose," lit. "the route [something's] use follows") both extend this
character's "road" image into the abstract.

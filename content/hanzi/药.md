## Etymology
Simplified from 藥. 艹 "plant" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 治病艸 "the herb
that treats illness." The traditional phonetic is 樂 *yuè* / *lè*; the
simplified form swaps in 约 (約) *yuē* as a lighter phonetic for *yào*.

## Mnemonic
The plant 艹 you take to get well: medicine.

## Notes
Senses: medicine / drug (吃药, 中药, 西药), certain chemicals (火药 gunpowder,
农药 pesticide, 炸药 explosive), to poison / treat with chemicals (药老鼠).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 妾 *qiè* gives the sound (a Middle
Chinese match for *jiē*). Shuowen: 交也 "to join, to bring together."

## Mnemonic
One hand 扌 reaching to meet another: to connect.

## Notes
Senses: join / connect (接触, 连接), receive / take (接电话, 接受, 接到),
meet / pick up (接人, 迎接), carry on (接着, 接下来).

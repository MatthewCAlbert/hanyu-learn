## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 曷 *hé* gives the sound (for
*kě*). Shuowen: 盡也 "used up, dried out" — the original sense was of water
running dry; it shifted to the person who feels the lack: thirsty.

## Mnemonic
The water 氵 all gone: parched.

## Notes
The 曷 phonetic also writes 喝 *hē* (drink), 歇 *xiē* (rest), 竭 *jié*
(exhaust), 揭 *jiē* (lift off). 口渴 "thirsty"; 渴望 "to thirst for, to
crave."

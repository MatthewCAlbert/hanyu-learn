## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 顆: 頁 (head — the same component that gives 头,
顶, 项 their "head/top" sense) supplies the meaning, 果 *guǒ* supplies the
sound, shifted to *kē*. The original sense was a small, round, head-like
lump; it generalised into the classifier for anything small and roughly
spherical — pearls, teeth, hearts, satellites, stars.

## Mnemonic
A small round head 页 sized exactly like a piece of fruit 果 — hence one
"unit" of anything round.

## Notes
Easy to confuse with 棵 kē, the classifier for whole trees/plants (木 semantic,
same 果 phonetic). 一颗树 is wrong; 一棵树 is correct — 颗 is for small round
things, 棵 is for standing plants.

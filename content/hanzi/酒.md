## Etymology
酉 is itself a pictograph of a wine jar; 氵 "liquid" was added to name the
drink rather than the vessel. Shuowen notes 酉 also carries the sound
(*yǒu* → *jiǔ*). Meaning: alcoholic drink — wine, spirits, beer.

## Mnemonic
Liquid 氵 in the wine jar 酉: 酒.

## Notes
酉 heads the "wine / fermentation" radical (醉 drunk, 酿 to brew, 醋 vinegar,
酸 sour). Senses: any alcoholic drink (白酒, 啤酒, 红酒, 敬酒), a banquet
(喜酒, 酒席). 酒吧 "bar."

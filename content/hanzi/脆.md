## Etymology
Ideographic: ⺼ (flesh/body) paired with 危 *wēi* ("dangerous, precarious")
— 危 supplies the sound and also a fitting echo of meaning, since something
brittle is flesh or material in constant danger of snapping. Not in
Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
Flesh ⺼ so fragile it's in constant danger 危 of breaking.

## Notes
脆 spans texture (脆皮 "crispy skin," 又酥又脆 "flaky and crunchy") and
fragility generally (脆弱 "fragile, vulnerable" — of objects, health, or
emotional state) — brittleness as both a culinary virtue and a
vulnerability.

## Etymology
Shuowen: 素 is plain white fine silk, from 糸 (silk) with an abbreviated top element, "taking its gloss". Plain, undyed silk gives the whole family of meanings: plain, unadorned, original, basic (元素, 要素, 因素), and, by extension, vegetarian food (素食). The modern top element 龶 is a corruption of the old shape, so the folk image of "silk hanging from a tree" is not how the character was built.

## Mnemonic
Undyed silk 糸 hanging out to dry, with nothing added — plain.

## Notes
素质 "inherent quality", 维生素 "vitamin", 素菜 "vegetarian dish". Often contrasted with 荤 (meat dishes).

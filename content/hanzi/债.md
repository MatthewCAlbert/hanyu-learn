## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — someone who owes — and 責 (simplified 责) *zé* supplies the sound (*zé* → *zhài*). 責 itself meant "to demand payment", so it is also the idea of obligation. A later graph, absent from the original Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 held responsible 责: in debt.

## Notes
责 is both sound and meaning. 债务 "debt", 债券 "bond", 负债 "be in debt", 欠债 "owe a debt", 国债 "national debt".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen: 又 (a hand) supplies the meaning — the
original sense was simply "to pick up, to gather" — and 尗 *shū*
(itself an old form of 菽, "beans") supplies the sound, doubling as the
thing being gathered: a hand picking up beans.

The kinship sense "uncle" (father's younger brother) is a separate
development with no clearly attested bridge from "to gather beans" —
most likely a phonetic loan onto this shape — so this entry keeps
confidence at medium for the meaning as a whole, though the
"gathering beans" etymology itself is securely sourced.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 gathering up beans 尗: "叔," to pick up — and, unrelated in
origin but sharing the shape, "uncle."

## Notes
叔叔 (paternal uncle, younger than one's father) is the everyday
address term; 叔父 is the more formal/written equivalent.

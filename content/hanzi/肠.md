## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 腸: ⺼ (flesh/body-part) supplies the meaning, 昜
*yáng* supplies the sound, shifted to *cháng*. Shuowen's gloss 大小腸也 "the
large and small intestines" names the organ directly, with no figurative
drift in the character's core meaning.

## Mnemonic
The long, winding organ ⺼ that stretches out like sunlight 昜 unspooling.

## Notes
肠 stays anatomical (大肠 "large intestine," 小肠 "small intestine," 肠胃
"digestive system") and extends to food: 香肠 "sausage," 肠粉 "rice-noodle
roll" — casings and dishes shaped like the organ they're named after.

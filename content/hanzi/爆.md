## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 火 (fire) supplies the meaning — burning, bursting — and 暴 *bào* supplies the sound; here 暴 also fits the meaning "sudden, violent", a rare phonetic that is semantically relevant too.

## Mnemonic
A violent 暴 fire 火 that bursts out suddenly.

## Notes
爆炸, 爆发, 爆竹 "firecracker", 爆满 "full house". 暴 *bào* is also the base of 曝 *pù*. 爆 is a good example of a phonetic that carries both sound and sense.

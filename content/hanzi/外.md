## Etymology
夕 "dusk" + 卜 "to divine." Shuowen's account: divination was properly done at
dawn; a divination taken in the evening (夕) fell "outside" the normal
practice — hence "outside, external." The bronze graph and the exact logic are
debated, so confidence is medium.

## Mnemonic
Reading the cracks 卜 after dark 夕 — off the beaten track: outside.

## Notes
Senses: outside / outer (外边, 门外), foreign (外国, 外语), other / beyond
(此外, 意外), and maternal-side kin (外公, 外甥). Opposite 里 or 内.

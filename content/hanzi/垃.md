## Etymology
As already covered in this app's entry for the word 垃圾, 垃 has no
independent meaning of its own — it exists essentially only to write the
first syllable of that one native dialectal word, its shape (土 plus 立)
chosen for an approximate sound rather than for any component meaning.
Confidence stays low because no independent semantic role can honestly
be assigned.

## Mnemonic
No real mnemonic to offer — 垃 is a sound-only character, the other half
of 垃圾, and essentially nowhere else.

## Notes
Like its partner 圾, 垃 essentially never appears outside 垃圾 in ordinary
reading.

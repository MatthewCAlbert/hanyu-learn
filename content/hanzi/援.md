## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to pull — and 爰 *yuán* supplies the sound. 爰 is itself an older graph of two hands passing a rod, "to pull, to lead", so the right-hand element is both the sound and the earlier word. The sense "to help, to cite" follows from pulling someone up.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pulling a rod 爰 to haul someone out.

## Notes

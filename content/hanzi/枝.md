## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree) supplies the meaning — a branch growing off the trunk — and 支 *zhī* supplies the sound. 支 is itself the older word for "branch" (a hand holding a twig), so it is both sound and meaning.

## Mnemonic
A tree's 木 branch, a twig held by a hand 支.

## Notes
支 is both. 树枝 "branch", 枝条 "twig", 枝叶, 枝头. As a measure word for pens and sticks (一枝笔), with 支 as the alternate.

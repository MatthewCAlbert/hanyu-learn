## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is generally read as a person carrying or handling a tool
— 㠯, an implement in use, beside 人. Shuowen: 用也 "to use." From "by means
of" grew the whole preposition-and-connective family: "with / by," "according
to," "in order to," "because," and the frame words 以后 / 以前 / 以上 / 以下.

## Mnemonic
Taking something up to use it: "by means of."

## Notes
以 A 为 B "take A as B"; 以…来说 "as far as … is concerned"; 所以 "therefore";
可以 "can." Largely a function word.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree) supplies the meaning, and 果 *guǒ* supplies an
approximate sound. 棵 functions almost entirely as a measure word rather
than a free noun, and its coinage postdates Shuowen, so its sound history
is less firmly documented; medium confidence reflects that gap.

## Mnemonic
A tree 木 heavy with fruit 果, counted one by one: "棵," the classifier for
plants and trees.

## Notes
棵 classifies individual plants and trees (一棵树, "a tree"; 一棵草, "a
blade of grass") — for produce harvested from a plant, other classifiers
like 个 or 只 take over instead.

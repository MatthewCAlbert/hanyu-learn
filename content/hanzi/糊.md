## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 米 (rice/grain) supplies the meaning, 胡 *hú* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The original object is a
grain-based paste or gruel — thick, sticky, and shapeless — which is
exactly why "muddled, confused" (糊涂) grew out of the same character: a
mind as indistinct as a bowl of paste.

## Mnemonic
Rice 米 boiled down to a shapeless paste, as foreign 胡 and unclear as a
stranger's face.

## Notes
Two readings by use: *hú* is the everyday adjective/noun (糊涂 "confused,"
糨糊 "paste"); *hù* survives in 糊弄 "to fob off/deceive carelessly," a
softer, more colloquial register of the same "indistinct, sloppy" idea.

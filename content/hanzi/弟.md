## Etymology
Pictographic — a leather cord wound in even turns around a stake or a bow.
Shuowen: 韋束之次弟也 "the order of a bound cord" — the word "sequence, order"
(later written 第). "Younger brother" grew from "the next one in the birth
order."

## Mnemonic
A cord wound round in order: next in line — the younger brother.

## Notes
弟 gave 第 "the ordinal prefix" and 递 / 遞 "to hand on in turn." Two readings:
*dì* (弟弟, 兄弟), *tì* "to defer to an elder brother" (孝弟, = 悌).

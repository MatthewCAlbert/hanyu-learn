## Etymology
Built on 廴 (a stretched-out roadway component, the semantic core of
建, 廷, and other characters about extended movement or space), depicting
travel drawn out over a long distance. Shuowen marks a stroke element as
phonetic, which is unusual enough that this entry does not treat the
sound mechanism as fully settled, keeping confidence at medium.

"A long journey" generalized to the abstract "to extend, to prolong, to
delay" (延长, 延迟, "to postpone").

## Mnemonic
A road 廴 stretched out so long that reaching the end keeps getting put
off: "延," to extend or delay.

## Notes
延长 ("to extend/lengthen," of time or distance) and 延迟 ("to delay, to
postpone") both build directly on this character's "drawn-out, extended"
core sense.

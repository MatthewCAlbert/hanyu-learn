## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 身 (body) supplies the meaning, 朵 *duǒ* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen; a later character built straightforwardly
from body + sound to mean "to hide (one's body), to dodge."

## Mnemonic
A body 身 folding up small like a flower bud 朵, tucked out of sight.

## Notes
躲 is the everyday verb for hiding or avoiding: 躲起来 "to hide," 躲雨 "to
take shelter from rain," 躲避 "to avoid/evade." Distinct from 藏 cáng, which
covers concealing an object as much as a person.

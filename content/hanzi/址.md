## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth, ground) supplies the meaning, and 止 *zhǐ*
supplies the sound, an exact match — 止's own root sense, "to stop, a
foot planted," fits naturally with the idea of a fixed location as well.

"A fixed site, a foundation" (旧址, "a former site") is the base sense,
narrowly specialized today into "an address" (地址, covered elsewhere in
this app) and, by extension, "a web address" (网址).

## Mnemonic
Ground 土 marked by a foot planted 止 and staying put: "址," a fixed
site.

## Notes
址 almost never appears outside 地址 and 网址 — unlike most characters, it
carries very little independent life of its own in everyday vocabulary.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 走 "to run" gives the meaning; 召 *zhào* gives the sound (for
*chāo*). Shuowen: 跳也 "to leap." From "to leap over" grew "to exceed, to
surpass" and the prefix "super-, ultra-, hyper-."

## Mnemonic
Running 走 and leaping clear over: to exceed.

## Notes
The 召 phonetic also writes 招 *zhāo*, 照 *zhào*, 绍 / 紹 *shào*, 昭 *zhāo*.
Senses: overtake / exceed (超过, 超速, 超载), surpass (超越), super- (超人,
超市, 超级).

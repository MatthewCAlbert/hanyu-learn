## Etymology
Not securely attested. The oracle-bone graph is usually read as a bent arm at
the elbow, or a hook — a shape "curved and drawn out to its limit," as Shuowen
puts it. It was borrowed for the number nine. Whatever it depicted, 九 is not
a tally of nine.

## Mnemonic
A crooked elbow, bent as far as it will go: borrowed for "nine."

## Notes
九 is the largest single-digit number and stands for "many / the utmost" in
idiom: 九牛一毛, 九死一生, 三教九流. 九 sounds like 久 "long-lasting," so it is
a favoured number.

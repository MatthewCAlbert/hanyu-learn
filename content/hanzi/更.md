## Etymology
攴 "a hand with a stick — to act on" with 丙 *bǐng* for sound. Shuowen: 改也
"to alter, to replace." From "to change / take turns" grew "a watch of the
night" (三更半夜 — the nightwatch that changes hands) and the comparative
adverb "still more, further" (更好).

## Mnemonic
Striking 攴 the old thing out and swapping in the new: to change; and so,
"even more."

## Notes
Two readings: *gèng* "even more / further" (更加, 更多), *gēng* "to change / a
night-watch / to experience" (更换, 打更, 少不更事).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning and 延 *yán* supplies the sound (*yán* → *dàn*). Shuowen's sense is boastful, extravagant speech; "absurd" (荒诞) keeps it. "Birth" (诞生) is an early loan, found already in the Odes, via the sense "big, extensive".

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 stretched 延 beyond belief; and a birth that is a big event.

## Notes
延 is sound only. 诞生 "be born", 诞辰 "birthday (formal)", 圣诞节 "Christmas", 荒诞 "absurd". The "birth" sense is far from the "boast" sense, but both are old.

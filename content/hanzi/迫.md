## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
is simply "near, close" — and 白 *bái* supplies the sound.

"To press close" generalizes to "to force, to compel" (强迫, 逼迫) and
"urgent" (急迫, 迫切) — something so close it presses upon you, leaving
no room to delay.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 closing in so near it turns the face white 白 with urgency:
"迫," to press — or urgent.

## Notes
强迫 ("to force, to compel") and 迫切 ("urgent, pressing") both build on
this character's core "pressing close, leaving no room" sense.

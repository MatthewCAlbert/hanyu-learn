## Etymology
女 "woman" gives the meaning; 生 *shēng* gives the sound (for *xìng*) and the
sense "born of." Shuowen: 人所生也 "what one is born into" — the oldest
surnames (姜, 姬, 姚…) descended through the female line, and many carry 女.

## Mnemonic
The line a person is born 生 into, once reckoned through the mother 女: the
surname.

## Notes
Senses: family name / surname (姓名, 贵姓, 复姓), and the verb "to be
surnamed" (我姓王, 他姓什么). 百姓 "the common people" (lit. "the hundred
surnames").

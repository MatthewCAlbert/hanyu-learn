## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 盤: 皿 (vessel, dish) supplies the
meaning, and 般 *bān* supplied the sound.

**The sound clue is gone in the simplified form.** Simplification kept only
舟 — the left-hand half of the phonetic 般 itself (般 = 舟 + 殳) — and
dropped 殳. So 舟 here is a fragment of the old phonetic, not the "boat"
radical doing new work, and on its own it gives no reliable hint toward
*pán*.

"Plate, dish" extended to any flat, circular surface: 磨盘 (millstone), 棋盘
(game board), and then abstractly to "to sort out, to itemize" (盘点, to take
inventory of stock laid out for inspection).

## Mnemonic
A dish 皿 balanced like a boat 舟 riding flat on water: a "盘," plate — steady
and level.

## Notes
The verb sense "to check/sort through" (盘问, 盘算) comes from laying goods
out on a tray to inspect them piece by piece — the same flat-surface image,
extended to an action performed on it.

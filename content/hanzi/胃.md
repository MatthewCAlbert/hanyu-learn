## Etymology
Pictographic/ideographic. Shuowen: 穀府也, "the storehouse of grain [in the
body]," and calls the graph a picture: the grid-like 田 shape represents food
sitting inside the stomach sac, drawn over ⺼ (flesh/body-part radical).
The top is not "abdominal muscles," as a common modern folk gloss has it —
it is the contents, not the surrounding tissue.

## Mnemonic
A sac of flesh ⺼ holding a stored meal, checkered 田 like grain in a
granary.

## Notes
⺼ is the "body-part" form of 肉 (meat/flesh), seen across the organ
vocabulary: 胸, 肚, 肝, 肺. 胃 anchors 胃口 "appetite" (literally "stomach's
mouth") and 胃炎 "gastritis."

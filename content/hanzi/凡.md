## Etymology
Shuowen explains 凡 as "the sum total", written from 二 with a hook. Oracle and bronze forms look quite different — a tray, mould or plate shape — and later scholars suggest 凡 may depict a container used as a general noun (compare 盘). The meaning "all, in general" is clear; the origin of the shape is not. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A plain flat dish: nothing special, an everyday object.

## Notes
凡是 "every", 平凡 "ordinary", 非凡 "extraordinary", 凡人 "mortal", 凡事. 凡 also occurs as a sound part in 帆 *fān*, 梵 *fàn*, 矾 *fán*.

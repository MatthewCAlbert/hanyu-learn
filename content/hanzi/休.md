## Etymology
An associative compound: 亻 "person" leaning against 木 "tree." Shuowen:
息止也。从人依木 — "to rest and stop; a person resting against a tree." The
oracle-bone graph shows exactly this. Both parts carry meaning; neither is
phonetic.

## Mnemonic
A traveller 亻 leaning on a tree 木 to rest.

## Notes
From "rest" come 休息, 退休 (retire), 休假 (take leave), and the literary 休
"do not!" (休想 "don't even think about it").

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 忄 (心) "heart" gives the meaning; 青 *qīng* gives the sound
(for *qíng*).

## Mnemonic
What stirs in the heart 忄: feeling.

## Notes
The 青 phonetic also writes 清 *qīng*, 请 / 請 *qǐng*, 晴 *qíng*, 睛 *jīng*,
精 *jīng*. Senses: emotion (感情, 热情, 情绪), affection / love (爱情, 情人,
谈情), circumstances (情况, 实情, 案情), reason / favour (人情, 求情, 情面).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 爪 *zhuǎ/zhǎo* supplies the
sound. 爪 was itself originally the verb "to grasp" — the oracle-bone form
is a hand reaching downward — before it narrowed to mean only the noun
"claw, talon." Once that happened, 扌 was added to make a new character,
抓, to carry on the old verb.

Confidence is medium because 爪 still visibly means "claw," so it reads as
carrying meaning here too, not sound alone — a case where the phonetic
component keeps a residual, distracting sense of its own.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 curling like a claw 爪 to grab hold of something.

## Notes

is close to what 爪 alone meant before it specialised down to "claw."

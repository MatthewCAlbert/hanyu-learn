## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — to bury — and 里, an abbreviation of 貍, supplies the sound (*mái*, close to *lí/lǐ*). Shuowen glosses it as burying. The sound match is partial, so it is a loose hint.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 piled on a village 里 buried underneath.

## Notes
里 is sound only. Two readings: *mái* (埋葬, 埋藏) and *mán* in 埋怨 "to blame". 埋伏 "ambush", 埋头 "bury oneself in".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — to pour in more — and 忝 *tiǎn* supplies the sound. 添 is a late graph, listed in the additions to Shuowen. The connection between "add" and water is the topping-up image.

## Mnemonic
Topping a cup with water 氵 and a little shame 忝 at not filling it.

## Notes
忝 is sound only. 添加 "add", 增添 "increase", 添麻烦 "cause trouble", 画蛇添足 "gild the lily" (adding feet to a snake).

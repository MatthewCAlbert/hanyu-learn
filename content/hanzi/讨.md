## Etymology
Simplified from 討. Associative: 讠 (言) "word, command" beside 寸 "hand,
measure / rule." Shuowen: 治也 "to bring to order" — issuing a decree and
enforcing it. From "to chastise" grew "to demand" and "to seek."

## Mnemonic
Backing a command 讠 with a firm hand 寸: to call to account — and to press
for what is owed.

## Notes
Senses: send a punitive force / denounce (讨伐, 声讨, 征讨), demand or beg
(讨债, 讨饭, 讨教, 讨个说法), marry a wife (讨老婆), incur (讨厌, 讨嫌,
自讨没趣), curry (讨好, 讨巧), and discuss (讨论, 商讨, 探讨).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 囗 (an enclosure — the same component that frames 国, 园, 图)
supplies the meaning "a bounded ring or area," and 卷 *juǎn/quān*-ish supplies
the sound (卷 itself means "to roll/curl," a fitting sound-donor even though
its own meaning plays no official role here).

"Circle, ring, pen" (羊圈, a sheep pen) generalizes naturally to "a social
circle" (朋友圈) — a bounded group, not a bounded physical space.

## Mnemonic
A boundary 囗 curled 卷 into a closed loop: a "圈," circle or pen.

## Notes
Three readings split by sense: *quān* (circle, ring, most senses), *juān*
(to pen up/imprison, 圈养), *juàn* (a livestock pen as a noun, 猪圈) — same
graph, distinct grammatical roles.

## Etymology
Pictographic — a heart, with the outline of the organ and its chambers or the
aorta. Shuowen places it "at the centre of the body." Chinese takes the heart
as the seat of thought and feeling alike.

## Mnemonic
The outline of a heart, chambers and all.

## Notes
心 is Kangxi radical 61; as a left component it becomes 忄 (忙, 快, 怕, 情),
at the foot 心 (思, 想, 忘, 意), and rarely 㣺 (恭). Senses fan out: heart /
mind (心里, 用心), centre (中心, 心脏), feeling / intention (关心, 小心,
决心).

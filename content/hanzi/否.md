## Etymology
Ideographic-and-phonetic together (亦聲). 口 (mouth) supplies the
meaning — a spoken response; 不 *bù* "not" supplies both the sound and,
directly, the sense: a mouth saying "no." Shuowen's gloss is simply 不也,
"is not, no."

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 saying "not" 不, flat out: no.

## Notes
否 covers formal negation (否定, 否认, 否决 "to veto") and the
yes-or-no marker in questions (是否 "whether or not") — always the
spoken "no" side of a binary choice.

## Etymology
The traditional form 徹 was more complex, built on 彳 (movement, a road)
with additional components including 育-related and 攴 (hand-action)
elements, together suggesting a path cleared and passed all the way
through.

**Simplification reduced the right-hand side to 切** (*qiè*, "to cut"),
which gives an approximate sound match toward *chè* but is not the
character's inherited phonetic — a shape substitution rather than a
preserved sound clue, which keeps this entry at medium confidence.

"To pass all the way through" is the sense that survives: 彻底
(thorough, covered elsewhere in this app) and 贯彻 ("to carry through,
to implement").

## Mnemonic
A road 彳 cut 切 straight through, with nothing left in the way: "彻,"
thoroughly.

## Notes
彻底 ("thorough, thoroughly") and 贯彻 ("to carry through, to
implement") both build on this character's core "passing all the way
through, leaving nothing incomplete" sense.

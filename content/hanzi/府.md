## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 广 (a building, shelter) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition is precisely "a place where documents are stored," an
archive — and 付 *fù* supplies the sound.

From "a storehouse for official documents," 府 broadened to "an official
residence or office" (政府, covered elsewhere in this app) and,
honorifically, "your home" (府上, a polite way to refer to someone else's
residence).

## Mnemonic
A building 广 entrusted 付 with the documents of state: a "府," an
official office.

## Notes
政府 (government) and 府上 (an honorific "your home") both extend this
character's core "an official seat, a place entrusted with authority"
sense.

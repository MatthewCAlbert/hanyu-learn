## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning; 库 (simplified 庫)
supplies the sound. 裤/褲 is a relatively late character, coined once
trousers had become a distinct, nameable garment separate from the older
一件长袍-style robes — a straightforward clothing-plus-sound
construction with no deeper symbolic story.

## Mnemonic
Cloth 衤, stored 库 in two separate legs rather than one long robe:
trousers.

## Notes
裤 covers "trousers, pants" broadly (裤子, 短裤 "shorts," 牛仔裤 "jeans" —
literally "cowboy trousers," a calque of the American loanword).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 非 "back to back, opposed" gives the meaning — two things
propped against each other; 告 *gào* gives the sound (for *kào*, via the 造 /
浩 series). Meaning: to lean on / against, to rest against, to be near; to rely
on.

## Mnemonic
Two things set back to back 非, each holding the other up: leaning on.

## Notes
The 告 phonetic also writes 造 *zào*, 浩 *hào*, 皓 *hào*. Senses: lean against
(靠墙), be near (靠窗, 靠海), rely on (依靠, 靠人帮忙), reliable (可靠).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — to go along a stream — and an old element 㕣 *yǎn* supplies the sound. In the modern graph 㕣 has been rewritten as 几 over 口, so the phonetic is no longer recognisable and is deliberately not declared here.

## Mnemonic
Follow the water 氵 along its bank.

## Notes
沿着 "along", 沿海 "coastal", 沿岸 "shore", 沿途. The same 㕣 sound part appears in 铅 *qiān* and 船 *chuán*.

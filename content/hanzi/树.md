## Etymology
Simplified from 樹. 木 "wood" gives the meaning; 尌 *shù* gave the sound.
Shuowen: 生植之緫名 "the general name for growing plants" — and as a verb, "to
plant, to set upright, to establish." Simplification reduces 尌 to 对, dimming
the sound clue.

## Mnemonic
A woody 木 thing planted upright: a tree.

## Notes
Senses: a tree (大树, 树林), to establish (树立, 建树), a set / row (三树梨花).
Measure word 棵: 一棵树.

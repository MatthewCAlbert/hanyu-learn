## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form: 土 (earth) supplied the meaning;
厭 *yàn* supplied the sound — 厭 itself originally meant "to press down,
to oppress," before that sense was borrowed away to mean "to be satisfied
with, to dislike," which is why a new, earth-specific character had to be
built to keep the "pressing" sense alive.

Simplification then compressed 厭's own already-complex shape down to 厂
plus a single dot. Confidence is medium: 土 is a genuine, checkable
semantic component, but no phonetic is declared, because what remains of
厭 in the modern glyph is a stroke-saving graphic reduction, not a
recoverable sound clue.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 piling up under a cliff 厂, crushing everything beneath it:
pressure.

## Notes
压 covers both literal pressure (气压, 压力) and social/emotional pressure
(压力大, 高压) — the physical metaphor of weight bearing down carries
straight through to the abstract sense.

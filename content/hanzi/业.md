## Etymology
Simplified from 業. The full graph pictures the toothed wooden board hung
across a bell-and-drum frame — Shuowen: 大版也，捷業如鋸齒 "a big board,
serrated like saw-teeth." Borrowed for "occupation, undertaking, studies."
The simplified 业 is the top of that board.

## Mnemonic
The serrated crossboard of the bell-rack, borrowed for the work one hangs
one's life on.

## Notes
Senses: trade / industry / occupation (职业, 工业, 农业, 行业), an
undertaking or enterprise (事业, 创业, 企业), studies (毕业, 学业, 结业),
property (家业, 产业, 基业), karma / sin (罪业, 造业, 业障), and "already"
in the literary 业已 / 业经.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition is
specifically "a wall," the kind built of packed earth — and 者 *zhě*
supplies the sound.

From the noun "a wall," 堵 grew into the verb "to block, to obstruct"
(堵车, traffic jam, lit. "to block the cars"; 堵住, "to plug/stop up") — an
obstacle functioning the way a wall does, blocking passage.

## Mnemonic
A wall of packed earth 土 built by someone 者: "堵," to block.

## Notes
堵 also survives as the classifier for walls specifically (一堵墙, "a
[section of] wall") — a fossil of its original noun sense living on
inside the grammar.

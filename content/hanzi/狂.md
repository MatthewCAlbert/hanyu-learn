## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 犭 (dog) supplies the meaning, 王 *wáng* supplies the sound,
shifted to *kuáng*. Shuowen's gloss 狾犬也 is "a rabid dog" — the character
originally named canine madness, and "wild, crazed, frenzied" is a direct
extension of that image to people and behaviour.

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭 gone mad, running wild like a king 王 who answers to no one.

## Notes
狂 shifted from "rabid" to a broad intensifier of unrestrained behaviour:
狂欢 "wild celebration," 狂风 "violent wind," 自大狂 "megalomaniac." The dog
radical 犭 (a bent variant of 犬) recurs across the animal-behaviour
vocabulary (猜, 狼, 猎) even where the animal itself is no longer literal.

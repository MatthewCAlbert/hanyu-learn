## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 宀 (roof, building) supplies the meaning and 乇 *zhé* supplies the sound (*zhé* → *zhái*). Shuowen glosses it as the place where one entrusts oneself, a dwelling. 乇 is rare outside this family (托, 讬, 宅).

## Mnemonic
A roof 宀 over a place you can rely on 乇.

## Notes
乇 is sound only. 住宅 "residence", 宅院 "compound", 宅男 "otaku" (a loan from Japanese), 宅在家 "stay at home".

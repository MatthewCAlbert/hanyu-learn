## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 宀 "roof" gives the meaning — a building sound and entire; 元
*yuán* gives the sound (for *wán*). Shuowen: 全也 "whole, intact." From "whole
/ undamaged" grew "to finish, to complete," and the resultative "done, used
up" (吃完, 说完).

## Mnemonic
A house 宀 standing whole and sound 元: complete.

## Notes
The 元 phonetic also writes 玩 *wán*, 院 *yuàn*, 远 / 遠 *yuǎn*. 完成 "to
accomplish"; 完全 "completely"; 完美 "perfect"; 完了 "it's over."

## Etymology
Pictographic — the right half of 木 "a tree" split lengthwise (爿 is the left
half). Shuowen: 判木也 "a split length of wood." From "a split piece" grew "a
thin flat slice / sheet / card," "a stretch (of land, water)," "a film," and
"partial, one-sided" (片面).

## Mnemonic
Half a tree-trunk, sawn down the middle: a flat slab.

## Notes
片 is Kangxi radical 91 (版, 牌). Two readings: *piàn* (common: 照片, 卡片,
一片面包), *piān* only in a few colloquial words (片子 "a film," 唱片
"a record"). Measure word for flat things and stretches: 一片叶子, 一片海.

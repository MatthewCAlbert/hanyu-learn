## Etymology
Simplified from 書. The old graph is 聿 "a hand holding a writing brush" with
者 for sound — "to write." From the act came "a written thing": a document, a
letter, a book. The simplified 书 is a cursive abstraction of the brush 聿.

## Mnemonic
A brush in hand, mid-stroke: writing — and what is written.

## Notes
聿 "brush" also underlies 笔 / 筆 (brush), 律, 建. Senses of 书: book (书本,
课本), letter (家书), to write (书写), calligraphy / script (楷书, 书法).

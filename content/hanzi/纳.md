## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional 納: 糸 (silk) for meaning, 內 for sound. Shuowen glosses the silk sense as silk soaking through; the common sense "to take in, receive" is generally taken as 內 itself — the word "to bring inside" was once also read *nà* — so 内 here is both the phonetic and the word it came from. That last point is a scholarly reading, not a settled one.

## Mnemonic
Silk 纟 taken inside 内 the house and accepted.

## Notes
In 采纳, 归纳, 接纳, 纳税. 纟 is the silk radical (糸) and rarely means silk in modern compounds. 内 *nèi* and 纳 *nà* differ only in the vowel — a rare phonetic that is also semantically close.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen: 巾 (cloth) supplies the meaning — a woven mat
made of cloth or rushes to sit on — and a reduced form of 庶 *shù*
supplied the sound; the modern shape's 广 and 廿 are what remains of that
reduction, no longer cleanly readable as a separate phonetic on their own,
which keeps this entry at medium confidence.

"A mat to sit on" extended metonymically to "a seat, a place" (座位/席位,
"a seat/place [at a meeting]") and, from there, to "to attend" (出席, "to
attend," lit. "to go out to one's seat") and the honorific "chairman"
(主席, "the head seat").

## Mnemonic
A woven mat spread out under a shelter 广, cloth 巾 laid flat to sit on:
"席," a seat.

## Notes
主席 (chairman/president of an organization) and 出席 (to attend) both
extend "a seat" from the physical object to the social role or act of
occupying it.

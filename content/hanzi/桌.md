## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 "wood" gives the meaning; 卓 *zhuó* "tall, standing high"
gives the sound (for *zhuō*) and a little of the sense — a table stands up off
the ground. A relatively late character; it also appears written 槕. Not in
Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A tall 卓 piece of wood 木 you set things on.

## Notes
The 卓 phonetic also writes 罩 *zhào*, 悼 *dào*. Compare 案 "a low table / a
case" and 台 / 檯 "a stand."

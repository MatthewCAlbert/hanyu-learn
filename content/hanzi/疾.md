## Etymology
Shuowen analyses 疾 as pictophonetic: 疒 (sickness) with 矢 (arrow) as sound. Oracle-bone forms show a person with an arrow beside or striking them, so many scholars read 矢 as meaning too: struck by an arrow, then ill, then fast as an arrow (疾风, 疾驰). Both readings are in circulation.

## Mnemonic
A sickness 疒 that strikes as quick as an arrow 矢.

## Notes
疾病 "illness", 残疾 "disability", 疾驰 "gallop", 疾风 "strong wind". 嫉 and 疾 are related by sound, and by "being hurt".

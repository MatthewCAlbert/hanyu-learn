## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning; 束 *shù* (recall from
earlier in this dataset: a bundle of sticks bound tight) supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 疾也, "swift, fast," is unchanged in the modern
word.

## Mnemonic
Moving 辶 as tightly and efficiently as a bundle bound close 束: speed.

## Notes
速 anchors the modern vocabulary of speed directly — 速度 "speed," 高速
"high-speed," 迅速 "rapid," 加速 "to accelerate" — a remarkably stable
character across two thousand years of sound and meaning change.

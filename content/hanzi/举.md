## Etymology
Simplified from 舉. 手 "hand" (written 扌 at the foot) gives the meaning —
Shuowen: 對舉也 "to raise with both hands." The phonetic was 與 *yǔ*;
simplification crushes it into the 兴-shaped top, so the sound element is
gone.

## Mnemonic
Both hands 扌 pushing something up over the head: to lift.

## Notes
Senses: raise / hold up (举手, 举重, 举杯), start / carry out (举办, 举行,
举例, 一举一动), elect / recommend (选举, 推举, 举荐), a deed or move
(义举, 壮举, 一举成名), and "entire" (举国, 举世闻名, 举家).

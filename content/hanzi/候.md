## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 矦 (the archaic form of 侯)
*hóu* supplies the sound. Some derive the marquis sense from a "watch over the
realm" word, with 候 the form that kept the extra 亻; the graph 侯 itself is
usually read as an archery target — an arrow 矢 at a target 厂 — borrowed for
"marquis." From "watch for" come "to wait," "to await" (等候), and "a juncture
in time" (时候, 气候).

## Mnemonic
A sentry 亻 posted to watch and wait.

## Notes
The 侯 phonetic also writes 喉 *hóu* (throat), 猴 *hóu* (monkey), 篌. Toneless
候 in 时候 "time / moment."

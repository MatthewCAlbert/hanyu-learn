## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 矦 (the archaic form of 侯)
*hóu* supplies the sound. 候 and 侯 were originally one word — "to watch, to
be on the lookout" — and 候 is the form that kept the extra 亻. From "watch
for" come "to wait," "to await" (等候), and "a juncture in time" (时候, 气候).

## Mnemonic
A sentry 亻 posted to watch and wait.

## Notes
The 侯 phonetic also writes 喉 *hóu* (throat), 猴 *hóu* (monkey), 篌. Toneless
候 in 时候 "time / moment."

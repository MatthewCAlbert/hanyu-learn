## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 空 *kōng* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 引也 is "to draw, to pull taut"
— originally drawing a bowstring — which generalised to "to control" (a
taut bowstring under the archer's control) and, separately, "to accuse"
(控告), likely via the idea of drawing/pulling a matter before the
authorities.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 drawing the bowstring taut into empty 空 air, fully in control of
the shot.

## Notes
控 anchors 控制 "to control," 遥控 "remote control," and 控告 "to accuse,
to sue" — two everyday domains, mechanical control and legal accusation,
sharing one character.

## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen calls it a picture of bound bamboo or wooden strips —
"one long, one short, bound together in the middle by two cords" — the
physical form of a book before paper: individual writing slats tied into a
bundle. This is one of the most concretely attested pictographs in the set.

## Mnemonic
Bamboo strips 冂冂, bound together with a cord 一 running through the
middle — a book.

## Notes
册 survives as the classifier and noun for bound printed matter: 一册书
"a volume of a book," 手册 "handbook," 画册 "picture album" — the physical
image of bound slats has outlasted the writing material it once described.

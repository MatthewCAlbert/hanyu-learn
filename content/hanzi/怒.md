## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 心 (heart, emotion) supplies the meaning — anger — and 奴 *nú* supplies the sound.

## Mnemonic
The heart 心 of a slave 奴, at last furious.

## Notes
奴 is sound only (the meaning "slave" doesn't matter). 发怒, 愤怒, 恼怒, 怒气. Series: 努 *nǔ*, 怒 *nù*, 驽 *nú*.

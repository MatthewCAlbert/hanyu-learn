## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 米 (rice, grain) supplies the meaning and 分 *fēn* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as face powder, which was made from ground rice. Powder in general, and pink/powdery colours, follow.

## Mnemonic
Rice 米 divided 分 into fine bits — powder.

## Notes
分 is sound only (although "dividing" suits the picture, that is not an etymology). 面粉 "flour", 奶粉 "milk powder", 粉丝 "vermicelli; also 'fans'" (a loanword pun), 粉红.

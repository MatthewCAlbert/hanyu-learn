## Etymology
Ideographic-leaning pictophonetic, traditional 鎖: 钅 (metal) supplies the
meaning. Shuowen glosses 鎖 as 鐵鎖也, "an iron lock/chain" — a straightforward
naming of the metal object. The right-hand component in the simplified form
no longer functions as a clear phonetic and is better treated as a
simplification-era shape rather than analysed piece by piece.

## Mnemonic
A metal 钅 clasp, small and closed tight.

## Notes
锁 covers both the object (门锁 "door lock," 密码锁 "combination lock") and
the verb (锁门 "to lock the door," 锁住 "to lock in place"), plus a modern
figurative use in computing: 锁屏 "to lock the screen."

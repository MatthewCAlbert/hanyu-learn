## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 兑 *duì* gives the sound
(for *shuō*). Shuowen: 釋也 "to explain, to set out in words."

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 handed over 兑 to another: to say.

## Notes
The 兑 phonetic also writes 悦 *yuè* (pleased), 脱 *tuō* (shed), 税 *shuì*
(tax), 锐 / 銳 *ruì* (sharp). Two readings: *shuō* "to speak," *shuì* "to
persuade" (游说).

## Etymology
𠂇 "a left hand" over 工 "a tool / square" — the left hand steadying the work
while the right does it. Shuowen: 手相左助也 "the hand that assists" — 左 was
also the word "to assist" (later written 佐). From there, "the left side."

## Mnemonic
The helping hand 𠂇 that holds the square 工 steady: the left.

## Notes
Mirror of 右 (a right hand + 口). 左 also "unorthodox / wrong" (旁门左道) and
"the Left" in politics. 左边, 左手, 向左转.

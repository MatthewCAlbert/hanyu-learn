## Etymology
你 "you" with 心 "heart" set beneath it — a northern form recorded from the
Song–Yuan on, the heart added to mark respect and warmth. It has become the
standard polite second-person pronoun. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
"You" 你 spoken with heart 心: the courteous form.

## Notes
您 has no standard plural — 您们 is non-standard; for a group you address
politely, use 大家 or 您 repeated. Use 您 for elders, teachers, customers,
strangers of standing.

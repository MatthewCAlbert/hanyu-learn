## Etymology
之 / 止 "a foot" above 儿 "a person" — a footstep set out ahead of the body.
Shuowen: 前進也 "to go forward, to precede." From "to go before" come "first,
earlier, ahead of," and "ancestor" (先人, 祖先).

## Mnemonic
A foot planted in front of the person 儿: going first.

## Notes
先…再… / 先…然后… "first … then …". 先生 "one born before" → a term of
respect. Opposite 后.

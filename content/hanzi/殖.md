## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 歹 (bone, decay) supplies the meaning and 直 *zhí* supplies the sound. Shuowen's sense is fat that has gone rancid with age; "to grow, to multiply" is a later and quite different use of the same graph.

## Mnemonic
Something decaying 歹 grows straight 直 and multiplies.

## Notes
直 is sound only. 繁殖 "breed", 养殖 "farming (fish etc.)", 殖民 "colonise", 殖民地.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 日 (sun, light) supplies the meaning — bright, radiant — and 王 *wáng* supplies the sound (*wáng* → *wàng*). The sense "flourishing, thriving" follows from "blazing". It is a later graph, so the Shuowen link is to the related 暀 (radiant), not to 旺 itself.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 shines on the king 王 and everything flourishes.

## Notes
王 is sound only. 兴旺 "flourishing", 旺季 "peak season", 旺盛 "vigorous", 火很旺 "the fire is roaring". Series: 汪 *wāng*, 旺 *wàng*, 往 *wǎng*, 枉.

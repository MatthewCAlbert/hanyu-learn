## Etymology
A pictograph, and an unusually direct one: the character draws an
umbrella's canopy spread out on top with its ribs and handle beneath —
the whole shape *is* a small picture of an open umbrella, not a
compound of separate meaning and sound parts.

## Mnemonic
A canopy spread wide over ribs and a handle: "伞," an umbrella — the
character looks like the thing it names.

## Notes
雨伞 (rain umbrella) and 太阳伞 (parasol/sun umbrella, lit. "sun-umbrella")
both build directly on this character's plain "umbrella" sense.

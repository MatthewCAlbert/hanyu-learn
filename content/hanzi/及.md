## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen reads it plainly: 从又从人, "hand" plus "person." The
oracle-bone form is vivid — a hand reaching out from behind to grab a person
fleeing in front — "to catch up with, to seize." The app's decomposition
data cannot resolve the "person" half as a separate leaf (it is recorded as
unknown), which is why only 又 is declared here, even though the person
component is well attested in every source.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 reaching out to catch someone just ahead: "to reach, and."

## Notes
"To catch up to" generalised into "to reach, extend to" (来得及, 来不及,
literally "arrive in time to catch it" / not) and, separately, into the
conjunction "and" (以及) — one event's reach extending to include another.

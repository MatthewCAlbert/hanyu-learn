## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 皮 *pí* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as surging water.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 rippling across a skin 皮 — the surface of a pond is its skin.

## Notes
皮 is sound only. Its series shows the p/b alternation: 坡 *pō*, 玻 *bō*, 破 *pò*, 披 *pī*, 被 *bèi*. Extended to any wave: 电波, 声波, 波动.

## Etymology
An associative compound: 壴 "a drum on its stand" over 口 "mouth." Drumming and
singing together at a happy occasion — Shuowen glosses it simply 樂也 "joy."
Both elements carry meaning; there is no phonetic.

## Mnemonic
A drum 壴 and a singing mouth 口: celebration.

## Notes
壴 (drum) also stands in 鼓 *gǔ* (drum), 彭. Doubled 囍 "double happiness" is the
wedding character. 喜欢 pairs this with 欢.

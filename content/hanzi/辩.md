## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 讠 (from 言, speech) sits caught between two
identical 辛 shapes (together read 辡 *biàn*, standing for two disputing
parties, the same 辛 that appears doubled in other litigation-related
characters). Speech wedged between two opposing sides is exactly a
debate or argument.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 pinned between two opposing parties 辛辛, each pressing their
own case: to "辩," debate.

## Notes
辩论 ("to debate," pairing 辩 with 论 "to discuss") and 辩护 ("to defend, to
argue in defense of," as in a legal defense) both keep this character's
core "speech contested between two sides" image.

## Etymology
㫃 "a banner on a pole" with 从 "people following" beneath it — soldiers
mustered under a standard. Shuowen: "500 men make a 旅" (a brigade). From "a
host on the march" grew "to travel" and "a journey."

## Mnemonic
People 从 gathered under the banner 㫃, ready to march.

## Notes
㫃 "banner" also heads 族 (clan), 旗 (flag), 游 (to rove). Senses: travel
(旅行, 旅游, 旅客), a brigade (军旅), lodging (旅馆, 逆旅).

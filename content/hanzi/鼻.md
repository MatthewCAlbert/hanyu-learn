## Etymology
Pictophonetic — and, like 蛇/它, a case of a character rescued from
pronoun drift. 自 was the original pictograph for "nose," but once it was
borrowed for the grammatical sense "self, oneself," 畀 *bì* was added to
build an unambiguous new character for the body part: 鼻, "to draw in
breath through 自 [the nose], with 畀 supplying the sound," per Shuowen's
own gloss 引氣自畀也.

## Mnemonic
The nose 自 that once meant "self" all on its own, now anchored by an
added sound 畀 so it never gets mistaken for the pronoun again.

## Notes
鼻 anchors 鼻子 "nose," 鼻涙 (rare) "runny nose," and 鼻祖 "the founding
ancestor of a lineage" (literally "the nose-ancestor" — the first, most
prominent point, the way a nose is a face's most forward feature).

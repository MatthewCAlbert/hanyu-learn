## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to stride across — and 夸 *kuā* supplies the sound (*kuā* → *kuà*). Shuowen glosses it as to cross over.

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 that boasts 夸 about its long step.

## Notes
夸 is sound only. Series: 夸 *kuā*, 垮 *kuǎ*, 挎 *kuà*, 胯 *kuà*, 跨 *kuà*. 跨国 "multinational", 跨越 "cross over", 跨年 "see in the new year", 跨界 "cross-over".

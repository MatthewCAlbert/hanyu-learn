## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 "heart / mind" gives the meaning; 勿 *wù* gives the sound
(for *hū*). Shuowen: 忘也 "to overlook, to neglect." From "to pay no heed"
grew "in a flash, suddenly" (忽然, 忽而).

## Mnemonic
Something the mind 心 lets slip 勿: gone in an instant.

## Notes
The 勿 phonetic also writes 物 *wù*, 惚 *hū*. Senses: neglect (忽视, 忽略),
suddenly (忽然), now… now… (忽冷忽热). Also a tiny unit (忽略不计).

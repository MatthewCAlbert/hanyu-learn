## Etymology
Simplified from 樂. The full graph pictures a stringed instrument: 木 "wood"
for the frame and sounding-board, 幺幺 "silk strings," and 白 a plectrum or
drum at the centre. From "music" (*yuè*) came "to take pleasure, joyful"
(*lè*). The simplified 乐 is a cursive shorthand of the whole.

## Mnemonic
Strings on a wooden frame — the picture of an instrument: music, and the joy
of it.

## Notes
*yuè*: music (音乐, 乐器, 乐队), surname *Yuè*; *lè*: happy (快乐, 欢乐,
乐意), to enjoy (乐于, 乐观, 助人为乐), laugh (把他逗乐了). 乐园 "paradise."

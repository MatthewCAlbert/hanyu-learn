## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 人 (person) over 止 (foot) — a person raised up
on the tips of their toes, heels lifted. Standing on tiptoe to see
further gave the extended sense "to look forward to, to hope for" (企
盼, 企望).

"To undertake, to plan" (企业, "an enterprise," 企图, "to attempt/scheme")
extends the same image once more: reaching up on tiptoe toward a goal
not yet within easy reach.

## Mnemonic
A person 人 rising onto their toes 止, straining to see or reach
something just out of view: "企," to look forward to — or to undertake.

## Notes
企业 ("enterprise, company") and 企图 ("to attempt, a scheme") both extend
this character's "straining toward a goal" image into the world of
ambition and undertaking.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was the obscure 𢀜. Shuowen's gloss, 懼也, "to fear, to dread,"
is unchanged in the modern word. The modern top component, 巩
(simplified from 鞏, "firm, secure"), is a later regularisation whose
phonetic value is only loosely related to *kǒng*.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 held firmly 巩 in the grip of dread: fear.

## Notes
恐 covers direct fear (恐惧, 恐怖 "terror") and the hedging "I'm afraid
that..." (恐怕) — the same emotional root, whether describing terror
outright or softening a statement with worry.

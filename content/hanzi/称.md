## Etymology
Simplified from 稱. 禾 "grain" gives the meaning — grain was what you put on
the balance. The traditional phonetic 爯 (a hand raising a weight) is replaced
in the simplified form by 尔, which does not carry the sound. Shuowen: 銓也
"to weigh."

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾 on the scale: weighing it — and so naming its worth.

## Notes
*chēng*: weigh (称重, 称一称), call / name (称呼, 简称, 自称), praise
(称赞, 称道); *chèn*: match / suit (称心, 相称, 对称); *chèng* = 秤
"steelyard." 称职 "competent for the post."

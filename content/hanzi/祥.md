## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 礻 (altar, omen) supplies the meaning — an auspicious sign — and 羊 *yáng* supplies the sound (*yáng* → *xiáng*). Sheep were common sacrificial animals, but Shuowen treats 羊 as the phonetic.

## Mnemonic
A sheep 羊 at the spirit altar 礻 foretells good fortune.

## Notes
羊 is sound only. 吉祥 "auspicious", 祥和 "peaceful", 慈祥 "kindly (of the old)", 不祥 "ominous". 详 *xiáng*, 样 *yàng*, 洋 *yáng* are in the same family.

## Etymology
Ideographic, with two connected images layered together. 耳 (ear) — Xu
Shen notes the ear is physically "connected to the cheek," an unbroken
join — combined with a component picturing unbroken silk thread,
"continuously linked without a break." Two different pictures of
physical continuity, stacked to reinforce one meaning: 連也, "to link, to
connect."

## Mnemonic
An ear 耳, joined seamlessly 关 to everything around it: connected,
linked.

## Notes
联 anchors the vocabulary of connection and alliance — 联系 "to contact,"
联合 "to unite," 联合国 "the United Nations," 联想 "to associate [an
idea]" — always two things joined without a gap.

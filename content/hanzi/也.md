## Etymology
Disputed. Shuowen reads the graph as a picture of the female genitals; others
take it as a serpent (making it the original of 它 / 蛇) or a water ladle
(the original of 匜). No reading is settled. Whatever it depicted, the
character was taken over entirely for grammatical use — the classical
sentence-final particle of judgement, and the modern adverb "also, too."

## Mnemonic
A curling shape kept only for its sound: "…also."

## Notes
Phonetic in 他 *tā*, 地 *dì*, 池 *chí*, 施 *shī*. Modern 也 = "also" (我也去);
classical 也 marks a nominal predicate (…者…也).

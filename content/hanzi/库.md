## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 庫: Shuowen glosses it directly as 兵車藏也, "where
military carriages are stored," and analyses the graph as 車 (cart) sitting
under 广 (a shelter/lean-to roof). "Warehouse, storehouse" generalised from
that specific cart-shed to any building for storing goods.

## Mnemonic
A cart 车 tucked safely under a shelter roof 广.

## Notes
The simplified form drops the visible 车 (kept in traditional 庫), so
today's 库 is closer to an abbreviation than a decomposable pictophonetic —
still, 车库 "garage," 仓库 "warehouse," and computing's 数据库 "database" all
carry forward the same "storage shelter" logic.

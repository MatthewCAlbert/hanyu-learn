## Etymology
Simplified from 馬 — a pictograph of a horse: the mane along the top, the body,
four legs, and a tail. Shuowen: "a picture of a horse's head, mane, tail and
four feet." The simplification keeps the mane-and-legs silhouette.

## Mnemonic
Mane on top, legs below: a horse.

## Notes
马 is Kangxi radical 187 and the phonetic in 妈 *mā*, 吗 *ma*, 骂 / 罵 *mà*,
码 / 碼 *mǎ* — four unrelated senses, so 马 there carries sound only. 马上
"at once" (lit. "on horseback").

## Etymology
亻 "person" + 十 "ten." The original word is *shí* "a unit of ten" — ten
households or soldiers bound together for mutual surety (什伍), and by
extension "assorted, miscellaneous" (什物). 十 carries both the sense "ten" and
the sound.

The reading *shén* in 什么 is a separate, much later development: the character
was pressed into service to write an unstressed syllable in the vernacular
interrogative (see the word entry for 什么). That use has nothing to do with
"ten."

## Mnemonic
Ten 十 people 亻 lumped together — a miscellaneous bunch.

## Notes
Two readings: *shí* "assorted" (什锦 "assorted, mixed"), *shén* only in 什么.

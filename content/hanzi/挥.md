## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 奮,
means "to raise up vigorously" — and 军 (軍) *jūn* supplies the sound.

"To wave, to swing vigorously" (挥手, to wave one's hand; 挥动) extends to
"to command, to direct" (指挥) — the same vigorous hand-gesture used to
give orders, and to "squander" (挥霍, "to spend extravagantly," a hand
flinging money away just as freely).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 waving with the sharp, commanding gesture of an army 军
officer: "挥," to wave or direct.

## Notes
指挥 ("to command, a conductor," lit. "to point and wave") and 挥霍 ("to
squander") both extend this character's vigorous hand-gesture image in
very different directions — one toward authority, one toward excess.

## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen reads the graph as breath spreading out; others
see a wind instrument, or a carpenter's tool. Whatever it pictured, 于 is a
grammatical word — the classical preposition "in, at, on, to, from, than,
regarding" — with no living lexical meaning. (In some texts it also does the
work of 於.)

## Mnemonic
A shape kept only to point relationships: "at, to, than."

## Notes
Fixed frames: 对于 / 关于 "concerning," 由于 "owing to," 属于 "belong to,"
等于 "equal to," 位于 "situated at," 大于 / 小于 "greater / less than." Also
the surname Yú.

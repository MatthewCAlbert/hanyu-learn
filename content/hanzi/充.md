## Etymology
Ideographic-and-phonetic. 儿 (a person, in its "legs" form) with an
abbreviated form of 育 above it — a picture of a child growing, becoming
tall. Shuowen's original gloss, 長也，高也 "tall, elevated," is a growth
sense; "full, to fill" is a later, closely related extension — something
grown to its full extent.

## Mnemonic
A child 儿, grown to fill 亠允 every bit of the space allotted: full,
complete.

## Notes
充 splits into "full, sufficient" (充满, 充分, 充足) and "to serve as, to
pose as" (充当, 冒充) — the second sense plays on "filling in" for a role
that isn't really yours.

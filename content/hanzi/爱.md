## Etymology
The traditional form 愛 has 心 "heart" at its centre, between 旡 (a figure
looking back) above and 夂 (a lingering foot) below — moving on while the heart
looks back with feeling. Shuowen: 行皃也 "a manner of walking."

**The simplified 爱 has no 心.** It replaces the heart with 友 "friend." Any
telling of this character that leans on "the heart" is describing 愛, not the
graph on the card — which shows a hand (爫), a cover (冖), and 友.

## Mnemonic
Reaching out a hand 爫 to draw a friend 友 close.

## Notes
The heart-loss here is the playbook's own example of why the simplified shape
can mislead. Compare 亲 / 親 (lost 見), 产 / 產 (lost 生).

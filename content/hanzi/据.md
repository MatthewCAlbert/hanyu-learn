## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; the traditional 據 used 豦
*jù* for sound. Shuowen's original sense, 杖持也, is "to lean on and hold a
staff" — something you physically rely on for support.

Simplification swapped the phonetic from 豦 to the much simpler, still
same-sounding 居 *jū/jù* — the same kind of substitution seen in 种 (重→中)
and 据's own close cousin in spirit, trading a rare component for a common
one with a near-identical reading.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 holding fast to where you live 居: your support, your evidence.

## Notes
"To lean on for support" extended cleanly into the abstract "evidence to
rely on" (根据, 证据, 数据) — the same shift English makes from a physical
"basis" to an argument's "basis."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, and unusually the "sound" component also draws the action:
壴 (a drum on a stand — recognisably pictographic on its own) supplies the
meaning, and 支/攴 (a hand holding a stick) both supplies the sound and
literally depicts the hand striking the drum. Shuowen says as much
directly: 象其手擊之也, "it pictures the hand striking it."

## Mnemonic
A drum on its stand 壴, struck by a hand with a stick 支.

## Notes
鼓 is Kangxi radical 207 and covers both the object (鼓 "drum") and the verb
"to encourage/rouse" (鼓励 "to encourage," 鼓掌 "to applaud" — literally "to
drum the palms") and "to bulge, swell" (鼓起勇气 "to work up courage,"
literally "puff up courage").

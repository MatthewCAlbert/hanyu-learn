## Etymology
Pictophonetic in Shuowen: 心 (heart) supplies the meaning and 中 *zhōng* supplies the sound. Shuowen also defines 忠 as "giving one's whole heart", and later commentators like to read 中 as "centred", but the Shuowen analysis calls it a sound element.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 that stays true to the centre 中.

## Notes
忠诚, 忠实, 忠心, 忠于. The 中 phonetic occurs in 钟, 种, 肿, 衷, 忠 — all *zhōng*.

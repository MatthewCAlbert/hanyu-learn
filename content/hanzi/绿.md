## Etymology
Simplified from 綠. Pictophonetic: 纟 (糸) "silk" gives the meaning — Shuowen
defines it as silk of a blue-yellow shade; 录 (彔) *lù* gives the sound (for
*lǜ*).

## Mnemonic
Dyed silk 纟 the colour of new leaves: green.

## Notes
The 录 phonetic also writes 碌 *lù*, 禄 / 祿 *lù*, 氯 *lǜ*, 逯 *lù*. Senses:
green (绿色, 绿叶, 草绿, 绿灯). Literary reading *lù* survives in 绿林
"greenwood outlaws" and the place name 鸭绿江.

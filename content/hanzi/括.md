## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 絜,
means "to measure around, to bind" — gathering something in with the
hands and tying it off. Shuowen names a different phonetic component
(昏-related) than the 舌 *shé* visible in the modern shape, so this entry
keeps confidence at medium rather than asserting 舌 as the securely
attested original sound-donor.

"To gather in and bind" generalizes to "to enclose, to include" (包括,
covered elsewhere in this app) and, concretely, to punctuation marks that
enclose text (括号, brackets/parentheses).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 drawing a boundary around something, tongue 舌 included, and
tying it all in: "括," to enclose.

## Notes
包括 ("to include," covered elsewhere in this app) and 括号 ("brackets,"
the punctuation marks that literally enclose text) both build on this
character's core "to gather in and bound off" sense.

## Etymology
Ideographic-and-phonetic (亦聲). 言 (speech) supplies the meaning; 敬
"reverence, respect" supplies both the sound and, per Shuowen's note, a
layer of meaning too — a warning delivered with the seriousness owed to
someone who commands respect. Shuowen's gloss, 戒也, is "to caution, to
put on guard."

## Mnemonic
Words 言, spoken with the gravity of respect 敬: a warning that must be
heeded.

## Notes
警 covers both the verb "to warn, to alert" (警告, 报警 "to call the
police") and, by extension, the profession whose job is exactly that:
警察 "police" — those charged with keeping watch and sounding the alarm.

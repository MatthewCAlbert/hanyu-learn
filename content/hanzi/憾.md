## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning — lingering dissatisfaction — and 感 *gǎn* supplies the sound (*gǎn* → *hàn*). A late graph (in the additions to Shuowen), glossed as regret.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 that feels 感 something it can't set down.

## Notes
感 is a sound clue and also a close meaning. 遗憾 "regret", 憾事 "regrettable matter", 缺憾 "defect". 撼 *hàn* and 憾 share it.

## Etymology
An associative compound: 冊 "a bound set of bamboo slips — a book" resting on
丌, a stand or a pair of hands. Shuowen: 五帝之書也 "the writings of the Five
Emperors" — a revered book set up for consultation. From "a canonical text"
grew "a standard, a statute, a ceremony."

## Mnemonic
A book 冊 raised on a stand 丌: a work to be honoured.

## Notes
The top is 冊 "book" (not 曲), the bottom 丌 "stand" (not 八). Senses:
a classic (经典, 古典), a dictionary (字典, 词典), a statute (法典), a
ceremony (典礼), an allusion (用典).

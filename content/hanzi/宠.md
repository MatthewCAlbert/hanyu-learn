## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional 寵: 宀 (roof, house) supplies the meaning — a dignified dwelling — and 龍 (simplified 龙) *lóng* supplies the sound (*lóng* → *chǒng*). Shuowen glosses it as an honoured place. The sense "to favour, to pamper" follows from honouring someone with a home.

## Mnemonic
A dragon 龙 kept under a roof 宀 and spoiled.

## Notes
龙 is sound only. 宠物 "pet", 宠爱 "dote on", 受宠 "in favour", 宠儿 "darling". Series: 笼 *lóng*, 聋 *lóng*, 拢 *lǒng*, 宠 *chǒng*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 刂 (knife) supplies the meaning; 半 *bàn* supplies the
sound and, fittingly, also means "half" — cutting something cleanly into
two matching parts. The original sense, 分也 "to divide," is the direct
ancestor of "to judge, to decide" — a judgment is a clean division
between right and wrong, guilty and innocent.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 splitting something exactly in half 半: a clear-cut decision.

## Notes
判 covers both the legal sense (判决, 判处 "to sentence") and the general
cognitive one (判断 "to judge, to determine") — both are, at root, a
sharp division drawn between alternatives.

## Etymology
Not securely attested. The oracle-bone graph has been read as a brazier with
sparks, or as sugarcane (the original of 蔗); Shuowen's analysis is of the
later shape. Whatever it depicted, 者 was grammaticalised very early as the
nominalising particle — "the one who / that which" — and that is essentially
all it does now.

## Mnemonic
A worn-down old graph kept purely to mean "the one who…".

## Notes
Attaches to verbs and adjectives: 读者 "reader," 强者 "the strong," 前者 /
后者 "the former / the latter," 记者 "reporter," 或者 "or."

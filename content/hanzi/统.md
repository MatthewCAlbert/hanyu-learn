## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
紀, means "the guiding main strand" of a skein of silk, the thread that
organizes and holds all the others together — and 充 *chōng* supplies the
sound.

"The main organizing thread" generalizes directly to "to unify, to
govern as a single whole" (covered in this app's entries for 系统,
传统, 总统) and "an unbroken lineage" (传统, "tradition," lit. "an
unbroken line handed down").

## Mnemonic
The one main thread 纟 that, once found 充 (充 here only sound), organizes
every other strand into a single whole: "统," to unify.

## Notes
系统, 传统, and 总统 (system, tradition, president — all covered
elsewhere in this app) all draw on this one character's core "the
organizing thread that holds a whole together" image.

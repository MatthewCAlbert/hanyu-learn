## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 十 (ten, standing for "many, all-encompassing") supplies the
meaning, 尃 *fū* ("to spread") supplies the sound, shifted to *bó*, while
also echoing meaning — knowledge or scope "spread wide." Shuowen's gloss
大通也, "vast and all-connecting," matches the modern "broad, extensive"
directly.

## Mnemonic
Ten 十 directions, all spread out 尃 wide — broad, comprehensive knowledge.

## Notes
博 anchors 博士 "doctorate/PhD," 广博 "extensive [of knowledge]," 博物馆
"museum" (literally "a hall of extensively [collected] things"). The
gambling sense (赌博 "to gamble") is a separate, later development —
staking wide bets rather than knowledge spread wide.

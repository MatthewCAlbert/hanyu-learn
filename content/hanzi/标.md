## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form: 木 (wood/tree) supplied the
meaning; 票 *piào* supplied the sound. The original, concrete sense,
木杪末也, is "the very tip of a tree branch" — the highest, most visible
point, which is why 標 came to mean "a mark, a marker."

Simplification kept only the bottom portion of 票, which happens to be
graphically identical to the unrelated character 示. Confidence is
medium, and no phonetic is declared, because that surviving piece is not
actually 示 in any meaningful sense — it is what is left of 票 once its
top half was cut away.

## Mnemonic
The very tip 示 of a tree 木: the highest point, the marker everyone can
see.

## Notes
"The topmost, most visible point of a tree" generalised into "a mark, a
label, a target" (标志, 标题, 目标) — the treetop as the natural place to
put something you want everyone to notice.

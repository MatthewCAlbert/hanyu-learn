## Etymology
棉 is a late graph made when the cotton plant spread widely in China. It is generally read as 木 (plant, tree) plus 帛, the cloth element of the older 綿 *mián* (silk floss), the name 棉 inherited. So 帛 gives the cloth, not the sound, and the exact formation (an abbreviation of 綿 with 木) is a scholarly reading. Not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A tree 木 that grows cloth 帛 on its branches.

## Notes
棉花 "cotton", 棉被 "quilt", 棉衣 "padded jacket", 棉签 "cotton swab". 绵 *mián* means "silk floss, continuous" (绵羊 "sheep", 绵长 "long-lasting").

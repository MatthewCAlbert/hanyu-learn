## Etymology
林 "woods" gives the meaning; 疋 gives the sound (a loose fit for *chǔ*).
Shuowen: 叢木。一名荊也 "a thicket-shrub, also called 荊 (the thorny vitex)."
From the thorn-bush grew "pain, suffering" (痛楚, 苦楚) and, oddly, "clear,
distinct, neat" (清楚, 楚楚动人). Also the name of the ancient state of Chu.

## Mnemonic
A thorn-bush in the thicket 林 — sharp enough to make things painfully clear.

## Notes
四面楚歌 "beset on all sides" (Chu songs); 一清二楚 "perfectly clear."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 力 "strength" gives the meaning; 且 *qiě* gives the sound (for
*zhù*, via the 祖 / 组 *zǔ* series). Shuowen: 左也 "to assist" (左 = 佐).

## Mnemonic
Lending your strength 力 to another: to help.

## Notes
The 且 phonetic also writes 祖 *zǔ*, 组 / 組 *zǔ*, 租 *zū*, 姐 *jiě*, 粗 *cū*.
帮助 pairs 助 with 帮; 助手 "assistant," 助学金 "study grant."

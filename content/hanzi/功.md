## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 力 (strength/effort) supplies the meaning; 工 *gōng*
supplies the sound and, per Shuowen's gloss, carries real weight too:
以勞定國曰功, "effort that stabilises the state is called 功" — merit
earned specifically through hard, productive labour, not luck or birth.

## Mnemonic
Effort 力, applied with real craft 工: merit earned, not given.

## Notes
功 covers "meritorious achievement" (功劳, 立功) and, in a more everyday
register, simply "skill built through practice" (功夫, 基本功) — both
senses insist that what you have was earned through sustained effort.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 阝 (here the "mound/hill" form of 阜, used for earthworks
and terrain) supplies the meaning; 方 *fāng* supplies the sound. The
original, concrete sense, 隄也 "an embankment, a dike," is the ancestor of
the modern "to defend, to guard against."

## Mnemonic
An earthen mound 阝, built square and true 方, holding back the flood:
to defend.

## Notes
"An embankment against water" generalised to "a defence against anything"
— 预防 "to prevent," 国防 "national defence," 防止 "to prevent [from
happening]" — the flood is long gone from the meaning, but the "holding
something back" logic remains.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 罵: originally written with 網 (net) rather than
the doubled 口 seen in the simplified 骂 — Shuowen glosses it 詈也, "to
verbally abuse," and analyses the old form as 网 (a net of words cast at
someone) over 馬 *mǎ* for sound. The modern simplified shape reinterprets the
top as two mouths 口口, which reads well as "loud, repeated shouting" even
though it postdates the original 網 analysis.

## Mnemonic
Two mouths 口口 shouting at a horse 马 that won't behave.

## Notes
马 is one of the most productive phonetics in the script for demonstrating
sound-only components: 妈, 吗, 骂, 码 all share 马 with four unrelated
meanings and four different tones off the same base sound.

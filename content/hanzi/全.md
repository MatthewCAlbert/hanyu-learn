## Etymology
入 "to bring in / put away" over 玉 "jade" (Shuowen's received text has 工).
A flawless jade kept safe — Shuowen: 完也 "whole, complete." From "unblemished,
entire" grew "all, entirely," "to keep intact / preserve" (保全, 成全), and
"perfect" (十全十美).

## Mnemonic
A perfect piece of jade 玉 put safely away 入: whole.

## Notes
全 + noun: 全国, 全家, 全身, 全部, 全体. As an adverb: 全对, 全知道. The
surname Quán.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 牛 (ox) supplies the meaning — an animal for sacrifice — and 羲 *xī* supplies the sound in the traditional 犧. Simplification replaced 羲 with 西 *xī*, which has exactly the same reading, so the sound clue is intact. 西 does not mean "west" here.

## Mnemonic
An ox 牛 pointed at the west 西 for the offering.

## Notes
西 is sound only. 牺牲 "sacrifice", 牺牲品 "victim", 英勇牺牲 "die heroically".

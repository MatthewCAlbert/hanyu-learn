## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 石 (stone) supplies the meaning, 卒 *zú* supplies the sound,
shifted to *suì*. Shuowen's gloss is close to "crushed into bits, like
husked grain" — stone being ground or shattered into small fragments — which
generalised into the everyday sense "to break into pieces, shattered."

## Mnemonic
A stone 石 pounded until it's finished 卒 — smashed to pieces.

## Notes
碎 pairs with 破 "broken/torn" but specifically implies many small
fragments rather than a single break: 打碎 "to smash," 心碎 "heartbroken"
(the heart shattering into pieces), 碎片 "fragments/shards."

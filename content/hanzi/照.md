## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 灬 (火) "fire, light" gives the meaning; 昭 *zhāo* gives the
sound (for *zhào*) and, meaning "bright," reinforces the sense.

## Mnemonic
Light 灬 thrown onto something bright 昭: to shine on it.

## Notes
Senses: shine on / light up (照亮, 阳光照进来), reflect (照镜子, 对照), take
a picture (照相, 拍照) and the picture itself (照片, 护照 passport), look
after (照顾, 照料, 关照), notify (照会, 知照), and "according to" (按照,
照办, 照样). 照常 "as usual."

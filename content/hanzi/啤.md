## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 marks it as a sound-transliteration character; 卑
*bēi* supplies the sound. Coined to write 啤酒 "beer" (卑 giving the
"be-" of the English loanword), another modern coinage with no Shuowen
attestation.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口, lowering itself 卑 for one more round: beer.

## Notes
啤 is used essentially only in 啤酒 "beer" — like 咖 and 啡, a character
invented for a single foreign borrowing rather than descended from older
Chinese vocabulary.

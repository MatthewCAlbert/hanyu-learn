## Etymology
Ideographic, formed from 女 (woman) beside 子 (child). Shuowen reads it simply
as "beautiful," analysing the character as 从女子 — composed of woman and child.

Confidence is medium rather than high because the *reason* the pairing means
"good" is not settled. The familiar telling — a mother with her child as an
image of what is good — is a later gloss, not something the oracle-bone record
establishes. Treat the composition as certain and the story as interpretation.

## Mnemonic
A woman 女 and her child 子 together: good.

## Notes
Two readings, and they are a common trap: *hǎo* "good" (adjective) and *hào*
"to be fond of" (verb). The verbal *hào* is what appears in 爱好 and 好奇.

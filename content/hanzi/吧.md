## Etymology
口 "mouth" marks it as a spoken particle; 巴 *bā* supplies the sound. 吧 is a
purpose-built graph for the sentence-final modal particle — softening a command
into a suggestion (走吧), or marking a guess (是他吧？). It has no pictorial
content.

A second 吧 is the loan "bar" (酒吧, 网吧), 巴 chosen for its sound.

## Mnemonic
A little puff of sound 口 at the end of a sentence.

## Notes
The 巴 phonetic also writes 爸 *bà*, 把 *bǎ*, 疤 *bā*, 芭 *bā*. Modal particles
built the same way: 吗, 呢, 啊, 呀.

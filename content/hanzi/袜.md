## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 衤 (from 衣, clothing) supplies the meaning, and 末 *mò*
supplies an approximate sound toward *wà*. This entry cannot cite a
Shuowen entry for the modern character, so it keeps confidence at
medium.

袜 names what is worn at the very tip 末 ("the end, the extremity," the
same character as in 末 alone) of the body's limbs — the feet.

## Mnemonic
Clothing 衤 worn at the very tip 末 of the leg: "袜," a sock.

## Notes
袜子 (socks, the everyday noun) is one of the more purely functional
clothing-vocabulary characters — worth learning as a fixed item rather
than expecting rich figurative extensions.

## Etymology
Ideographic. Three 口 stacked together — Shuowen glosses it as 眾庶也,
"many, a multitude," reading each 口 as standing for a person. A rival,
now more widely accepted reading takes the three 口 instead as a picture
of ritual vessels laid out for a feast or sacrifice, so "many mouths/many
vessels" both point toward the same underlying idea: multiplicity,
variety. Confidence is medium because which reading — people or
vessels — is original is not settled.

## Mnemonic
Three mouths 口口口, each with their own opinion: variety, and the goods
on offer to satisfy it.

## Notes
"Many, of many kinds" split into "product, article" (商品, 产品) and,
separately, "grade, character, quality" (品质, 人品) — quantity and
quality both descending from the same root sense of variety.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 立 "to stand" gives the meaning; 占 *zhān* gives the sound.
The "to stand upright" sense became current in the Yuan; a parallel sense "a
relay post on a route" (驿站) entered through Mongol postal usage, and from it
comes the modern "station, stop."

## Mnemonic
Standing 立 fixed in one spot 占: to stand; a stopping place.

## Notes
The 占 phonetic also writes 点 *diǎn*, 战 / 戰 *zhàn*, 沾 *zhān*, 粘 *zhān*.
Senses: stand (站住, 站起来), station / stop (车站, 地铁站, 网站).

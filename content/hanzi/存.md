## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 子 (child) supplies the meaning; 才 *cái* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's original sense, 恤問也, is "to inquire after [someone's
welfare], to comfort" — checking in on a child's wellbeing, by extension
anyone's. Confidence is medium because the modern dominant senses ("to
exist," "to save, to deposit") are a considerable semantic distance from
that original "to inquire with care," and the intermediate steps are not
well documented.

## Mnemonic
Checking that a child 子 is still there, safe and sound 才: to make sure
something still exists.

## Notes
"To check that someone is still there" generalised into the bare
existential "to exist, to remain" (存在, 生存) and, separately, into "to
set aside for safekeeping" (存款, 保存) — both senses keep the core idea
of confirming something is safely accounted for.

## Etymology
Simplified from 牆. The traditional graph is 嗇 "to store grain" (a granary
has thick walls) with 爿 *pán* for the sound. The simplified 墙 rebuilds it
as 土 "earth" for the meaning — walls were rammed earth — with 啬 (嗇) now
carrying the sound (for *qiáng*).

## Mnemonic
An earthen 土 barrier around the store: a wall.

## Notes
The 啬 phonetic also writes 蔷 / 薔 *qiáng* (蔷薇 "rose"). Senses: wall
(城墙, 围墙, 墙壁, 隔墙有耳). 墙角 "the corner where two walls meet";
挖墙脚 "to undermine someone."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (money, covered elsewhere in this app) supplies the
meaning — Shuowen's definition is simply "what people treasure" — and 才
*cái* supplies the sound.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 that becomes real once one has the talent 才 to earn it: "财,"
wealth.

## Notes
财富 ("wealth, riches") and 财产 ("property, assets") both build directly
on this character's plain "money, valuables" core sense.

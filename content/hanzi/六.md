## Etymology
Not securely attested. The oracle-bone graph for "six" is a simple hut-like
shape; one common suggestion is that it pictured a shelter (making it an early
form of 廬 "hut") and was borrowed for the number by sound. Shuowen offers only
a numerological gloss. Either way, the modern 六 is not a tally of six.

## Mnemonic
A little peaked shelter, borrowed to write "six."

## Notes
From 六 on, each number has its own distinct graph (unlike 一二三 and the
rewritten 四五). 六 is homophone-adjacent to 溜 in some slang (666 "awesome").

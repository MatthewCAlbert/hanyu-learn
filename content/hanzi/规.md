## Etymology
Ideographic. 夫 (a grown man) with 见 (to see, to perceive) — Shuowen's
gloss, 有法度也, "to have a standard, a rule," is explained through a
cited older source: 丈夫識用必合規矩，故規从夫也, "a grown man's judgment
and conduct must accord with the compass and square, which is why 規
takes 夫 as a component." Confidence is medium because this is an
interpretive, moralising gloss rather than a directly pictured tool
(a compass) the way the word's most common modern use ("a rule, a
regulation") might suggest.

## Mnemonic
A grown man's 夫 clear-eyed judgment 见: a rule to be followed.

## Notes
规 covers "a rule, a regulation" (规定, 规则, 法规) and "scale, scope"
(规模, 大规模 "large-scale") — both senses trace to the same idea of a
fixed, reliable standard everyone measures against.

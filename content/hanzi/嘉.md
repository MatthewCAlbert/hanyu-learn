## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 壴 (a drum) supplies the meaning — festivity, music — and 加 *jiā* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as excellent, fine. The drum's link to "joyous" is the standard reading, not directly attested.

## Mnemonic
A drum 壴 added 加 to the party: excellent!

## Notes
加 is sound only. 嘉宾 "honoured guest", 嘉奖 "commend", 嘉年华 "carnival" (sound loan), 嘉义 (Chiayi). 壴 is the base of 鼓 *gǔ* too.

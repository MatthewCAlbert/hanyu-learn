## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 犭 (dog) supplies the meaning — a strong, fierce dog — and 孟 *mèng* supplies the sound (*mèng* → *měng*).

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭 fierce as the eldest 孟 son.

## Notes
孟 is sound only. 猛烈 "fierce", 猛然 "suddenly", 凶猛, 猛兽 "beast". 孟 itself is "eldest" and a surname.

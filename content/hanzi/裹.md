## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 衣 (clothing, cloth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 纏, means "to bind, to wind around" — and 果 *guǒ* supplies
the sound, an exact match. Unusually, the phonetic 果 is written nested
*inside* the strokes of 衣 rather than beside it, visually enacting the
very idea of being wrapped up.

"To wind cloth around" generalizes to "to wrap" anything (包裹, "a
parcel," lit. "wrap-wrap").

## Mnemonic
A fruit 果 wrapped snugly inside cloth 衣, the character's own shape
enacting the wrapping: "裹," to wrap.

## Notes
包裹 (a parcel/package) pairs 裹 with the near-synonym 包 (to wrap) — see
this app's entry for 包括 for more on 包's own "to wrap" sense.

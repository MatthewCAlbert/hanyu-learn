## Etymology
An inverted 子 "child" (the top element 𠫓, a baby emerging head-first) over ⺼
"flesh" — giving birth. Shuowen: 養子使作善也 "to rear a child and guide it
toward good." From "to bear" grew "to rear, to raise, to nurture," and so "to
educate" (教育, 德育 / 智育 / 体育).

## Mnemonic
A baby emerging 𠫓 into the flesh-and-blood world ⺼: birth, and nurture.

## Notes
The inverted-child element 𠫓 also tops 流 (flowing away), 弃 / 棄 (to
abandon a child). Senses: give birth (生育, 育龄), rear (养育, 培育), educate
(教育, 育人).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 憶: 忄 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning; the
traditional phonetic was 意 *yì* ("intention, thought" — itself already a
mental-life character). Simplification replaced 意 with 乙, so today's
visible right-hand side is a graphic stand-in rather than a working sound
clue. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
The mind 忄 reaching back for a single thread 乙 of the past.

## Notes
忆 is almost always bound in compounds: 记忆 "memory," 回忆 "to reminisce,"
回忆录 "memoir." It rarely stands alone the way 想 or 记 do.

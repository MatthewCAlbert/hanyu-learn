## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 闖: 马 (horse) bursting through 门 (a gate) — a
direct, agreed picture of a horse charging past a barrier. Not in Shuowen,
so confidence on the exact age of the character stays medium even though
the picture itself is uncontested.

## Mnemonic
A horse 马 bolting straight through a gate 门 that wasn't fully open.

## Notes
闯 covers literal charging (闯进来 "to burst in") and figurative
risk-taking: 闯荡 "to go out and make one's way in the world," 闯红灯 "to run
a red light," 闯祸 "to cause trouble/an accident."

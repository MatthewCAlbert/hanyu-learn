## Etymology
辶 "movement" + 首 "head." Shuowen: 所行道也 "the road one travels; from
movement and head" — a head leading the way along a path. From "a road" grew
"a way / method," "a principle" (道理), the philosophical "the Way" (道家), and
"to say" (说道, 道歉). The sound link to 首 *shǒu* is weak.

## Mnemonic
A head 首 setting off down the path 辶: the way.

## Notes
Senses: road (道路, 街道), way / channel (河道, 味道), doctrine (道教), to say
(道谢), and a measure word (一道题, 一道菜, 一道河).

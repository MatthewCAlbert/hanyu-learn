## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is 禾 "grain" above 人 "person" — someone bearing the
harvested crop home. Shuowen: 穀孰也 "the grain ripening." Since the grain
ripened once a cycle, the word came to mean "a year." The modern shape, built
on 干 with an extra stroke, has lost the grain and the carrier.

## Mnemonic
A figure hauling in the ripe grain — one harvest, one year.

## Notes
过年 "to celebrate the New Year" still carries the harvest-festival flavour.
Time words: 今年 / 去年 / 明年, 年级, 年轻.

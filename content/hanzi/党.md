## Etymology
Traditional 黨 is pictophonetic: 黑 (dark) with 尚 *shàng* for sound (Shuowen: "not bright"). "Group, faction" is an extended sense (ancient village districts were 黨). The simplified 党 is not an abbreviation of 黨: it is a separate old character, a surname (*Dǎng*, the Tangut Dangxiang people), used for 黨 since it has the same sound. So the parts 尚 and 儿 of 党 do not mean "faction".

## Mnemonic
A person 儿 who still 尚 sticks to the same group.

## Notes
政党 "political party", 党员 "party member", 党派 "faction", 共产党. The surname Dǎng and the party sense are separate words merged by simplification.

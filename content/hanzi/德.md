## Etymology
Ideographic. The oracle-bone and bronze ancestor is 悳: 直 (straight, upright)
over 心 (heart/mind) — to look straight, act upright, from the heart. 彳
(to walk, conduct oneself) was added later to tie the inner uprightness to
outward conduct. Shuowen's gloss 升也, "to rise/ascend," reflects a
Han-dynasty reading (virtue elevates one) rather than the original picture,
which is why the composition is agreed on but its exact semantic logic is
medium rather than high confidence.

## Mnemonic
Walking 彳 a straight 直 path with your heart 心 in it — that is character.

## Notes
德 pairs with 道 in 道德 "morality," where 道 is the path and 德 is what
walking it rightly earns you. 得 dé "to obtain" is a distinct character with
the same reading, not a variant — the two are frequently confused by
beginners but share no etymology.

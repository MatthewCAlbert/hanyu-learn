## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 supplies the general "sound/transliteration" marker;
非 *fēi* supplies the sound. Coined alongside 咖 specifically to write
the second syllable of 咖啡 "coffee" — a modern loanword coinage, not a
Shuowen-attested character.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 sounding out something entirely foreign 非 to it: coffee.

## Notes
啡 also appears in 吗啡 "morphine," another purely phonetic loanword
transliteration — the character has no independent meaning of its own.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (shell money, wealth) supplies the meaning and 長 (simplified 长) *zhǎng* supplies the sound (*zhǎng* → *zhàng*). A late graph that took over the meaning "account, bill" from 帳 (巾 + 長, a curtain whose cloth was also used for account books). The 贝 radical makes the money association explicit.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 that grows long 长 on the bill.

## Notes
长 is sound only. 账户 "account", 账单 "bill", 算账 "settle the bill", 账号 "account number", 赖账 "refuse to pay". 帐 *zhàng* "curtain" is the older sibling.

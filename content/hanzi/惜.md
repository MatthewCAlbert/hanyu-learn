## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning, 昔 *xī* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 痛也 "pain, distress" is close to the
modern "to regret, to treasure" — a heart pained at losing, or at the
thought of losing, something valued.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 aching over what belongs to the past 昔.

## Notes
惜 spans two related feelings: valuing (珍惜 "to treasure," 爱惜 "to take
good care of") and regret (可惜 "what a pity," 惜别 "to be reluctant to
part") — treasuring something and regretting its loss are, in this
character, the same feeling seen from two sides.

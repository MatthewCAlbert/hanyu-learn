## Etymology
Ideographic. Four 口 (mouths, here standing for the open rims of
vessels) surrounding 犬 (a dog) — Shuowen's gloss makes the picture
explicit: 象器之口，犬所以守之, "it pictures the mouths of vessels, with
a dog there to guard them." Valuable stored goods, ringed by containers
and watched over by a guard dog.

## Mnemonic
Four vessels 口口口口, watched over by a loyal dog 犬: precious things,
kept safe.

## Notes
器 generalised from "a set of guarded vessels" to "any tool, instrument,
or device" (工具/器具 overlap, 电器 "electrical appliance," 武器
"weapon") and, separately, to "an organ" (器官) — a vessel for the body's
own functions.

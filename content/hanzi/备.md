## Etymology
Simplified from 備. The old graph has 亻 "person" beside 𤰇, a picture of a
quiver full of arrows — someone fully equipped, hence "prepared, complete, to
have in reserve." Shuowen glosses it 慎也 "careful," a related sense.

The simplified 备 reduces the quiver element to 夂 over 田; the arrows are no
longer legible.

## Mnemonic
Everything laid ready in the field 田 before the work begins.

## Notes
Senses: 准备 (prepare), 设备 (equipment), 备用 (spare), 具备 (possess). The
𤰇 "quiver" element is otherwise rare.

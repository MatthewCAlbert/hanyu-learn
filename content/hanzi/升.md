## Etymology
Pictographic. The oracle-bone form is a ladle with its handle pointing
up and its bowl to the left, a small mark inside showing it holds
something — a measuring scoop. Shuowen defines it precisely as a unit of
volume, ten 龠, and notes it is "also a picture" (亦象形) of that scoop,
distinguished from the closely related 斗 only by the extra mark inside
the bowl.

## Mnemonic
A ladle, filled to the mark: one measure, rising up and over the brim.

## Notes
"A unit of volume" was borrowed (假借) for the completely separate,
now-dominant sense "to rise, to ascend" (上升, 升级, 升学) — the ladle
and "going up" share no real etymological link, only the borrowed sound.

## Etymology
口 "mouth" enclosed by 勹, a wrapping stroke — words gathered and bounded into
a unit. Shuowen relates it to 曲 "bent" and to 丩 / 勾 "to hook." From "a bent /
bounded stretch of speech" grew "a sentence, a phrase, a line."

## Mnemonic
Speech 口 wrapped 勹 into one closed unit: a sentence.

## Notes
Two readings: *jù* "a sentence" (句子, 一句话, 造句), *gōu* (archaic) = 勾.
As a measure word for utterances: 说了三句.

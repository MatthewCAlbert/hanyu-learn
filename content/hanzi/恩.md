## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 心 (heart) supplies the meaning, 因 *yīn* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as kindness, favour.

## Mnemonic
The cause 因 behind kindness is a good heart 心.

## Notes
因 is sound only. 恩人, 恩情, 恩爱 "affectionate (couple)", 感恩, 恩怨. A teacher's 恩师 or parent's 养育之恩 uses it for deep gratitude.

## Etymology
Simplified from 國. 囗 "a boundary" encloses 或 — itself a halberd 戈 guarding a
patch of ground 口. A defended territory: a state, a country. Shuowen: 邦也 "a
state." Simplification replaces 或 with 玉 "jade," re-motivating it as "a
treasure within the borders."

## Mnemonic
A treasure 玉 held safe inside the borders 囗: the nation.

## Notes
或 "or / perhaps" is the same graph turned to grammatical use. Senses of 国:
country / nation / state (国家, 中国, 外国), national (国旗, 国语).

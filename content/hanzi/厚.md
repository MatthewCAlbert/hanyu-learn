## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 厂 (a cliff face or overhang) with an inverted,
abundant shape beneath it (𠫓) — the original sense is specifically about
the thickness/substantiality of a mountain ridge, a cliff so massive it
seems to hold abundance within it. The exact identity of the modern
bottom components (日, 子) as evolved descendants of that abundant-shape
element is not something this entry can pin down precisely, hence medium
confidence.

"Thick" (physical) extends figuratively to "generous, deep" (厚爱,
"profound affection"; 深厚, "deep and substantial," of feelings or
knowledge).

## Mnemonic
A cliff 厂 so massive it holds a whole hidden abundance within: "厚,"
thick.

## Notes
厚 and 薄 (thin) form the standard antonym pair for physical thickness and
extend in parallel to figurative senses — 厚道 ("kind, generous-natured")
versus 薄情 ("cold-hearted, lacking in affection").

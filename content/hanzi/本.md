## Etymology
Indicative: 木 "tree" with a short stroke at the foot marking the roots.
Shuowen: 木下曰本 "the base of a tree is called 本." From "root" grew "origin,
source, basis, fundamental" (本来, 基本), "one's own / this" (本人, 本地), and
"a bound volume" (书本, 一本书). The mirror graph is 末 (mark at the top =
"tip").

## Mnemonic
The stroke at the tree's foot 木: where it is rooted.

## Notes
本 and 末 differ only in where the extra stroke sits — 本末倒置 "put root and
tip the wrong way round." Measure word: 一本书.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 "wood" gives the meaning; 交 *jiāo* gives the sound. Shuowen:
木囚也 "wooden restraints" — a pen or stockade of crossed timbers. From a
fenced enclosure came "a training / drill ground," and so "school" (*xiào*);
a separate sense, "to cross-check, to collate" (*jiào*), grew from putting
things side by side.

## Mnemonic
A yard fenced with crossed 交 timbers 木, where lessons are drilled.

## Notes
Two readings: *xiào* "school" (学校), *jiào* "to proofread / collate"
(校对, 校稿). The 交 phonetic also writes 郊 *jiāo*, 较 / 較 *jiào*,
饺 / 餃 *jiǎo*, 效 *xiào*.

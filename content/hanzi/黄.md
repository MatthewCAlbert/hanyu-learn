## Etymology
Not securely attested. Guo Moruo read the graph as a person wearing a jade
pei-ornament (璜) at the waist; Shuowen instead calls it "the colour of the
earth" and analyses 田. Neither account is secure, but "yellow" has been the
meaning throughout.

## Mnemonic
A figure with a bright ornament hung at the belt: yellow.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 201). Senses: yellow (黄色, 金黄, 黄叶), (of a
plan) to fall through (事情黄了), pornographic (黄色书刊, 扫黄), and the
Yellow Emperor / Yellow River (黄帝, 黄河). Surname *Huáng*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, and 朝 *cháo* supplies the
sound, an exact match. 潮 is a post-classical character (not in Shuowen),
so its history is less firmly documented than the ancient pictophonetic
core; this entry keeps confidence medium for that reason, though the
sound match itself is exact and uncontested.

"Tide" (the sea's regular rise) generalized to "damp, moist" (潮湿) and,
figuratively, to "a trend, a wave [of activity]" (潮流, 人潮 "a crowd
surge") — all senses built on regular, wave-like rising and falling.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 that rises and falls with the regularity of morning 朝 itself:
"潮," tide.

## Notes
潮湿 (damp, covered in this app's entry for 湿) and 潮流 ("trend, current,"
lit. "tide-flow") both extend this character's core "rising, flowing
water" image in different directions — one literal, one figurative.

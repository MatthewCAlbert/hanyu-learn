## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning and 加 *jiā* supplies the sound. Shuowen defines 茄 as the stalk of the lotus; the eggplant sense is a later use of the same graph, with the reading *qié* (for 茄子) alongside the older *jiā* (雪茄 "cigar").

## Mnemonic
A plant 艹 you add 加 to every stir-fry: eggplant.

## Notes
加 is sound only. 番茄 "tomato" (the foreign 茄), 茄子 "aubergine", 茄克 "jacket" (sound loan), 雪茄 "cigar".

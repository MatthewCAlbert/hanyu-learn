## Etymology
Modern 法 is a reduction of ancient 灋. Shuowen breaks 灋 into 氵 "water" —
law level as water — plus 廌, a mythical beast that gored the guilty party, and
去 "to remove," i.e. removing wrongdoing. The modern form drops the beast,
leaving water and 去.

## Mnemonic
Judgement level as water 氵, sending wrongdoing away 去: the law.

## Notes
Senses: law (法律, 违法, 宪法), method / way (办法, 方法, 用法), standard /
model (法则, 效法), and Buddhist Dharma (佛法, 说法). 法国 "France" (sound
transcription).

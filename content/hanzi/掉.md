## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 卓 *zhuó* gives the sound (for
*diào*, via 悼 *dào*). Shuowen's old gloss is 搖也 "to shake, to swing"; the
everyday senses are "to drop / fall," "to lose," "to turn round" (掉头), and,
after a verb, "off / away" (吃掉, 忘掉).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 letting go: it drops.

## Notes
The 卓 phonetic also writes 桌 *zhuō*, 罩 *zhào*, 悼 *dào*. The complement use
(动词 + 掉) marks completion with removal: 擦掉, 关掉, 卖掉.

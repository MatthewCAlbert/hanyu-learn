## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the older form 祕: 示 (an altar, the ritual/spirit
radical — covered in this app's entry for 禁) supplied the meaning —
Shuowen's definition is simply "divine, sacred" — and 必 *bì* supplies
the sound.

**The modern common form swaps 示 for the unrelated 禾** (grain), a
visually similar but semantically unconnected substitute — this entry
does not declare a semantic component for that reason, and keeps
confidence at medium given the swap.

## Mnemonic
Something so sacred 示 it may not be spoken of, sealed away as certainly
必 as fate: "秘," secret.

## Notes
秘密 (secret, covered elsewhere in this app) and 秘书 ("secretary," lit.
"one who keeps secret documents") both build on this character's core
"hidden, not to be openly shared" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 走 "to move / go" gives the meaning; 己 *jǐ* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 能立也 "to be able to stand up" — to rise.

## Mnemonic
Getting the body up 己 and moving 走: to rise.

## Notes
The 己 phonetic also writes 记 / 記 *jì*, 纪 / 紀 *jì*, 忌 *jì*. 起 as a
complement marks an action beginning or an upward move (站起来, 想起, 拿起);
从…起 "starting from."

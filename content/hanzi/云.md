## Etymology
云 is the original pictograph of a cloud — a rising wisp curling back on
itself. When 雨 was added on top to make 雲 "cloud," the bare 云 was kept for
the classical verb "to say." Simplified script folds them back together.

## Mnemonic
A curl of vapour turning over on itself: a cloud.

## Notes
Senses: cloud (云彩, 白云, 乌云, 云海); and the literary "to say" (人云亦云
"to parrot others," 不知所云 "to make no sense"). Short for Yunnan (云南)
in 云贵. Surname *Yún*.

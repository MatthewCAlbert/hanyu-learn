## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 "wood" gives the meaning; 奇 *qí* gives the sound (for *yǐ*,
via 倚 *yǐ* "to lean on"). Shuowen records 椅 as the name of a tree; the sense
"a chair — a wooden thing you lean back against" is a later development.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 thing to lean against 奇 (倚): a chair.

## Notes
The 奇 phonetic also writes 骑 / 騎 *qí*, 寄 *jì*, 倚 *yǐ*, 崎 *qí*. Measure
word 把: 一把椅子.

## Etymology
The particle 么 (什么, 怎么, 那么) is a worn-down grammatical syllable, not a
picture of anything. It was historically written 麼 / 麽 — 麻 *má* as phonetic
over 幺 "tiny" — and the modern 么 is that character stripped to its lower
element. 幺 itself (Shuowen: 小也 "small") depicts a small skein of silk.

Treat 么 as a sound-tag for an unstressed suffix; there is no pictographic
story to tell.

## Mnemonic
A tiny curl of a sound tacked onto a question word.

## Notes
Also the simplified form of 麼 in 幺麼 "insignificant." The related question
particle 嗎 / 吗 puts the same weak syllable under a 口 "mouth."

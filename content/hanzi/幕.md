## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 巾 (cloth) supplies the meaning, 莫 *mò* supplies the sound,
shifted to *mù*. Shuowen distinguishes 幕 from other cloth-hangings as
specifically "the cloth hung above" — a canopy or overhead curtain — as
opposed to a side-curtain. That "hanging cloth divider" sense extends to a
theatre curtain and, from there, to "an act" of a play (第一幕 "act one").

## Mnemonic
A cloth 巾 hung high, in the dark 莫 (dusk) beyond the lamp light.

## Notes
幕 gives modern 屏幕 "screen" (a cloth-like surface something is projected
onto), 内幕 "inside story" (what happens behind the curtain), and 幕后 "behind
the scenes."

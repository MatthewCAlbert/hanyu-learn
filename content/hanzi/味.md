## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 未 *wèi* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 滋味也 "flavour, taste."

## Mnemonic
What the mouth 口 registers: taste.

## Notes
The 未 phonetic also writes 妹 *mèi*, 昧 *mèi*, 魅 *mèi*. Senses: taste /
flavour (味道, 口味), smell (香味, 气味), a hint of something (趣味, 意味),
and a measure word for ingredients in a prescription (五味药).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning — pleasure, ease — and 俞 *yú* supplies the sound. 俞 is sound only here (it also occurs as a word meaning "yes" in classical speech).

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 that says "yes 俞": glad.

## Notes
愉快 "happy", 愉悦 "joyful", 欢愉 "joy". Series: 愉 *yú*, 偷 *tōu*, 输 *shū*, 瑜 *yú*, 喻 *yù*, 榆.

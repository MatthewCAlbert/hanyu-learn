## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 擴: 扌 (hand) supplied the meaning
— to enlarge by hand, to press outward — and 廣 (simplified to 广, also
meaning "broad, wide") supplied the sound. Unusually, this simplification
kept a component that is both a plausible sound match and semantically
resonant ("hand" plus "broad" reads naturally as "to make broad by
hand"), though 广's official role remains phonetic rather than a second
semantic layer. Medium confidence reflects that this entry cannot fully
confirm which role 广 plays after simplification.

"To enlarge, to expand" (扩大, 扩展) is the straightforward modern sense.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pushing the walls 广 (broad, wide) outward: "扩," to expand.

## Notes
扩大 ("to enlarge") and 扩展 ("to expand, to extend") both build directly
on this character's plain "to make bigger" core sense.

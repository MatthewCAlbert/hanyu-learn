## Etymology
Two right hands (又), both reaching the same way — hands clasped or working
together. Shuowen: 同志為友。从二又相交 "those of one purpose are 友; from two
hands crossing." The oracle-bone graph is exactly two 又. An associative
compound; neither element is phonetic.

## Mnemonic
Two hands pulling in the same direction: friends.

## Notes
又 is the "right hand" element, seen also in 取, 受, 及, 反. 友 pairs with 朋 in
朋友 — classically two kinds of companion, now merged.

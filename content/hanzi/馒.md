## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 饣 (food) supplies the meaning and 曼 *màn* supplies the sound (*màn* → *mán*). A late graph, absent from Shuowen. A popular legend ties 馒头 to Zhuge Liang offering dough "heads" instead of human sacrifices, but this has no support in the character's formation.

## Mnemonic
Food 饣 in a long 曼 soft shape.

## Notes
曼 is sound only. 馒头 "steamed bun" (plain, unfilled; 包子 are filled), 馒首. Series: 慢 *màn*, 漫 *màn*, 馒 *mán*, 蔓 *màn*.

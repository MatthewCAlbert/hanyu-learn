## Etymology
矢 "arrow" gives the meaning — an arrow's length served as the yardstick for
"short," as a bamboo rod did for "long." 豆 *dòu* gives the sound (an
imperfect fit for *duǎn*).

## Mnemonic
Measured against the arrow 矢: it comes up short.

## Notes
The 豆 phonetic also writes 逗 *dòu*, 痘 *dòu*, 豆 itself *dòu*. Senses:
short in length (短发, 缩短, 短裤), brief in time (短期, 短暂), a weak point
(短处, 取长补短, 说人短), to owe (短款, 短了三块钱).

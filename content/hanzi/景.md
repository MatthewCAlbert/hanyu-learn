## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 日 (sun) supplies the meaning; 京 *jīng* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 光也 "light, brightness," is closely related
to (though distinct from) 影 "shadow" — both trace to how sunlight falls
and reveals a scene.

## Mnemonic
Sunlight 日 falling across a capital city 京: a scene worth taking in.

## Notes
"Light, brightness" narrowed into "scenery, a view" (风景, 景色) and, in
compounds, "a state of affairs" (情景, 背景 "background") — a
sunlit scene becoming the general word for anything laid out to be
observed.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 酉 (wine vessel) supplies the meaning — strong, heady drink — and 告 *gào* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as strong-tasting wine. "Intense, cruel" follows from "extremely strong", and the modern slang "cool" (酷) is a sound loan from English *cool*.

## Mnemonic
Strong wine 酉 announced 告 on the label: brutal.

## Notes
告 is sound only. Series: 浩 *hào*, 靠 *kào*, 酷 *kù*, 造 *zào*. 残酷 "cruel", 冷酷, 酷爱 "love passionately", 酷似 "strongly resembling", and slang 很酷 "cool".

## Etymology
Built on 知 ("to know" — itself 矢 "arrow" plus 口 "mouth," words shot out
straight and true, like an arrow's flight) with 日 ("sun," here echoing an
older 曰 "to speak" in some classical accounts). Knowledge given clear
expression, or knowledge shining as brightly as the sun — either reading
points to the same idea: wisdom is knowledge made bright and evident.
Given the variation between accounts (曰 versus 日), this entry keeps
confidence medium.

## Mnemonic
Knowledge 知 shining out as bright and clear as the sun 日: "智," wisdom.

## Notes
智慧 ("wisdom," pairing 智 with 慧, another wisdom-related character) and
智力 ("intelligence," lit. "the power of wisdom") both keep this
character's core "knowledge made bright/evident" sense.

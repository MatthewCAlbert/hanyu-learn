## Etymology
Shuowen reads 犯 as 犬 (dog) for meaning, with 㔾 (a kneeling-person shape) for sound. The picture of a dog attacking gives "to assault, offend, violate". The sound match is weak (*fàn* vs the old reading of 㔾), so treat the phonetic as a loose hint.

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭 breaking the rules by biting.

## Notes
犯罪, 犯规 "foul", 犯人, 侵犯. 范 and 氾 (*fàn*) are in the same sound family.

## Etymology
Pictographic — a hand: the wrist below and five fingers fanned out above (the
central three drawn long, the outer two curled). Shuowen: 拳也 "a fist / a
hand."

## Mnemonic
A palm with the fingers spread: a hand.

## Notes
手 is Kangxi radical 64; as a left-side component it compresses to 扌 (打, 拿,
找, 提). Senses: hand (手机, 洗手), a person skilled at something (高手, 歌手),
"personally" (亲手).

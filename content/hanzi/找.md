## Etymology
Not securely attested. 找 "to look for" is a late character with no Shuowen
entry. It is generally taken as a graphic variant that came to be written 扌
"hand" + 戈 — a hand groping about — but the derivation (possibly a corruption
of 划 or of 爪) is not settled. Learn it as 手-side action, sound-opaque.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 feeling around, weapon 戈 ready, hunting for something.

## Notes
One stroke away from 我 — a constant handwriting confusion (找 has the extra
left stroke of 扌). Senses: look for (找工作), call on (找他), give change
(找钱).

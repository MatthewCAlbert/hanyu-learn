## Etymology
Simplified from 見. An associative compound: 目 "eye" (worn down to 冂 in the
simplified form) on top of 儿 "a person on legs" — a person defined by the
eye, the act of seeing landing. Shuowen: 視也 "to see."

## Mnemonic
A person 儿 with the eye 目 wide open: to see.

## Notes
见 is Kangxi radical 147 and the semantic in 观 / 觀, 视 / 視, 现 / 現, 觉 /
覺. As a resultative it marks perception landing: 看见, 听见. 见面 "to meet,"
再见 "goodbye."

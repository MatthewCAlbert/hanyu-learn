## Etymology
Ideographic — grim, but well attested. Shuowen glosses 棄 as 捐也, "to
discard," and explains the graph directly: 𠫓 pictures an unfilial or
malformed newborn (𠫓 itself is a reversed/upside-down 子, a child born
"the wrong way"), and 廾 (two hands) push it away — an ancient practice of
abandoning infants deemed inauspicious. The character's meaning "to
abandon, to discard" comes straight from that documented, if disturbing,
source.

## Mnemonic
Two hands 廾 pushing away what they will not keep.

## Notes
弃 today is used far beyond its origin: 放弃 "to give up," 抛弃 "to abandon,"
弃权 "to abstain [from a vote]" — all everyday, unremarkable uses that carry
no trace of the character's dark original picture.

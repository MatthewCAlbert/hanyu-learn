## Etymology
Shuowen: 梁 is a water bridge, built from 木 (wood) and 氵 (water), with 刅 *chuāng* supplying the sound. From a beam laid across a stream it extends to a roof beam, the "ridge" or "backbone", and to a surname.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 beam laid across the river 氵.

## Notes
桥梁 "bridge", 栋梁 "pillar (of society)", 脊梁 "spine", 梁山 (Liangshan, of Water Margin). The 刅 phonetic is also in 创 *chuàng* (刅 is "to wound").

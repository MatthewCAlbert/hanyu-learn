## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 敵: 攵 (a hand wielding a tool,
"to act against") supplied the meaning — Shuowen's definition is simply
"an enemy, 仇" — and 啇 *dí* supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 啇 with the unrelated 舌** ("tongue"), which is
neither semantic nor phonetic here — a shape substitution that leaves no
reliable sound clue toward *dí* in the modern form.

## Mnemonic
A hand 攵 raised against a sharp tongue 舌 (all that's left of the
original sound clue): "敌," an enemy.

## Notes
敌人 (enemy, covered elsewhere in this app) is by far the most common
compound; 敌 itself rarely stands alone outside such compounds.

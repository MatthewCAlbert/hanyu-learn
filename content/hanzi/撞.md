## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — a hand-driven action of
force — and 童 *tóng* supplies the sound, shifted to *zhuàng*. Not in
Shuowen; the character is attested later. The meaning "to strike, to
collide" is consistent across its uses, from literal collision (撞车 "car
crash") to the figurative "to run into by chance" (撞见 "to bump into
someone").

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 shoving hard enough to knock a child 童 flying.

## Notes
撞 pairs with 碰 pèng as the two everyday "bump into" verbs — 撞 implies more
force and often accident (a crash), while 碰 is lighter and covers casual
encounters (碰到朋友 "ran into a friend") as much as physical contact.

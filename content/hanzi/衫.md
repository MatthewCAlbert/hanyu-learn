## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning; 彡 *shān* supplies
the sound — an unusual case where the "decorative lines" component 彡
(elsewhere purely semantic, as in 形, 彩) here serves purely as a sound
clue instead. Shuowen's gloss is simply 衣也, "a garment."

## Mnemonic
Clothing 衤, with fine decorative lines 彡 stitched along the seams: a
shirt.

## Notes
衫 is a bound form today — 衬衫 "shirt," together with the wider clothing
vocabulary — rarely standing alone the way 衣 "clothing" in general can.

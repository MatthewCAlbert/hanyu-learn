## Etymology
Not securely attested. The oracle-bone graph has been read as a thumbnail, an
acorn, a rice grain, or the first pale light of the rising sun; none is
confirmed. Shuowen's analysis ("from 入 joined to 二") is a reading of the
later shape. The meaning "white" is firm, and from it grew "plain / blank"
(白纸, 空白), "in vain" (白跑一趟), "clear" (明白, 表白), and "colloquial /
vernacular" (白话).

## Mnemonic
A single pale point of light: white.

## Notes
白 is Kangxi radical 106 (百, 的, 皂). Also the surname Bai, and "the Whites"
in Civil-War-era politics (白色恐怖).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 獨: 犭 (dog) supplies the meaning —
Shuowen's own account contrasts dogs, which fight rather than flock
together, with sheep (羊), which group naturally, and calls dogs
therefore the animal of solitude — and 蜀 *shǔ* supplied the sound.

**Simplification kept only a fragment of that phonetic.** 蜀 is itself
built partly from 虫 (its own bottom component); the simplified form 独
kept just that 虫 fragment, dropping the rest of 蜀 and losing the
original sound clue toward *dú* in the process.

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭, alone among insects 虫 rather than in a flock: "独," solitary.

## Notes
独立 ("independent," lit. "stand alone") and 单独 ("alone, by oneself,"
pairing 独 with 单) both draw on this same "solitary, apart from the
group" core image.

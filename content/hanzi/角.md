## Etymology
A pictograph of an animal's horn, the diagonal ridges marking its surface.
Shuowen: 獸角也 "a beast's horn; it depicts the form." From the pointed horn
grew "a corner, an angle," and from horn cups and horn instruments the other
senses.

## Mnemonic
The ridged cone on a bull's head: a horn — and any sharp corner.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 148). *jiǎo*: horn (牛角, 鹿角), corner / angle
(墙角, 角度, 三角), a tenth of a yuan (两角钱); *jué*: a role (角色, 主角,
配角), to contend (角逐, 口角 a quarrel), an ancient vessel, and 角 the note
in the pentatonic scale (宫商角徵羽).

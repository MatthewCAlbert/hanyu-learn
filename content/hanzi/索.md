## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 索 through its raw material — a plant with
stem and leaves fibrous enough to be twisted into rope — and analyses the
graph as a plant-fibre component over 糸 (thread/cord). "Rope, cable" is
the direct, literal sense; "to search, to demand" (搜索, 索取) are later
extensions, perhaps via the image of a rope being drawn out, hand over
hand, in a search.

## Mnemonic
Plant fibre twisted tight into cord 糸, strong enough to pull and search
with.

## Notes
索 spans the concrete (绳索 "rope/cable," 索道 "cable car [line]") and the
abstract: 搜索 "to search," 索取 "to demand/request," 线索 "a clue" (literally
"a thread of a lead" — the same rope image extended to detective work).

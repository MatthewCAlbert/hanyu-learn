## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (grass, plants) supplies the meaning, and 者 *zhě*
supplies the sound; the modern character carries several senses that
this entry cannot trace to one settled original meaning — "notable,
outstanding" (covered in this app's entries for 著名, 显著), "to write, an
author's work" (著作), and, in a separate colloquial reading (*zhe*), a
grammatical particle marking an ongoing state (拿着, "holding," lit.
"take-ongoing"). Given that spread, confidence stays at medium.

## Mnemonic
Plants 艹 tended by someone 者 until they stand out clearly from all the
rest: "著," notable — or set down in writing.

## Notes
Three distinct jobs share this one character: 著名/显著 ("notable,"
covered elsewhere in this app), 著作 ("a written work"), and the
grammatical suffix *zhe* (marking an action in progress) — worth learning
as three separate words rather than one meaning stretched three ways.

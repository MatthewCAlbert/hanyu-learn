## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 页 (head) supplies the meaning — a blockish, thick head — and 元 *yuán* supplies the sound. 元 is itself the old word for "head" (oracle forms show a person with an emphasised head), so it reinforces the meaning. "Thick-headed" gives "stubborn, obstinate" and, in a milder register, "naughty".

## Mnemonic
A head 页 that stays the original 元 shape: unyielding.

## Notes
顽强 "tenacious" (positive), 顽固 "stubborn" (negative), 顽皮 "naughty". Series: 完 *wán*, 玩 *wán*, 顽 *wán*, 元 *yuán*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 擁: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 用 *yòng*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The core senses —
embracing with the arms (拥抱), and a crowd pressing/thronging together
(拥挤) — both extend from hands/bodies drawn close together.

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 put to use 用, wrapped tight around someone in an embrace.

## Notes
拥 covers affection (拥抱 "to hug"), crowding (拥挤 "crowded"), and
possession/support (拥有 "to possess," 拥护 "to support [a leader/policy]")
— three senses unified by the image of holding something close.

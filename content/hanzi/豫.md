## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 象 (elephant) supplies the meaning, 予 *yú* supplies the
sound, shifted to *yù*. Shuowen's gloss 象之大者 is "a large elephant." An
elephant's slow, deliberate, careful movement plausibly gave rise to both
modern senses: "hesitant" (犹豫, weighing carefully before acting) and
"at ease, relaxed" (安豫), though the exact bridge from "elephant" to
either abstract sense is not fully spelled out in the source.

## Mnemonic
A great elephant 象, given 予 all the time it needs before it moves.

## Notes
豫 survives almost entirely in 犹豫 "to hesitate" — the disyllabic word is
what modern speakers know, rarely the character alone. 河南 province's
classical name 豫 is a separate, unrelated geographic use.

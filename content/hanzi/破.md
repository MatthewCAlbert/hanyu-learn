## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 石 (stone) supplies the meaning; 皮 *pí* supplies the sound.
The original, concrete sense — 石碎也, a stone breaking apart — generalised
to "broken" for anything, and then to the verb "to break, to defeat."

## Mnemonic
A stone 石 cracking, its skin 皮 splitting apart: broken.

## Notes
破 as a verb of defeat/exposure (破案 "to crack a case," 破产 "to go
bankrupt," 突破 "breakthrough") all keep the original image of something
solid finally giving way.

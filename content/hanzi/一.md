## Etymology
Indicative: a single horizontal stroke standing for the number one — the same
tallying logic as 二 (two strokes) and 三 (three). One of the oldest and most
transparent graphs in the script. Shuowen wraps it in cosmology ("the Way was
established in the One"), but the sign itself is simply "one."

## Mnemonic
One line: one.

## Notes
一 is Kangxi radical 1. Tone-sandhi is heavy: *yī* → *yí* before a fourth tone
(一样 *yíyàng*), → *yì* before other tones (一起 *yìqǐ*). Also pronounced *yāo*
when reading digits aloud.

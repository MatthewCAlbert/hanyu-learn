## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 丩 *jiū* (a twining shape) gives
the sound (a loose fit for *jiào*). Shuowen: 嘑也 "to call out, to shout."

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 letting out a cry.

## Notes
Senses: shout / cry (大叫), call / summon (叫他来), be named (叫什么名字), order
(叫外卖), and the passive "let / by" (被叫 / 叫他打了).

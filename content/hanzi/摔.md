## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 率 *shuài* supplies the sound,
shifted to *shuāi*. Not in Shuowen. The core sense — a forceful throwing or
dropping motion — covers both throwing something down deliberately (摔东西
"to throw things down [in anger]") and falling accidentally (摔倒 "to fall
over," 摔跤 "to fall/to wrestle").

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 leading 率 the whole body down to the ground.

## Notes
摔跤 does double duty as "to trip and fall" and "wrestling" (the sport) — the
second sense from the same "throwing someone down" image applied
competitively. 摔坏 "to break by dropping" is the everyday damage verb for
phones, dishes, and anything else fragile.

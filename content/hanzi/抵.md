## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 氐 *dī* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses pushing against.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pushing against the base 氐.

## Notes
氐 is sound only. Series: 低 *dī*, 底 *dǐ*, 抵 *dǐ*, 邸 *dǐ*. 抵抗 "resist", 抵达 "arrive", 抵押 "mortgage".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning, 荒 *huāng* ("wasteland,
desolate") supplies the sound and here also an echo of meaning — a mind
gone to waste, scattered and out of control, is exactly what panic feels
like. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 running wild like an untended wasteland 荒.

## Notes
慌 is the everyday word for panic and flurry: 慌张 "flustered," 慌乱 "in a
panic," and the intensifier use after 得 — 忙得慌 "unbearably busy," 累得慌
"exhaustingly tired."

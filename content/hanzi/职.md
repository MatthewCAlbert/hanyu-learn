## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 耳 (ear) supplies the meaning; the traditional phonetic
was 戠 *zhí*. Shuowen's gloss, 記微也, "to note down the smallest detail,"
pictures a subordinate listening carefully and recording every fine
point of an instruction — the root of "one's post, one's duty."

Simplification replaced 戠 with the simpler, similarly-pronounced 只
*zhǐ*. Confidence is medium because that substitution is a somewhat
loose match in sound.

## Mnemonic
An ear 耳, catching every single 只 instruction without missing a word:
a duty, carried out precisely.

## Notes
职 covers "duty, responsibility" (职责, 职务) and "a job, a profession"
(职业, 求职 "job-hunting") — a post defined by what you are listening for
and expected to carry out.

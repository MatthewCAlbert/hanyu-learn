## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 減: 氵 (water) supplied the meaning
— "to lessen, as water recedes" — and 咸 *xián* supplies the sound. The
modern left-hand 冫 (two strokes, usually "ice") is a graphic
abbreviation standing in for 氵/水 here, the same substitution seen in 冲
— not a reference to ice.

"To lessen, to reduce" (as water level drops) generalized to the ordinary
modern sense "to subtract, to decrease" (减少, 减价).

## Mnemonic
Water 冫 draining away bit by bit, matching the taste 咸 (salty) it leaves
behind as it evaporates: "减," to reduce.

## Notes
减 and 冲 share the same "冫 standing in for water" pattern — worth
remembering as a recurring simplification convention, not a coincidence
specific to either character.

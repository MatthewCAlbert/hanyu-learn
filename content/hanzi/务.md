## Etymology
Simplified from 務. 力 "strength, effort" gives the meaning; 敄 *wù* (矛 "spear"
+ 攵 "action") gave the sound. Shuowen: 趣也 "to press toward, to devote effort
to." Simplification reduces 敄 to 夂, dropping the sound clue. From "to apply
oneself to" grew "affairs, business, a task" and the adverb "be sure to"
(务必, 务须).

## Mnemonic
Throwing one's strength 力 into the job.

## Notes
Senses: affairs / duties (任务, 事务, 家务, 服务), to be engaged in (务农),
"must" (除恶务尽).

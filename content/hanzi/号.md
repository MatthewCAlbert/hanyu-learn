## Etymology
口 "mouth" over 丂 (breath forced out against an obstruction). Shuowen:
痛聲也 "a cry of pain." From "to cry out, to proclaim" grew "a signal / call,"
"a name or title" (国号, 外号), and "a number" (号码, 三号). The traditional
號 added 虎 "tiger" as a phonetic, dropped in simplification.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 letting out a long cry: a call, a name, a number.

## Notes
Two readings: *hào* "number / name / mark" (信号, 学号), *háo* "to howl / wail"
(号叫, 北风怒号).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 纟 (silk thread) supplies the meaning; the phonetic has
drifted. Shuowen's original character 線 used 戔 *jiān* for sound — already
a looser match to *xiàn* than most pairs in this dataset — and the modern
simplified 线 keeps a further-simplified 戋 in that slot. Confidence is
medium because the sound relationship, while traditionally accepted, is not
a clean tonal match the way 妈/马 is.

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟, thin and small 戋, wound into a line.

## Notes
线 was itself once a variant of an earlier form 綫; three spellings (綫,
線, 线) for one word is common when a character's phonetic stops matching
its pronunciation well and scribes keep tinkering with the sound side.

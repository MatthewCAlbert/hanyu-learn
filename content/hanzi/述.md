## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
循, means "to follow along [a path or precedent]" — and 术 *shù* supplies
the sound.

"To follow along a set path" extends to "to recount, to narrate" (叙述,
描述, both covered elsewhere in this app) — retelling events by following
their sequence exactly as they occurred.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 following a technique 术 laid down before, step by step: to
"述," narrate.

## Notes
描述 (to describe, covered elsewhere in this app) and 叙述 ("to narrate,
to recount") both build on this character's core "to follow and retell
in sequence" sense.

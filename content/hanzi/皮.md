## Etymology
Ideographic. 又 (a hand) below a shape depicting a hide being peeled from
an animal — Shuowen's gloss is exactly this action, 剥取獸革, "to strip the
hide from an animal." The app's decomposition data can only resolve the
hand (又, shown compressed as 攴 in the modern glyph); the upper piece is
what remains of the hide itself, no longer separable as its own component.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 pulling the skin off: hide, leather.

## Notes
皮 as a component marks "outer layer, surface" broadly: 皮肤 "skin," 皮鞋
"leather shoes," 调皮 "naughty" (literally "playing with one's skin/hide" —
testing limits).

## Etymology
Shuowen reads 侵 as 人 with a hand 又 holding a broom 帚, "like sweeping advancing": gradual encroachment. Some modern scholars read the oracle-bone graph differently (a hand leading an ox, hence "raid, seize"). Neither reading is settled. Not securely attested which is the original.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 sweeping ever closer with a broom, creeping onto your ground.

## Notes
侵略, 侵犯, 侵害, 侵权. The component 彐冖又 is the compressed 帚 + 又 in the modern graph.

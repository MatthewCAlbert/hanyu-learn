## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — a 棒 is a wooden stick or
club — and 奉 *fèng* supplies the sound, shifted to *bàng*. Not in Shuowen;
this word for "stick" is attested later than the classical corpus. The
extended sense "great, terrific" (真棒!) is a modern colloquial development
with no independent etymology of its own — it rides on the same character.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 club held up 奉 with both hands, ready to swing — and, these days,
ready to praise something as awesome.

## Notes
Concrete sense (棍棒, 接力棒 "relay baton") and the praise sense (你真棒!) are
worth teaching as two uses of one word, not two words — learners often assume
they must be unrelated homophones.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 鍵: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning, 建 *jiàn*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen; the earliest sense
recorded is a metal bolt or crossbar that locks a door or cart axle in
place — the one small piece that everything else depends on, which is
exactly why 关键 came to mean "crucial, key."

## Mnemonic
A metal 钅 bolt that holds the whole structure 建 together.

## Notes
键 spans the mechanical original (关键 "key/crucial point," literally "the
bolt that closes it") and every modern input device: 键盘 "keyboard," 按键
"button/key [on a device]."

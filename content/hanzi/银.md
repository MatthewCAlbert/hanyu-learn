## Etymology
Simplified from 銀. Pictophonetic: 钅 (金) "metal" gives the meaning; 艮
*gèn* gives the sound (for *yín*). Shuowen: 白金也 "the white metal."

## Mnemonic
The white metal 钅, its sound from 艮: silver.

## Notes
The 艮 phonetic also writes 眼 *yǎn*, 根 *gēn*, 恨 *hèn*, 很 *hěn*, 痕
*hén*, 限 *xiàn*. Senses: silver (银器, 银牌, 白银), silver-white (银发,
银河, 银装素裹), and money / the old silver currency (银两, 现银). 银行
"bank."

## Etymology
Ideographic in the traditional form: 阝 (here the "settlement" form of
邑) with 垂 "an edge, a border" — Shuowen's gloss, 境上行書舍, "a relay
station for official correspondence, set at the border," pictures the
waystations that passed messages along a route.

Simplification replaced 垂 with 由 *yóu* — an unusually clean
substitution, since 由 is an exact tonal match for the modern reading
*yóu*, functioning as a genuine phonetic where the traditional character
had none. Confidence is high because this swap, unlike many others in
this dataset, produces a real, checkable sound-plus-meaning pair.

## Mnemonic
A settlement 阝, the very source 由 messages set out from and arrive at:
the post.

## Notes
邮 anchors the entire postal vocabulary — 邮件 "mail," 邮票 "stamp," 邮局
"post office," 邮箱 "mailbox" — the border waystation generalised into
the postal system as a whole.

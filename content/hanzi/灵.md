## Etymology
Simplified from 靈. The traditional form is pictophonetic: 霝 *líng* (sound) over 巫 or 玉, the shaman who serves spirits with jade (Shuowen). The simplified 灵 is an older popular variant; its parts 彐 and 火 are neither meaning nor sound, so the phonetic 霝 is lost and the character is sound-opaque. Do not read 火 "fire" as meaningful.

## Mnemonic
A fire 火 spirit flickering with a hand 彐 in it — a quick, lively thing.

## Notes
灵活 "flexible", 心灵 "soul", 灵感 "inspiration". Related by sound only: 零 *líng*, 铃 *líng*, 龄 *líng* (which share the old 令 sound series).

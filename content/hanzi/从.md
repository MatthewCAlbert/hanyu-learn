## Etymology
An associative compound: two 人, one walking behind the other — "to follow."
The oracle bone shows exactly this. The fuller form 從 added 辶 "movement" and
止 "foot"; simplification restores the bare two-person core. From "to follow"
grew "from, since" and "to comply with."

## Mnemonic
One person 人 walking in another's footsteps: to follow; from.

## Notes
从 is Kangxi radical 36's neighbour; the same doubling logic builds 众 / 眾
(three people = a crowd) and 比 (two side by side). Prepositional 从…到…
"from … to …".

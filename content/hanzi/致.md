## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 至 (to arrive) with 攵 (a hand performing an
action, seen in many "to do X" characters) — a hand actively driving
something to its arrival: "to send, to deliver to its destination."

"To bring about, to cause (a result to arrive)" (导致, 致命 "to bring
about death") is a direct abstraction of that same delivering motion —
causing an outcome to arrive rather than a physical object.

## Mnemonic
A hand 攵 pushing something all the way to its arrival 至: "致," to
deliver — or to bring about.

## Notes
致 pairs with 导 in 导致 (to lead to) and stands alone in the formal
closing phrase 此致 ("with this, I convey..." — a letter-closing
convention), both drawing on the same "to deliver/bring to" core.

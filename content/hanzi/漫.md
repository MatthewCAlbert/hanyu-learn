## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 曼 *màn* ("stretched out,
extended") supplies the sound and here also an echo of meaning — water
spreading out over a wide, unrestrained area. That "spreading without
limit" image carries directly into the figurative senses "free,
unrestrained" (漫不经心 "careless, indifferent") and "everywhere" (漫山遍野
"all over the hills and fields").

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 stretching 曼 out past any bank that once held it.

## Notes
漫 anchors 浪漫 "romantic" (a phonetic loan from "romance," reusing this
character for its sound), 漫画 "comics/manga," and 漫游 "to roam freely."

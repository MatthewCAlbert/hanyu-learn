## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 濕: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning,
and 㬎 *xiǎn* (a component depicting silk hung out to dry in the sun)
supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 㬎 with the visually similar 显** (which also
happens to read *xiǎn*, so unusually the swap did not damage the sound
clue the way 权/宝/奖's substitutions did) — but 显 is not a real component
inherited from the traditional phonetic, so it is not declared here as an
attested phonetic.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 clinging visibly 显 to a surface: "湿," wet.

## Notes
潮湿 (humid, damp) pairs 湿 with 潮 (tide/moisture) as a near-synonym
compound — worth learning together since both describe ambient dampness
rather than something directly soaked.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
绰, means "slack, loose" — and 爰 *yuán* supplies the sound. A thread
strung loosely, not pulled taut, is the base image behind "slow,
unhurried" and "to ease, to relax" (缓解, "to relieve/ease").

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟 left loose and slack 爰 rather than pulled taut: "缓," slow,
unhurried.

## Notes
缓 shares its phonetic 爰 with 暖 (nuǎn, warm) and 援 (yuán, to aid/pull
toward) — a purely sound-based family, not a meaning-based one.

## Etymology
Simplified from 間 / 閒. 門 "gate" with 月 "moon" inside (now written 日) —
moonlight showing through the chink of a closed door. Shuowen: 隟也 "a
crack, a gap." From "a gap" grew "between, an interval, a space," and "a room"
(a space between walls).

## Mnemonic
A sliver of moonlight 日 in the gate 门: the gap between.

## Notes
Two readings: *jiān* "between / room / a while" (中间, 房间, 时间), *jiàn* "gap
/ to separate / to sow discord" (间接, 间断, 离间). Measure word for rooms:
一间房.

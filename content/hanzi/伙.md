## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, and 火 *huǒ* supplies the
sound. The specific extension to "companion, partner" is traditionally
linked to military organization: soldiers grouped into small units that
shared a single cooking fire (火) were themselves referred to by that
fire — messmates became, by association, comrades in general. This link
is well attested in general historical accounts of pre-modern Chinese
military organization, though this entry cannot cite Shuowen for it, as
伙 is a post-classical character; hence medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
People 亻 gathered around a shared cooking fire 火: "伙," companions.

## Notes
伙伴 ("partner, companion," pairing 伙 with the near-synonym 伴) and 一伙人
("a group/gang of people," using 伙 as a collective classifier) both
extend this "sharing a fire together" image into ordinary group vocabulary.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 广 "a building open on one side" gives the meaning; 占 *zhān*
gives the sound (for *diàn*). A late character; not in Shuowen. Meaning: an
inn, a stall, a shop.

## Mnemonic
A roofed stall 广 set up in a spot 占: a shop.

## Notes
The 占 phonetic also writes 点 *diǎn*, 站 *zhàn*, 战 / 戰 *zhàn*, 沾 *zhān* —
the readings scatter, so 占 here is essentially a sound-tag. 商店, 书店,
饭店, 酒店.

## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 執: Shuowen glosses it 捕罪人也, "to capture a
criminal," and analyses the graph as 丮 (a hand grasping) combined with 㚔
(a picture of manacles/shackles) — hands closing a shackle around a
prisoner's wrists. Not "a hand clutching a pebble," a folk gloss with no
source; the original picture is specifically an arrest.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 closing a shackle 丸-shape tight — seized, held fast.

## Notes
执 generalised far beyond arrest: 执行 "to execute/carry out," 坚持/坚执 "to
insist," 执照 "licence/permit" (literally "a document held"). 执法 "to
enforce the law" is the compound that keeps its original "arrest, seize"
flavour most visible.

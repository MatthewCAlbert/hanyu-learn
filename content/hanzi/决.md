## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 冫 (here standing in for 水, water) supplies the meaning;
夬 *guài* supplies the sound — the same phonetic family as 缺 and 玦,
sharing the "a gap, a breach" idea. The original, concrete sense, 行流也
"water breaking through and flowing," pictures a dike giving way: once
the water breaks through, there is no going back — the root of "decisive,
final."

## Mnemonic
Water 冫 breaking through a gap 夬: once it's decided, there's no
stopping it.

## Notes
"A dike breached, water rushing through" generalised fully into "to
decide, to determine" (决定, 决心, 坚决) — a decision, like a breach,
is a point of no return.

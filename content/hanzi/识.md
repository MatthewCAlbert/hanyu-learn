## Etymology
Simplified from 識. 讠 (言) "speech / knowing" gives the meaning; the
traditional phonetic is 戠 *zhí*, reduced in the simplified form to 只.
Shuowen: 知也 "to know." From "to know / recognise" grew "knowledge" and — read
*zhì* — "to record, to remember."

## Mnemonic
Knowing 讠 something well enough to name it: recognition.

## Notes
The 只 (for 戠) phonetic also writes 织 / 織 *zhī*, 职 / 職 *zhí*. Two
readings: *shí* "to know / knowledge" (认识, 知识, 常识), *zhì* "to record"
(标识, 博闻强识).

## Etymology
Ideographic. 刂 (knife) with 未 — Shuowen explains 未 here as standing for
something ripe, ready, "with flavour," and so ready to be cut and
trimmed to shape. "To cut to shape" is the source of the modern "to make,
to regulate, a system" — imposing a fixed form on raw material.
Confidence is medium because the 未 half of Shuowen's own explanation is
somewhat strained, and the app's decomposition data cannot fully resolve
the modern character's upper-left piece either.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 cutting cloth to its finished shape: a system, a set of rules.

## Notes
"To cut to a fixed shape" broadened into both "to manufacture" (制造,
制作) and "a fixed system of rules" (制度, 限制) — a made object and an
imposed structure share the same root idea of shaping something to spec.

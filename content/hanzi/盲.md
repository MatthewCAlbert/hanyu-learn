## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 目 (eye) supplies the meaning — an eye without a pupil — and 亡 *wáng* supplies the sound (*wáng* → *máng*). The "lose" meaning of 亡 happens to fit but Shuowen calls it a sound element.

## Mnemonic
An eye 目 that has lost 亡 its sight.

## Notes
亡 is sound only; the series 忙, 芒, 盲, 茫 *máng*, 忘 *wàng*. 盲人 "blind person", 盲目 "blind(ly)", 文盲 "illiterate", 盲点 "blind spot".

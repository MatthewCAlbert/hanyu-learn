## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 斗 (a dipper/ladle — the measuring-vessel radical) supplies
the meaning, 余 *yú* supplies the sound, shifted to *xié*. Shuowen's gloss
抒也 is close to "to ladle out, to pour" — a dipper tilts at an angle to
pour its contents, which is very likely the root of "slanting, oblique"
even though Shuowen states the "pour" sense rather than the "tilt" sense
outright.

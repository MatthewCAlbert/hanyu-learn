## Etymology
Simplified from 為. The oracle-bone graph is a hand (爪) leading an elephant
(象) by the trunk — putting the beast to work. Shuowen's "female monkey" gloss
is a misreading of the later shape. From "to make (something) work" grew "to
do, to make, to act as" (*wéi*), and "for the sake of, on account of" (*wèi*).

## Mnemonic
A hand 爪 leading an elephant to labour: to do; and, for whom it is done, "for."

## Notes
Two readings: *wéi* "to do / act as / be" (成为, 认为, 以为, 行为), *wèi*
"for / because of / on behalf of" (为什么, 为了, 为人民服务).

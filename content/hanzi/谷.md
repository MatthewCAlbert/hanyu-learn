## Etymology
Shuowen: a valley is where a spring comes out and flows into a river: the graph shows water 水 half-visible, issuing from an opening 口. The oracle forms agree with a stream-mouth shape. 谷 is also, separately, the simplified form of 穀 "grain" (稻谷, 谷物), which is a merge made by the simplification, not a shared meaning.

## Mnemonic
A stream pouring out of the opening 口 in the hills: a valley.

## Notes
山谷 "valley", 峡谷 "gorge", 低谷 "trough", 谷歌 "Google". 稻谷 and 谷物 use 谷 in the "grain" sense (穀). 谷 is a sound part in 浴 *yù*, 欲 *yù*, 容 *róng*, 俗 *sú*.

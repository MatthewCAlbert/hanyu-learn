## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — flood — and 共 *gòng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as an overflowing flood.

## Mnemonic
All 共 the water 氵 together surging: a flood.

## Notes
共 is sound only. Series: 红 *hóng*, 虹 *hóng*, 洪 *hóng*, 供 *gōng*, 港 *gǎng* (sound shift). 洪水 "flood", 洪亮 "resounding".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning "to shake, to wave," and 䍃
*yáo* supplies the sound. 䍃 is a rare component (originally connected to
a pottery vessel shape) seen almost nowhere else in the modern script, so
its sound-donor role here is noted with medium rather than high
confidence.

"To shake, to wave, to rock" (摇动, 摇头 "to shake one's head") covers both
deliberate hand motion and things swaying on their own, like a boat or a
tree in the wind.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 rocking a vessel 䍃 back and forth: to "摇," shake or sway.

## Notes
摇头 ("to shake one's head," a gesture of "no" or disapproval) and 摇晃
("to sway, to rock") are the two most common everyday compounds built on
this character.

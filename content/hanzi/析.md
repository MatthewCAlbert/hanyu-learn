## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 析 as 破木也, "to split wood," and describes
the graph directly: 斤 (an axe) applied to 木 (wood). "To divide, to break
apart" generalised from that concrete act of splitting a log to any kind
of separating or breaking something into parts — including, in modern use,
ideas: 分析 "to analyse."

## Mnemonic
An axe 斤 splitting a log 木 clean in two.

## Notes
析 is rarely used alone in modern Mandarin, appearing almost always in
分析 "to analyse," 解析 "to parse/resolve," and 剖析 "to dissect [an
issue]" — breaking a complex whole into its separate parts.

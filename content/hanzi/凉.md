## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 冫 "ice, cold" gives the meaning (the variant 涼 writes it
with 氵 "water"); 京 *jīng* gives the sound (for *liáng*).

## Mnemonic
A touch of ice 冫 in the air: cool.

## Notes
The 京 phonetic also writes 谅 / 諒 *liàng*, 掠 *lüè*, 景 *jǐng*, 惊 / 驚
*jīng*. *liáng*: cool / cold (凉水, 凉风, 天凉了, 冰凉), disheartened
(心都凉了, 透心凉); *liàng*: to let something cool down (把开水凉一凉).
凉快 "pleasantly cool"; 乘凉 "to enjoy the cool air."

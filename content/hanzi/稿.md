## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 禾 (grain, stalk) supplies the meaning — straw — and 高 *gāo* supplies the sound (*gāo* → *gǎo*). Shuowen glosses it as straw stalk. The sense "draft, rough copy" is a metaphor from something unprocessed.

## Mnemonic
Dry grain stalks 禾 piled high 高: a rough draft.

## Notes
高 is sound only. 稿子 "draft", 手稿 "manuscript", 投稿 "submit a piece", 稿费 "fee for a piece", 初稿 "first draft".

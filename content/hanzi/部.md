## Etymology
Pictophonetic. The right-hand 阝 is 邑 "a settlement, an administrative area,"
giving the meaning; 咅 *pǒu* gives the sound (for *bù*). Shuowen records it as
a place name. From "an administrative division" grew "a part, a section, a
category," "a ministry," and "a unit of troops."

## Mnemonic
An administrative patch 阝 marked off: a department.

## Notes
Right-side 阝 is 邑 "city" (都, 那, 郊); left-side 阝 is 阜 "mound" (阴, 阳,
院). Senses: part (部分, 全部), ministry / department (外交部, 部门), a
command (司令部), a work (of a book / film): 一部电影.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (from 言, speech) supplies the meaning, and 寺 *sì*
supplies the sound (寺's modern reading has drifted from *shī*, but the
same phonetic series also gives 时/時 *shí* and 侍 *shì*, showing the wider
range of readings this component covered historically). Shuowen's own
gloss, 志 ("intent, what is on one's mind"), reflects the classical view of
poetry as the direct expression of inner feeling through speech.

## Mnemonic
Speech 言 given the measured cadence of a temple's 寺 chant: "诗," a poem.

## Notes
诗人 (poet) and 诗歌 (poetry, pairing 诗 with 歌 "song") both keep the same
speech-radical core; 唐诗 (Tang-dynasty poetry) is the single most
culturally weighted compound built on this character.

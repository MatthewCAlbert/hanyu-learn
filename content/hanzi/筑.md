## Etymology
Traditional 築 is layered: ⺮ (bamboo) sits over 筑 *zhú*, itself the name
of an ancient bamboo-and-wood struck instrument, over 木 (wood) — the tool
used to pound and ram earth for building foundations borrowed its name
from that instrument. "To build, to construct" generalised from that
specific act of ramming earth into any construction work.

## Mnemonic
A bamboo 竹 tool, struck like an instrument 筑, ramming the foundation
solid.

## Notes
筑 today is almost always the formal/literary verb for building: 建筑
"architecture, to construct," 筑路 "to build a road." 建筑 is the standard
register; 盖 gài and 建 jiàn cover the same ground more colloquially.

## Etymology
夕 "evening, moon" gives the meaning; the rest of the graph is a worn 亦
*yì*, which supplied the sound. Shuowen: 舍也 "when all under heaven rests."

## Mnemonic
Under the evening 夕 sky, everything settles: night.

## Notes
Senses: night (夜晚, 半夜, 昨夜), nightly / by night (夜班, 夜行, 熬夜),
figurative darkness (夜幕). 夜以继日 "day and night without stop";
过夜 "to stay overnight."

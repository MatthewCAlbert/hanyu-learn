## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 刀 (knife) supplies the meaning; 七 *qī* supplies the sound.
七 was itself borrowed early on to write the numeral "seven," so 刀 was
added to keep the original verb "to cut, to slice" distinct and legible.

## Mnemonic
A knife 刀 making seven 七 even slices.

## Notes
"To cut, to slice" generalises into "close, pressing" (亲切, 迫切) and
into the emphatic "absolutely, without exception" (一切, 切实) — a clean
cut standing in for precision and thoroughness.

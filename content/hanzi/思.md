## Etymology
An associative compound: 囟 "the crown of the head, the brain" (worn down to
田) over 心 "the heart." Chinese thought located reflection in both — the head
and the heart together. Shuowen: 容也 "to take in and hold (an idea)."

## Mnemonic
Turning something over with head 囟 and heart 心: to think.

## Notes
The top is 囟 "brain," not 田 "field." Senses: think / ponder (思考, 思想),
miss / long for (思念, 相思), a train of thought (心思, 意思).

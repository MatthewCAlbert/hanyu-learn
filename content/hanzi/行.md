## Etymology
Pictographic — a crossroads, two roads meeting at right angles, seen from
above. Shuowen splits the later shape into 彳 + 亍 "small steps," but the
oracle-bone graph is unmistakably a four-way junction. From "a road / to go
along it" come "to walk, to travel, to do," "OK" (行不行), a "row" (*háng*),
and a "trade / firm" (银行, 行业).

## Mnemonic
Where four roads cross: a place to walk, and to get things done.

## Notes
行 is Kangxi radical 144. Two readings: *xíng* "to go / walk / act / OK"
(行动, 进行, 不行), *háng* "row / line / trade" (银行, 一行字, 同行).

## Etymology
Simplification merged two distinct traditional characters into this one
shape. 獲 meant "to catch (game)," built on 犬 (dog, for hunting) with a
phonetic 蒦; 穫 meant "to harvest (crops)," built on 禾 (grain) with the
same phonetic 蒦. Both were simplified down to 获, which keeps only a
fragment of the shared phonetic 蒦 (as the current 艹+犾-like shape) and
none of either original's distinguishing semantic component (犬 or 禾) —
so this entry declares neither semantic nor phonetic, and confidence is
medium given the merge.

That merger is exactly why modern 获 covers both "to catch/obtain" (获得,
捕获) and "to harvest" (收获) — it is doing the job of two ancestor
characters at once.

## Mnemonic
A net thrown wide to bring something in — whether prey or a crop: "获," to
obtain.

## Notes
收获 ("harvest," and figuratively "a gain, a takeaway") and 获得 ("to
obtain," more abstract) both descend from this one merged character, which
is why 获 alone can mean either "catch" or "harvest" depending on context.

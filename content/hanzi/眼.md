## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 目 "eye" gives the meaning; 艮 *gèn* gives the sound (for
*yǎn*). Shuowen simply: 目也 "the eye."

## Mnemonic
The eye 目, spelled out with 艮 for its sound.

## Notes
The 艮 phonetic also writes 跟 *gēn*, 根 *gēn*, 恨 *hèn*, 很 *hěn*, 银 / 銀
*yín*, 限 *xiàn*. Extended senses: a small hole (针眼, 泉眼, 枪眼), a crux
(节骨眼儿), and a measure word for wells and glances (一眼井, 看一眼).

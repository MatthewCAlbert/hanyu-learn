## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is 大 "a person" above 凵, an opening or pit — someone
stepping away from a dwelling. Shuowen: 人相違也 "people parting from each
other." From "to leave / depart" grew "to go (there)," "to remove," and the
directional complement "away."

The modern shape (土 over 厶) is a flattening of the original person-and-pit
and no longer shows either part.

## Mnemonic
A figure stepping out of the doorway and off: to go.

## Notes
去 as a complement marks motion away from the speaker (出去, 回去, 进去),
mirroring 来 "toward." In time words, 去 = "past" (去年).

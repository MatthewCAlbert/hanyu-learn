## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning "to encounter while
moving," and 禺 *yú* supplies the sound.

"To meet, to encounter" (相遇, 遇到) applies as readily to chance meetings
with people as to running into circumstances — 遇到困难, "to encounter
difficulty."

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 crossing paths with something unexpected 禺: "遇," to
encounter.

## Notes
待遇 ("treatment, how one is treated," also "pay/benefits") extends 遇 from
a chance meeting to how one is met/received by others or by circumstance.

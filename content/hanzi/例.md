## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 列 *liè* gives the sound (for
*lì*). Shuowen: 比也 "a case to compare with, a precedent."

## Mnemonic
A person's case 亻 lined up 列 as a precedent for the next.

## Notes
The 列 phonetic also writes 烈 *liè*, 裂 *liè*, 咧 *liě*. Senses: example
(例子, 例如), a precedent / rule (惯例, 条例), an instance (病例, 案例).

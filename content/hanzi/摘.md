## Etymology
Ideographic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 啇 (an obsolete component
related to a plant's base/root) sits on the right, providing at minimum
sound and possibly an echo of "detaching something at its base." Not in
Shuowen. The stable core sense across every use is "to detach by hand" —
picking fruit, taking off glasses, or lifting an idea out of a text.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 reaching up to pick a fruit clean off its stem 啇.

## Notes
摘 spans the concrete (摘苹果 "to pick apples," 摘眼镜 "to take off glasses")
and the textual (摘要 "summary/abstract," literally "picked-out essentials,"
摘录 "to excerpt") — always "taking the part you want and leaving the rest."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 雨 (rain) supplies the meaning — dew is moisture from the sky — and 路 *lù* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as moistening.

## Mnemonic
Raindrops 雨 left on the road 路 in the morning: dew.

## Notes
路 is sound only. Two readings: *lù* (dew, 露营) and colloquial *lòu* "to reveal" (露出, 露面); the "reveal" sense comes from dew being exposed outdoors. See 暴露, 透露, 揭露.

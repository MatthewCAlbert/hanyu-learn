## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 遲: 辶 (movement) supplied the
meaning — Shuowen defines it as "walking slowly" — and 犀 *xī* ("rhinoceros")
supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 犀 with the unrelated 尺** (*chǐ*, "a foot-rule,"
also seen in 尺's own entry), which is neither semantic nor phonetic here
— a shape substitution that leaves no reliable sound clue toward *chí*.

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 measured out slowly, foot-rule 尺 by foot-rule: "迟," late,
slow.

## Notes
迟到 ("to arrive late," lit. "late-arrive") and 推迟 ("to postpone," lit.
"push [to be] later") both build directly on this character's plain
"slow, late" core.

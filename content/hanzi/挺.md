## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 "hand" gives the meaning; 廷 *tíng* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 拔也 "to pull out, to draw." From "pull taut / straight" grew
"straight, erect," "to stick out" (挺胸), "to stand firm" (挺住), and the
colloquial intensifier "quite, rather" (挺好, 挺累).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pulling something bolt upright: straight.

## Notes
The 廷 phonetic also writes 庭 *tíng*, 停 *tíng*, 艇 *tǐng*, 蜓 *tíng*.
Measure word for machine guns and a few stiff long objects: 一挺机枪.

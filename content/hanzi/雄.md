## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 隹 (short-tailed bird) supplies the meaning, 厷 *gōng*
supplies the sound, shifted to *xióng*. Shuowen's gloss 鳥父也 is "a male
bird" — the character named the male of the species specifically, then
generalised to "male" for animals broadly and, figuratively, to "heroic,
manly, dominant" for people.

## Mnemonic
A bird 隹, broad-armed 厷 and bold — the male of the species.

## Notes
雄 anchors 雄性 "male [biological sex]," 英雄 "hero," and 雄伟 "grand,
imposing" — the male-animal sense and the "heroic/grand" figurative sense
sit side by side in everyday use.

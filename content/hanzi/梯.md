## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition is
directly "a wooden staircase" — and 弟 *dì* supplies the sound.

## Mnemonic
Wood 木 cut into steps, one after another like siblings 弟 in a row:
"梯," a ladder or staircase.

## Notes
楼梯 ("stairs, staircase," pairing 梯 with 楼, "a building/floor") and
电梯 ("elevator," lit. "electric ladder") both build directly on this
character's plain "steps for climbing" core sense.

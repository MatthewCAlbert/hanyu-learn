## Etymology
宀 "roof" over 豕 "pig." Shuowen treats 豕 (via 豭 "boar") as an abbreviated
phonetic, glossing 家 as 居也 "a dwelling." A widely repeated alternative reads
it as meaning-bearing — a pig kept under the house, the mark of a settled
domestic household. Both are current; hence medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
Livestock 豕 sheltered under the roof 宀: home.

## Notes
Senses: home / family (家里, 家人), a household as a unit (一家人), a school of
thought or an expert (儒家, 科学家, 画家). Also a noun suffix (国家).

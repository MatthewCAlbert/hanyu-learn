## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 心 (heart) supplies the meaning, 尉 *wèi* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 安也 "to settle, to calm" matches the
modern "to comfort, to console" directly.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 settled and calmed by an official's 尉 reassurance.

## Notes
慰 anchors 安慰 "to comfort," 慰问 "to express sympathy/concern for
(formally)," and 欣慰 "gratified, relieved" — always about settling
someone's (or one's own) unease.

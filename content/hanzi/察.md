## Etymology
Pictophonetic, with the phonetic carrying real weight. 宀 (roof/dwelling)
supplies part of the meaning; 祭 "sacrifice, ritual" supplies the sound
and, per Shuowen's own reasoning, the meaning too: sacrificial rites had
to be performed with absolute clarity and correctness (質明), and
"clarity" is exactly what 察 "to examine closely" requires. Confidence is
medium because Shuowen's literal gloss (覆也, "to cover") sits oddly
beside this explanation and the two do not fully reconcile.

## Mnemonic
Beneath one roof 宀, a ritual 祭 performed with total, careful clarity:
close examination.

## Notes
察 covers both formal investigation (考察, 视察 "to inspect") and
everyday perception (察觉 "to notice," 观察 "to observe") — both rooted
in the same demand for careful, undistracted attention.

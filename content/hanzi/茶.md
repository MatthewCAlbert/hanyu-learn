## Etymology
茶 is a deliberate modification of 荼 *tú*, an old name for a bitter herb. As
tea-drinking spread in the Tang, one stroke was dropped from 荼 to make a
distinct graph, 茶, and a distinct reading, *chá*. Lu Yu's 《茶經》 (8th c.)
records the change. 艹 "plant" is the semantic; the rest is the reshaped 余.

## Mnemonic
The bitter herb 荼, trimmed by a stroke, to name the cup you actually drink.

## Notes
茶 also names infusions and drinks generally: 奶茶 "milk tea," 凉茶 "herbal
cooling drink," 茶点 "refreshments." 喝茶.

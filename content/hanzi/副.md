## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 刂 (knife) supplies the meaning — to cut in two — and 畐 *fù* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as to split. From a split-off half come "the second one, assistant, deputy" and "copy, set" (as a measure word).

## Mnemonic
Cut 刂 into two so the other half 畐 becomes the deputy.

## Notes
畐 is sound only. Series: 福 *fú*, 富 *fù*, 幅 *fú*, 逼 *bī*. 副作用 "side effect", 副词 "adverb", 副总统, 名副其实. A measure word for pairs and sets: 一副眼镜.

## Etymology
Usually read as 儿 "a person" beneath a reduced 高 "a tall building" — someone
standing high where the light reaches. Shuowen's supplement simply glosses it
明 "bright." The early graph is too worn to be sure of.

## Mnemonic
Standing up on the high terrace, out in the open light: bright.

## Notes
Senses: bright / light (明亮, 天亮了, 亮光), loud and clear (响亮, 洪亮),
to shine / switch on (灯亮着, 亮灯), and to show openly (亮相, 亮牌,
亮出证件). 漂亮 "pretty" (*piàoliang*).

## Etymology
矢 "arrow" beside 口 "mouth." Shuowen reads it 詞也 "an utterance; from mouth
and arrow" — a statement released as straight and swift as an arrow, hence
sure knowledge put into words. Some scholars also take 矢 (*shǐ*) as a loose
phonetic. Composition is clear; the exact logic of the arrow is interpretation,
so confidence is medium.

## Mnemonic
Words 口 that fly straight to the mark, like an arrow 矢: to know.

## Notes
知 and 智 *zhì* "wisdom" were originally one word. Extended in 知道, 知识,
通知, 知己.

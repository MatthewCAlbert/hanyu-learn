## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 診: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning, 㐱 *zhěn*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The sense "to
diagnose, to examine a patient" ties directly to the spoken exchange
between doctor and patient during an examination.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 exchanged carefully, one by one 㐱, until the diagnosis is clear.

## Notes
诊 anchors 诊断 "to diagnose," 门诊 "outpatient clinic," and 就诊 "to see a
doctor" — the everyday medical-visit vocabulary.

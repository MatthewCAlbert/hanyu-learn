## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning and 冡 *méng* supplies the sound. In Shuowen 蒙 names a parasitic vine (dodder), which wraps over other plants; that image yields "to cover" and then "to be covered over, ignorant, in the dark". "Mongolia" (蒙古) is a transliteration.

## Mnemonic
A vine 艹 draped over a tomb 冡 and covering all.

## Notes
Readings: *méng* "to cover, ignorant" (启蒙 "enlighten"), *mēng* "to deceive; to guess" (蒙骗), *Měng* in 蒙古. 冡 is sound only.

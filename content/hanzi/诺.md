## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning — to answer yes — and 若 *ruò* supplies the sound (*ruò* → *nuò*). Shuowen glosses it as to respond.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 that agrees 若 "if so": a promise.

## Notes
若 is sound only here. 承诺 "promise", 诺言 "pledge", 一诺千金 "a promise is worth a thousand in gold", 许诺. Also in transliteration (圣马力诺).

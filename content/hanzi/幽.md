## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen glosses 幽 as 隱也, "hidden," and analyses the graph
as 山 (mountain) with 丝 (a pair of small, thread-like marks) tucked
within — small, dark recesses hidden deep inside a mountain, the picture
of a secluded, shadowed place.

## Mnemonic
Small hidden niches 幺幺 tucked deep inside a mountain 山 — quiet and dark.

## Notes
幽 anchors 幽静 "tranquil and secluded," 幽默 "humour" (a 20th-century
phonetic loan from English, reusing 幽 for its sound alone), and 幽灵
"ghost, apparition" — the "hidden, dim" sense extending naturally to
things that lurk unseen.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in Shuowen: 礻 (altar, spirit) supplies the meaning, 且 *qiě* the sound. Oracle-bone 且 is itself the word for "ancestor" — it depicts an offering stand or ancestral tablet — and 祖 was made by adding 示 to it, so 且 is both the sound and the original word. The "stand" reading of 且 is the standard modern one.

## Mnemonic
At the spirit altar 礻 a stack of tablets 且 for the forefathers.

## Notes
祖父, 祖母, 祖先, 祖国. The 且 family has *zǔ*, *jū*, *zū*, *qiě* readings: 租, 组, 阻, 助, 姐 all carry it.

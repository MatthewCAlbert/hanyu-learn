## Etymology
Shuowen: 旋 is 㫃 (a flag, shown as 方 and a stroke) over 疋 (foot): the banner whose waving makes troops turn about, so "to turn around". The modern shape has worn away the flag and foot, but the army-and-banner reading is how Shuowen explains it. From turning: to revolve, to spin, to return.

## Mnemonic
A banner 方 swirling above marching feet.

## Notes
旋转 "rotate", 旋律 "melody", 旋涡 "whirlpool", 周旋 "deal with", 凯旋 "return in triumph". The 方 here is part of the flag radical 㫃, not "square".

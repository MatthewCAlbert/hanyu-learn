## Etymology
Simplified from 興. The full form is 舁 "four hands lifting together" around
同 — a group hoisting something up in unison, hence "to rise, to raise, to
flourish." Shuowen: 起也 "to rise."

The simplified 兴 schematises the four hands and 同 into a symmetrical top over
八, so the "many hands lifting" image is lost.

## Mnemonic
Hands raised together, lifting spirits up.

## Notes
Two readings: *xīng* "to flourish, to rise" (兴起, 高兴 aside), and *xìng*
"interest, mood" (兴趣, 高兴, 扫兴). The verbal 高兴 uses *xìng*.

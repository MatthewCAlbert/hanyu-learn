## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 (woman) supplies the meaning; 马 *mǎ* supplies the sound —
*mā* and *mǎ* differ only in tone. 马 contributes nothing semantic here: there
is no horse in the idea of "mother."

## Mnemonic
A woman 女 you would ride through fire for.

## Notes
The 马 phonetic series is a clean demonstration that the sound component
carries no meaning: 妈 mā mother, 吗 ma question particle, 骂 mà to scold,
码 mǎ number. Four unrelated senses, one shared sound.

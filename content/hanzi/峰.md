## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 山 (mountain) supplies the meaning and 夆 *féng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as the tip of a mountain.

## Mnemonic
A mountain 山 whose summit meets 夆 the horizon.

## Notes
夆 is sound only. Series: 逢 *féng*, 蜂 *fēng*, 锋 *fēng*, 缝 *féng/fèng*, 峰 *fēng*. 山峰, 高峰, 峰会 "summit meeting", 驼峰 "camel hump".

## Etymology
目 "eye" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 視也 "to look closely, to inspect."
The upper element is worn (Shuowen reads a reduced 眉 or 屮) and no longer
tells us anything. The "economize" sense grew from "to look over and cut what
is not needed."

## Mnemonic
Running the eye 目 over things to see what can be cut: to spare.

## Notes
*xǐng*: examine oneself (反省, 内省), be aware (省悟, 不省人事), visit a
senior (省亲); *shěng*: save / economize (节省, 省钱, 省事), leave out
(省略, 省去), and a province (省份, 河南省, 省会).

## Etymology
A late graph, not in Shuowen. 扌 (hand) supplies the action — to turn, to snatch — but the right-hand 另 *lìng* does not match the reading *guǎi*, and why 另 was chosen is not securely attested. The "take another by force" gloss is a reading of the shape, not an etymology. Older writing used 枴 / 柺 for "crutch".

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 steering someone else 另 round the corner.

## Notes
Turn: 拐弯 "make a turn", 拐角 "corner". Abduct: 拐骗, 拐卖 "traffic (people)". 拐杖 "walking stick".

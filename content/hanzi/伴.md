## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, and 半 *bàn* supplies the
sound — an exact match, unlike most phonetic pairs. 伴 is a
post-classical character (not in Shuowen), so its history is less firmly
documented than the ancient pictophonetic core; medium confidence reflects
that gap rather than any doubt about the sound match itself.

半's own meaning, "half," is not officially part of the etymology — 半's
job here is sound only — though it offers a memorable resonance: a
companion as one's other half.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 who makes up the other half 半 of a pair: a "伴," a companion.

## Notes
陪伴 ("to accompany," pairing 伴 with 陪, another "to accompany" verb) and
伙伴 ("partner, companion," pairing 伴 with 伙) both build on this
character's core "companion" sense.

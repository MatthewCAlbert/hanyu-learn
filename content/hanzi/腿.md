## Etymology
Pictophonetic. ⺼ (肉) "body / flesh" gives the meaning; 退 *tuì* gives the
sound (for *tuǐ*). A late character — earlier Chinese used 股 and 胫 for the
parts of the leg.

## Mnemonic
The part of the body ⺼ you walk and retreat 退 on: the leg.

## Notes
Senses: leg (大腿, 小腿, 腿疼), a leg-like support (桌腿, 椅子腿), a cured
ham (火腿, 金华火腿). 拉后腿 "to hold someone back"; 飞毛腿 "a fleet-footed
runner / a Scud missile."

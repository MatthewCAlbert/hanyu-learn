## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 先 *xiān* gives the sound (for
*xǐ*). Shuowen: 洒足也 "to wash the feet" — the sense later broadened to
washing anything.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 over it first 先: to wash.

## Notes
先 as a phonetic is rare elsewhere (铣 *xǐ* "to mill"). Senses: wash (洗手,
洗衣服, 洗澡), develop film (洗照片), shuffle cards (洗牌), "wipe out"
(洗劫).

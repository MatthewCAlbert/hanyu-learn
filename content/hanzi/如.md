## Etymology
女 "woman" + 口 "mouth" — Shuowen reads it 从隨也 "to comply, to follow" (a
woman acting on what is said). From "to accord with" grew "to be like, as, in
accordance with," "if / supposing" (如果, 假如), and "such as" (例如,
诸如).

## Mnemonic
Falling in with what is said 口: to be as told — "like, as."

## Notes
如 A (一样 / 般) "like A"; 不如 "not as good as"; 如今 "nowadays"; 如何 "how";
比如 "for example." Also the Buddhist 如来.

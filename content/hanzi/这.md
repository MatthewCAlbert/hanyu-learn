## Etymology
Not securely attested as a demonstrative. The graph 這 (辶 "movement" + 言) is
old, but its use for the proximal demonstrative "this" is a Tang-era vernacular
development whose origin is disputed — possibly a phonetic borrowing, possibly
a fusion. The 辶 "walking" element carries no meaning in the modern word. The
simplified 这 reduces 言 to 文.

## Mnemonic
Point down at what is right here: this.

## Notes
Pairs with 那 "that" and 哪 "which." 这 *zhè* colloquially contracts with a
following 一 to *zhèi*. See the word entries 这些, 这里, 这样.

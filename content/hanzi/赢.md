## Etymology
贝 (shell/money, meaning "wealth, profit") is the one component every
source agrees on: Shuowen's original 贏 reads 有餘，賈利也, "a surplus, a
merchant's profit." The remaining pieces stacked above and around 贝 are
where sources disagree — Xu Shen calls them a phonetic; Duan Yucai argues
instead that they form a meaning-bearing picture, of a large, fleshy
animal, working alongside 貝 rather than merely supplying a sound.

Confidence is low because this is a genuinely unresolved case, not a
simple "component X, meaning Y" story, and the character's true internal
logic outside the money radical is not settled.

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 left over after the deal: you came out ahead — you won.

## Notes
"Surplus, profit" generalised smoothly into today's main sense, "to win" —
双赢 "win-win," 赢家 "the winner" — profit and victory being close cousins
in most languages.

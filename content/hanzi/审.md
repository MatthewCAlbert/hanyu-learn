## Etymology
Shuowen's 審 is 宀 (roof) with 番, glossed "to know in detail" and used for "to examine carefully". Simplification replaced 番 with 申 *shēn*, which resembles the old sound *shěn*, so in the character on the card 申 works as a sound hint — a re-made phonetic, not the original one.

## Mnemonic
Under the roof 宀 of the courthouse, a stretched-out 申 case is examined.

## Notes
审查, 审判, 审美, 评审. 申 also appears in 伸 and 神, but the meanings are unrelated.

## Etymology
Ideographic. 大 (a person, arms and legs spread wide) standing on 一 (a
line representing the ground) — Shuowen's own gloss makes it explicit:
大，人也；一，地也, "大 is a person; 一 is the ground." A person standing
firmly on the earth: to stand, to be established.

## Mnemonic
A person 大, planted firmly on the ground 一: standing.

## Notes
"To stand" extends into "to set up, to establish" (建立, 成立, 设立) —
the physical stability of standing upright becomes the metaphor for
founding something durable.

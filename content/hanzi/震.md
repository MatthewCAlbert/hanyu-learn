## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 雨 (rain — the weather-phenomena radical) supplies the
meaning, 辰 *chén* supplies the sound, shifted to *zhèn*. Shuowen's gloss
劈歷，振物者 describes thunder splitting the sky and shaking everything
below — the character originally named thunder specifically, then
generalised to any shaking, quaking, or shock, physical or emotional.

## Mnemonic
Rain-clouds 雨 cracking open at dawn 辰 with a shattering roar.

## Notes
震 anchors 地震 "earthquake" (literally "earth-shaking") and figurative
shock: 震惊 "to be stunned," 震动 "to vibrate/to cause a stir." One of the
Eight Trigrams ☳ in classical divination, also named 震, symbolising
thunder.

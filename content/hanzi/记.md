## Etymology
Simplified from 記. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech / words" gives the meaning;
己 *jǐ* gives the sound. From "to set down in words" grew "to record" and "to
keep in mind, to remember."

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 pinned down for oneself 己: a note; a memory.

## Notes
The 己 phonetic also writes 起 *qǐ*, 纪 / 紀 *jì*, 忌 *jì*. Senses: remember
(记得, 记住, 忘记), record (记录, 日记), a mark (标记), a written account
(游记).

## Etymology
粥 is the common form of the older 鬻: rice 米 between two curling shapes that stand for steam rising from a pot (the cauldron 鬲 beneath is dropped in 粥). So it shows rice cooked until thick. Two readings: *zhōu* "congee" and the rare *yù* "to sell" (the older 鬻 was also used for that, by sound). The "bow" shapes on each side are not 弓.

## Mnemonic
Rice 米 steaming gently between two curls: congee.

## Notes
粥 as congee is a staple breakfast. 白粥 "plain congee", 八宝粥 "eight-treasure porridge", 一锅粥 "a mess".

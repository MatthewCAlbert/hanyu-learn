## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to push aside, to set going — and 發 (simplified 发) *fā* supplies the sound (*fā* → *bō*). Shuowen glosses it as to put in order.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 sets it in motion 发: to flick it away.

## Notes
发 is sound only. Series: 拨 *bō*, 泼 *pō*, 废 *fèi*, 发 *fā*. 拨打 "dial", 拨款 "allocate funds", 拨通 "get through".

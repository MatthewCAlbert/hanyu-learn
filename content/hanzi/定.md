## Etymology
宀 "roof" over 正 (worn to 疋) "a foot reaching the right spot" — coming to
rest in one's proper place under a roof. Shuowen: 安也 "settled, at peace."
From "to settle" grew "to fix, to decide, to set," "fixed / certain"
(一定, 肯定), and "calm" (镇定, 安定).

## Mnemonic
A foot 正 come to rest under the roof 宀: settled — decided.

## Notes
Senses: decide / fix (决定, 规定, 定时), certain / definite (一定, 说不定,
必定), stable / calm (稳定, 定心丸).

## Etymology
Shuowen's pictophonetic: 氵 (water) for meaning, 朁 *qián* for sound; the senses are to wade, and to be hidden under water. The modern standard graph writes 朁 with 替-looking components, but that 替 is a shape variant of 朁 and does not carry the meaning "replace" or the sound *tì*, so the phonetic is not declared here.

## Mnemonic
Dive under the water 氵 and swap 替 your identity for stealth.

## Notes
潜水 "dive", 潜力 "potential", 潜意识 "subconscious", 潜在 "latent". 朁 also underlies 僭.

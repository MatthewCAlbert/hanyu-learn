## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning, and 乎 (covered in this
app's hanzi entry — itself a pictograph of breath rising and dispersing)
supplies the sound. The pairing is unusually apt: 乎's own oldest meaning
was already about breath and voice, so the phonetic here echoes the very
meaning it helps write.

"To exhale" (as in 呼吸, covered elsewhere in this app) extends to "to
call out" (呼叫, 称呼, "to address/call someone [by a name]").

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 releasing breath 乎, rising and calling out into the air:
"呼," to exhale — or to call.

## Notes
呼吸 ("to breathe") and 呼叫 ("to call out, to page") both build on this
character, one on its "exhale" sense and one on its "call out" sense.

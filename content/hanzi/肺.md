## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh, organ) supplies the meaning and 巿 *fú* supplies the sound (*fú* → *fèi*). 巿 is an old graph for an apron or knee-cloth; do not confuse it with 市 *shì* "market". Shuowen calls the lung the "metal" organ in the five-phases scheme.

## Mnemonic
A piece of flesh ⺼ that fills and empties like an apron in the wind.

## Notes
巿 is sound only (it is a rare character; the stroke order differs from 市). 肺部 "lungs", 肺炎 "pneumonia", 肺癌 "lung cancer", 肺活量 "lung capacity", 肺腑 "depths of the heart".

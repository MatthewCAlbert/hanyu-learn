## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — to approach closely, to press upon — and 畐 *fú* supplies the sound (*fú/bī*). 逼 is a late graph, listed among the later additions to Shuowen and sharing 畐 with the earlier 偪. The sense "to compel" follows from crowding someone.

## Mnemonic
Walking 辶 right up to someone with your full 畐 weight: pressure.

## Notes
畐 is sound only. Series: 福 *fú*, 副 *fù*, 富 *fù*, 幅 *fú*, 逼 *bī*. 逼近 "approach", 逼迫 "force", 逼真 "lifelike", 逼供 "extort a confession".

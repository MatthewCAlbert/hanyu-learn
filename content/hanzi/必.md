## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen reads a marker-stake 弋 with dividing strokes
八 — a fixed boundary post — and takes 弋 as the phonetic; the graph is not
in fact built on 心. The stable meaning is "certainly, without fail."

## Mnemonic
A stake driven in to fix the line for good: it must be so.

## Notes
Senses: must / have to (必须, 必需, 势必, 事必躬亲), certainly / bound to
(必然, 必定, 未必, 何必). 必要 "necessary"; 不必 "need not"; 想必
"presumably."

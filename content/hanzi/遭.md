## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — to meet, come upon — and 曹 *cáo* supplies the sound (*cáo* → *zāo*). Shuowen glosses it as to encounter; in modern use the encounter is nearly always unlucky.

## Mnemonic
Walking 辶 into a crowd 曹 of trouble.

## Notes
曹 is sound only. 遭到 "suffer", 遭遇 "encounter", 遭殃 "suffer a misfortune", 遭受. Also a measure word for rounds: 头一遭 "the first time".

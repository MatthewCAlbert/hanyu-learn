## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 土 "earth, ground" gives the meaning — being situated somewhere
— and 才 *cái* gives the sound. In the oracle-bone script 才 alone did the job
of "to be at"; 在 is the later form with 土 attached. Shuowen: 存也 "to exist,
to be present."

## Mnemonic
Planted on the ground 土: to be there.

## Notes
The 才 phonetic also writes 材 *cái*, 财 / 財 *cái*. Grammatical uses: locative
"at" (在家), progressive marker (在吃饭), "exist" (还在不在).

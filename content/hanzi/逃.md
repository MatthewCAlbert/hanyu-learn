## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning, 兆 *zhào* supplies the
sound, shifted to *táo*. Shuowen's gloss 亡也 "to flee, to be lost" matches
the modern sense of running away directly.

## Mnemonic
Moving 辶 away fast at the first sign 兆 of trouble.

## Notes
逃 covers both physical flight (逃跑, 逃走) and evasive avoidance (逃避 "to
evade," 逃税 "tax evasion"). 兆 also appears as phonetic in 桃 táo (peach) and
挑 tiāo/tiǎo — three different finals sharing one loose sound clue.

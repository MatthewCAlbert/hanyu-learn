## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 日 (sun) supplies the meaning — the absence or blocking of
sunlight is exactly what "dark" means — and 音 *yīn* supplies the sound.

"Dark" (physical, 黑暗) extends figuratively to "hidden, covert" (暗中,
secretly; 暗示, to hint — literally to signal in the dark, without open
light).

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 muffled to silence 音, its light gone out: "暗," dark.

## Notes
暗示 (to hint) and 明示 (to state explicitly) form a natural opposite pair,
both built on the same light/dark metaphor for openness versus
concealment.

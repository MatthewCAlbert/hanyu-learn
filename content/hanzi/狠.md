## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 犭 (dog) supplies the meaning — the snarl of fighting dogs — and 艮 *gèn* supplies the sound (*gèn* → *hěn*). "Fierce, ruthless" follows.

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭 that stays stubborn 艮 in a fight.

## Notes
艮 is sound only. Series: 狠 *hěn*, 恨 *hèn*, 根 *gēn*, 跟 *gēn*, 银 *yín*, 眼 *yǎn*. 凶狠 "ferocious", 狠心 "heartless", 狠毒 "vicious", 狠狠 "fiercely".

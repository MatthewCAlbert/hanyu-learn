## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 目 (eye) supplies the meaning, 焦 *jiāo* supplies the sound,
shifted to *qiáo*. Not in Shuowen; a later colloquial verb for "to look,"
now a close everyday synonym of 看 in northern Mandarin speech.

## Mnemonic
An eye 目 fixed and burning 焦 with attention.

## Notes
瞧 is markedly more colloquial than 看 — 瞧瞧 "take a look," 瞧不起 "to look
down on" — and rarely appears in formal writing. 瞧病 "to see a doctor" is a
regional variant of 看病.

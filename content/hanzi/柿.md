## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (tree) supplies the meaning, 市 *shì* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen with this exact gloss, though the
tree/fruit-name structure is uncontested. The character simply names the
persimmon tree and its fruit.

## Mnemonic
A fruit tree 木, its harvest sold right at market 市.

## Notes
柿 is almost entirely concrete: 柿子 "persimmon." One of the more
narrowly-scoped characters in the set, with no notable figurative
extensions.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — originally "to skim or
scoop up with the hand" — and 少 *shǎo* supplies an approximate sound.
This entry cannot cite a Shuowen entry directly matching the modern sense,
so it keeps confidence medium.

"To skim/take with the hand" branches into "to copy" (抄写, to transcribe
by hand) and "to raid, to seize" (抄家, "to raid and confiscate a
household's property") — both a hand reaching in and taking something,
whether words or possessions.

## Mnemonic

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 擔: 扌 (hand) supplied the meaning
— to carry, balanced by hand or shoulder pole — and 詹 *zhān* supplied
the sound, a looser match for the modern reading *dān/dàn*.

**Simplification replaced 詹 with 旦** (*dàn*), which happens to give an
exact sound match for this character's *dàn* reading — a rare case where
a simplification substitution actually sharpened the phonetic rather
than weakening it, though it is a fresh sound match rather than an
inherited one.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 shouldering a load steady as the sunrise 旦: to "担," carry.

## Notes
Two readings split by grammar: *dān* is the everyday verb "to carry, to
bear" (担心, 承担); *dàn* is the noun "a load, a picul" (一担水, "a
shoulder-pole load of water") and the classifier for such loads.

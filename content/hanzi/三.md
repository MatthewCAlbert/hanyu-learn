## Etymology
Three horizontal strokes standing for the number three — the same tally logic
as 一 and 二. Shuowen reads cosmic significance into it ("the way of heaven,
earth and humanity"), but the sign is simply "three."

## Mnemonic
Three lines: three.

## Notes
Numbers above three abandon the tally and use distinct graphs (四, 五 …). 三
often means "several / many" idiomatically: 三思而行, 三番五次.

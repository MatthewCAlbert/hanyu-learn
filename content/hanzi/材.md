## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 木 (wood) supplies the meaning; 才 *cái* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 木梃也, is "a straight length of usable timber" —
wood that has been selected and cut, ready for building or crafting.

## Mnemonic
Wood 木, chosen for its natural talent 才: good material, ready to use.

## Notes
材 generalised from "usable timber" to "raw material" of any kind
(材料, 教材 "teaching material," 题材 "subject matter") and, separately,
to "talent" in a person (人才 shares the same 才 root) — raw material,
whether wood or human potential, waiting to be shaped.

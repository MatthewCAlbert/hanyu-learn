## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; 韦 (simplified 韋)
supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 奇也 "extraordinary, remarkable," is
close to the modern "great, imposing" — Xu Xuan's added commentary,
人才傀偉 "a person of outstanding, impressive stature," makes the
connection explicit.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻, wrapped in tanned leather 韦 armour, standing larger than
life: great, imposing.

## Notes
伟 appears almost exclusively as an intensifying element in compounds —
伟大 "great," 雄伟 "magnificent," 宏伟 "grand" — rarely standing alone,
always amplifying another word's sense of scale.

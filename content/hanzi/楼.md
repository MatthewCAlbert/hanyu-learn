## Etymology
Simplified from 樓. 木 "wood" gives the meaning — timber-framed construction;
娄 (婁) *lóu* gives the sound. Shuowen: 重屋也 "a storeyed house."

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 building stacked storey on storey.

## Notes
The 娄 phonetic also writes 数 / 數 *shù*, 搂 / 摟 *lǒu*, 蒌. Senses: a
multi-storey building (大楼, 教学楼), a storey / floor (三楼, 楼上 / 楼下).

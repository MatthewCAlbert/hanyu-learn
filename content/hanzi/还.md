## Etymology
Simplified from 還. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; 瞏 (睘) *huán* gave the
sound. Shuowen: 復也 "to return, to go back" (*huán*). The adverb *hái*
"still, yet, also" grew from "again / back once more."

Simplification replaces 瞏 with 不, a bare shape-sign carrying no sound, so the
modern 还 keeps only the walking radical.

## Mnemonic
Turning 辶 and heading back the way you came: to return; still.

## Notes
Two readings: *hái* "still / also / even" (还没吃, 还有), *huán* "to return
something" (还书, 还钱). The lost phonetic 睘 also writes 环 / 環, 圆 / 圓's
cousin 圜.

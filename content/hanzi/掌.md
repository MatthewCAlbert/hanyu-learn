## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 手 (hand) supplies the meaning, 尚 *shàng* supplies the
sound, shifted to *zhǎng*. Shuowen's gloss 手中也 "the middle of the hand"
names the palm directly. "In charge of, to control" (掌握, 掌管) extends
from having something firmly in your palm.

## Mnemonic
The flat of the hand 手, held up high 尚 — in control, in charge.

## Notes
掌 spans the body part (手掌 "palm," 熊掌 "bear's paw," 鼓掌 "to applaud" —
literally "to drum the palms") and control (掌握 "to master/grasp,"
掌管 "to be in charge of").

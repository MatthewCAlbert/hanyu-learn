## Etymology
Simplified from 電. The full graph is 雨 "rain" over 申 — 申 being a pictograph
of a lightning bolt zigzagging from a cloud. Shuowen: 陰陽激燿也 "the fierce
flashing where yin and yang clash." The simplified 电 keeps just the lightning
bolt 申 (with its tail). From "lightning" comes "electricity," and the 电-
prefix (电话, 电视, 电脑).

## Mnemonic
A bolt of lightning, cloud stripped away: electricity.

## Notes
申 "to state / the ninth branch" is the same graph diverted to other uses;
神 (礻 + 申) "spirit" keeps the lightning as an image of the numinous.

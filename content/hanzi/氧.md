## Etymology
A modern coinage, not an ancient character. Nineteenth-century translators of chemistry (Xu Shou and Western collaborators) created gas names with the 气 radical; 氧 was based on 养气 "nourishing gas", with 羊 taken as a stand-in for the sound of 养 *yǎng*. Elements like 氢, 氮, 氯 follow the same pattern. The details of who chose which part are not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A gas 气 for the sheep 羊 to breathe and live.

## Notes
羊 is sound only. 氧气 "oxygen", 缺氧 "short of oxygen", 二氧化碳 "carbon dioxide", 吸氧. Compare 氢 *qīng*, 氮 *dàn*, 氯 *lǜ*.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 攵 (攴) "a hand with a stick — to act on" gives the meaning;
丩 *jiū* gives the sound. Shuowen: 捕也 "to seize, to arrest." From "to
seize" grew "to gather in, to take in, to receive, to put away."

## Mnemonic
A hand 攵 laying hold of something and drawing it in: to collect.

## Notes
Senses: receive / accept (收到, 收信, 接收), gather in / harvest (收割, 丰收,
收成), take in money (收费, 收入, 收支), put away (收拾, 收藏), rein in
(收心, 收敛). 收音机 "radio."

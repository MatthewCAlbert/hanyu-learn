## Etymology
辶 "movement" gives the meaning; the right element in the old form is 灷, a
gift or torch held up in two hands, carrying the sound. Shuowen: 遣也 "to
dispatch, to see off." Simplification reduces the two-hands element to 关.

## Mnemonic
Carrying something out 辶 to hand it over: to send, to see off.

## Notes
Three senses of 送: give a present (送礼), deliver (送货, 送到), escort out
(送客, 送行). As a coverb with 给: 送给你.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — a sloping bank — and 皮 *pí* supplies the sound (*pí* → *pō*). Shuowen glosses it as a hillside.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 whose skin 皮 curves down: a slope.

## Notes
皮 is sound only. Series: 波 *bō*, 玻 *bō*, 坡 *pō*, 破 *pò*, 披 *pī*. 山坡 "hillside", 上坡 "uphill", 下坡路, 新加坡 "Singapore" (transliteration).

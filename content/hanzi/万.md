## Etymology
Simplified from 萬 — a pictograph of a scorpion, pincers and curled tail.
Shuowen: 蟲也 "a creepy-crawly." It was borrowed for the number "ten
thousand" and never gave it back. 万 is an old abbreviated form adopted as the
standard.

## Mnemonic
A scorpion, borrowed to write "ten thousand."

## Notes
万 stands for "a vast number / absolutely" in idiom: 万一 "just in case,"
万分 "extremely," 万岁 "long live," 万事如意, 千军万马. Also the surname Wàn.

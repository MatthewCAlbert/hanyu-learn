## Etymology
儿 "a person" with two strokes marking the head — the top of the body, the
head. Shuowen: 始也 "the beginning, the first / primary." From "the head" grew
"the first, the origin, the chief" (元旦, 元首, 元素).

The currency unit 元 is a borrowing for 圆 / 圓 "a round (coin)."

## Mnemonic
The marked head of a standing figure 儿: the topmost, the first.

## Notes
元 is the phonetic in 玩 *wán*, 完 *wán*, 院 *yuàn*, 远 / 遠 *yuǎn*. Also the
Yuan dynasty, and the yuan (¥), colloquially 块.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread, silk) supplies the meaning and 彖 *tuàn* supplies the sound. The earliest sense is the decorated border on a garment (Shuowen: 衣純); from edge came "to follow along" (沿), and "predestined relationship" (缘分) is a Buddhist-influenced sense.

## Mnemonic
The silk 纟 edging that runs along the hem — the margin you can follow.

## Notes
彖 is sound only; the sound shifts from *tuàn* to *yuán*. 边缘, 缘故, 缘分 "fate" (the connection between people), 无缘无故.

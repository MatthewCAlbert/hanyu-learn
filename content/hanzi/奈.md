## Etymology
Not securely attested. The graph combines 大 (a person) with 示 (an altar/
spirit marker, the same component in 神, 祝, 祭), but 奈's grammatical use —
"how, what can be done about" (无奈, 怎奈) — has no clearly documented
semantic bridge back to that picture. Best treated as a grammatical word
whose original concrete meaning, if it ever had one distinct from its
modern use, is not recoverable with confidence.

## Mnemonic
A person 大 standing helpless before the altar 示 — what can be done?

## Notes
奈 survives almost entirely in fixed patterns: 无奈 "helpless, no choice,"
奈何 "what can be done" (classical), and the transliteration 奈良 for the
Japanese city Nara — a purely phonetic modern use.

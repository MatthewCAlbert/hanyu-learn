## Etymology
Ideographic. The original character 雧 shows 雥 (a cluster of three 隹
"bird") perched 木 (in a tree) — flocks of birds gathered on a tree, hence
"to gather, to collect." The modern 集 simplifies the three birds to one
隹, keeping the same picture in miniature.

## Mnemonic
A bird 隹 landing in a tree 木, soon joined by the rest of the flock:
to gather.

## Notes
From "birds gathering" the meaning generalised completely to "collecting,
assembling" of any kind — 集合, 集中, 收集 — and, separately, to "a bound
collection" (an episode of a show, a volume of essays: 一集, 全集).

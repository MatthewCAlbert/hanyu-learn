## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning, 辟 *bì* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 垺也 is close to "a rammed-earth wall"
— a defensive rampart. "Wall, partition" generalised from that
fortification sense to any solid dividing wall, indoor or out.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 packed and pounded solid, opened 辟 only where a gate allows.

## Notes
壁 covers architecture (墙壁 "wall," 壁画 "mural," 隔壁 "next door") and
figurative barriers (壁垒 "barrier, rampart"). Distinct from 墙 qiáng, the
more general everyday word for "wall" — 壁 leans more formal/literary.

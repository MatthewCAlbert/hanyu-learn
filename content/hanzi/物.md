## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 牛 "ox" gives the meaning — Shuowen calls the ox "a great thing,"
a fitting stand-in for "things" generally; 勿 *wù* gives the sound. Meaning:
a thing, matter, substance; a creature.

## Mnemonic
The ox 牛, greatest of things, standing for all things.

## Notes
The 勿 phonetic also writes 忽 *hū*, 惚 *hū*. Senses: object / thing (东西 vs
物品), matter / substance (物质, 生物), the world / others (人物, 物价,
待人接物).

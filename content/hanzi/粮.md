## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 米 (rice, grain — the harvested and hulled grain, distinct
from 禾, the growing plant) supplies the meaning, and 良 *liáng* (covered
elsewhere in this app) supplies the sound, an exact match.

"Grain" generalizes to "food provisions" broadly (粮食, 口粮, "rations")
— the staple stored to see a household or an army through.

## Mnemonic
Rice 米 stored away, good 良 and reliable provisions for whatever comes:
"粮," grain, food.

## Notes
粮食 ("food, grain, provisions") is the standard everyday compound —
broader than plain 米 (specifically rice) or 谷物 (grains as a botanical
category).

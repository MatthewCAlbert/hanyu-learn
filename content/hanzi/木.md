## Etymology
Pictographic. The oracle-bone form is a simple tree: a trunk with
branches spreading above and roots below. Shuowen reads it as built on 屮
(a sprouting plant), with the lower strokes standing for roots — "that
which pushes up out of the ground," associated with the eastern
direction in five-phase cosmology.

## Mnemonic
Branches spreading wide above, roots reaching down below: a tree.

## Notes
木 is one of the most productive semantic radicals in the entire script —
林, 森, 板, 树, 树, 校, 材 and hundreds more all group under it, any
character touching on wood, trees, or things made from them.

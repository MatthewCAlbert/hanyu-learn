## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 力 (effort) supplies the meaning, 堇 *jǐn* supplies the
sound, shifted to *qín*. Shuowen's gloss 劦也 ties it to combined,
concentrated effort — matching the modern "industrious, diligent"
directly.

## Mnemonic
Effort 力 applied steadily, day after day 堇.

## Notes
勤 is the everyday word for diligence: 勤劳 "hardworking," 勤奋 "diligent,"
出勤 "to be present at work/school" (literally "to go out and put in
effort"). Pairs naturally with 懒 lǎn "lazy" as its opposite.

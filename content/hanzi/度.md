## Etymology
Shuowen analyses it as 又 "hand" for the meaning — measuring is done by the
span of the hand and arm — with a reduced 庶 supplying the sound. From "to
measure" grew "a degree, a standard, an extent, a limit," and "to pass"
(time).

## Mnemonic
Marking off spans by hand 又 under the eaves 广: taking the measure of
things.

## Notes
Two readings: *dù* — degree / extent (温度, 程度, 角度), limit (限度, 适度),
pass time (度日, 欢度), a measure word for occurrences (一年一度); *duó* — to
estimate, to size up (揣度, 忖度).

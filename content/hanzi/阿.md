## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (a mound) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
is simply "a large hill" — and 可 *kě* supplies the sound.

**The everyday affectionate prefix sense has no clear link to "hill."**
阿 as a familiar prefix before names and kinship terms (阿姨, covered
elsewhere in this app; 阿姨's own use of it) is a much later colloquial
development, most likely borrowing the character purely for its sound —
this entry cannot confirm a bridge from "large hill" to that use, hence
medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
A great mound, agreeable 可 in sound alone — later borrowed just for
that friendly sound to soften a name: "阿," as in 阿姨.

## Notes
阿姨 (covered elsewhere in this app) is 阿's overwhelmingly dominant
modern use — its literal "large hill" sense survives only in place names
and classical texts.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 旨 *zhǐ* supplies the sound —
a clean, exact tonal match. The original sense, "finger," is unchanged in
modern Chinese.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pointing with purpose 旨 — indicating exactly what it means.

## Notes
"Finger" (手指) extends naturally to the verb "to point at, to indicate"
(指出, 指导) — the body part and the gesture it makes share one character,
as often happens across languages.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to grasp, to seize — and 足 *zú* supplies the sound (*zú* → *zhuō*). Shuowen glosses it as to seize.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 snatching at a foot 足.

## Notes
足 is sound only. 捉住 "catch", 捕捉 "capture", 捉迷藏 "hide-and-seek", 捉弄 "tease", 捉摸 "fathom".

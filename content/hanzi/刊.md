## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 刂 (knife) supplies the meaning — to cut, carve — and 干 *gān* supplies the sound. Cutting woodblocks to print gives "to carve, to engrave", and then "to publish" and "periodical".

## Mnemonic
A knife 刂 carves 干 the plate for the newspaper.

## Notes
干 is sound only. 报刊 "newspapers and periodicals", 刊登 "publish", 刊物, 创刊 "launch", 周刊 "weekly". Series: 刊 *kān*, 肝 *gān*, 竿 *gān*.

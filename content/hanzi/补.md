## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning. The traditional
phonetic was 甫 *fǔ*; simplification swapped it for the far simpler 卜
*bǔ* — an exact match in both sound and tone, unusually clean for a
simplification substitution. The original, concrete sense, 完衣也 "to
make a garment whole again," is a mending action — patching a hole in
fabric.

## Mnemonic
Cloth 衤, mended by a single well-placed stitch 卜, patch by patch.

## Notes
"To mend a garment" generalised into "to make up for any deficiency" —
补充 "to supplement," 弥补 "to compensate," 补课 "to make up a missed
class" — patching a hole in cloth as the model for filling any gap.

## Etymology
Simplified from 聲. The full graph is 耳 "ear" with 殸 (a hanging stone chime
struck by a mallet 殳) — the ring of a chime reaching the ear. Shuowen: 音也
"a sound." Simplification keeps only the top-left of 殸 and drops the ear, so
the picture is gone.

## Mnemonic
Learn 声 by its meaning — the ear and the chime it once had are lost.

## Notes
Senses: sound / voice (声音, 大声, 无声), a tone in phonology (四声, 声调),
reputation / fame (名声, 声望), and a measure word for cries (叫了一声).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 示 (an altar, the ritual/spirit radical seen in 神, 祝)
supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition names ritual taboos, things
forbidden according to omens of fortune or misfortune — and 林 *lín*
supplies the sound.

Ritual, sacred prohibition generalized to "to prohibit, to forbid" of
any kind (禁止, 禁烟) — an authority's ban carrying some of that original
sacred weight.

## Mnemonic
A sacred altar 示 standing before a forest 林, marking it as forbidden
ground: "禁," to prohibit.

## Notes
禁止 ("to prohibit," the everyday formal verb) and 情不自禁 ("unable to
help oneself," lit. "feelings cannot be forbidden/restrained") both build
on this character's core "forbidden, restrained" sense.

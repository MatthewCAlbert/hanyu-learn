## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 立 (to stand) supplies the meaning — to stand upright — and 耑 *duān* supplies the sound. 耑 is itself an older pictograph of a plant shoot with roots, used for "tip, beginning" before 端 took over that sense; so 耑 is both the sound and the earlier word for "end".

## Mnemonic
Stand 立 right at the very tip 耑 — upright and exact.

## Notes
"Straight, correct" (端正) gave "end, tip" (末端, 极端, 弊端) and "to hold level in both hands" (端茶). 端午节 is the Dragon Boat Festival, 端 here being the old word for "beginning" (the 5th day of the 5th month).

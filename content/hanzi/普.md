## Etymology
並 "side by side, all together" over 日 "sun." Shuowen: 日無色也 "sunlight
without a shadow" — light falling everywhere at once. From "spread over
everything" grew "universal, general, widespread."

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 reaching every place alike 並: universal.

## Notes
Senses: general / widespread (普遍, 普及, 普通), a census (普查). 普通话 "the
common speech" — Mandarin. Also the surname Pǔ.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning; the traditional form 種 used
重 *zhòng* for sound. Shuowen's original sense is agricultural and specific —
grain that "ripens early, matures late" — before it generalised to "seed,"
"type," and "to plant."

Simplification replaced the phonetic 重 with the visually simpler 中
*zhōng*, a swap made for the same reason 鐘/鍾 became 钟: 中 is close enough
in sound and much easier to write. So today's phonetic is 中, not 重.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾 planted in the middle 中 of a field.

## Notes
Two readings split the senses: *zhǒng* "seed, kind, type" (as in 种类, 一种)
versus *zhòng* "to plant" (种地, 种树). The pronunciation carries real
grammatical information — noun versus verb.

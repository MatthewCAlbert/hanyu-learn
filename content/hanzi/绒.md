## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 絨: 纟 (thread/fabric) supplies the meaning, 戎
*róng* supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The stable
core sense — a soft, fine-fibred fabric or fluff — covers wool, velvet,
and any downy textile surface.

## Mnemonic
Soft fibres 纟, gentle as down despite sharing a sound with soldiers' 戎
armour.

## Notes
绒 anchors 绒毛 "down, fine fluff," 天鹅绒 "velvet" (literally "swan-down"),
and 绒线 "knitting wool" — always about softness and fine texture in
fabric.

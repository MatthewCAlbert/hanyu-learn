## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph shows two hands raising a single object together
(the lower strokes are worn from 廾 "two hands"). Shuowen: 同也 "in common."
This is the parent graph of 供 "to supply" and 拱 "to cup the hands."

## Mnemonic
Two hands lifting the one thing between them: shared.

## Notes
Senses: together / jointly (共同, 共事, 同甘共苦), altogether (总共, 一共,
共计), share (共用, 共享, 共存), and short for 共产党 (中共) / communism
(共青团). 共鸣 "resonance, sympathy."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: the grass-like top 龶 (related to 生, "to grow") supplies the
meaning — Shuowen describes 毒 directly as 害人之草 "a plant that harms
people, growing here and there" — and 母 *mǔ* supplies the sound, shifted to
*dú*. The image is a harmful plant growing thick and abundant (厚也), the
source of poison.

## Mnemonic
A plant 龶 growing thick and dangerous over a mother's 母 field — do not eat
it.

## Notes
毒 spans literal poison (中毒 "to be poisoned," 病毒 "virus" — literally
"sickness-poison") and narcotics (吸毒 "to use drugs," 毒品 "narcotics"), plus
a figurative "vicious, cruel" sense (毒辣, 心狠手毒). 母 here is sound only,
unrelated to "mother."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 日 "sun / day" gives the meaning; 免 *miǎn* gives the sound
(for *wǎn*). Shuowen: 莫也 (莫 = 暮) "dusk, late in the day."

## Mnemonic
The day 日 winding down: evening; late.

## Notes
The 免 phonetic also writes 勉 *miǎn*, 冕 *miǎn*, 娩 *miǎn*, 挽 *wǎn*. Senses:
evening / night (晚上, 晚饭), late (来晚了, 晚点), a junior generation (晚辈).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning "to touch, to feel," and 莫
*mò* supplies the sound.

"To feel around" (摸黑, to feel one's way in the dark) extends naturally to
"to probe, to grope toward an understanding" (摸索, to feel one's way
forward; 摸清, to get a clear feel for something).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 feeling its way through darkness where the sun has vanished 莫
(莫's own old sense, "the sun setting into grass"): "摸," to touch/feel.

## Notes
摸索 ("to feel one's way, to explore tentatively") is the most common
extended use — learning or progress made step by uncertain step, the way
a hand gropes in the dark.

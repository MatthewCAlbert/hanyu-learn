## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 "female" gives the meaning; 未 *wèi* gives the sound (for
*mèi*). Shuowen: 女弟也 "a younger sister."

## Mnemonic
The female 女 who came later, not yet 未 grown: younger sister.

## Notes
The 未 phonetic also writes 味 *wèi*, 昧 *mèi*, 魅 *mèi*. 妹妹 doubled, second
syllable toneless; friendly 小妹, 表妹 "female cousin."

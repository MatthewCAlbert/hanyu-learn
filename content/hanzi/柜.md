## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 櫃: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning, 巨 *jù*
supplies the sound, shifted to *guì*. Not in Shuowen. The straightforward
"wooden storage box/cabinet" sense has stayed concrete and stable.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 box, big 巨 enough to store everything away.

## Notes
柜 anchors 柜子 "cabinet, wardrobe," 衣柜 "wardrobe/closet," 冰柜 "freezer,"
and 柜台 "shop counter" — the last extending "storage cabinet" to the
raised counter a shopkeeper stands behind.

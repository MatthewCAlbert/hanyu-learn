## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 誼: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning, 宜 *yí*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged — and 宜's own sense "fitting,
proper" also echoes meaning: what is spoken and agreed to be proper
between people. Shuowen's gloss 人所宜也 "what is fitting between people"
is the direct root of "friendship, bond."

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 exchanged the right, fitting 宜 way — the basis of friendship.

## Notes
谊 is almost always bound: 友谊 "friendship," 情谊 "bond of affection." A
more formal, written register than the everyday 友情 or 感情.

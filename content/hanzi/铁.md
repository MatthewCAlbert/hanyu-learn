## Etymology
Simplified from 鐵. 钅 (金) "metal" gives the meaning — Shuowen: 黑金也 "the
black metal." The traditional phonetic 𢧜 is elaborate; the simplified form
replaces it with the simple 失 *shī* (a rough match for *tiě*).

## Mnemonic
The common dark metal 钅: iron.

## Notes
Senses: iron (钢铁, 铁矿, 打铁), and figuratively hard, fixed, beyond doubt
(铁的纪律, 铁证如山, 铁心, 铁定). 手无寸铁 "without a weapon in hand";
铁路 "railway"; 地铁 "subway."

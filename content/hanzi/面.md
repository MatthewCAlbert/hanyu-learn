## Etymology
Pictographic — the outline of a face with an eye (目) set inside it. Shuowen:
顏前也 "the front of the head." From "face" grew "surface / side" (表面,
外面), "face to face" (面对面, 见面), and "aspect" (方面).

The "flour / noodles" sense (面包, 面条) belongs to a separate character 麵 /
麺, merged into 面 in simplification.

## Mnemonic
An eye 目 framed by the outline of a head: a face.

## Notes
面 is Kangxi radical 176. As a locative suffix ("-side"): 上面, 里面, 后面.
Measure word for flat things and for meetings: 一面镜子, 见过一面.

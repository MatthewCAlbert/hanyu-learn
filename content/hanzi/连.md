## Etymology
Ideographic, not pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) beside 车 (a cart) — a person
on foot pulling a cart along. Duan Yucai's commentary makes the picture
explicit: 負車也, "hauling a cart." Person and cart move as one unit, which
is exactly the sense that generalised to "linked, joined, continuous."

連 was also, in classical texts, an early name for what became 輦 (a
hand-pulled palanquin) — the concrete vehicle sense died out, leaving only
the abstract "connected" meaning that survives today.

## Mnemonic
Walking 辶 while pulling a cart 车: the two move connected, one after
the other.

## Notes
连 is unusual among 辶 characters in HSK 1–3 for being ideographic rather
than pictophonetic — compare 边 and 过, both of which lost a phonetic in
simplification; 连 never had one.

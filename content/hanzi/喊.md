## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 咸 *xián* gives the sound (for
*hǎn*). A late character, not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
Opening the mouth 口 wide to be heard: to shout.

## Notes
The 咸 phonetic also writes 减 / 減 *jiǎn*, 感 *gǎn*, 憾 *hàn*, 撼 *hàn*.
Senses: shout / yell (喊叫, 大喊, 呐喊), call out to (喊他一声, 喊救命),
and chant (喊口号). 喊冤 "to cry out that one has been wronged."

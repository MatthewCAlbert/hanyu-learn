## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh) supplies the meaning — a thin membrane between tissues — and 莫 *mò* supplies the sound (*mò* → *mó*).

## Mnemonic
A thin layer of flesh ⺼ at sunset 莫: delicate, translucent.

## Notes
莫 is sound only. 膜 as "film": 保鲜膜 "cling film", 薄膜 "thin film", 耳膜 "eardrum", 角膜 "cornea", 膜拜 "worship prostrate". Series: 墓, 幕, 慕, 暮, 模, 漠.

## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 啓/啟: 户 (a door) with 口 (a mouth/opening) below
— a door swung open, and, by extension, a mouth opening to speak or
explain. "To open," "to begin," and "to explain/enlighten" (启发 "to
inspire," literally "to open and set off") all extend from that single
image of something closed being opened up.

## Mnemonic
A door 户 swinging open 口 to let the light — and the explanation — in.

## Notes
启 anchors 开启 "to open/initiate," 启动 "to start up [a machine, a
project]," 启发 "to inspire/enlighten," and the polite letter-opener 敬启
"respectfully opened [i.e., please read]."

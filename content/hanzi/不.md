## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is now generally read as the calyx or pistil of a flower
(the original of 柎 "calyx"), borrowed early and permanently as the negative
adverb. Shuowen's picture — "a bird flying up and never coming down" — is a
folk reading of the later shape, not the origin.

## Mnemonic
A flower-base with nothing growing above it: not.

## Notes
不 is Kangxi radical 1's neighbour on the page but has no pictographic tie to
"one." Tone-sandhi: *bù* → *bú* before a fourth tone (不是 *búshì*). Compare
没(有) for past / existential negation.

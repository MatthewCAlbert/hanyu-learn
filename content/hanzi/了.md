## Etymology
Pictographic, per Shuowen: a child (子) drawn with no arms — 从子無臂 — the
limbs tucked or bound. The lexical word *liǎo* means "to finish, to settle, to
be able to" (了结, 了解, 受不了).

The aspect and modal particle *le* (吃了, 太贵了) is a grammaticalised, toneless
use of the same graph and carries none of the "child" or "finish" imagery.

## Mnemonic
A swaddled child, all wrapped up — done.

## Notes
Compare 子, the same figure with arms free. Two readings: *liǎo* (了不起,
一目了然) and toneless *le* (the completed-action / change-of-state particle).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh/body-part) supplies the meaning, 卑 *bēi* supplies
the sound, shifted to *pí*. Shuowen's gloss 土藏也, "the earth-organ,"
reflects Five-Phases medical theory, which assigned each organ an element —
the spleen was linked to earth, the way the liver was linked to wood. The
character has otherwise stayed anatomically stable.

## Mnemonic
The lowly ⺼ organ, humble 卑 and unsung compared to the heart or lungs.

## Notes
脾 is mostly medical vocabulary today (脾脏 "spleen," 脾气 "temperament" —
literally "spleen-energy," from the old belief that the spleen governed
mood and disposition, giving us 好脾气 "good-tempered" and 脾气不好
"bad-tempered").

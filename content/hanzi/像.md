## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 象 *xiàng* gives the sound — and
象 "an image, to resemble" also carries the sense. Meaning: to resemble, to be
like; a likeness, a portrait, a statue.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 rendered as an image 象: a likeness.

## Notes
象 alone (elephant → shape → phenomenon) covers "appearance / phenomenon"
(现象, 印象); 像 is specifically "resemble / an image" (好像, 画像, 雕像,
录像). The 象 phonetic also writes 橡 *xiàng* (oak / rubber).

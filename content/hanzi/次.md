## Etymology
欠 "a person exhaling, mouth agape" gives the meaning — hanging back, not
foremost; Shuowen adds 二 *èr* "two" as the phonetic and semantic hint, giving
"the one behind the first." From "second in order" grew "next," "an occasion /
a time" (一次, 上次), and "of lesser quality" (次品). The 二 phonetic is now
written 冫.

## Mnemonic
Falling a breath 欠 behind first place: the next one.

## Notes
As a verbal / nominal measure: 三次 "three times," 每次 "each time." 次 also
"order / sequence" (次序, 依次).

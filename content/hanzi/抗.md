## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 亢 *kàng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as defending, warding off.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 raised against a tall 亢 opponent.

## Notes
亢 is sound only. Its series: 炕 *kàng*, 坑 *kēng*, 航 *háng*, 抗 *kàng*. 抵抗, 对抗, 抗议, 反抗.

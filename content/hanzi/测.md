## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's own definition
is specifically about gauging a water depth — and 则 *zé* supplies the
sound.

"To gauge how deep the water is" generalized to the ordinary modern verb
"to measure" (测量) and, further, "to estimate, to guess at" a value one
cannot directly observe (测试, 预测).

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 sounded by a standard rule 则 (measure, covered in this app's
hanzi entry for 则) to find its depth: to "测," measure.

## Notes
测 and 则 share the same phonetic (则) — worth noting as one more member
of that phonetic family alongside 侧 (cè, side) and 厕 (cè, toilet).

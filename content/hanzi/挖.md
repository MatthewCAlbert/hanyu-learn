## Etymology
扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to dig by hand — and 穵 supplies both sound and meaning: an older graph for a hollow or hole (穴 with a bent stroke), which is also *wā*. 挖 is a late graph that adds the hand to specify the action.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 scooping a hole 穵 in the ground.

## Notes
穵 is rare outside this character. 挖掘 "excavate", 挖苦 "mock", 挖土, 挖潜 "tap potential". The sense of "poke fun" (挖苦) is metaphoric.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 巨 *jù* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 止也 "to stop, to block" matches the
modern "to refuse, to resist" directly — a hand held up to bar the way.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 held up, immovable as something huge 巨 — refused.

## Notes
拒 anchors 拒绝 "to refuse/reject," 抗拒 "to resist," 拒之门外 "to shut
someone out" (literally "refuse them outside the door").

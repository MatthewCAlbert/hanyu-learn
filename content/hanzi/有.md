## Etymology
A hand (又, now written across the top) holding a piece of meat (⺼, the 月-shaped
element) — to have something in hand, to possess. Shuowen instead treats 又 as
a phonetic and gives a strained gloss ("what should not be had"); the
hand-and-meat reading is the one modern scholarship supports.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 gripping a cut of meat ⺼: to have.

## Notes
The 月 here is 肉 "flesh," as in 肚, 脸, 胖 — but in 服 it is 舟 "boat" and in
期 it is the moon. Uses: possess (有钱), exist
(有问题), and — negated only by 没 — past / existential (没有去过).

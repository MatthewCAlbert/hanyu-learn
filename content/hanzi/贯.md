## Etymology
Traditional 貫 is 毌 (to pierce, string through) over 貝 (shell money): coins strung on a cord, the string of cash. 毌 is also read *guàn* and is both the sound and the idea. The sense extends to "to pierce through, to run throughout" (贯穿) and "consistent" (一贯).

## Mnemonic
Shells 贝 pierced 毌 on a string: a string of cash.

## Notes
In the simplified form, the top is written 毌 (a ring-bound shape). 贯穿 "run through", 贯彻 "carry out", 一贯 "consistent", 连贯 "coherent". 惯 *guàn* shares it.

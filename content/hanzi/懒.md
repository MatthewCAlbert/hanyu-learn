## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart, disposition) supplies the meaning — idle, listless — and 賴 (simplified 赖) *lài* supplies the sound. Shuowen's older graph is 嬾 with 女 as the radical; the form with 忄 is a later replacement that fits the sense better. The sound shifted from *lài* to *lǎn*.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 that merely relies 赖 on others.

## Notes
赖 is sound only. 懒惰 "lazy", 偷懒 "slack off", 懒得 "can't be bothered", 懒洋洋 "listless".

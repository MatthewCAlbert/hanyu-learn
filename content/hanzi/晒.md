## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 曬: 日 (sun) supplies the meaning,
and 麗 *lì* supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 麗 with the unrelated 西** (*xī*, "west"), which
supplies no reliable sound clue toward *shài* and no meaning either — a
shape substitution, not an inherited phonetic. Given the loss, this entry
declares only the surviving semantic 日.

## Mnemonic
The sun 日 beating straight down as it sets in the west 西: to "晒," expose
to sunlight.

## Notes
晒太阳 ("to sunbathe," lit. "to sun the sun") and 晒黑 ("to get sunburnt/
tanned," a resultative compound) both build on this character's plain
"exposed to sunlight" sense.

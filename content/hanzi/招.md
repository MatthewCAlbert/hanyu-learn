## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — "to call by waving the hand"
— and 召 *zhāo* supplies the sound. Unusually, 召 also pulls double duty on
meaning here: 召 on its own already means "to summon, to call" (口 mouth +
刀-shaped phonetic), so the hand-wave of 招 and the voice-call of 召 are two
concrete images of the same underlying act of summoning someone.

"To recruit, to enlist" (招聘, 招收) is a direct extension of "to summon" —
calling people in to fill a role.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 waving while a voice 召 calls out: "招," to beckon someone over.

## Notes
Compare 召 alone (to summon, more formal/authoritative — 召开 a meeting) with
招 (a more everyday beckoning or recruiting gesture) — related in both sound
and sense, but not interchangeable.

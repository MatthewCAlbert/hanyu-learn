## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning, 土 *tǔ* (earth) supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 寫也 "to discharge, to pour out"
matches the modern senses directly — "to vomit," "to spit out," and
figuratively "to pour out one's feelings" (吐苦水) are all the same "expel
from the mouth" image.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 pouring something out onto the earth 土.

## Notes
Two readings by transitivity: *tǔ* is the general verb (吐痰 "to spit
phlegm," 吐露 "to disclose"); *tù* is specifically "to vomit" (吐了 "threw
up"). 土 here is purely a sound clue, unrelated to the "earth/soil" meaning
it carries elsewhere.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning — mushrooms, fungi — and 囷 *qūn* supplies the sound (*qūn* → *jūn/jùn*). Shuowen glosses it as a mushroom growing from the ground. "Bacteria, germ" is an extension of the "fungus" sense in modern biology.

## Mnemonic
A plant 艹 shut up in a granary 囷: fungus.

## Notes
囷 is sound only (an old word for a round granary). 细菌 "bacteria", 真菌 "fungus", 病菌 "germ", 菌类, 蘑菇 is the everyday "mushroom". Two readings: *jūn* "germ" and *jùn* in 菌子 (colloquial mushroom).

## Etymology
Ideographic in the traditional form 奮: 奞 (a bird with wings spread wide,
built on 大 "big" over 隹 "bird") positioned 在田上, "above a field" — a
bird beating its wings vigorously as it takes flight over open ground.

**Simplification dropped the bird 隹 entirely**, leaving only 大 (big)
over 田 (field) — the wing-beating bird that gave the character its whole
meaning is no longer visible in the modern shape, even though the sense
"to exert oneself vigorously" survives intact.

## Mnemonic
A great effort spread wide across the whole field, the way a bird once
beat its wings above it: "奋," to rouse oneself to action.

## Notes
奋斗 ("to struggle, to strive," covered in this app's entry for 兴奋) and
兴奋 (excited) both keep this character's core "vigorous exertion" sense,
even without the bird that originally pictured it.

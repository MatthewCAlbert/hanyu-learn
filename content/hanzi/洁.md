## Etymology
Traditional 潔 is pictophonetic: 氵 (water) for meaning, 絜 *xié/jié* for sound. Simplification swapped in 吉 *jí* for the sound — a good match, so the new phonetic still works. Shuowen glosses it as clean, clear.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 for good luck 吉 — a washed, lucky clean.

## Notes
The simplified phonetic 吉 is a deliberate re-made clue and not a semantic claim about "luck". 清洁, 简洁, 纯洁, 洁净. Also 结 *jié/jiē*, which has the same sound part.

## Etymology
Pictographic: 壺 is a picture of a lidded pot — Shuowen says the top "大" part represents the cover. The simplified 壶 is a cleaned-up variant, so its parts (士, 冖, 业) are decorative rather than meaningful. The "jar of wine" description is right in sense but not in the parts.

## Mnemonic
A pot with a lid 冖 on top and a firm base.

## Notes
水壶 "kettle", 茶壶 "teapot", 酒壶 "flask", 壶口 "spout". Measure word for bottled liquids: 一壶茶.

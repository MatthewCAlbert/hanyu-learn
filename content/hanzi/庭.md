## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 广 "a building" gives the meaning; 廷 *tíng* "a court" gives the
sound and reinforces the sense. Shuowen: 宮中也 "the space within a palace" —
a hall and its courtyard.

## Mnemonic
The court 廷 enclosed by the building 广: a courtyard.

## Notes
The 廷 phonetic also writes 挺 *tǐng*, 停 *tíng*, 艇 *tǐng*, 蜓 *tíng*.
Senses: courtyard (庭院, 前庭), a family / household (家庭), a court of law
(法庭, 开庭).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh) supplies the meaning, 殳 *shū* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as the thigh. "Part of a whole, a share" is an extension, from the thigh as one "leg" of a body, and by then stock market "shares" (股票).

## Mnemonic
A piece of flesh ⺼ hit by a club 殳 — a thigh, a share of meat.

## Notes
殳 is sound only; the sound match *shū*/*gǔ* is distant. 股票, 股份, 股东, 炒股. As a measure word 股 counts streams and strands (一股香味).

## Etymology
Oracle-bone forms of 宮 show two boxes (rooms) under a roof, a large multi-room house, which is the modern 吕 shape. Shuowen instead analyses it as 宀 with an abbreviated 躬 *gōng* for sound. Both explanations agree on 宀 and on the "large building" reading, which then narrowed to "palace".

## Mnemonic
A roof 宀 over two rooms 吕: a palace with many halls.

## Notes
The 吕 here is the two-rooms shape, not the surname Lǚ or the "spine" word. 宫殿 "palace", 皇宫, 宫廷 "court", 子宫 "uterus", 故宫 "the Forbidden City".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 乍 *zhà* gives the sound (for
*zuò*) — and 乍 "for the first time, suddenly" chimes with the sense. Shuowen:
起也 "to rise up, to set to work."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 setting to it for the first time 乍: to make.

## Notes
The 乍 phonetic also writes 昨 *zuó*, 炸 *zhà*, 诈 / 詐 *zhà*, 怎 *zěn*
(loosely). 作 for abstract / bound uses (作业, 作用, 工作, 写作); 做 for
concrete ones — see 做.

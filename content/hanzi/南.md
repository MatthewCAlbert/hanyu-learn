## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph is generally read as a hanging bell or a percussion
instrument — a type associated with the music of southern peoples. It was
borrowed to write the direction "south." Shuowen's botanical gloss ("plants
reaching southward put out branches") is a later rationalisation of the
compass sense.

## Mnemonic
A bronze bell hung to be struck — borrowed to mean "south."

## Notes
Directions in Chinese compounds run 东/西 then 南/北: 东南, 西南. 南方 "the
South (of China)"; 南边 "the south side." Also the surname Nán.

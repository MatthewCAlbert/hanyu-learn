## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 詢: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning, 旬 *xún*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 謀也 "to consult,
to deliberate" matches the modern "to ask about, to inquire" closely — a
spoken exchange aimed at gathering counsel or information.

## Mnemonic
Words 讠 exchanged over ten days 旬, gathering opinions before deciding.

## Notes
询 anchors 询问 "to inquire," 咨询 "to consult [for advice]," and 征询 "to
seek [an opinion]" — always a formal, considered asking rather than
casual chit-chat.

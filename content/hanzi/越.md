## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 走 "to run, to go" gives the meaning; 戉 *yuè* (a broad-bladed
axe) gives the sound. Shuowen: 度也 "to cross over." From "to cross / go
beyond" grew "to exceed, to surpass," and the correlative "the more… the
more…" (越来越, 越…越…).

## Mnemonic
Running 走 clear over the barrier: to cross beyond.

## Notes
The 戉 phonetic also writes 钺 / 鉞 *yuè* (a battle-axe). Also the ancient
state of Yue (越国) and, today, Vietnam (越南). 超越 "to transcend"; 优越
"superior."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 憐: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning, 令 *lìng*
supplies the sound, shifted to *lián*. Not in Shuowen. The sense "to pity,
to sympathise with" has been stable, describing an ache of the heart felt
on another's behalf.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 moved by another's command 令 for sympathy — pity.

## Notes
怜 anchors 可怜 "pitiable, poor thing" and 怜惜 "to feel tender pity for" —
usually directed at someone in a weaker or more vulnerable position than
the speaker.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 车 (a vehicle/wheel) supplies the meaning; 专 *zhuān* supplies
the sound — both are, tellingly, in the same phonetic series as 专 itself,
which Shuowen and the oracle-bone record trace to a spindle-and-hand image
for "to spin." 转 keeps that "spinning, turning" sense on a wheel.

## Mnemonic
A wheel 车 turning round and round, dedicated 专 to one direction.

## Notes
The 专 phonetic series shows the sound clearly, without needing to invent a
meaning for 专 here: 转 zhuàn/zhuǎn "to turn," 传 chuán "to transmit," 团
tuán "round, group" — three different senses, one shared sound.

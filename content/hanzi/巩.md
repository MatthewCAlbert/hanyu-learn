## Etymology
巩 was the sound element of the older 鞏 (革 leather + 巩 *gǒng*, "to bind with leather", Shuowen), and in simplification it took over the whole word. As a separate graph it is read as hands holding something with 工 *gōng* as the sound (*gōng* → *gǒng*). The modern right half 凡 is a worn-down form of the hands, not the word "ordinary". The reading as hands is Shuowen-based and not secure.

## Mnemonic
Two firm hands holding a tool 工 steady: reinforcing.

## Notes
巩固 "consolidate", 巩膜 "sclera", also the surname Gǒng. 工 gives only sound.

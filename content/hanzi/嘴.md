## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 觜 *zuǐ* gives the sound — and
觜, used early on for "beak," adds a hint of sense. A late character that
displaced plain 口 as the everyday noun.

## Mnemonic
口 was the old word; 嘴 pins it down with 觜 for the sound.

## Notes
Extended to anything beak- or spout-shaped: 瓶嘴 "bottle spout," 山嘴
"a spur of hill," 烟嘴 "cigarette holder." 嘴巴 "mouth / a slap"; 多嘴
"to speak out of turn"; 嘴硬 "unwilling to admit fault."

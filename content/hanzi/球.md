## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 王 here is 玉 "jade" (the radical loses its dot), giving the
meaning; 求 *qiú* gives the sound. Shuowen: 玉磬也 "a jade chime-stone / a fine
jade." From "a rounded jade object" grew "a sphere, a ball," and so "the globe"
(地球) and "ball games" (足球, 篮球).

## Mnemonic
A polished jade 王 sphere, sought after 求.

## Notes
The 求 phonetic also writes 救 *jiù*, 逑 *qiú*. 求 itself is a pictograph of a
fur coat (the original of 裘).

## Etymology
Two originally separate characters converged here. 帘's own older meaning —
still visible in the meanings list, "a tavern's flag-sign" — named the
cloth banner 巾 hung outside a wine shop to advertise it, likely under a
covering shape read as a doorway or eave. Simplification merged in 簾
(bamboo 竹 + 廉, a window/door curtain), so modern 帘 carries 簾's job —
"curtain" — while its own shape preserves the old tavern-flag character.

## Mnemonic
A cloth 巾 hung out front to catch the eye — once a tavern's flag, now a
window's curtain.

## Notes
帘 today means "curtain" almost exclusively: 窗帘 "window curtain," 门帘
"door curtain." The "tavern sign" sense that the character's own shape
literally depicts has fallen out of use entirely.

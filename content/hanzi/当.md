## Etymology
Simplified from 當. 田 "field" gives the meaning; 尚 *shàng* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 田相值也 "two fields of equal worth, matched against each other."
From "to match / be equal to" grew "to serve as, to act as," "ought," "at
(that very time / place)," and — read *dàng* — "to treat as" and "to pawn."
The simplified 当 is a schematic reduction with no legible parts.

## Mnemonic
One field weighed against another as its equal: to match; to stand in for.

## Notes
Two readings: *dāng* "to be / serve as / at / ought" (当老师, 当时, 应当),
*dàng* "to regard as / pawn / proper" (当作, 当铺, 恰当, 上当).

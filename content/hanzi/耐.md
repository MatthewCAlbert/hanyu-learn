## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen defines 耐 (written 耏 in some editions) as a specific
legal term: "an offense not serious enough to warrant shaving the beard" —
一种较轻的刑罰, a lesser punishment. 而 pictures a beard/whiskers, and 寸
(a hand/measure) marks the legal act of meting out that penalty. "To
endure, to withstand" generalised from enduring this lighter sentence to
enduring anything difficult.

## Mnemonic
Keeping your beard 而 through a light sentence 寸 — you can take it.

## Notes
耐 today is almost always the "able to withstand" sense: 耐心 "patience"
(literally "an enduring heart"), 耐用 "durable," 忍耐 "to bear/endure" —
pairing naturally with 忍, its near-synonym.

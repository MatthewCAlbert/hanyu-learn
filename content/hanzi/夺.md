## Etymology
Traditional 奪 is built from 又 (a hand) and 奞, a bird with spread wings — a bird slipping out of a hand (Shuowen: "the hand holds it and it is lost"). That gives "to lose, to take away", then "to seize by force". In simplified 夺 the bird is reduced to 大 and the hand is 寸, so the scene is no longer visible; "a man grasping something" would describe the simplified shape only.

## Mnemonic
A big 大 hand 寸 closing on the prize.

## Notes
夺取, 争夺, 剥夺, 夺冠 "win the title". Compare 奋 *fèn* (奮), the same 奞 "bird wings" idea. 寸 here stands for a hand, not "inch".

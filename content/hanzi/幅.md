## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 巾 (cloth) supplies the meaning, 畐 *fú* supplies the sound and
reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 布帛廣也 is "the width of woven cloth" — the
standard bolt-width by which fabric, and later any flat sheet (paintings,
newspaper pages), came to be measured and counted.

## Mnemonic
A bolt of cloth 巾 measured out, jar-shaped and even 畐, one width at a time.

## Notes
Still active as the classifier for pictures and cloth-like flat objects:
一幅画 "a painting," 一幅地图 "a map." 畐 (a full jar) recurs as phonetic or
semantic in 副 fù, 富 fù, 福 fú — a cluster all built on the "full,
abundant" image behind the jar shape.

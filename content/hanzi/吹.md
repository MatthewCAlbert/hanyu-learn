## Etymology
Associative: 口 "mouth" beside 欠 "a person with the mouth wide open,
breathing out." Shuowen: 噓也 "to exhale, to puff." Meaning: to blow.

## Mnemonic
An open mouth 口 pushing out breath 欠: to blow.

## Notes
Senses: blow — of wind or breath (风吹, 吹灭蜡烛, 吹口哨), play a wind
instrument (吹笛子, 吹拉弹唱), boast (吹牛, 吹嘘, 自吹自擂), flatter
(吹捧), and (colloquial) to fall through / break up (事情吹了, 他们吹了).

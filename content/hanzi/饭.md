## Etymology
Simplified from 飯. Pictophonetic: 饣 (食) "food" gives the meaning; 反 *fǎn*
gives the sound (for *fàn*). Shuowen: 食也 "to eat / food." The noun senses —
cooked rice, and by extension "a meal" — became dominant.

## Mnemonic
Food 饣 in the bowl: rice, a meal.

## Notes
The 反 phonetic also writes 返 *fǎn*, 板 *bǎn*, 版 *bǎn*, 贩 / 販 *fàn*. 饭 as
"rice / meal" also in 米饭, 早饭, 吃饭, 做饭.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 卻: 卩 (a kneeling figure, used in
several characters for restraint or ceremony) supplies the meaning
"restraining/holding back," and 谷 *gǔ* supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced the phonetic.** 谷 was swapped for the
similar-looking 去 *qù*, which happens to still be a reasonable sound match for
*què* — closer, in fact, than 谷 was — but it is a shape substitution from the
2013 standard list, not the character's original phonetic. Treat 去 here as a
sound hint that survived by lucky coincidence, not as an inherited one.

"Restraining/holding back" extended naturally to the modern senses "but, yet,
however" — a却 clause holds back or pulls against what came before it.

## Mnemonic
去 (go) held back by 卩 (a figure kneeling, staying put) — going, and yet not:
"却," "however."

## Notes
却 is used almost entirely as a light adversative adverb before a verb (他却
不知道 "he, however, didn't know") — distinct from 但是/可是, which head a whole
clause.

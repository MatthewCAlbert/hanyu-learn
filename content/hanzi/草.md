## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (艸) "plants" gives the meaning; 早 *zǎo* gives the sound
(for *cǎo*). 艸 is the original drawing of two grass shoots (Shuowen glosses
it 百卉也 "all vegetation"); 草 — Shuowen's own entry makes it 草斗，櫟實
"the acorn, fruit of the oak" — was borrowed to write the common word and has
replaced it.

## Mnemonic
Low green growth 艹, spelled with 早 for its sound: grass.

## Notes
Senses: grass / straw / herbs (草地, 稻草, 草药, 花草), wild / rustic
(草根, 草莽), rough and hasty (草率, 潦草, 草草了事), a draft (草稿, 起草,
草拟), and the cursive script (草书, 狂草). 草坪 "lawn."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning, 匍 *pú* supplies the
sound. Not in Shuowen. Like its partner 萄, 葡 exists mainly to form the
loanword 葡萄 "grape," most likely borrowed along the Silk Road from a
Central Asian source rather than built from native Chinese roots —
confidence here again covers structure, not a fully traced loanword
history.

## Mnemonic
A vine plant 艹 that crawls and creeps 匍 along a trellis.

## Notes
葡萄 "grape" is the core word; 葡萄牙 "Portugal" is a much later, entirely
unrelated phonetic transliteration reusing 葡 purely for its sound — the
two uses of the character have nothing to do with each other.

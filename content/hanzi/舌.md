## Etymology
Pictographic: an oracle-bone form shows a tongue sticking out of a mouth (口), with dots for saliva. Shuowen reads the upper part as 干 (also a sound clue). In the modern graph that upper part has become 千 and is not the number "thousand", so any story using 千 describes the corrupted shape.

## Mnemonic
A tongue sticking out of the mouth 口 and tasting things.

## Notes
舌头 "tongue", 口舌, 舌尖. Compare 活 and 话 (舌 as sound or form). 七嘴八舌 "everyone talking at once".

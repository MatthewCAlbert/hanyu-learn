## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 犮 *bá* (a running dog)
supplies only the sound. Shuowen's gloss 擢也 "to pull up, to extract" is
identical to the modern meaning — a stable character with no notable drift.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 yanking something free as fast as a dog 犮 bolting away.

## Notes
拔 covers pulling things out (拔牙 "to pull a tooth," 拔草 "to weed") and
figuratively "to stand out, to excel" (出类拔萃 "to stand out from the
crowd") — the same "pulling up/out" image applied to rising above others.

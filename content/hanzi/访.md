## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning; 方 *fāng* supplies the
sound. Shuowen's gloss, 汎謀曰訪, is "broad consultation, seeking counsel
widely" — the original sense is closer to "to ask around, to seek
advice" than the modern, more neutral "to visit."

## Mnemonic
Words 讠, carried in every direction 方, seeking advice from all who
might know: to consult, to visit.

## Notes
访 today mostly means "to visit" in a purposeful, information-seeking
sense (访问, 采访 "to interview," 拜访 "to pay a formal visit") — the
older "to consult, to seek counsel" sense survives as the underlying
logic even where "visit" is the everyday gloss.

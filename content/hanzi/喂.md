## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 畏 *wèi* gives the sound. As an
interjection it is pure sound; the sense "to feed" (once also written 餵 /
餧) attached to the same graph.

## Mnemonic
A shout out of the mouth 口: "Hey!"

## Notes
Two uses: *wèi* / *wéi* — "hello, hey" (answering the phone, hailing
someone); *wèi* — to feed (喂鸡, 喂猪, 喂奶, 喂饭). 喂养 "to raise / rear
(an animal or child)."

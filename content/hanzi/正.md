## Etymology
止 "a foot" heading straight for 一 (in the oracle bone, 囗 — a walled town or
target). A foot marching directly on the goal: "on target, correct, upright,
straight." Shuowen: 是也 "right, so." From there: "exactly / just" (正好),
"just now, in the middle of" (正在), and "principal" (正门).

## Mnemonic
A foot 止 marching straight at the mark: dead on.

## Notes
Two readings: *zhèng* (common), *zhēng* only in 正月 "the first lunar month."
Opposite 反 or 歪 "crooked." 正 also heads 政 (government), 症 (symptom).

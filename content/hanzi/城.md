## Etymology
Pictophonetic, with the phonetic doing double duty (亦聲, "also carries
meaning"). 土 (earth) supplies the core sense — packed-earth walls; 成
*chéng* supplies the sound, and Shuowen notes it is not sound-only here: a
city wall is what "completes" (成) a settlement's defences, enclosing and
protecting the people within (以盛民也, "that which holds the people").

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 walls, completed 成 and standing guard: a city.

## Notes
成 as 亦聲 (semantic-and-phonetic) is worth flagging precisely because it
is the exception to "phonetic carries sound only" — most 形聲 characters
in this dataset are not like this, which is exactly why Shuowen bothers to
call it out here.

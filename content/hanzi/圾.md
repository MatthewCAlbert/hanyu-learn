## Etymology
As already covered in this app's entry for the word 垃圾, 圾 has no
independent meaning of its own — it exists essentially only to write the
second syllable of that one native dialectal word, its shape (土 plus 及)
chosen for an approximate sound rather than for any component meaning.
Confidence stays low because no independent semantic role can honestly
be assigned.

## Mnemonic
No real mnemonic to offer here — 圾 is a sound-only character. Just
remember it as the second half of 垃圾, and nowhere else.

## Notes
Encountering 圾 outside of 垃圾 is essentially never going to happen in
ordinary reading — treat any apparent second use as a typo or an
extremely rare/obscure exception.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 足 (foot) supplies the meaning; 兆 *zhào* supplies the sound.
Shuowen's gloss, 蹶也 "to stumble, to leap," already covers both the
"jump" and "trip" senses that 跳 carries in different contexts.

## Mnemonic
Feet 足 leaving the ground, betting everything on a good omen 兆: jump.

## Notes
跳 covers jumping, hopping and rhythmic movement alike — 跳舞 "to dance,"
跳高/跳远 "high/long jump," 心跳 "heartbeat" — all variations on "a
foot-driven bounce."

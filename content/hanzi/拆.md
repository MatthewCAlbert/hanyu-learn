## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 斥 *chì* supplies the sound,
shifted to *chāi*. Not in Shuowen. The sense "to tear open, to take apart"
has been stable and concrete since the character's earliest attested use.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pulling something apart, driving it back 斥 piece by piece.

## Notes
拆 covers opening packages (拆信 "to open a letter") and demolition (拆房子
"to tear down a building," 拆迁 "demolition and relocation," a common term
in modern Chinese urban redevelopment).

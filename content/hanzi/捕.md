## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to catch — and 甫 *fǔ* supplies the sound.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 reaching out to snatch up 甫 the thief.

## Notes
甫 is sound only. Series: 铺 *pù*, 浦 *pǔ*, 辅 *fǔ*, 傅 *fù*, 捕 *bǔ*, 哺 *bǔ*. 逮捕 "arrest", 捕捉 "catch", 被捕, 捕鱼.

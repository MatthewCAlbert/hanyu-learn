## Etymology
Two graphs meet here. 歬 — 止 "a foot" on top of 舟 "a boat," standing still
yet carried forward (Shuowen: 不行而進 "to progress without walking") — wrote
"front / advance." 前 itself (从刀，歬聲; Shuowen 齊斷也 "to cut evenly") was
the original graph for "to cut" — the original of 剪 "scissors." 前 was then
borrowed to write the "front" word, and 剪 was coined later to take the "cut"
sense back. In the modern shape 止 has worn to 丷一 and 舟 to 月.

## Mnemonic
A foot 止 riding a boat 月 forward: ahead.

## Notes
Senses: spatial "front" (前边, 面前), temporal "before / ago" (以前, 前天),
"former" (前任). 剪 is the descendant that kept "to cut."

## Etymology
The old form 歬 is 止 "a foot" on top of 舟 "a boat" — standing still yet being
carried forward: "to advance, the front." Shuowen: 不行而進 "to progress
without walking." A 刂 "knife" was later added, and the differentiated graph
剪 "scissors / to cut" took the "cut" sense back. In the modern shape 止 has
worn to 丷一 and 舟 to 月.

## Mnemonic
A foot 止 riding a boat 月 forward: ahead.

## Notes
Senses: spatial "front" (前边, 面前), temporal "before / ago" (以前, 前天),
"former" (前任). 剪 is the descendant that kept "to cut."

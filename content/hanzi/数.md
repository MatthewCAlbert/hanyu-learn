## Etymology
Simplified from 數. Pictophonetic: 攵 (攴) "action — to strike / tell off"
gives the meaning; 娄 (婁) *lóu* gives the sound. Shuowen: 計也 "to reckon,
to count."

## Mnemonic
Tapping items off one by one 攵: counting them.

## Notes
The 娄 phonetic also writes 楼 / 樓 *lóu*, 搂 / 摟 *lǒu*, 缕 / 縷 *lǚ*, 屡 /
屢 *lǚ*. Three readings: *shǔ* — count / enumerate / rank as (数数, 数一数二,
数落); *shù* — number / figure / several (数字, 数学, 数十人); *shuò*
(literary) — repeatedly (数见不鲜).

## Etymology
Shuowen: 倉 is a granary, drawn as an abbreviated 食 (food) over 口 (the building). Simplification drops the 口 and writes the top as 人, an old cursive form of the graph, so the picture of a food store is no longer visible in 仓. The explanation is of 倉, not of the shape on the card.

## Mnemonic
A person 人 stacking a heap of grain: a granary.

## Notes
仓库 "warehouse", 粮仓 "granary", 仓促 "hurried" (read *cāng*), 仓鼠 "hamster". 仓 is a sound part in 苍, 沧, 舱, 创 (*cāng/chuàng*).

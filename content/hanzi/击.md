## Etymology
Not clearly decomposable in its simplified form. Traditional 擊 combines a
hand-and-tool component with 手 (hand) below, describing a striking blow.
Simplification pared this down drastically to 击, whose current shape (a
mark over 凵) no longer preserves a legible picture of hand or weapon —
best treated as a fixed simplified form rather than analysed piece by
piece.

## Mnemonic
A blow landing squarely inside its mark — struck.

## Notes
击 anchors 打击 "to strike/hit (also figuratively, to deal a blow to),"
攻击 "to attack," 击败 "to defeat," 射击 "to shoot" — one of the more
frequent verb roots for forceful contact in modern Mandarin.

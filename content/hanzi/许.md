## Etymology
Simplified from 許. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 午
*wǔ* gives the sound (for *xǔ*). Shuowen: 聽也 "to listen to, to assent."

## Mnemonic
Hearing out 讠 a request and saying yes: to allow.

## Notes
Senses: permit / allow (允许, 准许, 不许), promise / betroth (许诺, 许配,
以身相许), praise (赞许, 推许), perhaps (也许, 或许, 兴许), an amount or
degree (许多, 些许, 少许), and "such" in classical usage (如许). Surname
*Xǔ*.

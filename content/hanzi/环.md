## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 王 here is the compressed left-side form of 玉 "jade"
(identical in shape to, but unrelated to, the character for "king"),
supplying the meaning; the traditional phonetic was the complex 瞏
*huán*. Shuowen's gloss is precise: a 環 is a jade ring where the band
and the hole are of equal width — 肉好若一.

Simplification replaced 瞏 with the much simpler, quite differently
pronounced 不 *bù*. Confidence is medium because that substitution is a
looser sound match than most pairs in this dataset, closer to a
stroke-saving convenience than a careful phonetic choice.

## Mnemonic
Jade 王, worked into a perfect circle, not 不 broken anywhere: a ring.

## Notes
"A jade ring" generalised fully into "anything ring-shaped" (光环) and,
by further extension, "to surround, to encircle" (环境, 环绕, 循环) —
the shape outlasting the material.

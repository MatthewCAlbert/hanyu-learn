## Etymology
Associative: 彡 "tufts of hair" drawn against 页 (頁) "the head / face."
Shuowen: 面毛也 "the hair on the face" — a beard. The graph was borrowed for
"must, ought," and the beard sense was re-marked as 鬚 (now simplified back
to 须).

## Mnemonic
Strokes of hair 彡 on the face 页: whiskers.

## Notes
Senses: must / have to (必须, 须知, 务须, 无须), to wait (须臾 "a moment"),
and a beard or beard-like fringe (胡须, 触须 feeler, 须根 fibrous root).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 主 *zhǔ* supplies the
sound. The original, concrete sense, 灌也 "to pour, to irrigate," is the
root of the modern "to pour attention into something" — 注意, 关注,
专注.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 poured straight to its owner 主: focused, directed attention.

## Notes
"To pour liquid into a fixed channel" extends into "to pour effort/focus
into a fixed target" (注意, 注重) and, separately, into "an added
explanatory note" (注释, 备注) — text poured alongside the main text to
clarify it.

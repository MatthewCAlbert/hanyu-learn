## Etymology
Not securely attested. The early graph shows a large standing figure with the
head, arms and legs (夊) all drawn prominently — Shuowen takes it as "a
person of the central states." From the Xia (夏) people came 华夏 "China," and
the graph was also used for "summer"; how the season sense arose is unclear.

## Mnemonic
A big figure standing tall in the open: the great bright season.

## Notes
Senses: summer (夏天, 夏季, 盛夏, 消夏), the Xia dynasty (夏朝), and 华夏 /
诸夏 for China and its people. Surname *Xià*.

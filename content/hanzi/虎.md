## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen calls 虎 "the lord of mountain beasts" and notes the
graph combines 虍 (the tiger's striped head, seen atop many tiger-related
characters) with legs drawn in a shape Shuowen compares to human feet —
essentially a full-body picture of a tiger, head to paws.

## Mnemonic
A striped head 虍 perched on sturdy legs 几 — the king of the mountain.

## Notes
虎 is Kangxi radical 141 and heads a small family of tiger-related and
"fierce, bold" characters. 老虎 (with the generic prefix 老) is the everyday
word; 虎 alone is common in idioms (马虎 "careless," 虎头蛇尾 "strong start,
weak finish").

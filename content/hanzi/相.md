## Etymology
目 "eye" beside 木 "tree" — Shuowen: 省視也 "to inspect closely," the classic
image being sizing up a tree for its timber. From "to examine one another"
grew the adverb "mutually, each other," and — read *xiàng* — "appearance,
face" (相貌) and "a chief minister" (丞相).

## Mnemonic
Eyeing 目 a tree 木 up and down: looking closely — and, of two people, looking
at each other.

## Notes
Two readings: *xiāng* "mutually / one another" (相信, 相同, 互相), *xiàng*
"appearance / photo / to appraise / minister" (照相, 相片, 长相, 首相).

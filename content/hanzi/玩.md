## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 王 here is 玉 "jade" (the radical loses its dot), giving the
meaning — turning a treasured object over in the hands; 元 *yuán* gives the
sound (for *wán*). Shuowen: 弄也 "to fiddle with, to handle." From "toy with"
grew "to play, to have fun, to trifle with."

## Mnemonic
Turning a piece of jade 王 over and over in your hands: playing with it.

## Notes
The 元 phonetic also writes 完 *wán*, 院 *yuàn*, 顽 / 頑 *wán*. 好玩儿 "fun,"
玩具 "toy," 开玩笑 "to joke."

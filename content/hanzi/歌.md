## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 欠 "a person with mouth open, exhaling" gives the meaning — the
element for words about voice and breath — and 哥 *gē* gives the sound. 哥
itself was originally the word "to sing."

## Mnemonic
An open mouth 欠 raised in song 哥.

## Notes
欠 "to gape / owe" also heads 歇 (rest), 欢 / 歡 (joy), 欲 (desire), 吹 (blow).
唱歌 "to sing a song" (verb + cognate object). 歌 alone: 歌曲, 歌手, 国歌.

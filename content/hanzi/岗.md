## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 山 (mountain) supplies the meaning and 冈 (the old 岡, "ridge") supplies the sound. 岡 was already "a hill ridge" on its own; 山 was added again in 崗 for a redundant clarification. The sense "post, station" is a metaphor (sentry on a mound).

## Mnemonic
Standing guard on a mountain 山 ridge 冈.

## Notes
冈 is both sound and meaning. 岗位 "post", 上岗 "start work", 下岗 "be laid off", 岗哨 "sentry post". The same 冈: 刚, 钢, 纲, 网 (cf. the Shuowen sound element 网).

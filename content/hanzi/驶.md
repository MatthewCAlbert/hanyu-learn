## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 駛: 马 (horse) supplies the meaning, 史 *shǐ*
supplies the sound and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The original sense
"a horse running fast" generalised smoothly to "to drive, to pilot" any
vehicle, and to "swift, fast" as a general descriptor.

## Mnemonic
A horse 马, driven on by an official's 史 urgent order — fast, and under
control.

## Notes
驶 anchors 驾驶 "to drive/pilot," 行驶 "to travel [of a vehicle]," and 驶入
"to enter/pull into [a lane, a harbour]" — always about a vehicle in
controlled motion, rarely used for a person on foot.

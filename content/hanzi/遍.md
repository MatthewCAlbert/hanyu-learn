## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning — going over the whole of
something; 扁 *biǎn* gives the sound (for *biàn*). Also written 徧 with 彳.

## Mnemonic
Covering every part of the ground 辶: all over.

## Notes
The 扁 phonetic also writes 编 / 編 *biān*, 骗 / 騙 *piàn*, 篇 *piān*, 偏
*piān*, 蝙 *biān*. Senses: everywhere (遍地, 遍布, 满山遍野), and a measure
word for a complete run-through (看一遍, 说三遍, 一遍又一遍).

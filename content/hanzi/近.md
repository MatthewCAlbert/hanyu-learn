## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 辶 "movement" gives the meaning; 斤 *jīn* gives the sound (for
*jìn*). Shuowen: 附也 "close by, adjoining."

## Mnemonic
Only a short way 辶 to go: near.

## Notes
The 斤 phonetic also writes 斧 *fǔ*, 欣 *xīn*, 芹 *qín*, 忻 *xīn*. Senses:
near in space (附近, 近处, 靠近), near in time (近来, 最近, 近代), close
(亲近, 近亲), approximately (近百人). 近视 "short-sighted."

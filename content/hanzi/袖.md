## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 衤 (clothing) supplies the meaning and 由 *yóu* supplies the sound (*yóu* → *xiù*). Shuowen lists 袖 as the vulgar form of the old word 裦 "sleeve".

## Mnemonic
Clothing 衤 that you pass your arms out through 由 — the sleeve.

## Notes
由 is sound only here. 袖子 "sleeve", 领袖 "leader" (collar and sleeve, the parts that show), 袖珍 "pocket-sized", 袖手旁观 "stand by".

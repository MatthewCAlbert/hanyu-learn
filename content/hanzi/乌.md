## Etymology
Simplified from 烏, a pictograph of a crow (Shuowen: "the filial bird, a picture"). It resembles 鳥 *niǎo*, except for the missing eye stroke; the common explanation is that the bird is so black the eye cannot be seen, which is later commentary rather than a recorded origin. Shuowen also notes the graph was borrowed for the interjection "alas" (呜呼), which shows 乌 / 呜 sharing a sound. The simplified shape's parts are not meaningful.

## Mnemonic
A crow with no visible eye — pitch black.

## Notes
乌云 "dark clouds", 乌鸦 "crow", 乌黑. In place names it transliterates *Wu-* (乌克兰 Ukraine, 乌干达 Uganda). Same sound family: 呜 *wū*, 邬 *wū*.

## Etymology
Associative: 卜 "the crack in a heated oracle bone" over 口 "mouth" — reading
the omen aloud from the crack. Shuowen: 視兆問也 "to inspect the sign and put
the question." The "occupy" sense (*zhàn*) is a later borrowing of the same
graph.

## Mnemonic
Speaking 口 the omen off the crack 卜: divination.

## Notes
占 is the phonetic in 站 *zhàn*, 战 / 戰 *zhàn*, 点 / 點 *diǎn*, 店 *diàn*,
沾 *zhān*, 粘 *nián*. *zhān*: divine (占卜, 占卦, 占星); *zhàn*: occupy /
take up (占领, 占用, 占地方, 独占), make up a share (占一半, 占多数).

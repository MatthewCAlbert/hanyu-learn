## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — a heavy weight that holds things down — and 真 *zhēn* supplies the sound (*zhēn* → *zhèn*). Shuowen glosses it as to press down. "Garrison, guard" and "market town" follow from a place held in check.

## Mnemonic
A heavy metal 钅 weight placed on the truth 真 to keep it still.

## Notes
真 is sound only. 镇定 "calm", 镇压 "suppress", 小镇 "town", 城镇 "towns", 乡镇 "villages and towns".

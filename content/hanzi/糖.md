## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 米 (grain) supplies the meaning; 唐 *táng* supplies the
sound. 糖 is not in Xu Shen's original Shuowen Jiezi at all — it was added
centuries later, in the Song-dynasty revision, defined simply as 饴,
"malt sugar." Early Chinese sugar was made by boiling down sprouted grain,
which is exactly why the grain radical 米 carries the meaning.

## Mnemonic
Grain 米 boiled down slowly, Tang-dynasty style 唐, until it turns sweet.

## Notes
糖 covers "sugar" as a substance (白糖, 砂糖) and "sweets, candy" as a
category (糖果, 喜糖) without distinguishing them — one character for both
the ingredient and the finished treat.

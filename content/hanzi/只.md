## Etymology
Two characters now share this shape.

**只 *zhǐ* "only"** — 口 with breath scattering below. Shuowen: 語已詞也 "a
sentence-final particle." It was borrowed for the restrictive adverb "only,
merely" (只有, 只要, 不只).

**只 *zhī*** is simplified from 隻 — 隹 "a bird" held in 又 "a hand": one of a
pair, and the measure word for birds, animals, and one of a paired set
(一只鸡, 两只手, 一只鞋).

## Mnemonic
A single word 只 — for "only," and for counting single things.

## Notes
Two readings: *zhǐ* "only" (只是, 只好), *zhī* the measure word (一只猫).

## Etymology
小 "small" plus one more stroke — a differentiated form. In the oracle bone,
"few" and "small" were written alike (a scatter of dots); the extra slash on
少 split them apart. Shuowen: 不多也 "not many." From "few" grew "young"
(*shào*) — few in years.

## Mnemonic
小 "small" with a stroke added: not many.

## Notes
Two readings: *shǎo* "few / less / to lack" (多少, 少数, 缺少), *shào* "young /
junior" (少年, 少爷, 老少).

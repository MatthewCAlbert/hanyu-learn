## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 喿 *zào* gives the sound (for
*zǎo*). Shuowen: 洒手也 "to wash the hands" — the sense later broadened to
bathing.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 all over: a wash.

## Notes
The 喿 phonetic also writes 燥 *zào* (dry), 躁 *zào* (rash), 操 *cāo*, 藻
*zǎo*. 澡 barely stands alone — it needs 洗 (洗澡) or 堂 (澡堂 "bathhouse").

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — a bend of water, a bay — and 彎 (simplified 弯) *wān* supplies both the sound and the idea of curving. A late graph, absent from Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 that curves 弯 around the land: a bay.

## Notes
弯 is both sound and meaning here. 海湾 "bay", 台湾 "Taiwan", 港湾, 湾区 "bay area". Also a measure word for a curve in a river.

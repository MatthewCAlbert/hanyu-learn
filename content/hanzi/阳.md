## Etymology
Simplified from 陽. The left 阝 is 阜 "a hill / mound," giving the meaning — the
sunlit, south-facing slope; the traditional phonetic 昜 *yáng* is written 日
"sun" in the simplified form, a fitting re-motivation. Shuowen: 高、明也
"high and bright." From "the sunny side" grew "the yang principle," "sun,"
"bright."

## Mnemonic
The bright 日 side of the hill 阝: the yang, the sun.

## Notes
Mirror of 阴 (阝 + 月, the moon / shaded side). Left-side 阝 is 阜 "mound"
(阴, 阳, 院, 除); right-side 阝 is 邑 "city" (都, 那, 部). 太阳 "the sun."

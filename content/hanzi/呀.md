## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning — this is a spoken particle —
and 牙 *yá* supplies the sound.

牙 appears purely for sound across an unrelated set of meanings: 牙 itself
(tooth), 芽 (sprout), 蚜 (aphid), 讶 (astonishment) — none of these share any
sense with "tooth" beyond the syllable *yá*. 呀 (particle, no tone) is another
member of that purely-phonetic series.

## Mnemonic
An open mouth 口 baring its teeth 牙 in surprise: "呀!"

## Notes
呀 functions like 啊 after a preceding vowel or diphthong sound (好呀!,
是呀?) — it is a sandhi variant, not a separate word with its own meaning.

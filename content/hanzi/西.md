## Etymology
Pictographic — a bird's nest, or a woven roosting-basket. Shuowen supplies the
link to the compass point: birds roost at sundown, and the sun sets in the
west, so the nest-graph was borrowed to write "west." (The compass sense is
thus a phonetic-and-association loan, not the picture.)

## Mnemonic
A nest where the birds settle at dusk — toward the setting sun: west.

## Notes
As a top component 西 flattens to 覀 / 襾 (票, 要, 覆). 西 also "Western" in
西方, 西医, 西餐. 东西 "east-west" → "a thing" (see the word entry).

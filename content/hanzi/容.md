## Etymology
Ideographic. 宀 (a roofed building) with 谷 (a valley) beneath it — Xu
Xuan's commentary spells out the logic directly: 屋與谷，皆所以盛受也,
"both a house and a valley exist to hold and receive things." A building
and a valley are both, at root, containers — the source of the modern
"to contain, to hold; capacity."

## Mnemonic
A house 宀 built over a valley 谷: room enough to hold anything.

## Notes
容 splits into "to contain, to allow" (容易 "easy" — literally "easily
contained/accommodated," 包容 "to tolerate") and "appearance, expression"
(容貌, 笑容 "a smiling face") — a container's capacity and a face's
expression share the idea of an outer form holding something within.

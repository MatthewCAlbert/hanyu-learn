## Etymology
Ideographic. 人 (a person) with 貝 (shell/money) beneath — Shuowen's
gloss, 恃也，有所恃也, is "to rely on, to have something to depend on":
a person with money in hand has something to fall back on. From "having
something to rely on" the sense extended to "to carry, to bear [a
burden]" — what you rely on, you also bear responsibility for.

The modern top component, ⺈, is a clerical-script regularisation of 人;
they no longer look alike, though the meaning is unchanged.

## Mnemonic
A person carrying money 贝 on their back: bearing a load, a
responsibility.

## Notes
负 splits into "to bear, to carry" (负担, 负责) and, separately, "to lose
[a contest]" (胜负) and "negative [in mathematics]" — all descend from
the idea of something weighing on you, whether a duty or a deficit.

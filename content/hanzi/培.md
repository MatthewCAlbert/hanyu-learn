## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 土 (earth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
describes banking up earth around the base of something, the literal act
of mounding soil around a plant's roots — and 咅 *pǒu* supplies the sound
(shared with 倍, covered elsewhere in this app).

"To bank up earth around a plant's roots" generalized directly to "to
cultivate, to nurture" (培养, 培育) — tending growth by literally, then
figuratively, building up the ground it needs.

## Mnemonic
Earth 土 banked up carefully around young roots, doubling 咅 (咅's own
family shares this root with 倍, double) their chance to thrive: "培,"
to cultivate.

## Notes
培养 ("to cultivate/train," of people or plants) and 培训 ("to train," of
skills specifically) both build on this character's literal "tending
growth by building up the ground" image.

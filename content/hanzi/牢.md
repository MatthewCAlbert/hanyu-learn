## Etymology
Pictographic-ideographic: 牛 (ox) inside an enclosure — the oracle form puts the animal in a pen. Shuowen treats the cover as an abbreviation of 冬 "winter", suggesting a closed-in shelter. "Pen for livestock" extends to "prison" and "firm, solid".

## Mnemonic
An ox 牛 safe under the roof 宀 of its pen: sturdy.

## Notes
牢固 "firm", 牢记 "remember firmly", 监牢 "jail", 牢骚 "complaints", 亡羊补牢 "close the pen after the sheep are gone".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic per Shuowen: 乑 (a group of standing figures, a crowd)
supplies the meaning "many people together," and 取 *qǔ* supplies the
sound — and, as with several other characters in this batch, 取 also
nudges the meaning, since 取 itself already means "to take, to gather
in." A crowd gathered together, and the sound-donor also whispers
"gathering."

## Mnemonic
A crowd of people 乑 all reached for and gathered 取 into one place: to
"聚," assemble.

## Notes
聚会 ("a get-together, a gathering," pairing 聚 with 会 "to meet") and
团聚 ("family reunion," lit. "group-gather") are the two most common
everyday compounds built on this character.

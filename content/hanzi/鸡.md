## Etymology
Simplified from 雞 / 鷄. 隹 / 鳥 "bird" gives the meaning — Shuowen calls the
chicken "the animal that knows the hour" — and 奚 *xī* gave the sound.
Simplification replaces 奚 with 又, a bare shape-sign, so the modern 鸡 keeps
only the bird 鸟 and no sound clue.

## Mnemonic
Just "bird" 鸟 — the one that crows the dawn.

## Notes
Same 奚 → 又 shortcut as 汉 (𦰩 → 又), 难 (堇 → 又), 观 (雚 → 又). 鸡蛋 "egg,"
鸡毛蒜皮 "trivial stuff."

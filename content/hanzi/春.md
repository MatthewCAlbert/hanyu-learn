## Etymology
日 "sun" gives the meaning; the top fuses 艸 "sprouting plants" with a worn 屯
*tún*, which carried the sound (for *chūn*). Shuowen: 推也 "the pushing-forth
season, when plants come up in the sun."

## Mnemonic
Sun 日 on new shoots: spring.

## Notes
Senses: spring, the season (春天, 春节, 立春, 早春), vitality and renewal
(春意盎然, 妙手回春, 大地回春), love and desire (春心, 怀春, 青春), and — in
old usage — a year (一卧东山三十春). 春联 "spring-festival couplets."

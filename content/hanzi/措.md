## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 置,
means "to place, to set" — and 昔 *xī* supplies the sound.

"To place, to arrange" generalizes to "to handle, to manage" (措施,
covered elsewhere in this app — a step taken to arrange/address a
problem) and "at a loss" (不知所措, "not knowing where to place oneself,"
i.e. bewildered).

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 placing things in order, drawing on lessons from long ago 昔:
"措," to arrange.

## Notes
措施 (a measure/step) and 不知所措 ("at a loss, not knowing what to do,"
a fixed four-character idiom) both build on this character's core
"to place/arrange" sense, one concretely and one as a negated inability.

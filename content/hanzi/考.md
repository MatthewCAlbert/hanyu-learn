## Etymology
考 and 老 were originally one word — 耂 "an aged person" with 丂 for sound,
glossed by Shuowen as 老也 "aged" (and specifically "a deceased father,"
still in 先考). The meanings "to examine, to test, to investigate" (考试,
考察, 思考) are borrowed extensions.

## Mnemonic
An elder 耂 putting the young through their paces: an examination.

## Notes
The 丂 phonetic also writes 巧 *qiǎo*, 号 *hào* (in the old form). Senses:
exam (考试, 高考, 考生), to test / check (考核, 考验), to look into (考古,
参考).

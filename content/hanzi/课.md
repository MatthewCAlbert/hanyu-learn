## Etymology
Simplified from 課. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 果 *guǒ*
gives the sound (for *kè*). Shuowen: 試也 "to examine, to assess." From
"assessment" grew "a set quota / tax" (课税), and "an assigned portion of
study" — a lesson.

## Mnemonic
A portion of learning 讠 to be examined for its fruit 果: a lesson.

## Notes
The 果 phonetic also writes 棵 *kē*, 颗 / 顆 *kē*, 裹 *guǒ*. 课 senses: a
lesson / class (上课, 下课), a subject (语文课), a section of a textbook
(第五课), a government section (课长, old).

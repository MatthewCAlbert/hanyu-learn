## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — the point of a blade — and 夆 *féng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as the tip of a weapon. "Vanguard, edge" (先锋) is an extension.

## Mnemonic
The metal 钅 tip that meets 夆 the enemy first.

## Notes
夆 is sound only. Series: 逢 *féng*, 蜂 *fēng*, 峰 *fēng*, 锋 *fēng*. 先锋 "vanguard", 交锋 "clash", 锋利 "sharp", 针锋相对 "tit for tat".

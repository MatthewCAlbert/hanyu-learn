## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) marks a sound word and 圭 *guī* supplies the sound (*guī* → *wā*). The graph is an imitation of a cry — crying, vomiting, a gasp of surprise. In Shuowen it is "a fawning noise"; the modern uses are interjection "wow!" and a baby's wail.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 open wide enough for a jade tablet 圭: wow!

## Notes
圭 is sound only. 哇哇大哭 "wail loudly", 哇 as an exclamation of delight, and a sentence-final particle *wa*. Series: 娃 *wá*, 洼 *wā*, 哇 *wā*.

## Etymology
Not securely attested in its concrete picture. The graph shows 大 (a
person, arms spread) inside 冂 (a boundary/frame), which different sources
read either as a person centred squarely within a space — giving "central,
middle" — or as a person caught/pinned within a frame, a reading proposed
to connect to the separate sense "to plead, to beg" (央求). Neither
reading is decisively settled, so confidence stays low.

## Mnemonic
A person 大 standing dead centre inside a frame 冂 — right in the middle.

## Notes
央 anchors 中央 "central, the centre [of government/authority]" and, in a
seemingly unrelated everyday sense, 央求 "to beg, to plead" — two senses on
one character whose connection, if any, is not clearly documented.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 页 (head) supplies the meaning and 客 supplies the sound; the traditional 額 uses 各 *gè*, and 客 is the form that simplification settled on, which keeps the same sound clue. Shuowen glosses the forehead.

## Mnemonic
The part of the head 页 where a guest 客 might tap you — your forehead.

## Notes
By extension, a fixed amount or "quota": 金额, 名额, 额度. 额外 "extra" is the same extension. Sound-only 客; 各 also appears in 格, 路, 洛.

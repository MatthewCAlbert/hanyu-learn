## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 胡 *hú* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 大陂也 "a large pooled body of water."

## Mnemonic
A broad still spread of water 氵: a lake.

## Notes
The 胡 phonetic also writes 蝴 *hú* (蝴蝶 butterfly), 糊 *hú*, 葫 *hú*
(葫芦 gourd), 湖 itself. Senses: lake (湖泊, 西湖, 人工湖), and short for
the Hunan–Hubei region (湖广, 两湖). 江湖 "the wider world / itinerant
life."

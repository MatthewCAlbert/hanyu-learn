## Etymology
虫 "small creature / reptile" gives the meaning — eggs are laid by such
creatures. The upper element 疋 is a worn-down remnant of an earlier phonetic
(the character has forms such as 蜑); in the modern shape it is neither
semantic nor a recoverable sound clue.

**Learn 蛋 as sound-opaque.** 虫 tells you it has to do with a laying creature;
nothing tells you it is *dàn*.

## Mnemonic
Something a small creature 虫 lays: an egg.

## Notes
One of eight HSK 1–2 characters whose phonetic was lost — compare 过 (咼),
边 (臱). 蛋 also an insult element: 笨蛋, 坏蛋, 混蛋.

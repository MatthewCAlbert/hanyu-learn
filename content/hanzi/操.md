## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition, 把
持, means "to grasp and hold" — and 喿 *zào* supplies the sound.

"To grasp and hold" (a tool) extends to "to operate, to control" (操作,
"to operate [a machine]"; 操纵, "to manipulate") and, separately, to
physical drill or exercise (操场, "a sports field"; 早操, "morning
exercises") — the disciplined, hands-on practice of a skill.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 gripping firmly, chattering birds 喿 (喿's own root sense, many
voices at once) all around as it works: "操," to operate — or to drill.

## Notes
操作 ("to operate," of a machine or system) and 体操 ("gymnastics," lit.
"body-drill") both build on this character's core "hands-on, practiced
control" sense.

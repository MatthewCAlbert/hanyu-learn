## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 懷: 忄 (heart) supplied the
meaning — to hold a feeling within — and 褱 *huái* supplied the sound.

**Simplification replaced 褱 with the unrelated 不** ("not"), which is
neither semantic nor phonetic here — a shape substitution that leaves no
reliable sound clue toward *huái* and, awkwardly, sits a negative word
"不" inside a character about holding feelings close, purely by visual
coincidence.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 that refuses 不 to let a feeling go: to "怀," harbor.

## Notes
怀疑 ("to doubt," covered elsewhere in this app) and 怀念 ("to cherish in
memory") both build on this character's core "to hold a feeling within
oneself" sense.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 糸 (thread) supplies the meaning — many threads tangled or
multiplied is a natural image for "numerous, complex" — with 敏 (itself a
compound of 每 and 攵) supplying the sound. The exact classical source for
this reading is less precisely documented than for many characters here,
though the "many threads" picture is a reasonable, uncontested reading of
the visible structure.

## Mnemonic
Threads 糸, so many and so quick 敏 to tangle, that untangling them takes
real effort.

## Notes
繁 spans "many, numerous" (繁多 "abundant/numerous") and "complicated"
(繁琐 "tedious and complex," 繁忙 "busy") — as well as naming traditional
Chinese characters themselves: 繁体字 "traditional [complex-form]
characters," as opposed to 简体字 "simplified characters."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart, mind) supplies the meaning — to awaken to, realise — and 吾 *wú* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as "to be aware".

## Mnemonic
My 吾 mind 忄 finally gets it.

## Notes
吾 is sound only (吾 is itself an old first-person pronoun). 领悟, 醒悟, 觉悟, 恍然大悟. Series: 悟, 梧, 吾, 语 (from 吾 sound family).

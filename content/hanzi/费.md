## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 贝 (money/valuables) supplies the meaning; 弗 *fú* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 散財用也, "to scatter wealth in use," is
already close to the modern "to spend, to use up."

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 that refuses 弗 to stay in your pocket: an expense.

## Notes
费 shifted smoothly from verb ("to spend") to noun ("a fee, an expense")
— 学费, 车费, 免费 — the two senses are still transparently the same
word, just different parts of speech.

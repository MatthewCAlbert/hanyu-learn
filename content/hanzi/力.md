## Etymology
Shuowen reads the graph as a picture of human sinew (筋). Guo Moruo argued
instead that it depicts a 耒, the head of an ancient plough — the tool through
which a body exerts force. Either way the meaning is "strength, force."

## Mnemonic
The taut curved line of a straining muscle — or a plough biting the earth:
force.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 19), heading 加 劝 助 势 男 努. Senses: physical
strength (力气, 体力, 用力), power / force in general (电力, 权力, 引力),
effort (努力, 尽力, 力争). 力量 "strength, power."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — a person of fine qualities — and 圭 *guī* supplies the sound (*guī* → *jiā*). Shuowen glosses it as good, fine.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 as fine as a jade tablet 圭.

## Notes
圭 is sound only. Series: 桂 *guì*, 鞋 *xié*, 娃 *wá*, 洼, 封. 最佳 "best", 佳节 "festival day", 佳作 "fine work", 佳肴.

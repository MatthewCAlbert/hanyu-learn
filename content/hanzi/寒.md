## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen, and vividly literal: a person 人 sheltering
under a roof 宀, covered over with straw or grass 茻 for warmth, with ice
仌 lying beneath. The whole character is a small scene of someone
huddled against the cold.

## Mnemonic
A person curled up under a roof, packed in straw for warmth, ice still
underfoot: "寒," bitter cold.

## Notes
寒假 ("winter vacation," lit. "cold-holiday") and 寒冷 ("cold, frigid,"
pairing 寒 with the near-synonym 冷) both build directly on this
character's vivid, literal winter-cold image.

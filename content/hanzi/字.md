## Etymology
宀 "roof" over 子 "child" — Shuowen: 乳也 "to give birth, to suckle; a child
under a roof, 子 also giving the sound." From "to breed, to multiply" came the
name for a written character: characters, in the Chinese view, are 文 (simple
graphs) that have "bred" into compounds — 字 "the offspring."

## Mnemonic
A child 子 born under the roof 宀 — and, by extension, a character born of
other characters.

## Notes
文字 pairs "simple graph" (文) with "compound / bred graph" (字). Senses:
character (汉字, 写字), a courtesy name (名字), handwriting (字写得好).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in origin: 巾 (cloth) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 父 *fù*. Shuowen's original sense is specific — cloth woven
from 枲, hemp fibre, the standard fabric before cotton reached China. The
app's decomposition data cannot resolve the top of the modern glyph as a
separate component, consistent with 父's role here no longer being legible
as a distinct sound clue in the current shape.

## Mnemonic
Cloth 巾 stretched wide, ready to be announced to everyone: to spread
word, to declare.

## Notes
"Woven cloth" extended, by the physical image of cloth being unrolled and
spread out, to the completely separate verb "to announce, to make known"
(公布, 宣布, 发布) — spreading fabric and spreading news share one root
picture.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 足 "foot" gives the meaning; 包 *bāo* gives the sound — the fit
runs through 炮 / 泡 *pào*. A late character; its earliest sense is an animal
pawing or scraping the ground with its hoof, from which "to run" developed.

## Mnemonic
Feet 足 pounding the ground: running.

## Notes
The 包 phonetic also writes 抱 *bào*, 饱 / 飽 *bǎo*, 泡 *pào*, 炮 *pào*, 袍
*páo*. 跑步 "to jog," 跑步机 "treadmill." Modern Mandarin uses 跑 for "run,"
having shifted 走 to "walk."

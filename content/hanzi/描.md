## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning "to trace with the hand,"
and 苗 *miáo* supplies the sound, an exact match.

"To trace an outline" (描边, "to trace an edge") generalizes to "to
depict, to describe" (描写, 描述) — tracing an image with a pen and
tracing a scene with words are the same underlying action of careful,
line-by-line rendering.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 carefully tracing the outline of a young seedling 苗: "描," to
trace or depict.

## Notes
描写 ("to describe, to depict," especially in writing) and 描述 (a close
synonym) both extend this character's "tracing an outline" image from the
literal to the verbal.

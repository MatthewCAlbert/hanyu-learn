## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 刂 "knife" gives the meaning; 亥 *hài* gives the sound (for
*kè*). Shuowen: 鏤也 "to engrave, to incise."

## Mnemonic
Cutting a mark with the knife 刂: to carve.

## Notes
The 亥 phonetic also writes 孩 *hái*, 该 / 該 *gāi*, 核 *hé*, 骸 *hái*.
Senses: carve / engrave (雕刻, 刻章, 刻骨铭心), a quarter-hour and hence a
brief moment (一刻钟, 此刻, 立刻, 时刻), harsh / biting (刻薄, 苛刻, 尖刻),
and rigidly (刻板, 刻舟求剑). 刻苦 "hardworking, assiduous."

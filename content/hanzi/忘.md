## Etymology
心 "heart / mind" gives the meaning; 亡 *wáng* "to be lost, to perish" gives
the sound — and fits the sense (something gone from the mind). Shuowen: 不識也
"to no longer recognise."

## Mnemonic
Something lost 亡 from the mind 心: forgotten.

## Notes
Compare 忙 *máng* (心 + 亡), "busy" — same two parts, different arrangement and
sense. 忘记 pairs 忘 with 记; 别忘了 "don't forget."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning and 尼 *ní* supplies the sound. In Shuowen 泥 is the name of a river; "mud" is a borrowed sense — a muddy ground by a stream.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 with a nun 尼 stuck in it: mud.

## Notes
尼 is sound only here (not "nun"). 水泥 "cement", 泥土 "soil", 泥潭 "quagmire", 泥沙. Two readings: *ní* and *nì* in 拘泥 "stick to rigidly".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 禾 (grain) supplies the meaning — taxes were paid in grain — and 兑 *duì* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as rent, tribute, land tax.

## Mnemonic
Grain 禾 taken at the exchange 兑 counter: taxes.

## Notes
兑 is sound only (not "exchange"). Series: 说 *shuō/shuì*, 脱 *tuō*, 锐 *ruì*, 悦 *yuè*, 阅 *yuè*, 税 *shuì*. 税收, 纳税, 关税, 避税.

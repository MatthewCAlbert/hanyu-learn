## Etymology
Pictophonetic — and a striking case of complete semantic drift. Shuowen's
original 驗 is, quite specifically, the name of a particular horse: 馬名,
built from 馬 (horse, meaning) and 僉 (sound). By the time of the
Guangyun rhyme dictionary, the character carried an entirely different,
unrelated sense: "to verify, to test, to have effect" — the meaning that
survives today, with no discernible bridge back to the original horse.

## Mnemonic
Everyone 佥 gathers to see the horse 马 tested and proven: an
examination.

## Notes
验 keeps 马 (horse) as its semantic component purely as etymological
fossil — 经验 "experience," 实验 "experiment," 检验 "to inspect" have
nothing to do with horses at all, a clear case of a word's sound
outliving its original sense entirely.

## Etymology
Pictographic — a hand-weapon: a long polearm with a saw-toothed or three-pronged
blade (the ancestor of a word for a kind of halberd). It was borrowed very
early as the first-person pronoun, a pure phonetic loan; the pronoun has no
pictorial basis. Shuowen already glosses it only as the pronoun.

## Mnemonic
A bladed weapon held out — "this is mine, this is me."

## Notes
The left side is a worn-down blade shape, not 手 "hand." 戈 "dagger-axe" on the
right also heads 战 / 戰, 或, 成. 我 is neutral in register; 咱 is inclusive
"we."

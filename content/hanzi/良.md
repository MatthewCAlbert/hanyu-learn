## Etymology
Shuowen glosses 良 straightforwardly as "good," with a reduced form of 畗
supplying the sound — but oracle-bone and bronze forms are more often
read by modern paleographers as an architectural shape, possibly a
covered corridor or granary structure, whose link to "goodness" is not
well settled. Given the gap between Shuowen's phonetic account and the
competing pictographic readings, this entry keeps confidence low rather
than asserting either as certain.

## Mnemonic
A well-built structure, sound and solid from foundation to roof: "良,"
good, fine.

## Notes
良好 ("good, favorable") and 善良 ("kind-hearted," pairing 良 with 善,
itself glossed by Shuowen as "good") both keep this character's core
positive-quality sense.

## Etymology
Pictographic — a slab of meat, drawn as an outline with two strokes inside for
the muscle fibres or ribs. Shuowen: 胾肉 "a cut of flesh."

## Mnemonic
A cut of meat, grain running through it.

## Notes
肉 is Kangxi radical 130; as a side component it takes the 月-shape ⺼ (肚, 脸,
脑, 胖, 服, 有), which is why so many "月" characters are really about the body.
Also "the flesh of a fruit" (果肉) and, colloquially, "spongy / mushy" or
"slow" (这人真肉).

## Etymology
Simplified from 績. 纟 (糸) "thread" gives the meaning; 责 (責) *zé* gives the
sound (for *jī*, via the 积 *jī* series). Shuowen: 緝也 "to twist fibres into
thread, to spin." From the patient work of spinning grew "an achievement, an
accomplishment, results."

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟 spun day after day: work that adds up to something.

## Notes
The 责 element also writes 债 / 債 *zhài*, 积 / 積 *jī*. 绩 rarely stands
alone: 成绩 "grades / results," 功绩 "merits," 业绩 "performance record."

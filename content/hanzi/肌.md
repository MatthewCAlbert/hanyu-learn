## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (flesh) supplies the meaning, 几 *jī* supplies the
sound and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 肉也 "flesh" is as direct as it
gets — the character simply names bodily muscle tissue.

## Mnemonic
Flesh ⺼, built up little by little 几 into muscle.

## Notes
肌 anchors 肌肉 "muscle," 皮肌 "skin and muscle" (in medical registers),
and 肌肤 "skin" (pairing with 肤) — almost entirely anatomical vocabulary
with no notable figurative extensions.

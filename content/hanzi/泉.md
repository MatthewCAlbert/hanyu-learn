## Etymology
A pictograph, per Shuowen: the character represents water flowing out
[of an opening] to form a stream — the top shape is a stylized spring
opening, not the color word "white" it happens to resemble in the modern
form, and 水 below is the flowing water itself.

## Mnemonic
Water 水 flowing straight up out of a hidden opening in the ground: a
"泉," spring.

## Notes
Do not read the top of 泉 as "白" (white) — it is a pictographic remnant
of the spring's opening, one more case (like 未, 雷, 兵) where a
component's modern shape coincidentally resembles an unrelated common
character.

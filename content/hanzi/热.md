## Etymology
Simplified from 熱. 灬 (火) "fire" gives the meaning; 埶 *yì* gave the sound.
Shuowen: 溫也 "warm." Simplification reduces 埶 to 执, which no longer carries
the sound clearly.

## Mnemonic
Fire 灬 under everything: hot.

## Notes
灬 at the foot of a character is usually 火 "fire" (煮, 蒸, 烈), though in 点,
鱼 it is not. Senses of 热: hot (天气热), to heat (热一下), fervent
(热情, 热爱), popular (热门, 热点).

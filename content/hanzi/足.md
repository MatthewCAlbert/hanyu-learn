## Etymology
Pictographic. The oracle-bone form shows the whole lower leg, foot
included — Shuowen's own analysis of the seal-script form reads it as 口
(here representing the round shape of the knee) above 止 ("foot," itself
already a foot-shaped pictograph) — the entire limb from knee to toes in
one composite picture.

## Mnemonic
A knee 口 set atop a foot 止: the whole leg, planted firmly.

## Notes
足 narrowed from "leg" to specifically "foot" in ordinary use, while
picking up a completely separate, unrelated sense via phonetic loan:
"sufficient, enough" (满足, 足够, 充足) — there is no bridge between a
foot and sufficiency beyond the shared sound.

## Etymology
The traditional 戲 was pictophonetic: 戈 (a dagger-axe, marking military
and combative senses) supplied the meaning; 䖒 supplied the sound.
Shuowen's original sense is military — a flanking division of an army —
with "play, drama, joke" a later, separate extension whose path from
"troop division" is not well documented.

Simplification replaced the complex phonetic 䖒 with the unrelated 又.
Confidence is low both because no phonetic is recoverable in the modern
shape and because the semantic path from "army division" to "theatrical
play" is itself uncertain.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 wielding a prop weapon 戈 on stage: not real combat, just a
show.

## Notes
戏 in the "drama, play" sense (戏剧, 演戏) is now far more common than any
military meaning, which has faded from ordinary use entirely.

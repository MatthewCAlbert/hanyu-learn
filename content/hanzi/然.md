## Etymology
灬 (火) "fire" gives the meaning; 肰 (犬 "dog" + 肉 "meat") *rán* gives the
sound. Shuowen: 燒也 "to burn" — 然 was the original word for "to burn." It was
borrowed wholesale for the grammatical senses "so, thus, correct" and the
adverb suffix "-ly," and 燃 (火 + 然) was coined to carry "burn" again.

## Mnemonic
Meat over the fire 灬 — the original "burn," lent out to grammar.

## Notes
As a suffix it forms adverbs and states: 忽然, 突然, 当然, 虽然, 自然, 果然.
Alone: 然后 "then," 不然 "otherwise."

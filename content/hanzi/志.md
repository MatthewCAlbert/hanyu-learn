## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning; the traditional
phonetic was 之 *zhī* "to go toward." Shuowen's own added gloss plays on
this directly: 心之所之也, "where the heart is heading" — the will, the
direction one's mind is set on.

The modern top component is written 士, a clerical-script regularisation
of 之 that no longer reads as a sound clue. Confidence is medium because
the phonetic role, while Shuowen-attested for the traditional shape,
cannot be checked against the modern glyph.

## Mnemonic
A scholar's 士 heart 心, set firmly on one direction: determination.

## Notes
志 covers "will, determination" (意志, 志愿 "to volunteer" — literally
"a wish of the will") and, separately, "to record" (杂志 "magazine,"
标志 "a marker") — a mind fixed on a goal, and a fact fixed in writing.

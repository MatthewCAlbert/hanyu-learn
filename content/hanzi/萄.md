## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning, 匋 *táo* supplies the
sound. Not in Shuowen. 萄 exists almost exclusively to form 葡萄 "grape" —
a disyllabic word that most scholars trace to an ancient loanword brought
along the Silk Road from Central Asian languages, so neither 葡 nor 萄
should be read as carrying independent native meaning; confidence here
covers the character's structure, not a securely traced origin story for
the loanword itself.

## Mnemonic
A vine plant 艹, its name borrowed and sound-fitted 匋 from a foreign
tongue.

## Notes
萄 essentially never stands alone. 葡萄 "grape," 葡萄酒 "wine," 葡萄干
"raisin" are the only common compounds it appears in.

## Etymology
The traditional 術 was pictophonetic: 行 (a crossroads, marking roads and
movement) supplied the meaning; a component itself written 术 supplied
the sound. Shuowen's original sense, 邑中道也, is concrete: "a road within
a settlement" — a specific route or way of getting somewhere, which
extended naturally into "a method, a way of doing something."

Simplification dropped 行 entirely, keeping only the phonetic core —
this is the same "meaning-side cut, sound-side kept" pattern as 总, 亲,
产, 显 elsewhere in this dataset. Confidence is medium because that
phonetic core's own internal picture is not independently recoverable.

## Mnemonic
A single fixed path, walked so often it becomes second nature: a
technique, a method.

## Notes
术 now stands almost entirely for "skill, method, technique" — 技术,
艺术 "art," 手术 "surgery," 学术 "scholarship" — with the original city
road fully abstracted away.

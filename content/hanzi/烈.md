## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 灬 (fire, in its four-dot compressed form) supplies the
meaning; the traditional phonetic 𠛱 is now written 列 *liè* in the
modern standard. Shuowen's gloss, 火猛也, "fierce fire," generalised
smoothly into "intense, violent" for anything, not just flame.

## Mnemonic
Fire 灬, burning in a fierce, unbroken row 列: intensity.

## Notes
烈 covers physical intensity (强烈, 剧烈, 猛烈) and honorific intensity
alike (烈士 "a martyr," someone whose sacrifice burned as fiercely as
the character itself suggests).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 申 *shēn* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. 申 itself was originally a pictograph — likely of
lightning, the same root image behind 电 — later borrowed for the ninth
Earthly Branch and for the abstract sense "to extend, to state." 伸 adds 亻
to make an unambiguous character specifically for "[a person/limb] to
stretch out," once 申 had drifted into grammatical and calendrical use.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 reaching out and extending 申 as far as they can.

## Notes
伸 anchors 伸手 "to hold out one's hand," 伸展 "to stretch/extend," 伸懒腰
"to stretch [after waking]." 申 alone survives mainly in 申请 "to apply" and
as the zodiac's Monkey-hour Branch — a separate life from 伸's physical
sense.

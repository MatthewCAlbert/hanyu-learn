## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 礻 (altar/ritual) supplies the meaning; 畐 *fú* supplies
the sound — and, per the oracle-bone record, more than sound: the earliest
forms show two hands raising a wine vessel (酉) before an ancestral altar
(示), an offering made in hope of blessing. Shuowen's gloss, 祐也, "divine
aid, blessing," is the abstracted result of that concrete ritual scene.

## Mnemonic
An offering raised at the altar 礻, full to the brim 畐: a blessing asked
for, and received.

## Notes
福 is one of the most visually loaded characters in Chinese culture — the
inverted 福 posted on doors at New Year plays on 倒 "upside-down" sounding
like 到 "to arrive": "blessing has arrived."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 攵 (a hand with a rod, associated with prompt action)
supplies the meaning, 每 *měi* supplies the sound and reads unchanged.
Shuowen's gloss 疾也 "quick, swift" matches the modern "quick, sharp,
sensitive" directly.

## Mnemonic
A hand quick to act 攴, every 每 single time.

## Notes
敏 anchors 敏感 "sensitive," 敏捷 "agile, quick," 聪明伶俐/机敏 "sharp-witted"
— always about speed of response, whether physical, mental, or emotional.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 禺 *yú* supplies the
sound, shifted to *ǒu*. Shuowen's gloss 桐人也 is "a figure carved from
paulownia wood" — a puppet or idol. "A pair, a match" (配偶 "spouse") likely
grew from puppets or idols being made and paired in twos; "by chance"
(偶然) is a separate, later extension whose exact bridge to the puppet
sense is not clearly documented.

## Mnemonic
A wooden figure 亻, carved as a match 禺 for another — a pair, however it
came about.

## Notes
偶 spans three senses that no longer feel connected: the "idol/figure"
(木偶 "puppet"), "pair/spouse" (配偶), and "by chance" (偶然, 偶尔) — all one
character carrying three separate lives.

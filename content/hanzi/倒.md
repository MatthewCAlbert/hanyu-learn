## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning — a person going down; 到 *dào*
gives the sound. The two readings split by sense: *dǎo* "to topple," *dào*
"to invert, to reverse, to pour out."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 who has gone right over: fallen.

## Notes
*dǎo*: fall / collapse (摔倒, 倒下, 倒闭), change hands (倒班, 倒手). *dào*:
upside down (倒立, 倒挂), pour (倒水, 倒垃圾), back up (倒车, 倒退), and the
adversative "yet, actually" (倒是, 倒也好). 到 phonetic also in 到 itself.

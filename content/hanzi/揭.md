## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to lift up — and 曷 *hé* supplies the sound (*hé* → *jiē*). Shuowen glosses it as raising high; "to lift the cover off, to reveal" is the usual modern sense.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 lifting a lid to see what 曷 is inside.

## Notes
曷 is sound only. Series: 喝, 渴, 褐, 竭, 谒, 揭. 揭露 "expose", 揭晓 "announce", 揭发 "denounce", 揭示 "reveal".

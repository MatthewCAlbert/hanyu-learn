## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 碩: 页 (traditional 頁, "head" — the same
component in 顶, 项, 颗) supplies the meaning, 石 *shí* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Shuowen's gloss 頭大也 "big-headed" is the literal
origin; "great, large, eminent" (硕大 "huge," 硕士 "master's degree")
generalised well beyond heads specifically.

## Mnemonic
A head 页 as solid and substantial as stone 石 — great in scale.

## Notes
硕士 "master's degree [holder]" is the everyday modern use, sitting between
本科 (bachelor's) and 博士 (doctorate) in the academic-title vocabulary.

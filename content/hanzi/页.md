## Etymology
Simplified from 頁 — a pictograph of a person (儿) with the head (𦣻) drawn
large and detailed. Shuowen: 頭也 "the head." The everyday sense "a page /
leaf / sheet" is a borrowing (a book's "leaves").

## Mnemonic
A figure with an outsized head: originally "the head" — now a leaf of paper.

## Notes
页 is Kangxi radical 181 and the semantic in a family of head- and face-words:
顶 (crown), 项 (nape), 须 / 鬚 (beard), 顺, 颜 / 顏, 题 / 題, 顾 / 顧. Measure
word: 第五页, 一页纸.

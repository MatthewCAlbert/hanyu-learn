## Etymology
Not securely attested. Shuowen's entry is itself flagged as doubtful — its
reading "from bamboo and dog" is plainly corrupt, and Duan Yucai discusses the
problem at length. The common modern account takes 夭 as a person with the body
thrown back or bent in laughter, and ⺮ as either a sound element or the image
of bamboo shaking; neither is confirmed by early graphs.

## Mnemonic
A body bent double 夭, shaking like bamboo ⺮: laughing.

## Notes
Senses: laugh, smile (微笑, 笑话), and "to laugh at, to mock" (笑人, 见笑).
好笑 "funny."

## Etymology
⺮ "bamboo" gives the meaning; 弟 *dì* gives the sound. The word "sequence,
order" was originally written 弟 itself (Shuowen glosses 弟 as "the order in
which a cord is wound"); ⺮ was added — order shown on bamboo slips — to make
the dedicated graph 第.

## Mnemonic
Bamboo slips ⺮ laid out one after another 弟: in sequence.

## Notes
第 turns a cardinal number into an ordinal: 第一, 第二, 第三. Also "a grand
residence" (府第) and, with 及, 及第 "to pass the imperial exam."

## Etymology
Traditional 爐 is pictophonetic: 火 (fire) for meaning and 盧 *lú* for sound — a stove, hearth, brazier. Simplification replaced 盧 with 户, which looks like "door" but does not sound like *lú*, so the sound clue is lost and 户 is only a shape substitute.

## Mnemonic
A fire 火 inside the door 户: a stove at home.

## Notes
户 *hù* gives no help with the reading. 炉子 "stove", 微波炉 "microwave", 火炉, 炉灶 "kitchen stove". 卢 and 芦 keep the *lú* sound.

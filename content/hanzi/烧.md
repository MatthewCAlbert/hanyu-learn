## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 火 (fire) supplies the meaning, and 尧 *yáo* (simplified from
堯) supplies the sound.

"To burn" extends naturally to "to cook by heat" (红烧, red-braising) and, in
the body, to "running a fever" (发烧) — heat doing its work whether in a
stove or in a sick body.

## Mnemonic
Fire 火 rising as high as 尧 (a legendary sage-king, 尧, whose name here
carries only sound): flames climbing — "烧," to burn.

## Notes
发烧 (to run a fever) uses 烧 metaphorically for body heat — a fever "burns,"
not that anything is literally on fire.

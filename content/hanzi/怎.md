## Etymology
A vernacular character, not attested before the Song. 心 "heart / mind" is the
semantic — it is a word about weighing a situation — and 乍 *zhà* is pressed
in as a rough phonetic for *zěn*. The sound fit is loose, which is typical of
Song–Yuan colloquial coinages.

## Mnemonic
The mind 心 caught short 乍, asking "how?"

## Notes
Family: 怎么, 怎样, 怎么样, 怎么办. Compare 甚 / 什 and 争 (an older root of the
same interrogative). See the word entry 怎么.

## Etymology
劇 is not in Xu Shen's original Shuowen; it was added later, defined as
尤甚也, "especially severe, extreme." The traditional phonetic was 豦
*jù* (itself built from 虍 "tiger" and 豕 "boar," picturing a fierce
struggle between the two) — 刂 (knife, a marker of sharp/intense action)
supplied the meaning.

Simplification swapped the complex 豦 for the much simpler, similarly
pronounced 居 *jū*. Confidence is medium because "theatrical drama," the
character's dominant modern sense, is a separate borrowing onto "extreme,
severe" whose path is not well documented.

## Mnemonic
A knife-edge 刂 of tension, playing out under one roof 居: a drama.

## Notes
剧 splits cleanly into "intense, severe" (剧烈, 剧痛) — closer to the
original sense — and "a play, a drama" (话剧, 电视剧, 京剧), now the more
frequent everyday use.

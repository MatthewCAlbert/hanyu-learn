## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 鋼: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning, 冈
(traditional 岡, "ridge") supplies the sound, shifted to *gāng*. Not in
Shuowen; refined steel as a distinct material postdates the classical
corpus, though iron-working itself is ancient.

## Mnemonic
Metal 钅 forged as hard and unyielding as a mountain ridge 冈.

## Notes
钢 anchors both the material (钢铁 "steel," 不锈钢 "stainless steel") and the
figurative "unyielding, firm" (钢铁般的意志 "an iron will") — the hardest,
least flexible member of the metal family, in contrast to 铜 (copper,
softer) and 铁 (iron, its raw precursor).

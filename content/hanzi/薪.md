## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning — firewood, brushwood — and 新 *xīn* supplies the sound. 新 itself began as the verb "to chop wood" (an axe 斤 at a tree) before it came to mean "new", so 薪 was made by adding 艹 to restore the "firewood" sense.

## Mnemonic
Fresh 新 cut wood 艹 stacked for the fire.

## Notes
新 "new" is the borrowed meaning. 薪水 "salary" (originally "firewood and water"), 年薪 "annual pay", 薪资, 加薪 "pay rise", 卧薪尝胆 "endure hardship to gain revenge".

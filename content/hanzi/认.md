## Etymology
Simplified from 認. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning — acknowledging in words;
the traditional phonetic is 忍 *rěn*. Simplification replaces 忍 with 人
*rén*, a rough phonetic for *rèn*. Senses: to recognise (认识, 认出), to
acknowledge / admit (承认, 认错), to take (something) as (认为, 认作).

## Mnemonic
Putting a name to a face in words 讠: to recognise.

## Notes
认 "recognise / acknowledge" vs 认识 "be acquainted with" vs 知道 "know a
fact" — a distinction learners have to build early.

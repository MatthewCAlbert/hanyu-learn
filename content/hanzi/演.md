## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 (water) supplies the meaning; 寅 *yín* supplies the
sound. The original sense, 長流也 "a long, continuous flow," is the root
of the modern "to develop, to unfold, to perform" — something continuing
and playing out over time, the way a river runs on.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 flowing on and on, as the hour 寅 passes: to unfold, to play out.

## Notes
"A long, continuous flow" branches into "to develop, evolve" (演变) and,
separately, into "to perform, to act out" (表演, 演员) — a performance
"unfolds" the same way a river does.

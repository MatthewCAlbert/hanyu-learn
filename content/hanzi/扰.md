## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 擾: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 尤 *yóu*
supplies the sound, shifted to *rǎo*. Not in Shuowen. The core sense —
poking, prodding, disturbing by hand — has stayed concrete and stable.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 poking at something especially 尤 annoying.

## Notes
扰 is almost always bound: 打扰 "to disturb/bother," 干扰 "to interfere
with," 骚扰 "to harass." 不要打扰我 "don't disturb me" is one of the most
common everyday uses.

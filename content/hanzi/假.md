## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 叚 *jiǎ* "to borrow" gives the
sound and a hint of sense. Shuowen: 非眞也 "not genuine." From "borrowed, not
one's own" grew "false, fake" and "if / suppose" (假如, 假设); a separate
reading *jià* means "leave, a holiday" (借用 time off).

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 running on borrowed 叚 credentials: fake.

## Notes
Two readings: *jiǎ* "false / if" (假的, 假装, 假如), *jià* "leave / vacation"
(放假, 请假, 寒假). The 叚 phonetic also writes 暇 *xiá*, 遐 *xiá*, 瑕 *xiá*.

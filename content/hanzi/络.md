## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
names loose floss silk, or unspun hemp fiber not yet twisted into thread
— a tangled mass of raw fiber — and 各 *gè* supplies the sound.

That image of many interwoven, unspun strands generalized to "a network
of interconnected threads or vessels" (经络, the body's channels in
Chinese medicine; 网络, covered elsewhere in this app).

## Mnemonic
Raw threads 纟, each 各 going its own way until woven into one connected
mesh: "络," a network.

## Notes
网络 (network, covered elsewhere in this app) pairs 络 with 网 (net) as a
near-synonym compound — see that entry for how the two halves reinforce
the same "interconnected mesh" idea.

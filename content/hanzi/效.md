## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 攵 (a hand with a tool, marking a deliberate action)
supplies the meaning; 交 *jiāo* supplies the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 象也
"to resemble, to imitate," is the root of the modern "result, effect" —
an outcome that matches, or resembles, what was intended.

## Mnemonic
A hand 攵 crossing 交 its work against the original, checking the result
matches: an effect achieved.

## Notes
效 splits into "to imitate, to emulate" (效仿) and, far more commonly
today, "result, effectiveness" (效果, 有效, 效率) — both trace back to
"a match between intention and outcome."

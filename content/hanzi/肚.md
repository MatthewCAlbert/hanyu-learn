## Etymology
Pictophonetic: ⺼ (肉, flesh/body) supplies the meaning, and 土 *tǔ*
supplies an approximate sound toward *dù*. This entry cannot cite a
Shuowen entry matching the modern sense, so it keeps confidence at
medium.

## Mnemonic
The body 肉, rounded out like a mound of earth 土: "肚," the belly.

## Notes
肚子 (belly, stomach, the everyday noun) is casual/colloquial — for the
formal anatomical or medical term, 胃 ("stomach," the organ specifically)
is the more precise word.

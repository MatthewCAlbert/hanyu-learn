## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 片 (a thin slice of wood — the same component that also
gives 片 "slice, film") supplies the meaning; 卑 *bēi* supplies the sound.
The original sense is a wooden signboard or nameplate — a flat piece of
wood used to post a name or notice.

## Mnemonic
A humble 卑 wooden slat 片, marked and hung up for all to see: a sign.

## Notes
"A marked wooden board" branches into "an official plaque/medal" (金牌,
车牌) and, separately, "a game tile you can display in your hand" (打牌,
麻将牌) — a signboard and a playing card share the idea of a flat marked
surface meant to be read.

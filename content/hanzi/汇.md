## Etymology
Simplification merged two originally distinct traditional characters
into this one shape. 匯 meant "to converge" — 氵 (water) flowing together
into 匚 (a container) — the sense behind 汇 in "to remit money" (汇款,
funds converging into an account) and "an exchange rate" (汇率). 彙 meant
"a bristling collection" (its own semantic component 希 pictured hedgehog
bristles), the sense behind 汇 in "a compilation" (词汇, "vocabulary," lit.
"a collection of words"). Both simplified to 汇, so this entry declares
only the water-based semantic 氵 and keeps confidence medium given the
merge.

## Mnemonic
Streams of water 氵 converging into one container 匚: to "汇," come
together — whether currents, funds, or words gathered into one list.

## Notes
汇率 (exchange rate) and 词汇 (vocabulary) look like the same character
doing two unrelated jobs because they are — one descends from 匯
"converge," the other from the separately-coined 彙 "collection."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 合 *hé* supplies the sound,
shifted to *shí* — and 合's own sense "to bring together, to combine" also
echoes the action of gathering things up by hand. Shuowen's gloss 掇也 "to
pick up" matches the modern meaning directly.

## Mnemonic
Hands 扌 bringing scattered things back together 合, one by one.

## Notes
拾 is the everyday verb for picking things up (拾起 "to pick up," 拾金不昧
"to return found money rather than keep it") and, in financial and legal
contexts, doubles as the anti-fraud numeral "ten" (used on cheques and
formal documents precisely because it cannot be altered into another
number as easily as 十).

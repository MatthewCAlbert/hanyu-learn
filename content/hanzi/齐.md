## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen reads it directly as a picture: three stalks of
grain, their heads all reaching the same height — 禾麥吐穗上平也, "wheat
and grain sending up level heads." Level, even growth is the source of
the modern "neat, uniform, all together."

## Mnemonic
Three stalks of grain, grown to exactly the same height: everything even,
everything in order.

## Notes
"Grain heads level with each other" generalised smoothly into "orderly,
uniform" (整齐) and "all together, simultaneously" (一齐, 齐心协力) — a
field ripening in unison as the image behind group coordination.

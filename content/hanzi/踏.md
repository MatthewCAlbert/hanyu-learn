## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 足 (foot) supplies the meaning — to tread — and 沓 *tà* supplies the sound. A later graph, in the additions to Shuowen, that glosses 踏 as stepping on.

## Mnemonic
A foot 足 treading on layered 沓 ground.

## Notes
沓 is sound only. 踏实 "down-to-earth", 踏步 "mark time", 踏上 "set foot on", 脚踏车, 踩踏 "stampede". Readings: *tà* (standard), *tā* in 踏实.

## Etymology
Ideographic: 欠 (breath, desire, want) sits below a component built from
士 and 示, tied to sincere or heartfelt intention. The classical sense is
"sincere, earnest [desire]," from which "a sum of money/funds" (款项) and
"a style, an item" (款式) both later branched — a written pledge or a
recorded item both being expressions of a settled intention.

## Mnemonic
An earnest wish 欠, put down in writing and made official.

## Notes
款 splits into finance (存款 "savings," 贷款 "loan," 罚款 "fine") and style
(款式 "style, design," 新款 "new model") — two everyday domains that no
longer feel connected to the character's original "sincere intent."

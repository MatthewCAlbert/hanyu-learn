## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 囗 (an enclosure) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 四塞, means "sealed on all four sides" — and 古 *gǔ* supplies
the sound.

"Sealed shut on every side" generalizes to "solid, firm, secure" (坚固,
巩固) and, as an adverb, "certainly, of course" (固然, "admittedly") —
something so firmly established it cannot be otherwise.

## Mnemonic
A boundary 囗 sealed as tightly as anything from ancient times 古: "固,"
firm and solid.

## Notes
坚固 ("solid, sturdy," pairing 固 with 坚) and 巩固 ("to consolidate,
strengthen") both build directly on this character's plain "firmly
sealed/secure" core sense.

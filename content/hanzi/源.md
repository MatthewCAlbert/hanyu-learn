## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, and 原 *yuán* supplies
the sound — and originally supplied the meaning too. 原 itself began as a
pictograph of a spring emerging from a cliff face (厂 plus 泉, covered
elsewhere in this app), meaning "a water source." Once 原 broadened to
its now-dominant abstract senses "original, primary" (原来, 原因), 氵 was
added back onto it to spin off 源, preserving the specific "water
source" meaning in its own dedicated character.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 welling up from its original 原 spring: "源," a source.

## Notes
源 and 原 are historically the very same word, split apart the way 帽 and
冒 were: one character kept the concrete "source" sense once the other
drifted toward the abstract.

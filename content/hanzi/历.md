## Etymology
The traditional 歷 was pictophonetic: 止 (foot, movement) supplied the
meaning; 厤 supplied the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 過也, "to pass through,"
is a picture of steps moving forward through time or space — the root of
both "calendar" (time passed through) and "history" (events passed
through).

Simplification dropped 止 entirely and reduced 厤 to a bare 厂+力.
Confidence is medium: 力 *lì* is a genuine, close phonetic match for
*lì*, worth declaring, but the meaning-bearing 止 is simply gone from the
modern shape, so no semantic component is offered.

## Mnemonic
Effort 力, applied under one roof after another 厂, year after year:
a record of everything passed through.

## Notes
历 covers both "calendar" (日历, 农历) and "history, experience" (历史,
经历, 简历 "résumé" — literally "a brief passing-through") — time is a
road you walk, in both senses of the word.

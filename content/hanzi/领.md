## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 页 (head — the same component as in 顿, 顶) supplies the
meaning; 令 *lìng* supplies the sound. The original, concrete sense is
项, "the neck" — the part of the body a collar sits around.

## Mnemonic
The neck 页, where an order 令 given from above must first pass through
on its way down: to lead.

## Notes
From "the neck [that a collar encircles]" the meaning branched two ways:
the literal 领子 "collar," and the abstract "to lead, to receive" (领导,
带领, 领取) — leading a group the way the neck heads and steadies the
body.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning and 弗 *fú* supplies the sound. Shuowen defines 佛 only as 仿佛, "as if, seeming", with no Buddhist sense. The Buddha meaning is a later transliteration of Sanskrit *Buddha*, using 佛 *fó* for its sound; the "as if" sense is read *fú*.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 who is not 弗 quite there: a vague likeness.

## Notes
Two readings: *fó* "Buddha" (佛教, 佛像) and *fú* in 仿佛 "as if". 弗 is the sound part: 费, 沸, 拂, 佛. The loan reading is unrelated to "person".

## Etymology
Pictographic — drops falling from a cloud beneath the sky. Shuowen reads the
top line as the heavens, the 冂 as a cloud, and the four dots as the water
falling between.

## Mnemonic
Drops falling from a cloud: rain.

## Notes
雨 is Kangxi radical 173 and the semantic top in 雪, 雷, 零, 雾, 需, 露. Two
readings: *yǔ* "rain" (下雨, 大雨), literary *yù* "to rain (down)" (雨雪).

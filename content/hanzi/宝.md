## Etymology
The traditional form 寶 packed four components under one roof 宀: 玉 (jade),
贝 (cowrie-shell money), and 缶 *fǒu* (an earthenware jar, supplying the
sound) — treasures kept safe in a house.

**Simplification dropped both 贝 and the phonetic 缶**, keeping only 宀
(house) and 玉 (jade) as a leaner ideographic pair: valuables sheltered
under a roof. So 宝 gives a clear meaning signal but, unlike its ancestor,
no sound clue at all toward *bǎo*.

## Mnemonic
Jade 玉 kept safe under a roof 宀: "宝," treasure.

## Notes
宝 survives as an affectionate term of endearment (宝贝, 宝宝) as well as its
literal "treasure" sense — a common pattern where "precious thing" extends
to "precious person."

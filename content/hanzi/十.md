## Etymology
The oracle-bone graph for "ten" is a single vertical line (sometimes with a
knot or bulge in the middle). The crossing horizontal was added later to keep
it distinct from 一 turned on its side. Shuowen reads the finished cross as
"the four directions and the centre complete." The sign is simply "ten."

## Mnemonic
A full cross: ten — a complete count.

## Notes
十 is Kangxi radical 24. 十分 "ten parts (out of ten)" → "fully, very." 十字
路口 "crossroads" (the shape). 什 (亻 + 十) writes the "assorted" word.

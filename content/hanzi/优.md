## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 (person) supplies the meaning; the traditional phonetic
was 憂 *yōu* (also, confusingly, the word for "worry" — an unrelated
homophone pairing, not a meaning link). Simplification swapped 憂 for the
much simpler, closely-sounding 尤 *yóu*. Shuowen's original sense, 饒也
"abundant, ample," is the root of the modern "excellent, superior" — an
abundance of good qualities.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 who stands out above 尤 all the rest: excellent.

## Notes
优 keeps a secondary, older sense too: 優 once named a court entertainer
or actor (俳優), someone whose "abundance" was of talent for performance
— a sense now written separately but sharing this same root.

## Etymology
Not securely settled between two competing readings. Shuowen's own account
is abstract: 乙 pictures a spring plant shoot bending and struggling as it
pushes up through resistance — 象形, "a picture," but of a process rather
than an object. A separate, popular modern theory instead reads the same
bent shape as a swallow in flight. Both are plausible readings of a simple
curved stroke; neither is decisively provable, so confidence stays low.

## Mnemonic
A single curved stroke, bent like a shoot pushing through soil — or a bird
banking mid-flight.

## Notes
乙 is the second Heavenly Stem 天干 and, by extension, stands for "B" or
"second" in ranked lists (甲乙丙丁 ≈ A, B, C, D) the way 甲 stands for "A."
Its concrete pictorial meaning, whichever theory is right, has left no
trace in modern vocabulary.

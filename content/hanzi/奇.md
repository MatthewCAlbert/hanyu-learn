## Etymology
Ideographic. 大 (a person) over 可 — Shuowen's gloss offers two related
senses side by side: 異也 "different, unusual," and 不耦 "unpaired" (as
opposed to 偶 "paired, even"). A figure standing alone, not matched with
another, is both literally unpaired and figuratively strange.

## Mnemonic
A person 大 standing alone, acceptable 可 as they are, yet somehow
different from everyone else: strange, remarkable.

## Notes
奇 splits into "strange, unusual" (奇怪, 奇迹 "miracle") and,
mathematically, "odd [number]" (奇数, as opposed to 偶数 "even") — both
senses trace back to the same idea of standing apart, unpaired.

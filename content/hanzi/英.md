## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (艸) "plant" gives the meaning; 央 *yāng* gives the sound
(for *yīng*). Shuowen: 艸榮而不實者 "a plant that flowers but bears no fruit"
— a blossom. From "the finest bloom" grew "outstanding, distinguished" (英才,
精英) and "a hero" (英雄).

## Mnemonic
The plant's 艹 showiest bloom: the flower of the crop.

## Notes
The 央 phonetic also writes 映 *yìng*, 秧 *yāng*, 殃 *yāng*. 英 also renders
"Eng-" in 英国 "England / Britain," 英语 / 英文 "English."

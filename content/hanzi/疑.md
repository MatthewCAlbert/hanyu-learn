## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen, depicting a figure hesitating: a person 子
whose feet 止 have stopped, uncertain which way to turn, with 匕 (a
turning/spoon-shaped mark) reinforcing the sense of indecision, and an
element related to 矢 lending sound. The overall picture — someone
stopped mid-path, unable to decide — matches the meaning "to doubt, to
be uncertain" closely, though the exact identity of every stroke in the
modern shape is not fully settled, hence medium confidence.

## Mnemonic
A figure stopped mid-stride 止, turning back and forth 匕, unable to
choose a direction: "疑," to doubt.

## Notes
怀疑 (to doubt/suspect, covered elsewhere in this app) and 疑问 ("a
question, a doubt") both build on this character's core "caught in
uncertainty" image.

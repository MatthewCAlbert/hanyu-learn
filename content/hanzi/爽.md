## Etymology
Shuowen defines 爽 as "bright", written from 大 (a person) with 爻爻 (a lattice of crossed strokes) in the armpits; the shape shows a person with light shining through, which is why it means clear and open. Why the lattice shape was chosen is not securely attested, and the usual story of "breathing fresh air" is a modern gloss. Senses "refreshing, frank, to feel good" are extensions.

## Mnemonic
A person 大 with air passing through the lattice 爻爻: refreshed.

## Notes
凉爽 "cool and refreshing", 爽快 "forthright", 爽口 "refreshing to the taste", 爽约 "break an appointment". Colloquial 好爽 "feels great".

## Etymology
牛 "ox" over 口 "mouth." Shuowen offers a folk account (a warning board tied to
a goring ox's horns); more plausibly, an ox presented with spoken words — a
report or announcement made to spirits. 牛 may also be a loose phonetic. From
"to announce" grew "to tell" (告诉), "to accuse / sue" (告状, 被告), and "to
declare" (告别, 报告).

## Mnemonic
Words 口 spoken over the sacrificial ox 牛: making a report.

## Notes
告 heads a phonetic series: 造 *zào*, 浩 *hào*, 靠 *kào*, 皓 *hào* — so the ox
here is carrying sound as much as sense.

## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 儿 (a standing/kneeling person) with 口 (mouth)
emphasized above — the family member entrusted to speak on the
household's behalf, marking them as "the elder" among siblings.

## Mnemonic
A person 儿 whose mouth 口 speaks for the whole family: "兄," older
brother.

## Notes
兄弟 (brothers, covered elsewhere in this app) pairs 兄 with 弟 (younger
brother) to name the sibling relationship by naming both roles at once.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 牛 (ox) supplies the meaning — a sacrificial animal, whole and unblemished — and 生 *shēng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as a complete, unblemished ox.

## Mnemonic
An ox 牛 kept alive 生 until it is offered.

## Notes
生 is sound only (although "alive" fits). 牺牲 "sacrifice", 牲畜 "livestock", 牲口, 三牲 (ox, sheep, pig).

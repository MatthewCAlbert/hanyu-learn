## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 乏 *fá* supplies the
sound, shifted to *fàn*. Shuowen's gloss 浮也 "to float" matches "to
drift, to float" directly. "Broad, general" (泛指 "to refer to broadly")
extends from something floating loose over a wide surface rather than
anchored to one point.

## Mnemonic
Something set adrift 氵, floating loose and lacking 乏 any fixed anchor.

## Notes
泛 spans the literal (泛舟 "to go boating," a classical-register phrase)
and the figurative "broad, unfocused": 泛滥 "to flood/be rampant," 广泛
"extensive, wide-ranging," 空泛 "vague, superficial."

## Etymology
Two characters now share this shape.

**后 "sovereign / queen"** is the original — a ruling figure with 口 "mouth"
for issuing commands. Shuowen: 繼體君也 "a ruler by succession." This survives
in 皇后 "empress," 太后.

**后 "after / behind"** is simplified from 後 (彳 "step" + 幺 + 夂 "a trailing
foot") — walking at the rear. Simplification merged it into 后.

## Mnemonic
A ruler giving orders 口 — and, by a merger, whatever comes behind.

## Notes
The temporal / spatial sense (以后, 后来, 后边, 最后) is the everyday one;
the "queen" sense is confined to a few fixed words.

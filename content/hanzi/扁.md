## Etymology
Shuowen: 扁 is 户 (door) over 冊 (bound slips, a written document): an inscription over a door, a plaque. That original sense is now written 匾 (a flat board). The meaning "flat" comes from the shape of a plaque; the reading *biǎn* "flat" is thus an extension. The modern shape 冊 is worn down, so "document" is not visible.

## Mnemonic
A flat board over the door 户 with a notice written on it.

## Notes
扁 as a sound part: 编 *biān*, 遍 *biàn*, 偏 *piān*, 篇 *piān*, 骗 *piàn*. Readings: *biǎn* "flat" (压扁, 扁平), *piān* in 扁舟 "small boat", and the verb 扁人 "to beat up".

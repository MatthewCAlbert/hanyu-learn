## Etymology
Simplified from 問. Pictophonetic: 口 "mouth" gives the meaning; 门 (門)
*mén* gives the sound (for *wèn*). Shuowen: 訊也 "to interrogate, to inquire."

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 at the gate 门: knocking with a question.

## Notes
The 门 phonetic also writes 们 *men*, 闷 / 悶 *mèn*, 扪 / 捫 *mén*, and (with
sound worn away) 间 / 間, 闻 / 聞. Senses: ask (请问, 问路), care about
(不问), a problem (问题).

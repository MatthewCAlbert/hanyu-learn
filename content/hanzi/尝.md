## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 嘗: 旨 (flavour, taste — related to 甘 "sweet")
supplied the meaning, 尚 *shàng* supplied the sound, shifted to *cháng*.
Simplification replaced 旨 with 云, so the modern 尝 no longer visibly
carries its original semantic component — the meaning "to taste" survives
by convention, not by a still-legible picture, which is why no `semantic`
is declared here.

## Mnemonic
A dish held up 尚 for a first taste before the clouds 云 of steam clear.

## Notes
尝 is the everyday verb for tasting or trying (尝一尝 "have a taste," 品尝 "to
sample/savour") and, in the literary register, "to have once [done
something]" (曾经/未尝 — a usage inherited unchanged from Classical Chinese).

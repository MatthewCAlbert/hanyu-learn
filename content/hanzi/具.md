## Etymology
Ideographic. 廾 (two hands, holding something up together) with an
abbreviated 貝 (shell/money) beneath — Shuowen's gloss, 共置也, "to set
out together, to provide jointly," pictures two hands presenting
valuables — the root of "equipment, tool; to possess, to have ready."

The modern top component is written 目, a graphic convergence with the
unrelated "eye" — what remains is the abbreviated 貝, not a second
appearance of "eye." Confidence is medium because that convergence makes
the semantic piece hard to point to cleanly in the modern shape.

## Mnemonic
Two hands 廾, holding up something valuable 目 for all to see: the tools,
laid out and ready.

## Notes
具 covers both the concrete "tool, implement" (工具, 家具, 玩具) and the
abstract "to possess, to be equipped with" (具有, 具备, 具体 "concrete,
specific") — having the right tools in hand either way.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — a person leaning to one side — and 扁 *biǎn* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as lopsided, tilted; the extension is "biased, partial, off-centre".

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 so flat 扁 against one side that they lean.

## Notes
扁 is sound only. Its series: 编 *biān*, 遍 *biàn*, 篇 *piān*, 骗 *piàn*, 偏 *piān*. Also a grammar word: 偏偏 "stubbornly, contrary to expectation", 偏要. 偏见 "prejudice".

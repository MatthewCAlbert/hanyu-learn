## Etymology
彳 "to step, to go" gives the meaning. Shuowen gives the phonetic as 㞷
*wǎng* (whose lower part is 王); the modern graph writes that element simply
as 王. Meaning: to go toward — and, of time, "past, gone by" (往事, 以往).

## Mnemonic
Setting off on foot 彳 toward somewhere: to go there.

## Notes
As a coverb it marks direction: 往东走 "head east," 往前看 "look ahead,"
开往北京 "bound for Beijing." 往来 "dealings"; 往返 "round trip";
往往 "more often than not."

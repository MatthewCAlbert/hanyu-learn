## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 心 (heart/mind) supplies the meaning; 今 *jīn* supplies
the sound. Shuowen's gloss, 常思也, "to think of constantly," is unchanged
in the modern word — 想念, 怀念 both keep this exact sense of persistent,
recurring thought.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 that keeps returning, again and again, to the present moment
今 you last shared: to miss someone, to long for them.

## Notes
念 splits between "to think of persistently, to miss" (想念, 惦念) and
"to read aloud, to study" (念书, 念课文) — both are, at root, a mind
returning again and again to the same content.

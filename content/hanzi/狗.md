## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 犭 (犬) "dog" gives the meaning; 句 *gōu* gives the sound (for
*gǒu*). Shuowen passes on a folk sound-gloss ("狗 is 叩 — it knocks / barks").

## Mnemonic
A dog 犭 that answers 句 to its name: 狗.

## Notes
犬 is the fuller "dog" graph (as in 犬牙, 猎犬); 狗 is the everyday word.
The 句 phonetic also writes 沟 / 溝 *gōu*, 钩 / 鉤 *gōu*, 苟 *gǒu*. 走狗
"running dog / lackey"; 狗屁 "nonsense."

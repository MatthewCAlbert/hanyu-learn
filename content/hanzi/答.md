## Etymology
⺮ "bamboo" over 合 *hé* for sound. The graph was originally 荼's neighbour 荅 "a
small bean" (艸 + 合); it was borrowed for the word "to reply, to respond"
(the meaning has no tie to bamboo or beans). Written with ⺮ as 答 for the
"answer" sense.

## Mnemonic
A reply that meets 合 the question, set down on a slip ⺮.

## Notes
The 合 phonetic also writes 给 / 給 *gěi*, 洽 *qià*, 拾 *shí*, 塔 *tǎ*. Two
readings: *dá* "to answer / a reply" (回答, 答案), *dā* "to respond / agree"
(答应, 答理).

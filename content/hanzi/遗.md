## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition
is simply "to lose" — and 贵 *guì* ("valuable/expensive") supplies the
sound; the readings *yí* and *guì* have drifted apart over the centuries,
a common outcome even where Shuowen itself records the pairing.

"To lose (something valuable) while traveling" broadened to "to leave
behind, to bequeath" (遗产, "an inheritance," lit. "left-behind
property"; 遗忘, "to forget," lit. "to lose from memory").

## Mnemonic
Movement 辶 that carelessly drops something valuable 贵 along the way:
"遗," to lose — or to leave behind.

## Notes
遗产 ("an inheritance/legacy") and 遗忘 ("to forget") both build on this
character's core "something valuable lost or left behind in passing"
sense.

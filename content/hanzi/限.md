## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (a mound — a natural barrier, the same component as in
隔, 阵) supplies the meaning "an obstruction, a boundary," and 艮 *gěn*
supplies the sound.

"A natural barrier" generalized to "a boundary, a limit" of any kind
(限制, 期限, "a deadline") — a mound blocking passage becomes any fixed
line that may not be crossed.

## Mnemonic
A mound 阝 as stubborn 艮 (艮 also names one of the Eight Trigrams,
associated with mountains and stillness) as a wall no one may pass: a
"限," a limit.

## Notes
限 shares its phonetic 艮 with 很 (hěn, very) and 恨 (hèn, to hate) — a
purely sound-based family, not a meaning-based one.

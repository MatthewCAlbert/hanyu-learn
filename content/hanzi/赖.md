## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 贝 (shell money) supplies the meaning — gain, profit — and 剌 *là* supplies the sound (*là* → *lài*). Shuowen glosses it as to gain. The senses developed from "profit" to "to benefit from, to rely on" and, with a pejorative turn, "to deny, to renege".

## Mnemonic
Money 贝 that you cut 剌 your way to and then lean on.

## Notes
剌 is sound only. 依赖 "depend", 信赖 "trust", 耍赖 "act shamelessly", 赖账 "refuse to pay", 无赖 "rogue". Series: 赖, 懒 *lǎn*, 癞 *lài*, 濑.

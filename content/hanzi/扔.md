## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 乃 *nǎi* supplies the sound,
shifted to *rēng*. Shuowen's gloss 因也 "to follow on, to rely on" is far from
the modern "to throw" — the character's meaning shifted substantially over
time, so treat the classical citation as a record of the graph's structure
rather than of its current sense.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 flinging something away, and then 乃 — gone.

## Notes
扔 is the everyday verb for throwing or discarding: 扔掉 "to throw away,"
扔垃圾 "to throw out the trash." Functionally overlaps with 丢 for
"discard," though 扔 leans toward an active throwing motion while 丢 covers
passive loss as well.

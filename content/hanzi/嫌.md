## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 兼 *jiān* supplies the sound; 女 is the nominal meaning component. Shuowen glosses it as dissatisfaction in the heart, or suspicion. Why 女 is used is not securely attested — it is a conventional radical here, and the character should not be read as being about women.

## Mnemonic
Dislike turns up uninvited 兼 and brings suspicion with it.

## Notes
兼 is sound only. Series: 谦 *qiān*, 廉 *lián*, 嫌 *xián*, 镰. 嫌弃 "dislike", 嫌疑 "suspicion", 涉嫌 "be suspected of", 嫌麻烦 "think it a bother".

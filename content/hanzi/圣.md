## Etymology
Traditional 聖 (holy, sage) is 耳 (ear) + 口 (mouth) over 壬/呈: one who hears and speaks, the sage. Simplification does not abbreviate it; it borrows an older, separate character 圣 (read *kū* in the Shuowen dialect gloss: "to labour on the land"), made of 又 (hand) and 土 (earth). The two have no shared history, so a story reading 又 and 土 as "holy" is a folk invention.

## Mnemonic
Hand 又 on the earth 土 — a saint who works the land.

## Notes
圣诞节 "Christmas", 神圣 "sacred", 圣人 "sage", 圣经 "Bible". The same 圣 shape stands in for 巠 in 经, 径 and 茎.

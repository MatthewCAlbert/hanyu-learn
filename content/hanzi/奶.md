## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 女 "female" gives the meaning; 乃 *nǎi* gives the sound. A
colloquial word — breast, milk, to suckle — resting on a nursing syllable, and
by extension the woman who nursed and raised a child: grandma (奶奶).

## Mnemonic
The female 女 who feeds the baby: milk; granny.

## Notes
乃 "then / thereupon" is the same graph in grammatical use. Senses: milk
(牛奶, 奶粉, 奶茶), breast, paternal grandmother (奶奶), and "-ish, cutesy"
in slang (奶里奶气).

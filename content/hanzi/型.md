## Etymology
Pictophonetic (Shuowen lists it under 从土从刑 without a 聲 marker, but 刑
*xíng* is also the exact reading, so it doubles as the phonetic): 土 (earth,
clay) supplies the meaning — a mold is made of packed clay — and 刑 supplies
the sound, and secondarily reinforces "a fixed pattern to be conformed to,"
since 刑 (punishment, law) is itself about enforcing a fixed standard.

"Mold for casting" generalized straightforwardly to "type, style, model"
(类型, 型号).

## Mnemonic
Clay 土 packed into a fixed pattern 刑 (a rule everyone must fit): the "型,"
mold or type.

## Notes
Same phonetic 刑 recurs in 形 (xíng, shape/form) — a useful pair to learn
together, since both circle the idea of a fixed, repeatable form.

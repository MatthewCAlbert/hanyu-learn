## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 车 (cart) supplies the meaning — the rut a wheel leaves, the track — and 九 *jiǔ* supplies the sound (*jiǔ* → *guǐ*). Shuowen glosses it as the width between wheels. Later, "rail, rule" follows.

## Mnemonic
Nine 九 carts have worn a track 车 into the road.

## Notes
九 is sound only. Series: 轨 *guǐ*, 究 *jiū*, 鸠 *jiū*, 仇 *chóu*. 轨道 "track, orbit", 轨迹 "trajectory", 接轨 "link up with", 越轨 "overstep".

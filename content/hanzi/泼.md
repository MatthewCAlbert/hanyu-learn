## Etymology
Ideographic, traditional 潑: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, paired with 发
(traditional 發, "to send forth, to release") — water sent flying out in one
motion. Not in Shuowen. The figurative "rough, coarse, brash" sense
(撇辣, 泼皮) extends from the same forceful, uncontained image as the
physical splash.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 sent flying 发 in one hard motion.

## Notes
泼 covers the literal (泼水 "to splash water," 泼水节 "Water-Splashing
Festival") and the character-trait sense: 撒泼 "to make an unreasonable
scene," 泼辣 "sharp-tongued and forceful" — usually describing a bold,
unrestrained woman in traditional usage.

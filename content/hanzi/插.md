## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 臿 *chā* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged — 臿 itself names a spade-like digging tool, so the
sound component here also carries an echo of "thrusting something into the
ground," even though its formal role in the character is phonetic. Shuowen's
gloss 刺內也 is "to thrust in," matching the modern sense directly.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 driving a spade 臿 straight down into the earth.

## Notes
插 covers inserting objects (插头 "electric plug," literally "insert-head")
and inserting oneself into a situation (插话 "to interrupt/cut in," 插队 "to
cut in line").

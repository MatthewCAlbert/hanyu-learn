## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning in this variant — Shuowen's
own entry is under 欬 with 欠 (breath) rather than 口 — 亥 *hài* supplies
the sound, shifted to *ké*. Shuowen's gloss 逆气也 "air moving the wrong
way" is a fitting clinical description of a cough.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 forced open by air pushing the wrong way, on the last 亥 hour of
the cycle.

## Notes
Two readings split cleanly: *ké* is the everyday verb "to cough" (咳嗽);
*hāi* is a separate interjection of sighing surprise or regret (咳, 算了吧
"ah, forget it") with no etymological connection to the cough sense beyond
sharing a shape.

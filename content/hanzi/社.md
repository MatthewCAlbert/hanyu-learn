## Etymology
Ideographic. 礻 (altar/ritual) beside 土 (earth) — Shuowen's gloss, 地主
也, is "the lord of the land": the deity of the local earth, worshipped
at a communal altar. The Zhou Li is cited directly: every twenty-five
households formed one 社, and planted a tree suited to their local soil
as its marker.

## Mnemonic
An altar 礻 built on the earth 土 itself, where the whole community
gathers.

## Notes
"The local earth-god's shrine, and the community that gathers there"
generalised completely into the modern "society, organisation" (社会,
社区, 报社) — a shared place of worship becoming the model for any
organised group.

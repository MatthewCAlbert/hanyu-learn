## Etymology
Pictographic. Shuowen calls it 穴蟲之總名也, "the general name for
burrowing creatures," and explicitly labels the graph 象形, "a picture" — a
rodent's head, feet, and long tail are all visible in the oracle-bone and
seal forms. One of the more securely attested pictographs in the whole set.

## Mnemonic
A rodent's whiskered head, small paws, and long tail, all in one silhouette.

## Notes
鼠 is Kangxi radical 208, with few characters under it in the modern HSK
set. It names the zodiac's first animal (鼠年 "Year of the Rat") and, via
English influence, the computer mouse (鼠标, "rat-indicator").

## Etymology
Not securely attested. The left element is 舟 "boat" (not 月 "moon / meat"),
worn to a 月-shape; the right, 𠬝, is a hand pressing down a kneeling person.
Shuowen glosses it 用也 "to use" and mentions a chariot-horse sense. The
familiar meanings — clothing / to wear, to submit / obey, to serve, a dose (of
medicine) — all developed from an unclear base.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 pressing someone to their knees 卩: made to submit.

## Notes
Senses: clothes (衣服, 服装), submit / be convinced (服气, 不服, 心服口服),
serve (服务, 服兵役), take medicine (服药). The 月 here is 舟, not flesh.

## Etymology
Simplified from 響. The traditional graph is 音 "sound" with 鄉 *xiāng* for
the sound — an echo. The simplified 响 rebuilds it as 口 "mouth" for the
meaning with 向 *xiàng* carrying the sound (for *xiǎng*).

## Mnemonic
Sound coming back out of the mouth of the valley 口: an echo.

## Notes
Senses: make a sound (钟响了, 手机响), a noise / echo (响声, 回响, 反响),
loud and resonant (响亮, 响彻), and a measure word for reports and gunshots
(一声闷响, 三响礼炮). 影响 "to influence."

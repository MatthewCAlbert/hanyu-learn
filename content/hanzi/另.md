## Etymology
Not securely attested. 另 does not appear in Shuowen Jiezi and has no
recorded oracle-bone or bronze form — it is a later character, and no
source consulted traces when or why 口 (mouth) and 力 (strength) were put
together for "separate, other." Any story linking "mouth" and "strength" to
"separately" would be invented, so none is offered here.

## Mnemonic
Speak up 口 with your own strength 力: do it separately, on your own.

## Notes
另 is often confused with 别 (also "other, don't") — 另外 "in addition,
separately" versus 别的 "other [things]." They are not etymologically
related; the overlap is purely semantic.

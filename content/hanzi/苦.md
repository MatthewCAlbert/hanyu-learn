## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 艹 (plant) supplies the meaning; 古 *gǔ* supplies the
sound. Shuowen names a specific bitter plant (苓/蘦); the general taste
sense, "bitter," generalised from there to become the character's whole
job today.

## Mnemonic
An old 古 plant 艹, bitter to the taste.

## Notes
"Bitter [taste]" extends smoothly to "bitter [experience]" — 痛苦
"suffering," 辛苦 "hard work, toil," 吃苦 "to endure hardship" — the same
metaphor English makes between a bitter flavour and a hard life.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 寸 (a hand, marking a controlled, deliberate action)
supplies the meaning; the traditional phonetic was 道 *dào* "a road, a
way." Shuowen's gloss, 引也, "to lead, to draw forward," is glossed
further by Xu Shen as 以寸引之也, "to draw someone forward step by
step" — a hand leading someone down a road.

Simplification compressed 道 down to a bare 巳-like remnant, no longer a
readable phonetic. Confidence is medium: 寸 remains a genuine, checkable
component, but the sound-carrying half is gone from the modern shape.

## Mnemonic
A hand 寸, drawing you forward down an unseen road: to guide.

## Notes
导 covers both physical guidance (导游 "tour guide," 向导 "a guide") and
directorial guidance (导演 "film director," 领导 "to lead") — a hand on
the road, whether literal or figurative.

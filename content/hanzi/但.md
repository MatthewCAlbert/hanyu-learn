## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning; 旦 *dàn* gives the sound.
Shuowen: 裼也 "to bare the upper body" — the original sense (later written
袒). The word was borrowed for "only, merely," and from "only that much" grew
the concessive "but, however."

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 with only what the dawn 旦 gave them: merely — and so, "but."

## Notes
The 旦 phonetic also writes 担 / 擔 *dān*, 胆 / 膽 *dǎn*, 坦 *tǎn*, 疸 *dǎn*.
但是 "but"; 不但 "not only"; 但愿 "if only."

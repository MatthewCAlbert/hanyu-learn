## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning, 甲 *jiǎ* supplies the sound
and reads unchanged. Not in Shuowen. The core sense — to hold something
under control by hand, whether as security for a debt or as custody of a
person — spans both "to pawn/mortgage" and "to detain, to escort under
guard."

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 holding tight to the first 甲 thing offered as a guarantee.

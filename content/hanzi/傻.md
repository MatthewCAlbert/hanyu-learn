## Etymology
Not securely attested. 傻 does not appear in Shuowen — it is a post-classical,
colloquial character, and the popular story that its right-hand side pictures
"being hit on the head" is a modern mnemonic circulating online rather than a
sourced etymology. What can be said with confidence is only that 亻 (person)
marks it as a word describing a person's trait; the rest of the graph is best
treated as non-decomposable for teaching purposes.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 whose head is somewhere else entirely.

## Notes
傻 describes foolish/simple behaviour without the harsher edge of 笨 (clumsy,
slow-witted) or the mocking edge of 呆 (blankly stupid) — 傻乎乎, 傻笑, 傻瓜 all
carry a note of endearment as often as insult.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 觸: 角 (horn) supplies the meaning, 蜀 *shǔ*
supplied the sound in the traditional form. Shuowen's gloss 牴也 "to butt,
to gore" describes an animal striking with its horns. Simplification
replaced 蜀 with 虫, so today's right-hand side is a graphic stand-in
rather than a working sound clue for *chù* — 虫 here does not mean
"insect."

## Mnemonic
A horn 角 making contact — a butt, a bump, any touch at all.

## Notes
触 generalised from animal-horn contact to any touch: 接触 "to come into
contact with," 触摸 "to touch," 感触 "a feeling stirred by an experience"
— touch, physical and emotional alike.

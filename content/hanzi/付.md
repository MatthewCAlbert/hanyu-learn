## Etymology
Ideographic. 寸 here stands for a hand (Shuowen glosses it directly: 寸
者，手也), holding an object out to another person — 亻 the recipient, 寸
the giving hand. The bronze-script form is vivid: a person on one side,
a hand offering something on the other.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻, and a hand 寸 placing something directly into theirs: to
hand over.

## Notes
付 keeps its literal "to hand over" sense almost unchanged in modern
compounds — 付钱 "to pay," 交付 "to deliver," 应付 "to deal with [by
handing over what's needed]."

## Etymology
Shuowen analyses the old form as 㕯 plus 章 *zhāng* abbreviated as the
phonetic — so historically a pictophonetic character, glossed "to know the
inside from the outside," i.e. to weigh, to deliberate.

**The sound clue is not recoverable in the modern shape.** The abbreviated 章
is no longer identifiable, and the standard graph offers no reliable
pronunciation hint. Learn 商 as sound-opaque. Its senses — to discuss (商量),
to trade (商业), the Shang dynasty, and a note of the old pentatonic scale —
all sit on the "deliberate / exchange" core.

## Mnemonic
Two parties talking a deal back and forth under one roof.

## Notes
One of eight HSK 1–2 characters whose phonetic was lost — compare 过 (咼),
边 (臱), 举 (舉). The oracle-bone graph for the dynasty name may be unrelated
in origin.

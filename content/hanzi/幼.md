## Etymology
Ideographic per Shuowen: 幺 (a tiny bundle of thread, "small") with 力
(strength) — someone whose strength is still small, not yet fully grown.

## Mnemonic
Strength 力 still as small and slight as a wisp of thread 幺: "幼," young.

## Notes
幼儿 ("a young child," lit. "young-child") and 幼儿园 ("kindergarten," lit.
"garden for young children") both keep this character's core "not yet
grown" sense.

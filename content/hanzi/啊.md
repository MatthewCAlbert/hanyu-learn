## Etymology
口 "mouth" marks it as a spoken sound; 阿 *ā* supplies the sound. A
purpose-built graph for the all-purpose interjection and sentence-final
particle. It pictures nothing.

## Mnemonic
An open mouth 口 letting out a sound: "ah!"

## Notes
As an interjection its tone shifts with feeling: *ā* (surprise), *á* (query),
*ǎ* (doubt), *à* (realisation / agreement). As a toneless final particle it
softens or affirms, and assimilates to the preceding sound (好啊 → 好哇,
是啊 → 是呀).

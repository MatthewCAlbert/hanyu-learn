## Etymology
杰 is a long-attested variant of 傑 / 桀. The core is 桀: feet 舛 up on a tree 木, a figure above the rest, hence "outstanding" (Shuowen also has the sense "wooden perch"). In 杰 the 灬 is what is left of the feet and is not "fire". 傑 adds 亻 (person) to mean "a person who stands out".

## Mnemonic
A tree 木 so tall that its top stands out.

## Notes
杰出 "outstanding", 豪杰 "hero", 俊杰, 杰作 "masterpiece". Also a common given-name character.

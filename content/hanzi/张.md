## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 弓 (bow) supplies the meaning; 长 *cháng* supplies the sound.
The original, concrete sense is "to string a bow" — to draw the bowstring
taut. 张弛 "tension and slackness" (of a bow) is the source of that word's
figurative use for alternating effort and rest.

## Mnemonic
Stretching 长 a bow 弓 until it's taut.

## Notes
From "to draw taut" the meaning spread to "to open up, spread out" (张开)
and then to a classifier for flat, spread-out things — paper, tickets,
tables — which is its most common modern job (一张纸, 一张票).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 細: 纟 (silk thread) supplies the
meaning — a fine, thin thread — and 囟 *xìn* (a pictograph of a skull's
soft fontanelle) supplied the sound.

**The modern right-hand component looks like 田 (field)**, but it is a
graphic descendant of 囟, not a reference to fields — this entry keeps
confidence medium since the phonetic value of that evolved shape is no
longer a reliable, cleanly attested sound clue.

## Mnemonic
A thread 纟 spun so fine it looks like it runs straight through a field
田 (really an old skull-shape 囟, worn down by time): "细," thin, fine.

## Notes
Do not read the right side of 细 as "field" — like 未, 雷, and 梦, it is
another case where a component's modern shape coincidentally resembles an
unrelated, more common character.

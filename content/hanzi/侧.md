## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning and 則 (simplified 则) *zé* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as "beside, at the side". The sense "lean to one side" follows.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 standing by the rule 则, off to the side.

## Notes
则 is sound only here. 侧面 "side", 两侧, 侧重 "put emphasis on", 侧身. Readings: *cè*, and *zhāi* in 侧歪 (colloquial, rare).

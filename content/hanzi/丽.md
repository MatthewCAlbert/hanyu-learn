## Etymology
The traditional 麗 was built on 鹿 (deer) with 丽 — a pictograph of a pair
of ornamental antlers or hanging jade pendants — supplying the sound.
Shuowen's own gloss is unexpected: 旅行也, "to travel together," because
deer instinctively move as a herd when startled toward food. The
"pairedness" visible in 丽's own paired shape (two matching hanging
marks) more plausibly underlies "matched, paired," and from there
"beautiful, elegant" — but Shuowen's herd-travel story and the
pair-of-ornaments story do not fully agree.

Modern simplification dropped 鹿 entirely, keeping only 丽. Confidence is
low both because the deer-based gloss is hard to reconcile with the
"paired" sense, and because no semantic component survives to check in
the modern shape.

## Mnemonic
A matched pair of ornaments, side by side: elegant, beautiful.

## Notes
丽 is now used almost exclusively for "beautiful" (美丽, 华丽, 秀丽) —
the deer, the herd, and the original ornament have all faded from
ordinary awareness of the character.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 阝 (a mound — a natural physical barrier) supplies the
meaning — Shuowen's definition, 险, means "dangerous, obstructed
terrain" — and 且 *qiě* supplies the sound.

"Obstructed, dangerous terrain" generalizes to the everyday verb "to
block, to hinder" (阻止, 阻碍) — anything, not just a hill, standing in
the way.

## Mnemonic
A mound 阝 blocking the path, and 且 (moreover) no way around it: "阻,"
to block.

## Notes
阻止 ("to stop, to prevent") and 阻碍 ("an obstacle, to hinder") both
build directly on this character's plain "blocking, obstructing" core
sense.

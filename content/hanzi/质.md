## Etymology
Ideographic in the traditional form 質: 貝 (money, valuables) with 斦 (a
pair of axes, here suggesting a balanced exchange of equal weight) —
Shuowen's definition is "to pledge objects to each other [as
collateral]," an even exchange of value.

**Simplification reduced 斦 to an unrecoverable fragment** (the modern
top of 质 no longer reads as a pair of axes), leaving only 贝 as a
declarable surviving component, which keeps this entry at medium
confidence.

From "an even exchange of value," 质 broadened to "substance, quality"
(质量, "quality" — the substance a thing is actually made of, as opposed
to mere appearance) and, separately, "to question, to interrogate" (质
问, 质疑 — pressing someone to account for the true substance of their
claim).

## Mnemonic
Goods 贝 exchanged evenly, weighed to their true substance: "质,"
quality — or the true nature of a thing.

## Notes
质量 ("quality," also "mass" in physics) and 本质 ("essence, true
nature") both build on this character's core "the real substance beneath
the surface" sense.

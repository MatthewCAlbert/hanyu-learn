## Etymology
Ideographic. Shuowen reads it as "a hand holding half a bamboo stalk" —
从手持半竹 — with 十 in the modern shape a clerical-script regularisation of
that half-bamboo, and 又 (hand) unchanged. 支 was originally the character
for a bamboo branch/stick, before 木 was added to make 枝 to carry that
concrete sense onward.

## Mnemonic
A hand 又 holding a bamboo stick 十: a branch, and something to lean on.

## Notes
支 kept the abstract senses once 枝 took over "tree branch": 支持 "to
support" (literally, to hold something up), 分支 "a branch/division," 支出
"to pay out" — all extensions of "holding, propping, dispersing outward."

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to twist, wring — and 丑 supplies the sound. The sound match is imperfect (*chǒu* vs *niǔ*), though the 丑 family includes both *niǔ* and *chǒu* readings. An earlier, related graph 杻 denoted a restraint (handcuff). The exact history is not securely attested.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 twisting an ugly 丑 knot.

## Notes
丑 is sound only here; 纽 and 钮 *niǔ* share it. 扭转 "reverse", 扭曲 "twist", 扭头 "turn one's head", 别扭 "awkward".

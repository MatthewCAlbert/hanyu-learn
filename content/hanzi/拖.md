## Etymology
拖 is the common variant of 拕, which Shuowen glosses "to drag". Both are pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to pull — and 它 (a snake shape, here only sound) supplies the sound *tā* → *tuō*; in the common form 它 is written like 也 (the two were once one graph). The sound element is not separately declared here because the standard graph does not resolve cleanly.

## Mnemonic
Dragging a wriggling snake 它 along with the hand 扌.

## Notes
拖鞋 "slippers", 拖延 "delay", 拖累 "burden", 拖欠 "be in arrears". 它, 也, 池, 驰, 他, 地 share the old 它/也 shape.

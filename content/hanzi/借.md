## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 亻 "person" gives the meaning — a transaction between people;
昔 *xī* gives the sound (for *jiè*). Shuowen: 假也 "to borrow, to lend" (one
graph does both directions).

## Mnemonic
One person 亻 handing something to another to use for a while: a loan.

## Notes
The 昔 phonetic also writes 惜 *xī*, 措 *cuò*, 错 / 錯 *cuò*, 醋 *cù*.
Senses: borrow (借钱, 借书, 借用) and lend (借给他, 出借) — direction comes
from context; make use of (借助, 借光, 凭借); use as a pretext (借口, 借故,
借题发挥).

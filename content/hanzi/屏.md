## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 尸 (a roofed structure; modern 户-like top) supplies the meaning — a screen, a shield — and 并 *bìng* supplies the sound (*bìng* → *píng*). Shuowen glosses it as to screen, to block. "Screen" now includes the display (屏幕). The top is not literally the door 户.

## Mnemonic
A door-sized shield 户 standing side by side 并 with another.

## Notes
并 is sound only. 屏幕 "screen", 屏风 "folding screen", 屏蔽 "block", 屏障 "barrier". Second reading *bǐng*: 屏息 "hold one's breath", 屏弃 "discard".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 譯: 言/讠 (speech) supplied the
meaning, and 睪 *yì* supplied the sound.

**Simplification reduced 睪 to an unrecoverable fragment**, leaving no
reliable sound clue toward *yì* in the modern shape — this entry
declares only the surviving semantic 讠 and keeps confidence at medium.

"To convert speech from one language into another" is 译's core sense
throughout its history, well before or after simplification.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 carried across from one tongue into another: "译," to
translate.

## Notes
翻译 ("to translate," pairing 译 with 翻, covered elsewhere in this app —
"to flip over," extended to converting meaning between languages) is the
standard modern compound built on this character.

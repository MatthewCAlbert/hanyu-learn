## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning; 寺 *sì* supplies the sound
— the same phonetic as 待, 诗, 特, a productive series. The original
sense, 握也 "to grasp, to hold," is unchanged in the modern word.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 holding steady, like a temple 寺 that has stood for centuries.

## Notes
"To hold in the hand" extends into "to hold to, to maintain" (坚持,
保持, 维持) — a physical grip becoming the metaphor for sustained,
determined effort.

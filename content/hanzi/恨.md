## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 忄 (heart) supplies the meaning, 艮 *gèn* supplies the sound,
shifted to *hèn*. Shuowen's gloss 怨也 "resentment" matches the modern sense
closely — one of the more stable characters in the set, with little drift
between the classical and current meaning.

## Mnemonic
A heart 忄 stuck stubbornly (艮 also means "tough, stubborn") on a grudge.

## Notes
恨 sits opposite 爱 in the emotional vocabulary (爱恨交织 "a tangle of love and
hate") and is milder than 仇 "hatred, enmity" — 恨 covers everyday regret and
resentment (真恨自己 "I really regret myself/kick myself") as much as deep
hatred.

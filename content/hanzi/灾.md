## Etymology
Ideographic — with more than one classical variant. Shuowen's 災ideographic entry glosses "fire from heaven" specifically, but Chinese
script history also records 灾 (火 fire under 宀 a roof — a house fire) and
灾's cousin 𢦏/烖, each foregrounding a different cause of disaster (fire,
flood, or both, depending on the variant's other component). The simplified
modern 灾 keeps 火 under 宀, favouring the "house fire" picture.

## Mnemonic
A house 宀 catching fire 火 — disaster.

## Notes
灾 covers disaster broadly, not just fire: 灾难 "calamity," 水灾 "flood
disaster," 地震灾区 "earthquake disaster zone" — the character generalised
well beyond its original fire-specific picture.

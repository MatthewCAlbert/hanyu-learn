## Etymology
Traditional 鑽 is pictophonetic: 金 (metal) supplies the meaning — a boring tool — and 贊 *zàn* the sound. Simplification replaced 贊 with 占 *zhàn/zhān*, which is a close substitute for the sound, so the new phonetic works as a rough hint. 占 here does not mean "to occupy".

## Mnemonic
A drill made of metal 钅 that claims 占 a spot in the wall.

## Notes
Two readings: *zuān* "to drill, to bore into" (钻研 "study deeply", 钻空子 "exploit loopholes") and *zuàn* "drill; diamond" (钻石, 钻头). 占 is only a sound clue.

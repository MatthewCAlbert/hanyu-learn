## Etymology
The traditional 團 was pictophonetic: 囗 (an enclosure, standing for
"round shape") supplied the meaning; 專 *zhuān* supplied the sound.
Shuowen's gloss is simply 圜也, "round."

Simplification replaced the phonetic 專 with the much simpler 才 *cái* —
a rougher sound match than most pairs in this dataset, closer in spirit to
a stroke-saving substitution than a precise phonetic swap. Confidence is
medium, and no semantic component is declared, because 囗 here is a
generic enclosure shape rather than a distinct, glossable meaning-bearing
piece on its own.

## Mnemonic
Talented people 才, gathered inside a circle 囗: a group, rolled into one.

## Notes
"Round" extended into "a rolled-up mass" (一团纸 "a wad of paper") and,
separately, into "an organised group" (代表团, 剧团, 集团) — a circle of
people is a natural next step from a round shape.

## Etymology
Pictographic — an eye. The oracle-bone graph is a horizontal almond shape with
the pupil; standing it upright gave the modern 目.

## Mnemonic
An eye stood on end: 目.

## Notes
目 is Kangxi radical 109 and the semantic in 看, 眼, 睛, 睡, 眉, 相. Senses:
eye (目光, 耳目), to look (目送, 目不转睛), an item / heading (项目, 目录,
题目), an aim (目的, 目标).

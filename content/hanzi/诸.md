## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 讠 (speech) supplies the meaning and 者 *zhě* supplies the sound (*zhě* → *zhū*). The Shuowen gloss is "to distinguish, to discuss"; the common sense "all, various" is a borrowed one. In classical Chinese 诸 is also a fusion of 之于 / 之乎.

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 by a person 者 to all and sundry.

## Notes
者 is sound only. Series: 著 *zhù*, 煮 *zhǔ*, 猪 *zhū*, 储 *chǔ*, 都 *dū*, 诸 *zhū*. 诸位 "everyone (polite)", 诸多 "many", 诸如此类 "and so on".

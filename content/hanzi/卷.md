## Etymology
Ideographic-leaning-pictophonetic per Shuowen: 㔾 (the same bent/kneeling
figure seen in 卩-series characters) supplies the meaning "bent, curled,"
and a component related to 𠔉 supplied the sound. The base meaning, per
Shuowen, is literally "a bent knee" — a curled shape — which is exactly the
image needed for "to roll up" (卷起来) and, as a noun, "a rolled scroll,"
hence "a volume" of a book (第一卷) and, by extension, an exam paper (试卷,
originally a rolled document).

## Mnemonic
Hands rolling something into a curl, bending it like a knee 㔾 folding: to
"卷," roll up.

## Notes
Two readings split the grammar: *juǎn* is the everyday verb "to roll up"
(卷起来); *juàn* is the noun "a volume, a scroll, an exam paper" (试卷, 第一
卷) — same shape, different word class.

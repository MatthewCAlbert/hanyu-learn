## Etymology
Pictophonetic in the traditional form 慮: 思 (to think — itself built
from 田 and 心, "mind" over "heart") supplied the meaning — Shuowen's
definition, 谋思, means "deliberate, planning thought" — and 虍 *hū* (a
tiger-stripe pattern, the top of 虎) supplied the sound.

**Simplification dropped 思 down to just its 心 (heart) half**, nested
inside the surviving 虍. The full "deliberate planning" image is
compressed, though the core sense — careful, worried thought — survives
intact.

## Mnemonic
A heart 心 circling warily, tiger-alert 虍, turning a worry over and
over: "虑," to consider carefully.

## Notes
考虑 (to consider, covered elsewhere in this app) and 顾虑 ("a
reservation, a worry that holds one back") both build on this
character's core "careful, anxious deliberation" sense.

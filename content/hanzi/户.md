## Etymology
A pictograph, and Shuowen already explains it plainly: 戶 is "half of a
门" — a single door-panel, as opposed to the double-panelled 门 (門) it is
paired with. Both 门 and 户 draw a door; 户 is simply the one-leaf version.

"Household" (户口, 一户人家) is a metonymic extension: the door standing for
the home and family behind it, the same shift English "household" makes
from "hold" to "house."

## Mnemonic
One door-leaf standing alone where 门 has two: "户," a single door — and the
home it opens onto.

## Notes
户口 (household registration) and 窗户 (window, lit. "window-door," pairing
户 with 窗) are the two most common everyday compounds built on 户.

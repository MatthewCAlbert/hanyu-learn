## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 木 (wood) supplies the meaning — a wooden pillar — and 主 *zhǔ* supplies the sound (*zhǔ* → *zhù*). 主 is itself an old picture of a lamp flame standing on a stand, but here it provides sound only.

## Mnemonic
A wooden 木 post where the host 主 stands.

## Notes
主 is sound only. Series: 住 *zhù*, 注 *zhù*, 柱 *zhù*, 驻 *zhù*, 蛀. 柱子 "pillar", 支柱 "mainstay", 柱状图 "bar chart".

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 繞: 纟 (thread) supplies the meaning, 尧
(traditional 堯) supplies the sound, shifted to *rào*. Shuowen's gloss 纠也
is "to twist, to wind" — thread winding around itself — which extends
smoothly to "to go around, to make a detour," and figuratively "to
confuse/perplex" (绕圈子 "to talk in circles").

## Mnemonic
Thread 纟 wound round and round a tall figure 尧.

## Notes
绕 covers literal winding (绕线 "to wind thread/wire") and spatial detours
(绕路 "to take a roundabout way," 绕过 "to bypass"). Shares 尧 with 浇 jiāo
(to water/pour) and 烧 shāo (to burn) — three different senses on one loose
sound.

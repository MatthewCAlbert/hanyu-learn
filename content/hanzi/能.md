## Etymology
Pictographic — a bear: 厶 the head, ⺼ the body, and 匕匕 the two clawed feet.
Shuowen: 熊屬 "of the bear kind." The bear, seen as powerful and capable, lent
its name to "ability, capacity," and from there the modal "can, be able."
When 能 was fully taken over by "can," 熊 (能 + 灬 "fire") was coined for the
animal.

## Mnemonic
A bear — strong, capable — standing in for "can."

## Notes
The modal 能 marks capacity or possibility (能来吗), against 会 (learned skill)
and 可以 (permission). Also a noun: 能力 "ability," 能源 "energy."

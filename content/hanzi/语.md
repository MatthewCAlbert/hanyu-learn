## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 吾 *wú* gives the sound
(for *yǔ*). Shuowen: 論也 "to discourse."

## Mnemonic
Speech 讠 spoken by me 吾: language.

## Notes
The 吾 phonetic also writes 悟 *wù*, 梧 *wú*, 晤 *wù*. Two readings: *yǔ*
"language / to speak" (语言, 汉语), *yù* "to tell" (不以语人). See also 话, 言.

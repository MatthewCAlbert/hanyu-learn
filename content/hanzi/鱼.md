## Etymology
A pictograph of a fish — head at the top, scaled body, fanned tail. Shuowen:
水蟲也 "a water creature; it depicts the form." In the simplified 鱼 the four
dots of the tail (火) are levelled to a single stroke 一.

## Mnemonic
Head, body, tail, top to bottom: a fish.

## Notes
It is its own radical (no. 195), heading 鲜 鲤 鲨 鳄 and hundreds of fish
names. 鱼 also puns on 余 "surplus" — hence fish at New Year (年年有余).

## Etymology
Pictophonetic, with the phonetic also carrying an echo of meaning. 缶 (a
ceramic vessel) supplies the meaning; 夬 *guài* supplies the sound. 夬
itself recurs with a "gap, missing piece" sense across a small family:
决 (jué, water breaching a gap), 玦 (jué, a jade ring with a gap cut in
it), and 缺 (a vessel broken/missing a piece) — all sharing the idea of
something whole with a piece taken out.

## Mnemonic
A ceramic pot 缶, broken open 夬, missing a piece: incomplete.

## Notes
"A broken, incomplete vessel" generalised fully into the abstract "to
lack, to be short of" (缺少, 缺乏, 缺点) — the specific pottery is gone,
but the "missing piece" logic survives intact.

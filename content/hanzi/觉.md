## Etymology
Simplified from 覺. 见 "to see, to perceive" gives the meaning; the top was 學
abbreviated, carrying the sound. Shuowen: 悟也 "to become aware, to wake up
to." Simplification reduces the phonetic top to ⺍.

## Mnemonic
The moment perception 见 clicks: to realise; to wake.

## Notes
觉 mirrors 学 / 學 (learning) — the top is shared. Two readings: *jué* "to
feel / perceive / realise" (觉得, 感觉, 自觉), *jiào* "a sleep" (睡觉, 午觉).

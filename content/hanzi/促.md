## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
迫, means "to press, to urge" — and 足 *zú* supplies the sound (the modern
readings *cù* and *zú* have drifted apart, a common outcome after two
millennia of sound change, even though Shuowen itself records the
pairing).

"To press, to urge" extends to "to hasten, to prompt" (促进, "to
promote/accelerate"; 催促, "to urge") and, separately, to "close, near"
(短促, "brief/hurried," of time).

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 pressing close on someone's heels 足, urging them onward:
"促," to urge.

## Notes
促进 ("to promote, to accelerate") and 督促 ("to supervise and urge on")
both build on this character's core "pressing someone toward action"
sense.

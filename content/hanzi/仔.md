## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 亻 (person) supplies the meaning, 子 *zǐ* ("child") supplies
the sound and reads unchanged, while also lending an echo of meaning — a
young, small thing, human or animal. Not in Shuowen.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 still small as a child 子 — young, or just getting started.

## Notes
仔 covers young animals (猪仔 "piglet," 鱼仔 "small fish") and, through
Cantonese-influenced Mandarin slang, young men or workers: 打工仔 "a young
wage worker." 仔细 "careful, meticulous" is a separate, unrelated
compound built on 仔's older "small, fine" sense.

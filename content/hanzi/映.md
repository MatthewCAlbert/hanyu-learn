## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 日 (sun) supplies the meaning — light reflecting or
shining upon a surface — and 央 *yāng* supplies an approximate sound.
This entry cannot cite a Shuowen entry for 映 directly, so it keeps
confidence at medium.

"To shine upon, to reflect" (反映, 映照) extends to "to project" (放映, "to
screen a film," lit. "to release and project light").

## Mnemonic
Sunlight 日 falling squarely in the center 央 of a surface, lighting it
up: "映," to reflect or shine upon.

## Notes
反映 ("to reflect," both literally light and figuratively a situation)
and 放映 ("to screen a film") both build on this character's core "light
cast upon a surface" image.

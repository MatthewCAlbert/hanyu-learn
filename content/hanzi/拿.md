## Etymology
An associative compound: 合 "to close, to bring together" over 手 "hand" — to
close the hand around something. A late character, a variant of the older 拏
(手 + 奴 phonetic). Meaning: to hold, to take, to grasp.

## Mnemonic
The hand 手 closing 合 on an object: to take hold of it.

## Notes
Also a coverb marking the thing handled (拿它当例子 "take it as an example").
As a resultative: 拿到 "get in hand," 拿出 "produce," 拿走 "take away."

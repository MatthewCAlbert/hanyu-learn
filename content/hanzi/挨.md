## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 扌 (hand) supplies the meaning — to strike, to press against — and 矣 *yǐ* supplies the sound (*yǐ* → *āi/ái*). Shuowen glosses it as striking the back. "To receive a blow, to suffer" (*ái*) and "to be close to" (*āi*) are both extensions.

## Mnemonic
A hand 扌 pushing up against you until you can't help but say "yes 矣".

## Notes
矣 is sound only here. Two readings: *āi* "next to, in turn" (挨着, 挨家挨户 "door to door") and *ái* "to suffer" (挨打, 挨饿, 挨骂).

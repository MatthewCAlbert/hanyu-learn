## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 氵 "water" gives the meaning; 票 *piào* gives the sound (for
*piāo*). Shuowen: 浮也 "to float."

## Mnemonic
Riding on the surface of the water 氵: to float.

## Notes
The 票 phonetic also writes 飘 / 飄 *piāo*, 瓢 *piáo*, 剽 *piāo*, 缥 *piāo*.
*piāo*: float / drift (漂浮, 漂流, 漂泊); *piǎo*: bleach or rinse (漂白,
漂洗); *piào*: in 漂亮 "pretty, good-looking; well done" and 漂没 "to
embezzle."

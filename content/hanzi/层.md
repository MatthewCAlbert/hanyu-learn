## Etymology
Simplified from 層. 尸 (here "building, roof") gives the meaning — Shuowen:
重屋也 "a house of stacked storeys." The phonetic 曾 *céng* is reduced to 云
in the simplified form and no longer carries the sound.

## Mnemonic
Storeys stacked one on another under the roof 尸: a layer.

## Notes
Senses: a storey / floor (三层楼, 底层), a layer / stratum (一层灰, 云层,
社会阶层), a measure word for layers, coats and steps in reasoning (一层意思,
两层皮, 更进一层).

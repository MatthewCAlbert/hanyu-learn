## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 手 (hand) supplies the meaning, 麻 *má* supplies the sound,
shifted to *mó*. Shuowen's gloss 研也 "to grind, to rub" matches the
modern sense directly — rubbing or grinding by hand, whether physically or
figuratively.

## Mnemonic
A hand 手 rubbing hemp fibre 麻 smooth between the palms.

## Notes
摩 anchors 摩擦 "friction," 按摩 "massage," and 观摩 "to observe and study
[a demonstration]" — the last a figurative "rubbing shoulders with" good
practice to learn from it.

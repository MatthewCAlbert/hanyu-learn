## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 月 (moon) supplies the meaning, 良 *liáng* supplies the
sound, shifted to *lǎng*. Shuowen's gloss 明也 "bright" matches the modern
sense directly — originally moonlight's brightness, generalised to
clarity of any kind.

## Mnemonic
Moonlight 月 as clear and good 良 as a cloudless night.

## Notes
朗 spans literal brightness (晴朗 "clear and bright [weather]") and clarity
of expression: 朗读 "to read aloud clearly," 朗诵 "to recite [poetry]" — a
bright, carrying voice as much as a bright sky.

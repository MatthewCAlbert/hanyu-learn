## Etymology
糸 "silk cord" gives the meaning; the top, now 田, is a worn-down 畾 (three
mounds), which carried the sound. Shuowen (under 纍): "to bind up neatly; also,
a thick rope." From "to bind / heap up" grew "to accumulate" (*lěi*), "to
implicate / burden" (*lěi*, 连累), and "worn out, tired" (*lèi*).

## Mnemonic
Bundle 糸 piled on bundle: it wears you down.

## Notes
Readings: *lèi* "tired / tiring" (太累了), *lěi* "to accumulate" (积累,
日积月累) and "to involve" (拖累), *léi* in 累赘 "cumbersome."

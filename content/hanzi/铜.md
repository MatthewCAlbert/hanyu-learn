## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 钅 (metal) supplies the meaning — copper, the "red metal" — and 同 *tóng* supplies the sound. Shuowen glosses it as red metal.

## Mnemonic
A metal 钅 shared by everyone 同 in the same colours: copper.

## Notes
同 is sound only here. 铜牌 "bronze medal", 铜像 "bronze statue", 青铜 "bronze", 铜钱 "copper coin". Series: 铜, 桐, 筒, 洞, 垌.

## Etymology
The traditional form 穩 paired 禾 (grain — a settled, well-stacked grain
pile as an image of stability) with a phonetic built on 隱 *yǐn* ("hidden,
concealed," also seen in 隐).

**Simplification replaced that phonetic with the unrelated 急** (*jí*,
"urgent"), which is neither semantic nor phonetic here — a shape
substitution that gives no reliable sound clue toward *wěn*. Because of
that loss, this entry declares only the surviving semantic 禾 and keeps
confidence medium.

## Mnemonic
A grain pile 禾 settled so firmly that nothing urgent 急 could ever topple
it: "稳," stable.

## Notes
稳 shows the same simplification pattern as 权, 宝, and 奖: a real
semantic component survives, but the phonetic has been swapped for an
unrelated look-alike, leaving no sound clue in the modern shape.

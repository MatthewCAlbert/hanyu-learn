## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 羽 (feather/wing) supplies the meaning — a bird's wing turning
over in flight — and 番 *fān* supplies the sound.

"To flip over" (a page, a pancake, a decision) generalizes the wing's
turning motion to anything turned over, including turning speech from one
language into another: 翻译, "to translate," literally "to flip over"
(convert) meaning from one code to another.

## Mnemonic
A wing 羽 taking a turn 番 (番 also means "a time, an occurrence"): flipping
over and over — "翻."

## Notes
Same phonetic 番 recurs in 藩 (fān, hedge/vassal state) and 幡 (fān, banner) —
a purely sound-based family, not a meaning-based one.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic in origin. 辶 (movement) supplies the meaning; the
traditional phonetic was 羍 *tà* "a lamb." Shuowen's own gloss is
puzzling on its face — 行不相遇也, "travelling without meeting" — most
plausibly describing a road so open and unobstructed that travellers
pass freely without needing to stop or cross paths: unimpeded passage,
which is the clear ancestor of "to reach, to arrive without hindrance."

Simplification replaced 羍 with the unrelated, similarly-shaped 大.
Confidence is medium because Shuowen's own definition is itself somewhat
opaque, and the modern phonetic is a pure graphic substitute rather than
a sound match.

## Mnemonic
Travelling 辶 on a road wide open 大 enough that nothing stands in your
way: to arrive, unimpeded.

## Notes
达 covers "to reach, to arrive at" (到达, 达到) and, by extension, "to
convey, to express" (表达, 传达) — an idea travelling from one mind to
another as unimpeded as a traveller on an open road.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning — to float — and 孚 *fú* supplies the sound. 孚 is itself an old graph of a hand above a child (to hatch, to brood), but in 浮 it carries sound only.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 holding up the child 孚 — floating.

## Notes
浮力 "buoyancy", 浮现 "emerge", 浮躁 "restless", 浮动 "fluctuate", 浮云. Series: 浮 *fú*, 俘 *fú*, 孵 *fū*.

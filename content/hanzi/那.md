## Etymology
Originally a place-name. Shuowen analyses it as 邑 "settlement" + 冄 *rǎn* for
sound, glossing it as the name of a western statelet. Its use for the distal
demonstrative "that / those" is a much later phonetic borrowing — the graph
has no pictorial or semantic tie to "that."

## Mnemonic
Point away at what is over there: that.

## Notes
Pairs with 这 "this" and 哪 "which" (哪 is 那 + 口). Colloquially *nà* contracts
with a following 一 to *nèi*. See the word entries 那些, 那里, 那边.

## Etymology
Pictophonetic: 口 (mouth) supplies the meaning — Shuowen's definition,
嗛, means "to hold in the mouth without swallowing" — and 今 *jīn*
supplies the sound.

"To hold in the mouth" generalized to the abstract "to contain, to hold
within" (包含, 含有) — holding an idea or ingredient the way a mouth holds
something without releasing it.

## Mnemonic
A mouth 口 closed around the present moment 今, holding it in without
letting go: to "含," contain.

## Notes
含 and 包 (to wrap) are close synonyms in the "contain" sense (包含 pairs
them directly) but keep distinct literal images — 含's mouth versus 包's
wrapping cloth.

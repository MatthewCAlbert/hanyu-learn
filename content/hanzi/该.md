## Etymology
Simplified from 該. Pictophonetic: 讠 (言) "speech" gives the meaning; 亥 *hài*
gives the sound (for *gāi*). Shuowen: 軍中約也 "a military agreement." From "an
agreed obligation" grew the modal "ought to, should" (应该), the anaphoric
"the said, that" (该公司), and "to be one's turn / to owe" (该我了, 该他还钱).

## Mnemonic
The obligation spoken 讠 and agreed: what one should do.

## Notes
The 亥 phonetic also writes 孩 *hái*, 刻 *kè*, 核 *hé*, 骸 *hái*. 应该 "should";
活该 "serves (one) right."

## Etymology
The character for "to hear" is 聽 — 耳 "ear" with 王 (壬) and 悳 (德), a compound
about attentive listening. The simplified form drops all of that.

听 by itself is an unrelated old graph — 口 "mouth" + 斤 — that meant "to
laugh / grin" (read *yǐn*). Simplification adopted it purely for its simple
shape, so the modern 听 "listen" shows a mouth and an axe and means neither.

## Mnemonic
Learn 听 by its job, not its parts — the ear that should be there is gone.

## Notes
A shape-borrowing simplification, like 体 (for 體) and 术 (for 術). Also
a measure word: 一听可乐 "a can of cola" (from English "tin").

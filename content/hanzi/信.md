## Etymology
An associative compound: 亻 "person" + 言 "speech" — a person standing by their
word. Shuowen: 誠也 "sincere, true." From "trustworthy" grew "to trust, to
believe," "a token that carries one's word" (a seal, a message, a letter), and
"faith" (信教).

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 whose word 言 holds: trustworthy.

## Notes
Senses: trust / believe (相信, 信任, 信心), a letter (写信, 家信), a message /
signal (信息, 信号), religious faith (信仰, 迷信).

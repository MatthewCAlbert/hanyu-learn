## Etymology
Pictophonetic. 竹 "bamboo" gives the meaning — texts were written on bound
bamboo strips; 扁 *biǎn* gives the sound (for *piān*). Shuowen: 書也 "a
writing."

## Mnemonic
A run of bamboo 竹 strips bound and written on: one piece of writing.

## Notes
The 扁 phonetic also writes 编 / 編 *biān*, 遍 *biàn*, 骗 / 騙 *piàn*, 偏
*piān*. Senses: a piece of writing, an essay, a chapter (篇章, 长篇, 短篇),
a sheet of paper (歌篇), and a measure word for articles, papers and pages
(一篇作文, 三篇论文).

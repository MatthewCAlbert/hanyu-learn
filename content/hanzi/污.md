## Etymology
Pictophonetic, traditional 汙: 氵 (water) supplies the meaning, 亏
(traditional 于) supplies the sound, shifted to *wū*. Shuowen gives two
senses: 薉也 "filthy, foul," and, separately, "a small pool is called 汙" —
stagnant water being the natural source of the "dirty" sense that won out.

## Mnemonic
Water 氵 gone stagnant, its level dropped short 亏 of clean and flowing.

## Notes
污 anchors 污染 "pollution," 污水 "sewage/wastewater," and figuratively
贪污 "corruption" (literally "greedy filth") — dirtiness extended from
water to conduct.

## Etymology
An associative compound: 八 "to split apart" over 刀 "knife" — Shuowen says it
plainly, 刀以分別物也 "the knife is what divides things." From "to divide" grew
"a part, a portion," "a minute" (a division of the hour), "a point / mark" (a
division of a score), and "a fraction."

## Mnemonic
A knife 刀 cutting a thing in two 八: to divide.

## Notes
Two readings: *fēn* "divide / minute / point / fraction" (分开, 十分, 分数),
*fèn* "a share, a duty, a component" (身分, 本分, 成分, 部分).

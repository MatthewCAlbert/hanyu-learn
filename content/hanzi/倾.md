## Etymology
Traditional 傾 is 亻 (person) with 頃 (simplified 顷), which Shuowen marks as "also sound": 頃 depicts a head tilted to one side, and 傾 is the same idea extended to the whole person, "to lean". "Pour out, overturn" extends it to a vessel tipped over.

## Mnemonic
A person 亻 whose head 顷 tips so far that everything pours out.

## Notes
顷 is both sound and meaning here, which is unusual. 倾向 "tendency", 倾听 "listen intently", 倾诉 "pour out one's feelings", 倾斜 "slope".

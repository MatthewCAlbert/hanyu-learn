## Etymology
Pictophonetic-and-ideographic together: 辶 (movement) supplies the
meaning; the right-hand component, 𠂤, supplies the sound but is not
sound-only. Shuowen notes 𠂤 originally meant "a small hill" and, in
oracle-bone usage, stood for "army/troops" (師) — so 追師, "to pursue an
army," is folded into the character's logic alongside the plain phonetic
role.

The app's decomposition data resolves 𠂤's modern descendant as 丿+㠯, a
graphic regularisation rather than a separately meaningful piece, so no
phonetic is declared — only the clearly checkable 辶.

## Mnemonic
Chasing 辶 after a fleeing troop 㠯: to pursue.

## Notes
追 covers physical pursuit (追赶) and, by extension, pursuing something
intangible — 追求 "to pursue [a goal, a person romantically]," 追问 "to
press [someone] with questions."

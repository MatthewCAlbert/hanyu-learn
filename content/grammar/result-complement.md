## Pattern
A second verb or adjective glued right after the main verb states its result: 写[[错]] "wrote wrong," 看[[懂]] "understood by reading," 洗[[干净]] "washed clean," 吃[[完]] "finished eating," 听[[清楚]] "heard clearly." The whole V+Result acts as one predicate.

## Usage
Negate a completed result with 没(有), dropping any 了: 我没写错, not *我不写错了. The object follows the whole complement: 我写错了一个字, not *我写一个字错了.

## Notes
Not every verb pairs freely with every result — these are set combinations to learn as chunks, not yet a fully generative rule.

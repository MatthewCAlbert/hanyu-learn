## Pattern
[[用不着]] means there is no need or no use: 用不着担心, 用不着这么多人. It is a frozen potential, not a freshly built 用 + 不 + 着.

## Usage
It can take a verb or a noun. The tone is often “don't bother” rather than “cannot physically use.” 用得着 is the less common affirmative.

## Notes
Same 得/不 slot family as [[grammar:keneng-complement]] and [[grammar:laideji]], but 着 here is “be needed,” not a durative.

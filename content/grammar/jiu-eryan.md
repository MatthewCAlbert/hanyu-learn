## Pattern
就 or 对 introduces the angle, and 而言 closes it: *就/对 A 而言，B*. B is then judged from that angle.

## Usage
就…而言 limits the topic; 对…而言 gives the point of view of a person or group. Both are written and give the sense of "as for". 对…来说 is the spoken equivalent.

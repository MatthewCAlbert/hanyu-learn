## Pattern
Two verb phrases with the same subject can stand together when their relationship is clear from meaning. Beyond simple sequence or purpose, VP1 may supply the cause, condition, or concession for VP2.

## Usage
Do not automatically insert a conjunction between the phrases. First identify the understood relation: 听了很高兴 is causal; 不认真准备很难通过 is conditional. Add an explicit connector when the intended relation could be misread.

## Notes
This develops the basic serial sequence in [[grammar:lianlian-ju]]. The structure is compact, so context and aspect markers carry more of the logical work.

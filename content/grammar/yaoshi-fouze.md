## Pattern
要是 sets a condition; 就 marks what follows if it holds; [[否则]] marks the fallback if it does not: *要是 A，就 B，否则 C*.

## Usage
B and C should be real alternatives. 否则 already means “if not A”; do not add another 不 before a copy of A. This extends [[grammar:yaoshi-jiu]] and [[grammar:fouze]] into one three-part plan.

## Notes
不然 can replace 否则 in speech. Keep 否则 in careful writing.

## Pattern
大A大B pairs two related words under 大 to make the scene large-scale or intense: 大风大雨. 一A一B pairs two counterparts under 一: 一左一右. Both are four-character frames, not free syntax.

## Usage
Stick to conventional pairs. Invented 大吃大睡 may not be accepted. 不A不B in [[grammar:bu-a-bu-b]] is the negative sibling.

## Notes
AABB adjective reduplication (高高兴兴) is a different system; see [[grammar:adj-reduplication]].

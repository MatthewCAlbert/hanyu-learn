## Pattern
[[只要]] names a condition that is enough; [[就]] marks the result that then follows: “so long as A, then B.” Unlike [[grammar:ruguo-jiu]], 只要 presents A as sufficient rather than a mere hypothetical.

## Usage
B should actually follow from A. 只要…就… does not mean A is the only possible condition; that stronger “only if” pattern uses 只有…才… at the next level.

## Notes
只要 is “as long as,” not 只 + 要 “only want.” Read it as one connector.

## Pattern
[[大概]] / [[大约]] before a number, 几 after a round number (三十几), adjacent numbers (三四天), and [[左右]] / 前后 after a quantity all mark an estimate rather than an exact count.

## Usage
Pick one marker; do not stack 大概三十左右个人. 几 in 三十几 still needs a classifier when a noun follows: 三十几个人. 左右 follows the whole quantity: 三十个左右.

## Notes
几 as “how many” in [[grammar:wh-questions]] is a question. After a number it is “and a few.”

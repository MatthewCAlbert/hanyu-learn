## Pattern
[[grammar:direction-complement]]'s 上/下/进/出/回/过/起 can combine with 来/去 into one compound complement: 走出去 "walk out," 跑上来 "run up (this way)," 走过去 "walk over (there)." The 来/去 at the end still fixes the viewpoint relative to the speaker.

## Usage
A place word can split the compound in two ways, both correct: 走出教室去 (place before 去) or 走出去教室 is not used — the place goes right after the direction word and before 来/去: V + Direction + Place + 来/去 (拿出一本书来 / 拿出来一本书 are both fine when there's no place, but a place word must sit before 来/去).

## Notes
The literal direction (physically up/out/etc.) is the meaning taught here; these same compounds also gain abstract senses (起来 "start to," 下去 "continue") at higher levels.

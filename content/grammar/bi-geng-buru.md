## Pattern
Adding [[更]] or 还 before the adjective in [[grammar:bi-comparison]] says the gap is bigger than expected: 他比我更高 "he's even taller [than you'd think]." [[不如]] flips the comparison around: *A 不如 B (+ Adj)* says A falls short of B, a plainer alternative to 没有.

## Usage
更/还 go between 比's two nouns and the adjective, never before 比 itself: 他比我更高, not *他更比我高. 不如 can stand with or without the adjective spelled out: 我的中文不如他 "my Chinese isn't as good as his" already implies "good" from context.

## Notes
更 also means "even more" without 比 at all (今天更冷了); this lesson covers only its use inside a comparison.

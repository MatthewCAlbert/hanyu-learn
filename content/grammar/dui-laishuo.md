## Pattern
对…来说 names whose standpoint the following claim uses: “for X / as far as X is concerned.” The 对 phrase is an adverbial, usually first.

## Usage
The noun after 对 is the evaluator, not the object of a verb of speaking. 对我说 is “speak to me”; 对我来说 is “in my view.” 来说 does not take its own object.

## Notes
对 in [[grammar:dui-li-wang]] introduces the target of an action. This frame introduces a viewpoint.

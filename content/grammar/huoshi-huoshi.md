## Pattern
Repeating [[或是]] before each option is a slightly more formal or written twin of [[grammar:huozhe-huozhe]]: *或是 A，或是 B*.

## Usage
Meaning matches 或者…或者…. 或是 can also join two nouns once (茶或是水). For a question, keep 还是.

## Notes
或是 is not 或 + 是 as a copula. Treat it as one connector.

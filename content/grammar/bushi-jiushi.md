## Pattern
不是 A，就是 B presents two options as covering the field: “if it isn't A, it's B.” The speaker is not asking; they are asserting an either–or situation.

## Usage
This is a statement, unlike [[grammar:haishi-choice]]. Both halves should be the same kind of predicate. 就是 here is “then it is,” not restrictive 就.

## Notes
A later 不是…而是… corrects a mistaken label. 不是…就是… does not correct; it exhausts the possibilities.

## Pattern
Two verb phrases share one subject with no conjunction: the first is motion ([[去]] / [[来]]), the second is the purpose. *去 + Place + V* is the everyday “go (somewhere) to do.”

## Usage
Keep this order. English “to” is not a separate word here. 我去买书 is fine when the place is obvious. Do not insert 和 between the verbs.

## Notes
[[grammar:lianlian-ju]] later adds sequence and manner pairs (开门出去) that are not only 去/来 + activity.

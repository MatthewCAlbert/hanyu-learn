## Pattern
[[否则]] states what would go wrong if A is not followed: "do A, or else B [an unwanted outcome]." It packs [[grammar:ruguo-jiu]]'s whole 如果不...就... into one word: 否则明天起不来 is short for 如果你不早睡，明天就起不来.

## Usage
B after 否则 should be a negative or unwanted consequence — 否则 always warns, it doesn't offer a neutral alternative. A itself is usually an instruction or recommendation (你要, 你得, 请).

## Notes
否则 opens its own clause and needs nothing else with it — no 就 or 不 required, since the negative condition is already built into the word's meaning.

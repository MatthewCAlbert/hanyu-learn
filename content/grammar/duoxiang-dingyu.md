## Pattern
Several modifiers can pile up in front of one noun in a fixed order: possessor first, then a [[grammar:measure-words]] quantity, then a longer descriptive phrase with [[grammar:de-attributive]]'s 的, then any short plain adjective right against the noun: 一条漂亮的红围巾 "a beautiful red scarf" — quantity (一条), description+的 (漂亮的), bare color adjective (红) tight against 围巾.

## Usage
A modifier built from a verb phrase (戴眼镜的老人 "the old man who wears glasses") always keeps its 的 and sits closest to the noun among the "described" modifiers, but still after any quantity: 那个戴眼镜的老人, not *那戴眼镜的个老人.

## Notes
Short, single-syllable adjectives (红, 大, 新) skip 的 and sit right next to the noun; longer or verb-based modifiers keep 的 — this is the same 的-or-not choice a single adjective already makes, just with more layers at once.

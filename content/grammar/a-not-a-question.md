## Pattern
Repeat the verb or adjective around [[不]] instead of adding [[吗]]: *V不V* or *Adj不Adj*. It asks the same yes–no question as [[grammar:ma-yes-no]], just in a different shape.

## Usage
Never combine the two question forms: 你去不去吗？ is wrong — pick one. With 有, use 有没有 rather than 有不有.

## Notes
In fast speech a two-syllable verb sometimes repeats only its first syllable before 不 (喜不喜欢), but the full form is always correct too.

## Pattern
[[就]] before a quantity or a focused element means “only / just that much.” It limits what follows rather than marking “then.”

## Usage
Stress falls on the limited piece: 就三个人, 就这本书. [[只]] is a close adverb at this level. Conditional 就 in [[grammar:ruguo-jiu]] is a different job.

## Notes
就 can also mean “as early as expected” in time sentences; that contrast with 才 is a later lesson.

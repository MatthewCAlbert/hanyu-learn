## Pattern
Slotting 得 or 不 between a verb and its [[grammar:result-complement-2]] or directional complement asks whether the result *can* happen: 看得懂 "can understand (by looking)," 看不懂 "can't understand," 做得到/做不到 "can/can't manage it."

## Usage
This is a question about capability or circumstance, distinct from [[grammar:keyi-yinggai-modals]]'s 可以/能 which sit before the verb: 我不能看这本书 says permission or context forbids it; 我看不懂这本书 says the content itself defeats me. The affirmative (得) is rarer in speech than the negative (不) — questions often use the A-not-A shape: 你看得懂看不懂？

## Notes
Not every result complement has a natural 得/不 form — this is most productive with the common set (懂, 完, 到, 住, 了(liǎo)).

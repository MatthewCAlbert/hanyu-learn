## Pattern
Reduplicating a classifier (个个, 天天, 本本) means “each / every” of that unit. It often pairs with [[都]]. Time classifiers like 天 reduplicate as a habit: 天天.

## Usage
The reduplicated classifier is the subject or an adverbial, not a counting phrase: 个个都很高兴, not *三个个. 每 + classifier is the analytic equivalent (每个学生).

## Notes
Not every classifier reduplicates freely. Learn 个个, 天天, 年年 as a set first.

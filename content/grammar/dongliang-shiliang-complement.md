## Pattern
A number-plus-word after the verb can count how many times an action happened (动量补语: 次, 遍, 回) or how long it lasted (时量补语: 年, 天, 分钟). Both sit where an object normally would: V + Quantity, or V + O + V + Quantity if the object is a full noun that needs its own slot: 我看了三遍这本书 or 我看了这本书三遍.

## Usage
去过两次中国 counts events with [[grammar:guo-experience]]'s 过; 学了两年中文 measures a stretch of time with [[grammar:le-completion]]'s 了. If the object is a pronoun (他, 它), the quantity must come first: 我找了他两次, not *我找了两次他.

## Notes
The same numeral-plus-classifier shape as ordinary [[grammar:measure-words]] — the difference is what it counts: a noun's amount there, an action's repetitions or duration here.

## Pattern
[[再]] looks forward to a repeat that has not happened yet: 再说一次, 明天再来. [[常常]] marks a habitual action: 常常去. Both sit before the verb.

## Usage
再 is a request or plan for another time; it is not “already happened again.” Frequency 常常 can also be 常. Negate habits with 不常, not 不再, unless you mean “not again.”

## Notes
又 for a past repeat is a later adverb. Mixing 再 and 又 is the usual HSK 1–2 mix-up.

## Pattern
未必 denies certainty. It means "not necessarily" or "probably not": *未必 + V / Adj*.

## Usage
It goes before the verb or adjective and can take 就 or 一定 after it. 不一定 is the everyday equivalent; 未必 is somewhat more written and a bit more doubtful.

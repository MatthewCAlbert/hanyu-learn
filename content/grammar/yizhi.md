## Pattern
The first clause gives the cause; 以致 or 以至于 introduces the result: *A，以致 B*. The result is usually bad or surprising.

## Usage
Use 以致 for results, usually undesirable. 以至于 can also introduce the extent, "so … that …", as in the second and third examples. The result is a fact, not a purpose.

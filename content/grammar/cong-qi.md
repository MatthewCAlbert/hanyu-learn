## Pattern
从…起 marks when a new stretch of time begins: *从 + time or event + 起*, then the main clause. [[从]] names the starting point; [[起]] grammaticalizes “from then on.”

## Usage
Use a time word (明天, 现在) or a short event (认识他, 开学). 从明天开始 is a close spoken variant. Do not drop 从 and keep only 起 after a date.

## Notes
This is not motion 从学校来 in [[grammar:zai-location]]. Here 从…起 frames a period, not a path.

## Pattern
[[由于]] introduces a reason in a more formal register than [[因为]]; [[因此]] or 所以 introduces the result. *由于 A，因此 B* is typical of explanations and reports.

## Usage
由于 often takes a noun phrase (由于下雨) as well as a clause. 因此 cannot open a reason the way 因为 can. In conversation, prefer [[grammar:yinwei-suoyi]].

## Notes
因此 means “for this reason.” It is not interchangeable with 因为 inside the first clause.

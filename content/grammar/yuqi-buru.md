## Pattern
与其 introduces the option being set aside; 不如 introduces the option judged better: *与其 A，不如 B*. The speaker prefers B.

## Usage
Both A and B are possible actions or descriptions. The frame 与其说 A，不如说 B means "it would be more accurate to say B than A". Compare [[grammar:bi-geng-buru]], which compares two things rather than choosing between two courses.

## Notes
宁可…也不… in [[grammar:ningke]] makes the opposite choice: it accepts a cost to avoid a worse outcome.

## Pattern
[[一点儿]]也不 emphatically denies any degree of a quality: 一点儿也不难 "not hard at all," stronger than plain 不难. With a counted noun, the same emphasis uses *一 + Classifier + Noun + 也/都 + 不/没*: 一句中文也不会 "not even one sentence of Chinese."

## Usage
也 and 都 are interchangeable in this pattern. The counted-noun version needs [[grammar:measure-words]]'s classifier for whatever is being denied entirely: 一个字都不认识 "doesn't recognize a single character."

## Notes
This is a stronger claim than plain [[grammar:bu-negation]] — 不难 just says "not hard," while 一点儿也不难 insists there is no hard part whatsoever.

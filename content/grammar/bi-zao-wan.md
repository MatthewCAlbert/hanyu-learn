## Pattern
A 比 comparison can sit on 早/晚/多/少 before a verb, then a quantity: *A 比 B + 早/晚/多/少 + V + Quantity*. This measures when or how much, not a simple adjective like 高.

## Usage
早/晚 need a time quantity (十分钟, 一天). 多/少 need a countable remainder (两个字, 三次). 他比我到得早 is the 得 form in [[grammar:bi-de-comparison]]; this pattern names the gap in numbers.

## Notes
Do not also add 更 before 早 here unless you are stacking emphasis: the quantity already measures the difference.

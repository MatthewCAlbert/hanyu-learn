## Pattern
[[似的]] follows a comparison standard and means “as if / like.” It often pairs with 像 or 跟: 像春天似的. It is a particle, not an adjective 似.

## Usage
The 的 in 似的 is part of the word; do not analyze it as attributive [[的]]. 一样 in [[grammar:gen-yiyang]] states equality; 似的 is looser resemblance.

## Notes
Pronunciation is *shìde* (or *sìde* in some regions). It is not 是的 “that's right.”

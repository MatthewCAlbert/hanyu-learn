## Pattern
Directional complements can describe an abstract state instead of motion. 下来 often settles or records a state, 下去 continues it, 起来 marks its beginning, 过来 returns to a normal or understood state, and 过去 moves away from consciousness or the current state.

## Usage
Learn the verb-complement combination as a unit: 安静下来 “calm down,” 坚持下去 “keep going,” 想起来 “recall,” 醒过来 “come to,” and 晕过去 “faint.” The literal direction helps, but does not predict every accepted combination.

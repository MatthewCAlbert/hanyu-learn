## Pattern
[[先]] marks the first step; [[再]] or [[然后]] marks the next: *先 A，再/然后 B*. 再 sits before the second verb; 然后 can open the second clause.

## Usage
Use this for planned or narrated sequence. Simultaneous actions are [[grammar:yibian-yibian]]. Immediate habit is [[grammar:yi-jiu]].

## Notes
然后 is a clause linker. 再 here means “then (next),” the same character as “again” in [[grammar:zai-changchang]].

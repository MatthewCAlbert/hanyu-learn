## Pattern
有A有B presents two related things as both being there: 有说有笑, 有高有低. It is a four-character frame, not two separate 有 sentences.

## Usage
The pair should be conventional or clearly opposite. 有高有低 means a mix, not “has height and has lowness” as two possessions.

## Notes
Contrast [[grammar:mei-a-mei-b]]. 又…又… in [[grammar:you-you]] is a clause pattern; 有A有B is a compact frame.

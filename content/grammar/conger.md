## Pattern
[[从而]] introduces a result that follows from the method or process just named: “thereby / thus.” It is formal and typical of explanations.

## Usage
The first clause should be a means, not a random event. 因而 in [[grammar:yiner-result]] is a general “therefore”; 从而 highlights that B is achieved *through* A.

## Notes
从而 is not 从 + 而 as two words to inflect. Keep it as one conjunction.

## Pattern
[[在]] locates a person or thing: *S 在 Place*, or marks where an action happens: *S 在 Place + V*. [[从]] names the starting point of motion: *从 Place + V*. Time words sit in the same adverbial slot: 我现在在家.

## Usage
这儿有书 says something exists here ([[grammar:you-existence]]); 书在这儿 says where a known book is. After 从, a motion verb is needed: 从学校来, not a bare 从学校 as a full sentence.

## Notes
This 在 is the location verb/preposition, not the progressive marker in [[grammar:zai-progressive]]. Same character; different job.

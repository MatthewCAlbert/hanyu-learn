## Pattern
A question word can make a rhetorical challenge rather than request information. 谁不知道 implies “everybody knows.” 有什么难的 implies “it is not hard.” Intonation is assertive.

## Usage
The implied answer is obvious and usually negative of the surface question. This extends [[grammar:nandao-rhetorical]] without 难道/吗. Do not answer with a literal person or place.

## Notes
Indefinite 谁 in [[grammar:yiwen-indefinite]] names someone unknown. Rhetorical 谁 names no one in particular because the point is the implication.

## Pattern
[[把]] moves a definite object in front of the verb to say what happens *to* it: *S + 把 + O + V + Other*. The verb can almost never stand alone after 把 — it needs a result ([[grammar:result-complement]]), a direction ([[grammar:direction-complement]]), or a landing place (在/到 + place), spelled out after it.

## Usage
The object of 把 must be something specific, already known to the listener — 把书 "the book," not 把一本书 "a book." Negate before 把, not before the verb: 我没把书放好, never *我把书没放好.

## Notes
把 is a tool for talking about what happened to a particular thing, not a general substitute for SVO word order — most sentences never need it at all.

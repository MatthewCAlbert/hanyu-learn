## Pattern
Open with a place, then [[有]] plus a new, usually indefinite thing: *Place + 有 + Quantity + Noun*. Place + [[是]] + Noun identifies what occupies that spot: 前边是学校.

## Usage
这儿有书 introduces something. 书在这儿 locates a known book ([[grammar:zai-location]]). 有 takes measure phrases (一本书); 是 takes a definite or unique identification.

## Notes
This is existence with 有/是. The later V着 pattern in [[grammar:cun-xian-ju]] adds how the thing is arranged.

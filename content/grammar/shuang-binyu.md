## Pattern
A handful of verbs — [[给]] "give," 送 "give as a gift," 借 "lend," 教 "teach" — take two objects in a row with no preposition: *S + V + Person + Thing*. The person comes first, the thing second.

## Usage
Do not insert 给 before the person with these verbs the way English inserts "to": 我给你一本书 already means "I give you a book," not "I give a book to you [again]." 给 only repeats when it is the preposition, with a different set of verbs (借给, 送给).

## Notes
Reversing the two objects changes the meaning, not just the emphasis: 我给你一本书 (a book, to you) is not the same claim as any reordering that puts the thing first.

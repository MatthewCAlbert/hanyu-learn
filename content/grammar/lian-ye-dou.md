## Pattern
[[连]] singles out the most surprising or extreme item and 也/都 confirms it fits the pattern anyway: "even X, too" — 连这个字也不认识 "doesn't even know this [simple] character." This is the same emphatic shape as [[grammar:yidian-ye-bu]]'s 一...也/都不..., just marked with 连 instead of a counted phrase.

## Usage
The item after 连 should be the extreme case that makes the point land — the easiest character, the shortest wait, the smallest amount — precisely because if even *that* fails, everything else does too.

## Notes
连 can front a whole clause instead of just a noun for extra emphasis in more literary registers, but the noun-phrase version here covers the everyday use.

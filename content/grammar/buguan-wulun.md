## Pattern
[[不管]] and [[无论]] both dismiss every possibility as irrelevant to the result: the clause right after them must be open-ended — a [[grammar:wh-questions]] word ([[grammar:yiwen-daici-renzhi]]-style), an [[grammar:a-not-a-question]] shape (下不下雨), or an "A or B" choice — and 都/也 always follows before the main clause.

## Usage
不管/无论 cannot introduce a single, settled fact the way 因为/如果 do — 不管明天下雨 alone is incomplete; it needs the open range (下不下雨, 怎么样, 什么时候) to make "no matter" meaningful. The two words are interchangeable; 无论 reads slightly more formal.

## Notes
This is a stronger, unconditional cousin of [[grammar:zhiyou-cai]]'s 只有...才... — 只有...才 names the one thing that must happen; 不管...都 says nothing changes the outcome at all.

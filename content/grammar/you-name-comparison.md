## Pattern
*A 有 B + 这么/那么 + Adj* says A reaches B's degree. [[这么]] points to something near; [[那么]] to something farther or just named. 没有 is the negative, extending [[grammar:mei-you-comparison]].

## Usage
这么/那么 are usual in this frame; dropping them is possible but less clear at this level. This is comparison of degree, not possession: 他有哥哥 is “he has an older brother.”

## Notes
A 有 B 这么 Adj is often a bit literary or emphatic next to plain 跟…一样 in [[grammar:gen-yiyang]].

## Pattern
拿…来说 introduces a concrete case used to support a point: “take X, for example.” It is a cousin of [[grammar:cong-laikan]], but names an illustration rather than a whole analytic angle.

## Usage
The 拿 phrase is an adverbial, usually first. 来说 does not take its own object. 就…来说 is a close variant.

## Notes
拿 is grammaticalized. There is no physical “holding” in 拿学习来说.

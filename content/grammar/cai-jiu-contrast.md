## Pattern
[[才]] before a verb marks that something happened later, or took longer, than expected: 我八点才来 "I didn't come until eight [later than you'd think]." 就 (already met in [[grammar:yi-jiu]] and [[grammar:ruguo-jiu]]) marks the opposite feeling: earlier or sooner than expected — 他六点就来了 "he already came by six."

## Usage
The same clock time can take either word depending on the speaker's attitude toward it — 才 and 就 encode expectation, not just the raw time. Neither is about truth of the time itself, only about whether it felt early or late.

## Notes
This is a different 才 from [[grammar:zhiyou-cai]]'s "only if" use; here it stands alone in a plain time sentence.

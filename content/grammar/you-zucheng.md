## Pattern
由 introduces the members or materials, and [[组成]] states that they form the whole: *Whole + 由 + Parts + 组成*. The whole is the topic even though the parts perform the composing in the underlying meaning.

## Usage
Keep the order straight: 小组由学生组成 means “the group consists of students.” Reversing whole and parts reverses the relationship. 是...的 may wrap the structure when the composition itself is being emphasized.

## Notes
This formal pattern is common in definitions, descriptions of organizations, and explanations of systems. It is close to 包括, but 包括 starts from the whole and lists what it includes.

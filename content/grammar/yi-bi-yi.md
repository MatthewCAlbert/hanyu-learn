## Pattern
一M比一M repeats a classifier around [[比]] to say each next unit exceeds the last: 一天比一天, 一个比一个. An adjective or verb phrase follows.

## Usage
This is change across a series, not a two-person 比 sentence. 越来越冷 is a close alternative without naming the unit.

## Notes
Keep the same classifier on both sides. 一天比一年 would mix units and collapse the pattern.

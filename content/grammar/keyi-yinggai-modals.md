## Pattern
Four more premodals join [[grammar:neng-yuan-modals]]: [[可以]] (permission or possibility), [[应该]] (should, ought to), [[可能]] (probably — a guess, not a permission), [[愿意]] (willing to).

## Usage
可以 asks for or grants permission: 老师，我可以进来吗？ 可能 states likelihood and often opens the sentence: 他可能出去了. 应该 gives advice or expectation and negates as 不应该. 愿意 negates as 不愿意.

## Notes
可以 also covers ground that 能 covers in casual speech, but keep it to permission/possibility here so it stays distinct from 能/会.

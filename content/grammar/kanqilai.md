## Pattern
[[看起来]] and [[看上去]] both mean "it looks/seems like," softening a plain [[grammar:hen-degree]] statement into a visual impression rather than a fact: 这个菜好吃 states it is tasty; 这个菜看起来好吃 only claims it looks that way.

## Usage
The two are interchangeable in most everyday sentences. Either can also front a whole clause rather than just an adjective: 他看起来不太高兴 "he doesn't look very happy."

## Notes
起来 and 上去 here are fixed with 看 and do not carry their separate directional-complement meanings from [[grammar:complex-direction-complement]].

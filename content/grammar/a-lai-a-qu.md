## Pattern
Put the same verb on both sides of 来...去 to show an action repeated in different directions or over and over: 想来想去, 看来看去. Repeating *A着* as A着A着 instead presents an action already under way before a new event or state appears.

## Usage
A来A去 often suggests searching, hesitation, or restless repetition rather than one completed action. A着A着 normally opens the first clause; the second clause tells what unexpectedly happened while that action continued.

## Notes
These are fixed four-character frames, not literal movement in every use. In 想来想去, 来 and 去 do not mean that thoughts physically come and go.

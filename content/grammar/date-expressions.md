## Pattern
Dates run from large unit to small: *year 年 + month 月 + day*. Weekdays are [[星期]] + 一…日/天. These phrases often stand as noun predicates: 今天星期一.

## Usage
Ask 几月几号 or 星期几. 日 and 号 both mark calendar days; 号 is common in speech. 星期日 and 星期天 are both Sunday.

## Notes
Ordinal 第 is not required for weekdays. 第一天 is “the first day,” not Monday.

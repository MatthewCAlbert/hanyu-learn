## Pattern
又…又… stacks two similar predicates for the same subject: two adjectives, or two verb phrases. It means “both A and B,” not “again.”

## Usage
Keep A and B parallel: 又大又干净, not a mix of a noun and a verb. For extra actions by the same person, 又…又… is stronger than a single [[还]].

## Notes
Single 又 still means “again (already happened)” in [[grammar:shijian-pinlv-fu]]. The doubled frame is a different job.

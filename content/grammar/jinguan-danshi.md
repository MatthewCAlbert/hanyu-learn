## Pattern
[[尽管]] concedes A; 但是/可是 (or 还是) insists B still holds. It is a stronger, often more written counterpart of [[grammar:suiran-danshi]].

## Usage
尽管 already means “even though”; you can still keep 但是. 尽管 also has a separate “feel free to” sense (尽管问); this lesson is only the concessive clause.

## Notes
哪怕/即使 in nearby lessons are more hypothetical. 尽管 often treats A as a real, already-known situation.

## Pattern
*A 跟 B 一样* states that A and B match; adding an adjective after 一样 says exactly what matches: 他跟我一样高 "he's as tall as me." This sits beside [[grammar:bi-comparison]] as the "equal" counterpart to 比's "greater than."

## Usage
Negate with 不一样 for "different," not with 不跟: 他的想法跟我不一样. Without an adjective, 一样 alone just means "the same" (in general): 这两本书一样.

## Notes
和 can substitute for 跟 here in more formal registers; they work identically in this pattern.

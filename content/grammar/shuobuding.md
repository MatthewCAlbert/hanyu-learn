## Pattern
[[说不定]] hedges a guess: “maybe / you never know.” It usually opens the clause. It is not a request to “say it less definitely.”

## Usage
Close to 可能, but 说不定 feels more spoken and leaves more room for surprise. Do not negate it as 说不一定.

## Notes
Literal 说 + 不定 is not how to parse it here. Treat 说不定 as one adverb.

## Pattern
便 links a situation or completed step to the result that promptly follows: A, then B. In this use it is close to 就, but is more compact and more common in narration or formal writing.

## Usage
Place 便 before the verb phrase in the second clause. It presents B as a natural or immediate consequence of A; it does not by itself mean that B happened reluctantly.

## Notes
The pronunciation here is *biàn*. Do not confuse it with 便宜 *piányi*. Compare [[grammar:yi-jiu]], where 一...就... explicitly packages the trigger.

## Pattern
在…[[方面]] names the domain a comment applies to: “in the area of X / with respect to X.” It is an adverbial, often first or after the subject.

## Usage
方面 takes an abstract field (工作, 学习, 生活), not a physical place. 在学校 is location; 在学习方面 is a topic domain.

## Notes
一方面…另一方面… in [[grammar:yifangmian]] contrasts two arguments. A single 在…方面 is just a scope marker.

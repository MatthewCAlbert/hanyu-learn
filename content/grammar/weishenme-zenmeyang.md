## Pattern
[[为什么]] asks for a reason and stays in the statement slot for “why.” [[怎么样]] / 怎样 ask for manner, quality, or an opinion. Word order still does not invert.

## Usage
Do not add 吗. 怎么样 after a noun is often “how is it / what do you think”: 今天天气怎么样？ 怎么 in [[grammar:wh-questions]] is more “how (do you do it).”

## Notes
什么时候 belongs with this same in-situ question set: 你什么时候来？

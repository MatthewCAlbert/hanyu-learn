## Pattern
[[的]] marks a modifier in front of a noun: *A 的 B* is “A’s B” or “the B that is A.” Pronouns (我, 你, 他) plus 的 are possessives. Longer modifiers work the same way once more vocabulary arrives.

## Usage
的 is required with most noun modifiers at this level. Close kinship and a few set phrases can drop it later (我妈); do not drop it yet in 我的老师 or 你的书. 的 does not make a yes–no question; that is [[grammar:ma-yes-no]].

## Notes
This 的 is the attributive particle (de), not a full verb. Other 的/得/地 distinctions wait until later levels.

## Pattern
Prices are noun predicates: *Number + [[块]] / [[元]]*. [[块]] is the spoken unit; [[元]] is more formal writing. Ask with [[多少]]钱. A number cannot sit on 钱 without a unit in the answer: 十块, not 十钱.

## Usage
两块, not 二块, before the classifier. Smaller units exist later; at this level 块/元 covers classroom prices.

## Notes
[[块]] is also a general classifier for chunks. After a number in a price it is the yuan unit.

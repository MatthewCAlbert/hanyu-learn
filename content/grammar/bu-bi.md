## Pattern
*A 不比 B + Adj* denies that A exceeds B. It is weaker than [[grammar:mei-you-comparison]]: 没有…高 means A is shorter; 不比…高 means A is not taller — they could be the same height.

## Usage
Use 不比 to reject an assumed gap. Use 没有 when A clearly falls short. Keep 很 out of both.

## Notes
不比 is not 比不上, a later potential-style comparison.

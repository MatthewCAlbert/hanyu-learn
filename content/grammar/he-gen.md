## Pattern
[[和]] and [[跟]] join noun phrases: *A 和 B*, *A 跟 B*. 和 is the default “and” for things and people. 跟 often adds a “with” reading before an action: 跟朋友去.

## Usage
Do not use 和 to join full clauses the way English “and” does: 我吃饭和我喝水 is unnatural; use two clauses or [[grammar:hai-ye-additive]]. 跟 can also introduce the other party of 说/学: 跟老师说.

## Notes
跟 as a verb still means “to follow.” This lesson covers the linker / preposition.

## Pattern
[[一般来说]] marks a general rule. [[看来]] marks an inference from what the speaker can see or has just learned. Both are sentence adverbs, usually first.

## Usage
一般来说 introduces a habit or typical case, not a one-off. 看来 is closer to “apparently.” Neither takes 吗.

## Notes
从…来看 names an explicit basis. [[grammar:zai-kanlai]] names whose view it is. 看来 often leaves the basis unspoken.

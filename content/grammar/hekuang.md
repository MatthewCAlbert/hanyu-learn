## Pattern
The first clause gives an extreme or easy case that already holds; 何况 then adds a harder or more obvious case: *A 都 …，何况 B (呢)*. B is even more clearly so.

## Usage
The first clause often has 都, 连…都 or 尚且. The ending 呢 is common. Both clauses share the same predicate in sense, so the second one is usually short.

## Notes
A rhetorical force: "if even A, then of course B". See [[grammar:lian-ye-dou]] for the 连…都 half.

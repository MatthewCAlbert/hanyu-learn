## Pattern
The same 上/出/起/下 from [[grammar:complex-direction-complement]] stop meaning literal direction here and instead mark an abstract result: 说出 "get [something] said/expressed" (not "speak outward"), 想起 "recall" (not "think upward"), 关上 "shut" (上 = closed, not "up"), 留下 "leave behind" (下 = staying in place, not "down").

## Usage
There is no physical up/out/down happening in any of these — the character is doing the same job an English particle verb does ("figure *out*," "look *up*"). Learn each V+Direction pairing as its own vocabulary item rather than composing the meaning from the parts.

## Notes
The same complement can be literal or abstract depending on the verb it attaches to — 拿出 (literal: take out) vs 说出 (abstract: get out/express) share the shape but not the kind of meaning.

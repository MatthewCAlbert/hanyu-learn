## Pattern
*不是 A，而是 B* rejects A and replaces it with the real answer B: "not A, but rather B." Unlike English "not A but B," 而是 cannot be shortened to just 而 in speech — the 是 stays.

## Usage
This directly corrects something the listener might assume, so it often follows a wrong guess or a previous statement: 我不是不想去，而是没有时间 pushes back on the idea that unwillingness is the reason. Compare [[grammar:suiran-danshi]]'s 但是, which concedes a point rather than replacing it.

## Notes
The two halves must be genuinely mutually exclusive — 不是 A 而是 B works because A and B can't both be true; it isn't used for a simple contrast between two things that could coexist.

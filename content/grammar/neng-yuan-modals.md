## Pattern
A modal verb sits directly before the main verb: [[会]] (a learned ability), [[能]] (able to, or permitted to, given the circumstances), [[想]] (want to, a desire), [[要]] (want to / will, a stronger intent). All four negate with 不 right before the modal: 我不会, 我不想去.

## Usage
会 vs 能: 我会游泳 "I know how to swim" (skill) vs 我今天不能游泳 "I can't swim today" (the pool's shut, say) — the two are not interchangeable. 想 is softer than 要: 我想喝水 "I'd like some water" vs 我要喝水 "I want water."

## Notes
All four also work as ordinary full verbs elsewhere (会 "to meet", 要 "to want [a thing]", 想 "to think of/miss"); this lesson covers only the premodal-before-a-verb use.

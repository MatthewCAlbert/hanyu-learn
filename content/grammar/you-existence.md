## Pattern
[[有]] covers both “have” (possession) and “there is” (existence). *A 有 B* means A possesses B, or B exists in/at A. It is not interchangeable with [[是]]: 我是老师 names what I am; 我有书 says what I have.

## Usage
Negate with 没, not 不: 我没有书. Location often uses 在 for “be at” and 有 for “there is”: 书在这儿 vs 这儿有书. Yes–no: 你有书吗？

## Notes
有 is a verb. English “there is/are” has no extra subject pronoun in the Chinese pattern.

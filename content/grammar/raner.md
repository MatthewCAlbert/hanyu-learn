## Pattern
[[然而]] contrasts B against A the same way [[grammar:suiran-danshi]]'s 但是 does, but reads more formal and literary — common in writing, speeches, and news, less so in casual chat.

## Usage
然而 does not pair with 虽然 the way 但是 does; it stands alone at the start of the contrasting clause, closer in feel to written English "however" than spoken "but."

## Notes
可是/但是 remain the everyday spoken defaults; reach for 然而 mainly in writing or a more formal register.

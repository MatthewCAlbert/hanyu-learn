## Pattern
[[一边]]…一边… links two actions happening at the same time. Both halves are verb phrases with the same subject.

## Usage
The two actions should be compatible in real time. Sequence (first A, then B) is [[grammar:xian-mashang]], not this frame. 一边 can be shortened to 边 in fast speech; keep the full form at this level.

## Notes
This is a coordinate pattern with a conjunction pair, unlike the unlinked serial verbs in [[grammar:lianlian-ju-1]].

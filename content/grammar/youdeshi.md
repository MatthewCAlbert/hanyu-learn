## Pattern
[[有的是]] means there is no shortage: “have plenty of.” It is a frozen phrase, not 有的 + 是.

## Usage
The thing in plentiful supply is the topic or the object: 时间有的是; 我们有的是办法. It often reassures or warns that quantity is not the problem.

## Notes
有的 as “some (of them)” in 有的人喜欢 is a different 有的. Do not split 有的是 with 的 as a modifier.

## Pattern
大不了 introduces the worst case and plays it down: *大不了 + (就) + V*. The speaker shows that the worst case is acceptable.

## Usage
It is colloquial and optimistic. The worst case follows, often with 就 or 再. Do not confuse it with 了不起 "remarkable", although both contain 了 and 不.

## Notes
A close cousin of [[grammar:napa]] ("even if…"), but 大不了 states a concrete fallback.

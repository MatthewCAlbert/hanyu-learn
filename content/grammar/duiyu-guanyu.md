## Pattern
[[对于]] and [[关于]] introduce a topic. 对于 often frames an attitude or action toward that topic. 关于 often labels content: a book, talk, or report about X.

## Usage
对于这个问题，我认为… is a stance. 关于中国的电影 is subject matter. They overlap, but 关于 is more “about (content)” and 对于 more “with respect to (evaluation).”

## Notes
对 without 于 remains the preposition in [[grammar:dui-li-wang]]. 对于 is the formal topic marker.

## Pattern
[[甚至]] tacks on a more extreme example to prove a claim already made: A states the general case, and 甚至 B pushes it to a surprising limit — "so much so that even B." It often combines with [[grammar:yidian-ye-bu]]'s 连...也/都 for the strongest version: 甚至连...也不....

## Usage
The B clause should genuinely be more extreme than A, not just another example — 他什么都不会，甚至连最简单的字也不认识 works because "not recognizing the simplest character" is a stronger claim than plain "can't do anything."

## Notes
甚至 can also sit mid-clause before just a phrase rather than a whole sentence: 甚至孩子都知道 "even a child knows [this]."

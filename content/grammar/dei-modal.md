## Pattern
[[得]] read *děi* is a modal meaning “must / have to.” It sits before the verb like 应该. This is not complement 得 *de* in [[grammar:de-complement]] or verb 得 *dé* “to get.”

## Usage
Negate as 不用 or 不必, not 不得, in this sense. 得 is stronger and more urgent than 应该. 该…了 in [[grammar:kuaiyao-le]] overlaps in “it's time to.”

## Notes
Tone and position tell the three 得 apart: *děi* before a verb, *de* after a verb before a description, *dé* as a full verb.

## Pattern
越 A，越 B pairs two scales: as A increases, B changes with it. A and B are adjectives or verb phrases. This is not the frozen [[越来越]] of [[grammar:yue-lai-yue]].

## Usage
Both halves need 越. 越来越好 is one growing quality; 越学越好 is “the more you study, the better.” Do not insert 很 after the second 越.

## Notes
The subject can appear once, before the first 越, or in both clauses when it changes.

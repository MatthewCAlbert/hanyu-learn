## Pattern
反正 says that whatever the circumstance, the result holds: *反正 + Claim*.

## Usage
It can come before or after the subject. The earlier clause often contains a choice or condition, and 反正 shows the speaker is not changed by it. The tone is casual and sometimes careless.

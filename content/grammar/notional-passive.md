## Pattern
A definite patient can take a result or 了 with no [[被]] and no agent: 饭做好了, 信寄走了. Chinese treats this as a topic-comment “notional passive”: the thing is what the sentence is about, and the verb describes what happened to it.

## Usage
The object must be specific and the verb must be telic (done, ready, broken). 饭吃 is incomplete; 饭吃完了 works. Use [[grammar:bei-passive]] when you need to name or emphasize an agent, or when the event is unfortunate.

## Notes
This is not a missing 被. Adding 被 (饭被做好了) is often unnecessary or odd for routine results.

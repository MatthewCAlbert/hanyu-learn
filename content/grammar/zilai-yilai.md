## Pattern
[[以来]] follows a starting time or event and covers the stretch up to now: *Event + 以来*. [[自从]] or 自/从 can open the phrase: 自从开学以来.

## Usage
The main clause is typically a state that has held through that period, often with 了 or 都. 从明天起 looks forward; 以来 looks back from now.

## Notes
以来 is not 以后. 以后 can be any later time, including the future. 以来 is always “from then until now.”

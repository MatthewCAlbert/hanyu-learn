## Pattern
再也 + 不/没 + V means the action will not (or did not) happen even one more time: “never again.” 了 often closes a present/future decision (再也不…了). 没 marks a past stretch with no repeat.

## Usage
再 alone can mean “again (next time).” 再也不 is an emphatic refusal or closed chapter. 也 here reinforces the total ban; it is not “also.”

## Notes
This is not [[grammar:zai-ye-unconditional]] 再难也要…. There 再 means “no matter how”; here 再也 means “not ever again.”

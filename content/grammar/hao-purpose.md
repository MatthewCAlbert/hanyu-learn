## Pattern
A comma plus [[好]] plus a verb phrase states the intended next step: “so that (we) can….” The first clause is what you do; 好 introduces why.

## Usage
This 好 is not “good” as a predicate. It is a spoken purpose linker, lighter than [[为了]]. The subject of the 好 clause is often the same as the first clause, or a 让/能 is added.

## Notes
把 in the second example is [[grammar:ba-sentence]]. 好让 is a common expansion.

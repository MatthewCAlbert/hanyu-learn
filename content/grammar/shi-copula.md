## Pattern
[[是]] links two noun phrases: *A 是 B* means “A is B.” Both sides are typically people, places, or things already known at this level — [[老师]], [[学生]], [[中国]]. It is not used with bare adjectives; those take [[grammar:hen-degree]].

## Usage
Use 是 to identify or classify. Negate with [[不]] before 是: 我不是老师. Yes–no questions add [[吗]] after the clause — see [[grammar:ma-yes-no]]. Do not say *我是好* for “I am good”; that is 我很好.

## Notes
是 is a verb here, not a pause filler. English “am/is/are” maps onto it only in this equative use.

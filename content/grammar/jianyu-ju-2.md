## Pattern
[[grammar:jianyu-ju]]'s shared-word shape covers two more everyday jobs: praising or blaming someone for an action (表扬/批评 + Person + V — 表扬他帮助同学 "praised him for helping"), and naming or electing someone into a role (叫/选/称 + Person + 做/当/为 + Role — 选他当班长 "elected him monitor").

## Usage
In the praise/blame version, the second verb explains *what* the person did that earned the reaction: 批评我不整理房间 "criticized me for not tidying my room." In the naming version, 当 fits a role someone takes on (当班长, 当老师); 做 and 为 work in more formal or written registers for the same idea.

## Notes
The shared word plays the same double role as in [[grammar:jianyu-ju]] — it never repeats.

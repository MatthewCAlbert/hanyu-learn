## Pattern
[[难道]]…吗 and 不是…吗 are questions in form but assertions in force. 难道 challenges an unexpected situation. 不是…吗 invites agreement that the claim is obvious.

## Usage
These are not information questions. A calm 你知道吗？ asks; 难道你不知道吗？ rebukes or expresses surprise. Answer by affirming the implied point, not by treating it as a fresh 吗 question.

## Notes
Dropping 吗 after 难道 is possible in speech. Keep 吗 at this level.

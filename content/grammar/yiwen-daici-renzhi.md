## Pattern
Followed by 都 (or 也), a [[grammar:wh-questions]] word stops asking and instead means "any/every": 谁都 "everyone/anyone," 什么都 "everything/anything," 哪儿都 "everywhere/anywhere." No question mark, no rising intonation — it is a plain statement.

## Usage
With a negative verb the same shape means total exclusion: 我哪儿都没去过 "I haven't been anywhere." 也 is more common than 都 in a negative sentence; either works with a positive one.

## Notes
Word order stays exactly like a question (谁都, not *都谁) — only 都/也 afterward changes the reading from "who?" to "everyone."

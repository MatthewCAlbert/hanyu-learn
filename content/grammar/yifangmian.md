## Pattern
[[一方面]]…[[另一方面]]… lays out two sides of one situation, often a tension. Both halves are full clauses.

## Usage
This is more analytical than 又…又…. It is common in giving a balanced opinion. 另一方面 can appear without repeating 一方面 when the first side is already clear.

## Notes
一方面 is not the same as 一边. 一边…一边… is simultaneous action; this frame is two arguments.

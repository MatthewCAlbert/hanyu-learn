## Pattern
从...来看 names the evidence, angle, or point of view used to reach the following conclusion: “judging from...” or “viewed from....” The basis goes between 从 and 来看; the assessment normally follows after a pause.

## Usage
Use a noun phrase such as 从结果来看 or 从他的表现来看. Do not confuse the frame with sentence-initial [[看来]], which gives an inference without explicitly naming its basis.

## Notes
来看 is grammaticalized here: it introduces an analytical viewpoint rather than a literal act of coming over to look.

## Pattern
[[除了]] names an item, and the rest of the sentence says whether it's included or excluded: with 还/也 ([[grammar:hai-ye-additive]]), A is added to what B already covers ("besides A, also B"); with 都, A is carved out as the exception ("except A, all B"). [[以外]] can optionally follow the excepted item for extra clarity: 除了他以外.

## Usage
还/也 signals addition — the sentence's main subject does more than just A. 都 signals exclusion — everyone/everything else shares B, only A doesn't. Reading the second half tells you which sense is meant, since 除了 alone is ambiguous between them.

## Notes
以外 is genuinely optional and often dropped in speech once 除了 has made the boundary clear.

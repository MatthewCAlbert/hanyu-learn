## Pattern
说A就A means the named action or change follows at once, with little gap between “saying” (or the first sign) and doing: 说走就走, 说停就停.

## Usage
A is typically a short verb. The tone can be admiration, complaint, or simple vividness. It is tighter than a plain [[grammar:yi-jiu]] 一…就….

## Notes
说 here is grammaticalized. In 天说变就变 there is no speaker announcing anything.

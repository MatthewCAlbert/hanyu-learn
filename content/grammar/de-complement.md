## Pattern
[[得]] after a verb introduces a description of how the action goes: *V+得+Adj*. It looks like [[的]] and [[grammar:de-attributive]] but is a different character with a different job — 得 links a verb to a description, not a noun to its modifier.

## Usage
If the verb has an object, repeat the verb before 得: 他说中文说得很好, not *他说中文得很好. The adjective after 得 usually still carries 很, the same as [[grammar:hen-degree]].

## Notes
Negate the description, not the verb: 他说得不好 "he doesn't speak well," not *他不说得好.

## Pattern
Sentence-final [[吧]] turns a statement into a suggestion, mild command, or tentative claim: 我们去吧; 好吧. It is not a yes–no marker; that is [[grammar:ma-yes-no]].

## Usage
With 我们, 吧 often means “let's.” With 你, it softens an instruction. A guessing 吧 (他是老师吧) waits until later confirmation questions.

## Notes
The same character is also the loanword “bar” (酒吧) with a different reading. Here it is the unstressed particle *ba*.

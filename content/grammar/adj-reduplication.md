## Pattern
Many adjectives can double: monosyllables as AA, disyllables as AABB. The reduplicated form often takes 的 (or 地 before a verb) and sounds vivid or mild rather than a plain 很 + Adj.

## Usage
高高兴兴地走, 大大的眼睛. Do not also add 很. Not every adjective reduplicates; learn the common ones with the word.

## Notes
Verb reduplication in [[grammar:verb-reduplication]] is a different system (看看, 休息休息). Keep the two apart.

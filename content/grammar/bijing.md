## Pattern
毕竟 reminds the listener of a basic truth that explains or justifies a claim: *毕竟 + Claim*.

## Usage
It goes before the verb or at the head of the clause. The speaker presents the point as something everyone should recognise. It sets up a reason without using 因为.

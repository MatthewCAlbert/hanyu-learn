## Pattern
偏偏 places an event against expectation or someone's wishes, or shows stubborn insistence: *偏偏 + V*.

## Usage
In the first reading something unlucky happens just when it matters. In the second the subject stubbornly does the opposite of what was asked. Context tells you which.

## Notes
Compare 偏 on its own, which is a shorter form of the same adverb, as in 偏要.

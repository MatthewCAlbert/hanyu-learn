## Pattern
[[以及]] joins the last item in a list, a more formal “and” than [[和]]. It typically appears once, before the final noun phrase.

## Usage
Use 以及 in written or careful lists of three or more. 和 remains the default for two items. 以及 does not join clauses the way 并且 does.

## Notes
以及 is not 及其 (later). Keep it to noun (and noun-like) lists at this level.

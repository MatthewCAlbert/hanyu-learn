## Pattern
*A 没有 B + Adj* says A falls short of B on that scale: “A is not as Adj as B.” It is the usual negative counterpart of [[grammar:bi-comparison]], not a word-for-word 不比.

## Usage
The adjective is required in the full pattern: 我没有他高. 不比 A + Adj often means “not necessarily more…,” which is a different claim. Keep 很 out of both 比 and 没有 comparisons.

## Notes
没有 here is the comparison verb, not simply “do not possess.” 我没有书 is possession; 我没有他高 is height.

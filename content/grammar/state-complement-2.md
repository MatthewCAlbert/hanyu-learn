## Pattern
After V/Adj + 得, the complement can be a whole verb phrase or subject-predicate phrase, not just one adjective. It describes the resulting degree or state: 忙得没时间吃饭; 笑得大家都停不下来.

## Usage
Everything after 得 belongs to the complement and explains how far the first state goes. If the complement has its own subject, state it there: 急得我不知道怎么办. Do not insert 很 directly after 得 before a full clause.

## Notes
This expands [[grammar:de-complement]]. The longer complement often sounds like a consequence in English, but grammatically it still describes the degree or state produced by the predicate.

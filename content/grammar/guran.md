## Pattern
固然 concedes a point as true; the clause after 但是, 可是 or 也 turns to the speaker's real point: *A 固然 …，但是 B*.

## Usage
The conceded point comes first and is accepted, not rejected. It is milder than 尽管. The turn can be signalled by 但是, 可是 or just 也.

See [[grammar:jinguan-danshi]] for the concessive 尽管.

## Pattern
An advanced 把 sentence may end in a brief 一V action, or let the verb take a second object after the affected object: 把问题想一想; 把情况告诉大家. O1 is still the thing being disposed of, while O2 completes the verb's relationship.

## Usage
Only verbs that can naturally take the second element allow the double-object form: 告诉某人某事, 称某物某名. Do not assume every transitive verb can simply add another noun after it.

## Notes
This extends [[grammar:ba-sentence]] and [[grammar:ba-sentence-2]]. The verb is still not left bare: reduplication, 一V, aspect, a complement, or another object makes the handling of O specific.

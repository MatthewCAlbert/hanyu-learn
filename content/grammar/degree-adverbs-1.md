## Pattern
These sit before an adjective like [[很]] in [[grammar:hen-degree]], but they are real intensifiers: [[太]] “too / extremely,” [[非常]] “very,” [[真]] “really,” [[最]] “the most.” 太 often takes sentence-final 了: 太好了.

## Usage
太…了 can praise (太好了) or complain (太贵了). 最 needs a set to be the top of: 最好的老师. 真 is vivid and spoken; 非常 is a plain high degree.

## Notes
Do not stack these with 很 (很非常好 is wrong). In a [[grammar:bi-comparison]] sentence, leave them out: 他比我高, not 他比我最高.

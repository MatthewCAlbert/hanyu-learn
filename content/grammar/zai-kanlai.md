## Pattern
在…看来 names whose judgment the following claim expresses: “in X's view.” The person sits between 在 and [[看来]].

## Usage
Sentence-initial 看来 in [[grammar:yiban-laishuo]] leaves the judge unspoken. 从…来看 in [[grammar:cong-laikan]] names evidence, not a person. 在我看来 is a stance; 从结果来看 is an angle on facts.

## Notes
并 in 并不难 is a later emphatic 并. The frame itself does not require it.

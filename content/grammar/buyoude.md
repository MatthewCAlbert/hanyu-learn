## Pattern
不由得 and 不禁 describe an involuntary reaction caused by what was just seen, heard or remembered: *不由得 + V*.

## Usage
The cause is usually stated first. The subject cannot control the reaction. 不禁 is somewhat more written. The reaction is usually an emotion or a physical response.

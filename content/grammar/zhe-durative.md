## Pattern
V+[[着]] marks a state or posture held in place, not a moment happening right now like [[grammar:zai-progressive]]. 开着 is "is [in the state of being] open"; 穿着 is "is wearing."

## Usage
Negate with 没(有)+V+着: 门没开着. Two clauses can chain, the first V着 setting the posture the second action happens in: 他站着说话 "he talks while standing."

## Notes
着 and 在 can combine for an action happening while a posture is held: 他躺着看电视 "he's lying down watching TV."

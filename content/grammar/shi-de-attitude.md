## Pattern
The same 是...的 frame from [[grammar:shi-de-emphasis]] can wrap a judgment instead of a past event: *S + 是 + Adj/Modal + V + 的* states the speaker's firm opinion or evaluation, not a fact about when/where/how something happened.

## Usage
Dropping 是...的 here (这个问题可以解决) is still grammatical but sounds like a plain, neutral statement; adding 是...的 makes it read as the speaker's considered stance — closer to "I'm telling you, this can be solved."

## Notes
Context, not grammar, tells the two 是...的 uses apart: a time/place/manner phrase inside signals [[grammar:shi-de-emphasis]]'s "which one," while a bare adjective or modal signals this lesson's "I mean it."

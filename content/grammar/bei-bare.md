## Pattern
[[被]] can drop the agent: *Patient + 被 + V + Other*. The verb still needs a result or other complement, as in [[grammar:bei-passive]]. 叫/让 still need an agent.

## Usage
Use agentless 被 when the doer is unknown, obvious, or unimportant. 手机被弄坏了 does not say who did it. Adding 他 restores the HSK 3 full pattern.

## Notes
A topic-comment sentence without 被 (书拿走了) can sound similar in speech; 被 makes the passive relation explicit.

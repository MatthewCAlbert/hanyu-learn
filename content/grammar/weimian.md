## Pattern
未免 gently criticises a statement or action as going too far: *未免 + 太/有点 + Adj*.

## Usage
It carries judgment, usually with 太, 有点 or 过于. The tone is mild; the speaker is polite but critical. It differs from 不免, which means "inevitably" ([[grammar:nanmian]]).

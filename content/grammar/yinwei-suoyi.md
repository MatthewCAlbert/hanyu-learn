## Pattern
[[因为]] introduces the reason, [[所以]] introduces the result: *因为 A，所以 B*, matching English "because...so." Either clause can come first in speech, but both connectors together is the neutral, textbook order.

## Usage
Unlike English, Chinese is comfortable keeping both 因为 and 所以 in the same sentence — it is not redundant the way "because...so" can feel in English. Either word alone is also fine once the relationship is clear from context: 很累，所以我不想做饭.

## Notes
因为 can also introduce just a reason phrase without 所以 as an answer to "why": 你为什么没来？因为我病了。

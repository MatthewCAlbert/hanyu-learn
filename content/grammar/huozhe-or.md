## Pattern
[[或者]] joins options in a statement: “A or B.” It does not turn the sentence into a question. Asking the listener to pick is [[grammar:haishi-choice]]'s 还是.

## Usage
Use 或者 when both options are possible and no choice is being demanded. 还是 inside a 吗-free question expects an answer. Do not mix them in one slot.

## Notes
或 is a shorter written variant. Keep 或者 at this level.

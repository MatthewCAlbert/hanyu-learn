## Pattern
以 A 为 B takes A as B: *以 A 为 B*. B is a role, standard or result: 主, 准, 乐, 荣, 生, 耻.

## Usage
Fixed verb-like phrases are common: 以…为主 "mainly", 以…为准 "subject to", 以…为荣 "take pride in", 以…为生 "make a living from". 为 here is *wéi* "to be".

## Pattern
无非 limits the claim to the smallest possible scope: *无非 + (是) + Claim*. It says "nothing but", often with a dismissive tone.

## Usage
It comes before the predicate. It frequently pairs with 就是: 无非就是… "it is nothing but…". It suggests the speaker thinks the matter is not important.

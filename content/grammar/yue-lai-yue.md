## Pattern
[[越来越]] sits before an adjective or a small set of verbs and marks a change that keeps growing: “more and more.” Sentence-final 了 often tags the new state.

## Usage
No 比 phrase is needed; the comparison is with an earlier time, not with another person. 越来越贵, 越来越好 are typical. Do not insert 很: 越来越很冷 is wrong.

## Notes
The correlated frame 越…越… is [[grammar:yue-yue]]. This lesson is only the frozen 越来越.

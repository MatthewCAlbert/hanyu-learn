## Pattern
[[除非]] names the indispensable condition. With [[才]], B happens only if A is met — close to [[grammar:zhiyou-cai]] from the opposite direction. With [[否则]] / [[不然]], B is what follows if A is *not* met: “unless A, otherwise B.”

## Usage
Do not mix 才 and 否则 in one pair. 除非你去，我才去 vs 除非你去，否则我不去. 除非 is stronger than 如果.

## Notes
除非 is “unless / only if,” not 除了 “besides / except” in [[grammar:chuleyiwai]].

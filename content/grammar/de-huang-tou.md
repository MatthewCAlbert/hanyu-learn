## Pattern
Beside [[grammar:de-budeliao-degree]], HSK 5 adds 得慌, 得厉害, and verb/adjective + 坏了 / 透了 as spoken extremes: 累得慌, 饿坏了, 坏透了.

## Usage
得慌 often marks an uncomfortable inner state (饿, 累). 坏了 after a feeling verb is hyperbolic (想坏了). 透了 means thoroughly, often negative. These are too colloquial for a careful report.

## Notes
慌 as a full verb “to panic” is different. After 得 it is a degree chunk, not a new action.

## Pattern
[[没]] / [[没有]] negate possession, existence, and completed actions: 没有书; 没去. [[不]] in [[grammar:bu-negation]] is the default for habits, futures, and copular 是. Do not write 不有.

## Usage
With [[有]], the full form 没有 is normal. Before most other verbs, 没 is enough: 没看, 没吃. For “not yet,” 没(有)…呢 is common: 他还没来呢.

## Notes
没 and 不 are not interchangeable. 他不看电视 is a habit; 他没看电视 reports that a particular watching did not happen.

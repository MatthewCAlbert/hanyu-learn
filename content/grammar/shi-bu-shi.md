## Pattern
[[是不是]] asks the listener to confirm a whole proposition. It is a ready-made A-not-A of [[是]], often placed after the subject: 他是不是老师？

## Usage
Answer 是 or 不是, not a full echo unless you want to. Compared with [[grammar:ma-yes-no]], 是不是 pushes a bit more for confirmation. Do not add 吗 after 是不是.

## Notes
是不是 can also sit at the end in speech (他老师，是不是？). Keep the mid-clause form at this level.

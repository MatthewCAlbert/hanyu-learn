## Pattern
之所以 marks the result or fact; 是因为 marks the cause: *之所以 A，是因为 B*. It answers "why is A the case?".

## Usage
The word order is the reverse of 因为 B，所以 A. Do not mix 之所以 with 所以 or 因为 in the first clause; 之所以 already does that job. The 是 before 因为 can be omitted in speech.

## Pattern
[[假如]] and [[万一]] both work like [[grammar:ruguo-jiu]]'s 如果, opening a hypothetical condition, with 就 optionally marking the result. They differ in flavor: 假如 is a neutral "if," often more formal or literary; 万一 specifically flags a small-chance, unwanted possibility — "just in case, on the off chance that."

## Usage
万一 fits worries and contingency plans (万一下雨, 万一没有人来), where 如果/假如 would sound oddly neutral about a bad outcome. 假如 is otherwise freely interchangeable with 如果 in ordinary hypotheticals.

## Notes
就 can be dropped from either pattern once the two clauses are short enough that the link is obvious, exactly as with 如果...就.

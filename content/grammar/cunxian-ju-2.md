## Pattern
[[grammar:cun-xian-ju]]'s "place first" shape extends to something newly appearing or disappearing, using a directional or result complement instead of 着: *Place + V + Complement + 了 + Quantity + Noun*. 前边开来一辆车 reports a car showing up; the same shape with 走/搬/调 and 走 as the complement reports something leaving.

## Usage
The new or missing person/thing stays indefinite and comes last, just like in [[grammar:cun-xian-ju]] — 一辆车, 一位老师, not a name or "the." This is how Chinese narrates something entering or leaving a scene without first mentioning it as the subject.

## Notes
The complement carries the real information: 开来 (arriving), 走出来 (emerging), 走了/搬走了 (leaving) — the place and the verb alone don't say which.

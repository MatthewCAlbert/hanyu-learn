## Pattern
V+[[过]] marks that an action has been experienced at least once, with no fixed link to when. Contrast with [[grammar:le-completion]]'s 了, which marks one specific completed event.

## Usage
Negate with 没, not 不, and keep 过: 我没去过中国, never *我不去过中国. Question forms: 你去过中国吗？ or 你去过中国没有？

## Notes
去过 vs 去了: 我去过北京 "I have been to Beijing (ever)" states an experience; 我去了北京 "I went to Beijing" reports that specific trip happened.

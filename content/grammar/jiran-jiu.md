## Pattern
既然 A，就 B draws a conclusion from a fact both speakers already accept: "given that A [is already true], then B [follows]." This differs from [[grammar:yinwei-suoyi]]'s 因为...所以, which explains a cause; 既然...就... instead reasons forward from something already settled to what should be done about it.

## Usage
A after 既然 is never news to the listener — it's a shared starting point, often something the listener themselves just said or did. B is typically a decision, suggestion, or plan that responds to it, not just another fact.

## Notes
既 here is unrelated to [[grammar:ji-you]]'s 既...又... — same character, an entirely different job.

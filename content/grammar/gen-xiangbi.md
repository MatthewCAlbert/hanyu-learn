## Pattern
跟/和…[[相比]] sets B as the reference point, then comments on A: “compared with B, A ….” The 相比 phrase is usually first.

## Usage
This is more formal than a bare [[比]] sentence. The main clause still needs its own predicate (提高了, 简单多了). Do not add a second 比 immediately after 相比.

## Notes
The compact *A + Adj + B + Quantity* of [[grammar:comparison-quantity-4]] measures a gap. 跟…相比 introduces the background of the comparison.

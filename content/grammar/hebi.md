## Pattern
何必 is a rhetorical question meaning "there is no need to": *何必 + V (呢)*. It suggests that the action is unnecessary or unwise.

## Usage
It is used to dissuade or criticise. The final 呢 softens it. 又何必 is common after a premise (既然 …，又何必 …). Do not use it for a genuine question.

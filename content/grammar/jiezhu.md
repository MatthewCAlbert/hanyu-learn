## Pattern
借助 and 通过 name the means or channel by which an outcome is reached: *借助/通过 A，B*.

## Usage
借助 emphasises using a tool or resource; 通过 emphasises the process or route. A can be a noun phrase or a verb phrase. The clause with 通过 often ends in a result.

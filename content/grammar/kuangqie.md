## Pattern
况且 introduces an additional reason or fact that strengthens the case already made: *A，况且 B*.

## Usage
It usually comes after a reason and heads a second reason, often with 又, 也 or 还. It is written-leaning but common in speech. 另外 is a looser "also".

## Notes
何况 builds an argument from a stronger case ([[grammar:hekuang]]); 况且 simply piles on one more reason.

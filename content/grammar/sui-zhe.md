## Pattern
[[随着]] introduces a developing situation that the main clause tracks: “along with / as.” The object of 随着 is usually a change noun (变化, 提高, 发展).

## Usage
The two sides should move together. 随着 is more written than 跟着. Do not use it for a static location (随着桌子).

## Notes
着 here is part of the word 随着, not the durative particle after an ordinary verb.

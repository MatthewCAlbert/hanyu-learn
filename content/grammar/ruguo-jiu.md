## Pattern
[[如果]] opens the condition and [[就]] opens the result: *如果 A，就 B*. [[的话]] can follow the condition instead of, or alongside, 如果 in more casual speech: A 的话，就 B.

## Usage
如果 and 的话 are not both required — pick one, or use 如果...的话 together for extra clarity in longer sentences. 就 stays right before the result's verb, not at the very front of the clause.

## Notes
就 shows up in several other constructions (see [[grammar:yi-jiu]]); here its job is specifically to mark "then" in a conditional.

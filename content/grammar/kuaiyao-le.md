## Pattern
快要 / 就要 + V + 了 says the event is just about to happen. [[该]] + V + 了 says the right time has arrived: “should / it's time to.” All three want sentence-final 了.

## Usage
快要 and 就要 are close; 就要 can feel a little more definite. 该…了 is advice or a schedule, not a weather forecast. Leave out 了 and the “about to / time to” reading weakens.

## Notes
This 了 is the change-of-state 了 in [[grammar:le-completion]], not a completed-object 了 after a verb-object split.

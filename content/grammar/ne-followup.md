## Pattern
A noun or pronoun plus [[呢]] asks a parallel question whose content is recovered from the previous sentence: 你呢 “and you?” After a location has been set, X呢 often asks where X is.

## Usage
Do not add [[吗]] after 呢. This 呢 is not the progressive particle in [[grammar:zai-progressive]] (他看书呢), though it is the same character.

## Notes
A first-turn 你呢？ with no prior sentence is incomplete. Give the model first: 我很好，你呢？

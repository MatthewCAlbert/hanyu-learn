## Pattern
Repeating [[或者]] in front of each option lays out a genuine choice between full actions, not just nouns: *或者 A，或者 B* — "either A, or B." Any number of 或者-clauses can chain if there are more than two options.

## Usage
This is the statement counterpart to [[grammar:haishi-choice]]'s 还是, which asks a question. Once a decision is being reported or offered rather than asked about, 或者 is the natural choice: 你喝茶还是喝水？ (asking) vs 茶或者水都可以 (offering either).

## Notes
或者 alone, without repeating it, still means plain "or" joining two nouns or short phrases: 茶或者水.

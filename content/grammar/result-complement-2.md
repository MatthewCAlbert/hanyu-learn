## Pattern
Three more results join the set from [[grammar:result-complement]]: [[到]] "reach/succeed in" (找到 "found," 买到 "managed to buy"), [[住]] "fixed in place" (站住 "stopped/stood still," 记住 "remembered — locked in"), [[走]] "away" (拿走 "took away," 卖走 sounds off, but 拿走/带走 are common).

## Usage
到 marks that an attempt succeeded, so it pairs naturally with verbs that can fail: 找到 vs plain 找 (which just means "look for," no guarantee). Negate a failed attempt with 没: 我没找到钱包.

## Notes
住 and 到 both suggest a fixed outcome, but 住 is about staying put (记住, 站住) while 到 is about arriving at a goal (找到, 看到).

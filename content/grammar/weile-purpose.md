## Pattern
[[为了]] introduces a goal, then the action taken for it: *为了 + purpose, action*. The 为了 phrase can open the sentence or sit after the subject.

## Usage
为了 names an intended aim, not a cause that already happened. 因为下雨所以没去 is cause; 为了不迟到所以早走 is purpose. Do not write 为了 because as a translation of 因为.

## Notes
为了 is not 为 in 为什么. 为什么 asks a reason; 为了 states a purpose.

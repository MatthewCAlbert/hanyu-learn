## Pattern
再 raises a quality or situation to any imagined degree, while 也 says the result still holds: “no matter how...” or “even if... still....” The subject can appear before 再 or in the result clause.

## Usage
The first part normally contains a gradable adjective or a situation that can be intensified: 再难, 再忙, 再有经验. Do not read 再 as “again” in this frame.

## Notes
This is a compact counterpart to [[grammar:buguan-wulun]]. 不管多忙也... spells out the open range; 再忙也... packages the same concession more tightly.

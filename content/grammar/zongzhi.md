## Pattern
总之 and 总而言之 summarise what came before; 换句话说 restates it in other words: *A。总之 / 换句话说，B*.

## Usage
These words stand at the start of a clause and are followed by a comma. 总之 closes a discussion; 换句话说 does not add new content, only clarifies. 也就是说 is the spoken form for 换句话说.

## Pattern
[[敢]] “dare to” and [[需要]] “need to” sit before a verb like the modals in [[grammar:neng-yuan-modals]]. 不敢 is the everyday negative of 敢. 需要 negates as 不需要.

## Usage
敢 is about courage or nerve, not ability: 不会问 is skill; 不敢问 is fear. 需要 can take a noun (需要时间) or a verb (需要休息).

## Notes
需要 is stronger and more objective than 想. It does not mean “would like.”

## Pattern
Some identifying details sit as the predicate with no [[是]]: dates, weekdays, clock time, age, and price. 今天星期一 is complete. 他是学生 still needs 是, because occupation is a [[grammar:shi-copula]] pattern.

## Usage
Adding 是 is possible in careful speech (今天是星期一) but the bare form is the basic HSK 1 pattern. Questions keep the same order: 今天星期几？ 他多大？

## Notes
Do not insert [[很]] before a noun predicate. 今天很好 is an adjectival sentence about the day, not a date.

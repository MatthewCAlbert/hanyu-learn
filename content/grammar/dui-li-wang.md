## Pattern
[[对]] introduces the person an attitude or speech is directed at. [[离]] measures distance from a place: 离学校很近. [[往]] and [[向]] introduce direction of motion: 往前走, 向学校去.

## Usage
对 is not 给: 对老师说 vs 给老师打电话. 离 needs a distance adjective (近/远), not a bare verb of going. 往/向 overlap; 往 is common with 前/后/左/右.

## Notes
对 also means “correct” as an adjective. This lesson is only the preposition.

## Pattern
百分之 + number reads a percentage: 百分之八十. Number + [[倍]] names a multiple: 两倍, 三倍. These are quantity expressions, often before 的 + noun or after 是.

## Usage
百分之百 is “one hundred percent.” 倍 compares against an understood base (是那个的两倍). 多一倍 means twice as much in everyday talk; stay with 两倍 at this level if unsure.

## Notes
分数 as “exam mark” is a different word. A true fraction 三分之二 is possible later; this lesson is percent and 倍.

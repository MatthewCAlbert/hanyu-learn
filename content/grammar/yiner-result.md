## Pattern
[[因而]] introduces a result inferred from the preceding cause: “therefore” or “as a result.” It usually begins the second clause and gives the relationship a formal, written tone.

## Usage
The cause should already be clear before 因而 appears. Unlike 因为, 因而 does not introduce the reason itself. In everyday conversation, 所以 is usually more natural.

## Notes
Compare [[grammar:yinwei-suoyi]]. 因为...所以... marks both halves explicitly; a single 因而 is often enough when connected prose has already established the cause.

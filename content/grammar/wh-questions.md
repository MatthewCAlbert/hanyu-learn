## Pattern
Replace the unknown piece of a statement with a question word in the same spot it would occupy in the answer — word order never inverts. 他是老师 becomes 他是谁 to ask "who." Do not add [[吗]] to a sentence that already has a question word; see [[grammar:ma-yes-no]] for the alternative.

## Usage
[[谁]] (who), [[什么]] (what), [[哪儿]] (where), [[怎么]] (how), [[几]] (how many, expects a small number and a classifier), [[多少]] (how many/much, no classifier needed).

## Notes
什么 also modifies a noun directly, with no 的: 什么书 "what book."

## Pattern
[[还是]] plus a verb plus [[吧]] recommends one option as wiser: “had better.” It is not asking the listener to pick A or B; that is [[grammar:haishi-choice]].

## Usage
The speaker has already decided which option is better. Soften with 吧; without 吧 it can sound like a firm conclusion. Do not add 吗.

## Notes
The same 还是 appears in choice questions. Tone and the final 吧 tell the two uses apart.

## Pattern
[[也]] "also/too" and [[还]] "in addition, on top of that" both sit directly before the verb (or before 是/很), not at the end of the clause the way English "too" does.

## Usage
也 marks that the same thing is true of a new subject: 我是学生，他也是. 还 adds another item to what the same subject already does or has: 他要吃饭，还要喝水. Neither goes after the verb.

## Notes
还 has other senses ("still", "even more") that show up at higher levels; this lesson covers only its additive "and also" use.

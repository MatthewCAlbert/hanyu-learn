## Pattern
[[首先]] marks the first step and 然后 marks the next, laying out an explicit sequence more formally than the bare back-to-back verbs of [[grammar:lianlian-ju]]: *首先 A，然后 B*. Extra steps can keep chaining onto 然后: 首先...，然后...，最后....

## Usage
首先 works for a list of steps or points, spoken or written; 先 alone (without 首先) is the everyday equivalent for two simple actions — 你先洗手，然后吃饭 says the same thing more casually.

## Notes
首先 can also open a list of reasons or points, not just actions: 首先，价钱太贵了 "first of all, it's too expensive."

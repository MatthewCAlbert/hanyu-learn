## Pattern
A question word can name an unspecified person or thing instead of asking: 谁 “someone,” 什么 “something.” There is no question intonation and usually no 吗.

## Usage
This is not “everyone” in [[grammar:yiwen-daici-renzhi]] (谁都). Indefinite 谁 is one unknown person; 谁都 is the whole set. 什么 after 点儿 is a common “something or other.”

## Notes
Context decides the reading. Rising intonation plus a question mark still asks 谁？

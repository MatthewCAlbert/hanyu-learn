## Pattern
[[要是]] is the spoken counterpart of [[如果]] in [[grammar:ruguo-jiu]]: *要是 A，就 B*. 就 still sits before the result verb.

## Usage
要是 is more casual than 如果. 的话 can still follow: 要是你去的话. Do not treat 要是 as 要 + 是 “want to be.”

## Notes
要是 and 如果 are interchangeable in meaning at this level; register is the difference.

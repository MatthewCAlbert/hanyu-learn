## Pattern
[[以便]] introduces a purpose after the action: “so that / in order that.” It is more formal than [[grammar:weideshi-purpose]] and [[grammar:hao-purpose]].

## Usage
The first clause is what you do; 以便 names the intended result. Both subjects are often the same, or the purpose has a different actor (以便老师看见). Do not use 以便 for an accidental outcome.

## Notes
以便 is not 方便. 方便 is “convenient”; 以便 is a purpose conjunction.

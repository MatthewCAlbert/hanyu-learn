## Pattern
When the event itself is already known and only the time, place, or manner is news, wrap that detail in 是...的 around the verb: 我是昨天来的 does not ask "did you come" (already settled) — it highlights *when*. This 是 is the same word as [[grammar:shi-copula]], repurposed to frame emphasis instead of equating two things.

## Usage
是 can be dropped in casual speech (我昨天来的) but 的 cannot. Use this only for completed, specific events — not for [[grammar:guo-experience]]'s general "have you ever" or for future/habitual statements.

## Notes
Do not add another 了: 我是昨天来了的 is wrong — 的 alone marks that the point of the sentence is settled.

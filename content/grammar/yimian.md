## Pattern
The first clause is an action; 以免 or 免得 names the bad outcome that the action is meant to prevent: *A，以免 B*.

## Usage
以免 is more formal. 免得 is colloquial and can follow its clause with a subject of its own. The thing avoided is always unwelcome.

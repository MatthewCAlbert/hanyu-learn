## Pattern
A full suggestion plus 好吗 / 可以吗 / 行吗 asks for agreement. 好吗 seeks a friendly yes; [[可以]]吗 asks permission; 行吗 asks whether the plan works.

## Usage
These tags follow a complete clause. They are milder than a bare imperative. Answer 好, 可以, 行 — or 不好, 不可以, 不行.

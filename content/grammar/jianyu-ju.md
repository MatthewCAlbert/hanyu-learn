## Pattern
The person right after 叫/让/[[请]] is both the object of the first verb and the subject of the second — one word doing double duty, hence "兼语" (shared word): *S1 + 叫/让/请 + S2 + V*. 让/叫 mean "have someone do it / tell someone to," 请 means "invite/ask (politely)."

## Usage
Do not repeat the person: 老师让我们我们做作业 is wrong — 我们 appears once, serving both roles. This looks like [[grammar:bei-passive]]'s 叫/让 but runs the opposite direction: here S1 causes S2 to act; in 被动句 S1 is acted upon.

## Notes
请 in this pattern ("请他来") is the same character as the greeting 请进/请坐, extended from "please" to "invite to."

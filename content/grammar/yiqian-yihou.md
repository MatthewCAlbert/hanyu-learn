## Pattern
[[以前]] and [[以后]] follow a time or event and mark “before / after that point.” Optional 在 can stand in front: 在吃饭以前. The whole phrase is an adverbial, usually before the main verb.

## Usage
以前/以后 can also stand alone for “in the past / later”: 我以前是学生. 前/后 after a short event (三天前) is a related but tighter form.

## Notes
These are not the same as 先, which orders two actions without naming a clock time.

## Pattern
[[凭]] and [[依据]] introduce what an action rests on: a ticket, a law, experience, evidence. 凭 is often short and concrete; 依据 is more official.

## Usage
凭票, 凭经验, 依据法律 are typical. [[根据]] in [[grammar:genju-zuowei]] is the everyday sibling of 依据. 凭 can also mean “even with (only)” in later emphatic uses.

## Notes
凭 as “lean on / proof” is related. This lesson is the preposition “by means of / on the strength of.”

## Pattern
Several adverbials can stack before the verb. The usual order is time, then place, then manner (including 地 phrases), then the verb: 昨天 + 在家 + 认真地 + 写.

## Usage
Time before place is the default. Manner 地 comes closest to the verb. Do not put the place after the object the way English “at home” can sit at the end.

## Notes
This is the HSK 5 多项状语 point. Individual adverb types were taught earlier; the new skill is their order when several appear together.

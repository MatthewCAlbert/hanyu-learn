## Pattern
宁可 (or 宁愿) marks the lesser evil that the speaker accepts; 也不 (or 也要) marks what the speaker refuses or insists on: *宁可 A，也不 B*.

## Usage
A is the cost the speaker is willing to pay; B is the thing to be avoided. With 也要 the second clause names a goal that must be reached, as in 宁可累一点，也要完成.

## Notes
Do not confuse with [[grammar:yuqi-buru]]: 与其…不如… says B is simply better, while 宁可…也不… says A is bad but still preferred to B.

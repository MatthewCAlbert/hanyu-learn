## Pattern
[[根据]] names the source a claim rests on: “according to.” [[作为]] names the role from which someone acts: “as (being) X.” Both are prepositional phrases, usually before the main clause.

## Usage
根据 takes a noun of evidence or a person (根据经验, 根据他). 作为 takes a role or identity. Do not use 作为 for “because.”

## Notes
按/按照 at HSK 3 is a close sibling of 根据. 作为 is not 因为.

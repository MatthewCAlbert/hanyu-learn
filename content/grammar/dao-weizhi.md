## Pattern
到…[[为止]] marks the endpoint of a stretch of time: “up until X.” [[到]] introduces the limit; 为止 closes the frame.

## Usage
Common with 现在, a date, or a named event. The main clause often uses 还, 已经, or 一直. This looks backward or up to a deadline, unlike 从…起 which opens a stretch.

## Notes
为止 is a chunk. Do not write 到现在为停.

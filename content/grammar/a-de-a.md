## Pattern
A的A，B的B splits people or things into two groups by what they do or how they are: 走的走，留的留. Each 的 marks a type; the repeated verb or adjective is the predicate for that type.

## Usage
The two halves should be a clear contrast. This is compact and spoken. A full form would be 走的人走了，留下的人留下.

## Notes
This 的 is the nominalizer of [[grammar:de-attributive]], not complement 得.

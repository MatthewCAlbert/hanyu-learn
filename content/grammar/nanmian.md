## Pattern
难免 and 不免 state that a result is natural or hard to avoid under the circumstances: *Condition，难免/不免 + V*.

## Usage
The result is nearly always an undesirable but understandable one. 难免 is often followed by 会. 难免 can be an adjective ("unavoidable"): 这是难免的.

## Notes
免不了 is a spoken near-synonym.

## Pattern
够…的 says the degree is already plenty, often with a hint of complaint: 够麻烦的, 够受的. Sentence-final [[的]] belongs to the frame.

## Usage
This is spoken and evaluative. 够 + Adj without 的 can still mean “sufficiently,” but the 的 form is the set pattern. Do not add 很.

## Notes
够 as “enough” before a noun (够时间) is ordinary. This lesson is the exclamatory 够…的.

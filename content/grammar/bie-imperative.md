## Pattern
[[别]] and 不要 tell someone not to do something: *别 / 不要 + V*. They are commands, not statements about habit. 不 + V describes what someone does not (usually) do; 别 + V tries to stop an action.

## Usage
别 is shorter and very common in speech. 不要 is a little fuller. Please-forms add [[请]]: 请别走. 没 is the wrong marker for “don't (do it).”

## Notes
别 also has other readings and senses (“other,” “leave”). This lesson is only the prohibitive adverb.

## Pattern
Words like [[见面]] "meet," [[结婚]] "marry," and [[帮忙]] "help" look like single two-character verbs but are secretly verb+object: 见 "see" + 面 "face," 帮 "help" + 忙 "[with something] busy." Aspect markers, quantities, and modifiers slide into the middle: 见了面, 帮了他一个忙, 结过婚.

## Usage
Because the second half is a noun, these words cannot take a direct object the way a normal verb does — 见面他 is wrong; say 跟他见面 instead, routing the person through a preposition. 了/过/着 and inserted quantities always split the two halves apart, never attach to the end of the whole word.

## Notes
These are the same words a beginner first meets as fixed phrases; this lesson is about noticing the internal verb-object seam once 了/过 or a quantity needs to be added.

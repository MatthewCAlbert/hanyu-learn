## Pattern
A quality word (好, 大, 小) does not take [[是]]. The basic predicate is *A 很 Adj*. At this level 很 is often a light linker more than a strong “very”; the sentence still means “A is Adj.”

## Usage
Do not say *他是好*. Use 他很好. Negation is 他不好, not 他不很是好. Comparison and stronger degree words come later. Yes–no: 他很好吗？

## Notes
很 can still mean “very” when stressed. The grammar point here is the adjectival predicate, not the intensifier reading.

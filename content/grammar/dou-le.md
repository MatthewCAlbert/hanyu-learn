## Pattern
都…了 presents a time, age, or degree as already past a reasonable point, often with a hint of complaint or surprise. [[都]] here means “already (to this extent),” not “all.”

## Usage
The phrase after 都 should be striking: a late hour, a large number, an obvious state. A following clause often says what should follow. This is not 我们都是学生 in [[grammar:dou-together]].

## Notes
都…了 and 已经…了 overlap, but 都…了 is more emotive. Stress 都.

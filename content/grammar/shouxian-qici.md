## Pattern
[[首先]]…[[其次]]… orders points or reasons, not only actions. 然后 in [[grammar:shouxian-ranhou]] is the next event; 其次 is the next argument.

## Usage
Common in explanations and short talks. A third point can be 最后. 其次 cannot replace 然后 for a physical next step (先吃饭，然后走).

## Notes
其次 also means “secondary / less important” as a predicate. Here it is the listing connector.

## Pattern
A number cannot sit directly on a noun in Chinese — a classifier goes between them: *Num + Classifier + Noun*. [[个]] is the default, all-purpose classifier; [[杯]] (cupfuls), [[本]] (bound volumes: books), [[家]]/[[间]] (places, rooms), [[口]] (family members), [[块]] (chunks, and money), [[页]] (pages) are shape- or category-specific and replace 个 where they apply.

## Usage
Learn the classifier together with the noun it pairs with, not as a separate rule: 一个人, 一杯水, 一本书. Use 两, not 二, directly before a classifier: 两个人, not 二个人.

## Notes
几 and 多少 both ask "how many," but 几 expects a small number and still needs a classifier (几个人？), while 多少 can drop the classifier (多少钱？).

## Pattern
[[一会儿]]…一会儿… switches between two states or activities in short succession. It describes instability or fidgeting, not two actions at the same instant.

## Usage
Contrast [[grammar:yibian-yibian]], which is simultaneous. 一会儿 A 一会儿 B is sequential and often slightly complaining or vivid.

## Notes
一会儿 alone still means “a little while.” Only the doubled frame has this alternating reading.

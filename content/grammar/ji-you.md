## Pattern
[[既]] A，又/也 B credits the same subject with two qualities or roles at once, both fully true: "not only A but also B, at the same time" — 既好看又便宜 "both good-looking and cheap." 又 pairs with adjectives and verbs; 也 pairs naturally with 是, echoing [[grammar:hai-ye-additive]].

## Usage
Unlike [[grammar:bujin-bingqie]]'s 不但...而且, which often builds up to the second point as the more important one, 既...又/也... presents both halves as equally weighted, simultaneous facts.

## Notes
A and B are usually short and parallel in form (两个形容词, or two 是-phrases) — 既...又... reads awkwardly with long, unbalanced clauses on either side.

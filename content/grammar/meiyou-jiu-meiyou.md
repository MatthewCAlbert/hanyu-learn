## Pattern
没有 A，就没有 B presents A as indispensable to B: without A, B cannot exist or occur. Repeating 没有 gives the compact statement a balanced, emphatic shape.

## Usage
This makes a strong necessity claim, not merely a likely prediction. Make sure the context supports A as a true condition for B. For a condition that is sufficient rather than necessary, use 如果...就....

## Notes
The logic resembles [[grammar:zhiyou-cai]], but the framing is negative: 只有 A 才有 B corresponds closely to 没有 A 就没有 B.

## Pattern
[[先]] orders one action before another: 先吃饭. [[马上]] says the action follows at once: 马上到. Both are adverbs before the verb.

## Usage
先 often pairs with a later step in the next clause (再, 然后 appear at the next level). 马上 is about delay, not sequence: it does not mean “first of two tasks.”

## Notes
先 is not the same as 前 in a time noun (以前). Here it is an adverb of order.

## Pattern
有着 presents an enduring possession or characteristic, often in descriptive prose. In V有, a verb such as 写, 印, or 画 is followed by 有 to say that something is attached, recorded, or present as the result of that action.

## Usage
Use 有着 for stable abstract features: 有着重要意义, 有着丰富经验. V有 is compact and formal: 信上写有地址. Ordinary everyday possession still normally uses plain 有.

## Notes
Although 着 suggests continuity, 有着 behaves as a fixed descriptive form; it does not take another aspect marker after it.

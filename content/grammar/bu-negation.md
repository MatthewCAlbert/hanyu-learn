## Pattern
[[不]] sits immediately before the verb it negates. With [[是]] that is 不是. It does not go at the end of the clause, and it does not invert word order.

## Usage
不 is the default present/future/habitual negation at this level. Existence and completed events use other patterns later (没有, 没). For adjectives, 不 often pairs with [[grammar:hen-degree]] as 不很 or, more naturally, 不太 — keep 不 directly before the adjective for now: 不好.

## Notes
Tone sandhi (bú before a fourth tone) is pronunciation, not a different word. The character is still 不.

## Pattern
[[使]] and [[令]] cause a person (or 人) to enter a state or action: 使我高兴, 令人担心. [[让]] already does this in [[grammar:jianyu-ju]]; at this level 使/令 are the more formal causative pair.

## Usage
使/令 often take an emotion or evaluation, not a concrete errand (让他去买书). 令人 + Adj/V is common with no specific person. 叫 remains more spoken and directive.

## Notes
令 is not the classifier or “season.” Here it is the causative verb. 使 as “to use” is a different sense.

## Pattern
A [[grammar:bi-comparison]] sentence can end with a quantity that states the gap: *A 比 B + Adj + Quantity*. The adjective names the scale; the number names how much.

## Usage
Keep 很 out. Common gaps: 大/小 + 岁, 贵/便宜 + 块, 高 + a length later. 他比我高 already compares; 他比我高很多 uses 多 as a vague amount.

## Notes
A compact form without 比 appears much later. At this level always keep 比.

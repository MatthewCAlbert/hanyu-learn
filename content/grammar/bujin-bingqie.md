## Pattern
[[不仅]] works exactly like [[grammar:buguo-erqie]]'s 不但, stacking a second point on the first: *不仅/不但 A，而且 B*. [[并且]] can replace 而且 in the second half, especially between two verb phrases describing the same subject rather than two different subjects.

## Usage
不仅 leans slightly more formal/written than 不但, but the two are interchangeable in this pattern. When A and B share the same subject, 而且/并且 in the second clause is often followed by 也/还 for extra flow: 不但简单，而且也很有意思.

## Notes
并且 alone (without 不但/不仅 before it) can also just mean "and, moreover," joining two related facts about the same thing.

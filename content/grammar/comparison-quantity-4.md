## Pattern
A measured difference can be expressed without 比: *A + scalar adjective + B + quantity*. The adjective establishes the scale, B is the comparison point, and the final quantity states the gap.

## Usage
This compact form works with measurable contrasts such as 高, 低, 大, 小, 多, and 少. Keep the difference after B. For a general quality without a numerical gap, use [[grammar:bi-comparison]] instead.

## Notes
The equivalent 比 sentence is usually more conversational: 哥哥比弟弟高五厘米. The compact form is common in reports and statistical comparisons.

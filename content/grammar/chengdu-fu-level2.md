## Pattern
These join [[grammar:hen-degree]]'s 很/太/非常/真/最 in front of an adjective: [[更]] "even more (than a point of comparison already mentioned)," [[特别]] "especially," [[有点儿]] "a bit, somewhat" — usually about something mildly unwanted.

## Usage
有点儿 leans negative or undesirable: 有点儿热/贵/累 sound natural; 有点儿高兴 sounds odd — for positive qualities use 很 or 真 instead. 更 implies a comparison already on the table, even without 比 spelled out: 他弟弟更高 only makes sense once someone's height was just mentioned.

## Notes
特别 also means "special" as a plain adjective (特别的日子); here it works only as an intensifier before another adjective.

## Pattern
[[grammar:ba-sentence]]'s "verb needs something after it" rule extends to a few more shapes: [[grammar:verb-reduplication]] after 把 softens the action (把字写一写 "give the characters a bit of writing"); 了 or 着 alone can close a 把 sentence when the point is a lasting change (把生词都忘了 "forgot all the vocabulary"); and a [[grammar:dongliang-shiliang-complement]] closes it when the point is how much (把他批评了一顿 "gave him a talking-to").

## Usage
把字写一写 is gentler than 把字写好 — it asks for some effort, not a guaranteed result. The quantity-complement version answers "how much/how many times," same slot as an ordinary [[grammar:result-complement]] would take.

## Notes
All of these still obey the core 把 rule: the bare verb alone, with nothing after it, never ends a 把 sentence.

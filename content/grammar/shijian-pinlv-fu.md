## Pattern
These adverbs sit before the verb like other adverbs learners already know (see [[grammar:hai-ye-additive]]): [[已经]] "already," [[刚]]/刚刚 "just [now]," [[又]] "again (something that already happened)," [[老]]/老是 "keeps on, always," [[经常]] "often, regularly."

## Usage
又 looks back at something already repeated: 他又来了 "he came again [and did]." 再 (a different word, met earlier) looks forward to a repeat that hasn't happened yet — do not swap the two. 已经 pairs naturally with a sentence-final 了: 他已经走了.

## Notes
老 as "keeps on" (老写错) is different from 老 the adjective "old"; context makes it clear which sense is meant.

## Pattern
[[即使]] presents A as not changing B: “even if / even though.” [[也]] (or 还) marks that the result stands. It is the standard written twin of [[grammar:napa]]'s 哪怕.

## Usage
即使 can be hypothetical or a known obstacle. 也 sits before the result verb. 即使…也… is calmer than 哪怕, which often exaggerates A.

## Notes
即使 is not 既然. 既然…就… treats A as given grounds; 即使…也… treats A as unable to block B.

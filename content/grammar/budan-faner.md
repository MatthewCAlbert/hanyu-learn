## Pattern
The first clause denies an expected outcome with 不但不 or 不但没有; [[反而]] then introduces the opposite or a more surprising result. The force is “not only did A not happen—quite the reverse, B happened.”

## Usage
Match negation to the predicate: 不但不 for a general or future action, 不但没有 for a completed or absent event. B must reverse or strongly frustrate the expectation created by A, not merely add another fact.

## Notes
This differs from ordinary addition in [[grammar:bujin-bingqie]]. 而且 adds a compatible point; 反而 turns the direction of the claim.

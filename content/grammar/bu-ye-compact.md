## Pattern
不 A 也 B packs a concession into one compact sentence: even without A, B still holds. There is no 如果 or 即使. 也 marks that B is unaffected.

## Usage
A is typically a verb. This is tighter than [[grammar:buguan-wulun]]. Do not insert 就 unless you want a different (conditional) reading.

## Notes
不…不… (不看不行) is necessity, a different compact pattern. Here 也 keeps B true despite missing A.

## Pattern
[[可见]] introduces a conclusion that the preceding facts make evident: “this shows that...” or “clearly....” Evidence comes first; the judgment follows 可见.

## Usage
Do not use 可见 to introduce an unsupported opinion. The first clause should give enough observable evidence for the listener to follow the inference. It is common in explanation and argument.

## Notes
Literal “can be seen” remains possible in other positions. Sentence-connecting 可见 is an inferential marker and is usually followed by a complete claim.

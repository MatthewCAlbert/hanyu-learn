## Pattern
[[给]] can introduce a recipient after another verb, or a beneficiary before the verb: 送给他; 给妈妈打电话. This is not the double-object verb in [[grammar:shuang-binyu]] (我给你一本书), though the character is the same.

## Usage
打电话 and 买东西 commonly take 给 + person. Do not double 给 when the verb is already 给: 我给你书, not 我给给你书.

## Notes
给 before the verb often marks who benefits (给朋友买书). 给 after some verbs marks the recipient of the action (送给他).

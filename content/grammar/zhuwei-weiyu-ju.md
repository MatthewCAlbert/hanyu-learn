## Pattern
A whole clause — subject plus [[grammar:hen-degree]] predicate — can itself serve as the comment on an outer topic: *Topic + (Subject + Predicate)*. 奶奶身体很好 reads as "as for Grandma, [her] health is good," not "Grandma [is] health very good."

## Usage
The inner subject is usually a part or attribute of the topic (身体 belongs to 奶奶; 颜色 belongs to 这件衣服). This lets one sentence describe an attribute of something without a separate 的 possessive: compare the plainer, also-correct 奶奶的身体很好.

## Notes
Only the inner clause takes 很/negation/等; the outer topic is not itself a grammatical subject taking a verb.

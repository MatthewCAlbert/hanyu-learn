## Pattern
不A不B places two related adjectives or verbs in a balanced “neither…nor….” Often the result is a moderate, fitting middle: 不大不小.

## Usage
A and B should be a conventional pair (大/小, 冷/热, 早/晚). Random pairings sound unfinished. This is not simple 不 + Adj of [[grammar:bu-negation]].

## Notes
Some 不A不B items are fixed (不三不四 is later and pejorative). At this level stick to size, temperature, and time pairs.

## Pattern
没A没B is a four-character frame that denies both members of a pair: 没吃没喝, 没日没夜. The two items are usually a conventional couple, not a free list.

## Usage
The frame often describes an extreme state (no rest, no food, no order). It is stronger than a single 没. Do not insert 和 between A and B.

## Notes
This is the negative sibling of [[grammar:you-a-you-b]]. Some pairs are fixed; invented combinations can sound unfinished.

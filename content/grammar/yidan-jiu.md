## Pattern
[[一旦]] presents A as a decisive threshold: once A happens, B follows or becomes true. 就 commonly marks that consequence. Unlike a neutral 如果 condition, 一旦 asks the listener to consider the point after the condition has become real.

## Usage
A is often serious, hard to reverse, or important for planning. 一旦 can come before the subject or after it. Do not use it for a routine “whenever”; it highlights the onset of one condition.

## Notes
The event may be possible rather than certain. The force of the pattern lies in the dependable consequence after the threshold, not in predicting whether A will occur.

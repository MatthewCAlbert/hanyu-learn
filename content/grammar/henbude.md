## Pattern
恨不得 expresses a strong wish for something that cannot happen or is very hard to do: *恨不得 + V*.

## Usage
The wish is usually impossible or exaggerated, so it feels vivid. It is followed by a verb phrase, often with 马上 or 立刻. Do not use it for a realistic plan.

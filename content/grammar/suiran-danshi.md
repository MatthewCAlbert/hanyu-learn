## Pattern
[[虽然]] marks the clause that concedes a point; [[但是]] marks the clause that pushes back against it: *虽然 A，但是 B* — "even though A, still B." B is the point the speaker actually wants to make.

## Usage
Both connectors together is normal in Chinese, unlike English where "although...but" is considered redundant. 但是 alone, without 虽然, is also common when the contrast doesn't need to be set up in advance: 我很累，但是我很高兴.

## Notes
可是 can replace 但是 in casual speech with no change in meaning.

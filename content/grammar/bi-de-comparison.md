## Pattern
To compare how an action is done, put [[grammar:bi-geng-buru]]'s 比 after [[grammar:de-complement]]'s 得: *A + V得 + 比 + B + Adj*. If the verb has an object, repeat the verb first, exactly as in a plain 得 complement: 说中文说得比他流利.

## Usage
比 can instead sit right before the whole V得 phrase — 我比他跑得快 — with the same meaning as 我跑得比他快; both orders are common and interchangeable.

## Notes
Do not put 很 after the adjective here the way [[grammar:hen-degree]] normally allows — 我跑得比他很快 is wrong; 比 already supplies the comparison.

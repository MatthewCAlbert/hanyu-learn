## Pattern
难怪 or 怪不得 expresses sudden understanding of a fact that was puzzling: *难怪 + Result*.

## Usage
The reason is usually known just before or after. 难怪 is more common and 怪不得 is slightly more colloquial. The result is a fact, not a command.

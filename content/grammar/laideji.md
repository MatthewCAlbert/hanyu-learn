## Pattern
[[来得及]] and [[来不及]] are frozen potential forms: there is / is not enough remaining time. A verb can follow: 来得及去, 来不及准备.

## Usage
还来得及 often reassures. 来不及了 is a close-of-window 了. These are not ordinary 来 + 得/不 + 及 built on the fly at this level — learn them as chunks.

## Notes
The same 得/不 slot as [[grammar:keneng-complement]], but 及 here means “make it in time,” not a result of 来 as motion.

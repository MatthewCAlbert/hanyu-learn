## Pattern
[[来]] and [[去]] after a verb show direction relative to the speaker: 进来 "come in" (toward here), 进去 "go in" (away from here). They can also follow another direction word: 走进来 "walk in," 拿回去 "carry back."

## Usage
来/去 fixes the viewpoint, so pick the one that matches where the speaker is standing: someone outside a room says 进去 about entering it; someone already inside says 进来. [[上]]/[[下]]/[[进]]/[[回]]/[[开]] alone (without 来/去) can also serve as the whole complement: 坐下, 打开.

## Notes
This is the plain directional meaning only — result and possibility readings of the same complements (走不进去, etc.) come later.

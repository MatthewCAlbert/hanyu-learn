## Pattern
不至于 says that something will not go as far as an extreme stated or feared: *不至于 + V / Adj*.

## Usage
It dismisses an exaggeration or worry. It is often used with 吧 and with 还 ("still not to that point"). The positive form 至于 is a different word (a topic marker).

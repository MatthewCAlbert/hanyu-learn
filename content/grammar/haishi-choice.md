## Pattern
[[还是]] between two options asks the listener to pick one: *A 还是 B？* Unlike [[grammar:ma-yes-no]]'s 吗, which expects yes/no, a 还是 question expects one of the named options back as the answer.

## Usage
Repeating the shared verb is the safe default: 你喝茶还是喝水 keeps 喝 in both halves. Dropping it (你喝茶还是水？) is common in speech. Never add 吗 to a 还是 question — the two markers do different jobs and don't combine.

## Notes
Outside a question, 还是 can also mean "still" or "had better" (还是老样子, 还是我们去吧) — this lesson covers only its use joining two choices.

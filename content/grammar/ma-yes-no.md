## Pattern
Add [[吗]] to the end of a statement to turn it into a yes–no question. The word order does not invert. 你是老师。 → 你是老师吗？ Content questions use words such as [[什么]] instead of 吗, and do not stack both.

## Usage
Answer 吗-questions with 是 / 不是, 有 / 没有, or a short verb repeat — not with 吗 itself. Rising intonation is common but 吗 is the written marker. Do not add 吗 to a sentence that already has 什么, 谁, or 哪.

## Notes
吗 is a sentence-final particle. It is not the same function as English “do/does.”

## Distinctions
不满 is literally 不 "not" + 满 "full/satisfied" — the direct negation of the
满意/满足 family, but it has lexicalised into its own word with a stronger,
more negative flavour than a plain "not satisfied": it leans toward
resentment or grievance, and works as a noun too (表达不满 "voice
discontent"). 满意 is the everyday positive counterpart. Note 不满 also has an
unrelated literal sense, "under/less than" (不满十八岁 "under eighteen"),
which is outside this axis.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 不满 as the negation of 满 "satisfied," with
an added sense of resentment beyond plain non-satisfaction.

## Distinctions
提高 is the everyday, general verb for raising a level, quality, or
efficiency (提高水平, 提高效率). 提升 shares that "raise/improve" sense in more
formal writing, but it alone also covers promotion to a higher rank or
position at work (提升为经理 "promoted to manager") — a sense 提高 does not
have. Use 提高 for everyday improvement talk; reach for 提升 in formal
writing or specifically for a job promotion.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉/CC-CEDICT list 提升 with both the figurative
"elevate, raise" sense shared with 提高 and a distinct "promote in rank"
sense that 提高 lacks.
Reviewed (R): 提高's own entry glosses it via the resultative 提 + 高
"lift to a higher point," a general-purpose pattern without the
rank-promotion sense.

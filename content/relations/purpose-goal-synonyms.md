## Distinctions
目的 asks "why" — the underlying reason or intention behind an action
(学习的目的 "the purpose of studying"). 目标 asks "what" — a concrete target
or objective you aim at and can, in principle, hit or miss (达到目标 "reach
the goal," 销售目标 "sales target"). A 目的 can motivate several different
目标; a 目标 is a step toward a 目的, not a restatement of it.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 目的 as "the point aimed at, intention" and
目标 as "the mark/objective aimed at," both built on 目 "eye, aim" but with
different second characters (的 "target point" vs 标 "marker").
Reviewed (R): near-synonymous, split by abstract intention vs concrete
target — medium confidence because the distinction is a usage tendency, not
an absolute rule; native usage sometimes blurs the two.

## Distinctions
Both name exactly the same unit, 1000 grams. 公斤 ("metric catty") is the
everyday word people actually say and write casually — on a bathroom scale,
at the market, in speech. 千克 ("thousand grams") is the formal SI unit name
used in scientific, technical, and legal/official writing (product labels,
physics textbooks, standards documents). Do not expect 千克 in casual speech,
or 公斤 in a formal weights-and-measures document.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 cross-defines 千克 as the SI name and 公斤 as its
everyday equivalent.

synonym of 公斤," directly naming the pair.

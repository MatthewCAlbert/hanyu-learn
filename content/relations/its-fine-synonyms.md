## Distinctions
This is the reassurance sense only — a reply to someone's apology or thanks
("no harm done"). In that sense the two are close to interchangeable: 没关系
is the neutral, textbook-safe reply to 对不起; 没事儿 is the colloquial spoken
equivalent, common in casual speech and chat. Do not conflate this with
没事儿's other, unrelated sense — "to be free, have nothing on" (我今天没事儿
"I'm free today") — which 没关系 does not share at all; see [[free-synonyms]]
for that sense against 有空儿.

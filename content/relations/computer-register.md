## Distinctions
电脑 "electric brain" is the everyday word and dominates in Taiwan speech.
计算机 "calculating machine" is the formal/technical Mainland term; in Taiwan
it often means "calculator." HSK teaches both; learners should not assume they
are regional copies of the same register.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 and CC-CEDICT note the Taiwan "calculator" sense of 计算机.
Reviewed (R): everyday vs formal/technical, with a Mainland/Taiwan split on 计算机.

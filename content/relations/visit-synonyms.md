## Distinctions
参观 is to go somewhere and look around — the object is a place you observe
(参观博物馆 "tour a museum"). 访问 is to visit and converse — the object is
typically a person, organisation, or country, especially in a formal or
official capacity (总统访问中国 "the president visits China," 记者访问她
"the reporter interviews her"). Do not swap them: you 参观 a factory but 访问
its director; a state visit is 访问, never 参观.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 参观 as touring a place to observe it, and
访问 as calling on and conversing with a person or entity, often formally.
Reviewed (R): both share a "go and engage with X" core but take different
kinds of objects (place vs person/entity).

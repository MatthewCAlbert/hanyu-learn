## Distinctions
In the "lunar New Year holiday" sense, 春节 and 新年 overlap: 过春节 and 过年/
过新年 both mean celebrating the holiday. 春节 is the specific, official
holiday name (the one on the calendar and in official contexts) and only ever
refers to the lunar New Year. 新年 is the general word "New Year" and is
ambiguous on its own — depending on context it can mean the lunar New Year
period or 1 January (元旦, the separate solemn/Western New Year). Do not
assume 新年 always means the same holiday as 春节.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 春节 as specifically the lunar New Year
festival; 新年 as the general term for "New Year," used of either calendar.
Reviewed (R): sense-specific overlap under "lunar New Year holiday" only —
medium confidence because 新年 is genuinely ambiguous outside that context.

## Distinctions
Both mean exercising caution to avoid mistakes. 谨慎 describes a person's
general manner or character — being careful in speech and action broadly
(为人谨慎 "cautious by nature"). 慎重 more often attaches to a specific
decision or matter being handled with care and deliberation (慎重考虑
"consider carefully," 慎重处理 "handle prudently"), and skews toward formal,
written contexts such as official statements.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 谨慎 as a general cautious manner and 慎重 as
prudent handling of a specific matter, with 慎重 marked more formal.

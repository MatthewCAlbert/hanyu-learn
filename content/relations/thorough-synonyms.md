## Distinctions
完全 simply means "entirely, 100%," a plain statement of totality (完全同意
"completely agree," 完全不同 "entirely different"). 彻底 means going all the
way to the root of something, leaving nothing partial or superficial —
especially solving a problem, reforming a system, or defeating something
(彻底解决 "thoroughly resolve," 彻底失败 "utter/total failure") — it carries a
"root-and-branch" thoroughness that plain totality (完全) does not
necessarily imply.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 完全 as a simple totality word and 彻底 tied
specifically to thoroughness that reaches the root of a matter.

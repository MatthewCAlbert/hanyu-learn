## Distinctions
This pair opposes 谦虚 against the negative "conceited/arrogant" sense of
骄傲, not its neutral "proud of" sense — 骄傲 is ambivalent (see
[[proud-synonyms]]), and only its unfavourable reading (typically when used
without an object, describing someone's character generally) is 谦虚's true
opposite: 骄傲使人落后 "pride/arrogance goes before a fall" vs 他很谦虚
"he's very modest." Context — an object present ("proud of X") vs a bare
character trait — decides which sense of 骄傲 is in play.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 lists 骄傲's "arrogant, conceited" sense as a
distinct headword sense from "proud of," and defines 谦虚 as its opposite in
that sense.
Reviewed (R): axis holds only for the negative reading of 骄傲.

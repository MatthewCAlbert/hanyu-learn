## Distinctions
Opposite halves of the day around 午 "noon": 上 = earlier, 下 = later.
Not the same axis as 早上 (early morning) or 晚上 (evening). 中午 sits between
them and is not a member of this pair.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): directional 上/下 around 午 is the dictionary analysis.
Reviewed (R): same POS, opposite on one time-of-day axis.

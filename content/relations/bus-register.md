## Distinctions
Same referent, a city bus. 公共汽车 "public + car" is the older, fully
transparent compound and the one HSK spells out in full. 公交车 clips 公共
交通 "public transport" + 车 "vehicle" and is the shorter word most commonly
used in speech today; it clips further still to bare 公交 (坐公交). Neither is
slang — this is a length/currency difference, not a formality one. 巴士 (from
English "bus") is a separate southern/Hong Kong-influenced form, not part of
this pair.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 cross-references 公交车 as the common clipped form of
公共汽车.
Reviewed (R): 公交车's own entry calls itself the modern everyday word,
shorter than 公共汽车 — i.e. the newer default.

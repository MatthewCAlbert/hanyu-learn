## Distinctions
孩子 is the neutral, written-safe word, and the only one of the two that
naturally extends to "my children" of any age (你的孩子多大了?). 小孩儿 (also
小孩, 小孩子) is a colloquial spoken synonym that reads more casual and stays
tied to young children — it does not carry the "one's own kids, any age"
extension the way 孩子 does. Default to 孩子 in writing or when the age of the
person referred to is not clearly young.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 小孩儿/小孩 as colloquial (口语) for 孩子, without
the "one's children" extension carried in 现汉's 孩子 entry.
Reviewed (R): register split (neutral vs colloquial) plus a scope difference,
not full synonymy.

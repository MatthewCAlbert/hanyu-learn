## Distinctions
广泛 is about breadth of scope or subject matter — wide-ranging interests,
extensive discussion, a broad range of topics (广泛的爱好, 广泛讨论). 普遍 is
about prevalence — how common or universal something is across cases or
people (普遍现象 "a widespread/common phenomenon," 普遍认为 "it is commonly
believed that..."). A single topic can be discussed 广泛地 (extensively)
without the underlying belief being 普遍 (widely held), and vice versa.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 广泛 as "extensive, wide in scope" and 普遍
as "universal, general, found everywhere," a scope-vs-prevalence split.
Reviewed (R): 普遍's own entry decomposes 遍 as "covering the whole extent,"
matching a prevalence sense distinct from 广泛's breadth sense.

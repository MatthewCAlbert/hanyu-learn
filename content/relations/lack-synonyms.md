## Distinctions
Both are built on 缺 "to lack, be short of." 缺少 pairs with concrete,
countable shortfalls — people, funds, supplies (缺少人手 "short-staffed,"
缺少材料 "short of materials"). 缺乏 pairs with abstract qualities or
resources that are not counted — experience, patience, confidence, common
sense (缺乏经验, 缺乏耐心, 缺乏常识). Do not swap them where the object is
countable: 缺少三个人, not ×缺乏三个人.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 缺少 as lacking a needed quantity and 缺乏
as being deficient in an abstract resource or quality.
Reviewed (R): 缺少's own entry glosses its second character 少 as "few,
little," fitting a countable shortfall, versus 乏's more abstract
"deficient, wanting" sense in 缺乏.

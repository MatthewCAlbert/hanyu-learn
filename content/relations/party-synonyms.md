## Distinctions
晚会 is specifically an evening social event with an organised programme —
performances, a gala (新年晚会, 春节联欢晚会 "the Spring Festival Gala"). 聚会
is the general word for any social gathering, at any time of day, with or
without a programme, and — unlike 晚会 — also works as a verb ("to get
together": 我们周末聚会). Do not use 晚会 for a casual daytime get-together; use
聚会 when time of day or formality is not the point.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 晚会 as an evening event with entertainment,
and 聚会 as the general noun/verb for a social gathering.
Reviewed (R): 聚会's own entry calls the 聚/会 pairing "the standard everyday
word for a social gathering," i.e. the unmarked general term against which
晚会 is a time/programme-specific subtype.

## Distinctions
Both describe being happy. 高兴 is the everyday, general word taught first
and used constantly in speech (很高兴认识你). 欣喜 is literary and describes a
stronger, often sudden or momentous joy — good news, an achievement, a
reunion (欣喜若狂 "wild with joy," a set phrase) — and is largely confined to
writing rather than casual talk.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 欣喜 as literary, describing intense delight,
against 高兴's general everyday register.

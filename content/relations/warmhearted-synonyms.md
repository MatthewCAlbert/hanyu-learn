## Distinctions
Both describe warmth toward others. 热情 is about a warm, enthusiastic
manner — a hospitable host, an outgoing personality (热情接待 "receive
warmly"). 热心 is specifically about eagerness to help — going out of one's
way for someone else's problem or a public cause (热心公益 "keen on charity
work," 对...很热心 "eager to help with..."). A person can be 热情 (warm in
manner) without being especially 热心 (proactively helpful), and vice versa.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 热情 tied to warmth of manner and 热心 tied
specifically to eagerness to help with a cause or another's need.

## Distinctions
短暂 describes something whose actual duration is short — a brief visit, a
short-lived era (短暂的停留 "a brief stay"); the emphasis is on brevity
itself. 暂时 describes a state as not permanent — true for now but expected
to change — without necessarily implying it will be brief (暂时的困难
"a temporary difficulty," which could persist for a long time before
resolving). A 暂时 arrangement can in principle last a long time; a 短暂 one
by definition cannot.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 短暂 tied to actual short duration and 暂时
tied to impermanence/provisionality regardless of length.

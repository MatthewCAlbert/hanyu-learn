## Distinctions
快乐 describes personal happiness directly, and is fixed in set greetings
(生日快乐 "happy birthday," 新年快乐 "happy new year"). 愉快 describes a
pleasant quality of an experience or occasion rather than a person's inner
state — a pleasant trip, a pleasant conversation, a good mood while doing
something (旅途愉快 "have a pleasant trip," 心情愉快 "in a good mood") — and
skews slightly more formal/written than 快乐.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 快乐 tied to personal happiness (and its set
greetings) and 愉快 tied to a pleasant quality of an experience or occasion.

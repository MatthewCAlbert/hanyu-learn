## Distinctions
认真 is the everyday, basic word for taking something seriously and doing it
properly — a student's attitude to homework, a worker's attitude to a task
(认真学习 "study seriously"). 严谨 is more formal and specifically about
rigor in reasoning, method, or scholarship — a tightly argued proof, a
carefully worded contract, a scientist's rigorous approach (治学严谨
"rigorous in scholarship"). 严谨 is not a general "serious attitude" word the
way 认真 is; it is about logical/methodological tightness.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 严谨's sense tied to rigor of logic/method/
scholarship, distinct from 认真's general "takes it seriously" sense.

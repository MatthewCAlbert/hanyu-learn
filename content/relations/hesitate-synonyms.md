## Distinctions
Both describe being unable to decide or act. 犹豫 is the everyday, general
word for indecision. 迟疑 is more literary and often emphasises a momentary
falter or delay right before or during an action (迟疑了一下才回答 "hesitated
a moment before answering"), reading more at home in narrative writing than
in casual speech.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 迟疑 as hesitation with a literary flavour,
often momentary, against 犹豫's general, unmarked register.

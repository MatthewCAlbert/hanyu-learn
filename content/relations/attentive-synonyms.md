## Distinctions
Both are positive opposites of carelessness. 仔细 usually modifies how an
action is carried out — 仔细检查 "check carefully," 仔细看 "look closely" — and
works adverbially before a verb. 细心 describes a trait of the person —
attentive, thoughtful, noticing what others miss, often toward people
(细心照顾 "attentive care") — and reads more like a character description
than a manner of doing a single task.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats 仔细 as manner-of-action ("carefully, in
detail") and 细心 as a trait of attentiveness/thoughtfulness.

## Distinctions
Both describe firmness, but 坚定 describes an unwavering inner state — a
belief, position, or resolve that does not waver (立场坚定 "firm in one's
position," 坚定信念 "unwavering conviction"). 坚决 describes the manner of an
action or decision — carried out firmly and without hesitation (坚决反对
"firmly oppose," 态度坚决 "resolute attitude"). 坚定 is more a noun-modifying
state; 坚决 more often modifies a verb.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 坚定 as an unwavering state of belief/stance
and 坚决 as resolutely carrying out an action — a state-vs-manner split.

## Distinctions
Both mean putting something into practice. 实行 is the more general,
register-neutral verb, used for systems, methods, or plans in speech and
writing alike. 实施 skews more formal and specifically legal/administrative —
laws, regulations, official policies (实施细则 "implementing rules") — and is
the word of choice in official documents and news reporting on policy.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 实施 as commonly used for laws/policies in
formal/official contexts, while 实行 is the general-purpose verb.

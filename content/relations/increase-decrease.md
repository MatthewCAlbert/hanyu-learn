## Distinctions
增加 (增 "increase" + 加 "add," themselves near-synonyms) and 减少 (减
"reduce" + 少 "few," also near-synonyms) are each internally a synonym
compound before they oppose each other — the standard verbs for a quantity
going up or down (人口增加/减少, 增加/减少收入). Both are neutral, statistical
register words, at home in reports and official contexts as much as
everyday speech.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines each as the standard verb for a rise/fall in
quantity.
Reviewed (R): both word files independently note their own two syllables are
"close synonyms" / "a near-synonym compound," confirming the parallel
internal structure before the pair opposes each other.

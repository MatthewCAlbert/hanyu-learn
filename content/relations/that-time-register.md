## Distinctions
All three point back to a period already established in the discourse. 那时
is the terser, more written/formal form. 那时候 is the neutral, all-purpose
form taught first and safe in speech or writing. 那会儿 is colloquial and
northern-flavoured, less at home in formal writing. They form a
written→neutral→colloquial spectrum rather than a strict formality contrast,
so treat the boundary as gradient, not a hard rule.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 lists all three as near-synonymous temporal
demonstratives.
Reviewed (R): 那时候's own entry states the three "run colloquial → written →
very colloquial," i.e. 那时候 in the middle, 那时 toward written, 那会儿 most
colloquial.

## Distinctions
Both name an eating establishment, and for an ordinary restaurant they are
close to interchangeable in speech. The important caution is that 饭店 has a
second, unrelated meaning 饭馆 does not share: a large 饭店 is often a hotel
(北京饭店 is a hotel, not a restaurant) — scale and context decide which sense
is meant. 饭馆 is unambiguous and always means "restaurant," never "hotel."
When precision matters (booking a room vs a table), do not rely on 饭店 alone.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉/汉语大词典 both note 饭店 covers "restaurant" and, for
larger establishments, "hotel," while 饭馆 is restricted to "restaurant."
Reviewed (R): 饭店's own entry states directly that "large lodging
establishments took the same name... 饭店 covers both restaurant and hotel."

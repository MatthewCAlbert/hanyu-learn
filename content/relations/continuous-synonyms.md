## Distinctions
Both describe something not stopping, but 连续 is about repeated discrete
events happening one after another without a gap (连续三天下雨 "rained three
days in a row" — three separate days), while 持续 is about a single
continuous state or process lasting without interruption (雨持续了三天 "the
rain continued/lasted for three days" — one uninterrupted spell). 连续
counts repetitions; 持续 measures duration of one ongoing thing.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 连续 tied to successive discrete occurrences
and 持续 tied to one uninterrupted ongoing state.

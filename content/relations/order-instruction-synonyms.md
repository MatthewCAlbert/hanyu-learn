## Distinctions
命令 is a direct order backed by authority, expected to be obeyed without
question — military, police, a superior's flat command (警察命令他们停手 "the
police ordered them to stop"). 指示 is closer to guidance or directives
handed down through an organisation — less peremptory, more about pointing
the way or laying out instructions to follow (领导的指示 "the leader's
directive"; also literally "to point out, indicate"). 命令 emphasises
compulsion; 指示 emphasises direction-giving.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉/CC-CEDICT gloss 命令 as an authoritative order to be
obeyed, and 指示 as instructions or directives, plus a separate literal
"point out, indicate" sense that 命令 does not have.
Reviewed (R): near-synonymous in bureaucratic/institutional contexts, split
by degree of compulsion — medium confidence as this is a usage tendency
rather than a strict lexicon cross-reference.

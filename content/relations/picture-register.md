## Distinctions
图画 is the formal, written word for a drawing or picture (儿童图画书
"children's picture book"). 画儿 is the everyday spoken word actually used to
talk about a specific picture (一张画儿, 画儿画得好); bare 画 without 儿 is the
same word and also the verb "to paint/draw." Reach for 图画 in writing or
compound/technical terms, 画儿 in ordinary conversation.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 图画 as the standard written noun and 画儿/画 as
the everyday spoken form.
Reviewed (R): 图画's own entry states directly that it "is the formal, written
word... 画儿, the everyday spoken word, is what is actually used in casual
conversation."

## Distinctions
In the "a joke/funny story you tell" sense, 笑话 and 笑话儿 are the same word
with and without erhua — 讲笑话 and 讲个笑话儿 both work. They are not fully
interchangeable outside that sense: bare 笑话 alone also covers "a
laughing-stock" (a noun) and "to laugh at / mock" (a verb, toneless second
syllable), and the mockery set phrases (闹笑话, 看笑话, 笑话人) normally drop
the 儿. 笑话儿 leans specifically toward the "funny story" sense.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats 笑话儿 as the erhua/colloquial variant of 笑话
restricted to the "joke, funny story" sense.
Reviewed (R): 笑话儿's own entry states the mockery senses "usually drop the 儿."

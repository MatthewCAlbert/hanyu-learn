## Distinctions
Both mean "together" and are close to interchangeable in casual speech
(我们一起/一块儿去吧 "let's go together"). 一起 is the neutral, all-purpose
word, safe in writing and formal contexts and the one taught first. 一块儿 is
colloquial and carries a northern Mandarin flavour; it reads too casual for
formal writing, where 一起 is the safer choice.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 一块儿 as colloquial (口语), with 一起 unmarked
and general.
Reviewed (R): 一块儿's own entry calls itself "the colloquial, northern
equivalent of 一起."

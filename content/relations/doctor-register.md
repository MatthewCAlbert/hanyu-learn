## Distinctions
医生 is the national, written, and HSK-taught word. 大夫 (*dàifu*) is the
everyday spoken word for a physician in much of the north (看大夫).
医师 is a more formal/occupational title and is not in this set.
Do not use the historical court-rank reading *dàfū* for the physician sense.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 separates 大夫 dàifu "doctor" from dàfū "grand master."
Reviewed (R): northern spoken vs nationwide textbook; same profession sense.

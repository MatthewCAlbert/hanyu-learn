## Distinctions
新闻 is specifically journalistic/media news — a broadcast segment, a
newspaper report, a news item (看新闻 "watch the news," 新闻记者 "reporter").
消息 is broader: it covers 新闻 in that sense too, but also personal or
informal news that never appears in a broadcast (她生孩子的消息 "the news that
she had a baby," 好消息 "good news"). 新闻 does not extend to a personal
piece of news the way 消息 does.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 新闻 as recently reported events via media,
and 消息 as news/information in the general sense, including but not limited
to media reporting.
Reviewed (R): 消息 is the superset; 新闻 is the media-specific subset.

## Distinctions
女孩儿 (also 女孩, 女孩子) covers a girl from infancy through childhood in
ordinary everyday speech; it is the colloquial erhua form. 女生 started as
"schoolgirl" (clipped from 女 + 学生) and has broadened among young people into
the neutral word for a young woman, but it keeps a school/youth-register
flavour — calling a toddler 女生 sounds off, and calling a university
classmate 女孩儿 sounds like baby talk. They are not freely interchangeable
across the full age range.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 女孩 as the general word for "girl," 女生
specifically as "female student," with the broadened youth sense noted in usage.
Reviewed (R): register/context split by age range, not full synonymy.

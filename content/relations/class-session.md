## Distinctions
上 / 下 here mean start / finish a session, not up / down. The per-day pair
is 上学 / 放学 — a different axis, not this antonym. Figurative 下课 "be sacked"
(a coach) is a later sense and does not reverse 上课.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): both are verb-object lesson words with opposite 上/下.
Reviewed (R): opposition holds only for the lesson-session sense.

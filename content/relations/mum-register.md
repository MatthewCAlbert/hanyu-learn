## Distinctions
妈妈 is the everyday address term (vocative 妈！). 母亲 is written/formal.
Same kinship role, different register — not slang.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 lists 妈妈 as the common spoken form, 母亲 as the
standard written noun.

## Distinctions
临时 describes something set up on short notice or improvised for an
occasion, without prior planning — a makeshift arrangement (临时决定 "decided
at the last minute," 临时工 "temp worker"). 暂时 describes a state as
provisional or not-yet-permanent, without implying it was unplanned — a
carefully considered arrangement can still be 暂时 (暂时住在朋友家 "staying at
a friend's place for now," which could be a deliberate, planned stopgap).
临时 is about being ad hoc/last-minute; 暂时 is about being not-yet-final.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 临时 tied to being set up on short notice/
improvised, and 暂时 tied to provisionality regardless of planning.

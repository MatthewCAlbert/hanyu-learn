## Distinctions
Both mean doing something alone, and are close to interchangeable in many
contexts (单独/独自完成 "complete it alone"). 单独 emphasises being separate
from a group — set apart rather than combined or accompanied (单独见面
"meet one-on-one" rather than in a group setting). 独自 emphasises the state
of solitude itself — no companion or helper present (独自一人在家 "alone at
home by oneself") — with more of an emotional/experiential flavour of being
by oneself than 单独's more neutral "separately."

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 单独 tied to being apart from a group and
独自 tied to the state of solitude/being unaccompanied.

## Distinctions
Both mean "enough," built on the same 充 "to fill." 充足 describes a
concrete, measurable supply being abundant — light (光线充足), sleep
(睡眠充足), funds, materials. 充分 leans abstract and often adverbial —
sufficient preparation, reasons, or evidence (准备充分 "well prepared,"
理由充分 "sufficient grounds") — and, unlike 充足, regularly modifies a verb
directly (充分利用 "make full use of"). Do not use 充分 for a literal physical
quantity like light or rainfall; reach for 充足 there.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 充足 as abundant/adequate in supply and 充分
as ample/thorough, with 充分's adverbial "fully" use not shared by 充足.
Reviewed (R): 充分's own entry glosses 分 as "a measure, a degree" (as in
十分), consistent with its more abstract, degree-of-completeness sense
versus 充足's concrete-supply sense.

## Distinctions
了解 is knowledge gained by finding out or having experience — you 了解 a
person, a market, a situation by learning facts about it (了解情况 "get the
facts of a situation"). 理解 is comprehension — grasping the meaning or logic
of something, or empathising with someone's feelings (理解课文 "understand
the text," 理解他的心情 "understand/empathise with his mood"). You can 了解 the
facts of a case without fully 理解 (comprehending/empathising with) why
someone acted the way they did.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 了解 as "to find out, to comprehend through
knowledge/experience" and 理解 as "to understand, to grasp the reasoning of."
Reviewed (R): both word files independently decompose their second
character (解 "untangle/analyse") the same way but build from different
first characters (了 "clear, finished" vs 理 "reason through"), matching the
fact-knowledge vs comprehension split.

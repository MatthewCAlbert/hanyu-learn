## Distinctions
Both translate as "to hold an event," but the emphasis differs. 举行 focuses
on the event taking place — often used passively/impersonally, with the event
as subject (比赛在北京举行 "the match is/was held in Beijing"; 举行婚礼 "hold a
wedding"). 举办 emphasises the organising, managing side, usually with a named
organiser doing the hosting (学校举办运动会 "the school is holding a sports
meet"). A large ceremony announced as happening reaches for 举行; an
institution running/organising something reaches for 举办.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 举行 as "to conduct, carry out (an event)"
and 举办 as "to hold, organise (an event)," both built on the same 举 "raise,
mount" root but combined with different second characters (行 "carry out" vs
办 "manage").
Reviewed (R): near-synonymous, split by whether the emphasis is on the event
happening or on who is running it.

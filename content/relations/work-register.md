## Distinctions
工作 is the neutral, written-safe word for "work," both as a verb and as the
noun for one's occupation or job (你在哪儿工作? "where do you work," i.e. which
employer). 干活儿 is colloquial and only a verb — it leans toward manual or
physical labour, or "doing the task at hand" (快去干活儿! "get to work"), and is
not used for "job/occupation" the way 工作 is. They are not interchangeable as
a noun.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 干活儿 口语 (colloquial), glossed as doing physical
or manual work; 工作 carries both the verb and the neutral occupation-noun sense.
Reviewed (R): register split plus a noun/verb asymmetry, not full synonymy.

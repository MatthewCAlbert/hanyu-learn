## Distinctions
一起 is the everyday word for doing something together with someone, in the
same place, at personal scale (我们一起去 "let's go together"). 共同 is more
formal and abstract, describing a jointly shared effort, interest, or
responsibility rather than physical togetherness — common ground between
parties, joint action by organisations or nations (共同努力 "joint effort,"
共同利益 "common interests"). 共同 does not require physical co-presence the
way 一起 implies; 一起 is not natural for an abstract shared interest.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 一起 tied to physical/personal togetherness
and 共同 tied to abstract joint effort or shared interest, more formal.

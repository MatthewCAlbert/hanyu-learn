## Distinctions
Both describe engaged, non-passive behaviour, and often co-occur
(积极主动 "proactive and enthusiastic"). 积极 is about the degree of
enthusiasm and effort put in — a positive, keen attitude (积极参加 "keenly
take part"). 主动 is specifically about who initiates — acting on one's own
without being asked or prompted (主动帮忙 "help without being asked"), and
its direct opposite is 被动 "passive." One can be 积极 (enthusiastic) about a
task assigned by someone else without being 主动 (the one who initiated it).

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 积极 tied to degree of enthusiasm/effort and
主动 tied to self-initiation, opposing 被动.

## Distinctions
Both describe a good mood. 高兴 is the safer classroom/neutral choice
(很高兴认识你). 开心 is warmer and more colloquial (玩得很开心). 拿…开心
"make fun of someone" is a sense 高兴 does not share. Do not swap them blindly
in set greetings or in the "make fun" construction.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats them as near-synonyms with different collocations.
Reviewed (R): 开心 is marked more colloquial in learner and dictionary notes;
the "make fun" sense is 开心-only.

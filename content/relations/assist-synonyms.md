## Distinctions
帮助 is the everyday, general word for helping — anyone helping with
anything (帮助朋友, 需要帮助). 协助 is more formal and specifically implies a
supporting, secondary role under someone else who leads the task —
common in institutional or official writing (协助调查 "assist [the police]
with an investigation," 协助领导工作). Reach for 帮助 in ordinary conversation;
协助 signals a formal division of labour where one party is clearly in
charge.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 帮助 as general assistance and 协助 as
formal, subordinate-role assistance under someone else's lead.
Reviewed (R): 帮助's own entry calls itself "the everyday verb and noun" for
help, against which 协助 is the formal/institutional register variant.

## Distinctions
外边 and 外面 are the same locative built on two parallel suffix series (边
vs 面), like 里边/里面, 上边/上面, 前边/前面 — 边 leans slightly more spoken, 面
slightly more neutral/written, but both are common in speech. 外边 also
carries "away from home" (在外边吃 "eat out"). 外面 additionally covers
"outward appearance, the exterior" (of a thing, e.g. its packaging) — a
figurative sense 外边 does not share.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 cross-lists both as the same locative meaning, with
外面 alone carrying the "outward appearance" extension.
Reviewed (R): both word files independently name the parallel 边/面 suffix
series (外边 vs [[里边]]; 外面 vs 里面/上面/后面/前面).

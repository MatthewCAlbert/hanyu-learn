## Distinctions
咱们 always includes the listener ("you and I"). 我们 may or may not.
咱们 is northern and colloquial; in the south and in writing, 我们 covers both.
Bare 咱 as "I" is dialectal and is not this set.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉: 咱们 inclusive; 我们 unmarked.
Reviewed (R): regional + register split, not a pure synonym pair.

## Distinctions
前边 / 后边 are the front/back pair of the 边 locative series (see also
里边/外边, 上边/下边). Both also carry a temporal, text-sequence sense — 前边
"earlier (in a text)," 后边 "further on / afterwards" — and that figurative
opposition tracks the same axis. Do not confuse this pair with 上边/下边
(above/below) or 里边/外边 (inside/outside), which oppose on a different axis.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines each locative word against the other by
position, with the same 边 suffix.
Reviewed (R): 前边's own entry names 后边 as its pair member.

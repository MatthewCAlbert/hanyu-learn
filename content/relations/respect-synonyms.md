## Distinctions
Both describe respecting a person. 尊敬 is the everyday, register-neutral
word, common in speech and writing alike (尊敬老师). 敬重 is more literary and
carries a stronger, weightier sense of reverence — closer to "hold in high
regard/deep esteem" — and reads more formal in writing than in casual talk.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 敬重 as a stronger, more formal degree of
respect than the general-purpose 尊敬.

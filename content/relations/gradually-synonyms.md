## Distinctions
Both mean a slow, step-by-step change over time and are close to
interchangeable (天气渐渐/逐渐变冷 "the weather is gradually getting colder").
渐渐 is the everyday word, common in speech. 逐渐 (literally "one by one,
gradually") reads more formal and is more at home in writing — reports,
essays, formal narration — than in casual conversation.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 逐渐 as more formal/written than the general
渐渐, with the same core meaning.

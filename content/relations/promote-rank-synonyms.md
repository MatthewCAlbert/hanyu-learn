## Distinctions
In the "promoted to a higher rank" sense, the two overlap closely (提升/晋升
为经理 "promoted to manager"). 提升 is broader and more common — it also
covers raising quality, level, or efficiency generally (see
[[raise-promote-synonyms]] against 提高), so its rank-promotion sense is one
use among several. 晋升 is narrower and exclusively about rank or status
advancement, and reads more formal — common in official, military, or
corporate contexts (晋升制度 "promotion system") rather than casual speech.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 晋升 as specifically advancing in
rank/status, formal register, while 提升 carries that sense alongside a
broader "raise, elevate" meaning.

## Distinctions
关心 is the everyday word: caring about current events, a subject, or a
person as an equal (关心国家大事 "follow/care about current affairs,"
关心朋友). 关怀 is more formal and written, and typically implies a
caretaking direction — an organisation, elder, or authority showing concern
for those it looks after (社会对老人的关怀 "society's care for the elderly").
Between friends and peers, 关心 is the natural choice; 关怀 sounds
institutional or paternalistic if used the same way.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 关怀 as carrying solicitude typically shown by
a superior/institution toward those in its care, while 关心 is unmarked and
general.
Reviewed (R): 关心's own entry glosses it as "to hold something or someone
in one's thoughts and wish them well" between equals, without the
institutional-care flavour of 关怀.

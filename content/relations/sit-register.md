## Distinctions
坐下 is the plain instruction or description of the action — 坐 "to sit" plus
the directional complement 下 "down," and it takes 来/去 for viewpoint
(坐下来). 请坐 is the fixed polite formula a host uses to invite a guest to take
a seat: 请 + the bare verb 坐, without 下. 请坐 is not simply "the polite version
of 坐下" in every context — the idiomatic greeting is 请坐 alone (请坐下 also
exists but is less idiomatic as a greeting), while a bare 坐下 without 请 can
read like an unadorned command rather than an invitation.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats 请坐 as a set polite formula distinct from the
plain verb-complement 坐下.
Reviewed (R): functional/politeness split on the same core action, not
interchangeable in the greeting use.

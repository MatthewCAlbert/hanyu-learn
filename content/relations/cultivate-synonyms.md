## Distinctions
Both mean nurturing something to grow or develop. 培养 is the general,
everyday word for developing a person's abilities, habits, or character
(培养兴趣 "cultivate an interest," 培养人才 "train talent"). 培育 keeps a
closer tie to its literal, agricultural sense — cultivating seedlings or
breeding new plant/animal varieties (培育新品种) — and, figuratively,
emphasises nurturing something from a very early or fragile stage; it reads
more formal and literary than 培养 in the figurative use.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 培育's primary sense agricultural/biological,
with 培养 as the general-purpose verb for developing people or abilities.

## Distinctions
那时候 points to a period already established elsewhere in the discourse
(distal, usually past); 这时候 points to the moment of speaking or a moment
just reached in a narrative (proximal). Each has its own register-spectrum
counterparts — 那时/那会儿 for 那时候 (see [[that-time-register]]), 这时 for
这时候 (see [[this-time-register]]) — this pair is only the neutral,
textbook-register member of each spectrum, opposed on near/far in time.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines each as the demonstrative-plus-时候 pair,
distal vs proximal.
Reviewed (R): both word files name each other as "mirror of" the other.

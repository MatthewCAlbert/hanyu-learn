## Distinctions
Both mean working hard without shirking. 勤奋 is the general word, especially
for study or mental effort — a diligent student (学习勤奋). 勤劳 leans toward
physical or manual labour and carries a traditional-virtue flavour, as in the
stock phrase 勤劳勇敢 "hardworking and brave" describing a people or a
farmer's industriousness — it reads more formal/literary and less natural
for "worked hard on an exam."

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 勤劳 tied to physical labour and traditional
virtue, distinct from 勤奋's broader, especially intellectual, diligence.

## Distinctions
Both cover the same three uses — an exact fit (大小正好/恰好), good timing
(来得正好/恰好), and coincidence (正好/恰好碰上他 "happened to run into him") —
and are close to fully interchangeable. 正好 is the more common, all-purpose
everyday choice; 恰好 reads slightly more formal or literary and is somewhat
more common in writing than in casual speech, though it is not restricted to
writing.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 cross-defines 恰好 and 正好 as synonyms with the same
three uses (fit, timing, coincidence), noting no clear meaning difference,
only a mild register/frequency skew.

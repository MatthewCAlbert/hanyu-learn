## Distinctions
难过 is the everyday word for feeling sad or upset about something — a
disappointment, an argument, bad news (心里很难过) — and, distinctly, can
also describe physical discomfort (a hard-to-get-through stretch: 胃难过 "my
stomach hurts"), a sense 悲伤 does not share. 悲伤 is a stronger, more literary
word for sorrow or grief — the kind used for loss or tragedy in writing and
narration, and it never means physical illness. Reach for 难过 in ordinary
conversation about everyday upset; 悲伤 for deeper grief or in writing.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 悲伤 as literary/written "sorrowful, grieving,"
while 难过 covers ordinary emotional upset and, separately, physical malaise.
Reviewed (R): 难过's own entry states it covers "hard to get through... of
life" and physical wretchedness, a scope 悲伤 lacks.

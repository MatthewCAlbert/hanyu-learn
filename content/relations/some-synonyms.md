## Distinctions
一些 is the general quantifier "some/a few," attaching directly to a noun
(一些人, 一些问题) and, after an adjective, meaning "a bit more" (好一些 "a bit
better"). 有些 fronts 有 "there are" and typically introduces a subset — most
often as the subject (有些人不同意 "some people disagree") — and, before an
adjective, works as a hedge, "somewhat" (有些累). 有一些 is the fuller, more
emphatic spoken form of 有些, sharing both of its uses. The three overlap in
meaning but favour different syntactic slots: reach for 一些 as a plain
noun-attaching quantifier, 有些/有一些 when introducing existence or contrast,
or hedging an adjective.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 一些 as the plain quantifier and 有些 as a
"there are some (of them)" existential quantifier that doubles as a hedge;
有一些 is cross-referenced as the fuller form of 有些.
Reviewed (R): near-synonymous, split by syntactic slot rather than register.

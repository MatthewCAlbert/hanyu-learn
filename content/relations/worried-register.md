## Distinctions
Both mean worrying about something. 担心 is the everyday, all-purpose word
for worry, personal or otherwise (担心考试 "worried about the exam"). 担忧 is
more literary and slightly weightier, often used for larger-scale or more
serious concerns — a country's future, a societal trend (为...担忧 "worried
for/about...") — and is more common in writing/reporting than in casual
speech about everyday matters.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 担忧 as more literary than the general,
unmarked 担心.

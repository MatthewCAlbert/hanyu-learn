## Distinctions
Both mean making something better, but they take different kinds of objects.
改进 applies to a method, technique, product, or way of doing something —
something you can refine step by step (改进工作方法 "improve working
methods"). 改善 applies to a state or condition — living standards,
relations between people or countries, an environment (改善生活条件 "improve
living conditions," 改善关系 "improve relations") — not to a technique. Do not
swap them: you 改进 a method but 改善 a condition.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 distinguishes 改进's object as methods/work from
改善's object as conditions/states.

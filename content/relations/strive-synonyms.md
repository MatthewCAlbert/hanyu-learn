## Distinctions
Both mean putting in effort. 努力 is the general, everyday word for trying
hard at anything, big or small (努力学习 "study hard"). 奋斗 is reserved for a
sustained, serious struggle toward a significant goal — a life's ambition, a
national cause (为理想奋斗 "strive for one's ideals") — and would sound
overblown applied to something minor like finishing a chore.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 奋斗 tied to sustained struggle for a
significant goal, against 努力's general, any-scale effort.

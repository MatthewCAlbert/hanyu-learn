## Distinctions
乘客 is the general word for a passenger riding any vehicle — bus, taxi,
train, ship, plane (出租车乘客 "taxi passenger"). 旅客 is narrower and
specifically implies travel/journey context — long-distance transit (train,
plane, station announcements: 旅客止步 "no entry to travellers") — and it
alone extends to "hotel guest," a sense 乘客 does not have. A city bus rider
is 乘客 but not naturally 旅客; a hotel guest is 旅客 but never 乘客.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 旅客 as someone in transit or a hotel guest,
and 乘客 as a rider of any vehicle, without the hotel-guest extension.
Reviewed (R): 旅客's own entry states it covers "a passenger on a train or
plane, a guest at a hotel," distinct from the vehicle-general 乘客.

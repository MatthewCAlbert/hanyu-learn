## Distinctions
Both mark "right now" or, in storytelling, "just then." 这时 is terser and
skews written/narrative (这时，门开了 "just then, the door opened"). 这时候 is
the fuller, more colloquial default, and it alone extends to "this time of
day" (这时候还没睡? "not asleep yet at this hour?"), a sense 这时 does not
carry. Colloquial 这会儿 is not in this set — it is not a corpus word at any
HSK level, only a lexeme-worthy spoken form.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats 这时 and 这时候 as near-synonymous temporal
demonstratives, with 这时候 additionally covering "time of day."
Reviewed (R): 这时's own entry states "这时 / 这时候 / 这会儿 run formal →
colloquial," i.e. 这时 toward written, 这时候 more colloquial.

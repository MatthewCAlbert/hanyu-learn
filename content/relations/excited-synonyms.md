## Distinctions
Both describe heightened emotional/physical arousal. 兴奋 leans toward
energised anticipation or stimulation — excited about an upcoming trip,
too wired to sleep (兴奋得睡不着). 激动 leans toward being emotionally moved or
stirred by something, often already happening or just happened — a touching
speech, reuniting with family, a dramatic sports win (激动得流泪 "moved to
tears"). 兴奋 is closer to "keyed up"; 激动 is closer to "emotionally
overcome."

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 keeps 兴奋 tied to energised/stimulated arousal and
激动 tied to a strong emotional reaction/being moved.

## Distinctions
满意 is the everyday word for being pleased with a specific outcome, answer,
or result (对结果很满意 "satisfied with the result"). 满足 is broader: as an
adjective it describes a general state of contentment (not tied to one
event), and — unlike 满意 — it works transitively as a verb meaning "to
satisfy / meet (a need, a demand)": 满足需求 "meet the demand." 满意 does not
take an object this way (there is no ×满意需求 for "satisfy a demand").

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 满意 as an adjective only, and 满足 as both
adjective and transitive verb ("to satisfy").
Reviewed (R): overlapping "satisfied/content" sense, but only 满足 covers the
"to satisfy a need" verb use.

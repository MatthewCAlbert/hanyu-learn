## Distinctions
男孩儿 (also 男孩, 男孩子) covers a boy from infancy through childhood in
ordinary everyday speech; it is the colloquial erhua form. 男生 started as
"schoolboy" (clipped from 男 + 学生) and has broadened among young people into
the everyday word for a boy or young man, but it keeps a school/youth-register
flavour — calling a toddler 男生 sounds off, and calling a university
classmate 男孩儿 sounds like baby talk. They are not freely interchangeable
across the full age range.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 glosses 男孩 as the general word for "boy," 男生
specifically as "male student," with the broadened youth sense noted in usage.
Reviewed (R): register/context split by age range, not full synonymy.

## Distinctions
喜欢 and 讨厌 are the basic positive/negative pair for reacting to a person,
thing, or activity (我喜欢猫 / 我讨厌猫). 讨厌 is stronger than a neutral "don't
like" — it leans toward active annoyance or dislike, and doubles as an
adjective for something/someone annoying (真讨厌! "so annoying!"), a use 喜欢
has no mirror of. The plain negation 不喜欢 ("don't like," milder) sits
between the two on the scale — do not treat 讨厌 as simply "喜欢, negated."

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 讨厌 as "to find repugnant/annoying; to
dislike," stronger than a bare negative of 喜欢, and additionally usable as
an adjective.
Reviewed (R): standard textbook opposite pair for reacting to things,
distinct from milder 不喜欢.

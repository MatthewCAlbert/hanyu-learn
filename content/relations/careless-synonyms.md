## Distinctions
Both describe carelessness. 粗心 is register-neutral and specifically about
missing details through inattention (粗心大意 "careless and inattentive," a
set phrase) — the classic word for a student's careless exam mistakes. 马虎
is more colloquial and broader — sloppy, slapdash work in general, not just
overlooked details — and its reduplicated form 马马虎虎 additionally means
"so-so, passable," a sense 粗心 has no equivalent of.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 马虎 as colloquial with the extra "so-so"
reduplicated sense, while 粗心 is unmarked and detail-specific.

## Distinctions
Both describe high frequency. 经常 is the everyday, general adverb for
"often" (经常锻炼 "exercise often"). 频繁 is more formal and technical-sounding
and often implies a rate high enough to be notable or excessive (交往频繁
"frequent contact," 事故频繁发生 "accidents occur frequently") — it reads
naturally in reports and statistics, less so in casual chat about daily
habits.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 频繁 as more formal, often carrying a sense of
a notably/excessively high rate, against 经常's neutral everyday register.

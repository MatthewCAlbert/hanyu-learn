## Distinctions
Both mean quick-thinking. 机智 emphasises resourcefulness under pressure —
thinking on one's feet to handle an awkward or difficult situation
(机智应对 "respond resourcefully") — and reads more neutral/formal. 机灵 is
more colloquial and describes an alert, sharp, nimble-minded quality of
personality, especially praised in children or pets (这孩子真机灵 "what a
sharp kid") — closer to "bright and quick" than to "resourceful under
pressure."

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 机灵 as colloquial, describing an alert/sharp
personality trait, distinct from 机智's resourceful-response sense.

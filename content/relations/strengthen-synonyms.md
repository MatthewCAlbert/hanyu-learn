## Distinctions
Both mean making something stronger, but the object differs. 加强 applies to
an activity or process that is reinforced by adding more of something —
加强管理 "step up management," 加强合作 "strengthen cooperation." 增强 applies
to a capability, quality, or intrinsic strength that itself becomes greater —
增强体质 "build up one's constitution," 增强信心 "boost confidence." Do not
swap them: you 加强 an effort but 增强 a capacity.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 distinguishes 加强's object (an activity reinforced
by more input) from 增强's object (an inherent quality or strength that
grows).

## Distinctions
Both mean making something go faster and are close to interchangeable
(加快/加速发展 "speed up development"). 加快 is the everyday, general verb.
加速 leans more technical or literary — it is the standard term in physics
for "acceleration" as a noun, and in general prose it reads slightly more
formal than 加快.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 加速 with an additional technical/physics noun
sense ("acceleration") that 加快 does not carry.

## Distinctions
道理 is a general principle or the underlying sense behind how things work —
often used with 有 "there is sense/logic in it" (有道理 "that makes sense,"
讲道理 "be reasonable"). 理由 is the specific reason or justification given
for one particular action, decision, or claim (他没有理由生气 "he has no
reason/grounds to be angry"). 道理 explains why something is true in
general; 理由 explains why someone did or wants a specific thing.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 道理 as "principle, sense, the rationale
behind [things]" and 理由 as "grounds, justification [for an act]," sharing
the root 理 "principle, logic" (see also [[理想]]).
Reviewed (R): near-synonymous "reason" words split by general principle vs
specific justification.

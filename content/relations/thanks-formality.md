## Distinctions
谢谢 is everyday spoken thanks (often *xièxie*, second syllable light).
感谢 is heavier and more formal — speeches, letters, 感谢您的帮助.
The usual reply to 谢谢 is 不客气, which is not a synonym of either member.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats 感谢 as a full verb of gratitude, 谢谢 as
the common formula.
Reviewed (R): register ladder, same speech-act.

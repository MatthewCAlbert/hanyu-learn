## Distinctions
All three describe a reciprocal relationship. 互相 and 相互 are near-
identical near-synonyms, both meaning "mutually" before a verb (互相/相互
帮助 "help each other"); 相互 reads slightly more formal and, unlike 互相,
can modify a noun directly (相互关系 "mutual relations" — ×互相关系 is
awkward). 彼此 is distinct in being able to stand alone as a pronoun rather
than only modifying a verb — 彼此彼此 is a fixed self-deprecating reply
("likewise," said back to a compliment), a use neither 互相 nor 相互 has.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 treats 互相 and 相互 as near-synonymous adverbs
(相互also nominal-attributive), and 彼此 as a distinct pronoun usable
standalone.

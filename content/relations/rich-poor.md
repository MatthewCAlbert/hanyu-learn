## Distinctions
富有 also has a figurative extension — "rich/replete in" a quality, not just
money (富有经验 "rich in experience") — that 贫困 does not mirror; 贫困 is
about material poverty only, and skews toward written/official register
(poverty-alleviation policy, statistics) rather than casual speech, where
穷 is the everyday word for "poor."

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 defines 富有 as wealthy, with a secondary figurative
"abundant in" sense, and 贫困 as materially impoverished, common in policy
and statistical writing.
Reviewed (R): standard wealth-axis antonym pair at the same
written/institutional register.

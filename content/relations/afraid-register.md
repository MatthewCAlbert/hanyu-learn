## Distinctions
Both mean being afraid. 害怕 is the everyday, general word for fear of
anything, mild or serious, in speech and writing alike (害怕黑暗 "afraid of
the dark"). 恐惧 is literary and weightier — a deep, often existential dread
(对死亡的恐惧 "fear of death") — and doubles as a noun ("terror, dread") more
readily than 害怕 does. Reach for 害怕 in ordinary conversation; 恐惧 in
literary or psychological writing about deep fear.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 恐惧 as literary and describing intense,
often abstract fear, against 害怕's general and unmarked register.

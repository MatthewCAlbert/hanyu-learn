## Distinctions
Both mean granting permission. 允许 is the general, everyday word, usable in
speech and writing for any kind of permission (父母不允许 "my parents don't
allow it"). 准许 is formal and specifically official/institutional —
authorities, regulations, or bureaucratic procedures granting permission
(未经准许不得入内 "no entry without authorisation") — and is not natural in
casual speech.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 准许 as formal, used for official authorisation,
against 允许's general and unmarked register.

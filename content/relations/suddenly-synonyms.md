## Distinctions
Both mean "suddenly" and are close to interchangeable as adverbs
(突然/忽然下起雨来 "it suddenly started raining"). The key difference is
part of speech: 突然 can also work as an adjective modifying a noun directly
(突然的变化 "a sudden change," 情况很突然 "the situation was sudden") — a use
忽然 does not have. 忽然 is adverb-only, always describing how an action
unfolded, never describing a noun by itself.

## Corpus evidence
Lexicographic (L): 现汉 marks 突然 with both adjective and adverb uses, while
忽然 is adverb-only.

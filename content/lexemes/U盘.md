U, from "USB," + 盘 "disk, drive" (see [[盘]]; the same 盘 as in 硬盘 "hard
disk" and 光盘 "optical disc," extended from its base sense "plate, tray" to
a computing storage medium) → literally "USB disk." The everyday, fully
standard word for a USB flash drive nationwide.

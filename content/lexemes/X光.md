X, from the English designation of the ray (itself from the "unknown"
sense of *x* in Wilhelm Röntgen's original naming), + 光 "light, ray" (see
[[光]]) → literally "X-ray/light." The everyday clinical and general term
(照X光 "get an X-ray taken," X光片 "X-ray film/image"); X射线 is the more
formal/technical physics term for the ray itself, with X光 the word people
actually use for the medical procedure and image.

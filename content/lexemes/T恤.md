T (the letter, describing the shirt's T-shaped silhouette when laid flat) +
恤, a transliteration syllable borrowed purely for its sound (*xù*, echoing
English "shirt"); the character's own meaning "to pity, relieve" (see 体恤)
plays no role here — it is a sound-only phonetic loan, the same principle as
[[沙发]] "sofa." Fully mainstream and dictionary-recorded; T恤衫 with the
explicit "shirt" suffix is a common fuller form.

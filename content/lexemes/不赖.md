不 "not" + 赖 in its colloquial sense "bad, poor" (as in 好赖 "good or bad") →
"not bad," a casual, mildly approving assessment: 这饭做得不赖. Northern
colloquial speech; the HSK-taught equivalent is 不错, which is
register-neutral and works everywhere 不赖 does, plus writing.

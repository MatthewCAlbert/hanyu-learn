AA, from English usage abbreviations (often folk-explained as "Algebraic
Average" or "Acting Appointment," though the exact origin is unsettled) +
制 "system" (as in 制度, [[制]]) → "the AA system" — splitting a bill evenly
among everyone present, rather than one person treating. 咱们AA制吧 "let's go
Dutch." A genuinely spoken, dictionary-recorded word (not a written-only
internet convention) — pronounced with the English letter names, "AA zhì" —
common in everyday speech nationwide, not literary or formal.

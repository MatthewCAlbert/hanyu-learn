死 "to the death" + 党 "a group, a faction" (as in 政党) → originally a
faction bound together to the death (a now-dated, more negative political/
factional sense). The modern everyday sense, gender-neutral, is a close,
utterly loyal friend — a "ride or die": 他是我死党. Casual and warm in speech
and chat; the older factional sense is largely historical and does not carry
the friendly tone.

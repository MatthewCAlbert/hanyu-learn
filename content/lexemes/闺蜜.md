闺 "a woman's private chamber" (as in 闺女 "daughter") + 蜜 "honey, sweet" →
a close female friend one shares one's private life with. Now a fully
mainstream, dictionary-recorded colloquialism, at home in speech, chat, and
casual writing (magazine features, social media) alike — one of the more
formal-leaning entries in this set, though still not used in official
registers. Specifically a *female* friendship term; 死党 or 哥们 cover the
gender-neutral or male equivalent.

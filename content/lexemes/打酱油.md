Literally 打 "[light verb] to buy/fetch" + 酱油 "soy sauce" — an errand to buy
soy sauce, i.e. a small, unremarkable task. It became a catchphrase after a
2008 street-interview clip in which a bystander, asked for his opinion on a
scandal, deflected with 我是来打酱油的 ("I'm just here to buy soy sauce" — not
my business, no comment). Now a lexicalised joke expression for staying
uninvolved or professing ignorance/indifference, still recognisably tied to
its meme origin. Casual, humorous; not for formal writing. Confidence medium:
the phrase's currency and precise nuance are attested mainly through media
usage rather than a settled dictionary sense (its literal soy-sauce meaning
remains the primary headword sense in 现汉).

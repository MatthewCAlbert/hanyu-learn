靠 "to rely on" + 谱 "a musical/reference score, a sense of what's expected"
(as in 离谱 "off the mark, outrageous") → something you can rely on because it
matches expectations: trustworthy, sound, sensible (这个人很靠谱, 这个计划不靠谱).
Originated as regional/informal usage and is now included in recent 现汉
printings as a standard colloquial adjective; still informal, not used in
official writing, but no longer niche slang.

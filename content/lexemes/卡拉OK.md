A phonetic loan from Japanese *karaoke* (itself 空 "empty" + English
"orchestra"), transliterated 卡拉 (*kǎlā*, sound only) + the retained English
"OK." One of the earliest and most established Latin-letter loanwords in
modern Mandarin, long since standard rather than fringe — it appears in
现代汉语词典 as a headword. 唱卡拉OK "to sing karaoke." The newer colloquial
verb K歌 (from KTV + 歌 "song") now competes with it in casual speech, but
卡拉OK remains the base noun for the activity/genre itself.

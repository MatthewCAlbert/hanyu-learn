得 "to obtain, to fit" + 劲 "strength, energy, the right feel" → something
that feels right or comfortable — a good fit, a satisfying stretch, a body
position without strain: 这个姿势得劲, 干这活儿真得劲. Often used negated to
flag mild discomfort or unease: 不得劲 "off, not feeling quite right" (physical
or situational). Northern and central-plains colloquial; 现汉 marks it
regional (方言). Confidence medium: well documented as a topolect term, but
its exact geographic spread and currency vary more than the fully mainstream
entries in this set.

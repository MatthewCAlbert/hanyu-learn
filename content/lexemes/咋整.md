A Northeastern (Dongbei) regional variant of [[咋办]], swapping 办 "handle"
for the Dongbei-flavoured light verb 整 "do, make, get" (as in the regional
整点儿 "get some/do a bit"). 这可咋整? carries a stronger regional/comedic
flavour than 咋办 — closely associated with Northeastern speech and the
Dongbei-accented comedy that popularised it nationwide, so it reads as
regionally marked even to speakers who use it. Confidence medium: the
underlying pieces (咋 + 整) are individually well documented, but the fused
expression itself is dialectal usage rather than a formal dictionary
headword.

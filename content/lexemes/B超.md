B, from the technical "B-mode" (brightness mode) of ultrasound imaging, +
超, clipped from 超声波 "ultrasonic wave" (超 "to exceed," here "beyond
[audible frequency]," see [[超]]) → literally "B-mode ultrasound." The
standard everyday clinical term nationwide — 做个B超 "get an ultrasound
done," very commonly said around pregnancy check-ups — rather than the
fuller technical 超声波检查.

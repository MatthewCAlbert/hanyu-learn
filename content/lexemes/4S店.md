4S, from the four English services the dealership model bundles — Sale,
Service, Spare parts, Survey — + 店 "shop" (see [[店]]) → an authorized
brand dealership that also handles repairs and parts, as opposed to an
independent showroom or garage. Standard, everyday term across the
mainland car market (去4S店保养 "get it serviced at the dealership");
not used in Taiwan in the same way.

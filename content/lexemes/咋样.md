Colloquial northern contraction of 怎么样, built on the same 咋 (*zǎ*, "how")
already covered by [[咋]]. 今天咋样? "how's today going?" Speech and chat only;
写作业写得咋样了? is fine to a friend, out of place in formal writing, where
怎么样 is the standard form.

Colloquial interrogative "what," the spoken/chat counterpart of textbook 什么.
Northern in origin and still northern-associated, but now widely understood
across Mainland chat and speech. Not slang by default: register is colloquial,
context is speech/chat, region is northern, currency is current — four facts,
not one label.

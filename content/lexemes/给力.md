给 "to give" + 力 "strength, power" → literally "giving strength." Rose from
internet slang (c. 2010) to mainstream colloquial use fast enough that it now
appears in 现汉's supplementary entries: as an adjective, "impressive, does
the job well" (这个电影真给力); as a verb, "to come through, deliver"
(兄弟们给力!). Casual and upbeat; still out of place in formal writing, but no
longer marked as fringe internet slang the way it was a decade ago.

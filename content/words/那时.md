## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + 时 "time." Points to a time already established in the
discourse, past or (less often) future: 那时我还小 "I was young then."

## Notes
那时 / 那时候 / 那会儿 (see [[那会儿]]) are near-synonyms, roughly formal →
colloquial. Mirrors [[这时]] "at this moment."

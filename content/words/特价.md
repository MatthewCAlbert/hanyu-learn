## Why this combination

特 ("special," as in 特别) plus 价 ("price," as in 价格) names a
specially reduced price: a bargain.

## Notes

特价商品 ("bargain-priced goods") and 特价出售 ("sold at a special price")
both build on this word — close in effect to 打折 (covered elsewhere in
this app), though 特价 names the resulting reduced price itself rather
than the act of discounting.

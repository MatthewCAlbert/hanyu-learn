## Why this combination
开 "to open" (see also 开发, 开始) combined with 放 "to release, to let
go, to set free" (as in 放假 "to have a holiday," literally "to release
[from duty]"): to open and release — to open up, whether a flower, a
place, or a policy.

## Notes
开放 covers a flower blooming (花开放了 "the flower has bloomed"), a
place open to visitors (对外开放 "open to the outside"), and, as an
adjective, an open-minded attitude (思想开放 "open-minded") — three
senses unified by the same core image of opening outward.

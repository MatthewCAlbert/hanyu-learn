## Why this combination

动 ("to move," as in 运动) plus 画 ("to draw, a picture," as in 图画)
plus 片 ("a flat piece, a film reel," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 照片/名片) names a film made of drawings brought to motion: an
animated film.

## Notes

看动画片 ("to watch cartoons") is the standard phrase for children's TV
— 动画 alone (dropping 片) names the broader medium of animation, while
动画片 specifically names a finished animated film or show.

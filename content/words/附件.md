## Why this combination
附 "attached, appended" plus 件 "an item, a piece" gives exactly "an
attached item" — the standard word for supplementary material accompanying
a main document.

## Notes
附件 anchors 邮件附件 "email attachment," 合同附件 "contract appendix" —
also names mechanical add-on parts (机器附件 "machine accessories"), all
sharing the sense of something supplementary attached to a main item.

## Why this combination
家 "household, family" + 长 (*zhǎng*) "head, elder, chief" (see [[校长]],
[[队长]]). Classically the patriarch; in modern school contexts, a pupil's
parent or guardian — 家长会 "parents' meeting," 请家长 "send for your parents."

## Notes
长 is *zhǎng* here (head / elder), the same as in [[长大]], [[成长]]. 家长制
"paternalism / autocratic rule."

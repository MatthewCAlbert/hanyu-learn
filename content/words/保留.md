## Why this combination
保 "to protect, to keep" (see also 保护, 保险) combined with 留 "to
remain, to stay, to leave [something] behind" (as in 留学 "to study
abroad," literally "to stay and study"): to protect and keep in place —
to retain.

## Notes
保留 covers keeping something as it is (保留传统 "to preserve tradition")
and, in speech, "to hold back an opinion, to have reservations" (对这个
计划有保留 "to have reservations about this plan") — the two senses
share the idea of not fully letting go or committing.

## Why this combination
低 "low" plus the comparative preposition 于 "than" gives exactly "lower
than" — a formal written comparison word, parallel to 高于 "higher than."

## Notes
低于 is used for measurable comparisons: 气温低于零度 "the temperature is
below zero," 低于预期 "below expectations" — a written/formal register,
where speech would use 比…低.

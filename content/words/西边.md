## Why this combination
西 "west" + the locative suffix 边. One of the four compass members of the 边
series — 东边, 南边, 西边, 北边 — alongside the up/down and front/back ones.

## Notes
西面 / 西部 are the synonyms with 面 / 部 as suffix. 西 also = "Western
(culture)" in 西方, 西餐.

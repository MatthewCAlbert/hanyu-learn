## Why this combination
大 "big, all" plus 伙 "a group, a company (of people)," with the
retroflex -儿 suffix common in northern colloquial speech, gives exactly
"the whole group" — a colloquial word for everyone present.

## Notes
大伙儿 is a spoken, informal register word: 大伙儿都同意 "everyone
agreed" — close to 大家, but distinctly colloquial/northern in flavor,
common in Beijing-area speech.

## Why this combination
Like [[星期天]], Sunday takes 星期 + a "day" word rather than a number, but here
the literary 日 instead of colloquial 天. 日 is the more formal register — hence
星期日 on calendars and official notices, 星期天 in speech.

## Notes
周日 is the 周-series form. Historically 日 in the day-names pointed to the Sun
(日曜日), the luminary that ruled Sunday.

## Why this combination
引 is "to draw, to lead" (as in 吸引, 引导), and 起 here is "to rise, to
arise" (as in 起来, 引起's own second half also seen in 想起). "To draw
[something] up into rising" is exactly what causing an effect or a
reaction — attention, controversy, illness — looks like: something that
was not there before is drawn up into existence.

Rated "semi" for 起's abstract "to arise/come into being" sense here,
distinct from its more concrete "to get up" sense most learners meet
first.

## Notes
引起 takes an abstract effect as its object (引起注意 "to attract
attention," 引起争议 "to cause controversy") — it does not describe causing a
concrete physical object to exist, which would use 造成 or 产生 instead.

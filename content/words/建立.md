## Why this combination
建 "to construct, to build [an institution]" (as in 建设 "to build up")
combined with 立 "to stand upright, to set up, to establish": to
construct something and set it standing — to establish, especially
something abstract or institutional rather than a physical building.

## Notes
建立 takes abstract or organisational objects — 建立关系 "to establish a
relationship," 建立公司 "to found a company" — while 建, 盖 or 建设 are
used for constructing actual physical structures.

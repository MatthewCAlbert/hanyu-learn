## Why this combination
距 carries an extended "apart, separated by a span" sense — its original,
literal meaning was a rooster's spur, and "apart" is a later phonetic-loan
use with no obvious pictorial link to spacing — while 离 is "to leave, to
be separated from" (离开, 分离). Two words for separation reinforce each
other into the standard noun for distance.

Rated "semi" specifically because 距's "apart" sense is a loan onto an
unrelated original word, not a natural extension a learner could guess.

## Notes
距离 works as both noun (距离很远, "the distance is great") and preposition
(距离北京不远, "not far from Beijing") — the second use takes a location
directly, no "from" particle needed.

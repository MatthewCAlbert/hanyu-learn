## Why this combination
老 "old, of long standing" (the same 老 as 老同学, 老顾客 — not "aged") +
[[朋友]] "friend" → a friend one has known for a long time: 我们是老朋友了.

## Notes
老 as "long-standing" is a productive prefix: 老同事, 老邻居, 老主顾. The
"aged friend" reading would be 年老的朋友.

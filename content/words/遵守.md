## Why this combination
遵 "to follow with due respect" (see 遵's own etymology) and 守 "to guard,
to keep to" are near-synonyms combined into the standard formal word for
compliance.

## Notes
遵守 always takes a rule or agreement as its object: 遵守纪律 "to observe
discipline," 遵守交通规则 "to obey traffic rules" — formal register for
compliance with an established standard.

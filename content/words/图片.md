## Why this combination
图 "picture, diagram, chart" (see [[地图]], [[图书馆]]) + 片 "a thin flat piece,
a card" (see [[照片]], [[影片]]). A picture on a sheet — a printed illustration,
and now any digital image: 上传图片, 一张图片.

## Notes
图片 covers drawings, diagrams and photos alike; [[照片]] is specifically a
photograph. 图 alone = "picture / to seek": 看图说话, 图方便.

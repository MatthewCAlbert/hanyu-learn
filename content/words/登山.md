## Why this combination

登 ("to ascend," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 登录) takes
山 ("mountain") as its object: to climb a mountain.

## Notes

登山运动 ("mountaineering as a sport") and 登山鞋 ("hiking/climbing
boots") both build on this word — the more formal/sport-specific
cousin of the everyday 爬山, "to climb a mountain [lit. crawl + mountain]."

## Why this combination
分 "a division, a fraction, a part" + 钟 "clock, bell" → a division on the
clock. 分 alone is "minute" when telling the time (三点十分 "3:10"); 分钟 is the
measure word for a *duration* — 等了十分钟.

## Notes
The 分 / 分钟 split mirrors 点 (o'clock) vs. [[小时]] (hour-long span). 秒
"second" behaves like 分.

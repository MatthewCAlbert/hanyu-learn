## Why this combination
当 "to face, to be current with" and 前 "front, ahead" combine directly:
what stands right in front of one now — the present moment.

## Notes
当前 is a formal written-register word for "currently, at present":
当前形势 "the current situation," 当前任务 "the present task" — a step up
in formality from everyday 现在.

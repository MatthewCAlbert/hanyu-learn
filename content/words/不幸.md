## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 幸 "fortunate, lucky" gives exactly "not fortunate" —
as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
不幸 works as noun ("a misfortune," 家庭不幸 "family misfortune") and
adverb/adjective ("unfortunately," 不幸去世 "unfortunately passed away") —
a formal, somewhat literary register for describing sad events.

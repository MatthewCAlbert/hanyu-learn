## Why this combination
有 "there is / exists" + 人 "person" → "there exists a person (who…)" = someone.
Also "occupied": 这儿有人吗？ "is this seat taken?"

## Notes
有人说… "some people say / it is said." Contrast 没有人 "no one," 有的人 "some
(of the) people" (see [[有的]]).

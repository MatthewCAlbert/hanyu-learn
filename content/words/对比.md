## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 对 ("facing, paired," as in 对话) and 比 ("to
compare," as in 比较) both independently name setting two things
alongside each other. Pairing them gives the word for a deliberate
contrast.

## Notes

对比 emphasizes the contrast/difference revealed by comparison (形成鲜明
对比, "to form a sharp contrast") — 比较 is the more neutral, general verb
"to compare," without necessarily implying a stark difference.

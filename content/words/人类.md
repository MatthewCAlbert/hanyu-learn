## Why this combination
人 "person" combined with 类 "category, kind, type": the human species
considered as a single category — humanity as a whole, as opposed to any
individual person.

## Notes
人类 is the scientific and philosophical register for the species (人类
历史 "human history," 人类学 "anthropology"); 人们 refers to people
collectively but informally in everyday contexts, not to the species as a
biological or historical category.

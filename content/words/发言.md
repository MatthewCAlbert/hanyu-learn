## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 发 "to send out, to issue" (see also 发出, 发表)
takes 言 "speech, words" (as in 语言 "language") as its object —
literally "to issue words" — to speak up, especially in a formal
setting.

## Notes
发言 names speaking up in a meeting or public forum (在会上发言 "to
speak at the meeting," 发言人 "spokesperson") — 说话, the everyday verb
"to speak," carries no such formal, turn-taking context.

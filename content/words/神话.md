## Why this combination

神 ("a god, divine, spirit," as in 精神, "spirit/energy") plus 话
("speech, a story," as in 说话) names stories of the divine: myths.

## Notes

神话故事 ("mythological stories") and 神话人物 ("mythological figures")
both build on this word; figuratively, 神话 also names a "legend" or
"miracle" in modern usage (创造神话, "to create a legend/miracle").

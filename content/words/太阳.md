## Why this combination
太 "supreme, utmost" + 阳 "yang — the bright, warm, active principle,"
originally "the sunlit side of a hill." "The supreme yang" is the source of all
brightness and warmth: the sun. 太阳 pairs with 太阴 (an old name for the moon).

## Notes
晒太阳 "sit in the sun." 阳 "yang / sun" also in 阳光 "sunshine," 阴阳.
太阳穴 "the temple (of the head)."

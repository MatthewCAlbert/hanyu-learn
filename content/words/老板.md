## Why this combination
老, here a familiar prefix rather than "old" (as in 老师), combined with
板 "a board, a plank" — traditionally explained as originally referring
to the shop counter or the board a merchant conducted business over,
extended metonymically to the proprietor behind it. The exact path from
"board" to "proprietor" is not securely documented.

## Notes
老板 names the owner or head of a business, from a small shop to a
company (我老板 "my boss"); 经理, by contrast, is a hired manager and not
necessarily the owner.

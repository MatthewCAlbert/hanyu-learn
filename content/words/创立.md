## Why this combination
创 "to create, to originate" and 立 "to establish, to set up" are
near-synonyms combined into a formal word for founding something new.

## Notes
创立 anchors 创立公司 "to found a company," 创立学说 "to originate a
theory" — always about bringing something into existence for the first
time, a more formal register than plain 建立.

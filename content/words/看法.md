## Why this combination
看 "to look at" + 法 "way, method" (see [[办法]], [[方法]], [[想法]]) → one's way
of looking at a matter, i.e. a considered view: 谈谈你的看法, 对这件事的看法.

## Notes
看法 = a settled view on something; [[想法]] = an idea / take; [[意见]] =
opinion, often for a decision or as a complaint; [[观点]] = a stated position.

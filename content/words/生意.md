## Why this combination
生 "life, to grow, to arise" combined with 意 "idea, intention" (see also
意义): originally, in classical and literary use, "the life-force, the
vitality [of growing things]" — a sense that survives in some literary
contexts. The modern everyday meaning "business, trade" developed as a
separate, later usage, read with a neutral tone on 意 (生意 *shēngyi*
rather than *shēngyì*) and is not a transparent extension of the literal
components.

## Notes
生意 in everyday speech means "business, commerce" (做生意 "to do
business," 生意好 "business is good") — this is overwhelmingly the sense
learners meet; the older "vitality" sense (生意盎然 "bursting with life")
is a separate, more literary register.

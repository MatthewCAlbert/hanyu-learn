## Why this combination
似 carries its "to resemble, to seem like" sense (covered in this app's
entry for 似乎), and 的 here functions as a sentence-final particle
marking a comparison — a distinct grammatical role from the far more
common possessive/modifying 的 (de). Placed at the end of a phrase, 似的
marks the whole preceding description as an approximation or comparison.

Rated "semi" because 的's comparison-marking role here is a specific,
non-obvious grammatical pattern, easy to conflate with the everyday
possessive particle that shares its written form.

## Notes
似的 always follows the noun or phrase it likens something to (像雪似的,
"like snow") — it closes out a comparison rather than opening one, unlike
English "like," which comes first.

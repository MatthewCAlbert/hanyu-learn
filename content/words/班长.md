## Why this combination
班 "a class (of pupils); a squad; a work shift" (see [[上班]], [[班级]]) + 长
(*zhǎng*) "head, chief" — the "-chief" suffix of [[队长]], [[组长]], [[校长]].
In school, the elected pupil in charge of a class; in the army, a squad leader.

## Notes
长 is *zhǎng* here. The title series by unit size: 组长 → 班长 → [[队长]] →
[[校长]].

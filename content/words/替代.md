## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 替 ("to replace," as in 替换) and 代 ("to
substitute for," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 代替, its
reverse-order twin) both independently name standing in for something.
Pairing them gives a formal word for replacement.

## Notes

替代 and 代替 (covered elsewhere in this app) are near-identical in
meaning, differing mainly in which syllable leads and in slight register
— 替代 skews toward technical/written contexts (替代品, "a substitute/
replacement product").

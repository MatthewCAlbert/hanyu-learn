## Why this combination

幼 ("young, immature," as in 幼儿, "an infant/young child") plus 儿
("child," as in 儿童) plus 园 ("a garden, an enclosed place for growing
things," as in 公园) names a garden-like place for young children: a
kindergarten — a calque of the German "Kindergarten," lit.
"children's garden."

## Notes

幼儿园 precedes 小学 ("primary school") in a child's education, and
shares its "garden" metaphor with 花园 ("flower garden") and 公园
("park") — all built on the same productive 园 root.

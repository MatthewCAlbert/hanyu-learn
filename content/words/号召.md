## Why this combination
号 (here "to call out," as in 号令) and 召 "to summon" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for publicly calling people to action.

## Notes
号召 anchors 发出号召 "to issue a call (to action)," 响应号召 "to answer
the call" — a formal, often political/organizational register word for
rallying collective action.

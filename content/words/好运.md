## Why this combination
好 "good" and 运 "fortune, fate" combine directly into "good fortune" —
as straightforward as a compound gets.

## Notes
好运 is often used as a short, standalone wish (祝你好运 "good luck to
you"); 幸运 is the more common adjective form ("lucky") in everyday
descriptions of a person or event.

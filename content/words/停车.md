## Why this combination
停 "to stop, to halt, to park" + 车 "vehicle" as its object. Both a brief halt
(前面停车) and leaving it parked (在门口停车). Verb-object, so it splits:
停一下车. [[停车场]] "car park."

## Notes
停 "stop" also as a resultative complement: 停下 "come to a stop." 停电
"power cut," 停课 "classes suspended."

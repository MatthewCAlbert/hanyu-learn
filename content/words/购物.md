## Why this combination
购 ("to purchase," covered elsewhere in this app) takes 物 ("a thing, an
object") as its object: purchasing goods, i.e. shopping. Fully
compositional.

## Notes
购物 is the formal/written register for shopping (购物中心, "shopping
center/mall") — 买东西 ("to buy things") is the everyday spoken
equivalent.

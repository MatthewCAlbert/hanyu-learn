## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 轻 ("light in weight," as in 轻重) and 松 (here in
its "loose, relaxed" sense, covered in this app's hanzi entry — a
character that itself merged two originally distinct words, "pine tree"
and "loose"). Something light and unclenched is, both physically and
mentally, relaxed.

## Notes
轻松 covers both a relaxed feeling (心情轻松, "feeling relaxed") and an
easy task (工作很轻松, "the work is easy/undemanding") — the same "light,
unburdened" image applied to a mood or a workload.

## Why this combination
机 (here "mechanism," from 机器 "machine") and 制 "system, to regulate"
combine into "the regulating mechanism of a system" — the standard word
for how a system's parts work together.

## Notes
机制 is used abstractly across science, economics, and administration:
市场机制 "market mechanism," 竞争机制 "competition mechanism" — always
about underlying structure and process, not a physical device.

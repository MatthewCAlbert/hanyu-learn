## Why this combination
见 "to see" + 面 "face" — literally "to see (someone's) face," i.e. to meet in
person. Verb-object, so it splits and cannot take another object: the person met
goes in with 跟 / 和 — 跟朋友见面, never *见面朋友.

## Notes
第一次见面 "first meeting." 见 alone can also mean "meet" (见老师), but 见面
specifically implies face-to-face.

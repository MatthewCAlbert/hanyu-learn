## Why this combination
间 (here "in between") plus 接 "to connect" gives exactly "connecting
through something in between" — the standard word for something mediated
rather than direct, opposite of 直接 "direct."

## Notes
间接 anchors 间接原因 "an indirect cause," 间接影响 "indirect influence" —
always implies an intermediary step or cause standing between two things.

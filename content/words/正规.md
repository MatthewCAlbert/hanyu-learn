## Why this combination
正 "correct, proper" plus 规 "rule, standard" gives exactly "conforming
to proper standards" — the standard word for something following
established, legitimate procedure.

## Notes
正规 anchors 正规军 "regular army (as opposed to militia)," 正规渠道
"official/proper channels," 正规医院 "a properly licensed hospital" —
always implies contrast with something informal, improvised, or
unlicensed.

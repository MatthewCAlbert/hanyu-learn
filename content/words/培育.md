## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 培 ("to cultivate," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 培养/培训) and 育 ("to nurture, to raise," as in 教育,
"education [lit. teach + nurture]") both independently name fostering
growth. Pairing them gives the formal word for cultivation, biological
or figurative.

## Notes

培育新品种 ("to breed a new variety [of plant/animal]") and 培育人才 ("to
cultivate talent") both build on this word — 培育 leans toward the
careful, extended process of raising something from an early stage,
close to but more agricultural/developmental than 培养.

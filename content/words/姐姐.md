## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term (see [[妈妈]]). The reduplication marks it as the intimate
address form; the neutral-tone second syllable confirms it. Chinese names
siblings by relative age: 姐姐 older sister, 妹妹 younger.

## Notes
Also a friendly address to a slightly older woman (王姐, 大姐). Contrast
[[小姐]] "miss," a different word.

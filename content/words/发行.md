## Why this combination
发 "to release, to send out" and 行 "to circulate, to go" combine
directly: releasing something so it circulates publicly.

## Notes
发行 spans publishing (发行量 "circulation [of a publication]"), finance
(发行股票 "to issue stock"), and media (发行电影 "to release a film") — one
word for putting something new into public circulation.

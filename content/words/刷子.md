## Why this combination

刷 ("a brush; to brush," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
牙刷/刷牙) plus the nominal suffix 子 names the everyday tool: a brush.

## Notes

刷子 is the general word for any brush (油漆刷子, "a paint brush") — 牙刷
(covered elsewhere in this app) names one specific kind for teeth.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 模 ("a mold, a pattern to copy from," covered
elsewhere in this app) and 型 ("a mold, a type," also covered elsewhere)
both independently name a template or scaled representation. Pairing
them gives the standard word for a physical or conceptual model.

## Notes
模型 covers both physical scale models (飞机模型, "a model airplane") and
abstract conceptual ones (数学模型, "a mathematical model") — the same
"a representative template" idea applied concretely and abstractly.

## Why this combination
[[马来]] + 文 "writing / language," the same mould as 法语, 日语 and [[英文]]. The stem
is still a country or people name — usually a phonetic loan — so do not re-
read those characters as a new story.

## Notes
See [[马来]]. 文 leans written / materials.

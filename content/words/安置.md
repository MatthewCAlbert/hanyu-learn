## Why this combination

安 ("peaceful, to settle," as in 安全, "safe") plus 置 ("to place, to
set," as in 位置/布置) names settling something into its proper place.

## Notes

安置灾民 ("to resettle disaster victims") and 妥善安置 ("to properly
arrange/settle") are common frames — 安置 implies finding a stable,
suitable place for people or things, more purposeful than plain 放
("to put").

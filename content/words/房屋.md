## Why this combination
房 "room, house" (as in 房子 "house") and 屋 "house, dwelling" (as in
屋子 "room") are close synonyms, paired into the formal, collective word
for housing/buildings.

## Notes
房屋 is the formal, often official or written register for housing as a
category (房屋出租 "house for rent," 房屋建筑 "building construction") —
房子, the everyday word, is what is actually used in casual conversation
about a specific house.

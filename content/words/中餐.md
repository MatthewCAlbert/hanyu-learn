## Why this combination
中 abbreviates 中国 (see [[中国]], [[中文]]) + 餐 "a meal, cuisine" (see
[[早餐]], [[快餐]]) → Chinese-style food, as against 西餐 (see [[西餐]]). A
separate sense, "the noon meal" (中 "middle" + 餐), overlaps with 午餐 in some
usage.

## Notes
中餐厅 "Chinese restaurant," 吃中餐. The 中 = "China" vs. 中 = "midday" ambiguity
is usually resolved by context.

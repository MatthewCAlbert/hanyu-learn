## Why this combination
以 "taking (a point) as reference" + 下 "below." "From (that point) downward" —
a level or amount and everything under it (十岁以下 "under ten," 零度以下), and,
in a text, "what comes next" (以下是名单 "the following is the list").

## Notes
Mirror of [[以上]]. The 以 + locative frame: [[以后]], [[以前]], [[以外]],
以内.

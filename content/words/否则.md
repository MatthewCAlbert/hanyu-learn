## Why this combination
否 is "not, no" (as covered in this app's entry for 是否), and 则 here
carries its conjunction sense "then" (covered in this app's hanzi entry
for 则 — a rule cut to match a consequence). "If not, then [the
alternative follows]" is precisely what "otherwise" states.

## Notes
否则 always follows the condition it negates: 快点，否则就迟了 ("hurry up, or
else we'll be late") — it never opens a sentence on its own without a
preceding condition to react against.

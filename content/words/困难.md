## Why this combination
困 "trapped, hemmed in, hard-pressed" and 难 "difficult, hard" are close
synonyms, paired into a fuller word for difficulty — both the adjective
and, just as commonly, the noun naming an obstacle to be overcome.

## Notes
困难 works equally as an adjective (这个问题很困难 "this problem is
difficult") and as a countable noun (克服困难 "to overcome difficulties,"
生活困难 "hardship in life"); 难 alone remains the lighter, more
colloquial word for "difficult" in everyday speech.

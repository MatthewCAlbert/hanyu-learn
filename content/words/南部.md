## Why this combination
南 "south" (as in 南方) combined with 部 "a part, a section" (see also
西部, 部门): the southern section — the south of a country or region.

## Notes
南部 follows the same "direction + 部" pattern as 西部 and 东部, naming
the internal southern region of a specific place (中国南部 "southern
China") — 南方, its close relative, refers to "the South" more broadly
as a cultural/climatic region, not a bounded internal section.

## Why this combination
许 in its sense "perhaps, possibly" (或许) + 也 "also, likewise" as a mild
softener — "it may also be that…". An adverb of uncertainty: 他也许不知道.

## Notes
也许 / 或许 (perhaps) · [[可能]] (possible; also adj./noun) · 大概 (probably,
higher confidence). 许 "allow" is a separate common sense (许可, 不许).

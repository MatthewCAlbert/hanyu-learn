## Why this combination
另 "another, other [than the one just mentioned]" combined with 一方面
"one aspect/side" (方面 "aspect," see also 全面): the other aspect [of
the matter] — used to pivot to a second, contrasting point.

## Notes
另一方面 pairs with a preceding 一方面 "on the one hand..." to structure
a two-sided point (一方面...另一方面... "on the one hand... on the other
hand...") — it can also open a sentence alone when the first side was
already implied by context.

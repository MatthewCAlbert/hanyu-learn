## Why this combination

名 ("famous, well-known," as in 有名) plus 人 ("person") names a person
who is famous — a celebrity.

## Notes

名人 is a broad, register-neutral word for anyone well known (historical
figures included); 明星 is the narrower, glitzier "star" of entertainment
or sport.

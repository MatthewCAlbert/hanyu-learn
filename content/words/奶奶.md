## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term (see [[妈妈]]), toneless second syllable. 奶 "milk / breast"
became the nursery word for the woman who nursed and raised you. Chinese
kinship splits the grandparents by side: 奶奶 is your father's mother.

## Notes
Mother's mother is 外婆 / 姥姥. Also a respectful address for any elderly woman
(老奶奶).

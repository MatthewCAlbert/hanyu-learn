## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 负 "to bear, to carry [a burden]" takes 责
"responsibility" as its object — literally "to shoulder responsibility."
Like other verb-object words in this dataset (起床, 睡觉), it behaves
grammatically like a phrase: 负这个责任 "to bear this [specific]
responsibility" can insert a modifier between the two halves.

## Notes
负责 can take 对 to mark what one answers to (对结果负责 "responsible for
the outcome") or 由 to mark who is in charge (由他负责 "he is the one in
charge") — flexible slotting typical of verb-object compounds.

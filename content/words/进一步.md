## Why this combination
进 "to advance, to move forward" (as in 进入 "to enter") combined with
一步 "one step": to advance one further step — to take things further,
to a greater degree than before.

## Notes
进一步 sits before a verb as an adverb urging further action or depth
(进一步了解 "to understand further/in more depth," 进一步发展 "to develop
further") — it stresses continuing progress from an already-established
point, not starting from scratch.

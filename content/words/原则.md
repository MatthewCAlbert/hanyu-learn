## Why this combination
原 carries its "original, primary" sense (the same root behind 源,
covered elsewhere in this app, before 氵 split off to hold the literal
"spring/source" meaning), and 则 is "a rule, a fixed standard," covered
elsewhere in this app. A foundational, original rule underlying
everything else is exactly a principle.

## Notes
原则上 ("in principle") is a common hedge — agreeing to the underlying
rule while leaving room for exceptions in specific cases, much like its
English equivalent.

## Why this combination
左 "left(-hand)" + the locative suffix 边 (as in [[右边]], 上边, [[旁边]]).
右边 is its mirror.

## Notes
左 classically the junior side in ranking (虚左以待 leaves the honoured seat
empty — on the *left* in that period, conventions varied), and "the Left" in
politics. The bare direction word is neutral.

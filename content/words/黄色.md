## Why this combination
黄 "yellow" + the "-colour" suffix 色 (see [[颜色]], [[红色]]). 黄 alone in
黄河 "the Yellow River," 黄金 "gold," 蛋黄 "egg yolk."

## Notes
The "pornographic" sense (黄色电影, 扫黄 "crack down on porn") is a 20th-century
Chinese development — unlike Western "blue" for the same idea. The colour word
is neutral.

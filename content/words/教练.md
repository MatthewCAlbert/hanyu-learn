## Why this combination
教 "to teach" combined with 练 "to practise, to drill" (see also 训练):
one who teaches through drilled practice — a coach or instructor,
especially in sports or a practical skill.

## Notes
教练 names the person, most often for a sport or a practical skill
(篮球教练 "basketball coach," 驾驶教练 "driving instructor") — 老师, the
general word for "teacher," is used for academic instruction and is not
the natural word for a sports or driving coach.

## Why this combination
请 "to invite" (see [[请问]], [[请求]]) + 客 "guest" (see [[客人]], [[顾客]]) as
its object — to have someone as one's guest, and so to foot the bill:
今天我请客 "it's on me," 请客吃饭. Verb-object: 请了一次客.

## Notes
请客 = pay for others (host); 做客 = be a guest at someone's home; 客气 =
stand on ceremony. Who 请客 (and the reluctance to let the other pay) is a
social ritual in its own right.

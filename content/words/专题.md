## Why this combination
专 "specialised, focused exclusively [on one thing]" (see also 专业,
专门) combined with 题 "a topic" (see also 题目, 话题): a topic focused on
exclusively — a special, dedicated subject.

## Notes
专题 names a single topic given dedicated, focused treatment (专题
报道 "a special/feature report," 专题讨论 "a topic-focused discussion")
— 话题, its neighbour, is a general conversation topic, without the
implication of dedicated, in-depth treatment 专题 carries.

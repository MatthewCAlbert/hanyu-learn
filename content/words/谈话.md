## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 谈 "to talk, to discuss" (see also 谈判) takes
话 "speech, words" (see also 话题) as its object — literally "to talk
words" — to have a conversation.

## Notes
谈话 names a conversation, especially a somewhat formal one-on-one talk
(找他谈话 "to have a talk with him," often implying a serious or formal
tone) — 说话, the everyday word for "to speak," is more general and
lighter in register.

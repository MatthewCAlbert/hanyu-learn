## Why this combination
天 "sky" + 气 "air, vapour, the qi of the atmosphere" — the state of the air
overhead. 气 is the same broad "gas / breath / energy" word as in 空气 (air),
气候 (climate).

## Notes
天气 is today's conditions; 气候 is the long-run pattern. 天气预报 "weather
forecast."

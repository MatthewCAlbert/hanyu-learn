## Why this combination
尽 "to the fullest extent" and 管 "to be in charge of, to bother about"
do not add up to "although" by any straightforward path — the
concessive-connective sense is a grammaticalized development that has to
be memorised rather than derived from the parts.

## Notes
尽管 has two unrelated jobs: the connective "although" (尽管很难，他还是
成功了 "although it was hard, he still succeeded") and the imperative "feel
free to" (尽管说 "go ahead and say it," literally "manage it to the
fullest") — easy to conflate, worth learning as two separate uses.

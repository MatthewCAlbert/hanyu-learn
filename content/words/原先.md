## Why this combination
原 "original" and 先 "before, earlier" are near-synonyms combined into a
time adverb for how things were before a change.

## Notes
原先 always contrasts with a present state: 原先住在这里 "used to live
here (implying they've since moved)" — close to 原来, largely
interchangeable, though 原先 leans slightly more formal.

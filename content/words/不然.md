## Why this combination
不 ("not") plus 然 (here in its classical independent sense "so, thus" —
the same root sense that later grammaticalized into the bare suffix seen
in 突然, 竟然, all covered elsewhere in this app) reads as "not so" —
either flatly denying a claim, or, at the head of a clause, introducing
the alternative that follows if the preceding condition doesn't hold.

Rated "semi" because 然's independent "so, thus" meaning here is a step
removed from its far more common role as an empty grammatical suffix
elsewhere.

## Notes
不然 functions much like 否则 (covered elsewhere in this app): 快点，不然
就迟到了 ("hurry up, or else we'll be late") — the two are close
synonyms, with 不然 slightly more conversational.

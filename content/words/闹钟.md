## Why this combination

闹 ("noisy, to make a commotion," covered elsewhere in this app as part
of 热闹) plus 钟 ("a clock, a bell," as in 分钟) names a clock that makes
a noisy commotion to wake someone: an alarm clock.

## Notes

定闹钟 ("to set an alarm") and 闹钟响了 ("the alarm went off") both build
on this word — 闹钟 names the physical device, distinct from 提醒, the
general verb "to remind."

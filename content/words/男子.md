## Why this combination
男 "male, man" combined with 子, the same generic "person" suffix seen in
女子 (see its entry): a male person — man.

## Notes
男子 is the formal, written counterpart to 男人, used in official and
sports contexts (男子组 "men's division," 男子汉 "a real man, a man of
character") — 男人 remains the everyday spoken word.

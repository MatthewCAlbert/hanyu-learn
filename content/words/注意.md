## Why this combination
注 here keeps its extended sense "to focus, to concentrate [on]"
(recall from its etymology: water poured toward a single point) applied
to 意 "thought, mind" — pouring one's mind toward something: to pay
attention.

## Notes
注意 is the general, neutral "to pay attention"; 小心 "to be careful"
adds a note of caution about danger that 注意 alone does not carry.

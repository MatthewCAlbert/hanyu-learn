## Why this combination
出 "out" + 口 "opening, mouth" (see [[门口]], [[路口]]) → the opening you go out
through — an exit. From "out of the country's mouth" comes 出口 "to export"
(opposite 进口), and from "out of one's mouth," 出口成章 "speak in polished
prose."

## Notes
安全出口 "emergency exit," 出口货物 "exports." 口 as "opening" also in
[[入口]], [[人口]], 窗口.

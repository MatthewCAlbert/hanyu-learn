## Why this combination
拍 (here "to shoot [with a camera]," from its "to strike/tap" sense) and
摄 "to capture, to take in" reinforce each other into the formal verb for
photography and filmmaking.

## Notes
拍摄 is the formal/technical register (拍摄现场 "filming location," 拍摄
角度 "camera angle"); everyday speech about a quick snapshot uses plain
拍 or 照 instead.

## Why this combination
斯瓦希里 is a phonetic rendering of Swahili, plus 语. 斯 is a dummy start; 瓦 is not
"tile"; 希 is not "to hope."

## Notes
A lingua franca of East Africa; Extra countries include [[肯尼亚]], [[坦桑尼亚]],
[[乌干达]].

## Why this combination
阶 carries its "steps, a staircase" sense (as in 台阶), and 段 is "a
section, a segment" (as in 一段时间, "a period of time"; 手段, "means,"
lit. "a hand-segment [of action]"). A staircase's discrete steps,
segmented, is a natural metaphor for the discrete stages of a process.

Rated "semi" for 阶's figurative extension from literal "stair-step" to
an abstract "stage."

## Notes
阶段 marks a bounded period within a larger process (初级阶段, "the
initial stage"; 发展阶段, "a stage of development") — always one part of
something ongoing, not a standalone period the way 时期 can be.

## Why this combination
成 (here "formed, established") plus 语 "expression, saying" gives
exactly "a fixed, established expression" — the standard word for the
classic four-character idioms drawn from history and literature.

## Notes
成语 anchors 成语故事 "idiom stories (the tales behind idioms)," 用成语
"to use an idiom" — usually four characters, densely allusive, and a
core part of literary and everyday Chinese.

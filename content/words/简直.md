## Why this combination
简 "simple, brief" (as in 简单 "simple") combined with 直 "straight,
direct" (as in 直到): [put] simply and directly — an intensifying
adverb for an exaggerated or hard-to-believe claim.

## Notes
简直 sits before the predicate to underline how extreme or unbelievable
a statement is (简直太好了 "it's simply wonderful," 简直不敢相信 "I
simply can't believe it") — it is emphatic rather than literal, closer
to English "practically, downright" than to any sense of "simplicity."

## Why this combination
Same 今 "present, current" as in [[今天]], with the unit swapped from 天 to 年.
去年 / 今年 / 明年 is the year row, parallel to 昨天 / 今天 / 明天.

## Notes
No preposition needed as a time word: 今年很忙 "busy this year."

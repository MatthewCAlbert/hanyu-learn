## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 迅 ("swift," covered elsewhere in this app) and
速 ("speed, fast," as in 速度) both independently name quickness. Pairing
them gives the standard formal word for rapidity.

## Notes
迅速 leans formal/written (迅速发展, "to develop rapidly") — 快 is the
everyday spoken equivalent for describing something fast.

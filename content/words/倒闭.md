## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 倒 ("to fall, to collapse," as in 倒下, "to
fall down") and 闭 ("to close," covered elsewhere in this app as part
of 封闭) both independently name a collapse into shutdown. Pairing them
gives the standard word for a business failing.

## Notes

公司倒闭 ("the company went bankrupt") and 面临倒闭 ("facing bankruptcy")
both build on this word — 倒闭 always describes a business or
enterprise ceasing operation due to financial collapse, distinct from
破产 (covered elsewhere in this app), the broader legal/financial term
for bankruptcy.

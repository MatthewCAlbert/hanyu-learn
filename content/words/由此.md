## Why this combination
由 "from, by way of" plus 此 "this" gives exactly "from this (point)" —
a formal connective marking a conclusion or consequence.

## Notes
由此 introduces a result or inference: 由此可见 "from this it can be
seen," 由此产生 "arising from this" — a written/formal register word, rare
in casual conversation.

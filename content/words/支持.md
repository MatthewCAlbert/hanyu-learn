## Why this combination
支 "to prop up, to support" (recall its etymology: a hand holding a
bamboo stick, used as a physical prop) and 持 "to hold, to grasp"
combine directly: holding something up and steady.

## Notes
支持 covers both physical support (rare in modern use) and, far more
commonly, backing of an opinion, cause or person (支持这个计划 "to
support this plan") — the physical prop generalised entirely into
figurative backing.

## Why this combination
中秋 "mid-autumn" (中 "middle" + 秋 "autumn," naming the 15th of the 8th
lunar month, autumn's midpoint) plus 节 "festival" gives exactly "the
mid-autumn festival" — a straightforward, compositional name.

## Notes
中秋节 anchors 中秋节吃月饼 "eating mooncakes for Mid-Autumn Festival,"
中秋节赏月 "moon-viewing on Mid-Autumn Festival" — one of the most
important traditional Chinese holidays, associated with family reunion
and the full moon.

## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term (see [[妈妈]]), the intimate address form, second syllable
toneless. 哥 is thought to have entered Chinese from a non-Han northern word for
"brother / father" during the Tang, displacing the classical 兄.

## Notes
兄 survives in compounds: 兄弟 (brothers), 兄长. Friendly address 大哥, or online
帅哥 "handsome guy."

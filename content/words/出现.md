## Why this combination
出 "to come out" + 现 "to appear, to become visible" (see [[现在]]) — a
near-synonym pair for coming into view or into being: 出现问题 "a problem
arose," 天上出现了彩虹.

## Notes
出现 is something coming into existence / view on its own; [[发现]] is an agent
finding it. Same 现 in both.

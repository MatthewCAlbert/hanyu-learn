## Why this combination
年 "year" combined with 底 "the bottom, the base" (see also 底下, 到底):
the bottom of the year — its very end.

## Notes
年底 names the final part of the calendar year (年底总结 "year-end
summary") — its opposite is 年初 "the beginning of the year" (see its
own entry), built the same way with 初 "beginning" in place of 底.

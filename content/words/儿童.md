## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 儿 ("child," as in 儿子) and 童 ("child," as in
童年, "childhood") both independently mean "child." Pairing them produces
the standard formal/written noun for children as a category.

## Notes
儿童 is the formal, category-level word (儿童节, Children's Day; 儿童
文学, children's literature) — for one's own kids specifically, 孩子 is
the everyday choice.

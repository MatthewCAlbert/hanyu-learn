## Why this combination
文 "writing, refinement, the marks of civilisation" combined with 化 "to
transform, to turn into" (as in 变化): to be transformed through
refinement and learning — a classical compound (文治教化, "to govern
through culture and moral instruction") shortened into the modern word
for culture, later also carrying the sense borrowed to translate Western
"culture."

## Notes
文化 names both the abstract concept of culture (中国文化 "Chinese
culture," 文化差异 "cultural differences") and, colloquially, a person's
level of education (他没什么文化 "he doesn't have much schooling") — a
sense with no direct match in English "culture."

## Why this combination

从 ("from," as in 从来) plus 此 ("this, this point," a literary
demonstrative) names the point in time from which something holds: "from
this point on."

## Notes

从此 is a connector marking a lasting change after an event (他们从此再
也没见过面, "from then on, they never saw each other again") — function
word, not tied to any topic.

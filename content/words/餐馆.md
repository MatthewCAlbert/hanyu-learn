## Why this combination
餐 "a meal" and 馆 "an institutional building" (the same 馆 as in 博物馆,
宾馆) combine directly: a building for eating meals.

## Notes
餐馆 is a close, largely interchangeable synonym of 饭馆 "restaurant" —
餐 leans slightly more formal/written, appearing in compounds like 中餐馆
"Chinese restaurant."

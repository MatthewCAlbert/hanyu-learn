## Why this combination
珍 "a treasure" (see 珍's own etymology) and 惜 "to treasure, to be loath
to lose" (see 惜's own etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the
standard verb for cherishing something.

## Notes
珍惜 anchors 珍惜时间 "to make the most of one's time," 珍惜生命 "to value
life," 珍惜眼前人 "to cherish those in front of you" — always about
valuing something precisely because it could be lost.

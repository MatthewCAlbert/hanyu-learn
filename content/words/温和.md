## Why this combination
温 "warm" and 和 "harmonious, mild" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for a gentle, unforceful quality.

## Notes
温和 describes both temperament (性格温和 "mild-tempered") and physical
conditions (气候温和 "mild climate," 温和的药 "a gentle/mild medicine") —
always the absence of harshness or extremity.

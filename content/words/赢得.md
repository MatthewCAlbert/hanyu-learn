## Why this combination
赢 ("to win," as in 赢家, 输赢) takes 得 as a resultative complement
marking that the winning actually landed successfully — the same
"achieved result" role 得/到 play after many other verbs. "Winning,
successfully" is simply "to win/earn."

## Notes
赢得 pairs naturally with abstract prizes (赢得尊重, "to earn respect";
赢得比赛, "to win a competition") — slightly more formal than plain 赢
alone, which is equally at home in casual speech about games.

## Why this combination
饭 "meal" + 店 "shop, establishment" → an eating house. Large lodging
establishments took the same name (北京饭店), so 饭店 covers both "restaurant"
and, especially for big ones, "hotel" — context or scale tells them apart.

## Notes
Unambiguous alternatives: 餐馆 / 餐厅 for eating, 宾馆 / 酒店 for lodging.

## Why this combination
接 "to receive, to meet [someone arriving]" (see also 接近, 迎接)
combined with 待 "to treat, to deal with" (see also 对待, 等待): to
receive and attend to — to host, to receive a visitor.

## Notes
接待 names the formal reception of guests or visitors (接待客人 "to
receive guests," 接待处 "reception desk") — 迎接, its close relative,
is specifically the act of going to greet an arrival, while 接待 covers
the broader hosting that follows.

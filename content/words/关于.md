## Why this combination
关 carries its "to relate to, to concern" sense (as in 关系, 关心), and 于 is
the classical preposition "at, to, in regard to" that survives mostly bound
into compounds like this one (对于, 由于). Fused, "关于" heads a whole topic
the rest of the sentence is about: 关于中国历史 "regarding Chinese history."

The classical, no-longer-independent 于 is what keeps this "semi" rather
than fully transparent — a learner needs to already know 于's preposition
role from 对于/由于 to parse 关于 cleanly.

## Notes
关于 almost always opens a sentence or a title, marking the whole rest of the
clause as "on the topic of X" (关于这个问题，我们需要讨论) — it does not sit
mid-sentence the way English "about" often does.

## Why this combination
言 "words, speech" and 语 "language, speech" are near-synonyms combined
into a formal word for spoken expression.

## Notes
言语 covers how something is said: 言语粗鲁 "rude speech," 言语不清
"unclear speech" — a more written/formal register than everyday 话 or
说话, often referring to manner of speaking rather than content.

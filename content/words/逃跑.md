## Why this combination
逃 "to escape, to flee" (see 逃's own etymology) and 跑 "to run" are
near-synonyms combined into the everyday word for fleeing.

## Notes
逃跑 emphasizes urgent, often panicked flight (吓得逃跑 "fled in fear") —
more physical and immediate than the broader 逃避 "to avoid/evade," which
can apply to abstract responsibilities too.

## Why this combination

交 ("to exchange, to associate," as in 交流, "to interact and exchange
ideas") plus 际 ("a border, a boundary between," as in 国际,
"international [lit. between + nations]") names the space where people
meet and interact: social interaction.

Rated "semi" for 际's abstract "between/among" contribution here, less
transparent outside 国际 and similar compounds.

## Notes

社交 and 交际 both name social interaction, with 交际 leaning slightly
more toward the skill or practice of getting along with others
(交际能力, "social skills") in professional or public settings.

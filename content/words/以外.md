## Why this combination
以 "taking (a point) as reference" + 外 "outside." "Outside (that boundary)" —
in space (三米以外), and, with 除了…以外, "apart from…": 除了他以外，都同意了
"everyone but him agreed."

## Notes
The 以 + locative frame: [[以内]], [[以上]], [[以下]], [[以后]], [[以前]].
除了…以外 can mean either "except" or "besides," told apart by 都 vs. 也.

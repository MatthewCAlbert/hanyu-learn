## Why this combination
后 "behind, after" combined with the locative suffix 面 — the same
"direction word + 面" pattern as 前面, 上面, 里面 and 外面 — naming the
space or time that comes after.

## Notes
后面 covers both spatial "behind" (在我后面 "behind me") and temporal
"later, next" (后面的内容 "the content that follows") — 后边 is a
colloquial synonym limited to the spatial sense.

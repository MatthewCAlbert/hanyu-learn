## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 亲 ("close, intimate, related by blood," as in
亲爱) and 密 ("dense, close together," as in 秘密, "secret") both
independently name closeness. Pairing them gives the standard word for
an intimate relationship.

## Notes

亲密 describes relationships (亲密的朋友, "close friends"; 亲密关系, "an
intimate relationship") rather than physical proximity, which 靠近 or
挨着 would cover instead.

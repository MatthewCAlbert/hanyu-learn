## Why this combination

口 ("mouth," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 口袋) plus 语
("language," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 语法/汉语) names
language as spoken by mouth: oral language.

## Notes

口语练习 ("oral practice") and 口语表达能力 ("oral expression ability")
both build on this word — 口语 contrasts with 书面语 ("written language,
lit. book-face language") as the two registers of a language.

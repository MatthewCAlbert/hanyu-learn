## Why this combination
少 (*shǎo*) "few" + 数 (*shù*) "number" (see [[多数]], [[人数]]) → the smaller
number, the minority: 少数人反对, 少数民族 "ethnic minority,"
少数服从多数.

## Notes
少 is *shǎo* here (few), not *shào* "young" ([[少年]]). Opposite [[多数]] /
[[大多数]].

## Why this combination
此 "this" and 时 "time, moment" combine directly into "this moment" — a
formal written-register equivalent of everyday 现在/这时候.

## Notes
此时 anchors formal/literary phrases: 此时此刻 "at this very moment," 此时
无声胜有声 "silence speaks louder than words at this moment [poetic
line]" — rarely used in casual conversation.

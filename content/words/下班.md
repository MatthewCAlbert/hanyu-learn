## Why this combination
班 "shift, duty period" as the object; 下 "to come off / step down from" (the
counterpart of 上 "go on" in [[上班]]). Splits: 下了班 "after work,"
还没下班 "not off yet."

## Notes
上 / 下 as board / alight and start / finish runs across 上车 / [[下车]],
[[上课]] / [[下课]], [[上班]] / 下班.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 抄 ("to copy," as in 抄袭, "to plagiarize [lit.
copy + attack]") and 写 ("to write," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 写作/描写) both independently name reproducing text.
Pairing them gives the standard word for copying by hand.

## Notes

抄写课文 ("to copy out the text") and 抄写作业 ("to write out homework by
copying") both build on this word — always implies faithful reproduction
of existing text, not composing something original.

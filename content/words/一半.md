## Why this combination
半 alone already means "half" (半年, 半个). 一半 is the noun "a half" of some
whole — 分一半给你 "give you half." The 一 supplies the "one portion" framing,
as it does in [[一起]], 一部分.

## Notes
半 as a bound numeral goes *before* a measure word (半天); 一半 stands alone as
a quantity noun.

## Why this combination
加 "to add, to apply" plus 以 "by means of, with" combine into "to apply
(a following action) by means of" — a grammaticalized formal construction
whose logic is clearer once its function (introducing a two-syllable verb
acting on something already mentioned) is known.

## Notes
加以 always precedes a disyllabic verb and refers back to something named
earlier: 对这个问题加以研究 "to look into this problem" — a formal,
written-register substitute for simply attaching the verb directly.

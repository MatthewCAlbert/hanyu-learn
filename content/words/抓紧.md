## Why this combination

抓 ("to grab, to seize") plus 紧 ("tight," as in 紧张) describes gripping
something firmly — literally, or figuratively gripping time or an
opportunity so it doesn't slip away.

## Notes

抓紧时间 ("to lose no time, to make the most of the time") is the
dominant figurative use — a boss's or teacher's stock phrase for "hurry
up and don't waste this."

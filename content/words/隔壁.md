## Why this combination
隔 "to separate" and 壁 "wall" (see 壁's own etymology) combine into
"separated [only] by a wall" — close enough to share a party wall, i.e.
next door.

## Notes
隔壁 names both the location (隔壁房间 "the next room") and, by
extension, the people living there (隔壁邻居 "next-door neighbour," or
隔壁 alone as a casual stand-in for "the neighbours").

## Why this combination
同 "same, together" (the "fellow-" prefix of [[同学]], [[同时]]) + 事 "work,
affairs, a job" → someone you share work with. Originally a verb "to serve
together"; now chiefly the noun.

## Notes
同事 = workmate; [[同学]] = schoolmate; 同行 (*tóngháng*) = someone in the same
trade. 老同事 "a long-time colleague."

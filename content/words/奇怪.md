## Why this combination
奇 "strange, remarkable, unusual" (as in 奇迹 "miracle") and 怪 "strange,
bizarre, to blame" (as in 怪不得 "no wonder") are close synonyms, paired
into the everyday adjective for anything odd.

## Notes
奇怪 works as adjective ("很奇怪的声音" "a very strange sound") and,
less commonly, as a verb meaning "to find strange, to be puzzled by"
(我奇怪他为什么没来 "I find it odd that he didn't come") — 怪 alone can
carry this verb use even more directly.

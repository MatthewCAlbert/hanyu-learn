## Why this combination
出 "to go out" acting on the object 差 (here an old sense, "an official
errand or assignment," read chāi rather than the more common chà/chā)
gives "to go out on assignment" — splittable, as in 出了三天差. The
reading and sense of 差 here differ from its everyday "difference/poor"
uses, so the logic isn't obvious without knowing this older meaning.

## Notes
出差 anchors 出差费 "travel expense (for business)," 去上海出差 "to go to
Shanghai on business" — always work travel, distinct from personal
旅行/旅游.

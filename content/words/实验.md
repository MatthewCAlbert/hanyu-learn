## Why this combination
实 "solid, actual, real" (as in 事实, 现实) combined with 验 "to test, to
verify" (see also 经验): to test something for real — an experiment.

## Notes
实验 names a controlled test, especially scientific (做实验 "to run an
experiment," 实验室 "laboratory") — 试验, a near-homophone in meaning,
leans towards trial-and-error testing in engineering or product
development rather than formal scientific method.

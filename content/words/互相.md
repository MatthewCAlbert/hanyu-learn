## Why this combination
互 "mutually, reciprocally" (as in 互联网 "the internet," literally
"mutually-connected network") and 相 "mutually, [towards] each other"
(see also 相当, 相关) are close synonyms, paired into the standard
adverb for reciprocal action.

## Notes
互相 sits before a two-place verb to mark the action as reciprocal
(互相帮助 "to help each other," 互相了解 "to understand each other") — it
functions much like English "each other" but sits before the verb rather
than after it as an object.

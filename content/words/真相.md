## Why this combination
真 "true" and 相 "appearance, form" combine directly: the true form of a
matter, once every disguise or distortion is stripped away.

## Notes
真相 anchors 真相大白 "the truth comes to light [idiom]" and 揭开真相 "to
uncover the truth" — always implying a truth that was previously hidden
or obscured, not a neutral fact.

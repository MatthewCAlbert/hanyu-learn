## Why this combination
补 "to mend, to supplement" and 偿 "to repay" (see 偿's own etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for making up a loss or
deficiency.

## Notes
补偿 covers financial compensation (经济补偿 "financial compensation") and
emotional/relational make-up (弥补/补偿感情 "to make up for hurt feelings")
— always restoring balance after a loss or shortfall.

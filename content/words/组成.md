## Why this combination
组 "to组织 organise, to group" + resultative 成 "into, so as to form" (see
[[变成]], [[成为]]) — parts brought together so as to form a whole:
由三部分组成 "made up of three parts," 组成一个小组.

## Notes
组 "group / organise" also in [[小组]], [[组长]]. 成 as the "-into" complement
also in 分成, 翻译成.

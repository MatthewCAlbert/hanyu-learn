## Why this combination
走 "to walk" (see [[走路]], [[走过]], [[走开]]) + directional complement 进 "in,
into" → walking into a place: 走进教室. Figuratively "to get closer to / into":
走进大自然, 走进他的内心世界.

## Notes
走进 (walk in) vs. [[进入]] (enter, any manner, more formal) vs. 进去 / 进来
(go / come in, with a viewpoint). 进 as a complement also in 搬进, 住进.

## Why this combination
饮 "to drink" (see 饮's own etymology) and 料 "material, ingredient"
combine directly: material meant for drinking.

## Notes
饮料 is the general umbrella term covering soft drinks, juices, and other
non-alcoholic beverages (碳酸饮料 "carbonated drinks") — distinct from 酒
"alcoholic drink" and specific named drinks like 茶, 咖啡.

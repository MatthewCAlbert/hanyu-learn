## Why this combination

唱 ("to sing," as in 唱歌) plus 片 ("a flat piece, a slice," as in 照片,
"photograph") names a flat disc that holds recorded singing/music: a
record.

## Notes

唱片 covers vinyl records and, loosely, any music disc/album in
retrospect; 唱片公司 ("record company/label") extends the same root into
the music industry.

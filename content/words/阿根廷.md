## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Argentina. 阿, 根 and 廷 are chosen for
sound. 根 is not "root"; 廷 is not "court."

## Notes
阿 as a dummy first syllable also in 阿拉伯, [[阿富汗]].

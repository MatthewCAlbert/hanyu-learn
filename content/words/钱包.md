## Why this combination
钱 "money" + 包 "bag, pouch" → a small bag for money. 包 is the generic
"carry-bag" morpheme in 书包 (schoolbag), 背包 (backpack), 面包 (loaf).

## Notes
Now also the digital sense: 电子钱包 "e-wallet." 丢了钱包 "lost my wallet."

## Why this combination
体 "body, to embody" (see also 体验, 体会) combined with 现 "to appear,
to show, present" (see also 出现, 现在): to give bodily/visible form
to — to embody, to manifest.

## Notes
体现 describes an abstract quality made concretely visible through
something else (体现价值 "to embody value," 体现了努力 "reflects/embodies
the effort put in") — 表现, its neighbour, is used more for a person's
own conduct or performance, while 体现 is about an abstraction showing
through in something.

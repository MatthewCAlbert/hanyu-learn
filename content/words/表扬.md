## Why this combination

表 ("to express, to show," as in 表示) plus 扬 ("to raise, to make
widely known," as in 发扬, "to promote/carry forward [a tradition]")
names expressing and publicly raising up someone's good conduct: to
commend.

Rated "semi" for 扬's "make known/raise up" sense here, distinct from
its more physical uses like "to winnow/scatter."

## Notes

受到表扬 ("to receive commendation") and 表扬信 ("a commendation letter")
both build on this word — 表扬 often names formal or institutional
praise (school, workplace), close to 称赞 (covered elsewhere in this
app) but leaning toward a more official register.

## Why this combination
毕 "to finish, to complete" and 竟 "in the end, finally" combine into "when
everything is finished and accounted for" — a grammaticalized connective
whose "after all" sense follows from, but is not immediately obvious from,
the parts.

## Notes
毕竟 introduces a clinching, can't-be-ignored reason: 他毕竟是个孩子 "he
is, after all, just a child" — always framing what follows as the
underlying, decisive fact.

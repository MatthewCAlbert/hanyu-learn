## Why this combination
公 "public" and 告 "to announce, to inform" combine into exactly "to
announce publicly" — the standard word for an official notice.

## Notes
公告 works as noun ("发布公告" "to issue a notice") and, less often, as
verb — more formal and official than 通知, typically posted for general
public awareness rather than sent to a specific person.

## Why this combination
珍 "precious, treasured" and 珠 "a pearl, a bead" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for the gem produced by oysters.

## Notes
珍珠 anchors 珍珠项链 "pearl necklace," 珍珠奶茶 "bubble/pearl milk tea" —
also the source of "pearl" in bubble tea's tapioca "pearls," named for
their round, pearl-like appearance.

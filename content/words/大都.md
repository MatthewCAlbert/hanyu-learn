## Why this combination
大 (here an intensifying adverb, "mostly, generally") plus 都 "all"
gives exactly "mostly all" — a written/formal register adverb for a
general, though not absolute, statement.

## Notes
大都 anchors 大都是新手 "mostly beginners," 参与者大都同意 "most
participants agreed" — close to 大多, both common hedges expressing "the
great majority."

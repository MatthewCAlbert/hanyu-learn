## Why this combination
石 "rock, stone, mineral" (as in 石头 "rock/stone") combined with 油
"oil" (as in 加油 "to refuel," literally "to add oil"): oil that comes
from rock — petroleum, the mineral oil drawn from underground.

## Notes
石油 specifically names petroleum, the mineral resource (石油工业
"petroleum industry," 石油价格 "oil prices") — 油, alone, is the general
word for any oil, edible cooking oil included, so 石油 marks out the
mineral, fuel sense specifically.

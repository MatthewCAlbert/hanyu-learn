## Why this combination

晚 ("late," as in 晚上) plus 点 ("a point [in time], o'clock," as in 一点)
names arriving past the scheduled point in time: delayed.

## Notes

火车晚点了 ("the train is delayed") and 航班晚点 ("flight delay") both
build on this word — the standard term on transit schedules, distinct
from 迟到, which describes a person arriving late to an appointment.

## Why this combination
拜 (here "to pay respects to," as in 拜年 "to pay a New Year call") and
访 "to visit" are near-synonyms combined into a formal, respectful word
for visiting someone.

## Notes
拜访 is used for visiting someone of higher status or as a courtesy call:
拜访客户 "to call on a client," 登门拜访 "to visit someone's home" — more
polite/formal than plain 看望 or 去看.

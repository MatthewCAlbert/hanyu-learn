## Why this combination
银 "silver" combined with 牌 "a plaque, tablet, medal" (as in 牌子
"brand, sign"): the silver-colored medal — second place.

## Notes
银牌 names the second-place medal in competition (获得银牌 "to win the
silver medal"), sitting between 金牌 "gold medal" and 铜牌 "bronze medal"
in a matched three-way set built on the same 牌 pattern.

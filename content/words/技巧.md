## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 技 ("skill, technique," as in 技术) and 巧
("clever, skillful," as in 巧妙) both independently name adeptness at a
task. Pairing them gives the standard word for a refined, practiced
technique.

## Notes

技巧 leans toward the trained, repeatable skill of a craft or sport
(表演技巧, "performance technique"), distinct from 天才 ("innate talent")
or 能力 ("general ability").

## Why this combination
难 "hard, difficult" + 过 "to pass, to get through" → "hard to get through."
Literally of a tough stretch of life (日子难过), and by extension of the inner
state during one — feeling low (心里很难过) or physically wretched (胃难过).

## Notes
难 + verb = "hard to —": [[难受]] (hard to bear), [[难看]] (hard on the eye),
[[难听]], [[难题]]. Opposite of the emotion sense: [[高兴]], [[开心]].

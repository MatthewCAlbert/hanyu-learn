## Why this combination

会 here is read *kuài* ("to reckon, to add up"), a distinct reading from
the far more common *huì* ("to meet; can, to know how to," as in 开会/会
说话). Paired with 计 ("to calculate, to count," as in 计算), it names the
practice of reckoning accounts: accounting.

Rated "semi": the tone/reading switch is the whole key, much like 好 in
爱好 — read 会 as *huì* and the compound stops making sense.

## Notes

会计 covers both the field (学会计, "to study accounting") and the
person who practices it (她是会计, "she's an accountant").

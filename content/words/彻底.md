## Why this combination
彻 carries its "to penetrate all the way through" sense (surviving mostly
bound, as in 贯彻, "to carry through/implement"), and 底 is "the bottom"
(as in 到底, "to the very bottom/end"). "Penetrating all the way to the
bottom" of something is exactly what doing it thoroughly means.

Rated "semi" for 彻's standalone sense, met almost entirely inside
compounds like this one and 贯彻.

## Notes
彻底 sits before a verb as an intensifying adverb (彻底改变, "to
completely change") at least as often as it functions as a plain
adjective — a productive, high-frequency modifier in both writing and
speech.

## Why this combination
游 here carries an extended "to enjoy oneself, to play about" sense
(beyond its core "to swim, to travel"), paired with 戏 "play, sport,
drama" (the same 戏 seen in 游戏 elsewhere in this dataset). Two
near-synonyms for unstructured, enjoyable play.

## Notes
游戏 covers everything from a child's playground game to a modern video
game (电子游戏, 网络游戏) — the word scaled up with the technology
without needing to change.

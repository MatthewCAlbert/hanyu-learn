## Why this combination

尺 ("a *chi* [a traditional unit of length], a ruler," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 尺寸) plus the nominal suffix 子 names
the everyday measuring tool: a ruler.

## Notes

用尺子量一下 ("to measure with a ruler") is the standard everyday
phrase — 尺子 names the physical tool, distinct from 尺寸 (covered
elsewhere in this app), which names the resulting measurement itself.

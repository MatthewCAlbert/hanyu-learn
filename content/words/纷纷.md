## Why this combination

纷 ("numerous, disorderly, in confusion," part of the 纟-thread semantic
family covered elsewhere in this app alongside 络/统/维/综, all rooted in
tangled or numerous threads) doubled into 纷纷 turns the idea of
profusion into an adverb describing many things happening one after
another.

Rated "semi" for 纷's specialized "numerous/disorderly" sense, a
character with little independent everyday use outside this and similar
compounds.

## Notes

大家纷纷发言 ("everyone spoke up one after another") and 纷纷表示支持
("expressed support one after another") both build on this word —
纷纷 always describes multiple people or things acting in quick, loosely
ordered succession.

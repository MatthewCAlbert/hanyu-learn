## Why this combination
羽绒 "down" (羽 "feather" + 绒 "soft down/fluff") plus 服 "garment"
gives exactly "a down-filled garment" — a straightforward, compositional
word.

## Notes
羽绒服 anchors 穿羽绒服 "to wear a down jacket" — the standard winter
outerwear filled with bird down for insulation, distinct from 棉衣
"cotton-padded coat."

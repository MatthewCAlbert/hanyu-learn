## Why this combination
一 "one" and 路 "road, route" combine directly: a single, continuous
road — travelled the whole way, or shared by two people going the same
direction.

## Notes
一路 covers duration (一路上 "along the way," 一路平安 "safe travels [set
farewell phrase]") and shared direction (我们一路 "we're going the same
way").

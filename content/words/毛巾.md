## Why this combination

毛 ("hair, fuzz, wool," as in 毛衣, covered elsewhere in this app) plus
巾 ("a piece of cloth, a towel," a character occurring mainly in this
role) names a piece of fuzzy/soft cloth: a towel.

## Notes

毛巾 (a towel) and 毛衣 (a sweater) share the same 毛 "wool/fuzz" root but
name distinct household and clothing items — 手绢 ("a handkerchief") is a
smaller cousin in the same 巾-cloth family.

## Why this combination
行 (*xíng*) "to walk, to go" (see [[走路]]'s cousin 步行, [[行动]]) + 人
"person" → a person on foot, especially on or beside a road: 行人道 "footpath,"
行人过街 "pedestrian crossing," 请行人注意.

## Notes
行 is *xíng* here. Traffic vocabulary: 行人 pedestrians vs. [[车辆]] vehicles
vs. 骑车人 cyclists.

## Why this combination
普 "widespread, general" (as in 普通 "ordinary, common") combined with
遍 "all over, everywhere, [covering] the whole extent" (as in 遍地
"everywhere on the ground"): widespread over the whole extent —
universal, common.

## Notes
普遍 describes something found everywhere or affecting everyone (普遍
现象 "a widespread phenomenon," 普遍认为 "commonly believed") — 普通, its
close relative, means "ordinary, common" in the sense of unremarkable,
not "widespread in occurrence."

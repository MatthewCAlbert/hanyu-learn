## Why this combination
等 "equal, of the same rank" (相等, 平等) + 于 "to, at" (a classical preposition
marking the standard of comparison, as in 大于, 属于) → "equal to." Arithmetic
(二加二等于四) and figurative (不回答等于默认 "no answer amounts to consent").

## Notes
于 "to / than" also in [[对于]] (not this level), 关于, 由于. 等 "equal" here,
not 等 "to wait" ([[等到]]) or 等 "etc." (…等等).

## Why this combination

论 ("to argue, to discuss, a theory," as in 讨论 and 理论) plus 文
("writing, a piece of writing") names a piece of writing that argues or
discusses a position: an academic paper or thesis.

## Notes

论文 spans undergraduate essays through doctoral dissertations
(毕业论文, "graduation thesis") — scale, not genre, distinguishes them.

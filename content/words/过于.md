## Why this combination
过 "to exceed" plus the classical comparative particle 于 ("than," as
also seen in 用于, 出于) combines into "exceeding [what's reasonable]" — a
formal written-register equivalent of everyday 太.

## Notes
过于 always precedes an adjective in formal writing: 过于紧张 "overly
tense," 过于自信 "overconfident" — everyday speech prefers plain 太 for
the same idea.

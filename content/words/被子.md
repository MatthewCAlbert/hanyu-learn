## Why this combination
被, here "a quilt, a cover" (also, in a separate grammatical use, the
passive-voice marker "by," from the same root sense "to be covered
by/subjected to") combined with the light noun suffix 子 (as in 被子's
own pattern, matching 桌子, 房子): the everyday concrete noun for a
quilt.

## Notes
被子 names the bedding one sleeps under (盖被子 "to cover oneself with a
quilt," 叠被子 "to fold the quilt") — 被, alone, is far more often
encountered as the passive marker (被打 "to be hit") than as this
concrete noun, which almost always takes the 子 suffix in speech.

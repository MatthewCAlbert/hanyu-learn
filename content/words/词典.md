## Why this combination
词 "a word" (one or more characters forming a unit; see [[词语]], [[生词]]) +
典 "a canonical, authoritative work" (经典, [[字典]]) → the authoritative book of
words. Also written 辞典.

## Notes
词典 has entries for 词 (words); [[字典]] has entries for single 字 (characters).
《现代汉语词典》 is the standard one — the source cited throughout this project.

## Why this combination

微 ("micro, tiny," as in 微笑, "to smile [lit. tiny + laugh]") plus 信
("a message, a letter," as in 短信, "a text message [lit. short +
message]") names a lightweight messaging service: WeChat, the brand
name chosen to describe the app's function.

## Notes

发微信 ("to send a WeChat message") and 微信朋友圈 ("WeChat Moments, lit.
friend circle") both build on this word — one of very few brand names
in this app whose literal parts also plainly describe what the product
does.

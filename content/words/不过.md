## Why this combination
"Not going past" — 不 + 过 "to exceed." From "not more than" came two senses:
the limiting "only, merely" (不过是个孩子 "he's only a child") and the
concessive "but" (东西不错，不过太贵 "it's nice, but too dear"). After an
adjective, 再…不过了 = "couldn't be more…".

## Notes
Softer than [[但是]] as "but." The "only" sense is close to 只是.

## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" and 本 (here "capital, the invested root
sum," not its everyday "book" sense) combine into "the capital that goes
into completing [a product]" — the standard economic term for production
cost.

## Notes
成本 anchors 生产成本 "production cost," 成本效益 "cost-effectiveness" — a
purely economic/business term, unrelated to 本 elsewhere meaning "book."

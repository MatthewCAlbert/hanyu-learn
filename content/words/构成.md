## Why this combination

构 ("to construct, to build up," as in 结构, "structure") plus 成 ("to
become, to complete," as in 成功) names parts building up into a
completed whole: to constitute, to form.

## Notes

构成 frames a whole in terms of its parts (由…构成, "constituted by…") and
also names an abstract formation (构成威胁, "to constitute a threat") —
both senses share the "parts assembling into a whole" image.

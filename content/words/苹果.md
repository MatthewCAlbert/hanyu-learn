## Why this combination
苹, a character coined specifically for the apple plant (its 艹 "grass/
plant" radical marks it as a plant name, with the rest of the character
supplying sound), combined with 果 "fruit" (as in 水果 "fruit," 结果
"result," literally "the fruit [of an action]"): the fruit of the 苹
plant — apple.

## Notes
苹果 is a transparent plant-name-plus-果 compound, the same pattern as
many other fruit names in Chinese (香蕉 does not follow it, but 苹果's
own 苹 exists essentially only in this word, a sign it was coined
specifically to name the apple).

## Why this combination
夫 ("husband," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 妻 ("wife," also
covered elsewhere) names the marital pair by naming both roles directly
— the most transparent of the several couple-naming words in this family
(compare 夫妇, covered elsewhere in this app, and 老公/老婆, the casual
pair).

## Notes
夫妻 is the everyday-to-neutral register — more common in speech than the
more formal 夫妇, and more neutral than the casual 老公老婆.

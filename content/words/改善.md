## Why this combination
改 ("to change") plus 善 ("good," as noted in this app's entry for 良,
where Shuowen glosses 良 using this very character) names changing
something toward the good: to improve.

## Notes
改善生活 ("to improve one's standard of living") and 改善关系 ("to improve
relations") both build on this pair's core "changing for the better"
sense — always a positive direction, unlike neutral 改变 ("to change,"
which can go either way).

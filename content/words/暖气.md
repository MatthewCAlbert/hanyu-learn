## Why this combination

暖 ("warm," as in 暖和) plus 气 ("air, gas," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 气球) names warmed air circulated through a building:
central heating.

## Notes

开暖气 ("to turn on the heating") and 暖气片 ("a radiator, lit. heating
panel") both build on this word — names the whole heating system,
distinct from 空调 (air conditioning), which can both heat and cool.

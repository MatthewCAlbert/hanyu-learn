## Why this combination
新 translates English "New"; 西兰 is the phonetic rendering of Zealand (西蘭),
using the same 兰 closer as [[荷兰]]. Half calque, half transcription — not "new
+ west + orchid."

## Notes
Contrast [[新加坡]], where 新 is phonetic of Sing- and does not mean "new."

## Why this combination
放 "to put, to place, to release" (see also 开放) combined with the
resultative complement 到 "to arrive at, [reaching] a target": to put
something so that it reaches a specified place — to put it there.

## Notes
放到 is followed by a location marking exactly where something ends up
(放到桌子上 "put it on the table") — the resultative 到 confirms the
placement was completed at that specific spot, unlike bare 放, which
only names the act of putting something down without specifying the
result's location.

## Why this combination
责 "duty, responsibility" (recall its etymology: a creditor pressing for
what is owed) and 任 here in its own "duty, appointed responsibility"
sense are close synonyms, paired for a fuller, more standard word.

## Notes
责任 is the general noun for "responsibility, duty, blame" — 负责任
"to be responsible" and 责任心 "a sense of responsibility" both build
directly on it.

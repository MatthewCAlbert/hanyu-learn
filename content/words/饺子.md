## Why this combination
饺 "a filled dumpling" (食 "food" radical + 交 as the phonetic) + the toneless
noun suffix 子. Older names include 角子 and 交子 — 角 "horn / corner" for the
crescent shape, 交 "join" for the joining of old year and new. 饺子 is
traditionally eaten at 除夕 New Year's Eve, punning on 交子 "the hour when the
years meet."

## Notes
包饺子 "wrap dumplings" (a family activity), 一盘饺子. 水饺 boiled, 蒸饺
steamed, 煎饺 pan-fried.

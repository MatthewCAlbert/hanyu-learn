## Why this combination
只 "only" + 要 "to need, to require" → "the only thing needed is…" — a
sufficient condition. Correlates with 就: 只要努力，就能成功.

## Notes
Contrast 只有…才… "only if… then…" (a *necessary* condition). 只要 = enough;
只有 = required.

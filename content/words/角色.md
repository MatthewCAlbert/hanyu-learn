## Why this combination
角 here takes the *jué* reading and sense "a role-type" — the stock
character categories of traditional Chinese opera (生, 旦, 净, 丑) — rather
than its everyday *jiǎo* reading "horn" or "corner." 色 carries its "kind,
category" sense (as in 出色, 角色's own second half). "A category of
role-type" generalized from the stage to any role a person plays, in
fiction or in life.

Rated "semi" specifically for that reading switch on 角 (*jué*, not
*jiǎo*) — get the tone wrong and the word looks like it should mean
"corner."

## Notes
Read 角 as *jué* in 角色 — a different word from the everyday *jiǎo*
("horn, corner") most learners meet first in 角 alone.

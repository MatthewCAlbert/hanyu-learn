## Why this combination
有 "to have" + 用 "use, usefulness" → "to have use" = useful. The 有 + noun
pattern that also gives [[有名]] (have fame → famous), 有钱 (rich), 有意思
(interesting).

## Notes
Negative 没用 "useless" (often of people, dismissively). 有用的信息 "useful
information."

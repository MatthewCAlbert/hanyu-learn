## Why this combination
打 and 击 are near-synonyms, both meaning "to hit, to strike" — combined
for the formal verb/noun covering both physical blows and figurative
setbacks.

## Notes
打击 spans law enforcement (打击犯罪 "to crack down on crime") and
emotional impact (受到打击 "to be hit hard [emotionally]," 打击信心 "to
undermine confidence") — a physical strike extended figuratively to any
serious setback.

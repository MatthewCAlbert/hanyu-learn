## Why this combination
处 here is not "to reside" but its extended verbal sense "to deal with,
to be situated in relation to [a matter]." 理 originally names the
natural grain or pattern in wood or jade, extended to "to put in order,
to manage" — following the grain to sort something out properly.
Together: to manage a matter according to its natural order.

## Notes
处理 covers everything from bureaucratic paperwork (处理文件) to
technical processing (图像处理 "image processing") to selling off stock
at a discount (处理价 "clearance price") — a genuinely broad, all-purpose
verb for "to competently deal with."

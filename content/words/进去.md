## Why this combination
进 "to enter" + 去 "to go (away from the speaker)" — entering, away from where
you stand. [[进来]] is the same entry coming toward you. As a complement:
放进去 "put in," 走进去 "walk in."

## Notes
Rounds out the directional set with 出/回/上/下 + 来/去.

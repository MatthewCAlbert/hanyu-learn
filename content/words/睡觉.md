## Why this combination
A verb-object phrase: 睡 "to sleep" takes 觉 "a sleep" as a cognate object, like
English "sleep a sleep." So it splits — 睡了一个好觉 "had a good sleep,"
睡午觉 "take a nap." It cannot take another object.

## Notes
觉 is *jiào* here (a sleep), not *jué* "to sense" as in [[觉得]]. Same
verb + generic-object shape as 吃饭, [[做梦]] — only 睡觉 itself has a true
cognate object.

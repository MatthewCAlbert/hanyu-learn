## Why this combination
大奖 "grand prize" (大 "big" + 奖 "prize") plus 赛 "competition" gives
exactly "a competition for a grand prize" — the standard word for a major
sporting or artistic competition, calquing the French/English "grand
prix" idea rather than transliterating it.

## Notes
大奖赛 anchors F1大奖赛 "the Formula 1 Grand Prix," 摄影大奖赛
"photography grand prix" — used for high-profile competitions across
sports and other fields.

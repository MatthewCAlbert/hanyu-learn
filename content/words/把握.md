## Why this combination
把, here in its "to hold, to take hold of" sense (as in 把 the
grammatical marker, ultimately from "to grasp") combined with 握 "to
grip, to hold in the hand" (as in 握手 "to shake hands"): to grasp and
grip firmly — to seize [an opportunity], or the resulting confidence.

## Notes
把握 works as verb ("把握机会" "to seize an opportunity") and, very
commonly, as a noun for one's confidence in an outcome ("有把握"
"to be sure/confident [of success]," "没有把握" "not sure/no
guarantee") — the noun use is one of the most frequent everyday patterns.

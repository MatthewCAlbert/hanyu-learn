## Why this combination
课 "lesson" + 文 "text, writing" → the main passage a lesson is built around,
the thing you read aloud and analyse. Sits inside the [[课本]] (textbook).

## Notes
背课文 "memorise the text," 第五课的课文 "the passage in Lesson 5."

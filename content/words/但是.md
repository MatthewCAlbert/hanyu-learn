## Why this combination
但 alone already means "but / only" (classical 但…). 是 "is" was appended to
make a stable disyllabic conjunction, exactly as in [[还是]], [[要是]], 于是.
可是 and 不过 are the softer alternatives.

## Notes
Often correlates with a concessive 虽然 in the first clause: 虽然累，但是很开心.
但 alone survives in formal writing (但求无过).

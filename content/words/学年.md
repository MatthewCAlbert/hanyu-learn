## Why this combination

学 ("to study," as in 学习) plus 年 ("year") names a year measured by
the school calendar: an academic year.

## Notes

新学年 ("the new academic year") and 学年结束 ("the academic year ends")
both build on this word — the school-calendar counterpart to 学期
(covered elsewhere in this app), which names a single term rather than
the full year.

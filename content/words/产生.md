## Why this combination
产 "to produce, to give birth to" (as in 生产 "to produce") and 生 "to be
born, to arise, to live" are close synonyms, paired into a word for
something new coming into existence — an idea, a feeling, a problem,
rather than a physical product.

## Notes
产生 takes abstract results as its object (产生影响 "to produce an
effect," 产生兴趣 "to develop interest," 产生矛盾 "for conflict to
arise") — 生产, its reversed twin, is the word for manufacturing physical
goods.

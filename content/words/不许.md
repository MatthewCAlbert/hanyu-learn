## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 许 "to permit, to allow" gives exactly "not permitted"
— as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
不许 is a direct, often forceful prohibition: 不许动! "don't move!," 不许
哭 "no crying allowed" — blunter than the softer 不可以/不能 "cannot,
shouldn't."

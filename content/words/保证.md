## Why this combination
保 here carries its extended "to guarantee, to ensure" sense (alongside
its more literal "to protect"), paired directly with 证 "proof,
certificate" — guaranteeing something with proof to back it up.

## Notes
保证 works as both verb ("I guarantee...") and noun ("a guarantee, a
pledge"), and appears in the fixed phrase 保证书 "a written guarantee/
pledge" — a document form of the same promise.

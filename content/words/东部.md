## Why this combination
东 "east" (as in 东方) combined with 部 "a part, a section" (see also
西部, 南部): the eastern section — the east of a country or region,
completing the same "direction + 部" set.

## Notes
东部 names the internal eastern region of a specific country or area
(中国东部 "eastern China") — 东方, its close relative, names "the East"
as a broad civilisational or directional concept rather than a bounded
internal region.

## Why this combination
"Northern capital." The Ming moved the capital here from Nanjing 南京 ("southern
capital") in 1421 and renamed the city 北京 to match. The 京 "capital" element
is the same in 京剧 (Peking opera), 京城.

## Notes
Earlier names: 北平 ("northern peace," used 1928–49), 大都 (Yuan), 燕京
(historical). 京 alone abbreviates it: 京津 (Beijing–Tianjin).

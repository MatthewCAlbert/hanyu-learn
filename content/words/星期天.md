## Why this combination
星期一 to 星期六 number the days one to six; Sunday breaks the pattern and takes
星期 + 天 "day" (or 星期日, with literary 日). It reflects Sunday's status as the
"whole day" of rest that heads or closes the week rather than a numbered
weekday.

## Notes
星期日 (see the corpus entry) is the more formal written form; 星期天 the
everyday spoken one. 周日 is the 周-series equivalent.

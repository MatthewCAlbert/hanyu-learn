## Why this combination

陆 (here "continuous, unbroken," an old extended sense related to
solid, unbroken land, covered elsewhere in this app in its more literal
role as part of 大陆) plus 续 ("to continue," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 延续) names things happening in an unbroken, ongoing
succession.

Rated "semi" for 陆's archaic "continuous" sense here, distinct from its
everyday "land" meaning.

## Notes

客人陆续到达 ("guests arrived one after another") and 陆续发布 ("released
successively") both build on this word — describes a spread-out
sequence of separate events, distinct from 连续 (covered elsewhere in
this app), which implies an unbroken run without gaps.

## Why this combination

随 ("to follow, along with," as in 随便, "as one pleases [lit. follow +
convenient]") plus 手 ("hand") names an action done as the hand happens
to be there: casually, in passing.

Rated "semi" for 随's abstract "as it happens/along with" contribution
here, one step from more literal uses like "to follow someone."

## Notes

随手关门 ("close the door on your way out, lit. follow-hand close the
door") and 随手带走垃圾 ("take the trash with you as you go") both build
on this word — always names a minor action folded into something one
is already doing.

## Why this combination
免 ("to exempt, to avoid, to be excused from," as in 免除, 避免) takes 费
("a fee, a cost") as its object: to be exempted from the fee is simply to
be free of charge. Fully compositional once 免's "exempt" sense is known.

## Notes
免费 sits before the noun or verb it describes (免费的书, "a free book";
免费参观, "free admission to visit") — it behaves as an adjective/adverb,
not as a standalone predicate meaning "is free."

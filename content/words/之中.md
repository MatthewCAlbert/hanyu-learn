## Why this combination
之 (a classical possessive/linking particle, "'s/of") plus 中 "middle,
inside" gives a formal, written-register equivalent of plain 里/中 "in,
inside."

## Notes
之中 trails a noun phrase in formal writing: 众人之中 "among the crowd,"
工作之中 "in the course of work" — 之下, 之内, 之外 form a small family of
similar classical-flavoured locative words.

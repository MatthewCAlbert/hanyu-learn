## Why this combination
地 "ground, place" + 点 "point, spot" → a specific point on the ground, a
pinpointed location. More precise and formal than [[地方]]: 时间、地点 "time and
place" on an invitation.

## Notes
Pairs with 时间 in fixed listings. 现场 is "the scene / on site"; 地址 is a
postal address.

## Why this combination
雨 "rain" plus 水 "water" gives exactly "rain water" — a straightforward
compositional word, also the name of one of the 24 solar terms (雨水, in
early spring).

## Notes
雨水 anchors 雨水充沛 "abundant rainfall," 收集雨水 "to collect
rainwater" — covers both the substance and the phenomenon of rain
falling.

## Why this combination
扩 ("to expand," covered elsewhere in this app) takes 大 ("big") as its
result: expanding until something becomes bigger. Fully compositional.

## Notes
扩大范围 ("to expand the scope") and 扩大规模 ("to expand the scale") both
build on this pair's core "making something bigger" sense — the standard
everyday verb for expansion.

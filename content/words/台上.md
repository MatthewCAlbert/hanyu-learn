## Why this combination

台 ("a platform, a stage," as in 舞台) plus 上 ("on, above") locates
something on a raised platform: on stage.

## Notes

台上 pairs with 台下 ("in the audience, lit. below the stage") to split
a performance into performer and audience space — parallel to 楼上/楼下
naming floors of a building.

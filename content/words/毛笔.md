## Why this combination
毛 "hair, fur" plus 笔 "pen, writing instrument" gives exactly "a
hair-tipped writing tool" — naming the brush by its animal-hair tip, as
distinct from 钢笔 "fountain pen" (steel nib) and 铅笔 "pencil" (lead).

## Notes
毛笔 anchors 毛笔字 "brush calligraphy," 毛笔画 "brush painting" — the
traditional writing/painting instrument central to Chinese calligraphy
and ink painting.

## Why this combination
工艺 "craftsmanship" (see its own entry) plus 品 "item, product" gives
exactly "a crafted item" — the standard word for a handmade decorative
object.

## Notes
工艺品 anchors 民间工艺品 "folk handicrafts," 工艺品店 "craft/gift shop" —
always a made-by-hand decorative or artistic object, distinct from mass-
produced industrial goods.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 显 ("to show, to be manifest/obvious," as in 显
示) and 著 (its "outstanding, notable" sense, covered in this app's entry
for 著名) both independently mark something as plainly visible or
standing out. Pairing them intensifies "obvious" into "strikingly,
significantly obvious."

## Notes
显著 is a formal/written register word, common in reports and academic
writing (显著提高, "a significant increase") — 明显 is the everyday spoken
equivalent for "obvious, clear."

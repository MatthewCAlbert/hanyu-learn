## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + 些, the vague-plural word "some / a few." 这 + 些 =
"this batch" = these; 那些 = those; 哪些 = which ones. A tidy three-way set.

## Notes
些 already carries plurality, so no measure word follows: 这些书, never
这些本书.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 色 ("color," as in 颜色) and 彩 ("bright,
variegated color," as in 精彩, "brilliant") both independently name
color. Pairing them gives a slightly more vivid, often figurative word
for color or stylistic flavor.

## Notes

色彩 leans figurative more readily than plain 颜色 (神秘色彩, "an air of
mystery"; 政治色彩, "a political tinge") — describing a mood or
character as much as a literal hue.

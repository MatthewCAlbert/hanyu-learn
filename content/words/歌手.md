## Why this combination
歌 "song" (as in 唱歌 "to sing") combined with 手 in its "a person
skilled at [an activity]" sense (see also 选手, 对手): one skilled at
song — a singer.

## Notes
歌手 names a professional or performing singer (流行歌手 "pop singer")
— the same 手 pattern used for other skill-based roles (水手 "sailor,"
高手 "expert") rather than 歌者, a more literary, less common alternative.

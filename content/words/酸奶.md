## Why this combination

酸 ("sour," as in 酸痛, "sore [lit. sour-pained]") plus 奶 ("milk," as in
牛奶) names milk fermented sour: yogurt.

## Notes

酸奶 follows the same "descriptive property + base food" pattern as 豆腐
(covered elsewhere in this app) — naming a processed food by what
happened to its base ingredient.

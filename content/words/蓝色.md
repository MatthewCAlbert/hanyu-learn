## Why this combination
蓝 "blue" + the "-colour" suffix 色 (see [[颜色]], [[红色]]). 蓝 originally named
the indigo plant (蓼蓝), the source of blue dye — 青出于蓝 "the blue dye comes
from the indigo plant (and is bluer than it)."

## Notes
蓝天 "blue sky." Chinese 青 historically blurred blue and green; 蓝 and 绿 are
the modern, distinct terms.

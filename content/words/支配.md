## Why this combination
支 (here "to allot, to disburse," as in issuing funds) and 配 "to
distribute, to allocate" are near-synonyms combined into a word for
directing how something is used or who is in charge.

## Notes
支配 covers allocating resources (支配时间 "to manage one's time") and
exerting control (受人支配 "to be dominated/controlled by someone") —
spans from neutral resource management to power dynamics between people.

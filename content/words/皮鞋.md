## Why this combination
皮 "leather, hide" plus 鞋 "shoes" gives exactly "leather shoes" — a
straightforward, fully compositional word.

## Notes
皮鞋 anchors 一双皮鞋 "a pair of leather shoes," 擦皮鞋 "to shine shoes" —
contrasts with 运动鞋 "sneakers" and 布鞋 "cloth shoes" by material.

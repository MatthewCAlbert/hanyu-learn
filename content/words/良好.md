## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 良 ("good, fine," covered elsewhere in this app)
and 好 ("good," the everyday word) both independently mean "good."
Pairing them produces a more formal, written-register synonym of plain
好.

## Notes
良好 reads more formal than plain 好 — common in evaluations and reports
(表现良好, "good performance"; 状态良好, "in good condition") rather than
casual speech.

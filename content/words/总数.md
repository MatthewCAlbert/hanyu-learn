## Why this combination
总 "overall, altogether" plus 数 "number" gives exactly "the overall
number" — a straightforward, compositional word for a grand total.

## Notes
总数 anchors 人口总数 "the total population," 总数达到 "the total reaches"
— the standard word for a summed figure across all categories or items.

## Why this combination
性 "nature, an inherent quality" (see also 个性) combined with 格 "a
pattern, a standard, a fixed form" (see also 价格, 资格): the pattern of
one's inherent nature — character, personality.

## Notes
性格 names the general traits that make up a person's character
(性格开朗 "an outgoing personality," 性格不合 "incompatible
personalities") — 个性, its close relative, stresses what makes someone
distinctively their own, while 性格 describes character more generally.

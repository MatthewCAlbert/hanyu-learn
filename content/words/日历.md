## Why this combination

日 ("day," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 日记) plus 历
("to pass through, a record of passage," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 简历) names a record of days passing: a calendar.

Rated "semi" for 历's "record of passage" contribution here, one step
from its more familiar role naming a personal history (as in 经历,
简历).

## Notes

台历 ("a desk calendar") and 挂历 ("a wall calendar," lit. "hang +
calendar") both build on 历's "record of time passing" root — 日历
specifically emphasizes daily pages, one for each day.

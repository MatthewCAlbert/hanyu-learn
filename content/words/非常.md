## Why this combination
非 "not" + 常 "ordinary, constant" = "out of the ordinary." That original
adjective survives in 非常时期 "extraordinary times," but the workaday use has
bleached into a plain intensifier: 非常好 "very good."

## Notes
Stronger and more formal than 很 (which is often just a grammatical filler).
非常 always adds real emphasis.

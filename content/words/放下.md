## Why this combination
放 "to put, to release, to let go" + directional 下 "down" — setting something
down. Literal (放下书包) and figurative (放下工作 "set work aside,"
放下架子 "drop the airs," 拿得起放得下 "able to let go").

## Notes
下 as the "settle / release" complement also in [[留下]], [[坐下]], 停下.
Opposite 拿起 / 举起.

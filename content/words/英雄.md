## Why this combination
The early text Renwu Zhi explains 英 as the finest of plants and 雄 as the mightiest of beasts: so 英雄 is a person who stands out in talent or courage. The words now just mean "hero".

## Notes
Do not read 雄 as only "male" here; it is "mighty".

## Why this combination
要 as the modal "must, need to"; 不要 negates it into a prohibition — "don't":
不要走 "don't go." Distinct from 不要 "not want" (不要这个 "don't want this
one"), where 要 is the full verb.

## Notes
别 is the compact colloquial equivalent for the prohibition: 别走 = 不要走.

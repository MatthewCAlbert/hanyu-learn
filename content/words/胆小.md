## Why this combination
胆 (here "courage, nerve," from the gallbladder's traditional association
with bravery) plus 小 "small" gives exactly "(one's) courage is small" —
a subject-predicate description, opposite of 胆大 "bold, daring."

## Notes
胆小 anchors 胆小鬼 "coward (derogatory)," 胆小如鼠 "timid as a mouse"
(idiom) — the everyday word for timidity, drawing on the traditional
Chinese medicine idea that 胆 (gallbladder/courage) governs decisiveness.

## Why this combination
脸 "face" plus 盆 "a basin" gives exactly "a basin for the face" — a
straightforward, compositional household word.

## Notes
脸盆 anchors 洗脸盆 (a synonymous fuller form), 脸盆架 "washstand" — the
everyday basin for washing up, before or alongside modern sinks became
standard.

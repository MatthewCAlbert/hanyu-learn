## Why this combination
流 "to flow" and 通 "to pass through, to be unobstructed" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for something moving freely
through a system.

## Notes
流通 spans economics (货币流通 "currency circulation," 流通领域
"circulation/distribution sector") and physical airflow (空气流通 "air
circulation") — always about unimpeded movement through a network.

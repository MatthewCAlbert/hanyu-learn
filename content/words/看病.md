## Why this combination
看 "to look at, to attend to" + 病 "illness" as its object — "to attend to an
illness." Symmetrical: the patient 看病 (goes to have the illness looked at) and
the doctor 看病 (looks at illnesses). Context decides which. Splits: 看了半天病.

## Notes
看 = "attend to / deal with professionally" also in 看孩子 "mind the children,"
看家 "watch the house."

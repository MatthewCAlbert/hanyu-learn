## Why this combination
大 "big, general" combined with 概 "a rough outline, the gist" (as in
概念 "concept"): the general outline [of something] without exact
detail — roughly, approximately, or, as a guess, probably.

## Notes
大概 hedges both quantities (大概三十个人 "roughly thirty people") and
whole statements as a probable guess (他大概不知道 "he probably doesn't
know") — 也许, a near-synonym, expresses possibility without the same
sense of a rough, estimated figure.

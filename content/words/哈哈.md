## Why this combination
A reduplicated onomatopoeia: 哈, the syllable imitating a laugh, doubled
the same way laughter itself repeats — 哈哈 renders the sound directly,
with the 口 "mouth" radical on both characters marking them as sound
words rather than words with independent lexical meaning.

## Notes
哈哈 stands alone as an interjection (哈哈，太好笑了 "haha, that's
hilarious") and also modifies 笑 as an adverb (哈哈大笑 "to laugh out
loud"); 呵呵, a similar reduplicated laugh sound, can carry a flatter or
even sarcastic tone in text that 哈哈 does not.

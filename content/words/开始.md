## Why this combination
开 "to open" and 始 "to begin" reinforce each other directly — opening
something and starting it are, for most activities, the same act. A
clean, transparent pairing of near-synonyms.

## Notes
Like 需要, 开始 covers both verb ("to begin") and noun ("the beginning")
uses, giving it more grammatical range than 始 alone, which reads as more
literary in isolation.

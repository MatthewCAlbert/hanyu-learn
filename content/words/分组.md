## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 分 "to divide" (see also 分别, 分配) takes 组
"a group, a set" (as in 组合, 组成) as its object — literally "to divide
into groups" — to split a larger group into subgroups.

## Notes
分组 names dividing people or items into smaller groups for an activity
(分组讨论 "group discussion," 分组比赛 "group-stage competition") — 组,
alone, is the noun "group" itself or the verb "to organise into a
group," while 分组 specifically frames the division of a larger whole.

## Why this combination
收, here "to receive [a broadcast signal]" (as in 收音机 "radio,"
literally "sound-receiving machine") combined with 看 "to watch, to
look": to receive and watch — to tune in and view a broadcast.

## Notes
收看 is used specifically for watching television broadcasts (收看
电视节目 "to watch a TV programme") — 看, alone, is the general verb
"to watch/look," while 收看 stresses receiving a transmitted signal, and
收听, its parallel word with 听 "to listen" in place of 看, is used for
radio.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 辩 ("to argue, to dispute a position," a
character occurring mainly in this role) and 论 ("to discuss, to argue,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 争论 and 讨论) both independently
name contesting a claim in words. Pairing them gives the standard word
for formal debate.

## Notes

辩论赛 ("a debate competition") and 辩论会 ("a debate session") both build
on this word — 辩论 implies structured, often formal argumentation over
a defined proposition, distinct from 争论, a heated but less formal
dispute.

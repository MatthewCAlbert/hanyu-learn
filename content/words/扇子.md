## Why this combination
扇 "a fan" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a fan" — the suffix
adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of 桌子, 椅子.

## Notes
扇子 anchors 摇扇子 "to fan oneself," 折扇子 "a folding fan" — the
everyday hand fan, distinct from 电扇 "electric fan."

## Why this combination
黄 ("yellow") plus 金 ("metal," and by extension "gold specifically," as
in 金子) names the metal by its color: literally "yellow metal."

## Notes
黄金 extends figuratively to "prime, most valuable" (黄金时期, "a golden
era/prime period"; 黄金时间, "prime time") — the same association of gold
with peak value applied to time.

## Why this combination
果 "fruit" (see also 水果, 苹果) combined with 汁 "juice, sap" (as in 果
汁's own 汁, whose 氵 water-radical marks it as a liquid): the juice of
fruit — fruit juice.

## Notes
果汁 names juice pressed from fruit (喝果汁 "to drink fruit juice") — 汁,
alone, is productive in other juice/liquid compounds too, though less
commonly standing alone than in this compound.

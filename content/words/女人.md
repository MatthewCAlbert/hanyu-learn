## Why this combination
女 "female" + 人 "person." Plain and transparent. Note the register: 女人 can
sound blunt; 女士 (lady) is polite and 女生 (female student, young woman) is the
neutral choice among young people.

## Notes
Toned *nǚrén* = woman; toneless *nǚren* = wife (dialectal). Pairs with [[男人]].

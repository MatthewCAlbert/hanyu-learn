## Why this combination
房 "house, chamber" + 间 "space between, compartment" — 间 is also the measure
word for rooms (一间房). The compound names the individual room within a
building.

## Notes
房 + 子 = [[房子]] the whole building; 房 + 间 = the room inside it. 洗手间,
房间号.

## Why this combination
优 "excellent, superior" and 惠 "favor, benefit" (see 惠's own etymology
— giving generously) combine into "an especially favorable benefit" — the
standard commercial word for preferential terms or a discount.

## Notes
优惠 anchors 优惠价 "discounted price," 优惠政策 "preferential policy" —
used for both government incentives and retail discounts, always framed
as a benefit extended to a buyer or recipient.

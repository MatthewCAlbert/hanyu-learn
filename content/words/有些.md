## Why this combination
有 "there are" + 些 "some" → "there are some…" as a quantifier (有些人不同意),
and, before an adjective, a hedged "somewhat" (有些累 "a bit tired").

## Notes
The adjective-hedging use overlaps with 有点儿; 有些 is slightly more written.

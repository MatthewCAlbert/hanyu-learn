## Why this combination
不 "not" combined with 论 "to discuss, to argue" (see also 理论,
讨论 in an unrelated compound): not [needing] discussion [because it
makes no difference] — no matter what, regardless.

## Notes
不论 opens a clause naming a range of possibilities, typically paired
with 都/也 in the result clause (不论刮风下雨，他都去跑步 "no matter the
weather, he goes running") — 无论, a close synonym, works identically
and is somewhat more formal in tone.

## Why this combination
预 "in advance, beforehand" (see also 预计) combined with 防 "to guard
against, to prevent" (see also 防止): to guard against in advance — to
take preventive measures.

## Notes
预防 names precautionary action taken ahead of a potential problem,
especially illness (预防疾病 "to prevent disease," 预防措施
"preventive measures") — 防止, its close relative, is used for
preventing a specific unwanted outcome from happening, often more
immediate than 预防's forward-looking precaution.

## Why this combination
入 "to enter" (see [[进入]], [[收入]]) + 口 "opening, mouth" (see [[出口]],
[[门口]], [[路口]]) → the opening you go in through. Mirror of [[出口]]: as
"import" (进口 is the commoner term) and, literally, "into the mouth"
(入口即化 "melts in the mouth").

## Why this combination
蛋 "egg" and 糕 "cake" (the same 糕 as in the native 年糕 "rice cake")
together calque the Western sponge cake specifically — "egg" flags the
ingredient that sets it apart from traditional Chinese steamed or fried
糕, which are typically rice- or flour-based without egg as a defining
ingredient.

## Notes
蛋糕 names Western-style cake specifically (生日蛋糕 "birthday cake"),
while native rice/glutinous-rice cakes keep their own names (年糕, 米糕) —
两 words that both end in 糕 but describe different culinary traditions.

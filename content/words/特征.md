## Why this combination
特 ("special, particular") plus 征 (in its merged-in sense "a sign, a
symptom, evidence summoned into notice," covered elsewhere in this app —
inherited from the separately-coined traditional 徵, unrelated to 征's
own "to march" sense) names a special sign that marks something out: a
distinguishing feature.

## Notes
特征 names an identifying trait used to recognize or classify something
(外貌特征, "physical characteristics") — the same 征 that appears in this
compound is unrelated in origin to 征 as in 征服 ("to conquer") or 出征
("to go on campaign"), despite sharing the modern character.

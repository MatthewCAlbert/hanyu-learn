## Why this combination
存 "to survive, to remain" and 在 "to be present at, to be located"
combine directly: something that remains, present somewhere — to exist.

## Notes
存在 is more abstract and philosophical in register than plain 有 "to
have, there is" — 存在 asks whether something is real or present at all,
where 有 simply states possession or presence.

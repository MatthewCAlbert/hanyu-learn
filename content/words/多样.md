## Why this combination

多 ("many") plus 样 ("a kind, an appearance," as in 一样, "the same
[lit. one + kind]") names having many kinds: diverse.

## Notes

多样化 ("diversification") and 形式多样 ("varied in form") both build on
this word — 多样 always describes variety among kinds or forms, distinct
from plain 多, which simply names a large quantity.

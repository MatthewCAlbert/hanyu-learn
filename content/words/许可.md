## Why this combination
许 "to permit" and 可 "may, can" are near-synonyms combined into the
formal word for official permission.

## Notes
许可 anchors 许可证 "licence, permit [document]," 未经许可 "without
authorization" — a formal, often legal/administrative register for
granted permission.

## Why this combination
美 "beautiful, fine, good" (see also 美丽, 美好) combined with 食 "food,
to eat" (see also 食品, 食物): fine food — a culinary delicacy.

## Notes
美食 names food prized for its quality or craftsmanship (美食家
"gourmet, food connoisseur," 美食节 "food festival") — 食物, the neutral
word for "food," carries no such appreciative or gourmet connotation.

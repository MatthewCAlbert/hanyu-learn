## Why this combination
另 "other, another" and 外 here in its extended sense "additional,
outside [the current set]" (as in 外加 "on top of") combine directly:
something beyond and separate from what's already been mentioned.

## Notes
另外 works as both a determiner before a noun ("another, a different")
and a sentence-level connector ("besides, in addition") — 另外一个问题
"another question" versus 另外，我还想说... "besides, I also want to
say...".

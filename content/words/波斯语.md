## Why this combination
波斯 is the phonetic rendering of Persia, plus 语. Names Persian / Farsi. 波 is
not "wave"; 斯 is a closer.

## Notes
伊朗 is the Extra country; 波斯语 is the usual language name, from the older
exonym.

## Why this combination
手 here is the same metaphorical "hand" seen in 手段 — a way of handling
something — combined with 法 "method." Together: a particular technique
or trick for accomplishing something, often with craft or cunning
involved.

## Notes
手法 covers artistic technique (写作手法 "writing technique") and,
critically, deceptive tactics (欺骗手法 "deceptive tactics") — context
decides whether it's admired craft or a suspicious trick.

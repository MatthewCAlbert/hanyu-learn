## Why this combination

简 ("simple, brief," as in 简单) plus 历 ("to pass through, experience,"
as in 经历, "experience [lit. pass through + pass through]") names a
brief account of what one has passed through: a résumé.

## Notes

投简历 ("to submit a résumé") and 简历模板 ("a résumé template") both
build on this word — 简历 always implies brevity, a condensed summary
rather than the full detailed 经历 it draws from.

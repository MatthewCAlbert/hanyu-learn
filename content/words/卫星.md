## Why this combination
卫 "to guard, to defend" and 星 "star, celestial body" combine into "a
guarding star" — a small body that circles and, metaphorically, watches
over a larger one, coined to translate the Western astronomical concept
of a satellite/moon.

## Notes
卫星 covers both natural moons (in older or technical usage) and
human-made satellites (人造卫星 "artificial satellite," 卫星电视 "satellite
TV") — the modern sense now dominates everyday use.

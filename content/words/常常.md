## Why this combination
常 "often, constant" doubled. Reduplicating an adverb like this gives it a
steady, habitual feel — a smoother rhythm than bare 常, which sounds clipped in
modern speech.

## Notes
Negated as 不常 (not 不常常). 经常 is the near-synonym and can itself be an
adjective ("regular"); 常常 is adverb-only.

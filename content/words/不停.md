## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 停 "to stop" gives exactly "not stopping" — the
standard word for an action continuing without pause.

## Notes
不停 typically follows the verb it modifies: 说个不停 "talking
non-stop," 下不停 "raining non-stop" — a common pattern pairing 不停 with
an action to emphasize its relentlessness.

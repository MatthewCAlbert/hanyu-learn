## Why this combination
提 "to raise, to bring up" (see [[提出]], [[提高]]) + resultative 到 "reach" —
raising a subject so it lands in the conversation: 他提到你了 "he mentioned
you," 上次提到的那件事.

## Notes
提到 = mention in passing; [[提出]] = put forward formally (a proposal, a
demand); 说到 = "speaking of…".

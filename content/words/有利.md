## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 有 "to have" takes 利 "advantage, profit,
benefit" (as in 利用 "to make use of") as its object — literally "to
have advantage" — used adjectivally for anything favourable.

## Notes
有利 modifies conditions or factors working in one's favour (有利条件
"favourable conditions," 有利于健康 "beneficial to health") — its
opposite is 不利 "unfavourable, disadvantageous," the plain negation.

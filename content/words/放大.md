## Why this combination
放 "to put, to release" with the resultative complement 大 "big" gives
"to make [something] big" — enlarging it.

## Notes
放大 covers literal enlargement (放大照片 "to enlarge a photo," 放大镜
"magnifying glass") and figurative exaggeration (放大问题 "to blow a
problem out of proportion").

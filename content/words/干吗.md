## Why this combination
干, here "to do" (as in 干活 "to work," a colloquial synonym of 做)
combined with 吗, here not the yes/no question particle but a
colloquial variant of 嘛/呢 seen in interrogatives: "doing what" — used
to ask what someone is doing or why, in casual speech.

## Notes
干吗 asks either "what are you doing" (你干吗呢 "what are you up to?")
or "why" (你干吗生气 "why are you angry?") depending on context — it is
strictly colloquial and spoken, not used in formal writing.

## Why this combination
反 "reversed, contrary" (see also 反对, 反应) combined with 正 "upright,
correct, straight": whether reversed or set right — either way, the
outcome is the same — grammaticalized into "anyway, regardless."

## Notes
反正 asserts that the outcome holds no matter the circumstances (反正我
不去 "I'm not going, anyway [whatever you say]") — it dismisses
alternatives as not mattering, a stronger, more conclusive stance than
plain 总之 "in short, to sum up."

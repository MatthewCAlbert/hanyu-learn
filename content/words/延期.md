## Why this combination

延 ("to extend, to delay," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
延长/延续) takes 期 ("a period, a term," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 期限/学期) as its object: extending a time period.

## Notes

延期付款 ("to defer payment") and 延期一周 ("postponed by a week") both
build on this word — 延期 always names pushing an existing deadline
later, distinct from 推迟 (covered elsewhere in this app), the more
general verb for postponing any event.

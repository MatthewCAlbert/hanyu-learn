## Why this combination
保 "to protect, to guard" (see also 保护, 保险) combined with 安 "safe,
secure" (as in 安全): to protect and keep secure — public security, and,
as the person who does it, a security guard.

## Notes
保安 names both the abstract activity of maintaining security (保安
工作 "security work") and, very commonly, the individual guard doing it
(当保安 "to work as a security guard") — 安全, its close relative, is the
general adjective/noun "safe, safety" without this occupational sense.

## Why this combination
白 "white, bright" + 天 "day" → the light hours, as opposed to 黑夜 "the dark
night." 白 for "bright / daylit" also in 白日 (broad daylight), 明白.

## Notes
白天 vs. 晚上 is the everyday day/night split. 白 here is not "blank" or
"in vain" (its other senses).

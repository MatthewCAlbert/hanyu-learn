## Why this combination
展 "to unfold, to spread out for viewing" and 览 "to view" (see 览's own
etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a public
display.

## Notes
展览 anchors 展览会 "exhibition," 艺术展览 "art exhibition" — a formal,
organized public display, distinct from casual 展示 "to show/display."

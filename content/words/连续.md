## Why this combination
连 "to link, to connect [without gap]" (as in 连接 "to connect") combined
with 续 "to continue, to connect on" (see also 持续): linked one after
another without a gap — consecutive, in a row.

## Notes
连续 modifies events repeated without a break (连续三天下雨 "it rained
for three days in a row," 连续剧 "a [TV] serial drama") — 持续, its close
relative, describes one state or process lasting continuously, rather
than discrete events recurring in sequence.

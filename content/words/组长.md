## Why this combination
组 "a group, a section, a set" (see [[小组]], [[组成]]) + 长 (*zhǎng*) "head,
chief" — the "-chief" suffix of [[班长]], [[队长]], [[院长]]. The person in
charge of a small group.

## Notes
长 is *zhǎng*. The smallest rung of the 长 title series: 组长 → [[班长]] →
[[队长]] → [[校长]].

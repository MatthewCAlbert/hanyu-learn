## Why this combination
数 "number" and 目 (here "an item, an entry," as in a counted list) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a counted total.

## Notes
数目 anchors 数目不小 "a considerable number," 精确数目 "the exact
figure" — close to plain 数量, though 数目 leans toward a countable,
specific total rather than quantity in general.

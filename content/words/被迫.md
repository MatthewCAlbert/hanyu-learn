## Why this combination

被 (the passive marker, "by," as in 被发现, "to be discovered") plus 迫
("to force, to compel," as in 压迫, "to oppress") fuses into a fixed
adverbial: "forced (by circumstance)."

## Notes

被迫 sits before the verb it modifies without needing an explicit agent
(被迫离开, "forced to leave") — unlike a full passive sentence with 被,
it doesn't require naming who or what is doing the forcing.

## Why this combination
母 "mother" paired with 亲 in its "close kin, parent" sense (see 亲's own
etymology: 見 "to see" plus a sound component, glossed by Shuowen as
"to reach [a depth of feeling]") — the formal, written counterpart to
colloquial 妈妈.

## Notes
母亲 is the register used in writing, official contexts and formal speech
(母亲节 "Mother's Day"); 妈妈 is what is actually said at home and is not
interchangeable with 母亲 in casual address.

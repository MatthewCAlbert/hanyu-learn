## Why this combination
高 "high," 跟 "heel," and 鞋 "shoes" stack into exactly "high-heel
shoes" — a direct, fully compositional description.

## Notes
高跟鞋 anchors 穿高跟鞋 "to wear high heels" — the standard word,
contrasting with 平底鞋 "flat shoes" (平底 "flat sole").

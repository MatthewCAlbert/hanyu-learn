## Why this combination
如 "if, supposing, as" (classical 如…則…) + 果 "in fact, as expected, indeed"
(the same 果 as 果然). Roughly "supposing it does turn out that…". The 果 element
is now inert; 如果 is learned whole as the ordinary "if."

## Notes
Often correlates with 就 or 的话 in the main clause: 如果下雨，就不去了 /
如果下雨的话…. Near-synonyms: 要是 (colloquial), 假如 (formal).

## Why this combination
除 "to remove, to exclude" carries the core meaning; 了 here is a fixed
grammatical marker rather than a separate "to finish" or aspectual
meaning, helping fix 除 as a complete preposition. "With [this] removed
[from consideration]" gives both readings the word carries: exclusion
("except for") and, in the common 除了...还... pattern, addition
("besides...also...").

## Notes
除了...以外 is the fuller, more explicit version of the same
construction; 除了 alone is the everyday contracted form.

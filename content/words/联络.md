## Why this combination
联 "to link, to unite" and 络 "a net, a network" (the same 络 as in 网络)
are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for staying in contact.

## Notes
联络 is close to 联系 "to contact" in meaning; 联络 leans slightly more
toward maintaining an ongoing connection or liaison role (联络员 "liaison
officer") than a single act of reaching out.

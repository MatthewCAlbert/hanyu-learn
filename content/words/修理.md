## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 修 ("to repair, to mend," as in 装修, "to
renovate") and 理 ("to manage, to put in order," as in 管理) both
independently name restoring something to working order. Pairing them
gives the everyday word for repair.

## Notes

修理 covers everyday fixes (修理自行车, "to repair a bicycle") — 维修
(covered elsewhere in this app) leans toward more technical/professional
maintenance and service.

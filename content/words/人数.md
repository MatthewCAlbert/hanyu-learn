## Why this combination
人 "person" + 数 (*shù*) "number, count" (see [[数字]], [[大多数]]) → how many
people: 人数不够 "not enough people," 报名人数.

## Notes
人数 = a headcount for an event / group; [[人口]] = the population of a place;
[[人们]] = people in general. 数 is *shù* here (number), not *shǔ* "to count."

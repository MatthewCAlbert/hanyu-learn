## Why this combination
Two members of the "happy" family: 喜 "to be pleased, to delight in" and 欢
"joyful, merry." The pair names the mild positive attitude "to like" — weaker
than 爱, and applicable to things, activities, and people alike.

## Notes
喜 keeps its stronger flavour in 喜事 (a happy occasion) and 喜欢 vs 恭喜
(congratulations). Here it has softened to everyday "like."

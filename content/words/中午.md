## Why this combination
午 is the noon double-hour; 中 "middle" pins it to the centre of the day. Sits
between [[上午]] (forenoon) and [[下午]] (afternoon), which take their names from
this same 午 pivot.

## Notes
中午 covers roughly 11–1, the lunch-and-nap window (午饭, 午睡). 正午 is exactly
12.

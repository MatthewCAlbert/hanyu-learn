## Why this combination
句 "a sentence, a phrase, a line" (一句话, 诗句) + the toneless noun suffix 子
(see [[孩子]], [[例子]]) → the everyday grammatical noun "a sentence":
造句子 "make sentences," 这个句子太长.

## Notes
句 alone is also the measure word for utterances: 说了一句 "said one thing,"
三句话. 单位 for language: 字 → 词 → [[词语]] → 句子 → 段 → [[课文]].

## Why this combination
小 "little" + 朋友 "friend" — a friendly, slightly institutional way to say
"child," used by adults addressing or referring to kids collectively:
小朋友们，坐好 "children, sit nicely."

## Notes
Warmer and more public-facing than 孩子; a teacher or TV host uses 小朋友, a
parent says 孩子. Jocular for an adult: 你这个小朋友.

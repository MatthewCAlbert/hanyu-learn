## Why this combination
果 alone is "fruit / a fruit-bearing result." 水 "water" marks the juicy, fresh
kind you eat raw — against 干果 "dried fruit and nuts" and 坚果 "hard-shelled
nuts." So "watery fruit."

## Notes
果 keeps the abstract sense in 结果 (result), 效果 (effect), 后果 (consequence).
水果店 greengrocer.

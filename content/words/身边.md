## Why this combination
身 "body" + 边 "side" → right beside a person: 带在身边 "keep it on you,"
身边的朋友 "the friends around one." 边 as a bare locative, like 身上, [[路边]].

## Notes
One of the 身X locatives with [[身上]] "on one's person" and 身后 "behind one."

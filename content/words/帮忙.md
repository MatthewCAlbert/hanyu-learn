## Why this combination
忙 "busy" is nominalised here to "a pressing task"; 帮 "to help" takes it as an
object — "to help with (someone's) busy business." So it splits: 帮个忙 "do
(me) a favour," 帮了我很多忙 "helped me a lot."

## Notes
Because 帮忙 already has an object, a person goes in with 给 or before the verb:
帮我个忙 / 给他帮忙. Transitive 帮助 takes the person directly: 帮助他.

## Why this combination
[[瑞典]] + 语 "language," the same mould as 法语, 日语 and [[英文]]. The stem is still
a country or people name — usually a phonetic loan — so do not re-read those
characters as a new story.

## Notes
See [[瑞典]]. 语 leans spoken.

## Why this combination
地 "earth, ground" and 震 "to shake, to quake" (see 震's own etymology —
originally naming thunder) combine directly into "the earth shaking" —
as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
地震 anchors 地震带 "earthquake zone," 地震预警 "earthquake early warning" —
the standard scientific/everyday term, alongside figurative uses like
政坛地震 "a political earthquake."

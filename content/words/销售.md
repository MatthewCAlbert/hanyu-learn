## Why this combination
销 carries its "to dispose of, to get rid of (stock)" sense (as in 销毁,
"to destroy/dispose of"), and 售 is the plain verb "to sell" (as in 出
售). Getting rid of stock by selling it off gives the formal, commercial
register's word for sales.

Rated "semi" for 销's "to dispose of" sense, one step removed from its
literal origin "to melt down."

## Notes
销售 is the business/commercial register (销售部, "sales department";
销售额, "sales figures") — for an everyday casual sale, 卖 is the plain,
common verb.

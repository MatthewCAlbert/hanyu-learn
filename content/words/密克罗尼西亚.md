## Why this combination
A phonetic rendering of Micronesia (密克羅尼西亞). Not a calque of "small islands"
(that would be 小岛群). 密 is not "secret"; 克 is not "to be able to."

## Notes
尼西亚 as in [[印度尼西亚]] (-nesia). Names the Federated States in ordinary use.

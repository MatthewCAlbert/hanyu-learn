## Why this combination
迷 "confused, lost (one's way)" plus 信 "to believe" gives exactly "a
confused belief" — the standard word for belief without rational
grounding.

## Notes
迷信 works as noun ("破除迷信" "to dispel superstition") and verb ("迷信
权威" "to blindly believe in authority," a figurative extension beyond
folk religion) — always implies belief held uncritically.

## Why this combination
主 "main, primary, taking the lead" (see also 主意, 主动) combined with
张, here "to stretch, to open out, to put forward [a view]" (as in 张开
"to open up"): to put forward as one's primary [view] — to advocate, a
stance.

## Notes
主张 works as verb ("主张改革" "to advocate reform") and noun ("他的
主张" "his position/stance") — 建议, its neighbour, is a suggestion
offered to others, while 主张 is one's own held position or belief.

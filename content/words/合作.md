## Why this combination
合 "to join, to close together" and 作 "to do, to act" combine
directly: joining together to act as one — to cooperate.

## Notes
合作 names cooperation between distinct, often equal parties (companies,
countries, colleagues); contrast 帮助 "to help," which implies one party
assisting another rather than two parties acting jointly.

## Why this combination
实 ("real, actual") plus 施 ("to carry out, to spread into action,"
covered elsewhere in this app — a banner unfurling in the wind) names
carrying a plan out for real, into actual practice.

## Notes
实施 pairs naturally with formal plans and policies (实施计划, "to
implement a plan"; 正式实施, "to formally take effect") — a written
register, more formal than the everyday 做.

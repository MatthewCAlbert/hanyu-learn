## Why this combination
漏 "to leak" and 洞 "hole" combine into exactly "a hole that leaks" — the
literal sense extends readily to any gap that lets something slip
through, whether logic, security, or law.

## Notes
漏洞 anchors 安全漏洞 "security vulnerability," 法律漏洞 "legal loophole,"
逻辑漏洞 "a hole in the logic" — the figurative senses now dominate
everyday and technical use over the literal physical leak.

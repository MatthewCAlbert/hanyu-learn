## Why this combination
调 (here "to shift, to reassign") and 动 "to move" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for reassigning people or resources.

## Notes
调动 covers job transfers (调动工作 "to transfer jobs") and mobilizing
resources or enthusiasm (调动积极性 "to bring people's motivation into
play") — always about redirecting something to where it's needed.

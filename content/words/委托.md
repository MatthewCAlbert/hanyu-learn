## Why this combination
委 "to entrust" (see 委's own etymology — from 女 + 禾, "to yield
compliantly," extended to "entrusting a matter to someone's handling")
and 托 "to support, to entrust" (see 托's etymology — a palm holding
something steady) are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for
delegation.

## Notes
委托 is the formal/legal register for delegation: 委托律师 "to retain a
lawyer," 委托书 "power of attorney [document]" — always implies formal,
often written authorization.

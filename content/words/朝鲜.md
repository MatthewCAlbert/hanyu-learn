## Why this combination
The Chinese reading of Korean Joseon (조선): 朝 "morning" + 鲜 "fresh / bright,"
traditionally "morning calm." In PRC usage 朝鲜 names the DPRK and,
historically, the peninsula. It is not a phonetic of "Korea."

## Notes
Same stem in [[朝鲜语]] (the DPRK name for Korean). 韩国 / 韩语 are omitted: 韩 is not
in the HSK hanzi set.

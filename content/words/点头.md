## Why this combination
点 "to tap, to touch briefly, to dot" (点一下) + 头 "head" as its object — to
"tap" the head downward, i.e. nod. In assent (点头同意), in greeting (点头打招呼),
or dozing (点头打瞌睡). Verb-object: 点了点头, 点一下头.

## Notes
点头 = nod (yes / acknowledge); 摇头 "shake the head" (no). 点 "tap / dot" also
in [[重点]], [[观点]], 一点儿.

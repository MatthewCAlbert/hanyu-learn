## Why this combination
紧 "tight, urgent" (see also 紧张, 赶紧) and 急 "urgent, pressing,
anxious" (as in 着急 "anxious") are close synonyms, paired into the
standard adjective for a situation demanding immediate action.

## Notes
紧急 modifies situations requiring an immediate response (紧急情况
"an emergency situation," 紧急会议 "an emergency meeting") — it is
stronger and more formal than 急, which can describe ordinary personal
haste as well as genuine urgency.

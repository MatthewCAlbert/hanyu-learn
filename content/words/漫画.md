## Why this combination
漫 here carries its "free, unrestrained" sense (see 漫's own etymology)
rather than a literal meaning, combined with 画 "picture" — loose,
free-flowing drawings, as opposed to formal, realistic painting.

## Notes
漫画 covers editorial cartoons (政治漫画 "political cartoon") and the
Japanese comic-book tradition specifically (日本漫画 "Japanese manga") —
one word spanning both a drawing style and an entire narrative medium.

## Why this combination
收 "to gather in, to collect" and 购 "to purchase" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for buying up something from multiple
sources.

## Notes
收购 covers everyday bulk buying (收购废品 "to buy up scrap") and
corporate acquisition (收购公司 "to acquire a company") — always
purchasing something in quantity or as a controlling stake, not a single
retail purchase.

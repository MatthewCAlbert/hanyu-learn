## Why this combination
冲 "to rush at, to clash" and 突 "to rush forward suddenly" (see 突's role
in 突破) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for conflict
or collision, whether physical, military, or of interests.

## Notes
冲突 spans armed conflict (武装冲突 "armed conflict"), scheduling clashes
(时间冲突 "a scheduling conflict"), and interpersonal disagreement (发生
冲突 "a conflict arose") — one word for collision of any kind.

## Why this combination
身 "body" + the locative 上 "on." Where something is carried or worn, or,
figuratively, what a person is responsible for: 钱在他身上 "he's got the money on
him," 责任在你身上 "the responsibility falls on you."

## Notes
One of the 身X locatives with 身边 (at one's side) and 身后 (behind one). 上 as a
bare locative suffix, not "up."

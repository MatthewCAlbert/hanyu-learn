## Why this combination
力 "power, force" and 量 "capacity, quantity" combine directly: the
measurable amount of force something can bring to bear.

## Notes
力量 covers physical strength, social/political power, and abstract
influence alike (团结的力量 "the power of unity") — a single word for
"strength" in both the literal and figurative sense, much as English
"power" and "force" both do.

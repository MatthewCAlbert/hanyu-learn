## Why this combination
受 "to receive, to undergo, to bear" + resultative 到 "reach." Marks being on
the receiving end of an action, good or bad: 受到欢迎 "be welcomed,"
受到批评 "come in for criticism," 受到影响.

## Notes
受到 + abstract noun is a common way to render an English passive. Contrast
[[接受]] (actively accept) and [[收到]] (physically get).

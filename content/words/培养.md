## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 培 ("to bank up earth around a plant's roots,"
covered elsewhere in this app) and 养 ("to nurture, to raise," as in
营养) both independently name tending growth. Pairing them gives the
standard word for cultivating a person's skills, habits, or character.

## Notes
培养习惯 ("to cultivate a habit") and 培养人才 ("to train talented people")
both extend this pair's literal "tending a plant's growth" image into
human development.

## Why this combination
课 "class, lesson" (as in 上课 "to attend class") combined with 程 "a
stage, a course, a measured progression" (see also 过程): the staged
progression of lessons — a course of study.

## Notes
课程 names a structured academic course or curriculum (课程表 "class
schedule," 必修课程 "required course") — 课, alone, names a single class
session, while 课程 refers to the whole organised sequence of study.

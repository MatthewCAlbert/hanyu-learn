## Why this combination

成 ("to become, to complete," as in 成功) takes 人 ("person") as its
object: to become a (full) person — an adult.

## Notes

成人 works as a noun (成人票, "adult ticket") much like other
verb-object compounds that shifted to naming a role (对象, "target,"
from "facing an object"); 成年 ("to come of age") is a close
near-synonym built the same way, with 年 "year" instead of 人.

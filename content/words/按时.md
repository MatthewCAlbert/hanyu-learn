## Why this combination

按 ("according to, in line with," as in 按照, "in accordance with") plus
时 ("time," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 准时) names acting
in line with a set time: on schedule.

## Notes

按时完成 ("to finish on schedule") and 按时吃药 ("to take medicine on
schedule") both build on this word — close to 准时 (covered elsewhere in
this app) but emphasizing adherence to a plan or routine rather than
punctuality to a single moment.

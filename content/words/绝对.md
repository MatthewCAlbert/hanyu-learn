## Why this combination
绝 here carries its extended sense "cut off entirely, with no
exception" (recall its etymology: a thread cut clean through) rather
than the more basic "to sever." 对 contributes "correct, exact." Together:
"exactly so, with nothing left over or excepted" — absolute.

## Notes
绝对 as an adverb intensifies strongly — 绝对不行 "absolutely not" —
ruling out any possible exception, a stronger claim than plain 不 alone
would make.

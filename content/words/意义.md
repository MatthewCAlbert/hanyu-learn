## Why this combination
意 "thought, idea, meaning" and 义 here in its extended sense "the sense
[of a word or matter]" (as in 字义 "the meaning of a character," a
different job from 义's more common "righteousness") are close
synonyms, paired for a fuller word covering both linguistic meaning and
broader significance.

## Notes
意义 covers both the narrow "meaning [of a word]" and the broad
"significance, importance [of an action or event]" — 有意义 "meaningful,
worthwhile" leans on the second sense.

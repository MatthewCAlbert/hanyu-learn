## Why this combination
本, here "root, origin, one's own" (see also 基本, 本来) combined with
事 "affair, matter" (see also 事情, 事故): the matter [one is] rooted
in/capable of — one's own inherent ability.

## Notes
本事 in colloquial speech means "skill, ability" (真有本事 "really
skilled/capable"), read with a neutral tone on 事 — a separate,
literary sense, read with full tone, names the original source material
behind an adapted work (这部电影的本事是一本小说 "this film's source
material is a novel").

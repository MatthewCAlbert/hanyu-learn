## Why this combination
保 "to keep, to protect" and 持 "to hold, to grasp" combine directly:
holding onto something so as to keep it as it is.

## Notes
保持 takes an existing state as its object and keeps it unchanged
(保持安静 "keep quiet," 保持联系 "stay in touch") — distinct from 维持,
which leans more toward keeping something going under strain or
difficulty.

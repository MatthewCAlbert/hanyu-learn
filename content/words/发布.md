## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to release" and 布 (here "to spread, to disseminate,"
not its everyday "cloth" sense) combine directly: releasing something so
it spreads widely.

## Notes
发布 is the formal register for official announcements and releases:
发布会 "press conference" (literally "release meeting"), 发布消息 "to
release news," 发布产品 "to launch a product."

## Why this combination
补 "to supplement, to make up for" and 贴 (here "to aid, to add to," as
in 贴补) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for financial
support added on top of regular income.

## Notes
补贴 anchors 生活补贴 "living allowance," 政府补贴 "government subsidy" —
always extra money provided to offset a cost or shortfall, distinct from
a full salary or price.

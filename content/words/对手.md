## Why this combination
对 "facing, opposite" (see also 对方) combined with 手 in its "a person
skilled at [something]" sense (see also 选手): the skilled person facing
one across a contest — an opponent.

## Notes
对手 specifically names the person or side one competes against, with an
implication of being a worthy or well-matched challenger (强大的对手
"a formidable opponent") — 对方, its close relative, is more neutral and
does not carry the same competitive charge.

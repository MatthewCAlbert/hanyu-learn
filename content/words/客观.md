## Why this combination
客, here "guest, external, from outside oneself" (as in 客人 "guest")
combined with 观 "view, outlook" (see also 观察, 乐观): a view from
outside oneself — objective, unbiased.

## Notes
客观 describes judgment or facts independent of personal feeling
(客观事实 "objective fact," 客观地看 "to look at objectively") — its
opposite is 主观 "subjective," built the same way with 主 "self, host"
in place of 客.

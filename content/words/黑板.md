## Why this combination
黑 "black" (see [[黑色]]) + 板 "board, plank, plate" (木板, 地板) → the dark
board a teacher writes on with chalk. 黑板报 "wall newspaper (on a blackboard),"
擦黑板 "clean the board."

## Notes
Now often a 白板 "whiteboard" or 电子白板 "interactive whiteboard," but 黑板 is
still the default word for the classroom writing surface. 板 also in 老板
"boss," [[水平]]'s cousin 木板.

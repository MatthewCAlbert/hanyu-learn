## Why this combination
增 "to increase, to add to" (as in 增长 "to grow") and 加 "to add" (as in
更加 "even more") are close synonyms, paired into the standard verb for
an increase in quantity.

## Notes
增加 takes a countable or measurable quantity as its object (增加收入
"to increase income," 人口增加 "the population increases") — its opposite
is 减少 "to decrease, to reduce."

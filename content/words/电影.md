## Why this combination
"Electric shadows." When film reached China it was understood through the older
shadow-play tradition (皮影戏) — moving silhouettes thrown on a screen — now
driven by electricity. The 电 prefix is the same one in 电话, 电视, 电脑.

## Notes
看电影 "see a film," 电影院 "cinema." 影 alone still means "shadow" (影子).

## Why this combination
差 "to fall short, to lack" plus 一点儿 "a little bit" gives exactly
"short by a little bit" — the fuller form of 差点儿, built on the same
near-miss logic: something almost happened but fell just short.

## Notes
差一点儿 anchors 差一点儿就赢了 "almost won," 差一点儿摔倒 "nearly fell" —
functionally identical to the more compact 差点儿, with 一 optionally
inserted for emphasis or rhythm.

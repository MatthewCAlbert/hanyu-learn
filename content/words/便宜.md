## Why this combination
便 "convenient, advantageous" + 宜 "fitting, proper" (适宜, 相宜). Read
*biànyí*, the pair means "convenient, expedient" (行方便，得便宜). Read
*piányi*, it narrowed to "advantageous in price" — cheap — and to "an
unearned advantage": 占便宜 "get the better of someone," 便宜了他 "he got off
lightly."

## Notes
Two readings, two meanings: *biànyí* "expedient" (便宜行事) vs. *piányi* "cheap."
便 is the same character as in [[方便]], [[随便]].

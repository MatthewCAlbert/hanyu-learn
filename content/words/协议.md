## Why this combination
协 "to cooperate, to unite" and 议 "to discuss, an opinion" combine
directly: a discussion that ends in cooperative agreement.

## Notes
协议 spans formal treaties (贸易协议 "trade agreement") and casual
arrangements (私下协议 "a private understanding") — always a mutually
agreed outcome, written or spoken.

## Why this combination
请 "to request, please" + 问 "to ask" — "may I ask." A politeness frame placed
before a question to a stranger: 请问，洗手间在哪儿？

## Notes
请 + verb is the general polite-request pattern (请坐, 请进, 请等一下). 请问
specifically softens an information question.

## Why this combination

纯 ("pure," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 单纯) plus 净
("clean," as in 干净, "clean [lit. dry + clean]") plus 水 ("water")
names water made pure and clean through purification: purified water.

## Notes

纯净水 (purified/distilled water) contrasts with 矿泉水 (covered
elsewhere in this app, mineral water, which retains natural minerals)
— both are bottled drinking water, differing in processing method.

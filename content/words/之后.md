## Why this combination
后 is the ordinary word for "behind, after" in time or space; 之 is the
literary connector (equivalent to 的) that links a time or event to that
position — "[event] 之 [after]" reads as "[event]'s after," i.e. the time
that follows it. The same 之ATTR pattern builds the whole family 之前
(before), 之间 (between), 之内 (within), 之外 (outside).

Once a learner knows 之 as a possessive/linking particle rather than a
separate meaningful word, 之后 becomes fully predictable — hence "semi"
rather than "opaque."

## Notes
之后 can stand alone at the start of a sentence to mean "afterwards" (之后，
他就走了) or follow a noun/clause to mean "after X" (下课之后) — no 的 is
inserted, since 之 already does that job.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 阻 ("to block, to hinder," covered elsewhere in
this app) and 止 ("to stop") both independently name obstruction.
Pairing them gives the standard word for actively preventing something
from happening or continuing.

## Notes
阻止 always takes an action or event as its object (阻止事故发生, "to
prevent an accident from happening") — it stops something from occurring,
distinct from 阻碍, which describes an ongoing hindrance rather than an
outright block.

## Why this combination
画 "a painting, a drawing; to paint / draw" with the erhua suffix 儿, which
here makes it the everyday spoken noun for a picture: 画儿画得好, 一张画儿.
Non-erhua 画 is the same word.

## Notes
画 also a verb (画画儿 "draw a picture" — verb + object) and in compounds:
[[画家]] "painter," 漫画 "comics," [[图画]] (not this level).

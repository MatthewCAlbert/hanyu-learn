## Why this combination

充电 ("to charge [a battery]," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 器
("a device, an instrument," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
器官) names the device used to charge: a charger.

## Notes

手机充电器 ("a phone charger") is the everyday household-object phrase —
充电器 names the physical tool, distinct from 充电, the verb naming the
action it performs.

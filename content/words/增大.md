## Why this combination
增 "to increase" plus the resultative complement 大 "big" gives exactly
"to increase, becoming bigger."

## Notes
增大 anchors 音量增大 "the volume increased," 风险增大 "the risk has
grown" — used for measurable increases in size, volume, or degree, close
to 增加 but emphasizing the resulting larger magnitude specifically.

## Why this combination
风 here carries its figurative "manner, custom, bearing" sense (as in
作风, 风气), not its literal "wind" sense, and 格 is "a pattern, a
standard, a framework" (as in 格式, 性格). A person or work's characteristic
manner, held to its own consistent pattern, is exactly what "style" means.

Rated "semi" for 风's figurative "manner" sense, a real but non-obvious
extension of its everyday "wind" meaning.

## Notes
风格 covers both personal style (个人风格) and artistic/period style
(建筑风格, "architectural style") — a broad, mostly formal/written word
for characteristic manner.

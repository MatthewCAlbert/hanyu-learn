## Why this combination
物 "thing, object" and 质 "substance, quality, essence" combine directly:
the physical stuff a thing is made of.

## Notes
物质 anchors the philosophical opposition 物质/精神 "material/spiritual"
and the adjective 物质的 "materialistic" — always concrete, physical
reality as opposed to ideas or feelings.

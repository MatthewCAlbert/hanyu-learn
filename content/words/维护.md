## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 维 ("to hold together, to maintain," covered
elsewhere in this app) and 护 ("to protect," as in 保护) both
independently name keeping something intact and safe. Pairing them gives
the standard word for defending or upholding something.

## Notes
维护权益 ("to protect one's rights/interests") and 维护和平 ("to
safeguard peace") both build on this pair's core "actively protecting
something from harm or decay" sense.

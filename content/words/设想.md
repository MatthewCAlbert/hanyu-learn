## Why this combination
设 (here "to set up, to plan") and 想 "to think" combine into "to plan
out in thought" — the standard word for imagining a scenario or
formulating a tentative idea.

## Notes
设想 works as verb ("设想一下" "imagine for a moment") and noun ("最初的
设想" "the original idea/plan") — also carries a "consider (someone
else's needs)" sense: 为他人设想 "to think of others."

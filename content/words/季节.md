## Why this combination

季 ("a season, a quarter of the year," as in 四季, "the four seasons")
plus 节 ("a joint, a segment, a point in time," as in 春节, "Spring
Festival") names a segment of the year: a season.

Rated "semi" for 节's more abstract "segment/point in the calendar" role
here, distinct from its literal "bamboo joint" origin.

## Notes

季节 names the general concept of "season" (换季节, "to change seasons");
a specific season is named directly (春天/夏天/秋天/冬天), with 季节
reserved for talking about seasonality itself.

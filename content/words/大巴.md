## Why this combination

大 ("big") plus 巴 (a bound syllable borrowed purely for its sound from
"bus," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 巴士) names a large
bus: a coach.

Rated "semi": 大 carries its literal meaning, but 巴 contributes only
borrowed sound, not native meaning — half the word is meaningful, half
is a phonetic loan.

## Notes

大巴 truncates 大型巴士 ("large-type bus") into a compact everyday word —
小巴 ("a minibus," covered elsewhere in this app's 巴士 entry) is built
the same way with 小 "small" instead of 大.

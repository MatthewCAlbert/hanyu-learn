## Why this combination
地 ("place, ground") plus 址 ("a site, a foundation location," bound
almost exclusively into this word and 旧址 "former site") names the fixed
location a place can be found at — an address.

Rated "semi" because 址 essentially never appears alone or in other
everyday compounds, so learners have to take its meaning largely on trust
from this one word.

## Notes
网址 (web address, URL) extends the same 址 morpheme from physical
addresses to a "location" on the internet.

## Why this combination
春 "spring" + 天, here "season / time of year" — the same suffix as 夏天,
秋天, [[冬天]]. Not "sky" or "day."

## Notes
春 alone in [[春节]] "Spring Festival," 青春 "youth" (lit. "green spring"),
春假 "spring break." Figurative 春天 = a time of renewal / hope.

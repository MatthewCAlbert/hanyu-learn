## Why this combination
真 "true, genuine, real" and 实 "solid, actual, true" (as in 事实 "fact,"
现实 "reality") are close synonyms, paired into the standard adjective
for truthfulness and authenticity.

## Notes
真实 describes information, feelings and accounts as genuine and
accurate (真实的故事 "a true story," 真实的感情 "genuine feelings") —
真, alone, is the more general "real, true" and also functions as an
intensifying adverb ("真好" "really good"), a use 真实 does not share.

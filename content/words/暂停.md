## Why this combination
暂 "temporary" (see 暂's own etymology) modifying 停 "to stop" gives
exactly "to stop temporarily" — a pause rather than a permanent halt.

## Notes
暂停 spans sports (叫暂停 "to call a time-out"), media (暂停播放 "to pause
playback"), and policy (暂停营业 "to suspend business operations") — a
temporary stop across very different contexts.

## Why this combination
凉 "cool, cold" (as in 凉快) combined with 水 "water": cold water.

## Notes
凉水 usually implies cold, often unboiled tap water, as contrasted with
开水 "boiled water" — drinking 凉水 rather than 热水 or 开水 is a
recurring topic in Chinese health-conscious small talk.

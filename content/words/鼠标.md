## Why this combination
鼠 "mouse (the rodent)" plus 标 (here "pointer, indicator," as in 标记)
calques English "mouse" — the shape and cable resembling a small
creature — while adding 标 to specify the input device's pointing
function.

## Notes
鼠标 anchors 鼠标垫 "mouse pad," 无线鼠标 "wireless mouse" — the standard
term for the computing device, distinct from 老鼠, the everyday word for
the animal.

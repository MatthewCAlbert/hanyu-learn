## Why this combination
国 "country" + 外 "outside" → outside the country. 国内 "domestic" is its
mirror. 在国外 "abroad," 国外市场 "overseas markets."

## Notes
Overlaps 海外 "overseas" (literally "beyond the seas"), which carries a
diaspora nuance. [[外国]] flips the order for "a foreign country" as an entity.

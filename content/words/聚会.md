## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 聚 ("to gather, to assemble," covered elsewhere
in this app) and 会 ("to meet," as in 会议) both independently name
coming together. Pairing them gives the standard everyday word for a
social gathering.

## Notes
聚会 leans social and casual (朋友聚会, "a friends' get-together") — for a
formal business or organizational meeting, plain 会议 is the more
appropriate word.

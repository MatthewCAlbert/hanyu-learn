## Why this combination
设 "to set up, to arrange" and 计 "to calculate, to plan" combine
directly: arranging something according to a calculated plan — to
design.

## Notes
设计 covers both creative design (服装设计 "fashion design") and
technical/engineering design (工程设计) without distinguishing them —
one word for both artistic and functional planning.

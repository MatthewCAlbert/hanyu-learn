## Why this combination
护 ("to protect, to guard over," as in 保护) plus 士 (a professional-title
suffix, covered elsewhere in this app) names one whose profession is
protecting/caring for the sick: a nurse.

Rated "semi" for 士's productive title-suffix role, the same pattern
covered in this app's entries for 女士 and 士兵.

## Notes
护士 (nurse) pairs naturally with 医生 (doctor) in describing hospital
staff — both essential but distinct roles, 护士 focused on patient care
rather than diagnosis.

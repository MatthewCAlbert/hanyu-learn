## Why this combination
学 "to study" + 生, here in its old sense "person, one engaged in" — the same
生 as in 医生 (doctor), 先生 (see [[先生]]). So "one who studies."

## Notes
生 toneless (*xuésheng*). 大学生 university student, 中学生 secondary,
小学生 primary — the 学生 core with a school-level prefix.

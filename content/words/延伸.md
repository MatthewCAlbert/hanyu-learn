## Why this combination
延 "to extend, to prolong" and 伸 "to stretch out" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for something extending further in space
or scope.

## Notes
延伸 covers physical extension (道路向远方延伸 "the road stretches into
the distance") and figurative extension (延伸到其他领域 "extending into
other fields") — always growth outward from an existing point.

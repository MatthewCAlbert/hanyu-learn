## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 合 "to fit, to match, to accord with" (合身, 符合) + 适 "to suit,
to be fitting" (适合, 适应). What matches the need or occasion: 合适的时间,
这件衣服很合适.

## Notes
合适 (predicative: 很合适) and 适合 (transitive: 适合我) are the same idea with
the parts reversed — a common Chinese pattern (合适 / 适合, [[互相]] / 相互).

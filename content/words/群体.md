## Why this combination
群 "a crowd, a group" and 体 "body, entity" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for a collective body of people or organisms.

## Notes
群体 favours sociological and biological groupings (社会群体 "social
group," 弱势群体 "vulnerable/disadvantaged group," 蜂群体 "bee colony") —
a formal, structural sense of "group" beyond a casual gathering.

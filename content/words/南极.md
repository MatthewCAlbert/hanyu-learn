## Why this combination
南 "south" plus 极 "pole, extreme point" gives exactly "the southern
pole" — the standard geographic term, mirroring 北极 "North Pole."

## Notes
南极 anchors 南极洲 "Antarctica (the continent)," 南极探险 "Antarctic
expedition" — the geographic pole and, loosely, the surrounding polar
region.

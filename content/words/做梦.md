## Why this combination

做 ("to do, to make") takes 梦 ("a dream") as its object: to dream.

## Notes

做梦 covers both literal sleep-dreams (昨晚做梦了, "I had a dream last
night") and, said dismissively, wishful fantasizing (你做梦吧, "you're
dreaming/keep dreaming" — i.e. "that'll never happen").

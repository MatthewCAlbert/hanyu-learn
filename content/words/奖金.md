## Why this combination
奖 ("a prize, an award," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 金 ("money,
metal") names a prize paid out in money: a cash bonus or award.

## Notes
奖金 names the money itself (发奖金, "to pay out a bonus") — distinct
from 奖品 (a physical prize/gift) and from plain 奖 (the award/honor in
the abstract).

## Why this combination
渴 "thirsty" is used figuratively here for an intense craving, combined
with 望 "to look toward, to hope for" — together, a desire as urgent as
thirst, fixed on a hoped-for goal.

## Notes
渴望 is stronger and more emotionally charged than plain 希望 "to hope":
渴望自由 "to yearn for freedom," 渴望成功 "to crave success" — always an
intense, felt longing rather than a mild wish.

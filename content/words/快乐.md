## Why this combination
快 here in its older sense "pleased, gratified" (痛快, 快意) — not "fast" — +
乐 (*lè*) "joyful, happy" (see 音乐, where the same character is *yuè*
"music"). A synonym pair for cheerful contentment.

## Notes
The go-to word in good wishes: 生日快乐, 新年快乐, 节日快乐. 乐 is *lè* here.

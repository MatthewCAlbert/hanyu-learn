## Why this combination
游 "to roam, to tour" (see [[旅游]], 游泳) + 客 "guest, visitor" (see [[客人]],
[[顾客]], [[旅客]]) → someone touring a place: 景区里游客很多.

## Notes
游客 = a tourist / sightseer; [[旅客]] = a traveller / passenger (on a train,
in a hotel); [[客人]] = a guest in one's home. In online games, 游客 = "guest
account."

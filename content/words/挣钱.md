## Why this combination
挣 "to earn (through effort/struggle)" acting on the object 钱 "money"
gives exactly "to earn money" — splittable, as in 挣了不少钱 "earned quite
a bit of money."

## Notes
挣钱 is the everyday word for earning income through work: 出去挣钱 "to
go out and earn a living" — more colloquial and effort-focused than the
formal 赚钱, though the two are largely interchangeable in casual speech.

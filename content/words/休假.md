## Why this combination
休 "to rest, to stop work" (see [[休息]]) + 假 (*jià*) "leave, holiday" (see
[[放假]], [[请假]], [[假期]]) as its object — to take one's leave. Verb-object:
休了三天假, 带薪休假 "paid leave."

## Notes
休假 = take (authorised) leave, employee's side; [[放假]] = the institution
granting a break; [[请假]] = ask for leave off. 假 is *jià* here.

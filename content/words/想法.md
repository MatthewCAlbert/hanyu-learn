## Why this combination
想 "to think" + 法 "way, method" (see [[办法]], [[方法]]) → one's way of thinking
about something, hence a notion or opinion: 说说你的想法, 我有个想法.

## Notes
想法 = a personal idea / take; [[意见]] = opinion (often for a decision, or a
gripe); [[看法]] = a considered view. Colloquial 想法子 "think of a way" uses 法
literally.

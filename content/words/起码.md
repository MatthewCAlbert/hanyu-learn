## Why this combination
起 "to start, to rise" and 码 "a numeral, a marker [as on a price tag or
gauge]" combine into "the starting number" — the baseline figure below
which nothing less is acceptable, i.e. the bare minimum.

## Notes
起码 modifies both quantities and standards: 起码要三天 "it'll take at
least three days," 起码的礼貌 "basic courtesy" — always marking a floor,
not an ideal.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 想 ("to think," as in 想要) and 念 (here in its
"to think of with longing, to hold in mind" sense, as in 怀念 — distinct
from 念's more common "to read aloud/study" sense in 念书) both
independently name thinking of someone. Pairing them gives the standard
word for missing someone.

Rated "semi" for 念's "to long for" sense here, a step removed from its
more familiar "to read/recite" meaning.

## Notes
想念 always takes a person (or, less often, a place) as its object —
想念家乡, "to miss one's hometown"; 想念朋友, "to miss a friend" — it does
not apply to missing an ordinary object.

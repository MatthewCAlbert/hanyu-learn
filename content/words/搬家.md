## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 搬 "to move, to carry, to relocate" (as in 搬
运 "to transport") takes 家 "home" as its object — literally "to move
[one's] home" — to move house.

## Notes
搬家 is intransitive and splits for aspect like other verb-object
compounds (搬过三次家 "moved house three times") — 搬, alone, is the
general verb "to move/carry [an object]," used more broadly than the
specific "to relocate one's home" sense of 搬家.

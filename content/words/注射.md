## Why this combination
注 "to pour into, to concentrate on" and 射 "to shoot" (see 射's own
etymology — a bow-and-arrow picture) combine metaphorically: liquid
"shot" into the body under pressure, the way an arrow is shot toward a
target.

## Notes
注射 is the clinical/formal term (注射器 "syringe," 注射疫苗 "to administer
a vaccine injection"); everyday speech about getting a shot more often
uses 打针 (which itself uses 针 "needle").

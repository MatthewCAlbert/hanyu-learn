## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to issue" (see also 发出, 发展) combined with 送 "to
send off, to deliver, to see off" (as in 送礼 "to give a gift"): to
issue and deliver — to transmit, to send.

## Notes
发送 is the formal word for sending information or documents,
especially electronically (发送邮件 "to send an email," 发送信号 "to
transmit a signal") — 寄, the everyday verb, is used for sending
physical mail instead.

## Why this combination

毫 ("a tiny fraction, a thousandth part," a unit character used for
very small measures) plus 米 ("a meter," the base unit borrowed and
adapted from the metric system) names a very small fraction of a
meter: a millimeter.

Rated "semi" for 毫's specialized fractional-unit sense, distinct from
its broader "extremely small/slightest" meaning in other contexts.

## Notes

毫米 (millimeter) and 厘米 (covered elsewhere in this app, centimeter)
both build on the same 米 "meter" root, with different fractional
prefixes marking the scale.

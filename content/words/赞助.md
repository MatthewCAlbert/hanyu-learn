## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 赞 ("to praise, to assist," as in 赞成, "to
approve of") and 助 ("to help," as in 帮助) both independently name
lending support. Pairing them gives the standard word for financial
sponsorship.

## Notes

赞助商 ("a sponsor," lit. "sponsoring merchant") is the standard noun
form built on this verb — 赞助 specifically implies financial or
material backing, unlike the more general 支持 ("to support," which can
be purely moral/verbal).

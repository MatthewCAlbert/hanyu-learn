## Why this combination
舍 "to give up, to part with" plus the potential complement 得 ("can
manage to," from 得 "to manage to") gives "can bring oneself to give
up" — the affirmative counterpart of 舍不得 "reluctant to part with,"
built on the same potential-complement grammar.

## Notes
舍得 anchors 舍得花钱 "willing to spend money," 舍得投入 "willing to
invest" — always implies overcoming reluctance to give something up for
a worthwhile purpose.

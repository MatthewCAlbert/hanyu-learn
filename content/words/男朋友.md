## Why this combination
男 "male" + 朋友 "friend," with the romantic sense fixed by convention (like
[[女朋友]]). A male friend who is *not* a partner is 男性朋友 or 男的朋友.

## Notes
找男朋友 "look for a boyfriend," 前男友 "ex."

## Why this combination
Both characters already mean "clear" in their own way — 明 bright, 白 white and
so plain or blank. Stacking two near-synonyms to make a stronger, more stable
word is one of the most productive patterns in Chinese, and it is why so many
two-character words look redundant when read literally.

The metaphor is the same one English uses: something becomes *clear* to you,
therefore you understand it.

## Notes
Same doubling pattern in 帮助 (help + assist) and 快乐 (fast + happy). When a
compound looks like it says the same thing twice, this is usually why.

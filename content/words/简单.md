## Why this combination
简 "brief" (recall its etymology: a single bamboo writing-slip, holding
only a little text) carries the core meaning. 单 here draws on its
extended sense "single, uncomplicated" (as in 单纯 "simple, pure")
rather than its more basic "list, bill" — something reduced to one
simple element, without complication.

## Notes
简单 is the general, everyday "simple"; 容易 (elsewhere in this dataset)
means "easy to do," a related but distinct idea — a task can be simple
in structure yet still not easy to execute.

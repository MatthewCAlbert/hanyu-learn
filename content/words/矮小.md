## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 矮 ("short, low," used specifically of height)
and 小 ("small") both independently name diminutive size. Pairing them
gives the standard word for a small physical stature.

## Notes

矮小的身材 ("a short, small build") describes physical size specifically
— 矮 alone contrasts directly with 高 ("tall"), the standard antonym
pair for describing a person's height.

## Why this combination
打 as the all-purpose light verb "do, get" (see [[打电话]], [[打开]]) + 车
"vehicle" — specifically, hire one. Splits: 打个车 "grab a cab," 打不到车
"can't get a taxi."

## Notes
打的 (*dǎ dī*) is the older Cantonese-derived synonym (的士 "taxi"). Ride-hailing
is 叫车 / 打车 on an app.

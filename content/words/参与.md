## Why this combination
参 here carries its "to take part in" sense (as in 参加, 参观), and 与 takes
its yù reading — "to take part in, to be involved in" — distinct from its
more familiar yǔ reading ("and, together with," covered in this app's
hanzi entry for 与). Two words for "taking part" reinforce each other into
a slightly more formal synonym of 参加.

Rated "semi" specifically because of that tone/reading distinction on 与:
get the reading wrong and the character looks like it should mean "and,"
not "to join."

## Notes
参与 leans more formal/abstract than 参加 — 参与讨论 ("to participate in a
discussion") reads naturally, while 参加婚礼 ("to attend a wedding") keeps
参加, which covers concrete events more comfortably.

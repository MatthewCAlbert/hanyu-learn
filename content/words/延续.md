## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 延 ("to extend, to delay," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 延长, "to lengthen") and 续 ("to continue," as in 继续)
both independently name stretching something onward. Pairing them gives
the standard word for something persisting over time.

## Notes

延续传统 ("to carry on a tradition") and 延续至今 ("to continue up to the
present") both build on this word — 延续 emphasizes an unbroken thread
across time, distinct from 继续, the everyday verb for "to continue [an
action]."

## Why this combination
展 "to unfold, to display, to extend" (as in 发展) combined with 开 "to
open" (see also 开放, 开发): to unfold open — to spread out, and,
figuratively, to launch an activity.

## Notes
展开 covers literal unfolding (展开地图 "to spread out a map") and
launching an organised activity (展开调查 "to carry out an
investigation," 展开讨论 "to open up a discussion") — the figurative
sense treats an activity as something "unfolded" from a compact start.

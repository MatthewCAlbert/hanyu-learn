## Why this combination
见 "to see / meet" + resultative 到 "reach" — the seeing succeeded. Emphasises
that a hoped-for or hard-to-arrange sighting came off: 终于见到他了 "finally got
to see him."

## Notes
见到 vs. 看到: 见到 leans toward encountering a person; 看到 toward catching
sight of anything. Bare 见 also works: 很高兴见到你.

## Why this combination
房 "house, room" (as in 房子, 房间) combined with 租 "rent, to lease"
(as in 出租 "to rent out"): the rent for a room/house — housing rent.

## Notes
房租 names the rent paid for housing specifically (交房租 "to pay
rent," 房租贵 "expensive rent") — 租, alone, is the more general verb
"to rent, to lease [anything]," while 房租 is the noun for this
particular, very common payment.

## Why this combination

汽 ("steam, vapor, gas," as in 汽车, "automobile [lit. steam/gas-powered
vehicle]") plus 油 ("oil," as in 石油, "petroleum") names the refined
volatile fuel oil used in engines: gasoline.

## Notes

汽油 (gasoline) contrasts with 柴油 ("diesel, lit. firewood oil") as the
two common vehicle fuels — 加油 ("to refuel," lit. "to add oil") applies
to both.

## Why this combination
中 "centre" + 间 "the space between." Both the middle of one thing (房间中间)
and the gap between two (两座山中间). 间 is the same "interval" morpheme as in
[[时间]], [[房间]].

## Notes
Lives with the direction set (旁边, 左边…) but names a position between
reference points rather than a compass side.

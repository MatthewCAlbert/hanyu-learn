## Why this combination
电视 "television" (see [[电视]]) + 机 "machine, set" — the physical appliance,
as against 电视 the medium ("watch TV") or 电视台 the station. The same 机 that
turns 收音 into 收音机 (radio set), 照相 into 照相机 (camera).

## Notes
In speech people usually just say 电视 for the box too; 电视机 disambiguates
when needed (买了台电视机).

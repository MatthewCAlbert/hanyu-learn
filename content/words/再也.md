## Why this combination
再 "again" and 也 "also, even" combine into an emphatic adverb that,
paired with a negative (不/没), rules out any further recurrence — the
grammaticalized combination carries more force than 再 alone.

## Notes
再也 is always followed by a negative: 再也不去了 "never going again,"
再也没见过 "never saw (them) again" — a fixed emphatic pattern, not used
in positive statements.

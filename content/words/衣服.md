## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 衣 "clothing" (classically the upper garment) + 服 "dress,
garment" (as a verb, "to wear"). Doubled for the everyday mass noun "clothes."

## Notes
服 toneless here. 衣 alone in 大衣 (overcoat), 毛衣 (sweater); 服 alone in 制服
(uniform), 衣服 the generic word.

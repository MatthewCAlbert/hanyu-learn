## Why this combination
专 "exclusive, sole" and 利 "benefit, right" combine into exactly "an
exclusive right" — the standard legal/commercial word for a patent.

## Notes
专利 anchors 申请专利 "to apply for a patent," 专利权 "patent right" —
also used figuratively for something one group alone has: 年轻人的专利?
"is that only for young people?" (rhetorically challenging exclusivity).

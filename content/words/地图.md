## Why this combination
地 "land, ground" + 图 "diagram, picture, chart" → a diagram of the land. 图 is
the same "chart" morpheme in 图书 (books — "pictures and writings"),
图表 (charts), 地图.

## Notes
看地图 "read a map." 图 also = "to seek / scheme" (图利 "seek profit").

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 丈 carries an old honorific "gentleman, grown
man" sense (distinct from its everyday role as a unit of length, 一丈),
and 夫 is "man, husband" (also seen in 夫妻, husband and wife). Two words
for "adult man" narrow together into the specific relational sense
"husband."

Rated "semi" for 丈's honorific sense, which most learners meet only
through this word and the measure-word usage, not as an independent
"gentleman" morpheme elsewhere.

## Notes
大丈夫 ("a real man," an idiom of manly resolve) keeps 丈's honorific sense
alive outside this word — worth learning alongside 丈夫 to see that sense
is not a one-off.

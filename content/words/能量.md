## Why this combination
能 "ability, capacity" and 量 "quantity, measure" combine directly: a
measurable quantity of capacity to do work — the modern physics term for
"energy," extended figuratively to a person's inner drive or influence.

## Notes
能量 spans physics (能量守恒 "conservation of energy") and everyday
figurative use (正能量 "positive energy/vibes," 释放能量 "to unleash
one's potential") — a term borrowed from science into everyday
self-help language.

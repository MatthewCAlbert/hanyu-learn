## Why this combination
肚 ("belly," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 子 (the generic nominal
suffix, as in 桌子, 椅子) gives the everyday two-syllable noun for the
belly.

## Notes
肚子疼 ("stomachache") and 肚子饿 ("hungry," lit. "the belly is hungry")
are two of the most common everyday phrases built on this word.

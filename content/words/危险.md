## Why this combination
危 "perilous, precarious, in peril" and 险 "steep, treacherous, risky"
(as in 冒险 "to take a risk") are close synonyms, paired into the general
word for danger — both the adjective and the noun.

## Notes
危险 works unchanged as adjective ("这条路很危险" "this road is
dangerous") and noun ("有生命危险" "there is danger to life") — 危 alone
survives in compounds like 危机 "crisis" and 危害 "to endanger, harm."

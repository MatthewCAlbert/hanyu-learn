## Why this combination
菜 "dish, cooked food, vegetable" (see [[做饭]]'s cousin 做菜, [[点菜]]) + 单
"a list, a sheet" (see [[名单]], [[账单]]) → a list of dishes. Reused in
computing for the on-screen menu.

## Notes
单 as "-list / form" also in [[名单]], 账单 "bill," 订单 "order." 看菜单,
点菜 "order dishes."

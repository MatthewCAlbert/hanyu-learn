## Why this combination
方 "a way, a means, a formula" (the same 方 as 方向, 药方) + 法 "method, rule."
A near-synonym pair: a systematic way of doing something — 学习方法, 科学方法.

## Notes
方法 = a technique or approach for a task; [[办法]] = a practical fix for a
problem. 法 alone: 用法, 说法, 语法.

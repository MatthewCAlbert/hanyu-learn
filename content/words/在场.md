## Why this combination
在 "to be located at" acting on the object 场 "a place, scene, venue"
gives "to be at the scene" — physically present where something is
happening.

## Notes
在场 emphasizes eyewitness presence: 在场证人 "an eyewitness present at
the scene," 当时我不在场 "I wasn't there at the time" — often used in
contexts where whether someone witnessed an event matters.

## Why this combination
难 "difficult" (as in 困难) combined with 度 "a measure, a degree" (see
also 程度, 态度): the degree to which something is difficult —
difficulty level.

## Notes
难度 measures how hard a task or challenge is (难度很大 "very
difficult," 降低难度 "to reduce the difficulty") — 困难, the noun for
"difficulty, hardship," names the obstacle itself, while 难度 names its
measured intensity.

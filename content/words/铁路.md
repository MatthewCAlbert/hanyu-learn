## Why this combination
铁 "iron" (as in 地铁 "subway," literally "underground iron [track]")
combined with 路 "road, way" (see also 路线, 马路): a road made of iron —
a railway.

## Notes
铁路 names the railway system and its infrastructure (铁路公司 "railway
company," 高铁 in a related compound "high-speed rail") — 火车, the word
for "train," names the vehicle itself rather than the track and system
it runs on.

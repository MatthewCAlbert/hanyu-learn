## Why this combination
年 "year" and 度 "a measured period, an interval" combine directly into
"a defined yearly period" — as in fiscal or academic years that don't
necessarily match the calendar year.

## Notes
年度 anchors 年度报告 "annual report," 财政年度 "fiscal year," 年度总结
"year-end summary" — always a formally bounded twelve-month cycle, not
just "a year" casually.

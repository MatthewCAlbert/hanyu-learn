## Why this combination
今 "now, the present" (see [[今天]], [[今年]]) + 后 "after" → "after now,"
from this point onward: 今后要多注意.

## Notes
今后 (from now on, forward-looking) ≈ 以后 in the "in future" sense, but 以后
also frames "after (some event)." 从今以后 is the emphatic version.

## Why this combination
入 "to enter" acting on the object 门 "door" gives exactly "to enter the
door" — splittable, as in 入了门. The figurative sense — grasping the
basics of a subject — treats mastering fundamentals as "getting through
the door" into a field.

## Notes
入门 anchors 入门书 "an introductory/beginner's book," 编程入门 "an intro
to programming" — the figurative "getting started with a subject" sense
is now at least as common as the literal "entering a doorway."

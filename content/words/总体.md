## Why this combination
总 "overall, general" plus 体 "body, whole" gives exactly "the whole,
taken overall" — the standard word for something considered in its
entirety rather than part by part.

## Notes
总体 anchors 总体来看 "overall, looking at it," 总体规划 "master plan" —
always frames a big-picture view, distinct from 整体, a near-synonym
emphasizing the unity/integrity of the whole rather than the aggregate
view.

## Why this combination
安 "peaceful, settled, at ease" + 全 "whole, intact, unharmed" → nothing
disturbed, nothing damaged: safe. Both adjective (很安全) and noun
(注意安全, 国家安全).

## Notes
安 = "at ease" also in 安静, [[平安]], 安心. 安全 pairs with 危险 "danger."

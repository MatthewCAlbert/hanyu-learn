## Why this combination
后 "back / after" + 天 "day" → the day one step past 明天, mirroring [[前天]].
A separate lexicalised sense, "after birth," gives 后天 = "acquired, nurtured"
as opposed to 先天 "innate" — 后天努力 "effort acquired through life."

## Notes
The time-word row: [[前天]] · 昨天 · 今天 · 明天 · 后天. The "acquired" sense is
common in discussions of talent and health.

## Why this combination
举 "to raise, to lift" and 动 "to move" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for an observable action.

## Notes
举动 describes a single noticeable act, often as seen by an observer:
奇怪的举动 "strange behaviour," 一举一动 "every single move [someone
makes]" — always about visible conduct, not internal thought.

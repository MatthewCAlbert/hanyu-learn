## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 神 ("spirit, divine") and 秘 ("secret, hidden,"
covered elsewhere in this app — the older form shares the same 示
"sacred" semantic root as 神) both independently point to something
beyond ordinary understanding. Pairing them gives the standard word for
"mysterious."

## Notes
神秘 describes anything hard to explain, from a person's manner to an
unsolved event (神秘失踪, "a mysterious disappearance") — a broadly
applicable word for "shrouded in mystery."

## Why this combination
喜 "joy" and 剧 "drama" (see 剧's role in 悲剧) calque the Western dramatic
genre "comedy" — a play built around joyful, happy outcomes — the direct
counterpart to 悲剧 "tragedy," both translating the classical Western
dramatic-genre pairing.

## Notes
喜剧 covers stage comedy and, in modern use, comedic film and stand-up
(喜剧演员 "comedian," 喜剧片 "comedy film") — paired with 悲剧 as the two
classical dramatic modes.

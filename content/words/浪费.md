## Why this combination
浪, here in its "unrestrained, extravagant" sense (as in 浪漫 "romantic,"
a phonetic loan from "romantic" that reused this character) combined
with 费 "to spend, an expense" (as in 花费 "to spend"): to spend without
restraint — to waste.

## Notes
浪费 takes time, money or resources as its object (浪费时间 "to waste
time," 浪费钱 "to waste money") and works as a noun too (这是浪费 "this is
a waste") — its opposite is 节约 "to economise, to save."

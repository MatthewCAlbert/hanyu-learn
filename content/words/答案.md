## Why this combination
答 ("to answer, to reply," as in 回答) plus 案 ("a case, a file, a written
record," as in 档案, 方案) names the recorded solution to a posed question
— the written answer on file, as opposed to the spoken act of answering
itself.

Rated "semi" for 案's "case/record" sense, distinct from and less
immediately obvious than 答's plain "to answer."

## Notes
答案 is specifically the content of a correct or proposed solution (标准
答案, "the standard/model answer") — for the act of replying itself, 回答
is the verb to reach for.

## Why this combination
面 "face, surface" (see also 表面, 全面) combined with 积 "to accumulate,
[measured] area" (as in 积极 in an unrelated sense): the accumulated
extent of a surface — area.

## Notes
面积 measures the size of a flat surface (房子的面积 "the area of the
house," 面积很大 "a large area") — it names two-dimensional extent
specifically, distinct from 大小 "size" in general, which can refer to
any dimension.

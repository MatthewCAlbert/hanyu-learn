## Why this combination
空 "empty, void, the open sky" + 气 "gas, breath, vapour" (see [[天气]]) → the
"empty" stuff that fills space — air. Figuratively the "atmosphere" of a
gathering: 空气很紧张 "the mood was tense."

## Notes
空 is *kōng* here (empty), not *kòng* "free time / a gap." 新鲜空气 "fresh
air," 空气污染 "air pollution."

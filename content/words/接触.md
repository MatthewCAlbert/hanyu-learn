## Why this combination
接 "to receive, to connect" and 触 "to touch" reinforce each other into
the general verb for contact — physical, electrical, or social.

## Notes
接触 spans literal touch (皮肤接触 "skin contact"), electrical contact
(接触不良 "a bad connection"), and social/professional contact (接触新
同事 "to get to know new colleagues") — one word across three domains.

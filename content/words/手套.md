## Why this combination

手 ("hand") plus 套 ("a cover, a case that slips over something," as in
套餐, "a set meal [lit. set + meal, things bundled together]") names a
covering that slips over the hand: a glove.

## Notes

戴手套 ("to wear/put on gloves") pairs 手套 with 戴, the verb used for
accessories worn on the extremities (also 戴帽子, "to wear a hat"; 戴眼镜,
"to wear glasses") — distinct from 穿, used for garments worn on the
body.

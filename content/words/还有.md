## Why this combination
Literally "there is still" — 还 "still, yet" over 有 "there is." Used to add an
item to a list or a clause to an argument: 我买了茶，还有咖啡.

## Notes
还 alone as "also/still" (他还小) and 有 alone as "there is" both survive; 还有
is the fixed connective built from them.

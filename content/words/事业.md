## Why this combination
事 "affair, matter" combined with 业 "a trade, a field of endeavour" (as
in 职业 "occupation," 专业 "major"): an undertaking pursued as one's
larger endeavour in life — grander in scope than a mere job.

## Notes
事业 names a lasting, meaningful pursuit — one's career in the sense of
a life's work (事业成功 "professional success," 教育事业 "the cause of
education") — rather than a specific job, which is named by 工作 or 职业
instead.

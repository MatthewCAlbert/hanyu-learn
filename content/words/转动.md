## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 转 ("to turn," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 转身) and 动 ("to move," as in 运动) both independently name
motion. Pairing them gives the standard word for rotational movement.

## Notes

齿轮转动 ("the gears turn/rotate") and 转动方向盘 ("to turn the steering
wheel") both build on this word — 转动 names ongoing rotational motion,
distinct from 转身, a single act of turning one's body.

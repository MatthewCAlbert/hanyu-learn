## Why this combination
约鲁巴 is a phonetic rendering of Yoruba, plus 语. 约 is not "appointment"; 鲁 is
not the old state of Lu.

## Notes
A major language of [[尼日利亚]]. Contrast [[约旦]], another 约- transcription.

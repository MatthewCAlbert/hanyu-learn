## Why this combination
咖啡 *kāfēi* transliterates English "coffee" (itself from Arabic qahwa
via Ottoman Turkish and Dutch/French) purely by sound — both characters
carry the 口 "mouth" radical, marking them as sound-only syllables coined
for transliteration rather than words chosen for their meaning.

## Notes
咖啡 is a pure phonetic loanword with no native semantic history to draw
on: 咖 and 啡 essentially exist only inside this word (and its
derivatives, e.g. 咖啡厅 "coffee shop"), a strong signal of a borrowed
term.

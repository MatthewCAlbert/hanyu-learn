## Why this combination
家 "home, family" + locative 里 "inside" → the space of the home, and by
extension the people in it: 家里有事 "something's come up at home / in the
family," 家里人 "family members."

## Notes
在家 vs. 在家里 overlap; 家里 leans a touch more concrete-spatial. 里 is a bare
locative suffix here, as in 屋里, 车里.

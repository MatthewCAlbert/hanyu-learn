## Why this combination
A potential-complement phrase: 对得起 "able to face (someone) squarely" — to
have done right by them — negated to 对不起 "unable to face you," i.e. I have
wronged you. The apology sense froze into a fixed expression.

## Notes
The literal machinery still works: 对得起良心 "have a clear conscience,"
对不起父母 "have let one's parents down." As a bare apology it is just "sorry."

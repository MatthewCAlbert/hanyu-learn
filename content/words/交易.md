## Why this combination
交 "to hand over, to deliver" and 易 "to exchange, to trade" combine
directly: handing goods or money over in an exchange — a transaction.

## Notes
交易 is the general commercial word for any exchange of value (股票交易
"stock trading," 公平交易 "fair trade") — more formal and business-
oriented than everyday 买卖 "buying and selling."

## Why this combination
初 "beginning" (as in 初中, 年初) combined with 一 "one" names the first
in a sequence. It compresses two distinct everyday senses: 初一 the first
grade of junior middle school (short for 初中一年级), and, separately, the
first day of a lunar month, especially 大年初一 "Lunar New Year's Day."

## Notes
Context disambiguates: 我上初一 "I'm in first-year middle school" versus
大年初一去拜年 "visiting relatives on the first day of the new year" — the
same 初 + number pattern extends to 初二, 初三 for later grades or days.

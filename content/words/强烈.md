## Why this combination
强 "strong, powerful" (see also 强大) combined with 烈 "intense, fierce,
ardent" (as in 热烈 "warm, enthusiastic"): strong to the point of
intensity — strong, intense.

## Notes
强烈 describes intensity of feeling, reaction or sensory experience
(强烈反对 "to strongly oppose," 强烈的阳光 "intense sunlight") — plain 强
"strong" is used more for physical or comparative strength, while 强烈
stresses a sharp, forceful degree.

## Why this combination
墙 "wall" and 壁 "wall, partition" (as in 壁画 "mural" and 隔壁 "next
door") are near-synonyms combined into a slightly more formal word for a
wall.

## Notes
墙壁 anchors 墙壁上挂着画 "a painting hangs on the wall" — largely
interchangeable with plain 墙, though 墙壁 leans toward written/descriptive
register.

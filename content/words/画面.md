## Why this combination
画 "picture, painting" and 面 "surface, face" combine directly: the
visible surface a picture presents — a single frame or scene as seen.

## Notes
画面 covers film/video (画面质量 "image/picture quality"), art (画面构图
"composition of a painting"), and vivid description (脑海中的画面 "the
scene in one's mind's eye") — always a static, viewable visual scene.

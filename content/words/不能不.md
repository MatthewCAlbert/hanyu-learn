## Why this combination
不能 "cannot" negated again by a following 不 gives a double negative —
"cannot not (do something)" — which logically flips back to a strong
obligation, the same double-negative-as-emphasis pattern as 不得不.

## Notes
不能不 always precedes a verb: 不能不承认 "cannot but admit," 不能不佩服
"can't help but admire" — near-synonymous with 不得不, both expressing
reluctant necessity.

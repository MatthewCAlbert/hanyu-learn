## Why this combination
Two characters that each independently gesture at totality — 世 (an age,
a generation, extended to "the age we live in") and 界 (a bounded
realm) — combine into "the entire bounded realm of our age": the world.
The compound is Buddhist in origin, translating Sanskrit *loka-dhātu*
("world-realm," itself time-plus-space), which is why 世 (time) and 界
(space) pair up rather than a purely spatial word alone.

## Notes
界 alone keeps the narrower "boundary, realm" sense in compounds like
边界 "border" and 商界 "the business world" — 世界 is the one case where
it expands all the way out to everything there is.

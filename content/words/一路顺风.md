## Why this combination
一路 "all the way, the whole journey" + 顺风 "a following wind" (顺 with the
flow + 风 wind; see [[顺利]]) — a wind at your back the whole way, from the age
of sailing junks. A parting wish for a smooth journey.

## Notes
一路顺风 · 一路平安 (see [[一路平安]]) · 一路顺利 are interchangeable farewells.
Some avoid 顺风 for air travellers (a tailwind buffets a plane) and say
一路平安 instead.

## Why this combination
严 is "strict, severe" (as in 严格, 严肃), and 重 here takes its "heavy,
weighty" reading zhòng (not the "repeat" reading chóng) — a "severely heavy"
matter is a serious one. The tone/reading distinction on 重 is exactly the
kind of thing that makes this "semi" rather than fully transparent.

## Notes
严重 describes problems, illnesses, consequences, and mistakes (严重的问题,
病情严重) — it describes how bad a situation is, and is not used to describe
a person's strict character (that stays with 严格 or 严厉).

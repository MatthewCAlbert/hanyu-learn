## Why this combination
人 "person" and 员 "a counted member [of a group]" combine directly:
people counted as belonging to some organised group — staff, personnel.

## Notes
人员 is always a bound, formal collective noun — it names a category of
people (工作人员 "staff," 医护人员 "medical personnel"), never a single
individual the way 人 alone can.

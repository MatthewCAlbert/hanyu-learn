## Why this combination
天 "sky, heaven" + locative 上 "on, above" → up in the sky: 天上有星星. Also the
realm of gods and immortals (天上人间 "heaven and the mortal world").

## Notes
天上 "in the sky" vs. [[地上]] "on the ground." 上 as a bare locative suffix,
like [[路上]], [[车上]], [[网上]].

## Why this combination
启 (here "to disclose, to state openly") plus 事 "a matter" gives exactly
"to disclose a matter (to the public)" — the standard word for a posted
public announcement.

## Notes
启事 anchors 寻人启事 "missing person notice," 招聘启事 "job posting" —
always a formal notice posted for public attention, distinct from a
private 通知 "notification" sent to specific people.

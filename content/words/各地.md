## Why this combination
各 "each, every [one individually]" (see also 各种) combined with 地
"land, place" (see also 当地, 地区): each and every place — various
regions.

## Notes
各地 is used for describing something happening broadly across a
country or area (来自各地 "coming from all over," 各地的风俗 "customs of
various regions") — it is always used generally, never with a specific
place named alongside it.

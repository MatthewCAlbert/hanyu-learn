## Why this combination
摄 "to capture (an image)" acting on the object 像 "image" gives exactly
"to capture images" — splittable, as in 摄了一段像, and the base of
摄像机 "video camera."

## Notes
摄像 anchors 摄像头 "webcam, security camera (lens)," 摄像师 "camera
operator, videographer" — the moving-image counterpart to 照相 "to take a
photo."

## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Finland (芬蘭). 芬 and 兰 are chosen for
sound, not for "fragrance + orchid."

## Notes
兰 as in [[荷兰]], [[波兰]]. Same stem in [[芬兰语]].

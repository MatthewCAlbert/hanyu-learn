## Why this combination
幽默 transliterates English "humour," coined by the writer Lin Yutang
in the 1920s. 幽 "quiet, secluded" (see 幽's own etymology) and 默
"silent" were picked for their sound, though the pairing happens to
suggest a quiet, understated wit — a happy resonance rather than the
actual reason for the choice.

## Notes
幽默 covers both the quality (幽默感 "sense of humour") and describing a
person or remark as funny (他很幽默 "he's very funny") — now completely
naturalized, with no sense of being a foreign borrowing in everyday use.

## Why this combination
身 ("body") plus 材 ("material, the substance/build something is made
of," covered in this app's entry for 材料) names a person's physical
build — the "material" their body is shaped from.

Rated "semi" for 材's figurative extension from "raw material" to a
person's physical proportions.

## Notes
身材 describes a person's build and proportions (身材好, "to have a good
figure") — distinct from 个子 ("height" specifically) and 体重 ("weight"
specifically), each naming one dimension where 身材 covers the overall
impression.

## Why this combination
拒 "to block, to refuse" and 绝 "to cut off, absolute" reinforce each
other: blocking something and cutting it off completely — a firm,
unambiguous refusal.

## Notes
拒绝 is the standard formal verb for refusing a request, offer, or demand
(拒绝邀请 "to decline an invitation") — firmer in tone than the softer
不同意 "to disagree" or 不愿意 "unwilling."

## Why this combination
消 "to fade, to dissolve, to dissipate" plus 极 "extreme, pole" forms a
scientific-register antonym pair with 积极 "positive, active" (积 "to
accumulate" + 极): where 积极 is energy building toward a pole, 消极 is
energy dissipating away from it — the physics-flavored logic behind the
pair is not obvious without that context, hence marked semi-transparent.

## Notes
消极 modifies attitude and behavior: 消极情绪 "negative emotions," 消极
应对 "a passive response" — always the direct opposite of 积极, and the
two are almost always taught and remembered as a pair.

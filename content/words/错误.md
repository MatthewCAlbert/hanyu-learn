## Why this combination
错 "wrong, mistaken" and 误 "to err, a mistake, to mislead" (as in 误会
"misunderstanding") are close synonyms, paired into the standard formal
word for error — both noun and adjective.

## Notes
错误 works as a countable noun ("犯了一个错误" "made a mistake") and as an
adjective ("错误的做法" "a mistaken approach"); bare 错 is the lighter,
more colloquial word for "wrong" in casual speech ("你错了" "you're
wrong").

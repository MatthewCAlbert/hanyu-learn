## Why this combination
路 "road" (see [[马路]], [[道路]], [[路口]], [[路上]]) + 边 "side, edge" (the
locative suffix of [[旁边]], [[身边]], [[海边]]) → the edge of the road:
路边停车, 路边摊 "a roadside stall."

## Notes
边 "side / edge" as a bare locative: 路边, [[海边]], [[身边]]. Compare
[[路上]] "on the road / on the way" — 上 for the surface, 边 for the margin.

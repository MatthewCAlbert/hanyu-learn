## Why this combination
对 "facing, directed at" (see also 对方, 对象) combined with 待 "to
treat, to deal with, to wait for" (see also 等待): the manner directed
at someone/something in dealing with them — to treat.

## Notes
对待 takes a manner adverb or complement showing how something is
treated (公平对待 "to treat fairly," 认真对待 "to take seriously") — it
describes an attitude or approach taken towards a person, issue or task,
not a mere action performed on an object.

## Why this combination
辞 "to resign, to decline" (see 辞's own etymology) acting on the object
职 "post, position" gives exactly "to resign from one's post" —
splittable, as in 辞了职 "resigned."

## Notes
辞职 is the standard verb for quitting a job (提交辞职信 "to submit a
resignation letter") — distinct from 离职, which more neutrally covers
leaving a position for any reason, resignation included.

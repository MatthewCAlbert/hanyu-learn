## Why this combination
传 "to transmit" and 真 (here "a true copy, an authentic likeness")
combine into "to transmit a true copy" — the standard word for a fax,
which reproduces a document's exact appearance at a distance.

## Notes
传真 works as noun ("发一份传真" "to send a fax") and verb ("传真给我"
"fax it to me") — largely obsolete in casual use today but still standard
in business/legal contexts requiring a document image.

## Why this combination
总 carries its "overall, taken as a whole" sense (as in 总统, 总共, both
covered elsewhere in this app), and 之 is the literary linking particle
covered in this app's hanzi entry — the same connector behind 之前,
之后, 之间. "Taken as a whole, [it is this]" is exactly the summarizing
job "in short" does.

Rated "semi" because 之's grammatical role here needs to already be
known from words like 之前 to parse the compound cleanly.

## Notes
总之 opens a summarizing statement after a list of points or a long
discussion (总之，我们需要更多时间, "in short, we need more time") — always
a wrap-up move, never a mid-discussion connector.

## Why this combination

看 ("to look, to regard") plus the potential-complement negative 不起
("cannot rise to," 起 as in 了不起, covered elsewhere in this app) fuses
into a set phrase: unable to look up to someone — to look down on them.

Rated "semi": the potential-complement grammar (verb + 不 + result,
"unable to look-and-rise-to") is transparent to anyone who knows that
pattern, but not obvious without it.

## Notes

看不起人 ("to look down on people") is the standard phrase — 看得起
("to think highly of, to hold in regard") is its positive counterpart,
built the same way with 得 replacing 不 in the same potential-complement
slot.

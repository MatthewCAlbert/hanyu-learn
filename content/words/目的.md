## Why this combination
目 "eye; what the eye is on, an aim" + 的 (*dì*) "a target, a bullseye" (its
original noun sense, as in 有的放矢 "shoot with a target in mind"). Where you are
aiming — the point of an action: 学习的目的, 达到目的.

## Notes
的 is *dì* here, a rare survival of its old "target" meaning; everywhere else it
is the toneless particle *de*. 目 "aim" also in [[目标]] (not this level).

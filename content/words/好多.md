## Why this combination
好 here as an adverb "quite, rather" (see [[好像]], [[好几]]) + 多 "many" → "quite
a lot." Colloquial: 好多人, 好多年没见了.

## Notes
好 + adjective = "quite …": [[好久]] (quite a while), 好大 (pretty big). In some
dialects 好多 also asks "how many?" (= 多少).

## Why this combination
提 "to lift, to raise" with the directional complement 起 "up" gives the
literal "to lift up," which extends figuratively to "raising" a topic in
conversation — bringing it up for discussion.

## Notes
提起 spans the literal (提起箱子 "to lift up a suitcase") and the
figurative (提起这件事 "to bring up this matter," 提起精神 "to buck up,
raise one's spirits").

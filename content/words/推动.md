## Why this combination
推 "to push" (see also 推进) combined with 动 "to move" (see also 自动,
运动): to push into motion — to promote, to drive forward.

## Notes
推动 takes an abstract process as its object (推动发展 "to promote
development," 推动改革 "to give impetus to reform") — 推进, its close
relative, is used the same way and largely overlaps, though 推动 stresses
initiating motion while 推进 stresses continuing it forward.

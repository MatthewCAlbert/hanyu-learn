## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of España. 西, 班 and 牙 are chosen for
sound, not for "west + class + tooth." The same pattern as [[意大利]]: a full
transcription, no clipped 国.

## Notes
The same stem in 西班牙语 (an HSK word). 西 here is not the compass point.

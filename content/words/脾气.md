## Why this combination
脾 "spleen" (see 脾's own etymology and notes — old Five-Phases medical
theory held the spleen governed mood and disposition) and 气 "energy, qi"
combine directly: the vital energy associated with the spleen, believed
to shape one's temperament.

## Notes
脾气 covers general disposition (好脾气 "good-natured") and specifically
a quick temper (发脾气 "to lose one's temper," 脾气不好 "short-tempered") —
context decides whether it's neutral character or a flare of anger.

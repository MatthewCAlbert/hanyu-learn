## Why this combination
出 "to go out" + 门 "door, gate" as its object — stepping out of the door.
Both a short errand (我出门一下) and leaving on a journey (出门在外 "away from
home"). Verb-object, so it splits: 出了门才想起.

## Notes
出门 (leave the house) vs. [[出发]] (set off on a journey) vs. [[出国]] (leave
the country). Of a woman, 出门 archaically also meant "to marry out."

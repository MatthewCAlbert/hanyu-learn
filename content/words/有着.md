## Why this combination
有 "to have" takes the continuous aspect marker 着, emphasising an
ongoing state of possession rather than a one-off fact — a more formal,
written-register alternative to plain 有.

## Notes
有着 favours abstract, ongoing qualities in formal writing (有着深厚的
友谊 "sharing a deep friendship") over concrete, countable objects, where
plain 有 (有一本书 "have a book") is standard.

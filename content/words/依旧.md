## Why this combination
依 "according to, in line with" and 旧 "old, former" combine into
"following the old [state]" — unchanged from before, i.e. "still."

## Notes
依旧 is a formal/literary equivalent of everyday 还是/仍然: 风景依旧 "the
scenery remains unchanged," 情况依旧 "the situation is as before."

## Why this combination

口 ("mouth, an opening") plus 袋 ("a bag, a sack") names a bag defined by
its opening — extended to a garment's pocket, a small sewn-in bag.

## Notes

口袋 covers both a literal sack (一口袋米, "a sack of rice") and a
garment pocket (裤子口袋, "trouser pocket") — context, not a different
word, distinguishes them.

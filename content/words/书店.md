## Why this combination
书 "book" + 店 "shop" — one of the 店 family with [[商店]], 饭店, 花店. A shop
that sells books.

## Notes
新华书店 (Xinhua Bookstore), the state chain, is the default high-street name.
A library — where you borrow, not buy — is [[图书馆]].

## Why this combination
超 "to exceed, to surpass" (as in 超过 "to exceed") combined with 级 "a
level, a rank, a grade" (as in 年级 "grade level"): surpassing the
[ordinary] rank — a prefix for "super-," borrowed to translate the
English prefix.

## Notes
超级 attaches to a noun much as English "super-" does (超级市场
"supermarket," 超级明星 "superstar") and, in casual speech, works loosely
as an intensifier before an adjective (超级好吃 "super delicious").

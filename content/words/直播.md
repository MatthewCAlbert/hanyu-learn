## Why this combination
直 "direct, straight, without intermediary" (see also 直到, 一直)
combined with 播 "to broadcast" (see also 广播): to broadcast directly,
without delay — a live broadcast.

## Notes
直播 originally named live television and radio broadcasting (现场直播
"live on-the-scene broadcast") and has since extended naturally to
internet livestreaming (网络直播 "internet livestream," 直播带货
"livestream selling") — a modern coinage still fully productive today.

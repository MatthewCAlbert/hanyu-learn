## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 力 ("strength, force," as in 力量) and 气
("energy, vital breath, qi," as in 空气no — here in its "life-force"
sense as in 生气") both independently name bodily energy. Pairing them
gives the everyday word for physical strength or exertion.

## Notes

费力气 ("to expend effort, lit. to spend strength") and 没有力气 ("to
have no strength left") both use 力气 for concrete physical exertion —
力量 is the more abstract cousin, covering figurative "power" as well.

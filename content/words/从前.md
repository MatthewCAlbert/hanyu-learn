## Why this combination
从 "from" (see also 从来, 自从) combined with 前 "before, front [in
time]" (see also 以前, 提前): [looking] from before — in the past,
formerly.

## Notes
从前 opens a reflection on a bygone time, often nostalgically or as a
story opener (从前有一个... "once upon a time there was a...," 从前
的日子 "the days of old") — 以前, its close relative, is the more
neutral, everyday word for "before, previously."

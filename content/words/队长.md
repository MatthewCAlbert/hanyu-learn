## Why this combination
队 "team, squad, line" (see [[球队]], [[排队]]) + 长 (*zhǎng*) "head, chief"
(see [[校长]], [[家长]], [[组长]]) → the one in charge of a team: 球队队长,
消防队长.

## Notes
长 is *zhǎng* here (head), the "-chief" suffix across 班长, 组长, 院长, 市长.
Contrast 长 *cháng* "long."

## Why this combination

夏 ("summer," as in 夏天) plus 季 ("a season," as in 季节) names the
summer season.

## Notes

夏季 is the formal/written register — 夏天 is the everyday spoken word
for the same season, the same pattern seen in 冬季/冬天 (covered
elsewhere in this app).

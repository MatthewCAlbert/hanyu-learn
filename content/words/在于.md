## Why this combination
在 ("to exist, to be located at") plus 于 (the classical preposition "at,
in," covered in this app's entries for 关于, 对于, 处于) doubles up on
"being located at" to mark where the real, essential explanation of
something is found — "the answer lies in..."

Rated "semi" for 于's classical preposition role, which needs to be
already known from words like 关于 to parse this compound cleanly.

## Notes
在于 introduces the deciding factor behind a situation (成功在于坚持,
"success lies in persistence") — it explains *why* something is the way
it is, rather than stating a plain physical location, which would use 在
alone.

## Why this combination
收音 "to receive sound [broadcasts]" (收 "to receive," see also 收看,
收费, plus 音 "sound," see also 声音) combined with 机 "a machine" (see
also 机器, 手机): the machine that receives sound broadcasts — a radio.

## Notes
收音机 names the physical radio receiver device (打开收音机 "to turn on
the radio") — 广播, its neighbour, names the broadcast content/medium
itself, while 收音机 is the hardware that receives it.

## Why this combination
考, here in its "to examine, to test" sense (as in 考试 "exam") combined
with 验 "to test, to verify" (see also 经验, 实验): to examine and
verify — to test rigorously, especially character or resolve.

## Notes
考验 names a demanding test of character, ability or a relationship
(经受考验 "to withstand a trial," 严峻的考验 "a severe test") — 考试, its
close relative, is specifically an academic exam, while 考验 is broader,
often figurative.

## Why this combination
阻 "to block" and 碍 "to hinder, to obstruct" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for something that impedes progress.

## Notes
阻碍 works as verb ("阻碍发展" "to hinder development") and noun ("扫清
阻碍" "to clear away obstacles") — close to 障碍, though 阻碍 leans
slightly more toward the act of hindering than the obstacle itself.

## Why this combination
好 "good" + 玩儿 "to play / have fun" (see [[玩儿]]) → good to play with, fun.
Part of the 好 + verb frame with [[好看]], [[好吃]], [[好听]]. Of games, places,
gadgets, outings.

## Notes
The non-erhua 好玩 is the same word. Also of a person: 他很好玩儿 "he's good
fun."

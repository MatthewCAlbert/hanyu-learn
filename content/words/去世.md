## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 去 "to leave, to go" takes 世 "the world" (see
also 世界, 世纪) as its object — literally "to leave the world" — to
pass away, a polite euphemism for death.

## Notes
去世 is the standard polite, respectful word for someone's death
(他去世了 "he passed away") — 死, the blunt, direct verb "to die," is
avoided in polite conversation about someone's passing in favour of
去世 or the even more formal 逝世.

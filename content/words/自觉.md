## Why this combination
自 "self" (see also 自己, 自动) combined with 觉 "to be aware, to sense,
to perceive" (as in 感觉 "to feel"): aware of oneself — self-aware, and,
by extension, acting on one's own sense of duty without being told.

## Notes
自觉 describes being self-aware (自觉性 "self-awareness") and, very
commonly, acting responsibly of one's own accord (自觉遵守规则 "to
conscientiously abide by the rules, without needing to be made to") —
主动, its neighbour, stresses taking initiative, while 自觉 stresses an
inner sense of duty prompting the action.

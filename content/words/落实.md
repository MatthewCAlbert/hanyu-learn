## Why this combination
落 (here "to settle, to come to rest") plus the resultative complement
实 "solid, real" gives exactly "to settle into something solid" — the
standard word for turning a plan into concrete action.

## Notes
落实 anchors 落实政策 "to implement a policy," 落实到位 "properly
implemented" — always about moving from intention/plan to actual,
verifiable execution.

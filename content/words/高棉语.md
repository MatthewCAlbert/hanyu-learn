## Why this combination
高棉 is the established Chinese name for the Khmer (historically also Cambodia);
+ 语. 高 is not "tall"; 棉 is not "cotton" as a meaning here — the binome is the
ethnonym.

## Notes
柬埔寨 is omitted (埔 is not an HSK character), so Khmer is listed via 高棉.

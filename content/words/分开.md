## Why this combination
分 "to divide, to split" + resultative 开 "apart" (the same 开 in [[离开]],
[[打开]]) — dividing so that the parts come apart: 把两个分开, 我们分开走
"let's split up," 分开付 "pay separately."

## Notes
开 as an "apart / open" resultative also in 拉开, 打开, 散开. Opposite 合在一起
"put together." 分手 "break up (a couple)."

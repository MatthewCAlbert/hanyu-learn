## Why this combination
逻辑 transliterates English "logic" sound-for-sound. 逻 "to patrol" (see
逻's own etymology) and 辑 "to compile" (see 辑's own etymology) were
chosen for their sound; neither contributes meaning to the modern
philosophical/reasoning sense.

## Notes
逻辑 anchors 逻辑思维 "logical thinking," 不合逻辑 "illogical" — completely
naturalized in modern Mandarin, with no sense of its component characters'
original, unrelated meanings.

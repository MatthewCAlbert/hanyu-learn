## Why this combination
助 "to assist" and 理 "to manage, to handle" combine directly: one who
assists in managing affairs.

## Notes
助理 names a formal job title (助理教授 "assistant professor," 总裁助理
"assistant to the CEO") — a step below the person they assist, distinct
from 助手, which is a more general, less title-bound word for a helper.

## Why this combination
电视 "television" (see its own entry) combined with 剧 "a drama, a
play" (as in 京剧 "Peking opera"): a drama made for television — a TV
series.

## Notes
电视剧 names serialised television drama specifically (看电视剧 "to
watch a TV drama") — 节目, the general word for "programme," covers any
type of television content, not only dramatic series.

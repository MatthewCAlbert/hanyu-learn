## Why this combination
石 "rock, stone, mineral" (see also 石油) combined with the light noun
suffix 头 "head," here grammaticalised (as in 木头 "wood," 舌头 "tongue")
rather than meaning "head" literally: a concrete noun formed the same
way many everyday object-words are.

## Notes
石头 is the everyday, colloquial word for a stone or rock (一块石头 "a
stone/rock") — 石, alone, is the more literary/scientific form seen in
compounds like 石油 "petroleum" and 岩石 "rock (geological)."

## Why this combination
夫 carries its "man, husband" sense (also seen in 丈夫, 夫妻, both covered
elsewhere in this app), and 人 is simply "person." Together they form a
formal, honorific way to refer to a married woman — literally "the
[gentle]man's person," i.e. his lady.

Rated "semi" because the honorific role — referring to *someone else's*
wife respectfully, not one's own — has to be learned as a social
convention rather than derived purely from the parts.

## Notes
夫人 is used for someone else's wife in formal contexts (总统夫人, "the
president's wife/First Lady") — for one's own wife, 妻子 or 太太 is the
natural everyday choice.

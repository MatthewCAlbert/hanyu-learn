## Why this combination

一 ("one") plus 律 ("a law, a standard," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 法律/规律) names being governed by a single standard applied
to all: uniformly, without exception.

Rated "semi" for 律's abstract "governing standard" contribution here,
applied to universal application rather than a specific law.

## Notes

一律不准 ("without exception, not allowed") and 一律免费 ("free for
everyone, without exception") both build on this word — an emphatic
formal adverb close to "all alike, no exceptions."

## Why this combination
家 "home, family" + 庭 "courtyard, hall" (the same 庭 as 法庭 courtroom,
庭院 "courtyard"). The house-and-its-yard stands for the family unit as a social
institution: 家庭生活, 单亲家庭 "single-parent family."

## Notes
家庭 is the family as a concept / unit; [[家人]] is its members; 家里 is the
place. More formal than 一家人.

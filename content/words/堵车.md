## Why this combination

堵 ("to block, to plug up," as in 堵塞, "to be clogged") takes 车
("vehicle") as its object: vehicles blocked up — a traffic jam.

## Notes

路上堵车 ("stuck in traffic on the road") and 严重堵车 ("a severe traffic
jam") both build on this word — the everyday spoken word for congestion,
distinct from 交通堵塞, its more formal written equivalent.

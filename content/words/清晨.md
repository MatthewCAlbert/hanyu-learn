## Why this combination
清 "clear, fresh" and 晨 "early morning, dawn" combine into "the fresh
early morning" — a literary intensifying compound naming the hours right
after daybreak.

## Notes
清晨 is a written/literary time word, typically used for scene-setting:
清晨的阳光 "the morning light" — earlier and more evocative than everyday
早上, which covers a broader stretch of the morning.

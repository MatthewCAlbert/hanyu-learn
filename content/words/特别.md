## Why this combination
特 "special, exceptional" + 别 "distinct, set apart" (as in 区别, 分别). Marked
off from the ordinary. Adjective (特别的日子) and adverb (特别好, 特别是… "in
particular").

## Notes
As an intensifier it is colloquial and very common — near 非常. 特 alone:
特点, 特价.

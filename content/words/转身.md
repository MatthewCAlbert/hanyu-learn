## Why this combination

转 ("to turn, to rotate") takes 身 ("body") as its object: turning one's
body around.

## Notes

转身就走 ("to turn and walk away right then") is a common set phrase
pairing 转身 with an immediately following action, emphasizing how
abruptly it happens.

## Why this combination
手, here in an extended "process, method" sense (rather than the literal
body part, similar to how 手法 means "technique") combined with 续 "to
continue, to connect on" (see also 持续, 连续): the connected steps
carried out in hand — a procedure.

## Notes
手续 names the formal steps required to complete a bureaucratic process
(办手续 "to go through the formalities," 入学手续 "enrollment
procedures") — 步骤, its neighbour, names the steps of any process in
general, not specifically official paperwork.

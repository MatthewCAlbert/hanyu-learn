## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 鲜 ("fresh, bright," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 新鲜/鲜花) and 明 ("bright, clear," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 透明) both independently name vivid clarity. Pairing
them gives the standard word for something vividly distinct.

## Notes

色彩鲜明 ("vivid colors") and 立场鲜明 ("a clear-cut stance") both build
on this word — 鲜明 covers both visual vividness and figurative
clarity/distinctness of a position or contrast.

## Why this combination
味 "taste, flavour" + 道 here as a bound nominal element (as in 力道, 门道) that
turns a quality word into an everyday noun. The perceived flavour of food
(味道不错), and figuratively the "feel" of a thing (有点儿伤感的味道).

## Notes
味道 covers both taste and, dialectally, smell. Compare 气味 "odour,"
香味 "fragrance." 道 here is not "road" or "the Way."

## Why this combination
人民 "the people" (as in 人民, 人民代表) combined with 币 "currency, coin"
(as in 货币 "currency" in an unrelated compound): "the people's
currency" — the official name of China's currency.

## Notes
人民币 (abbreviated RMB, symbol ¥) is the formal name of the currency
whose basic unit is 元/块 "yuan" — much as "sterling" names the currency
while "pound" names the unit, 人民币 names the currency while 元 or the
colloquial 块 names the unit of account.

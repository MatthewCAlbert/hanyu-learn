## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term (see [[妈妈]]), intimate address form, toneless second
syllable. Siblings by relative age: 哥哥 older brother, 弟弟 younger.

## Notes
弟 alone in 兄弟 (brothers), 弟子 (disciple). Friendly address 小弟, 老弟.

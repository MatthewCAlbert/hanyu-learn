## Why this combination
立 "to stand" and 场 "field, ground" combine metaphorically: the ground
one stands on, extended to the ideological or political position one
holds.

## Notes
立场 anchors 政治立场 "political standpoint," 坚定立场 "to hold firm to
one's position," 立场不同 "differing viewpoints" — always about a
considered stance, not a physical location.

## Why this combination
显 "to make visible, to make evident" and 示 "to show, to indicate" are
near-synonyms, paired for the standard verb "to display" — used for
instruments, data and evidence as readily as for outward appearance.

## Notes
显示 is the word for instruments and information making something visible
(显示屏 "display screen," 数据显示 "the data shows"); 表示 is closer to
"to express, to signify" and is used for meaning and attitude rather than
readouts.

## Why this combination

秘 ("secret," as in 秘密) plus 书 ("document, book, to write," as in 书记,
"a scribe/secretary [in a party or organizational role]") named someone
who handles an official's confidential documents and correspondence —
the origin of the modern "secretary."

Rated "opaque": the modern job title isn't recoverable from "secret
document" without knowing this historical role.

## Notes

秘书 (a personal/executive secretary) and 书记 (a party or organizational
secretary, e.g. 党委书记) both descend from the same idea of a trusted
document-handler but now name distinct roles.

## Why this combination
环 "a ring, to surround, to encircle" combined with 境 "boundary,
territory, realm" (as in 边境 "border"): the encircling territory around
something — its surroundings, whether natural, social or domestic.

## Notes
环境 covers the natural environment (环境保护 "environmental protection")
and any surrounding setting alike (学习环境 "study environment," 工作
环境 "work environment") — unlike English "environment," it is not
restricted to the ecological sense by default.

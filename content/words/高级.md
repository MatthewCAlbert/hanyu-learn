## Why this combination
高 "high" + 级 "grade, rank, level, step" (年级, 班级) → of a high grade — in
rank (高级官员), in skill level (高级课程), or in quality (高级餐厅).

## Notes
级 "grade / step" also in [[年级]], 初级 / 中级 / 高级 (the difficulty series),
[[高级]]'s cousin 等级.

## Why this combination

遗 ("to leave behind, to bequeath," as in 遗忘, "to forget [lit. leave
behind and lose]") plus 产 ("property, assets," as in 财产) names
property left behind: an inheritance, or figuratively a cultural legacy.

## Notes

遗产 covers both literal inheritance (继承遗产, "to inherit an
estate") and cultural heritage (世界文化遗产, "UNESCO World Heritage" —
lit. "world cultural heritage").

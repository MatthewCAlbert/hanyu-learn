## Why this combination
调 (here "to adjust, to tune") and 节 (here "to regulate, to moderate,"
as in 节制) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for fine-
tuning something to the right level.

## Notes
调节 is used for adjusting mechanisms and conditions: 调节温度 "to adjust
the temperature," 调节心态 "to regulate one's mindset" — close to 调整,
though 调节 leans toward continuous fine control (like a dial) rather
than a one-time change.

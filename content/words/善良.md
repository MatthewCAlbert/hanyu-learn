## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 善 ("good, kind, virtuous," as in 完善, "to
perfect [lit. complete + good]") and 良 ("good, fine," as in 良好) both
independently name moral goodness. Pairing them gives the standard word
for a kind, good-hearted disposition.

## Notes

善良 describes character rather than skill or looks — 她很善良 ("she's
very kind-hearted") is a statement about someone's nature, not their
competence.

## Why this combination
硬 "hard" plus 件 "a piece, a component" directly calques English
"hardware" — physical, tangible components, as opposed to 软件
"software" (软 "soft" + 件), which calques "software" the same way.

## Notes
硬件 anchors 电脑硬件 "computer hardware," and, figuratively, "hardware"
in the sense of physical infrastructure (硬件设施 "hardware/physical
facilities") as opposed to intangible systems or processes.

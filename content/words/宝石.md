## Why this combination

宝 ("a treasure, precious," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
宝贵) plus 石 ("stone," as in 石头) names a precious stone: a gem.

## Notes

宝石戒指 ("a gemstone ring") and 天然宝石 ("a natural gem") both build on
this word — shares its 宝 "treasure" root with 珠宝 ("jewelry, lit.
pearl + treasure") and 宝贵, covered elsewhere in this app.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 密 ("dense, close together," as in 秘密, "secret
[lit. hidden + dense]") and 切 ("close, urgent, pressing," as in 亲切,
"warm and kind") both independently name closeness. Pairing them gives
the standard word for a close relationship or connection.

## Notes

密切 describes relationships and connections (密切关系, "close
relationship") and, as an adverb, the manner of watching or following
something (密切关注, "to follow closely") — 亲密 leans more personal/
emotional, while 密切 covers formal ties too (国际合作日益密切,
"international cooperation grows ever closer").

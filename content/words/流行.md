## Why this combination
流 "to flow" + 行 (*xíng*) "to go, to circulate, to be in effect" → to move
through a population like water. Of fashions and songs, "popular" (流行歌曲,
今年流行…); of illness, "to go around" (流行性感冒 "flu").

## Notes
流 "flow" also in [[流利]] (flowing speech), 流水. 行 is *xíng* here (circulate),
as in [[进行]], [[行动]].

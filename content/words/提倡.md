## Why this combination
提 (here "to raise, to bring up") and 倡 "to advocate, to initiate" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for actively encouraging
something.

## Notes
提倡 anchors 提倡节约 "to promote thrift," 大力提倡 "to strongly advocate"
— always about publicly encouraging a practice or value, distinct from
建议 "to suggest," which is more personal/individual.

## Why this combination
西 "west" (as in 西方 "the West, westward") combined with 部 "a part, a
section, a division" (see also 部门): the western section — the west of
a country or region.

## Notes
西部 names the western region of a specific country or area (中国西部
"western China," 美国西部 "the American West") — 西方, its close
relative, refers instead to "the West" as a broad civilisational or
directional concept, not a specific internal region.

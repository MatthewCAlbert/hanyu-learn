## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 亲 ("close, related by blood or affection," as in
父亲, 亲人) and 爱 ("to love") both independently name closeness and
affection. Together they form the standard salutation adjective, as in
letters and speeches: 亲爱的朋友们, "dear friends."

## Notes
亲爱的 (with 的) is the fixed form used to address someone directly — it
almost never appears without 的 attached when used this way, unlike most
adjectives, which can modify a noun with or without 的 depending on
register.

## Why this combination
前 "front, before" and 提 "to raise, to bring up" (see 提's role in 提示)
combine into "what is raised/put forward beforehand" — the condition
that must be established before anything else can follow.

## Notes
前提 anchors 前提条件 "precondition," 以...为前提 "on the premise/condition
that..." — always the logical or practical ground something else depends
on.

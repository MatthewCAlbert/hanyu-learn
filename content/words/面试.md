## Why this combination

面 ("face, face-to-face," as in 面对) plus 试 ("to test," as in 考试)
names a test conducted face-to-face: an interview.

Rated "semi" for 面's extended "in person" sense here, distinct from its
more literal "face" meaning.

## Notes

面试 contrasts with 笔试 ("written exam, lit. brush-test") as the two
stages of a hiring or admissions process — the 笔 (writing implement) /
面 (face) pairing mirrors how the tests are actually conducted.

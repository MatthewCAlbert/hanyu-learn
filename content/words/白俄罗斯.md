## Why this combination
白 "white" + [[俄罗斯]]: a character-for-character reading of Belarus / White Rus
(Беларусь). 白 here really is "white," unlike the dummy phonetics in most Extra
country names.

## Notes
Same stem in [[白俄罗斯语]]. Contrast [[俄罗斯]], which is a transcription of Rossiya.

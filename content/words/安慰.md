## Why this combination
安 "peace, calm" and 慰 "to comfort" (see 慰's own etymology — 安也,
Shuowen's gloss "to settle, to calm") are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for consoling someone.

## Notes
安慰 works as verb ("to comfort someone in distress") and noun ("a
comfort, a consolation," 聊以安慰 "small comfort") — always aimed at
easing someone's distress or disappointment.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 诚 ("sincere, genuine," as in 诚恳, "earnest")
and 实 ("real, true," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 老实 and
事实) both independently name genuineness. Pairing them gives the
standard word for honesty.

## Notes

诚实 is the neutral, positive word for honesty (诚实的人, "an honest
person") — 老实, covered elsewhere in this app, overlaps closely but can
carry a milder tinge of naivety that 诚实 does not.

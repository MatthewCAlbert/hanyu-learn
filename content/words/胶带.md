## Why this combination
胶 "glue, rubber, adhesive" plus 带 "a strip, a band" gives exactly "an
adhesive strip" — the standard word for sticky tape, later extended to
magnetic recording tape by shape.

## Notes
胶带 anchors 透明胶带 "clear tape," 录音胶带 "cassette/recording tape" —
covers both everyday sticky tape and, historically, the ribbon inside
cassette tapes.

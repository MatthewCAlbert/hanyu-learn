## Why this combination

期 ("a term, a period," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
期限/学期) plus 末 ("end," a bound morpheme naming the tail end of a
period) names the end of a term: end-of-term.

## Notes

期末考试 ("final exam, lit. end-of-term exam") is the standard school
compound — 期初 ("start of term," lit. "term + beginning") is its direct
opposite, built the same way.

## Why this combination
教 (*jiào*) "to teach" + 师 "master, one who instructs" (see [[老师]], 师父) →
one whose work is teaching. 教师 is the occupational term — 教师节 "Teachers'
Day," 人民教师 — where [[老师]] is the term of address.

## Notes
教 is *jiào* here. 师 "master / teacher" also in [[老师]], [[教师]]'s cousin
律师 "lawyer," 工程师 "engineer."

## Why this combination
A five-syllable phonetic rendering of Ecuador (厄瓜多爾). The characters are
chosen for sound, not for "disaster + melon + many + you." The name does not
calque "equator" (that would be 赤道).

## Notes
Contrast [[赤道几内亚]], where 赤道 really is "equator."

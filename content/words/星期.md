## Why this combination
"Star period." The seven-day cycle reached China with the seven classical
"stars" (Sun, Moon and five planets), each ruling one day. The old day-names
used those bodies (日、月、火…); modern usage keeps the 星期 label but numbers
the days 星期一…星期六, with 星期日/天 for Sunday.

## Notes
礼拜 (from Christian worship) and 周 are the everyday synonyms: 礼拜三 = 星期三 =
周三.

## Why this combination
冰 "ice" + 岛 "island": a character-for-character reading of Iceland. Unlike
most Extra country names, the characters mean what they say.

## Notes
Same stem in [[冰岛语]]. Contrast [[爱尔兰]], which looks like it might be a calque
and is not.

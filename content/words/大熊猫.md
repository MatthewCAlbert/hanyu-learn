## Why this combination
大 "big" plus 熊猫 "panda" (熊 "bear" + 猫 "cat," describing the animal's
bear-like body and cat-like face) gives exactly "the big bear-cat" —
distinguishing it from the smaller, unrelated 小熊猫 "red panda."

## Notes
大熊猫 is the full, formal name; 熊猫 alone is the everyday shorthand
almost everyone uses — both refer to the same iconic black-and-white
bear, China's most famous conservation symbol.

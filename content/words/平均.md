## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 平 ("level, flat, even") and 均 ("equal, evenly
spread," covered elsewhere in this app — leveled land divided evenly)
both independently name evenness. Pairing them gives the standard word
for an average or an even distribution.

## Notes
平均分配 ("to distribute evenly") and 平均数 ("the average, a mean")
both build on this pair's core "spread level and equal" sense.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 刺 ("to stab, to prick," covered in this app's
hanzi entry) and 激 ("to stir up, to incite," covered in this app's entry
for 激动) both independently name a sharp, provoking action. Pairing them
gives the standard word for anything that provokes a strong reaction —
physical, emotional, or economic.

## Notes
刺激 spans registers from the neutral/scientific (刺激反应, "stimulus
response") to the emotional (受刺激, "to be [emotionally] provoked/upset")
to the recreational (很刺激, "thrilling," of a ride or experience).

## Why this combination

加油 ("to refuel, lit. add oil"; also the common cheer "go for it!")
plus 站 ("a station," as in 车站, "a bus/train stop") names the place
where one adds fuel to a vehicle: a gas station.

## Notes

加油站 builds directly on 加油's literal "add fuel" sense — the same
phrase 加油 doubles as an everyday exclamation of encouragement,
unrelated to this compound's specific place-noun meaning.

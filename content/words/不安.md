## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 安 "peaceful, at ease, secure" (see also 安排, 安装):
not at peace — uneasy, restless.

## Notes
不安 describes a state of anxious unease, often with a physical
component (心里不安 "uneasy at heart," 坐立不安 "unable to sit still, on
tenterhooks") — it names an unsettled state rather than a specific fear,
distinguishing it from 害怕's more focused "afraid of [something]."

## Why this combination

分 ("to divide," as in 分开) plus 布 (here "to spread out, to
distribute," an extended verbal sense beyond its literal noun meaning
"cloth," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 布置, "to arrange")
names being divided and spread across an area: distributed.

Rated "semi" for 布's extended "to spread" sense here, one step from its
more common noun meaning "cloth, fabric."

## Notes

人口分布 ("population distribution") and 广泛分布 ("widely distributed")
both build on this word — 分布 always describes how something is spread
across a space or range, geographic or statistical.

## Why this combination
专 "specialised, focused exclusively [on one thing]" (see also 专业,
专家) combined with 门 in its "category, specialty" sense (see also
部门): focused exclusively within one category — specialised, or, as an
adverb, "specially, for that purpose alone."

## Notes
专门 works as adjective ("专门知识" "specialised knowledge") and adverb
("我专门去看你" "I came specially to see you") — the adverb use marks an
action as done for one dedicated purpose, not as a side effect of
something else.

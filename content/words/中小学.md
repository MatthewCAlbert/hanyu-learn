## Why this combination
A telescoping of [[中学]] "secondary school" and [[小学]] "primary school":
中 + 小学 covers the whole span of school education below university, as one
category — 中小学教育, 中小学生, 中小学老师.

## Notes
Same blending as [[青少年]] ([[青年]] + [[少年]]). The three levels are
小学 → 中学 (→ 初中 / 高中) → [[大学]].

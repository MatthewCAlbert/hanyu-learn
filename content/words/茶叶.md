## Why this combination

茶 ("tea," as in 红茶/绿茶) plus 叶 ("leaf," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 叶子) names the dried leaves used to brew tea: tea
leaves.

## Notes

买茶叶 ("to buy tea [leaves]") names the raw product, distinct from 茶,
which more often names the brewed drink itself once steeped.

## Why this combination

孙 ("a grandchild, a descendant") plus the nominal suffix 子 names a
son's or daughter's son: a grandson.

## Notes

孙子 (grandson) and 孙女 (granddaughter, with 女 "female") both build on
子孙 ("descendants, offspring"), the broader family-line word that names
this same 孙 root without specifying generation or gender.

## Why this combination
意, here in its "expectation, what is anticipated" sense (as in 意思
"meaning," 注意 "attention"), combined with 外 "outside of": outside
what was expected — unexpected, and, as a noun, an unexpected mishap.

## Notes
意外 works as adjective ("意外的结果" "an unexpected result") and noun
("发生意外" "for an accident/mishap to occur") — as a noun it leans
towards a sudden, unwanted event, closer to 事故 "accident" than to a
merely pleasant surprise.

## Why this combination
台 (here "stage," as in 舞台) plus 风 (here "manner, bearing," as in
作风) gives exactly "one's bearing on stage" — the standard word for a
performer's composure and presence.

## Notes
台风 in this sense anchors 台风好 "has good stage presence" — a homograph
of the far more common 台风 "typhoon" (a phonetic loan unrelated to
either meaning here); context and the surrounding words distinguish them
completely.

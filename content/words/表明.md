## Why this combination
表 "to display, to show outwardly" (see also 表达, 表示) combined with 明
"clear, evident, bright" (as in 显然): to display something so it becomes
clear — to indicate, to make plain.

## Notes
表明 is used for evidence, data or statements clarifying a fact
(数据表明 "the data shows," 表明立场 "to make one's position clear") — it
takes a clause or fact as its object, closer to "to indicate" than
表达's "to express [an inner feeling]."

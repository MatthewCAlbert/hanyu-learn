## Why this combination
人 "person" combined with 群 "a group, a crowd, a herd" (as in 羊群
"flock of sheep"): a group of people — a crowd.

## Notes
人群 names a gathered mass of people (穿过人群 "to push through the
crowd," 人群中 "among the crowd") — 群众, a close relative built on the
same 群, is used instead for "the masses" as a political or social
category, not a literal physical crowd.

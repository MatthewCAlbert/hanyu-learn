## Why this combination
初 "beginning, initial" plus 期 "period" gives exactly "the initial
period" — the standard word for the early stage of a longer process.

## Notes
初期 anchors 改革初期 "the early stage of reform," 疾病初期 "the early
stage of an illness" — always the opening phase of something ongoing,
opposite of 末期 "final stage."

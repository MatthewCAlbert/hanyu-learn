## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to issue, to bring forth" (see also 发展, 发现) combined
with 明 "clear, bright, to make evident" (see also 明确, 说明): to bring
forth something and make it clear — to invent, to bring a new idea to
light.

## Notes
发明 names creating something genuinely new that did not exist before
(发明家 "inventor," 伟大的发明 "a great invention") — 发现, its close
relative, is "to discover" something that already existed but was
unknown, a clean discovery/invention distinction the two words maintain.

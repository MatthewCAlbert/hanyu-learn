## Why this combination
即 ("right at, arriving at [this point]," covered in this app's hanzi
entry) plus 将 (a grammaticalized "about to, going to" marker of the near
future, as in 将来) doubles up on imminence: right at the point of being
about to happen.

Rated "semi" for 将's grammaticalized future-marker role, a distinct use
from its more concrete senses elsewhere (将军, general; 将...拿走, "to take
...away").

## Notes
即将 is formal/written — 快要...了 or 马上 read more naturally in everyday
speech for the same "about to happen" meaning.

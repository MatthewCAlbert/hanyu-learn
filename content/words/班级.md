## Why this combination
班 "a class, a squad, a shift" (as in 上班 "to go to work," 班长 "class
monitor") combined with 级 "a level, a grade" (see also 年级, 初级): a
class organised by grade level — a school class.

## Notes
班级 is the formal, collective term for classes as an institutional unit
(班级活动 "class activity," 班级管理 "class management") — 班, alone, is
used in ordinary speech for "class" (我们班 "our class"), while 班级
belongs to more administrative or written contexts.

## Why this combination
短 "short" (as in 短裤, figuratively "lacking, deficient") combined with
处 "place, aspect, point" (as in 好处, 长处): the aspect where one falls
short — a weakness.

## Notes
短处 names a person's weakness or flaw (揭人短处 "to expose someone's
shortcomings") and forms the direct opposite of 长处 "strong point," its
close relative built the same way — the pair 长处/短处 mirrors 优点/缺点
in usage.

## Why this combination
历 carries its "to pass through, to go through (a sequence of events)"
sense (as in 经历, 历经), and 史 is "records (of the past), a chronicle" (as
in 史书, and the bound title 历史学家, historian). "The record of what has
been passed through" is history.

Rated "semi" because 史 is a relatively rare standalone morpheme for most
learners — met almost exclusively inside compounds like this one.

## Notes
历史 covers both the academic discipline (学历史, to study history) and the
past itself as a fact (这段历史 "this period of history") — a single word
doing the job of two related English senses.

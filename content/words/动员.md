## Why this combination
动 "to move, to act" plus 员 "personnel, member" gives exactly "to set
personnel in motion" — the standard word for rallying people into
coordinated action, most familiar from its military origin.

## Notes
动员 anchors 动员大会 "a mobilization rally," 全民动员 "mobilizing the
whole population" — used for both military mobilization and, more
broadly, rallying people for any collective effort.

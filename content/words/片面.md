## Why this combination

片 (here "partial, one slice of," an extended sense beyond its literal
"a flat piece," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 照片) plus 面
("a side, a face," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 面试) names
seeing only one partial side of something: one-sided.

Rated "semi" for 片's extended "partial" sense here, one step from its
literal "flat piece" meaning.

## Notes

片面的看法 ("a one-sided view") and 片面地看问题 ("to look at an issue
one-sidedly") both build on this word — 全面 (covered elsewhere in this
app) is its direct antonym, "comprehensive," built the same way with
全 "whole" instead of 片 "partial."

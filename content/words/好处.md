## Why this combination
好 "good" + 处 (*chù*) "a point, an aspect, a place" (see [[到处]], [[长处]]) →
"the good aspects" of something — its advantages. 有什么好处？ "what's the
benefit?"; also a payoff / kickback: 拿了好处.

## Notes
Opposite [[坏处]] "harm, drawbacks." 处 "aspect / point" also in 长处 / 短处
"strengths / weaknesses."

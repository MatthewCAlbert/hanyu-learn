## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Nigeria (尼日利亞). 日 is phonetic *rì*, not
"Japan" and not "sun" as a meaning. Do not confuse with [[尼日尔]] (Niger).

## Notes
The extra 利亚 is the difference between Niger and Nigeria in Chinese.

## Why this combination
状 "form, appearance, condition" (see also 状态) combined with 况
"condition, circumstance" (as in 情况 "situation"): two near-synonyms for
a condition, paired into a somewhat more formal word for the state
something is in.

## Notes
状况 overlaps heavily with 状态 and 情况 (健康状况 "state of health,"
经济状况 "economic conditions"); it leans towards formal, often written
or official contexts describing an ongoing condition rather than a
single event.

## Why this combination
干 (*gàn*) "to do" (see [[干什么]], [[打工]]) + 活儿 "work, a job, a task" (活
with erhua — the same 活 as [[生活]], [[活动]], here "a piece of work") → to do
work, especially physical or hands-on work. Verb-object: 干了一天活儿.

## Notes
干活儿 is blue-collar and colloquial; [[工作]] is the neutral word. Non-erhua
干活 is the same. 干 is *gàn* here.

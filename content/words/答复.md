## Why this combination
答 "to answer" and 复 (here "to reply," as in 回复) are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for a considered response.

## Notes
答复 is used for official or substantive replies: 给予答复 "to give a
reply," 尚未答复 "not yet responded" — more formal than casual 回答, often
implying a reply to a request or inquiry that required deliberation.

## Why this combination
From "still is" — 还 "still" + 是 "to be." Two modern senses grew out of it:
"it is still the case that…" (你还是老样子) and, by a longer path, the
alternative-question "or" — 喝茶还是喝咖啡？

## Notes
Question "or" is 还是; statement "or" is 或者. Mixing them up (or者 in a
question) is a very common beginner error.

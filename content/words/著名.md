## Why this combination
著 here carries its "outstanding, notable" sense (as in 显著, distinct from
its unrelated "to write" sense in 著作), and 名 is "name, fame" (as in
名字, 有名). "Outstandingly named/known" is famous.

Rated "semi" for 著's "outstanding" sense, which is easy to conflate with
its far more common "to write, an author's work" sense elsewhere.

## Notes
著名 and 有名 are close synonyms; 著名 reads slightly more formal/written,
while 有名 is equally at home in casual speech.

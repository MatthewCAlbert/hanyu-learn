## Why this combination
部 "a section, a part" and 位 "position, location" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for a specific part or location within a
larger whole.

## Notes
部位 favours anatomical and technical precision: 身体部位 "body part,"
敏感部位 "a sensitive area" — more clinical than everyday 地方 "place" or
plain 部分 "part."

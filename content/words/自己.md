## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 自 "self" (as in 自动, 自我) + 己 "self" (as in 知己, 利己).
Doubling makes the free-standing reflexive pronoun — 我自己, 你自己, 他自己 —
and, alone, a generic "oneself": 靠自己 "rely on yourself."

## Notes
自 also = "from" (来自, 自古). 己 barely occurs alone in the modern language
outside this word and 知己.

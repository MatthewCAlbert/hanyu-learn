## Why this combination
博览 "to view widely" (博 "broad" + 览 "to view") plus 会 "a gathering,
an event" gives exactly "an event for broad viewing" — the standard word
for a large public exhibition.

## Notes
博览会 anchors 世界博览会 "World Expo" (often shortened to 世博会), 广交会
"Canton Fair" (a specific 博览会) — the formal term for a large-scale
trade or cultural exhibition.

## Why this combination
批 "to annotate, to comment on [a document]" (as in 批评 "to criticise")
combined with 准 "to permit, accurate, a standard" (as in 准备 "to
prepare," 标准 "standard"): to annotate as permitted — to officially
approve.

## Notes
批准 names formal, official approval, especially of a request or
proposal (批准申请 "to approve an application") — 同意, a near-synonym,
is the general word for "to agree" and does not carry the same formal,
authoritative weight.

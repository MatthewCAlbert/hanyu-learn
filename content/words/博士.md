## Why this combination
博 "extensive, broad [learning]" and 士 "scholar, learned person" combine
directly: a scholar of the broadest, deepest learning — the modern
equivalent of a doctoral degree holder.

## Notes
博士 historically named an academic official position in imperial China
before being repurposed for the Western PhD; 博士生 "PhD student," 读博
"to pursue a doctorate" are common derived terms.

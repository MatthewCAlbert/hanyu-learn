## Why this combination
"Born before" = one's senior = someone owed respect. Classical Chinese used it
for elders and then teachers; the "teacher" sense dominated into the 20th
century and still shows in addressing scholars. Modern default is the polite
title "Mr," placed after the surname: 王先生.

## Notes
王先生 "Mr Wang"; 我先生 "my husband." The teacher sense is now largely
replaced by [[老师]].

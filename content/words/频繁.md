## Why this combination
频 "frequent" and 繁 "numerous, many" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for something happening often and repeatedly.

## Notes
频繁 modifies actions that recur closely in time: 联系频繁 "frequent
contact," 频繁出错 "frequent errors" — stronger than plain 常常, implying
a rate high enough to be notable or excessive.

## Why this combination
到 "to arrive at" and 达 "to reach, to get through to" (see also 达到)
are close synonyms, paired into the standard verb for physically arriving
somewhere.

## Notes
到达 is used for physically arriving at a place (到达机场 "to arrive at
the airport") — 达到, the same two characters reversed, is used instead
for reaching an abstract goal or standard, a clean division of labour
between the pair.

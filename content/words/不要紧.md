## Why this combination

不 ("not") negates 要紧 ("important, critical" — 要, "to need, to
require," plus 紧, "tight, urgent," as in 紧张) to name something not
urgent or serious enough to worry about.

Rated "semi" for 要紧's fixed idiomatic sense as "critical/serious,"
built from parts that don't individually spell out that meaning.

## Notes

不要紧 reassures that a problem is minor ("别担心，不要紧," "don't worry,
it's not serious") — close to 没关系/没事儿 in function, though 不要紧
specifically addresses a stated concern's severity.

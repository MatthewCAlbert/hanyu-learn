## Why this combination
病 "illness, to be ill" modifies 人 "person" → a person who is ill. Same
modifier-head shape as 客人 (guest), 工人 (worker), [[老人]].

## Notes
病 as a verb: 他病了 "he's fallen ill." 看病 (see [[看病]]) is what the 病人 goes
to do; 病房 is the ward.

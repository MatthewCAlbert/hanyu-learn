## Why this combination
返 "to return" (see 返's own etymology) and 回 "to return" are
near-synonyms combined for emphasis into the standard formal word for
going back.

## Notes
返回 is the formal/technical register (返回原地 "to return to the original
spot," 程序返回值 "a program's return value" in computing) — everyday
speech more often uses plain 回 or 回去/回来.

## Why this combination
先 "first, ahead, to precede" (see also 领先) combined with 进 "to
advance" (see also 前进, 进步): advancing first, ahead of others —
advanced.

## Notes
先进 describes technology, methods or individuals leading the field
(先进技术 "advanced technology," 先进人物 "an exemplary/advanced
individual" in a formal recognition sense) — its opposite is 落后
"backward, lagging behind."

## Why this combination
不 "not" combined with 光, here a colloquial "only, merely" (a
separate, spoken-register sense from 光's literal "light"): not only —
a colloquial equivalent of 不仅/不但.

## Notes
不光 is regularly paired with 而且/还 in the second clause, just like
不仅 and 不但 (不光聪明，还很努力 "not only smart, but also
hardworking") — it is the most colloquial of the three near-synonyms,
common in everyday speech but avoided in formal writing.

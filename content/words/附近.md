## Why this combination
附 is "to attach to, to adhere to" (as in 附加, 附件), and 近 is the plain
adjective "near." Something "attached close by" is in the vicinity — 附
narrows 近's plain nearness into the specific sense of being attached to a
particular reference point (a place, an address).

Rated "semi" for 附's less common standalone sense, usually met first in
compounds like 附件 (attachment) rather than alone.

## Notes
附近 functions as a noun on its own (在附近, "nearby, in the vicinity") as
well as an attributive modifier before another noun (附近的商店, "the nearby
shops") — no 的 is needed when it directly follows a location.

## Why this combination
反 ("reverse, opposite") plus 而 (the classical conjunction "but, yet,"
covered in this app's entries for 而是 and 然而) names a reversal that
runs against expectation: "instead, contrary to what one would expect."

Rated "semi" because 而's classical conjunction role, while covered
elsewhere in this app, is still a step removed from its everyday
transparency.

## Notes
反而 signals that what follows contradicts the natural expectation set
up by what came before (他不但没生气，反而笑了, "far from getting angry, he
actually laughed") — always marking a surprising reversal, not a plain
contrast.

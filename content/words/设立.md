## Why this combination
设 "to establish, to arrange, to set up" (see also 建设, 设备) combined
with 立 "to stand, to be established" (see also 建立, 成立): to arrange
and set standing — to establish.

## Notes
设立 takes an institution, department or fund as its object (设立奖学金
"to establish a scholarship," 设立机构 "to set up an organisation") —
建立, its close relative, is used the same way and is largely
interchangeable, though 设立 leans slightly more formal/official.

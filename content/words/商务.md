## Why this combination

商 ("commerce, trade," as in 商店) plus 务 ("affairs, business," as in
服务, "service [lit. serve + affairs]") names the affairs of trade:
business.

## Notes

商务旅行 ("business travel") and 电子商务 ("e-commerce, lit. electronic
commerce affairs") both build on this word — 商务 leans toward
institutional/corporate trade activity, distinct from 生意, the
everyday word for "business" as a livelihood.

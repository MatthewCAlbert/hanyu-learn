## Why this combination
投 ("to throw, to commit," covered in this app's hanzi entry) plus 入 ("to
enter") names throwing something so that it lands inside — money into a
venture (投入资金), effort into a task, or a person's whole attention into
what they're doing (工作很投入, "engrossed in the work").

## Notes
投入 spans concrete investment (资金投入, capital committed) and a
person's psychological state (他很投入, "he's fully absorbed/engrossed") —
both readings of the same "thrown all the way in" image.

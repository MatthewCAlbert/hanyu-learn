## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 曾 "ever, once (in the past)" gives exactly "has not
ever" — the negative counterpart of 曾经 "once, previously."

## Notes
不曾 is a written/literary register word: 不曾见过 "have never seen (it)"
— close to 没有…过 or 从未, all negating a past experience, but 不曾 leans
more formal/literary than everyday 没.

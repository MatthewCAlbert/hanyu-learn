## Why this combination
北 "north" + 方 "region, quarter" (see [[方向]], [[南方]], [[西方]]). In China,
roughly the land north of the Yellow River, with its own climate, wheat-based
food and speech: 北方人, 北方话.

## Notes
The 方 compass set: [[东方]], [[南方]], [[西方]], 北方. [[北边]] is the plain
"north side"; 北方 leans toward "the North" as a region.

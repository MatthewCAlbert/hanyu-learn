## Why this combination
演 "to act out, to develop [a role]" (see also 表演) combined with the
directional complement 出 "out, to emerge": to bring a performance out
[before an audience] — to perform, or the show itself.

## Notes
演出 names a staged public performance — theatre, music, dance
(一场精彩的演出 "a wonderful performance") — and doubles directly as a
verb ("今晚演出" "performing tonight") without changing form, the same
verb/noun pattern as 表演.

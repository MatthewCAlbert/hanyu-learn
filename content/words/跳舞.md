## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 跳 "to jump, to leap" takes 舞 "dance" (see also
舞台) as its object — literally "to leap a dance" — to dance.

## Notes
跳舞 is intransitive in form and splits for aspect like other
verb-object compounds (跳了一支舞 "danced a dance," 跳过舞 "have danced
before") — 舞蹈, a more formal noun for "dance" as an art form, is used
in contexts like 舞蹈课 "dance class."

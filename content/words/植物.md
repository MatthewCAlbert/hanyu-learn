## Why this combination

植 ("to plant, to grow," as in 种植) plus 物 ("thing, matter," as in
动物, "animal") names the category of living things that are planted and
grow rooted in place: plants.

## Notes

植物 pairs with 动物 ("animal," lit. "moving thing") as biology's basic
two-way split — the same 物 "thing" suffix names both broad categories.

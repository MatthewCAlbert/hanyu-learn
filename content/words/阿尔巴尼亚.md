## Why this combination
A five-syllable phonetic rendering of Albania (阿爾巴尼亞). The characters are
chosen for sound, not for "ā + you + hope + nun + Asia."

## Notes
Same stem in [[阿尔巴尼亚语]]. 阿尔 also starts [[阿尔及利亚]] — different names.

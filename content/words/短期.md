## Why this combination
短 "short" combined with 期 "a period, a term" (see also 长期, 星期): a
short span of time — short-term.

## Notes
短期 modifies plans and effects spanning only a short stretch of time
(短期计划 "short-term plan," 短期内 "in the short term") — its opposite
is 长期 "long-term," built the same way with 长 "long" in place of 短.

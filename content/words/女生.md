## Why this combination
女 "female" + 生, clipped from 学生 "student." Originally "schoolgirl," it has
broadened among young people to the neutral word for a young woman — softer than
[[女人]]. 男生 is the counterpart.

## Notes
女生朋友 "a friend who is a girl" vs. 女朋友 "girlfriend" — a distinction the
compound's breadth forces you to make.

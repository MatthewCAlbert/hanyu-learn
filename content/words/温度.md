## Why this combination
温 "warm, temperature" + 度 "degree, measure" (see [[角度]], [[态度]]) → the
measured degree of warmth. 量体温 "take one's temperature," 室温, [[气温]]
"air temperature."

## Notes
度 "degree" is also the unit (三十度 "thirty degrees"). 温 "warm" also in
温暖, [[温度]]'s cousin 气温, 水温.

## Why this combination
从 "from, following" plus 而 (a classical connective linking cause and
effect) combines into a formal connective marking a resulting outcome —
而's grammatical role here is not obvious from its everyday uses.

## Notes
从而 links a cause to its consequence in formal writing: 提高效率，从而
降低成本 "to raise efficiency, thereby lowering costs" — a step up in
formality from everyday 所以 "so, therefore."

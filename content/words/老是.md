## Why this combination
老 here as the adverb "always, keep on" (a sense also in 老毛病 "a chronic
problem," 老这样) + 是 "to be" as a filler, giving a stable adverb. 他老是迟到
"he's always late," 老是下雨.

## Notes
老是 usually carries irritation, where [[一直]] / [[总是]] are neutral. 老
"always" is unrelated to 老 "old."

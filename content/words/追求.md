## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 追 ("to chase, to pursue," the everyday word for
chasing something down) and 求 ("to seek, to request," as in 要求, 请求)
both independently name going after something wanted. Pairing them gives
the standard word for pursuing a goal — including, colloquially, romantic
pursuit.

## Notes
追求 covers pursuing an abstract goal (追求梦想, "to pursue a dream") as
well as, colloquially, courting someone romantically (追求她, "to pursue/
court her").

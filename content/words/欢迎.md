## Why this combination
欢 "joyful, merry" + 迎 "to greet, to go out to meet" (迎接) → to greet gladly.
As a greeting (欢迎光临 "welcome!") and a verb (欢迎提意见 "comments are
welcome").

## Notes
迎 "to go toward and meet" is the opposite of 送 "to see off": 迎来送往.

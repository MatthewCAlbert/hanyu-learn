## Why this combination
大 "big, great" + 量 "quantity, amount, measure" (数量, 分量) → a large quantity.
As a quantifier: 大量资金, 大量时间, 大量生产 "mass production."

## Notes
A separate reading *dàliàng* also means "magnanimous, generous" (大量 as
"large capacity / tolerance"). Context tells them apart.

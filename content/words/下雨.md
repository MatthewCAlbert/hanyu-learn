## Why this combination
下 "to descend, to come down" + 雨 "rain" — weather that falls is expressed as
下 + the precipitation: 下雨, 下雪 snow, 下雹子 hail. Grammatically 雨 is the
object of 下, so 下了一场大雨 "there was a downpour."

## Notes
No subject needed — 下雨了 "it's raining." The sky as agent is left unspoken,
like English "it."

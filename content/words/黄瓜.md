## Why this combination

黄 ("yellow") plus 瓜 ("a melon, a gourd," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 西瓜) names a melon named for its color (its skin yellows as
it ages, or by the color of an older variety): cucumber.

Rated "semi" for the color-naming logic, not obvious from a typically
green modern cucumber.

## Notes

黄瓜 shares its 瓜 "melon" root with 西瓜 (watermelon) and 南瓜 (pumpkin)
— a small family of gourds named by color or geographic origin rather
than botanical classification.

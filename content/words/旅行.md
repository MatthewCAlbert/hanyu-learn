## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 旅 "a journey, to travel, a traveller" (旅客, 旅馆) + 行 (*xíng*)
"to go, to travel." A trip, especially a longer one: 出去旅行, 旅行社 "travel
agency."

## Notes
旅行 stresses the journeying; [[旅游]] adds sightseeing / leisure and is the
word for "tourism." 旅 "travel" also in [[旅客]], [[旅游]], 旅馆.

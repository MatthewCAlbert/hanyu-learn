## Why this combination
最 "most" + 近 "near, close" — closest in time to now, in either direction:
最近很忙 "busy lately," 最近要去一趟 "going soon." Also literal "nearest (in
space)": 最近的车站.

## Notes
近 for time-distance also in [[附近]] (not this level), 近来. 最近 pairs loosely
with [[将来]] / 以后 for the future sense.

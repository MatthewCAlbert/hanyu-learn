## Why this combination

中 ("middle," as in 中午) plus 介 ("to be between, to introduce," as in
介绍, "to introduce [lit. between + continue/carry on]") names a party
standing between two others to connect them: an intermediary.

Rated "semi" for 介's "to be between/connect" sense here, less
transparent outside its more familiar role in 介绍.

## Notes

房产中介 ("a real estate agency") and 中介费 ("agency/brokerage fee") both
build on this word — 中介 names the connecting party or service itself,
whether a person, company, or abstract mechanism.

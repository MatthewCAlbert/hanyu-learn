## Why this combination
饮 "to drink" (see 饮's own etymology) and 食 "to eat" combine directly
into the standard word for eating and drinking together — diet.

## Notes
饮食 anchors 饮食习惯 "dietary habits," 健康饮食 "a healthy diet" — the
formal, holistic term for what and how one eats and drinks, as opposed to
a single meal (饭) or dish (菜).

## Why this combination
例 "an example, a precedent, a rule" (例子, [[比如]]'s cousin 举例) + 如 "like,
as, such as" (see [[如果]], [[比如]]) → "as an example." Introduces one or more
instances, often in writing: 很多国家，例如中国、日本、韩国.

## Notes
例如 (formal / written) ≈ [[比如]] (neutral) ≈ [[比如说]] (spoken). Often
followed by a list and closed with 等 "etc."

## Why this combination
事 "matter, affair" combined with 件, the classifier-turned-noun for a
discrete item or case: a matter that stands out as one countable
occurrence — an event, especially one significant or newsworthy enough to
be named and tracked.

## Notes
事件 leans towards notable or disruptive occurrences (交通事件 "traffic
incident," 历史事件 "historical event"); 事情, the general word for
"matter, thing that happens," carries no such weight and covers the
mundane as easily as the significant.

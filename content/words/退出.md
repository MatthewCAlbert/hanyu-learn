## Why this combination
退 "to retreat, to withdraw, to step back" combined with the directional
complement 出 "out, to emerge": to withdraw out of something — to quit,
to exit.

## Notes
退出 takes an organisation, competition or system as its object (退出
比赛 "to withdraw from the competition," 退出登录 "to log out") — 离开,
the general word for "to leave," does not carry the same sense of
formally exiting a membership or a system one was part of.

## Why this combination

反 (here "back, in return," rather than its more familiar "opposite"
sense as in 反对) plus 映 ("to reflect light, to project," as in 放映,
"to screen a film") names light bouncing back off a surface — extended
figuratively to a situation "bouncing back" into visibility: to reflect,
or to report up the chain.

Rated "semi" for the figurative extension from optical reflection to
reporting a state of affairs.

## Notes

反映情况 ("to report/reflect a situation," e.g. to a manager or
authority) is the dominant modern use — closer to English "report" or
"flag" than to literal mirror-reflection.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 选 and 择 both independently mean "to select, to
pick" (选 survives alone in 选举 "to elect," 择 in 择日 "to pick a date").
Pairing two words that already mean nearly the same thing is one of the most
common ways Mandarin builds a two-syllable word out of classical
single-syllable ones — no new meaning is created, just a fuller, more
standard-sounding word.

## Notes
选择 works as both verb ("to choose," 选择一个) and noun ("a choice," 做出
选择, "to make a choice") without any change in form — common for
verb-derived nouns built this way in Mandarin.

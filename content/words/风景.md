## Why this combination

风 (here "scene, aspect," an extended sense beyond its literal "wind," as
in 风光 also "scenery") plus 景 ("a view, a scene," as in 景色) names the
visible natural scenery of a place.

Rated "semi" for 风's extended "scene" sense here, distinct from its
literal "wind" meaning.

## Notes

风景 pairs closely with near-synonyms 景色 and 风光 — all three name
natural scenery, with 风景 the most common in everyday speech (风景很美,
"the scenery is beautiful"; 风景区, "scenic area").

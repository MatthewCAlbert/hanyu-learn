## Why this combination

男 ("male," as in 男人) plus 士 (a polite title suffix, covered elsewhere
in this app in 女士 and 护士) names a man addressed with polite formality:
a gentleman.

Rated "semi" for 士's productive suffix role, the same pattern as in
女士 and other title words covered elsewhere in this app.

## Notes

男士 pairs with 女士 ("lady, madam") as the standard polite address forms
(男士, 女士们, "ladies and gentlemen") — 男人/女人 are the plain, less
formal everyday words for "man/woman."

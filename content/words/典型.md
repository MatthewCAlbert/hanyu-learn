## Why this combination
典 carries its "a standard, an authoritative reference" sense (covered
in this app's entry for 经典), and 型 is "a type, a mold" (also covered
elsewhere). A type held up as the standard reference example is exactly
"typical, representative."

Rated "semi" for 典's "standard/canon" sense, less common on its own
outside compounds like 经典 and 词典.

## Notes
典型 describes something that perfectly represents its category (典型
案例, "a typical/representative case") — the clearest, most standard
instance of a kind, not merely an average or common one.

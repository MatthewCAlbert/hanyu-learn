## Why this combination
资 carries its "resources, funds, means" sense (covered in this app's
entry for 资料), and 源 is "a source" (covered elsewhere in this app —
split off from 原 once that character drifted abstract). The source that
resources are drawn from is exactly what "resources" names.

## Notes
资源 covers both natural resources (水资源, "water resources") and
abstract ones (人力资源, "human resources") — a broad word for anything
drawn upon as a supply.

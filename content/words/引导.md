## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 引 ("to draw, to lead," covered elsewhere in
this app) and 导 ("to guide," as in 领导, 导演) both independently name
directing someone's path. Pairing them gives the standard word for
guidance.

## Notes
引导 implies actively steering someone's direction or development
(引导孩子, "to guide a child"; 正确引导, "to guide correctly") — more
purposeful than passive 带领 ("to lead along"), which simply goes first.

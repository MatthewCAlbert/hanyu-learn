## Why this combination
举 "to lift, to raise, to hold up [an event]" (as in 举行 "to hold [an
event]") combined with 办 "to handle, to manage, to do" (as in 办法
"method," 办公室 "office"): to lift up and manage — to hold, to organise
an event.

## Notes
举办 takes an organised event as its object (举办比赛 "to hold a
competition," 举办展览 "to hold an exhibition") — 举行, a close synonym,
is used the same way and is largely interchangeable in this sense.

## Why this combination
热 "hot, warm" + 情 "feeling, emotion" (see [[心情]], [[爱情]]) → warmth of
feeling. Of people, welcoming and eager (热情招待 "a warm welcome"); as a noun,
zeal (工作热情).

## Notes
热 "hot / keen" also in 热闹 "lively," 热门 "popular." Opposite 冷淡
"cold, indifferent."

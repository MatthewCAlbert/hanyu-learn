## Why this combination
讨 here carries an older sense, "to incur, to bring upon oneself," not
its everyday "to discuss." Combined with 厌 "to detest," 讨厌 literally
means "to incur someone's detestation" — the modern sense "annoying, to
dislike" follows directly once that older meaning of 讨 is known.

## Notes
讨厌 doubles as a verb ("to dislike," 我讨厌这个 "I hate this") and an
adjective ("annoying," 真讨厌! "so annoying!") — one of the most common
everyday words for mild-to-strong dislike.

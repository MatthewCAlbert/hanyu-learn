## Why this combination
治 "to govern, to manage" and 安 "peace, safety" combine directly:
governing so as to maintain peace and order.

## Notes
治安 anchors 社会治安 "public order/security," 治安管理 "public security
administration" — the formal register for law-and-order matters, distinct
from 安全 "safety" in a personal, everyday sense.

## Why this combination

圆 ("round, a circle," as in 圆形) plus 满 ("full," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 满足, "satisfied") names something perfectly round
and full — extended figuratively to a fully satisfactory outcome.

Rated "semi" for the figurative leap from "physically round and full" to
"a satisfying result," a metaphor of wholeness/completeness.

## Notes

圆满结束 ("to conclude successfully") and 圆满解决 ("resolved
satisfactorily") both build on this word — the image of a perfect
circle (圆), with nothing missing, standing in for a flawless outcome.

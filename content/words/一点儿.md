## Why this combination
点 "a dot, a spot" → a tiny quantity; 一 "one" frames it as "one small amount."
A small indefinite quantity of a noun (一点儿水), a small degree after an
adjective (快一点儿 "a bit faster"), or, with a negative, emphatic "not at all":
一点儿都不累.

## Notes
点儿 with erhua; the non-erhua 一点 is the same word. Distinct from
[[一会儿]] (a short *time*).

## Why this combination
半 "half" as a bound numeral before the time unit 年 "year" — the same frame as
[[半天]], 半个月, 半小时. 前半年 / 后半年 "the first / second half of the year."

## Notes
半 goes directly before a unit (半年) but needs 个 before a countable noun
(半个苹果).

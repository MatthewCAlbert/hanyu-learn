## Why this combination
[[比如]] "for example" + 说 "to say" — the 说 makes it conversational, like
English "say" in "say, someone like you." Introduces a spoken example:
你可以做很多事，比如说去散步.

## Notes
比如说 (chatty) ≈ [[比如]] (neutral) ≈ [[例如]] (formal / written). 说 as a
filler also in 你说 "you know," 我说 "listen…".

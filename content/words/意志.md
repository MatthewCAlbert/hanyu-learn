## Why this combination
意 "intention" and 志 "aspiration, will" (see 志's role in words like 志愿)
are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for willpower.

## Notes
意志 anchors 意志力 "willpower," 意志坚强 "strong-willed" — the mental
faculty of sustained determination, distinct from momentary 决心
"resolve/decision."

## Why this combination
效 "effect, efficacy, result" (as in 有效 "effective") combined with 果
"fruit, outcome, result" (as in 结果 "result, outcome"): the fruit an
effect produces — the observable result of an action or method.

## Notes
效果 measures whether something worked and how well (治疗效果 "treatment
result," 音响效果 "sound effect" in a theatrical sense); 结果, the more
neutral word for "result, outcome," does not carry the same implication
of success or failure being judged.

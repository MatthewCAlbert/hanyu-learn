## Why this combination
整 "orderly, whole" (see also 整理, 整个) combined with 齐 "even, uniform,
level" (as in 一齐 "all together, simultaneously"): orderly and even —
neat, tidy.

## Notes
整齐 describes things arranged in neat, even order (排列整齐 "arranged
neatly," 房间很整齐 "the room is very tidy") — 干净, its neighbour, is
"clean" specifically, a distinct quality from the orderliness 整齐
describes; a room can be tidy without being clean, or vice versa.

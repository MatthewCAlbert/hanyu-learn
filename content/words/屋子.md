## Why this combination
屋 "house, dwelling" (see also 房屋) combined with the light noun suffix
子 (as in 房子, 桌子): the everyday concrete noun for a room or dwelling.

## Notes
屋子 is the colloquial, spoken word for "room" in many dialects,
overlapping with 房间 (打扫屋子 "to clean the room") — 房屋, its more
formal relative, is used in writing for housing/buildings collectively
rather than a single room in speech.

## Why this combination
可 "worthy of, -able" + 怕 "to fear" → worthy of fear, fearsome. The 可 + verb
adjective frame, same as [[可爱]] (lovable), [[可能]], 可惜 (regrettable).

## Notes
可怕 = the thing inspires fear; 害怕 = the person feels fear. 怕 alone: 我怕黑
"I'm afraid of the dark," 别怕.

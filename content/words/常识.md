## Why this combination

常 ("common, usual," as in 非常, "extremely [lit. not + usual]") plus 识
("knowledge, to know," as in 知识) names knowledge that is common and
ordinary: common sense.

## Notes

生活常识 ("everyday common sense") and 缺乏常识 ("lacking common sense")
both build on this word — 常识 names basic, widely shared knowledge,
distinct from 知识, the broader word covering all knowledge, common or
specialized.

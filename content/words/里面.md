## Why this combination
里 "the inner lining, the interior" and 面 "a face, a side" combine
directly: the side that faces inward — the inside of something.

## Notes
里面 pairs with 外面 "outside" as the basic inside/outside locative
pair, following the same "direction word + 面" pattern as 前面, 后面,
上面 and 下面.

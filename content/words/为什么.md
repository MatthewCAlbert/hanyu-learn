## Why this combination
为 (*wèi*) "for, for the sake of" + 什么 "what" → "for what (purpose / reason)."
Transparent once you read 为 as the fourth-tone "for." Answered with 因为….

## Notes
为什么 asks for a cause or purpose; 怎么 can also ask "how come" but is more
about manner. 为何 is the literary contraction.

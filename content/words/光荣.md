## Why this combination
光 "light, radiance, glory" and 荣 "glory, honour" (see 荣's own
etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for
honourable glory.

## Notes
光荣 anchors 光荣传统 "a glorious tradition," 感到光荣 "to feel honoured" —
a formal, often patriotic or ceremonial register.

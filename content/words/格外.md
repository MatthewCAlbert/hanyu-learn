## Why this combination

格 ("a pattern, a standard," as in 严格, "strict [lit. strict + standard]")
plus 外 ("outside, beyond") names something beyond the usual standard:
especially, unusually.

Rated "semi" for 格's abstract "standard" sense here, one step from more
concrete uses like "grid square" or "case (grammar)."

## Notes

格外小心 ("especially careful") and 格外高兴 ("particularly happy") both
build on this word — an intensifying adverb close to 特别, emphasizing
that something exceeds the normal degree.

## Why this combination
快 "fast" + 点儿 "a bit" (comparative — "a bit more"; see [[一点儿]]) →
"(be) a bit faster." As an urging: 快点儿，要迟到了！ Non-erhua 快点 is the
same.

## Notes
adjective + 点儿 = "a bit more —": 快点儿, 慢点儿, 小心点儿. Doubled for
insistence: 快点快点.

## Why this combination
小 "small" + 组 "a group, a set, to organise." A subdivision of a larger body —
a study group, a working party, a squad: 分小组讨论, 学习小组.

## Notes
组 "group / set" also in [[组成]], [[组长]], 小组 / 大组. 小组赛 "group stage"
(in a tournament).

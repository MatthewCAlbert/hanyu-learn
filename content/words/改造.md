## Why this combination
改 "to change, to correct" (as in 改变 "to change") combined with 造 "to
make, to build" (see also 创造, 制造): to change by rebuilding — to
transform, to remodel.

## Notes
改造 takes an existing thing — a building, a system, sometimes a
person's outlook — and rebuilds it into something better or different
(改造旧城区 "to redevelop the old city district") — 改变, its close
relative, is the more general word for "to change" and does not carry
改造's implication of active rebuilding.

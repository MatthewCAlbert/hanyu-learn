## Why this combination
样 "appearance, shape, kind" (see [[一样]], [[这样]]) + the toneless noun suffix
子 (see 孩子) → the everyday noun for how something looks or seems:
这个样子 "like this," 看样子要下雨 "looks like rain."

## Notes
装样子 "put on an act." 样 alone in 样品 "sample," 花样 "variety, trick."

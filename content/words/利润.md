## Why this combination
利 "profit, benefit" is transparent; 润 here draws on its figurative
"to enrich, to embellish" sense (see 润's own etymology — moisture that
nourishes) rather than its literal "moist" meaning — profit pictured as
the enriching moisture a business draws from its activity.

## Notes
利润 is the standard financial term (利润率 "profit margin," 净利润 "net
profit") — more technical than everyday 好处 "benefit, advantage."

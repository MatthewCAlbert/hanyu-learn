## Why this combination
团 "a group, to gather, to unite" (as in 团体 "organisation, group")
combined with 结 "to tie, to knot, to bind" (see also 结合, 结婚): to
gather and bind together — to unite, unity.

## Notes
团结 describes people or groups bound together in solidarity (团结
一致 "united as one," 团结起来 "unite!") — 联合, its neighbour, names a
more formal, often organisational alliance, while 团结 stresses the
emotional bond of solidarity itself.

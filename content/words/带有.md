## Why this combination
带 "to carry, to bring along" and 有 "to have" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for possessing a trait or element.

## Notes
带有 always names an accompanying quality: 带有恶意 "carrying malicious
intent," 带有讽刺意味 "carrying a note of irony" — a formal alternative to
带着 for describing an inherent characteristic.

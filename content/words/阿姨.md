## Why this combination

阿 (a familiar/affectionate prefix carrying no independent meaning of its
own, also seen in 阿公, "grandpa") plus 姨 ("mother's sister," an aunt on
the maternal side) names a maternal aunt, extended as a polite address
for any older woman.

Rated "semi" for 阿's role as a bare kinship-term prefix rather than a
meaning-bearing element.

## Notes

阿姨 is also the standard polite/neutral word for a domestic helper or
nanny (家政阿姨) — a job title, not necessarily a blood relation, which
can surprise learners who only know the "aunt" sense first.

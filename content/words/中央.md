## Why this combination
中 and 央 are near-synonyms, both meaning "middle, centre" — paired for
emphasis into the standard word for the geometric or organisational
centre of something.

## Notes
中央 spans the literal (广场中央 "the centre of the square") and the
political (中央政府 "central government," 党中央 "the Party's central
leadership") — a country's or organisation's seat of authority, by
metaphor from the physical centre.

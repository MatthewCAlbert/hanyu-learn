## Why this combination
争 "to contend, to compete for, to strive" (as in 战争 "war," literally
"battle-contention") combined with 取 "to take, to obtain" (see also
采取): to strive and obtain — to fight for something through effort.

## Notes
争取 takes a goal one is actively working towards as its object (争取
机会 "to strive for an opportunity," 争取时间 "to race against time") —
it also takes a clause meaning "to try one's best to" (争取按时完成
"to try to finish on time").

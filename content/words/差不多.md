## Why this combination
差 "to fall short, to differ" + 不多 "not by much" → "the gap is small." Used
for approximation (差不多十点 "nearly ten"), similarity (两个差不多 "much the
same"), and a resigned "that'll do" (差不多就行了).

## Notes
差 is *chà* here (to differ / be short). The famous essay 《差不多先生传》
satirises 差不多 as a national habit of near-enough thinking.

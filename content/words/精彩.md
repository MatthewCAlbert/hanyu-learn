## Why this combination
精 "refined, the finest essence [of something]" (as in 精神 "spirit,"
originally "refined vital essence") combined with 彩 "splendour, vivid
colour" (as in 彩色 "colourful"): the finest, most vivid part on
display — wonderful, brilliant.

## Notes
精彩 praises a performance, moment or piece of writing for being
outstanding (精彩的表演 "a brilliant performance") — it is not used to
describe a person's character or a plain physical object the way 好 or
美丽 might be.

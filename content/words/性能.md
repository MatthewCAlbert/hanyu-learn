## Why this combination
性 (here "property, nature," as in 特性) plus 能 "ability, capability"
gives exactly "the functional properties (of a thing)" — the standard
technical word for how well something performs its function.

## Notes
性能 anchors 性能优良 "excellent performance," 汽车性能 "car performance"
— always about a product or system's functional capability, not a
person's ability (that would be 能力).

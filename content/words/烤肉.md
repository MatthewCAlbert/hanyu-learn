## Why this combination
烤 "to roast, to grill" acting on 肉 "meat" gives exactly "roasted meat"
— a straightforward, fully compositional food word.

## Notes
烤肉 anchors 烤肉店 "barbecue restaurant," 韩式烤肉 "Korean barbecue" — the
standard word for grilled/roasted meat dishes, whether at a restaurant or
cooked at home.

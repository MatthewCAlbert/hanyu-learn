## Why this combination

穿 ("to wear, to put on [clothing]," as in 穿衣服) plus the directional
complement 上 ("on, onto") marks the action's completion: to get
[clothing] on.

## Notes

穿上外套 ("to put on a coat") pairs 穿上 with a garment; 脱下 ("to take
off," lit. "shed + down") is its direct opposite, built the same way
with 下 "down" instead of 上.

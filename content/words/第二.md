## Why this combination
第 is a prefix that turns a cardinal number into an ordinal: 第一 first, 第二
second, 第三 third. Without 第, 二 is just the quantity "two." The system is
perfectly regular.

## Notes
第二天 "the next day" (within a narrative) contrasts with 明天 "tomorrow"
(relative to now).

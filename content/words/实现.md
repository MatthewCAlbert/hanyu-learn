## Why this combination
实 "real, actual, to make real" + 现 "to appear, to become manifest" (see
[[现在]], [[发现]]) → to make something real, to bring it into being.
实现梦想 "make a dream come true," 目标实现了.

## Notes
实 "real / solid" also in [[实在]], [[实际]], [[老实]]. 实现 takes an aim,
plan or wish as its object.

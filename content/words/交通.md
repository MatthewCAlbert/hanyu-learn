## Why this combination
交 "to cross, to intersect, to exchange" + 通 "to go through, to be connected"
→ routes crossing and running through, and the movement along them. 交通规则
"traffic rules," 公共交通 "public transport," 交通不便 "poorly connected."

## Notes
Classical 交通 also meant "to be in collusion / secret contact" — that sense is
now obsolete. 交 also in [[交给]], [[交朋友]].

## Why this combination
区, here in its "to divide, to demarcate" sense (see also 地区) combined
with 别 "to distinguish, other, separate" (as in 别人 "other people"): to
divide and set apart — to distinguish, or the resulting difference.

## Notes
区别 works as noun ("有区别" "there's a difference") and verb ("区别对待"
"to treat differently/distinguish in treatment") — 差别 and 差异, close
synonyms, name the same idea of "difference" with slightly different
collocational habits but largely overlapping meaning.

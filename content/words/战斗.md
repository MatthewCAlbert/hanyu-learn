## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 战 ("battle, to make war," covered in this app's
entry for 战争) and 斗 (here in its *dòu* "to fight" sense, covered in
this app's hanzi entry — a sense merged into 斗's shape purely by
simplification, from the originally unrelated 鬥). Two words for combat
combine into the standard noun/verb for engaging in fighting.

## Notes
战斗 names a single engagement (a battle or skirmish), smaller in scale
than 战争 (a whole war) — the same relationship as English "battle" versus
"war."

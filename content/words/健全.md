## Why this combination
健 "healthy, strong" and 全 "complete, whole" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for something strong precisely because nothing is
missing from it.

## Notes
健全 modifies both people (身心健全 "sound in body and mind") and systems
(健全的制度 "a well-developed system"), and as a verb means to make
something complete: 健全法律体系 "to perfect the legal system."

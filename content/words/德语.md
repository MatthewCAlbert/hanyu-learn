## Why this combination
德 clipped from [[德国]] + 语 "spoken language." The same mould as 法语, 日语 and
[[英文]]. 德 is not "virtue."

## Notes
Parallel [[德文]] for the written language. 德语 leans spoken; 德文 leans written /
materials.

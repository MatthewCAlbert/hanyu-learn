## Why this combination
杀 "to kill" acting on the object 毒 "poison, virus" gives exactly "to
kill germs/viruses" — splittable, as in 杀了毒, extended from medical
disinfection to computer security by the shared metaphor of "virus."

## Notes
杀毒 anchors 杀毒软件 "antivirus software," 给电脑杀毒 "to scan a computer
for viruses" — the computing sense is now at least as common as the
original medical/household disinfection sense.

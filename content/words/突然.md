## Why this combination
突 "sudden" already carries the full meaning (recall its etymology: a
dog bursting abruptly from its den); 然 is the same classical
manner-suffix ("in that way") seen in 当然 and 显然 elsewhere in this
dataset, turning 突 into a full adverb/adjective rather than adding new
content of its own.

## Notes
突然 joins 当然, 显然, 果然 and 虽然 as another member of this
productive 然-suffix family — recognising the pattern makes each new
然-word far easier to absorb.

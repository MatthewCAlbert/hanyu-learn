## Why this combination
风, here evoking sudden, unpredictable change (as weather driven by
wind can turn without warning) combined with 险 "steep, treacherous,
dangerous" (see also 危险, 保险): the danger of a sudden, unpredictable
turn — risk, especially of a financial or strategic kind.

## Notes
风险 names a calculable, often financial or strategic danger (投资风险
"investment risk," 风险很高 "the risk is high") — 危险, its broader
relative, covers physical danger generally and is not limited to this
calculated, probabilistic sense.

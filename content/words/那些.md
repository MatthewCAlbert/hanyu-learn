## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + the vague-plural 些. Completes the set with [[这些]]
(these) and 哪些 (which ones). 些 supplies the plurality, so no measure word
follows.

## Notes
Distance pair: 这 near the speaker, 那 away from the speaker — the same split as
English this/that.

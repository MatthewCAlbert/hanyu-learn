## Why this combination
价 "price, cost" (see also 价值) combined with 格 "a measure, a standard,
a fixed pattern" (as in 格式 "format"): price fixed to a standard measure
— the formal word for a set price.

## Notes
价格 names the set price of goods in commerce or economics (价格上涨
"prices rise") — 价钱, a near-synonym, is the everyday, more colloquial
word for the price one actually pays or haggles over in a shop.

## Why this combination
西 "West, Western" (see [[西方]], [[西餐]]) + 医 "medicine, healing, doctor"
(see [[医生]], [[中医]]) → biomedicine as practised in the West, and a doctor
of it. Coined as the counterpart to [[中医]] when the two systems met.

## Notes
西医 covers the discipline and the practitioner; its drugs are 西药. 中西医
结合 "combining Chinese and Western medicine."

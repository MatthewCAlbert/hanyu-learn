## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 思 ("to think," as in 思想, 意思) and 考 ("to
examine closely," covered in this app's entry for 考虑) both independently
name mental deliberation. Pairing them gives the standard word for
sustained, careful reflection.

## Notes
思考 implies sustained, effortful reasoning about a problem — more
deliberate and prolonged than 想 (the everyday, general "to think"),
which covers any mental activity from a passing thought to deep
reflection.

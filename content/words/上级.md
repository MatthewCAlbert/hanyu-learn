## Why this combination
上 "up, above" and 级 "level, rank" (the same 级 as in 等级) combine
directly: the rank above one's own — one's superiors.

## Notes
上级 anchors 上级领导 "higher-level leadership," 向上级汇报 "to report to
one's superiors" — the standard bureaucratic/organizational term,
opposite of 下级 "subordinates."

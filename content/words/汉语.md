## Why this combination
汉 "the Han (people / ethnicity)" + 语 "spoken language" → the language of the
Han. The 汉 framing is ethnolinguistic: it names one of China's languages,
distinguishing it from 藏语 (Tibetan), 蒙语 (Mongolian) and the rest.

## Notes
汉语 the spoken Han language · [[中文]] the written / national language ·
普通话 the standard spoken form. 汉语 is the usual term in language teaching
(对外汉语).

## Why this combination
想 "to think" + directional 起, here "up into awareness" — something surfacing
from memory: 突然想起 "suddenly remembered," 想起一件事. Often with 来:
想起来了 "it's come back to me."

## Notes
想起 = a memory resurfacing (something you knew but had lost). Contrast [[想到]]
(occur to; anticipate) and 记得 (have in memory, ongoing).

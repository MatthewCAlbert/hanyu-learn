## Why this combination

汇 ("to exchange [currency]," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
外汇/汇报) plus 率 ("a rate," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
效率) names the rate at which currencies exchange: the exchange rate.

## Notes

汇率变动 ("exchange rate fluctuation") and 固定汇率 ("a fixed exchange
rate") both build on this word — a core finance term sharing its 汇
root with 外汇 and its 率 root with 效率, both covered elsewhere in this
app.

## Why this combination
电子 "electronic" plus 版 "edition" gives exactly "the electronic
edition" — the standard word for a digital format of a publication,
opposite of 纸质版 "print/paper edition."

## Notes
电子版 anchors 电子版教材 "digital textbook edition," 下载电子版 "to
download the e-version" — the everyday term distinguishing digital from
physical publication formats.

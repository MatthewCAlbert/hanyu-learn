## Why this combination
药 ("medicine") plus 物 (the generic "thing, substance" suffix seen in
动物, 植物, 食物) names medicine as a category of substance — the formal,
category-level word for medication.

## Notes
药物 is the formal/technical register (药物治疗, "drug therapy"; 药物
反应, "a drug reaction") — for medicine bought at a pharmacy or taken
casually, plain 药 is the everyday word.

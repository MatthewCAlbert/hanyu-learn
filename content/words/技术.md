## Why this combination
技 and 术 both independently mean "skill, technique" — 技 with an
emphasis on manual dexterity (recall its etymology: a hand skilled
enough to work bamboo), 术 on method more generally. Paired for a
broader, more formal word.

## Notes
技术 covers both concrete craft skill and, in the modern era, the whole
domain of "technology" — 高科技 "high technology," 技术人员 "technical
staff" — the older "skilled craft" sense scaling up to the industrial
and digital age.

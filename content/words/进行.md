## Why this combination
进 "to advance" + 行 (*xíng*) "to go, to proceed, to act" — a synonym pair for
"proceed with." A formal light verb that carries a disyllabic noun-verb as its
object: 进行讨论, 进行检查, 进行到一半.

## Notes
进行 is characteristic of written / official register — it lets a two-syllable
verb sit in an object slot. In speech people just use the plain verb.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 获 ("to obtain, to catch," covered elsewhere in
this app as a character where simplification merged two distinct
traditional characters, 獲 "to catch game" and 穫 "to harvest crops,"
into one shape) and 取 ("to take, to fetch," as in 取得) both
independently name acquiring something. Pairing them gives the formal
word for obtaining.

## Notes

获取 is the written/formal register (获取信息, "to obtain information";
获取资格, "to obtain qualification") — everyday casual acquiring uses
plain 得到 or 拿到 instead.

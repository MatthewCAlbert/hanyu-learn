## Why this combination
运 "to move, to transport" and 行 "to go, to move" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for sustained, ongoing operation or
movement.

## Notes
运行 spans astronomy (行星运行 "planetary motion"), machinery (系统运行
正常 "the system is running normally"), and transit schedules (列车正常
运行 "trains running on schedule") — always ongoing, regular operation
rather than a one-off action.

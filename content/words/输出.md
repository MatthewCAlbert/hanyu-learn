## Why this combination
输 (here "to transport, to convey," as in 运输) plus the directional
complement 出 "out" gives exactly "to convey out" — the standard word for
sending something outward, whether goods or data.

## Notes
输出 spans economics (输出商品 "to export goods") and computing (数据输出
"data output") — the mirror of 输入 "to import/input," both built on the
same 输 + directional-complement pattern.

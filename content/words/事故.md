## Why this combination
事 "matter, affair" combined with 故 "a reason, a cause, an [untoward]
incident" (as in 故意 "on purpose," 事故 contrasting with intent): an
unintended, harmful occurrence — an accident.

## Notes
事故 specifically names an unfortunate, often dangerous mishap
(交通事故 "traffic accident," 出事故 "for an accident to happen") — 事情,
the general word for "matter, thing that happens," carries no such
negative or accidental implication.

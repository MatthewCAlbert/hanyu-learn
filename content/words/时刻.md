## Why this combination
时 "time" combined with 刻 "a carved mark [on a water-clock], a quarter
of an hour" (as in 立刻 "immediately," literally "at once carved/marked"):
time marked down to a precise point — a moment, and, doubled in force as
an adverb, "at every moment, constantly."

## Notes
时刻 names a specific point in time, often a significant one (关键时刻
"a critical moment") and, as an adverb, means "constantly, at all times"
(时刻准备着 "always ready") — 时候, the everyday word for "time, moment,"
carries neither this precision nor the adverbial "constantly" sense.

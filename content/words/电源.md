## Why this combination

电 ("electric," as in 电脑) plus 源 ("a source," as in 来源) names the
source that supplies electricity: a power supply.

## Notes

打开/关闭电源 ("to turn the power on/off") is the standard frame — 电源
names the supply itself, distinct from 电池 ("a battery," one particular
kind of power source).

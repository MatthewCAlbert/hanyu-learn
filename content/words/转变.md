## Why this combination
转 "to turn, to rotate, to shift direction" (as in 转身 "to turn around")
combined with 变 "to change" (see also 变化): to turn and change — to
transform, a shift.

## Notes
转变 describes a fundamental shift in direction, attitude or state
(观念转变 "a shift in thinking," 态度转变 "a change in attitude") — 变化,
the general word for "change," need not carry 转变's sense of turning
from one clear state or direction to another.

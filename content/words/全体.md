## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" (see [[全部]], [[全国]]) + 体 "body" (see [[身体]], [[体育]])
→ the whole body of members: 全体员工 "all staff," 全体起立 "everyone stand."

## Notes
全体 = every member of a group (people); [[全部]] = the whole of something
(often things / quantity); 整体 = a thing viewed as an undivided whole.

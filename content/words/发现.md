## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to bring to light" + 现 "to appear, to become visible" (see
[[现在]]) → to bring something into view that was there unseen. Both a mental
noticing (我发现他不在) and a scientific discovery (新发现).

## Notes
发 is highly polysemous — "send," "emit," "develop," "get" — and carries little
weight here beyond "cause to". 现 supplies the "come to light" core.

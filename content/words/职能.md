## Why this combination
职 "post, duty" plus 能 "ability, capacity" gives exactly "the capacity
(built into) a post" — the standard word for a role's designated
function.

## Notes
职能 anchors 政府职能 "government functions," 职能部门 "functional
department" — always about an institution's or role's defined
responsibilities, distinct from 功能, which is used more for objects and
systems.

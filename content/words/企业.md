## Why this combination
企 carries its "to plan, to undertake, to strain toward a goal" sense
(covered elsewhere in this app — a person standing on tiptoe, reaching
for something not yet within reach), and 业 is "an undertaking, an
occupation" (as in 职业, 作业). An ambitious undertaking is exactly a
business enterprise.

Rated "semi" for 企's figurative "reaching toward a goal" sense, distinct
from its more concrete role in 企图 ("to attempt/scheme").

## Notes
企业 names the organization/company itself (国有企业, "a state-owned
enterprise") — for the general activity of doing business, 商业 or 生意
is the more natural word.

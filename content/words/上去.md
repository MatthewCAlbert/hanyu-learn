## Why this combination
上 "up, onto" combined with the directional complement 去 "to go, away
from the speaker": to move upward and away — the standard directional
verb complement for going up, mirroring 上来 "to come up" [towards the
speaker].

## Notes
上去 marks motion away from the speaker's position (爬上去 "to climb up
[and away]," 走上去 "to walk up to [it]") — 上来, its mirror pair, marks
the same upward motion but coming towards the speaker instead.

## Why this combination

大 ("big") plus 陆 ("land, dry ground," as in 陆地) names a large
continuous landmass — a continent, or (used on its own) the Chinese
mainland as distinct from its islands.

## Notes

中国大陆 specifically contrasts with 台湾, 香港 and 澳门 in cross-strait and
regional usage; 大陆 alone, in a geography context, means "continent"
(七大洲, "the seven continents," pairs with 大陆 loosely in that sense).

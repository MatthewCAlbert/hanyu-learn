## Why this combination
电 "electric" plus 饭锅 "rice pot" (饭 "cooked rice/meal" + 锅 "pot")
gives exactly "an electric rice pot" — a straightforward, compositional
appliance name.

## Notes
电饭锅 anchors 用电饭锅煮饭 "to cook rice in a rice cooker" — the
standard modern kitchen appliance that replaced cooking rice over an open
flame.

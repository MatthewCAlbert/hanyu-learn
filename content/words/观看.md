## Why this combination
观 "to watch, to view" (see also 观察, 观众) combined with 看 "to look,
to see": to watch by looking — a formal, fuller verb for "to watch."

## Notes
观看 is used for watching an event, performance or broadcast in a
formal register (观看比赛 "to watch a match," 观看表演 "to watch a
performance") — 看, alone, is the plain everyday verb used in casual
speech.

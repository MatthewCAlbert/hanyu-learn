## Why this combination
大 "big" and 厅 "hall" (see 厅's own etymology) combine directly: a
large hall.

## Notes
大厅 anchors 机场大厅 "airport terminal hall," 酒店大厅 "hotel lobby" — a
large, open public reception space, distinct from 客厅, the private
living room of a home.

## Why this combination
解, here "to loosen, to untie, to separate" (see also 解决 "to
resolve," literally "to untie a solution") combined with the
resultative complement 开 "open, apart": to untie apart — to undo, and,
figuratively, to work out a puzzle.

## Notes
解开 covers physically untying something (解开绳子 "to untie a rope,"
解开扣子 "to undo a button") and figuratively resolving confusion or a
mystery (解开谜团 "to solve a mystery") — the resultative 开 marks that
the untying/solving was completed successfully.

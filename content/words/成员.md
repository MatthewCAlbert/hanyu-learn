## Why this combination
成 "to form, to become [part of]" combined with 员 "a member, a person
holding a role" (as in 人员 "personnel," 演员 "actor"): one who has
become part of a group — a member.

## Notes
成员 names a member of any organised group — a family, a team, an
organisation, a committee (家庭成员 "family member") — and is more formal
than saying 人 "person" alone when membership itself is the point.

## Why this combination
电视 "television" (see its own entry) combined with 台 "a platform, a
stand, a station" (as in 舞台 "stage," 阳台 "balcony"): the station
[broadcasting] television — a TV station.

## Notes
电视台 names the broadcasting organisation itself (中央电视台 "China
Central Television," CCTV) — 电视机, its neighbour, is the physical
television set, and 电视 alone can mean either the medium or, casually,
the set.

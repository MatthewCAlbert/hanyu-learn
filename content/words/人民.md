## Why this combination
人 "person" and 民 "the common people, the masses" are near-synonyms,
paired into the collective political noun for a nation's population as a
body — the People, as opposed to the government or the state.

## Notes
人民 carries a formal, political register (人民代表 "people's
representative," 人民币 "the people's currency," i.e. RMB) — 老百姓,
informal and unofficial, is the everyday word for "ordinary people."

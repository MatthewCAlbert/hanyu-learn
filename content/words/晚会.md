## Why this combination
晚 "evening" + 会 "a gathering, a meeting" (see [[开会]], [[音乐会]]) → an
evening social event, usually with a programme of performances:
新年晚会, 春节联欢晚会 "the Spring Festival Gala."

## Notes
会 "gathering / event" also in [[开会]], [[音乐会]], 运动会, 舞会 "dance."
晚会 vs. [[聚会]] (not this level — an informal get-together).

## Why this combination
无 ("to lack, to be without") takes 论 ("discussion, debate," as in 讨论,
理论) as its object: there being no need for discussion of which case
applies is exactly what "no matter" or "regardless" means — the outcome
holds whichever way you'd argue it.

## Notes
无论 sets up a concessive clause and is answered later by 都/也, the same
pattern as 不管: 无论多难，我们都要试试 ("no matter how hard, we have to try") —
无论 is simply the more formal/written register of 不管.

## Why this combination
联 "to link, to connect" and 想 "to think" combine into exactly "to think
by connection" — the standard word for one idea triggering another.

## Notes
联想 works as verb ("这让我联想到…" "this makes me think of…") and noun
("产生联想" "to trigger associations") — also, as a proper noun, the name
of the computer company Lenovo (联想集团), unrelated to this HSK sense.

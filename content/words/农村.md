## Why this combination
农 "agriculture, farming" (see also 农民, 农业) combined with 村
"village" (as in 乡村 "countryside"): a farming village — rural area,
countryside.

## Notes
农村 names the countryside as a social and economic category, contrasted
with 城市 "city" (农村人口 "rural population," 农村地区 "rural areas") —
乡下, its more colloquial relative, is the everyday spoken word for "the
countryside" without 农村's more formal, statistical register.

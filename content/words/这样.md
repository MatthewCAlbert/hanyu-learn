## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + 样 "manner, kind, appearance" (see [[一样]]) → "in this
manner." The set is 这样 / 那样 / 怎样 (this way / that way / how). Refers back to
a described situation: 这样不行 "this won't do."

## Notes
就这样 "that's it / leave it at that." The northern colloquial contraction is
这么样 or just 这么.

## Why this combination
月 "month, moon" + 份 "a portion, a share, a period" (see [[部分]], 年份,
[[身份]]) → a named month of the year as a slice of it: 三月份, 这个月份.

## Notes
份 marks a named division: 年份 "(a specific) year," 月份 "(a specific) month."
For counting a *span*, use bare 月: 三个月 "three months" (not 三个月份).

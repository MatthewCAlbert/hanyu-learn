## Why this combination
加 "to add" acting on the object 速 "speed" gives exactly "to add speed"
— splittable, as in 加了速 "sped up."

## Notes
加速 covers literal acceleration (汽车加速 "the car accelerates") and
figurative speeding-up of a process (加速发展 "to accelerate development")
— the opposite of 减速 "to decelerate."

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 符 ("a talisman, a symbol," as in 符合, "to
match/tally with") and 号 ("a sign, a number," as in 号码) both
independently name a marked representation. Pairing them gives the
standard word for a symbol or sign.

## Notes

标点符号 ("punctuation marks") and 数学符号 ("mathematical symbols") both
build on this word — 符号 names any abstract mark that stands for
something else, from punctuation to musical notation.

## Why this combination
地 "ground, floor" + locative 上 "on." Covers both the earth outdoors and the
floor of a room: 坐在地上 "sit on the floor," 地上有雪 "there's snow on the
ground."

## Notes
Contrasts with 地下 "underground / under the floor." 地 is *dì* here (toned).

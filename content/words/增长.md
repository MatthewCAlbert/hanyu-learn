## Why this combination
增 "to increase, to add to" (see also 增加) combined with 长 "to grow,
to increase [in size]" (see also 成长): to increase through growth — to
grow, especially in an ongoing, developmental sense.

## Notes
增长 describes ongoing growth over time, often economic (经济增长
"economic growth," 增长速度 "growth rate") — 增加, its close relative, is
used more for a specific, discrete increase in quantity rather than a
sustained growth trend.

## Why this combination
为 (here "to become") plus 难 "difficult" gives exactly "to become
difficult (for someone)" — used both for one's own awkward predicament
and for deliberately putting someone else in one.

## Notes
为难 works reflexively ("我很为难" "I'm in an awkward spot") and
transitively ("别为难他" "don't make it hard for him") — context decides
whether the difficulty is one's own or imposed on another.

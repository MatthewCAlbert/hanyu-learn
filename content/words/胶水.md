## Why this combination
胶 "glue, adhesive" plus 水 (here "liquid," a common suffix for liquid
substances, as in 香水 "perfume") gives exactly "adhesive liquid" — the
everyday word for liquid glue.

## Notes
胶水 anchors 一瓶胶水 "a bottle of glue," 用胶水粘 "to stick with glue" —
the everyday household/office glue, distinct from 胶带 "adhesive tape."

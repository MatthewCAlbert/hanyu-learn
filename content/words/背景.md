## Why this combination
背 ("back, behind") plus 景 ("scenery, a view," as in 风景) names the
scenery lying behind the main subject: the background.

Rated "semi" for 景's specific "scenery" sense, one branch of a character
that also covers "circumstance" more broadly.

## Notes
背景 covers the literal backdrop (照片背景, "the photo's background"),
context (历史背景, "historical background"), and, colloquially, a
person's powerful connections (他有背景, "he has powerful backing/
connections").

## Why this combination
邮 "postal, mail" (see also 邮件, 邮箱) combined with 票 "a ticket, a
note, a certificate" (as in 车票 "bus ticket," 机票 "plane ticket"): the
ticket for mail — a postage stamp.

## Notes
邮票 names the stamp affixed to mail to prove postage paid (贴邮票 "to
stick on a stamp," 集邮 "stamp collecting," literally "to gather
stamps") — 票, the general word for "ticket," is productive across many
compounds for tickets and certificates of many kinds.

## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to issue" (see also 发展, 发出) combined with 动 "to
move, to act" (see also 主动, 自动): to send out into motion — to start
up, to launch, to mobilise.

## Notes
发动 covers starting a machine (发动汽车 "to start a car"), launching a
war or campaign (发动战争 "to launch a war"), and mobilising people
(发动群众 "to mobilise the masses") — a broad "to set something large
into motion" sense across mechanical, military and social contexts.

## Why this combination

网 ("net, network," as in 互联网, "the internet") plus 址 ("an address,
a site," as in 地址) names the network address of a website: a URL.

## Notes

输入网址 ("to enter a URL") and 网址链接 ("a website link") both build on
this word — shares its 址 "address" root with 地址, the general word
for a physical address.

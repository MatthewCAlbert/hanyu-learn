## Why this combination
广 "wide, broad" (see [[广告]]) + 场 (*chǎng*) "an open ground, an arena"
(see [[机场]], [[商场]], [[球场]]) → a large open space in a city. 天安门广场
"Tiananmen Square," 广场舞 "public-square group dancing."

## Notes
场 is *chǎng* here. Modern shopping developments also take the name:
万达广场.

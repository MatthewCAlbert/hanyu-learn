## Why this combination
明 in time words means "the next" — 明年 next year, 明早 tomorrow morning — from
its sense "the coming light, dawn." So 明天 is "the next day," tomorrow.

## Notes
Sits between 昨天 and 后天 on the day row; see [[今天]] for the full grid. Don't
read 明 here as "bright."

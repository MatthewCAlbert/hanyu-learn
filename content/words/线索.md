## Why this combination
线 "thread, line" and 索 "cord, rope" (see 索's own etymology — twisted
plant fibre made into rope) are near-synonyms combined into the vivid
metaphor of a single thread that, if followed, leads somewhere.

## Notes
线索 covers criminal investigation (提供线索 "to provide a lead") and
narrative structure (故事线索 "the story's thread/plotline") — both senses
built on "the strand that, once pulled, unravels the whole picture."

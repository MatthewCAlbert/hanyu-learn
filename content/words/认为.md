## Why this combination
认 "to recognise, to take as" + 为 (*wéi*) "to be, as" — "to take (it) as being…".
States a considered opinion or judgement: 我认为他是对的.

## Notes
Firmer and more formal than 觉得 (feel that) and 以为 (mistakenly think). 为 is
*wéi* here. Same "take as" 认 in [[认真]], 认错.

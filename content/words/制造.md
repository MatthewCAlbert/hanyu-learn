## Why this combination
制 "to fashion, to cut out [a pattern], to control" (as in 制作 "to
produce") combined with 造 "to make, to build" (see also 创造): to
fashion and build — to manufacture.

## Notes
制造 is used for industrial and mass production (制造业 "manufacturing
industry," 制造飞机 "to manufacture aircraft") and, figuratively, for
deliberately bringing about a situation (制造麻烦 "to make/cause
trouble") — 做, the general "to make/do," carries neither the industrial
scale nor this figurative sense.

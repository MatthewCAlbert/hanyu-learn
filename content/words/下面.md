## Why this combination
下 "below, under" combined with the locative suffix 面 — completing the
same "direction word + 面" set as 上面, 里面, 后面 and 外面 — naming the
space below something.

## Notes
下面 covers spatial "below" (桌子下面 "under the table") and, in speech
and writing, "next, what follows" (下面我们来看 "next, let's look at") —
下边 is a colloquial synonym limited to the spatial sense.

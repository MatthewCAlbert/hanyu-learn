## Why this combination
火 "fire" and 灾 "disaster" (see 灾's own etymology) combine directly into
"a fire disaster" — as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
火灾 names a fire serious enough to count as a disaster (火灾现场 "the
scene of a fire," 森林火灾 "forest fire") — distinguishing it from a small,
contained 火 like a campfire or stovetop flame.

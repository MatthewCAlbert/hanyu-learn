## Why this combination
机 clipped from 飞机 "aeroplane" + 场 "open ground, field, arena" (the same 场
as 广场, 商场, 操场). An "aeroplane field."

## Notes
去机场 "go to the airport." The larger 航空港 / 空港 is the formal term.
飞机场 is the fuller colloquial form.

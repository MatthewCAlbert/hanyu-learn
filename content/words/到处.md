## Why this combination
到 "to reach, to (every)" + 处 "place, spot" → "to every place one goes" =
everywhere. 到处都是人 "people everywhere," 到处找 "look all over."

## Notes
处 is *chù* here (place), also in [[好处]], [[坏处]], 住处. Near 处处 "in every
respect" and 各处 "various places."

## Why this combination
酸, 甜, 苦, 辣 name four basic tastes; strung together as a set idiom,
they stand for the full range of life's experiences — good and bad,
pleasant and painful — the same taste-for-experience metaphor found
across many languages, but fixed here as a specific four-character
idiom.

## Notes
酸甜苦辣 anchors 人生的酸甜苦辣 "the ups and downs of life," 尝遍酸甜苦辣
"to have tasted every flavor of life" — always figurative in this
four-character form, rarely describing actual food flavors together.

## Why this combination
眼 ("eye") plus 泪 ("a tear," covered elsewhere in this app — water
straight from the eye) names tears by naming both the source and the
substance, reinforcing 泪's already-transparent meaning.

## Notes
流眼泪 ("to shed tears") and 眼泪汪汪 ("eyes brimming with tears") are
common everyday phrases — 泪 alone is more literary/compound-only, while
眼泪 is the natural free-standing noun in speech.

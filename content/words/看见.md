## Why this combination
看 "to look" + resultative 见 "to perceive, to register" — the looking that
landed. 见 is the older perception complement; [[看到]] with 到 "reach" is the
newer near-synonym. Both contrast with bare 看 "look at."

## Notes
听见 (hear) is the same pattern for sound. 见 also stands alone as a verb "to
meet": 见面, 再见.

## Why this combination
服务 "to serve, service" (服 "to submit, to serve," 务 "affair, duty")
combined with 员 "a member of staff" (see also 成员, 员工): a staff
member who serves — a waiter, an attendant.

## Notes
服务员 names frontline service staff in restaurants, hotels and shops
(叫服务员 "to call the waiter over") — 服务, alone, is the more general
noun/verb "service, to serve," the root this occupational title is
built on.

## Why this combination
外 "foreign, external" (as in [[外国]], 外交) + 语 "language, speech." A language
from outside — any that is not one's own.

## Notes
外语 stresses spoken competence; 外文 leans written ("foreign-language
materials"). 学外语 "study a foreign language."

## Why this combination
时 "time" + 间 "interval, gap, space between." The compound is time seen as an
extent you can have, spend, or run out of — 有时间, 花时间, 没有时间.

## Notes
Pairs with [[时候]]: 时间 is the measurable resource, 时候 is the specific
moment. "What time is it" is 几点, not either of these.

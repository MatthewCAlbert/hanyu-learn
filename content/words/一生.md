## Why this combination
一 "one, a whole" + 生 "life, lifetime" → "one (entire) life," from birth to
death: 一生的心血 "a lifetime's effort," 幸福的一生.

## Notes
一生 (a lifetime as a span) vs. [[生活]] (life as lived, day to day) vs. 命
(life vs. death). 一辈子 is the colloquial synonym.

## Why this combination
[[银行]] "bank" + 卡 "card" (a phonetic borrowing of English "card"; see
[[信用卡]]) → a card issued by a bank for withdrawing cash and making payments.
Parse 银行 + 卡. 刷银行卡, 办一张银行卡.

## Notes
银行卡 is the general term, covering 借记卡 "debit card" and [[信用卡]] "credit
card." 卡 (*kǎ*) also in 公交卡, 会员卡, [[菜单]]'s cousin 卡片.

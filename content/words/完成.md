## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 完 "to finish" + 成 "to accomplish, to bring about." Carrying a
task through to its end: 完成作业, 完成任务.

## Notes
完成 = bring a defined task to completion; 结束 = (something) comes to an end;
完 as a bare complement = "used up / done" (吃完了).

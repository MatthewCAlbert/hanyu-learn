## Why this combination
复 "again, to return, to repeat" (see [[重复]], [[回复]]) + 习 "to practise, to
study" (see [[学习]], [[练习]], [[实习]]) → to go over material already learned:
复习功课, 考前复习.

## Notes
The study triad: 预习 "prepare a lesson beforehand" → 学习 "learn it" → 复习
"go back over it." Contrast [[练习]] "drill a skill."

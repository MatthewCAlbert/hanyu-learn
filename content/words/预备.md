## Why this combination
预 "in advance, beforehand" and 备 "to prepare" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for getting something ready ahead of
time.

## Notes
预备 anchors 预备铃 "the warning bell (before class starts)," 预备党员
"probationary Party member" — close to 准备, though 预备 leans slightly
more formal and often names a preparatory stage before the main event.

## Why this combination

打 ("to do, to perform [an action]," a light verb as in 打电话) takes
针 ("a needle," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 方针) as its
object: administering a needle — an injection.

## Notes

去医院打针 ("to go to the hospital for a shot") is a common everyday
phrase — 打针 covers both giving and receiving an injection, with
context settling which side the subject is on.

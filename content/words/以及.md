## Why this combination
以 and 及 are both classical conjunctions that, on their own, can mean "and"
in literary registers (以 more often "by means of, in order to" in modern
use; 及 surviving as "and" mainly in fixed expressions like 以及 and 涉及).
Pairing them produces a more formal, written-register "and," typically used
to add one more, slightly less central item to a list.

## Notes
以及 joins list items, not clauses — it cannot connect two full sentences the
way 而且 or 并且 can; save it for "A, B, 以及 C" style enumerations in formal
writing.

## Why this combination
风 (here "bearing, manner," as in 风格) plus 度 "degree, measure" gives
exactly "the degree of one's bearing" — the standard word for graceful,
composed manner.

## Notes
风度 anchors 绅士风度 "gentlemanly poise," 风度翩翩 "elegant and
graceful" (idiom) — always about outward composure and grace, typically
used of men in traditional usage though now applied more broadly.

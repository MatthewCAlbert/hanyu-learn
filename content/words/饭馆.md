## Why this combination
饭 "meal, cooked rice" (see [[吃饭]], [[米饭]]) + 馆, the suffix for a public
establishment of a given function (see [[图书馆]], [[体育馆]], 茶馆) → an
eating house.

## Notes
饭馆 (small, everyday) vs. 餐厅 (a dining room / mid-range restaurant) vs.
酒楼 / 酒店 (a big banqueting place; see [[酒店]]). 饭店 (see [[饭店]]) can mean
either a restaurant or a hotel.

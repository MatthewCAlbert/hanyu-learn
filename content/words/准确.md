## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 准 "accurate, on the mark" (see [[准备]], 说不准) + 确 "certain,
solid, definite" (see [[正确]], 确定). Matching the true value exactly:
准确的数字, 时间不准确.

## Notes
准确 = precise to a value (a figure, a time); [[正确]] = right / sound (a method,
a judgement); 精确 = to a fine degree of precision.

## Why this combination
中 (here "midway, partway through") plus 断 "to break off, to sever"
gives exactly "to break off midway" — the standard word for an ongoing
process stopping before completion.

## Notes
中断 anchors 中断联系 "to lose contact," 通话中断 "the call was
disconnected" — always implies something was in progress and got cut
off, distinct from 停止, which need not have been interrupted mid-course.

## Why this combination
差 (here "to fall short, to lack") plus 点(儿) "a little bit" gives
exactly "short by a little bit" — the standard word for something that
almost happened but didn't quite (or, with a desired outcome, that barely
did).

## Notes
差点儿 anchors 差点儿摔倒 "almost fell," 差点儿就成功了 "almost/barely
succeeded" — whether the near-miss is good or bad news depends entirely
on context, a classic feature of this construction.

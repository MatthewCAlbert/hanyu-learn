## Why this combination
马拉地 is a phonetic rendering of Marathi, plus 语. 马 is not "horse"; 地 is not
"earth." Contrast [[印地语]], whose 地 is also phonetic.

## Notes
A language of western India. 马拉 as in [[马拉维]] — different names.

## Why this combination
交警 shortens 交通警察 "traffic police" to its first character from each
half: 交 from 交通 "traffic" (see also 交流, 交往) and 警 from 警察
"police" — the same compression pattern as 空调 and 科技.

## Notes
交警 names police specifically directing and enforcing traffic law
(交警指挥交通 "traffic police direct traffic") — 警察, the general word
for "police," covers officers in any role, not only traffic duty.

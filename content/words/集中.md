## Why this combination
集 "to gather, to assemble" (as in 集合 "to assemble") combined with 中
"centre, middle": to gather towards a centre — to concentrate, whether
attention, resources or people.

## Notes
集中 takes both mental and physical objects — 集中精神 "to concentrate
one's mind," 集中力量 "to pool [one's] strength" — and works as an
adjective too (人口集中的地区 "a densely populated area," literally
"population-concentrated").

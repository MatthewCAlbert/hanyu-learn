## Why this combination
走 "to walk" + 过 "to pass, to cross" (see [[通过]], [[经过]], [[过去]]) —
walking past a point: 从门前走过. With 过 as an experiential marker it also means
"to have come through": 走过一段艰难的日子.

## Notes
走过 (pass by on foot) vs. 路过 (pass by, any means, incidentally) vs. [[经过]]
(pass through; also "process"). 走 "walk" also in [[走路]], [[走开]], [[走进]].

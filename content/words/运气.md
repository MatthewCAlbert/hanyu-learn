## Why this combination
运 here carries a "fortune, the turning of one's circumstances" sense
(distinct from its more common "to move, to transport" sense in 运动,
运输), and 气 is "vital energy, disposition" (the same word covered in
this app's entry for 勇气). Together they name the flow of fortune one
happens to carry.

Rated "semi" for 运's "fortune" sense, a specific extension of its more
common "to move" meaning.

## Notes
运气 is neutral — good or bad — and needs 好/坏 to specify which: 运气好
("good luck"), 运气不好 ("bad luck"). On its own, 运气 leans toward "good
luck" by default in casual speech, but is not exclusively positive.

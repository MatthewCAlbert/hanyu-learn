## Why this combination
超 "to leap over, to exceed" and 越 "to cross over" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for going beyond a limit or rival.

## Notes
超越 spans competition (超越对手 "to surpass a rival") and abstract limits
(超越自我 "to transcend oneself," 超越时空 "to transcend time and space") —
more formal and elevated than everyday 超过 "to exceed."

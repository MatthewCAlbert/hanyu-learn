## Why this combination
学 "study" + 院, the walled-institution suffix seen in [[医院]], 法院, 电影院.
A teaching establishment — either a specialist college (美术学院) or a faculty
within a 大学.

## Notes
Ranks below 大学: a 大学 contains several 学院. 学校 is the general word for a
school of any level.

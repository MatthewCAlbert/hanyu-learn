## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 做 "to act as, to be" (as in 做饭 in an
unrelated sense) takes 客 "guest" (as in 客人, 顾客) as its object —
literally "to act as a guest" — to visit someone as their guest.

## Notes
做客 names visiting someone's home as an invited guest (去朋友家做客
"to visit a friend's home as a guest") — 客人, the noun "guest," names
the role itself, while 做客 is the verb for occupying that role at
someone else's home.

## Why this combination
剪 "to cut (with scissors)" plus 刀 "knife, blade" gives exactly "a
cutting blade (tool)" — the standard word for the two-bladed cutting
tool.

## Notes
剪刀 anchors 一把剪刀 "a pair of scissors," 剪刀石头布 "rock-paper-scissors
(the game)" — the everyday household tool, distinct from single-bladed
刀 "knife."

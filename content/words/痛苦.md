## Why this combination
痛 "pain, ache" (physical, as in 头痛 "headache") combined with 苦
"bitter, suffering" (as in 苦难 "hardship," and figuratively "bitter"
emotional suffering): pain paired with bitterness — suffering, whether
of the body or the mind.

## Notes
痛苦 covers both physical and emotional suffering without distinction
(身体上的痛苦 "physical pain," 心里很痛苦 "in emotional pain") — 疼, a
near-synonym limited to physical pain, does not extend to the emotional
sense the way 痛苦 does.

## Why this combination
实 "real, actual, solid" (see also 事实, 现实) combined with 行 "to do,
to carry out, to travel" (as in 行动 "to act"): to make real by carrying
out — to implement.

## Notes
实行 takes a policy, system or plan as its object (实行改革 "to
implement reform," 实行新政策 "to put a new policy into practice") —
执行, a close synonym, is used the same way and is preferred in more
bureaucratic or official contexts.

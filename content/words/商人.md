## Why this combination
商 "commerce, trade" (see [[商店]], [[商场]], [[商量]]) + 人 "person" → one whose
occupation is trade. Modifier-head, like [[工人]], [[主人]], [[客人]].

## Notes
商 "trade" is said to derive from the Shang (商) people, famed as traders after
their dynasty fell. 生意人 is the colloquial "businessperson"; 企业家
"entrepreneur."

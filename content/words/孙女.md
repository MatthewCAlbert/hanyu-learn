## Why this combination

孙 ("a grandchild, a descendant," covered elsewhere in this app as part
of 孙子) plus 女 ("female," as in 女人) names a son's or daughter's
daughter: a granddaughter.

## Notes

孙女 pairs directly with 孙子 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the two
gendered forms built on the same 孙 "grandchild" root.

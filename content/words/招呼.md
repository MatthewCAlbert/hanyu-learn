## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 招 ("to beckon, to summon [with a gesture]," as
in 招手, "to wave") and 呼 ("to call out, to shout," as in 呼喊) both
independently name catching someone's attention. Pairing them gives the
standard word for greeting or hailing someone.

## Notes

打招呼 ("to say hello, to greet") is the standard verb-object frame;
招呼 alone can also mean "to look after/attend to" (招呼客人, "to attend
to guests"), a related but distinct sense.

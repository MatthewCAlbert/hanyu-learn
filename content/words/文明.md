## Why this combination
文 "writing, refinement, the marks of civilisation" (see also 文化)
combined with 明 "clear, bright, enlightened" (see also 明确): refined
and enlightened — a classical phrase repurposed in the modern era to
translate the Western concept of "civilisation."

## Notes
文明 names a civilisation as a historical entity (古代文明 "ancient
civilisation") and, as an adjective, "civilised, polite" behaviour
(文明礼貌 "civilised and courteous") — 文化, its close relative, is the
broader word for "culture" without civilisation's connotation of social
advancement.

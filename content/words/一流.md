## Why this combination
一 "one, first" and 流 (here "class, rank," as in 名流 "prominent people")
combine into "first-rank" — the top tier of a graded scale.

## Notes
一流 anchors 一流大学 "top-tier university," 一流水平 "world-class standard"
— always the highest rank, distinct from 流 elsewhere meaning "to flow"
or "current."

## Why this combination
医 ("medicine, healing") plus 学 ("study, a field of learning," as in
科学, 化学) names medicine as a scholarly discipline, distinct from
医疗 (covered elsewhere in this app), which names the practice/system of
delivering care.

## Notes
医学 is the academic field (医学院, "medical school"; 医学研究, "medical
research") — 医疗 is the healthcare system in action; the two are close
but not interchangeable.

## Why this combination
永 "eternal, perpetual" (永久) + 远 "far, distant" — time stretching away without
end. Adverb (永远记得) and, attributively, "eternal" (永远的朋友).

## Notes
永远不 "never (ever)." 远 "far" is metaphorical here — distance in time, as in
长远 "long-term."

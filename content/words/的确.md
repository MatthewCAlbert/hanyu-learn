## Why this combination
的 here takes a classical "certainly, truly" reading (dí), a completely
separate word from the ubiquitous grammatical particle 的 (de) that marks
possession and modification — the two are historically and functionally
unrelated despite sharing a character. 确 is "true, certain, confirmed"
(as in 确定, 正确). Two words for certainty reinforce each other into an
emphatic "indeed, really."

Rated "semi" specifically because of that easy-to-miss distinction between
的 (dí, "certainly") here and 的 (de, the grammatical particle) elsewhere —
same character, unrelated word.

## Notes
Read the initial 的 in 的确 as *dí*, not the neutral-tone *de* of the
possessive particle — a distinct reading worth drilling on its own.

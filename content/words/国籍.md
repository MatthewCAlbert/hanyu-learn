## Why this combination
国 "country" plus 籍 "a register, a record (of belonging)," as in 户籍
"household register," gives exactly "one's country of registration" — the
standard legal term for nationality.

## Notes
国籍 anchors 加入中国国籍 "to acquire Chinese nationality," 双重国籍 "dual
nationality" — the formal, legal-status term, distinct from 民族
"ethnicity."

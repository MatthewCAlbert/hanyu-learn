## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 承 ("to receive and bear, to accept," covered
elsewhere in this app — many hands lifting up what is passed to them)
and 担 ("to carry, to bear," also covered elsewhere — a hand shouldering
a load) both independently name taking on a weight. Pairing them gives
the standard word for formally taking on a responsibility, cost, or
consequence.

## Notes
承担责任 ("to bear/take responsibility") and 承担费用 ("to bear the cost")
are the two most common everyday frames — 承担 always takes something
burdensome as its object.

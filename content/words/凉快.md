## Why this combination
凉 "cool" + 快 here in its old sense "pleasant, gratifying" (the same 快 as in
[[快乐]], 痛快 — not "fast") → a cool that feels good, a relief from heat:
树下很凉快. As a verb, 凉快一下 "cool off for a bit."

## Notes
凉 "cool" also in 凉水 "cold water," 着凉 "catch a chill." 快 "pleasant" also in
[[快乐]], 凉快 — a reminder that 快 is not always "fast."

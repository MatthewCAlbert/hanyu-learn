## Why this combination
射 "to shoot" (see 射's own etymology — a bow-and-arrow picture) and 击
"to strike" (see 击's own notes) are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for firing a weapon.

## Notes
射击 names both the act (射击训练 "shooting practice") and the Olympic
sport (射击比赛 "shooting competition") — the formal, technical term
alongside more casual 开枪 "to fire a gun."

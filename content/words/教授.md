## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 教 ("to teach") and 授 ("to bestow, to confer,"
as in 授予, "to award/confer") both independently name the act of passing
something on to someone. As a verb, "to teach by conferring knowledge";
as a noun, the title for someone whose profession is doing exactly that —
a professor.

Rated "semi" for 授's less common standalone "to bestow/confer" sense.

## Notes
教授 as a noun is specifically the academic rank/title (distinct from 老师,
the general word for "teacher") — a professor is always a 老师, but not
every 老师 is a 教授.

## Why this combination
旅 "journey, to travel" (see [[旅行]], [[旅游]]) + 客 "guest, visitor" (see
[[客人]], [[游客]], [[顾客]]) → someone in transit — a passenger on a train or
plane, a guest at a hotel: 旅客登机, 旅客止步 "no entry to travellers."

## Notes
旅客 = a passenger / traveller (on the move, lodging); [[游客]] = a tourist
(sightseeing); [[乘客]] (not this level) = a fare-paying passenger.

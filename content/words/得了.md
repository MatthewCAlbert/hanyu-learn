## Why this combination
得 in its "to manage, good enough" sense (as in 要得, 使得) plus the
completion marker 了 gives "[that will] do" — a fixed colloquial
interjection whose exclamatory force isn't obvious just from knowing the
two pieces separately.

## Notes
得了 shuts down a conversation or objection: 得了，别说了 "all right,
that's enough, stop talking" — a mildly impatient or dismissive
interjection, common in casual speech.

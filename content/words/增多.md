## Why this combination
增 "to increase" plus the resultative complement 多 "more" gives exactly
"to increase, becoming more."

## Notes
增多 anchors 人口增多 "the population is growing," 数量增多 "the number
is increasing" — used for measurable growth in quantity, close to 增加
but emphasizing the resulting larger count.

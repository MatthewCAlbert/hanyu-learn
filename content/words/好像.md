## Why this combination
像 "to resemble, to be like" softened by 好 here as an adverb "rather, pretty
much" — "pretty much resembles." Two uses: hedged assertion (他好像走了 "he
seems to have left") and simile (好像做梦一样 "as if in a dream").

## Notes
好 as "quite / rather" also in [[好多]], [[好久]], [[好几]]. 像 alone = "to
resemble" / "a portrait" (画像).

## Why this combination
快 "fast, quick" + 餐 "a meal" (see [[早餐]], [[午餐]], [[晚餐]]) → food served
and eaten quickly. Both the Western sense (快餐店, 洋快餐) and a Chinese
set-plate lunch (中式快餐, 一份快餐).

## Notes
Figurative 文化快餐 "cultural fast food" — shallow, easily consumed content.
餐 "-meal" also in [[西餐]], [[中餐]], 正餐.

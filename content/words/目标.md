## Why this combination
目 here stands for "where the eye is aimed," not the physical organ,
paired with 标 "a mark, a sign" (recall its etymology: the very tip of a
tree branch — the highest, most visible point). Together: the marked
point one's eye — and effort — is aimed at.

## Notes
目标 covers both the concrete "a target [to shoot at]" and the abstract
"a goal [to work toward]" — 目的 "purpose" is a close relative, leaning
more toward the reason behind an action than the fixed point aimed at.

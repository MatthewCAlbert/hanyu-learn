## Why this combination
性, here "gender, sex" (as in 男性 "male," 女性 "female") combined with
别 "to distinguish, other" (see also 区别, 分别): the distinction of
gender/sex — the category itself.

## Notes
性别 names the category "gender/sex" on forms and in demographic
contexts (性别歧视 "gender discrimination," 性别平等 "gender equality")
— 性, alone, is productive across many gender-related compounds (男性,
女性, 性格 in an unrelated sense).

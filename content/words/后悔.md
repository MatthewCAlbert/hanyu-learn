## Why this combination
后 "after, behind" and 悔 "to regret" combine directly: regret felt
after the fact, once it's too late to change course.

## Notes
后悔 is the everyday verb for regret: 后悔莫及 "too late for regret
[idiom]," 不后悔 "no regrets" — always tied to a decision or action
already taken.

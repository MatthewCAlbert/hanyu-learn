## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 种 ("a kind, a seed," as in 种子) and 类 ("a
category, a kind," as in 人类, "humankind [lit. person + category]")
both independently name a grouping by shared characteristics. Pairing
them gives the standard word for a category or type.

## Notes

种类繁多 ("a great variety of kinds") and 分种类 ("to classify by
category") both build on this word — the everyday general word for
"kind/type," close in meaning to 类型 but usable more broadly across
registers.

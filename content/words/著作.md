## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 著 ("to write, to compose [a book]," as in
著名 no — there 著 instead means "notable," a separate extended sense
from its "to author" role here) and 作 ("to make, to work," as in 作品,
"a [creative] work") both independently name producing written work.
Pairing them gives the formal word for a scholarly or literary work.

## Notes

著作 leans scholarly/literary and often substantial (学术著作, "an
academic work") — 作品 is the broader term covering any creative
output, written or otherwise (art, film, music included).

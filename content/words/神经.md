## Why this combination
神 here draws on the older sense of a vital, animating force (as in
traditional medicine's "spirit-energy"), and 经 is a channel or meridian
the body's vital force runs through — 神经 was coined to translate the
Western anatomical "nerve" using that pre-existing channel imagery.

## Notes
神经 is standard anatomy (神经系统 "nervous system," 神经痛 "neuralgia") and,
colloquially, an insult or self-deprecating joke about being "unhinged"
(你神经病啊? "are you crazy?") — the medical and the slang senses coexist
without confusion in context.

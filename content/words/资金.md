## Why this combination
资 "capital, resources, assets" (as in 工资 "wages") combined with 金
"metal, money, gold" (as in 现金 "cash"): monetary capital held in
reserve — funds.

## Notes
资金 names money set aside for a specific purpose, especially business or
project funding (筹集资金 "to raise funds," 资金不足 "insufficient
funds") — 钱, the everyday word for "money," makes no distinction
between funding held for a purpose and money in general.

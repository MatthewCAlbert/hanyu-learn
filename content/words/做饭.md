## Why this combination
做 "to make, to do" + 饭, "cooked rice" standing for "a meal" generally (see
[[吃饭]], [[米饭]]) — "to make the meal." Verb-object, so it splits:
做了一顿饭, 做什么饭？

## Notes
做饭 = cook (prepare food, generic); 做菜 = make dishes specifically; 下厨
"go into the kitchen (to cook)." 做 "make" also in [[做法]], [[做到]].

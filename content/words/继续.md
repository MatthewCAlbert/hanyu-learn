## Why this combination
继 and 续 both independently mean "to continue, to carry on" — 继 with a
slight emphasis on succession (thread picked up from where it broke),
续 on replenishment (thread extended further). Paired for emphasis, in
the same near-synonym pattern as 需要 and 开始.

## Notes
继续 is the general, all-purpose "to continue"; 持续 (with 持 "to hold")
leans toward continuing in an unbroken, sustained state, while 陆续
("one after another") describes a continuing series of separate events.

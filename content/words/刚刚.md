## Why this combination
刚 "just, only a moment ago" doubled. The reduplication makes the adverb fuller
and more emphatic in speech than the clipped bare 刚. Time (刚刚到) and degree
(刚刚好 "just right").

## Notes
刚才 (see [[刚才]]) is a *noun* of time ("a moment ago") and can be a subject or
take 的; 刚 / 刚刚 are adverbs and sit before the verb.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 稳 ("stable, steady," covered in this app's
hanzi entry — a grain pile settled so firmly nothing could topple it) and
定 ("fixed, settled," as in 决定, 一定) both independently name a settled,
unmoving state. Pairing them reinforces the sense into the standard
modern word for stability.

## Notes
稳定 works as both adjective (稳定的工作, "a stable job") and verb (稳定
物价, "to stabilize prices") without changing form.

## Why this combination
主 "main, primary, taking the lead" (see also 主任, 主动) combined with
持 "to hold, to maintain, to sustain" (see also 持续): to hold the main
[role] — to preside over, to be in charge.

## Notes
主持 covers presiding over a meeting or event (主持会议 "to chair a
meeting") and, very commonly, hosting a TV or radio programme (主持人
"host, anchor") — the same word spans formal chairing and media hosting.

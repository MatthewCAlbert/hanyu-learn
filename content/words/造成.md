## Why this combination
造 "to make, to build" paired with 成 as a result complement ("to
completion, into being") — making something come fully into being: to
cause.

## Notes
造成 is almost always used for causing negative or unintended outcomes
(造成损失 "to cause a loss," 造成误解 "to cause a misunderstanding") —
contrast 产生, a more neutral word for "to give rise to" that carries no
such negative bias.

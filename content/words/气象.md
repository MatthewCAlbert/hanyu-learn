## Why this combination
气 (here "weather, atmosphere") and 象 "appearance, phenomena" combine
into exactly "the appearance of the weather" — the standard word for
meteorology and atmospheric phenomena.

## Notes
气象 anchors 气象台 "weather bureau," 气象预报 "weather forecast" — also
used figuratively for a scene or mood: 一派新气象 "a whole new atmosphere
(of change)."

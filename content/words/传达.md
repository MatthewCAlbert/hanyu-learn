## Why this combination
传 "to pass on, to transmit" and 达 "to reach, to convey" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for relaying a message.

## Notes
传达 anchors 传达指示 "to convey instructions," 传达室 "reception/gate
office (that passes along messages and visitors)" — always about
delivering information or intent from one party to another.

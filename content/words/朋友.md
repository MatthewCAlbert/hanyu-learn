## Why this combination
A synonym pair. Classically 朋 meant fellow-students under one teacher and 友
meant companions who shared ideals — a real distinction, now merged into the
single everyday word "friend."

## Notes
The compound is also the base for 男朋友 / 女朋友 (boyfriend / girlfriend) and
女生朋友 vs. 女朋友, a distinction learners quickly need.

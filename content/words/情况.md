## Why this combination
情 here carries its broader sense "state of affairs" (as in 事情 "a
matter") rather than narrowly "feeling, emotion." 况 independently means
"condition, circumstance." Two near-synonyms for "how things currently
stand," paired for a fuller, more general word.

## Notes
况 rarely appears alone in modern speech outside fixed compounds
(何况 "moreover," 状况 "condition") — 情况 is the form a learner will
meet by far the most often.

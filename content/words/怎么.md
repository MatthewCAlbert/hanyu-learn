## Why this combination
怎 by itself is just "how" and barely occurs outside this family; 么 is the same
unstressed interrogative suffix as in [[什么]]. The word is a Song-era
vernacular form (earlier 争 / 作么) and is best learned whole.

## Notes
Three jobs: manner (怎么走 how do I get there), cause (你怎么来了 why are you
here), and, doubled with 也, concession (怎么也不行 no way it works).

## Why this combination
皇 "august" and 帝 "sovereign" were both old titles for supreme rulers. The First Emperor of Qin combined them in 221 BCE and the double title stuck.

## Notes
皇 and 帝 each lasted separately in other words (皇宫, 上帝).

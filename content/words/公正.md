## Why this combination
公 "public, impartial" and 正 "correct, upright" combine directly: acting
correctly on behalf of everyone, without favouritism.

## Notes
公正 anchors 公正的裁判 "a fair referee," 公正性 "fairness/impartiality" —
a formal word for institutional or judicial fairness, distinct from
everyday 公平, which covers fairness more broadly.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 宝 ("a treasure, precious," as in 宝贝,
"treasured baby/darling") and 贵 ("expensive, valuable," as in 贵" [also
"noble/honored"]) both independently name high worth. Pairing them gives
the standard word for something precious.

## Notes

宝贵 most often modifies intangible things worth protecting (宝贵的时间,
"precious time"; 宝贵的经验, "valuable experience") — 贵 alone more often
names literal expensiveness (这个很贵, "this is expensive").

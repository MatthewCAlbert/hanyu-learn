## Why this combination
Not decomposable from the modern characters. 什 normally means "ten" or
"assorted"; here it writes an unstressed syllable that historically fused from
是勿 / 甚 (shèn, "what") in Tang–Song vernacular, with 么 an interrogative
suffix. The characters are phonetic shells — read them literally and you get
nothing.

## Notes
The same 么 suffix is in [[怎么]] (how) and 那么 / 这么 (so, like that). Treat 么
as a question-word ending rather than a meaningful morpheme.

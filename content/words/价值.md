## Why this combination
价 "price, cost" and 值 "worth, value" (recall 值得, "to be worth [it]")
are close synonyms, paired into the general noun for value — economic,
but just as easily moral, artistic or personal.

## Notes
价值 extends readily to the plural, abstract sense "values" (价值观
"values, outlook on values") in a way English "price" cannot — the word
covers both "how much something is worth" and "what one holds to be
worthwhile."

## Why this combination
外 "outside" (see [[外边]], [[以外]]) + 卖 "to sell" → selling food for
consumption outside the restaurant, i.e. takeaway. With the rise of delivery
apps it now mostly means "food delivered": 点外卖, 叫外卖, 外卖小哥 "delivery
rider."

## Notes
外卖 (leaves the premises) ↔ 堂食 "dine-in." 打包 "get it boxed to take away."

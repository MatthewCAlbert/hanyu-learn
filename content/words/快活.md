## Why this combination
快 (here "pleased, joyful," as in 快乐) and 活 (here "lively, full of
life") combine into "pleased and full of life" — the pairing works once
快's "pleased" sense (rather than "fast") is recognized.

## Notes
快活 anchors 快活的日子 "happy days," 玩得很快活 "had a very cheerful time"
— a somewhat informal, spoken-register synonym of 快乐/高兴.

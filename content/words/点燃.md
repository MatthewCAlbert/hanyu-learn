## Why this combination
点 (here a verb, "to light," as in 点火) and 燃 "to burn" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for setting something
alight.

## Notes
点燃 anchors 点燃蜡烛 "to light a candle," and figuratively 点燃希望 "to
ignite hope" — a slightly more formal/vivid register than plain 点 alone.

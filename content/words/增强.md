## Why this combination
增 "to increase" and 强 "strong" combine directly: increasing something's
strength.

## Notes
增强 modifies abstract capacities: 增强信心 "to boost confidence," 增强
体质 "to strengthen one's physique" — always about intensifying an
existing quality, not creating something from nothing.

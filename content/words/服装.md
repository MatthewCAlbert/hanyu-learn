## Why this combination
服 "clothing, to wear" (as in 衣服 "clothes") combined with 装 "to
adorn, attire, to dress up" (as in 化装 "to make up/disguise"): clothing
put on as attire — dress, costume.

## Notes
服装 is the formal, collective word for clothing as a category (服装店
"clothing store," 民族服装 "traditional/ethnic dress") — 衣服, the
everyday word, is what one actually says when talking about the clothes
someone is wearing.

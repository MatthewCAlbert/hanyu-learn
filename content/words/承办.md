## Why this combination
承 "to undertake, to bear responsibility for" and 办 "to handle, to
manage" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for taking on
responsibility for organizing an event or task.

## Notes
承办 anchors 承办奥运会 "to host the Olympics," 承办单位 "the organizing
body" — always about formally taking responsibility for executing
something, often on behalf of another party.

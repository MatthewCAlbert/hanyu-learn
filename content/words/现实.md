## Why this combination
现 "present, now, appearing" (as in 现在 "now") combined with 实 "solid,
real, true" (as in 事实 "fact"): what actually is, right now — reality,
as against imagination, hope, or theory.

## Notes
现实 works as noun ("面对现实" "to face reality") and adjective ("很现实的
问题" "a very practical/realistic problem," also, of a person, "很现实"
"pragmatic, materialistic") — the adjective use can carry a mild negative
shade of self-interest, unlike the neutral noun.

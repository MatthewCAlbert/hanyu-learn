## Why this combination
由 "from, by way of, [a cause] originating in" fused with the classical
preposition 于 "at, in, from" — two words already close in the sense of
"source, origin" reinforcing each other into a single grammaticalized
conjunction for stating a cause.

## Notes
由于 introduces a reason clause and can open a sentence (由于下雨，比赛
取消了 "because it rained, the match was cancelled") — 因为 is the more
general, more colloquial word for "because" and imposes fewer register
constraints.

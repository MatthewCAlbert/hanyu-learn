## Why this combination
主 "main, host" and 导 "to lead, to guide" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for the dominant, leading factor in a situation.

## Notes
主导 works as adjective ("主导地位" "dominant position") and verb ("主导
市场" "to dominate the market") — always about the factor or party that
sets the direction, distinct from 领导, which more narrowly means to lead
people/an organization.

## Why this combination
岸 "shore, bank" plus 上 "on, above" gives exactly "on the shore" — a
straightforward locative phrase.

## Notes
岸上 anchors 走到岸上 "to walk ashore," 站在岸上 "standing on the bank" —
the standard phrase for being on dry land near a body of water, opposite
of 水里/船上 "in the water/on the boat."

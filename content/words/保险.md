## Why this combination
保 "to protect, to guarantee" (as in 保护 "to protect") combined with 险
"danger, risk" (as in 危险 "dangerous"): to guard against risk — the
financial arrangement of insurance, and, by extension, the adjective
"safe, sure, foolproof."

## Notes
保险 works as the noun "insurance" (买保险 "to buy insurance," 保险公司
"insurance company") and, colloquially, as an adjective meaning "safe,
a sure bet" (这样做比较保险 "doing it this way is safer/more of a sure
thing") — the two senses are related by the same underlying idea of
being guarded against risk.

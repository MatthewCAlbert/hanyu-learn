## Why this combination
画 "painting, to paint / draw" + 家 as the "-ist / master" suffix (see
[[作家]], [[科学家]], 音乐家). A recognised painter. 著名画家, 国画家 "traditional
Chinese painter."

## Notes
画 "painting / draw" also in [[画儿]], [[图画]] (not this level), 漫画 "comics."
The 家 "-ist" suffix implies standing, not just doing it.

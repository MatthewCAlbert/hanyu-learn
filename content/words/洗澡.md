## Why this combination
洗 "to wash" + 澡 "a bath, bathing" as its object — "to wash a bath," a cognate
object like [[睡觉]] (sleep a sleep). It splits: 洗个澡, 洗了半天澡. 澡 barely
occurs outside this word and 澡堂 "bathhouse."

## Notes
洗 alone with a real object: 洗手, 洗衣服, [[洗手间]]. For a shower specifically,
洗淋浴 / 冲澡.

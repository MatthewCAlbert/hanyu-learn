## Why this combination
或 "or, perhaps" + the particle 者, which packages it as a standalone
connective. Used for "or" in statements — 喝茶或者咖啡都行 — and, doubled,
"either… or…". As an adverb it can mean "maybe" (他或者会来).

## Notes
Statement "or" is 或者; question "or" is [[还是]]. Mixing them (还是 in a
statement) is a classic error.

## Why this combination

热 ("hot, warm," and figuratively "enthusiastic," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 热闹) plus 心 ("heart") names a warm-hearted
enthusiasm for helping others.

Rated "semi" for 热's figurative "enthusiastic" contribution here, one
step from its plainer literal "hot" sense.

## Notes

热心人 ("a warmhearted person") and 热心帮助 ("to enthusiastically help")
both build on this word — 热心 always implies enthusiasm directed toward
helping or serving others, distinct from 热情, the broader word for
"enthusiasm/warmth" in general.

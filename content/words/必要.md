## Why this combination
必 "certainly, necessarily, without fail" combined with 要 "to need, to
want, to require": something needed without fail — necessary, essential.

## Notes
必要 modifies a noun directly (必要的准备 "necessary preparations") and
also forms 没有必要 "there's no need to..." — 需要, a near-synonym, is the
verb "to need [something]" and does not double as this adjective.

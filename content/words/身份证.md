## Why this combination
身份 "status, identity, one's standing as a person" (身 "body/self" plus
份 "a share, a portion [of identity]") combined with 证 "a proof, a
certificate" (see also 证明, 证据): a certificate proving one's
identity — an ID card.

## Notes
身份证 is the standard term for a national identity card (查身份证 "to
check ID," 身份证号 "ID number") — 证, alone, is productive across many
compounds naming certificates and permits (护照 "passport" being a
separate word, not built on 证).

## Why this combination
关 "to shut, to switch off" (see [[关上]], [[关心]]'s other sense) + 机 "machine,
device" (see [[手机]], [[飞机]]) → to power a device down: 手机关机了,
请关机. In film-making, 关机 = "that's a wrap."

## Notes
关机 ↔ [[开机]] "boot up / start the engine." 关 "switch off" pairs with 开
"switch on" across 开灯 / 关灯, [[开车]] / 关车.

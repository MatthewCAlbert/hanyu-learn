## Why this combination
实 "real, practical, actual" (see [[实际]], [[实在]], [[实现]]) + 习 "to
practise" (see [[学习]], [[练习]], [[复习]]) → practising in a real workplace,
applying what was learned in class: 在医院实习, 实习生 "an intern."

## Notes
实习 (on-the-job training as part of study) vs. [[练习]] (drill a skill) vs.
[[打工]] (a casual paid job). 实习期 "probationary / internship period."

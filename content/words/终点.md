## Why this combination
终 "end" plus 点 "point" gives exactly "the end point" — the standard
word for where a journey, race, or process concludes.

## Notes
终点 anchors 终点站 "terminus (last stop)," 到达终点 "to reach the finish
line" — used for both literal routes (races, transit lines) and,
figuratively, the endpoint of a process.

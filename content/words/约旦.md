## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Jordan (約旦). 约 and 旦 are chosen for
sound. 约 is not "appointment"; 旦 is not "dawn."

## Notes
Contrast 约会, 旦夕, where the characters keep their ordinary senses.

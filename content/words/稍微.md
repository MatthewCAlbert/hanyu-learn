## Why this combination
稍 "slightly" (see 稍's own etymology) and 微 "tiny, minute" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard everyday word for a small
degree.

## Notes
稍微 always precedes an adjective or verb it modifies: 稍微贵一点 "a bit
more expensive," 稍微等一下 "wait just a moment" — one of the most common
degree-softeners in spoken Mandarin.

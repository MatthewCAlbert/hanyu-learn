## Why this combination
不 "not" + 少 "few, little" — litotes, "not few" = a good many. 来了不少人
"quite a few people came," 花了不少钱.

## Notes
Same understatement move as English "not a little." Contrast [[多少]] (how
many), where 多 and 少 sit together as a scale.

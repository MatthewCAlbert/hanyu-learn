## Why this combination
拆 "to dismantle, to take apart" and 除 "to remove, to get rid of" are
near-synonyms combined into a formal word for demolition.

## Notes
拆除 anchors 拆除违章建筑 "to demolish illegal structures," 拆除炸弹 "to
defuse (dismantle) a bomb" — more formal/official than plain 拆, often
used for large-scale or sanctioned demolition.

## Why this combination
度 here carries a verbal "to pass through (a stretch of time)" sense
(distinct from its more common noun role in 温度, 态度), and 过 is "to
pass, to go through" — reinforcing the same idea. Two words for passing
through combine into the standard verb for spending a period of time.

Rated "semi" for 度's verbal "to pass through time" sense, a less
common role than its everyday noun uses.

## Notes
度过难关 ("to get through a difficult period," lit. "to pass through a
hard pass") and 度过假期 ("to spend a vacation") both build on this
character pair's core "getting through a stretch of time" sense.

## Why this combination
感 "to feel, to be moved by" (as in 感动 "to be moved") combined with 情
"emotion, feeling, sentiment" (as in 心情 "mood"): the feelings one
senses — emotion in general, and specifically the bond of affection
between people.

## Notes
感情 covers both general emotion (感情丰富 "emotionally rich") and, very
commonly, the affection in a relationship (他们的感情很好 "they [have a
relationship that] gets on very well," 培养感情 "to build a bond"), a
sense 心情 "momentary mood" does not carry.

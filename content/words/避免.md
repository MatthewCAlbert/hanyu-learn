## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 避 ("to turn away from, to avoid," covered
elsewhere in this app) and 免 ("to be excused from, to avoid," also
covered elsewhere) both independently name evading something unwanted.
Pairing them gives the standard word for actively preventing an
undesirable outcome.

## Notes
避免 takes an undesirable event or state as its object (避免误会, "to
avoid a misunderstanding") — it describes preventing something from
happening, not physically dodging a moving object, which would use 躲避
or 躲开 instead.

## Why this combination
家 "family, home" (as in 家庭) combined with 属 "to belong to, to be
attached to" (see also 属于): those attached to the family — dependents,
family members.

## Notes
家属 is a formal, official register naming the family members
attached to an individual, especially in institutional contexts (病人
家属 "patient's family," 军人家属 "military dependents") — 家人, its
everyday relative, is used in ordinary speech instead.

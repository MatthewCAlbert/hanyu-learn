## Why this combination

新 ("new") plus 郎 ("a young gentleman," a character occurring mainly in
this role) names a man newly [married]: a bridegroom.

## Notes

新郎 pairs with 新娘 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the standard
wedding-day terms for the couple — the same "new" 新 prefix, applied to
male and female counterparts.

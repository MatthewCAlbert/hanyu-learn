## Why this combination
想 ("to think," as in 想要, 想到) plus 象 ("an image, an appearance, a
likeness" — the same character as in 象, elephant, here used for its
unrelated "image/shape" sense, as in 现象, 形象) names thinking that forms
a mental picture: imagination.

Rated "semi" for 象's "image/likeness" sense here, easily overshadowed by
its far more common "elephant" meaning.

## Notes
Do not confuse 象 in 想象 with "elephant" — this is the same character
carrying a completely different, unrelated sense, the same "image, shape"
role it plays in 现象 and 形象.

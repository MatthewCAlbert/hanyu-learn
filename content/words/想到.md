## Why this combination
想 "to think" + resultative 到 "reach" — the thinking arrived at something. Both
"it occurred to me" (我想到一个办法) and "I anticipated it" (没想到 "didn't
expect (that)").

## Notes
没想到 "who would have thought" is one of the highest-frequency uses. Compare
[[想起]] (recall something known before) and [[想法]] (a thought, as a noun).

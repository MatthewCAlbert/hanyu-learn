## Why this combination
华 (here "Chinese," from 中华, the classical name for China) plus 语
"language" gives exactly "the Chinese language" — the term most common
outside mainland China, in Singapore, Malaysia, and overseas Chinese
communities.

## Notes
华语 anchors 华语电影 "Chinese-language film," 华语流行乐 "Mandopop
(Chinese-language pop music)" — largely synonymous with 汉语/中文, but the
preferred term in Southeast Asian and diaspora contexts.

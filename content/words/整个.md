## Why this combination
整 "whole, complete" modifying 个 (the general classifier for
individual items) — "the whole of [this one unit]," treating an entire
thing as one complete piece rather than a sum of parts.

## Notes
整个 always precedes a noun (整个世界 "the whole world," 整个过程 "the
entire process") — it functions as a determiner, not a standalone noun
the way 全部 "all of it" can.

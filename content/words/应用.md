## Why this combination
应, here "to respond to, to correspond, to answer [a need]" (as in 应该
"should") combined with 用 "to use" (see also 有用, 适用): to use in
response to a need — to apply.

## Notes
应用 names putting theory or technology to practical use (应用技术
"applied technology," 应用范围 "scope of application") and, in modern
tech usage, is shortened to 应用 alone, or 应用程序, for a computer/phone
"application, app" — 使用, its neighbour, is the more general everyday
verb "to use."

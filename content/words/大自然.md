## Why this combination
大 "great, grand" + 自然 "nature; natural" (自 self + 然 so — "what is so of
itself") → the natural world as a whole, often with a note of awe:
亲近大自然, 大自然的力量.

## Notes
Parse 大 + 自然. 自然 alone = "nature" or "natural(ly)"; 大自然 specifically
means the outdoors / the natural world as an entity. 大 adds scale, as in
[[大海]].

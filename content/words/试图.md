## Why this combination
试 "to try" is transparent; 图 here carries its less common "to plan, to
scheme toward a goal" sense (as in 图谋), not its everyday "picture" —
together, "to try toward a planned goal."

## Notes
试图 is formal/written, almost always followed directly by a verb (试图
逃跑 "attempted to escape") — everyday speech prefers 想 or 打算 for
plainer "trying/planning to."

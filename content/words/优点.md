## Why this combination
优 "excellent, superior" (as in 优秀 "outstanding," 优势 "advantage")
combined with 点 "a point, a spot" (as in 缺点, 特点): a point where
something excels — a strong point.

## Notes
优点 names a specific merit or strength of a person or thing (性格
优点 "a character strength," 找优点 "to find the good points") — its
opposite is 缺点 "shortcoming," the two forming the standard
strengths/weaknesses pair.

## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of El Salvador (薩爾瓦多). Not a calque of "the
Saviour." 萨, 尔, 瓦 and 多 are chosen for sound.

## Notes
瓦 as in [[委内瑞拉]], [[摩尔多瓦]].

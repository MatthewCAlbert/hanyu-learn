## Why this combination
深 "deep" and 处 "place" (see 处's role in 处在) combine directly: the
deepest place within something.

## Notes
深处 spans the physical (森林深处 "deep in the forest," 海底深处 "the ocean
depths") and the emotional (内心深处 "in the depths of one's heart") —
always the innermost or most distant part of something.

## Why this combination
口 (here "spoken, by mouth") plus 号 (here "a call, a cry," as in 号召 "to
call for") gives exactly "a spoken call" — the standard word for a
chanted or repeated rallying phrase.

## Notes
口号 anchors 喊口号 "to chant a slogan," 空喊口号 "to just talk without
action" (idiomatic criticism) — a phrase meant to be repeated aloud,
distinct from a written 标语 "banner slogan."

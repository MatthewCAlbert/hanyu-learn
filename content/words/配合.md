## Why this combination
配 "to pair, to match, to mix in the right proportion" (as in 配合's own
配 seen in 分配 "to distribute") combined with 合 "to close together, to
fit" (see also 合理, 联合): to match and fit together — to coordinate.

## Notes
配合 describes people or parts working together smoothly towards a
shared outcome (互相配合 "to coordinate with each other," 配合治疗 "to
cooperate with treatment") — 合作, a near-synonym, more specifically
means "to work together on a joint project" as equal partners.

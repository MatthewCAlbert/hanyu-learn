## Why this combination
学 "to learn (acquire)" + 习 "to practise, to rehearse repeatedly" — the two
halves of learning, taking in and drilling. The pairing is as old as the
opening line of the Analects, 學而時習之.

## Notes
Both verb (学习中文) and noun (学习很重要). Narrower than English "study": for
"study a subject at university" the word is 学 or 读, e.g. 学医.

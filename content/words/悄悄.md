## Why this combination
悄 "quiet, hushed" reduplicated (AA pattern) softens and adverbializes
the meaning into "quietly, without notice" — the same pattern as 慢慢
"slowly" and 轻轻 "gently."

## Notes
悄悄 typically modifies actions done without others noticing: 悄悄离开
"to leave quietly," 悄悄话 "a whispered secret" — carries a sense of
discretion or secrecy beyond simple physical quietness (which would be
安静).

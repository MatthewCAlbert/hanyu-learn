## Why this combination
英 clipped from 英国 "Britain" + 文 "writing, text, language" (see [[中文]],
[[语言]]). Parallel to 中文 / 日文 / 法文. 英文 leans toward the written
language and English-language materials; [[英语]] toward the spoken.

## Notes
英文名 "an English name," 英文版 "the English edition." In practice 英文 and
英语 overlap heavily.

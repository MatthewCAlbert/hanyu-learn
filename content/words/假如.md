## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 假 (here in its "to suppose, hypothetically," as
in 假设, rather than its more common "false, fake" sense) and 如 ("if, as,"
covered in this app's entry for 如今) both independently introduce a
hypothesis. Pairing them gives the standard conditional conjunction "if."

Rated "semi" for 假's "to suppose" sense, distinct from its everyday
"fake" meaning.

## Notes
假如 is interchangeable with 如果 in most contexts — 假如 reads slightly
more literary/formal, common in essays and careful spoken argument.

## Why this combination
提 is "to raise, to lift, to bring up" (as in 提高, 提出), and 供 is "to
supply, to furnish" (as in 供应, 供给). Lifting something up and offering it
forward, then supplying it, together give "to provide."

Rated "semi" since 提's "raise/bring up" sense, while common elsewhere,
takes a small extra step to connect to "provide" rather than the more
directly synonymous 供.

## Notes
提供 always takes a direct object naming what is supplied (提供服务, 提供帮助,
提供信息) — it is not used alone the way "to provide" sometimes stands
without an object in English.

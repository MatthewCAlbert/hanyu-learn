## Why this combination
制 "to make, to manufacture" plus the resultative complement 成 "to
become" gives exactly "to make, resulting in (a finished product)."

## Notes
制成 always takes a following result: 用大豆制成的食品 "food made from
soybeans," 制成品 "finished/manufactured product" — states both the
process and its successful completion.

## Why this combination
普 "universal, widespread" (as in 普通 "ordinary," 普遍 "universal")
combined with 及 "to reach, to extend to": to extend universally — to
become widespread, to popularize.

## Notes
普及 describes something spreading to reach everyone (普及教育 "universal
education," 电脑已经普及 "computers have become universal") — 普遍, its
close relative, describes the resulting widespread state itself, while
普及 emphasizes the spreading process.

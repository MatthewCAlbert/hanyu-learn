## Why this combination
大 "big" and 胆 "gall bladder" — the organ traditionally associated with
courage in Chinese (see 胆's own etymology and notes) — combine directly:
a big, courageous gall bladder, i.e. boldness.

## Notes
大胆 spans praise (大胆的想法 "a bold idea") and mild criticism (太大胆了
"too audacious/presumptuous") — context and tone decide which reading
applies.

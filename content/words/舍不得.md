## Why this combination
舍 "to give up, to part with" plus the negative potential complement 不得
("cannot bring oneself to," from 得 "to manage to") gives "cannot bring
oneself to give up" — the logic depends on the potential complement
grammar rather than being obvious from the characters alone.

## Notes
舍不得 anchors 舍不得花钱 "reluctant to spend money," 舍不得离开 "hate to
leave" — the negative counterpart of 舍得 "willing to give up/spend,"
always implies an emotional attachment being weighed against giving
something up.

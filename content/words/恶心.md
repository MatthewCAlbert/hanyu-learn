## Why this combination
恶 takes its *ě* reading here (covered in this app's hanzi entry — one of
three distinct readings the character carries) plus 心 ("heart," standing
more broadly for one's inner/bodily sensation) — together naming the
queasy, unsettled feeling in one's core: nausea.

## Notes
恶心 describes both the physical sensation (我觉得恶心, "I feel nauseous")
and, figuratively, something morally repulsive (他的行为很恶心, "his
behavior is disgusting") — the same queasy reaction applied to the body
and to conduct.

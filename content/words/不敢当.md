## Why this combination
不敢 "not dare" plus 当 (here "to accept, to take on," as in taking on a
compliment or honor) gives literally "I dare not accept (such praise)" —
a fixed, formulaic expression of humility rather than a literal statement
of fear.

## Notes
不敢当 is a set polite response to a compliment or honor: someone praises
you, and you reply 不敢当 to modestly deflect it — a fixed courtesy
phrase, always used this way rather than built freely from its parts.

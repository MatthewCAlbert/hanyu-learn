## Why this combination
实际 "actual, real, practical" (实 "solid, real" plus 际 "a border where
two things meet," extended to "actual circumstances") combined with the
locative suffix 上 "in regard to, on [that footing]": grounded in actual
circumstances — a discourse adverb introducing what is really the case,
often against a stated appearance.

## Notes
实际上 typically opens a clause that corrects or qualifies what came
before (他看起来很自信，实际上很紧张 "he looks confident, but is actually
very nervous") — functionally close to 其实, though 实际上 leans slightly
more formal.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 告 "to announce, to report" + 诉 "to relate, to state" (the same
诉 as in 诉说). Together, the plain verb "to tell someone something."

## Notes
诉 is toneless here (*gàosu*). Takes a person object directly: 告诉我
"tell me" — no preposition, unlike English "tell *to*."

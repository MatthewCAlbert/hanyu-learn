## Why this combination
爱 "to love" + 情 "feeling, sentiment, affection" (感情, 热情) → the emotion of
romantic love, specifically. 爱情故事 "love story," 爱情电影.

## Notes
爱情 = romantic love; 感情 = feelings / the bond between people (broader);
爱 alone covers love of any kind (爱国, 母爱).

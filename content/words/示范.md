## Why this combination
示 "to show" and 范 "a model, a pattern" combine directly: showing a
model for others to follow.

## Notes
示范 anchors 示范作用 "a demonstrative/exemplary role," 现场示范 "an
on-site demonstration" — always showing how something should be done, not
just displaying it.

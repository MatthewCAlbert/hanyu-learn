## Why this combination
婚 ("marriage," as in 结婚, "to get married") plus 礼 ("a ceremony, a
rite," as in 礼物, "a gift," lit. "a ceremonial object") names the
ceremony that marks a marriage: a wedding.

## Notes
婚礼 names the ceremony/event itself (参加婚礼, "to attend a wedding") —
distinct from 结婚 (the verb "to get married") and 婚姻 ("marriage" as an
ongoing institution/relationship).

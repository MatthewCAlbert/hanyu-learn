## Why this combination
药 "medicine, drug" (see [[药店]], [[药水]]) + 片 "a thin flat piece" (see
[[照片]], [[图片]]) → medicine pressed into a flat disc — a tablet. 吃两片药.

## Notes
The 药 forms: [[药片]] (tablet), 药丸 (round pill), [[药水]] (liquid), 胶囊
(capsule). 片 is also the measure word: 一片药.

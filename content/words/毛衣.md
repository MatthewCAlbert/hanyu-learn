## Why this combination

毛 ("hair, wool," as in 头发's related sense) plus 衣 ("clothing," as in
上衣) names clothing made of wool: a sweater.

## Notes

毛衣 covers any knit sweater regardless of actual fiber content in
everyday speech — 毛 here names the traditional wool material the word
was coined for, much like English "sweater" doesn't require sweat.

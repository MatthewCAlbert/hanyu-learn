## Why this combination
象 alone already means "elephant" (a pictograph of the animal, its
oldest attested sense); 大 "big" was added as a size-emphasizing prefix,
giving "the big 象" to keep the animal sense distinct once 象 also came to
mean "image, appearance" (as in 现象, 形象).

## Notes
大象 is the everyday word for the animal; 象 alone now more often means
"image, appearance" in compounds, so 大象 disambiguates when the animal
is meant.

## Why this combination
紧 "tight, taut" and 张 "to stretch, to draw taut [a bow]" are
near-synonyms, paired into a word for a state pulled tight — extended from
physical tautness to a mind pulled tight with nerves, a situation pulled
tight with urgency, or supply pulled tight to scarcity.

## Notes
紧张 covers three related senses without changing form: emotional (考试
让我很紧张 "the exam makes me nervous"), situational (关系紧张 "tense
relations"), and resource-related (水资源紧张 "water is in short
supply").

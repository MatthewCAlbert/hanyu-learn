## Why this combination
开 "to open" extends to "to operate / start up a machine" (开机, 开灯, 开会); with
车 as its object it is "to drive." Verb-object, so it splits: 开了一天车 "drove
all day." From the driver's seat it also means "the vehicle departs": 车快开了.

## Notes
开 = operate/start pairs with 关 = shut off across 开灯/关灯, 开机/关机.
(Internet slang 老司机…开车 is a separate, jokey extension.)

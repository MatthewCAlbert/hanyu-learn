## Why this combination
红 "red" combined with 酒 "alcohol, wine" (as in 啤酒 "beer"): red-colored
wine.

## Notes
红酒 is the common colloquial name for red wine (喝红酒 "to drink red
wine"), alongside the more formal 葡萄酒 "grape wine," which covers wine
generally regardless of color.

## Why this combination
视 (here "to regard, to look upon") acting through 为 "to be, to serve
as" gives exactly "to look upon (something) as (something)" — a formal
verb-complement pattern.

## Notes
视为 always takes a following complement: 被视为榜样 "to be regarded as a
role model" — a written/formal register word, more common in reports and
essays than in speech, where 当作 or 看作 are typical.

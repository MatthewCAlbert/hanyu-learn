## Why this combination
面 "wheat flour / dough" (see [[面包]]) + 条儿 "strips, long thin pieces" (条
with erhua) → flour worked into strips. The non-erhua 面条 is the same word.

## Notes
一碗面条儿 "a bowl of noodles." Often just 面 in dish names: 牛肉面, 炸酱面.
条 is the measure word for long thin things (一条鱼, 一条路).

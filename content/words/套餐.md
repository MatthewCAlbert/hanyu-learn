## Why this combination

套 ("a set," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 手套) plus 餐
("a meal," as in 早餐/午餐/晚餐) names a fixed bundled meal: a combo meal.

## Notes

午餐套餐 ("a set lunch") and 流量套餐 ("a data plan, lit. traffic-flow
set-package") both build on 套's "bundled set" sense — the second
example shows the word extending well beyond literal food to any
bundled package deal.

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Portugal. 葡萄 "grape" and 牙 "tooth" are
coincidences of sound, not a semantic story.

## Notes
Same stem in [[葡萄牙语]], [[葡萄牙文]]. Contrast 葡萄 the fruit.

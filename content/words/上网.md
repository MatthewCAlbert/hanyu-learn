## Why this combination
网 "net" (clipped from 网络, see [[网上]]) as the object of 上 "to get on / board"
(cf. 上车, [[上班]]) — treating the internet as a place you step onto. Splits:
上了一天网 "was online all day."

## Notes
Also, off the internet, "to move to the net" in volleyball/tennis. Opposite
下线 "go offline / log off."

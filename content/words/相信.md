## Why this combination
信 "to trust, to believe." 相 here is not "mutual" but the 相 that directs a
verb at an object (as in 相识, 相约) — "to place trust in." Takes a person
(相信他) or a proposition (相信明天会更好).

## Notes
相信 = believe / trust; 信 alone can also mean "a letter" or "faith" (信教).
不相信 "don't believe it."

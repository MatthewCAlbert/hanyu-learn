## Why this combination
会 (here "to meet") and 谈 "to talk" combine into exactly "to meet and
talk" — the formal word for an official conversation between parties.

## Notes
会谈 is used for diplomatic or business-level talks: 双方举行会谈 "the
two sides held talks" — more formal than everyday 谈话, reserved for
official or high-level discussion.

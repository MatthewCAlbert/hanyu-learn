## Why this combination
可 "able, worth, -able" + 以, classically "to use, by means of." "Able by which
to…" contracted into the plain modal "can / may." Covers ability, permission
(你可以走了) and, colloquially, "good enough" (这个还可以).

## Notes
Ability-can is often 能 or 会; 可以 leans toward permission and possibility.
可 alone survives in 可怕, 可爱, 认可.

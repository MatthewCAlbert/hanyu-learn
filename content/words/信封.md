## Why this combination
信 "a letter" (as in 相信 in an unrelated sense, and 短信 "text
message") combined with 封 "to seal, an envelope, a classifier for
sealed letters" (as in 一封信 "a letter"): what a letter is sealed
in — an envelope.

## Notes
信封 names the paper envelope a letter is mailed in (贴邮票在信封上 "to
stick a stamp on the envelope") — 封, alone, doubles as the classifier
for letters (一封信 "one letter"), a neat overlap between the object and
its counting word.

## Why this combination
"Hand machine" — a handheld device. 机 is the generic "machine" morpheme
(飞机, 电视机, 相机); 手 marks it as the pocket-sized one you carry. A clean
native coinage, no borrowed sound.

## Notes
Older 移动电话 / 大哥大 (the 1990s brick) have given way entirely to 手机.
玩手机 "be on one's phone."

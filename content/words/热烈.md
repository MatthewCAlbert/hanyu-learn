## Why this combination
热 "hot" (as in 热情 "warm, enthusiastic") combined with 烈 "intense,
fierce, ardent" (see also 强烈): hot and intense — enthusiastic, ardent.

## Notes
热烈 describes an intensely warm, enthusiastic atmosphere or response
(热烈欢迎 "a warm/enthusiastic welcome," 热烈讨论 "a heated/lively
discussion") — 热情, its close relative, describes a person's own warmth
and enthusiasm rather than the atmosphere of an event.

## Why this combination
古 "ancient, old" (as in 古老 "ancient") combined with 代 "a generation,
an era" (see also 时代, 现代): the ancient era — antiquity.

## Notes
古代 opens the standard periodising sequence 古代/近代/现代 "ancient/
early-modern/modern" (see 现代's own entry) — 古老, its close relative,
is the adjective "ancient, age-old" describing a thing's age, not the
era as a noun.

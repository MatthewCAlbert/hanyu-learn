## Why this combination
赔 "to compensate for a loss" (see 赔's own etymology) and 偿 "to repay"
(see 偿's own etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard
legal/financial word for compensation.

## Notes
赔偿 is the formal legal register: 赔偿损失 "to compensate for losses,"
赔偿金 "damages, compensation payment" — more formal than the general
补偿 "to make up for."

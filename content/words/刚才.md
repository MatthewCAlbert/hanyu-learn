## Why this combination
刚 "just, only a moment ago" + 才 "only then, just" — a near-synonym pair,
lexicalised into a *noun* of time meaning "the recent past just now":
刚才的电话 "the call just now," 你刚才说什么？

## Notes
刚才 is a time noun (can take 的, be a subject); [[刚刚]] / 刚 are adverbs
(before the verb). "刚才我在忙" vs. "我刚忙完."

## Why this combination
The everyday clip of [[印度尼西亚]]: 印 from 印度 + 尼 from 尼西亚. Spoken and written
shorthand, like [[沙特]] from [[沙特阿拉伯]].

## Notes
Same clip in [[印尼语]], [[印尼文]]. Not a separate country.

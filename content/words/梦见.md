## Why this combination

梦 ("a dream," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 做梦, "to
dream") plus 见 ("to see," as in 看见) names seeing something within a
dream: to dream about.

## Notes

梦见 always takes the dreamed-of content as its object (梦见妈妈, "dreamt
about mother") — distinct from 做梦, which describes the general act of
dreaming without naming its content.

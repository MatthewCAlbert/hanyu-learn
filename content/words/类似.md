## Why this combination
类 "category, kind, type" (as in 种类 "sort, kind") combined with 似 "to
resemble, similar" (as in 好似 "to seem like"): resembling the same
category — similar, analogous.

## Notes
类似 describes things that resemble each other without being identical
(类似的情况 "a similar situation," 类似的问题 "an analogous problem") —
一样, "the same," asserts identity rather than mere resemblance.

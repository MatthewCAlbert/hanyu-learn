## Why this combination

哪 ("which, what") plus 怕 ("to fear, to be afraid of") fused into a
concessive conjunction: something like "whatever one might fear (still…)"
bleached into the modern "even if."

Rated "opaque": the concessive-conjunction sense isn't recoverable from
"which" plus "fear" without already knowing the fixed phrase.

## Notes

哪怕…也… ("even if…, still…") is the standard paired-clause pattern:
哪怕下雨，我也要去 ("even if it rains, I'm still going").

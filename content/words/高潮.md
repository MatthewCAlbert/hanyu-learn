## Why this combination

高 ("high") plus 潮 ("tide, a surge of water," as in 潮水) names the tide
at its highest point — extended figuratively to the peak or climax of
any event.

Rated "semi" for the figurative extension from ocean tides to a story or
event's turning point.

## Notes

高潮 covers the literal tide (涨潮/高潮, "the tide rises/high tide") and,
far more commonly in everyday speech, a narrative or emotional climax
(比赛进入高潮, "the match reaches its climax").

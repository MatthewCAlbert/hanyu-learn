## Why this combination
汉 "the Han" + 字 "written character." The script named by the people who
developed it — distinguishing it, when needed, from the borrowed forms used
elsewhere: Japanese 漢字 *kanji*, Korean 한자 *hanja*, Vietnamese *chữ Hán*.

## Notes
写汉字 "write characters," 简化汉字 "simplified characters." Here 字 is "a
written character," its core sense (cf. [[名字]], where it is "courtesy name").

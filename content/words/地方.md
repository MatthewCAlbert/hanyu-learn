## Why this combination
地 "ground, earth" + 方 "square, region, direction" → a patch of ground, a
locality. From there: an unspecified "place" (什么地方), physical "room"
(没地方了), and the administrative sense "local" opposed to 中央 "central."

## Notes
方 as "region" also sits in 东方 (the East), 对方 (the other party). Toneless
方 (*dìfang*) signals this everyday noun rather than the literal "square."

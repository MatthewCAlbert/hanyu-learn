## Why this combination
资 "capital, resources" and 产 "property, to produce" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard financial/legal word for assets.

## Notes
资产 is formal/financial register: 资产负债表 "balance sheet," 固定资产
"fixed assets," 资产阶级 "the bourgeoisie [Marxist economic term]" —
distinct from everyday 财产 "property/belongings."

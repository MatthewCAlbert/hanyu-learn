## Why this combination

列 ("to arrange in a row," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
列入) plus 为 ("to be, to become," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 分为) names being placed into a category by arrangement: to be
classified as.

## Notes

被列为重点保护对象 ("classified as a key protected object") and 列为首要
任务 ("listed as the top priority") both build on this word — 列为
always takes a category or status as its complement, close in
structure to 列入 but naming the resulting classification rather than
mere inclusion.

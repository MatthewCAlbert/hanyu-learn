## Why this combination
自 "from, since" (as in 自己 in an unrelated sense, and 来自 "to come
from") and 从 "from, following on" (as in 从来) are close synonyms,
paired into the standard preposition marking the starting point of a
span of time.

## Notes
自从 introduces the starting point of an ongoing situation and precedes
the time expression, unlike 以来, which follows it (自从他走了以后 "ever
since he left" / 他走了以来 "since he left") — the two are often combined,
自从...以来, for extra clarity.

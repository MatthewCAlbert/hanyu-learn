## Why this combination
分 "a point, a mark (in scoring)" (考了九十分 "scored 90") + 数 (*shù*) "number"
(see [[数字]], [[人数]]) → a number of points, a score: 考试分数, 分数线
"the cut-off mark." In maths, 分 "a division / part" gives 分数 "a fraction."

## Notes
分 is the point-unit in a mark; 分数 the number of them. 数 is *shù* here.
成绩 (see [[成绩]]) is the broader "results / achievement."

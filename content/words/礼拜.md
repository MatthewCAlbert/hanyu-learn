## Why this combination
礼 "ritual propriety" and 拜 "to bow, to pay respects/worship" combine
into "an act of ritual worship" — the everyday senses "week" and "Sunday"
are a colloquial extension from Sunday as the day of Christian worship
(做礼拜 "to attend church service"), a leap not recoverable from the
characters alone.

## Notes
礼拜 covers three senses: the religious service itself (做礼拜), a
colloquial word for "week" (上个礼拜 "last week"), and, short for 礼拜天,
"Sunday" — all everyday and informal compared to 星期 and 周.

## Why this combination

能 ("ability, capable," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 智能)
plus 干 ("to do, to work," as in 干什么, "to do what") names being
capable of doing work well: competent.

## Notes

她很能干 ("she's very capable") is the standard predicate use — 能干
describes someone who gets things done effectively, close to but more
casual/spoken than 有能力, "to have the ability."

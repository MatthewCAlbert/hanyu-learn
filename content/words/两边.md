## Why this combination

两 ("two," the counting form used before classifiers, as in 两个) plus
边 ("a side," as in 旁边, "beside [lit. side + side]") names the two
sides of something.

## Notes

马路两边 ("both sides of the road") and 两边都对 ("both sides are right")
both build on this word — 两边 names exactly two sides, distinct from
双方 (covered elsewhere in this app), which names two parties in a
negotiation or dispute rather than physical sides.

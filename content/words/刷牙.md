## Why this combination

刷 ("to brush, to scrub," as in 刷卡, "to swipe a card") takes 牙
("tooth") as its object: to brush one's teeth.

## Notes

刷牙 is a standard part of the daily-routine verb family alongside 洗澡
("to shower") and 洗脸 ("to wash one's face") — all naming basic morning
and evening hygiene.

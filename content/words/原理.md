## Why this combination
原 "original, basic" plus 理 "principle, reason" gives exactly "the
underlying principle" — the standard word for the fundamental law behind
a mechanism or phenomenon.

## Notes
原理 anchors 工作原理 "operating principle," 物理原理 "physical
principle" — always about the fundamental "why it works," distinct from
理论, which is a broader, more developed theoretical framework.

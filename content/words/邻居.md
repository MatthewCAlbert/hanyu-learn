## Why this combination
邻 "neighbour" (see 邻's own etymology — from 鄰, "five households make
one 鄰") and 居 "to reside" combine directly: one who resides nearby.

## Notes
邻居 is the everyday word for a neighbour (老邻居 "an old/longtime
neighbour"); 邻 alone survives mainly in compounds like 邻国 "neighbouring
country" and 邻近 "nearby."

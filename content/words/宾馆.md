## Why this combination
宾 "guest" (see 宾's own etymology) and 馆 "a hall, an institutional
building" (the same 馆 as in 博物馆) combine directly: a building that
houses guests.

## Notes
宾馆 sits between 旅馆 (a modest inn) and 酒店 (an upscale hotel) in
register, often used for mid-range or officially designated
accommodation.

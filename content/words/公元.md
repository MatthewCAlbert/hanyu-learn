## Why this combination

公 ("public, common," as in 公共) plus 元 ("origin, the first," as in
元旦, "New Year's Day [lit. origin + dawn]") names the commonly shared
starting point of the calendar era: the Common Era.

Rated "semi" for 元's "origin/starting point" sense here, one step from
its more familiar monetary "yuan" meaning.

## Notes

公元前 ("BC/BCE, lit. before the common era") and 公元后 ("AD/CE") both
build on this word — the standard era marker used alongside a year
number in formal and historical writing.

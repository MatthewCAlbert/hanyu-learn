## Why this combination
自 "self" (see also 自己, 自动) combined with 身 "body, oneself" (as in
身体 "body"): one's own self and body — itself, one's own.

## Notes
自身 emphasises something belonging to or originating from the entity
itself, often in formal or written contexts (自身利益 "one's own
interests," 事物自身 "the thing itself") — 自己, the everyday reflexive
"oneself," is used far more broadly in ordinary speech.

## Why this combination
证 "proof, evidence" and 明 "to make clear" combine directly: making
something clear and beyond doubt through evidence.

## Notes
证明 works as both verb ("to prove") and noun ("a certificate,
documentary proof") — 学生证明 "student certificate" uses the noun sense
for an official document attesting to a fact.

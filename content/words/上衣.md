## Why this combination
上 "upper, above" (see also 上面, 上课 in an unrelated sense) combined
with 衣 "clothing, garment" (as in 衣服): the garment worn on the upper
body — a jacket, a top.

## Notes
上衣 names any upper-body garment generically (穿上衣 "to put on a
jacket/top") — 衣服, the general word for "clothes," covers clothing as
a whole without specifying upper or lower body.

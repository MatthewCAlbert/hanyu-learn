## Why this combination
考 "to examine, to test" and 核 (here "to check, to verify," as in 核实)
are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a formal
performance evaluation.

## Notes
考核 anchors 年度考核 "annual performance review," 考核标准 "assessment
criteria" — a formal, often workplace/institutional evaluation, distinct
from 考试, which specifically tests knowledge via an exam.

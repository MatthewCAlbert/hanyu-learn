## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term, like [[妈妈]], 哥哥, 姐姐 — the reduplication marks it as an
intimate address word. 爸 rests on the nursery syllable *ba*; the character
stacks 父 "father" on top of a 巴 phonetic.

## Notes
Second syllable toneless. Formal / written: 父亲. Vocative 爸！alone is normal.

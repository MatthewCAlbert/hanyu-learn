## Why this combination

同 ("same, shared," as in 同学) plus 情 ("feeling, emotion," as in 感情)
names sharing another's feeling: to sympathize.

## Notes

同情 takes a person or their situation as its object (同情他, "to feel
sorry for him"; 值得同情, "worthy of sympathy") — closer to compassion for
someone's hardship than to agreement with an opinion.

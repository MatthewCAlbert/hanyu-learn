## Why this combination
出 "to go out" + 发 "to set in motion, to start off" (see [[发现]], 出发) →
leaving to begin a journey: 明天早上出发. Figuratively, "starting from (a
basis)": 从大局出发 "proceeding from the overall interest."

## Notes
出发点 "starting point / underlying motive." Contrast [[起飞]] (a plane taking
off) and [[出门]] (leaving the house).

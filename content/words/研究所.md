## Why this combination
研究 "to research, research" plus the place suffix 所 "place, institute"
gives exactly "a place for research" — the standard word for a dedicated
research institution.

## Notes
研究所 names an institute (中科院研究所 "a Chinese Academy of Sciences
institute") in mainland usage; in Taiwan Mandarin it additionally means
"graduate school" (读研究所 "to attend grad school"), where mainland usage
prefers 研究生院.

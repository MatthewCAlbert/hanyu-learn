## Why this combination
问 "to ask" + 路 "road, way" (see [[马路]], [[路口]], [[道路]]) as its object —
to ask the way to somewhere: 向路人问路. Verb-object, so it splits: 问了半天路.

## Notes
问 "ask" also in [[请问]], [[问题]], 问好 "send regards." Answered with
directions: 一直走, 左转, 到十字路口….

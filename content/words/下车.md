## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" as the object of 下 "to get down / off" — the counterpart of 上 in
[[上车]]. Splits: 下了车往左走 "get off and go left."

## Notes
The 上 / 下 = board / alight pair, matching 上船 / 下船, 上飞机 / 下飞机.

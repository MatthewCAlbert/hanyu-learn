## Why this combination
耳 "ear" is transparent on its own; 朵 (elsewhere the classifier for
flowers and clouds — see 朵's own etymology, a bloom hanging on its
branch) is added here as a familiar noun-suffix, its bloom-shaped
association echoing the ear's rounded, hanging outer shape rather than
contributing separate meaning.

## Notes
耳朵 is the everyday word for the body part; 耳 alone survives mainly in
compounds (耳机 "headphones," 耳环 "earrings") rather than standing on its
own the way 耳朵 does.

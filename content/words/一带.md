## Why this combination
一 "one" plus 带 "a belt, a zone" gives exactly "one stretch/belt (of
land)" — used to vaguely delimit an area around a reference point.

## Notes
一带 always follows a place name or location: 长江一带 "the region around
the Yangtze," 这一带 "this area" — a loose, informal way of referring to
a surrounding zone rather than a precisely bounded area.

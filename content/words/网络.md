## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 网 ("net, mesh," as in 上网, "to go online," lit.
"to get on the net") and 络 ("interconnected threads or vessels," as in
经络, the body's network of channels in Chinese medicine) both
independently picture things linked in a mesh. Pairing them names any
system of interconnected nodes — internet, transport, or social.

Rated "semi" for 络's specialized "interconnected vessels/threads" sense,
met almost exclusively in this word and 经络.

## Notes
网络 covers the internet (网络购物, online shopping), transport systems
(交通网络), and social ties (关系网络) alike — any structure of
interconnected nodes.

## Why this combination
目 here is not the physical eye but stands in for "what is right in
front of one's gaze" — combined with 前 "in front, before," giving "that
which lies before one's eyes right now": the present moment. Semi rather
than transparent because that "eye standing for immediate view" step is
a small figurative leap.

## Notes
目前 is more formal/written than 现在; it appears constantly in reports,
news and official statements ("at the present time...") where 现在 would
sound slightly more conversational.

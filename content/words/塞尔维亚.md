## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Srbija / Serbia (塞爾維亞). 塞, 尔, 维 and 亚
are chosen for sound. 塞 is not "to stuff."

## Notes
Same stem in [[塞尔维亚语]]. 塞 as a dummy also in [[塞内加尔]], [[塞舌尔]].

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 装 ("to install, to load," as in 安装) and 置
("to place, to set up," covered elsewhere in this app) both independently
name setting equipment in place. Pairing them gives the standard
technical word for a device or the act of installing one.

## Notes
装置 leans technical (安全装置, "a safety device") — for everyday software
installation, 安装 alone is the more common verb.

## Why this combination
基 "foundation, base" (see also 基本) combined with 础 "a plinth, the
stone base under a pillar" (as in 基础's own 础, a character whose
simplification, per this dataset's convention, is worth noting swapped
its phonetic component while keeping 石 "stone" as the meaning-bearer):
the stone foundation something rests on — the basis of anything built up
from it.

## Notes
基础 names the groundwork something is built on, literal or figurative
(打基础 "to lay a foundation," 基础知识 "basic/foundational knowledge") —
基本, its close relative, is the adjective "basic, fundamental" rather
than the noun naming the foundation itself.

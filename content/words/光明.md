## Why this combination
光 "light, brightness" (as in 阳光) and 明 "bright, clear" (see also 明确,
说明) are close synonyms, paired into a fuller word for radiant light,
extended figuratively to a bright, hopeful outlook.

## Notes
光明 names literal light (追求光明 "to pursue the light," in a
figurative/poetic register) and, more often at this level, a bright,
promising outlook (光明的前途 "a bright future") — it also describes an
open, honest character (光明正大 "open and aboveboard," a fixed
four-character idiom).

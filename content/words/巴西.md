## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Brasil / Brazil. 巴 and 西 are chosen for
sound, not for "to hope + west."

## Notes
巴 as a phonetic also writes 巴基斯坦, 古巴 — different names, same convenient
syllable. Contrast 西 "west" in 西边, 东西.

## Why this combination
可 here is the productive "worthy of / able to be" prefix seen in 可爱
"lovable," 可怕 "fearsome" — 可 + 怜 "pity" gives "worthy of pity."

## Notes
可怜 works as an adjective ("pitiful") and, less often, a verb ("to take
pity on," 可怜可怜他 "have mercy on him") — the 可-prefix pattern is worth
learning as a template, since it recurs across many adjectives.

## Why this combination
缺 "lacking, missing" and 乏 "deficient, scarce" (see 乏's own etymology —
literally "the reverse of 正, correct") are near-synonyms combined into
the standard formal verb for lacking something.

## Notes
缺乏 is more formal/written than everyday 缺 or 没有: 缺乏经验 "lacking
experience," 缺乏信心 "lacking confidence" — always about an ongoing
insufficiency rather than a one-off absence.

## Why this combination
放 ("to release, to let go") plus 松 (in its "loose, relaxed" sense,
covered elsewhere in this app — a character that itself merged two
originally distinct words, "pine tree" and "loose") names releasing
tension until things go slack: to relax.

## Notes
放松心情 ("to relax, to unwind," lit. "to loosen one's mood") and 放松
警惕 ("to let one's guard down," lit. "to loosen vigilance") both build on
this character's core "release from tension" sense.

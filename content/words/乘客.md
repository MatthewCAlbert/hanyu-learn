## Why this combination
乘 "to ride" (see 乘's own etymology — a person climbing atop a tree) and
客 "guest" combine directly: a guest riding along on someone else's
vehicle.

## Notes
乘客 is the standard formal term across all transport (公交车乘客 "bus
passengers," 航班乘客 "flight passengers") — 旅客 overlaps but leans more
toward travelers generally, not specifically someone mid-ride.

## Why this combination
适 "to fit, to suit" (see also 适合, 适应) combined with 用 "to use" (see
also 有用, 使用): fitting for use — applicable.

## Notes
适用 describes a rule, method or tool as applicable to a given case
(适用范围 "scope of application," 这个方法不适用 "this method isn't
applicable") — 适合, its close relative, is used more broadly for
"suitable for" a person or purpose, not specifically the applicability
of a rule or method.

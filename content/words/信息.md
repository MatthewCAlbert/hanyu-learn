## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 信 "a message, a letter" + 息 "news, tidings" (消息). Together, the
general "information" — 信息时代 "the information age," 个人信息 "personal
data," 发信息 "send a message."

## Notes
消息 leans toward "a piece of news"; 信息 toward "information" as a mass concept
and, colloquially, a text message.

## Why this combination
教 (*jiào*) "to teach, to instruct" + 育 "to raise, to nurture, to bring up"
(养育, 培育). Teaching plus rearing — the whole formation of a person, hence
"education" as a system (义务教育) and "to educate." Also "to tell off":
被老师教育了一顿.

## Notes
教 is *jiào* here. 教育 the system / process; [[教学]] the activity of teaching;
[[教师]] the person.

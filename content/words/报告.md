## Why this combination
报 "to announce, to inform" and 告 "to tell, to inform" are close
synonyms — both name the act of formally passing information along —
paired for a more official register than either alone.

## Notes
报告 works as both verb ("to report [to a superior]") and noun ("a
report, a formal talk") — 做报告 "to give a talk/lecture" uses it in its
noun sense as a set, deliverable piece of speech or writing.

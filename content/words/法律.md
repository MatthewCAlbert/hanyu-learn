## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 法 ("law, method, rule" — as in 办法, 方法) and 律
("statute, regulated rule" — as in 规律, 律师) both independently name a
binding rule; pairing them produces the standard modern word for the
formal, codified body of law.

## Notes
律师 (lawyer) uses the same 律 half; 法 alone is the more general,
higher-frequency morpheme (appearing in 方法, 办法, 想法 with the unrelated
"method" sense) — context, not the character, decides which sense of 法 is
active.

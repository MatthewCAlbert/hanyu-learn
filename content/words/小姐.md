## Why this combination
姐 "elder sister" softened by 小 "young" into a term for a young unmarried
woman, historically a daughter of a good family (大小姐 "the young mistress").
Used after a surname as a courtesy title: 王小姐.

## Notes
Register has slipped: in parts of the mainland calling a stranger 小姐 can imply
"prostitute," so 女士 or 美女 is now safer. Context-sensitive.

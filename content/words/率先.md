## Why this combination

率 (here "to lead," a verbal sense distinct from its more familiar noun
role "rate," as in 效率) plus 先 ("first, before," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 事先) names leading by going first.

## Notes

率先垂范 ("to take the lead by setting an example") and 率先完成 ("to be
the first to finish") both build on this word — always implies both
being first and thereby leading others, distinct from plain 先, which
simply marks temporal order without the leadership implication.

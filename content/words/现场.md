## Why this combination
现 "present, current" and 场 "a place, a site" combine directly: the
actual site where something is currently happening or happened.

## Notes
现场 covers both the neutral "the site of an event" (事故现场 "accident
scene") and, as a modifier, "live, in person" (现场直播 "live broadcast")
— the physical site standing in for immediacy and authenticity.

## Why this combination
周 "week" (the 周 series: 周一…周日, [[上周]], [[下周]]) + 末 "end, tip, final
part" (末尾, 期末). The end of the week — Saturday and Sunday.

## Notes
周 and 星期 are interchangeable for "week"; 周末 is the standard word for
"weekend" in both. 末 "end" also in 月末, 年末, 世界末日.

## Why this combination

期 ("a term, a period," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
学期/期末) plus 中 ("middle," as in 中午) names the middle of a term:
midterm.

## Notes

期中考试 ("midterm exam") pairs directly with 期末考试 (covered elsewhere
in this app's 期末 entry, "final exam") as the two standard school-term
checkpoints.

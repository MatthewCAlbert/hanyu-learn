## Why this combination
印地 is a phonetic rendering of Hindi, plus 语. Not [[印度]] + 地 "land of India."
The extra 地 vs 度 is the difference between Hindi and India.

## Notes
Parallel [[印地文]]. Contrast [[印度]], [[印度尼西亚]].

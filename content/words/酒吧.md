## Why this combination
吧 is chosen purely for sound, transliterating the English word "bar" —
it carries no meaning of its own here (its more common job elsewhere is
as a sentence-final suggestion particle, 走吧, "let's go," an unrelated
grammatical use). 酒 ("alcohol") was added to make the meaning clear
beyond pure transliteration, giving a hybrid: half borrowed sound, half
native clarifying label.

Rated "semi" because 酒's contribution genuinely is meaningful, even
though 吧's is not — a true half-and-half construction.

## Notes
Do not read meaning into 吧 here — it is doing the same job as 发 in 沙发
(sofa), representing a foreign sound with no semantic content of its
own.

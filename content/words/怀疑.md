## Why this combination
怀 carries its "to harbor, to hold (a feeling) within" sense (as in 怀念,
"to cherish in memory"; 怀孕, "to carry [a pregnancy]"), and 疑 is "to
doubt." Holding doubt within oneself is exactly "to be suspicious of, to
doubt."

Rated "semi" for 怀's "to harbor a feeling" sense, distinct from its more
concrete "bosom, to carry" sense in other compounds.

## Notes
怀疑 covers both mild uncertainty ("to doubt whether...") and active
suspicion of wrongdoing ("to suspect someone of...") — context, not the
word itself, signals which shade is meant.

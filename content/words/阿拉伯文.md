## Why this combination
阿拉伯, the same stem as HSK 阿拉伯语, + 文 "writing, text." Parallel to [[英文]] /
阿拉伯语.

## Notes
阿拉伯语 is HSK and is the spoken-leaning name; 阿拉伯文 leans written / script.

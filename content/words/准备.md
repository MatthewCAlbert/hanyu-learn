## Why this combination
备 carries the weight: "to make ready, to equip, to have in reserve" (as in
设备, 备用). 准 adds "duly, in the proper way." The pair means getting things
duly ready, and by a short step "to intend to" — 我准备明天走.

## Notes
Both a verb (准备考试) and a noun (做好准备). The "plan to" sense is very common
and easy to miss for learners who only learn it as "prepare."

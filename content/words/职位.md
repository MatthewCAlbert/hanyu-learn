## Why this combination
职 "post, duty" and 位 "seat, rank" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for a job position.

## Notes
职位 names a specific role within an organization (高级职位 "a senior
position," 空缺职位 "a vacant post") — more formal and structural than
everyday 工作 "job/work."

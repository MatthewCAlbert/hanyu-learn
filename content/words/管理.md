## Why this combination
管 "to be in charge of, to take responsibility for" (as in 不管 "no
matter") combined with 理 "to put in order, to arrange, to attend to" (as
in 理发 "to arrange the hair, i.e. to get a haircut"): to take charge of
something and keep it in order — to manage.

## Notes
管理 covers overseeing people, organisations and systems alike
(管理公司 "to manage a company," 时间管理 "time management"); 经理
"manager" (the person who manages) is built directly on this same 理.

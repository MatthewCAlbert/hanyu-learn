## Why this combination
结 "to tie, to knot, to bind" (see also 结婚) combined with 合 "to
combine, to close together" (see also 联合, 配合): to bind and combine —
to integrate two things into one.

## Notes
结合 is used for combining two distinct elements into a working whole
(理论结合实际 "to combine theory with practice") and, more formally, for
a couple's union in marriage (两人结合 "the union of two people") —
组合, its near-neighbour, more often names the resulting combination
itself rather than the act of joining.

## Why this combination
模 "a mold, a model" and 范 "a pattern, an example" (see 范's own
etymology — a bamboo casting mold) are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for something held up as an example to follow.

## Notes
模范 anchors 劳动模范 "model worker," 模范丈夫 "an exemplary husband" —
a formal, often honorific title given to someone worth emulating, distinct
from the more neutral 榜样 "role model."

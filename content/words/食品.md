## Why this combination
食 "to eat, food" (as in 零食 "snacks") combined with 品 "an item, a
product" (see also 作品, 商品): food treated as a category of product —
foodstuffs, especially as sold or manufactured.

## Notes
食品 is the commercial and formal word for food products (食品安全
"food safety," 食品公司 "food company") — 食物, its close relative, is the
more general, everyday word for "food" as something eaten, without the
product/commerce framing.

## Why this combination
手 "hand, wrist" + 表, here "a gauge, a meter, a dial" (电表 electricity meter,
温度表) → a timepiece worn on the wrist, as against the older 怀表 "pocket
watch." 戴手表, 智能手表 "smartwatch."

## Notes
表 "dial / meter" is a different sense from 表 "surface / to express" ([[表示]]
not this level, 表面). 钟 is a clock; 表 the portable / wearable timepiece.

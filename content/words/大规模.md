## Why this combination

大 ("big") plus 规模 ("scale, scope," covered elsewhere in this app)
names something on a large scale.

## Notes

大规模 modifies actions and events with wide scope (大规模生产, "mass
production"; 大规模调查, "a large-scale investigation") — 小规模 ("small-
scale") is its direct antonym, built the same way.

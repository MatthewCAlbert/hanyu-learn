## Why this combination
品 (here "an item, a product") and 种 "type, kind" (also "seed," the
biological sense) combine into "a type of product/organism" — the
standard word for a specific variety within a category.

## Notes
品种 spans both biology (小麦品种 "wheat variety") and commerce (产品品种
"product range") — always a sub-type within a larger category, distinct
from 种类, which is category/kind more broadly.

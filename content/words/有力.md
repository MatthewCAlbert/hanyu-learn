## Why this combination
有 "to have" and 力 "strength" combine directly into "having strength" —
as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
有力 modifies both physical strength (有力的双手 "strong hands") and
persuasive force (有力的证据 "compelling evidence") — strength extended
from the physical to the rhetorical.

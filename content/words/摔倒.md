## Why this combination
摔 "to fall, to throw down" plus the resultative complement 倒 "to
topple, to fall over" gives exactly "to fall, ending up toppled."

## Notes
摔倒 anchors 摔倒在地 "to fall to the ground," 老人摔倒了 "the elderly
person fell" — the standard word for an accidental fall, distinct from
摔 alone, which can also mean "to throw (something) down" transitively.

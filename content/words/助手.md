## Why this combination
助 "to assist" and 手 (here the same metaphorical "hand" as in 高手,
"one who [does something]," not the body part) combine into "one who
lends a hand in assisting" — an assistant.

## Notes
助手 is a general, non-title word for anyone helping with a task (实验
助手 "lab assistant," 得力助手 "a capable right-hand [person]") — less
formal and title-bound than 助理.

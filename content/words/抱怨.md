## Why this combination
抱 "to hold, to embrace" is used metaphorically here — holding onto a
grievance the way one holds an object — combined with 怨 "resentment"
(see 怨's own etymology, 恚也, "resentment"). Together: carrying a grudge
and voicing it.

## Notes
抱怨 is the everyday verb for grumbling and complaint: 抱怨天气 "to
complain about the weather," 别抱怨了 "stop complaining" — milder than
formal 投诉 "to file a complaint [against a business/service]."

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 包 ("to wrap, to enclose," as in 包裹, "a
parcel") and 含 ("to contain, to hold in," as in 含有) both independently
name enclosing something within. Pairing them gives the broader,
formal word for inclusion.

## Notes

包含 works with abstract as well as concrete contents (这句话包含两层意思,
"this sentence contains two meanings") — broader in scope than 含有,
which more often names a specific ingredient or substance.

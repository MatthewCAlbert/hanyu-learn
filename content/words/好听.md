## Why this combination
好 "good" + 听 "to listen / hear" → good to hear. Part of the 好 + verb frame
with [[好看]], [[好吃]], 好玩. Of music, voices, names, and languages.

## Notes
Ironic 说得好听 "easier said than done / that's just talk." 难听 is the
opposite — and also means "coarse, offensive" of language.

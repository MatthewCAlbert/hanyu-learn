## Why this combination
注 (here "to focus, to concentrate," as in 注意) and 视 "to look, to gaze"
combine into "to focus one's gaze" — the standard word for sustained,
attentive looking.

## Notes
注视 is a written/literary register word: 目光注视着远方 "gazing into the
distance" — more formal and sustained than plain 看 or 望.

## Why this combination
向 "toward" and 前 "front, ahead" combine directly into "toward the
front" — as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
向前 covers literal movement (向前走 "to walk forward") and figurative
progress (向前看 "to look forward/be optimistic," 勇往直前 "to press
bravely onward [idiom]").

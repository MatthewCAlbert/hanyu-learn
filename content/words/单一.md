## Why this combination
单 "single" and 一 "one" are near-synonyms combined into the standard
word for something existing in only one form, lacking variety.

## Notes
单一 anchors 单一产品 "a single product (line)," 结构单一 "a uniform,
undiversified structure" — always implies a lack of variety, often with a
mild critical undertone.

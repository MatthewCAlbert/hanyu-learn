## Why this combination
评 "to evaluate, to judge" and 估 "to estimate" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for systematic assessment.

## Notes
评估 is the formal/technical register (风险评估 "risk assessment," 评估
报告 "assessment report") — more structured and official-sounding than
everyday 估计 "to guess/estimate."

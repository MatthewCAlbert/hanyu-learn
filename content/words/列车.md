## Why this combination

列 ("a row, a file, to arrange in order," as in 列出, "to list out")
plus 车 ("vehicle") names a vehicle made of cars arranged in a row: a
train.

## Notes

列车 is the formal/technical register used on schedules and
announcements (火车 is the everyday word); 列车员 ("train attendant") and
高铁列车 ("high-speed rail train") both build on this formal root.

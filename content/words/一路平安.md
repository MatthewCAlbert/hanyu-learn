## Why this combination
一路 "all the way, the whole journey" + [[平安]] "safe and sound" → a wish that
the traveller comes through the journey unharmed. Said on parting.

## Notes
一路平安 · [[一路顺风]] · 一路顺利 are interchangeable farewells; 一路平安 is
the safest for air travellers, since it does not mention wind.

## Why this combination
确 "certain, true, accurate" and 定 "to fix, to settle" combine
directly: fixing something so firmly that it becomes certain and beyond
doubt.

## Notes
确定 works as both a verb ("to confirm, to determine") and an adjective
("certain, definite") without changing form — 你确定吗？"Are you sure?"
versus 确定的答案 "a definite answer."

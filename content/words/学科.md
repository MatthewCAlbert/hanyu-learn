## Why this combination
学 "study, learning" plus 科 (here "a branch, a category," as in 科目)
gives exactly "a branch of learning" — the standard word for an academic
field.

## Notes
学科 anchors 学科建设 "discipline development (in academia)," 跨学科
"interdisciplinary" — used for formal academic fields, distinct from 课程
"course," which is a specific class taught within a discipline.

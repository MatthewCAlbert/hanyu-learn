## Why this combination
课 "lesson, course" + 本 "a bound volume" (see [[本子]]) → the book that holds
the lessons. Near-synonym 教科书 ("teaching-subject book") is the formal term.

## Notes
语文课本 "the Chinese-language textbook." Contrast [[课文]] — a single passage
*within* the textbook.

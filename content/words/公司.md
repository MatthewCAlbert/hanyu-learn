## Why this combination
公 "public, common" + 司 "to manage, an office / bureau" (as in 司令, 上司) → a
jointly-run enterprise. A 19th-century term for the Western joint-stock company,
where capital and management are held in common.

## Notes
分公司 "branch," 总公司 "head office," 上市公司 "listed company." 司 alone as
"bureau" in government (外交部…司).

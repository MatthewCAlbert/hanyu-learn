## Why this combination
制 "to fashion, to cut out [a pattern], to make" (see also 制造)
combined with 作 "to make, to create" (as in 作品 "a work"): to fashion
and create — a verb used for crafted, often skilled production rather
than mass manufacturing.

## Notes
制作 is used for producing something with craft or design involved —
制作节目 "to produce a [TV] programme," 手工制作 "handmade" — while 制造,
built on the same 制, leans towards industrial-scale manufacturing.

## Why this combination
清 "clear, clean" and 理 "to arrange, to put in order" combine directly:
clearing something out and putting what remains in order.

## Notes
清理 spans physical clutter (清理房间 "to tidy up a room") and
administrative backlogs (清理债务 "to settle debts," 清理数据 "to clean up
data") — always removing what's unwanted and organizing what stays.

## Why this combination
珍 "a treasure" (see 珍's own etymology) and 贵 "expensive, valuable" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for something precious.

## Notes
珍贵 describes both material worth (珍贵的文物 "precious artefacts") and
emotional value (珍贵的回忆 "precious memories") — value that goes beyond
mere monetary price.

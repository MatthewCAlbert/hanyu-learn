## Why this combination

舒 ("relaxed, to stretch out at ease," as in 舒服) plus 适 ("suitable,
fitting," as in 合适) names a state that is both at-ease and well-fitted:
comfortable.

Rated "semi" for 适's "fitting/suitable" contribution, one step removed
from the plainer physical-comfort sense of 舒.

## Notes

舒适 is the written/formal register (舒适的环境, "a comfortable
environment") — everyday speech reaches for plain 舒服 instead.

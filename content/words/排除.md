## Why this combination
排 "to arrange, to push aside" and 除 "to remove, to get rid of" combine
directly: pushing something aside and removing it entirely.

## Notes
排除 covers physical removal (排除故障 "to troubleshoot/eliminate a
fault") and logical elimination (排除可能性 "to rule out a possibility") —
always the deliberate elimination of an unwanted option or obstacle.

## Why this combination
独 ("alone, solitary," covered in this app's hanzi entry) plus 立 ("to
stand") names exactly what independence looks like: standing on one's
own, without support from others.

## Notes
独立 covers political independence (国家独立, national independence),
personal self-sufficiency (经济独立, financially independent), and simply
doing something unaided (独立完成, "to complete [something] independently")
— all variations on the same "standing alone" image.

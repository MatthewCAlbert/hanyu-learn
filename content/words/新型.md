## Why this combination

新 ("new") plus 型 ("a type, a model," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 小型/型号) names a newly designed type of something.

## Notes

新型冠状病毒 ("novel coronavirus," lit. "new-type crown-shaped virus") and
新型材料 ("new-type materials") both build on this word — 新型 marks a
category as newly developed, distinct from 新, which can modify anything
simply recently made or acquired.

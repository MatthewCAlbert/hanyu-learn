## Why this combination
紧 "tight" doubled for adverbial rhythm and mild intensification gives
"tightly, closely" — the reduplication smooths the word into a natural
adverb, as is common when single adjectives double for adverbial use.

## Notes
紧紧 modifies verbs of holding and following closely: 紧紧抓住 "to hold
tightly onto," 紧紧跟随 "to closely follow" — a common intensified adverb
in narrative description.

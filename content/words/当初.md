## Why this combination
当, here in its "this very, precisely this" sense (see also 当时, 当地,
当中) combined with 初 "the beginning, the start" (as in 最初 "at
first"): precisely at the beginning — back then, originally.

## Notes
当初 looks back from the present to an earlier starting point, often with
a note of hindsight or regret (早知如此，当初就不该... "had I known,
I shouldn't have... back then") — 当时, its close relative, refers
neutrally to "at that [later] time" without this beginning-point,
hindsight framing.

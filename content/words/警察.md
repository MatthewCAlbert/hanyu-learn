## Why this combination
警 "to warn, to be alert to danger" combined with 察 "to inspect, to
examine closely" (as in 观察 "to observe"): those who stay alert and
watch closely [to keep order] — the police, a modern compound coined for
the institution rather than inherited from classical usage.

## Notes
警察 names both the institution and an individual officer; 警 alone
survives productively in compounds built after this word — 报警 "to
report to the police," 警车 "police car," 警官 "police officer [rank]."

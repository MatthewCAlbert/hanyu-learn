## Why this combination
The 大 / 中 / 小 school series: 大学 the top, 中学 the middle, 小学 the entry
level for young children. (Confusingly, classical 小學 also named the study of
characters and philology — a separate scholarly sense.)

## Notes
小学生 "primary pupil," 小学老师. Six years in the mainland system, feeding into
中学.

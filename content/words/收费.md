## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 收 "to receive, to collect" (as in 收入
"income") takes 费 "a fee, an expense" (see also 费用, 浪费) as its object
— literally "to collect a fee" — to charge.

## Notes
收费 works as verb ("这里收费吗" "is there a charge here?") and, in
compounds, as a modifier ("收费站" "toll station," "免费" the negated form
"free of charge," literally "exempt from fee") — 免费 is the everyday
antonym pattern worth knowing alongside it.

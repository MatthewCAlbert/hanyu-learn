## Why this combination
劳 "to toil" (see 劳's own etymology — effort that burns on like a fire)
and 动 "to move, to act" combine directly: exerting effort through
physical action.

## Notes
劳动 anchors 劳动节 "Labour Day," 体力劳动 "physical/manual labour," 劳动力
"labour force" — a formal, often political-economic register for work,
distinct from the everyday, general 工作 "job/work."

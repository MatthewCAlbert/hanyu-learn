## Why this combination

分 ("to divide," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 分为) plus
之 (a classical possessive/genitive particle, fossilized in many fixed
formal phrases) fuses into the grammatical marker for fractions: "out
of," read between a denominator and numerator.

Rated "semi": 之's classical grammatical role is opaque without prior
exposure to other fixed 之-phrases, though the overall pattern is
mechanical once learned.

## Notes

百分之五十 ("fifty percent, lit. 100-分之-50") reads as "50 out of 100" —
分之 always sits between the denominator (said first) and numerator
(said second), the reverse order from English fraction reading.

## Why this combination
Neither character carries "surprise" on its own — 居 "to dwell, to occupy
a position" and 然 "thus, in this way" combine into a fixed adverb whose
"unexpectedly" sense has to be learned as a whole rather than assembled
from the parts.

## Notes
居然 marks the speaker's own surprise at a fact turning out true: 他居然
及格了 "he actually passed [surprisingly]." Close in sense to 竟然, its more
common near-synonym.

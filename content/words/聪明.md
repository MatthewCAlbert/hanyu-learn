## Why this combination
聪 (keen hearing/perception) and 明 (bright, clear-minded) both describe
sharpness of mind from slightly different angles — perceiving keenly, and
thinking clearly — and combine into the everyday word for intelligence.

## Notes
聪明 is the most common compliment for intelligence in Mandarin, spanning
book-smarts and street-smarts alike; 小聪明 "petty cleverness" narrows it
to a mildly critical sense — clever in a shallow, self-serving way.

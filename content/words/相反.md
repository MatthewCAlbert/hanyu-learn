## Why this combination
相 ("mutually, each other," as in 相信, 相处) plus 反 ("reverse, opposite,"
as in 反对) names two things standing in mutual reversal of each other:
opposites.

## Notes
相反 works as both adjective (相反的意见, "opposing views") and sentence
connector ("on the contrary," 我不但不生气，相反很高兴, "I wasn't angry —
on the contrary, I was happy") without changing form.

## Why this combination
经 "to manage, to administer" (see also 经济, 经验) combined with 营 "to
seek, to build up, to plan and construct" (as in 营养 in an unrelated
compound, and military 军营 "camp," from "to build/establish"): to
manage and build up — to run a business.

## Notes
经营 is the standard verb for operating a business or enterprise
(经营一家餐馆 "to run a restaurant," 经营策略 "business strategy") — 管理,
its near-neighbour, is the more general word for "to manage/administer"
and applies beyond just running a commercial enterprise.

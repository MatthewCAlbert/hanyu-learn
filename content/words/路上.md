## Why this combination
路 "road" + locative 上 "on." Both the literal road surface (路上有车) and the
period of travelling (路上小心 "take care on your way," 在路上 "en route").

## Notes
上 as a bare locative suffix, like 身上, 车上, 网上 — not "up."

## Why this combination
曾 "once, already" (recall its etymology: originally a steaming vessel,
later borrowed purely for this grammatical sense) carries the core
meaning. 经 here is not "classics" but its extended verbal sense "to
pass through, to experience" — something one has, at some point, lived
through.

## Notes
曾经 marks a past experience without implying it continues now (曾经
爱过 "once loved [but not now]") — distinct from 已经 "already," which
marks a completed action with present relevance.

## Why this combination

尾 ("a tail") plus the nominal suffix 巴 (as in 嘴巴, "mouth," covered
elsewhere in this app) names the everyday body part: a tail.

## Notes

尾巴 covers a literal animal's tail and, figuratively, a trailing loose
end (留个尾巴, "to leave a loose end") — 结尾 ("an ending," lit. "knot +
tail") shares its 尾 root for the figurative "end of something" sense.

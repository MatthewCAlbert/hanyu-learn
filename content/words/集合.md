## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 集 ("to gather, to collect," as in 集体,
"collective") and 合 ("to combine, to join," as in 合作) both
independently name bringing things together. Pairing them gives the
standard word for assembling.

## Notes

集合 gives the command to gather (在这里集合, "assemble here") and, in
math class, names a "set" (集合论, "set theory") — the same underlying
"gathering together" image applies to both people and abstract elements.

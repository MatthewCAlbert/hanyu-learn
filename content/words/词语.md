## Why this combination
词 "a word (one or more characters forming a unit)" + 语 "language, an
expression, a saying" (see [[语言]], [[汉语]], 成语) → words and set phrases
taken together — the lexical items of a text or lesson: 生词和词语, 常用词语.

## Notes
字 = a single character; 词 = a word; 词语 = words and phrases as a group;
[[句子]] = a sentence. 词典 (see [[词典]]) "dictionary of words."

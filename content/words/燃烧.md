## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 燃 ("to burn," as in 燃料, "fuel" — covered
elsewhere in this app as one half of the 然/燃 pair, where 然 kept the
abstract "so, thus" sense while 燃, marked with the fire radical 火, split
off to carry the literal "to burn" meaning) and 烧 ("to burn, to heat,"
as in 发烧, "to run a fever") both independently name fire consuming
something. Pairing them gives the standard word for combustion.

## Notes

燃烧 is the more technical/scientific register for combustion (燃烧
反应, "combustion reaction") — everyday casual burning more often uses
plain 烧 alone.

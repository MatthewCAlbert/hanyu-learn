## Why this combination
周 "a full cycle, to go round, complete" (周末, 一周) + 年 "year" → a year come
full circle. 十周年 "the tenth anniversary," 结婚三周年.

## Notes
周 "complete circuit" also in [[周末]] (the turn of the week), 周围 "all
around," 周到 "thorough." 纪念日 is "an anniversary (of an event)" without a
number.

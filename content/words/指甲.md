## Why this combination
指 "finger" plus 甲 (here "a hard covering, a nail" — the same 甲 as in
armor) gives exactly "the hard covering on a finger."

## Notes
指甲 anchors 剪指甲 "to trim one's nails," 指甲油 "nail polish" — the
everyday body-part word; 脚趾甲 specifies the toenail when the distinction
matters.

## Why this combination
工作 "work" plus 日 "day" gives exactly "a day for working" — a
straightforward compositional word.

## Notes
工作日 anchors 工作日上午 "on a weekday morning," 三个工作日内 "within
three business days" — opposite of 周末 "weekend," the standard term in
scheduling and business contexts.

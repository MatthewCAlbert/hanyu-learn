## Why this combination
人 "person" plus 力 "strength, power" gives exactly "the strength of
people" — the standard word for labor as a resource, parallel to 物力
"material resources" and 财力 "financial resources."

## Notes
人力 anchors 人力资源 "human resources," 人力成本 "labor cost" — always the
economic/organizational sense, distinct from 体力, which is an
individual's physical strength.

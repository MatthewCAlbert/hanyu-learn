## Why this combination
能 "can, to be able to" immediately followed by its own negation, 不能
"cannot": the standard A-not-A pattern (能不能, 是不是, 好不好) that forms
a yes/no question directly from a verb, without needing a final 吗.

## Notes
能不能 is not a fixed lexical word so much as a productive grammatical
pattern applied to 能 — 能不能帮我 "can you help me?" asks the same
question as 你能帮我吗?, with the A-not-A form favoured in more direct,
immediate requests.

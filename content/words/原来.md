## Why this combination
原 "origin, the original state" + 来 "from (that point), coming along." Two
senses: "originally / formerly" (原来住在南方) and the discovery marker
"so that's it / it turns out" (原来是你！) — realising the real state of things.

## Notes
The "aha" sense is very common in speech: 原来如此 "so that's how it is." Near
原本, 本来 for the "originally" sense.

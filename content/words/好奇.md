## Why this combination
好, here "to be fond of, to like" (as in 爱好, read hào rather than
hǎo) combined with 奇 "strange, remarkable" (see also 奇怪): to be drawn
to what is strange — curious.

## Notes
好奇 describes an eager interest in the unfamiliar (好奇心 "curiosity,"
对...好奇 "curious about...") — it is always about wanting to know or
explore something new, distinct from 感兴趣 "interested in," which is
more neutral about novelty.

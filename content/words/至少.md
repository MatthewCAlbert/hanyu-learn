## Why this combination
至 here carries its extended "to the utmost degree" sense (as in 至今
"up until now") rather than plain "to arrive," paired with 少 "few,
little": reaching down to the smallest acceptable amount — at least.

## Notes
至少 sets a lower bound on a claim or requirement — 至少要三天 "it will
take at least three days" — the mirror-image counterpart to 至多 "at
most," which sets the upper bound instead.

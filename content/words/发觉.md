## Why this combination
发 (here "to discover, to bring to light") and 觉 "to sense, to perceive"
are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for becoming aware of
something.

## Notes
发觉 often implies noticing something previously hidden or unnoticed: 发觉
不对劲 "to sense something's wrong," 我发觉他在撒谎 "I realized he was
lying" — close to 发现, though 发觉 leans more toward perception than
active discovery.

## Why this combination
收 "to collect, to receive, to take in" + resultative 到 "reach" — the thing
sent got to you. 收到信 "got the letter," 收到消息.

## Notes
收到 = physically / actually get something. Contrast [[接受]] (accept an idea or
offer), [[受到]] (be subjected to an action). 收 alone: [[收入]], 收拾.

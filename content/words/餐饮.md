## Why this combination
餐 "meal" and 饮 "drink" are near-synonyms combined into the standard
industry term for food and drink service.

## Notes
餐饮 anchors 餐饮业 "the catering/restaurant industry," 餐饮服务 "food
service" — the formal, commercial-register word, where everyday speech
would just say 吃喝 or name specific dishes.

## Why this combination
摩 "to rub, to grind" and 擦 "to rub, to wipe" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for physical friction, extended figuratively to
interpersonal friction.

## Notes
摩擦 names the physics phenomenon (摩擦力 "frictional force") and,
figuratively, low-level conflict between people or groups: 产生摩擦 "to
create friction" — the same physical-to-social metaphor as English
"friction."

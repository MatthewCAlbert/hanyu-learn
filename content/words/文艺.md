## Why this combination
文 "literature, writing" and 艺 "art" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard umbrella term for the arts broadly.

## Notes
文艺 anchors 文艺晚会 "a variety/arts performance evening," 文艺青年 "an
artsy/literary young person (a cultural stereotype)" — covers literature,
performance, and visual arts collectively.

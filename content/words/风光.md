## Why this combination
风 (here "scenery, atmosphere," as in 风景) and 光 "light, view" combine
into "the scenery/atmosphere of a place." The figurative sense — a person
being "impressive" or "in the spotlight" — extends visual splendor to
social standing, a step beyond the literal.

## Notes
风光 names literal scenery (风光秀丽 "beautiful scenery") and,
colloquially, a person's glory or prestige (他现在很风光 "he's doing
great/is in the spotlight now") — the figurative sense is common in
speech.

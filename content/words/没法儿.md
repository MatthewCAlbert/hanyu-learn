## Why this combination

没 ("to not have") plus 法 ("a method, a way," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 语法) plus the retroflex diminutive suffix 儿
names having no method available: no way, can't help it.

## Notes

没法儿说 ("there's no way to explain it, words fail") and 真没法儿
("truly nothing can be done") both build on this word — the everyday
casual counterpart to the more formal 没有办法.

## Why this combination
月 "moon" + 亮 "bright, light" → "the bright (thing in the night sky)." The 亮
distinguishes the celestial body from 月 "month" (the moon's other, calendrical
job). 月亮很圆 "the moon is full."

## Notes
Literary / poetic synonyms: 明月, 月. 月 "month" also in [[月份]], [[月末]],
下个月.

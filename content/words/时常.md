## Why this combination
时 "time" and 常 "often, constantly" combine into a time adverb meaning
"often" — parallel in structure to 时时 "constantly" and 常常 "often."

## Notes
时常 is largely interchangeable with 常常 and 经常: 我时常想起他 "I often
think of him" — a written/spoken adverb with no strong register
difference from its near-synonyms.

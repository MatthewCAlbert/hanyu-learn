## Why this combination
周 "week" + 上 "previous" — the same temporal 上 as [[上次]], 上个月. The set is
上周 / 这周 / [[下周]]. 周 and 星期 are interchangeable, so 上周 = 上星期.

## Notes
周 "week" also in [[周末]], [[周年]], 周一…周日.

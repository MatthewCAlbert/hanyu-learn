## Why this combination
精 "the refined essence [of something]" and 神 "spirit, deity, that
which animates" combine directly: the essential, animating core of a
person or idea — the mind or spirit as opposed to the body or letter of
something.

## Notes
Two readings carry different senses: *jīngshén* (neutral tone on 神) is
the noun "spirit, mind, energy" (他很有精神 "he's full of energy");
*jīngshen* (light tone) leans toward "lively, in good spirits" as a
simple adjective.

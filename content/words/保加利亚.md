## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Bulgaria (保加利亞). 保, 加, 利 and 亚 are
chosen for sound. 保 is not "to protect."

## Notes
Same stem in [[保加利亚语]]. 加 as in [[加拿大]], [[新加坡]].

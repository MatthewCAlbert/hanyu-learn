## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 经 "to pass through, to undergo" + 过 "to cross, to pass." As a
verb, to go past a place (经过学校) or through a process (经过讨论 "after
discussion"); as a noun, the course of an event (事情的经过).

## Notes
经过 + time / effort → result is a common written frame, close to "through /
after." 经 also in [[已经]], [[经常]], [[经理]].

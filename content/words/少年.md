## Why this combination
少 (*shào*) "young" + 年 "years, age" → the young years, specifically early
adolescence — below 青年 (see [[青年]]) and above 儿童. 少年宫 "children's
palace," 少年时代.

## Notes
少 is *shào* here (young), not *shǎo* "few." The literary 少年 can also mean a
young man in general (翩翩少年).

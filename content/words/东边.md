## Why this combination
东 "east" + the locative suffix 边 — one of the four compass members of the 边
series (东/南/西/北 + 边). 东面 / 东部 are the 面 / 部 synonyms.

## Notes
东 also "host" (做东 "stand treat") and, clipped, "thing" in [[东西]] — a
reminder that the compound there is idiomatic, not literal.

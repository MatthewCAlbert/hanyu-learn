## Why this combination

西 ("west," as in 西方) plus 瓜 ("a melon, a gourd," a character
occurring mainly in this role) names a melon that reached China from
the west (Central Asia, in antiquity): watermelon.

Rated "semi" for 西's historical-geographic origin story, not obvious
from the character alone without that background.

## Notes

西瓜 shares its 瓜 "melon" root with 南瓜 ("pumpkin, lit. south melon")
and 黄瓜 ("cucumber, lit. yellow melon") — a small family of melons
named by color or origin direction.

## Why this combination
无 "without" negating 疑 "doubt" gives exactly "without doubt" — as
transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
无疑 is a formal written-register adverb (毫无疑问, its fuller cousin, adds
毫 "the slightest" for extra emphasis) — everyday speech more often uses
一定/肯定 for certainty.

## Why this combination
姑, originally "father's sister, an unmarried woman," combined with 娘
"a young woman" (also, elsewhere, "mother," as in 姑娘's near-opposite
老娘): two words for a woman, worn down through use into the ordinary
term for "girl, young woman" rather than retaining either character's
original narrower sense.

## Notes
姑娘 names a girl or young unmarried woman generally (小姑娘 "little
girl"), distinct from 姑 alone, which now survives mainly in kinship
terms like 姑姑/姑妈 "paternal aunt."

## Why this combination
小 "small" + 声 "voice, sound" (see [[声音]], [[大声]]) → a small voice =
quietly. Adverbial: 小声说话, 小声一点儿 "keep it down." Chinese sizes volume as
大 / 小, exactly mirroring [[大声]].

## Notes
小声 (quietly) ↔ [[大声]] (loudly). 悄悄 "stealthily / on the quiet" is about
secrecy rather than volume.

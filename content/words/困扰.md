## Why this combination
困 "trapped, difficulty" and 扰 "to disturb" (see 扰's own etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a persistent,
unsettling problem.

## Notes
困扰 describes an ongoing, nagging problem (深受困扰 "deeply troubled,"
心理困扰 "psychological distress") — a sustained state, distinct from a
single momentary 打扰 "disturbance."

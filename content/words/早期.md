## Why this combination
早 "early" and 期 "period" (see 期's role in 期望) combine directly: the
early stage of a longer span of time.

## Notes
早期 anchors 早期治疗 "early treatment," 早期作品 "early works [of an
artist]" — always marking the initial phase of a longer process or
career.

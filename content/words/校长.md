## Why this combination
校 (*xiào*) "school" (see [[学校]], [[校园]]) + 长 (*zhǎng*) "head, chief" — the
"-chief" suffix of [[队长]], [[家长]], [[市长]], [[院长]]. The head of a school
or university.

## Notes
校 is *xiào* here. 长 *zhǎng* "head" throughout the title series; contrast
长 *cháng* "long."

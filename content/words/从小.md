## Why this combination
从 "from (a starting point)" + 小 "young, little" → "starting from when one was
small." An adverb: 从小在这里长大, 从小就喜欢音乐.

## Notes
从 "from" also in 从…到…, 从来 "ever / always." 从小 ≈ [[小时候]] but adverbial
and stressing "ever since then."

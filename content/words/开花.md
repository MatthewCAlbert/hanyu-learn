## Why this combination

开 ("to open," as in 打开) takes 花 ("a flower," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 鲜花) as its object: a flower opening — to bloom.

## Notes

树开花了 ("the tree has bloomed") is the literal everyday sense;
figuratively, 遍地开花 ("blossoming everywhere, lit. blooming across the
whole ground") extends the image to widespread success or proliferation.

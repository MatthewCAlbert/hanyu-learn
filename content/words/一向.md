## Why this combination
一 "one" and 向 "direction" combine into "[heading in] one direction [the
whole time]" — a consistent, unchanging course extended to describe
consistent behaviour over time.

## Notes
一向 describes a habit or trait that has held steady up to now: 他一向
很守时 "he's always been punctual" — implying "as far back as relevant,"
distinct from 总是 "always," which doesn't carry that same "up to now"
framing.

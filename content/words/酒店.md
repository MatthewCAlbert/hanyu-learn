## Why this combination
酒 "wine, liquor" + 店 "shop, establishment." Historically a place to drink and
eat (and lodge for the night), like an English "inn." Modern large hotels took
the name, so 酒店 today mostly means "hotel" — 五星级酒店.

## Notes
For lodging: 酒店 / 宾馆 / [[饭店]] (see the note there). For eating out:
[[饭馆]], 餐厅. Context and scale disambiguate.

## Why this combination
球 "ball" + 队 "team, squad, line" → a team for a ball game: 足球队, 篮球队,
国家队. 队 also in [[排队]] (queue), [[队长]] (captain), 队员 "team member."

## Notes
For non-ball sports the word is just 队 with the sport: 游泳队 "swimming team."

## Why this combination
推 "to push" (see also 推动, 推进) combined with the resultative
complement 开 "open, apart" (see also 打开, 解开): to push [something]
so it opens or comes apart — to push open, or to push away.

## Notes
推开 marks that pushing succeeded in opening or separating something
(推开门 "to push the door open," 把他推开 "to push him away") — the
resultative 开 confirms the effect, whether literal (a door) or
figurative (推开责任 "to push away/reject responsibility").

## Why this combination
外 "outside" combined with the locative suffix 面 — the same
"direction word + 面" pattern as 里面, 上面, 后面, 前面 — naming the
space outside something.

## Notes
外面 is the exact synonym of 外边 (see [[外边]]) and additionally covers
"outward appearance" in writing (外面看起来很好 "on the surface it looks
fine"); 外边 stays closer to the plain spatial sense.

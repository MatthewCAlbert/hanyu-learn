## Why this combination
好 "good" + 看 "to look at" → good to look at. 好 + a verb is a productive frame
for "pleasant to —": 好吃 (tasty), 好听 (nice to hear), 好玩 (fun). Applies to
people, clothes, films and books alike.

## Notes
Ironic use 让你好看 "I'll make things nasty for you," and 面子 sense 给我好看
"make me look good" — both context, not separate words.

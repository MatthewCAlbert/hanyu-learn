## Why this combination
记 "to record, to remember" and 忆 "to recall" are near-synonyms,
combined for the formal noun/verb "memory, to remember."

## Notes
记忆 is more formal/abstract than everyday 记得 "to remember [a fact]" —
记忆力 "memory [capacity]," 记忆中 "in one's memory" both lean toward the
noun sense.

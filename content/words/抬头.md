## Why this combination
抬 "to raise, to lift" acting on the object 头 "head" gives exactly "to
raise one's head" — splittable, as in 抬起头. The figurative sense
("a trend regaining strength," 通货膨胀抬头 "inflation is rearing its
head") extends the physical gesture to something previously suppressed
becoming visible again.

## Notes
抬头 also names, as a noun, the addressee/payee line on a check or
letterhead — a separate, specialized sense that shares the characters but
not the verb-object logic.

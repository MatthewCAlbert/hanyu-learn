## Why this combination
摩托 transliterates English "motor" (mó-tuō approximates the sound); 摩
"to rub" and 托 "to support" were chosen for their sounds, not their
meanings, the same principle as 巧克力 "chocolate" and 咖啡 "coffee."

## Notes
摩托 is now mostly used short for 摩托车 "motorcycle": 骑摩托 "to ride a
motorbike" — rarely appears meaning "motor" alone outside this compound
today.

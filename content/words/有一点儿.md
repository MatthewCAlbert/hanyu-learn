## Why this combination
有 "there is" + [[一点儿]] "a little" → the fuller form of [[有点儿]]. Before an
adjective or verb it is a hedge, generally about something unwelcome:
有一点儿冷, 有一点儿担心.

## Notes
有一点儿 / [[有点儿]] (a hedge: 有点儿贵) vs. adjective + 一点儿 (a comparison:
贵一点儿). Non-erhua 有一点 is the same.

## Why this combination

长 ("long") plus 途 ("a path, a way," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 前途/途中/用途) names a long path: long-distance.

## Notes

长途汽车 ("a long-distance bus/coach") and 长途电话 ("a long-distance
phone call") both build on this word — a modifier for anything that
covers a long stretch of distance.

## Why this combination
表 "to display, to show outwardly" (see also 表示, 表演) combined with
达 "to convey, to get through to, to reach" (as in 达到): to display
something so that it reaches [another person] — to express.

## Notes
表达 takes thoughts and feelings as its object (表达感情 "to express
feelings," 表达能力 "expressive ability/command of language") — it names
the act of putting an inner state into words or form, distinct from 说
"to say," which is not limited to conveying something inward.

## Why this combination
些 is the indefinite-plural word "some"; 一些 is its standalone form for "a
certain amount" — 一些人, 一些问题. After an adjective it means "a bit more":
好一些 "a bit better."

## Notes
些 also cliticises onto demonstratives: [[这些]], 那些, 哪些. 一些 is the version
that stands free without 这/那.

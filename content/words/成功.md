## Why this combination
成 "to complete, to finish" and 功 "achievement, meritorious result"
reinforce each other directly: bringing an effort to completion and
having it count as a real achievement.

## Notes
成功 works as both verb ("to succeed") and noun ("success") without
changing form — 他成功了 "he succeeded" versus 他的成功 "his success."

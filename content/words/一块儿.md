## Why this combination
块 "a lump, a chunk" + 一 → "in one lump," i.e. all together in one spot. The
colloquial, northern equivalent of [[一起]]: 一块儿去吧 "let's go together."

## Notes
The non-erhua 一块 also means "one yuan" (coll.) and "a piece" — same
characters, different senses split by context and the 儿.

## Why this combination
多 "much, many" + 云 "cloud" → plenty of cloud about. A fixed forecast label,
between 晴 "clear" and 阴 "overcast": 晴转多云 "clearing, then cloudy,"
多云间晴.

## Notes
The forecast scale: 晴 → 多云 → 阴 ([[阴天]]) → 雨. 云 "cloud" also in 白云,
云彩.

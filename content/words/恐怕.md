## Why this combination
恐 "to fear, to dread" (as in 恐怖 "terror") and 怕 "to fear, to be
afraid of" (see also 害怕) are close synonyms, paired into a
grammaticalized hedging adverb — "I'm afraid [that], probably" — that
softens an unwelcome guess or prediction.

## Notes
恐怕 opens a clause to hedge bad news or an uncertain guess (恐怕要下雨了
"I'm afraid it's going to rain," 他恐怕不会来了 "I'm afraid he's probably
not coming") — unlike its literal components, it carries no strong fear
in this use, only a polite softening of an unwelcome likelihood.

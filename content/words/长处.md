## Why this combination
长, here read cháng "strong, excelling" (as in 所长, distinct from
cháng "long") combined with 处 "a place, an aspect" (as in 好处
"benefit," literally "a good place/aspect"): the aspect where one
excels — a strong point.

## Notes
长处 names a person's strength or good quality (发挥长处 "to make the
most of one's strengths") — its opposite is 短处 "shortcoming, weak
point," built the same way with 短 "short" in place of 长.

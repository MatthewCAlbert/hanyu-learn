## Why this combination
红 "red" combined with 茶 "tea": tea named for the reddish color of its
liquor once brewed — what English calls "black tea," named instead in
Chinese for the color of the steeped tea rather than the dry leaf.

## Notes
红茶 is the fully-oxidized tea variety, contrasted with 绿茶 "green tea,"
its close relative and near-opposite in processing — the English/Chinese
color-naming mismatch (leaf color vs. liquor color) is a well-known
translation trap.

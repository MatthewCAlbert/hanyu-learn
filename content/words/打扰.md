## Why this combination
打 here is a light verb contributing little independent meaning (as in
打扮, 打算) paired with 扰 "to disturb" (see 扰's own etymology) — together,
the everyday verb for disturbing or bothering someone.

## Notes
打扰 is the standard polite phrase for interrupting someone: 打扰一下
"excuse me for a moment," 不好意思打扰你了 "sorry to bother you."

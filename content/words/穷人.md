## Why this combination

穷 ("poor," as in 贫穷, "impoverished") plus 人 ("person") names a person
who is poor.

## Notes

穷人 contrasts with 富人 ("a rich person," lit. "rich + person") as the
standard pair for describing economic classes — both built the same way
from an adjective plus 人.

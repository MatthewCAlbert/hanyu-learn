## Why this combination
出 "to go out, to leave" + 院, clipped from [[医院]] "hospital" (see [[住院]],
[[学院]]) → to leave hospital as a recovered patient. Verb-object:
出了院. Opposite [[住院]] "be admitted."

## Notes
院 as clipped "hospital" also in [[住院]], 出院手续 "discharge formalities."

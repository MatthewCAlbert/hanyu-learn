## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 土 ("earth, soil," as in 土壤) and 地 ("ground,
land," as in 地方, 地球) both independently name the earth underfoot.
Pairing them gives the standard noun for land as territory or farmable
ground.

## Notes
土地 leans toward land as a resource or territory (土地资源, 农业土地),
while 地 alone or 地方/地球 cover the broader senses "place" and "the
planet Earth."

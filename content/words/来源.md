## Why this combination
来 ("to come") plus 源 ("a source," covered elsewhere in this app — split
off from 原 once that character drifted abstract) names the place
something comes from: its source.

## Notes
来源 works as both noun (资金来源, "source of funds") and verb (这个词
来源于拉丁语, "this word originates from Latin") without changing form.

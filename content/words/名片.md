## Why this combination

名 ("name," as in 名人, "a famous person," covered elsewhere in this
app) plus 片 ("a flat piece, a card," as in 照片/唱片, both covered
elsewhere in this app) names a card bearing one's name: a business
card.

## Notes

交换名片 ("to exchange business cards") is a standard networking phrase —
名片 shares its 片 "flat card" root with 照片 (photo) and 唱片 (record), all
naming thin, flat objects.

## Why this combination
定 "to fix, to settle, to determine" (see also 决定, 规定) combined with
期 "a period, a term" (see also 长期, 短期): a period fixed in advance —
regular, periodic.

## Notes
定期 modifies actions repeated at fixed intervals (定期检查 "regular
inspection," 定期存款 "fixed-term deposit") — it stresses a schedule
fixed by design, unlike 经常 "often," which describes mere frequency
without implying a set schedule.

## Why this combination

二 ("two") plus 手 ("hand") names something that has passed through a
second hand before reaching its current owner: second-hand.

Rated "semi" for the metaphorical "hand = owner" extension, a calque
close to English's own "second-hand."

## Notes

二手车 ("a used car") and 二手房 ("a resale home, lit. second-hand house")
both build on this word — always modifies goods previously owned by
someone else, parallel in logic to the English idiom "second-hand."

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Afghan (阿富汗). The -stan of Afghanistan
is not written. 富 is not "rich"; 汗 is not "sweat" (it is the historical 可汗
syllable).

## Notes
Contrast [[哈萨克斯坦]], where 斯坦 is kept.

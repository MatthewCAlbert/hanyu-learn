## Why this combination
过 "to pass, to spend (time), to observe (a festival)" (过节, 过生日) + 年
"year" as its object — "to see the year through / over," i.e. to keep the New
Year. Verb-object: 过了个好年. 回家过年 "go home for New Year."

## Notes
过年 = the celebration (≈ 过春节); [[新年]] leans toward the calendrical turn.
过 "observe (a festival)" also in 过节, 过生日.

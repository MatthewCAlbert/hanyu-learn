## Why this combination
高 "tall" and 大 "big" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for something impressively tall and large.

## Notes
高大 modifies people, buildings, and trees: 高大的建筑 "a towering
building," 身材高大 "tall and imposing physique" — implies both height
and substantial bulk, unlike 高 alone, which can describe something tall
but slender.

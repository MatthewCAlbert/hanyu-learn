## Why this combination
叫 "to call, to be named" + 作 "as, to be" (the same 作 = "as" in 当作, 看作,
称作) → "to call (it) as," i.e. to be called. 这种花叫作牵牛花. Also written
叫做.

## Notes
叫作 (a touch more formal) ≈ bare 叫 (这叫什么？). 作 "as" in the "regard-as"
family: 当作, 看作, 称作.

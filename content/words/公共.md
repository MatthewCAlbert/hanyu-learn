## Why this combination
公 "public, communal" (see also 公司, 公开) and 共 "shared, in common,
together" (see also 共同) are close synonyms, paired into the standard
adjective for anything belonging to or used by the community.

## Notes
公共 modifies facilities and spaces shared by everyone (公共汽车 "public
bus," 公共场所 "public places") — it always precedes a noun as a modifier
and, unlike 公开 "open, made public," does not mean "not secret."

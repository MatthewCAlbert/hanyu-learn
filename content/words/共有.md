## Why this combination
共 "together, shared, in common" (see also 共同, 公共) combined with 有
"to have": to have all together — to total, or to hold jointly.

## Notes
共有 sits before a number to give a grand total (共有一百人 "there are
a hundred people in total") and, in legal/formal contexts, names joint
ownership (共有财产 "jointly-owned property") — the two senses share
the core idea of a quantity or holding shared/summed across a group.

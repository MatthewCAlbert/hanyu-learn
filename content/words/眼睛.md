## Why this combination
眼 "eye" + 睛 "eyeball, pupil" (as in 画龙点睛 "add the pupil to the dragon"). A
near-synonym pair for the everyday body-part noun. 睛 barely occurs outside this
word.

## Notes
眼睛 the organ; 眼 alone in compounds — 眼泪 tears, 眼镜 glasses, 眼光 vision /
judgement.

## Why this combination
面 "face" + 前 "front" → the space directly before someone's face. Physical
(站在我面前) and figurative (在困难面前 "in the face of difficulty,"
在事实面前).

## Notes
面前 = right before a person; [[前边]] / 前面 = the front side of anything;
[[对面]] = directly opposite.

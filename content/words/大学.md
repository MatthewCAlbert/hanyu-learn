## Why this combination
"Great learning" — the title of the classic 《大學》, higher education as
opposed to elementary study. Reused in the modern era for the Western-style
university. The 大 / 中 / 小 series then filled in 中学 and 小学 below it.

## Notes
上大学 "go to / attend university." 大学生 university student, 大学城 campus
district.

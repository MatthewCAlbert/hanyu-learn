## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 认 "to recognise, to identify" + 识 "to know, to discern." The
word is knowing-by-recognition — people, places, written characters: 我认识他,
我认识这个字.

## Notes
Split from [[知道]] (know a fact). "Do you know him?" is 你认识他吗; "do you know
he's here?" is 你知道他在这儿吗.

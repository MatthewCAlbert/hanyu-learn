## Why this combination
尊 "to honour, to venerate" and 重 "weight, to value highly" combine
directly: holding someone in weighty, honoured regard.

## Notes
尊重 covers respecting people (尊重老师 "to respect one's teacher") and
abstract things (尊重事实 "to respect the facts," 尊重意见 "to respect an
opinion") alike.

## Why this combination

快 ("fast") plus 递 ("to deliver, to hand over," as in 传递, "to
transmit/pass along") names fast delivery: express courier service.

## Notes

寄快递 ("to send via courier") and 快递员 ("a courier/delivery person")
both build on this word — 快递 has become the everyday word for parcel
delivery in China's booming e-commerce era.

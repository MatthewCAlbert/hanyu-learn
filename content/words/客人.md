## Why this combination
客 "guest, visitor, traveller" + 人 "person." Modifier-head, like [[老人]],
[[工人]], [[病人]]. Someone received in one's home, and also a shop's customer
(店里来客人了).

## Notes
客人 (a guest, personal) vs. [[顾客]] (a retail customer) vs. [[游客]] (a
tourist) vs. [[旅客]] (a passenger). 客 "guest" also in [[请客]], 客厅
"living room."

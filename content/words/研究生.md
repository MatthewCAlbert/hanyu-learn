## Why this combination

研究 ("to research, to study deeply") plus 生 ("a student," as in 学生)
names a student engaged in research-level study: a graduate student.

## Notes

研究生院 ("graduate school") and 读研究生 ("to pursue graduate studies,
lit. to read graduate-student [level]") both build on this word —
本科生 ("undergraduate student," lit. "root-course student") is its
level below.

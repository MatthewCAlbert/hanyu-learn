## Why this combination
代表 "to represent; a representative" (see its own entry) combined with
团 "a group, an organisation" (see also 团结, 团体): a group formed of
representatives — a delegation.

## Notes
代表团 names an official group sent to represent a country, company or
organisation (访问代表团 "visiting delegation") — 代表, alone, names a
single representative or the verb "to represent," while 代表团 is the
whole delegated group.

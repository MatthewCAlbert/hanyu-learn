## Why this combination
解 "to untie, to loosen" and 放 "to release, to let go" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for liberation — untying and releasing
someone from constraint.

## Notes
解放 covers personal/social liberation (思想解放 "liberation of thought,"
妇女解放 "women's liberation") and, capitalized in context, specifically
refers to the Chinese Communist Party's 1949 victory (解放前/后 "before/
after Liberation" — a standard historical dividing line in Mainland
Chinese usage).

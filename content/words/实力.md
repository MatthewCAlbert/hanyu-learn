## Why this combination
实 "real, actual, solid" (see also 现实, 事实) combined with 力 "force,
strength" (see also 力量): real, actual strength — as opposed to
apparent or claimed strength.

## Notes
实力 names a party's genuine capability, especially in competition
(经济实力 "economic strength," 实力强 "strong in actual ability") — 力量,
the broader word for "force, strength," need not carry this "actual, as
opposed to claimed" contrast that 实力 specifically implies.

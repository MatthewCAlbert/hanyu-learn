## Why this combination
Historically 名 was the personal name given at birth and 字 the courtesy name
taken at adulthood — two distinct things. The courtesy-name custom faded, and
the pair collapsed into the single everyday word for "name."

## Notes
叫什么名字 "what's your name." 字 keeps its "written character" sense elsewhere
(汉字, 写字); the "courtesy name" sense now survives mainly here.

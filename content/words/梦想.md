## Why this combination
梦 ("dream," covered elsewhere in this app's hanzi entry) plus 想 ("to
think, to wish for," as in 想要, 想念) names a wish held as vividly as a
dream — an aspiration, rather than the literal dream experienced while
asleep.

## Notes
梦想 is specifically an aspiration or ambition (我的梦想是..., "my dream/
aspiration is..."); the literal dream experienced during sleep stays with
plain 梦 (做梦, "to have a dream").

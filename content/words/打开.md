## Why this combination
开 "open" is the result; 打 is a bleached light verb — it carries almost no
"hit" meaning here, just "perform the action." 打 heads a whole family of such
verbs: 打电话, 打车, 打扫, 打包. So "effect an opening."

## Notes
Covers doors, books, boxes, and switches: 打开灯 "turn on the light,"
打开电脑 "boot up." Opposite: [[关上]].

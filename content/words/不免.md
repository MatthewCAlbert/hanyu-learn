## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 免 "to avoid" gives exactly "cannot avoid" — a formal
written adverb for an unavoidable consequence.

## Notes
不免 anchors 不免有些紧张 "inevitably a bit nervous," 心中不免担忧
"couldn't help but worry" — a written/formal register word, softer and
more literary than 难免, a near-synonym.

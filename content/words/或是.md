## Why this combination
或 "or" and 是 "to be" combine into a slightly more formal/written
alternative to plain 或者 — 是 here reinforces the choice being presented
rather than asserting identity.

## Notes
或是...或是... sets up a pair of alternatives (或是今天，或是明天 "either
today or tomorrow") the same way 或者...或者... does, with a marginally
more literary flavour.

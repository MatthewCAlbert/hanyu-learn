## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 积 ("to accumulate, to pile up," as in 积极,
"positive [lit. piled-up + extreme]") and 累 ("to accumulate, to pile
up; also tired," as in 累了, "tired") both independently name gradual
buildup. Pairing them gives the standard word for accumulation.

## Notes

积累经验 ("to accumulate experience") and 长期积累 ("long-term
accumulation") both build on this word — 累 here carries its
"accumulate" reading (*lěi*), distinct from its far more common
"tired" reading (*lèi*) elsewhere.

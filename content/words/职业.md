## Why this combination
职 "a post, an official duty" (as in 职员 "staff member") combined with
业 "a trade, an occupation" (as in 专业 "major, specialty"): the trade
attached to one's post — one's occupation.

## Notes
职业 names one's line of work in general (你的职业是什么 "what's your
occupation?") and, as a modifier, means "professional" in the sense of
"done for a living" (职业球员 "a professional player") — distinct from
专业's "professional" in the sense of "expert-level, specialised."

## Why this combination
A phonetic rendering of Costa Rica (哥斯達黎加). Not a calque of "rich coast" (that
would be 富饶海岸). 哥, 斯, 达, 黎 and 加 are chosen for sound.

## Notes
Contrast [[科特迪瓦]], another name that Chinese could have calqued and instead
transcribed.

## Why this combination
没 "not have" over 什么 in its indefinite reading "anything" → "there isn't
anything (to it)." A brush-off for thanks or apologies, or a literal "nothing
much": 没什么事 "nothing's up."

## Notes
Close to 没关系 in the reassurance use, but 没什么 can also be a plain negative
answer ("not much"), which 没关系 cannot.

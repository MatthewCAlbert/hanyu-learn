## Why this combination

进 ("to enter, to advance," as in 进步) plus 口 (here "a port, an
opening," an extended sense beyond its literal "mouth," as in 港口,
"harbor") names goods entering through a port: to import.

Rated "semi" for 口's extended "port" sense here, one step from its
literal "mouth" meaning.

## Notes

进口 pairs with 出口 ("to export," lit. "exit port") as the standard
trade terms — the same 口 "port" root, opposite direction of 进/出
("enter/exit").

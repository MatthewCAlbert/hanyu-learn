## Why this combination
实验 "experiment" (see its own entry) combined with 室 "a room, a
chamber" (as in 教室 "classroom"): the room where experiments are
conducted — a laboratory.

## Notes
实验室 follows the same "field/activity + 室" pattern as 教室 "classroom"
and 办公室 "office" — a productive way Chinese names rooms by the
activity that happens in them.

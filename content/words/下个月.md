## Why this combination

下 ("next," as in 下次, "next time") plus the classifier 个 plus 月
("month") names the month after this one: next month.

## Notes

下个月 pairs directly with 上个月 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the
two directions built on the same 个月 base — 下 marks "next," 上 marks
"previous," the same pattern also seen in 下星期/上星期.

## Why this combination
齐 "uniform, all present" and 全 "complete, whole" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for nothing being missing.

## Notes
齐全 anchors 设备齐全 "fully equipped," 品种齐全 "a complete range of
varieties" — always about a set having every expected item present, not
about quality.

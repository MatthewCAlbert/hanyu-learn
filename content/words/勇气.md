## Why this combination
勇 ("brave," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 气 ("vital energy,
spirit" — the very word Shuowen itself uses to define 勇) names the inner
spirited energy that lets someone act despite fear: courage as an
internal resource, distinct from 勇敢 (covered elsewhere in this app),
which describes the outward quality of being brave.

## Notes
鼓起勇气 ("to summon up courage," lit. "to drum up courage") and 有勇气
("to have the courage [to do something]") both treat 勇气 as a resource
one draws upon — a noun, where 勇敢 is the adjective describing the
resulting behavior.

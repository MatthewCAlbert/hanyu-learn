## Why this combination
铃 "a small bell" plus 声 "sound" gives exactly "the sound of a bell" —
the standard word for a bell's ring, now extended to phone ringtones.

## Notes
铃声 anchors 手机铃声 "phone ringtone," 上课铃声 "the class-start bell" —
covers both mechanical bells and their electronic imitations.

## Why this combination
开 "to open, to start up" (see [[开会]], [[开心]], [[开机]]) + 学, nominalised
"schooling / the term" (see [[上学]], [[放学]]) → term begins. 什么时候开学？
开学第一天. Verb-object: 开过学了.

## Notes
开学 (term starts) ↔ 放假 (term ends). Contrast [[上学]] (go to school daily)
and [[入学]] (enrol / start school as a life stage).

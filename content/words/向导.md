## Why this combination
向 (here "direction") plus 导 "to guide, to lead" gives exactly "one who
guides direction" — the standard word for a person who leads the way.

## Notes
向导 anchors 当向导 "to serve as a guide," 登山向导 "mountain-climbing
guide" — a person leading others through unfamiliar terrain, distinct from
导游, which specifically means a tourist guide.

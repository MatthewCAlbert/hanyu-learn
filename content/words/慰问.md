## Why this combination
慰 "to comfort, to console" and 问 (here "to ask after, to inquire about
someone's well-being") are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for a formal show of care.

## Notes
慰问 anchors 慰问信 "a letter of condolence/greeting," 慰问灾区群众 "to
extend sympathy to disaster-affected residents" — a formal, often
official register word for expressing care toward those suffering
hardship.

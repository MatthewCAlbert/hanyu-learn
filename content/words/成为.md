## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" + 为 (*wéi*) "to be, to act as" — two "be / become"
verbs paired. Always takes a complement: 成为老师, 成为可能. 为 is *wéi* here.

## Notes
变成 (see [[变成]]) stresses transformation from a prior state; 成为 is the
neutral "come to be." 成 alone: 成年, 完成.

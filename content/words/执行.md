## Why this combination
执 "to hold, to carry out" and 行 "to go, to act" reinforce each other:
holding to a plan and acting it out — carrying an order or decision
through to completion.

## Notes
执行 covers orders and law (执行命令 "to carry out an order," 执法 "to
enforce the law" shares 执), computing (执行程序 "to run a program"), and
formal titles (执行董事 "executive director") — a broad, formal register
verb.

## Why this combination

保 ("to protect, to guard," as in 保护) takes 密 ("a secret," as in 秘密)
as its object: to guard a secret.

## Notes

对…保密 ("to keep something confidential from…") and 保密协议
("non-disclosure agreement, lit. confidentiality agreement") are the
standard frames — 保密 names the ongoing state of secrecy, not a single
act of telling or not telling.

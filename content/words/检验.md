## Why this combination
检 "to inspect, to check" and 验 "to test, to verify" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for rigorous inspection or testing.

## Notes
检验 covers quality control (产品检验 "product inspection") and figurative
testing (实践是检验真理的唯一标准 "practice is the sole criterion for
testing truth," a well-known Chinese political slogan) — always
verification against a standard.

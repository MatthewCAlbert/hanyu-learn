## Why this combination
摄 "to draw in, to capture" (see 摄's own etymology) and 影 "shadow,
image" combine directly: capturing a light-image — the standard term for
the technical art of photography and filmmaking.

## Notes
摄影 is the formal/professional term (摄影师 "photographer," 摄影展 "photo
exhibition"); everyday snapshot-taking more often uses plain 拍照 "to
take a photo."

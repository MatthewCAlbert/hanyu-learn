## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 良 "good" gives exactly "not good" — as transparent as
a compound gets.

## Notes
不良 is a formal register for negative effects and conduct: 不良反应
"adverse reaction [to medicine]," 不良行为 "misconduct" — more clinical/
official than everyday 坏 "bad."

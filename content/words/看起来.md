## Why this combination
看 "to look" combined with the resultative-directional complement 起来
"upward, arising [into view/being]" (as in 站起来 "to stand up"): to look
and have [an impression] arise — it appears.

## Notes
看起来 is followed by an adjective or clause describing the apparent
impression (看起来很开心 "looks very happy") — functionally close to
看上去 (see its entry), the two are largely interchangeable in this use.

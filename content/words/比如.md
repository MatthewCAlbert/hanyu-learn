## Why this combination
比 "to compare, to take as a parallel" + 如 "to be like, as" (see [[如果]]) →
"taking as a comparable case." Introduces an example: 很多水果，比如苹果、香蕉.

## Notes
比如 ≈ 例如 (see [[例如]], slightly more formal) ≈ [[比如说]] (with 说, more
conversational).

## Why this combination

办 ("to handle, to do," as in 办公室) takes 事 ("a matter, an affair," as
in 事情) as its object: getting things done.

## Notes

办事处 ("an office, lit. a place for handling affairs") and 办事效率
("efficiency in getting things done") both build directly on this
verb-object base.

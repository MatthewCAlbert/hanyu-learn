## Why this combination
真 "true" and 理 "principle, reason" combine directly: a principle that
is genuinely true — objective truth, as opposed to a single true
statement.

## Notes
真理 names truth as a philosophical or universal principle (追求真理 "to
pursue truth," 检验真理的标准 "the standard for testing truth") — distinct
from 事实 "fact," a specific, concrete piece of information.

## Why this combination
题, here "a topic, a question" (as in 问题) combined with 目 "eye,"
extended to "an item, an entry [in a list]" (as in 目录 "table of
contents," 目标, literally "eye-target"): a topic entry — a title, a
question set as a topic.

## Notes
题目 names the title of a written work (文章的题目 "the title of the
essay") and an exam question (考试题目 "exam question") alike — 话题, its
neighbour, is specifically a conversation topic, not a formal title or
test question.

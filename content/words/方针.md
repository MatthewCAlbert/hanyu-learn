## Why this combination

方 ("direction, method," as in 地方) plus 针 ("a needle," as in 打针,
"to give an injection" — here extended to a compass needle that points
a direction) names a needle-pointed direction for action: a guiding
policy.

Rated "semi" for the compass-needle metaphor, not obvious without
knowing 针's directional-pointer role here.

## Notes

政策方针 ("policy guidelines") and 长远方针 ("a long-term policy") both
build on this word — 方针 names the overall guiding direction, while
政策, its near-synonym, names the specific rules that implement it.

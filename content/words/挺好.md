## Why this combination
挺 "quite, rather, pretty" (a colloquial degree adverb, roughly between 很 and
非常) + 好 "good" → "pretty good." 挺 + adjective often takes 的:
挺好的, 挺方便的.

## Notes
挺 is northern and casual; 挺好 as an answer ("things are fine") is deliberately
low-key. Same 挺 in 挺累, 挺有意思.

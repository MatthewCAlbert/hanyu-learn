## Why this combination
早 "early, long ago" + 就 "already, as early as" → something that happened well
before now, often earlier than expected: 我早就知道了 "I've known for ages,"
早就该走了.

## Notes
就 "as early as / already" also in 昨天就来了 "came as early as yesterday." 早就
carries a note of "and you're only realising now."

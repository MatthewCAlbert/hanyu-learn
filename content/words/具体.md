## Why this combination
具 "to possess, to have, to be equipped with" (as in 工具 "tool")
combined with 体 "a body, a substance, a form": possessing a [definite]
body/substance — concrete, as opposed to abstract or vague.

## Notes
具体 modifies plans, examples and information that are detailed and
particular rather than general (具体的例子 "a concrete/specific example,"
具体来说 "specifically speaking") — it pairs against 抽象 "abstract" as
the standard concrete/abstract contrast.

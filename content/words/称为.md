## Why this combination
称 "to call, to name, to address as" (as in 称呼 "a form of address")
combined with 为 "to be, to act as, to serve as": to be called, to be
named as.

## Notes
称为 introduces a name or label given to something in formal or written
contexts (被称为"中国硅谷" "known as 'China's Silicon Valley'") — 叫, the
plain everyday verb "to call/be called," is used in speech instead.

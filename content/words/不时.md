## Why this combination
不 "not" plus 时 (here "a fixed time") gives exactly "at no fixed time" —
the standard word for something happening irregularly but repeatedly.

## Notes
不时 anchors 不时传来笑声 "laughter drifts over now and then," 不时之需
"a need that might arise anytime" (idiom, "for a rainy day") — a
written/formal register adverb, close to 时不时 in meaning.

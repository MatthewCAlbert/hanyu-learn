## Why this combination
好 "good" + 人 "person." Chiefly "a good, kind person" (他是个好人). Two narrower
senses: someone in good health (病好了，又是个好人) and, faintly critical, one
who avoids offending anyone even at the cost of principle (老好人).

## Notes
好人卡 "the 'you're a good person' card" — a modern idiom for a gentle romantic
rejection.

## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 就, here "to take up, to approach, to move
towards" (as in 成就's own 就, and 就职 "to take office") takes 业 "trade,
occupation" (see also 职业, 工业) as its object — literally "to take up
an occupation" — to gain employment.

## Notes
就业 is the formal, economic term for employment (就业率 "employment
rate," 就业机会 "job opportunity") — 工作, the everyday word, covers both
"job" as a noun and "to work" as a verb, without 就业's specifically
formal, statistical register.

## Why this combination
羽毛 "feathers" (羽 "feather" + 毛 "hair/down") plus 球 "ball" gives
exactly "a feathered ball" — describing the shuttlecock, and by extension
the sport played with it.

## Notes
羽毛球 names both the object (打羽毛球 "to play badminton," literally "to
hit the shuttlecock") and the sport itself — the same word covers both,
context deciding which is meant.

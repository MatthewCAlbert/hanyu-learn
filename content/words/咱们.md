## Why this combination
咱 "I / we" + the plural suffix 们 (see [[我们]]). The key point is inclusivity:
咱们 always includes the listener, where [[我们]] may or may not. 咱们走吧
"let's (you and I) go."

## Notes
Northern and colloquial. In the south and in writing, 我们 covers both. Bare
咱 (singular "I / me") is dialectal.

## Why this combination

百 ("hundred," here meaning "many kinds," an extended sense beyond its
literal number, as in 百分之, "percent [lit. hundred parts of]") plus
货 ("goods, merchandise," as in a broader trade vocabulary) names the
full range of merchandise: general goods.

Rated "semi" for 百's extended "many kinds" sense here, distinct from
its literal numeral meaning.

## Notes

百货商店 ("a department store, lit. hundred-goods store") is the
standard compound built on this word — 百货 names the broad category of
varied consumer goods sold together under one roof.

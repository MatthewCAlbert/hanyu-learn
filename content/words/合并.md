## Why this combination
合 "to combine, to join" and 并 "together, simultaneously" are
near-synonyms combined into the formal word for merging separate things
into one.

## Notes
合并 covers business (公司合并 "company merger") and administrative
consolidation (合并部门 "to merge departments") — always two or more
separate entities becoming one.

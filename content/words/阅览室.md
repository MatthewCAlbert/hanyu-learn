## Why this combination
阅览 "to read, to peruse" (阅 "to read" + 览 "to view") plus 室 "room"
gives exactly "a room for reading" — the standard word for a library's
reading room.

## Notes
阅览室 anchors 图书馆阅览室 "library reading room," 报刊阅览室 "newspaper
and periodical reading room" — a designated quiet space for reading,
distinct from the 借阅处 "checkout desk" or book stacks.

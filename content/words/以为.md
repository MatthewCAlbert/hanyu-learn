## Why this combination
以 "to take (something) as" + 为 (*wéi*) "to be" — "to take it to be (the
case)." In modern use it almost always flags a belief that turned out wrong:
我以为你走了 "I thought you'd left (but you hadn't)."

## Notes
Contrast [[认为]] (a considered, not-necessarily-wrong opinion) and 觉得
(a feeling / impression). The "mistaken" nuance of 以为 is the key teaching
point.

## Why this combination
安, here in its "to place securely, to settle in place" sense (see also
安排, 安全) combined with 装 "to load, to install, to fit" (see also 服
装): to place securely and fit into position — to install.

## Notes
安装 covers installing physical equipment (安装空调 "to install air
conditioning") and, by extension, software (安装软件 "to install
software") — the same physical-to-digital extension English "install"
makes.

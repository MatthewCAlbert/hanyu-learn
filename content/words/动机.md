## Why this combination
动 "to move, to act" is transparent; 机 here carries its "critical
trigger-point" sense (as in 时机, 转机) rather than "machine" — together,
"the trigger point that sets action in motion," i.e. the underlying
motive.

## Notes
动机 always asks about the "why" behind an action: 犯罪动机 "criminal
motive," 动机不纯 "having ulterior motives" — the psychological or moral
reason driving behaviour.

## Why this combination
具, here in its "to possess, to be equipped with" sense (see also 具体)
combined with 有 "to have": two words for possession paired into a more
formal verb, used for abstract qualities rather than everyday objects.

## Notes
具有 takes abstract qualities or characteristics as its object (具有意义
"to be significant," 具有重大意义 "to carry great significance") — plain
有 "to have" is the everyday verb for possessing a concrete thing;
具有 is reserved for formal, often written contexts.

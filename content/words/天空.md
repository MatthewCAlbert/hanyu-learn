## Why this combination
天 "sky, heaven" (as in 天气 "weather") combined with 空 "empty, hollow,
the void" (as in 空气 "air"): the empty expanse of the sky — the sky.

## Notes
天空 is the fuller, more literary word for "sky" (蓝色的天空 "the blue
sky") — 天, alone, is used far more often in everyday compounds (今天
"today," 天气 "weather") without specifically meaning the visible sky
overhead.

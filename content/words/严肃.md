## Why this combination
严 "strict, severe" and 肃 "solemn, reverent" (see 肃's own etymology —
handling affairs with trembling respect) are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for gravity and seriousness of manner.

## Notes
严肃 describes demeanour (严肃的表情 "a serious expression") and the
weight of a topic (严肃的问题 "a serious issue") — a formality and gravity
distinct from merely "not joking" (认真 leans more toward diligence).

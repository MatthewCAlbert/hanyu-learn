## Why this combination
女 ("woman") plus 士 (a formal title suffix, covered elsewhere in this
app — originally a capable man of standing, now productive in titles
like 护士, 博士) names the formal, respectful address for a woman,
paralleling 先生 for men.

Rated "semi" for 士's productive suffix role, a pattern worth learning on
its own rather than assuming from 士's other senses.

## Notes
女士 is the formal written/spoken register (女士们先生们, "ladies and
gentlemen") — for everyday reference to a woman, plain 女人 or a specific
term like 小姐/阿姨 is more natural depending on age and context.

## Why this combination
"Ten parts out of ten" — the whole of something, hence "completely / to the
full." As an intensifier: 十分感谢, 十分重要. The literal "ten tenths" survives in
十分之九 "nine-tenths."

## Notes
十分 (formal / written) ≈ 非常 ≈ [[特别]] as an intensifier. Same "X out of ten"
logic as 满分 "full marks."

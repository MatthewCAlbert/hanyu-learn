## Why this combination
防 "to prevent, to guard against" and 治 "to cure, to treat" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word covering both stopping
something before it happens and treating it if it does.

## Notes
防治 anchors 疾病防治 "disease prevention and treatment," 病虫害防治
"pest control" — always spans both proactive prevention and reactive
treatment together, common in medical and agricultural contexts.

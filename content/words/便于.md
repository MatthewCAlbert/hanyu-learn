## Why this combination
便 "convenient" plus the preposition 于 "for, to" gives exactly
"convenient for (doing something)" — a formal written construction.

## Notes
便于 always takes a following verb: 便于携带 "convenient to carry," 便于
管理 "easy to manage" — a written/formal register, where speech would use
方便…的.

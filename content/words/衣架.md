## Why this combination
衣 "clothing" (as in 衣服, 大衣) combined with 架 "a shelf, a rack, a
frame" (as in 书架, its close relative): the rack for clothes — a hanger
or coat rack.

## Notes
衣架 names either a hanger for individual garments or a standing coat
rack — 架 recurs productively across furniture-like framed objects (书架,
衣架), a consistent word-formation pattern worth recognizing.

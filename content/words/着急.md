## Why this combination

着 here is read *zháo* ("to be touched by, to be affected by [a state],"
the same reading as in 着凉, "to catch a cold" — distinct from *zhe*, the
durative aspect particle, and *zhuó*, "to wear/apply") plus 急 ("urgent,
anxious," as in 紧急") names being overtaken by an anxious, urgent
feeling.

Rated "semi": the *zháo* reading is the key that makes the compound work,
and easy to miss for a learner who has only met 着 as the aspect
particle.

## Notes

着急 is the everyday word for feeling anxious or rushed (别着急, "don't
worry/don't rush") — 担心 is closer to worrying about a specific outcome,
while 着急 leans toward restless, time-pressured anxiety.

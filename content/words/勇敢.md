## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 勇 ("brave, courageous," covered elsewhere in
this app — vital energy channeled into strength) and 敢 ("to dare") both
independently name fearlessness in the face of danger. Pairing them
gives the standard everyday word for bravery.

## Notes
勇敢 describes acting despite fear (勇敢地面对, "to face [something]
bravely") — for the abstract quality/inner resource itself, 勇气
(covered elsewhere in this app) is the noun to reach for.

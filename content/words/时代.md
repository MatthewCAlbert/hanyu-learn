## Why this combination
时 "time" combined with 代 "a generation, an era, a dynasty" (as in 现代
"modern era," literally "the current generation/era"): a span of time
marked off as one generation or age.

## Notes
时代 names both grand historical eras (互联网时代 "the internet age") and
a stretch of one's own life (学生时代 "one's student years") — 年代, a
near-synonym, more specifically names a decade of a century (八十年代
"the eighties").

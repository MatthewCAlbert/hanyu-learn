## Why this combination
上 "on top of, above" combined with the locative suffix 面 "face, side" —
the same "direction word + 面" pattern as 里面, 外面, 前面, 后面 and 下面
— naming the space above something.

## Notes
上面 also extends figuratively to superiors in a hierarchy (上面的意思
"what the higher-ups want") and to "the aforementioned" in writing (上面
提到的问题 "the problem mentioned above") — 上边 is a colloquial synonym.

## Why this combination
硕 "great, eminent" (originally "large") plus 士 "a scholar, an educated
man" gives exactly "an eminent scholar" — repurposed as the modern
translation for the academic master's degree, one level above 学士
"bachelor" and below 博士 "doctor."

## Notes
硕士 anchors 硕士学位 "master's degree," 硕士研究生 "master's degree
student" — part of a three-tier academic ladder (学士-硕士-博士) that
maps ancient scholarly-rank vocabulary onto the modern Western degree
system.

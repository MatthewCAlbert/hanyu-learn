## Why this combination
经 (here "to manage, to administer," as in 经营) and 费 "expenses, fees"
combine into "administered funds" — the standard word for a budget
allocated to a specific purpose.

## Notes
经费 anchors 教育经费 "education funding," 经费不足 "insufficient funds" —
always institutional or project money, distinct from personal 费用
"expenses."

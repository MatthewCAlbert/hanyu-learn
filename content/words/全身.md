## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" + 身 "body" → the entire body: 全身都湿了 "soaked all over,"
全身检查 "a full physical."

## Notes
全 + noun: [[全国]], [[全家]], [[全体]], [[全部]]. 身 "body" also in [[身体]],
[[身上]], [[身边]].

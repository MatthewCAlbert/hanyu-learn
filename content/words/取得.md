## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 取 "to take, to fetch, to obtain" (取钱, 录取) + 得 "to get."
Doubled for a formal "obtain / achieve," usually with an abstract object:
取得成功, 取得联系, 取得进步.

## Notes
取得 (formal, abstract results) vs. [[得到]] (get / receive) vs. [[拿到]]
(physically get in hand). 得 is *dé* here.

## Why this combination
Two near-synonyms welded together: 时 "time" and 候 "a period, a juncture" (the
same 候 as in 气候 climate). Doubling a sense to make a stable disyllable is the
commonest word-building pattern in Mandarin; here it gives the everyday noun for
"a point in time."

## Notes
Mostly grammatical: …的时候 = "when …". Distinct from [[时间]] (time as a
quantity or span): 没时间 "no time," but 小时候 "in childhood."

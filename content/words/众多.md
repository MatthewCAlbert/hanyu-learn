## Why this combination
众 "a multitude, many" and 多 "many" are near-synonyms combined for
emphasis into the formal word for a large quantity of people or things.

## Notes
众多 is a formal written-register intensifier (众多观众 "numerous
spectators," 品种众多 "a great variety of types") — more formal than
everyday 很多 "a lot of."

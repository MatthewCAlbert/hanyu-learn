## Why this combination

奋 ("to exert oneself, to rouse up one's strength," as in 兴奋, "excited")
plus 斗 ("to fight, to struggle," covered elsewhere in this app as a
character that absorbed the meaning of a separate traditional character,
鬥, in simplification) names exerting oneself in a sustained fight
toward a goal: to strive.

## Notes

为…而奋斗 ("to strive for…") is the standard frame — 奋斗 names effortful,
often long-term striving, distinct from a single quick 努力 ("effort").

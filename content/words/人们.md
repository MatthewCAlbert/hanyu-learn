## Why this combination
人 "person" + the plural suffix 们 (see [[我们]]) — one of the few nouns, not
pronouns, that regularly takes 们. Denotes people at large: 人们常说….

## Notes
Generic and slightly bookish. Everyday speech prefers 大家 or 别人. Never used
with a number (三个人, not 三个人们).

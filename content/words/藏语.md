## Why this combination
藏 clipped from 西藏 + 语. An ethnolinguistic name, parallel to [[汉语]] (Han +
language) rather than to 法语 (country clip + language).

## Notes
Parallel [[藏文]] for the written language. 藏 here is the toponym/ethnonym, not
"to hide."

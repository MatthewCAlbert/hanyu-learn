## Why this combination
分 "to divide" and 析 "to split [wood]" are near-synonyms reinforcing each
other: breaking a complex whole into its separate parts in order to
understand it — exactly what "to analyze" means.

## Notes
分析 is the standard verb/noun for analysis across registers: 数据分析
"data analysis," 分析问题 "to analyze a problem," 分析师 "analyst."

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 规 ("a rule, a standard," as in 规定) and 律
("a law, a rule," as in 法律) both independently name a governing
pattern or standard. Pairing them gives the word for a natural or
predictable law/pattern.

## Notes

生活规律 ("regular habits, lit. life pattern") uses 规律 as an adjective
("regular, orderly") — distinct from its noun use for a scientific or
natural law (自然规律, "laws of nature").

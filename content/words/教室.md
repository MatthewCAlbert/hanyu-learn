## Why this combination
教 (*jiào*) "to teach" + 室 "room" (see [[办公室]], 卧室, 会议室) → a room for
teaching. 多媒体教室 "multimedia classroom."

## Notes
教 is *jiào* here. Don't confuse with 教师 (teacher, see [[教师]]) or [[教学楼]]
(the classroom *building*). 室 "-room" is a productive suffix.

## Why this combination
熟, here "familiar, well-known" (as in 熟悉 "to be familiar with," an
extension of 熟's literal "ripe, cooked," see also 成熟) combined with
人 "person": a person one is familiar with — an acquaintance.

## Notes
熟人 names someone one knows, short of being a close friend (找熟人
帮忙 "to ask an acquaintance for help") — 朋友, "friend," implies a
closer bond, while 熟人 marks only familiarity, useful in describing
social-network-based favours common in Chinese social life (关系,
"connections").

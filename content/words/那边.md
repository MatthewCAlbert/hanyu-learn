## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + 边 "side" → the side away from the speaker. 这边 here,
那边 there, 哪边 which way. 边 here is a region relative to the speaker, not a
compass point.

## Notes
Northern erhua form 那边儿. With people or organisations it means "their side":
你跟那边说一下 "have a word with them."

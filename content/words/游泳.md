## Why this combination
游, here "to swim, to roam through water" (as in 旅游 "to travel,"
originally "to roam") combined with 泳 "to swim" (an atomic character
whose 氵 water-radical marks the swimming sense): two near-synonyms for
swimming, paired into the standard word for the activity.

## Notes
游泳 names both the activity and the sport (游泳池 "swimming pool," 游泳
比赛 "swimming competition") — 泳 alone survives productively in
compounds naming swimming strokes and venues (泳衣 "swimsuit").

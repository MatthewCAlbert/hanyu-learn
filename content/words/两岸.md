## Why this combination
两 "two" plus 岸 "shore, bank" gives exactly "the two shores" — literally
both banks of a river or strait, and, by extension, the two sides
separated by the Taiwan Strait.

## Notes
两岸 anchors 河的两岸 "both banks of the river" (literal) and, very
commonly in political and news contexts, 两岸关系 "cross-strait relations
(mainland China and Taiwan)" — context decides which sense applies.

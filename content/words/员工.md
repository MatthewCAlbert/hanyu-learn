## Why this combination
员 "a member, a person on staff" (see also 成员, 人员) combined with 工
"a worker, labour" (as in 工人 "worker"): staff members who work — an
employee.

## Notes
员工 is the standard collective and individual term for a company's
workforce (公司员工 "company staff/employees") — 人员, its broader
relative, applies to personnel in any organisation, not specifically
paid staff in a business.

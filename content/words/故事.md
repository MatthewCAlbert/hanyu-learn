## Why this combination
故 "old, former, past" (as in 故乡, 故人) + 事 "affair, event." "Things of the
past" — events told about, hence a story. 讲故事 "tell a story,"
故事书 "storybook."

## Notes
The everyday word "story" is toneless *gùshi*. Full-toned *gùshì* is a separate
formal/literary word: "old practice, set routine" (奉行故事 "follow set
routine").

## Why this combination
缺 "to lack, to be short of, incomplete" (as in 缺点 "shortcoming") and
少 "few, little, lacking" are close synonyms, paired into the standard
verb for insufficiency.

## Notes
缺少 takes what is missing as its object (缺少经验 "to lack experience,"
缺少资金 "short of funds") — 缺, alone, is used the same way and is
somewhat more colloquial; 缺乏, a more formal near-synonym, is
preferred in written and academic contexts.

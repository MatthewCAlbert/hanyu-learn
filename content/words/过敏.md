## Why this combination
过 "to exceed" modifying 敏 "sensitive" (see 敏's own etymology) gives
"excessively sensitive" — the body reacting more strongly than normal,
i.e. an allergic reaction.

## Notes
过敏 covers medical allergy (花粉过敏 "pollen allergy," 过敏反应 "allergic
reaction") and, figuratively, emotional oversensitivity (对批评过敏
"oversensitive to criticism").

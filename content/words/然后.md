## Why this combination
然 "so, thus, in that state" (the classical 然 of 当然, 虽然) + 后 "after" →
"after that state of things," i.e. next in sequence. Chains steps:
先…然后…最后….

## Notes
然后 = sequence in time. 而后 is the literary equivalent. Do not confuse with
后来 (see [[后来]]), which frames a past turn of events.

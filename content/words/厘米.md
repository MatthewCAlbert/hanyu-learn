## Why this combination

厘 ("a hundredth part," a unit character used for small fractional
measures, a character occurring mainly in this role) plus 米 ("a
meter," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 毫米) names a
hundredth of a meter: a centimeter.

Rated "semi" for 厘's specialized fractional-unit role, a character
with little independent everyday use outside measurement words.

## Notes

厘米 (centimeter) and 毫米 (covered elsewhere in this app, millimeter)
both build on the same 米 "meter" root, scaled by a different
fractional prefix.

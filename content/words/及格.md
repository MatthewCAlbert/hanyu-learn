## Why this combination

及 ("to reach," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 来得及/来不及)
takes 格 ("a standard, a pattern," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 格外) as its object: reaching the required standard.

## Notes

考试及格 ("to pass an exam") and 不及格 ("to fail") both build on this
word — 及格 names the minimum passing bar, distinct from 合格
(covered elsewhere in this app), which more broadly names meeting any
qualifying standard, not only on an exam.

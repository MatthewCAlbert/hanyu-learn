## Why this combination

营 ("to operate, to manage," as in 经营, "to run a business") takes 业
("business, occupation," as in 职业) as its object: running a business —
being open and operating.

## Notes

营业时间 ("business hours") and 营业员 ("a shop clerk/salesperson") both
build on this word — 营业 names the state of being open and actively
doing business, distinct from 开业, which names the one-time act of
opening.

## Why this combination
各 "each, every [one individually]" (see also 各种, 各地) combined with
自 "self" (see also 自己, 自动): each one's own self — respectively,
each one apiece.

## Notes
各自 marks that a following action or possession applies separately to
each member of a group (各自的想法 "each person's own idea," 各自回家
"each went home separately") — 每个, "each/every [one]," modifies a
following noun directly, while 各自 stands more independently, often
right before a verb.

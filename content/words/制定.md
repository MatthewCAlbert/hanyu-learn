## Why this combination
制, here in its "to establish, to fashion [a system]" sense (see also
制度, 制造) combined with 定 "to fix, to settle, to determine" (see also
规定, 决定): to establish and fix in place — to draw up formally.

## Notes
制定 collocates with laws, plans and policies (制定法律 "to draw up
laws," 制定计划 "to formulate a plan") — 规定, its close relative, is
used instead for the specific rules that result, or as the verb "to
stipulate" a particular requirement.

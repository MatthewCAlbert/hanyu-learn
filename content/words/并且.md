## Why this combination
并 and 且 both independently function as classical conjunctions meaning
"and, furthermore" — paired into a single, more formal modern connector.

## Notes
并且 links two verb phrases or clauses (adding a further point), unlike
和, which links nouns — 我喜欢跳舞，并且喜欢唱歌 "I like dancing, and
[also] like singing," not *我和喜欢唱歌.

## Why this combination
统 carries its "to unify, to bring together as a whole" sense (covered
elsewhere in this app), and 计 is "to count, to calculate" (as in 计算).
Bringing together many individual counts into one organized whole is
exactly what statistics does.

Rated "semi" for 统's abstract "unifying" sense applied here to data
rather than governance.

## Notes
统计数字 ("statistical figures/data") and 据统计 ("according to statistics")
are common everyday frames — 统计 names both the field/method and the
act of tallying data.

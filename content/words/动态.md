## Why this combination
动 "movement" plus 态 "state, condition" gives exactly "the state of
movement" — a word for how something is changing over time, opposite of
静态 "static state."

## Notes
动态 anchors 市场动态 "market trends," 最新动态 "the latest developments"
— used for tracking ongoing change in a situation, from physics (dynamic
vs. static) to news updates on a person or organization.

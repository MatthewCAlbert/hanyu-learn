## Why this combination
足 "foot" (see also 足够 in an unrelated sense) combined with 球 "ball"
(as in 篮球 "basketball," 网球 "tennis"): the ball played with the feet —
football/soccer.

## Notes
足球 names both the sport and the physical ball (踢足球 "to play
football," 一个足球 "a football") — the same word covers game and
equipment, following the same pattern as 篮球 and 网球.

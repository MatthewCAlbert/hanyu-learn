## Why this combination
爬 "to climb, to crawl, to clamber" + 山 "mountain, hill" as its object — to
make one's way up a slope. As leisure it covers everything from a hill walk to
mountaineering: 周末去爬山. Verb-object: 爬了一座山.

## Notes
爬 "climb / crawl" also in 爬树 "climb a tree," 爬行 "crawl." 登山 is the more
serious "mountaineering."

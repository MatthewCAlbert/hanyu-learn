## Why this combination
军 "army, military" (see 军's own etymology) and 人 "person" combine
directly: a person belonging to the military.

## Notes
军人 is the general term for any member of the armed forces (退伍军人
"veteran," 现役军人 "active-duty personnel") — 士兵 more specifically names
a rank-and-file soldier.

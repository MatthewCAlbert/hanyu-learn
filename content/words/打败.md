## Why this combination

打 ("to hit, to strike," as in 打电话) plus 败 ("to lose, to be defeated,"
as in 失败) names striking someone into defeat: to beat, to defeat.

## Notes

打败 takes the loser as its object (打败对手, "to defeat the opponent"),
the opposite orientation from 打输 ("to lose a match," where the subject
itself is the loser) — the same 败 root, opposite grammatical roles.

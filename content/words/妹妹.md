## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term (see [[妈妈]]), intimate address form, second syllable
toneless. Siblings are named by relative age: 姐姐 older sister, 妹妹 younger.

## Notes
Also a familiar address to a younger woman (小妹, 表妹 cousin). 兄弟姐妹
"siblings" collectively.

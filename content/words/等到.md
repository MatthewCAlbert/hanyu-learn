## Why this combination
等 "to wait" + resultative 到 "reach" — waiting that has arrived at a point in
time: 等到天黑 "wait until dark," 等到你回来 "by the time you get back."

## Notes
等到 introduces a time-clause, usually paired with 再 or 就 in the main clause:
等到放假，我就去旅行. 等 alone: 等一下, 等车.

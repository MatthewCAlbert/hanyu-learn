## Why this combination

市 ("city," as in 城市) plus 区 ("a district, an area," as in 地区) names
the built-up district of a city: downtown, the urban core.

## Notes

市区 contrasts with 郊区 ("the suburbs, lit. outskirts district") — both
share the 区 root, marking two zones of the same city.

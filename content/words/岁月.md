## Why this combination
岁 "year" and 月 "month" are near-synonyms of time-passing combined into
a literary word for the passage of years.

## Notes
岁月 is used reflectively, often with a wistful tone: 岁月如梭 "time
flies," 那段艰难的岁月 "those hard years" — a more poetic register than
neutral 时间 or 时候.

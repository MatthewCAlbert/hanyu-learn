## Why this combination
午 "noon" as the pivot; 上 "the earlier / upper half" of the day before it, with
[[下午]] the later half after. The same 上 / 下 = before / after axis as
上次 / 下次.

## Notes
Overlaps [[早上]] (dawn to mid-morning) at its early end and 中午 (noon) at its
late end.

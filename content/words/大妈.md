## Why this combination

大 ("big," here a term of respect/seniority rather than literal size)
plus 妈 ("mother," as in 妈妈) names an older woman addressed
respectfully.

## Notes

大妈 pairs with 大爷 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the standard
warm, respectful street-level address for an older woman — like 大爷,
it can occasionally carry a mildly teasing or stereotyping connotation
in casual usage.

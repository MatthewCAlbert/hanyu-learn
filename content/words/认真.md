## Why this combination
认 "to take as, to acknowledge" + 真 "real, genuine" — to treat a thing as
real, i.e. not to take it lightly. Describes a person's manner (工作认真) or an
attitude in the moment (别认真 "don't take it to heart").

## Notes
认 in this "regard as" sense also in 认错 (admit a fault), 认输 (concede). The
opposite manner is 马虎 (careless).

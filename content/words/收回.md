## Why this combination

收 ("to gather in, to receive," as in 收入) plus 回 ("to return, to come
back," as in 回家) names taking something back into one's own hands: to
withdraw, to retract.

## Notes

收回 covers the literal (收回借出的东西, "to take back a lent item") and
the figurative equally (收回一句话, "to take back [retract] a statement";
收回权力, "to withdraw authority").

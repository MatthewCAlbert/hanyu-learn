## Why this combination
请 "please" + 坐 "to sit" — the 请 + verb polite-request frame (请进, 请问). The
bare 坐 here, without the complement 下, is the settled invitation; [[坐下]] adds
the motion "sit down."

## Notes
请 = "invite / please" as in [[请进]], 请客 "treat someone." 请坐请坐 doubled
is a warm "do sit, do sit."

## Why this combination
数 ("number, figure," as in 数字, 数量) plus 据 ("evidence, a basis to rely
on," as covered in this app's entry for 根据) names numerical evidence —
figures used as a basis for a claim or conclusion, which is exactly what
"data" means.

Rated "semi" for 据's "evidence/basis" sense, which learners typically
first meet through 根据 rather than as a free-standing idea.

## Notes
数据 is the standard modern word for data in any technical context
(数据库, database; 数据分析, data analysis) — distinct from 资料, which leans
toward documents/reference material rather than specifically numerical
figures.

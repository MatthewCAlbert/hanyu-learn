## Why this combination
服 "to serve, to submit to, to be in service" + 务 "affairs, duties, to attend
to" (任务, 事务). Attending to others' needs: 为人民服务, 服务态度, 服务行业
"the service sector."

## Notes
服务员 "waiter / attendant," 客服 "customer service." 务 "duties" also in
[[任务]] (not this level), 义务.

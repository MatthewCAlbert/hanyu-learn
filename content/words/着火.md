## Why this combination

着 here is read *zháo* ("to be touched by, to be affected by [a
state]," the same reading covered elsewhere in this app as part of
着急) plus 火 ("fire") as its object: to be caught by fire.

Rated "semi": the *zháo* reading is the key that makes the compound
work, distinct from *zhe*, the durative aspect particle, or *zhuó*, "to
wear/apply."

## Notes

房子着火了 ("the house caught fire") is the standard alarm phrase — 着火
names the state of being on fire, distinct from 放火 ("to set fire," the
deliberate act of starting one).

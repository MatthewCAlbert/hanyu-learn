## Why this combination
顾 "to turn and look at, to attend to, to patronise (a shop)" — the same 顾 as
光顾 "grace with one's custom" and [[照顾]] — + 客 "guest, visitor." A shop
treats its buyers as guests: 顾客至上 "the customer comes first."

## Notes
顾客 (retail buyer) vs. 客户 (a business client / account) vs. 客人 (a guest in
one's home). 客 "guest" also in [[客人]], [[请客]], [[游客]].

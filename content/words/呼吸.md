## Why this combination
A paired-opposite compound: 呼 ("to exhale, to call out") and 吸 ("to
inhale," covered in this app's hanzi entry) name the two opposite halves
of one cycle. Naming both directions together is exactly how Mandarin
builds a word for the whole cycle, the same pattern as 买卖 (buy-sell,
"business") and 反正 (right-wrong, "anyway").

## Notes
呼 alone survives independently too, mostly in "to call out" (呼叫, 呼喊)
rather than "to exhale" — it is really only inside 呼吸 that its
breathing sense is active on its own.

## Why this combination
连续 "continuous, in a row" (see its own entry) combined with 剧
"drama, a play" (see also 电视剧, 话剧): drama continued episode after
episode — a serialised show.

## Notes
连续剧 is the general term for any serialised drama, television or
otherwise (电视连续剧 is a fuller variant of 电视剧 emphasising the
serial format) — 电视剧 alone already implies episodic television
drama, so 连续剧 is often used interchangeably or to stress the
multi-episode structure specifically.

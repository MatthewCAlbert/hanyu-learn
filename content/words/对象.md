## Why this combination
对 "facing, opposite, directed at" (see also 对方, 对手) combined with
象 "image, appearance, likeness" (as in 想象 "to imagine"): the image one
is directed towards — the target or object of an action, and, by
extension, a romantic partner one is "targeting" for marriage.

## Notes
对象 names both the abstract target of an action (研究对象 "research
subject," 攻击对象 "target of attack") and, colloquially, a boyfriend or
girlfriend (谈对象 "to be dating," literally "to discuss a partner") —
two senses that share the same underlying "object aimed at" logic.

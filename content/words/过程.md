## Why this combination
过 "to pass through, to go past" combined with 程 "a stage, a measured
distance, a course" (as in 路程 "the distance/course of a journey"): the
stages passed through on the way from a start to an end — a process.

## Notes
过程 emphasises the stages and duration between a beginning and an end
(学习过程 "the learning process," 在...过程中 "in the course of..."); 步骤
names the discrete steps of a procedure rather than its overall unfolding.

## Why this combination
精 carries its "essence, the refined vital core" sense (as in 精神,
"spirit/mind"), and 力 is "strength, force." One's refined vital essence
expressed as strength is exactly personal energy or stamina.

Rated "semi" for 精's abstract "essence/vital core" sense, distinct from
its more concrete meanings elsewhere (e.g. "refined," as in rice
processing).

## Notes
精力充沛 ("full of energy/vigor," lit. "energy abundant") and 精力旺盛
("vigorous, full of pep") are common fixed collocations built on this
word.

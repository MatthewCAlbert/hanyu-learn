## Why this combination
人 "person" and 物 "thing, object" combine into "a person considered as an
entity/figure" — 物 here does not diminish the person to an object, but
generalizes "thing" to any notable entity worth naming.

## Notes
人物 leans toward the notable or fictional: 重要人物 "an important
figure," 小说人物 "a novel's characters" — plain 人 is used for a person
without that added weight of significance.

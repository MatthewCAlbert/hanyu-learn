## Why this combination
内 "inside, inner" (see also 内容) combined with 心 "heart, mind" (as in
心情 "mood"): the heart on the inside — one's innermost feelings, as
opposed to what is shown outwardly.

## Notes
内心 names the private, unexpressed layer of feeling (内心的想法 "one's
innermost thoughts," 内心深处 "deep down inside") — 心里, a near-synonym,
is the more everyday, spoken equivalent for the same idea.

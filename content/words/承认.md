## Why this combination
承 carries its "to accept, to bear, to take on" sense (as in 承担, 继承),
and 认 is "to recognize, to acknowledge" (as in 认为, 认识). To accept and
formally recognize something — a fact, a mistake, a state's legitimacy —
is exactly "to admit/acknowledge."

Rated "semi" for 承's "to accept/bear" sense, which needs to be
distinguished from 认's more familiar "to recognize/know" sense that
learners meet earlier.

## Notes
承认错误 ("to admit a mistake") and 承认一个国家 ("to [diplomatically]
recognize a country") show the word's range from the personal to the
formal/political.

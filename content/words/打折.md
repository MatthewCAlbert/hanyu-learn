## Why this combination

打 ("to do, to perform [an action]," a light verb as in 打电话) takes 折
(here "a discount," an extended sense from its literal "to fold/break,"
as in 打折扣, the fuller phrase this word abbreviates) as its object:
to apply a discount.

Rated "semi" for 折's extended "discount" sense here, one step from its
literal "to fold, to break" meaning — a folded price, in effect.

## Notes

打八折 ("20% off, lit. 'strike an eight-tenths fold'") shows how a
number before 折 names the fraction of the original price still charged
— 折扣 is the fuller noun form, "a discount," that 打折 abbreviates into
a verb.

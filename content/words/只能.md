## Why this combination
只 "only" + 能 "can, be able." The one course left open: 只能等 "can only wait,"
只能这样了 "there's no other way."

## Notes
只 as "only" also in [[只要]], [[只有]], 只是. 只能 often carries a note of
resignation.

## Why this combination
复 "repeated, layered, doubled" (as in 重复 "to repeat") combined with
杂 "miscellaneous, mixed" (see also 杂志): many mixed layers folded
together — complicated, complex.

## Notes
复杂 is the standard opposite of 简单 "simple" (问题很复杂 "the problem is
complicated," 复杂的关系 "a complicated relationship") — it describes
something hard to untangle because of the many parts or factors involved,
not merely "difficult" in the sense 难 covers.

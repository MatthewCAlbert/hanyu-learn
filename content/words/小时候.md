## Why this combination
小 "small, young" + 时候 "time" (see [[时候]]) → "the time when one was small."
A fixed time expression: 小时候我住在农村, 从小时候起.

## Notes
Parse 小 + 时候, not [[小时]] + 候 — this is not about hours. Near 童年
"childhood" (a noun); 小时候 is adverbial ("back when I was a kid").

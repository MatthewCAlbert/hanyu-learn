## Why this combination
认, here "to recognise" (see also 认识, 认出) combined with 得, a light
resultative particle marking capability/achievement (see also 值得,
显得): to be able to recognise — to know someone or something on sight.

## Notes
认得 is colloquial and stative, close to 认识 in meaning but focused
purely on visual/nominal recognition rather than deeper acquaintance
(我认得他，但不认识他 "I recognise him [by sight], but I don't [really]
know him") — this contrast with 认识 is a fine but real distinction
worth noting.

## Why this combination
物 (here "property, goods") plus 业 "estate, business, undertaking"
gives exactly "property assets" — the standard commercial term for real
estate holdings.

## Notes
物业 anchors 物业费 "property management fee," 物业公司 "property
management company" — in everyday apartment-living contexts, 物业 is
often short for 物业管理(处) "the property management (office)."

## Why this combination
分 "to divide" acting on the object 类 "category, kind" gives exactly "to
divide into categories" — splittable, as in 分了三类 "sorted into three
categories."

## Notes
分类 anchors 垃圾分类 "waste sorting," 分类管理 "categorized management" —
the standard word for organizing things by type, from library cataloging
to recycling.

## Why this combination
联 and 系 both independently mean "to connect, to link" — recall 联's
own etymology (an ear "unbroken" against the cheek, and unbroken silk
thread) and 系's core sense "to tie together." Paired for emphasis.

## Notes
联系 covers both the abstract noun "a connection" (这两件事有联系
"these two things are connected") and the everyday verb "to contact
[someone]" (跟我联系 "get in touch with me").

## Why this combination
模 "mould, model" and 式 "style, pattern, formula" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for a recurring, replicable pattern of
doing something.

## Notes
模式 spans business (商业模式 "business model") and computing (飞行模式
"airplane mode," 编辑模式 "edit mode") — a general-purpose word for "the
fixed shape/pattern something follows."

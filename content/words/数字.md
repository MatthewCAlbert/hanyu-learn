## Why this combination
数 "number, quantity" + 字 "written character" → the written signs for numbers
— 0–9, 一二三, Roman numerals. By extension a quantitative "figure"
(经济数字) and, in modern coinages, "digital": 数字电视, 数字化.

## Notes
数 is *shù* here (number), not *shǔ* "to count." 数 alone: [[大多数]],
[[少数]], [[人数]].

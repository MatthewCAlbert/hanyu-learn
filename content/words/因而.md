## Why this combination
因 "because, cause" plus 而 (here a connective linking cause to effect)
gives exactly "because (of this), and so" — a formal written connective
introducing a conclusion.

## Notes
因而 always follows the stated cause: 天气恶劣，因而航班取消 "the weather
was bad, so the flight was cancelled" — a written/formal register word,
close to 因此, largely interchangeable.

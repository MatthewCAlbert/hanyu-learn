## Why this combination
木 "wood, tree" (as in 树木 "trees") combined with the light noun suffix
头, grammaticalised here just as in 石头 "stone" and 舌头 "tongue": the
everyday concrete noun for wood/timber.

## Notes
木头 names wood as a material (木头桌子 "wooden table") and, in casual
speech, is used figuratively to call someone slow-witted or emotionally
unresponsive ("真是个木头" "what a blockhead," i.e. as unresponsive as a
piece of wood) — 木, alone, is the more literary form and a productive
bound component (木材 "timber," 木工 "carpentry").

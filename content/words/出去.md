## Why this combination
出 "to exit" + 去 "to go (away from the speaker)" — leaving, heading away from
where you are. [[出来]] is the same exit coming toward you. As a complement:
拿出去 "take out," 走出去 "walk out."

## Notes
去 = away from speaker, 来 = toward. That single contrast runs through the whole
directional-complement set.

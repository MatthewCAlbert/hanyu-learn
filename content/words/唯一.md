## Why this combination
Both characters point the same direction: 唯 "only, alone" reinforces 一
"one." Together they simply mean "the one and only" — as transparent as a
compound gets.

## Notes
唯一 modifies a noun (唯一的机会 "the only chance") the way English "the
only" does; it does not stand alone as a full sentence predicate the way
独一无二 "unique, unparalleled" can.

## Why this combination
形 "form, shape" (see also 形象, 形成) combined with 式 "pattern, style,
formula" (as in 方式 "manner, method"): the outward pattern something
takes — form, as distinct from content.

## Notes
形式 pairs against 内容 "content" as the classic form/content contrast
(形式和内容 "form and content") and, in its critical sense, "mere
formality" (走形式 "to go through the motions") — it names the shape
something takes, not the shape of a physical object, which would be 形状.

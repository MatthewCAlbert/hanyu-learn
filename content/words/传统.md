## Why this combination
传 is "to transmit, to pass down" (as in 传说, 传给), and 统 carries its
"an unbroken line, a lineage" sense (as also seen in this app's entry for
系统, where 统 means "governed as one whole"). "What has been transmitted
down an unbroken line" is exactly what a tradition is.

Rated "semi" for 统's "lineage" sense, one step removed from its more
familiar "system" association.

## Notes
传统 works as both noun (一个传统, "a tradition") and adjective (传统文化,
"traditional culture") without changing form — a common pattern for
Chinese abstract nouns used attributively.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 指 ("to point") and 挥 ("to wave, to direct,"
covered elsewhere in this app — a hand waving with commanding vigor)
both independently name directing with a gesture. Pairing them gives the
standard word for commanding troops or conducting an orchestra — both
literally directed by hand gestures.

## Notes
指挥 works as both verb ("to command/conduct") and noun ("a
commander/conductor," 乐队指挥, "orchestra conductor") without changing
form.

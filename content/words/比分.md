## Why this combination

比 ("to compare," as in 比较, and also "a contest," as in 比赛) plus 分
("points, a score," as in 分数) names the compared point tally between
two sides: the score.

## Notes

比分领先 ("to lead in the score") and 比分落后 ("to trail in the score")
are the standard sportscasting phrases built on this word.

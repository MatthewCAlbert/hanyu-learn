## Why this combination
例 "an example, a precedent, a rule" (see [[例如]], [[比如]]) + the toneless
noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]], [[句子]]) → the everyday noun "an example":
举个例子, 一个典型的例子.

## Notes
举例子 "give an example," 拿…做例子 "take … as an example." 例 alone in
[[例如]], 例外 "exception," 案例 "a case."

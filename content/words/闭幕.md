## Why this combination
闭 "to close" acting on the object 幕 "curtain" gives exactly "to close
the curtain" — splittable, as in 闭了幕, extended from theater to any
formal event's ending, the mirror of 开幕 "to open."

## Notes
闭幕 anchors 闭幕式 "closing ceremony," 大会闭幕 "the conference
concludes" — the standard word for the formal end of an event.

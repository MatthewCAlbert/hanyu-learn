## Why this combination
儿 "child, son" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]]). Here the two
morphemes both originally meant "child," so the compound is close to a
same-sense pair with 子 also doing suffix duty.

## Notes
Pairs with 女儿 "daughter" — which does *not* take 子, since 儿 there is the
"child" root and 女 the modifier.

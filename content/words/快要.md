## Why this combination
快 "fast, soon" + 要 "will, be going to" → an imminent event, almost always
closed with 了: 电影快要开始了, 快要考试了.

## Notes
快要…了 and [[就要]]…了 both mark the near future; only 就要 can take a specific
time (明天就要走了). Bare 快…了 is the terser version.

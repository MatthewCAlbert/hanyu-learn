## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" acting on the object 交 "exchange, trade"
gives "for the exchange to become complete" — a deal has been struck.

## Notes
成交 is standard business and auction vocabulary: 成交价 "the closing/
sale price," 成交量 "trading volume" — the moment a negotiation
concludes successfully.

## Why this combination
要, here in its conditional "if" sense (as in 要不 "otherwise"), combined
with 是 "to be, this [being the case]": a grammaticalized conjunction
introducing a hypothetical condition, more colloquial in flavour than
如果.

## Notes
要是 opens a conditional clause and is regularly followed by 就 in the
result clause (要是明天下雨，我们就不去了 "if it rains tomorrow, we won't
go") — it is used almost exclusively in speech, where 如果 is neutral
across registers.

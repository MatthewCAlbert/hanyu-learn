## Why this combination
场, here "a site, a scene, a set of circumstances" (see also 场所)
combined with 合 "to fit, to match" (see also 合适, 配合): the
circumstances that fit [a moment] — an occasion, a setting.

## Notes
场合 names the social context or type of occasion something happens in
(正式场合 "formal occasion/setting," 公共场合 "public setting") — 场所,
its close relative, names a physical location, while 场合 stresses the
social/situational context regardless of the physical place.

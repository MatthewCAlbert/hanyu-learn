## Why this combination
房 "house, room" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]]). The suffix turns
the bare root into the countable everyday noun for a dwelling — a flat or a
detached house alike.

## Notes
房 without 子 means "room" in compounds: [[房间]], 书房, 厨房. 买房 "buy
property" drops the suffix.

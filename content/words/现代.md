## Why this combination
现 "present, now" (see also 现在, 现实) combined with 代 "a generation,
an era" (see also 时代, 年代): the present era — modern times.

## Notes
现代 names the current historical era as a period (现代社会 "modern
society," 现代化 "modernisation") and pairs against 古代 "ancient times"
and 近代 "the early-modern/pre-modern period" in the standard periodising
sequence 古代/近代/现代.

## Why this combination
报 (here "to repay, to reciprocate") and 答 (here "to respond") are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for repaying kindness.

## Notes
报答 anchors 报答父母 "to repay one's parents (for their care)," 报答
恩情 "to requite a kindness" — always about repaying a debt of gratitude,
not a monetary loan.

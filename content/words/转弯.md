## Why this combination

转 ("to turn," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 转身/转动) takes
弯 ("a bend, a curve") as its object: turning into a bend.

## Notes

前方转弯 ("turn ahead") and 转弯抹角 ("to speak in a roundabout way, lit.
turning corners and rounding edges") both build on this word — literal
for physical turns, figurative for indirect speech or thinking.

## Why this combination
用 "to use" is transparent. 利 here carries its extended sense
"advantage, benefit" (rather than "sharp") — using something in a way
that draws out its benefit. Marked semi because the "for benefit" nuance
adds meaning beyond a plain "to use."

## Notes
利用 can be neutral ("to make good use of a resource") or, depending on
context, carry a negative shade close to English "to exploit [a person]"
— the same word covers both, distinguished only by what or who is being
used.

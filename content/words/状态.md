## Why this combination
状 "form, appearance, condition" (as in 情状 "circumstances") combined
with 态 "manner, condition, attitude" (as in 态度 "attitude"): the
condition something is presently in — its state.

## Notes
状态 names a condition observed at a given moment, often physical or
mental (身体状态 "physical condition," 精神状态 "mental state") — 情况,
the more general word for "situation," covers circumstances more broadly
and is not limited to a single subject's condition.

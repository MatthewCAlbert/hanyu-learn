## Why this combination
胜 "to win, to overcome" (see also 胜's own entry — the simplified
character replaced 勝 with an ancient Shuowen graph originally meaning
"the smell of raw meat," borrowed purely for its sound) combined with 利
"gain, advantage, profit" (as in 有利 "advantageous"): the gain that
comes from winning — victory.

## Notes
胜利 names victory in war, competition or struggle broadly (取得胜利
"to achieve victory," 胜利者 "the victor") — 赢, a more colloquial verb,
is used for the everyday sense of "to win" a game or bet.

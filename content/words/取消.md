## Why this combination
取, here in its "to remove, to take away" sense (as in 采取's "to take
up," used oppositely here) combined with 消 "to eliminate, to dissolve
away" (see also 消失): to take away and eliminate — to cancel.

## Notes
取消 takes a planned event or arrangement as its object (取消比赛 "to
cancel the match," 取消资格 "to disqualify," literally "to cancel
qualification") — its rough opposite is 举行 "to hold [an event]."

## Why this combination

海 ("sea," as in 大海) plus 鲜 ("fresh," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 新鲜/鲜花) names fresh food from the sea: seafood.

## Notes

海鲜市场 ("a seafood market") and 海鲜大餐 ("a seafood feast") both build
on this word — 海鲜 covers fish, shellfish, and other sea creatures as
food, distinct from 海水 (covered elsewhere in this app), the water
itself.

## Why this combination
转 "to transfer, to pass along" and 让 "to yield, to give up" are
near-synonyms combined into the formal legal/commercial word for handing
over ownership.

## Notes
转让 anchors 转让股份 "to transfer shares," 商铺转让 "shop for transfer
(sale of a lease/business)" — always about formally passing ownership or
rights to another party, usually for compensation.

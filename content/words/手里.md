## Why this combination
手 ("hand") plus 里 ("inside, within" — the locative suffix seen after
many place words: 屋里, 心里) names the space inside someone's grasp: what
is literally or figuratively in their hand.

## Notes
手里 extends past the literal (钱在我手里, "the money is in my hand") to
figurative control (决定权在他手里, "the power to decide is in his hands")
— the same metaphor English "in someone's hands" uses.

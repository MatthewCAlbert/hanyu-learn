## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 沉 (here in its figurative "heavy, weighty,
withdrawn" sense, covered elsewhere in this app) and 默 ("silent,"
also covered elsewhere — a dog's sudden silent pursuit) both
independently name a withdrawn quietness. Pairing them gives the
standard word for silence, especially a person's habitual or pointed
silence.

## Notes
保持沉默 ("to remain silent") and 沉默寡言 ("taciturn, a man of few
words," a fixed four-character phrase) both build on this pair's core
"withdrawn, deliberate silence" sense — distinct from 安静 (physical
quiet, an absence of noise).

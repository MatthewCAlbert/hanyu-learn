## Why this combination
回 "to return, to go back" and 忆 "to recall" combine directly: going
back mentally to recall the past.

## Notes
回忆 works as verb ("to reminisce," 回忆过去 "to look back on the past")
and noun ("memories," 童年回忆 "childhood memories") — 回忆录 "memoir" is a
common derived compound.

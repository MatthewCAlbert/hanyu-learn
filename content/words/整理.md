## Why this combination
整 "orderly, whole" (see also 整个, 调整) combined with 理 "to put in
order, to arrange" (see also 管理, 理解): to bring order to the whole —
to tidy up, to organise.

## Notes
整理 covers tidying a physical space (整理房间 "to tidy a room") and
organising information (整理资料 "to sort out/collate data") — it is
used for putting disorder into order, distinct from 打扫 "to sweep/clean,"
which is specifically about cleanliness.

## Why this combination
基 "foundation, base" and 本 "root, origin" are close synonyms, paired
into a word for whatever underlies everything else — the fundamentals a
thing cannot do without.

## Notes
基本 works as both an adjective (基本情况 "basic situation") and an adverb
meaning "basically, more or less" (工作基本完成了 "the work is basically
done") — the adverb use hedges a claim as approximately, not exactly,
true.

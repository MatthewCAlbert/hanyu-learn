## Why this combination

季 ("a season, a quarter of the year," as in 季节) plus 度 ("a degree, a
measured extent," as in 温度) names a measured quarter-year period.

## Notes

第一季度 ("first quarter, Q1") and 季度报告 ("quarterly report") are the
standard business/finance terms built on this word — a more precise,
numbered cousin of plain 季节 ("season" in the everyday climate sense).

## Why this combination
新 "new" + 闻 "to hear; what is heard" (见闻, 新闻 — the same 闻 as 闻名). "Newly
heard things" — reports of recent events. 看新闻 "watch / read the news,"
新闻记者 "reporter."

## Notes
新闻 = news as reportage; [[消息]] (not this level) = a piece of news / word from
someone; [[信息]] = information. 闻 also "to smell" (闻到).

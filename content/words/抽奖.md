## Why this combination

抽 ("to draw out, to pull," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
抽烟) takes 奖 ("a prize," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
奖金/获奖) as its object: drawing out a prize.

## Notes

抽奖活动 ("a prize draw event") and 幸运抽奖 ("a lucky draw") both build
on this word — 抽奖 names the drawing mechanism itself, distinct from
中奖 (covered elsewhere in this app), the outcome of actually winning.

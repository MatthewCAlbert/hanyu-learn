## Why this combination
"To make the heart small" — 小 as a verb "shrink, keep small" + 心 "heart" as
its object. A small, tight heart is a watchful one; a large, easy heart is
careless (大意 "negligent" is literally "big idea"). 小心台阶 "mind the step,"
小心开车.

## Notes
Same 心-object pattern as [[关心]], [[放心]]. 小心 as a warning label = "Caution."

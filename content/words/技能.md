## Why this combination
技 "skill, technique" and 能 "ability" are near-synonyms combined into
the formal word for a learned, practical capability.

## Notes
技能 favours trained, practical skills (职业技能 "vocational skills," 技能
培训 "skills training") — more concrete and hands-on than the broader
能力 "ability, capacity" in general.

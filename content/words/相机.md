## Why this combination
Clipped from 照相机 — 照相 "to take a photograph" (照 shine + 相 image) + 机
"machine" (see [[手机]], [[电视机]], [[飞机]]). The first syllable drops, leaving
相 + 机. 数码相机 "digital camera."

## Notes
A separate word 相机 (*xiàngjī*) means "to watch for the right moment"
(相机行事) — 相 as "to look at / assess." Same characters, unrelated.

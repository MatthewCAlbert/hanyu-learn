## Why this combination
自 "self" as the object of 杀 "to kill" gives exactly "to kill oneself"
— as transparent as a compound gets, and splittable (自杀了 works, but so
does the fuller 自我了断 "to end one's own life" in more careful registers).

## Notes
自杀 is the direct, clinical term; Mandarin also uses softer euphemisms
in sensitive contexts (轻生, literally "to take life lightly") where 自杀
might feel too blunt.

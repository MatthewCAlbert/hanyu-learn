## Why this combination
修 "to repair, to mend, to trim" (as in 修理 "to repair") combined with
改 "to change, to correct" (see also 改造): to correct by mending — to
revise, to amend.

## Notes
修改 is used for revising written work, plans or laws (修改文章 "to
revise an essay," 修改计划 "to amend a plan") — 修, alone, is used more
for physically repairing an object, while 改, alone, is the plainer word
for "to change" without 修改's connotation of careful correction.

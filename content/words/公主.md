## Why this combination
公 "duke, public" plus 主 "master, host". The usual explanation is that a king's daughter was married off under the care of a duke, so she was "the duke's responsibility"; not firmly attested. The modern meaning is simply "princess".

Opaque-ish: the parts don't spell it out.

## Why this combination
功 "achievement, effect, work done" (as in 成功 "success," 用功 "to work
hard") combined with 能 "ability, capability" (as in 可能 "possible"):
the effective capability something has — its function.

## Notes
功能 names what a device, organ or system is designed to do (手机的
功能 "a phone's functions," 功能强大 "highly functional/powerful") — it
is used for built-in capability, distinct from 作用 "effect, role,"
which names the effect something has rather than its designed purpose.

## Why this combination

诗 ("poetry") plus 人 ("person") names one who writes poetry: a poet.

## Notes

诗人 follows the same "field + person" pattern as 作家 ("writer") and
科学家 ("scientist," lit. "science + house/expert") — the productive
"person of X" family for naming professions and pursuits.

## Why this combination
化 "to transform, to change into" plus 石 "stone" gives exactly "(what
has) transformed into stone" — the standard scientific term describing
how organic remains become fossilized.

## Notes
化石 anchors 恐龙化石 "dinosaur fossil," 活化石 "living fossil" (a species
essentially unchanged for a very long time) — the standard word across
paleontology and everyday use.

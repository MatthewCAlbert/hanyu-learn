## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 财 ("wealth, money," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 富 ("rich, abundant," as in 富有) both independently name
material abundance. Pairing them gives the standard word for wealth,
emphasizing abundance rather than specific owned assets (that role
belongs to 财产, covered elsewhere in this app).

## Notes
财富 extends figuratively to non-material abundance (精神财富, "spiritual
wealth," e.g. wisdom or culture) — the same "valuable abundance" idea
applied beyond money.

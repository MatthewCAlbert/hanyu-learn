## Why this combination
所罗门 is the phonetic rendering of Solomon; 群岛 "archipelago" translates Islands.
Half transcription, half calque.

## Notes
群岛 as in [[马绍尔群岛]]. 所罗门 is also the biblical Solomon in other words.

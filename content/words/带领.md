## Why this combination
带 "to bring, to carry along, to lead" (as in 带来 "to bring") combined
with 领 "to lead, to guide" (see also 领导, 领先): to bring along and
guide — to lead a group.

## Notes
带领 takes a group being led as its object (带领团队 "to lead a team,"
带领学生参观 "to lead students on a tour") — it stresses physically or
directly guiding people along, closer to "to lead the way for" than
领导's more abstract "to lead an organisation."

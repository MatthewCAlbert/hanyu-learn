## Why this combination
乘 "to ride" (see 乘's own etymology) and 坐 "to sit" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for riding a vehicle.

## Notes
乘坐 is the formal register (乘坐飞机 "to travel by plane," announcements
like 请勿乘坐电梯 "please do not use the elevator") — everyday speech
prefers plain 坐 (坐飞机 "to fly").

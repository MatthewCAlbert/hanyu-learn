## Why this combination
落 "to fall, to drop" (as in 落地 "to land") combined with 后 "behind,
after" (see also 后面, 以后): to fall behind — backward, lagging.

## Notes
落后 describes lagging behind in development or a race (经济落后
"economically backward," 落后国家 "backward/developing country") — its
opposite is 先进, "advanced," built the same way with "ahead" logic in
place of "behind."

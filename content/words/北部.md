## Why this combination
北 "north" (as in 北方) combined with 部 "a part, a section" (see also
南部, 东部, 西部): the northern section — the north of a country or
region, completing the full four-way "direction + 部" set.

## Notes
北部 names the internal northern region of a specific place (中国北部
"northern China") — 北方, its close relative, names "the North" as a
broad cultural/climatic region rather than a bounded internal section.

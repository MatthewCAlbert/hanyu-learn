## Why this combination
正 (here "exactly, precisely") plus 如 "like, as" gives exactly "exactly
as" — a formal written connective introducing a confirming comparison.

## Notes
正如 typically opens a clause echoing a known saying or fact: 正如大家
所说 "just as everyone says" — a written/formal register word common in
essays and speeches.

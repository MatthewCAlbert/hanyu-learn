## Why this combination
职 "a post, an occupation" (see also 职业, 职员) combined with 工
"worker, labour" (see also 工人, 工资): those holding posts as workers —
staff, employees collectively.

## Notes
职工 names the entire workforce of an organisation, blue- and
white-collar together (工厂职工 "factory workers/staff") — 员工, its
close synonym, is used the same way and is largely interchangeable,
with 职工 carrying a slightly older, more institutional flavour.

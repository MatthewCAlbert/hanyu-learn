## Why this combination
"Flying machine," on the same generic 机 "machine" morpheme as 手机, 电视机.
Fully transparent, a native coinage with no borrowed sound. It sits under both
[[travel]] (a thing you travel in) and [[technology]] (a machine).

## Notes
坐飞机 "go by plane." The airport is 机场 ("machine field"); a plane ticket is
机票.

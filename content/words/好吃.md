## Why this combination
好 "good" + 吃 "to eat" → good to eat. Part of the productive 好 + verb frame:
[[好看]] (good to look at), [[好听]] (nice to hear), 好玩 (fun). Applies to food
and drink both.

## Notes
Stress distinguishes a second word: *hàochī* (好 fourth tone) = "gluttonous,"
好 as *hào* "to be fond of" (cf. [[爱好]]).

## Why this combination
男 "male" + 孩儿 "child" (孩 with erhua). The everyday northern word; 男孩 and
男孩子 are the same. Covers boys from infancy to young adulthood, less formal
than [[男生]].

## Notes
Pairs with [[女孩儿]]. 小男孩儿 "a little boy," 大男孩 "a big kid" (of a grown
man).

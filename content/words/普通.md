## Why this combination
普 "universal, general, widespread" (普遍, 普及) + 通 "common, going all the way
through, current everywhere." What is found everywhere and shared by all — hence
"ordinary": 普通人, 普通话 "the common (standard) language."

## Notes
普通 (plain, unremarkable) vs. [[一般]] (typical; also "so-so") vs. [[平常]]
(usual, everyday).

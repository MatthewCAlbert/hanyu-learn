## Why this combination
生 "to be born, to give rise to" and 成 "to become, to form" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for something coming into
existence through a process.

## Notes
生成 anchors 自动生成 "auto-generated," 云层生成 "cloud formation" — used
broadly for natural, chemical, and computational processes that produce a
result, more technical than everyday 产生.

## Why this combination

减 ("to reduce, to subtract," as in 减少) takes 肥 ("fat," as in 肥胖,
"obese") as its object: reducing body fat.

## Notes

减肥 is the everyday word for dieting/weight loss, whatever the method
(exercise, diet, or both) — the object 肥 stays fixed even though what's
actually being reduced is one's weight, not literal grease.

## Why this combination
Two near-synonyms placed side by side: 需 "to require" and 要 "to want,
to need." Neither adds new information the other lacks; pairing them
gives the compound more weight and a more formal register than 要 alone.

## Notes
需要 works as both verb ("I need...") and noun ("a need, a requirement"),
unlike 要, which stays a verb — a common pattern where the two-character
compound gains grammatical flexibility the single character lacks.

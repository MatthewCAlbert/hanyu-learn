## Why this combination
教学 "teaching, instruction" (教 teach + 学 study) + 楼 "multi-storey building."
The building on a campus where classes are held, as against 宿舍楼 (dormitory
block), 办公楼 (office block), 实验楼 (labs).

## Notes
Parse 教学 + 楼. 楼 as "-block / -building" is a productive suffix for named
buildings.

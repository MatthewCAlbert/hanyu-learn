## Why this combination
"Shadow and echo" — a shadow follows its object, an echo follows its sound. The
classical phrase 如影随形，如響應聲 ("like a shadow tracking a shape, an echo
answering a sound") gives the image of one thing inevitably producing another.
Now the plain word for influence.

## Notes
Often negative in the verb use: 影响休息 "disturb (someone's) rest,"
影响不好 "makes a bad impression." Noun: 有影响力 "influential."

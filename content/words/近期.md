## Why this combination
近 "near, close [in time or space]" (as in 最近 "recently") combined
with 期 "a period, a term" (see also 长期, 短期): a period close by [in
time] — the near future, or recently.

## Notes
近期 covers both "in the near future" (近期计划 "near-term plan") and
"recently" (近期发生的事 "something that happened recently") depending
on context — it always marks a time close to the present, whether just
past or just ahead.

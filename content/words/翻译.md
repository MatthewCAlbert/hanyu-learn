## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 翻 (here "to turn/convert," extended from its
literal "to flip over," as in 翻身) and 译 ("to translate," a character
that occurs almost exclusively in this role, as in 译者, "translator")
both independently gesture at converting something into another form.
Pairing them gives the standard word for translation.

Rated "semi" for 翻's extended sense here versus its more literal
"flip/turn over" meaning.

## Notes

翻译 covers both the act (把这句话翻译成英文, "translate this sentence
into English") and the person who does it (他是翻译, "he's a
translator/interpreter") — noun and verb share the same form.

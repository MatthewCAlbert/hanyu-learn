## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + 里 "place." Completes the set: 这里 here, 那里 there,
[[哪里]] where. Northern speech uses 那儿 for the same.

## Notes
With a person it means "at X's place": 在老师那里 "at the teacher's."

## Why this combination

围 ("to surround, to encircle," as in 周围, "surroundings") plus 巾 ("a
piece of cloth," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 毛巾, "towel")
names a piece of cloth wrapped around the neck: a scarf.

## Notes

围巾 shares its 巾 "cloth" root with 毛巾 (towel) and 手绢 (handkerchief) —
all naming small textile items, distinguished by which body part or
purpose 围/毛/手 specifies.

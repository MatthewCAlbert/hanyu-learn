## Why this combination
豆 "bean" plus 制品 "manufactured product" (制 "to make" + 品 "item")
gives exactly "a bean-made product" — the standard umbrella term for
tofu, soy milk, and related foods.

## Notes
豆制品 anchors 豆制品加工 "soy product processing," covering 豆腐 "tofu,"
豆浆 "soy milk," and similar foods — the general commercial/culinary
category, parallel to 水产品 "aquatic products" and 农产品 "agricultural
produce."

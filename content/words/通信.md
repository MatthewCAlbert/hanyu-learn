## Why this combination
通 "to go through, to connect, to be current" (see also 通常, 通知)
combined with 信 "a letter, a message" (as in 相信 in an unrelated
sense, and 短信 "text message"): to keep letters/messages flowing
between two parties — to correspond, and, in the technical sense,
telecommunications.

## Notes
通信 covers both personal correspondence (笔友通信 "pen-pal
correspondence") and the technical field of telecommunications (通信
技术 "communications technology," 通信公司 "telecom company") — 交流,
its neighbour, is the broader word for "exchange" of ideas generally,
not specifically message-based correspondence.

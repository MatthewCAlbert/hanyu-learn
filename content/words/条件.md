## Why this combination
条 "an item, a clause, a strip" (条款, 一条) + 件 "an item, a piece" (文件,
零件). "The itemised points" — the terms of an agreement, the prerequisites for
something, or one's material circumstances: 有条件, 生活条件, 无条件.

## Notes
条件 also = "have what it takes" in 有条件…. In grammar, 条件句 "conditional
clause."

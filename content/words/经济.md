## Why this combination
经济 is a shortening of the classical phrase 经世济民 "to govern the age
and relieve the people" — 经 "to manage, to govern" plus 济 "to aid, to
help across [a difficulty]" (as in 救济 "to give relief"). The modern
sense "economy" was borrowed via Japanese 経済 (keizai), itself coined
from this classical phrase to translate the Western concept.

## Notes
经济 names both the economy as a system (国家经济 "the national economy")
and, as an adjective, "economical, cost-effective" (这样比较经济 "this
way is more economical") — the second sense keeps the original "to save,
to be sparing" flavour more directly.

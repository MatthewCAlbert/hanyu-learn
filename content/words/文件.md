## Why this combination
文 "writing, a written text" (as in 文章 "essay, article") combined with
件, the classifier-turned-noun for a discrete item (see also 事件): a
piece of writing treated as a discrete item — a document, and by
extension, a computer file.

## Notes
文件 covers paper documents (政府文件 "government document") and digital
computer files alike (打开文件 "to open a file") — the same semantic
extension English "document/file" makes.

## Why this combination
脚 "foot" and 步 "step" are near-synonyms combined into the everyday word
for a footstep or pace.

## Notes
脚步 covers the literal sound (脚步声 "the sound of footsteps") and
figurative pace of progress (加快脚步 "to quicken one's pace," 跟上时代的
脚步 "to keep up with the times").

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 纪 ("discipline, order," as in 世纪, "century
[lit. generation + order]") and 律 ("a law, a rule," as in 法律/规律)
both independently name a governing order. Pairing them gives the
standard word for institutional discipline.

## Notes

遵守纪律 ("to abide by the rules") and 纪律严明 ("strict discipline")
both build on this word — 纪律 names externally imposed rules of
conduct (school, military, workplace), distinct from personal 自觉
("self-discipline").

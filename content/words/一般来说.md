## Why this combination

一般 ("general, ordinary") plus 来说 ("speaking of, in terms of," 说 "to
speak") fuses into a fixed opening phrase framing a general statement.

## Notes

一般来说 typically opens a sentence to signal the claim that follows is
a broad generalization, not an absolute rule — 总的来说 ("all things
considered") and 严格来说 ("strictly speaking") are built the same way,
swapping in a different qualifying word before 来说.

## Why this combination
理 "reasoning, logic, principle" (as in 道理 "reason, sense") combined
with 由 "to arise from, [a cause] originating in" (as in 由于 "due to"):
the principle from which [a decision or claim] arises — the reason
behind it.

## Notes
理由 names the justification offered for an action or claim (有理由
"to have grounds [to]," 没有理由 "there's no reason to") — 原因, a
near-synonym, is the more neutral word for "cause" and does not carry the
same sense of a justification put forward in argument.

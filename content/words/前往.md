## Why this combination
前 "forward, ahead" (see also 前面) combined with 往 "to go towards, to
head for" (as in 往前 "onward," 往返 "to go back and forth"): to head
forward towards a destination — to proceed to.

## Notes
前往 is a formal, written verb for travelling to a destination (前往
机场 "to proceed to the airport") — 去, the plain everyday verb "to go,"
is used in speech instead; 前往 belongs to announcements, news and formal
writing.

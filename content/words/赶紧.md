## Why this combination
赶 "to hurry, to rush, to catch up [with something]" (as in 赶上 "to
catch up to") combined with 紧 "tight, urgent" (see also 紧张): to hurry
under [a sense of] urgency — to act without delay.

## Notes
赶紧 sits directly before the verb it urges (赶紧走吧 "hurry up and go,"
赶紧回家 "hurry home") and is used in speech to press immediate action —
赶快, a near-synonym, is used the same way and is largely interchangeable.

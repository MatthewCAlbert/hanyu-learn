## Why this combination
时 "time" is transparent; 光 "light" here is used poetically for the
passage of time — light moving across the sky/seasons — rather than its
literal sense, giving 时光 a more evocative, literary flavour than plain
时间.

## Notes
时光 favours nostalgic or poetic contexts: 美好时光 "wonderful times,"
时光倒流 "time flowing backward [idiom, e.g. in fiction]" — where
neutral, everyday time is just 时间.

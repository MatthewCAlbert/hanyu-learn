## Why this combination
背 "the back [of the body]" (as in 后背 "one's back") combined with 后
"behind, after": the space behind one's back — behind, and figuratively,
done without someone's knowledge.

## Notes
背后 covers literal "behind" (房子背后 "behind the house") and the fixed
idiom sense "behind someone's back" (在背后说别人坏话 "to bad-mouth
someone behind their back") — 后面, its close relative, is the more
neutral, general word for "behind/at the back" without this idiomatic
shade.

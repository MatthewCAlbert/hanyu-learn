## Why this combination
推 "to push" (as in 推荐 "to recommend," literally "to push forward")
combined with 进 "to advance" (see also 前进, 进步): to push and advance
— to drive forward.

## Notes
推进 describes actively pushing a process or plan forward (推进改革
"to push forward reform") — 前进, its neighbour, more often describes
the subject itself moving forward, while 推进 frames the subject as
actively driving something else ahead.

## Why this combination
现 "present, ready, immediately available" (as in 现在 "now") combined
with 金 "metal, money, gold" (as in 资金): money ready in hand right
now — cash, as opposed to credit or electronic payment.

## Notes
现金 is used to specify payment made in physical cash rather than by
card or transfer (用现金支付 "to pay in cash," 现金不够 "not enough cash
on hand") — 钱, the general word for "money," makes no such distinction
of form.

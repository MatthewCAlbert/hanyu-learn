## Why this combination
复, here "to duplicate, again" (as in 反复, 重复 "to repeat") combined
with 印 "to print, to stamp" (as in 印象): to print again, duplicating
— to photocopy.

## Notes
复印 names making a photocopy of a document (复印件 "a photocopy,"
复印机 "photocopier") — 印, alone, is the more general verb "to print,"
while 复印 specifically names duplicating an existing document rather
than original printing.

## Why this combination
大使 "ambassador" (大 "great," 使 "envoy, one sent [on a mission]," as
in 使用 in an unrelated sense) combined with 馆 "a hall, a building for
public use" (see also 旅馆, 图书馆): the building housing the
ambassador — an embassy.

## Notes
大使馆 names the diplomatic mission itself (中国大使馆 "the Chinese
Embassy") — 馆 is broadly productive for buildings serving a public
function (图书馆 "library," 博物馆 "museum"), a naming pattern this word
shares with many others.

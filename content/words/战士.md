## Why this combination
战 ("battle, to make war," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 士 ("a
capable man of standing, here in its military branch," also covered
elsewhere) names one who fights battles: a soldier.

## Notes
战士 can be used more broadly and honorifically than 士兵 (covered
elsewhere in this app) — 战士 fits a fighter for any cause (环保战士, "an
environmental warrior"), while 士兵 stays strictly military and
rank-and-file.

## Why this combination
访 "to visit, to call on" (as in 访问's own 访, seen also in 采访 "to
interview," literally "to gather and visit") combined with 问 "to ask,
to inquire" (as in 问题 "question"): to visit and inquire — to visit
formally, especially to converse and learn.

## Notes
访问 names a formal visit, often diplomatic or professional (国事访问
"state visit," 访问学者 "visiting scholar") and, by extension, a
computer's act of accessing data (访问网站 "to visit/access a website")
— both senses share the underlying "to go to and engage with" logic.

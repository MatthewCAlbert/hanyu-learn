## Why this combination
回 "to turn back, to return" acting on the object 头 "head" gives the
literal "to turn one's head [to look back]," which extends naturally to
"later" — something to be dealt with once you've turned back to it.

## Notes
回头 covers the literal gesture (回头看 "to look back/turn around") and
the everyday time adverb "later, in a bit" (回头再说 "we'll talk about
it later") — a common softener for postponing something in casual speech.

## Why this combination
停 "to stop" and 留 "to remain, to stay" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for a temporary halt or stay.

## Notes
停留 is used for a brief, non-permanent stay: 在北京停留三天 "to stop
over in Beijing for three days" — distinct from 居住 "to reside," which
implies settling in.

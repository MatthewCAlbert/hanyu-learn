## Why this combination

矿 ("mineral, ore") plus 泉 ("a spring," a natural water source) plus
水 ("water") names water from a mineral spring: mineral water.

## Notes

一瓶矿泉水 ("a bottle of mineral water") is the standard everyday phrase
— the compound stacks three descriptive nouns, each narrowing the
category: water, from a spring, containing minerals.

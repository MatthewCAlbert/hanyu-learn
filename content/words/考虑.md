## Why this combination
考 is "to examine, to test" (as in 考试), and 虑 is "to ponder, to turn over
in one's mind, to worry about" (as in 忧虑, 顾虑). Examining a decision
carefully while turning it over in one's mind is exactly what "to consider"
means — a clean near-synonym pairing.

## Notes
考虑一下 ("to think it over a bit") is a very common softener when declining
or delaying a decision — it does not commit to a yes, unlike a direct
answer would.

## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 创 "to start, to found, to originate" (see also
创造, 创新) takes 业 "a trade, an undertaking" (see also 事业, 职业) as its
object — literally "to found an undertaking" — to start a business.

## Notes
创业 names starting a business or venture from scratch, with an
entrepreneurial connotation (创业公司 "startup company," 创业精神
"entrepreneurial spirit") — 就业, its near-neighbour built on the same
业, means instead "to gain employment [with an existing organisation]."

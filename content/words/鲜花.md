## Why this combination

鲜 ("fresh," as in 新鲜) plus 花 ("a flower") names flowers still fresh
and blooming: fresh flowers, as opposed to dried or artificial ones.

## Notes

送鲜花 ("to give/send fresh flowers") is the standard gift-giving phrase
— 鲜花 specifically emphasizes freshness, distinct from plain 花, the
general word for "flower" regardless of state.

## Why this combination
预 ("in advance, beforehand," as in 预习, 预报) plus 测 ("to measure,"
covered elsewhere in this app) names measuring or gauging something
before it happens: a prediction.

## Notes
预测 leans technical/statistical (天气预测, "weather forecasting"; 预测
结果, "to predict an outcome") — a measured, evidence-based forecast,
distinct from a casual guess.

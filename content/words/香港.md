## Why this combination
香 "fragrant" + 港 "harbour / port": the Cantonese toponym, not a transcription
of English Hong Kong. The English name is itself from the Cantonese reading.

## Notes
港 as "harbour" also in 港口, 海港. Extra geography, not an HSK word.

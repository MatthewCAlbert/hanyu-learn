## Why this combination
之 (classical possessive/linking particle) plus 外 "outside" gives a
formal written equivalent of plain 外 "outside." Pairs with 之中, 之内, 之下
as one small family of classical-flavoured locatives.

## Notes
之外 trails a noun phrase (工作之外 "outside of work") and, in 除...之外,
sets up an "apart from X" exception clause — a common formal-writing
pattern.

## Why this combination
开幕 "to open (an event)" (see its own entry) plus the suffix 式
"ceremony, style" gives exactly "the ceremony of opening" — the standard
word for the formal event marking a start.

## Notes
开幕式 anchors 奥运会开幕式 "the Olympics opening ceremony" — always the
formal event itself, opposite of 闭幕式 "closing ceremony."

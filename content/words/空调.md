## Why this combination
空调 shortens 空气调节 "air adjustment/conditioning" to its first
character from each half: 空 from 空气 "air" and 调 from 调节 "to
adjust" (see also 调整) — the same compression pattern seen in 科技 and
环保.

## Notes
空调 names both the technology and, casually, the unit itself (开空调
"to turn on the AC," 中央空调 "central air conditioning") — the full form
空气调节 is rarely used in everyday speech, having been fully replaced by
this shortened form.

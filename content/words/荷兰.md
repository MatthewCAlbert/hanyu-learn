## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Holland. 荷 and 兰 are chosen for sound,
not for "lotus + orchid." In ordinary Mandarin 荷兰 names the Netherlands, not
the Holland provinces alone.

## Notes
兰 is a favourite phonetic closer for foreign -land / -lan names ([[芬兰]],
[[波兰]], [[爱尔兰]], [[新西兰]]). Same stem in [[荷兰语]], [[荷兰文]].

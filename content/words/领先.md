## Why this combination
领 "to lead, to guide" (see also 领导) combined with 先 "first, ahead, to
precede": to lead by being first — to be ahead, in the lead.

## Notes
领先 describes being ahead in a race, competition or field of
development (比分领先 "leading in score," 技术领先 "technologically
ahead") — 领导, its close relative built on the same 领, is instead
"to lead an organisation" as a person or authority, not "to be ahead" in
a ranking.

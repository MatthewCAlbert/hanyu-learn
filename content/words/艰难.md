## Why this combination
艰 (see 艰's own etymology — 土難治也, "hard-to-work soil") and 难
"difficult" are near-synonyms combined into the standard formal word for
serious difficulty.

## Notes
艰难 is more formal/weighty than everyday 难: 艰难的决定 "a difficult
decision," 艰难岁月 "hard times" — implies sustained struggle rather than
a single tricky problem.

## Why this combination
剩 "to remain" with the directional complement 下 "down, behind" gives
"to be left [behind]" — the complement marks the result of something
being used up or taken away.

## Notes
剩下 is the everyday verb for leftovers of any kind: 剩下的钱 "the money
left over," 只剩下他一个人 "only he was left."

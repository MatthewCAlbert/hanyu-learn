## Why this combination
年 "year" + 级 "grade, rank, level" (see [[高级]], [[班级]]) → a year-group in a
school: 三年级 "third grade / Year 3," 高年级学生 "older pupils."

## Notes
年级 = a year-cohort; [[班级]] = a class within it; [[学期]] = a term. 级 "grade
/ step" also in [[高级]], 初级, 升级.

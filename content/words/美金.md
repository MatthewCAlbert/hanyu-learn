## Why this combination

美 ("America," as in 美国) plus 金 ("gold, money," as in 金子) names
American money: the US dollar.

## Notes

美金 and 美元 are near-synonyms for the US dollar — 美元 is the more
formal/official term (used in banking and finance), while 美金 is common
in everyday speech, especially in Chinese-speaking communities outside
mainland China.

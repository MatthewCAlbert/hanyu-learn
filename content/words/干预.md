## Why this combination
干 (here "to interfere, to concern oneself with," as in 干涉) and 预
(here "to take part in," as in 参预/参与) are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for inserting oneself into a matter.

## Notes
干预 anchors 政府干预 "government intervention," 干预他人生活 "to meddle
in others' lives" — usually implies an outside party acting on a matter
not fully theirs to control.

## Why this combination
放 "to release, to set down" + 心 "heart" as its object — "to let the heart
rest," to stop it churning with worry. 你放心 "don't worry," 不放心 "uneasy
about it," 放心不下 "can't stop worrying."

## Notes
Same 心-object family as [[小心]], [[关心]]. Opposite: 担心 "worry" (lit. "carry
the heart").

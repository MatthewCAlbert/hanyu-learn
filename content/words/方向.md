## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 方 "a direction, a quarter" (东方, [[方面]]) + 向 "toward, facing,
a bearing" (向前, 方向). The way something faces or heads — literal (往这个方向)
and figurative (人生的方向, 政策方向).

## Notes
方向盘 "steering wheel." Sits with the [[direction]] set but names a heading /
course rather than a fixed side.

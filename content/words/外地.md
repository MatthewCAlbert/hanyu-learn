## Why this combination
外 "outside, other" (see [[外国]], [[外语]], [[以外]]) + 地 "place, locality"
(see [[地方]], [[地点]]) → somewhere other than one's own locality — but still
within the country. 外地人 "someone from out of town," 去外地出差
"travel elsewhere for work."

## Notes
外地 = elsewhere *in the country* (vs. 本地 "local"); [[国外]] = abroad.
A common word in a society where 户口 ties people to a home place.

## Why this combination
信 "to believe, to trust" and 念 "thought, idea" combine directly: a
belief held firmly in mind.

## Notes
信念 anchors 坚定的信念 "unwavering conviction," 失去信念 "to lose faith" —
a personal, often ideological or moral conviction, distinct from
religious 信仰.

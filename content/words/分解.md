## Why this combination
分 "to divide" and 解 (here "to separate, to dissolve," as in 溶解) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for breaking something into
its component parts.

## Notes
分解 spans chemistry (物质分解 "chemical decomposition"), math (因式分解
"factorization"), and figurative use (把任务分解成几步 "to break a task
down into steps") — always separation into constituent pieces.

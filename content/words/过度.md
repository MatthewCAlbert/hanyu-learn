## Why this combination
过 "to exceed, to go past" and 度 "degree, measure" combine directly:
exceeding the proper measure.

## Notes
过度 modifies actions and states that have gone past a healthy or
reasonable limit: 过度劳累 "overexertion," 过度紧张 "excessive tension" —
always implies a limit that should not have been crossed.

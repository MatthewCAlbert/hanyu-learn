## Why this combination
首 "head, first" (recall the body-part metaphor: the head leads, so
"head" comes to mean "first, foremost") and 先 "early, before" are close
synonyms, paired for a formal sequencing word.

## Notes
首先...其次...最后... is a fixed enumerating pattern ("firstly...
secondly... finally...") that structures formal speech and writing —
recognising 首先 as its opening marker helps a listener track the whole
structure ahead of time.

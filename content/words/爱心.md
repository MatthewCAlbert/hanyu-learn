## Why this combination
爱 "to love" (as in 爱好, 热爱) combined with 心 "heart" (see also 心情,
内心): a heart full of love — compassion, caring for others.

## Notes
爱心 names selfless caring extended to others in general, especially in
charitable contexts (献爱心 "to show one's compassion/give charitably,"
爱心活动 "charity event") — 爱情, its neighbour, is romantic love between
partners, a quite different, narrower sense.

## Why this combination
皮 (skin/hide, of people or animals) and 肤 (skin, specifically human)
are near-synonyms combined into the standard clinical/everyday word for
human skin.

## Notes
皮肤 is the formal/medical-adjacent term (皮肤科 "dermatology"); 皮 alone
is used more casually and also covers animal hide and fruit peel, which
皮肤 does not.

## Why this combination
牛仔 "cowboy" (牛 "cattle" + 仔 "lad, young hand," i.e. a cattle-herder)
plus 裤 "trousers" calques the American association of denim trousers
with cowboys — "cowboy pants" — rather than transliterating English
"jeans."

## Notes
牛仔裤 anchors 一条牛仔裤 "a pair of jeans," 牛仔裤外套 (colloquially)
"denim jacket" — 牛仔 alone extends to "denim" generally in some
compounds (牛仔外套 "denim jacket").

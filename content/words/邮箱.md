## Why this combination
邮 "postal, mail" (see also 邮件, 邮局 "post office") combined with 箱 "a
box, a case" (as in 行李箱 "suitcase"): the box for mail — a mailbox,
extended naturally to a digital email inbox.

## Notes
邮箱 names both the physical postbox (信件放在邮箱里 "the letter's in the
mailbox") and, just as commonly today, an email account (电子邮箱
"email inbox," often just 邮箱) — the same physical-to-digital extension
seen in 邮件 "mail/email."

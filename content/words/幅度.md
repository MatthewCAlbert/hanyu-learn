## Why this combination
幅 (here "breadth, extent," as in 篇幅) plus 度 "degree, measure" gives
exactly "the degree of breadth" — the standard word for how much
something has changed or varies.

## Notes
幅度 anchors 大幅度 "by a large margin," 涨价幅度 "the extent of a price
increase" — always used to quantify the size of a change, not a static
measurement.

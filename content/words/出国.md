## Why this combination
出 "to go out" + 国 "country" as its object (like [[回国]], [[回家]]) — to
leave one's own country: 出国留学, 出国旅游. Verb-object: 出过国.

## Notes
出国 (leave the country) vs. [[出门]] (leave home) vs. [[出发]] (set off).
Opposite [[回国]] "return to one's home country."

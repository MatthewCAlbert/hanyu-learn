## Why this combination

楼 ("a multi-story building," as in 大楼) plus 梯 ("a ladder, steps," as
in 电梯, "elevator") names the ladder-like steps connecting a building's
floors: a staircase.

## Notes

上楼梯/下楼梯 ("to go up/down the stairs") pairs 楼梯 with directional
verbs; 电梯 (elevator) shares the same 梯 root, the electrified version
of the same "steps between floors" idea.

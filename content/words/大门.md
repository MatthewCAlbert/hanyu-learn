## Why this combination
大 "big, main" + 门 "door, gate" → the principal gate of a compound or
building, as against a side door (侧门) or back door (后门). 学校大门口.

## Notes
门 "gate / door" also in [[门口]], [[门票]], [[出门]]. The "grand family"
sense (名门大户) is dated.

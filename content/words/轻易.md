## Why this combination

轻 ("light in weight, light in effort," as in 轻松) and 易 ("easy," as in
容易) both name effortlessness; paired as an adverb they describe doing
something without difficulty or without due care — "lightly, easily."

## Notes

轻易 leans negative in tone: 不要轻易放弃 ("don't give up easily") and
他不轻易生气 ("he doesn't get angry easily/rashly") both frame the
effortlessness as something that shouldn't happen so readily.

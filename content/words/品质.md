## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 品 ("an item, and by extension moral character,"
as in 商品, "merchandise," and 人品, "personal character") and 质
("substance, quality," as in 质量) both independently name an inherent
nature or standard. Pairing them gives the word for quality, whether of
goods or of character.

## Notes

品质 covers both a product's quality (产品品质, "product quality") and a
person's moral character (品质优秀, "of excellent character") — 质量
leans more toward the former sense alone.

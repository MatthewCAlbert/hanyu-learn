## Why this combination
答 already means "to answer" (答案 answer, 回答). 回 "back, in return" adds the
direction — sending a response back. Close to a same-sense pair, with 回
supplying the "in return" nuance.

## Notes
Both verb (回答问题) and noun (满意的回答). Contrast 答应 "to agree / to promise,"
where 答 pairs with a different second element.

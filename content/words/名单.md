## Why this combination
名 "name" + 单 "a sheet, a list, a form" (菜单 menu, 账单 bill, [[清单]]). A
sheet of names — a roster, a shortlist: 参加名单, 黑名单 "blacklist."

## Notes
单 as "-list / form" also in [[菜单]], [[分数]]'s cousin 成绩单 "report card,"
订单 "order form."

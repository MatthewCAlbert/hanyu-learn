## Why this combination
就 here is not the everyday "then" of 就走 "go right away" — it is the
adverbial 就 that means "precisely, exactly," pinning down 是 "to be" so
the whole phrase asserts an identification firmly: "that's exactly it."
The transparency is semi rather than transparent because a learner who
only knows 就 as "then" will not immediately land on "precisely."

## Notes
就是 also works as a concessive conjunction — 就是...也 "even if..." —
extending the "precisely, even in that exact case" logic to hypothetical
situations.

## Why this combination
草 "grass" + 地 "ground, land" (see [[地方]], [[地上]]) → grass-covered ground —
a lawn in a park, a mountain meadow, open grassland. 在草地上野餐.

## Notes
草地 = grassy ground generally; 草原 "the steppe / prairie" (a vast wild
grassland); 草坪 "a manicured lawn." 草 "grass" also in 草药 "herbs," 除草.

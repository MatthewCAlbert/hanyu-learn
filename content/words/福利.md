## Why this combination
福 "blessing, good fortune" and 利 "benefit" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for material welfare and benefits.

## Notes
福利 anchors 社会福利 "social welfare," 员工福利 "employee benefits" — the
formal term for institutionally provided material support, distinct from
personal 幸福 "happiness."

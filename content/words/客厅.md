## Why this combination
客 "guest" and 厅 "hall" (see 厅's own etymology) combine directly: the
hall of a home where guests are received.

## Notes
客厅 is the standard word for a home's living/reception room, distinct
from 卧室 "bedroom" and 大厅 "a large public hall" — 客厅 is specifically
private and residential.

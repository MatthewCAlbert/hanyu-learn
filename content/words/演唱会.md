## Why this combination
演唱 "to sing for an audience" (演 "to perform," see also 表演, plus 唱
"to sing," see also 唱歌) combined with 会 "a gathering, a meeting" (see
also 会议, 音乐会): a gathering to hear singing performed — a concert.

## Notes
演唱会 names specifically a vocal/singing concert (开演唱会 "to hold a
concert") — 音乐会, its close relative, is the broader word for any
musical concert, instrumental or vocal.

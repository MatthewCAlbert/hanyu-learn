## Why this combination
怎么 "how" (see [[怎么]]) + 办 "to handle, to deal with, to manage" (see
[[办法]]) → "how to handle it." A cry for a solution: 钱丢了，怎么办？

## Notes
该怎么办 "what should be done"; 那可怎么办 "now what?". 办 = "get sth done"
also in [[办公室]], 办事.

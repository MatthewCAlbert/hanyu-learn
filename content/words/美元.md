## Why this combination
美 stands in for 美国 "America" (itself an abbreviation, 美 being the
sound-transliteration syllable chosen for "America"), attached to 元
"a unit of currency" (the general word behind 一元 "one yuan/dollar").
Semi-transparent: readable once you know 美 abbreviates "America," but
not obvious without that piece.

## Notes
The same pattern names other currencies by country abbreviation plus 元
— 日元 "yen" (Japan), 欧元 "euro" (Europe) — a small, productive family
worth recognising as a set.

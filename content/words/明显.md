## Why this combination
明 "bright, clear" and 显 "visible, apparent" (recall its etymology: a
head-ornament that catches the light) are close synonyms, both about
something being easy to perceive, paired for emphasis.

## Notes
明显 describes how easily something can be perceived or understood;
contrast 清楚, which leans toward clarity of expression or thought
rather than visibility of a fact.

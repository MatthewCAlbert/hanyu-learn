## Why this combination
速 "quick, fast, rapid" (as in 高速 "high-speed") combined with 度 "a
measure, a degree, an extent" (as in 温度 "temperature," 长度 "length"):
the measured degree of quickness — speed, rate.

## Notes
速度 measures how fast anything moves or changes — traffic, typing, an
economy's growth (发展速度 "rate of development") — while 快 is simply
the adjective "fast" and cannot be modified by a number the way 速度 can
(时速 100 公里 "100 km/h").

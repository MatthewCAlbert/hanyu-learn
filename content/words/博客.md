## Why this combination
博客 transliterates English "blog" (bó-kè approximates the sound); the
characters 博 "extensive, erudite" and 客 "guest, visitor" were chosen for
sound, not sense, though they read agreeably as "a place of broad
knowledge for visitors." Do not treat that as the coinage's actual logic.

## Notes
博客 names both the site/post ("a blog") and, informally, the writer
("a blogger," more precisely 博主). Largely displaced in everyday use by
微博 "Weibo" and other social platforms, but still the standard word for
"blog" as a format.

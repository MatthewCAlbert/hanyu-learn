## Why this combination

预 ("in advance, beforehand," as in 预报, "a forecast") plus 订 ("to
order, to book, to subscribe," as in 订婚, "to get engaged [lit. book +
marriage]") names arranging something ahead of time: to book in advance.

## Notes

预订机票 ("to book a flight ticket") and 预订酒店 ("to reserve a hotel")
both build on this word — the "in advance" 预 prefix is productive
elsewhere too (预习, "to preview/preread"; 预测, "to predict").

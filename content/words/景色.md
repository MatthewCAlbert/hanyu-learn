## Why this combination
景 "scenery, a view" (as in 风景 "scenery") combined with 色 "colour,
appearance" (as in 特色, 彩色): the colourful appearance of a view —
scenery, landscape.

## Notes
景色 names a scenic view, often praised for its beauty (景色优美
"beautiful scenery," 山上的景色 "the view from the mountain") — 风景, a
close synonym, is used the same way and is largely interchangeable, with
风景 being somewhat more common overall.

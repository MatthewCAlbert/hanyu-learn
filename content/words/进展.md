## Why this combination
进 "to advance, to move forward" (see also 进一步) combined with 展 "to
unfold, to extend, to spread out" (as in 发展 "to develop"): to advance
by unfolding further — to make progress.

## Notes
进展 names measurable headway on a project or process (工作进展顺利
"work is progressing smoothly," 没有进展 "no progress") — it works as
both verb and noun without changing form, and is more formal than the
everyday 进步 "to improve, progress," which applies more to a person's
own improvement.

## Why this combination
有 "to have" + 名 "name, reputation" → "to have a name (that gets around)" =
famous. The 有 + noun pattern of [[有用]], 有钱, 有意思. Here 名 is "renown,"
not just "a name."

## Notes
Near-synonym 著名 is more formal / written; 出名 is "become famous" (the
inceptive). 名 as "renown" also in 名人, 名牌.

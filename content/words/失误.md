## Why this combination
失 "to lose, to miss" and 误 "mistake, error" combine directly: missing
the mark through error.

## Notes
失误 favours performance contexts where a mistake breaks otherwise
correct execution: 操作失误 "an operational error," 关键失误 "a critical
mistake [in a game]" — a specific slip rather than a general error in
judgement.

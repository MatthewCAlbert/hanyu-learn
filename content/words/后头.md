## Why this combination

后 ("behind, after," as in 后来) plus 头 (here a bare directional
suffix, an extended sense beyond its literal "head") names the
location behind: in back.

Rated "semi" for 头's suffix role here, distinct from its literal
"head" meaning.

## Notes

后头 and 后面 are close near-synonyms for "behind" — 后头 leans more
casual/colloquial, common in northern spoken Mandarin, while 后面 is
the neutral, more widely used everyday term.

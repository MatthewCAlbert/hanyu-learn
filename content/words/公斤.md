## Why this combination
斤 is the traditional Chinese weight unit, the catty (now fixed at 500 g). 公
marks the metric standard, as in [[公里]] (kilometre), 公分 (centimetre) — so
公斤 is a "metric catty" defined as 1000 g, i.e. a kilogram.

## Notes
Markets still price by 斤 (三块钱一斤); official and scientific weights use 公斤
or 千克 (the fully explicit synonym). 半公斤 = 一斤.

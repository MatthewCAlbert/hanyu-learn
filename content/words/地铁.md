## Why this combination
地下 "underground" + 铁路 "railway" (铁 "iron" standing for the iron rails),
each clipped to one syllable: 地 + 铁. 坐地铁, [[地铁站]] "metro station,"
一号线 "Line 1."

## Notes
铁 = "railway" in 铁路, 高铁 "high-speed rail." Full form 地下铁道 / 地下铁, now
rarely written out.

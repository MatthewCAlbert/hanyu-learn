## Why this combination
保 "to protect" and 卫 "to guard" (see 卫's role in 卫星) are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for active defence.

## Notes
保卫 favours defending something valuable or under threat: 保卫祖国 "to
defend one's homeland," 保卫和平 "to safeguard peace" — a more formal,
active-defence register than everyday 保护 "to protect."

## Why this combination
打 as the all-purpose light verb "to do" (see [[打电话]], [[打车]]) + 工 "work,
labour" as its object. It entered the mainland from Cantonese in the reform era
and carries a flavour of hired, non-permanent labour: 出去打工 (migrant work),
学生打工 (a student job), 打工人 (self-deprecating "wage slave").

## Notes
打工 = work for someone else, often temporary; [[工作]] = work / job (neutral);
上班 = go to (one's regular) work. Splits: 打过工, 打零工 "odd jobs."

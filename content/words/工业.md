## Why this combination
工 "work, labour" (see also 工厂, 工具) combined with 业 "a trade, a
field of endeavour" (see also 职业, 商业): labour organised as a trade —
industry.

## Notes
工业 names the industrial/manufacturing sector as a whole (工业化
"industrialisation," 重工业 "heavy industry") — 工厂, its close relative,
names an individual factory rather than the sector at large.

## Why this combination

粗 ("coarse, rough," as in 粗糙, "rough in texture") plus 心 ("heart,
mind") names a rough, inattentive state of mind: careless.

Rated "semi" for the figurative "rough mind = inattentive" extension,
paralleling the finer, more attentive image behind 细致 (covered
elsewhere in this app).

## Notes

粗心大意 ("careless and inattentive," a common set phrase) and 粗心的
错误 ("a careless mistake") both build on this word — 细心 ("careful,
attentive," lit. "fine + mind") is its direct antonym, built the same
way with 细 "fine/thin" instead of 粗 "coarse."

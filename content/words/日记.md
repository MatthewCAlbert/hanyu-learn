## Why this combination

日 ("day") plus 记 ("to record, to note down," as in 记得) names a
day-by-day written record: a diary.

## Notes

写日记 ("to keep a diary, lit. to write a diary") is the standard verb
pairing — 日记 names the ongoing practice as much as any single entry.

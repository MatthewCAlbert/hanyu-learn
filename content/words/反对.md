## Why this combination
反 "to turn against, to reverse, to be contrary to" (see also 反应)
combined with 对 "to face, to oppose, to be directed at": to stand facing
against something — to oppose it.

## Notes
反对 takes a plan, opinion or person's stance as its object (反对这个
计划 "to oppose this plan") and forms the fixed phrase 反对意见 "objection,
opposing view" — its opposite is 支持 "to support."

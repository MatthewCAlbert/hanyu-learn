## Why this combination
学 "schooling, classes" as the object of 放 "to release, to let go" (the same
放 as [[放假]]) — the school lets the pupils go. 放学后 "after school,"
还没放学 "school's not out yet."

## Notes
Note the asymmetry: you go to school with [[上学]], but the day ends with 放学,
not *下学. 上课 / [[下课]] is the per-lesson pair.

## Why this combination
送 "to deliver, to send, to see off" (see [[送给]], [[外卖]]) + resultative 到
"reach (a destination)" — the delivery arrives: 送到家门口, 把客人送到车站.

## Notes
送 covers three senses — give (送礼), deliver (送货), and escort out (送客).
送到 pins down the arrival point.

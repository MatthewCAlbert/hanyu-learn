## Why this combination
装 "to install, to dress up" and 饰 "to decorate, to adorn" (see 饰's own
etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for
decoration.

## Notes
装饰 covers interior design (室内装饰 "interior decoration") and personal
adornment (装饰品 "an ornament/accessory") — always about enhancing
appearance rather than function.

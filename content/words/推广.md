## Why this combination
推 "to push" (see also 推动, 推进) combined with 广 "wide, broad" (as in
广场, 广告): to push out wide — to popularise, to extend the reach of
something.

## Notes
推广 takes a technology, method or product as its object being spread
to wider use (推广新技术 "to popularise new technology," 推广产品 "to
promote a product") — it stresses broadening reach and adoption,
distinct from 宣传's "to publicise, to advertise" focus on messaging.

## Why this combination

总 ("overall, chief, in charge of the whole," as in 总统, "president")
plus 理 ("to manage, to administer," as in 管理) names the person who
manages the whole (government): premier, prime minister.

## Notes

总理 (premier, head of the State Council in China) and 总统 (president,
a head of state elsewhere) both build on 总 "chief/overall" but name
distinct roles — the same root, different offices.

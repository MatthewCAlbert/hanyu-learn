## Why this combination
Traditionally a 时 (时辰) was a double-hour, one twelfth of the day. When the
Western 60-minute hour arrived it was named the "small 时" against that older
"big" unit, and 小时 stuck.

## Notes
A measure-like noun: 三个小时 "three hours" needs 个, but 三点 "three o'clock"
does not. 钟头 is the colloquial synonym.

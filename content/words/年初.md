## Why this combination
年 "year" combined with 初 "beginning, start" (as in 初中 "junior middle
school," literally "beginning-level middle school"): the beginning of
the year.

## Notes
年初 pairs with 年底 "end of the year" (see its own entry) as a matched
opposite — 初 as "beginning" also appears in 月初 "start of the month" and
起初 "at first," a consistent pattern across time words.

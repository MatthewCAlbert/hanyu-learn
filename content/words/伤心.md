## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 伤 "to injure, to wound" (see also 受伤) takes
心 "heart" as its object — literally "to wound the heart" — to be
deeply hurt.

## Notes
伤心 names deep emotional pain, especially grief or sorrow (让人伤心
"heartbreaking," 伤心地哭 "to cry sorrowfully") — 难过, a near-synonym,
covers a broader range of unhappy feeling and is somewhat milder than
伤心's specifically wounded, grieving sense.

## Why this combination

奖 ("a prize, an award," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 奖金
and 获奖) plus 学 ("study," as in 学习) plus 金 ("money," as in 美金)
names money awarded for study: a scholarship.

## Notes

获得奖学金 ("to receive a scholarship") is the standard frame — 奖学金
shares its 奖 "prize" root with 奖金 ("a bonus"), differing in that the
recipient here is specifically rewarded for academic study.

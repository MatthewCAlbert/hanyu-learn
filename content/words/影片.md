## Why this combination
影 "shadow, image" (see [[电影]]) + 片 "a thin strip / flat piece" — here the
strip of film. A more formal / written synonym of [[电影]]: 一部影片,
纪录影片 "documentary."

## Notes
片 as "-strip / piece" also in [[照片]], [[图片]], 片子 (colloquial for a
film / disc). 影片 vs. [[电影]]: same thing, 影片 bookish.

## Why this combination
模 "a mould, a model" pairs with 糊 "paste" (see 糊's own etymology, a
grain-based paste with no clear shape) — an outline as indistinct as
something smeared with paste, giving "blurred, vague."

## Notes
模糊 covers visual blur (照片模糊 "the photo is blurry") and conceptual
vagueness (记忆模糊 "hazy memory," 态度模糊 "an ambiguous attitude") — one
word for unclear boundaries, visual or conceptual.

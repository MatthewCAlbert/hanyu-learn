## Why this combination
心 "heart" plus 疼 "to ache" gives exactly "the heart aches" — a
subject-predicate description of feeling pained on someone's (or
something's) behalf.

## Notes
心疼 covers both tender affection (奶奶心疼孙子 "grandma dotes on her
grandson") and regret over loss (心疼钱 "to grudge spending money,"
pained at the cost) — always an emotional ache tied to care.

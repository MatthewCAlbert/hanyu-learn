## Why this combination
流 "to flow" and 动 "to move" combine directly: continuous movement, as
of a liquid or, figuratively, of people and things.

## Notes
流动 covers literal flow (水流动 "water flows") and social mobility
(人口流动 "population movement," 流动人口 "migrant/floating population") —
extending fluid dynamics to social and economic circulation.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 学 ("to study," as in 学习) and 问 ("to ask,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 问候) both independently name
the pursuit of knowledge through study and inquiry. Pairing them gives
the everyday word for learning.

Rated "semi" for 问's contribution here — naming the questioning half of
learning rather than a literal single question.

## Notes

有学问 ("learned, knowledgeable, lit. has scholarship") and 大学问
("a deep subject, lit. big scholarship") both build on this word — a
person's accumulated erudition, distinct from 知识, the more general
word for "knowledge" as content.

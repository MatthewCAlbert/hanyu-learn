## Why this combination
午 "noon, midday" (see [[中午]], [[午饭]]) + 睡 "to sleep" (see [[睡觉]]) → the
after-lunch nap, an established part of the daily rhythm in China: 睡午睡,
午睡时间.

## Notes
午睡 ≈ 午觉 (with the cognate object 觉, like [[睡觉]]). 午休 is the "midday
rest / break" more broadly (which may just mean lunch).

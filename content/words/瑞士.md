## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of French Suisse. 瑞 and 士 are chosen for
sound, not for "auspicious + scholar." Do not confuse with [[瑞典]].

## Notes
瑞 is shared with [[瑞典]]; the second character is what splits Switzerland from
Sweden.

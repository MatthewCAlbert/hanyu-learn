## Why this combination
果, here in its "result, outcome" sense (see also 结果, 效果) combined
with the classical manner-suffix 然 "so, in that way" (as in 自然, 突
然): turning out to be so, as a result — confirming what was expected.

## Notes
果然 confirms that reality matched a prior expectation or prediction
(我猜他会迟到，他果然迟到了 "I guessed he'd be late, and sure enough he
was") — it always implies the outcome was foreseen, unlike 竟然
"surprisingly," which marks the opposite, an unexpected outcome.

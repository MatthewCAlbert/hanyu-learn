## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 分 ("to divide," as in 分开) and 散 ("to
scatter, to disperse," the same character that gives the "stroll" verb
散步 its "spreading out one's steps" flavor) both independently name
spreading things apart. Pairing them gives the standard word for
dispersal.

## Notes

分散 covers physical scattering (人群分散, "the crowd dispersed") and
attention (分散注意力, "to distract one's attention") equally
comfortably.

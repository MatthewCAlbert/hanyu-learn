## Why this combination
看 is the activity "to look"; 到 "to reach, to arrive" is a resultative
complement saying the looking succeeded — you looked *and got there*, i.e. you
saw. Without a complement, 看 is only the attempt.

## Notes
Near-identical to [[看见]] (look + perceive). Both answer "did the looking land?"
— 我看了但是没看到 "I looked but didn't see it" is perfectly ordinary.

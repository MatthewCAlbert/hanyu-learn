## Why this combination
图 "a chart, a picture, a diagram" (as in 地图 "map") combined with 画
"to draw, a painting" (as in 画家 "painter," 画儿 "picture"): a pictured
drawing — an illustration.

## Notes
图画 is the formal, written word for a drawing or picture (儿童图画书
"children's picture book") — 画儿, the everyday spoken word, is what is
actually used in casual conversation about a specific picture or
drawing.

## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" (see also 成功, 成立) combined with 就 "to
accomplish, to move towards completion" (as in 成就's own 就 seen
independently as "namely, then"): to become accomplished — achievement.

## Notes
成就 names a significant accomplishment, often in a career or field
(取得成就 "to achieve success/an accomplishment," 巨大的成就 "a huge
achievement") — 成功, its close relative, is more often the general
adjective/verb "successful, to succeed," while 成就 leans towards the
noun naming the accomplishment itself.

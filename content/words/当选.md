## Why this combination
当 (here "to serve as, to become") acting through 选 "to select, to
elect" gives exactly "to become the selected one" — the standard word for
winning an election.

## Notes
当选 always names the resulting office or role: 当选主席 "elected as
chairman," 当选总统 "elected president" — a resultative pattern, always
implying a formal vote or selection process took place.

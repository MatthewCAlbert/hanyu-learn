## Why this combination
多 "many" + 数 (*shù*) "number" (see [[数字]], [[人数]]) → the larger number,
the majority: 多数人赞成, 少数服从多数 "the minority yields to the majority."

## Notes
多数 (the majority) vs. [[少数]] (the minority) vs. [[大多数]] (the *great*
majority). 数 is *shù* here.

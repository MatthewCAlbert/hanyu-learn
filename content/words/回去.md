## Why this combination
回 "to return" + 去 "to go (away from the speaker)": returning to somewhere the
speaker is not. [[回来]] is the same return coming toward the speaker. As a
complement: 送回去 "send back," 放回去 "put back."

## Notes
The 回来 / 回去 choice is entirely about where the speaker stands, like
[[出来]] / [[出去]].

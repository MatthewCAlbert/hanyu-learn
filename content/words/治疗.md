## Why this combination
治 carries its "to bring under control, to cure" sense (as in 治病, distinct
from its more famous "to govern" sense in 治理), and 疗 is "to heal, to
treat medically" (as in 疗养, and the bound morpheme in 治疗's own mirror
word 医疗). A clean near-synonym pairing focused specifically on medical
cure, not political governance.

## Notes
Do not confuse this 治 (cure) with 治理 (to govern/manage) — same character,
genuinely different domain depending on the compound it appears in.

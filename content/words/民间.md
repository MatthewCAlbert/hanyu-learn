## Why this combination
民 "the people, the populace" (see also 人民, 农民) combined with 间
"among, between" (as in 中间, 时间): among the common people — folk,
grassroots, as opposed to official or governmental.

## Notes
民间 modifies things originating from ordinary people rather than the
state (民间故事 "folk tale," 民间组织 "non-governmental organisation") —
its implicit contrast is with 官方 "official," making 民间 the standard
term for "grassroots, unofficial."

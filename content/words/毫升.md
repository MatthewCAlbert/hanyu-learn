## Why this combination

毫 ("a tiny fraction, a thousandth part," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 毫米) plus 升 ("a liter," a volume unit, distinct from its
more literal "to rise" sense as in 升起, "to rise up") names a
thousandth of a liter: a milliliter.

Rated "semi" for 升's specialized "liter" unit sense here, distinct
from its literal "to rise" meaning.

## Notes

毫升 (milliliter) and 毫米 (covered elsewhere in this app, millimeter)
share the same 毫 "thousandth" prefix, applied to volume and length
respectively.

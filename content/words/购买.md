## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 购 ("to purchase," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 买 ("to buy") both independently mean "to buy." Pairing them
gives a more formal, written-register synonym of plain 买.

## Notes
购买 reads more formal/written (购买力, "purchasing power") — for
everyday casual buying, plain 买 is the natural spoken word.

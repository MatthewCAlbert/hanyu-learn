## Why this combination
仍 "still, yet" and 旧 (here "as before, unchanged") are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for something remaining unchanged despite
time or circumstance.

## Notes
仍旧 is a written/formal adverb: 仍旧如此 "still the same as before" —
close to 仍然 and 依旧, all near-synonyms differing mainly in register and
collocational preference.

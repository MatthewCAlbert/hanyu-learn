## Why this combination
区 "area, district" and 域 "territory, domain" (the same 域 as in 领域,
王国 originally, "a bounded territory") reinforce each other into the
formal, general word for a defined area.

## Notes
区域 favours formal and technical contexts (区域经济 "regional economy,"
时区 shares the 区 root for "time zone"), where everyday speech about a
neighbourhood uses 地方 or 地区.

## Why this combination
道 "the way, the course of things" + 理 "the underlying pattern, principle,
reason" (see [[经理]], [[理想]]) → how things rightly work, the sense behind
something. 讲道理 "be reasonable," 有道理 "that makes sense," 什么道理？.

## Notes
道理 = the reasoning / rightness of a matter; [[原因]] = the cause that produced
it; [[理由]] (not this level) = the grounds one gives.

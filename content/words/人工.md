## Why this combination
人 "person" combined with 工 "work, labour" (see also 工人, 工具): work
done by a person — human labour, and, as a modifier, "artificial" (made
by human hands rather than occurring naturally).

## Notes
人工 modifies things made or done by humans rather than occurring
naturally (人工智能 "artificial intelligence," 人工湖 "man-made lake")
and, as a noun, names labour cost or manpower (人工费 "labour cost") —
its opposite in the "artificial" sense is 天然 "natural."

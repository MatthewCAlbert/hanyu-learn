## Why this combination
感 "to feel" and 想 "to think" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for one's reflective response to something experienced.

## Notes
感想 anchors 谈谈感想 "to share one's thoughts," 读后感想 "reflections
after reading" — always personal, reflective impressions, distinct from
the more objective 意见 "opinion" or 看法 "view."

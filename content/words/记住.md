## Why this combination
记 "to note in the mind" + resultative 住, which means "hold firm, make stick"
(as in 站住 "halt," 抓住 "grip"). So "get it to stick" — the active effort of
memorising, as against [[记得]] "have in memory."

## Notes
记不住 "can't retain it," 记住了 "got it." The 住 complement quietly appears on
many verbs to mean "and it held."

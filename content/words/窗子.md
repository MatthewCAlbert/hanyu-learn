## Why this combination

窗 ("window," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 窗户) plus the
nominal suffix 子 names the everyday object in casual speech.

## Notes

窗子 and 窗户 (covered elsewhere in this app) are near-perfect
synonyms for "window" — regional and personal preference, rather than
any real distinction in meaning, decides which one a speaker reaches
for.

## Why this combination
女 "female, woman" combined with 子, here a generic suffix for "a
person" (as in 男子, 孩子 in an unrelated formation): a female person —
woman.

## Notes
女子 is a formal, written register for "woman," used in official and
sports contexts (女子组 "women's division," 女子足球队 "women's football
team") — 女人, the everyday word, is used in ordinary speech instead.

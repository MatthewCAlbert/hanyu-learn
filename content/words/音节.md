## Why this combination
音 "sound" (see [[声音]], [[音乐]]) + 节 "a segment, a joint, a unit" (see
[[节目]], [[节日]]) → a segment of sound — the unit into which speech breaks.
In Chinese, one 音节 ≈ one 字: 汉语大部分是单音节词根.

## Notes
音节 = a phonological unit; 字 = the written character that usually maps to it;
声调 "tone" is a property of the 音节.

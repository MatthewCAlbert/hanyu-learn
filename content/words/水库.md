## Why this combination
水 "water" plus 库 "storehouse, depot" gives exactly "a storehouse for
water" — the standard word for a man-made water-storage facility.

## Notes
水库 anchors 修建水库 "to build a reservoir," 水库大坝 "reservoir dam" —
always an engineered structure for storing water, distinct from a
natural 湖 "lake."

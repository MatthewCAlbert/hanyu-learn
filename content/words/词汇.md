## Why this combination

词 ("a word, a unit of language," as in 词语) plus 汇
("to converge, to collect," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
汇报, where simplification merged two distinct traditional characters,
匯 and 彙, into one shape) names words gathered together into a body:
vocabulary.

Rated "semi" for 汇's "gather/collect" sense here, less obvious once
divorced from its water-radical origin.

## Notes

词汇量 ("vocabulary size") and 词汇表 ("a glossary, lit. vocabulary
table") both build on this word — 词汇 names the entire collected stock
of words in a language, distinct from 词, a single word.

## Why this combination
门 "door, gate" + 口 "opening, mouth" — the mouth of the doorway, i.e. the
entrance and the spot right by it. 口 is the same "opening" morpheme in
[[路口]] (road junction), 出口 (exit), 窗口 (window / counter).

## Notes
在门口等 "wait at the door." 家门口 "right outside home," often figurative for
"on one's doorstep."

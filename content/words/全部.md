## Why this combination
全 "whole, complete" + 部, here "the set / body of parts" (see [[部分]]) → all
the parts together, the entirety: 全部完成, 我的全部. Quantifier and noun.

## Notes
全部 (the whole set) contrasts directly with [[部分]] (a part of it). 全 alone:
[[全国]], [[全家]], 安全.

## Why this combination
种, here "seed" (as in 种类 in an unrelated sense "kind, category," both
derived from the same root idea of "type/that which is planted")
combined with the light noun suffix 子 (as in 桌子, 儿子): the everyday
concrete noun for a plant seed.

## Notes
种子 names the literal seed of a plant (种子发芽 "the seed sprouts") and,
figuratively, the origin of something that will grow (希望的种子 "the
seed of hope") — 种, alone, more often functions as the verb "to plant"
(种花 "to plant flowers") or a classifier for "kind, type."

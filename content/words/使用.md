## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 使 "to cause, to employ, to send on an errand" + 用 "to use."
Doubled for the formal transitive "to use": 使用工具, 使用方法 "instructions
for use."

## Notes
使用 (formal, written) vs. 用 (everyday: 用筷子). 使 alone = "to make / cause"
(使人高兴) and "envoy" (大使).

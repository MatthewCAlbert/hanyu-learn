## Why this combination
日 "day" (as in 日期) combined with 常 "constant, usual, ordinary" (as in
常常 "often," 通常 "usually"): what happens as a constant part of every
day — everyday, routine.

## Notes
日常 modifies the ordinary, routine parts of life (日常生活 "daily
life," 日常用品 "everyday items") — 每天, "every day," is an adverbial
time expression, while 日常 is an adjective describing something as
routine or ordinary in nature.

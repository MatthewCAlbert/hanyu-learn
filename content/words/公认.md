## Why this combination
公 "public" plus 认 "to recognize, to acknowledge" gives exactly
"recognized by the public" — the standard word for a widely shared
judgment.

## Notes
公认 anchors 公认的事实 "a universally acknowledged fact," 公认为 "widely
regarded as" — always implies broad consensus, not one person's opinion.

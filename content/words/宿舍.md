## Why this combination
宿 "to lodge, to stay overnight" (see 宿's own etymology) and 舍
"dwelling" (see 舍's own etymology) combine directly into the standard
word for a communal residential building.

## Notes
宿舍 anchors student and worker housing (学生宿舍 "student dormitory," 宿舍
楼 "dormitory building") — always shared, institutional lodging rather
than a private home.

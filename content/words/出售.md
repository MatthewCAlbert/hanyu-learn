## Why this combination
出 ("out") plus 售 ("to sell," covered elsewhere in this app) names
putting goods out for sale.

## Notes
出售 is the formal/written register (公开出售, "offered for public sale")
— for everyday casual selling, plain 卖 is the natural spoken verb.

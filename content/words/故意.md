## Why this combination
故 here "on purpose, deliberately" (an old adverbial sense, as in 明知故犯) +
意 "intention, mind" → "with set intent." 故意的 "it was deliberate,"
不是故意的 "didn't mean to."

## Notes
故 "deliberate" is distinct from 故 "old / former" ([[故事]], 故乡) and 故
"reason / therefore" (无故, [[所以]]'s cousin 故而). Opposite 无意 / 不小心.

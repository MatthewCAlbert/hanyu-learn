## Why this combination
达 "to reach, to get through to" (as in 到达 "to arrive") and 到 "to
arrive at" are close synonyms, paired for the verb "to reach [a goal,
level or standard]."

## Notes
达到 takes an abstract target as its object — 达到目的 "to achieve one's
goal," 达到标准 "to meet the standard" — while 到达 (the same two
characters reversed) is used for physically arriving somewhere.

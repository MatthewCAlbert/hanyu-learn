## Why this combination
产 "to produce" and 业 "occupation, undertaking" (see 业's own etymology
— the serrated crossboard of a bell-rack, extended to "trade, industry")
are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a productive
economic sector.

## Notes
产业 anchors 产业结构 "industrial structure," 高新技术产业 "high-tech
industry" — the formal economic term, distinct from its older, now rarer
sense of "family property, estate."

## Why this combination
有 "to have" + 空儿 "spare time, a gap" (空 *kòng* "empty / free" with erhua;
the same 空 as [[空气]] but in its *kòng* reading) → to have time free:
你明天有空儿吗？ Verb-object: 有点儿空儿, 没空儿.

## Notes
空 is *kòng* here (free time), not *kōng* "empty (space)." Non-erhua 有空 is the
same. 有空 vs. [[没事儿]] both mean "free," but 没事儿 also means "never mind."

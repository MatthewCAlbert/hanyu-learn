## Why this combination
证 "to prove, evidence" and 实 "real, true" combine directly: to prove
something to be true.

## Notes
证实 is the formal register for confirmation, especially in news and
science (消息已被证实 "the news has been confirmed") — a stronger claim
than the softer 确认 "to confirm/verify [administratively]."

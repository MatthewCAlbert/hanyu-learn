## Why this combination
典 "canon, standard, a fixed model" and 礼 "ritual, ceremony" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard formal word for a ceremonial
occasion.

## Notes
典礼 anchors 毕业典礼 "graduation ceremony," 开幕典礼 "opening ceremony" —
always a formal, structured event marking a significant occasion.

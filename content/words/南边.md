## Why this combination
南 "south" + the locative suffix 边 — a compass member of the 边 series. Often
loosely "the South" of China (南边人 "a southerner"). 南面 / 南部 are the 面 /
部 synonyms.

## Notes
By convention Chinese lists directions 东南西北, and a map faces north, so 南边
is "toward the bottom" on a map — but the word is just "south."

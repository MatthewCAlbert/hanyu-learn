## Why this combination
里 "inside" + 头 as a toneless locative-noun suffix (the same 头 in 外头,
上头, 后头, 前头) → "the inside." A colloquial equivalent of [[里边]] /
里面: 屋子里头, 里头有人.

## Notes
The 头-suffixed locatives (里头, 外头, 上头…) are the most colloquial of the
three sets — 边 (里边) neutral, 面 (里面) slightly written, 头 spoken.

## Why this combination
传 "to pass on, to transmit" (see also 传统, 宣传) combined with 说 "to
speak, to say": what is said and passed on [from generation to
generation] — a legend, folk tale.

## Notes
传说 names a legend or piece of folklore passed down orally (民间传说
"folk legend") and, as a verb-like phrase, introduces unverified hearsay
("传说他很有钱" "it's said/rumoured that he's very rich") — the two uses
share the same "passed along by word of mouth, not confirmed" logic.

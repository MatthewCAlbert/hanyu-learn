## Why this combination
地 ("ground, place") plus 位 ("a position, a rank," covered in this
app's entry for 位置) names one's place within a social or professional
order — figuratively grounded, like a place on the ground, but referring
to standing rather than location.

Rated "semi" because the leap from "physical place" to "social standing"
is a real but non-obvious figurative extension.

## Notes
地位 names social or professional standing (社会地位, "social status";
提高地位, "to raise one's status") — for physical location, plain 位置 is
the word to reach for.

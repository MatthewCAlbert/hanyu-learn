## Why this combination
早 "early" + the bare time-noun suffix 上, the same 上 as in [[晚上]] and
路上 — not "up." The early part of the day, roughly dawn to mid-morning.

## Notes
早晨 is the near-synonym; 上午 is the longer stretch to noon. 早上好 / just 早！
as a greeting.

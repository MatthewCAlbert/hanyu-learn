## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 寻 ("to search," as in 寻求, 寻求帮助) and 找 ("to
look for," the everyday word covered elsewhere in this app, 找到, 找工作)
both independently mean "to search for." Pairing them produces a slightly
more formal/written synonym of plain 找.

## Notes
寻找 reads more literary than 找 alone — reach for it in writing (寻找
答案, "to seek an answer") where casual speech would just use 找.

## Why this combination
东 "east" + 方 "region, quarter" (see [[方向]], [[南方]], [[西方]]). The compass
sense (东方发白 "the sky lightens in the east") and, in the wider sense, "the
East" — Asia, opposed to 西方 "the West": 东方文化.

## Notes
The 方 compass set: 东方, [[南方]], [[西方]], [[北方]]. [[东边]] is the plain
"east side." 东 alone also = "host" (做东).

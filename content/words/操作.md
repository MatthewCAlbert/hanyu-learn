## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 操 ("to grasp and control, to operate," covered
elsewhere in this app) and 作 ("to do, to act," as in 工作, 作为) both
independently name performing an action. Pairing them gives the standard
word for operating a tool, machine, or system.

## Notes
操作 leans technical (操作系统, "operating system"; 操作简单, "simple/easy
to operate") — for a general everyday action, plain 做 is the natural
choice instead.

## Why this combination
[[南非]] + [[荷兰]] + 语: a transparent calque of the idea "the Dutch of South
Africa," i.e. Afrikaans. Not a phonetic of Afrikaans.

## Notes
荷兰语 is Dutch in Europe; 南非荷兰语 is Afrikaans. The extra 南非 is the whole
difference.

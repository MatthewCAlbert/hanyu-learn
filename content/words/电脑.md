## Why this combination
Completely transparent once you have both characters: an electric brain. A
20th-century coinage, built from native morphemes rather than borrowed
phonetically — contrast 沙发 *shāfā* (sofa), which is pure sound.

## Notes
Competes with 计算机 ("calculating machine"), which is the more formal and
mainland-technical term. 电脑 is the everyday word and dominates in Taiwan.

## Why this combination
Pronoun 他 plus the plural suffix 们. See [[我们]] for the suffix. 他们 is the
default plural for a group of people of any gender, or of mixed gender; the
all-female 她们 exists but the generic 他们 covers it.

## Notes
Speech makes no distinction — 他们 and 她们 are both *tāmen*. The split is only
on the page.

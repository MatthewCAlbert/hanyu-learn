## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 具 ("to have, to possess," as in 具有;
also "a tool/implement," as in 工具) and 备 ("to prepare, to be equipped
with," as in 准备) both independently name having something ready at
hand. Pairing them gives the formal word for possessing a condition or
qualification.

## Notes

具备 takes abstract qualifications or conditions as its object (具备条件,
"to meet the conditions"; 具备能力, "to possess the ability") — it is
more formal than plain 有 and doesn't take everyday concrete objects.

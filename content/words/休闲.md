## Why this combination
休 "to rest" and 闲 "leisure, free time" (see 闲's own etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard modern word for leisure
activity.

## Notes
休闲 anchors 休闲时间 "leisure time," 休闲服 "casual wear," 休闲活动 "leisure
activities" — a 20th-century term reflecting the modern concept of
dedicated leisure time.

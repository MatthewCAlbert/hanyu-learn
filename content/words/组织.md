## Why this combination
组 "to group, to assemble" is transparent; 织 "to weave" is the half that
needs unpacking — organizing people or ideas is figured as weaving
separate threads into one coherent fabric. That same weaving metaphor is
why 组织 also names biological tissue: cells woven together into one
structure.

## Notes
Three senses share one word: the verb "to organize" (组织活动 "to organize
an event"), the noun "an organization" (国际组织 "international
organization"), and the biological "tissue" (肌肉组织 "muscle tissue") —
all descending from the same "woven-together structure" image.

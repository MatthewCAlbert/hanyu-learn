## Why this combination
网 "net" (= the internet, see [[网上]]) + 站 "station, a post, a stop" (see
[[车站]]) → a "station on the net," a fixed point you visit — a website.
上网站, 官方网站.

## Notes
站 as "-site / -station" also in 电视台, 加油站, [[地铁站]].
网页 "web page" is one page of a 网站.

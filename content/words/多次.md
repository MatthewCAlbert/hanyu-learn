## Why this combination

多 ("many") plus 次 ("a time, an occurrence," the measure word for
repeated actions, as in 一次) names something happening many times.

## Notes

多次 sits before the verb as an adverbial (多次提到, "mentioned many
times") — unlike 好几次 ("quite a few times"), it doesn't give a rough
count, just "more than once, repeatedly."

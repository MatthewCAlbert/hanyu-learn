## Why this combination
航 ("to navigate a crossing," covered elsewhere in this app — originally
boats lashed together, later extended by analogy to travel through the
sky) plus 空 ("sky, air") names exactly what aviation is: navigating
through the air.

## Notes
航空公司 ("airline," lit. "aviation company") and 航空母舰 ("aircraft
carrier") both build directly on this character pair's plain "air
travel" core sense.

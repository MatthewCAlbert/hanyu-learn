## Why this combination
符 carries its "tally, a token split in two so the halves can be matched
to verify authenticity" sense (as in 符号, "a symbol/mark," a descendant
of the same matching-token idea), and 合 is "to join, to fit together"
(covered in this app's entry for 合同). Two halves of a tally fitting
together perfectly is exactly what "to match, to accord with" describes.

Rated "semi" for 符's specific "matching tally/token" sense, a real but
non-obvious historical image behind its more familiar "symbol" meaning.

## Notes
符合条件 ("to meet the conditions/requirements") and 符合实际 ("to accord
with reality") are the two most common everyday frames — 符合 always
checks one thing against a standard or expectation, never describes a
simple resemblance between two unrelated things.

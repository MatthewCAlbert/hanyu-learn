## Why this combination
直 "straight" + 接 "to connect, to join" → connected in a straight line, with
nothing in between. Adjective (直接原因) and adverb (直接告诉他 "tell him
directly").

## Notes
Opposite 间接 "indirect." 直 "straight" also in [[一直]], 直到.

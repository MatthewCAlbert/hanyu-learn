## Why this combination
控 (originally "to draw a bow taut," extended to "to control") and 制
"to regulate, to restrain" point at the same idea from two angles;
together they reinforce each other into the general, everyday verb "to
control."

## Notes
控制 covers machines (遥控 "remote control" shares 控), emotions (控制情绪
"to control one's emotions"), and populations/situations (控制人口 "to
control population growth") — a very general-purpose verb.

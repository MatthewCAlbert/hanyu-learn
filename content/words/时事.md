## Why this combination
时 (here "current, of the time") plus 事 "affairs, matters" gives exactly
"current affairs" — the standard word for ongoing news and events.

## Notes
时事 anchors 时事新闻 "current-affairs news," 关心时事 "to stay informed
on current events" — always about ongoing political/social happenings,
not personal or historical matters.

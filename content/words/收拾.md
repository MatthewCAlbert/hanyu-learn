## Why this combination
收 "to gather in, to receive" and 拾 "to pick up" (see 拾's own etymology
— 掇也, "to pick up") are near-synonyms combined into the everyday verb
for tidying and organizing.

## Notes
收拾 spans housework (收拾房间 "to tidy a room") and, colloquially, a
threat of punishment (我要收拾你! "I'm going to deal with you!" — putting
someone "in order" the hard way).

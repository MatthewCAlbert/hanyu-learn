## Why this combination
加 "to add" with the directional complement 上 "on, onto" gives "to add
on" directly — as a connective, it extends to "on top of that,
furthermore."

## Notes
加上 works as a literal verb (加上盐 "add salt") and a sentence-connecting
adverb (加上他病了，就没去 "and on top of that, he was sick, so he didn't
go") — the same "adding on" logic in two grammatical roles.

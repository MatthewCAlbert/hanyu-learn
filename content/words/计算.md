## Why this combination
计 "to count, to plan, to calculate" (as in 计划 "plan") and 算 "to
count, to calculate" (as in 打算 "to plan, to intend") are close
synonyms, paired into the standard verb for mathematical calculation.

## Notes
计算 names arithmetic and computation (计算结果 "calculation result,"
计算机 "computer," literally "calculating machine") — 数, the simple verb
"to count," is used for counting discrete items rather than working out
a calculation.

## Why this combination
桃 "peach" plus 树 "tree" gives exactly "a peach tree" — a
straightforward, fully compositional plant word.

## Notes
桃树 anchors 种桃树 "to plant a peach tree" — the source tree for both
the fruit (桃子) and the ornamental blossom (桃花).

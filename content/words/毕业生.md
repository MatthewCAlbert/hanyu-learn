## Why this combination

毕业 ("to graduate," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 生 ("a student,
a person," as in 学生) names a person who has graduated: a graduate.

## Notes

大学毕业生 ("a university graduate") and 应届毕业生 ("a graduating-this-
year student," i.e. a fresh graduate) both build on this word — 生 here
functions as the same productive "person of X" suffix seen in 医生,
学生.

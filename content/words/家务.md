## Why this combination

家 ("home, family") plus 务 ("affairs, business," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 商务) names the affairs of running a household:
chores.

## Notes

做家务 ("to do housework/chores") and 家务活 ("household work") both
build on this word — 家务 covers the general category of domestic
tasks (cleaning, cooking, laundry), not any single specific chore.

## Why this combination
竹 "bamboo" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "bamboo" — the suffix
adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of 桌子, 椅子.

## Notes
竹子 anchors 熊猫吃竹子 "pandas eat bamboo," 竹子做的 "made of bamboo" —
the everyday word; 竹 alone is common in compounds and classical/literary
contexts (竹林 "bamboo grove," 竹简 "bamboo slips").

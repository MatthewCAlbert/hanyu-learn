## Why this combination
调 "to adjust, to tune, to mix to the right proportion" (as in 空调 "air
conditioning," literally "adjusted air") combined with 整 "orderly,
whole, to put in order" (as in 整个 "the whole"): to tune something back
into good order — to adjust.

## Notes
调整 is used for adjustments to plans, prices, positions and schedules
(调整计划 "to adjust a plan," 价格调整 "price adjustment") — it works as
both verb and noun without changing form, and is more formal than
everyday 改 "to change."

## Why this combination
措 carries a "to arrange, to handle, to place appropriately" sense
(surviving mostly bound, as in 措辞, "choice of words/phrasing"), and 施
is "to carry out, to implement" (as in 实施). An arrangement put into
action is exactly a "measure" — a concrete step taken to address a
problem.

Rated "semi" because both halves are rarely met as free-standing words,
appearing almost entirely inside formal compounds like this one.

## Notes
措施 always names a concrete, actionable step (采取措施, "to take
measures") — it describes what will be *done*, not an abstract policy or
plan, which would use 政策 or 方案 instead.

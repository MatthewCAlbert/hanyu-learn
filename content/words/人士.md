## Why this combination
人 "person" is transparent; 士 (originally a scholar or gentleman of rank
in classical society) adds a note of standing or respectability that a
plain "person" wouldn't carry — hence semi rather than fully transparent.

## Notes
人士 always describes people worth naming with some respect: 各界人士
"people from all walks of life [formal]," 知名人士 "well-known figures" —
never used for an anonymous or unremarkable individual.

## Why this combination

手 ("hand") plus 工 ("work, craft," as in 工作) names work done by hand:
handicraft.

## Notes

手工制作 ("handmade, handcrafted") contrasts with 机器制造 ("machine-
made") — 手工 modifies goods to emphasize the human labor and skill
behind them, often implying higher value or care.

## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 止 "to stop" gives "not stopping" — either continuing
without end, or exceeding a given number (going past a limit without
stopping there).

## Notes
不止 covers ongoing action (哭笑不止 "unable to stop laughing/crying") and
exceeding a count (不止一次 "more than once") — distinguish from the
identical-sounding 不只 "not only," a different word entirely.

## Why this combination
们 is a bound plural suffix for people, not a word on its own. Attach it to a
personal pronoun and you pluralise: 我 → 我们, 你 → 你们, 他 → 他们. Fully
transparent once you know the suffix, which is why these three are usually the
first "words" a learner meets.

## Notes
们 only pluralises pronouns and nouns denoting people (老师们). It is never used
with a number — you say 三个老师, never 三个老师们.

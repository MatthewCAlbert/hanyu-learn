## Why this combination
诗 "poem" and 歌 "song" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for poetry, reflecting poetry's historical roots in sung verse.

## Notes
诗歌 anchors 诗歌朗诵 "poetry recitation," 古代诗歌 "classical poetry" —
the general umbrella term for poetry as a genre, where 诗 alone can name
an individual poem or a specific verse form.

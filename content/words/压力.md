## Why this combination
压 "to press down, to weigh on" plus 力 "force" gives, quite literally,
"pressing force" — a word borrowed directly for physical pressure
(气压力) and extended, just as readily in English, to the felt weight of
stress and obligation.

## Notes
压力 covers both senses without a separate word: 工作压力 "work
pressure/stress" and 血压 (a related but separate word, "blood pressure")
share the same root logic of a force bearing down.

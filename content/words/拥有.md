## Why this combination
拥 "to hold, to embrace" and 有 "to have" reinforce each other: something
held close is something possessed. A more formal, weightier synonym of
plain 有.

## Notes
拥有 is reserved for substantial possessions — wealth, rights, territory,
qualities (拥有权力 "to hold power," 拥有丰富的经验 "to possess extensive
experience") — not for small everyday objects, where 有 alone is natural.

## Why this combination
特 "special" and 定 "fixed, settled" combine directly: a fixed, specially
designated case rather than a general one.

## Notes
特定 always narrows a general category to one particular instance: 特定
条件下 "under specific conditions," 特定人群 "a particular demographic" —
a formal word for "this one, not others in general."

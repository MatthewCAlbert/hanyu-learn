## Why this combination
华, a classical, flourishing self-designation of the Chinese
civilisation (see also 中华民族) combined with 人 "person": a person of
Chinese ethnicity — often specifically one living outside China.

## Notes
华人 typically refers to people of Chinese ethnic descent living abroad,
regardless of citizenship (海外华人 "overseas Chinese") — 中国人, by
contrast, specifically means a citizen of China; the distinction matters
because many 华人 hold other nationalities.

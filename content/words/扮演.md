## Why this combination
扮 "to dress up as, to play a part" (see 扮's own etymology — 分 "a
portion/role" lending its sound and an echo of meaning) and 演 "to
perform" are near-synonyms combined into the standard verb for playing a
role.

## Notes
扮演 covers literal acting (扮演主角 "to play the lead role") and
figurative role-taking (扮演重要角色 "to play an important part [in a
situation]") — the same "taking on an assigned part" logic in both.

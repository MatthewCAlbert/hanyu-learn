## Why this combination

收 ("to gather in, to receive," as in 收入) plus 益 ("benefit, profit,"
as in 利益) names the benefit gathered in from an activity: profit,
gain.

## Notes

收益 leans financial/investment register (投资收益, "investment returns")
— 收入 is the more general everyday word for "income," while 收获
(covered elsewhere in this app) leans figurative, "what one gained from
an experience."

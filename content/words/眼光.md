## Why this combination
眼 "eye" is transparent; 光 "light" here is the same metaphorical usage
as in 目光 — the gaze pictured as light shining from the eyes, extended
figuratively to judgement and foresight.

## Notes
眼光 spans literal gaze (投来眼光 "to cast a glance") and figurative
judgement (眼光独到 "having a discerning eye," 有眼光 "having good taste/
judgement") — perception extended to discernment.

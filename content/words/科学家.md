## Why this combination
[[科学]] "science" + 家 as the "-ist / expert" suffix (see [[作家]], [[画家]],
音乐家). One whose field is science. Parse 科学 + 家.

## Notes
The 家 "-ist" suffix is highly productive: 艺术家, 企业家, 政治家. It implies
recognised standing, not just employment.

## Why this combination
天 "heaven, nature" (marking something innate rather than learned) and
才 "talent, ability" combine directly: talent bestowed by nature rather
than acquired through effort.

## Notes
天才 works as a noun for the person ("a genius") and, attributively, for
the trait itself (天才的想法 "a brilliant/inspired idea") — always
emphasizing natural gift over hard-won skill.

## Why this combination
歌 "song" (see also 歌手, 歌声) combined with 迷 "one captivated, a fan"
(see also 球迷): someone captivated by song — a music/singer fan.

## Notes
歌迷 names a fan devoted to a singer or music generally (他的歌迷很多
"he has many fans") — 迷 is the same productive suffix seen in 球迷
"sports fan," attaching to whatever the enthusiasm centres on.

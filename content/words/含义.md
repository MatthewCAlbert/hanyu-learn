## Why this combination

含 ("to contain," as in 含有) plus 义 ("meaning, significance," as in
意义) names the meaning contained within words or a symbol: connotation,
implication.

## Notes

深层含义 ("deeper meaning") and 象征含义 ("symbolic meaning") both build
on this word — 含义 leans toward the meaning implied or embedded within
something (a word, a gesture), distinct from 意思, the more general,
everyday word for "meaning."

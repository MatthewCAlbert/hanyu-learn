## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 流 ("to flow," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 潮流, "a trend") and 传 ("to pass on, to transmit," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 遗传, "heredity") both independently
name movement from one point to another over time. Pairing them gives
the standard word for a story or tradition spreading through a
community across generations.

## Notes

民间流传 ("circulating among the people/folk tradition") and 广为流传
("widely circulated") both build on this word — 流传 always implies
transmission across time and people, distinct from 传播, which more
often names a deliberate act of spreading information.

## Why this combination
垃 and 圾 carry no independent meaning anywhere else in the language —
they exist essentially only to write this one word. 垃圾 is generally
traced to a native southern (Wu-dialect) word for rubbish, written down
with two characters chosen for their sound rather than any component
meaning, the same way 沙发 (sofa) borrows sound for a foreign word, except
here the word itself is native Chinese rather than borrowed from abroad.

## Notes
Neither 垃 nor 圾 appears in any other common word — encountering either
character outside 垃圾 is a strong signal something else is going on (a
rare name or a typo), not a hidden second meaning to look for.

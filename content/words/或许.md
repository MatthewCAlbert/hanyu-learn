## Why this combination
或 carries its classical "perhaps, possibly" sense (distinct from its
everyday "or" sense in 或者), and 许 here carries its "perhaps, maybe"
sense (as in 也许) rather than its more common "to permit, to allow" sense
(允许). Two words that each independently hedge toward possibility are
paired into a more formal synonym of 也许/可能.

Rated "semi" because both halves lean on a less common sense of an
otherwise familiar character.

## Notes
或许 is more written/formal than 也许 or 可能 — reach for it in essays and
reports, and for the everyday spoken register use 也许 instead.

## Why this combination
如 "to be like" and 同 "the same" are near-synonyms combined into the
formal written-register word for comparison.

## Notes
如同 introduces a simile in formal/literary writing (如同亲人一般 "just
like family") — everyday speech uses 像 instead for the same comparative
function.

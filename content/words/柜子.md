## Why this combination
柜 "a cabinet, a chest" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a
cabinet" — the suffix adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of
桌子, 椅子.

## Notes
柜子 anchors 衣柜子/衣柜 "wardrobe," 书柜子/书柜 "bookcase" — often shortened
further to just the modifier + 柜 (e.g. 衣柜) in compound furniture names.

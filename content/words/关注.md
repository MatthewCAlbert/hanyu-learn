## Why this combination
关 here in its extended "to concern, to be related to" sense (as in 关心
"to care about") combined with 注 "to focus, to pour attention into" (as
in 注意 "to pay attention"): concern focused steadily on something — to
follow it closely.

## Notes
关注 implies sustained, often public or professional attention (关注社会
问题 "to follow social issues closely"), heavier than plain 注意 "to
notice, to pay attention," which can be momentary.

## Why this combination
老 functions here as the same familiar, affectionate prefix covered in
this app's entry for 老婆 (not literally "old"), and 公 carries a "male"
sense in this context (公 elsewhere means "public, fair," but also marks
the male of a pair, as in 公鸡, "rooster"). Paired with the 老-prefix
pattern, it colloquially narrows to "husband" — the direct mirror of 老婆
("wife").

Rated "semi" because, exactly as with 老婆, the sense only resolves once
the 老-prefix pattern is recognized — 公 alone does not suggest "husband."

## Notes
老公 is casual/spoken, mirroring 老婆 exactly — the neutral, more formal
register uses 丈夫 instead.

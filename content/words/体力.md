## Why this combination
体 "body" plus 力 "strength, power" gives exactly "the strength of the
body" — parallel to 智力 "intellectual power" and 财力 "financial power."

## Notes
体力 anchors 体力劳动 "physical labor" (opposite of 脑力劳动 "mental
labor"), 体力活 "physical work," 消耗体力 "to expend physical energy" —
always the body's capacity for exertion.

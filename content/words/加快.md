## Why this combination
加 "to add" (see also 加强, 增加) combined with 快 "fast, quick": to add
speed — to accelerate, to speed up.

## Notes
加快 takes a process or pace as its object (加快速度 "to speed up,"
加快发展 "to accelerate development") — 加强, its close relative built on
the same 加, is used instead for strengthening a quality rather than
speeding up a pace.

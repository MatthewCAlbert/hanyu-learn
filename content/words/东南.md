## Why this combination
东 "east" + 南 "south" → the intercardinal southeast, in the fixed Chinese
order 东/西 then 南/北 (see [[东北]], [[西北]], [[西南]]) — the reverse of
English "southeast." As a region, 东南 is the coastal southeast; 东南亚 is
"Southeast Asia."

## Notes
The four intercardinals: [[东北]], [[西北]], 东南, [[西南]].

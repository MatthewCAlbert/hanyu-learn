## Why this combination
重 (*chóng*) "again, to repeat" + 新 "new, afresh" → "again, from a fresh
start." An adverb: 重新开始 "start over," 重新考虑 "reconsider."

## Notes
重 is *chóng* here (again), not *zhòng* "heavy" (as in [[重要]], [[重点]]).
重新 implies doing it over properly; 又 / 再 are plain "again."

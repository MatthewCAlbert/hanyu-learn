## Why this combination

老 (here a bleached familiar/affectionate prefix, not its literal "old,"
the same role it plays in 老实, covered elsewhere in this app) plus 家
("home, family") names one's original family home: hometown.

Rated "semi" for 老's non-literal prefix role, which contributes
familiarity rather than the meaning "old."

## Notes

回老家 ("to go back to one's hometown") is the standard phrase, especially
around holidays — 老家 specifically names one's ancestral or childhood
home, distinct from 家乡 (a close synonym) and from 家, one's current
residence.

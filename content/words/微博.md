## Why this combination
微 "micro, tiny" plus 博 (short for 博客 "blog," itself a phonetic loan —
see its own entry) gives exactly "a micro-blog" — a direct calque of
English "microblog," describing short-form posts as a miniature version
of a full blog.

## Notes
微博 anchors 发微博 "to post on Weibo," 微博热搜 "Weibo trending topics" —
also, capitalized as a proper noun, the name of China's dominant
Twitter-like social platform, Sina Weibo.

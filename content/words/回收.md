## Why this combination
回 "to return" and 收 "to gather in, to collect" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for taking something back to reuse it.

## Notes
回收 anchors 垃圾回收 "waste recycling," 回收站 "recycling bin" (also,
in computing, the "Recycle Bin" — a direct interface metaphor) — always
about reclaiming a resource for further use.

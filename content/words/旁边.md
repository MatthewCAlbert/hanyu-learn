## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 旁 "side, beside" + 边 "edge, side." The 边 is the same suffix that
builds 上边, 左边, [[里边]]; 旁边 is the one meaning "adjacent," without a
compass or up/down axis.

## Notes
学校旁边 "next to the school." Distinct from 中间 "in the middle" and 对面
"opposite."

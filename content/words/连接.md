## Why this combination
连 "to link, to join" and 接 "to connect, to receive" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard general-purpose word for connection.

## Notes
连接 spans the physical (电线连接 "wire connection"), digital (网络连接
"network connection"), and abstract (连接过去与未来 "connecting past and
future") — a general-purpose word for joining two things.

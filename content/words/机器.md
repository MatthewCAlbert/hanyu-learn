## Why this combination
机 "a mechanism, a device, a pivot" (as in 手机 "mobile phone," 飞机
"airplane") combined with 器 "an implement, a vessel, a tool" (see also
武器, 容器): a mechanical implement — a machine.

## Notes
机器 is the general word for a mechanical device or machine (机器人
"robot," 洗衣机 in a related compound "washing machine") — 机 alone
attaches productively as a suffix naming specific machines (电视机
"television set"), while 机器 stands for "machine" as a general category.

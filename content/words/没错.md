## Why this combination

没 ("to not have") plus 错 ("a mistake," as in 错误) names the absence of
any mistake: correct, right.

## Notes

没错 is a common standalone reply confirming something is correct
("Right!"), and also modifies a clause (没错，他是对的, "That's right, he's
correct") — a casual, spoken-register cousin of the more formal 正确.

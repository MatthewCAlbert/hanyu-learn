## Why this combination
高 "high" plus 原 "a plain, an open field" gives exactly "a high plain" —
the standard geographic term for an elevated flatland.

## Notes
高原 anchors 青藏高原 "the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau," 高原反应 "altitude
sickness" — contrasts with 平原 "(low) plain" by elevation, both sharing
the flat-terrain sense of 原.

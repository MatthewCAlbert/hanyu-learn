## Why this combination
工 "work, labour" (see also 工具, 工资) combined with 厂 "a shed, an open
building, a workshop": a building where labour is carried out — a
factory.

## Notes
工厂 names an industrial manufacturing site (工厂工人 "factory worker")
— 公司, its near-neighbour, names the business entity/company rather
than the physical production site itself; a company can own many
factories.

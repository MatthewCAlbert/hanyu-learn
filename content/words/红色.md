## Why this combination
红 "red" + 色 "colour" — the bound morpheme 红 plus the "-colour" suffix 色
(see [[颜色]]), the standard shape of a colour word: 红色, 蓝色, 黑色, 白色.

## Notes
红 alone in 口红 "lipstick," 红茶 "black tea (lit. red tea)." The political
sense — 红色政权, 又红又专 — comes from the red flag.

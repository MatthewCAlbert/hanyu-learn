## Why this combination
饼 "a flat cake" and 干 "dry" combine into exactly "a dry flatcake" — the
standard word for baked, dry snack cakes.

## Notes
饼干 covers the whole category of crisp baked snacks: 苏打饼干 "soda
crackers," 巧克力饼干 "chocolate cookies" — distinguished from soft
pastries (糕点) by its dry, crisp texture.

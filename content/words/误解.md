## Why this combination
误 (here an adverb, "mistakenly, erroneously") modifying 解 "to
understand, to interpret" gives exactly "to understand incorrectly."

## Notes
误解 works as verb ("你误解我了" "you've misunderstood me") and noun
("消除误解" "to clear up a misunderstanding") — a neutral word for
mistaken understanding, without necessarily implying blame.

## Why this combination
和 "harmonious, in accord" (as in 和好 "to reconcile") combined with 平
"level, even, calm" (as in 平安 "safe and sound"): harmony maintained
level and calm — peace, the absence of conflict.

## Notes
和平 names peace between nations or groups (世界和平 "world peace,"
和平解决 "to resolve peacefully") — 安静, its near-neighbour "quiet,
peaceful [of a place or mood]," describes a calm atmosphere rather than
the absence of war or conflict.

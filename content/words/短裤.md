## Why this combination
短 "short" (see also 短期) combined with 裤 "trousers" (see also 裤子):
short trousers — shorts.

## Notes
短裤 names shorts as a garment (穿短裤 "to wear shorts") — it follows
the same transparent "adjective + garment" pattern as 短袖 "short
sleeves," an easy formation to extend to other lengths and garments.

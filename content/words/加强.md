## Why this combination
加 "to add" (see also 更加, 增加) combined with 强 "strong" (see also
强大, 强烈): to add strength — to reinforce, to strengthen.

## Notes
加强 takes an abstract quality or effort as its object (加强管理 "to
strengthen management," 加强合作 "to enhance cooperation") — 增加, its
neighbour built on the same 加, is used instead for increasing a
countable quantity rather than strengthening a quality.

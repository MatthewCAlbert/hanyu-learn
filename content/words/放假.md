## Why this combination
假 (*jià*) "leave, vacation" as the object of 放 "to release, to let go" — an
institution "lets its people off." Splits: 放三天假 "have three days off,"
放暑假 "break up for the summer."

## Notes
假 is *jià* here (leave), not *jiǎ* "false." 请假 (see the corpus entry) is the
individual "ask for leave"; 放假 is the institution granting it.

## Why this combination
一 "one" + 边 "side, edge." Concretely "one side" (站在一边 stand to one side).
Doubled, 一边…一边… frames two simultaneous actions — "on one side… on the
other side…" of the same stretch of time: 一边走一边说 "talking as they walked."

## Notes
边 as "side" also builds the direction words 旁边, 左边, 上边. 一边儿 with erhua
= "out of the way!"

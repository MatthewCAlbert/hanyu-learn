## Why this combination
当, here in its "this very, precisely this" sense (as in 当时 "at that
very time") combined with 地 "land, place": precisely the place [in
question] — local, of the place itself.

## Notes
当地 modifies a noun to mark it as belonging to the place under
discussion (当地人 "a local person," 当地政府 "the local government") —
本地, a close synonym, leans slightly more towards "one's home area"
from the speaker's own perspective.

## Why this combination
竞 and 争 are near-synonyms, both meaning "to contend, to vie" — paired
together for the standard modern word "competition."

## Notes
竞争 covers markets (市场竞争 "market competition"), sport, and everyday
rivalry (竞争对手 "competitor, rival") — a neutral, formal register word
for contest of any kind.

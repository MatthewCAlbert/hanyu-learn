## Why this combination
乐, here read yuè "music" (as in 音乐 "music") combined with 队 "a team,
a group organised in line" (see also 队员, 球队): a group organised
around music — a band, an orchestra.

## Notes
乐队 names any organised group of musicians, from a rock band to a
symphony orchestra (摇滚乐队 "rock band," 交响乐队 "symphony orchestra")
— note the character 乐 is read yuè here, not lè as in 快乐 "happy."

## Why this combination
杯 "cup" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]]). Covers any drinking
vessel; the material or use is specified separately — 玻璃杯 glass, 茶杯 teacup,
纸杯 paper cup.

## Notes
Bare 杯 is also the measure word: 一杯水 "a cup of water," 三杯咖啡. And "cup"
as a trophy: 世界杯 the World Cup.

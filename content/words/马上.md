## Why this combination
Literally "on horseback." From the idea that a message or a person on a horse
arrives fast, 马上 lexicalised into the plain adverb "right away." The horse is
long gone from the meaning — you say 马上到 of a train.

## Notes
Still literal in a few phrases: 马上得天下 "won the empire on horseback."
Elsewhere purely "immediately."

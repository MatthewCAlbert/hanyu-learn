## Why this combination
表 "to express, to make visible on the surface" and 现 "to appear, to
manifest" reinforce each other directly: bringing something to the
surface so it becomes visible.

## Notes
表现 doubles as a noun for "how someone conducts themselves, their
observed behaviour" (他的表现很好 "his performance/conduct is very
good") — the visible surface of a person's inner qualities.

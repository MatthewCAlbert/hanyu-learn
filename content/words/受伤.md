## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 受 "to receive, to undergo" (see also 感受) takes
伤 "injury, a wound" as its object — literally "to receive an injury" —
to be hurt or wounded.

## Notes
受伤 can split for aspect and degree (受了伤 "got injured," 受了重伤
"was severely injured") like other verb-object compounds; the resulting
noun 伤 alone names the wound itself (他身上有伤 "he has an injury on
him").

## Why this combination
对 "facing, opposite" combined with 方 "side, direction, party [to an
interaction]" (as in 双方 "both sides"): the side facing one in an
exchange, negotiation or contest — the other party.

## Notes
对方 is impersonal and neutral, standing in for whoever is on the other
side of an interaction without naming them (听对方说 "listen to what the
other side says," 对方的电话 "the other person's phone number") — it does
not carry the adversarial charge that "opponent" can in English.

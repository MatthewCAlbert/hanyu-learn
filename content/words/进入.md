## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 进 "to advance, to go forward" + 入 "to enter." Both physical
(进入房间) and figurative (进入历史新阶段 "enter a new phase").

## Notes
More formal than the colloquial 进去 / 进来 (see [[进去]]). 入 alone in 入口,
加入, 收入.

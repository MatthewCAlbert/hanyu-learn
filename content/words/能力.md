## Why this combination
能 "to be able to" and 力 "power, strength" combine directly: the power
one is able to bring to bear. A clean, transparent pairing.

## Notes
能力 names an inherent or developed capacity ("the ability to swim");
contrast 能 alone, which stays a modal verb ("can [do something right
now]") rather than a standalone noun for the capacity itself.

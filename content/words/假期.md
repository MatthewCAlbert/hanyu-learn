## Why this combination
假 (*jià*) "leave, holiday" (see [[放假]], [[请假]]) + 期 "a period, a set span
of time" (see [[星期]], [[日期]], [[学期]]) → a stretch of time off:
寒假 / 暑假 "winter / summer vacation," 假期作业 "holiday homework."

## Notes
假 is *jià* here (holiday), not *jiǎ* "false." 假期 = the vacation span;
[[放假]] = to break up for it; 休假 (see [[休假]]) = to take leave.

## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + 边 "side" → the side where the speaker is. Pairs with
[[那边]] "over there" and 哪边 "which way." 边 marks a region relative to the
speaker, not a compass bearing.

## Notes
Used to usher someone along: 这边请 "this way, please." Northern erhua 这边儿.

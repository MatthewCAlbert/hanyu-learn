## Why this combination
葡萄 "grape" (see its own entry — a sound-borrowed word, not
decomposable) modifies 酒 "wine, alcohol," giving exactly "wine made from
grapes" — the standard word distinguishing grape wine from other alcohols
like 米酒 "rice wine" or 啤酒 "beer."

## Notes
葡萄酒 anchors 红葡萄酒 "red wine," 白葡萄酒 "white wine" — the full,
formal term; casual speech often just says 红酒/白酒 by context (though
白酒 alone usually means Chinese liquor, not white wine — context matters).

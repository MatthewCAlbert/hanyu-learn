## Why this combination
从 "from" and 中 "middle, within" combine directly: originating from
within a given situation or process.

## Notes
从中 always precedes a verb describing benefit or extraction: 从中获利
"to profit from it," 从中调解 "to mediate between the two sides [from
within the situation]."

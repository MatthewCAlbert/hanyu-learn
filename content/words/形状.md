## Why this combination
形 "form, shape" (see also 形式, 形象) combined with 状 "appearance,
condition" (see also 状态, 状况): the shape something appears in — its
physical form.

## Notes
形状 names the physical outline or shape of an object (什么形状 "what
shape," 圆形形状 "a round shape") — 形式, its close relative, is used
for abstract "form" (as opposed to content), while 形状 is reserved for
concrete, physical shape.

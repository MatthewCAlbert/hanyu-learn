## Why this combination
太 "great, extreme" and 空 "empty, sky" combine directly: the vast empty
expanse beyond the ordinary sky.

## Notes
太空 anchors 太空人/宇航员 "astronaut," 太空船 "spacecraft," 太空探索 "space
exploration" — the everyday word for outer space, alongside more
technical 宇宙 "the cosmos, universe."

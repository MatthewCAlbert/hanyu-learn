## Why this combination
逃 "to flee" and 走 (here "to go, to leave") are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for escaping on foot.

## Notes
逃走 anchors 趁机逃走 "to seize the chance to flee," 犯人逃走了 "the
prisoner escaped" — close to 逃跑, both common; 逃走 slightly emphasizes
successfully getting away.

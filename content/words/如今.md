## Why this combination
如 here carries a classical "arriving at, reaching [this point]" sense
(distinct from its everyday "like, as" sense in 如果, 比如), combined with
今 ("now, the present"). "Having arrived at now" is exactly "nowadays."

Rated "semi" for 如's less common "arriving at" sense here.

## Notes
如今 typically opens a sentence, contrasting the present with a
previously described past (以前...，如今..., "before..., but now...") — a
narrative pivot word, more literary than plain 现在.

## Why this combination
之 (classical possessive/linking particle) plus 下 "under, below" gives a
formal written equivalent of plain 下 "under" — completing the small set
之中/之外/之下 of classical-flavoured locatives.

## Notes
之下 trails a noun phrase (压力之下 "under pressure," 相比之下 "by
comparison") and, less commonly, marks a quantity as "less than" a given
figure in formal statistics writing.

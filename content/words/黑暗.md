## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 黑 ("black") and 暗 ("dark," covered elsewhere
in this app) both independently name the absence of light. Pairing them
gives the standard word for darkness, literal or figurative.

## Notes
黑暗 extends figuratively to corrupt or oppressive conditions (黑暗
时代, "a dark age") — the same "absence of light" metaphor English
"darkness" uses for moral or social bleakness.

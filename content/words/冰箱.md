## Why this combination

冰 ("ice") plus 箱 ("a box, a case," as in 箱子) names a box that keeps
things ice-cold: a refrigerator — the modern name preserving the
appliance's older function as an actual ice box.

## Notes

冰箱 covers both fridge and freezer loosely; 冰柜 ("ice cabinet") is the
more specific word for a chest freezer.

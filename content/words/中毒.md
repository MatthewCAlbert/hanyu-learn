## Why this combination
中 (here "to be struck by, to be hit," as in 中弹 "to be shot") taking the
object 毒 "poison" (see 毒's own etymology) gives exactly "to be struck by
poison" — splittable, as in 中了毒 "has been poisoned."

## Notes
中毒 covers literal poisoning (食物中毒 "food poisoning") and, in modern
slang, obsessive fondness (对游戏中毒 "addicted to a game," playing on
"poisoned" as "hooked").

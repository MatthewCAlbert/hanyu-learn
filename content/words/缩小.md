## Why this combination

缩 ("to shrink, to contract," as in 收缩, "to contract") plus 小
("small") names becoming smaller: to shrink, to reduce.

## Notes

缩小差距 ("to narrow the gap") and 缩小范围 ("to narrow down the scope")
are common figurative uses — 扩大 ("to expand," covered elsewhere in
this app) is its direct antonym, built the same way with 大 "big"
instead of 小.

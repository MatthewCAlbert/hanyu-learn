## Why this combination
充 "to fill, to be sufficient" (as in 充分 "ample, fully") and 满 "full,
filled to capacity" are close synonyms, paired into a verb for being
completely filled with something.

## Notes
充满 usually takes an abstract quality as its object (充满信心 "brimming
with confidence," 充满希望 "full of hope") more often than a physical
substance, where plain 满 alone ("杯子满了" "the cup is full") is more
natural.

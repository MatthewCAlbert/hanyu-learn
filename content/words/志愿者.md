## Why this combination
志愿 "aspiration, to be willing" (志 "will, aspiration," 愿 "to wish, to
be willing," see also 愿望) combined with 者 "one who [performs the
action]" (see also 作者, 读者): one who acts on their own free will —
a volunteer.

## Notes
志愿者 names someone who offers service freely, without pay (当志愿者
"to work as a volunteer," 志愿服务 "volunteer service") — 志愿, alone,
also names a preference or choice (as in 高考志愿 "college application
preferences"), a separate but related sense built on the same "will,
wish" root.

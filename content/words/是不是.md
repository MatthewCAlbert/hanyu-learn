## Why this combination
The A-不-A question pattern applied to 是: offering both poles, "is / is not,"
so the hearer picks one. It works on any verb or adjective (去不去, 好不好); with
是 it becomes a general tag: 你是学生，是不是？

## Notes
Equivalent to ending with 吗, but 是不是 can also float to the front (是不是你拿
了？) or middle for emphasis.

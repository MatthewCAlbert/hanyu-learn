## Why this combination
正 "exactly, precisely" + 是 "to be" → emphatic identity: "this is exactly
(it)." 正是他 "it's precisely him," 正是时候 "just the right moment."

## Notes
正 "exactly" also in [[正好]], [[正确]], [[真正]]. 正是…才… "it is precisely
because… that…".

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 新 ("new") and 鲜 ("fresh," covered elsewhere in
this app) both independently name freshness or novelty. Pairing them
gives the standard everyday word for fresh food as well as, figuratively,
a fresh/novel experience.

## Notes
新鲜 covers both literal freshness (新鲜水果, "fresh fruit") and
figurative novelty (新鲜事, "something novel/unusual") — the same "not
yet stale" idea applied to food and to experience.

## Why this combination
脱 "to shed, to slip off" and 离 "to leave, to separate" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for breaking free from something.

## Notes
脱离 covers danger (脱离危险 "out of danger"), relationships (脱离关系
"to sever ties"), and abstraction (脱离实际 "divorced from reality") —
always a deliberate or decisive separation, not a gradual drift.

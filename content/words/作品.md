## Why this combination
作 "to make, to create" (as in 作家 "writer") combined with 品 "an item,
a product, a class of thing" (as in 商品 "merchandise"): a made thing —
specifically, a creative work of art or writing.

## Notes
作品 is reserved for creative output — literature, art, music, film
(文学作品 "literary works," 艺术作品 "artwork") — while 产品, built on the
same 品, names manufactured goods instead.

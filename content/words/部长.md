## Why this combination
部 "a section, a division, a ministry" (see also 部门, 西部) combined
with 长, here "head, chief" (as in 校长 "school principal," 班长 "class
monitor," literally "the eldest/senior of the group"): the head of a
department — a minister.

## Notes
部长 names the head of a government ministry (外交部长 "Minister of
Foreign Affairs") — 部门, its close relative, names the department
itself, not the person leading it.

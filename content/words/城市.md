## Why this combination
城 (a walled settlement) and 市 (a marketplace) name the two defining
features of an ancient Chinese city: defensive walls, and the commercial
market that grew up within or beside them. Together, the settlement in
full: a city.

## Notes
市 alone survives as the administrative word for "city" in place names
(北京市 "Beijing Municipality") and as a general "market" (股市 "stock
market") — 城市 is the everyday word for the urban place itself.

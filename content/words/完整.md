## Why this combination
完 "complete, finished, without lack" (see also 完美) combined with 整
"orderly, whole" (see also 整理, 整个): complete and whole — intact,
with nothing missing or broken.

## Notes
完整 stresses that nothing has been lost, damaged or left out (保持
完整 "to keep intact," 完整的故事 "the whole/complete story") — 完全
"completely, entirely," is an adverb of degree rather than this
adjective describing a thing's wholeness.

## Why this combination
欢 "joyful, delighted" (as in 喜欢 "to like," literally "to delight
in") and 乐 "happy, joyful" (as in 快乐 "happy") are close synonyms,
paired into a fuller word for shared, festive joy.

## Notes
欢乐 describes a lively, collective happiness, often at a gathering or
celebration (欢乐的气氛 "a joyous atmosphere," 欢乐的时光 "joyful times")
— 快乐, its close relative, is the more everyday word for personal
happiness, used in greetings like 生日快乐 "happy birthday."

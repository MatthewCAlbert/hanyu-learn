## Why this combination
升 "to rise" plus the resultative complement 高 "high" gives exactly "to
rise (becoming) higher."

## Notes
升高 is used for measurable increases: 气温升高 "the temperature is
rising," 水位升高 "the water level is rising" — always a gradual increase
in level or degree, not a one-time jump.

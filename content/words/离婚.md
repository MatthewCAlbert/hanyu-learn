## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 离 "to leave, to separate from" (as in 离开 "to
leave") takes 婚 "marriage" as its object — literally "to leave the
marriage" — to divorce.

## Notes
离婚 is intransitive like 结婚, its verb-object opposite: one says 跟
他离婚 "to divorce him" (marking the partner with 跟/和), and the compound
splits for aspect like other verb-object words (离过婚 "to have been
divorced before").

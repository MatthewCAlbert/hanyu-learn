## Why this combination
胜 "victory, to win" and 负 "defeat, to lose" are direct opposites
combined into a single noun naming the outcome of a contest, whichever
way it goes — the same antonym-pair pattern as 好坏 "quality (good or
bad)" and 大小 "size."

## Notes
胜负 anchors 决定胜负 "to decide the outcome," 不分胜负 "a tie, no clear
winner" — always about the result of a competition, not the emotional
experience of winning or losing.

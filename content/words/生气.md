## Why this combination
生 "to produce" + 气 "qi, breath, temper." In traditional physiology anger is
qi surging up, so "to generate qi" = to become angry. Verb-object: 生他的气
"be angry at him," 生了半天气 "sulked for ages."

## Notes
A separate noun 生气 "life force, vitality" (生气勃勃) keeps 气 in its "vital
energy" sense — same characters, unrelated meaning.

## Why this combination
长 "long" combined with 城, here "a wall, a fortified boundary" (its
older sense, before 城 narrowed mainly to "city," see also 城市): the
long wall — the Great Wall of China.

## Notes
长城 is the fixed proper name for the Great Wall (万里长城 "the
ten-thousand-li Great Wall," a traditional epithet for its length) —
城's dual sense of "wall" and "city" reflects that a Chinese city was
historically defined by its surrounding wall.

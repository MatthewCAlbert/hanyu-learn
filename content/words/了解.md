## Why this combination
了 here is its "clear, finished" sense (as in 明了, 了结) rather than the
grammatical aspect particle most learners meet first, and 解 is "to untie,
to resolve" (as in 解开, 解释): together, "to fully untangle" something,
which is exactly what understanding a situation or a person feels like.

The transparency is "semi" specifically because of that 了: a learner who
only knows 了 as the sentence-final particle has to relearn it here as a
free content morpheme meaning "clear/settled," which is not obvious from
its far more frequent grammatical use.

## Notes
了解 covers both "to understand" (in the sense of grasping how something
works) and "to find out, to get information about" (了解情况) — English
splits these into two different verbs, but 了解 does not distinguish them.

## Why this combination
主 "main, primary" (see also 主任, 主动) combined with 意 "idea,
intention" (see also 意义, 同意): the primary idea [settled on] — a
plan, a decision.

## Notes
主意 names a specific plan or course of action decided on (出主意 "to
come up with an idea," 拿定主意 "to make up one's mind") — 想法, its
neighbour, is a more general "thought, idea" and does not carry 主意's
implication of a chosen course of action.

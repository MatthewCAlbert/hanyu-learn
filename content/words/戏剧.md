## Why this combination
戏 "play, performance" and 剧 "drama" (see 剧's role in 悲剧) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard umbrella term for theatrical
drama.

## Notes
戏剧 covers the art form broadly (戏剧史 "theatre history," 戏剧性 "dramatic,
theatrical [of a situation]") — 剧 alone is the more common suffix for a
specific genre or piece (悲剧 "tragedy," 喜剧 "comedy," 话剧 "spoken drama").

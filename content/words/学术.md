## Why this combination

学 ("to study, learning," as in 学习) plus 术 ("technique, art, a
discipline," as in 技术) names the disciplined pursuit of learning:
scholarship, academia.

## Notes

学术论文 ("academic paper") and 学术界 ("the academic world") both build
on this word — 学术 names formal, rigorous scholarship specifically,
distinct from 知识, the broader everyday word for "knowledge."

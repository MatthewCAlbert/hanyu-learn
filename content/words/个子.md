## Why this combination
个 here carries its old sense "a person's body / frame" (as in 个头儿, 单个儿)
+ the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]], [[瓶子]]) → a person's physical
size, chiefly their height: 个子高 "tall," 小个子 "a short person."

## Notes
个子 = a person's height / build; 身高 = height as a measured figure
(身高一米八). 个 is otherwise the general measure word.

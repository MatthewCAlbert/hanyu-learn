## Why this combination
坚 "firm, hard" and 定 "settled, fixed" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for steadfastness.

## Notes
坚定 describes unwavering resolve: 坚定的信念 "firm convictions," 立场坚定
"steadfast in one's position" — always about resistance to being swayed.

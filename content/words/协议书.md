## Why this combination
协议 "agreement" (协 "joint, cooperative" + 议 "discussion") plus 书
"document" gives exactly "a document of agreement" — the formal written
record of a negotiated agreement.

## Notes
协议书 anchors 离婚协议书 "divorce agreement document," 合作协议书
"cooperation agreement document" — the physical/formal document, as
opposed to 协议 alone, which can refer to the agreement itself in the
abstract.

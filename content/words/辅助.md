## Why this combination
辅 "to assist" and 助 "to help" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for a supporting, secondary role.

## Notes
辅助 works as verb ("辅助治疗" "adjunct/supportive treatment") and
adjective ("辅助工具" "auxiliary tool") — always names a secondary,
supporting function, never the primary/main one.

## Why this combination
定 "fixed, settled, decided" + 一 "one, single" → "settled as one thing," hence
"certain." Two branches: the adverb "surely" (一定去) and the adjective "a
certain / a fixed" (一定的条件 "certain conditions," 一定之规 "a set rule").

## Notes
不一定 "not necessarily" — a hedge, not a flat negative. 说不定 "who knows /
maybe."

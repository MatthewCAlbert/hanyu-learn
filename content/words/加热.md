## Why this combination
加 "to add" acting on the object 热 "heat" gives exactly "to add heat" —
splittable, as in 加了一下热.

## Notes
加热 is a technical/practical word for raising temperature: 加热食物 "to
heat up food," 加热到100度 "heat to 100 degrees" — more precise than
everyday 热一下 "to warm (something) up."

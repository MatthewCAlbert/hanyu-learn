## Why this combination

图 ("a picture, a chart," as in 地图, "a map") plus 案 ("a plan, a
proposal," as in 方案) names a planned pictorial design: a decorative
pattern.

Rated "semi" for 案's extended "planned design" sense here, one step
from its more common "proposal/case" meaning.

## Notes

图案 names a repeating decorative motif (花纹图案, "a floral pattern") —
distinct from 图片/图画, which name a standalone picture or illustration
rather than a repeating design element.

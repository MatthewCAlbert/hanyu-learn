## Why this combination
最 "most" + 好 "good" = best. From "the best option would be…" it also works as
soft advice: 你最好早点走 "you'd better leave early."

## Notes
最 is the regular superlative prefix (最大, 最快). The advice sense is
context, not a separate word.

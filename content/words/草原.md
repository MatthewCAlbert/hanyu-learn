## Why this combination
草 "grass" plus 原 "a plain, an open field" gives exactly "a grassy
plain" — the standard word for a natural grassland landscape.

## Notes
草原 anchors 内蒙古草原 "the Inner Mongolian grassland," 草原文化 "steppe
culture" — the vast, open landscape, distinct from 草地, which can be any
patch of grass, including a small lawn.

## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" + 站 "a stopping point, a post." 站 as a verb is "to stand"; as a
noun it is a place where transport halts — 火车站, 地铁站, 加油站. So "where
vehicles stop."

## Notes
公交车站 / 公共汽车站 "bus stop," 长途汽车站 "coach station." 下一站 "next
stop."

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 动 "to move" + 作 "to act, to do." Names a physical movement of
the body — a gesture, a dance step, a move in sport — and, loosely, a party's
"move" in negotiation.

## Notes
Narrower than 行动 "action, operation" (a campaign or undertaking). 动作片
"action film."

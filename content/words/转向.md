## Why this combination
转 "to turn" acting on the object 向 "direction" gives exactly "to turn
[toward a new] direction" — splittable, as in 转了向 "changed direction."

## Notes
转向 covers literal steering (汽车转向系统 "car steering system") and
figurative shifts in position (政策转向 "a policy shift," 态度转向 "a
change in attitude") — direction changed, physically or ideologically.

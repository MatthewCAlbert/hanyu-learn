## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 有 "to have" takes 效 "effect, efficacy" (see
also 效果) as its object — literally "to have effect" — used adjectivally
for anything that works, or any document still legally in force.

## Notes
有效 covers both "produces the intended result" (有效的方法 "an
effective method") and "still legally valid" (护照已过期，不再有效 "the
passport has expired and is no longer valid") — its opposite is 无效
"ineffective, invalid."

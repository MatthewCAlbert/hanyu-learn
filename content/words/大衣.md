## Why this combination
大 "big" + 衣 "garment, upper clothing" (see [[衣服]]) → a large outer garment
worn over everything else — a coat. 穿大衣, 羽绒大衣 "down coat."

## Notes
衣 as a bound "-garment" morpheme: 大衣, 毛衣 "sweater," 睡衣 "pyjamas,"
雨衣 "raincoat." The free word for clothes is [[衣服]].

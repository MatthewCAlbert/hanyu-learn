## Why this combination
尊 "to honour, to venerate" (see 尊's own etymology) and 敬 "to respect"
(see 敬's own etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard
word for deep respect.

## Notes
尊敬 also opens formal address: 尊敬的各位来宾 "distinguished guests" — a
polite honorific phrase used to open speeches and formal letters.

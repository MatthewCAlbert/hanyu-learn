## Why this combination
接 "to receive, to take, to answer (a call)" + resultative 到 "reach" — the
message got to you. 接到电话, 接到通知, 接到任务.

## Notes
接到 = receive something sent or assigned; [[收到]] overlaps (letters, gifts)
but 接 leans toward calls and orders. 接 alone: 接电话, 接朋友 "pick sb up."

## Why this combination

疑 ("to doubt, to suspect," as in 怀疑) plus 问 ("a question, to ask")
names a doubt held as a question: uncertainty about whether something is
true.

## Notes

毫无疑问 ("without a doubt, undoubtedly") and 疑问句 ("interrogative
sentence," a grammar term) both build on this word — 疑问 doubts a fact's
truth, distinct from plain 问题 ("a problem/question"), which need not
imply doubt.

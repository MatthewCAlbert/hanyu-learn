## Why this combination
个 (the generic measure word for individual items/people) with the
retroflex -儿 suffix common in colloquial northern speech extends "an
individual (unit)" to "a person's physical size" — measuring the person
as a unit rather than counting items.

## Notes
个儿 anchors 个儿高 "tall (in stature)," 大个儿 "a tall/big person" — an
informal, spoken-register word for physical size, especially height.

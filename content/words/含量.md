## Why this combination

含 ("to contain, to hold in the mouth," as in 包含) plus 量 ("quantity,
amount," as in 数量) names the amount of something contained within a
substance: content.

## Notes

含量 always follows the substance it measures (糖含量, "sugar content";
脂肪含量, "fat content") — a nutrition-label and lab-report staple.

## Why this combination
演 carries its "to perform, to deliver publicly" sense (as in 表演), and
讲 is "to speak, to explain" (as in 讲话). Delivering speech as a public
performance is exactly what giving a lecture or speech involves.

Rated "semi" for 演's "to perform/deliver publicly" sense here, an
extension of its role in 表演 rather than a fully separate meaning.

## Notes
演讲比赛 ("a speech contest") and 发表演讲 ("to deliver a speech") are
common frames — 演讲 always implies a formal, public address, not casual
conversation.

## Why this combination
闭幕 "to close (an event)" (see its own entry) plus the suffix 式
"ceremony, style" gives exactly "the ceremony of closing" — the standard
word for the formal event marking an end, mirroring 开幕式 "opening
ceremony."

## Notes
闭幕式 anchors 奥运会闭幕式 "the Olympics closing ceremony" — always the
formal event itself, opposite of 开幕式.

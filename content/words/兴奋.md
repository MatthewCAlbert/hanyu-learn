## Why this combination
兴 here takes the *xīng* reading, its "to rise, to flourish" sense applied
to mood or energy (as in 兴旺, "flourishing"), and 奋 is "to rouse
oneself, to exert vigorously" (as in 奋斗, "to struggle hard"). A mood
rising and energy rousing itself together is exactly "excitement."

Rated "semi" for 奋's "to rouse oneself" sense, met mostly in compounds
like this one and 奋斗.

## Notes
Read 兴 as *xīng* here, not the *xìng* of 兴趣 (interest) — the same
character, a different reading tied to a different, though related,
sense of "mood/energy rising."

## Why this combination
西 "west" + 北 "north" → the intercardinal northwest, in the fixed Chinese
order 西/东 then 南/北 (see [[西南]], [[东北]], [[东南]]). As a region, 西北
covers Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai and Xinjiang — arid, high, sparsely
peopled.

## Notes
The four intercardinals: [[东北]], 西北, [[东南]], [[西南]].

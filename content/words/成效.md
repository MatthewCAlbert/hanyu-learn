## Why this combination
成 (here "to achieve, resulting in") and 效 "effect" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for the positive outcome of an effort.

## Notes
成效 anchors 取得成效 "to achieve results," 收效甚微 "little effect
achieved" (using the related 收效) — always the (usually positive) payoff
of deliberate action, distinct from neutral 结果 "result," which need not
be positive.

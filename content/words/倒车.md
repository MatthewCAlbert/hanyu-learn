## Why this combination

倒 ("to reverse, to fall backward," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 倒闭) takes 车 ("vehicle") as its object: reversing a vehicle.

## Notes

倒车 covers literal reversing (倒车入库, "to reverse-park") and, in a
transit context, transferring between buses or trains — the same
"switching vehicles" logic as English "to change buses," though here
built on 倒's "reverse/switch" sense.

## Why this combination
办 "to handle, to manage, to get done" (办事, 办公) + 法 "method, way" → a way of
getting something done, a practical solution. 想办法 "think of a way,"
没办法 "nothing to be done."

## Notes
办法 is a practical fix for a problem; [[方法]] (方 + 法) is a method or
technique for a task. 有办法 "resourceful."

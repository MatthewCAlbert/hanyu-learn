## Why this combination
消费 "to consume" plus the agentive suffix 者 "one who does X" gives
exactly "one who consumes" — the standard economic term for a buyer of
goods and services.

## Notes
消费者 anchors 消费者权益 "consumer rights," 消费者协会 "consumer
association" — always the formal/economic register; casual speech
typically just says 顾客 "customer" for a specific shopper.

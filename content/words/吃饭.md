## Why this combination
饭 "cooked rice" stands in for "food / a meal" generally — rice being the
default staple — so 吃饭 is the verb-object phrase for "eat a meal." It splits:
吃过饭了 "have eaten," 吃了两碗饭 "ate two bowls." By extension "make a living":
靠…吃饭.

## Notes
Same rice-for-food logic in 早饭, 午饭, 晚饭. To eat a *specific* thing, drop 饭:
吃面, 吃苹果.

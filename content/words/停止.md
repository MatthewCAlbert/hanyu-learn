## Why this combination
停 "to stop, to halt [motion]" (as in 停车 "to park [a car]") and 止 "to
stop, to cease" (as in 为止 "up until") are close synonyms, paired into
the standard formal verb for bringing something to an end.

## Notes
停止 takes an ongoing action or process as its object (停止工作 "to stop
working," 停止呼吸 "to stop breathing") and is more formal than bare 停,
the everyday word for "to stop" a vehicle, a sound, or an activity.

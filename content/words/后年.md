## Why this combination
后 "after, behind" (as in 以后, 后来) combined with 年 "year": the year
after [next] — two years from now.

## Notes
后年 extends the 明年/后年 sequence one step further out ("this
year → 明年 next year → 后年 the year after"), mirroring 前年 "the year
before last," its opposite-direction counterpart.

## Why this combination
听 "to hear" + 说 "to say" → "to hear (someone) say." A hearsay / evidential
marker placed at the front: 听说他要结婚了 "I hear he's getting married."

## Notes
听说 flags second-hand information — the speaker is not vouching for it.
据说 is the more formal equivalent.

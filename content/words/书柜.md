## Why this combination
书 "book" plus 柜 "a cabinet, a chest" gives exactly "a cabinet for
books" — a straightforward, compositional furniture word.

## Notes
书柜 anchors 摆满书柜 "a bookcase filled (with books)" — a general term
for shelved storage for books, whether open shelving or an enclosed
cabinet.

## Why this combination
颜 "the face, complexion" (容颜, 笑颜) + 色 "colour, look, hue." Classically the
pair meant "the look on one's face" (给他点颜色看看 "teach him a lesson" keeps
that sense); the everyday meaning narrowed to "colour" as a property of things.

## Notes
色 alone is the bound morpheme in the colour words: 红色, 蓝色, 黑色. 颜色 is the
free noun: 什么颜色？

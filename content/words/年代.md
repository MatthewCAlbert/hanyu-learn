## Why this combination
年 "year" combined with 代 "a generation, an era" (see also 时代): years
grouped into an era — specifically, in modern usage, a decade of a named
century.

## Notes
年代 most often names a specific decade (九十年代 "the nineties," literally
"the 90s era") and, more loosely, "a period, an age" in general (那个
年代 "that era"); 时代, its close relative, is the broader word for "age,
epoch" without the decade-specific reading.

## Why this combination
提 "to lift, to raise [a point]" and 示 "to show, to indicate" combine
directly: raising something into view for someone's attention.

## Notes
提示 covers gentle reminders (提示你一下 "just a heads-up") and technical
prompts (系统提示 "a system prompt/notification," 提示音 "notification
tone") — always something brought forward for attention, not a full
explanation.

## Why this combination
农 "agriculture, farming" (as in 农业 "agriculture") combined with 民
"the people" (see also 人民, 公民): people engaged in farming — peasants,
farmers.

## Notes
农民 names those who work the land as their occupation (农民工 "migrant
worker," originally "farmer-turned-worker") — 农业, its close relative
built on the same 农, names the agricultural sector itself rather than
the people working in it.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 顺 ("to follow, in accordance with," as in
顺利, "smoothly") and 序 ("order, sequence," as in 秩序, "order [in a
system]") both independently name an orderly arrangement. Pairing them
gives the everyday word for a sequence.

## Notes

按顺序 ("in order, sequentially") and 顺序颠倒 ("the order is reversed")
are common frames — 顺序 names the arrangement itself, distinct from 步骤
("steps," the individual stages of a process).

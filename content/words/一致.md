## Why this combination
一 ("one") plus 致 ("to arrive at, to bring about," covered in this app's
hanzi entry) names everyone or everything arriving at the very same
point — one shared conclusion, opinion, or form.

Rated "semi" for 致's "to arrive at/bring about" sense, distinct from a
simpler reading of "one" plus an intensifier.

## Notes
一致 covers both opinions (意见一致, "views in agreement") and physical
matching (颜色一致, "matching in color") — any case of separate things
converging on the same point.

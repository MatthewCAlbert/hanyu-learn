## Why this combination
裤, whose 衤 "clothing" radical marks it as a garment word, names
trousers specifically, combined with the light noun suffix 子 (as in
桌子 "table," 儿子 "son"): the everyday concrete noun for the garment.

## Notes
裤子 is the standard word for trousers of any kind (牛仔裤 "jeans," 短裤
"shorts") — 裤 alone appears mainly as a bound component in these
compounds rather than standing on its own in speech.

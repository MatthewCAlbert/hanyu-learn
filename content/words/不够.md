## Why this combination
不 "not" + 够 "enough, to reach the mark." Falls short of what is needed:
钱不够, 时间不够, 做得不够好 "not done well enough."

## Notes
够 alone = "enough / to reach" (够了 "that's enough," 够不着 "can't reach").
不够 + adjective = "not … enough."

## Why this combination
用 "to use" and 户 "household" combine directly: the household or party
that uses a service — originally utility subscribers (water, electricity),
now generalised to any account holder or user.

## Notes
用户 is standard in tech and services: 用户名 "username," 用户体验 "user
experience" — the everyday word for "user" in any digital or utility
context.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 居 ("to dwell," as in 居民, "resident") and 住
("to live, to reside," as in 住在") both independently name making one's
home somewhere. Pairing them gives the formal word for residing.

## Notes

居住 is the written/formal register (居住条件, "living conditions";
居住证, "residence permit"); everyday speech simply uses 住.

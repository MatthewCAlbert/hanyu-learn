## Why this combination
实 "real, substantial" plus 惠 "benefit, favor" gives exactly "a real,
substantial benefit" — the standard word for value that is concrete
rather than merely nominal.

## Notes
实惠 works as noun ("图个实惠" "seeking practical benefit") and adjective
("价格实惠" "reasonably/economically priced") — always emphasizes genuine
practical value over appearance or prestige.

## Why this combination
公 (here "publicly established, standard") plus 式 "formula, pattern"
gives exactly "a publicly recognized formula" — the standard word for a
mathematical or scientific formula.

## Notes
公式 anchors 数学公式 "mathematical formula," 套用公式 "to apply a
formula mechanically" (sometimes critically, implying rote thinking) —
the technical term for a fixed relational expression.

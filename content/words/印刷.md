## Why this combination
印 "to stamp, to print" and 刷 (here "to print," from its base sense "to
brush," recalling the brush-based printing process) are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for the printing process.

## Notes
印刷 anchors 印刷厂 "print shop," 印刷术 "printing technology" (as in the
historic Chinese invention) — the industrial/technical process, distinct
from 打印 "to print (from a computer/printer)."

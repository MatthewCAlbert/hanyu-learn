## Why this combination
书 "book" (as in 书店, 书包) combined with 架 "a shelf, a rack, a
frame" (as in 衣架 "clothes hanger"): the rack for books — a bookshelf.

## Notes
书架 names the furniture piece for holding books (放在书架上 "put it on
the bookshelf") — 架 is broadly productive for framed or rack-like
structures (书架, 衣架, 相框 in a related sense), a consistent
"frame/rack" pattern.

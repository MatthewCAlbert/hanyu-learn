## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Sri Lanka (斯里蘭卡). 斯, 里, 兰 and 卡 are
chosen for sound. 兰 is not the orchid; 卡 is not "card."

## Notes
兰 as in [[荷兰]]. Older Chinese name 锡兰 (Ceylon) is outside this list.

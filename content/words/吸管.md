## Why this combination

吸 ("to inhale, to suck," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
吸收/吸烟) plus 管 ("a tube, a pipe") names a tube used for sucking up a
drink: a straw.

## Notes

用吸管喝 ("to drink through a straw") is the standard phrase — 吸管
shares its 吸 root with 吸收 (to absorb) and 吸烟 (to smoke), all built
on the same "drawing in" image.

## Why this combination
商 "commerce" + 场 "an open arena / ground" (广场 square, 操场 sports ground,
[[机场]]) → a large trading space. Bigger than a [[商店]]. Figuratively "the
world of business": 商场如战场.

## Notes
逛商场 "go around the mall." 场 is *chǎng* here. Modern near-synonym 购物中心
"shopping centre."

## Why this combination
基 "foundation, base" and 金 "money" combine directly: a base sum of
money set aside for a purpose.

## Notes
基金 anchors 基金会 "foundation [organization]," 养老基金 "pension fund,"
and modern investment vocabulary (基金经理 "fund manager").

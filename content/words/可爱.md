## Why this combination
可 "-able, worthy of" + 爱 "to love" → worthy of love, lovable. The 可 + verb
adjective frame also gives [[可怕]] (fearsome), [[可能]], 可靠 (reliable),
可惜 (regrettable).

## Notes
可 as "worthy of" derives adjectives from verbs across the board — a productive
and very transparent pattern.

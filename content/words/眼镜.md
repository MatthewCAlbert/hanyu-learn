## Why this combination

眼 ("eye") plus 镜 ("mirror, lens," as in 镜子) names a lens worn over the
eyes: glasses.

## Notes

太阳眼镜 (or shortened 太阳镜) is "sunglasses"; 隐形眼镜 ("invisible-shape
glasses") is "contact lenses" — 镜 as the productive "lens/mirror" root
runs through the whole family.

## Why this combination
花 "flower" + 园 "garden, grounds" (see [[公园]], [[校园]], [[动物园]]) → a
garden of flowers, a planted ornamental space. Often in place / estate names:
XX花园.

## Notes
花园 (private / ornamental) vs. [[公园]] (public park) vs. 院子 (yard).
花 "flower" also in 花儿, 花钱 "spend money" (a separate sense).

## Why this combination
博 "extensive, broad" (see 博's own etymology), 物 "things," and 馆 "a
hall, an institutional building" combine directly: a hall housing an
extensive collection of things — a direct calque of the Western
"museum" concept.

## Notes
博物馆 anchors 自然历史博物馆 "natural history museum," 科技博物馆 "science
museum" — 馆 alone is a productive suffix for institutional buildings
(图书馆 "library," 体育馆 "gymnasium," 美术馆 "art gallery").

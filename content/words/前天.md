## Why this combination
前 "front / before" + 天 "day" → the day one step before 昨天. 前 as "earlier in
time" is the same use as 前年 "the year before last," 以前 "before." Extends the
昨/今/明 row backward.

## Notes
The row: 前天 · 昨天 · 今天 · 明天 · [[后天]]. 前 (before) and 后 (after) bracket
it.

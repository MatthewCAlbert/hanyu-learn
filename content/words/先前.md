## Why this combination
先 "before, earlier" and 前 "before, in front" are near-synonyms combined
into a written word for an earlier point in time.

## Notes
先前 is used in written/narrative contexts: 先前的想法 "the earlier
idea," 与先前相比 "compared to before" — close to 以前 and 原先, all
largely interchangeable.

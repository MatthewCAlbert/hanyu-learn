## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 应 "ought, should" (应当) + 该 "ought, should" (activated in this
modal sense). Doubled for the everyday modal "should" — obligation (你应该道歉)
or expectation (他应该到了 "he should be here by now").

## Notes
该 alone also works as a terser "should" (你该走了). Negative 不应该 /
不该 "shouldn't."

## Why this combination
千 "thousand" + 克 "gram" (a phonetic borrowing of "gram") → a thousand grams,
a kilogram. The fully transparent, SI-style synonym of [[公斤]] (a "metric
catty").

## Notes
千克 is the term in science and on official labels; [[公斤]] in everyday speech;
斤 (500 g) in markets. Same 千 prefix in 千米 (= [[公里]] kilometre).

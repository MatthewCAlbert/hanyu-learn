## Why this combination
强 "strong, powerful" (as in 坚强 "resilient") combined with 大 "big,
great": strong and great together — powerful on a large scale, most
often said of nations, organisations or forces.

## Notes
强大 describes power at scale — a country, an army, an economy
(强大的国家 "a powerful nation") — 强壮, its near-synonym built on the
same 强, describes an individual body's physical strength instead
(身体强壮 "physically strong/robust").

## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 划, here "to row, to paddle [with an oar]" (a
distinct word from the homograph 划 huà "to draw a line, to delimit," as
in 计划), takes 船 "boat" as its object — to row a boat.

## Notes
划船 names the activity of rowing or paddling (去划船 "to go rowing," as
a recreational sport or on a lake) — the reading huá on 划 here contrasts
with huà in 计划 "plan" and 划分 "to divide," an unrelated pair of senses
sharing one character.

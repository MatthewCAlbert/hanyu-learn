## Why this combination
眼 "eye" combined with 前 "in front of, before" (see also 前面, 前往):
right in front of the eyes — immediately visible, and, by extension,
"right now, at the present moment."

## Notes
眼前 covers literal "right before one's eyes" (眼前的景象 "the sight
before one's eyes") and figurative "at present, for now" (眼前的问题
"the problem at hand right now") — the same literal-to-temporal
extension "before one's eyes" makes in English.

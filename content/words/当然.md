## Why this combination
当 here carries "ought to, should [be the case]" rather than its more
common "to act as, to serve as." 然 is a classical suffix meaning
"in that manner, so" (compare 突然 "suddenly," 显然 "obviously"). Together:
"how things ought naturally to be" — of course.

## Notes
然 as this kind of manner-suffix is worth flagging as a recurring
pattern across HSK vocabulary — 突然, 显然, 果然, 虽然 — a small,
productive family built the same way.

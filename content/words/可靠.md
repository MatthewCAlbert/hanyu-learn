## Why this combination
可 "can, able to, worthy of [being]" (as in 可能 "possible") combined
with 靠 "to rely on, to lean on" (as in 靠近 "to draw close to"): worthy
of being relied on — reliable.

## Notes
可靠 describes people, sources or systems that can be trusted to perform
as expected (可靠的朋友 "a reliable friend," 数据可靠 "the data is
reliable") — 信任, "to trust," names the act of trusting, while 可靠
describes the trustworthy quality itself.

## Why this combination
西 "western" (marking a foreign origin), 红 "red," and 柿 "persimmon"
combine into "a western red persimmon" — the tomato was named by analogy
to the already-familiar persimmon's shape and color when introduced from
the West.

## Notes
西红柿 anchors 西红柿炒鸡蛋 "tomato and scrambled eggs" (a classic home
dish) — the everyday Mandarin word for tomato; 番茄 is a common synonym,
especially in the south and in scientific/commercial contexts (e.g.
番茄酱 "ketchup").

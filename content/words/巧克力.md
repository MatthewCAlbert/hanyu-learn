## Why this combination
A phonetic transliteration of English "chocolate," three characters
chosen to approximate the foreign sound (*qiǎo-kè-lì*) with no regard to
their individual meanings ("clever," "gram/overcome," "power"). None of
those meanings combine to describe chocolate in any way — the characters
are pure sound-carriers, exactly like 沙发 (sofa) or 咖啡 (coffee).

## Notes
As with any transliterated loanword, do not look for meaning in the
individual characters — 巧克力 is borrowed sound from start to finish,
not a description built from its parts.

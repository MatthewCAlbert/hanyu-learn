## Why this combination
体 "body, to embody, to experience with one's whole self" (as in 具体)
combined with 验 "to test, to verify" (see also 经验, 实验): to test
something through embodied, first-hand experience — to experience
personally.

## Notes
体验 stresses direct, first-hand experience gained by actually living
through something (体验生活 "to experience life first-hand," 体验感
"a sense of [lived] experience") — 经历, its close relative, names the
events lived through, while 体验 stresses the felt, subjective quality
of undergoing them.

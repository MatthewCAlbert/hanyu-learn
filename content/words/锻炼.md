## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 锻 ("to forge metal," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 炼 ("to refine with fire," also covered elsewhere) both
independently name shaping and strengthening metal through intense
process. Pairing them gives the standard figurative word for exercising
the body — training it the way metal is forged and tempered into
strength.

## Notes
锻炼身体 ("to exercise, to work out," lit. "to forge the body") is the
character pair's overwhelmingly dominant modern use — the literal
metalworking sense survives mostly as this etymology.

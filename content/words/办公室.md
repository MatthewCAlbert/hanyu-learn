## Why this combination
办公 "to handle official business, to work in an office" (办 handle + 公 public
affairs) + 室 "room." Parse 办公 + 室. 室 "-room" also in 教室, 卧室, 会议室.

## Notes
办公 alone: 办公时间 "office hours," 在家办公 "work from home." 办 "handle"
also in [[办法]], 办事.

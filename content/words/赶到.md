## Why this combination
赶 "to hurry, to rush" (see also 赶紧, 赶快) combined with the
resultative complement 到 "to arrive at": to hurry and successfully
arrive — to rush to a place.

## Notes
赶到 marks that the hurrying resulted in arrival (赶到机场 "to rush to
the airport [and get there]") — bare 赶, without 到, only names the
hurrying itself and does not guarantee arrival was achieved.

## Why this combination
双 "a pair, both" and 手 "hands" combine directly into "both hands" — as
transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
双手 emphasizes using both hands together, often for effort or ceremony:
双手奉上 "to offer with both hands [respectfully]," 双手赞成 "wholeheartedly
in favour" (literally "both hands in support").

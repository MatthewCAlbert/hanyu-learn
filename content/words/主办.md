## Why this combination
主 "main, host" plus 办 "to handle, to manage" gives exactly "to be the
main organizer of" — the standard word for the lead body running an
event, distinct from 承办, which executes on behalf of the主办 party.

## Notes
主办 anchors 主办单位 "the host organization," 主办方 "the organizer" —
identifies who bears primary responsibility, contrasted with 协办
"co-organizing" or supporting roles.

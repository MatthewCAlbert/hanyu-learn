## Why this combination
快 "fast, quick" combined with 速 "speed" (see also 速度): fast in speed
— a compound emphasising rapidity, used mainly as a formal modifier.

## Notes
快速 modifies nouns and verbs in formal or technical contexts (快速
发展 "rapid development," 快速反应 "quick response") — bare 快, the
everyday adjective "fast," is used far more freely in casual speech.

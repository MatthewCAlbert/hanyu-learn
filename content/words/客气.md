## Why this combination
客 "guest" and 气 "air, manner, bearing" combine metaphorically: the
careful, deferential manner one adopts as a guest in someone else's
space, extended to politeness generally.

## Notes
客气 anchors the common phrase 别客气/不客气 "don't stand on ceremony /
you're welcome" — 你太客气了 "you're too polite" is a standard response to
someone's generosity or formality.

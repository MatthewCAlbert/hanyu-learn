## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" (as in 全部 "all, entirety") combined with 球 "a ball,
a sphere" here standing for 地球 "the globe, the Earth": the entire
globe — worldwide.

## Notes
全球 is used as a modifier meaning "global" (全球经济 "the global
economy," 全球化 "globalisation") — 世界, the more general word for
"world," names the world as a place, while 全球 emphasises its totality
as a single, unified sphere.

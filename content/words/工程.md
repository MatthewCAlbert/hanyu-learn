## Why this combination
工 ("work, labor") plus 程 ("a course, a measured schedule," covered in
this app's entry for 程序) names organized, scheduled work carried out
in planned stages: an engineering project.

## Notes
工程师 (engineer, lit. "engineering-master," using the same 师 suffix
covered in this app's entry for 律师) and 工程 itself both cover both
literal construction/engineering and, by extension, any large,
methodically planned undertaking (希望工程, "Project Hope," a real
charitable initiative).

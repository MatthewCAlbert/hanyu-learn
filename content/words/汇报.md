## Why this combination

汇 ("to converge, to gather together," covered elsewhere in this app as a
character where simplification merged two distinct traditional
characters, 匯 "to converge [water]" and 彙 "to collect [into a
category]," into one shape) plus 报 ("to report," as in 报告) names
gathering scattered information together and reporting it up the chain.

Rated "semi" for 汇's "gather/converge" sense here, less obvious once
divorced from its water-radical origin.

## Notes

汇报工作 ("to report on one's work," typically to a superior) is the
set phrase — 汇报 leans more formal/upward than plain 报告, which can go
in any direction.

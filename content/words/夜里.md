## Why this combination
夜 "night" + locative 里 "inside, within" (a bare time-suffix here, as in
心里, 家里) → within the hours of night: 夜里下了雨, 半夜里.

## Notes
夜里 (the deep of night) vs. [[晚上]] (evening through bedtime) vs. [[半夜]]
(midnight / the small hours). 夜 also in [[半夜]], 熬夜, 夜市.

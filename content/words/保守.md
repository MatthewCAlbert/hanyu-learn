## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 保 ("to protect," as in 保护) and 守 ("to
guard, to keep," as in 遵守, "to abide by") both independently name
holding on to something protectively. Pairing them gives the standard
word for guarding against change: conservative.

## Notes

保守 works as an adjective describing a stance resistant to change
(思想保守, "conservative in thinking") and as a verb meaning to keep
something guarded (保守秘密, "to keep a secret").

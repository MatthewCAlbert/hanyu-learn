## Why this combination
工 "labour, work" + 人 "person" → one who does manual work, specifically a
wage-earning industrial or manual worker, as against 农民 (farmer) or 干部
(cadre).

## Notes
工 also "skill / craftsman" (木工 carpenter) and, bleached, the light verb of
工作. The class term 工人阶级 "the working class."

## Why this combination
家 "home, family" (as in 家庭) combined with 乡 "village, one's native
countryside" (as in 乡下 "the countryside"): one's home village — the
place one is from.

## Notes
家乡 names the place someone was born and raised, often with an
emotional pull (想家乡 "to miss one's hometown," 回家乡 "to go back to
one's hometown") — 老家, a close, more colloquial synonym, is used the
same way in everyday speech.

## Why this combination
值 (here "to be on duty for, to take one's turn at") acting on the object
班 "a shift, a work period" gives exactly "to take one's turn at a shift"
— splittable, as in 值了一天班.

## Notes
值班 anchors 值班医生 "the doctor on duty," 今晚我值班 "I'm on duty
tonight" — the standard word for scheduled duty shifts, common in
hospitals, offices, and dormitories.

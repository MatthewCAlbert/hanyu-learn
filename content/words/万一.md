## Why this combination
万 ("ten thousand," standing more loosely for "a great many") plus 一
("one") pictures a one-in-ten-thousand chance — vanishingly unlikely, but
not quite impossible. That image of a remote possibility gives both the
noun ("a slim chance," 以防万一, "just in case") and the conditional
conjunction ("if by any chance," 万一下雨怎么办, "what if it happens to
rain").

Rated "semi" because the "one chance in ten thousand" logic, while
transparent once pointed out, is a specific idiomatic image rather than
a literal calculation.

## Notes
万一 introduces a hedge against an unlikely but consequential event —
以防万一 ("just in case," lit. "to guard against the one-in-ten-thousand
chance") is the most common fixed frame built on it.

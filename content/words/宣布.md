## Why this combination
宣 "to proclaim widely, to make known" (as in 宣传 "to publicise")
combined with 布 "to spread out, to announce, to lay out [publicly]" (as
in 公布 "to make public"): to spread a proclamation widely — to announce
formally.

## Notes
宣布 is used for formal, often official announcements (宣布结果 "to
announce the results," 宣布开始 "to declare [something] open/begun") —
告诉, by contrast, is the everyday word for telling someone something,
with no formal or public weight.

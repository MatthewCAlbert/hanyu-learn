## Why this combination
皮 "leather, skin" (as in 皮肤 "skin") combined with 包 "a bag, to wrap"
(as in 面包 in an unrelated sense "bread," and 书包 "schoolbag"): a bag
made of leather — a handbag or briefcase.

## Notes
皮包 names a leather bag, typically a handbag or briefcase (背皮包 "to
carry a handbag") — 包, alone, is the general word for "bag" of any
material, with 皮 specifying the leather material.

## Why this combination
帽 ("a hat," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 子 (the generic nominal
suffix, as in 桌子, 椅子) gives the everyday two-syllable noun for a hat.

## Notes
戴帽子 ("to wear a hat," using 戴, covered elsewhere in this app for
things worn on the head) and 扣帽子 ("to pin a label on someone," lit. "to
clamp a hat on," a figurative idiom for unfair labeling) both build on
this everyday word.

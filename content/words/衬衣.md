## Why this combination
衬 "to line, to lie beneath [an outer layer]" (see also 衬衫) combined
with 衣 "clothing" (see also 上衣): the garment worn beneath — a shirt.

## Notes
衬衣 is an exact synonym of 衬衫 (see its own entry), differing only in
the second character: 衣 "clothing" in general versus 衫 specifically
"a shirt/upper garment" — both name the same button-front shirt.

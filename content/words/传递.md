## Why this combination
传 "to transmit, to pass on" and 递 "to hand over, to deliver" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for passing something
along a chain.

## Notes
传递 covers information (传递信息 "to transmit information"), physical
objects (传递火炬 "to pass the torch [relay]"), and emotion (传递爱心 "to
spread love/kindness") — always something moving from one point to the
next.

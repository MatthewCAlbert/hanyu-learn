## Why this combination
罚 "to punish" acting on the object 款 "a sum of money" gives exactly "to
punish with money" — splittable, as in 罚了他一百块款 "fined him 100
yuan."

## Notes
罚款 works as verb ("被罚款" "to be fined") and noun ("交罚款" "to pay a
fine") — the standard word for a monetary penalty, distinct from
non-monetary punishment (处罚 more broadly).

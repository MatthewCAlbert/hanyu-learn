## Why this combination
相 "mutually, each other" plus 等 "equal" gives exactly "equal to each
other" — the standard word for two or more values being the same.

## Notes
相等 anchors 数量相等 "equal in quantity," 地位相等 "equal in status" —
always a comparison between two or more things, never a standalone
description of one thing.

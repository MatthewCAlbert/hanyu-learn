## Why this combination
找 "to look for" + resultative 到 "reach" — searching that arrived at its
target. 找 alone is only the searching: 我找工作 "I'm job-hunting" vs. 我找到
工作了 "I've landed a job."

## Notes
到 is the generic "success" complement on many verbs of pursuit and perception:
[[看到]], [[听到]], [[得到]], 买到, 借到.

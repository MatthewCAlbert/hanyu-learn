## Why this combination

重 ("heavy," as in 重要) plus 量 ("a quantity, an amount," as in 数量)
names the measured amount of heaviness: weight.

## Notes

重量 is the physics/technical register for weight (重量单位, "unit of
weight") — everyday speech about a person's weight more often uses
体重 (covered elsewhere in this app).

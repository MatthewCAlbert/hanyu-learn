## Why this combination
联合 "to unite, joint" (see its own entry) combined with 国 "nation,
country" (as in 国家): nations united — a direct translation of "United
Nations," the proper name for the international organisation.

## Notes
联合国 is a fixed proper noun (联合国大会 "UN General Assembly") —
straightforward calque of the English name, built from the exact
components 联合 "united" and 国 "nations."

## Why this combination
航 ("to navigate, to fly," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 班 (a
"scheduled run/shift," as in 上班, "to go to work [one's shift]") names a
scheduled voyage or flight running on a fixed timetable.

Rated "semi" for 班's "scheduled run" sense here, an extension of its
more familiar "work shift" meaning.

## Notes
航班号 ("flight number") and 转机航班 ("connecting flight") both build on
this pair's core "a scheduled flight run" sense — specifically air
travel in modern usage, even though 航 itself covers sea travel too.

## Why this combination
传 "to pass on, to transmit" (see also 传说, 宣传) combined with the
directional complement 来 "to come, towards the speaker": to be
transmitted and arrive — for a sound or piece of news to reach the
listener.

## Notes
传来 is used for sounds and news arriving from elsewhere (远处传来歌声
"singing carries over from the distance," 传来消息 "news arrives/comes
in") — it marks the source as external, something reaching the speaker's
position from somewhere else.

## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 录 "to record" (see also 记录, 纪录) takes 音
"sound" (as in 声音) as its object — literally "to record sound" — to
make an audio recording.

## Notes
录音 works as verb ("录音一段对话" "to record a conversation") and noun
("听录音" "to listen to a recording") — 录像, built the same way with 像
"image" in place of 音, is the parallel word for video recording.

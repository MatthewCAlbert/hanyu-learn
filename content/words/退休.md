## Why this combination
退, here in its "to withdraw, to step back [from a role]" sense (see
also 退出) combined with 休 "to rest" (as in 休息 "to rest"): to
withdraw into rest — to retire.

## Notes
退休 is used specifically for leaving the workforce at the end of a
career (退休金 "pension," 退休年龄 "retirement age") — 休息, its close
relative, is the general verb "to rest," used for any break rather than
permanently leaving work.

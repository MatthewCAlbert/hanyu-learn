## Why this combination
增 "to increase" acting on the object 产 (short for 生产 "production")
gives exactly "to increase production" — splittable, as in 增了产
"boosted output."

## Notes
增产 is an economic/agricultural term: 粮食增产 "grain output increase,"
often paired with 增收 "increased income" — the standard pairing for
"more output, more earnings."

## Why this combination
治 "to govern, to rule, to cure" and 理 "to manage, to put in order" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for administering or
bringing order to something.

## Notes
治理 spans political governance (国家治理 "national governance") and
environmental remediation (河流治理 "river remediation") — always about
systematically managing something toward a better, ordered state.

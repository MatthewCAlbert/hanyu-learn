## Why this combination
情 "situation, state of affairs" and 形 "shape, form" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for the shape a situation takes.

## Notes
情形 is close to 情况 "situation" in meaning; 情形 leans slightly more
toward describing a scene or state as observed, while 情况 covers
developments and news about a situation more broadly.

## Why this combination
英 clipped from 英格兰, the phonetic rendering of "England," + 国 "country." In
ordinary use 英国 names the UK, not England alone. The same mould as [[美国]],
[[法国]]. 英 here is not "hero / flower."

## Notes
英 as the country-abbreviation also in [[英语]], [[英文]], 英镑 "pound sterling."

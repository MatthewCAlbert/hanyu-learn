## Why this combination
局 originally named a layout on a game board (as in 棋局 "a chess game/
position"), extended metaphorically to any complex, evolving state of
affairs; 面 "face, aspect" adds "the face [that situation presents]."
Together: the current shape of a complex, unfolding situation.

## Notes
局面 anchors 打开局面 "to break open a stalemate/situation," 混乱的局面
"a chaotic situation," 政治局面 "the political situation" — always a
whole, complex state of affairs, not a single event.

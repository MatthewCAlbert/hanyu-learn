## Why this combination
年 "year" combined with 纪 "a record, a chronicle, a thread kept in
order" (see also 世纪, 纪念): the years recorded [of one's life] —
age.

## Notes
年纪 asks and states a person's age in everyday speech (多大年纪了
"how old are you," said politely to an older person) — 年龄, a close
synonym, is the more formal, written register used in official contexts
and documents.

## Why this combination
观 "to watch, to view" (as in 观察 "to observe") combined with 众 "the
masses, a crowd" (as in 众多 "numerous"): the crowd doing the watching —
an audience.

## Notes
观众 is used for anyone watching a performance, match, film or broadcast
(电视观众 "TV viewers"); 听众 is the parallel word for a listening
audience (radio, a lecture), and 读者 for readers.

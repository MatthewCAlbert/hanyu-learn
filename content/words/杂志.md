## Why this combination
杂 "miscellaneous, varied, mixed" (as in 复杂 "complicated") combined
with 志 "a record, a chronicle" (as in 日志 "a log, a diary"): a record
of miscellaneous [topics] — a magazine, gathering varied articles under
one publication.

## Notes
杂志 names a periodical publication distinct from 报纸 "newspaper" (which
prints news on a daily/regular schedule in a different format) — 时尚
杂志 "fashion magazine," 杂志社 "magazine publisher."

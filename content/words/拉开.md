## Why this combination

拉 ("to pull") plus 开 ("to open, apart") names pulling something until
it opens or separates: to pull open, and figuratively to widen a
distance or gap.

## Notes

拉开距离 ("to widen the distance/gap," literal or figurative, as in a
race or a competition) is the common figurative use — 拉开序幕 ("to raise
the curtain, lit. pull open the prologue") extends the same physical
image to "to kick off" an event.

## Why this combination
Interrogative 哪 "which" + 里 "place" (see [[这里]]) → "which place" → where.
Doubled or alone, 哪里哪里 / 哪里 is also the ritual deflection of a compliment —
"where [do you see that]?", i.e. you flatter me.

## Notes
哪 "which" (question) vs. 那 "that" (statement) differ only by tone — *nǎ* vs.
*nà* — and are a constant reading trap.

## Why this combination
意 "thought, idea" and 识 "to know, to recognize" combine straightforwardly
into "recognized thought" — the mind's awareness of itself and its
surroundings.

## Notes
意识 works as a noun (意识形态 "ideology," 潜意识 "subconscious") and, in
the pattern 意识到, as a verb "to realize/become aware of" (我意识到问题了
"I realized there was a problem").

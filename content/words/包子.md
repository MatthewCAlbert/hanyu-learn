## Why this combination
包 "to wrap, to enclose" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]]) → "a
wrapped thing" = a bun with the filling wrapped inside dough. The unfilled
version is 馒头; the filling "wrapped" is the whole point of the name.

## Notes
Same 包 = "wrapped parcel of dough" in [[面包]]; 饺子 is the same kind of
wrapped food, though its name doesn't contain 包. Insult: 你这个软包子 "you
pushover."

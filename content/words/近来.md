## Why this combination
近 "recent, close (in time)" plus 来 (here a marker of "up to the
present," as in 以来 "since") gives exactly "the recent period leading to
now."

## Notes
近来 is a time adverb placed at the start of a sentence or before the
verb: 近来身体怎么样? "How have you been lately?" — close to 最近, largely
interchangeable in everyday use.

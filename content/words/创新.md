## Why this combination
创 "to start, to found, to originate" (see also 创造, 创作) combined with
新 "new": to originate something new — innovation.

## Notes
创新 names bringing forth genuinely new ideas or methods (科技创新
"technological innovation," 创新能力 "capacity for innovation") — 创造,
its close relative, is broader, covering creation of anything new, while
创新 specifically stresses novelty and improvement over what existed.

## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Polska / Poland (波蘭). 波 and 兰 are chosen
for sound, not for "wave + orchid."

## Notes
兰 as in [[荷兰]], [[芬兰]]. Same stem in [[波兰语]], [[波兰文]].

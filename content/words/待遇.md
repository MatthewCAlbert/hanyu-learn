## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 待 ("to treat, to wait upon," as in 等待,
"to wait") and 遇 ("to encounter, to meet with," as in 遇到) both
independently name how one is met or handled by others. Pairing them
gives the standard word for how someone is treated, especially at work.

Rated "semi" for the shift from "being encountered/met" to the specific
modern sense of employment terms and compensation.

## Notes

待遇 most often means job compensation (工资待遇, "salary and benefits")
but also covers treatment in a broader sense (公平待遇, "fair
treatment").

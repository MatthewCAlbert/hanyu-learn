## Why this combination
贺 "to congratulate, to celebrate" plus 卡 "card" gives exactly "a
congratulatory card" — a straightforward, compositional word.

## Notes
贺卡 anchors 生日贺卡 "birthday card," 新年贺卡 "New Year greeting card" —
the general word for any occasion-marking greeting card.

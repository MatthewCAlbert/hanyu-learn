## Why this combination
叉 "a fork, a prong" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a fork" — the
suffix adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of 桌子, 椅子.

## Notes
叉子 anchors 用叉子吃 "to eat with a fork" — the Western-style eating
utensil, alongside 刀 "knife" and 勺子 "spoon," contrasting with the
traditional 筷子 "chopsticks."

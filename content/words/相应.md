## Why this combination
相 "mutually, each other" plus 应 "to respond" gives exactly "to respond
to each other" — the standard word for matching or corresponding
elements.

## Notes
相应 works as verb ("相应地调整" "to adjust accordingly") and adjective
("相应措施" "corresponding measures") — always implies one thing changing
or existing in step with another.

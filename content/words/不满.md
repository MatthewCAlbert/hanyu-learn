## Why this combination
不 "not" + 满 "full, satisfied" (see [[满意]]) → not satisfied. As an adjective
(对现状不满) and a noun (表达不满 "voice discontent"). Also literal "less than":
不满十八岁 "under eighteen."

## Notes
不满 (a grievance / discontent) is stronger and more pointed than 不满意
(merely "not pleased with"). 满 "full" also in [[满足]] (not this level),
充满.

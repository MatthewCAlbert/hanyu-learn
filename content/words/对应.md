## Why this combination
对 (here "facing, matched with") and 应 "to respond, to correspond" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a one-to-one
relationship between two things.

## Notes
对应 anchors 一一对应 "one-to-one correspondence," 对应关系 "a
corresponding relationship" — used across math, linguistics, and everyday
comparison to describe matched pairs or equivalents.

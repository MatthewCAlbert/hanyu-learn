## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 跑 "to run" takes 步 "steps, pace" (see also
进步, 散步) as its object — literally "to run steps" — to jog, to run.

## Notes
跑步 names running as an exercise or activity (去跑步 "to go for a run,"
跑步机 "treadmill") — 跑, alone, is the general verb "to run," used for
any running, not only exercise.

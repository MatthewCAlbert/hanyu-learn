## Why this combination
地 ("ground, earth") plus 面 ("surface," as in 面对, "to face") names the
surface of the ground: the floor or the earth's surface underfoot.

## Notes
地面 names the surface itself (地面湿滑, "the ground is wet and
slippery") — distinct from 地下 (covered elsewhere in this app, "below
ground") and from 地球 (the planet as a whole).

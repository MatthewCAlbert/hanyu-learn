## Why this combination
前 is the ordinary word for "in front, before" in time or space; 之 links a
time or event to that position the same way it does in 之后 — "[event] 之
[before]" reads as "before [event]." The two words form a matched pair
(之前/之后) built on the same connector.

## Notes
之前 can stand alone to mean "previously, before now" (之前我没听说过) or
follow a noun/clause for "before X" (下课之前) — parallel to 之后 in every
way except the direction of time.

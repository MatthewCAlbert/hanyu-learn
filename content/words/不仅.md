## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 仅 "only, merely" (as in 仅仅 "merely"): not [only]
limited to one thing — the standard conjunction opening a "not
only... but also..." construction.

## Notes
不仅 is regularly paired with 而且 or 也 in the second clause (不仅
聪明，而且努力 "not only smart, but also hardworking"); 不但, a close
synonym, is used the same way and is somewhat more common in speech.

## Why this combination
上 "up, upper" + the locative suffix 边. The up member of the 上边 / [[下边]]
pair. 上 also = "higher authority," so 上边的意思 = "what the people above
want."

## Notes
上面 is the exact synonym and is slightly more common in writing.

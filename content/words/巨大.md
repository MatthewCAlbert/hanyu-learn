## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 巨 ("giant, enormous," as in 巨人, "giant") and 大
("big") both independently name great size. Pairing them intensifies
plain 大 into "huge, immense" — a common Mandarin strategy for degree,
stacking two words for the same quality.

## Notes
巨大 modifies abstract as well as physical scale (巨大的压力, "immense
pressure"; 巨大的变化, "huge change") — not limited to physically large
objects the way English "huge" sometimes leans.

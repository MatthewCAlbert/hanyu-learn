## Why this combination
沙 "sand" and 漠 "desert" (see 漠's own etymology — Shuowen's "shifting
sands of the north") are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for a desert.

## Notes
沙漠 names the literal landscape (撒哈拉沙漠 "the Sahara Desert") and,
figuratively, any barren expanse (知识沙漠 "a desert of knowledge,"
i.e. an intellectually barren environment).

## Why this combination

遗 ("to leave behind, to bequeath," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 遗产, "heritage/inheritance") plus 传 ("to pass on, to transmit,"
as in 宣传, "to publicize [lit. proclaim + transmit]") names traits left
behind and transmitted to offspring: heredity.

## Notes

遗传病 ("a hereditary disease") and 遗传基因 ("hereditary gene") both build
on this word — 遗传 specifically names biological transmission across
generations, distinct from 传统 ("tradition," cultural transmission).

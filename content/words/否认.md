## Why this combination
否 "to negate, no" (as in 是否 "whether or not") combined with 认 "to
recognise, to admit" (as in 认识 "to recognise/know," 认为 "to
consider"): to negate an admission — to deny.

## Notes
否认 takes a fact or accusation as its object (否认错误 "to deny a
mistake," 否认指控 "to deny an accusation") — its opposite is 承认 "to
admit, to acknowledge," built the same way with 承 "to receive" in place
of 否.

## Why this combination
报 "to report; a report, a bulletin" (see [[通知]]'s cousin 报告, [[日报]]) +
纸 "paper" → the printed paper carrying reports. Also the physical newsprint:
用报纸包 "wrap it in newspaper."

## Notes
报 alone means "newspaper" in titles and compounds: [[日报]], [[晚报]], 看报.
纸 "paper" in 报纸, 纸条, 白纸.

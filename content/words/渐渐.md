## Why this combination

渐 ("gradual, by degrees") doubled into 渐渐 turns the idea of
gradualness into an adverb describing a slow, steady change — the
reduplication smooths the single character into a natural adverbial
rhythm, much like other doubled adverbs in the language.

## Notes

渐渐 always precedes the verb it modifies (天渐渐黑了, "it gradually grew
dark") — bare 渐 survives mainly in set phrases like 逐渐 ("progressively")
rather than standing alone.

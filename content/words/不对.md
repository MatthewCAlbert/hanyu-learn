## Why this combination
不 "not" + 对 "correct, right." Plain negation of correctness. Beyond "wrong
answer," it flags that something is amiss: 他今天不对劲 "he's not himself
today," 气氛不对 "the mood is off."

## Notes
对 as "correct" (对不对？ "right?") is distinct from 对 as "toward / facing"
(对着) and 对 "a pair."

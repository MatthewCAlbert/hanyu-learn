## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Österreich / Austria (奧地利). 奥, 地 and 利
are chosen for sound. This is not 地利 "favourable terrain."

## Notes
奥 as a phonetic also in older 澳洲 for Australia, which this corpus cannot spell
(澳 is not an HSK character).

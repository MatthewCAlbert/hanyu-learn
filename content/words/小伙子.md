## Why this combination

小 ("small, young") plus 伙 ("companion, fellow," as in 伙伴) plus the
nominal suffix 子 names a young male person: a lad, a young fellow.

## Notes

小伙子 is casual, warm spoken register — a middle-aged or older speaker
addressing or describing a young man; 姑娘 is its rough female
counterpart.

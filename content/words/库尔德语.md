## Why this combination
库尔德 is a phonetic rendering of Kurd, plus 语. 库 is not "warehouse"; 尔 is a
closer; 德 is not [[德国]].

## Notes
Spoken across [[土耳其]], [[伊拉克]], [[伊朗]], [[叙利亚]] in this Extra set.

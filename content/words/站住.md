## Why this combination
站 "to stand" (see [[车站]]'s literal 站) + resultative 住, which means "hold
firm, make stick, come to a stop" (the same 住 as [[记住]], 抓住, 停住). As a
shout, 站住！"stop right there!"; figuratively, 这个理由站不住 "that reason
doesn't hold."

## Notes
住 as a "hold / halt" complement is highly productive: 记住 "remember," 停住
"come to a stop," 忍不住 "can't help it," 站不住脚 "doesn't stand up."

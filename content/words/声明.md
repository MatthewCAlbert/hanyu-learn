## Why this combination
声 "sound, voice" (as in 声音 "sound") combined with 明 "to make clear,
bright, evident" (see also 明确, 说明): to voice something clearly — to
declare, or the resulting formal statement.

## Notes
声明 names a formal, often public statement (发表声明 "to issue a
statement," 官方声明 "an official statement") — 说明, its close relative,
is used instead for explaining or clarifying something, without 声明's
formal, official weight.

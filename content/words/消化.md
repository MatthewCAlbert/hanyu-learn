## Why this combination

消 ("to dissipate, to eliminate," as in 消失, "to disappear") plus 化
("to transform, to turn into," as in 变化) names food breaking down and
dissolving away into the body: to digest.

Rated "semi" for the extension from "eliminate/dissolve" to the specific
biological process of digestion.

## Notes

消化 covers literal digestion (消化不良, "indigestion") and, just as
commonly, absorbing new information (消化知识, "to digest [absorb]
knowledge") — the same "breaking something down to take it in" image
applied to food and to learning alike.

## Why this combination
千 "thousand" combined with 万 "ten thousand" gives the literal number
ten million, and, from stacking two of the largest ordinary number words
together, an extended sense of "an uncountable multitude" — which in
turn grammaticalized into an urgent adverb, "whatever you do, by all
means."

## Notes
As an adverb, 千万 precedes an imperative, almost always with 不要/别
(千万不要迟到 "whatever you do, don't be late") — the numerical sense
"ten million" (一千万) is comparatively rare next to this urgent-warning
use at the HSK 3 level.

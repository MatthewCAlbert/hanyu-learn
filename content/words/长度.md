## Why this combination
长 "long" plus 度 "degree, measure" gives exactly "the degree of
longness" — a measurement noun, parallel to 高度 "height" and 宽度
"width."

## Notes
长度 anchors 测量长度 "to measure the length," 文章长度 "the length of an
essay" — the standard word for the extent of something along its longest
dimension.

## Why this combination
开 "to open, to start up" (see [[开会]], [[开学]], [[开车]]) + 机 "machine,
device" (see [[手机]], [[关机]]) → to power a device up. 电脑开机, 一键开机.
In film-making, 开机 = "start filming" (opposite: 关机 "wrap").

## Notes
开机 ↔ [[关机]] "shut down." 开 "switch on" pairs with 关 "switch off" across
开灯 / 关灯, 开机 / 关机.

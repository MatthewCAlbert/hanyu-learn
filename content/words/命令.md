## Why this combination
命 (a mandate, an order with the weight of fate behind it) and 令 "to
command, an order" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for
an authoritative directive.

## Notes
命令 implies real authority behind the order (军事命令 "military order,"
下命令 "to issue an order") — stronger and more formal than the everyday
request-flavoured 要求 "to ask/require."

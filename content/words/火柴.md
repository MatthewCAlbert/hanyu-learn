## Why this combination
火 "fire" plus 柴 "firewood, kindling" gives exactly "a fire-stick" — the
standard word for the small wooden matchstick used to start a fire.

## Notes
火柴 anchors 一盒火柴 "a box of matches," 划火柴 "to strike a match" — a
household item increasingly replaced by lighters (打火机) but still the
standard word for a match.

## Why this combination
自 ("self") takes 信 ("to trust, to believe") as its object: trusting
oneself is exactly what confidence means. Fully compositional.

## Notes
自信 works as both adjective (他很自信, "he's very confident") and noun
(缺乏自信, "lacking confidence") without changing form.

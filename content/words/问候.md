## Why this combination

问 ("to ask") plus 候 (here "to inquire after [someone's condition],"
an extended sense beyond its more familiar "to wait/a season," as in
气候, "climate [lit. air + season]") names asking after someone's
well-being: to send regards.

Rated "semi" for 候's less common "to inquire after" role here, distinct
from its everyday "to wait" or "season/climate" senses.

## Notes

代我向他问候 ("give him my regards on my behalf") and 问候语 ("a greeting
phrase") both build on this word — more formal/written than the
everyday act of simply saying hello (打招呼, covered elsewhere in this
app).

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 乐 ("happy, joyful," as in 快乐) and 趣
("interest, fun," as in 兴趣) both independently name enjoyment. Pairing
them gives a noun for the pleasure found in an activity.

## Notes

乐趣 is always a noun (生活的乐趣, "the joys of life"; 阅读的乐趣, "the
pleasure of reading") — unlike 高兴/快乐, it never functions as a
predicate adjective describing a person's mood.

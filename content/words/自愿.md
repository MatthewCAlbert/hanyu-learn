## Why this combination
自 "self" and 愿 "to wish, to be willing" combine directly: acting
according to one's own wish, without being made to.

## Notes
自愿 anchors 自愿者/志愿者 "volunteer," 自愿参加 "to take part voluntarily"
— always contrasting with being compelled or assigned.

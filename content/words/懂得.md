## Why this combination
懂 "to understand" + resultative 得 marking the understanding as achieved. Used
for grasping something that takes insight — a principle, a skill, a person's
worth: 懂得感恩, 不懂得拒绝.

## Notes
懂得 + abstract object / how-to; bare 懂 for plain comprehension (听懂, 看懂,
你懂吗？). 得 is toneless here.

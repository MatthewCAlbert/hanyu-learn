## Why this combination
法 clipped from 法兰西, the phonetic rendering of "France," + 国 "country." The
same mould as [[美国]], [[英国]]. 法 here is not "law."

## Notes
法 as the country-abbreviation also in [[法语]], [[法文]]. Contrast 法律, 办法,
where 法 keeps its ordinary sense.

## Why this combination
消 "to extinguish, to dispel" and 防 "to prevent, to guard against"
combine directly: both putting out fires and preventing them from
starting.

## Notes
消防 anchors 消防员 "firefighter," 消防车 "fire truck," 消防栓 "fire
hydrant" — always the institutional/technical vocabulary of fire safety.

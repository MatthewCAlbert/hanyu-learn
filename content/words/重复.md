## Why this combination
重 (*chóng*) "again, to repeat" + 复 "to return, to do over" (复习, 恢复) → to do
or say the same thing over: 重复一遍 "say it again," 重复的工作 "repetitive
work," 重复劳动.

## Notes
重 is *chóng* here (again), as in [[重新]], not *zhòng* "heavy" ([[重要]]).
复 "again / return" also in [[复习]], [[回复]] (not this level).

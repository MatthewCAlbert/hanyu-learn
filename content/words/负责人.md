## Why this combination
负责 "to be responsible" (itself 负 "to bear, to carry" + 责
"responsibility") plus 人 "person" combines directly: the person who
bears responsibility for something.

## Notes
负责人 is the standard title for whoever is accountable for a project,
department, or organization — 项目负责人 "project lead," 单位负责人 "the
head of a work unit."

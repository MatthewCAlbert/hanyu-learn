## Why this combination
问 "to ask" + 题 "topic, heading, exam item" (the same 题 as 题目, 标题) → a
matter put up for asking. Spans "a question" (问个问题), "a problem"
(出问题了) and "an issue" (环境问题).

## Notes
没问题 "no problem." The breadth — question / problem / issue — is wider than
any single English word, so context decides.

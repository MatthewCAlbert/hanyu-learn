## Why this combination
牛 "cow / ox" + 奶 "milk." Chinese names milk by its animal: 牛奶 is the
default, 羊奶 goat's milk, 母乳 / 人奶 human milk. Unmarked "milk" is 牛奶.

## Notes
奶 also "breast" and, as a verb, "to nurse." 奶粉 powdered milk, 酸奶 yoghurt.

## Why this combination
法 "law" and 规 "rule, regulation" are near-synonyms combined into the
formal umbrella term for laws and regulations together.

## Notes
法规 is used broadly for the body of rules governing an area: 交通法规
"traffic regulations," 法律法规 "laws and regulations" — a collective
term, distinct from 法律, which more narrowly means formally enacted law.

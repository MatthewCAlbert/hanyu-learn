## Why this combination
共 "together, shared, in common" (as in 一共 "altogether") and 同 "same,
identical" (as in 同意 "to agree," literally "same idea") are close
synonyms, paired into the standard word for something shared jointly.

## Notes
共同 works as adjective ("共同的目标" "a shared goal") and adverb ("共同
努力" "to work together") — it stresses joint ownership or joint action
between two or more parties, unlike 一起, which simply means "together
[in the same place/time]" without implying shared ownership.

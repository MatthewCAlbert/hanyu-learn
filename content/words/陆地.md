## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 陆 ("land," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 大陆) and 地 ("earth, ground," as in 土地, "soil/land") both
independently name solid earth. Pairing them gives the standard word
for land as distinct from water.

## Notes

海洋与陆地 ("ocean and land") is the standard geographic pairing —
陆地 always contrasts with water (sea, ocean, lake), distinct from 土地,
which contrasts more with built structures or emphasizes land as a
resource (for farming, ownership).

## Why this combination
早 "early" + 饭, "cooked rice" for "meal" (see [[吃饭]]). The first of the day's
three: 早饭 / 午饭 / [[晚饭]].

## Notes
早餐 is the formal register; 早点 (*zǎodiǎn*) is "breakfast items" — the buns,
congee and fried dough eaten in the morning.

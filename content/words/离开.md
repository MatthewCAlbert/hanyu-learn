## Why this combination
离 "to be apart from, to separate" + 开 as a resultative "apart, away" (the same
开 in 分开, 打开). To move away from a place or person: 离开北京, 离开家.

## Notes
离 alone measures distance (离这儿很远 "far from here"); 离开 is the act of
departing. Antonym 到达 "arrive."

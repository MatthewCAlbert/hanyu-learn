## Why this combination
键 "a key (of a piano, typewriter, or computer)" plus 盘 "a tray, a
board, a flat surface" gives exactly "a board of keys" — the standard
word for both piano and computer keyboards.

## Notes
键盘 anchors 电脑键盘 "computer keyboard," 钢琴键盘 "piano keyboard" — one
word spans both musical and typing contexts, since both are literally
boards of keys.

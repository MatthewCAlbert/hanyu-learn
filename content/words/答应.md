## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 答 "to answer, to reply" (see [[回答]]) + 应 (*yìng*) "to respond,
to comply." From "to respond (when called)" came "to respond favourably" —
to agree or promise: 他答应了, 答应帮忙.

## Notes
应 is *yìng* here (respond / comply), not *yīng* "ought" (as in [[应该]]). 答应
is a spoken assent; 同意 "agree with" and 承诺 "make a formal promise" are
narrower.

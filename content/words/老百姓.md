## Why this combination
老, here a familiar prefix (as in 老师, 老太太) combined with 百姓 "the
hundred surnames," a classical phrase for the common populace (百 "a
hundred," 姓 "surname," from the notion that ordinary families were
counted by their many surnames, as opposed to the ruling nobility): the
familiar way of naming the common people.

## Notes
老百姓 is the everyday, warm word for ordinary people as distinct from
officials or the powerful (为老百姓办事 "to work for the ordinary
people") — 人民, its more formal relative, is the standard political
term for "the people" as a collective political body.

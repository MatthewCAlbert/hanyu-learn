## Why this combination
打 as the all-purpose light verb "do, perform" (see [[打开]]) taking 电话 as its
object. It splits like any verb-object: 打个电话 "give (someone) a call,"
打了半天电话 "was on the phone for ages," 给他打电话 "call him."

## Notes
Why 打 and not 说 or 用? Convention — 打 got attached to using telephones,
telegrams (打电报) and later 打车. Just idiom.

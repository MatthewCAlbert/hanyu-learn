## Why this combination
满 "full, filled" (see also 充满) combined with 足 "sufficient, enough"
(as in 足够 "enough"): filled to sufficiency — satisfied, or, as a verb,
to bring something to that filled, sufficient state.

## Notes
满足 works as an adjective describing a satisfied state (感到满足 "to
feel satisfied") and as a transitive verb meaning "to satisfy, to meet
[a need]" (满足要求 "to meet the requirements") — the two uses share the
same underlying image of a need filled to its full measure.

## Why this combination
最 "most" + 后 "back, rear, after" → the most-to-the-rear position, the last
one. The spatial 后 (behind) doubles as temporal (after), so 最后 works for both
the last item in a row and the final moment in a sequence.

## Notes
Mirror of 最初 / 最先 "at the very first." 后 alone as "after" is in 以后,
然后, 后天.

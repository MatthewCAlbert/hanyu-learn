## Why this combination
搞 "to do, to handle" plus the resultative complement 好 "well" gives
exactly "to do (something), achieving a good result."

## Notes
搞好 always takes an object: 搞好关系 "to maintain good relations," 搞好
工作 "to do a good job of the work" — a colloquial, versatile verb
paralleling 做好 but with a more informal register.

## Why this combination
敲 "to knock, to strike" acting on the object 门 "door" gives exactly "to
knock on the door" — splittable, as in 敲了敲门.

## Notes
敲门 anchors 敲门声 "the sound of knocking," 敲门砖 "a stepping stone (lit.
a brick used to knock, then discarded)" — the standard word for the
knocking gesture before entering.

## Why this combination

再 ("again," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 一再) plus 三
("three," here used loosely to mean "several times" rather than an
exact count) names doing something again and again.

Rated "semi" for 三's non-literal "several" role here, the same pattern
Chinese uses elsewhere to mean "many" with a small round number rather
than an exact count.

## Notes

再三考虑 ("to consider again and again") and 再三强调 ("to repeatedly
emphasize") both build on this word — close in meaning to 一再 (covered
elsewhere in this app), both formal written-register adverbs for
repetition.

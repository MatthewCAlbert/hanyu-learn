## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 道 "a way, a road, a course" + 路 "road, path." Both literal
(道路两旁 "on both sides of the road") and figurative (人生道路 "life's road,"
发展道路 "path of development").

## Notes
道路 is more formal / abstract than [[马路]] (a street) or [[公路]] (a
highway). 道 "way" also in [[道理]], [[知道]], [[味道]].

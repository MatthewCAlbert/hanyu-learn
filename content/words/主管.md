## Why this combination
主 "main, chief" and 管 "to manage, to be in charge of" combine directly:
the person or department chiefly responsible for managing something.

## Notes
主管 works as a verb ("to be in charge of," 主管财务 "to oversee
finances") and a job title (业务主管 "operations manager") — a mid-level
management title, below 经理 in many company hierarchies.

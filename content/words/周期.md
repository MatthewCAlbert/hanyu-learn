## Why this combination
周 (here "a cycle, a round," as in 周而复始) and 期 "period" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a recurring interval.

## Notes
周期 anchors 生命周期 "life cycle," 周期性 "periodic, cyclical" — used in
science, economics, and biology for anything that recurs at regular
intervals.

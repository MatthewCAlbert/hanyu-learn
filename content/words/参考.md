## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 参 (here "to consult, to take into account," an
extended sense of the verb otherwise seen as "to take part in," as in
参加) and 考 ("to examine, to study closely," as in 考试) both
independently name looking something over carefully. Pairing them gives
the standard word for consulting a source.

Rated "semi" for 参's extended "to consult" sense here, one step from
its more common "to participate" meaning.

## Notes

参考书 ("a reference book") and 仅供参考 ("for reference only") are the
standard set phrases — 参考 implies weighing a source without
necessarily following it exactly, distinct from 遵守 ("to abide by,"
which implies compliance).

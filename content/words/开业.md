## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 开 "to open" (see also 开放, 开始) takes 业
"trade, business" (see also 商业, 职业) as its object — literally "to
open [for] business" — to open a shop or practice.

## Notes
开业 marks the formal opening of a business or professional practice
(新店开业 "new shop opening," 开业典礼 "opening ceremony") — 开始, the
general verb "to begin," is used for starting any activity, not
specifically a business opening.

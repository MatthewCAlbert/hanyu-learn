## Why this combination
以 "taking (a point) as reference" + 上 "above." "From (that point) upward" — a
level and everything over it (十八岁以上 "eighteen and over," 一米以上), and, in a
text, "the foregoing" (以上是我的看法; 以上。 "That is all.").

## Notes
Mirror of [[以下]]. Inclusive by default: 60分以上 usually includes 60.

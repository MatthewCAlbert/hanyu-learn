## Why this combination
面 "face" and 貌 "appearance" are near-synonyms combined into a formal
word for how something looks overall.

## Notes
面貌 is most often figurative: 城市面貌 "the appearance/look of a city,"
精神面貌 "one's overall demeanor/spirit" — used more for the general
"look" of a place or situation than a specific person's face (that would
be 面容 or 脸).

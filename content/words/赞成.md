## Why this combination

赞 ("to praise, to assist," as in 赞助, "to sponsor") plus 成 ("to
succeed, to bring about," as in 成功) names lending one's support to
help something succeed: to approve of.

Rated "semi" for 成's extended "to help bring about" sense here, one
step from its plainer "to become/succeed" meaning.

## Notes

赞成 votes yes on a proposal (我赞成这个建议, "I agree with this
suggestion") — 反对 ("to oppose") is its direct antonym, the standard
pair used in formal voting and debate.

## Why this combination
易 "easy, simple" is the transparent half. 容 here carries its "to
permit, to accommodate" sense rather than "appearance" — something that
"allows itself" to be done without resistance is, by extension, easy.

## Notes
容易 doubles as "prone to, likely to [happen]" when applied to
unwelcome events — 容易感冒 "prone to catching colds" — the ease with
which something occurs, whether wanted or not.

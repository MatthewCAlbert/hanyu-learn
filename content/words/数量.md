## Why this combination
数 "number, count" (as in 数字 "number, digit") combined with 量 "amount,
capacity" (see also 尽量, 力量): the counted amount of something —
quantity.

## Notes
数量 measures how many/much of something there is (数量很大 "a large
quantity," 数量和质量 "quantity and quality") — it pairs as the standard
opposite of 质量 "quality" in the classic quantity/quality contrast.

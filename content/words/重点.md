## Why this combination
重 (*zhòng*) "heavy, weighty, important" + 点 "point, spot" → the point that
carries the weight, the crux. Noun (抓住重点), attributive (重点大学 "key
university"), adverb (重点讲一下 "focus on…").

## Notes
重 is *zhòng* here (as in [[重要]], [[重视]]), not *chóng* "again" (as in
[[重新]], [[重复]]).

## Why this combination
Borrowed from Japanese 日本, "origin of the sun" — east of the continent, where
the sun rises. The characters were chosen in Japan; Mandarin took the written
form and read it with Chinese pronunciations *rì běn*. Not a native Chinese
coinage, and not derivable from 日 "sun" + 本 "root" as an everyday compound.

## Notes
日 also abbreviates 日本 in 日语, 日文, 日元. Contrast [[中国]]. 韩国 is not in
this corpus (韩 is not an HSK character); historical 朝鲜 names the peninsula
and today's DPRK.

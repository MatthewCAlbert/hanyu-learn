## Why this combination

冬 ("winter," as in 冬天) plus 季 ("a season," as in 季节) names the
winter season.

## Notes

冬季 is the formal/written register (冬季奥运会, "Winter Olympics") —
冬天 is the everyday spoken word for the same season, differing only in
register.

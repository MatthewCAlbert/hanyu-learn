## Why this combination

零 (here "fragmentary, odd, in small bits," an extended sense beyond
its more familiar "zero," as in 零钱, "small/loose change") plus 食
("food," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 食物/粮食) names food
eaten in small, casual bits between meals: snacks.

Rated "semi" for 零's extended "small/fragmentary" sense here, distinct
from its everyday "zero" meaning.

## Notes

吃零食 ("to eat snacks") is the standard verb-object phrase — 零食
covers casual between-meal food generally, unlike 快餐 (covered
elsewhere in this app), which names quick full meals.

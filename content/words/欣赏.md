## Why this combination
欣 "delight, joy" and 赏 "to appreciate, to view with pleasure" combine
directly into "to take joyful pleasure in viewing/considering something."

## Notes
欣赏 spans art and beauty (欣赏音乐 "to appreciate music") and people's
qualities (欣赏他的勇气 "to admire his courage") — always positive regard,
never neutral observation.

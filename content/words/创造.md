## Why this combination
创 "to start, to found, to originate" (as in 创作 "to create [a work]")
and 造 "to make, to build, to bring about" (as in 造成) are close
synonyms, paired into the verb for bringing something new into being.

## Notes
创造 takes abstract or novel results as its object (创造机会 "to create
opportunities," 创造历史 "to make history") — it stresses originality and
newness, unlike 做 or 造, the plain verbs for "to make," which carry no
such implication.

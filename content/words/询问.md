## Why this combination
询 "to inquire, to consult" (see 询's own etymology — 謀也, "to consult")
and 问 "to ask" are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for
inquiry.

## Notes
询问 is more formal than everyday 问: 询问情况 "to inquire about the
situation," 询问处 "information/inquiry desk" — a considered, often
official asking rather than casual conversation.

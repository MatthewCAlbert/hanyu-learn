## Why this combination
训 "to instruct, to teach [a lesson]" paired with 练 "to practise, to
drill, to refine through repetition": instruction combined with repeated
practice — training, in the sense that produces a skill rather than mere
knowledge.

## Notes
训练 is used for physical, military and vocational skill-building (训练
运动员 "to train athletes"); 教育 covers general schooling and
intellectual formation instead.

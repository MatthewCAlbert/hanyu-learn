## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 型 ("a type, a model," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 小型/新型) and 号 ("a number, a sign," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 符号) both independently name a way of
classifying and labeling something. Pairing them gives the technical
word for a product's model designation.

## Notes

手机型号 ("phone model") and 型号不对 ("wrong model") both build on this
word — 型号 always names a specific labeled variant within a product
line, distinct from 类型, the more general word for "category/type."

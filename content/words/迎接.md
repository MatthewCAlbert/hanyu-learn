## Why this combination
迎 "to welcome, to greet, to go towards [an arrival]" (as in 欢迎 "to
welcome") combined with 接 "to receive, to meet [someone arriving]" (see
also 接近): to go and receive with welcome — to greet.

## Notes
迎接 is used for meeting an arriving guest or greeting an approaching
event alike (迎接客人 "to welcome guests," 迎接挑战 "to meet a challenge
head-on") — 欢迎, its close relative, is used for the act of welcoming
in general, including as a standalone greeting ("欢迎光临" "welcome").

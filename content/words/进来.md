## Why this combination
进 "to enter" + 来 "to come (toward the speaker)" — entering, toward where you
are; 请进来 "come on in." [[进去]] is the same entry going away from you. As a
complement: 拿进来 "bring in."

## Notes
请进 (the sign on a door) drops 来 because there is no speaker viewpoint to
encode.

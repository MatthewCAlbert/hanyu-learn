## Why this combination

相 ("mutually, each other," as in 相同) plus 处 ("to dwell with, to deal
with," read here in its verbal fourth-tone sense as in 相处/处理) names
living or dealing with one another — getting along.

Rated "semi" for 处's less common verbal reading here versus its more
familiar noun sense in words like 好处 ("benefit").

## Notes

相处得好/不好 ("to get along well/badly") is the standard way to grade a
relationship's ease — roommates, coworkers, couples all "相处."

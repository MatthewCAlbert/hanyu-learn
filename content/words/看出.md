## Why this combination
看 "to look" with the directional complement 出 "out" gives "to see [a
fact] emerge/come out" through observation — discerning something not
immediately obvious.

## Notes
看出 always implies discernment beyond a casual glance: 看出问题 "to spot
the problem," 看得出/看不出 "can/can't tell" — the potential-complement
forms are extremely common in speech.

## Why this combination
钢 "steel" plus 笔 "pen, writing instrument" gives exactly "a steel-nibbed
pen" — naming the fountain pen by its metal nib, as distinct from
traditional brush (毛笔) or pencil (铅笔).

## Notes
钢笔 anchors 钢笔字 "fountain-pen handwriting," 钢笔墨水 "fountain pen ink"
— the standard word, though today often used loosely for any modern
pen, including ballpoints.

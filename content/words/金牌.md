## Why this combination
金 "gold" (as in 现金 "cash," literally "present metal") combined with
牌 "a plaque, a medal" (see also 牌子): the gold-coloured medal — a gold
medal.

## Notes
金牌 names the top prize in a competition, especially sporting (拿金牌
"to win the gold medal") — 银牌 "silver medal" and 铜牌 "bronze medal"
are built the same way with 银 "silver" and 铜 "copper/bronze" in place
of 金.

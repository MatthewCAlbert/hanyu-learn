## Why this combination

耳 ("ear") plus 机 ("a machine, a device," as in 手机, "mobile phone")
names a device worn on the ear: earphones.

## Notes

戴耳机 ("to wear earphones," using 戴, the verb for accessories worn on
the extremities) pairs 耳机 with the same verb used for 眼镜 and 手套,
both covered elsewhere in this app.

## Why this combination
称 (here "to call, to name") and 号 (here "a title, a designation," as in
外号 "nickname") are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for an
official or honorific title.

## Notes
称号 anchors 荣誉称号 "honorary title," 获得称号 "to be awarded a title" —
usually an earned or bestowed designation, distinct from 名字 (a personal
name) or 职务 (a job post).

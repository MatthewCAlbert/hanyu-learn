## Why this combination
查 "to check, to look up" and 询 "to inquire" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for looking up information.

## Notes
查询 anchors 查询余额 "to check one's balance," 在线查询 "online
inquiry/lookup" — the standard word for checking a record or database,
now also the technical term for a database "query."

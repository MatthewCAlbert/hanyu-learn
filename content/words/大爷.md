## Why this combination

大 ("big," here a term of respect/seniority rather than literal size)
plus 爷 ("grandfather, sir," as in 爷爷) names an older man addressed
respectfully: "sir," "uncle," an elderly gentleman.

## Notes

大爷 is a warm, respectful street-level address for an older stranger
(like 大妈 for an older woman) — colloquially it can also mock someone
acting self-importantly entitled ("摆大爷架子," "putting on airs like a
big shot").

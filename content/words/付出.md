## Why this combination
付 ("to pay," as in 支付) takes 出 as a resultative complement marking
that what was paid actually left the payer's hands — money paid out, or
by extension, effort and time expended.

## Notes
付出 pairs naturally with abstract costs (付出努力, "to put in effort";
付出代价, "to pay a price") more often than with literal cash, where plain
付 or 支付 is the more common choice.

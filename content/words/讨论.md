## Why this combination
讨 "to seek out, to demand, to go into" (探讨) + 论 "to discuss, to argue,
to expound" (议论, 理论). Going into a question together: 讨论问题, 分组讨论.

## Notes
讨论 = discuss (weigh a matter); [[商量]] = consult (to reach a decision, often
practical); 谈 = talk. 论 alone: [[讨论]], 无论, 结论.

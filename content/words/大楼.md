## Why this combination

大 ("big") plus 楼 ("a multi-story building," as in 楼上/楼下 "upstairs/
downstairs") names a large multi-story building — an office tower or
apartment block, as opposed to a low single-story 房子.

## Notes

大楼 is the generic word for a sizeable building (办公大楼, "office
building"; 教学大楼, "teaching building/academic block") — for a
specifically tall skyscraper, 高楼 or 大厦 is more precise.

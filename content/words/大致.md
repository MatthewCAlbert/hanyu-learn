## Why this combination
大 (here "roughly, in general") plus 致 (here "extent, degree") gives
exactly "to a general extent" — the standard word for an approximate
estimate.

## Notes
大致 modifies both statements and situations: 大致情况 "the general
picture," 大致相同 "roughly the same" — close to 大概, both common
hedging words for approximation.

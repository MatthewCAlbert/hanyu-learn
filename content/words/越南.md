## Why this combination
A character-for-character reading of Vietnamese Việt Nam: 越 for the Việt
people, 南 "south." Unlike [[法国]] this is not a phonetic clip plus 国; the
name already meant "Việt of the south" in the region before modern Mandarin
standardised it.

## Notes
The same stem in [[越南语]]. 南 here really is "south"; 越 is the ethnonym, not
"to exceed" as in 越过.

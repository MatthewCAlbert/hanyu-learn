## Why this combination
The native Vietnamese name Việt Nam, long written in chữ Hán and read with
Mandarin pronunciation — closer to a direct graphic borrowing than a calque.
越 stands for the Việt people, 南 "south." Unlike [[法国]] this is not a
phonetic clip plus 国; the name already meant "Việt of the south" in the
region before modern Mandarin standardised it.

## Notes
The same stem in [[越南语]]. 南 here really is "south"; 越 is the ethnonym, not
"to exceed" as in 越过.

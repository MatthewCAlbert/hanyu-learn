## Why this combination
飞 "to fly" (as in 飞机 "airplane") combined with 行 "to go, to travel,
to move" (as in 旅行 "to travel"): to travel by flying — flight, to fly.

## Notes
飞行 names sustained flight, especially of aircraft (飞行员 "pilot,"
飞行时间 "flight time") — 飞, alone, is the simple verb "to fly" used for
a bird, an object thrown, or a plane's momentary action, without 飞行's
technical, sustained-flight framing.

## Why this combination
感 "to feel, to sense" combined with 受 "to receive, to undergo" (as in
接受 "to accept"): to receive something through feeling — a felt
impression taken in from experience.

## Notes
感受 as a noun names one's subjective impression of an experience (谈谈你
的感受 "tell me your impressions/what it felt like"); as a verb it takes
an experience as its object (感受幸福 "to feel/experience happiness"),
closer to "to take in through feeling" than the blunter 觉得 "to think,
to feel [that]."

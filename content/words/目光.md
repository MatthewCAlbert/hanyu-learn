## Why this combination
目 "eye" is transparent; 光 "light" here is used metaphorically for the
gaze that seems to shine out from the eyes, rather than literal
illumination — the same poetic logic behind English "the light in her
eyes."

## Notes
目光 covers the literal gaze (目光集中 "focused gaze") and figurative
foresight (目光长远 "far-sighted," 目光短浅 "short-sighted [in judgement]")
— vision as both eyesight and judgement.

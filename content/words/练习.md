## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 练 "to train, to drill, to temper" (训练, 练功) + 习 "to practise
repeatedly, to be used to" (see [[学习]], [[习惯]]). Repeated doing to build
skill — the verb (练习写字) and the noun (做练习, 练习题).

## Notes
练习 (drill a skill) vs. [[复习]] (review what was learnt) vs. 预习 (prepare a
lesson in advance). 练 alone: [[练习]]'s cousin 锻炼 "work out."

## Why this combination
严 "strict" and 厉 "severe" (see 厉's own etymology — a whetstone,
sharpened to its keenest edge) are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for harsh strictness.

## Notes
严厉 describes strict discipline and harsh consequences (严厉批评 "harsh
criticism," 严厉的惩罚 "severe punishment") — stronger and less warm than
plain 严格 "strict [but fair]."

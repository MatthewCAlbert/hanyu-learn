## Why this combination
连, here contributing a sense of immediacy (as in 连续 "continuous," 连忙
extending that to "right away") combined with 忙 "busy, hurried" (as in
帮忙): acting hurriedly and at once.

## Notes
连忙 is an adverb describing an immediate, hurried reaction to something
just said or happened (他连忙道歉 "he promptly apologized") — 赶紧, its
close synonym, is largely interchangeable, though 连忙 more strongly
implies a reaction narrated after the fact in a story.

## Why this combination

途 ("a path, a way," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 前途,
"future prospects") plus 中 ("middle, in the midst of," as in 中午)
names being in the middle of a journey: en route.

## Notes

在…途中 ("on the way to…, en route to…") is the standard frame — 半途
("halfway," lit. "half + path") shares the same 途 root for a related
"partway through" sense.

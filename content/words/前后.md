## Why this combination
前 "front, before" (see also 前面, 以前) combined with 后 "behind, after"
(see also 后面, 以后): before and after together — around a point in
time, or the whole span from start to finish.

## Notes
前后 approximates a time by bracketing it from both sides (春节前后
"around Chinese New Year") and, separately, names the full span of an
event (三年前后 "over the span of three years, start to finish") —
both senses share the image of the two ends bracketing a middle.

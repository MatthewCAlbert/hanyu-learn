## Why this combination
细 "fine, tiny" plus 胞 "sac, womb": a tiny sac. The word is a calque of "cell", reportedly coined in Japanese (細胞 *saibō*) and adopted into Chinese.

## Notes
The 胞 sense "sac" is also in 同胞 (the same womb).

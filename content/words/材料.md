## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 材 ("timber, usable wood," extended to "raw
material" generally, as in 木材, 人才's homophone-but-different 才) and 料
("material, ingredient," covered in this app's hanzi entry) both
independently name raw substance set aside for use. Pairing them gives
the ordinary word for material of any kind.

## Notes
材料 covers physical raw material (建筑材料, "building materials") and
informational material alike (写作材料, "material for writing") — see this
app's entry for 资料 for how the two overlapping words divide the
informational sense between them.

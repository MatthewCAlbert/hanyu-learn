## Why this combination
完 "finished, complete" with the completion marker 了 gives the literal
"[it] has finished" — from which the colloquial exclamation "we're
done for, it's over" develops: something finishing in the worst possible
sense.

## Notes
完了! as a standalone exclamation means "oh no, we're doomed" — very
different in tone from the neutral completed-action sense in 事情完了
"the matter is finished." Context and tone separate the two readings.

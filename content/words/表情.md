## Why this combination
表 ("to express, to show on the surface," as in 表示, 表达) plus 情
("feeling, emotion," as in 感情, 心情) names exactly what a facial
expression is: emotion shown outwardly.

## Notes
表情 is specifically the visible, facial display of feeling — 感情 or 心情
name the feeling itself, while 表情 names how it shows on someone's face.

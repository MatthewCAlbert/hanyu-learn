## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 加 "to add" (see also 加强, 加快) takes 工 "work,
labour" (see also 工厂, 工具) as its object — literally "to add labour
[to raw material]" — to process.

## Notes
加工 names transforming raw materials into a finished product (食品
加工 "food processing," 加工厂 "processing plant") — 制造, its
neighbour, is the broader verb "to manufacture," while 加工 specifically
emphasises the labour applied to transform existing material.

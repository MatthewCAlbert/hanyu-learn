## Why this combination
Simple reduplication of 宝 ("treasure," covered in this app's hanzi
entry). Doubling a noun is one of Mandarin's standard ways to soften a
word into an affectionate, diminutive form — the same pattern behind many
terms of endearment and baby-talk vocabulary.

## Notes
宝宝 is used both as a direct term of endearment for a baby or young
child and, increasingly in casual internet Mandarin, as a cutesy way to
refer to oneself or a pet.

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Singapore. 新 is the Sing- syllable, not
the adjective "new" (contrast [[新西兰]]). 加 and 坡 finish the sound. 坡 is not
"slope."

## Notes
The calque of "lion city" is 狮城, a nickname, not the official name.

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Italia. 意, 大 and 利 are chosen for
sound, not for "idea + big + benefit." Unlike [[法国]] or [[美国]], there is no
clipped 国 — the full transcription *is* the country name.

## Notes
The same stem in [[意大利语]]. Contrast 意 "meaning" in 意思, 注意.

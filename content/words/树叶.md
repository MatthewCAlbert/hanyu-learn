## Why this combination

树 ("a tree," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 树林) plus 叶
("a leaf," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 叶子/茶叶) names the
leaves of a tree.

## Notes

树叶落下 ("leaves fall") and 树叶变黄 ("leaves turn yellow") both build on
this word — 树叶 specifically names leaves still on or fallen from a
tree, distinct from 茶叶 (covered elsewhere in this app), which names
tea leaves harvested for drinking.

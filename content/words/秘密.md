## Why this combination
秘 carries its "mysterious, hidden" sense (as in 神秘), and 密 here takes
its "concealed, close/dense" sense (rather than its more common "dense,
tight" sense in 密切, 密集) — something packed so tightly closed that it
cannot be seen into. Two words for hiddenness reinforce each other.

Rated "semi" for 密's "concealed" sense specifically, which is a
figurative extension of its more physical "dense/tight" meaning.

## Notes
秘密 works as both noun (一个秘密, "a secret") and adverb (秘密进行, "to
carry out secretly") — the same dual role many two-syllable descriptive
words play in Mandarin.

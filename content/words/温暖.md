## Why this combination
温 "warm, mild [in temperature]" (as in 温度 "temperature") and 暖
"warm" (as in 暖和 "warm and comfortable") are close synonyms, paired
into a fuller, often figurative word for warmth.

## Notes
温暖 covers both literal warmth (温暖的天气 "warm weather") and
figurative emotional warmth (温暖的家 "a warm [loving] home," 心里很
温暖 "feeling warm inside") — 暖和, its close relative, is used more for
everyday physical comfort in temperature alone.

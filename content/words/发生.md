## Why this combination
生 here keeps its core sense "to arise, to be born" cleanly. 发 is the
less obvious half: not its common "to send out (mail, a signal)" sense,
but an extended "to emit, to bring forth" — something coming into
being and making itself apparent. Together, "coming forth into being":
to happen.

## Notes
发's phonetic-adjacent sense here (things "coming forth") also underlies
发现 "to discover" and 发展 "to develop" — a small family of 发-verbs
about things emerging into view or existence.

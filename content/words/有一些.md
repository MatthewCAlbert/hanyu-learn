## Why this combination
有 "there are" + [[一些]] "some, a bit" — the fuller form of [[有些]]. As a
quantifier "there are some (that…)," and before an adjective a hedged
"somewhat": 有一些累 "a bit tired."

## Notes
有一些 / 有些 / 有点儿 shade into each other; 有一些 is the most spelt-out and
least idiomatic.

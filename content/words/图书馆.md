## Why this combination
图书 "books" (图 pictures + 书 writings, together the general word for
published matter) + 馆, the suffix for a public building of a certain function:
博物馆 museum, 餐馆 eatery, 体育馆 gymnasium. A "books building."

## Notes
Parse it 图书 + 馆, not 图 + 书馆. 去图书馆看书 "go to the library to read."

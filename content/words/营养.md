## Why this combination
营, here "to seek, to manage, to build up" (see also 经营) combined with
养 "to nourish, to raise, to feed" (as in 养成 "to develop [a habit]"):
to manage nourishment — nutrition.

## Notes
营养 names the nourishing content of food and the science of nutrition
(营养丰富 "rich in nutrition," 营养师 "dietitian") — 养, alone, is
productive across many compounds about raising and caring for living
things (养育 "to raise/nurture," 养家 "to support a family").

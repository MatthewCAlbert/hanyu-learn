## Why this combination
采, here in its "to select, to choose" sense (see also 采取) combined
with 用 "to use": to select and put to use — to adopt.

## Notes
采用 takes a method, technology or proposal as its object (采用新技术
"to adopt new technology," 采用建议 "to adopt a suggestion") — 采取, its
close relative, collocates instead with a course of action (采取措施
"to take measures"), a fine but consistent distinction between the two.

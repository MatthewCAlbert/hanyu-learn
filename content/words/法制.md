## Why this combination
法 "law" plus 制 "system" gives exactly "the system of law" — the
standard word for the institutional framework of laws.

## Notes
法制 anchors 法制建设 "legal system development," 法制观念 "awareness of
the rule of law" — distinct from the near-homophone 法治 "governance by
law (as a principle/ideal)," a common confusable pair in formal writing.

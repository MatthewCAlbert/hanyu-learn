## Why this combination
修 "to cultivate, to refine" and 养 "to nourish, to develop" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for the ongoing cultivation
of one's character and ability.

## Notes
修养 anchors 文化修养 "cultural refinement," 有修养 "well-cultivated,
mannerly" — always about accumulated inner development through
deliberate effort, spanning morality, knowledge, and taste.

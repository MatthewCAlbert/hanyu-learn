## Why this combination
The middle rung of the 大 / 中 / 小 school series (see [[小学]], [[大学]]) —
between primary school and university. Splits into 初中 (junior) and 高中
(senior).

## Notes
中学生 "secondary student." A separate 19th-century sense "Chinese learning"
(中学为体，西学为用) is unrelated.

## Why this combination
大, here in its "roughly, generally" sense (see also 大概) combined with
约 "a bond, a rough estimate, to make an appointment" (as in 大约's own
约 surviving independently in 预约 "to book an appointment"): a rough
estimate — approximately.

## Notes
大约 precedes a number or quantity to mark it as an estimate (大约十个人
"approximately ten people") — it functions almost identically to 大概
in this use, though 大概 additionally covers the broader "probably"
sense for whole statements more readily.

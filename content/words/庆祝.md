## Why this combination
庆 "to celebrate, to congratulate" (as in 国庆 "National Day," literally
"national celebration") combined with 祝 "to wish, to pray for
[blessing]" (as in 祝贺 "to congratulate"): to celebrate with good
wishes — to celebrate.

## Notes
庆祝 takes an occasion as its object (庆祝生日 "to celebrate a birthday,"
庆祝成功 "to celebrate a success") — 祝, alone, is used instead for
offering a wish directly to someone (祝你生日快乐 "wishing you a happy
birthday").

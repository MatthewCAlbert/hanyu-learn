## Why this combination
原 "original" plus 有 "to have" gives exactly "originally had" — a
modifier for something that existed before a change took place.

## Notes
原有 always modifies a following noun: 原有的计划 "the original plan,"
恢复原有面貌 "to restore the original appearance" — always implies a
contrast with a new or altered state.

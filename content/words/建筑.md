## Why this combination
建 and 筑 are near-synonyms, both meaning "to build, to construct" —
paired together for the formal modern word covering both the act of
building and its result.

## Notes
建筑 works as verb ("to construct") and noun ("a building," 古建筑
"ancient architecture") — 建筑师 "architect" and 建筑物 "a building
[structure]" are the everyday derived compounds.

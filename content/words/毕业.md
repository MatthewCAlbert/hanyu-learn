## Why this combination
毕 ("to finish, to complete," surviving bound in 毕竟) takes 业 ("a course
of study, an occupation," as in 专业, 作业, 职业) as its object: finishing
one's course of study is graduating. As a verb-object compound it can
split: 毕不了业 ("cannot graduate").

Rated "semi" for 业's specific "course of study" sense here, one of
several related senses (occupation, undertaking) the character carries.

## Notes
毕业 cannot take a further object the way many English verbs can ("to
graduate school" is not idiomatic) — say 从…毕业 or 大学毕业, naming the
institution with 从 or as an attributive modifier instead.

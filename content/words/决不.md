## Why this combination
决 "decidedly, resolutely" intensifying 不 "not" gives a firm, absolute
negation — as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
决不 is a strong, resolute denial: 决不放弃 "will never give up," 决不
妥协 "absolutely no compromise" — stronger and more formal than plain 不.

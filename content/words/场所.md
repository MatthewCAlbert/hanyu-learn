## Why this combination
场 "a site, an open ground, a venue" (as in 市场, 广场) combined with 所
"a place" (as in 厕所 "toilet," literally "a place [for that
purpose]"): a venue-place — a location for an activity.

## Notes
场所 names a place defined by its function or purpose (公共场所 "public
place/venue," 娱乐场所 "entertainment venue") — 地方, the general word for
"place," carries no such implication of a designated function.

## Why this combination
采 "to pick, to gather, to select" (as in 采访 "to interview," literally
"to gather and visit") combined with 取 "to take, to fetch, to obtain":
to select and take up — to adopt a course of action.

## Notes
采取 collocates almost exclusively with abstract nouns naming a course
of action — 采取措施 "to take measures," 采取行动 "to take action" — it is
not used for physically picking up an object, where 拿 or 取 alone would
apply instead.

## Why this combination
勤 "diligent, industrious" and 奋 "to exert oneself, to strive" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for sustained hard work.

## Notes
勤奋 anchors 勤奋学习 "to study diligently," 勤奋好学 "hardworking and
eager to learn" — always praises sustained effort, close to 努力 but
describing a character trait rather than a single effort.

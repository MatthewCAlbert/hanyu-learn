## Why this combination

轮 ("a wheel," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 轮椅) plus the
nominal suffix 子 names the everyday round rolling object: a wheel.

## Notes

车轮子 ("a car/vehicle wheel") and 轮子转动 ("the wheel turns," using
转动, covered elsewhere in this app) both build on this word — the
casual spoken form, where bare 轮 mostly stays bound in compounds like
轮椅 and 车轮.

## Why this combination
相 "mutually, [towards] each other" (see also 相当) combined with 关 "to
concern, to be related to, to be connected" (as in 关注, 关系): related
to each other — connected, relevant.

## Notes
相关 modifies a noun to mark it as bearing on the topic at hand (相关
信息 "relevant information," 与...相关 "related to...") and, in
statistics, translates "correlated" (相关性 "correlation") — 关系密切,
a fuller phrase, expresses the same idea of connection more descriptively.

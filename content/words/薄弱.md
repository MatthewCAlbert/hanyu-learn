## Why this combination
薄 "thin" and 弱 "weak" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for a weak point lacking substance or strength.

## Notes
薄弱 anchors 意志薄弱 "weak-willed," 基础薄弱 "a weak foundation" — always
abstract weakness (in resolve, structure, or capability), not physical
thinness of an object.

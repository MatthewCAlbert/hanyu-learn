## Why this combination

未 ("not yet, not," as in 未来, "the future [lit. not-yet-come]") plus
必 ("certainly, necessarily," as in 必须) names something not certainly
so: not necessarily true.

## Notes

未必是好事 ("may not necessarily be a good thing") is a common hedging
frame — 未必 softens a claim without flatly denying it, a more measured
alternative to plain 不.

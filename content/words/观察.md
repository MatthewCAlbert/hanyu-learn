## Why this combination
观 "to watch, to view" (see also 观众) combined with 察 "to inspect, to
examine closely" (see also 警察): to watch closely and examine — to
observe, in a deliberate, attentive sense.

## Notes
观察 implies sustained, careful watching for a purpose — scientific,
investigative, or evaluative (观察病人 "to observe/monitor a patient,"
观察力 "power of observation") — 看, the plain verb for "to look/watch,"
carries no such deliberateness.

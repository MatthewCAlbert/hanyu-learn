## Why this combination
灾 "disaster" plus 区 "area, district" gives exactly "a disaster-hit
area" — a straightforward compositional word.

## Notes
灾区 anchors 支援灾区 "to support disaster-hit areas," 灾区重建
"disaster-area reconstruction" — always the geographic zone affected by a
calamity, whether natural or otherwise.

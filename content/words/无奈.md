## Why this combination
无 "without" plus 奈 (here "a means to cope," as in 无可奈何 "nothing can
be done about it") gives exactly "without any way to deal with it" — the
standard word for feeling stuck with no options.

## Notes
无奈 works as adjective ("很无奈" "feeling helpless"), and, at the start
of a clause, as a conjunction meaning "but unfortunately": 想去，无奈没时间
"wanted to go, but sadly had no time."

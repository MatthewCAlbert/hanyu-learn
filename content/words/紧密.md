## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 紧 ("tight," as in 紧张) and 密 ("dense, close
together," as in 秘密/密切) both independently name a lack of slack or
gap. Pairing them gives the standard word for a tight, close connection.

## Notes

紧密联系 ("close connection/ties") and 紧密合作 ("close cooperation") both
build on this word — overlapping heavily with 密切 (covered elsewhere in
this app), 紧密 leans slightly more toward physical or structural
tightness alongside the figurative sense.

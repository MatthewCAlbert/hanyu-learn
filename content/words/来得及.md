## Why this combination

来 ("to come") plus the potential-complement marker 得 and 及 ("to reach,
to catch up to," as in 及时) fuses into a set phrase: able to arrive in
time.

Rated "semi": the potential-complement grammar (verb + 得 + result,
"able to come-and-catch-up") is transparent to anyone who knows that
pattern, but not obvious without it.

## Notes

来得及 is the positive counterpart of 来不及 (covered elsewhere in this
app), formed by swapping 得 for 不 in the same potential-complement
slot — "there's still time" versus "too late."

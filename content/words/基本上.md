## Why this combination
基本 "basic, fundamental" (see its own entry) combined with the locative
suffix 上 "in regard to, on [that footing]" (see also 表面上, 事实上):
grounded on the basics — basically, on the whole.

## Notes
基本上 hedges a claim as approximately, not exactly, true (基本上完成了
"basically finished," 基本上同意 "mostly agree") — it functions the same
way as bare 基本 used adverbially, with 上 adding a slightly more
formal, qualifying tone.

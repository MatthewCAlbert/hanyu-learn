## Why this combination

多 ("many") plus 种 ("a kind, a type," as in 种类, "category [lit. kind +
category]") names many different kinds: various.

## Notes

多种原因 ("various reasons") and 多种方式 ("multiple methods") both build
on this word — 多种 always precedes a noun to mark its variety, unlike
plain 多, which can also mean simply "a lot" in quantity.

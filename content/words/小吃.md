## Why this combination

小 ("small") plus 吃 ("to eat") names a small, casual thing to eat: a
snack.

## Notes

小吃街 ("a street-food street") and 地方小吃 ("local specialty snacks")
both build on this word — 小吃 names a whole category of casual light
food, distinct from a full meal like 午饭/晚饭.

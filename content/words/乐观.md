## Why this combination
乐, here read lè "happy, joyful" (as in 快乐) combined with 观 "view,
outlook, perspective" (see also 观察, 观众): a happy outlook — optimism.

## Notes
乐观 describes a hopeful attitude towards the future (对未来乐观
"optimistic about the future," 乐观的态度 "an optimistic attitude") —
its opposite is 悲观 "pessimistic," built the same way with 悲 "sad" in
place of 乐.

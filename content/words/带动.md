## Why this combination
带 "to bring, to lead along" (see also 带领) combined with 动 "to move"
(see also 推动, 动力): to lead into motion — to drive, to spur forward.

## Notes
带动 describes one thing pulling another along with it, often
figuratively (旅游带动经济发展 "tourism drives economic development") —
带领, its close relative, is used for leading people specifically, while
带动 more often describes one process or sector driving another.

## Why this combination
老 here functions as a familiar, affectionate prefix rather than
literally meaning "old" — the same role it plays in 老公 (husband), 老师
(teacher), 老板 (boss). 婆 independently means "old woman, grandmother" (as
in 婆婆, mother-in-law); paired with the affectionate 老 prefix, it
colloquially narrows to "wife."

Rated "semi" because 婆's shift from "old woman" to "wife" only makes
sense once the 老-prefix pattern is known — nothing in 婆 alone suggests
"spouse."

## Notes
老婆 is casual/spoken; the neutral, more formal register uses 妻子
instead — choose based on how informal the conversation is.

## Why this combination
会, here "an association, a society" (as in 协会 "association") combined
with 员 "a member" (see also 成员, 队员): a member of an association — a
member.

## Notes
会员 names a formal member of a club, gym or subscription service (会员
卡 "membership card," 会员制 "membership system") — 成员, its broader
relative, applies to membership in any group, whereas 会员 specifically
implies a formal, often paid membership.

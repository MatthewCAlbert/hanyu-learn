## Why this combination
前 "front / before, earlier in time" (see [[前天]], 以前) + 年 "year" → one year
further back than 去年 "last year." Extends the year row backward, as [[前天]]
does the day row.

## Notes
The year row: 前年 · 去年 · 今年 · 明年 · 后年. Note 去年 (not *昨年) for "last
year" — the row is not fully regular.

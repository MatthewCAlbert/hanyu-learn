## Why this combination
作 "to make, to create, to write" (see also 作品, 作家) combined with 者
"one who [performs the action]" (as in 记者 "journalist," literally "one
who records"): one who creates [a written work] — the author.

## Notes
作者 names the author of a specific piece of writing (这篇文章的作者
"the author of this article") — 作家, its close relative, names a
professional writer as an occupation, regardless of any one particular
work.

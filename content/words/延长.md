## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 延 ("to extend, to delay," as in 延误, "to
delay") and 长 ("long") both independently name stretching something
out. Pairing them gives the standard word for extending in time or
space.

## Notes

延长时间 ("to extend the time") and 延长线 ("an extension cord") both
build on this word — 延长 works for duration and physical length alike.

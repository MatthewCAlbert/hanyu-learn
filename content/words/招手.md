## Why this combination
招 "to beckon, to summon" acting on the object 手 "hand" gives exactly
"to beckon with one's hand" — splittable, as in 招了招手.

## Notes
招手 anchors 招手示意 "to signal by waving," 挥手招手 (loosely overlapping
with 挥手) — the standard gesture for calling someone over or hailing a
vehicle.

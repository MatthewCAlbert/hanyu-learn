## Why this combination

豆 ("bean") plus 腐 (here "curdled, coagulated," a specific culinary
sense, distinct from 腐's more common "to rot, decay") names bean liquid
curdled into a solid: tofu.

Rated "semi" for 腐's specific "curdled" culinary sense, which reads
strangely if taken as its everyday "rotten" meaning without the
specialized food context.

## Notes

豆腐 is one of the oldest and most productive Chinese food words —
豆浆 ("soy milk," lit. "bean thick-liquid") and 豆芽 ("bean sprouts")
share its 豆 "bean" root.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 急 ("urgent, anxious," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 着急) and 忙 ("busy," as in 帮忙, "to help [lit. help
+ busy]") both independently name pressed, rushed activity. Pairing
them gives the standard adverb for doing something hurriedly.

## Notes

急忙跑过去 ("hurried over") and 急忙解释 ("hurriedly explained") both build
on this word — always modifies an action rushed due to urgency, close
to but more urgent-toned than plain 匆忙.

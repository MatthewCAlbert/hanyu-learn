## Why this combination
从 "from" combined with 来 "to come, [up] until now" (the same logic seen
in 以来): from the very start, up to now — an adverb asserting that
something has held true across the entire span of time in view.

## Notes
从来 is overwhelmingly used with a negative that follows it (从来不 "never
[has/does]," 从来没 "have never") — without a negative, it asserts a
constant, unbroken truth rather than a mere habit, closer to "invariably"
than to 总是 "always, usually."

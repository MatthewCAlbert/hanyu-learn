## Why this combination
无 ("to lack, to be without") takes 法 ("method, way") as its object: to
lack any method for doing something is, plainly, to be unable to do it.
Straightforward once 无 and 法 are each known on their own — no hidden step.

无法 is always followed directly by another verb (无法接受 "unable to
accept," 无法理解 "unable to understand") — it functions as an adverbial
modal, not a standalone predicate.

## Notes
Compare 没法 (colloquial equivalent) and 无法无天 (idiom, "lawless," lit. "no
method, no heaven") — the same 无+法 core recurs in a much more vivid fixed
expression.

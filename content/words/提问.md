## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 提 "to raise, to bring up [in speech]" (see also
提前, 提高) takes 问 "question" (as in 问题) as its object — literally
"to raise a question" — to ask a question, often formally.

## Notes
提问 names posing a question in a formal or instructional setting
(老师提问 "the teacher asks a question," 举手提问 "to raise a hand to ask
a question") — 问, the plain everyday verb "to ask," is used far more
generally in ordinary conversation.

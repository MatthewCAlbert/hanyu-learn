## Why this combination
过 "to pass" + 去 "to go (away)." As a directional verb, "to go over / across"
(走过去). As a noun of time, "time that has gone away" = the past
(过去的事). The 去 marks motion away — from now, from here.

## Notes
Time triad: 过去 · 现在 · 未来 (past · present · future). As a complement,
过去 = away from the speaker (拿过去), against [[过来]].

## Why this combination
大 "big, greater" + [[部分]] "part" → the greater part of a whole:
大部分人 "most people," 大部分时间. Parse 大 + 部分.

## Notes
Pairs with 一部分 "some / a portion" and 小部分 "a small part." For counting
people specifically, [[大多数]] "the great majority."

## Why this combination
"An opened heart" — 开 "to open" + 心 "heart." An unclenched, expansive heart is
a cheerful one, the same image as English "open-hearted" or "light-hearted."
玩得很开心 "had a great time."

## Notes
拿…开心 "make fun of someone" is a separate, older sense (to open oneself up at
another's expense). Near-synonym [[高兴]]; 开心 is warmer and more colloquial.

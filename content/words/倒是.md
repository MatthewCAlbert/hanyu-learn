## Why this combination
倒 here carries its shifted adverbial sense "on the contrary, actually"
(from "to reverse, to fall over," the idea of turning expectations upside
down) rather than any literal falling — combined with 是 "to be," it
marks a statement that runs counter to what was expected.

## Notes
倒是 flags a surprising or contrasting truth: 他倒是很聪明 "he's actually
quite clever [contrary to what you might think]" — a common
conversational hedge that softens a mild correction or contrast.

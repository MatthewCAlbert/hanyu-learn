## Why this combination
奖 "a prize, to reward" and 励 "to encourage, to spur on" (the same 励 as
in 鼓励 and 激励) reinforce each other into the standard word for a reward
given specifically to encourage further good behaviour.

## Notes
奖励 emphasizes the encouraging function of a reward — 奖励优秀员工 "to
reward outstanding employees [to motivate them]" — distinct from 礼物
"gift," which carries no such incentive purpose.

## Why this combination
生 "life, to arise, to live" (see also 生命, 生长) combined with 动 "to
move" (see also 动人, 自动): moving with life — lively, vivid.

## Notes
生动 describes description, teaching or performance that feels alive
and vivid rather than dull (生动的描写 "vivid description," 生动有趣
"lively and interesting") — its opposite is 死板 "rigid, wooden,
lifeless."

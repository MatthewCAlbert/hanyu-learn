## Why this combination

五 ("five") and 六 ("six") pair with 颜 ("color, face," as in 颜色) and
色 ("color," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 色彩) in a fixed
four-character idiom: using two small numbers loosely to mean "many
different" colors, rather than an exact count.

Rated "semi" for the numbers' non-literal "many/various" role, the same
pattern Chinese idioms often use small round numbers to suggest
abundance rather than precision.

## Notes

五颜六色的气球 ("multicolored balloons") is a common everyday image —
the idiom always describes a lively mix of many colors together, not a
literal eleven distinct hues.

## Why this combination
科 "a branch, a division, a subject" (学科, 科目) + 学 "learning, study." "The
branched disciplines" — knowledge organised into fields. A late-19th-century
term (borrowed via Japanese 科学) for Western science. Also an adjective:
不科学 "unscientific / irrational."

## Notes
科 "branch / department" also in [[科学家]], 内科 "internal medicine,"
文科 / 理科 "arts / sciences stream."

## Why this combination
危 "danger, peril, perilous" (see also 危险) combined with 害 "harm, to
harm" (as in 害怕, 厉害 in unrelated compounds): dangerous harm — to
jeopardise, damage.

## Notes
危害 names harm done to health, society or the environment (危害健康
"to harm health," 危害社会 "to endanger society") — it works as both
verb and noun, and is stronger and more formal than plain 害 "to harm"
alone.

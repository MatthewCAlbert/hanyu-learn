## Why this combination
改 "to change, to correct" (see also 改造, 修改) combined with 进 "to
advance" (see also 进步, 前进): to change for the better — to improve.

## Notes
改进 takes a method or product as its object, implying refinement of
something already functional (改进工作方法 "to improve working methods,"
改进产品 "to improve a product") — 提高, a near-synonym, is used more
broadly for raising a level or standard, not only refining a method.

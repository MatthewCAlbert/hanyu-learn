## Why this combination
高 "high" plus 温 "temperature" gives exactly "high temperature" — a
straightforward descriptive compound, opposite of 低温 "low temperature."

## Notes
高温 anchors 高温天气 "hot weather," 高温消毒 "high-temperature
sterilization" — used both for weather and technical/industrial heat
levels.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 考 "to examine, to test" + 试 "to test, to try." Both verb
(明天考试) and noun (期末考试). 考 also stands alone: 考大学 "sit the university
entrance exam."

## Notes
Splittable in the verb use: 考了三次试. The exam-heavy culture gives 高考
(the college entrance exam) its own household status.

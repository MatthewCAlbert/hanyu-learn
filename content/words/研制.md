## Why this combination

研 ("to research," as in 研究) plus 制 ("to make," as in 制作) names
researching in order to make something new: to develop.

## Notes

研制新药 ("to develop a new drug") and 研制成功 ("successfully developed")
both build on this word — 研制 always implies a research-driven
development process, distinct from plain 制造, "to manufacture," which
can describe routine production of an already-designed product.

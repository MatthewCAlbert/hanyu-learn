## Why this combination
敏 "sharp, quick" (see 敏's own etymology — 疾也, "swift") and 感 "to
feel, to sense" combine directly: quick to feel/react.

## Notes
敏感 spans physical sensitivity (皮肤敏感 "sensitive skin"), emotional
touchiness (敏感的话题 "a sensitive topic"), and political caution
(敏感信息 "sensitive information") — quickness to react extended across
very different domains.

## Why this combination
出 "to emerge, to come out" acting on the object 汗 "sweat" gives exactly
"sweat comes out" — splittable, as in 出了一身汗.

## Notes
出汗 anchors 出汗多 "sweats a lot," 紧张得出汗 "sweating from nervousness"
— the standard word for perspiring, whether from heat, exertion, or
anxiety.

## Why this combination
活 "alive, lively" + 动 "to move" → living movement. As a noun, an organised
event or activity (课外活动, 促销活动); as a verb, to move around / get exercise
(出去活动活动); as an adjective, "loose, not fixed" (这颗牙活动了).

## Notes
活 "alive / movable / flexible" also in [[生活]], 灵活. The adjective sense
("loose") is easy to miss.

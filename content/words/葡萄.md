## Why this combination
葡萄 is a disyllabic loanword — traditionally traced to an Iranian source
(compare Persian "budāwa") entering Chinese via Central Asia in antiquity.
Neither 葡 nor 萄 occurs as a free, meaningful morpheme outside this pair;
the two characters were chosen purely to spell the borrowed sound.

## Notes
葡萄 is the everyday word for "grape" and the base of 葡萄酒 "wine" and
葡萄干 "raisin" — like 玻璃 "glass" and 骆驼 "camel," it is a case where
asking what each character "means" on its own is the wrong question.

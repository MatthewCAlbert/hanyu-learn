## Why this combination
有 "there is / to have" + 点儿 "a dot, a small amount" (see [[一点儿]]) → "there
is a little (of)." As an adverb before an adjective or verb: 有点儿累,
有点儿不高兴. It usually flags something mildly negative.

## Notes
有点儿 + adjective (a hedge: 有点儿贵 "a bit pricey") vs. adjective + 一点儿
(a comparison: 便宜一点儿 "a bit cheaper"). Non-erhua 有点 is the same word.

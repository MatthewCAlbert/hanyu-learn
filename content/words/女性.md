## Why this combination
女 "female" and 性 "sex, gender, nature" combine directly into "the female
sex/gender" — a formal, clinical/sociological register term.

## Notes
女性 is the formal register for gender categorisation (女性权益 "women's
rights," 女性主义 "feminism"), where 女人/女生 are the everyday words for
an individual woman or girl.

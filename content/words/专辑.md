## Why this combination
专 "specialized, dedicated [to one thing]" and 辑 "a compiled collection"
(see 辑's own etymology — a cart's parts working smoothly together,
extended to compiling/editing) combine directly: a collection dedicated
to one theme or artist.

## Notes
专辑 anchors music (音乐专辑 "music album," 首张专辑 "debut album") and, more
broadly, any themed compilation (专题专辑 in publishing) — the "dedicated
to one subject" sense carries across media.

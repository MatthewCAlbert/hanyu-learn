## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 扩 ("to expand," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 扩大) and 展 ("to unfold, to extend," as in 发展, "development
[lit. sprout + unfold]") both independently name growing outward.
Pairing them gives the standard word for expansion.

## Notes

扩展业务 ("to expand business") and 扩展知识面 ("to broaden one's
knowledge") both build on this word — close to 扩大 (covered elsewhere
in this app) but leaning slightly more toward extending reach or scope
than sheer size.

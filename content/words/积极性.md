## Why this combination
积极 "positive, active, enthusiastic" combined with the nominal suffix
性 "-ness, -ity" (as in 可能性 "possibility"), turning the adjective into
an abstract noun: the quality of being active and engaged — enthusiasm,
initiative.

## Notes
积极性 names the abstract quality or drive behind enthusiastic
participation (调动积极性 "to mobilize enthusiasm/initiative," often said
of workers or students) — 性 as a noun-forming suffix is highly
productive, turning many adjectives into abstract nouns.

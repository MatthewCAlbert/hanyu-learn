## Why this combination

大 ("big, eldest") plus 姐 ("older sister," as in 姐姐) names the eldest
sister among siblings, extended as a polite address for a woman
somewhat older than the speaker.

## Notes

大姐 as a form of address (to a stranger or colleague) parallels 大哥
(covered elsewhere in this app) — both use a kinship term generously,
regardless of actual blood relation.

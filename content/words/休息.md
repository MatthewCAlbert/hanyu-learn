## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 休 "to stop, to rest" (休假 take leave) + 息 "to rest, to cease"
(also "breath" — 息 is what you catch when you stop). Doubled for the everyday
verb "to rest."

## Notes
息 toneless here. 休息一下 "take a short break." 午休 "midday rest."

## Why this combination

阳 ("sun, the sunny side, yang," as in 太阳) plus 台 ("a platform," as in
台上, "on stage," covered elsewhere in this app) names a sun-facing
platform attached to a building: a balcony.

Rated "semi" for 阳's figurative "sun-facing" contribution here, a
descriptive extension rather than a literal "sun platform."

## Notes

晒太阳 ("to sunbathe, lit. bask in the sun") is the classic activity
associated with a balcony's 阳 "sun" root — 阳台 shares its 台 "platform"
root with unrelated words like 舞台 ("stage") and 讲台 ("lectern").

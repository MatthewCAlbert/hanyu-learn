## Why this combination
再 "again" plus 次 (a classifier/noun for "an occurrence, a time")
literally gives "another occurrence" — a formal, written-register
alternative to plain 再 or 又.

## Notes
再次 favours formal writing and announcements (再次强调 "to emphasize once
again," 再次感谢 "thanks once again") over casual speech, where 再 alone
or 又 is more natural.

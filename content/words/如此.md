## Why this combination
如 "to be like" plus 此 "this" gives exactly "like this" — a formal,
written-register stand-in for the everyday 这样.

## Notes
如此 often opens a clause summarizing what was just said (如此看来 "seen
this way..."), and pairs with 既然 in the classical-flavoured 既然如此
"since that's the case."

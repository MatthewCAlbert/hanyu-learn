## Why this combination
考 "to test, to sit an exam" (see [[考试]]) + 生, here "a person, a candidate"
(the same 生 as in [[学生]], [[医生]], [[先生]]) → someone sitting an
examination: 高考考生 "gaokao candidates," 参加考试的考生.

## Notes
生 as "-person / candidate" also in 新生 "new student," 毕业生 "graduate,"
[[留学生]], 考生.

## Why this combination
学 "to study" (as in 学习, 学校) combined with 费 "a fee, an expense"
(see also 费用, 收费): the fee for studying — tuition.

## Notes
学费 names the fee paid for schooling (交学费 "to pay tuition," 学费
贵 "expensive tuition") — 费用, the general word for "expense," is used
for any cost, not specifically educational fees.

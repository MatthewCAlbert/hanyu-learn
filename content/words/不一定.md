## Why this combination
不 "not" + [[一定]] "certainly, definitely" → "not definitely so" — a hedge,
leaving the matter open: 他不一定来 "he might not come" (not "he definitely
won't"). 不一定 as a full answer = "not necessarily / we'll see."

## Notes
Weaker than 一定不 "definitely not." 说不定 is the close synonym meaning
"maybe / who knows."

## Why this combination

高 ("high") plus 价 ("price," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
价格/特价) names a high price.

## Notes

高价出售 ("to sell at a high price") and 高价商品 ("high-priced goods")
both build on this word — 低价 ("low price") is its direct antonym,
built the same way with 低 "low" instead of 高.

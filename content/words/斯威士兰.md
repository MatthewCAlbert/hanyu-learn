## Why this combination
The established Mandarin name for eSwatini, still based on Swaziland (斯威士蘭).
斯, 威, 士 and 兰 are chosen for sound. 士 is not "scholar"; 兰 is not the orchid.

## Notes
兰 as in [[荷兰]]. English has shifted to eSwatini; Chinese usage still
overwhelmingly uses 斯威士兰.

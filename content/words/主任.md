## Why this combination
主 "main, principal, presiding" (as in 主人 "host, master") combined with
任 "duty, a post held" (see also 责任, 信任): the one holding the
principal duty — the director or head of an office.

## Notes
主任 names the head of a department, office or class-level administrative
unit (班主任 "head teacher of a class," 部门主任 "department director") —
经理, its near-neighbour, is specifically a business manager rather than
an administrative office head.

## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" plus 主 "owner, master" gives exactly "the owner of a
vehicle" — the standard word for identifying who a car belongs to.

## Notes
车主 anchors 车主信息 "vehicle owner information," 联系车主 "to contact
the car's owner" — a common administrative/legal term for vehicle
registration and parking contexts.

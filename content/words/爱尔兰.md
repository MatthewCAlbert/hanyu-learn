## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Ireland (愛爾蘭). 爱, 尔 and 兰 are chosen
for sound. 爱 is not "love."

## Notes
兰 as in [[荷兰]]. Same stem in [[爱尔兰语]].

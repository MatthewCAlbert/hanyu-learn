## Why this combination
结 "to tie a knot" and 束 "to bind into a bundle" are both, literally,
about fastening something shut. The metaphor moves from "tying off" a
physical thing to "wrapping up" an event or activity — marked semi
because that figurative leap, while natural, is not stated outright by
either character alone.

## Notes
结束 is the general "to end" for events, activities and periods of time
(会议结束了 "the meeting ended") — distinct from 终于 "finally," which
marks the arrival of an end point rather than naming the ending itself.

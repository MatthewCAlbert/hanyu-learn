## Why this combination

青 ("green/blue, and by extension youthful," as in 青年, "youth [person]")
plus 春 ("spring," the season of new growth) names youth as life's
springtime: the fresh, green season of a person's years.

Rated "semi" for the seasonal metaphor, transparent once seen but not a
literal description of a life-stage.

## Notes

青春 names the abstract stretch of youthful years (青春岁月, "the years
of one's youth"), distinct from 年轻, the adjective for "being young."

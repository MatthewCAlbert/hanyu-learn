## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 限 ("a limit, a boundary," covered elsewhere in
this app) and 制 ("to control, to regulate," as in 制度, 控制) both
independently name a bound placed on something. Pairing them gives the
standard word for restriction.

## Notes
限制 works as both verb (限制人数, "to limit the number of people") and
noun (没有限制, "no restrictions") without changing form.

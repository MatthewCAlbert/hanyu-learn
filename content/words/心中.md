## Why this combination
心 "heart, mind" + 中 "within, in the middle." Where feelings and thoughts are
held: 记在心中, 心中有数 "have a clear idea." Chinese seats both emotion and
cognition in the 心.

## Notes
心中 (a touch literary) ≈ [[心里]] (everyday). 中 as a bare locative "in" also
in [[其中]], 空中, 家中.

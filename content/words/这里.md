## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + 里 "inside, in" → "in this (place)" → here. 那里 =
there, 哪里 = where. 里 has bleached from "inside" to a bare place-marker.

## Notes
Northern speech prefers the erhua forms 这儿 / 那儿 / 哪儿 for the same meanings;
这里 is the neutral written and southern form.

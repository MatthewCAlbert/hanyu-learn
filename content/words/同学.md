## Why this combination
同 "same, together" + 学 "study" → people who study together. 同 is the same
"fellow-" prefix as in 同事 (colleague), 同乡 (fellow townsman), 同事.

## Notes
Also a teacher's way of addressing a pupil (这位同学) or a whole class
(同学们). 老同学 "old schoolmate."

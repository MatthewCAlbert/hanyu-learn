## Why this combination
一 "one, a" + [[部分]] "part" → a portion of a whole, often in a paired
contrast: 一部分人同意，一部分人反对. Parse 一 + 部分.

## Notes
一部分 (a portion) vs. [[大部分]] (most) vs. [[全部]] (all). Often with 的:
一部分学生.

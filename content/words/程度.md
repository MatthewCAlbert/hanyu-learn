## Why this combination
程 "a stage, a measured course" (see also 过程) combined with 度 "a
measure, a degree" (as in 速度, 温度): the stage or measure something has
reached — its degree or level.

## Notes
程度 measures how far along a scale something sits — ability, severity,
education (汉语水平达到什么程度 "what level has your Chinese reached,"
严重程度 "degree of severity") — 水平, a near-synonym, is the more
specific word for a level of skill or standard.

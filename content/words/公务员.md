## Why this combination
公务 "public affairs, official duty" (公 "public," see also 公共, 公开,
plus 务 "affairs, duty," see also 服务) combined with 员 "a member of
staff" (see also 成员, 队员): a staff member handling public affairs —
a civil servant.

## Notes
公务员 names someone employed in government administration (考公务员
"to take the civil service exam") — 员工, its close relative, is the
general word for staff/employees in any organisation, not specifically
government service.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 宁 ("peaceful, tranquil," as in 安宁) and 静
("quiet, still," as in 安静) both independently name calm stillness.
Pairing them gives the formal, often literary word for tranquility.

## Notes

宁静的夜晚 ("a tranquil night") and 内心宁静 ("inner peace") both build on
this word — leans written/poetic register, distinct from the more
everyday 安静, "quiet" (as in "be quiet!").

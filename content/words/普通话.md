## Why this combination
[[普通]] "common, general, shared by all" + 话 "speech, spoken language"
(北京话, 上海话) → "the common speech" — the standard spoken form promoted for
use across all of China's dialect areas. Based on Beijing pronunciation and
northern grammar. 说普通话.

## Notes
普通话 = the standard *spoken* form; [[汉语]] = the Han language broadly;
[[中文]] = Chinese (esp. written); 国语 (Taiwan) / 华语 (Singapore, Malaysia)
name the same standard.

## Why this combination
干 "dry" and 脆 "crisp" (see 脆's own etymology) combine metaphorically:
something dry and crisp snaps cleanly, with no lingering fibres — the
same clean, decisive quality applied to speech and action that gets
straight to the point.

## Notes
干脆 describes decisive manner (说话很干脆 "speaks very directly") and,
as an adverb, "simply, just" (干脆别去了 "let's just not go") — a common
everyday word for cutting through hesitation.

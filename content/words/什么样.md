## Why this combination
什么 "what" (see the corpus entry) + 样 "kind, manner" (see [[一样]], [[样子]]) →
"of what kind." 他是什么样的人？ "what sort of person is he?", 什么样的都行.

## Notes
什么样 asks about type / quality; [[怎么样]] asks about state or opinion; 哪样 /
哪种 asks you to pick from options.

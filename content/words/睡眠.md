## Why this combination
睡 "to sleep" and 眠 "to sleep" (see 眠's own etymology) are near-synonyms
combined into the formal, clinical noun for sleep.

## Notes
睡眠 is the formal/technical register (睡眠质量 "sleep quality," 睡眠不足
"sleep deprivation," 电脑的睡眠模式 "computer sleep mode") — everyday speech
about the act of sleeping uses 睡觉 instead.

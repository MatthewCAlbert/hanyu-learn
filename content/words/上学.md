## Why this combination
学 nominalised to "schooling"; 上 the "go on / attend" verb (as in [[上班]],
[[上课]]). Both the daily trip (该上学了) and the life stage (七岁上学 "started
school at seven"). Splits: 上过学 "has had schooling."

## Notes
The 上 / 下 pair: 上学 "go to school," but "finish school for the day" is 放学,
not *下学.

## Why this combination
显 "to make visible, to make evident" (see also 显示) combined with the
classical manner-suffix 然 "so, in that way" (as in 自然, 仍然): being so
in a way that is plainly evident — obviously, clearly.

## Notes
显然 opens a clause to mark what is plain from the evidence (显然，他没有
准备 "clearly, he hadn't prepared") — it comments on the speaker's
certainty about a fact, unlike 明显, its close synonym, which more often
modifies a noun or describes a trait directly (很明显的错误 "an obvious
mistake").

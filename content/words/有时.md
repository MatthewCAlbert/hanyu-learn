## Why this combination
有 "there are" + 时 "times, occasions" → "there are occasions when…" = sometimes.
Often doubled for contrast: 有时…有时… "sometimes X, sometimes Y."

## Notes
有时候 is the fuller colloquial form (see the corpus entry). 偶尔 "occasionally"
is rarer than 有时.

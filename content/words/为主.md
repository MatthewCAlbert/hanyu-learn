## Why this combination
为 "to be, to act as" plus 主 "main, primary" gives exactly "to be the
main (one)" — the standard word for identifying the dominant element
among several.

## Notes
为主 always follows the thing being emphasized: 以学习为主 "with study as
the priority," 农业为主 "agriculture-dominated" — a common pattern
pairing 以…为主 to state a governing priority.

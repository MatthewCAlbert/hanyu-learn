## Why this combination
重 "heavy, weighty, of consequence" (as in 重要 "important") combined
with 大 "big, great": heavy and great [in consequence] — significant,
major.

## Notes
重大 modifies events, decisions and discoveries of serious consequence
(重大事件 "a major event," 重大发现 "a major discovery") — it is stronger
than plain 重要 "important" and reserved for matters of real weight, not
everyday importance.

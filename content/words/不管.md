## Why this combination
管 here carries its "to be concerned with, to bother about, to look after"
sense (as in 管事, 别管我), not its more common "to control, to manage"
sense (as in 管理); "not to be concerned with [the difference]" is exactly
what "regardless of" or "no matter" means. Once 管's "bother about" sense is
known, the compound is fully predictable — hence "semi."

## Notes
不管 sets up a concessive clause that is answered by 都/也 later in the
sentence: 不管天气怎么样，我们都去 ("no matter what the weather's like, we're
going") — the 都/也 is the part that actually carries "regardless."

## Why this combination
背 "the back (of the body)" plus 包 "bag" gives exactly "a bag (carried
on) the back" — the standard word for a backpack.

## Notes
背包 anchors 背包客 "backpacker," 背上背包 "to put on a backpack" — the
same characters also form the verb phrase 背包 "to carry a pack on one's
back," context distinguishing noun from verb-object use.

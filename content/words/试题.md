## Why this combination
试 "to test, to try" (as in 考试 "exam") combined with 题 "question,
topic" (as in 问题, 题目): the questions that make up a test.

## Notes
试题 refers to the actual questions on an exam (做试题 "to work through
exam questions") — 题目, its close relative, more generally names any
question or topic, exam-related or not.

## Why this combination
舞 "to dance" (as in 跳舞 "to dance") combined with 台 "a platform, a
raised stand" (as in 阳台 "balcony"): a raised platform for dancing —
a stage, extended figuratively to any arena where one performs or acts.

## Notes
舞台 names the literal theatre stage (舞台表演 "stage performance") and,
figuratively, any arena of action or achievement (人生的舞台 "the stage
of life," 国际舞台 "the international stage") — the same literal/
figurative split English "stage" makes.

## Why this combination
外 "outside, foreign" (as in 外国, 外语, its close relative) combined
with 文 "writing, language, script" (as in 中文, 文字): foreign-language
writing.

## Notes
外文 largely overlaps with 外语 "foreign language," though 外文 leans
toward the written/literary form of a foreign language (外文书 "a
foreign-language book," 外文系 "department of foreign languages/literature")
while 外语 is more neutral between speech and writing.

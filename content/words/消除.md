## Why this combination
消 "to dispel, to dissolve away" and 除 "to remove" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for eliminating something unwanted.

## Notes
消除 favours abstract, gradual removal (消除误会 "to clear up a
misunderstanding," 消除疲劳 "to relieve fatigue") over the sudden,
forceful 排除 "to rule out/eliminate."

## Why this combination
东 "east" + 北 "north" → the intercardinal direction northeast. Chinese orders
these compounds 东/西 first, then 南/北 — hence 东北, 西北, 东南, 西南.
Capitalised in sense, 东北 is the region of Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang.

## Notes
The four are [[东北]], [[西北]], [[东南]], [[西南]] — note the fixed order,
opposite to English "northeast." 东北话 "the Northeastern dialect."

## Why this combination
A 20th-century technical calque: English "video" comes from Latin
"I see," and the engineering behind it (a moving image encoded as a
frequency-modulated signal) is what 视 "to view" plus 频 "frequency"
translates — a technically precise coinage rather than a simple, everyday
metaphor.

## Notes
视频 is the standard modern word for digital video (视频通话 "video call,"
视频网站 "video website"), distinct from older 录像 "video recording,"
which leans toward the analog-era sense of the medium.

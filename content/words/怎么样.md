## Why this combination
怎么 "how" (see [[怎么]]) + 样 "manner" → "in what manner / condition." Asks
about state (最近怎么样？), makes a suggestion (去吃饭怎么样？), and, negated,
softens a judgement (不怎么样 "nothing special").

## Notes
Colloquial counterpart of [[怎样]]. 不怎么 + adjective = "not particularly"
(不怎么好).

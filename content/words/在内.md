## Why this combination
在 "to be at" and 内 "inside" combine directly: being situated inside a
group or count — i.e. included.

## Notes
在内 always follows the item being included: 包括我在内 "including me,"
五人在内 "five people included" — a formal way to flag that something is
counted within a total.

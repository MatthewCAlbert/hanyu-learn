## Why this combination
当, here in its "this very, precisely this" sense (see also 当地, 当时)
combined with 中 "middle, centre" (as in 中间): precisely in the
middle — among, in the center of a group or space.

## Notes
当中 follows the group or space it locates within (我们当中 "among us,"
房间当中 "in the middle of the room") — 中间, its close relative, is used
the same way and is somewhat more common in everyday speech.

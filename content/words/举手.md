## Why this combination
举 "to lift, to raise, to hold up" (see [[举行]], [[举办]]) + 手 "hand" as its
object. To answer in class (举手回答), to vote (举手表决), to surrender
(举手投降). Verb-object: 举了举手.

## Notes
举 "raise" also in 举行 "hold (an event)," 举例 "give an example,"
举重 "weightlifting." 举手之劳 "as easy as raising a hand — no trouble at all."

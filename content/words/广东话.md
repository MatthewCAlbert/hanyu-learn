## Why this combination
广东 + 话 "speech": the ordinary name for Cantonese. 话 names a spoken variety, as
in 普通话, 上海话.

## Notes
Another name is 粤语, omitted here (粤 is not an HSK character). 广东 is the
province; the prestige Cantonese of Hong Kong is still called 广东话 in the
north.

## Why this combination
义 carries its "righteousness, moral duty" sense (as in 意义's unrelated
"meaning" sense — a different word sharing the character), and 务 is "an
affair, a task to attend to" (as in 任务, 服务). A task one is morally
bound to attend to is exactly an obligation.

Rated "semi" for 义's specific "moral duty" sense, distinct from its role
in 意义 ("meaning").

## Notes
义务教育 ("compulsory education," lit. "obligatory education") and 尽义务
("to fulfill an obligation") both build on this pair's core "a duty one
is bound to perform" sense.

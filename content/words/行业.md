## Why this combination
行 here takes the *háng* reading, "a line of business, a trade" (as in
银行, "bank," lit. "silver trade/guild" — a very different word from the
everyday *xíng*, "to go/walk"), and 业 is "an occupation, an undertaking"
(as in 职业, 企业, both covered elsewhere in this app). Two words for a
line of work reinforce each other into the standard term for an
industry.

Rated "semi" specifically for that reading switch on 行 (*háng*, not
*xíng*) — easy to miss without already knowing 银行 uses the same
reading.

## Notes
Read 行 as *háng* in 行业 — the same reading it takes in 银行, distinct
from the far more common *xíng* ("to walk, to be OK") most learners meet
first.

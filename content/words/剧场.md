## Why this combination
剧 "drama, a play" (see also 电视剧, 京剧) combined with 场 "a site, a
venue" (see also 场所, 场合): the venue for drama — a theatre.

## Notes
剧场 names the physical venue where plays and performances are staged
(去剧场看戏 "to go to the theatre to watch a play") — 舞台, its
neighbour, is the stage itself within such a venue, a part rather than
the whole building.

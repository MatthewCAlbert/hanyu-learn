## Why this combination

光 ("light," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 光临) plus 盘
("a plate, a disc," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 盘子)
names a disc read by light: an optical disc.

Rated "semi" for 光's technical "laser-read" role here, distinct from
its everyday literal "light" meaning.

## Notes

烧光盘 ("to burn a CD/DVD") and 光盘刻录机 ("a disc burner") both build
on this word — 光盘 covers CDs and DVDs generically, now a somewhat
dated technology alongside modern streaming and flash storage.

## Why this combination
动 "to move, motion" (see also 自动, 运动) combined with 力 "force" (see
also 力量, 实力): the force that drives motion — motive power, and,
figuratively, the drive behind a person's effort.

## Notes
动力 covers mechanical driving force (动力系统 "power system") and
figurative motivation (学习动力 "motivation to study," 有动力 "to have
drive/motivation") — the same literal/figurative split English "drive,
motive power" makes.

## Why this combination
信 "to trust, to believe" (see [[相信]], [[信息]]) + 心 "heart, mind" → a
trusting heart — the settled belief that something will work out. 有信心,
对未来充满信心, 增强信心.

## Notes
信心 = confidence one holds inside; [[相信]] = the act of believing;
[[信任]] (not this level) = trust placed in a person.

## Why this combination

身 ("body," as in 身体) plus 高 ("tall, height") names the height of the
body: a person's stature.

## Notes

身高 pairs with 体重 ("body weight," covered elsewhere in this app) as
the two basic vital statistics asked on forms and at checkups.

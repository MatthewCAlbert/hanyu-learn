## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 含 ("to contain, to hold in the mouth," as in
包含) and 有 ("to have") both independently name possessing something
within. Pairing them gives the standard word for a substance or quality
being present inside something else.

## Notes

含有 always takes a specific contained substance or quality as its
object (含有维生素C, "contains vitamin C") — closer to 含 alone in
register than to the broader, more abstract 包含 ("to include,
encompass").

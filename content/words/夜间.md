## Why this combination
夜 "night" plus 间 "between, during" gives exactly "during the night" —
parallel to 日间 "daytime" and 期间 "during the period."

## Notes
夜间 is a formal/written time word: 夜间施工 "nighttime construction,"
夜间行驶 "night driving" — where speech more often uses 晚上 or 夜里.

## Why this combination
师 alone means "master, teacher" (师父, 教师). 老 here is not "old" but the
respectful prefix also seen in 老板 (boss), 老兄 (old chap) — it marks
familiarity plus deference. So "esteemed master."

## Notes
Used as a title after a surname (王老师) and as a stand-alone address (老师好).
Has largely taken over the "teacher" sense from [[先生]].

## Why this combination
评 "to comment on, to judge, to critique" (as in 批评 "to criticise")
combined with 价 "value, worth" (see also 价值, 价格): to judge
something's value — to evaluate.

## Notes
评价 works as verb ("评价一部电影" "to evaluate/review a film") and noun
("很高的评价" "a very high evaluation/rating") — 评论, a near-synonym, is
closer to "to comment, a commentary" and does not carry the same
judgment-of-worth as 评价.

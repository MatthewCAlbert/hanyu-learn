## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 阅 ("to peruse, to review [documents]," as in
检阅, "to review [troops]") and 读 ("to read," as in 读书) both
independently name taking in written text. Pairing them gives the
formal word for reading.

## Notes

阅读 is the written/formal register (阅读理解, "reading comprehension";
阅读能力, "reading ability") — everyday casual reading is simply 看书
or 读书.

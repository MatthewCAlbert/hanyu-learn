## Why this combination
一 "one" plus 身 "body" gives exactly "one (whole) body" — extended to a
measure-word-like use for a full set of clothing covering the body.

## Notes
一身 anchors 一身汗 "sweat all over," 一身新衣服 "a whole new outfit" —
functions almost like a measure word for something covering the entire
body, whether sweat, dirt, or clothing.

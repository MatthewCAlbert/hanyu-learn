## Why this combination
印 "to stamp, to print, to imprint" (as in 印章 "seal") combined with 象
"image, appearance" (as in 想象 "to imagine," 对象): an image stamped
[on the mind] — an impression left by an experience.

## Notes
印象 is what stays with someone after an experience (印象深刻 "a deep
impression," 第一印象 "first impression") — it names the lasting mental
trace, distinct from 感觉 "feeling, sensation," which names the
immediate experience itself.

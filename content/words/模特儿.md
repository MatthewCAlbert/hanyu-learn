## Why this combination

模特 transliterates English "model" using two characters chosen for
their sound, carrying no meaning of their own in this word; the
retroflex 儿-suffix is added in casual spoken Mandarin, especially in
Beijing-area speech.

## Notes

时装模特 ("a fashion model") and 模特公司 ("a modeling agency") both build
on the borrowed 模特 syllables — the plain form 模特 (without 儿) is
equally common and interchangeable in most contexts.

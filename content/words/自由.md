## Why this combination
自 "self" + 由 "to proceed from, to be up to" → "proceeding from oneself,"
acting by one's own will rather than another's. An old word (自由自在) that took
on the modern political sense "liberty" in the 19th century.

## Notes
言论自由 "freedom of speech," 自由市场 "free market," 行动自由. 由 "from / by /
up to" also in 由于 "owing to," 理由.

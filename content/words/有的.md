## Why this combination
有 "there exist" + nominalising 的 → "the ones that exist," i.e. some. Typically
in a paired list: 有的人喜欢，有的人不喜欢 "some people like it, some don't."

## Notes
Overlaps with [[有些]]; 有的…有的… is the natural frame for contrast, 有些 for a
single hedged "some."

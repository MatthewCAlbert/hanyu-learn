## Why this combination
实 "real, actual" + 际 "the edge, the point where things meet, the actual
situation" (see [[国际]], 之际). "Where things really stand" — the facts on the
ground: 实际情况, 实际上 "in fact," 不切实际 "unrealistic."

## Notes
实际 (the real state of affairs) vs. [[实在]] (adverb "really" / adjective
"solid") vs. [[实现]] (verb "to realise"). 实际上 is a very common "actually."

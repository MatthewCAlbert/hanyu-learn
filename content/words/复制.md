## Why this combination

复 ("to repeat, to duplicate," as in 复习, "to review [lit. repeat +
practice]") plus 制 ("to make," as in 制作) names making a repeated
version of something: to copy, to duplicate.

## Notes

复制粘贴 ("copy and paste") is the standard computing phrase built on
this word; 复制品 ("a replica, a reproduction") names the resulting
object.

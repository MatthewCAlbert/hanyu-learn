## Why this combination
位 ("a position," covered in this app's entry for 位置) plus 于 (the
classical preposition "at," covered in this app's entries for 关于,
对于, 在于) names holding a position at a particular place.

Rated "semi" for 于's classical preposition role, which needs to already
be known from words like 关于 to parse this compound cleanly.

## Notes
位于 is formal/written, used for stating a fixed geographic location
(北京位于中国北方, "Beijing is located in northern China") — plain 在 is
the everyday equivalent.

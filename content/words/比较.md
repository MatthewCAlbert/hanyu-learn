## Why this combination
比 and 较 both independently mean "to compare" — the same 较 seen
earlier in this dataset (从車交聲, originally a carriage's handrail,
extended via "measuring oneself against" to "to compare"). Paired for
emphasis and a more formal register than 比 alone.

## Notes
As an adverb before an adjective, 比较 softens a claim into "relatively,
somewhat" (比较好 "fairly good") — comparison against an implicit
standard, rather than a stated explicit one.

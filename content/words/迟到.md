## Why this combination
迟 ("late, slow," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 到 ("to arrive")
names arriving in a late manner: to be late. Fully compositional.

## Notes
迟到 always describes arriving late to a specific event or appointment
(上课迟到, "to be late for class") — for a general sense of being behind
schedule without a specific arrival, 晚了 or 来不及 is more natural.

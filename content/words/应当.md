## Why this combination
应, here a modal "should, ought to" (as in 应该) combined with 当 "to
be fitting, to correspond to, to serve as" (see also 当地, 当时): what
is fitting to do — should, ought to.

## Notes
应当 is a slightly more formal synonym of 应该, used the same way before
a verb (应当遵守规则 "one should abide by the rules") — 应该 is the more
common, everyday choice in speech, while 应当 leans towards written or
official registers.

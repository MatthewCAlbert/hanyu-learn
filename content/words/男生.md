## Why this combination
男 "male" + 生, clipped from 学生 "student." Like [[女生]], it started as
"schoolboy" and broadened among young people to the everyday word for a young
man — less blunt than [[男人]].

## Notes
男生 / 女生 is the default pairing in campus and dating talk; 男孩 / 女孩 skews
younger, 男人 / 女人 older.

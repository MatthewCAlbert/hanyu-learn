## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 顾 "to look after, to attend to, to care about" gives
exactly "without attending to" — the standard word for disregarding a
consideration.

## Notes
不顾 takes an object naming what is disregarded: 不顾一切 "regardless of
everything," 不顾危险 "in spite of the danger" — often implies a degree of
recklessness or determination overriding caution.

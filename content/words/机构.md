## Why this combination
机 carries its "mechanism, pivotal apparatus" sense (as in 机器, 机会's own
"pivotal moment" root), and 构 is "to construct, a structure" (as in
结构, 建构). A "constructed mechanism" applies just as naturally to a
physical machine's inner workings as to an organized body of people —
hence the word covers both a literal mechanism and an institution.

Rated "semi" for 构's less common standalone verb/noun sense, usually met
first inside compounds like this one or 结构.

## Notes
机构 leans toward naming the organizational body itself (政府机构,
government agency; 金融机构, financial institution), while 机制 (a related,
distinct word) names how something works internally, its mechanism of
action.

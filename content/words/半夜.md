## Why this combination
半 "half" + 夜 "night" → the halfway point of the night, midnight and the small
hours around it: 半夜醒来 "woke in the middle of the night," 三更半夜 "the dead
of night."

## Notes
半 + time-unit: [[半天]], [[半年]], 半小时. 夜 "night" also in [[夜里]],
熬夜 "stay up late," 夜市 "night market."

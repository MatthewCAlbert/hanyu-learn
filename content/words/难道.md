## Why this combination
难 "hard, difficult [to]" combined with 道 "to say" — literally "[is it]
hard to say [that...]" — grammaticalized into a rhetorical-question
adverb that expects the answer "yes," used to voice surprise or
incredulity.

## Notes
难道 sits at the front of the clause it questions and is regularly paired
with a clause-final 吗 (难道你不知道吗？"Don't tell me you don't know?");
it does not itself mean "difficult" and cannot be used as a plain
adjective.

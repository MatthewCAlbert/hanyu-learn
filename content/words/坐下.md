## Why this combination
坐 "to sit" + directional complement 下 "down" — lowering into a seat, with 下
marking the settling. 请坐 alone is the polite "have a seat"; 坐下 adds the
motion, and takes 来 / 去 for viewpoint: 坐下来 "sit yourself down."

## Notes
下 as a directional complement (motion downward, or settling) also in
放下, 躺下, 停下.

## Why this combination
父 "father" and 亲 "a parent, close kin" (the same 亲 seen standalone
elsewhere in this dataset, itself a case of simplification keeping only
the phonetic half of 親) combine directly, giving 父 a fuller, more
formal standalone form than the bound character alone.

## Notes
父亲 is the formal/written register; 爸爸 is the everyday, affectionate
term for the same person — the two sit at opposite ends of the same
register scale that 母亲/妈妈 mirror for mothers.

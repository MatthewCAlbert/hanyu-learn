## Why this combination
将, here a future-marking adverb "about to, going to" (as in 将要),
combined with 来 "to come": what is about to come — the future.

## Notes
将来 names the future from the present moment onward (将来的计划 "future
plans," 在不久的将来 "in the near future") — 未来, a close synonym, is
somewhat more formal and literary, while 将来 is the everyday word.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 检 "to examine, to restrain" (检验, 检讨) + 查 "to look into, to
check" (调查, 查看). Going over something for faults — homework, a machine, one's
body: 检查身体, 检查作业.

## Notes
检查 also has a self-critical sense: 写检查 "write a self-criticism." 查 alone:
查字典 "look sth up in a dictionary."

## Why this combination
敌 ("hostile, an enemy," surviving bound in 敌意, "hostility") plus 人
("person") names exactly what it says: a hostile person. Fully
compositional.

## Notes
敌人 names a person or side in active opposition or conflict; for a
milder rival without hostility, Mandarin uses 对手 ("opponent, rival")
instead.

## Why this combination
依 "to rely on, to depend on" and 据 "evidence, basis" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for a relied-upon basis.

## Notes
依据 works as a preposition ("according to," 依据法律 "according to the
law") and a noun ("basis," 缺乏依据 "lacking a basis/evidence") — a formal,
often legal or academic register.

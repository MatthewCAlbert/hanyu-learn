## Why this combination
私 "private" and 人 "person" combine directly: matters or relationships
belonging to a specific individual rather than to the public or an
organization.

## Notes
私人 modifies property and relationships (私人财产 "private property," 私
人关系 "personal connections") and, as a noun, names a close personal
contact (他的私人 "his personal confidant/associate" — a more classical
usage now rarer in everyday speech).

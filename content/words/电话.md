## Why this combination
"Electric speech" — a native coinage on the 电 "electric-" prefix that also
built 电脑, 电视, 电影, 电灯. Transparent once you have both characters, and no
borrowed sound in it.

## Notes
Means both the device and the call: 打电话 "make a call," 接电话 "answer,"
电话号码 "phone number."

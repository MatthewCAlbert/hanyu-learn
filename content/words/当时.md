## Why this combination
当 "at, right at (a time or place)" + 时 "time" → "at that (past) time." Points
back to a moment already established in the discourse: 我当时不知道.

## Notes
当 = "at the time of" also in 当天, 当年, 当地. Read *dāng* here. Near-synonyms
那时, 那时候 (see [[那时]]).

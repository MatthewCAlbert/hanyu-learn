## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 交 ("to exchange, to hand over," as in 交流,
"to exchange ideas") and 换 ("to swap, to change") both independently
name trading one thing for another. Pairing them gives the standard word
for exchange.

## Notes
交换意见 ("to exchange views") and 交换礼物 ("to exchange gifts") both
build on this pair's core "giving and receiving in return" sense.

## Why this combination
留学 "to study abroad" (留 stay + 学 study — "stay (in another country) to
study"; see [[留下]], [[学习]]) + 生 "student" (see [[学生]]) → a student
based abroad for study. Parse 留学 + 生. Used both ways: 中国留学生 abroad,
外国留学生 in China.

## Notes
留 "stay / remain" also in [[留下]], 保留. 海归 "a returnee" (someone who has
come back after 留学).

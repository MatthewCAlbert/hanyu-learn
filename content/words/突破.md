## Why this combination
突 "suddenly, to rush forward" and 破 "to break" combine directly:
breaking through an obstacle with a sudden forceful push.

## Notes
突破 spans the literal (military 突破防线 "to break through defensive
lines") and the figurative (科技突破 "a technological breakthrough," 突破
记录 "to break a record") — always overcoming a barrier that was
previously holding something back.

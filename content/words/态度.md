## Why this combination
态 "form, bearing, manner" (姿态, 状态) + 度 "degree, measure, bearing" (风度,
气度). The outward bearing and its "gauge" — how one carries oneself toward
something: 学习态度, 服务态度不好, 态度很好.

## Notes
态度 = disposition shown outwardly; [[心情]] = inner mood; 立场 = position /
stance on an issue. 度 toneless-ish (*tàidu*).

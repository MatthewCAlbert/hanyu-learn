## Why this combination
外 "outside, foreign" (see also 国外, 外国) combined with 交 "to
interact, to exchange" (see also 交流, 交往): interaction with the
outside [world] — diplomacy, foreign affairs.

## Notes
外交 names relations and negotiation between states (外交部 "Ministry of
Foreign Affairs," 外交官 "diplomat") — 交往, its close relative, is used
for personal or social association between individuals, not
state-to-state relations.

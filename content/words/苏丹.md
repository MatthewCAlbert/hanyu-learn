## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Sūdān / Sudan (蘇丹). 苏 is not "to revive";
丹 is not "cinnabar." Also the title sultan in other words — here it is the
country.

## Notes
南苏丹 is [[南苏丹]]. Contrast 素丹 / 苏丹 as a monarch title in older texts.

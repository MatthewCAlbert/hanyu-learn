## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 部 "a section, a category, a ministry" + 分 (*fèn*) "a share, a
portion." A piece of a whole: 大部分 "most of it," 一部分人 "some people,"
分成三部分 "divide into three parts."

## Notes
分 is *fèn* here (a share), not *fēn* "to divide." 部 alone: 部门, 全部,
[[大部分]].

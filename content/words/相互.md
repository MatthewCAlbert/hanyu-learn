## Why this combination
相 "mutually, [towards] each other" (see also 互相, 相当) and 互
"mutually, reciprocally" (see also 互相) are the same two characters as
互相, simply reversed — both orders are standard, near-interchangeable
adverbs for reciprocal action.

## Notes
相互 and 互相 are functionally identical (相互帮助/互相帮助 "to help each
other") — 相互 is somewhat more common as a modifier before a noun
(相互关系 "mutual relations"), where 互相 sits more naturally directly
before a verb.

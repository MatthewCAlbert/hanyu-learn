## Why this combination
因 "cause, reason" pointing at 此 "this" — "because of this [preceding
fact]" — a transparent connector linking a stated cause to its result.

## Notes
因此 sits at the formal end of a small register scale for "therefore" —
所以 is the everyday equivalent; 因此 and 因而 both read as more
written/formal.

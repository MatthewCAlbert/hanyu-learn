## Why this combination
说 "to speak" + resultative 明 "clear" — to talk until it is clear. Verb
"explain" (说明原因), verb "indicate / prove" (这说明他错了 "this shows he was
wrong"), and noun "the instructions" (使用说明).

## Notes
明 as a "make-clear" resultative also in [[明白]], 查明, 证明. 说明书 "instruction
manual."

## Why this combination
不 "not" + 但 "only, merely" (the same 但 as in [[但是]]) → "not merely," which
sets up a stronger second point. Correlates with 而且 / 还 / 也:
不但便宜，而且好用.

## Notes
不但 ≈ 不仅 (more formal). The second clause must add something — often with a
rising force (不但…，甚至…).

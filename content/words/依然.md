## Why this combination
依 ("to lean on, to rely on," covered in this app's hanzi entry) plus 然
(the grammaticalized adverb-forming suffix seen in 突然, 竟然) extends
"leaning on" into "still resting on/relying on the same state as
before" — unchanged.

Marked "idiom" for 然's fixed suffix role, which contributes no
independent meaning of its own here.

## Notes
依然 and 仍然 are close synonyms for "still" — 依然 leans slightly more
literary/written, while 仍然 is equally comfortable in speech.

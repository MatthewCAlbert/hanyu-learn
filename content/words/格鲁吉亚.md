## Why this combination
A phonetic rendering of Russian Gruziya / Georgia the country (格魯吉亞), not the
US state. 鲁 and 吉 are chosen for sound.

## Notes
Same stem in [[格鲁吉亚语]]. English "Georgia" is ambiguous; the Chinese name is
not.

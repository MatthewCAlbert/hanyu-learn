## Why this combination
裁 "to decide, to judge" (see 裁's own etymology — cutting cloth to a
pattern, extended to judgement) and 判 "to judge, to decide" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for judging a contest.

## Notes
裁判 names both the person ("referee, umpire," 裁判员 formally) and the
act/ruling ("a judgment," 公正裁判 "a fair ruling") — sports and legal
registers share the same word.

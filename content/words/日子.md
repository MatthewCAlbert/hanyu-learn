## Why this combination
日 "day, sun" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see 孩子). Three uses: a particular
day (定个日子 "set a date"), a stretch of time (这些日子 "these days"), and life
as lived (过日子 "get by," 苦日子 "hard times").

## Notes
日子 = days as experienced; [[日期]] = a calendar date; [[日报]] = a daily paper.

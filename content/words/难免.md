## Why this combination

难 ("difficult," as in 困难) plus 免 ("to avoid, to be exempt from," as
in 免费, "free [lit. exempt + fee]") names something difficult to
avoid: unavoidable.

## Notes

在所难免 ("bound to happen, cannot be avoided") and 难免会犯错 ("bound to
make mistakes") both build on this word — softer and more measured than
plain 一定, framing an outcome as understandably inevitable rather than
simply certain.

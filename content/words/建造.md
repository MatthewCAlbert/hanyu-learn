## Why this combination
建 "to build, to construct" and 造 "to make, to create" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for large-scale construction.

## Notes
建造 favours substantial structures (建造大桥 "to build a bridge," 建造
房屋 "to construct a house") — more formal and weightier than everyday
盖 "to build [a house]."

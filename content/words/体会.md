## Why this combination
体, here in its "body, to embody, to experience with one's whole self"
sense (see also 体验) combined with 会 "to understand, to comprehend"
(as in 理解's own logic, and 会 in its "to know how to" sense): to
comprehend through lived experience — to come to understand deeply.

## Notes
体会 names understanding gained through personal experience, often
emotional or practical (深有体会 "to have deep personal understanding
[of something]") — 理解, the general word for "to understand," does not
require this first-hand, lived-through quality that 体会 implies.

## Why this combination
传 "to pass on, to transmit" (see also 传说, 宣传) combined with 播 "to
sow, to scatter, to broadcast" (see also 广播): to pass on by scattering
widely — to disseminate, to spread.

## Notes
传播 covers spreading information, culture, disease and more (传播
知识 "to disseminate knowledge," 病毒传播 "virus transmission") — a broad
verb for anything spreading outward from a source, whether by media,
contact, or word of mouth.

## Why this combination
住 "to stay, to live, to lodge" + 院, clipped from [[医院]] "hospital" (see also
[[学院]], [[出院]]) → to lodge in hospital as an in-patient. Verb-object:
住了一个星期院. Opposite [[出院]] "be discharged."

## Notes
院 as clipped "hospital" also in 出院, 住院部. Full form 住医院 is also heard.

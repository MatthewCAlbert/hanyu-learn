## Why this combination
同 "same" + 样 "kind, manner" (see [[一样]], [[这样]]) → of the same kind.
Attributive (同样的问题 "the same problem") and adverbial/connective
(同样，我们也… "likewise, we also…").

## Notes
同样 = "the same (kind)" as a modifier; [[一样]] = "the same as" in a comparison
frame (A 和 B 一样).

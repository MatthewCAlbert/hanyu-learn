## Why this combination
脑 ("brain") plus 袋 ("a bag," covered elsewhere in this app) pictures the
head casually as the "bag" that holds the brain — a colloquial, slightly
playful image rather than a clinical anatomical term.

Rated "semi" because 袋's "bag" sense here is a loose, informal metaphor
rather than a literal description.

## Notes
脑袋 is casual/spoken, exactly parallel to English "noggin" or "head" in
informal registers — for the formal anatomical term, 头 alone is neutral
and 头部 more clinical still.

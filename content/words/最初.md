## Why this combination

最 ("most, -est," as in 最好) plus 初 ("beginning," as in 初中, "the
beginning [level] of middle school") names the very earliest point:
"at first, initial."

## Notes

最初 as a time-word can open a clause on its own (最初，我们并不认识,
"at first, we didn't know each other") or modify a noun (最初的想法, "the
initial idea") — 起初 is a near-synonym in the same slot.

## Why this combination
浪漫 transliterates English/French "romance/romantique" sound-for-sound.
浪 "wave" and 漫 "to overflow, unrestrained" were chosen for their sound,
though the pairing happens to evoke a fittingly sweeping, unrestrained
image — a coincidence of sound-matching, not the word's actual origin.

## Notes
浪漫 covers both romantic relationships (浪漫的爱情 "romantic love") and
the broader literary/artistic sense "Romantic" (浪漫主义 "Romanticism,"
the 19th-century movement) — both senses borrowed together from the
European term.

## Why this combination
交 "to hand over" and 代 (here "in place of, to substitute") combine into
"to hand over (a duty or account) in one's place" — the base sense of
transferring responsibility; from there it extends to instructing someone
on that duty, explaining the details, or (under pressure) confessing.

## Notes
交代 spans several related senses by context: 交代工作 "to hand over/brief
on work," 交代清楚 "to explain clearly," and, in an interrogation context,
"to confess" — all trace back to "accounting for a matter to someone
else."

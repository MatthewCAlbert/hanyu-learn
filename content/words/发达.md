## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to develop, to grow" (see also 发展, 发生) combined
with 达 "to reach, to arrive at" (see also 到达, 达到): to grow and
reach [a high level] — developed, flourishing.

## Notes
发达 describes an advanced state of economic or physical development
(发达国家 "developed country," 肌肉发达 "well-developed muscles") — 发展,
its close relative, is the general verb "to develop," while 发达
describes the resulting advanced state.

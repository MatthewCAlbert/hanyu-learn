## Why this combination

新 ("new") plus 娘 ("a young woman," as in 姑娘) names a woman newly
[married]: a bride.

## Notes

新娘 pairs with 新郎 ("bridegroom," lit. "new young gentleman") as the
standard wedding-day terms for the couple.

## Why this combination
冬 "winter" + 天, here "season / time of year" — the same suffix as in 春天,
[[夏天]], 秋天. Not "sky" or "day."

## Notes
冬 alone in 冬季 "winter (season)," 过冬 "get through the winter," 冬眠
"hibernate." 冬天很冷.

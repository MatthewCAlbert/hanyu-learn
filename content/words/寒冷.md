## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 寒 ("cold, wintry," as in 寒假, "winter break")
and 冷 ("cold," the everyday word) both independently name low
temperature. Pairing them gives the formal, written-register word for
cold.

## Notes

寒冷的冬天 ("a cold winter") is a common written-register frame — everyday
spoken Mandarin more often reaches for plain 冷 alone (今天很冷, "it's
cold today").

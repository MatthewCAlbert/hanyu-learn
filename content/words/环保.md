## Why this combination
环保 shortens 环境保护 "environmental protection" to its first character
from each half: 环 from 环境 "environment" (see its own entry) and 保
from 保护 "to protect" — a standard compression pattern for paired
disyllabic terms, the same way 科技 shortens 科学技术.

## Notes
环保 is used both as a noun ("重视环保" "to value environmental
protection") and, very commonly today, as an adjective ("环保材料"
"eco-friendly material") — the full form 环境保护 is reserved for more
formal contexts.

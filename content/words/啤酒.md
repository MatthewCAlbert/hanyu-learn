## Why this combination
啤 transliterates the "b/p" sound of English "beer" purely by sound
(marked with the 口 "mouth" radical, the standard signal of a coined
transliteration syllable, the same device seen in 咖啡) combined with 酒
"alcohol, wine," supplying the actual meaning: a "bee-alcohol," i.e.
beer.

## Notes
啤酒 is a half-loan: 啤 alone carries no meaning outside this word and
exists purely to render the foreign sound, while 酒 supplies the real
semantic content — a hybrid pattern distinct from a pure phonetic loan
like 咖啡, where neither syllable carries native meaning.

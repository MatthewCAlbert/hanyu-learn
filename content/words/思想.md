## Why this combination
思 "to think, to ponder" (as in 意思 "meaning," 思考 "to think over")
and 想 "to think, to consider, to imagine" are close synonyms, paired
into the noun for organised thought — an idea, a philosophy, or the
faculty of thinking itself.

## Notes
思想 names both the process of thinking and its product — a system of
ideas or ideology (思想家 "thinker," 思想开放 "open-minded") — 想法, its
near-synonym, is the lighter word for a specific, individual idea or
opinion rather than a system of thought.

## Why this combination
法 ("law," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 官 ("an official," also
covered elsewhere — a roofed hall where a crowd serves the ruler) names
the official charged with law: a judge.

## Notes
法官 (the office-holder) is distinct from 法院 (the court as an
institution, covered elsewhere in this app) and from 律师 (a lawyer, who
argues before the judge rather than presiding).

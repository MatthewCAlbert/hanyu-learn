## Why this combination
商 "commerce, business" plus 标 "mark, sign" gives exactly "a commercial
mark" — the standard legal/commercial word for a trademark.

## Notes
商标 anchors 注册商标 "registered trademark," 商标权 "trademark rights" —
distinct from 品牌 "brand," which is the broader identity a trademark
protects.

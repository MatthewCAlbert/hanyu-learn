## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" (as in 成功 "success," 成员 "member")
combined with 熟 "ripe, cooked, familiar" (as in 熟悉 "familiar with"):
to become fully ripe/cooked — mature, extended from fruit to people and
plans alike.

## Notes
成熟 covers literal ripening (水果成熟了 "the fruit has ripened") and
figurative maturity — of a person's character (他很成熟 "he's very
mature") or a plan's readiness (条件成熟 "conditions are ripe/mature") —
the same extension English "ripe/mature" makes.

## Why this combination
基 "foundation" and 地 "ground, place" combine directly: the ground a
whole operation is founded on.

## Notes
基地 covers military and industrial bases (军事基地 "military base," 生产
基地 "production base") and training centres (训练基地) — always a fixed
home location an operation is run out of.

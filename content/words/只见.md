## Why this combination
只 "only" plus 见 "to see" gives exactly "only see" — used at the start
of a clause to focus attention sharply on what happens next, a common
narrative device.

## Notes
只见 typically opens a vivid descriptive clause: 只见他猛地站了起来
"[the narrator] saw him suddenly stand up" — a written/literary device
for drawing the reader's eye to a striking moment.

## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" (see [[全部]], [[全国]], [[全家]]) + 年 "year" → the year in
its entirety: 全年营业 "open all year," 全年总收入.

## Notes
全 + time-unit: 全年, 全天 "all day," 全月. Compare [[半年]] "half a year."

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Russian Rossiya (Россия). 俄, 罗 and 斯
are chosen for sound. An older two-character form 俄国 still appears; 俄罗斯 is
the usual full name.

## Notes
俄 as the country-abbreviation in 俄语 (an HSK word), 俄文. 斯 is a favourite
phonetic closer for foreign names (巴基斯坦, 威尼斯).

## Why this combination
光 "light" plus 线 (here "a ray, a line of travel") gives exactly "a line
of light" — the physics-flavored word for light as a directional beam.

## Notes
光线 is used for both physical description (光线充足 "well-lit," 光线
昏暗 "dim lighting") and photography (调整光线 "to adjust the lighting") —
more precise/technical than plain 光.

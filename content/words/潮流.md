## Why this combination

潮 ("a tide," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 高潮, "climax")
plus 流 ("to flow, a current," as in 交流, "to exchange [lit.
cross-flow]") names a flowing tide, extended figuratively to a social or
cultural current: a trend.

## Notes

时代潮流 ("the tide/trend of the times") and 赶潮流 ("to follow/chase a
trend") both build on this word — the same "flowing current" image as
English "the tide of history" or "swim against the current."

## Why this combination

近 ("near, recent," as in 最近) plus 代 ("an era, a generation," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 现代/古代) names the recent historical
era: modern times.

## Notes

近代史 ("modern history") sits between 古代 ("ancient times," covered
elsewhere in this app) and 现代 ("the present era," also covered
elsewhere) — Chinese historiography conventionally dates 近代 from the
mid-19th century to the early 20th.

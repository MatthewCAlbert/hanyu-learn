## Why this combination
[[笑话]] "joke" (笑 laugh + 话 words) with the erhua suffix 儿, the everyday
spoken form for a joke you tell: 讲个笑话儿. Non-erhua 笑话 covers the same
plus "to laugh at" (a verb) and "a laughing-stock."

## Notes
The 儿 form leans toward the "a funny story" sense; the mockery senses
(闹笑话, 看笑话, 笑话人) usually drop the 儿.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 潮 ("tide, dampness," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 潮流/高潮) and 湿 ("wet, damp," as in 湿润, "moist") both
independently name moisture. Pairing them gives the standard word for
dampness in the air or a place.

## Notes

天气潮湿 ("the weather is damp/humid") and 房间潮湿 ("the room is damp")
both build on this word — names ambient moisture in the environment,
distinct from 湿, which more often describes something directly wet to
the touch.

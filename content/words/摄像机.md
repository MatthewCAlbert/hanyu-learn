## Why this combination
摄 "to capture (an image)," 像 "image," and 机 "machine" stack into
exactly "a machine that captures images" — a moving-image counterpart to
照相机 "camera" (which captures still images, 照相 "to photograph").

## Notes
摄像机 specifically means a video camera (continuous recording), while
照相机 is a still camera — the distinction hinges on 摄 (capturing motion
footage) versus 照 (照相, taking a still photo).

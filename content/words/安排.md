## Why this combination
安 "to make calm, to settle" and 排 "a row; to line up in order"
combine directly: settling things into an orderly row — to arrange.

## Notes
安排 covers everything from a daily schedule (安排时间 "to arrange
one's time") to a formal plan (人事安排 "personnel arrangements") —
always the act of putting things into a workable order ahead of time.

## Why this combination
武 "military, martial" (as in 武术 "martial arts") combined with 器 "a
tool, an instrument, a vessel" (as in 机器 "machine"): the instrument of
war — a weapon.

## Notes
武器 covers weapons literal and figurative alike (核武器 "nuclear
weapon," 语言是他的武器 "language is his weapon"); 器 alone survives
productively in many compounds naming instruments and devices (乐器
"musical instrument," 容器 "container").

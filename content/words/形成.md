## Why this combination
形 "form, shape" (see also 形象) combined with 成 "to become, to
complete" (see also 成功, 成长): to become a shape — to form, to take
shape gradually.

## Notes
形成 describes something gradually coming into being through a process
(形成习惯 "to form a habit," 形成规模 "to take shape/reach scale") — 成为,
its near-relative, means simply "to become [something]" without the same
gradual, developmental nuance.

## Why this combination
远 "far" plus 处 "place" gives exactly "a far place" — the standard word
for somewhere distant from the speaker's position.

## Notes
远处 anchors 远处传来的声音 "a sound coming from the distance," 望向远处
"to look into the distance" — the mirror of 近处 "a nearby place."

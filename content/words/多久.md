## Why this combination
多 "how much / to what degree" (see [[多么]], [[多少]]) + 久 "long (of time),
a long while." Asks about duration: 学了多久？ "how long have you studied?",
没多久 "not long after."

## Notes
多 + adjective to ask a degree: 多大 (how big / old), 多高, 多远, 多久. 久
"long in time" also in [[好久]], [[不久]], 长久.

## Why this combination
决 "to decide" (etymologically water breaking through a dike — a point
of no return) and 定 "to fix, to settle" reinforce each other directly:
deciding is settling on one fixed course.

## Notes
决定 covers both the verb "to decide" and the noun "a decision," letting
it stand alone as a full sentence predicate or as an object — 做出决定
"to make a decision."

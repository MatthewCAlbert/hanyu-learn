## Why this combination
纪 "a record, a chronicle kept in order" (see also 世纪, 纪念) combined
with 录 "to record" (see also 记录, 录音 "to record sound"): the two
characters that also form 记录, here in the specific reading reserved
for a best-result record.

## Notes
纪录 is the preferred written form specifically for a competitive record
(打破纪录 "to break a record," 世界纪录 "world record") — 记录, its
near-twin, is the general word for "to record" and "a written record,"
covering the broader everyday sense that 纪录 does not.

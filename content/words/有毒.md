## Why this combination
有 "to have" plus 毒 "poison" gives exactly "to have poison (in it)" — the
standard predicate for describing something toxic, the same 有+noun
pattern as 有害 "harmful" and 有用 "useful."

## Notes
有毒 modifies substances, plants, and animals: 有毒气体 "toxic gas," 有毒
植物 "poisonous plant" — a straightforward descriptive predicate rather
than a fused single-concept adjective.

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Jamaica (牙買加). 牙, 买 and 加 are chosen
for sound. 牙 is not "tooth"; 买 is not "to buy."

## Notes
加 as in [[加拿大]], [[新加坡]].

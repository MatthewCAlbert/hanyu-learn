## Why this combination
平 "level, ordinary, calm" (see [[平常]], [[平安]]) + 时 "time" → "in ordinary
times," as opposed to a special occasion or an emergency: 平时不锻炼,
比赛时就不行了.

## Notes
平时 (in the normal run of things) ≈ [[平常]] (which can also be an adjective,
"unremarkable"). Contrast 战时 "in wartime," 临时 "temporarily / at the last
minute."

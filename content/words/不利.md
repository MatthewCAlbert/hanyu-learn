## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 利 "beneficial, advantageous" gives exactly "not
beneficial" — as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
不利 modifies conditions and outcomes: 不利因素 "unfavourable factors," 对
健康不利 "bad for one's health" — the standard antonym of 有利
"beneficial, favourable."

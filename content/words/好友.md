## Why this combination

好 ("good") plus 友 ("friend," as in 朋友) names a good, close friend.

## Notes

好友 is the compact written/formal form (男朋友's 朋友 doubled into a
single-syllable-feeling pair) — also the standard term for a "friend" on
a social-media contact list (加为好友, "to add as a friend").

## Why this combination
优 "excellent, superior" (as in 优秀 "outstanding") combined with 势
"force, momentum, a position of strength" (as in 形势 "the state of
affairs"): a superior position of strength — an advantage.

## Notes
优势 names a comparative advantage held over rivals or alternatives
(有优势 "to have an advantage," 优势明显 "the advantage is clear") — its
opposite is 劣势 "disadvantage, inferior position," built on the same 势.

## Why this combination
市 "market, marketplace, city" combined with 场 "an open ground, a site
[for an activity]" (as in 操场 "sports ground"): the site where a market
is held — extended, as in English, from the physical marketplace to the
abstract economic market.

## Notes
市场 covers a literal market for produce and goods (菜市场 "food market")
as fluently as the abstract economic sense (市场经济 "market economy,"
就业市场 "job market") — the same dual concreteness/abstraction split as
English "market."

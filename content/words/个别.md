## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 个 ("individual," the generic classifier) and
别 ("separate, distinct," as in 区别, "difference") both independently
name a single, distinct instance apart from the whole. Pairing them
gives the standard word for isolated, individual cases.

## Notes

个别情况 ("isolated cases, individual instances") and 个别学生 ("a small
number of specific students") both build on this word — 个别 always
implies "a few, not the general rule," distinct from 每个, "each and
every one."

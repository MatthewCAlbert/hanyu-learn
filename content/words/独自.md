## Why this combination
独 ("alone, solitary," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 自 ("self")
names doing something entirely on one's own.

## Notes
独自一人 ("all alone, by oneself," reinforcing 独自 with 一人 "one
person") is a common intensified frame — 独自 and 单独 (covered elsewhere
in this app) are close synonyms, with 独自 slightly more literary.

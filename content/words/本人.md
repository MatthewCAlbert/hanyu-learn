## Why this combination
本 "this, the original [one]" and 人 "person" combine directly: the
person themselves, as opposed to any representative or stand-in.

## Notes
本人 is a formal, official-register way to say "I/myself" (本人保证...
"I hereby guarantee...") or to refer back to "the person in question"
(需要本人到场 "the person concerned must be present") — distancing,
official language rather than everyday 我.

## Why this combination
灰 "ash, dust" plus 色 "color" gives exactly "the color of ash" — the
standard color word, extended figuratively to moods and ambiguous
situations, the same metaphor as English "gray area."

## Notes
灰色 names the literal color and, figuratively, dispiritedness (灰色情绪
"a gloomy mood") or moral/legal ambiguity (灰色地带 "gray area") —
parallel in structure to other color-plus-色 words like 红色, 蓝色.

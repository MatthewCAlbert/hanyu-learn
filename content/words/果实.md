## Why this combination

果 ("fruit," as in 水果) plus 实 ("real, substantial," covered elsewhere
in this app as part of 现实/事实) names the solid, substantial fruit
that a plant produces — extended figuratively to any tangible result of
effort.

Rated "semi" for the figurative "results of labor" extension, one step
from the literal botanical fruit.

## Notes

结出果实 ("to bear fruit," literal or figurative) and 劳动果实 ("the fruits
of one's labor") both build on this word — 水果 is the everyday word for
edible fruit, while 果实 leans more botanical/formal and carries the
figurative sense far more readily.

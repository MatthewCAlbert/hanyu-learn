## Why this combination
文 "writing" (see also 文化, 文章) combined with 学 "a field of study,
learning" (as in 学习, 科学): the study of writing as an art form —
literature.

## Notes
文学 names literature as a field and art form (文学作品 "literary work,"
文学家 "a person of letters, writer") — 文章, its close relative, is a
single piece of writing (an essay, article), not the field as a whole.

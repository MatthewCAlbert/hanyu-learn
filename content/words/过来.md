## Why this combination
过 "to pass, to cross" + 来 "come (toward the speaker)" — crossing over to where
you are: 你过来一下. As a complement it marks turning round (回过头来), managing
a load (忙不过来 "can't cope"), or returning to a normal state (醒过来 "wake
up," 缓过来 "recover").

## Notes
[[过去]] is the away-from-speaker twin. The "back to normal" complement sense
(过来 = regain) is worth learning as a set.

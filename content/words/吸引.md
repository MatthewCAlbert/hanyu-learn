## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 吸 ("to inhale, to draw in," covered in this
app's hanzi entry) and 引 ("to draw, to lead," also covered) both
independently name a pulling-toward motion. Pairing them gives the
standard word for attraction — pulling attention or interest inward, the
way breath pulls air in.

## Notes
吸引力 ("attractive force, appeal," adding 力 "force") is the common noun
form — used for both literal physical attraction (magnetism, gravity) and
figurative appeal.

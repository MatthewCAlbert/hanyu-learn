## Why this combination
期 ("a period of time," as in 时期, 假期) plus 间 ("an interval, a space
between," covered in this app's entry for 空间) names the stretch of time
between two points — a period, or "during [that period]."

## Notes
期间 always follows a named time span (春节期间, "during Spring Festival")
— it does not open a sentence the way English "during" can when a clause
follows.

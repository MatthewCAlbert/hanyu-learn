## Why this combination
本 "root, origin, the base [of something]" combined with 来 "to come":
[the state something] comes from at its root — how things were at the
outset, before any change.

## Notes
本来 sets up a contrast with how things turned out (本来我不想去，后来还是
去了 "originally I didn't want to go, but in the end I went anyway"); 原来
overlaps heavily but leans more towards "as it turns out" (a realisation)
than "at the outset."

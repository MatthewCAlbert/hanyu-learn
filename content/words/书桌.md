## Why this combination
书 "book" plus 桌 "table" gives exactly "a table for books" — the
standard word for a desk used for reading and writing.

## Notes
书桌 anchors 坐在书桌前 "sitting at the desk," 书桌灯 "desk lamp" — the
everyday word for a study desk, distinct from 餐桌 "dining table" and
办公桌 "office desk" by function.

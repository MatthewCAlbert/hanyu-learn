## Why this combination
白 "white, clear" plus 酒 "alcohol" gives exactly "clear liquor" —
naming the traditional distilled spirit by its clear appearance, as
opposed to fermented drinks like 黄酒 "yellow wine."

## Notes
白酒 anchors 茅台白酒 "Moutai baijiu" — the strong, clear distilled
spirit central to Chinese banquet culture and toasting customs, distinct
from Western "white wine" (which would instead be 白葡萄酒).

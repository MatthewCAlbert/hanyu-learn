## Why this combination
鸭 "duck" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a duck" — the suffix
adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of 兔子, 鸽子.

## Notes
鸭子 anchors 北京烤鸭 "Peking duck" (using 鸭 alone in this fixed dish
name), 一只鸭子 "a duck" — the everyday word for the bird, both live and
as food.

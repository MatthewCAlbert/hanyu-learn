## Why this combination
更 (here "to change") and 换 "to exchange, to replace" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for swapping something out.

## Notes
更换 is used for replacing parts, staff, or settings: 更换轮胎 "to
replace a tire," 更换密码 "to change a password" — more formal/written
than the everyday 换.

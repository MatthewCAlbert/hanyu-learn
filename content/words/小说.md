## Why this combination
Classically 小說 was "minor talk" — trivial street gossip and anecdote, beneath
the serious writing of history and philosophy (庄子 already uses the phrase
dismissively). That low-status "storytelling" category became the name for
prose fiction, which rose to prominence in the Ming and Qing.

## Notes
长篇小说 "novel," 短篇小说 "short story," 科幻小说 "sci-fi." The old
"insignificant" flavour is entirely gone.

## Why this combination
推 (here "to push forward") and 行 "to carry out, to do" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for implementing a policy
or system.

## Notes
推行 anchors 推行新政策 "to implement a new policy," 全国推行 "rolled out
nationwide" — always about actively putting a plan or system into
practice, typically from an authority.

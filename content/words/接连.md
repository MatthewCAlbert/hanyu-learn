## Why this combination
接 "to connect" and 连 "to link" are near-synonyms combined into an
adverb describing events happening back-to-back with no gap.

## Notes
接连 anchors 接连不断 "one after another without stopping," 接连三天
"three days running" — always emphasizes rapid, unbroken repetition of
events.

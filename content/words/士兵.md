## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 士 ("a capable man of standing," here in its
military branch — 战士, "a fighter/soldier" — covered elsewhere in this
app) and 兵 ("a soldier, a weapon," also covered elsewhere — hands
gripping an axe) both independently name a fighting man. Pairing them
gives the standard word for a soldier, specifically a rank-and-file one.

## Notes
士兵 names an ordinary soldier, as opposed to an officer (军官) — the
lowest rung of a military hierarchy, distinct from 战士, which can be
used more broadly and honorifically for any fighter for a cause.

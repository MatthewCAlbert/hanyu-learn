## Why this combination
个 "individual, a unit" (as in the general classifier 个) combined with
性 "nature, character, an inherent quality" (as in 可能性
"possibility"): the nature particular to an individual — personality,
individuality.

## Notes
个性 names what makes a person distinctive (个性很强 "a strong
personality," 有个性 "to have character/individuality") — 性格, a close
synonym, names character traits more generally, without 个性's emphasis
on standing out as distinctive.

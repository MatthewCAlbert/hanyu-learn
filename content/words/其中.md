## Why this combination
其 "that, its" (classical pronoun, see [[其他]]) + 中 "within, in the middle" →
"within that (set already mentioned)." Points back to a previously named group:
来了三十人，其中十人是学生.

## Notes
其中 must have an antecedent — it cannot open a topic. 乐在其中 "find joy in
it."

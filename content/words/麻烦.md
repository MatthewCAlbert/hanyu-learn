## Why this combination
麻, literally "hemp" and by extension "numb, tangled" (as in 麻木
"numb"), combined with 烦 "vexed, irritated, bothered" (see also 心烦):
a tangled, vexing state — the everyday word for trouble or inconvenience.
The exact path from "numb/tangled" to "troublesome" is folk-etymological
rather than securely documented.

## Notes
麻烦 works as noun ("给你添麻烦了" "sorry to trouble you"), adjective
("这件事很麻烦" "this matter is troublesome"), and verb ("麻烦你开一下门"
"could I trouble you to open the door") — the verb use is a standard
polite way to make a request.

## Why this combination
激 ("to stir up, to incite," as in this app's entry for 刺激) takes 动
("to move") as what it stirs into motion: emotions set moving, i.e.
excitement or being emotionally moved. As a verb-object-shaped compound
it behaves as one word today, but the "stirring something into motion"
logic is still visible in its parts.

Rated "semi" for 激's "to stir up" sense, one specific extension among
several the character carries (激烈, "intense"; 刺激, "to stimulate").

## Notes
激动 describes a heightened emotional state generally — happy, moved, or
agitated — context decides the flavor; it does not specify which emotion
is doing the moving.

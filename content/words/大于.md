## Why this combination
大 "big" plus the comparative preposition 于 "than" gives exactly "bigger
than" — a formal written comparison word, the mathematical/formal
equivalent of the > symbol.

## Notes
大于 is used in math and formal comparisons: A大于B "A is greater than
B" — a written/technical register, where speech would use 比…大.

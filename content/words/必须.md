## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 必 "necessarily, certainly" (必然, 必要) + 须 "must, need to"
(须知). The strong obligation modal — external necessity, stronger than
[[应该]].

## Notes
必须 (an adverb, modifies verbs: 必须走) vs. 必需 (an adjective, "essential":
必需品). Same sound, different second character. Negative: 不必 "need not,"
不能 "must not."

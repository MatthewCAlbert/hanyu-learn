## Why this combination

眼 ("eye") plus 里 ("inside, within," as in 心里) locates something inside
one's eyes: figuratively, in one's view or estimation.

## Notes

在他眼里 ("in his eyes/view") is the standard frame for stating someone's
subjective perspective, parallel to 在他心里 ("in his heart," for
feelings) and 在他看来 ("as he sees it," for opinions).

## Why this combination
联 "to connect, to link" (as in 联系 "to contact, to connect") combined
with 合 "to combine, to close together" (see also 合理, 适合): to link
and combine — to unite into a joint whole.

## Notes
联合 names a formal alliance or joint action between separate parties
(联合国 "the United Nations," 联合声明 "a joint statement") — 一起,
"together," describes simple co-presence and carries none of 联合's
formal, organisational weight.

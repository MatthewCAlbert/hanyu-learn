## Why this combination
极 "extreme, to the utmost degree" already carries the full intensifying
force; 了 here is a fixed grammatical marker that locks 极 into place as
a completed, emphatic degree complement following an adjective, rather
than contributing its own separate meaning.

## Notes
极了 always follows the adjective it intensifies — 好极了 "wonderful,"
高兴极了 "delighted" — and cannot be used before one, unlike 很 or 非常,
which both precede.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 降 ("to descend, to fall," as in 下降, "to
decline") and 落 ("to fall, to land," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 落后, "to fall behind") both independently name downward
movement. Pairing them gives the standard word for a controlled
descent.

## Notes

飞机降落 ("the plane lands") is the standard aviation frame — 降落伞
("a parachute, lit. descend + umbrella") shares this word's root for
its own controlled-fall device.

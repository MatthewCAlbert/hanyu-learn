## Why this combination
以, here marking a starting point ("from, since"), combined with 来 "to
come, [up] until now": from a point in the past, coming forward to the
present — the grammaticalized word for "since," always placed after the
time expression it marks.

## Notes
以来 always follows the reference point rather than preceding it (今年以来
"since this year," 长期以来 "for a long time now"), unlike English
"since," which comes before its time phrase.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 号 ("a mark, a sign, a designated number," as in
口号, 号召) and 码 ("a number, a code," covered elsewhere in this app)
both independently name an identifying figure. Pairing them gives the
standard everyday word for a number used to identify something.

## Notes
号码 identifies things (phone numbers, seat numbers, sizes) — for
counting or arithmetic quantities, 数字 ("numeral, digit") is the word to
reach for instead.

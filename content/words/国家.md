## Why this combination
In the classical order, 国 was the domain of a feudal lord and 家 the estate of
a minister or noble house — the two tiers of political authority. The phrase
"国与家" contracted into one word for the political community as a whole, the
modern "nation-state."

## Notes
The 家 "household" sense is nearly invisible now, but it echoes in 大家
(everyone), 国家 (nation) and 家国情怀 (patriotic sentiment).

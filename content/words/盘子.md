## Why this combination

盘 ("a plate, a tray," as in 键盘, "a keyboard [lit. key + tray]") plus
the nominal suffix 子 names the everyday tableware item: a plate.

## Notes

一盘子菜 ("a plate of food") uses 盘子 both as the dish itself and, with
盘 alone as a classifier, to count servings on a plate — the same
character doing double duty as noun and measure word.

## Why this combination
剪 "to cut (with scissors)" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a
cutting tool" — a colloquial equivalent of the more formal 剪刀 (剪 +
刀 "blade").

## Notes
剪子 anchors 一把剪子 "a pair of scissors" — an everyday, spoken-register
synonym of 剪刀, both naming the same two-bladed cutting tool.

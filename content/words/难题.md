## Why this combination
难 "hard, difficult" + 题 "problem, question, topic" (see [[问题]], 题目) → a
hard problem — an exam question or a real-world predicament: 一道难题,
出难题 "pose a tough problem," 世界性难题.

## Notes
难 + noun/verb = "hard —": [[难题]], [[难过]], [[难受]], [[难看]], [[难听]].
Opposite of a 难题 is a 送分题 "a gift question."

## Why this combination

抽 ("to draw out, to pull," as in 抽屉, "a drawer") takes 烟 ("smoke; a
cigarette") as its object: drawing smoke in — to smoke.

## Notes

抽烟 and 吸烟 are near-synonyms built on the same object 烟 with
different first verbs ("to draw" vs. "to inhale") — 吸烟 is the more
formal/written register (禁止吸烟, "no smoking" signage), while 抽烟 is
the everyday spoken word.

## Why this combination
向 "toward" plus 上 "up" gives exactly "toward up, upward" — the
figurative sense (力争上游/向上 "striving to improve") extends physical
upward direction to self-improvement or moral progress, a common metaphor
in Chinese.

## Notes
向上 anchors 向上看 "to look upward" (literal) and 积极向上 "positive and
striving to improve" (figurative, common in describing attitude or
youth) — the figurative sense is now at least as common as the literal.

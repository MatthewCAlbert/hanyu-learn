## Why this combination
回 "to return" + 国 "country" as its object (the same frame as [[回家]],
[[回到]]) — to go back to one's own country from abroad. Verb-object:
回过一次国. 学成回国 "return home after finishing one's studies."

## Notes
回国 (come back to one's country) ↔ [[出国]] (leave it). [[回家]] for home,
回校 for school.

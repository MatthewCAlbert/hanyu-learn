## Why this combination
A Tang-era transcription of Sanskrit Indu (popularised by Xuanzang), replacing
the older transcription 天竺. 印 and 度 are chosen for sound, not
for "print + degree."

## Notes
印 as the country-abbreviation in 印地语 "Hindi," 中印 "China–India." Contrast
打印, 印象, where 印 keeps "print / mark."

## Why this combination

内 ("inside," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 以内) plus 科
("a department, a branch of study," as in 科学) names the medical
department treating internal conditions: internal medicine.

## Notes

内科 contrasts with 外科 ("surgery, lit. outside department") as the two
foundational branches of hospital medicine — 内 treats what's inside
the body without cutting it open, 外 operates on it.

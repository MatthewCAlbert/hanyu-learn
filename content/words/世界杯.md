## Why this combination
世界 "world" (see its own entry) combined with 杯 "a cup" (as in 奖杯
"trophy," literally "an award cup"): the world's cup — a direct
translation of "World Cup," the international football tournament and
its trophy.

## Notes
世界杯 is a fixed proper noun following the same "[event] + 杯" pattern
used for other cup competitions (足球世界杯 "the football World Cup") —
杯 alone names any trophy-cup competition, from small local tournaments
up to this global one.

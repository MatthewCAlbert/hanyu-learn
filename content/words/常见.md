## Why this combination
常 "often, frequently" (see [[常常]], [[经常]]) + 见 "to see" (see [[看见]],
[[见到]]) → often seen, therefore common: 常见问题 "FAQ," 一种常见的错误.

## Notes
常见 = frequently encountered (of a thing / phenomenon); [[普通]] / [[一般]] =
ordinary (of quality). Opposite 罕见 "rare."

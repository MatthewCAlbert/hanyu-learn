## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 宝 ("treasure," the jade kept safe under a roof)
and 贝 ("cowrie shell," the ancient form of money) both independently name
something precious. As with many "precious thing" words, the sense
naturally extends to a precious *person* — a term of endearment for a
child or loved one.

## Notes
宝贝 as endearment (对孩子说"宝贝") is neutral tone throughout (bǎobei in
casual speech) — the literal "treasure" sense keeps the fuller tones
(bǎobèi) more consistently.

## Why this combination
睡 ("to sleep") takes 着 in its *zháo* reading — a resultative complement
marking that an action has successfully taken hold or landed (as also in
找着, "to have found [it]") — distinct from the far more common *zhe*
reading, the ongoing-action particle (拿着, "holding"). Sleep,
successfully arrived at, is exactly "to fall asleep."

Rated "semi" specifically for that reading switch: *zháo* here, not the
everyday *zhe*.

## Notes
Read 着 as *zháo* in 睡着 — a different word from the *zhe* particle most
learners meet first; 睡不着 ("unable to fall asleep") keeps the same
*zháo* reading in its negated form.

## Why this combination
太 "highest, most senior" doubled — from 太夫人, the honorific for a high
official's mother — into a term for the mistress of a household, then a polite
"Mrs" and "wife." 王太太 "Mrs Wang," 我太太 "my wife."

## Notes
Register: 太太 sounds a touch bourgeois or old-fashioned; 老婆 (colloquial),
爱人 (PRC neutral, see [[爱人]]), 妻子 (formal) are the alternatives. 老太太
"an old lady."

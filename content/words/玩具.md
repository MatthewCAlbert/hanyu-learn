## Why this combination
玩 "to play, to have fun with" (as in 玩儿 "to play") combined with 具
"an implement, a tool" (see also 工具, 家具 "furniture"): the implement
used for play — a toy.

## Notes
玩具 is the standard word for a plaything (玩具店 "toy store," 玩具车
"toy car") — 玩, alone, is the verb "to play [with something]," while
玩具 is the object itself.

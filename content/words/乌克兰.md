## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Ukraïna / Ukraine (烏克蘭). 乌, 克 and 兰 are
chosen for sound. 乌 is not "crow"; 克 is not "to be able to."

## Notes
兰 as in [[荷兰]]. Same stem in [[乌克兰语]].

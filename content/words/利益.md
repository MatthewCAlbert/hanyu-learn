## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 利 ("benefit, profit," as in 有利, 利用) and 益
("benefit," covered elsewhere in this app — water overflowing a dish, a
picture of abundance) both independently name gain or advantage. Pairing
them gives the standard formal word for benefit or interest.

## Notes
利益 leans formal/abstract (国家利益, "national interest"; 共同利益, "shared
interest") — for a casual everyday "benefit" or "advantage," 好处 is the
more conversational choice.

## Why this combination
清 "clear, limpid, unclouded" + 楚 "clear, neat, distinct" (its sense in 楚楚,
苦楚 aside). A synonym pair: visually sharp (看得清楚), and mentally sure
(我不清楚 "I'm not clear on that").

## Notes
As a resultative complement: 说清楚 "say it clearly," 想清楚 "think it through."
楚 also the name of the ancient state of Chu.

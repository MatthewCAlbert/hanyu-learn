## Why this combination
依 "to rely on, to follow" and 照 (here "according to, in the manner of")
are near-synonyms combined into a formal preposition for basing something
on a standard or rule.

## Notes
依照 introduces a basis for an action: 依照规定 "in accordance with the
regulations," 依照法律 "according to the law" — a written/formal register
word, close to 按照, both prepositions of the same function.

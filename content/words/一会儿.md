## Why this combination
会儿 (*huìr*) is a bound noun meaning "a brief span of time"; 一 gives it "one
(brief span)." Used for a short duration (等一会儿 "wait a bit") and, doubled,
for alternation: 一会儿…一会儿… "one moment… the next…".

## Notes
会儿 only occurs with a preceding number or demonstrative: 这会儿 "right now,"
那会儿 "back then," 多会儿 "when."

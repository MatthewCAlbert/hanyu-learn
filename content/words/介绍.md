## Why this combination
介 "to be in between, to act as go-between" + 绍 "to join, to carry on." To
stand between two parties and link them — hence introducing people, and by
extension presenting information about a thing (介绍产品).

## Notes
自我介绍 "introduce oneself." 绍 barely occurs outside this word (and the place
name 绍兴).

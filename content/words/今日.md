## Why this combination
今 "now, the present" and 日 "day" combine directly into "the present
day" — a formal written-register equivalent of everyday 今天.

## Notes
今日 favours headlines, formal writing, and set phrases (今日头条, a major
news app's name, literally "today's headlines"; 时至今日 "up to this
day") over casual conversation, where 今天 is standard.

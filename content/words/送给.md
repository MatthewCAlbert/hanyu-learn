## Why this combination
送 "to give (a gift), to see off, to deliver" + 给 "to give (to)" marking the
recipient (as in [[交给]], 卖给) → hand a present to someone: 这本书送给你.

## Notes
送 without 给 also works with a direct recipient (送你一本书). 送 "see off"
(送客) and "deliver" ([[送到]], [[外卖]]) are the same character.

## Why this combination

下 ("to go down, to descend") takes 楼 ("a multi-story building, a
floor," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 大楼/楼梯/上楼) as its
object: going down to a lower floor.

## Notes

下楼 pairs directly with 上楼 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the
opposite direction of the same verb-object pattern — 下/上 mark descent
and ascent on the same shared object.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 结 ("to tie, to knot together," as in 结婚,
"to marry," lit. "to tie together") and 构 ("to construct," covered
elsewhere in this app) both independently name building something by
joining parts. Pairing them gives the standard word for structure.

## Notes
结构 applies to buildings, sentences, organizations, and more (句子结构,
"sentence structure"; 组织结构, "organizational structure") — a broadly
applicable word for how parts fit together into a whole.

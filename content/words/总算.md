## Why this combination
总 "overall, altogether" and 算 "to calculate, to count" combine into
"counting it all up [in the end]" — after a long process, the tally
finally comes out as hoped, i.e. "finally, at last."

## Notes
总算 marks relief after a long wait or effort: 总算完成了 "finally got it
done," 总算松了一口气 "finally breathed a sigh of relief" — always implies
the outcome was in doubt or delayed.

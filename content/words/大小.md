## Why this combination
Antonym pair 大 "big" + 小 "small" yoked to name the whole dimension — size —
the same trick as [[多少]] (much/few → amount), 长短 (length), 高矮 (height).
这双鞋大小正好 "these shoes fit."

## Notes
Also "regardless of magnitude": 大小是个官 "an official, however minor." And
"seniority": 没大没小 "no respect for elders."

## Why this combination
事 "matter, affair" combined with 实 "solid, real, true" (as in 真实
"true, real"): a matter confirmed as real — a fact, as distinct from
opinion, rumour or supposition.

## Notes
事实上 "in fact, actually" is a fixed adverbial built directly on this
word, used to introduce what is really the case, often against an
expectation just stated.

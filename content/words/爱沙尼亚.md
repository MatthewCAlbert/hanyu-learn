## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Estonia (愛沙尼亞), via a form closer to
Est- than to Eesti. 爱 is not "love"; 沙 is not "sand."

## Notes
Same stem in [[爱沙尼亚语]]. Contrast [[爱尔兰]], another 爱- transcription.

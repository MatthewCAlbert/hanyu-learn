## Why this combination

试 ("to test," as in 考试) plus 卷 ("a scroll, a rolled paper," a
character originally naming rolled bamboo or paper documents) names the
paper on which a test is given: an exam paper.

## Notes

发试卷 ("to hand out exam papers") and 批改试卷 ("to grade exam papers")
both build on this word — names the physical paper/document, distinct
from 考试, the test-taking event itself.

## Why this combination
课 (here "subject of study") plus 题 "topic, question" gives exactly "a
topic to be studied" — the standard word for a research problem or
assigned task.

## Notes
课题 anchors 研究课题 "research topic," 重要课题 "an important issue (to
address)" — always a substantial matter requiring investigation or
solution, more formal than 问题 "problem/question" in general.

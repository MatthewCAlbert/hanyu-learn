## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 逐 ("one by one, to pursue in sequence,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 逐渐, "gradually") and 步 ("a
step," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 步行) both independently
name proceeding one unit at a time. Pairing them gives the standard
adverb for a step-by-step process.

## Notes

逐步实现 ("to gradually realize/achieve") and 逐步改善 ("to progressively
improve") both build on this word — 逐步 emphasizes discrete, sequenced
steps, while 逐渐 (its close near-synonym, covered elsewhere in this
app) emphasizes smooth, continuous gradualness.

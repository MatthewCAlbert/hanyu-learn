## Why this combination
围 "to encircle" and 绕 "to wind around" (see 绕's own etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for orbiting or centring on
something.

## Notes
围绕 covers literal orbit (地球围绕太阳转 "the Earth revolves around the
sun") and figurative focus (围绕这个话题 "centring on this topic," 围绕
核心问题 "focused on the core issue") — always organized around one
central point.

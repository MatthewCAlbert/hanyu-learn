## Why this combination
香 "fragrant, aromatic" (as in 香水 "perfume") combined with 蕉, a
character coined specifically for the banana/plantain plant (its 艹
"grass/plant" radical marks it as a plant name): the fragrant fruit of
the 蕉 plant — banana.

## Notes
香蕉 is a transparent "quality + plant-name" compound — unlike 苹果's
"plant-name + fruit" pattern, 香蕉 leads with the fragrance quality; 蕉
alone appears in related compounds like 芭蕉 (a related banana-family
plant).

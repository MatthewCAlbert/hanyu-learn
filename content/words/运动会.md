## Why this combination

运动 ("sports, to exercise") plus 会 ("a meeting, a gathering," as in
开会) names a gathering organized around sports: a sports meet.

## Notes

学校运动会 ("school sports day") and 奥林匹克运动会 ("the Olympic Games,"
usually shortened to 奥运会) both build on this word — 运动会 names the
organized event itself, distinct from 运动, the general activity of
exercising.

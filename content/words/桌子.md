## Why this combination
桌 "table" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]]) making the countable
everyday noun. The bare 桌 survives in compounds: 书桌 desk, 饭桌 dining table,
桌面 tabletop / desktop.

## Notes
Measure word 张 (for flat-topped things): 一张桌子.

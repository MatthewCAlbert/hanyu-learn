## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 总 ("overall, in total," as in 总理, "premier
[lit. overall + manage]") and 共 ("together, in common," as in 公共,
"public") both independently name a combined whole. Pairing them gives
the everyday adverb for a grand total.

## Notes

总共多少钱 ("how much in total") is a standard everyday question — 一共 is
a close near-synonym built the same way, with 一 "one/altogether"
instead of 总.

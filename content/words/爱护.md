## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 爱 ("to love") and 护 ("to protect," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 护士) both independently name caring
for something. Pairing them gives the standard word for cherishing and
protecting.

## Notes

爱护环境 ("to protect the environment") and 爱护公共财产 ("to take care
of public property") both build on this word — 爱护 implies affectionate
protective care, distinct from plain 保护, which can be purely
functional protection without any warmth.

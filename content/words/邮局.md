## Why this combination

邮 ("post, mail," as in 邮票, "a stamp") plus 局 ("an office, a bureau,"
a common suffix for government-run administrative offices) names the
office that handles mail: a post office.

## Notes

去邮局寄信 ("to go to the post office to mail a letter") is the standard
everyday phrase — 邮局 shares its 邮 "mail" root with 邮票, 邮箱, and
邮件, all covered elsewhere in this app.

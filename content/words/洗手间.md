## Why this combination
洗手 "to wash one's hands" + 间 "room" — a polite euphemism, exactly like
English "restroom" or "washroom." Parse 洗手 + 间, not 洗 + 手间.

## Notes
Register ladder: 洗手间 / 卫生间 (polite, public), 厕所 (plain), 茅房 (crude).
On signs, 男 / 女 or 男厕 / 女厕.

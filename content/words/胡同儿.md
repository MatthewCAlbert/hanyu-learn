## Why this combination
胡同 is widely believed to derive from a Mongolian word for a water
well or a settlement built around one (transcribed into Chinese via
sound, not sense) entering Mandarin during the Yuan dynasty, when
Mongolian rule reshaped Beijing's street planning; 胡 and 同 carry no
combined meaning of their own here, and the colloquial retroflex -儿
suffix is simply added in speech.

## Notes
胡同儿 anchors 北京胡同 "Beijing's hutongs" — the narrow traditional
alleyways between siheyuan courtyard houses, an iconic feature of old
Beijing urban geography.

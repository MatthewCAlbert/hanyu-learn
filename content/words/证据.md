## Why this combination
证 "to prove, evidence" (see also 证明) combined with 据 "to rely on, a
basis, grounds" (as in 根据 "according to, on the basis of"): what one
relies on to prove something — evidence.

## Notes
证据 names material or testimony offered to establish a fact, especially
in a legal or investigative context (找到证据 "to find evidence," 证据
不足 "insufficient evidence") — 证明, its close relative, is the verb
"to prove" or the document that does the proving, rather than the raw
material itself.

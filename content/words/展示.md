## Why this combination
展 "to unfold, to spread out for viewing" and 示 "to show" reinforce each
other into the formal verb for putting something on display.

## Notes
展示 favours formal contexts (展示会 "exhibition," 展示才能 "to showcase
one's talent") over the everyday 给...看 "to show [someone] something."

## Why this combination

开 (here "to boil," an extended sense beyond its far more common "to
open," as in 打开) plus 水 ("water") names water that has been boiled:
boiled/boiling water.

Rated "semi" for 开's "to boil" sense here, distinct from its
everyday "to open" meaning.

## Notes

喝开水 ("to drink boiled water," a common health habit in China) and
凉开水 ("cooled boiled water," lit. "cool + boiled water") both build on
this word — 开水 names water that has been made safe to drink by
boiling, not necessarily still hot.

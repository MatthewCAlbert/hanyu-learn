## Why this combination
歌 "song" (see also 唱歌, 歌手) combined with 声 "sound, voice" (see also
声明): the sound of song — singing voice, the sound of singing heard.

## Notes
歌声 names the audible sound of singing as heard by a listener (远处
传来歌声 "singing carries over from the distance," see also 传来's own
entry for this exact collocation) — 唱歌, the verb phrase "to sing,"
names the act rather than the sound produced.

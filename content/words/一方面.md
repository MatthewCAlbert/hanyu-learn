## Why this combination
一 "one" combined with 方面 "aspect, side" (方 "side," see also 全面, 前
往 in unrelated senses): one aspect [of a matter] — used to open the
first of two contrasting points.

## Notes
一方面 pairs with 另一方面 (see its own entry) to structure a two-sided
statement (一方面...另一方面... "on the one hand... on the other
hand...") — it can also appear alone when only one side needs framing
explicitly.

## Why this combination
门 "gate, door" + 票 "ticket" → the ticket that gets you through the gate, for a
park, museum, exhibition or gig. Also the price: 门票五十块.

## Notes
票 is the generic ticket morpheme: 车票, 机票, 电影票, 门票 — each named for
what it admits you to.

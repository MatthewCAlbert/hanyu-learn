## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 动 ("to move," as in 运动) and 摇 ("to shake, to
sway," as in 摇头, "to shake one's head") both independently name
unsteady motion. Pairing them gives the standard word for wavering,
literal or figurative.

## Notes

信念动摇 ("one's convictions waver") and 动摇不了 ("cannot be shaken") both
build on this word — 动摇 leans figurative for confidence, resolve, or
foundations, distinct from plain 摇, which more often describes literal
physical shaking.

## Why this combination
来 "to come" + resultative 到 "reach" — coming that has arrived at a specific
place. The 到 lets a destination follow directly: 来到北京 "arrived in Beijing,"
where bare 来 would need 来北京.

## Notes
Parallel to [[回到]] (return to). More vivid / narrative than plain 到达
"to arrive."

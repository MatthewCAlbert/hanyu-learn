## Why this combination
悲 "sorrow" (see 悲's own etymology) and 剧 "drama, play" calque the
Western dramatic genre "tragedy" — a play built around sorrow — directly
translating the Greek-derived concept rather than growing from a native
Chinese dramatic category.

## Notes
悲剧 covers both the literary genre (古希腊悲剧 "ancient Greek tragedy")
and, in everyday speech, any deeply unfortunate turn of events (真是场
悲剧 "what a tragedy") — paired with 喜剧 "comedy" as the two classical
dramatic modes.

## Why this combination
座 "a seat, a stand, a base" (座椅, and the measure word 一座山) + 位 "a place,
a slot, a position" (see [[单位]], [[水平]]'s cousin 地位) → a place to sit:
找座位, 座位号.

## Notes
座位 = a seat as a spot / slot (on a train, in a hall); [[椅子]] = a chair as an
object; 位子 (*wèizi*) is the colloquial equivalent of 座位.

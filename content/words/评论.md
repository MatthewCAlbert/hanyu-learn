## Why this combination
评 "to evaluate, to judge" and 论 "to discuss, to argue" combine directly
into the standard word for critical commentary.

## Notes
评论 spans media (新闻评论 "news commentary," 影评 shares 评 for "film
review") and everyday internet use (在帖子下评论 "to comment on a post") —
judgement expressed in words, formal or casual.

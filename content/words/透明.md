## Why this combination

透 ("to penetrate, to pass through," as in 透气, "to let air through")
plus 明 ("bright, clear," as in 说明, "to explain [lit. speak + clear]")
names something light can pass through clearly: transparent.

Rated "semi" for the compositional but figurative-leaning result — the
parts combine sensibly, but "透明" as a fixed single concept is more than
a literal sum of "penetrate" and "clear."

## Notes

透明 covers both literal transparency (透明的玻璃, "transparent glass")
and figurative openness (信息透明, "information transparency," as in
government or corporate openness) — the same "can be seen through"
image applied to both.

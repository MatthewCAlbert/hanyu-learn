## Why this combination
好 "good" doubled. Reduplicating a one-syllable adjective before a verb
is a standard way to turn it into a vivid manner adverb in Mandarin —
"good-good [do something]" reads naturally as "do it properly, do it
well."

## Notes
好好 is almost always followed directly by a verb it modifies — 好好
学习 "study properly," 好好休息 "rest well" — rather than standing alone
as a plain adjective, which stays simply 好.

## Why this combination
大 "big, major" and 事 "matter, affair" combine directly: a matter of
major significance, as opposed to 小事 "a trivial matter."

## Notes
大事 covers personal milestones (人生大事 "a major life event," often
implying marriage) and public events alike (国家大事 "matters of state") —
scale of importance is the common thread.

## Why this combination
值 "value, worth" (as in 价值 "value") combined with 得 in its
achievement-complement sense "to manage to, to attain": to attain a
[given] value — to be worth the cost, effort or attention put in.

## Notes
值得 is followed directly by a verb with no 去/要 needed (值得看 "worth
watching," 值得一试 "worth a try"); its negative 不值得 works the same way
and is more common in speech than 没有价值.

## Why this combination
家 "home, family" (as in 家庭) combined with 具 "an implement, a tool"
(see also 工具, 玩具): the implements of the home — furniture.

## Notes
家具 names household furnishings collectively (家具店 "furniture store,"
买家具 "to buy furniture") — individual pieces are named separately
(桌子 "table," 椅子 "chair"), with 家具 serving as the umbrella category.

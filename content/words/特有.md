## Why this combination
特 "special, distinctive" plus 有 "to have" gives exactly "to specially
have (something no one else has)" — the standard word for a trait unique
to a specific person, place, or group.

## Notes
特有 always modifies what belongs uniquely to something: 中国特有的植物
"a plant unique to China," 女性特有的细腻 "sensitivity characteristic of
women" — close to 独有, both stressing exclusivity.

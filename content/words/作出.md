## Why this combination
作 ("to do, to make") takes 出 as a resultative complement marking that
the making produced a visible, finished result: something brought forth
into existence.

## Notes
作出 pairs with abstract nouns that name a produced result (作出决定, "to
make a decision"; 作出贡献, "to make a contribution") — it does not
combine with concrete physical objects, where plain 做 or 造 is used
instead.

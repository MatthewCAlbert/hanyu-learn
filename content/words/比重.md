## Why this combination
比 "to compare" plus 重 "weight" gives exactly "weight in comparison" —
the standard physics term for relative density, extended figuratively to
proportion within a whole.

## Notes
比重 spans physics (物质的比重 "the specific gravity of a substance") and
economics/statistics (占很大比重 "accounts for a large proportion") —
both senses measure something's weight or share relative to a whole.

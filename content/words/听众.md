## Why this combination
听 "to listen" combined with 众 "the masses, a crowd" (see also 观众,
group): the crowd doing the listening — an audience, listeners.

## Notes
听众 names those listening to a broadcast, speech or lecture (广播
听众 "radio listeners") — 观众, its close parallel, is for a watching
audience, and 读者 for a reading one, together forming a matched set of
audience-by-sense words.

## Why this combination

大 (here an intensifying "greatly," as in 大概, "roughly, probably")
plus 多 ("many, most") names the great majority of a group: mostly.

## Notes

大多 sits before the verb as an adverb (学生大多来自本地, "the students
mostly come from the local area") — 大多数 adds 数 ("number") to make an
explicit noun, "the majority."

## Why this combination
得 (*dé*) "to get, to obtain" + directional 出 "out" — a result coming out of a
process of reasoning or calculation: 得出结论, 得出答案.

## Notes
得出 is for outcomes of thinking / working-out; [[得到]] is "get / receive"
generally. 出 as "produce / bring out" also in [[提出]], 看出, 想出.

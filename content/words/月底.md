## Why this combination

月 ("month") plus 底 ("the bottom, the end," as in 年底, "year end")
names the tail end of a month.

## Notes

月初 ("the start of the month") is its direct opposite, built the same
way with 初 "beginning" instead of 底 — 年底/年初 and 月底/月初 form a
matched pair of time-frame pairs.

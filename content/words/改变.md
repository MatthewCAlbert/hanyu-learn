## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 改 "to correct, to alter" (修改, 改正) + 变 "to change, to become
different." Transitive (改变主意 "change one's mind") and intransitive
(情况改变了).

## Notes
改变 = alter something to a different state; 变化 = undergo change (a noun /
intransitive); [[变成]] = turn into (takes a result).

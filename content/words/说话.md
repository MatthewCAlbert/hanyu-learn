## Why this combination
Verb 说 "to say" with its generic object 话 "words, speech" — a verb-object
phrase, so it splits: 说了半天话 "talked for ages," 没说几句话 "said only a few
words." Bare 说 needs a real object (说中文, 说什么); 说话 is "speak" in the
abstract.

## Notes
Same shape in [[唱歌]] (sing + song), [[走路]] (walk + road). 说 also = "to
tell off": 我妈说我了.

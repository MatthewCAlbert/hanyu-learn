## Why this combination
自 "self" (see also 自己, 自动) combined with 主 "host, master, the one
in charge" (see also 主人, 主动): to be one's own master — independent,
self-determining.

## Notes
自主 describes acting or deciding without outside control (自主决定
"to decide independently," 自主品牌 "an independent/domestically-owned
brand") — it stresses freedom from external control specifically,
distinct from 独立's broader "independent, standing alone."

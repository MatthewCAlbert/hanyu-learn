## Why this combination
神 (here "spirit, expression," as in 眼神 "the look in one's eyes") and
情 "feeling" combine into "the feeling shown in one's expression" — the
standard word for what a face reveals of inner state.

## Notes
神情 anchors 神情严肃 "a serious expression," 神情紧张 "a tense look" —
close to 表情, though 神情 leans toward the emotional quality read from a
face rather than a specific expression like a smile or frown.

## Why this combination
更 "even more, further" combined with 加 "to add": to add still further
[to an already-present degree] — a comparative degree adverb reinforcing
bare 更.

## Notes
更加 is the fuller, slightly more formal form of 更 and is used the same
way, directly before an adjective or verb, usually in an explicit or
implied comparison (今天更加冷 "today is even colder [than yesterday]").

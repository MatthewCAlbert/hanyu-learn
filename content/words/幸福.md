## Why this combination
幸 "good fortune, luck" and 福 "blessing, good fortune" (recall its
etymology: hands raising an offering at the altar) are close synonyms,
paired into a word for deep, sustained wellbeing rather than a single
lucky moment.

## Notes
幸福 names a settled, lasting state of contentment (幸福的生活 "a happy
life"); 高兴 and 快乐 both name a more immediate, momentary feeling of
happiness by comparison.

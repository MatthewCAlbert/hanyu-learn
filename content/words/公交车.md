## Why this combination
公交 is clipped from 公共交通 "public transport" (公共 public + [[交通]]
transport) + 车 "vehicle" → a public-transport vehicle, i.e. a city bus. The
modern everyday word, shorter than 公共汽车 (see [[公共汽车]]).

## Notes
Often clipped further to 公交 (坐公交). 公交卡 "transit card," 公交站
"bus stop."

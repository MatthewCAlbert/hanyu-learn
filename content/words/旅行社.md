## Why this combination
旅行 "to travel" (see its own entry) combined with 社 "an association,
an agency, a society" (as in 报社 "newspaper agency"): the agency for
travel — a travel agency.

## Notes
旅行社 names the business that arranges travel bookings and tours
(找旅行社 "to use a travel agency") — 社, alone, is productive across
many organisation names (报社, 出版社 "publishing house"), functioning
much like English "-agency" or "-house."

## Why this combination
伟 "grand, remarkable, outstanding" (as in 伟人 "a great figure")
combined with 大 "big, great": grand and great together — worthy of
admiration on a large scale.

## Notes
伟大 praises achievements and figures of historic significance (伟大的
成就 "a great achievement," 伟大的人物 "a great figure") — it carries more
weight than plain 大 "big" or 好 "good" and is not used for everyday
"greatness" in a casual sense.

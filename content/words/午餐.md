## Why this combination
午 "noon" (see [[中午]], [[上午]], [[下午]]) + 餐 "a meal" (see [[早餐]],
[[晚餐]]). The 餐 series is the more formal register beside 午饭 (see
[[午饭]]).

## Notes
工作午餐 "working lunch," 午餐时间. 餐 as "-meal / dining" also in [[快餐]],
[[西餐]], 餐厅.

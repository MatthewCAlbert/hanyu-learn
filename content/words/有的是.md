## Why this combination
有 "to have" combined with 的是, a construction marking emphatic
sufficiency ("what there is, is..."): to have [what there] is [plenty
of] — a fixed colloquial phrase for abundance.

## Notes
有的是 stresses that something is available in plentiful, unworried
supply (时间有的是 "there's plenty of time," 有的是办法 "there's no
shortage of ways [to do it]") — it is more emphatic and reassuring in
tone than plain 很多 "a lot of."

## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Malaysia (馬來西亞). 马来 is the same stem as
[[马来语]]; 西亚 finishes the transcription. Not "horse + come + west + Asia."

## Notes
马来 also writes the Malay people; 马来西亚 is the state.

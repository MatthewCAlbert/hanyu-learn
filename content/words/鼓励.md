## Why this combination
鼓 (in its "to rouse, to stir up" sense, from drumming as a call to
action) and 励 "to encourage, to spur on" reinforce each other into the
everyday word for encouragement.

## Notes
鼓励 is the standard verb/noun for encouragement across contexts:
鼓励孩子 "to encourage a child," 政策鼓励 "policy incentives" — always
positive, spurring someone toward action.

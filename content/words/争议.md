## Why this combination
争 "to contend, to argue" and 议 "to discuss, opinion" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for a contested issue.

## Notes
争议 works as noun ("有争议" "to be controversial," 引发争议 "to spark
controversy") and, less commonly, as verb — the neutral formal term for
disagreement over facts or decisions, distinct from 争吵, which implies a
heated verbal quarrel.

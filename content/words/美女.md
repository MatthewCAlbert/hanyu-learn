## Why this combination
美 ("beautiful") plus 女 ("woman") names exactly what it says: a
beautiful woman. Fully compositional.

## Notes
美女 has drifted, in casual modern speech, into a generic friendly form
of address for a young woman regardless of actual looks — much like
"miss" softened into politeness rather than literal description.

## Why this combination
偶 here carries its "by chance, occasional" sense (see 偶's own
etymology) with 尔, a classical adverb-forming suffix (as in 偶尔's
cousin 忽尔). Together: happening only by occasional chance.

## Notes
偶尔 sits at the low end of a frequency scale opposite 经常/总是
"often/always" — 偶尔吃一次 "to eat it once in a while" — a common,
everyday frequency adverb.

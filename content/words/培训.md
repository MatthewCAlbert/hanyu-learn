## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 培 ("to cultivate," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 训 ("to instruct," covered in this app's entry for 教训) both
independently name developing someone's ability. Pairing them gives the
standard word for structured, formal training.

## Notes
培训 leans toward formal, professional skill-building (职业培训,
"vocational training") — for developing a person's character or general
growth more broadly, 培养 (covered elsewhere in this app) is the wider
word.

## Why this combination
持 "to hold, to maintain, to sustain" (as in 坚持 "to persist") combined
with 续 "to continue, to connect on" (as in 继续 "to continue"): to
hold and keep connecting onward — to persist without break.

## Notes
持续 describes a state or process continuing over a stretch of time
(持续发展 "sustainable development," 持续下雨 "rain continuing without
let-up") — 继续, its close relative, more often describes resuming or
carrying on an activity after or despite interruption.

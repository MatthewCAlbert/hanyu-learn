## Why this combination
前 "front, ahead, before" combined with the locative suffix 面 — the
last of the core "direction word + 面" set alongside 后面, 上面, 下面,
里面 and 外面 — naming the space ahead of something.

## Notes
前面 covers spatial "in front" (前面有一个公园 "there's a park up
ahead") and, in writing, "the preceding [material]" (前面提到的问题
"the problem mentioned earlier/above") — 前边 is a colloquial synonym
limited to the spatial sense.

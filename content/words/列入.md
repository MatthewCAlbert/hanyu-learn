## Why this combination

列 ("a row, to arrange in order," covered elsewhere in this app as part
of 列车) plus 入 ("to enter," as in 收入) names entering something into
an ordered list: to include.

## Notes

列入计划 ("to include in a plan") and 列入名单 ("to place on a list") both
build on this word — 列入 always implies formal inclusion in a
structured list or plan, distinct from 包含, the more general word for
"to contain/include."

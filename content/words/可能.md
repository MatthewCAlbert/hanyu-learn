## Why this combination
可 "-able, worth" + 能 "able, can" — two ability morphemes stacked to mean "it
can be that…", i.e. possible. Works as adverb (他可能不来), adjective (可能的
结果) and noun (有这个可能).

## Notes
不可能 "impossible." Contrast 也许 / 或许 (perhaps — adverb only) and 大概
(probably).

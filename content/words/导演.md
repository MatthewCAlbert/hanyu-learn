## Why this combination
导 "to guide, to lead, to direct" (see also 领导) combined with 演 "to
act out, to perform" (see also 表演, 演出): to guide a performance — to
direct, or the person who does so.

## Notes
导演 works as both verb ("导演一部电影" "to direct a film") and noun
("他是有名的导演" "he's a famous director") without changing form — the
same verb/noun pattern seen in 演出 and 表演, all built on 演.

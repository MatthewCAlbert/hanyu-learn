## Why this combination
十 "ten" (standing for a complete, full count) and 足 "sufficient, full"
combine into "fully to the count of ten" — nothing lacking, i.e.
completely full or pure.

## Notes
十足 intensifies nouns and adjectives to their fullest degree: 信心十足
"brimming with confidence," 十足的傻瓜 "an absolute fool" — always
emphasizing totality, positive or negative.

## Why this combination
抓 "to grab, to claw at, to seize" combined with the resultative
complement 住 "to hold firm, to fix in place" (as in 记住 "to remember,"
literally "to remember and hold fast"): to grab and successfully hold —
to catch, to seize.

## Notes
抓住 takes both physical objects (抓住绳子 "to grab hold of the rope")
and abstract ones (抓住机会 "to seize the opportunity") — the resultative
住 marks that the grabbing succeeded, distinguishing it from bare 抓,
which only names the action.

## Why this combination

吸 ("to inhale, to breathe in," as in 呼吸) takes 烟 ("smoke; a
cigarette") as its object: inhaling smoke — to smoke.

## Notes

吸烟 and 抽烟 (covered elsewhere in this app) share the same object 烟
but differ in first verb and register — 吸烟 is the formal/written word
seen on signage (禁止吸烟, "no smoking"), while 抽烟 is the everyday
spoken word.

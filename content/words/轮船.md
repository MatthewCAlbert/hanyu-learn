## Why this combination

轮 ("a wheel," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 轮椅/轮子 — here
extended to a paddle wheel) plus 船 ("a boat, a ship") names a boat
driven by a paddle wheel: a steamship, and by extension any large ship.

Rated "semi" for 轮's historical "paddle wheel" reference, a detail
mostly lost now that the word covers modern engine-driven ships too.

## Notes

坐轮船 ("to travel by ship") and 轮船公司 ("a shipping company") both
build on this word — the everyday general word for a large ship,
regardless of its actual propulsion method today.

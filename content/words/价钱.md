## Why this combination
价 "price, cost" (see also 价格, 价值) combined with 钱 "money" (as in
钱包 "wallet"): the money-price of something — price.

## Notes
价钱 is the everyday, colloquial word for the price one actually pays,
often negotiable (讲价钱 "to haggle over the price") — 价格, its formal
relative, is used in written and economic contexts for a fixed,
official price.

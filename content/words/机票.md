## Why this combination
机 clipped from 飞机 "aeroplane" + 票 "ticket." One of the 票 family with 车票,
[[门票]], 电影票. 订机票 "book a flight," 打折机票 "discounted fare."

## Notes
电子机票 "e-ticket." The boarding pass is 登机牌.

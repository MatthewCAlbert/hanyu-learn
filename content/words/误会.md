## Why this combination

误 ("a mistake, in error," as in 错误) plus 会 (here "to understand, to
grasp," a sense related to 理解/领会 rather than "to meet/can") names
grasping something mistakenly: to misunderstand.

## Notes

误会 doubles as noun and verb (产生误会, "to give rise to a
misunderstanding"; 你误会我了, "you've misunderstood me") — the same
pattern seen in other verb-object-shaped compounds like 结婚.

## Why this combination
得 "to be able to" and 以 (classical "by means of") combine into a
formal connective marking that some prior condition makes an action
possible — 以's grammatical role here is not obvious from its everyday
uses.

## Notes
得以 always follows the enabling condition: 由于大家的帮助，问题得以解决
"thanks to everyone's help, the problem could be resolved" — a formal
written-register alternative to everyday 能够.

## Why this combination
照 "to shine light on; to photograph" (照相, 拍照) + 片 "a thin flat piece,
a card" (卡片, 名片). A "photographed card" — a print. 拍照片 / 照相 "take a
photo."

## Notes
片 as "-card / thin sheet" also in [[图片]], [[影片]], 明信片. Digital images
are more often 图片 or 照片 both.

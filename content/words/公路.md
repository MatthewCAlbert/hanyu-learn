## Why this combination
公 "public" (see [[公园]], [[公司]]) + 路 "road" → a public through-road, as
against a private track or a city street ([[马路]]). 高速公路 "expressway,"
国道 / 省道 are its classes.

## Notes
路 "road" also in [[马路]], [[道路]], [[路口]], [[路上]]. 公 "public" here
contrasts with 私 "private."

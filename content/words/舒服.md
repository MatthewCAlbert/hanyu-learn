## Why this combination
舒 "to stretch out, to unfurl, to relax" (舒展, 舒适) + 服 here "at ease,
settled, agreeable" (a sense also in 舒服, 不服) → relaxed and settled, in body
or mood: 床很舒服, 今天不舒服 "not feeling well."

## Notes
不舒服 covers both "uncomfortable" and "unwell." Near 舒适 (of surroundings,
more formal) and 难受 (see [[难受]], the opposite feeling).

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 维 ("to maintain, to hold together," as in 维护
— part of the 纟-thread semantic family covered elsewhere in this app
alongside 纷/络/统/综) and 修 ("to repair, to fix," as in 修理) both
independently name upkeep. Pairing them gives the standard technical word
for maintenance and repair.

## Notes

维修 leans toward mechanical/technical upkeep (维修汽车, "to service a
car"; 维修站, "repair station") — a narrower, more professional register
than everyday 修理.

## Why this combination
爱 "to love" + 人 "person" → "the one loved." In the PRC this became the
standard neutral word for one's husband or wife (我爱人是老师), replacing the
"feudal" 太太 / 丈夫. Outside the mainland it keeps its literal "lover /
sweetheart" sense.

## Notes
A genuine cross-strait trap: 我爱人 to a mainlander means "my spouse"; to a
Taiwanese it can sound like "my mistress." Neutral alternatives: 配偶 (formal),
另一半 (colloquial).

## Why this combination
才 "talent" (see also 人才) combined with 能 "ability, capability" (as in
可能 "possible," 功能 "function"): innate talent combined with ability —
one's capacity to do something well.

## Notes
才能 names a person's demonstrated skill or capability (有才能 "to be
talented," 领导才能 "leadership ability") — 能力, its close relative, is
the more neutral, general word for "ability" without 才能's connotation
of a special, standout talent.

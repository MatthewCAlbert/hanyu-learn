## Why this combination
奶 "milk" (as in 牛奶) combined with 茶 "tea": tea made with milk — milk
tea.

## Notes
奶茶 names the milk-and-tea drink, now especially associated with
sweetened bubble tea shops (奶茶店 "milk tea shop") — parallel in
formation to 红茶 and 绿茶, its close relatives, which name tea by color
rather than by an added ingredient.

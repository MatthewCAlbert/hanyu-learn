## Why this combination
签 "to sign, to inscribe" (see 签's own etymology — a bamboo slip once
used for marking or drawing lots) acting on the object 名 "name" gives
exactly "to sign one's name."

## Notes
签名 works as verb ("to sign," 请在这里签名 "please sign here") and noun
("a signature," 签名档 "email signature/footer") — 签字 is a near-synonym
used more in legal/official contexts.

## Why this combination

实 ("real, actual," as in 事实/现实) plus 用 ("to use, useful") names
something whose usefulness is real and actual: practical.

## Notes

实用性 ("practicality") and 实用主义 ("pragmatism") both build on this
word — 实用 always emphasizes function over form or theory, distinct
from 好看, "good-looking," or 理论, "theoretical."

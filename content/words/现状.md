## Why this combination
现 "present, current" and 状 "condition, shape" combine directly: the
shape a situation currently takes.

## Notes
现状 anchors 改变现状 "to change the status quo," 维持现状 "to maintain the
current state" — a formal term for describing an existing state of
affairs, especially one open to question or change.

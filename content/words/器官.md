## Why this combination

器 ("a vessel, an implement," as in 机器) plus 官 (here "a functional
part," an extended sense beyond its far more common "official," as in
五官, "the facial features/sense organs") names a functional body part:
an organ.

Rated "semi" for 官's extended "functional bodily part" sense here,
distinct from its everyday "government official" meaning.

## Notes

器官 names any bodily organ generically (内脏器官, "internal organs");
五官 specifically covers the face's sense organs (eyes, ears, nose,
mouth, and — depending on the count — eyebrows or tongue).

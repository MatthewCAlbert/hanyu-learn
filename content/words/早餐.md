## Why this combination
早 "early" + 餐 "a meal" (餐厅, 用餐). The 餐 series — 早餐 / [[午餐]] / [[晚餐]]
— is the slightly more formal register beside 早饭 / 午饭 / 晚饭 (see
[[早饭]]).

## Notes
吃早餐, 早餐店. 餐 as "-meal / dining" also in [[快餐]], [[西餐]], [[中餐]],
餐馆.

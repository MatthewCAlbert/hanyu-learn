## Why this combination
听 "to listen" + resultative 见 "to perceive" — listening that registered. The
sound counterpart of [[看见]] (look + perceive). 我听了，但没听见 "I listened
but didn't hear it" is a normal thing to say.

## Notes
听到 is the newer synonym with the 到 complement. Bare 听 is the deliberate act;
听见 / 听到 report the outcome.

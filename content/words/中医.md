## Why this combination
中 abbreviates 中国 (see [[中国]], [[中文]]) + 医 "medicine, healing, doctor"
(see [[医生]], [[医院]]) → Chinese medicine as a system, and by extension a
doctor trained in it. Coined to contrast with 西医 (see [[西医]]) once Western
medicine arrived.

## Notes
中医 covers both the discipline (看中医, 学中医) and the person. Its remedies are
中药; Western drugs are 西药.

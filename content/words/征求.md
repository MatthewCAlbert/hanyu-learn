## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 征 ("to seek, to levy," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 征服, "to conquer") and 求 ("to seek, to request," as
in 要求, "to demand/require") both independently name actively asking
for something. Pairing them gives the formal word for soliciting input.

## Notes

征求意见 ("to solicit opinions") and 征求同意 ("to seek consent") both
build on this word — 征求 always implies formally inviting input from
others, especially before making a decision.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 政 ("governance, affairs of state," as in 政府)
and 治 (here in its "to govern" sense, covered in this app's hanzi entry —
originally the name of a river, extended via flood control to
governance). Two words for governing paired into the standard modern noun
for the whole domain of governing a state.

## Notes
政治 names the domain/activity itself; the institution that carries it out
is 政府 (government) — the two share 政 but are not interchangeable.

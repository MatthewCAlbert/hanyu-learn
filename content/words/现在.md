## Why this combination
现 is "to appear / present," 在 is "to be at / exist." Together: the time that
is presently at hand — now. Once you read 现 as "present" rather than its other
sense "to show," the compound is clear.

## Notes
Parallel to 正在 (right now, in the middle of), which is adverbial, where 现在
is a noun of time and can be a sentence topic: 现在几点？

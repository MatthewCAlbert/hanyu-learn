## Why this combination

讲 ("to speak, to lecture," as in 讲话) plus 座 ("a seat," as in 座位)
names a seated occasion for lecturing: a lecture, a seminar.

Rated "semi" for 座's contribution here — naming the occasion by the
listeners' seats rather than describing the speech itself.

## Notes

学术讲座 ("an academic lecture") and 系列讲座 ("a lecture series") both
build on this word — 讲座 names a single organized event, distinct from
授课/讲课, the general verb for "to give a lecture/lesson."

## Why this combination
年 "year" and 龄 (age, marked with the tooth radical from the ancient
practice of judging age by tooth wear) are near-synonyms combined into
the standard, neutral word for age.

## Notes
年龄 is the formal/written register; 岁数 and 年纪 are the more common
spoken alternatives, with 年纪 also carrying a slight connotation of
advancing years.

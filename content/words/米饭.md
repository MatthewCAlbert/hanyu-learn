## Why this combination
米 is the raw grain, 饭 the cooked staple (and, loosely, "a meal"). 米饭 pins 饭
down to specifically steamed rice — needed because 饭 alone can mean any meal
(see [[吃饭]]) and 饭 in a restaurant might arrive as noodles.

## Notes
一碗米饭 "a bowl of rice." Contrast 炒饭 (fried rice), 稀饭 (congee).

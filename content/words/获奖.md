## Why this combination

获 ("to obtain," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 获取) takes 奖
("a prize, an award," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 奖金)
as its object: to win a prize.

## Notes

获奖作品 ("an award-winning work") and 获奖者 ("a prize winner") both build
on this word — 获奖 names the act/state of winning, distinct from 颁奖
("to present an award," the giving side of the same transaction).

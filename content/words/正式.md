## Why this combination
正 "correct, proper, standard" combined with 式 "form, pattern, style"
(as in 方式 "manner, method"): conforming to the proper, standard
form — formal, official, as opposed to provisional or casual.

## Notes
正式 marks something as officially confirmed rather than merely
polite-sounding (正式员工 "a permanent/formal employee," 正式通知 "an
official notice") — its opposite, 非正式, is "informal, unofficial."

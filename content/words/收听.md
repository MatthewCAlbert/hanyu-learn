## Why this combination
收 "to receive [a broadcast signal]" (see also 收看, 收音机) combined
with 听 "to listen": to receive and listen — to tune in.

## Notes
收听 is the exact radio parallel of 收看 "to watch (TV)" (收听广播 "to
listen to a broadcast") — the two words divide the senses cleanly
between hearing and seeing broadcast media.

## Why this combination

用 ("to use") plus 途 ("a path, a way," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 前途/途中) names the way in which something is used: its
application or purpose.

Rated "semi" for 途's figurative "way/path" contribution here, applied
to abstract usage rather than a literal route.

## Notes

多种用途 ("multiple uses") and 用途广泛 ("widely applicable") both build on
this word — 用途 names what something is used *for*, distinct from 使用,
the verb "to use" something.

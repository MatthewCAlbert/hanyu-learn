## Why this combination
上 "up, onto" combined with the directional complement 来 "to come,
towards the speaker": to move upward towards the speaker — mirroring
上去's "upward, away from the speaker" (see its entry).

## Notes
上来 also serves as a general achievement complement after verbs of
skill or recall (说不上来 "can't quite put it into words," 想不上来
"can't quite recall") — an extended, non-directional use worth
recognising alongside the literal "come up" sense.

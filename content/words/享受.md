## Why this combination
享 "to enjoy [the benefit of]" and 受 "to receive" reinforce each other:
receiving and taking pleasure in something at once.

## Notes
享受 covers pleasures both earned and entitled: 享受生活 "to enjoy life,"
and 享受待遇 "to enjoy [certain] benefits/treatment" — the latter closer
to "to be entitled to" than to simple pleasure.

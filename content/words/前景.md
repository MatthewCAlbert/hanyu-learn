## Why this combination
前 "front" and 景 "scene, view" combine into "the scene in front" — the
literal art/photography sense (foreground) extends metaphorically to
"what lies ahead," i.e. future prospects, a spatial-to-temporal metaphor.

## Notes
前景 is now used mostly figuratively: 发展前景 "development prospects,"
就业前景 "job prospects" — the literal art-composition sense (foreground,
opposite of 背景 "background") is comparatively rare in daily speech.

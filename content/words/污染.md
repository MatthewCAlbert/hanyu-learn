## Why this combination
污 "filthy, dirty" (see 污's own etymology — stagnant water) and 染 "to
dye, to stain, to be tainted by" (see 染's own etymology) combine
directly: to stain something with filth.

## Notes
污染 covers environmental pollution (空气污染 "air pollution," 环境污染
"environmental pollution") and, figuratively, corrupting influence
(精神污染 "spiritual/ideological pollution," a term from 1980s Chinese
political discourse).

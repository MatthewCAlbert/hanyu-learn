## Why this combination
农 "agriculture, farming" (see also 农民) combined with 业 "a trade, a
field of endeavour" (see also 工业, 商业): farming as a field of
endeavour — agriculture as a sector.

## Notes
农业 names the agricultural sector as a whole (农业发展 "agricultural
development," 农业社会 "agrarian society") — 农民, its close relative,
names the people who work in it, not the sector itself.

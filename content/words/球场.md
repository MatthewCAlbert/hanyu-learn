## Why this combination
球 "ball" + 场 (*chǎng*) "an open ground, an arena" (see [[广场]], [[机场]],
[[商场]]) → the marked ground for a ball game: 篮球场, 足球场, 网球场.

## Notes
场 is *chǎng* here. The venue-suffix set: 球场, 操场 "sports field,"
[[体育场]] "stadium," [[停车场]] "car park."

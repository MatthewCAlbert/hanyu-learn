## Why this combination
关 (a gate/checkpoint) and 键 (the bolt or pin that locks a mechanism
closed) combine into "the one bolt a gate depends on" — the single point
that everything else hinges on. Once you know 键's older "bolt, linchpin"
sense, the leap to "crucial point" is short.

## Notes
关键 works as a noun (问题的关键 "the crux of the problem") and an
adjective (关键时刻 "a critical moment") — always marking the one factor
that decides everything else.

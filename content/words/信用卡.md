## Why this combination
信用 "credit, trustworthiness" (信 trust + 用 use — "trust extended for use";
see [[信心]], [[信号]]) + 卡 "card" (a phonetic borrowing of English "card").
Parse 信用 + 卡. 刷信用卡 "pay by credit card."

## Notes
卡 (*kǎ*) "card" also in [[银行卡]], [[菜单]]'s cousin 卡片, 公交卡. 借记卡
"debit card."

## Why this combination

得 ("to obtain, to get," as in 得到) takes 意 (here "satisfaction, one's
wish fulfilled," an extended sense beyond its more familiar "meaning,"
as in 意思) as its object: getting exactly what one wished for — feeling
pleased with oneself.

Rated "semi" for 意's extended "satisfaction/wish" sense here, distinct
from its everyday "meaning" role.

## Notes

得意 can read as innocent pride (他很得意, "he's quite pleased with
himself") or tip into smugness depending on tone; 得意忘形 ("to be so
pleased one loses composure") is the set-phrase warning against
overdoing it.

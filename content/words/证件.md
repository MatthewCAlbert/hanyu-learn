## Why this combination
证 "to prove, evidence" (see also 证明, 证据) combined with 件, the
classifier-turned-noun for a discrete item (see also 文件, 邮件): a
document that proves — a certificate, ID.

## Notes
证件 is the general category word for official identifying documents
(带好证件 "bring your ID/documents") — 身份证, its specific relative,
names the identity card in particular, one type of 证件 among others
(护照, 学生证, etc.).

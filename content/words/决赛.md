## Why this combination
决 "to decide, to settle [an outcome]" (as in 决定) combined with 赛 "a
match, a contest" (as in 比赛): the match that decides the final
outcome — the finals.

## Notes
决赛 names the final round of a tournament (进入决赛 "to make it to the
finals") — 半决赛 "semifinal" and 初赛 "preliminary round" are built the
same way on 赛, naming other stages of a competition.

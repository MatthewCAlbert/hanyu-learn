## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 征 ("to conquer, to campaign against," as in
长征, "the Long March" — covered elsewhere in this app as a character
that absorbed the meaning of a separate traditional character, 徵, in
simplification) and 服 ("to submit, to yield," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 说服, "to persuade") both independently name bringing
someone under control. Pairing them gives the standard word for
conquest.

## Notes

征服 covers military conquest (征服敌国, "to conquer an enemy nation")
and figurative "winning over" (征服观众, "to win over the audience") —
the same "bring to submission" image applied to both.

## Why this combination
食 "food; to eat" + 物 "thing, substance" → edible substances, "food" as a
general category: 健康食物, 食物中毒 "food poisoning."

## Notes
食物 = food as material / category (formal-ish); 饭 / 吃的 = a meal / things to
eat (everyday); 粮食 = grain, staples. 食 also in 食品, 零食.

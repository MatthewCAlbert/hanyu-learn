## Why this combination
A telescoping of [[青年]] "youth" and [[少年]] "youngster": 青 + 少年 covers the
whole span from early adolescence into the twenties as one demographic —
青少年教育, 青少年犯罪 "juvenile crime."

## Notes
Same blending as 中小学 (中学 + 小学, see [[中小学]]). 青少年 is the term of
public policy and journalism.

## Why this combination
保 "to protect, to preserve" and 养 "to nourish, to care for" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for ongoing upkeep.

## Notes
保养 covers both health (保养身体 "to take care of one's health") and
machinery (汽车保养 "car maintenance") — the standard word for preventive
care that keeps something in good condition over time.

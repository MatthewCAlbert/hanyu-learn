## Why this combination
特 "special, distinctive" (as in 特别 "special") combined with 色,
here "character, quality" (as in 特色's own logic, extending 颜色
"colour" figuratively to "character/flavour"): a distinctive character —
a defining feature.

## Notes
特色 names what makes something distinctively itself, especially
regional or cultural traits (地方特色 "local characteristics," 特色
菜 "signature/specialty dish") — 特点, a close synonym, is used the same
way and is largely interchangeable.

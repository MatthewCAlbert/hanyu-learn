## Why this combination
停 ("to stop") takes 下 as a resultative complement marking that the
stopping action actually settled into place — the same "coming to rest"
sense 下 carries after other motion verbs (坐下, 躺下, 放下). "Stopping,
settled" is simply "to come to a halt."

## Notes
停下来 (with 来 added) is an equally common variant, adding a sense of the
action's result being oriented toward the speaker/present moment — the
plain 停下 and the fuller 停下来 are largely interchangeable in casual
speech.

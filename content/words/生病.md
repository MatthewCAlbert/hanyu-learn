## Why this combination
生 "to produce, to develop" + 病 "illness" as its object — the body "produces"
an illness. Verb-object, so it splits: 生了一场大病 "had a serious illness,"
没生过病 "has never been ill."

## Notes
Same 生 = "develop (something on the body)" in 生气 (see [[生气]]), 生锈 "rust,"
生疮 "get a boil." Bare 病 is also a verb: 他病了.

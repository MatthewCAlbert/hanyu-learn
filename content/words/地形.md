## Why this combination
地 "land" plus 形 "shape, form" gives exactly "the shape of the land" —
the standard geographic term for terrain features.

## Notes
地形 anchors 地形复杂 "complex terrain," 地形图 "topographic map" — always
about the physical shape and contour of land, distinct from 地质
"geology" (composition) or 地貌, a close synonym.

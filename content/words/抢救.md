## Why this combination
抢 (here "to race against time, to act before it's too late") plus 救
"to rescue" gives exactly "to rescue urgently" — the standard word for an
emergency, time-critical rescue.

## Notes
抢救 anchors 抢救病人 "to resuscitate a patient," 抢救文物 "to salvage
cultural relics (before they're lost)" — always implies urgency, distinct
from ordinary 救 or 救援, which don't necessarily carry the race-against-
time sense.

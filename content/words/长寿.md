## Why this combination
长 "long" plus 寿 "longevity, lifespan" gives exactly "long lifespan" —
a straightforward compositional word.

## Notes
长寿 anchors 长寿老人 "a long-lived elderly person," 祝您长寿 "wishing you
long life" (a traditional blessing) — a positively valued trait,
frequently invoked in birthday and New Year wishes.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 机 ("an opportune moment," as in 机会,
"opportunity") and 遇 ("to encounter, to meet with," as in 遇到) both
independently name a favorable circumstance one meets. Pairing them
gives a formal word for opportunity.

Rated "semi" for 机's "opportune moment" sense, less transparent than
其 plainer "machine" meaning seen elsewhere.

## Notes

机遇 leans formal and often historic-scale (历史机遇, "a historic
opportunity") — 机会 is the everyday, general-purpose word for
"opportunity" in any context.

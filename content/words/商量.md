## Why this combination
商 "to consult, to confer" (see [[商店]]'s 商, and 协商) + 量 (*liáng*) "to
measure, to weigh up" (see [[大量]], 考量) → to talk something over and weigh it
together, aiming at a decision: 我们商量一下, 有事好商量.

## Notes
商量 = confer to decide something practical; [[讨论]] = discuss / examine a
question; 谈判 = negotiate. 量 is *liáng* here (weigh), not *liàng*
"quantity."

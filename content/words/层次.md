## Why this combination
层 "layer" and 次 "sequence, order" combine into "layers in sequence" —
the standard word for a structured hierarchy of levels.

## Notes
层次 covers organizational levels (管理层次 "management levels"),
structure in writing (文章层次分明 "the essay's structure is clear"), and
figuratively a person's caliber or sophistication (有层次 "cultured,
refined").

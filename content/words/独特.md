## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 独 ("alone, solitary," covered elsewhere in
this app) and 特 ("special, particular," as in 特殊, 特别) both
independently mark something as set apart. Pairing them gives the
standard word for uniqueness.

## Notes
独特 describes something with a one-of-a-kind character (独特的风格, "a
distinctive style") — a positive, often admiring word for what sets
something apart, distinct from 奇怪 ("strange"), which carries no such
positive connotation.

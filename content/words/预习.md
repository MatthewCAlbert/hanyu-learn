## Why this combination
预 "in advance, beforehand" (as in 预计, 预报) combined with 习 "to study,
to practice" (as in 学习, 复习's own family): to study in advance.

## Notes
预习 names studying material before it is taught in class (预习课文 "to
preview the text") and pairs opposite 复习 "to review [after class],"
its close relative built on the same 习 root.

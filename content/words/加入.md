## Why this combination
加 ("to add") plus 入 ("to enter") gives, quite literally, "to add
[oneself/something] into" a group or a mixture — whether that is joining a
club (加入俱乐部) or adding an ingredient into a dish (加入盐). One
compositional image covers both the social and the literal sense.

## Notes
加入 takes the group or mixture as a direct object (加入我们, "join us") —
no preposition is needed the way English "join *in*" sometimes suggests.

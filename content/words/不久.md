## Why this combination
不 "not" + 久 "long (of time)" (see [[多久]], [[好久]]) → within a short time,
in either direction from a reference point: 不久以后 "soon after,"
成立不久 "not long after it was founded."

## Notes
不久 needs a reference point (before/after some event); 很快 "quickly / soon"
is freer. 久 alone: 好久不见.

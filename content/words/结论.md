## Why this combination
结 carries its "to tie off, to conclude" sense (as in 结束, "to end"),
and 论 is "to discuss, an argument/thesis" (as in 讨论, 理论). A discussion
tied off and finished is exactly a conclusion.

Rated "semi" for 结's "to conclude" sense here, distinct from its more
common "to tie/knot" meaning in 结婚, 结构 (both covered elsewhere in this
app).

## Notes
得出结论 ("to reach/draw a conclusion") is the standard everyday frame —
结论 names the endpoint of a chain of reasoning, not a casual opinion.

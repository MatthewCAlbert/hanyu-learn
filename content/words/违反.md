## Why this combination
违 "to depart from, to violate" and 反 "to oppose, to turn against" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard formal verb for breaking a rule
or law.

## Notes
违反 always takes an abstract rule as its object: 违反规定 "to violate a
regulation," 违反法律 "to break the law" — distinct from 违背, which more
often applies to promises and principles rather than formal rules.

## Why this combination
难 "difficult" combines with 以 in its classical "by means of, in order
to" sense — not its rare standalone modern use — to form a fixed pattern
meaning "difficult to [verb]." Knowing 以's classical function is what
makes the combination click.

## Notes
难以 always precedes a two-syllable verb in formal writing: 难以想象
"hard to imagine," 难以置信 "hard to believe." Everyday speech prefers
很难 + verb instead.

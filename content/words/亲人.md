## Why this combination
亲, here "close kin, a parent" (see also 亲自, 母亲, and 亲's own
etymology, "to reach [a depth of feeling]") combined with 人 "person":
a person close by kinship — a close family member.

## Notes
亲人 names one's close family members collectively, with an emphasis on
emotional closeness (想念亲人 "to miss one's family," 亲人团聚 "family
reunion") — 家人, its close relative, is the more neutral word for
household family members.

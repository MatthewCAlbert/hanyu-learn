## Why this combination
高 "high" combined with 速 "speed" (see also 速度, 快速): high in
speed — high-speed.

## Notes
高速 modifies fast-moving things and processes (高速发展 "rapid
development," 高速公路 "expressway/highway") and, informally, is short
for 高速公路 itself (走高速 "to take the expressway") — a common
abbreviation pattern in everyday speech.

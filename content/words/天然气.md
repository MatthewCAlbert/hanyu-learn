## Why this combination
天然 "natural" plus 气 "gas" gives exactly "natural gas" — a
straightforward technical/commercial term, contrasting with 煤气 "coal
gas" as a household fuel source.

## Notes
天然气 anchors 天然气管道 "natural gas pipeline," 液化天然气 "liquefied
natural gas (LNG)" — the fossil-fuel gas extracted from the ground,
increasingly replacing 煤气 in household use.

## Why this combination
以 "taking (a point) as reference" + 前 "before." "Reckoned back from (that
point)." Alone: "in the past" (我以前住在这儿). After a phrase: "before X" —
睡觉以前, 三年以前.

## Notes
Mirror of [[以后]]. 从前 is the storybook "once upon a time"; 以前 is the neutral
"before / previously."

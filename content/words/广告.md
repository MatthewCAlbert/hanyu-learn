## Why this combination
广 "wide, broad" + 告 "to tell, to announce" (see [[告诉]]) → a "broad
announcement," made to the public at large. 做广告 "advertise," 广告牌
"billboard."

## Notes
广 "wide" also in [[广场]] (public square), [[广播]] (not this level — broadcast).

## Why this combination
正 "precisely, right" + 在 "to be at / in progress." "Precisely in the course
of" — the progressive-aspect adverb: 他正在打电话 "he's on the phone right now."

## Notes
在 alone already marks progressive (他在吃饭); 正在 adds "right at this moment."
Often paired with sentence-final 呢.

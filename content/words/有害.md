## Why this combination
有 "to have" plus 害 "harm" gives exactly "to have harm (in it)" — the
same 有+noun predicate pattern as 有毒 "poisonous" and 有用 "useful."

## Notes
有害 modifies substances and behaviors: 有害物质 "harmful substance," 有害
健康 "harmful to health" — opposite of 有益/有利 "beneficial."

## Why this combination
网 "net" + 球 "ball" — the game named by the net stretched across the court,
just as [[篮球]] is named by its basket. 打网球, 网球场, 网球拍 "racket."

## Notes
The 球 ball-games set: [[篮球]], [[排球]], [[足球]], 乒乓球, 羽毛球. 网 "net"
also in [[网站]], [[网上]] (the internet sense) and 鱼网 (a fishing net).

## Why this combination
出 "to come out" + 生 "to be born, life" → to come out into life. 1990年出生,
出生地 "birthplace," 出生率 "birth rate."

## Notes
出生 (be born) vs. 出身 (*chūshēn*, "family background one is born into") — same
sound, different second character and meaning. 生 alone: [[生日]], [[生活]],
[[出生]]'s cousin 生病.

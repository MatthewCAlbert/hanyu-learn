## Why this combination
儿 "son" and 女 "daughter" combine into "sons and daughters" — the
standard word for one's children collectively, regardless of gender.

## Notes
儿女 anchors 儿女双全 "blessed with both a son and a daughter," 儿女情长
"sentimental attachment (idiom, of romantic/family feelings)" — always
from a parent's perspective toward their children.

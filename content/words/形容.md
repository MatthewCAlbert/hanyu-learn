## Why this combination

形 ("shape, form," as in 形状) plus 容 ("appearance, countenance," as in
容貌) named a person's outward form and bearing; extended to the verb
"to depict/describe" that form in words.

Rated "semi" for the shift from naming an appearance to naming the act
of describing one.

## Notes

形容词 ("adjective," lit. "describing word") is the grammar term built
directly on this verbal sense — an adjective is, definitionally, a word
that 形容s something.

## Why this combination
电 "electricity" is transparent; 池 here is used metaphorically for a
reservoir that holds something in store — the same "pool, reservoir"
image as 池's literal water sense (see 池's own etymology), extended to a
device that stores electrical charge.

## Notes
电池 anchors 充电电池 "rechargeable battery," 电池组 "battery pack" — the
standard modern word, a 20th-century coinage translating the Western
technical concept.

## Why this combination
坏 "bad, spoilt, broken" + 人 "person" → a person of bad character. The
child's-eye division of the world into 好人 (see [[好人]]) and 坏人.

## Notes
坏 also "to break / go off" (东西坏了) and, as a resultative, "badly / to
ruin" (气坏了 "furious"). 坏人 = a morally bad person, not a broken one.

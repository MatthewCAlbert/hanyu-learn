## Why this combination
提 "to lift, to raise" + resultative 高 "high" — lifting something to a higher
point: 提高水平, 提高效率, 提高警惕 "heighten vigilance."

## Notes
提高 (raise a level / degree, abstract) vs. 抬高 (physically lift, or inflate a
price). Antonym 降低 "lower." 提 also in [[提出]], [[提到]].

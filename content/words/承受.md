## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 承 ("to bear, to accept," covered elsewhere in
this app — many hands receiving what is passed up to them) and 受 ("to
receive," also covered elsewhere — the receiving half of 授's
hand-to-hand exchange) both independently name taking something on.
Pairing them gives the standard word for bearing weight, pressure, or
hardship.

## Notes
承受压力 ("to bear pressure/stress") and 承受能力 ("capacity to bear
[something], resilience") both build on this character pair's core
"taking on a weight without breaking" sense.

## Why this combination
音 "sound, tone" + 乐 (*yuè*) "music" — 乐 already means music on its own; 音 is
added for a clear disyllable (as in 学校, 明白). Note the reading split: 乐 is
*yuè* "music" here but *lè* "happy" in [[快乐]].

## Notes
听音乐 "listen to music," [[音乐会]] "concert," 音乐家 "musician." 古典音乐
"classical music."

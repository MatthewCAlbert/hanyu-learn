## Why this combination
人 "person" and 生 "life, to live" combine directly: a person's life,
considered as a whole span and journey.

## Notes
人生 names life as a lived human experience, with philosophical weight
(人生的意义 "the meaning of life"); 生命 (elsewhere in this dataset)
leans more toward the biological fact of being alive.

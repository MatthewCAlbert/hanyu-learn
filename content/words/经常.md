## Why this combination
经 "constant, regular, to pass through steadily" + 常 "usual, ordinary,
often." A synonym pair: as an adverb "frequently" (经常见面), as an adjective
"regular, everyday" (经常性开支 "recurring expenses").

## Notes
经常 can be an adjective; [[常常]] is adverb-only. Negated as 不经常 or,
colloquially, 不常.

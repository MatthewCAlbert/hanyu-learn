## Why this combination
干 "to do" (the colloquial verb, cf. 干活) + 什么 "what" as its object.
Literally "doing what?" — a question about activity (你在干什么), purpose
(学这个干什么 "what's the point of learning this"), or an objection (你干什么！).

## Notes
干 is *gàn* here. Blunter than 做什么; 干嘛 (*gànmá*) is the even more casual
contraction.

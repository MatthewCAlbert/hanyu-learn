## Why this combination
害, here in its "calamity, that which harms" sense (as in 害处 "harm,"
灾害 "disaster") combined with 怕 "to fear, to be afraid of": fear
prompted by the thought of harm — to be afraid.

## Notes
害怕 takes the thing feared as a direct object (害怕黑暗 "afraid of the
dark," 害怕失败 "afraid of failure") — bare 怕, its shorter synonym, works
the same way in speech and is somewhat more colloquial.

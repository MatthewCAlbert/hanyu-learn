## Why this combination
之 is the same literary linking particle seen in 之前/之后/之间, and 一 is
simply "one." "[Group] 之 [one]" reads as "one of [the group]" — the same
之-as-connector pattern applied to a number instead of a position word.

## Notes
之一 always follows the group it is drawn from, at the end of the phrase:
他是我最好的朋友之一 ("he is one of my best friends") — it cannot come before
the noun the way English "one of" does.

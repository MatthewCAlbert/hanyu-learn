## Why this combination
大 "big" and 脑 "brain" combine directly: originally distinguishing the
cerebrum (大脑, the largest part) from the smaller cerebellum (小脑), now
used as the everyday word for "the brain" generally.

## Notes
大脑 anchors both anatomy (大脑皮层 "cerebral cortex") and figurative
"mind, thinking" (用大脑想一想 "use your brain and think") — the everyday
substitute for more clinical 脑 alone in casual speech.

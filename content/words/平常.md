## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 平 "level, plain, ordinary" (see [[平安]], [[平时]]) + 常 "usual,
constant" (see [[经常]], [[常常]]). As an adjective, "unremarkable"
(他很平常); as a time adverb, "usually" (平常我不喝酒).

## Notes
平常 = both "ordinary" (adj.) and "usually" (adv.); [[平时]] is adverb-only.
[[普通]] is "ordinary" without the time sense.

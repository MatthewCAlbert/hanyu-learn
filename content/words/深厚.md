## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 深 ("deep," as in 深入, "to go deep [lit. deep
+ enter]") and 厚 ("thick," a character mainly seen in this and similar
roles) both independently name substantial depth. Pairing them gives
the standard word for profound depth.

## Notes

深厚的感情 ("deep feelings") and 深厚的基础 ("a solid/deep foundation")
both build on this word — describes emotional or foundational depth
built up over time, distinct from literal physical depth (that would be
plain 深).

## Why this combination

步 ("a step, a pace," as in 进步, "progress [lit. advance + step]") plus
行 ("to walk, to go," as in 旅行, "to travel") names moving by stepping:
to walk.

## Notes

步行街 ("a pedestrian street") and 步行十分钟 ("a ten-minute walk") both
build on this word — 步行 is the formal/written register for walking as
a mode of transport, distinct from everyday 走路.

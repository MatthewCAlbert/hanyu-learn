## Why this combination
观 "to view, to observe, an outlook" (参观, 世界观) + 点 "point" → the point from
which one views a matter, and the position one holds: 我的观点是…, 不同观点.

## Notes
观点 = a stated position on an issue; [[看法]] = a considered view; [[角度]] =
the angle you approach from; [[意见]] = opinion / suggestion.

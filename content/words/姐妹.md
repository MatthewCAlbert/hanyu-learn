## Why this combination
姐 ("older sister") plus 妹 ("younger sister") names the sibling
relationship by naming both roles, the same pattern as 兄弟 (covered
elsewhere in this app) for brothers.

## Notes
姐妹 extends figuratively to close female friendship (好姐妹, "close
girlfriends," lit. "good sisters") — the same sibling bond used as a
metaphor for close, chosen kinship.

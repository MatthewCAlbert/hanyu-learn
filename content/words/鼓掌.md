## Why this combination
鼓 (here a verb, "to beat, to strike," from its base noun sense "drum")
acting on the object 掌 "palm (of the hand)" gives exactly "to strike
one's palms (together)" — splittable, as in 鼓了掌.

## Notes
鼓掌 anchors 热烈鼓掌 "to applaud enthusiastically," 鼓掌欢迎 "to welcome
with applause" — the standard word for clapping in approval, distinct
from 拍手, a near-synonym with a more literal, less ceremonial register.

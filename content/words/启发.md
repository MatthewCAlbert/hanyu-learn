## Why this combination
启 "to open (up)" and 发 (here "to develop, to bring forth") are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for opening someone's mind
to new understanding.

## Notes
启发 works as verb ("这个故事启发了我" "this story inspired me") and noun
("很有启发" "very enlightening") — always about prompting insight rather
than directly teaching a fact.

## Why this combination
理 here carries its extended verbal sense "to reason through, to sort
out" (built on its root image, the natural grain in wood or jade,
followed to put something in order) and 解 keeps its own etymological
sense "to take apart, to analyse" (a knife dividing an ox at the
joints). Together: to take a matter apart and reason it into order — to
understand.

## Notes
理解 is more analytical/cognitive than 明白 "to understand, to be clear
about" — 理解 often implies working something out, where 明白 can be as
simple as a fact clicking into place.

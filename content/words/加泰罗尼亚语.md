## Why this combination
加泰罗尼亚 is a phonetic rendering of Catalunya / Catalonia (加泰羅尼亞), plus 语. Not
[[意大利]]'s 泰 and not 亚洲.

## Notes
A language of Spain; Extra still lists [[西班牙]] as the state.

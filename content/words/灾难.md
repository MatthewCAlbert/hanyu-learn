## Why this combination
灾 "disaster" (see 灾's own etymology) and 难 "hardship, difficulty" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for large-scale calamity.

## Notes
灾难 covers natural disasters (自然灾难 "natural disaster") and
figurative catastrophe (一场灾难 "a disaster/catastrophe" of any kind) —
a formal, weighty term for calamity on a large scale.

## Why this combination
绿 "green" combined with 茶 "tea" (as in 喝茶 "to drink tea"): tea that
stays green — green tea, unfermented in processing.

## Notes
绿茶 names the unoxidised tea variety (喝绿茶 "to drink green tea") — in
recent internet slang, 绿茶 has also picked up a pejorative figurative
sense describing someone who appears sweetly innocent while actually
being manipulative, a modern colloquial extension entirely separate
from the beverage.

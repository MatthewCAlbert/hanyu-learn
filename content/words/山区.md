## Why this combination
山 "mountain" plus 区 "area, district" gives exactly "a mountainous
area" — a straightforward geographic term.

## Notes
山区 anchors 山区学校 "a school in the mountains," 偏远山区 "a remote
mountainous region" — the standard word for terrain-defined areas,
distinct from an administrative 山市/县 naming.

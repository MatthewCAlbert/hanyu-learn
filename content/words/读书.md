## Why this combination
读 "to read (aloud)" + 书 "book" as its generic object. Beyond literal reading,
it is the standard way to say "to be a student / get an education":
在北京读书 "studying in Beijing," 读到高中 "went to school through senior
secondary." Splits: 读了很多书.

## Notes
念书 is the close northern synonym. For "read a specific book," drop the
generic: 读《红楼梦》.

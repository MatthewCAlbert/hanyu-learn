## Why this combination
是 ("is, yes") and 否 ("not, no," surviving mostly bound, as in 否定, 否则)
placed side by side spell out both sides of a yes/no question at once — "is
or is not" is precisely what "whether" means. Fully derivable once 否's
"not/no" sense is known.

## Notes
是否 is more formal/written than the everyday 是不是 — 是否 heads an embedded
question (我不知道他是否来了 "I don't know whether he came"), while 是不是 is
equally at home as a spoken tag question (他是不是来了?).

## Why this combination
照 "to shine light on" (阳光照进来) + 相 "an image, a likeness, an appearance"
(照相, 相片) — a photograph is made by light falling to fix an image. Verb-object:
照了几张相, 给我照张相. 照相机 "camera," 照相馆 "photo studio."

## Notes
照相 (a touch dated) ≈ 拍照 (now more common) ≈ 拍张照片. The device is
[[相机]] (clipped from 照相机).

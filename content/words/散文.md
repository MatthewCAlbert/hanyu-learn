## Why this combination
散 "loose, unstructured" plus 文 "writing" gives exactly "unstructured
writing" — naming prose by contrast with the fixed meter and rhyme of
poetry (诗).

## Notes
散文 anchors 散文集 "prose collection," 抒情散文 "lyrical essay" — the
standard literary category for non-verse writing with artistic/expressive
intent, distinct from plain 文章 "article" in general.

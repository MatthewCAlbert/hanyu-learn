## Why this combination
大 ("big") plus 众 ("a crowd, many people," as in 观众, "audience") names
the crowd at its largest scale: the general public.

## Notes
大众 names ordinary people as a broad social category (大众文化, "popular/
mass culture") — distinct from 人民 (citizens in a political/national
sense) and 观众 (a specific audience watching something).

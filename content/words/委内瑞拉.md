## Why this combination
A five-syllable phonetic rendering of Venezuela. 委, 内, 瑞, 拉 are chosen for
sound. 委 is not "to appoint"; 内 is not "inside."

## Notes
瑞 as in [[瑞士]], [[瑞典]] — different names.

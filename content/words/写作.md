## Why this combination
写 "to write" (as in 写字 "to write characters") combined with 作 "to
make, to create" (see also 作品, 创作): to write and create — to
compose, writing as a craft.

## Notes
写作 names writing as a skill or activity in a formal/academic register
(写作能力 "writing ability," 写作技巧 "writing technique") — 写, alone, is
the plain everyday verb for the physical or general act of writing
anything.

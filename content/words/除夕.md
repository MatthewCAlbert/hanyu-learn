## Why this combination
除 (here "to remove, to sweep away," as in 除旧布新 "ridding the old,
ushering in the new") plus 夕 "evening" gives "the evening the old year
is swept away" — the "removal" sense of 除 is less common today than its
"except" sense, so the logic needs that context to click.

## Notes
除夕 anchors 除夕夜 "New Year's Eve night," 除夕晚会 "New Year's Eve gala"
— the last night of the lunar year, the eve before 春节 "Chinese New
Year."

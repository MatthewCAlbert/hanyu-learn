## Why this combination
用 "to use" plus the directional complement 来, here marking purpose
rather than literal direction, gives "used [toward the purpose of]" — a
colloquial counterpart to the more formal 用于.

## Notes
用来 always precedes a verb describing the purpose: 这个用来做什么? "what's
this used for?" — everyday register, common in spoken Mandarin.

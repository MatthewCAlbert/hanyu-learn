## Why this combination
她 "she" + the plural suffix 们 (see [[我们]]). 她 is a 20th-century invention —
before that 他 covered all genders — and 她们 followed it. For a mixed group,
写作 falls back to 他们.

## Notes
她们 and [[他们]] are both *tāmen* in speech; the distinction exists only in
writing.

## Why this combination
停车 "to park a vehicle" (see [[停车]]) + 场 (*chǎng*) "an open ground, an
area" (see [[广场]], [[球场]], [[机场]]) → ground set aside for parking:
地下停车场 "underground car park."

## Notes
Parse 停车 + 场. 场 is *chǎng*. 车位 "a parking space," 收费停车场 "paid car
park."

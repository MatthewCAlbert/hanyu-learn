## Why this combination
椅 "chair" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]], [[桌子]], [[杯子]]) —
the everyday word for a chair (one with a back, as against a stool 凳子).

## Notes
Measure word 把: 一把椅子 (把 for things with a handle or that you grasp).
轮椅 "wheelchair," 摇椅 "rocking chair."

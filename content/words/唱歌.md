## Why this combination
Verb 唱 "to sing" with its generic object 歌 "song" — the same verb-object shape
as [[说话]], [[走路]]. It splits: 唱了一首歌 "sang a song," 唱首歌吧 "sing us
one." To sing a *named* song, drop 歌: 唱《茉莉花》.

## Notes
唱 alone also = "to call out" (唱票 count votes aloud) and, figuratively, 唱反调
"sing a different tune."

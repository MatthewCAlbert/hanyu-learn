## Why this combination
计 "to reckon, to calculate, a scheme" (计算, 会计) + 划 "to mark out, to
delineate" (划分, 规划). Working out and laying down a course of action — both
the noun (制定计划) and the verb (计划去旅行).

## Notes
划 is *huà* here (to plan / delineate), not *huá* "to paddle / scratch." Formal
synonym 规划; looser 打算 (see [[打算]]).

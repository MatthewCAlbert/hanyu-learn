## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 概 ("general, approximate," as in 大概, "roughly,
probably") and 括 ("to include, to enclose," as in 包括, "to include
[lit. wrap + enclose]") both independently name capturing something in
broad, encompassing terms. Pairing them gives the standard word for
summarizing.

## Notes

概括起来 ("to summarize [it]") and 概括能力 ("summarizing ability") both
build on this word — 概括 always condenses a larger body of content
into its essential, general points.

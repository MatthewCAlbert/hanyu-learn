## Why this combination
可 (the "worthy of" prefix) plus 惜 "to treasure, to regret losing"
gives "worth regretting" — the everyday exclamation for a missed
opportunity or unfortunate outcome.

## Notes
可惜 often opens or closes a sentence as a standalone comment: 可惜，他没
来 "pity he didn't come" — functioning almost like an interjection rather
than a full predicate.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 转 ("to turn, to pass along," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 转身/转动) and 告 ("to tell," as in
告诉) both independently name conveying something onward. Pairing them
gives the standard word for relaying a message.

## Notes

请转告他 ("please pass this on to him") is the standard set phrase — 转告
always implies passing a message from a third party, distinct from
plain 告诉, which can name any act of telling regardless of source.

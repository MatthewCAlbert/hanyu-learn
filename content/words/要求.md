## Why this combination
要 "to want, to demand" + 求 "to seek, to request." A near-synonym pair;
together it is stronger and more formal than either — a demand or a stipulated
condition. Verb (要求他道歉) and noun (符合要求 "meet the requirements").

## Notes
Firmer than 请求 (a polite request, see [[请求]]) and 需要 (need). 求 alone:
求助, 要求.

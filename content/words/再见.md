## Why this combination
再 "again" + 见 "to see / meet" → "(let's) meet again," a parting that projects
a next meeting — the same sentiment as French *au revoir*. 再 is the "again in
the future" word (再说, 再来); 又 is "again in the past."

## Notes
Casual variants keep the frame: 回头见 "see you later," 明天见 "see you
tomorrow," 一会儿见.

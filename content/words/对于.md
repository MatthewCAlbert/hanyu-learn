## Why this combination
对 carries its "facing, directed toward" sense (as in 对着, 面对), and 于 is
the same classical "at/to/in regard to" preposition seen in 关于 and 由于.
Fused, 对于 marks the person or thing a following statement is directed at
or concerns: 对于这个问题 "with respect to this problem."

对于 and 关于 overlap but are not interchangeable: 对于 marks the object of an
attitude, judgment, or effect (对于我来说 "as far as I'm concerned"), while
关于 marks a topic being discussed or written about — the distinction is
worth drilling since both translate to English "about."

## Notes
对于...来说 ("as far as ... is concerned") is an extremely common fixed frame
built on 对于 for stating a personal or relative perspective.

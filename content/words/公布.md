## Why this combination
公 "public, communal" (see also 公开, 公共) combined with 布 "to spread
out, to announce" (see also 宣布): to spread out to the public — to
make public, to announce officially.

## Notes
公布 is used for official announcements of results, laws or lists
(公布成绩 "to announce results," 公布名单 "to publish a list") — 宣布, a
close synonym, is used the same way; 公布 leans slightly more towards
releasing written information for public record.

## Why this combination
夫 ("man, husband," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 妇 ("a married
woman," also covered elsewhere) names the marital pair by naming both
roles, the same pattern as 兄弟 and 姐妹 (both covered elsewhere in this
app).

## Notes
夫妇 leans formal/written — 两口子 or plain 夫妻 read more naturally in
everyday conversation for "a married couple."

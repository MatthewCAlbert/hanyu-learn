## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 装 ("to fit out, to install," as in 安装) and 修
("to repair, to fix up," as in 修理) both independently name working on a
building's fabric. Pairing them gives the standard word for interior
renovation.

## Notes

装修 covers the whole fit-out process (装修房子, "to renovate a home") —
distinct from 修理, which is narrower repair of something broken.

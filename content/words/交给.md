## Why this combination
交 "to hand over, to deliver" + 给 "to give (to)" marking the recipient — "hand
it over to (someone)." 把作业交给老师, 这件事交给我.

## Notes
给 here is the coverb introducing the receiver, the same 给 as in 送给
(see [[送给]]), 卖给, 借给. 交 alone: [[交通]], 交朋友, 上交.

## Why this combination

童 ("a child," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 儿童 and 童年)
plus 话 ("a story, speech," as in 说话) names a story told for children:
a fairy tale.

## Notes

童话故事 ("fairy-tale story") and 童话世界 ("a fairy-tale world," used
figuratively for anything magically idyllic) both build on this word —
sharing its 话 "story" root with the unrelated-content but same-pattern
神话 ("myth," covered elsewhere in this app).

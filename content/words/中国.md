## Why this combination
"The central state(s)." In the Zhou period 中国 meant the domains of the central
plain, as against the peripheral 四方. Over two millennia it narrowed to the
name of the whole country. The self-image of centrality is baked into the word.

## Notes
中 also abbreviates 中国 in compounds: 中文 (Chinese language), 中餐 (Chinese
food), 中美 (China–US).

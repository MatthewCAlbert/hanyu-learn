## Why this combination
小 (here "petty, minor") plus 偷 "to steal" gives 小偷 "a petty thief,"
with the colloquial retroflex -儿 suffix common in northern speech added
to give 小偷儿 — the same word, spoken register.

## Notes
小偷儿 anchors 抓小偷儿 "to catch a thief" — the everyday, spoken-Beijing-
flavored form of 小偷, referring to someone who steals in a small,
opportunistic way rather than an organized criminal.

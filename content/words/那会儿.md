## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + 会儿 "a short span of time" (see [[一会儿]]) → "at that
moment / period." Colloquial and northern: 那会儿我还在上学.

## Notes
会儿 needs a preceding demonstrative or number: 这会儿 "right now," 那会儿
"back then," 多会儿 "when," [[一会儿]] "a while." Formal equivalent of 那会儿 is
[[那时]].

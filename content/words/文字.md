## Why this combination
文, here "writing" (see also 文化, 文章) combined with 字 "a character, a
written symbol" (as in 汉字 "Chinese character"): writing made of
characters — script, written language.

## Notes
文字 names writing systems and written text in general (中文文字
"Chinese script," 文字资料 "written materials") — 字, alone, names an
individual character, while 文字 refers to writing as a whole system or
body of text.

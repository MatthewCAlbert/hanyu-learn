## Why this combination
忍 "to endure" (see 忍's own etymology) and 受 "to receive, to undergo"
are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for enduring hardship.

## Notes
忍受 always takes something unpleasant as its object: 忍受痛苦 "to endure
pain," 无法忍受 "unbearable" — a formal counterpart to everyday 受不了
"can't stand it."

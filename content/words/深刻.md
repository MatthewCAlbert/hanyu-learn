## Why this combination
深 "deep" combined with 刻 "to carve, to engrave" (see also 时刻, 立刻):
carved in deep — a mark or impression that goes deep, profound.

## Notes
深刻 describes understanding, impressions and analysis that go beneath
the surface (深刻的印象 "a deep impression," 深刻理解 "profound
understanding") — 深, the plain adjective "deep," is used more for
literal physical depth as well as this figurative sense.

## Why this combination
排 "to arrange in a row, to line up" + 队 "a line, a queue, a team" (see
[[球队]], [[队长]]) as its object — "to form a line." Verb-object, so it splits:
排了很长的队, 排半天队.

## Notes
排 "arrange / row" also in 安排 "arrange (a schedule)," [[排球]] "volleyball,"
一排 "a row." 插队 "jump the queue."

## Why this combination
打, here a light verb "to carry out [an action]" (as in 打电话) combined
with 听 "to listen": to carry out listening [for information] — to
inquire, to ask around.

## Notes
打听 names casually asking around to gather information (打听消息 "to
inquire about news," 打听一下 "to ask around a bit") — 问, the plain
verb "to ask," is used for a direct question, while 打听 implies
seeking out information more informally, often from multiple sources.

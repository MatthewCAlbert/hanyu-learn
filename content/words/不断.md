## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 断 "to break off, to sever, to be cut short": not
broken off — continuing without interruption.

## Notes
不断 sits directly before a verb as an adverb of manner (不断努力 "to
keep working hard without let-up," 不断发展 "to develop continuously") —
it describes an ongoing, repeated or continuous process rather than a
single completed action.

## Why this combination
签 "to sign" and 证 "certificate, proof" combine into exactly "a signed
certificate" — the standard word for the official travel authorization
document.

## Notes
签证 anchors 办签证 "to apply for a visa," 签证到期 "the visa has
expired" — always the immigration document, distinct from 护照
"passport," which the visa is affixed to or paired with.

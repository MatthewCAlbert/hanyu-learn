## Why this combination
可乐 *kělè* transliterates the "co-la" of English "cola" while also
managing to mean something pleasant in Chinese — 可 "can be" plus 乐
"happy, enjoyable" — a phono-semantic matching loanword that captures
both sound and a flattering sense, unlike a pure sound-only loan such as
咖啡 or 沙发.

## Notes
可乐 is the generic word for cola-flavoured soft drinks (可口可乐
"Coca-Cola," literally "tasty and enjoyable cola," an especially
well-matched full phono-semantic translation) — a textbook case of a
loanword chosen for both sound and flattering meaning together.

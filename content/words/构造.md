## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 构 ("to construct," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 构成) and 造 ("to make, to build," as in 创造, "to
create") both independently name building something up from parts.
Pairing them gives the technical word for structure.

## Notes

身体构造 ("bodily structure/anatomy") and 语言构造 ("linguistic
structure") both build on this word — 构造 names how something is put
together internally, distinct from 结构, its close synonym that leans
slightly more abstract/organizational.

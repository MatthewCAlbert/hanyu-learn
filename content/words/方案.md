## Why this combination
方 carries its "method, way" sense (as in 方法, 方式), and 案 is "a
proposal, a case laid out for review," covered elsewhere in this app — a
method written up and formally proposed.

Rated "semi" for 案's specific "proposal/file" sense, one branch among
several the character carries (also "table," also "legal case").

## Notes
方案 names a concrete, worked-out plan of action (解决方案, "a solution");
for a looser, more general intention, 计划 or 打算 read more naturally.

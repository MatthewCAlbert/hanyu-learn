## Why this combination
武 "military, martial" (see also 武器) combined with 术 "art, technique,
craft" (see also 艺术, 技术, 美术): the martial arts — techniques of
combat, self-defence and, today, performance sport.

## Notes
武术 names the formal category of Chinese martial arts, including
competitive and performance forms (武术表演 "martial arts performance,"
武术比赛 "martial arts competition") — 功夫, its close everyday synonym
(and the source of the English loanword "kung fu"), is used more
casually and internationally for the same broad tradition.

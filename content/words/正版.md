## Why this combination
正 "correct, authentic" plus 版 "edition" gives exactly "the authentic
edition" — the standard word for a legitimately licensed copy, opposite
of 盗版 "pirated edition" (盗 "to steal" + 版).

## Notes
正版 anchors 正版软件 "licensed software," 支持正版 "support legitimate
copies" — always contrasts with unauthorized, pirated versions of media
or software.

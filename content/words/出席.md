## Why this combination
出 ("to go out, to emerge") takes 席 ("a seat," covered elsewhere in this
app — extended from a woven mat to a place, a seat, a role) as its
object: going out to occupy one's seat is exactly "to attend."

## Notes
出席 is the formal register for attending a meeting or ceremony
(出席会议, "to attend a meeting") — for everyday casual attendance
(showing up to a party, say), 参加 or plain 来 reads more naturally.

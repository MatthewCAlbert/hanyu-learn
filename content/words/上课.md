## Why this combination
课 "lesson, class" as the object of 上 "attend / go on" (cf. [[上班]], [[上学]]).
The same word covers the student's side and the teacher's — context decides.
Splits: 上了三节课 "had three periods."

## Notes
Opposite [[下课]] "class ends." 上 / 下 here = start / finish a session, not
up / down.

## Why this combination

海 ("sea," as in 大海) plus 水 ("water") names the water of the sea:
seawater.

## Notes

海水浴场 ("a seaside bathing beach") and 海水淡化 ("seawater desalination")
both build on this word — a plain descriptive compound, distinct from
海鲜 (covered elsewhere in this app), which names food from the sea
rather than the water itself.

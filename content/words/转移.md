## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 转 ("to turn, to rotate," as in 转弯) and 移 ("to
shift," covered in this app's hanzi entry) both independently name a
change of position or direction. Pairing them gives the standard word for
moving something — physically, or figuratively, like attention — from one
place to another.

## Notes
转移注意力 ("to divert/shift attention") is a very common figurative use —
the same "moving something from one place to another" image applied to
focus rather than a physical object.

## Why this combination
命 "life, fate, what is decreed" (as in 生命 "life") combined with 运
"fortune, luck, the turning [of events]" (see also 幸运): the decreed
turning of one's fortunes — fate, destiny.

## Notes
命运 names the larger arc of fortune governing a life, a nation or an
era (改变命运 "to change one's destiny," 国家的命运 "the nation's fate")
— it carries a weightier, more literary register than everyday 运气
"luck."

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 摆 ("to shake, to swing," covered elsewhere in
this app) and 脱 ("to shed, to escape," also covered elsewhere — flesh
wasting away, extended to slipping free of something) both independently
name breaking free of a hold. Pairing them gives the standard word for
escaping a burden, habit, or difficulty.

## Notes
摆脱困境 ("to escape a difficult situation") and 摆脱束缚 ("to shake off
constraints") both build on this pair's core "breaking free from
something that has a grip on you" sense — abstract burdens, not physical
restraint.

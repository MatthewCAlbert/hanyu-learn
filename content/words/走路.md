## Why this combination
走 "to walk / go" + 路 "road" as its generic object — the verb-object shape of
[[说话]], [[唱歌]]. It splits: 走了很多路 "walked a long way," 走五分钟路
"a five-minute walk."

## Notes
走 in modern Mandarin is "walk / leave" (classical "run" — still in 走狗
"running dog," 跑 having taken over "run"). 走路上班 "walk to work."

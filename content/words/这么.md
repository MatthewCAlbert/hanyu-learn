## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + the unstressed suffix 么. The near-speaker member of
the 这么 / [[那么]] pair: manner (这么做 "do it this way") and degree
(这么好 "this good").

## Notes
这么着 "like so," 这么点儿 "this little." Colloquial and high-frequency in
speech; writing often prefers 这样 / 如此.

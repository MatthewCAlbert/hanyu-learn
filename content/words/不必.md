## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 必 "necessarily, must" (see also 必要): not
necessary — there is no need to.

## Notes
不必 sits before the verb it negates (不必担心 "no need to worry," 不必
着急 "no need to rush") and negates necessity, not ability or
permission — distinct from 不能 "cannot" and 不可以 "not allowed to."

## Why this combination
公 "public, common" (see [[公司]], [[公平]]) + 园 "garden, grounds" → a garden
for public use. A 19th-century notion and term; contrast the private 花园
"garden" and 院子 "yard."

## Notes
园 "grounds" also in [[花园]], [[动物园]], [[校园]], 幼儿园 "kindergarten."
去公园散步 "take a walk in the park."

## Why this combination
多 "much, to what extent" + the unstressed suffix 么 (see [[那么]], 什么). Chiefly
exclamatory: 多么美啊！ "how beautiful!", 无论多么困难 "however hard it is."

## Notes
Often shortened to 多 in speech: 你多大？ "how old are you?", 多好啊！. The 么
adds emphasis and a slightly written flavour.

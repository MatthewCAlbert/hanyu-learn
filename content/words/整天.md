## Why this combination
整 "whole, entire" (see also 整个, 整理) combined with 天 "day, sky" (see
also 今天, 天空): the entire day — all day long.

## Notes
整天 often carries a mild complaint about something going on
continuously (他整天玩手机 "he's on his phone all day long") — 一整天,
its fuller form with 一 added, is used the same way and is equally
common.

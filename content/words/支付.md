## Why this combination
支, here in its "to disburse, to pay out [funds]" sense (as in 开支
"expenditure") combined with 付 "to pay, to hand over" (as in 付钱 "to
pay money"): to disburse a payment — to pay.

## Notes
支付 is the formal, often written word for paying money (支付方式
"payment method," 在线支付 "online payment") — 付, alone, is the everyday
verb used in ordinary speech (付钱 "to pay").

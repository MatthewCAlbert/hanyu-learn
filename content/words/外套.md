## Why this combination
外 ("outside, outer") plus 套 (here in its "a covering, a sheath" sense,
as in 手套, "gloves," lit. "hand-covering") names the outermost covering
worn over other clothes: a coat.

Rated "semi" for 套's "covering, sheath" sense, distinct from its role as
a measure word for sets (一套书, "a set of books").

## Notes
外套 is the general word for an outer coat/jacket — for specific types,
Mandarin has more precise words (大衣, a longer overcoat; 夹克, a
transliteration of "jacket").

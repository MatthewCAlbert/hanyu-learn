## Why this combination
一句话 "one sentence['s worth of] words" is used literally and, as a
discourse marker, figuratively — condensing everything just said into a
single summarizing sentence.

## Notes
一句话 opens a summary clause the way English "in short" or "bottom
line" does: 一句话，这事儿不行 "bottom line, this won't work" — a common
spoken-register transition.

## Why this combination
笔记 "notes (taken by pen)" (笔 pen + 记 record; see [[笔记]]) + 本 "a bound
book / volume" (see [[本子]], [[课本]]) → a book to write notes in. "Laptop" is
笔记本电脑, usually clipped to 笔记本.

## Notes
Parse 笔记 + 本. Plainer synonym [[本子]]. 笔 "pen" also in [[铅笔]] / 钢笔
(not this level), 毛笔.

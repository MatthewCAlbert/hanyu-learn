## Why this combination
坏 "bad" (see [[坏人]]) + 处 (*chù*) "a point, an aspect" (see [[好处]],
[[到处]]) → "the bad aspects" — the harm or drawbacks of something:
抽烟对身体有坏处, 没什么坏处.

## Notes
Direct opposite of [[好处]] "benefit." 处 "aspect / point" also in 长处 /
短处 "strengths / weaknesses."

## Why this combination
驾 "to drive" plus 照 (here "a license, a permit," as in 执照) gives
exactly "a driving license" — 驾照 is itself a shortened form of the
fuller 驾驶执照.

## Notes
驾照 anchors 考驾照 "to take the driving test (get a license)," 驾照到期
"the license has expired" — the everyday, colloquial term, where formal
documents use the full 驾驶执照.

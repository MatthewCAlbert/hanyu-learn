## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 失 ("to lose," as in 失去, 丢失) and 败 ("to be
defeated," as in 打败, "to defeat") both independently name loss.
Pairing them gives the standard modern word for failing at an attempt or
losing a contest.

## Notes
失败 covers both "to fail" (attempt did not succeed) and "to lose" (a
competition), the same range English splits between two separate verbs —
context decides which sense is meant.

## Why this combination
依 "according to, in accordance with" plus 法 "law" gives exactly
"according to the law" — a formal legal adverb.

## Notes
依法 always precedes a verb: 依法办事 "to handle matters according to
law," 依法治国 "to govern the country by law" — a written/formal
register word standard in legal and government texts.

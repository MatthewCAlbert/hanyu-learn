## Why this combination
优 "superior, excellent" and 先 "first" combine directly: being placed
first because of superior standing or importance.

## Notes
优先 anchors 优先权 "priority right," 优先考虑 "to give priority
consideration to" — always about ranking one thing ahead of others in
order of importance.

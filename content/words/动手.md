## Why this combination
动 "to move" acting on the object 手 "hand" gives "to move one's hands [to
start doing something]" — splittable, as in 动过手 "has [already] taken
action."

## Notes
动手 spans starting a task (动手做饭 "to get down to cooking") and physical
aggression (别动手! "don't hit!") — context decides which sense applies.

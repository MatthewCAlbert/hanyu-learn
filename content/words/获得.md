## Why this combination
获 originally meant "to catch (game), to harvest (crops)" (its semantic
component 犬, dog, points to hunting), and 得 is the plain verb "to get, to
obtain." A near-synonym pairing that keeps a slightly more formal,
achievement-flavored tone than plain 得到 — you 获得 an award or success, but
you just 得到 an ordinary object.

## Notes
获得 pairs naturally with abstract, earned nouns (获得成功, 获得支持, 获得
奖励) more than with ordinary physical objects, where 得到 or 拿到 sounds more
natural.

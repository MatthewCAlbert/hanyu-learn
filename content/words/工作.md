## Why this combination
A synonym pair: 工 "labour, work" and 作 "to do, to make." Stacked, they cover
both the verb ("to work") and the noun ("a job") with no shift in meaning.

## Notes
Noun and verb without any marking: 我工作 "I work," 我的工作 "my job." Compare
the verb-object [[上班]], which is specifically "to go on shift."

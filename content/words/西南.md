## Why this combination
西 "west" + 南 "south" → the intercardinal southwest. Chinese fixes the order
西/东 before 南/北 in these compounds — hence 西南, [[西北]], [[东南]], [[东北]]
(opposite to English "southwest"). As a region, 西南 covers Sichuan, Yunnan,
Guizhou and around.

## Notes
The four intercardinals: [[东北]], [[西北]], [[东南]], 西南.

## Why this combination
乌尔都 is a phonetic rendering of Urdu (烏爾都), plus 语. 乌 is not "crow"; 都 is not
"capital."

## Notes
Closely related to Hindi; Extra also has [[印地语]] and [[巴基斯坦]].

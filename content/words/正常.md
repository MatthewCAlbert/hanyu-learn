## Why this combination
正 "proper, upright, correct" + 常 "usual, constant, ordinary" → in its proper,
customary state. 一切正常 "all normal," 不正常 "abnormal / not right."

## Notes
正 "proper / exact" also in [[正好]], [[正确]], [[真正]]. 常 "usual" in
[[经常]], [[平常]], [[常见]].

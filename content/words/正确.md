## Why this combination
正 "upright, proper, exact" + 确 "certain, solid, true" (确定, 准确) → properly
and definitely right. Of answers, methods, attitudes: 正确答案, 正确的做法.

## Notes
正确 (of judgements / methods being right) vs. 对 (a plain "correct") vs.
[[准确]] (accurate, precise to a value).

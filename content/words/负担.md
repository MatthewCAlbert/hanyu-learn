## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 负 ("to carry, to bear," as in 负责, "to be
responsible," lit. "to bear the duty") and 担 ("to carry, to shoulder,"
covered elsewhere in this app) both independently name bearing a weight.
Pairing them gives the standard word for a burden, financial or
otherwise.

## Notes
负担 works as both noun ("a burden," 经济负担, "financial burden") and
verb ("to bear," 负担费用, "to bear the cost") without changing form.

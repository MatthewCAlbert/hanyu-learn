## Why this combination

箱 ("a box, a case," as in 冰箱, "refrigerator [lit. ice box]") plus the
nominal suffix 子 names the everyday object: a box or case.

## Notes

箱子 covers everything from a storage box to a suitcase (行李箱, "luggage
case," is the more specific travel term) — context, not a different
word, usually settles which is meant.

## Why this combination
国 "country" (as in 国家) combined with 庆 "to celebrate" (see also 庆祝):
the country's celebration — National Day.

## Notes
国庆 names China's National Day (October 1st, marking the founding of
the People's Republic) and, by extension, the associated 国庆节 "National
Day holiday" and its 国庆假期 "National Day break" — a fixed calendrical
term parallel to 春节 "Spring Festival."

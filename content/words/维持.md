## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 维 ("to hold together, to maintain," covered
elsewhere in this app — the cords that keep a carriage canopy in place)
and 持 ("to hold, to grasp," as in 坚持, "to persist") both independently
name sustaining something in place. Pairing them gives the standard word
for maintaining a state or condition.

## Notes
维持秩序 ("to maintain order") and 维持生活 ("to sustain a livelihood")
both build on this character pair's core "keeping something from
collapsing or changing" sense.

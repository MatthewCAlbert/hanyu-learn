## Why this combination
心 "heart" + 情 "feeling, emotion" (感情, [[热情]]) → the state of one's heart at
a given time: 心情不好 "in a bad mood," 心情愉快.

## Notes
心情 = passing mood; 性格 = fixed character; [[态度]] = attitude. 心 as the seat
of feeling also in [[心里]], [[开心]], [[关心]].

## Why this combination
意 "idea, intention" + 见 "view, what one sees" (见解) → one's view on a matter.
Neutral "opinion / suggestion" (提意见 "offer suggestions") but also, pointedly,
a grievance: 对他有意见 "have a problem with him."

## Notes
The "complaint / gripe" sense is easy to miss — 大家意见很大 means people are
quite unhappy, not that they have many ideas.

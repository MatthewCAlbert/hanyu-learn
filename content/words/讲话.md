## Why this combination
讲 "to speak, to explain, to tell" + 话 "words, speech" as its generic object —
the verb-object shape of [[说话]]. Splits: 讲了半天话. As a noun, a formal
address: 领导讲话.

## Notes
讲 leans toward explaining or holding forth (讲课, 讲故事, [[讲道理]]); [[说话]]
is plainer "talk." 讲话 the noun is more formal than 说话.

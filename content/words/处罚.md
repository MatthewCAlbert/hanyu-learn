## Why this combination
处 "to handle, to deal with" and 罚 "to punish" (see 罚's own etymology)
are near-synonyms combined into the standard formal/legal word for
punitive action.

## Notes
处罚 is the formal legal/administrative register: 依法处罚 "to penalize
according to law," 从重处罚 "to punish severely" — more official than
everyday 惩罚 "to punish."

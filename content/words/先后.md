## Why this combination
先 "before, first" and 后 "after, later" are direct opposites combined
into a single word naming the order in which things happen — the same
antonym-pair pattern as 大小 "size" and 胜负 "outcome."

## Notes
先后 works as an adverb ("先后参观了三个城市" "visited three cities one
after another") and noun ("分不清先后" "can't tell the order/priority") —
always about temporal sequence, not simultaneity.

## Why this combination
消, here "to use up, to dissolve away" (see also 消失, 消息 in an
unrelated original sense) combined with 费 "to spend, an expense" (see
also 费用, 浪费): to use up by spending — to consume.

## Notes
消费 is the economic term for consumer spending (消费者 "consumer,"
消费水平 "level of consumption") — 花, the everyday verb "to spend
[money]," is used in casual speech, while 消费 belongs to a more formal,
economic register.

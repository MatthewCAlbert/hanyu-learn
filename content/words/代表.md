## Why this combination
代 "on behalf of, in place of" (recall its etymology: one person
standing in for another) combines with 表 "to express, to show" —
speaking or acting on someone else's behalf, showing their position as
if it were your own: to represent them.

## Notes
代表 covers both the verb "to represent" (这幅画代表了... "this painting
represents...") and the noun "a representative, a delegate" (人民代表
"a people's delegate") — the role and the act of filling it share one
word.

## Why this combination
师 "teacher, master" and 傅 "tutor, one who assists in growth" (see 傅's
own etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard term of
respect for a skilled worker or master craftsman.

## Notes
师傅 is used both as a formal title for a trade master (拜师傅 "to
apprentice oneself to a master") and, very commonly, as a polite way to
address a stranger doing manual or skilled work (taxi driver, chef) —
distinct from 老师, reserved for academic teachers.

## Why this combination
主 ("main, primary, host") plus 席 ("a seat," covered elsewhere in this
app — extended from a literal woven mat to a seat, a place, a role) names
the person who occupies the primary seat: the one presiding.

Rated "semi" for 席's extended "seat → role/office" sense, which needs to
be known before the compound reads transparently.

## Notes
主席 names the head of a meeting, organization, or (in China) the head of
state (国家主席) — distinct from 总统 (covered elsewhere in this app),
the presidential title used in republics with a directly elected head of
state.

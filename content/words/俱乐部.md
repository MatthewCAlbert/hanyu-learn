## Why this combination
俱乐部 transliterates English "club" sound-for-sound, but the three
characters chosen are not meaningless — 俱 "together," 乐 "joy," 部
"group, division" happen to also sketch "a group that gathers together
for enjoyment," a rare case where a sound-matching transliteration was
picked specifically because its meaning also fit. Treat the sound-match
as the real reason for the choice; the tidy meaning is a bonus, not the
origin.

## Notes
俱乐部 covers both sports/social organisations (足球俱乐部 "football club")
and their physical premises — the same double life English "club" has.

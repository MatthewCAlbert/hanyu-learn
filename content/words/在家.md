## Why this combination
在 "to be located at" + 家 "home" — a verb-object phrase, so 不在家 "not (be
at) home," 在不在家 "are you home?" In a Buddhist frame 在家 also means "to
remain a layman" (opposed to 出家 "leave home" = take orders).

## Notes
在 as a full verb here, not the progressive marker. Compare [[家里]] (the noun
"home").

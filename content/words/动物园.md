## Why this combination
[[动物]] "animal" + 园 "garden, grounds" (see [[公园]], [[花园]], [[校园]]) →
grounds where animals are kept for the public to see. Parse 动物 + 园.

## Notes
园 "-grounds" for enclosed public spaces: [[公园]], 植物园 "botanical garden,"
游乐园 "amusement park," 动物园.

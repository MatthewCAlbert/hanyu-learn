## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 赞 ("to praise," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 赞成/赞助/称赞) and 赏 ("to appreciate, to enjoy the beauty
of," as in 欣赏, "to appreciate [art, scenery]") both independently name
recognizing something's worth. Pairing them gives a formal word for
admiring approval.

## Notes

表示赞赏 ("to express admiration") and 深表赞赏 ("to deeply appreciate")
both build on this word — more formal/written than plain 喜欢, and
implies recognizing quality or merit rather than simple personal
enjoyment.

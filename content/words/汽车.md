## Why this combination
车 is the generic wheeled vehicle (a word older than the chariot). 汽 "steam,
vapour" dates the coinage: the first self-propelled road vehicles ran on steam,
and the name outlived the technology, just as English "car" outlived the
tramcar.

## Notes
汽 "steam" still literal in 汽水 (soda), 汽油 (petrol — "steam oil"). 火车
(train) uses 火 "fire" on the same logic.

## Why this combination
春 "spring" (see [[春天]]) + 节 "festival" (see [[节日]]) → the festival at the
start of spring — the lunar New Year, China's biggest holiday. The name 春节
dates from 1914, when the Republic adopted the Western calendar and needed to
distinguish the old New Year from 1 January (元旦).

## Notes
春节 = the festival; 过年 (see [[过年]]) = celebrating it; [[新年]] leans toward
the calendrical turn. 春运 "the Spring Festival travel rush."

## Why this combination
程 carries its "a course, a schedule, a measured stretch" sense (as in
过程, 日程, 路程), and 序 is "order, sequence" (as in 顺序, 秩序). A
schedule of steps taken in their proper sequence is exactly a procedure —
and, in computing, a program: a fixed sequence of instructions.

Rated "semi" for 程's "course/schedule" sense, distinct from its role as
a plain measure-word-like unit in words like 路程 (distance traveled).

## Notes
程序 covers both bureaucratic procedure (法律程序, "legal procedure") and
computer programs (编程序, "to write a program") — the same "fixed
sequence of steps" idea in two very different domains.

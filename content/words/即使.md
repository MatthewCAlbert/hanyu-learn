## Why this combination
即 here carries its "even, granted that" sense (as in 即便), and 使 carries
a "supposing, hypothetically making it so" sense rather than its everyday
"to use/to make." Together the two characters build a single
grammaticalized connective rather than a literal picture — the meaning is
recoverable from the parts, but only once you know 即's less common
"granted that" sense.

## Notes
即使 always pairs with a following 也 to complete the clause: 即使下雨，我们
也去 "even if it rains, we're still going." Interchangeable in most
contexts with 即便, 哪怕, and 尽管 — a small cluster of near-synonymous
concessive connectives.

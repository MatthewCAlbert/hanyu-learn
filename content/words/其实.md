## Why this combination
实 "real, true" is transparent. 其 is a classical third-person/possessive
pronoun ("its, his, that") whose referent here is not any specific noun
but "the matter [under discussion]" left implicit — "the truth of it."
That backgrounded referent is why transparency sits at semi rather than
fully transparent.

## Notes
其实 typically signals a correction or a contrast with what was just
said or assumed — 我以为他生气了，其实他没有 "I thought he was angry;
actually, he wasn't."

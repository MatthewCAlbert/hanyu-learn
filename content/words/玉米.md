## Why this combination
玉 ("jade," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 米 ("rice, hulled grain")
names corn kernels by comparing them to small, lustrous jade beads
strung in rows on the cob — a poetic, visual naming rather than a
literal description of substance.

Rated "semi" because the "jade" half is a figurative comparison a
learner has to be told, not a description of what corn is made of.

## Notes
玉米 is a comparatively late addition to the Chinese diet (maize arrived
from the Americas), which is why its name is a fresh descriptive
compound rather than an ancient character with deep etymological roots.

## Why this combination
晚 "evening / late" + 饭, "cooked rice" standing for "meal" (see [[吃饭]]). The
day's meals are named by their slot: 早饭 / 午饭 / 晚饭.

## Notes
晚餐 is the slightly more formal register; 晚饭 the everyday word.
吃晚饭 "have dinner."

## Why this combination
所 "that which" + 有 "to have" → "that which one has," everything in the set.
From the possessive noun (个人所有 "personal property," 所有权 "ownership") came
the quantifier "all": 所有人都知道.

## Notes
As a quantifier it usually wants 的 or 都: 所有的书, 所有人都…. 所 + verb also in
[[所以]], 所谓.

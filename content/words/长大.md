## Why this combination
长 (*zhǎng*) "to grow" + resultative 大 "big" — growing to full size. Of
children (在农村长大 "grew up in the countryside") and, loosely, anything that
develops.

## Notes
长 is *zhǎng* here (to grow), not *cháng* "long." Same *zhǎng* in [[长大]]'s
relatives 长高, 成长, and the "elder / head" words [[家长]], [[校长]], [[队长]].

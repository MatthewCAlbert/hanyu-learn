## Why this combination
按 "to press" and 摩 "to rub" (see 摩's own etymology — 研也, "to grind,
to rub") are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for massage.

## Notes
按摩 anchors 按摩师 "massage therapist," 按摩椅 "massage chair" — the
everyday clinical and leisure term for this specific bodywork technique.

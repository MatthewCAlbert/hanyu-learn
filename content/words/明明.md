## Why this combination
明 "clear, bright" doubled for emphasis gives "clearly, plainly" — the
reduplication intensifies rather than changes the meaning.

## Notes
明明 often sets up a contrast with what actually happened: 明明知道，还是
做错了 "he clearly knew, yet still got it wrong" — flagging an obvious
fact that contradicts the following clause.

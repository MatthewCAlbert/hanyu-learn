## Why this combination
志 "will, aspiration, ambition" (as in 志愿者's own 志) combined with 愿
"to wish, to be willing" (see also 愿望): an aspiration one is willing
to pursue — a stated goal or wish.

## Notes
志愿 names a freely chosen aspiration or, distinctively, the ranked
preferences filled in on college/job application forms (高考志愿 "college
entrance exam preferences") — 志愿者, built directly on this word, is
the person who acts on it (see its own entry).

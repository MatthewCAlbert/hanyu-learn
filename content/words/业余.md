## Why this combination

业 ("occupation, one's job," as in 职业) plus 余 ("surplus, what remains,"
covered elsewhere in this app as a character where simplification merged
two distinct traditional characters, 余 and 餘, into one shape) names
time left over after one's job: spare time, and by extension what one
does in it — amateur pursuits.

Rated "semi" for the shift from "surplus time" to the specific "amateur/
non-professional" sense.

## Notes

业余爱好 ("a hobby, lit. spare-time fondness") and 业余选手 ("an amateur
athlete," as opposed to 职业选手, "a professional") both build on this
"outside one's main job" sense.

## Why this combination
划 (here "to mark off, to delineate") and 分 "to divide" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for splitting something
into defined parts.

## Notes
划分 anchors 划分区域 "to divide into zones," 划分标准 "the criteria for
classification" — always about creating clear boundaries or categories,
distinct from 分 alone, which can be a simpler, less formal split.

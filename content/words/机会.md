## Why this combination
机 "a pivotal moment, a trigger" (时机, 契机) + 会 "a juncture, an occasion" — a
favourable moment that has come round. 有机会 "have the chance," 抓住机会 "seize
the opportunity."

## Notes
机 "crucial moment / mechanism" also in 机器, [[危机]] (not in this level), 飞机.
Here it is opportunity, not machine.

## Why this combination
被 (here the passive-voice marker, "acted upon by") plus 动 "to act, to
move" gives exactly "acted upon (rather than acting)" — the standard word
for a passive stance or role, opposite of 主动 "active, taking
initiative."

## Notes
被动 anchors 被动局面 "a passive/reactive position," 处于被动 "to be in a
passive situation" — used for both grammar (被动语态 "passive voice") and
everyday descriptions of someone reacting rather than initiating.

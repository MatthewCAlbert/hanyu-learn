## Why this combination
空 "empty, the sky" and 中 "middle, within" combine directly: within the
open space of the sky.

## Notes
空中 anchors 空中飞人 "trapeze artist" (literally "a flying person in the
air"), 空中花园 "hanging/rooftop garden," and, figuratively, 空中楼阁 "a
castle in the air [idiom for an unrealistic plan]."

## Why this combination
证 (here "proof, certification," from 证明) and 书 "document, written
record" combine into exactly "a document that certifies" — the standard
word for a formal certificate.

## Notes
证书 anchors 毕业证书 "diploma," 结婚证书 "marriage certificate," 资格证书
"qualification certificate" — always an official document attesting to a
status or achievement.

## Why this combination
好 here is *hào* (fourth tone, "to be fond of"), not *hǎo* ("good"). The tone
change marks a verbal reading, so the pair is verb + verb — "to love and to
favour" — which then nominalised into "hobby."

Read 好 as *hǎo* and you get "love good," which explains nothing. The tone is
the whole key, and it is why this word feels opaque to beginners who have only
met 好 as an adjective.

## Notes
Same verbal 好 in 好奇 (hàoqí, curious — "to favour the strange").

## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" (see also 成功, 成就) combined with 果 "fruit,
outcome" (see also 效果, 后果): the fruit that comes from completion —
a positive, hard-won result.

## Notes
成果 names a valuable outcome, especially of sustained work (研究成果
"research findings," 劳动成果 "the fruits of one's labour") — unlike the
neutral 结果 "result," 成果 always carries a positive, achievement-like
connotation.

## Why this combination
属 "to belong to a category, to be affiliated with" combined with the
classical preposition 于 "at, to" (see 于's own entry — a grammatical
word marking a relationship, with no independent lexical meaning of its
own): to belong, [with respect] to a larger whole or category.

## Notes
属于 states category membership or ownership in a formal register (这
属于个人隐私 "this falls under personal privacy," 中国属于亚洲 "China is
part of Asia") — 是 states plain identity and does not carry the same
"belongs within a larger set" sense.

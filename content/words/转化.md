## Why this combination
转 "to turn, to shift" and 化 "to transform, to change into" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for one thing changing into
another.

## Notes
转化 anchors 能量转化 "energy conversion," 转化为动力 "transformed into
motivation" — a formal/technical word for a qualitative change from one
state or form into another.

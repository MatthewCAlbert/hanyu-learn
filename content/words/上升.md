## Why this combination
上 "up" combined with 升 "to rise, to ascend" (as in 升起 "to rise up"):
to rise upward — to ascend, to increase.

## Notes
上升 describes gradual increase or ascent, literal or figurative
(温度上升 "temperature rises," 地位上升 "one's status rises") — its
opposite is 下降 "to fall, to decrease," the parallel compound built with
下 "down" in place of 上.

## Why this combination
次 "an occurrence, a countable time" + 上 "previous," the same temporal 上 as in
上周, 上个月. The set is 上次 / 这次 / [[下次]].

## Notes
Here 上 = "the one before," a metaphorical use of the spatial "above / earlier
in a list."

## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + 时 "time" → "at this point in time." In storytelling
it marks "just then": 这时，门开了.

## Notes
这时 / 这时候 / 这会儿 run formal → colloquial. Mirror of [[那时]] "at that
time."

## Why this combination
整 "whole, entire" (see also 整个, 整理) reduplicated: doubling
intensifies the sense of completeness — a full, entire span with nothing
left out.

## Notes
整整 emphasises a precise, complete quantity or duration (整整一年
"a whole/full year," 整整齐齐 in a different reduplicated pattern "neat
and tidy") — it stresses that a round figure is exact, not approximate.

## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" as the object of 上 "to get on / mount" (cf. 上船, 上飞机 — but note
上 is also the verb in [[上班]], [[上课]]). It splits: 上了车才发现 "only
realised after getting on."

## Notes
Opposite [[下车]]. Chinese boards a bus, train or car with 上 (never 里),
matching the locative [[车上]].

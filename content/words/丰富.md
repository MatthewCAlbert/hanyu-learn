## Why this combination
丰 "abundant, plentiful, bountiful" (as in 丰收 "bumper harvest")
combined with 富 "rich, wealthy" (as in 富有 "wealthy"): abundant and
rich — plentiful.

## Notes
丰富 modifies things that come in great, varied abundance (丰富的经验
"rich/extensive experience," 内容丰富 "content-rich") and works as a
verb too ("丰富知识" "to enrich [one's] knowledge") — it applies to
non-monetary abundance (experience, resources, content), while 富, alone,
is used more narrowly for financial wealth.

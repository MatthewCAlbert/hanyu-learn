## Why this combination
钢 "steel" (see 钢's own etymology) and 琴 "zither, stringed instrument"
(see 琴's own etymology, the general instrument-naming component seen in
小提琴 "violin" too) combine to describe the piano as a steel-stringed
member of the 琴 instrument family — a calque built on the piano's
distinguishing steel strings rather than a sound-based loan.

## Notes
琴 is a productive suffix for keyboard and stringed instruments generally
(小提琴 "violin," 口琴 "harmonica") — 钢琴 singles out the piano by its
steel-string construction.

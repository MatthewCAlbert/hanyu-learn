## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 单 ("single, one") and 独 ("alone, solitary,"
covered elsewhere in this app) both independently name being by oneself.
Pairing them gives the standard adverb for doing something apart from
others.

## Notes
单独行动 ("to act alone/independently") and 单独谈谈 ("to talk one-on-one")
both build on this pair's core "apart from the group" sense — always an
adverb modifying an action, not a standalone adjective describing a
person's general disposition (that would be 孤独).

## Why this combination
合 "to conform to, to match" (see also 合理, 合法) combined with 格 "a
standard, a pattern" (see also 资格, 价格): conforming to the standard —
qualified, up to standard.

## Notes
合格 describes something or someone meeting a required standard (合格
产品 "a product that meets standards," 合格的老师 "a qualified teacher")
— its opposite is 不合格 "substandard, unqualified," the plain negation.

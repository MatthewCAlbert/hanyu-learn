## Why this combination
教 "to teach" (as in 教育, 教师) combined with 材 "material, timber" (as
in 人才 in an unrelated sense "talent," 材料 "material"): material for
teaching — teaching material, a textbook.

## Notes
教材 names the textbooks and materials used for instruction (选用教材
"to select teaching materials") — 课本, its close relative, more
specifically names the physical textbook itself, while 教材 covers
teaching material more broadly, print or otherwise.

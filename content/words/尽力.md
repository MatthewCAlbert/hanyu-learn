## Why this combination

尽 ("to exhaust, to use up to the utmost," as in 尽管, "although [lit.
even exhausting the extent]") takes 力 ("strength, effort") as its
object: using up all one's strength.

## Notes

尽力 pairs naturally with 而为 in the set phrase 尽力而为 ("to do one's
utmost"), and stands alone as an adverbial (尽力帮助, "to help to the
best of one's ability").

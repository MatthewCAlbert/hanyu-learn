## Why this combination
仍 "still, yet again, [to continue] as before" combined with 然, the
classical manner-suffix "so, in that way" seen elsewhere in this dataset
(自然, 突然, 当然): "continuing to be so" — still, unchanged despite the
passage of time or a change in circumstance.

## Notes
仍然 is the fuller, slightly more formal form of bare 仍; both stress
that a state persists in spite of something (虽然...仍然... "although...
[it] still...") — 还 is the more colloquial, more general word for
"still."

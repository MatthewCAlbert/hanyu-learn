## Why this combination
更 "to change, to replace" and 新 "new" combine directly: replacing the
old with something new.

## Notes
更新 spans technology (软件更新 "software update") and general renewal
(更新观念 "to update one's thinking," 新陈代谢 shares the same "old-for-
new" logic in a biological context) — always a refresh from old to new.

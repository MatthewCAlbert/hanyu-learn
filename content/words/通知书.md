## Why this combination

通知 ("to notify," as in 通知) plus 书 ("a document, a letter," as in
秘书, "a secretary [lit. secret + document-handler]") names a written
notice: a notification letter.

## Notes

录取通知书 ("an admission letter") is the standard set phrase students
wait for — 通知书 names the formal written document, distinct from 通知,
which can be a notice given by any means, spoken or written.

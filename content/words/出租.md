## Why this combination
出 "out" (giving out, away from oneself) + 租 "to rent, rent" → to let something
out for payment: 房屋出租 "house to let," 出租设备. It is the landlord's side;
the tenant's side is 租 alone (租房) or 承租.

## Notes
Same 出 = "give out" logic in [[出口]] (export), 出售 (sell), 出借 (lend out).
出租 + 车 = [[出租车]] "a vehicle let out by the ride," i.e. a taxi.

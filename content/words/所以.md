## Why this combination
A frozen classical phrase: 所 "that which" + 以 "by means of / by reason of" =
"that by which," "the means / reason." From "the reason for it" it became the
consequence marker "therefore." The older nominal sense survives in
所以然 "the why of it."

## Notes
Correlates with [[因为]]: 因为…所以…. Colloquial 所以呢？ "so what?" 之所以…是
因为… is the formal inversion.

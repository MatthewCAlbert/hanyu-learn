## Why this combination

天 (here "nature, heaven-given," an extended sense beyond its literal
"sky," as in 天气) plus 真 ("true, genuine," as in 真的) names a
genuineness given by nature, unshaped by worldly experience: innocence,
naivety.

Rated "semi" for the figurative "nature-given" reading of 天, one step
removed from its literal "sky" sense.

## Notes

天真 can read warmly (天真可爱, "innocently endearing," said of a child)
or as a mild criticism of an adult (太天真了, "too naive") depending on
who it describes.

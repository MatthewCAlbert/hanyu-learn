## Why this combination
子, here "son" (as in 儿子) combined with 女 "daughter" (as in 女儿):
sons and daughters together — one's children, as a formal collective
term.

## Notes
子女 is the formal, written word for one's own children, regardless of
gender (独生子女 "an only child," 子女教育 "children's education") —
孩子, the everyday word, is used in ordinary speech; 子女 belongs to more
official or written registers.

## Why this combination
活 "alive, lively" plus 泼 (here "vigorous, splashing," extended from its
base sense "to splash/spill") combine into "as lively as splashing
water" — the imagery of energetic movement carries the figurative
extension to a person's vivacious character.

## Notes
活泼 describes energetic, cheerful character or manner: 活泼的孩子 "a
lively child," 活泼可爱 "lively and endearing" — almost always positive,
describing youthful energy.

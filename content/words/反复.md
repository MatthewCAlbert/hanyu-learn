## Why this combination
反 "to reverse, to turn over" (see also 反应, 反对) combined with 复 "to
repeat, to return, again" (as in 复习 "to review," literally "to
repeat-study"): to turn over and repeat — again and again.

## Notes
反复 modifies verbs of repeated action (反复练习 "to practise
repeatedly," 反复强调 "to stress again and again") and, as a noun/verb,
names an unstable back-and-forth (病情反复 "the illness has a
relapse/recurrence") — both senses share the image of going back over
the same ground.

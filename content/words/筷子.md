## Why this combination
筷 "chopsticks" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]], [[瓶子]]). The
older word was 箸 (*zhù*), but 箸 sounds like 住 "to stop / stay" — bad luck for
boatmen, who wanted a fast passage — so they switched to 快 "fast," later
written with a 竹 "bamboo" top as 筷.

## Notes
A rare case where a character was changed for a taboo, then re-spelled. Measure
word 双: 一双筷子 "a pair of chopsticks."

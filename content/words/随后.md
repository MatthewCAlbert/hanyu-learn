## Why this combination
随 "to follow" and 后 "after, behind" combine directly: following right
behind in time — soon after.

## Notes
随后 is a formal written-register connective linking sequential events:
他讲完话，随后大家鼓掌 "he finished speaking, and shortly after everyone
applauded" — a step up in formality from everyday 然后.

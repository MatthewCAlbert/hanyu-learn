## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 交 "to hand over, to submit" (as in 交流 in an
unrelated sense, 交通) takes 费 "fee, expense" (as in 学费, 房租's own
family) as its object — to hand over a fee, to pay.

## Notes
交费 names the act of paying a required fee (在网上交费 "to pay fees
online," 交电费 "to pay the electricity bill") — 付费, its close relative,
is largely interchangeable, though 交 leans slightly more toward
submitting a payment to an institution or authority.

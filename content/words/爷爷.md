## Why this combination
Doubled kinship term (see [[妈妈]]), toneless second syllable. 爷 was an old word
for "father," then "grandfather / master" (老爷 "lord"). Like [[奶奶]] it is the
father's-side grandparent.

## Notes
Mother's father is 外公 / 姥爷. Also a deferential or ironic address
(张爷, 老天爷 "Heavens!").

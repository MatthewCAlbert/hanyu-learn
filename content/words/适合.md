## Why this combination
适 "to fit, to suit, to be comfortable" (as in 舒适 "comfortable") and 合
"to match, to be fitting, to close together" (as in 合适 "suitable") are
near-synonyms, paired into the transitive verb "to suit."

## Notes
适合 takes a direct object without any preposition (适合你 "suits you,"
适合初学者 "suitable for beginners") — its close relative 合适, built from
the same two characters reversed, is a stative adjective instead ("这件
衣服很合适" "this piece of clothing is a good fit") and does not take an
object.

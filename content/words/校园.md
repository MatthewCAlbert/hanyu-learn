## Why this combination
校 (*xiào*) "school" (see [[学校]], [[校长]]) + 园 "garden, grounds" (see
[[公园]], [[花园]], [[动物园]]) → the grounds of a school or university:
校园生活, 校园歌曲 "campus songs."

## Notes
校 is *xiào* here. 园 "-grounds" is a productive suffix for enclosed
institutional spaces.

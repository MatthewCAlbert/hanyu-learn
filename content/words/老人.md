## Why this combination
老 in its literal sense "old, aged" (not the respectful prefix of [[老师]]) +
人 "person." Modifier-head, like 工人, 客人, [[病人]].

## Notes
家里的老人 "the elders at home." 老人家 is the polite address form; 老年人 the
formal demographic term.

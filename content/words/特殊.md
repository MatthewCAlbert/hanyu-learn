## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 特 ("special, particular," as in 特别, 特点) and
殊 ("distinct, outstanding, markedly different," surviving mostly bound
into words like 殊荣) both independently mark something as set apart from
the ordinary.

Rated "semi" for 殊's less common standalone sense, encountered almost
exclusively inside compounds like this one.

## Notes
特殊 and 特别 overlap heavily; 特殊 leans toward "exceptional, requiring
different treatment" (特殊情况, "a special/exceptional case"), while 特别
covers the broader everyday "especially, particularly."

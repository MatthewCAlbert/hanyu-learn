## Why this combination
桃 "peach" plus 花 "flower" gives exactly "peach blossom" — the literal
flower; the figurative "romance" sense draws on the peach blossom's long
cultural association with spring, beauty, and romantic fortune in
Chinese folklore (as in 桃花运 "luck in love").

## Notes
桃花 names the literal spring flower (桃花盛开 "peach blossoms in full
bloom") and, figuratively, romantic luck: 走桃花运 "to have good luck in
love" — the figurative sense is a fixed cultural idiom, not a
transparent extension.

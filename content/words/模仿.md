## Why this combination
模 "a mould, a model" and 仿 "to imitate" (see 仿's own etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for imitation.

## Notes
模仿 covers learning through imitation (模仿老师的动作 "to copy the
teacher's movements") and comedic mimicry (模仿秀 "impersonation show") —
copying a model, whether to learn or to entertain.

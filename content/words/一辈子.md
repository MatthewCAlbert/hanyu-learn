## Why this combination
一 "one" and 辈 (generation, lifespan — the same 辈 as in 长辈, 同辈) with
the noun suffix 子 give "one whole generation['s span]" — a lifetime.

## Notes
一辈子 is emphatic and personal (我这一辈子 "in my whole life"), more
colloquial and vivid than the neutral 一生 "a lifetime [formal]."

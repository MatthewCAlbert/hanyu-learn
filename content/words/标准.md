## Why this combination
标 "a marker, a sign, that which marks [a level]" (as in 目标 "target")
combined with 准 "accurate, exact, precise": a marker set at an exact,
agreed level — a standard, or, as an adjective, conforming exactly to
one.

## Notes
标准 works as noun ("按照标准" "according to the standard") and adjective
("标准的普通话" "standard Mandarin," "标准答案" "the standard/model
answer") without changing form.

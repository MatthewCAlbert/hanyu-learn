## Why this combination
作 "to do, to carry out" + 业 "task, work, undertaking, study" (学业, 事业) →
work to be done. In school, homework (做作业, 交作业); in industry, an operation
carried out (水下作业 "underwater work," 高空作业).

## Notes
业 "work / undertaking" also in [[毕业]] (not this level), 行业, 作业. School
sense is by far the commonest for learners.

## Why this combination
顺 "going with the flow / grain, compliant, in the right direction" + 利
"favourable, advantageous, sharp." Everything running the easy way, nothing
snagging: 工作很顺利, 一路顺利 "have a smooth trip."

## Notes
顺 "smooth / along" also in [[一路顺风]], 顺便 "on the way." Antonym: 不顺 /
遇到困难.

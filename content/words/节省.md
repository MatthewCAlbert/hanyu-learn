## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 节 ("to economize, to be moderate," as in 节约)
and 省 ("to save, to economize," as in 省钱, "to save money") both
independently name using less than one could. Pairing them gives the
standard word for economizing.

## Notes

节省时间 ("to save time") and 节省开支 ("to cut expenses") both build on
this word — 节省 works for time and resources broadly, not just money,
distinct from 省钱, which specifically names saving money.

## Why this combination
Verb 回 "to return" taking 家 "home" as its object — like [[起床]], a
verb-object phrase, not a two-verb compound. It can split: 回一趟家 "make a trip
home," 回不了家 "can't get home."

## Notes
No preposition: 回家, never 回到家 unless you want the arrival sense "get back
home." Same frame in 回国 (return to one's country), 回校.

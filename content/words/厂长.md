## Why this combination
厂 "factory" plus 长 "chief, head" gives exactly "the head of a factory"
— the same 长-suffix pattern names the head of whatever 厂 (or other
institution) precedes it, as in 校长 "school principal" and 团长 "head of
a group."

## Notes
厂长 anchors 工厂厂长 "factory director" — the standard title for the
person in charge of running a factory's operations.

## Why this combination

发 ("to issue," as in 发生, "to occur") plus 票 ("a ticket, a note," as
in 车票, "a bus/train ticket") names a note issued as proof of purchase:
an invoice, a receipt.

## Notes

开发票 ("to issue an invoice/receipt") is the standard business phrase —
发票 is the official tax-relevant receipt in China, distinct from a
plain 小票 (a simple sales slip without the same formal status).

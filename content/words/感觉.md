## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 感 "to feel, to be affected" + 觉 (*jué*) "to sense, to become
aware" (see [[觉得]]). Both the noun "a feeling / sensation" (一种感觉) and the
verb "to feel" (感觉很累).

## Notes
感觉 spans bodily sensation and vague impression; 觉得 is verb-only and leans
toward opinion; 感到 (see [[感到]]) is verb-only and takes an object directly.

## Why this combination
详 "detailed, thorough" (see 详's own Shuowen etymology, 審議也 "to
deliberate fully") and 细 "fine, minute" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for thoroughness of detail.

## Notes
详细 modifies both accounts (详细说明 "to explain in detail") and
information itself (详细信息 "detailed information") — the everyday
opposite of 简单/简略 "brief, simplified."

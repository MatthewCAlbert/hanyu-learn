## Why this combination
中华, a classical, literary name for China (中 "central," 华
"splendour, flourishing," an old self-designation of the Chinese
civilisation) combined with 民族 "ethnic group, nation" (see its own
entry): the Chinese people as a collective ethnic-national body.

## Notes
中华民族 is used in a broad, unifying, often patriotic register to name
all the ethnic groups of China together as one nation (中华民族的伟大
复兴 "the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation") — 中国人, the plain
everyday phrase, simply means "Chinese person/people."

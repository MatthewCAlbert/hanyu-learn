## Why this combination

促 ("to urge, to hasten," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
促进/促使) plus 销 ("to sell," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
推销) names urging sales along: to promote sales.

## Notes

促销活动 ("a sales promotion event") and 打折促销 ("discount promotion")
both build on this word — 促销 names the broader marketing effort,
while 推销 (covered elsewhere in this app) leans toward one salesperson
actively pushing a specific product.

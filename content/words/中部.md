## Why this combination
中 "middle, centre" (as in 中间, 中国 "the central kingdom") combined
with 部 "a part, a section" (see also 东部, 西部, 南部, 北部): the central
section — completing the full compass-plus-centre "direction + 部" set.

## Notes
中部 names the central region of a country or area (中国中部 "central
China") — 中间, its close relative, is the general word for "the middle
[of anything]," not specifically a bounded geographic region.

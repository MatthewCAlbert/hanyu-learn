## Why this combination
关 (here "to be concerned about," as in 关心) and 怀 (here "to think of,
to cherish in mind") are near-synonyms combined into a warmer, more
formal word for caring about someone's well-being.

## Notes
关怀 anchors 关怀备至 "utmost solicitude," 社会关怀 "social care/concern"
— close to 关心, though 关怀 carries a more tender, often paternal or
institutional register (elders, children, the vulnerable).

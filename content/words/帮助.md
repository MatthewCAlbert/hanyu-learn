## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 帮 "to help" + 助 "to assist" (援助, 助手). Doubled for the
everyday verb and noun.

## Notes
帮助 is transitive and takes the person directly (帮助他). Contrast the
verb-object [[帮忙]] (帮 + 忙), which already has an object and so needs
帮我的忙 / 给我帮忙.

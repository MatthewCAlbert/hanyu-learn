## Why this combination
处 here carries its "to be situated, to dwell (in a state)" sense (a less
common reading than its more familiar "to handle, to deal with" in 处理),
and 于 is the classical preposition "at, in" (covered in this app's
entries for 关于, 对于). "Situated at [a state]" is exactly "to be in
[that condition]."

Rated "semi" for 处's "to be situated" sense, one step removed from the
"to handle/manage" sense most learners meet first.

## Notes
处于 always takes an abstract state or position as its object (处于困境,
"to be in a difficult situation"; 处于领先地位, "to be in the lead") — never
a physical location, which would use 在 instead.

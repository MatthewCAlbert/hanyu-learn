## Why this combination
系 "to connect, to tie" is the more transparent half. 关 is less obvious:
not its common "to close, to shut off" sense, but an extended "to
involve, to be linked to" (as in 关于 "regarding"). Two verbs of linkage,
paired into the noun "a relationship" and the verb "to have bearing on."

## Notes
关系 has a well-known extended social sense in modern Chinese — 拉关系,
搞关系 — networks of personal connection and obligation that go beyond
the literal "relationship" a dictionary gloss suggests; that sense is
cultural rather than etymological, and worth flagging without treating
it as part of the character-level meaning.

## Why this combination
配 (here "to allocate, to fit out") and 备 "to prepare, to equip" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for outfitting something
with necessary equipment or staff.

## Notes
配备 anchors 配备设备 "to equip with facilities," 配备人员 "to allocate
personnel" — always about supplying what's needed to make something
functional or complete.

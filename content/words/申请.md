## Why this combination
申 carries its "to state, to explain formally" sense (as in 申报, "to
declare/report"), and 请 is "to request" (as in 请求, 请客). Formally
stating a request in writing is exactly what "to apply" means.

Rated "semi" for 申's "to state formally" sense, distinct from and less
common than most learners' other exposure to it.

## Notes
申请 always takes the thing applied for as its object (申请签证, "to apply
for a visa"; 申请工作, "to apply for a job") and pairs naturally with 表
(form) in 申请表, "application form."

## Why this combination
一 "one" and 下 (here a measure word for a brief instance/action, as in
等一下 "wait a moment") with the suffix 子 combine into "in the space of
one instant" — something happening suddenly, all in one go.

## Notes
一下子 emphasizes suddenness or completeness of a change: 一下子就懂了
"understood it all at once," 天气一下子冷了 "the weather turned cold all
of a sudden."

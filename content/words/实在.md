## Why this combination
实 "real, solid, true" + 在 "to exist, to be there" → "really existing, actually
so." As an adverb, an earnest "really / honestly" (实在对不起, 实在太难了); as an
adjective, "solid, down-to-earth, dependable" (这个人很实在, 活儿干得实在).

## Notes
The adverb 实在 concedes something with feeling — "I really (must admit)…".
实 also in [[实际]], [[实现]], [[老实]].

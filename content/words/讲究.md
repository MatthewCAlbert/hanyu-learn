## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 讲 ("to speak, to attend to," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 讲座) and 究 ("to investigate
thoroughly," as in 研究, "research") both independently name careful
attention. Pairing them gives the word for being fussy or particular
about details.

Rated "semi" for the compound's shift from "discuss thoroughly" to a
fixed sense of "picky, particular."

## Notes

讲究穿着 ("particular about one's dress") describes someone who cares
about doing things a certain way; 讲究 also works as a noun for the
careful conventions themselves (这里有很多讲究, "there are a lot of
particular customs here").

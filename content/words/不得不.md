## Why this combination
不得, "not permitted to, not able to," negated again by a second 不:
[it is] not [permitted] not to [do something] — a double negative that
grammaticalized into "to have no choice but to."

## Notes
不得不 always sits directly before the verb it governs (我不得不同意
"I had no choice but to agree") and expresses reluctant necessity, unlike
必须 "must," which carries no implication of reluctance.

## Why this combination
软件 calques English "software" morpheme by morpheme: 软 "soft"
translates "soft-," and 件 "item, piece" stands in for "-ware" — a
20th-century technical coinage built directly on the English term's own
internal logic (soft as opposed to the physical, "hard" machine).

## Notes
软件 pairs with 硬件 "hardware" as the standard modern computing
vocabulary — both calques built the same way, translating the English
soft/hard distinction piece by piece.

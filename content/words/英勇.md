## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 英 ("outstanding, heroic," as in 英雄, "a
hero") and 勇 ("brave, courageous," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 勇气, "courage") both independently name outstanding bravery.
Pairing them gives the formal word for heroic valor.

## Notes

英勇牺牲 ("to heroically sacrifice oneself") and 英勇作战 ("to fight
bravely") both build on this word — leans formal/written and often
describes conduct in danger or battle, distinct from everyday 勇敢, the
plainer word for "brave."

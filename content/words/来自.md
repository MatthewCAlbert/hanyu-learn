## Why this combination
来 "to come" + 自 "from" (自 as a preposition, as in 自古, 自…以来). "Comes
from" — a place, a source, a cause: 来自中国, 力量来自团结.

## Notes
自 = "from" (bound; always before its object) vs. 从 (free): 从中国来 = 来自中国.
来自 is a touch more formal and often keeps the source in focus.

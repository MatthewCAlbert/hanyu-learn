## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 结 "to tie, to knot, to form [a bond]" takes 婚
"marriage" as its object — "to tie the marriage-knot," echoing the same
image English keeps in "to tie the knot."

## Notes
结婚 is intransitive in form — one says 跟他结婚 "to marry him" (marking
the partner with 跟/和), never *结婚他; 结婚 also splits for aspect and
quantity (结过婚 "have been married before," 结了三次婚 "married three
times").

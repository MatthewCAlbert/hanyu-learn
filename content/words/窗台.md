## Why this combination

窗 ("window," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 窗户/窗子) plus
台 ("a platform," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 阳台/台上)
names the ledge platform at the base of a window: a windowsill.

## Notes

窗台上的花盆 ("a flowerpot on the windowsill") is a common everyday image
— 窗台 names the ledge itself, distinct from the window's glass and
frame.

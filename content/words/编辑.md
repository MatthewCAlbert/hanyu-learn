## Why this combination
编 "to compile, to weave together [pieces into a whole]" and 辑 "to
gather, to compile" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for
editing and compiling written or media work.

## Notes
编辑 works as verb ("to edit a document") and noun ("an editor," 主编
"editor-in-chief") — the same word for the act and the person who does
it.

## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Perú / Peru (秘魯). 秘 is read *bì* in this
name, not *mì* "secret." 鲁 is phonetic, not the old state of Lu.

## Notes
The *bì* reading is the whole point for a learner: 秘密 *mì* vs 秘鲁 *bì*.

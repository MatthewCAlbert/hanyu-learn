## Why this combination
跟 (here "close to, right by") plus 前 "front" gives exactly "right in
front of" — a spatial phrase for immediate proximity, close enough to be
within someone's presence.

## Notes
跟前 always follows a person or reference point: 在妈妈跟前 "right by
mother's side," 春节跟前 "just before Spring Festival" — the temporal
sense extends spatial "right before" to "just prior in time."

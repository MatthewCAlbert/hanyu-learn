## Why this combination
瓶 "bottle, vase, jar" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]], [[杯子]],
[[桌子]]) → the everyday word for a bottle. Bare 瓶 is also the measure word:
一瓶水, 两瓶酒.

## Notes
瓶 alone in compounds: 花瓶 "vase," 保温瓶 "thermos," 瓶盖 "bottle cap."

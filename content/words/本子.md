## Why this combination
本 "a bound volume, a booklet" (also the measure word for books) + the toneless
noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]]) → the everyday word for a blank writing book.

## Notes
Bare 本 in compounds: [[课本]] textbook, 笔记本 notebook / laptop, 剧本 script.
Measure use: 一本书.

## Why this combination

智 ("wisdom, intelligence," as in 智能, "smart/AI") plus 力 ("power,
strength," as in 力量) names the mental power of intelligence.

## Notes

智力测试 ("IQ test, lit. intelligence test") and 智力发展 ("intellectual
development") both build on this word — 智力 names innate cognitive
capacity, distinct from 知识 ("knowledge," what one has learned).

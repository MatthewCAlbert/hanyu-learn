## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 告 "to tell, to inform" (as in 告诉) takes 别
"departure, to part" (see also 分别, 别人 in an unrelated sense) as its
object — literally "to announce a parting" — to say goodbye.

## Notes
告别 is more formal and often more emotionally weighted than everyday
再见 "goodbye" (告别仪式 "farewell ceremony," 告别过去 "to bid farewell
to the past") — it can also extend figuratively to leaving behind a
stage of life, not only parting from a person.

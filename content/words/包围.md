## Why this combination
包 "to wrap" and 围 "to encircle" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for surrounding something from all sides.

## Notes
包围 covers military encirclement (包围敌军 "to surround enemy troops")
and everyday crowding (被记者包围 "surrounded by reporters") — always
closing in from every direction.

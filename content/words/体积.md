## Why this combination
体 "body, form" plus 积 (here "an accumulated product," the mathematical
sense as in 面积 "area") gives exactly "the accumulated space a body
occupies" — the standard geometric term.

## Notes
体积 anchors 体积大 "large in volume," 计算体积 "to calculate the volume"
— a spatial measurement distinct from 重量 "weight" and 面积 "area."

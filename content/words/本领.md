## Why this combination
本, here "root, origin, one's own" (see also 基本, 本来) combined with
领, extended from "to lead, to command" (see also 领导, 领先) to "to
grasp, to command [a skill]": the ability one commands at its root —
one's own capability.

## Notes
本领 names practical, demonstrated skill or capability (有本领 "to be
capable," 学本领 "to learn a skill") — 才能, its neighbour, leans more
towards innate talent, while 本领 stresses skill acquired and put to
practical use.

## Why this combination
随 "to follow" and 意 "intention, wish" combine directly: following
one's own wishes without constraint.

## Notes
随意 is a common polite invitation ("请随意" "help yourself, feel free")
and, in a more critical register, describes carelessness (随意涂鸦
"careless scribbling," 不要太随意 "don't be too casual about it").

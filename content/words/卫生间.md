## Why this combination
卫生 "hygiene, sanitation" (see its own entry) combined with 间 "a
room, a space" (as in 房间, 时间 in an unrelated sense): the room for
hygiene — a bathroom, restroom.

## Notes
卫生间 is a polite, standard term for a bathroom/toilet, common in public
and formal settings (公共卫生间 "public restroom") — 厕所, more direct
and slightly less formal, and 洗手间, a softer euphemism ("hand-washing
room"), are close synonyms in everyday use.

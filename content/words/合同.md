## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 合 ("to join, to agree, to fit together," as in
合作, 符合) and 同 ("same, together," as in 同意, 同学) both independently
name agreement or unity. A contract is exactly two parties joined
together in agreement — the document that records their shared terms.

Rated "semi" because the leap from "joined together" to the specific
legal-document sense "contract" is a real but non-obvious narrowing.

## Notes
合同 names the formal, usually written legal document; for a looser,
informal agreement between people, 约定 or plain 说好了 ("agreed upon
verbally") is more natural.

## Why this combination
南 "south" + 方 "region, quarter, direction" (see [[方向]], [[地方]]) → the
southern region — in China, roughly south of the Qinling–Huaihe line, with its own climate,
food and speech (南方人, 南方话).

## Notes
The 方 compass set: 东方, [[南方]], [[西方]], [[北方]]. 南边 (see [[南边]]) is
the plain "south side"; 南方 leans toward "the South" as a region.

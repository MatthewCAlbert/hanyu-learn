## Why this combination
电 "electric" (as in 电视 "television," 电脑 "computer") combined with 台
"a platform, a station" (see also 电视台, 舞台): the station broadcasting
by electric [wave] — a radio station.

## Notes
电台 names a radio broadcasting station or transceiver (广播电台 "radio
broadcasting station") — 电视台, its close relative built the same way,
is the parallel word for a television station.

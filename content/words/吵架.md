## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 吵 "to make a noisy racket, to bicker loudly"
(as in 吵闹 "noisy") takes 架 "a fight, a quarrel, a framework" (as in
打架 "to fight") as its object — literally "to noisily quarrel" — to
have a row.

## Notes
吵架 is intransitive and splits for aspect (吵了一架 "had a row," 跟他
吵架 "to quarrel with him," marking the other party with 跟/和) — 打架,
built the same way with 打 "to hit" in place of 吵, escalates from
verbal quarrelling to physical fighting.

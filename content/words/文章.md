## Why this combination
文 "writing, ornamental pattern" (see also 文化, 文件) combined with 章
"a pattern, a chapter, a seal's carved design" (as in 印章 "a seal,"
第一章 "chapter one"): patterned writing organised into a whole — a
written piece, an essay.

## Notes
文章 names any piece of writing from a short essay to a news article
(写文章 "to write an article/essay") and, idiomatically, extends to
"a hidden angle or agenda" (这里面有文章 "there's more to this than meets
the eye," literally "there's an article/pattern inside this").

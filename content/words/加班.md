## Why this combination

加 ("to add," as in 参加) takes 班 ("a shift, a work period," as in 上班,
"to go to work") as its object: adding extra work time — to work
overtime.

## Notes

加班费 ("overtime pay") is the standard compound built on this verb —
加班 names the extra hours worked, regardless of whether they're paid.

## Why this combination
注 (here "to focus, to concentrate on") acting on 重 "important, weighty"
gives exactly "to concentrate on what matters" — the standard word for
giving something priority.

## Notes
注重 takes an abstract object: 注重质量 "to prioritize quality," 注重细节
"to pay attention to detail" — close to 重视, though 注重 leans slightly
more toward sustained practical attention than 重视's broader "to value
highly."

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 运 "to move, to revolve, to transport" (运行, 运转) + 动 "to
move." Broad: physical exercise / sport (做运动, 运动员), motion in physics
(物体的运动), and a mass political or social campaign (五四运动).

## Notes
运动会 "sports meet," 奥运会 "Olympics." The "campaign" sense is historically
loaded in the PRC (文化大革命…运动).

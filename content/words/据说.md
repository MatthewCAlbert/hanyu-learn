## Why this combination
据 "to rely on, according to" (see also 根据, 证据) combined with 说 "to
say": according to what is said — reportedly, hearsay.

## Notes
据说 opens a sentence to introduce unverified secondhand information
(据说他已经走了 "it's said that he's already left") — 听说, its close
relative, is used the same way but stresses the speaker personally heard
it, while 据说 is more neutral about the source.

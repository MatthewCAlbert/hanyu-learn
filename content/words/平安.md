## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 平 "level, calm, uneventful" (see [[平常]], [[平时]]) + 安
"peaceful, safe" (see [[安全]], [[安静]]). Nothing gone wrong, all quiet and
well: 一路平安 "have a safe trip," 平平安安 "safe and sound."

## Notes
平安 = came through without harm (an outcome); [[安全]] = safe (a condition,
a precaution). 报平安 "send word that one is all right."

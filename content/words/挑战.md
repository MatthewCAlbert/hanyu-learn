## Why this combination
挑 takes its *tiǎo* reading, "to provoke, to stir up" (covered in this
app's hanzi entry), with 战 ("battle, fight") as its object: to provoke a
fight is to issue a challenge. Fully compositional once 挑's *tiǎo* sense
is known — the same word family as 挑逗 (to provoke/tease).

## Notes
挑战 works as both verb (挑战自己, "to challenge oneself") and noun (面对
挑战, "to face a challenge") without changing form.

## Why this combination
冲 "to rush, to surge" (see 冲's role in 冲突) and 动 "to move" combine
directly: a sudden surge of movement — an urge that pushes one to act
without thinking.

## Notes
冲动 works as noun ("购物冲动" "the urge to shop") and adjective ("冲动的
决定" "an impulsive decision") — always about acting on a sudden urge
rather than careful deliberation.

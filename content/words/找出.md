## Why this combination
找 "to look for" (see [[找到]]) + directional 出 "out" — searching that brings
something out into view: 找出问题所在 "identify where the problem lies,"
从抽屉里找出一张照片.

## Notes
找出 emphasises producing the thing from where it was hidden; [[找到]]
emphasises the success of locating it. 出 "bring out" also in [[提出]],
[[得出]], 看出.

## Why this combination
糟 alone already means "spoiled, in a mess" (see 糟's own etymology —
酒滓也, wine dregs). 糕 "cake" contributes little independent meaning here
and functions mainly as an echoing second syllable, giving the phrase its
familiar rhythm — the compound's force comes almost entirely from 糟.

## Notes
糟糕! is a common standalone exclamation ("oh no! terrible!"), and 糟糕的
天气 "terrible weather" shows its adjectival use — always negative,
usually about an unwelcome turn of events.

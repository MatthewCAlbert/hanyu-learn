## Why this combination
判 "to judge, to distinguish, to sentence [in court]" (as in 判决 "to
rule, verdict") combined with 断 "to decide, to cut off, to sever" (as
in 不断): to judge and decide — to reach a conclusion.

## Notes
判断 works as verb ("很难判断" "hard to judge/tell") and noun ("正确的
判断" "a correct judgment") — it names the mental act of weighing
evidence to reach a conclusion, distinct from 决定 "to decide," which
names choosing a course of action.

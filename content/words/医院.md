## Why this combination
医 "medicine, to heal" + 院, the suffix for a walled institution or
establishment — the same 院 in 学院 (college), 法院 (court), 电影院 (cinema). A
"healing establishment."

## Notes
去医院看病 "go to the hospital to see a doctor." 住院 "be hospitalised,"
门诊 "outpatient clinic."

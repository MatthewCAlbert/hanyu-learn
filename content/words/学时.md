## Why this combination

学 ("to study," as in 学习) plus 时 ("time, an hour," covered elsewhere
in this app as part of 准时) names a unit of instructional time: a
class hour.

## Notes

一个学期共有多少学时 ("how many class hours in one term") is a typical
academic-planning question — 学时 names the standard unit used to
measure course workload, distinct from 学分 (covered elsewhere in this
app), the credit earned for completing that workload.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 平 ("flat, even," as in 平均, "average [lit.
even + equal]") and 稳 ("stable, steady," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 稳定) both independently name a lack of disruptive change.
Pairing them gives the standard word for smooth stability.

## Notes

平稳飞行 ("a smooth flight") and 经济平稳发展 ("steady economic
development") both build on this word — 平稳 emphasizes the absence of
sudden jolts or fluctuation, close to but more physical/concrete than
the more abstract 稳定.

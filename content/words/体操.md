## Why this combination

体 ("body," as in 身体) plus 操 ("to exercise, to drill," as in 操作,
"to operate [lit. drill + do]") names disciplined bodily exercise:
gymnastics.

## Notes

做体操 ("to do gymnastics/exercises") and 体操运动员 ("a gymnast") both
build on this word — 体操 spans both the competitive sport and simple
morning calisthenics (广播体操, "radio calisthenics," a shared routine
once common in Chinese schools and workplaces).

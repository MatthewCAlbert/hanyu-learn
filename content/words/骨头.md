## Why this combination

骨 ("bone") plus the nominal suffix 头 (as in 石头, "stone") names the
everyday object: a bone.

## Notes

骨头 is the everyday spoken word; 骨 alone survives in more formal/
technical compounds (骨骼, "skeleton"; 骨折, "a fracture") without the
suffix.

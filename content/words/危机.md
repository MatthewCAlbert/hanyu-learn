## Why this combination
危 "danger" plus 机 "critical point, mechanism". The popular reading of 机 as "opportunity" is a modern myth; 机 means a pivotal moment, which is both a threat and a chance.

## Notes
契机 shows the real sense of 机 in "turning point".

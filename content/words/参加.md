## Why this combination
参 "to join, to take part" (参与) + 加 "to add (oneself)." To add yourself to an
activity or body: 参加考试, 参加会议, 参加工会.

## Notes
参加 takes the event or organisation directly. Contrast 加入 "join (a group as a
member)" and 参与 "be involved in / a party to."

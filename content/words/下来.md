## Why this combination
下 "down" and 来 "to come" combine transparently for the literal sense —
motion downward, toward the speaker. Semi rather than transparent
because 下来's very common grammatical uses (as a directional/aspect
complement after another verb, marking continuation "up to now" or
completion) are considerably more abstract than the plain literal
motion.

## Notes
As a complement, 下来 marks two related grammaticalised senses:
continuation into the present (坚持下来 "held on, [and still is]") and
a process reaching completion or settling (安静下来 "to settle down/grow
quiet") — both extensions of "coming down to rest."

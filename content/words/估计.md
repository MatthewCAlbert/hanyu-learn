## Why this combination
估 "to appraise/guess a value" and 计 "to calculate, to reckon" reinforce
each other into "to estimate."

## Notes
估计 covers numeric estimates (估计成本 "to estimate the cost") and, in
casual speech, a softer "I'd guess/suppose" (我估计他不会来 "I don't
think he'll come") — closer to a hedge than a precise calculation there.

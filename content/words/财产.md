## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 财 ("wealth, money," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 产 ("to produce," extended here to "assets, what one owns," as
in 产品, 生产) both independently point to valuable possessions. Pairing
them gives the standard legal/formal word for property.

## Notes
财产 names owned assets broadly — money, goods, real estate — while 财富
("wealth") emphasizes abundance itself rather than the specific things
owned.

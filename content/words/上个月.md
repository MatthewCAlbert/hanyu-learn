## Why this combination

上 ("previous, last," as in 上次, "last time") plus the classifier 个
plus 月 ("month") names the month before this one: last month.

## Notes

上个月 sits in the same family as 上个星期/上星期 ("last week") and 上次
("last time") — 上 marks "the previous one" across several time-unit
words, mirrored by 下 ("next") in 下个月, 下星期, etc.

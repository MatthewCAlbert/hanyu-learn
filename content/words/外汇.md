## Why this combination

外 ("outside, foreign," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
外交官) plus 汇 ("to converge, to exchange [money]," covered elsewhere
in this app as part of 汇报/词汇) names money exchanged with foreign
currencies: foreign exchange.

Rated "semi" for 汇's specialized "monetary exchange" sense here, one
step from its more general "gather/converge" meaning.

## Notes

外汇储备 ("foreign exchange reserves") and 外汇市场 ("the foreign exchange
market") both build on this word — the standard finance term, 汇率
("exchange rate") shares its 汇 root.

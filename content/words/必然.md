## Why this combination
必 "certainly, necessarily, without fail" (see also 必要, 必须) combined
with the classical manner-suffix 然 "so, in that way" (as in 自然, 显
然): certainly so, without fail — inevitable.

## Notes
必然 describes an outcome that logically must follow (必然结果
"inevitable result," 历史的必然 "historical inevitability") — it pairs
against 偶然 "by chance, accidental" as the standard
necessary/contingent philosophical contrast.

## Why this combination
大 "big, great" doubled into an adverb of degree — "to a great extent":
大大提高了效率, 大大超过预期. Reduplicating a monosyllable to make an intensive
adverb is a regular pattern (深深, 好好).

## Notes
A separate dialectal noun 大大 means "dad" or "uncle" — same characters,
unrelated. The adverb always sits before the verb it boosts.

## Why this combination
现 "to appear, to show, present" (see also 现实, 现在) combined with 象
"image, appearance" (see also 印象, 对象): an appearance that presents
itself to observation — a phenomenon.

## Notes
现象 names an observable occurrence worth noting, especially in a
scientific or social context (自然现象 "a natural phenomenon," 社会现象
"a social phenomenon") — it names the observable pattern itself, distinct
from 原因 "the cause" behind it.

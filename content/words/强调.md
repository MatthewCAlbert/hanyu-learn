## Why this combination
强 "strong" (see also 强大, 强烈) combined with 调, here "to tune, to
pitch, to adjust the tone of" (as in 调整): to pitch something with
extra strength — to emphasise, to stress.

## Notes
强调 takes a point or fact as its object (强调重要性 "to emphasise the
importance," 一再强调 "to stress repeatedly") — it names the act of
drawing special attention to a point in speech or writing.

## Why this combination
失 ("to lose," covered in this app's entry for 失败) takes 望 ("hope,
expectation," as in 希望) as its object: losing the hope one held for
something is exactly disappointment.

## Notes
对…感到失望 or 对…很失望 ("to feel disappointed in/by...") is the standard
frame — 失望 needs 对 to mark what the disappointment is directed at,
rather than taking a bare object.

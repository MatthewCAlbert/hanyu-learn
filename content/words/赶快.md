## Why this combination
赶 "to hurry, to rush" (see also 赶紧) combined with 快 "fast, quick": to
hurry, quickly — an urging adverb for immediate action.

## Notes
赶快 sits before the verb it urges, exactly like its close synonym 赶紧
(赶快走 "hurry up and go") — the two are essentially interchangeable in
everyday speech.

## Why this combination
项 is "item" (as introduced in this app's hanzi entry — originally "back of
the neck," extended to a numbered entry on a list), and 目 here is its
bound "category, listed entry" sense (as in 题目, 科目, 栏目) rather than its
far more common literal sense "eye." Two words for "a listed entry" are
paired to name a single unit within a larger scheme — a task, a
competition event, or a funded undertaking.

Rated "semi" because 目's "category/entry" sense is easy to miss under its
dominant "eye" meaning.

## Notes
项目 spans registers: 一个项目 can be a business/engineering project, or —
in sports — a specific competition event (游泳项目, "the swimming event").

## Why this combination
在 ("to exist, to be located at") plus 乎 (here functioning as a
classical preposition equivalent to 于 "at, in," the same root sense
covered in this app's hanzi entry for 乎 — breath rising and dispersing,
later grammaticalized into a preposition role). This mirrors 在于
(covered elsewhere in this app) exactly, with 乎 standing in for 于: what
one cares about is what one's attention "rests at."

Rated "semi" for 乎's classical preposition role here, a specific,
less-common grammaticalized use.

## Notes
在乎 is casual/spoken (不在乎, "doesn't care/mind") — 在于 (covered
elsewhere in this app) is the more formal, written-register cousin built
on the exact same 在 + [preposition] pattern.

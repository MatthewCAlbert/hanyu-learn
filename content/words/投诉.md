## Why this combination

投 ("to throw, to submit," as in 投资, "to invest [lit. throw in
capital]") plus 诉 ("to tell, to state one's grievance," as in 告诉)
names formally submitting a grievance to an authority: to complain, to
file a complaint.

Rated "semi" for 投's extended "to submit (a document/case)" sense here.

## Notes

投诉 is the formal, often written or hotline-mediated complaint (向消费
者协会投诉, "to complain to the consumer association") — distinct from
plain 抱怨, casual grumbling with no formal target.

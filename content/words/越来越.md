## Why this combination
Built on the 越…越… correlative ("the more X, the more Y"): 越 "to exceed" +
来 "as it comes along (in time)" + 越 "to exceed." "The further it goes, the
more so" — a change tracking with the passage of time: 天气越来越冷.

## Notes
The full pattern 越A越B still works: 越忙越乱 "the busier, the more chaotic."
越来越 is its fixed "over time" version.

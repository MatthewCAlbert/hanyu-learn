## Why this combination
公共 "public, communal" (公 public + 共 shared; see [[公园]], [[公司]]) +
[[汽车]] "car / motor vehicle" → a motor vehicle for shared public use. Parse
公共 + 汽车. 坐公共汽车 "go by bus."

## Notes
Usually clipped to 公交车 or 公交 (公共交通 "public transport"); 巴士 (from
"bus") is used in the south. 校车 "school bus," 大巴 "coach."

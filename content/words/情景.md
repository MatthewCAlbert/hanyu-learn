## Why this combination

情 ("feeling, emotion," as in 感情) plus 景 ("a scene, a view," as in
风景) names a scene imbued with feeling: a vivid situation or moment.

## Notes

情景 names a specific, often memorable scene (那个情景, "that scene/
moment") — 情况 is the more neutral, general word for "situation,"
without the emotional coloring.

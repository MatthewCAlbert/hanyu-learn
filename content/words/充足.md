## Why this combination
充 "full, ample" and 足 "sufficient, enough" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for an ample supply.

## Notes
充足 modifies resources or conditions: 阳光充足 "plenty of sunlight,"
理由充足 "sufficient reasons" — always about quantity being enough or
more than enough, not quality.

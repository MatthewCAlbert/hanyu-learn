## Why this combination

中 here is read *zhòng* ("to hit [a target], to score"), a distinct
tone from the far more common *zhōng* ("middle," as in 中午). Read as
*zhòng*, it takes 奖 ("a prize," covered elsewhere in this app as part
of 奖金/获奖) as its object: to hit a prize — to win the lottery.

Rated "semi": the tone switch is the whole key, the same pattern seen
in 会计 and 好 in 爱好, both covered elsewhere in this app — read 中 as
*zhōng*, and the compound stops making sense.

## Notes

中彩票 ("to win the lottery") is a close near-synonym built the same
way, with 彩票 "lottery ticket" spelled out instead of the generic 奖.

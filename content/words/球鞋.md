## Why this combination
球 "ball (game)" (see [[篮球]], [[打球]]) + 鞋 "shoes" → shoes for ball sports,
now the general word for canvas or rubber-soled casual shoes: 一双球鞋,
穿球鞋.

## Notes
鞋 "shoes" also in 皮鞋 "leather shoes," 拖鞋 "slippers," 高跟鞋 "high heels."
运动鞋 is the broader "sports shoes."

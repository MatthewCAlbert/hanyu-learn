## Why this combination
一口 "one mouthful" plus 气 "breath, air" gives exactly "one breath's
worth" — the standard idiom for doing something continuously without
pausing, as if in a single exhale.

## Notes
一口气 anchors 一口气跑完 "ran the whole way in one go," 一口气读完这本书
"read the whole book in one sitting" — always emphasizes uninterrupted
continuity, whether physical breath or metaphorical momentum.

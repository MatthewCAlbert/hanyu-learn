## Why this combination
烤 "to roast" acting on 鸭 "duck" gives exactly "roasted duck" — a
straightforward, fully compositional food word.

## Notes
烤鸭 anchors 北京烤鸭 "Peking duck" — one of China's most internationally
recognized dishes, named simply for its preparation method and main
ingredient.

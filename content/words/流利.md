## Why this combination
流 "to flow" (see [[流行]], 流水) + 利 "smooth, sharp, going easily" (see
[[顺利]], 便利) → moving along without a snag, like flowing water. Of language:
说一口流利的中文, 文笔流利.

## Notes
流利 = fluent (no hesitation); 熟练 = skilled / practised; 地道 = idiomatic,
native-like. 流 "flow" also in [[流行]], 交流 "exchange."

## Why this combination
间 is "space, gap, interval" (as in 房间, 中间), and 之 is the same literary
linking particle seen in 之前/之后 — "[things] 之 [space-between]" reads as
"the space between [things]." Requires two things (or a plural/collective
noun) before it: 朋友之间, 中国和美国之间.

## Notes
之间 always needs plural or paired reference before it — 我和他之间 ("between
him and me") is fine, but a single unpaired noun cannot take 之间 the way it
can take 之前/之后.

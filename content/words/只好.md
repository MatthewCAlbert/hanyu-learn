## Why this combination
只 "only" combined with 好, here a light "fine, in that case" sense
rather than "good": only [this option is] fine — to have no choice but
to accept the one remaining course of action.

## Notes
只好 sits before the verb, marking reluctant necessity forced by
circumstance (下雨了，只好留在家里 "it started raining, so I had no
choice but to stay home") — 不得不, a close synonym, works the same way;
只好 is slightly more colloquial.

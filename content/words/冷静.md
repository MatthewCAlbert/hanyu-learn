## Why this combination
冷 ("cold") plus 静 ("still, quiet," as in 安静) pictures a mind as cold
and unmoving as still water, undisturbed by heated emotion — exactly
what "calm, level-headed" describes.

Rated "semi" for the figurative leap from literal "cold" to emotional
composure, the same metaphor English "cool-headed" makes.

## Notes
保持冷静 ("to stay calm") is the standard everyday frame — 冷静 describes
a mental state, distinct from 安静 (physical quiet/silence) even though
both share 静.

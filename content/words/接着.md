## Why this combination
接 "to join, to connect, to follow on" + the durative particle 着 "keeping (in
that state)" → "carrying straight on from." As a connective, "next / and then"
(接着他说…); as a verb, "continue" (接着做) or "catch" (接着球).

## Notes
接着 chains an action tightly onto the previous one (no gap), where [[然后]]
allows a pause. 着 is toneless *zhe* here.

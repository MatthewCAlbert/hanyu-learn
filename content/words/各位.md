## Why this combination
各 "each, every" modifying 位 (a polite classifier for people, literally
"a position, a seat") — each person present, addressed individually and
respectfully: everyone.

## Notes
各位 is specifically a term of address, used to open a speech or greet a
group directly (各位同学 "fellow students," 各位来宾 "honoured guests") —
it is not used as a plain third-person "everyone" the way 大家 is.

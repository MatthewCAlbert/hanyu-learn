## Why this combination

利 ("profit, benefit," as in 利益) plus 息 (here "interest [on money],"
an extended financial sense of a character more commonly meaning "to
rest" or "breath," as in 休息) names the profit that accrues on money:
interest.

Rated "semi" for 息's specialized financial sense here, distinct from
its everyday "to rest/breath" meaning.

## Notes

利息 (interest earned/owed) sits alongside 本金 ("principal") as the two
components of a loan or deposit — 利率 ("interest rate," lit. "interest
+ rate") names the percentage that generates it.

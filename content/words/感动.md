## Why this combination
感 "to feel, to be affected" + 动 "to move" — one's feelings set in motion, the
same metaphor as English "moved / touched." Transitive (这个故事感动了很多人)
and stative (我很感动).

## Notes
感 "be affected" also in [[感觉]], [[感到]], [[感谢]]. 令人感动 "moving,
touching."

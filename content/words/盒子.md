## Why this combination
盒 "a box, a case" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a box" — the
suffix adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of 桌子, 椅子.

## Notes
盒子 is the general word for a small container with a lid: 一盒子巧克力
"a box of chocolates," 饭盒子 "lunch box" — smaller and more rigid than a
bag (袋子), typically not stackable open like 箱子 "a crate/case."

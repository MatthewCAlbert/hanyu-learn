## Why this combination

以 (a bound-marking preposition, as in 以后/以前) plus 内 ("inside,
within," as in 国内, "domestic [lit. within the country]") names being
within a stated range or limit.

## Notes

一小时以内 ("within one hour") and 范围以内 ("within the scope") both
build on this word — 以外 ("outside of, beyond") is its direct opposite,
built the same way with 外 "outside" instead of 内.

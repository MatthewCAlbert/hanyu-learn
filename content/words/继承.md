## Why this combination
继 "to continue" and 承 "to receive, to carry on" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for inheritance and succession.

## Notes
继承 covers property (继承遗产 "to inherit an estate"), political
succession (继承王位 "to succeed to the throne"), and cultural
transmission (继承传统 "to carry on a tradition") — receiving and
continuing what came before.

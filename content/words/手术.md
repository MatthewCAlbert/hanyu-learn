## Why this combination
手 ("hand") plus 术 ("skill, technique," as in 技术, 武术) names surgery
exactly as what it is: a technique performed by hand. Fully transparent
once 术's "skill/technique" sense — distinct from any of 手's more common
"hand" compounds — is known.

## Notes
做手术 ("to have/perform an operation") and 动手术 (the same, more
colloquial) are the standard verb phrases; 手术 itself is a noun and does
not act as a verb alone.

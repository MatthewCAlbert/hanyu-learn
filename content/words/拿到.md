## Why this combination
拿 "to take, to hold, to grasp" + resultative 到 "reach" — the taking
succeeded, it is now in hand: 拿到工资 "got paid," 拿到签证, 拿到第一名.

## Notes
拿到 = get physically in hand or in one's possession; [[得到]] = get / receive
(broader, often abstract); [[取得]] = achieve (formal, abstract). 拿 alone:
拿筷子, 拿主意.

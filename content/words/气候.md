## Why this combination
气, here "weather, atmosphere" (as in 天气 "weather") combined with 候
"a season, a period of time" (as in 时候, 气候's own 候 tied to the
classical division of the year into 24 solar terms): the seasonal
pattern of weather — climate.

## Notes
气候 names the long-term pattern of weather in a region (气候变化
"climate change") — 天气, its close relative, names the short-term,
day-to-day weather condition, a distinction Chinese maintains as
sharply as English "climate" vs. "weather."

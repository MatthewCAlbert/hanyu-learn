## Why this combination
旅 "to travel" (as in 旅行, 旅游) combined with 馆 "a lodge, a hall, a
building for public use" (as in 图书馆 "library," 宾馆 "guesthouse"): a
lodging hall for travellers — an inn, a hotel.

## Notes
旅馆 is a general, somewhat plainer word for lodging than 宾馆 or 酒店
"hotel" — the three overlap heavily, with 旅馆 often implying a
smaller, simpler establishment.

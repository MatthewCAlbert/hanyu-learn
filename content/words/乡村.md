## Why this combination
乡 "village, one's home region" and 村 "village" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for rural areas generally.

## Notes
乡村 covers the countryside broadly (乡村生活 "rural life," 乡村音乐
"country music," a modern calque of the American genre name) — distinct
from 农村, which leans specifically toward agricultural villages.

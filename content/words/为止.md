## Why this combination
为 here carries its classical "to serve as, to count as" sense rather
than the everyday "for, because of" — 为止 literally "to serve as the
stopping point." Once that sense of 为 is known, the leap to "until" is
short.

## Notes
为止 almost always follows 到 or 至 in a fixed pattern: 到目前为止 "up until
now," 到2020年为止 "up until 2020" — rarely stands alone without one of
those markers.

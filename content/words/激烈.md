## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 激 ("to stir up, to provoke," covered elsewhere
in this app — turbulent water breaking against an obstacle) and 烈
("fierce, intense," as in 强烈) both independently name intensity.
Pairing them gives the standard word for a heated, fierce competition or
conflict.

## Notes
激烈 describes competitions, debates, and fighting (激烈的竞争, "fierce
competition") — a heated, high-intensity clash, not a calm or gradual
process.

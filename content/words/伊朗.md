## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Īrān. 伊 and 朗 are chosen for sound. 朗 is
not "bright."

## Notes
伊 as a phonetic also in [[伊拉克]], [[以色列]] — different names, same convenient
syllable.

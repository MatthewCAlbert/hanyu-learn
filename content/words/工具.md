## Why this combination
工 "work, craft, labour" (as in 工人 "worker") combined with 具 "a tool,
an implement, [something] equipped/prepared" (see also 具体, 具有): the
implement used for work — a tool.

## Notes
工具 covers physical hand tools (工具箱 "toolbox") and, figuratively,
any means to an end (语言是交流的工具 "language is a tool for
communication") — the same literal/figurative extension "tool" makes in
English.

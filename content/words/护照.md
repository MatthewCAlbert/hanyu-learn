## Why this combination
护 "to protect, to guard" (保护, 护士) + 照, here "an official permit / warrant"
(执照 licence, 牌照 registration plate). Historically a document from the
authorities granting safe passage and protection to a traveller — a
"protection warrant." 办护照, 护照过期.

## Notes
照 "permit / warrant" also in 执照, 牌照; distinct from 照 "to shine / photograph"
([[照片]], [[照相]]). 签证 "visa" goes inside the 护照.

## Why this combination

智 ("wisdom, intelligence," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
智力) plus 能 ("ability, capability," as in 可能) names the ability
associated with intelligence: smart, intelligent.

## Notes

人工智能 ("artificial intelligence") and 智能手机 ("smartphone") are the
standard modern tech compounds — 智能 as an adjective now most often
describes devices with built-in computing ability, not just human minds.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 清 ("clear") and 醒 ("to wake up, to sober up,"
covered elsewhere in this app) both independently name a state of clear
awareness. Pairing them gives the standard word for a clear, alert mind.

## Notes
清醒 covers both literal wakefulness (从昏迷中清醒, "to regain
consciousness from a coma") and mental clarity/rationality (保持清醒,
"to stay clear-headed") — the same "clear awareness" core in both.

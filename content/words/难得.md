## Why this combination
难 "difficult" modifying 得 "to obtain" gives exactly "difficult to
obtain" — as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
难得 praises something as precious precisely because it's rare: 难得的
机会 "a rare opportunity," 难得一见 "seldom seen" — implies the rarity adds
to, not detracts from, its value.

## Why this combination
不 "not" + 如 "to be like, to come up to, to match" (see [[如果]], [[比如]]) →
"does not come up to," falls short of. Comparison (今年不如去年 "this year isn't
as good as last") and suggestion (不如我们走吧 "we might as well go").

## Notes
A 不如 B = "A is worse than B" — note the order is the reverse of A 比 B 好.
The "might as well" use often opens with 与其…不如… "rather than… better to…".

## Why this combination
信 "to trust, to believe" (as in 相信 "to believe") combined with 任 "to
entrust, to appoint, to rely on [someone] with a duty" (see also 责任):
to trust and entrust — to place confidence in someone.

## Notes
信任 names trust placed in a person's reliability or integrity (互相
信任 "to trust each other," 信任危机 "a crisis of trust") — 相信, its
close relative, more often means "to believe [a statement is true]"
rather than to trust a person's character.

## Why this combination
变 "to change, to become different" (see also 变化, 转变) combined with
为 "to become, to be, to act as" (see also 称为, 认为): to change into
being [something else] — to turn into.

## Notes
变为 is a formal, written verb marking a transformation from one state
to another (荒地变为良田 "wasteland turned into fertile fields") — 变成,
its close synonym, is used the same way and is somewhat more common in
everyday speech.

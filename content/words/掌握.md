## Why this combination
掌 "the palm of the hand" and 握 "to grasp, to hold" combine directly:
holding something firmly in the palm — literally at first, then
figuratively for skill, information, or power.

## Notes
掌握 spans mastering a skill (掌握一门语言 "to master a language"),
holding information (掌握情况 "to have a grasp of the situation"), and
control (掌握大权 "to hold power") — all "having a firm grip on."

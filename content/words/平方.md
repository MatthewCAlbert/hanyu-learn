## Why this combination

平 ("flat, even," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 平稳) plus
方 ("square," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 大方, there used
figuratively) names a flat square shape, extended to the mathematical
operation and unit built on it.

## Notes

平方米 ("square meter") and 平方公里 ("square kilometer") both build on
this word as a unit of area — 平方 alone also names the math operation
"to square [a number]" (三的平方是九, "three squared is nine").

## Why this combination
汇 "to remit (money through a bank)" acting on the object 款 "a sum of
money" gives exactly "to remit funds" — splittable, as in 汇了一笔款.

## Notes
汇款 works as verb ("向银行汇款" "to remit money to a bank") and noun
("收到一笔汇款" "received a remittance") — the standard banking term for
transferring money, whether domestic or international.

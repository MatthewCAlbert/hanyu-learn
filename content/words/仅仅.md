## Why this combination
仅 "only, merely" (see also 不仅) reduplicated for emphasis: doubling the
adverb intensifies its restrictive sense — "only, and nothing more than
that."

## Notes
仅仅 sits before the element it restricts to underline how limited it is
(仅仅是开始 "this is merely the beginning") — it is more emphatic than
bare 仅 or 只, stressing that the amount or scope really is that small.

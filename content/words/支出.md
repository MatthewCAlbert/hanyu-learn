## Why this combination
支 (here "to pay out, to disburse") plus the directional complement 出
"out" gives exactly "to pay out" — the standard financial word for
outgoing money, opposite of 收入 "income."

## Notes
支出 works as verb ("支出一笔款项" "to disburse a sum") and noun ("家庭
支出" "household expenses") — the formal accounting term, more precise
than everyday 花钱 "to spend money."

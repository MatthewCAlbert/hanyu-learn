## Why this combination
英 clipped from 英国 "England / Britain" (英 being the phonetic rendering of
"Eng-") + 语 "language." The same mould as 汉语, 日语, 法语. 英语 stresses the
spoken language; 英文 the written.

## Notes
英 as the country-abbreviation also in 英镑 "pound sterling," 中英 "China–UK."

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 原 "origin, source, the original" (原来, 原本) + 因 "cause" (see
[[因为]]). The root behind an outcome: 找出原因 "find the cause,"
原因很复杂.

## Notes
原因 pairs with 结果 (cause / effect). 理由 "grounds, justification" is what you
give to defend a position; 原因 is what actually produced something.

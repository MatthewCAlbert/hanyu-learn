## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 立 (here in its "at once, immediately" sense, as
in 立刻) and 即 ("right at, arriving at [this moment]," covered in this
app's hanzi entry) both independently mark immediacy. Pairing them
intensifies plain "at once" into a more formal "immediately."

## Notes
立即 is the more formal/written register's "immediately" — 马上 or 立刻
read more naturally in everyday spoken Mandarin.

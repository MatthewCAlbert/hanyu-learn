## Why this combination

隔 ("to separate, to be apart," as in 隔壁, "next door") plus 开 ("apart, open," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 拉开) names creating a separation between things: to
partition.

## Notes

用墙隔开 ("to separate with a wall") and 把他们隔开 ("to keep them apart")
both build on this word — describes creating physical or figurative
distance between things that would otherwise be together.

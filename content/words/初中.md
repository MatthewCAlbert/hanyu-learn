## Why this combination
初中 shortens 初级中学 "junior-level middle school" to its first
character from each half: 初 from 初级 "beginning level" (see also 初步,
当初) and 中 from 中学 "middle school" — the same compression pattern as
科技 and 环保.

## Notes
初中 names the first three years of secondary education (初中生
"junior high student") — 高中, built the same way with 高 "senior" in
place of 初, names the following three years of senior high school.

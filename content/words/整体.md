## Why this combination
整 "whole, entire" (see also 整个, 整理) combined with 体 "body, entity"
(see also 具体, 团体): the whole body [of something] — the entirety,
considered as one unit.

## Notes
整体 treats a complex thing as a single, unified whole (整体上 "on the
whole," 整体规划 "an overall/holistic plan") — 全部, "all of it," simply
totals up the parts, while 整体 emphasises the parts functioning as one
integrated system.

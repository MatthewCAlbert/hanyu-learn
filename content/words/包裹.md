## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 包 ("to wrap, to package," as in 书包, "school
bag") and 裹 ("to wrap up," a character occurring mainly in this role)
both independently name enclosing something in a wrapping. Pairing them
gives the standard word for a wrapped parcel.

## Notes

包裹 is the postal/shipping-register noun (寄包裹, "to mail a parcel") —
everyday casual wrapping more often uses plain 包 alone (书包, 面包).

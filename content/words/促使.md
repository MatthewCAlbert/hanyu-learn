## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 促 ("to urge, to hasten," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 促进, "to promote") and 使 ("to make, to cause," as
in 即使 — here in its causative "to cause X to do Y" role) both
independently name pushing an outcome to happen. Pairing them gives the
formal word for prompting a result.

## Notes

促使他改变主意 ("prompted him to change his mind") is the standard
causative frame — 促使 always leads to a change in someone's action or
state, distinct from 促进, which more often names advancing an abstract
process.

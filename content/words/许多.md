## Why this combination
许 in its sense "an amount, a degree" (少许 "a little," 些许) + 多 "many" → "a
large amount." A quantifier taking no measure word: 许多人, 许多问题.

## Notes
许多 (slightly formal / written) ≈ 很多 (neutral) ≈ [[好多]] (colloquial). 许
"perhaps" and "allow" are separate senses of the same character.

## Why this combination
可 in its adversative sense "but, yet" + 是 "is," appended to make a stable
conjunction (compare [[但是]], [[还是]]). The everyday spoken "but"; also for
emphasis before a verb: 这可是真的 "this really is true."

## Notes
可是 (colloquial) · [[但是]] (neutral) · 然而 (formal). 可 keeps the "but" sense
alone in 可惜 "what a pity."

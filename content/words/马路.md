## Why this combination
Standard account: a "horse road" — one wide and firm enough for horses and
carriages, as against a mere footpath. (A folk story links it instead to
"macadam" 马卡当 road-surfacing; this is not the accepted etymology.)

## Notes
过马路 "cross the street," 马路对面 "the other side of the road." Now any paved
urban road, horses long gone.

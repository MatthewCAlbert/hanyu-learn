## Why this combination
严 ("strict, severe," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 格 ("a
standard, a pattern to be held to," as in 格式, 性格) names a standard
held to with severity — no deviation allowed.

Rated "semi" for 格's abstract "standard/pattern" sense, distinct from
its role in more concrete compounds like 表格 (a form).

## Notes
严格 modifies both people (严格的老师, "a strict teacher") and processes
(严格要求, "to demand strictly/rigorously") — a broadly applicable word
for "no room for laxity."

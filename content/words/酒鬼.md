## Why this combination
酒 "alcohol" plus 鬼 (here a derogatory suffix, "a fiend for," as in 烟鬼
"chain smoker" and 懒鬼 "lazybones") gives exactly "someone possessed by
drink" — a mildly contemptuous label for habitual drinking.

## Notes
酒鬼 is informal and disapproving: 他是个酒鬼 "he's a drunk" — part of a
productive 鬼-suffix pattern labeling someone excessively given to a
vice or trait.

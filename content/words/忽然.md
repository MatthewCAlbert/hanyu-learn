## Why this combination
忽 "abruptly, in a flash" (忽而, 疏忽) + 然 the adverb-forming suffix "-ly, in
that manner" (see [[然后]], [[虽然]], 当然). "In an abrupt manner" — an
unexpected turn: 忽然下起雨来.

## Notes
忽然 ≈ 突然 (which can also be an adjective: 太突然了). 忽然 is adverb-only.
Both mark an event as sudden and unforeseen.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 平 ("level, flat, even," as in 平均) and 静
("quiet, still") both independently name an undisturbed, level state.
Pairing them gives the standard word for calm — of water, of a
situation, or of a person's emotional state.

## Notes
平静 describes both a literal calm surface (湖面平静, "the lake surface
is calm") and an emotional state (心情平静, "feeling calm") — the same
level-and-still image applied to water and to mind alike.

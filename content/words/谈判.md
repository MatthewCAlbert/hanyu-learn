## Why this combination
谈 "to talk, to discuss" (as in 谈话 "to converse") combined with 判 "to
judge, to decide [an outcome]" (see also 判断): to talk through to a
decision — to negotiate.

## Notes
谈判 names formal negotiations, often between organisations or nations
(贸易谈判 "trade negotiations," 谈判桌 "the negotiating table") — 讨论, its
neighbour, is used for a general discussion and does not carry the same
sense of two sides working towards a binding agreement.

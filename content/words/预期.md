## Why this combination
预 "in advance, beforehand" and 期 "to expect, to await" (see 期's role in
期望) combine directly: expecting something ahead of time.

## Notes
预期 is the formal register for forecasted expectation: 预期效果 "expected
result," 超出预期 "exceeding expectations" — more technical/formal than
everyday 希望 or 以为.

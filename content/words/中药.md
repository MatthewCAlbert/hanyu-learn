## Why this combination
中 (short for 中国 "China") plus 药 "medicine" gives exactly "Chinese
medicine" — the standard word for herbal/traditional remedies, as opposed
to 西药 "Western medicine."

## Notes
中药 anchors 熬中药 "to brew Chinese herbal medicine," 中药店 "traditional
pharmacy" — names the medicine/remedies themselves, distinct from 中医,
which is the medical system/practice or a practitioner.

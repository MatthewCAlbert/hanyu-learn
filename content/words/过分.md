## Why this combination
过 ("to exceed, to go past") takes 分 (a "measure, degree," covered in
this app's entry for 充分) as its object: exceeding the proper degree of
something is exactly "going too far."

Rated "semi" for 分's abstract "measure/degree" sense here.

## Notes
过分 criticizes something as having crossed a reasonable line (你说得太
过分了, "you've gone too far with what you said") — always implies a
judgment that a proper limit was exceeded.

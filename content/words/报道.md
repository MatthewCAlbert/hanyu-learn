## Why this combination
报 "to announce, to inform" is transparent. 道 here is not "road" but a
literary verb sense "to say, to speak" (as in 道谢 "to express thanks").
Together: to announce by speaking — to report.

## Notes
报道 (news reporting) and 报到 (to check in, to register — a homophone
built on a different second character, 到 "to arrive") are easy to
confuse in speech; only the second character distinguishes them in
writing.

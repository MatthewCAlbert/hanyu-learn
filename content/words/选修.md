## Why this combination
选 "to choose, to select" plus 修 (here "to study, to take a course")
gives exactly "to choose to study" — the standard word for elective,
optional coursework, opposite of 必修 "required course."

## Notes
选修 anchors 选修课 "elective course," 选修学分 "elective credits" — used
across the education system for courses students choose rather than are
required to take.

## Why this combination
贸 "trade, commerce" (see 贸's own etymology — 易財也, "to exchange goods")
and 易 "to exchange" are near-synonyms combined into the standard
economic term for trade.

## Notes
贸易 anchors 国际贸易 "international trade," 自由贸易 "free trade," 贸易
战 "trade war" — the formal economic register, more technical than
everyday 买卖 "buying and selling."

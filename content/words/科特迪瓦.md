## Why this combination
A phonetic rendering of Côte d'Ivoire. Mandarin also has the calque 象牙海岸
"ivory coast"; 科特迪瓦 is the official transcription. 科, 特, 迪 and 瓦 are chosen
for sound.

## Notes
A useful pair with [[哥斯达黎加]]: one Rica transcribed, one Ivory Coast
transcribed rather than translated.

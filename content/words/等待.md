## Why this combination
等 "to wait" and 待 "to wait for, to await, to treat [someone a certain
way]" (as in 对待 "to treat") are close synonyms, paired into a fuller,
more formal word for waiting.

## Notes
等待 is more formal and written than bare 等, and often takes an
abstract or extended wait as its object (等待机会 "to wait for an
opportunity," 等待结果 "to await the results") — 等 alone is the ordinary
spoken word ("等我一下" "wait for me a moment").

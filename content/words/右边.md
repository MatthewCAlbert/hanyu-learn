## Why this combination
右 "right(-hand)" + 边 "side" — the 边 suffix that builds the whole locative set
(左边, 上边, [[里边]], [[旁边]]). 左边 is its mirror.

## Notes
In classical seating and ranking, 右 was often the senior side (无出其右);
modern 右 in politics means "the Right." The direction word is neutral.

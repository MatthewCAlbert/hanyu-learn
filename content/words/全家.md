## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" (see [[全部]], [[全国]], [[全身]]) + 家 "family, household" →
everyone in the family: 全家人, 全家福 "a family photo."

## Notes
全家 (everyone in it) vs. [[家庭]] (the family as an institution) vs. [[家人]]
(family members). The convenience-store chain FamilyMart is 全家 in Chinese.

## Why this combination
明 "bright" and 亮 "bright, shining" are near-synonyms combined into an
intensified word for brightness.

## Notes
明亮 describes both light (明亮的灯光 "bright light") and figuratively
eyes or a room (眼睛明亮 "bright, clear eyes," 房间明亮 "a well-lit room")
— a general, everyday word for pleasant brightness.

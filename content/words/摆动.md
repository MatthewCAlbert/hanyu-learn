## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 摆 ("to swing, to place [with a swaying
motion]," as in 摆脱, "to shake off [lit. swing + break free]") and 动
("to move," as in 运动) both independently name back-and-forth motion.
Pairing them gives the standard word for swinging.

## Notes

树枝摆动 ("the branches sway") and 钟摆摆动 ("the pendulum swings") both
build on this word — describes rhythmic back-and-forth motion, literal
or, in figurative use, hesitation between choices.

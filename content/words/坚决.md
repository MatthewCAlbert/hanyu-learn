## Why this combination
坚 "firm, hard, unyielding" (see also 坚持, 坚强) combined with 决 "to
decide, to settle [an outcome]" (see also 决定, 决心): firm in
decision — resolute.

## Notes
坚决 modifies actions taken without wavering (坚决反对 "to firmly
oppose," 坚决执行 "to resolutely carry out") — 坚强, its close relative,
describes inner resilience of character, while 坚决 describes the
firmness of a decision or action taken.

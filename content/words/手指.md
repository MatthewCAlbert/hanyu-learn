## Why this combination
手 "hand" combined with 指 "finger, to point" (see also 指出, 指导, all
built on the same character's "to point" sense): the pointing parts of
the hand — fingers.

## Notes
手指 names the fingers specifically (十个手指 "ten fingers") — 指, alone,
mainly survives as the verb "to point [at]" and in compounds, while
手指 is the concrete body-part noun used in everyday speech.

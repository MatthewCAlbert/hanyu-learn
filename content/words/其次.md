## Why this combination
其, a classical pronoun "that, its" combined with 次 "next, second [in
sequence]" (as in 第一次 "the first time"): the next one after that —
secondly, in second place.

## Notes
其次 is used to list items in order, often after 首先 "firstly" (首先...
其次... "firstly... secondly...") and also to mark something as
secondary in importance (这是次要的，其次才考虑 "this is secondary,
consider it only after [the main thing]").

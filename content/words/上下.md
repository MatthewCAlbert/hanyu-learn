## Why this combination
上 "up" and 下 "down" — an antonym pair, the same pattern as 大小 "size"
or 买卖 "business" — combine into a word spanning the whole range between
two poles.

## Notes
上下 covers physical direction (上下楼梯 "up and down the stairs"),
approximation (五十岁上下 "around fifty years old"), and social hierarchy
(上下级 "superiors and subordinates") — always a range between two ends.

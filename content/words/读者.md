## Why this combination
读 "to read" (as in 读书) combined with 者 "one who [performs the
action]" (see also 作者, 记者): one who reads — a reader.

## Notes
读者 names the audience of a written work (读者群 "readership," 亲爱的
读者 "dear reader," a common salutation) — the parallel words 观众
"viewer/audience" and 听众 "listener" follow the same underlying pattern
of naming an audience by the sense/action they engage in.

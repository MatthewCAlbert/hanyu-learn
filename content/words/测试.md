## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 测 ("to measure, to survey," as in 测量, 预测) and
试 ("to try, to test," as in 试试, 考试) both independently name checking
something by trial. Pairing them gives the standard word for formal
testing, whether of a machine, software, or a person's ability.

## Notes
测试 leans toward technical/formal testing (软件测试, software testing;
能力测试, ability test); everyday academic testing more often uses 考试
instead.

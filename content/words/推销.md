## Why this combination

推 ("to push," as in 推广/推迟) plus 销 ("to sell, to market," as in 销售)
names pushing goods toward sale: to market, to promote sales.

## Notes

推销员 ("a salesperson") and 推销产品 ("to promote/push a product") both
build on this word — 推销 always implies active persuasion to sell,
distinct from plain 卖 or 销售, which can be entirely passive.

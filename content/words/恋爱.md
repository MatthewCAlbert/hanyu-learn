## Why this combination
恋 "to be attached to, to long for" and 爱 "to love" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for romantic love specifically (as
opposed to 爱 alone, which covers all kinds of love).

## Notes
恋爱 anchors 谈恋爱 "to date, to be in a relationship" (literally "to
discuss/conduct a romance") and 初恋 "first love" — always romantic, never
familial or platonic the way plain 爱 can be.

## Why this combination
女 "female" modifies 儿 "child" → female child → daughter. Note it does *not*
take the 子 suffix that [[儿子]] does: here 儿 is the head noun "child," not the
"son" root, so 女 + 儿 is enough.

## Notes
儿 is full-toned *ér* here, not the erhua *-r*. Compare 儿子 (son), where 儿 is
the root and 子 the suffix.

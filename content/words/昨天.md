## Why this combination
昨 already means "yesterday / the past" on its own (昨夜 last night, 昨日); 天
"day" is added for the standard disyllable. Contrast [[明天]], where 明 needs
its "next" sense unpacked — 昨 is unambiguous.

## Notes
前天 the day before yesterday; 昨天 / 今天 / 明天 the core row (see [[今天]]).

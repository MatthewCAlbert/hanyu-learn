## Why this combination
收 "to collect, to receive, to gather in" (see [[收到]]) + 入 "in, to enter"
(see [[进入]]) → what comes in. As a noun, income (月收入, 收入不高); as a verb,
to take in / include (被收入词典 "included in the dictionary").

## Notes
Opposite 支出 "expenditure." 入 "in" also in [[出口]]'s pair 进口, 加入,
[[收入]]'s cousin 输入.

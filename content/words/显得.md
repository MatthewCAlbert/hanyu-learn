## Why this combination
显 "to make visible, to appear evidently" (see also 显示, 显然) combined
with 得, here a light resultative particle (see also 值得): to come to
visibly appear [a certain way] — to seem, to look.

## Notes
显得 is followed directly by an adjective describing how something
appears (她今天显得很累 "she looks very tired today") — 看起来, a
near-synonym, is built differently (on 看 "to look") but functions much
the same way in speech.

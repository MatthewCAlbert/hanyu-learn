## Why this combination
必 "must, certainly" modifying 需 "to need" gives exactly "must be
needed" — the standard word for something absolutely required.

## Notes
必需 anchors 生活必需品 "daily necessities," 必需的条件 "essential
conditions" — always modifies a noun naming what is required; the near-
homophone 必须 is the adverb "must" before a verb — a common
confusable pair.

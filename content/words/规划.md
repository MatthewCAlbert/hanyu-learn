## Why this combination
规 "a standard, a compass [for drawing circles]" and 划 "to delineate, to
mark out" are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for careful,
long-term planning.

## Notes
规划 favours large-scale, long-term plans (城市规划 "urban planning," 五年
规划 "five-year plan") — more formal and structural than everyday 计划
"plan," which covers smaller-scale intentions too.

## Why this combination
女 "female" + 孩儿 "child" (孩 with erhua). The erhua form 女孩儿 is the everyday
northern word; 女孩 without it is the same. Covers girls from infancy to young
adulthood, less formal than [[女生]].

## Notes
Pairs with [[男孩儿]]. 小女孩儿 "a little girl." 女孩子 is another common variant.

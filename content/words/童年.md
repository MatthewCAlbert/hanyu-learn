## Why this combination

童 ("a child," as in 儿童) plus 年 ("year(s)") names the years of being a
child: childhood.

## Notes

童年时代 ("the years of one's childhood") and 童年回忆 ("childhood
memories") both build on this word — 青春 (covered elsewhere in this app)
is its later-life counterpart, naming the years of youth instead.

## Why this combination

暑 ("summer heat," as in 中暑, "heatstroke") plus 假 ("a vacation, a
holiday," as in 放假, "to have a break [lit. release + vacation]")
names the vacation taken during summer heat: summer break.

## Notes

暑假 pairs with 寒假 ("winter break, lit. cold vacation") as the two main
school breaks — 暑/寒 mark the season's temperature extreme, not the
literal weather during the break itself.

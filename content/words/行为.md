## Why this combination
行 (*xíng*) "to act, to do" + 为 (*wéi*) "to do, to act as" — a synonym pair for
the doings of a person, viewed morally or legally: 行为不端 "improper conduct,"
违法行为 "an illegal act."

## Notes
行为 = conduct as judged; [[行动]] = a planned operation; [[动作]] = a physical
movement. Both characters are read in their verbal tones (*xíng*, *wéi*).

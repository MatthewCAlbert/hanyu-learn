## Why this combination
后 "after, later" (see also 后面, 以后) combined with 果 "fruit, outcome"
(see also 效果, 结果): the fruit that comes after — the consequence.

## Notes
后果 is used almost exclusively for negative, unwanted consequences
(严重后果 "serious consequences," 承担后果 "to bear the consequences")
— 结果, the neutral word for "result, outcome," carries no such negative
bias.

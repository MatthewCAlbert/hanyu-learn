## Why this combination
服 (here "to submit to") and 从 "to follow" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for obeying an authority or rule.

## Notes
服从 takes an abstract object — an order, arrangement, or law:
服从命令 "to obey orders," 服从安排 "to comply with an arrangement" —
distinct from 听话, which is about a person's general obedient
disposition rather than complying with a specific directive.

## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" + 票 "ticket" — one of the 票 family with [[机票]], [[门票]],
电影票. 买车票 "buy a ticket," 火车票 / 汽车票 when the vehicle needs
specifying.

## Notes
For a boarded bus with a conductor, 车票 is the fare stub; for rail, the
reserved-seat ticket.

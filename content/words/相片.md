## Why this combination

相 ("appearance, image," as in 照相, "to take a photo [lit. shine +
image]") plus 片 ("a flat piece, a card," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 照片/名片) names a captured image on a flat card: a
photograph.

## Notes

相片 and 照片 are near-perfect synonyms for "photo" — 照片 leans slightly
more common in everyday Mandarin, with 相片 favored in some regional and
older usages.

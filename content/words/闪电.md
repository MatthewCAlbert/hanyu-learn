## Why this combination

闪 ("to flash, to flicker," as in 闪烁, "to twinkle/flicker") plus 电
("electricity," as in 电脑) names the flashing electrical discharge in a
storm: lightning.

## Notes

闪电战 ("blitzkrieg, lit. lightning war") and 闪电般 ("lightning-fast, as
if lightning") both extend 闪电's speed and suddenness figuratively — the
same association English makes with "lightning-quick."

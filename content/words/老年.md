## Why this combination
老 "old, aged" + 年 "years, age" → the aged stage of life, the last of the
series 少年 → 青年 → 中年 → 老年 (see [[少年]], [[青年]], [[中年]]).
老年人 "the elderly," 老年生活.

## Notes
老年 (the life stage / demographic) vs. [[老人]] (an old person). 老龄化
"population ageing."

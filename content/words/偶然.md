## Why this combination
偶 "by chance" (see 偶's own etymology) with the adverb-forming suffix
然 (as in 忽然, 突然) combines into the standard word for happening by
chance.

## Notes
偶然 works as adjective ("an accidental event," 偶然事件 "a chance
occurrence") and adverb ("我偶然遇见他" "I ran into him by chance") — the
formal counterpart to conversational 偶尔 "occasionally," though the two
are not interchangeable: 偶然 marks chance, 偶尔 marks frequency.

## Why this combination
碰 "to bump, to knock against, to happen upon" + resultative 见 "to perceive"
(see [[看见]], [[听见]]) — an unplanned encounter came off: 在超市碰见了同事.

## Notes
碰见 / [[碰到]] = meet by chance (碰到 also covers hitting an object or a
problem); 遇见 / 遇到 are near-synonyms; [[见到]] often implies a meeting that
was arranged.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 接 "to receive, to take hold of" + 受 "to receive, to undergo."
Accepting something offered — an invitation, a condition, education, criticism.

## Notes
接受 = take it and go along with it (abstract). 收到 (see [[收到]]) = physically
get something. 受到 (see [[受到]]) = be on the receiving end of an action.

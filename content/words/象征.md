## Why this combination
象 here carries its "image, representation" sense (as in 形象, 想象) rather
than the literal "elephant," combined with 征 "a sign, evidence" — an
image that serves as evidence or a sign of something beyond itself, i.e.
a symbol.

## Notes
象征 works as a noun ("和平的象征" "a symbol of peace") and a verb ("鸽子
象征和平" "the dove symbolizes peace") — always standing for something
larger or more abstract than the object itself.

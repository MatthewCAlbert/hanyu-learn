## Why this combination
Two characters that independently mean "to hope" — 希 (to hope for, to
strive for) and 望 (to hope, to gaze into the distance) — combined for
emphasis and a more formal register than either alone.

## Notes
望's role here traces back to its root sense "to gaze at [something
distant]" — hoping for something is, etymologically, gazing toward it
from where you currently stand.

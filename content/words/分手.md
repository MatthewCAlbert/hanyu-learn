## Why this combination
分 ("to divide, to separate") takes 手 ("hand") as its object — parting
hands, the image of two people releasing a handclasp and going their
separate ways. Applied first to companions parting after traveling
together, and now most commonly to romantic breakups.

Rated "semi" because the leap from the literal "parting hands" image to
its now-dominant romantic-breakup sense is a specific narrowing a
learner needs to be told, not one they'd necessarily guess.

## Notes
分手 today overwhelmingly means "to break up" romantically (我们分手了,
"we broke up") — for parting ways with a friend or business partner
without romantic connotation, 分开 is the more neutral choice.

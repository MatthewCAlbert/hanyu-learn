## Why this combination
方 here carries its extended sense "a method, a means" (as in 方法), not
"square." Paired with 式 "a form, a pattern," the compound names the
particular shape a method or action takes.

## Notes
方式 pairs closely with 方法 "method" — 方式 leans toward the manner or
style something is done in, while 方法 leans toward the concrete steps
or technique used.

## Why this combination
赠 "to give as a gift" and 送 "to give, to send" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for giving something without payment.

## Notes
赠送 anchors 赠送礼品 "to give a gift," 免费赠送 "given away for free" —
more formal than plain 送, often used in commercial or ceremonial
contexts (promotions, honors, donations).

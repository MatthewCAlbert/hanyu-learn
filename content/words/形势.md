## Why this combination
形 ("shape, form," as in 形状, 形式) plus 势 ("momentum, a tendency, an
inclining force," as in 趋势, "a trend") names the current shape and
direction things are moving in: the overall situation.

Rated "semi" for 势's "momentum/tendency" sense, a more abstract idea
than either character's everyday meaning alone suggests.

## Notes
形势 leans toward the state of larger affairs — political, economic, or
international (国际形势, "the international situation") — for a smaller,
personal circumstance, 情况 is the more natural everyday word.

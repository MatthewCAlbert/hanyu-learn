## Why this combination
从, here in its "to follow, to engage in, to be occupied with" sense (as
in 从来 in an unrelated formation) combined with 事 "affair, matter,
work" (see also 事情, 事业): to engage oneself in an affair — to
undertake, to pursue as one's work.

## Notes
从事 is a formal word for the field or profession someone works in
(从事教育工作 "to be engaged in education work") — 做, the plain verb
"to do," is used far more generally and lacks 从事's sense of a
sustained professional pursuit.

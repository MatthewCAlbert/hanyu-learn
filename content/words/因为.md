## Why this combination
因 "cause, reason" + 为 (*wèi*) "for the sake of, on account of." A
near-synonym pair naming the causal link. Correlates with [[所以]] "therefore":
因为下雨，所以没去.

## Notes
为 is *wèi* here (fourth tone). 因 alone = "reason" in [[原因]], 因此.

## Why this combination

个 ("individual," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 个别) plus 体
("a body, an entity," as in 身体) names a single distinct entity: an
individual, as opposed to a group.

## Notes

个体户 ("a self-employed person/small business owner, lit. individual
household") and 个体与集体 ("the individual versus the collective") both
build on this word — 个体 leans philosophical/sociological, distinct
from 个人, the everyday word for "a person" as an individual.

## Why this combination
古 "ancient" and 老 "old" are near-synonyms combined into the standard
word for great age, especially of civilizations and traditions.

## Notes
古老 favours grand, historical antiquity (古老的文明 "an ancient
civilization," 古老的传说 "an age-old legend") — 老 alone can describe an
individually old object or person, while 古老 implies age on a
historical, cultural scale.

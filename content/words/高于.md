## Why this combination
高 "high" plus the comparative preposition 于 "than" gives exactly
"higher than" — a formal written comparison word, the mirror of 低于
"lower than."

## Notes
高于 is used for measurable comparisons: 收入高于平均水平 "income above
average" — a written/formal register, where speech would use 比…高.

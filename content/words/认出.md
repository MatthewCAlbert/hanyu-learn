## Why this combination
认, here "to recognise" (as in 认识 "to know, to recognise") combined
with the directional complement 出 "out, to emerge": to recognise
[something] out [from memory/a crowd] — to identify, to recognise
successfully.

## Notes
认出 marks that the act of recognising succeeded (一眼认出 "recognised
at a glance," 认不出 "unable to recognise") — the resultative 出
distinguishes successful recognition from the plain attempt named by
bare 认 or 认识.

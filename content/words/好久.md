## Why this combination
好 as the adverb "quite, rather" (see [[好多]], [[好像]]) + 久 "long (of time)"
(see [[多久]], [[不久]]) → "quite a long while." 好久不见 "long time no see,"
等了好久.

## Notes
好 + adjective = "quite …": 好大, 好远. 半天 (see [[半天]]) is the colloquial
"ages" with a note of impatience.

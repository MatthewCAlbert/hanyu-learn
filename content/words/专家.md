## Why this combination
专 "specialised, focused exclusively [on one thing]" combined with 家 in
its "school of thought, recognised practitioner" sense (as in 作家
"writer," 科学家 "scientist" — a person's whole identity organised around
one field): someone whose focus has become their recognised specialty.

## Notes
专家 names recognised expertise in a field, often the object of
consultation (请专家看看 "have an expert take a look"); it always modifies
or is followed by the field itself when specified (教育专家 "education
expert").

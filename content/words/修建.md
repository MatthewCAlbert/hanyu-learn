## Why this combination
修 (here "to build, to repair") and 建 "to construct" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for constructing infrastructure.

## Notes
修建 anchors 修建道路 "to build roads," 修建水库 "to build a reservoir" —
used for large-scale infrastructure and public works, more formal than
everyday 盖 "to build (a house)."

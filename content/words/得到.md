## Why this combination
得 "to obtain" + resultative 到 "reach" — obtaining that arrives at its object.
The 到 makes the getting definite and complete: 得到帮助 "receive help,"
得到结果 "obtain a result."

## Notes
得 is *dé* here (to get), not the toneless structural particle 得 (*de*) or
*děi* "must." Antonym 失去 "to lose."

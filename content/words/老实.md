## Why this combination

老 (here a bleached intensifying prefix, not its literal "old," as also
seen in 老实's fellow set phrases) plus 实 ("real, true, honest," as in
事实/现实) names being thoroughly true and honest in character.

Rated "semi" for 老's non-literal prefix role, which contributes emphasis
rather than the meaning "old."

## Notes

老实 ranges from a compliment (老实人, "an honest, dependable person") to
a mild criticism implying naivety or being too easily pushed around
(太老实了, "too much of a pushover") depending on tone and context.

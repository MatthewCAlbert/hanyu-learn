## Why this combination
煤 "coal" plus 气 "gas" gives exactly "gas from coal" — historically coal
gas, now the everyday word for household fuel gas generally.

## Notes
煤气 anchors 煤气灶 "gas stove," 煤气中毒 "gas poisoning (carbon
monoxide)" — used colloquially for household gas even where the fuel is
now often natural gas (天然气) rather than literal coal gas.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 描 ("to depict, to trace an outline," as in
描述, "to describe [lit. depict + narrate]") and 写 ("to write," as in
写作) both independently name representing something. Pairing them
gives the standard literary word for descriptive writing.

## Notes

描写景物 ("to describe scenery") and 人物描写 ("character description")
both build on this word — 描写 specifically names vivid depiction in
written prose, overlapping with but more literary than 描述, the
general verb "to describe."

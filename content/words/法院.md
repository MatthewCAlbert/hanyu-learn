## Why this combination
法 ("law," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 院 (an institutional
suffix, as in 医院, "hospital"; 学院, "college") names the institution
dedicated to law: a court.

Rated "semi" for 院's productive institutional-suffix role, worth
learning as a pattern rather than deriving from 院's literal "courtyard"
sense.

## Notes
法院 (the court as an institution) is distinct from 法官 (the judge as a
person, covered elsewhere in this app) — the building/institution versus
the office-holder.

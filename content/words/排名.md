## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 排 "to arrange in a row, to line up" (as in 排球
"volleyball," literally "row-ball") takes 名 "name" as its object —
literally "to arrange names [in order]" — to rank.

## Notes
排名 names both the act of ranking (排名第一 "ranked first") and, as a
noun, a ranking list itself (世界排名 "world ranking") — 名次, a close
relative, names the specific rank/position a person or thing holds.

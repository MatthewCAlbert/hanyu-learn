## Why this combination
起 (here "to arise, to produce") plus the resultative complement 到 "to
reach, to achieve" gives exactly "to produce, achieving (an effect)" — a
fixed pattern always paired with 作用 "effect/role."

## Notes
起到 almost always appears in the set phrase 起到…作用 "to play a … role":
起到重要作用 "to play an important role," 起到推动作用 "to have a driving
effect" — rarely used with any other following noun.

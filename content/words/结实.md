## Why this combination
结, here "to tie, to bind tightly" (see also 结合, 结婚) combined with
实 "solid, real, substantial" (see also 事实, 现实): tied tight and
solid — sturdy, durable.

## Notes
结实 describes objects built to last (结实的桌子 "a sturdy table") and,
of a person, a strong, robust physique (身体很结实 "physically robust")
— it is read jiēshi here, a colloquial reading distinct from 结 jié in
compounds like 结婚.

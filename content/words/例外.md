## Why this combination
例 "a precedent, an established case" and 外 "outside" combine into
"outside the established precedent/rule" — a case that falls outside the
general pattern.

## Notes
例外 works as noun ("an exception," 毫无例外 "without exception") and
verb ("to be exceptional," 他也不例外 "he's no exception either") —
always marking a departure from an otherwise consistent rule.

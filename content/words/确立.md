## Why this combination
确 (here "certain, firm") and 立 "to establish, to set up" are
near-synonyms combined into a formal word for firmly setting something in
place.

## Notes
确立 anchors 确立目标 "to establish a goal," 确立地位 "to secure a
position" — a formal, often institutional register word, close to 建立
but emphasizing that the result is now firm and settled.

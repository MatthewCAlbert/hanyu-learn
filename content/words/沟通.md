## Why this combination
沟 "a ditch, a channel" (see 沟's own etymology — a water channel) and 通
"to pass through, to connect" combine on a physical metaphor: digging a
channel to connect two separated bodies of water, extended to connecting
two people or groups through communication.

## Notes
沟通 is the standard word for meaningful two-way communication —
沟通不畅 "poor communication," 有效沟通 "effective communication" — implying
mutual understanding, not just one-way information transfer (which 告诉
or 通知 cover instead).

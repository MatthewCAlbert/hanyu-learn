## Why this combination
大 beyond "big" also means "greatly, to a large degree." Negated, 不大 + an
adjective or verb = "not very / not much": 不大清楚 "not too clear,"
不大喜欢 "don't much like it," 不大来 "doesn't come often."

## Notes
A softened negative — gentler than 不 alone. Compare 不太, which is close but
slightly more emphatic.

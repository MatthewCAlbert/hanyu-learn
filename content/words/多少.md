## Why this combination
Antonym pair 多 "many" + 少 "few." Yoking opposites to name the whole scale is a
regular pattern (大小 size, 长短 length, 快慢 speed); here it produces the
question "where on the much–little scale?" — how much.

## Notes
多少 asks about any number (多少人); 几 expects a small one and needs a measure
word (几个人). Toned *duōshǎo* = "how much"; *duōshao* toneless can mean "a
certain amount."

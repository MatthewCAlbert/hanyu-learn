## Why this combination
确 ("true, certain," covered in this app's entry for 的确) plus 认 ("to
recognize, to acknowledge," covered in this app's entry for 承认) names
acknowledging something as certainly true — exactly what confirming or
verifying means.

## Notes
确认 always takes the fact or item being verified as its object (确认
订单, "to confirm an order"; 确认身份, "to verify identity") — a formal,
often procedural act, more deliberate than casual 确定 ("to decide,
to be sure").

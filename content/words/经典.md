## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 经 (here in its "a canonical, authoritative
text" sense, as in 圣经, "the Bible," lit. "sacred canon" — a distinct
sense from the "to pass through" root behind 经济, 经验) and 典 ("a
standard reference text," as in 词典, dictionary) both independently name
an authoritative written work. Pairing them gives the word for a classic.

Rated "semi" for 经's "canonical text" sense specifically, easy to
conflate with its far more common "to pass through / economy" family.

## Notes
经典 covers both religious/philosophical canon (佛教经典, "Buddhist
scripture") and secular classics (经典电影, "a classic film") — anything
held up as an authoritative, enduring standard in its field.

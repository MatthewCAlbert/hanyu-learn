## Why this combination
防 "to guard against, to prevent" (as in 预防 "to prevent [illness]")
combined with 止 "to stop, to cease" (see also 停止): to guard against
and stop — to prevent something from happening.

## Notes
防止 takes an unwanted outcome as its object (防止事故 "to prevent
accidents," 防止感染 "to prevent infection") — it is used specifically
for averting a negative outcome, not for the general sense of
"prevention" as a field, which would be named by 预防.

## Why this combination
规 "a compass, a rule, a standard" (the tool for drawing a true circle,
extended to "a fixed standard") combined with 定 "to fix, to settle, to
determine": to fix something as a standard — to stipulate a rule, or the
rule itself once fixed.

## Notes
规定 works as both verb ("学校规定学生穿校服" "the school stipulates that
students wear uniforms") and noun ("按照规定" "according to the
regulations") without changing form — a pattern shared with 计划,
决定 and other verb/noun compounds in this dataset.

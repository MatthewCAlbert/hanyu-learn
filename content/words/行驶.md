## Why this combination
行 "to go, to move" and 驶 "to drive" (see 驶's own etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a vehicle in motion.

## Notes
行驶 always describes a vehicle rather than a person: 车辆行驶 "vehicles
travelling," 禁止行驶 "no through traffic" — distinct from 步行 "to walk,"
which describes a person on foot.

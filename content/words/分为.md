## Why this combination

分 ("to divide," as in 分开) plus 为 ("to become, to be," as in 成为)
names dividing something so that it becomes several parts: to be
divided into.

## Notes

分为两组 ("divided into two groups") is the standard frame — 分为 always
takes the resulting categories as its object, distinct from plain 分,
which can describe division without specifying the outcome.

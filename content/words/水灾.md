## Why this combination
水 "water" plus 灾 "disaster" gives exactly "a water disaster" — the
standard word for flood damage, parallel to 火灾 "fire disaster."

## Notes
水灾 anchors 遭受水灾 "to suffer flood damage," 防止水灾 "to prevent
flooding" — always the natural disaster of flooding, not everyday water
issues.

## Why this combination
请 "please" + 进 "to enter" — the 请 + verb polite-invitation frame, as in
请坐, 请问. Said at a door, or written on one. Note it drops 来 / 去: there is no
speaker viewpoint to encode on a sign.

## Notes
Fuller spoken form: 请进来. 请 here = "invite / please" as in [[请坐]], 请客.

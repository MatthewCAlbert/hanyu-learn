## Why this combination
等 "rank, class" and 级 "level, grade" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for a ranked classification.

## Notes
等级 anchors 等级制度 "hierarchical system," 信用等级 "credit rating" — a
formal, structural ranking system, whether social, technical, or
administrative.

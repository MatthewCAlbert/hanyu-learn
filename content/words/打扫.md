## Why this combination

打 ("to do, to perform [an action]," a light verb as in 打电话) plus 扫
("to sweep," as in 扫地, "to sweep the floor") names performing the act
of sweeping/cleaning.

## Notes

打扫房间 ("to clean/tidy a room") and 打扫卫生 ("to clean up, lit. sweep
hygiene") are the standard everyday frames — 打扫 covers general
cleaning, wider than the literal sweeping named by bare 扫.

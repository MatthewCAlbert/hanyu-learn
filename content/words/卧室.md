## Why this combination
卧 "to lie down" (see 卧's own etymology) and 室 "room" combine directly:
the room where one lies down to sleep.

## Notes
卧室 is the standard formal word for bedroom (三室两厅 "three bedrooms,
two living rooms" — common in real-estate listings); 睡房 is a more
regional/informal alternative in some areas.

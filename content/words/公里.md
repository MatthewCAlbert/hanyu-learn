## Why this combination
里 is the traditional Chinese distance unit (about 500 m today). 公 marks the
metric ("public / international standard") version — a "metric li" defined as
1000 m. The same 公 prefix built 公斤 (kilogram), 公分 (centimetre),
[[公斤]]'s cousin 公升 (litre).

## Notes
Still counted with 里 for rough or traditional distances (十里八乡 "the
surrounding villages"); 公里 for measured ones. 千米 is the fully explicit
synonym.

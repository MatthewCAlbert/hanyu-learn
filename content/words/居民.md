## Why this combination
居 ("to reside, to dwell," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 民
("people, citizens," as in 人民) names people who reside somewhere:
residents.

## Notes
居民 names people by where they live (社区居民, "community residents") —
distinct from 公民 ("citizen," a legal/political status) and 人口
("population," a demographic count).

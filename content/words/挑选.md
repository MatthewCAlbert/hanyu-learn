## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 挑 (here "to pick out, to select," a distinct
sense from its more literal "to carry on a shoulder pole," and read
*tiāo* in this role) and 选 ("to select," as in 选择) both independently
name choosing among options. Pairing them gives the standard word for
careful selection.

## Notes

挑选 implies deliberate comparison among options (挑选礼物, "to pick out a
gift") — closer to "handpick" than the more neutral, general 选择.

## Why this combination
四 "four" and 周 "circumference, perimeter" combine directly: the full
circle in all four directions — all around.

## Notes
四周 describes the surrounding area of a place: 四周安静 "quiet all
around," 环顾四周 "to look around on all sides" — a common spatial
descriptor in narrative writing.

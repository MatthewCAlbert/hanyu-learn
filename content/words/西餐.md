## Why this combination
西 "West, Western" (see [[西方]], [[西医]]) + 餐 "a meal, cuisine" (see
[[早餐]], [[中餐]], [[快餐]]) → European / American-style food, as against
[[中餐]] "Chinese food." 吃西餐, 西餐厅.

## Notes
西餐 comes with its own etiquette word set: 刀叉 "knife and fork" (vs.
[[筷子]]), 前菜 / 主菜 / 甜点 "starter / main / dessert."

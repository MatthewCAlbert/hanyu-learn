## Why this combination
Clipped from 高级中学 "senior-level secondary school" — 高 "high, senior" + 中,
itself short for [[中学]]. The junior half is 初中 (from 初级中学). 上高中,
高中生, 高中三年.

## Notes
A separate word 高中 (*gāozhòng*) means "to pass the imperial exam / an entrance
exam" (金榜高中) — same characters, unrelated.

## Why this combination

有 ("to have") takes 劲 ("strength, energy," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 使劲) plus the retroflex diminutive suffix 儿 as its
object: having strength.

## Notes

真有劲儿 ("really strong/powerful") and 没劲儿 ("no energy, listless," its
negated opposite) both build on this word — casual spoken Mandarin,
covering both physical strength and, loosely, something engaging or
"worth doing" (这个游戏真有劲儿, "this game is really fun/engaging").

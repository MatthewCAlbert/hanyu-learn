## Why this combination
青 "green, blue-green; young, unripe" + 年 "years, age" → the "green years" of
life, roughly late teens to mid-thirties. The green-for-young metaphor is the
same as English "green" (inexperienced) and 年轻 (see [[年轻]]).

## Notes
青年 (a demographic: 青年人, 有为青年) sits between 少年 (see [[少年]], early
teens) and 中年 (see [[中年]]). 青 "young / green" also in [[青少年]].

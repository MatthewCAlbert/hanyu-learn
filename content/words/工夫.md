## Why this combination
工 "work, labour" (see also 工具, 工资) combined with 夫, the same light
"effort spent" suffix seen in 功夫 (see its own entry): time and effort
invested — a variant spelling largely overlapping with 功夫 in meaning.

## Notes
工夫 and 功夫 are close variants: 工夫 leans towards "a span of time" or
"spare time" (没工夫 "no time [to spare]," 这几天工夫 "in the space of
these few days"), while 功夫 leans towards "skill" and specifically
martial arts, though the two overlap heavily and are sometimes used
interchangeably.

## Why this combination
根 is "root" (as in 树根), extended figuratively to "the underlying basis of
something," and 据 is "to rely on, evidence" (as in 证据, 依据). "The rooted
evidence [something] rests on" gives both the preposition "according to" and
the noun "grounds, basis."

Rated "semi" for 根's figurative jump from literal "root" to "underlying
basis" — natural once pointed out, but not obvious cold.

## Notes
Works as both preposition (根据规定 "according to the rules") and noun (有
充分的根据 "there is ample basis/grounds") without changing form.

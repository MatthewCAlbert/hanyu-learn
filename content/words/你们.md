## Why this combination
Pronoun 你 plus the plural suffix 们 — see [[我们]]. The polite singular 您 has
no standard 您们; for a group you address politely, 您 several times or 大家 does
the work.

## Notes
们 is the whole of the plural here; 你们 two people or two thousand is the same
word.

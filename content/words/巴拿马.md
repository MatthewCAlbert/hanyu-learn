## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Panamá / Panama (巴拿馬). 巴, 拿 and 马 are
chosen for sound, not for "to hope + to take + horse."

## Notes
拿 as in [[加拿大]]. 马 as a dummy phonetic.

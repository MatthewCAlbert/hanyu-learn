## Why this combination
有利 "advantageous, beneficial" (有 "to have" + 利 "benefit") plus the
preposition 于 "to, for" gives exactly "beneficial to (something)" — a
formal written construction stating what an action or condition helps.

## Notes
有利于 always takes a following object: 有利于健康 "good for one's
health," 有利于发展 "conducive to development" — a written/formal register
word, common in essays and reports.

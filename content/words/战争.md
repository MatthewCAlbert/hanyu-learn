## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 战 ("to do battle, war," as in 战斗, 战场) and 争
("to contend, to fight over," as in 竞争, 争论) both independently name
armed or contentious struggle. Pairing them produces the standard modern
noun for large-scale, sustained armed conflict.

## Notes
战争 names the large-scale, sustained conflict itself; a single engagement
within it is 战斗 (a battle) or 战役 (a campaign) — 战争 is not used for a
single skirmish.

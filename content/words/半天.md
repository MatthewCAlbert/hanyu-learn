## Why this combination
Literally "half a day" — a morning or an afternoon (上半天 / 下半天). In speech
it exaggerates into "a long time": 等了半天 "waited ages," 说了半天 "went on
and on."

## Notes
半 + time-unit is a productive frame: [[半年]], 半个月, 半小时. Only 半天 has
picked up the "ages" sense.

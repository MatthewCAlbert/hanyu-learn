## Why this combination
班 "a shift, a duty period" (also "a class"). 上 here is the verb "to go
on / to board" — the same 上 as 上车, 上课. So "to go on shift." Splits:
上了一天班 "worked all day," 不想上班 "don't want to go to work."

## Notes
Opposite [[下班]] "come off shift." 上 / 下 as get-on / get-off runs through
上车 / 下车, [[上课]] / [[下课]], [[上学]].

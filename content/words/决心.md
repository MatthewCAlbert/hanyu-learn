## Why this combination
决 "to decide, to settle [an outcome]" (see also 决定, 决赛) combined
with 心 "heart, mind" (see also 心情, 内心): a mind made up decisively —
determination, resolve.

## Notes
决心 works as noun ("下决心" "to make up one's mind, to resolve") and,
less commonly, as a stative verb ("我决心改变" "I'm determined to
change") — 决定, its close relative, is the more general verb "to
decide," while 决心 stresses the firmness of resolve behind a decision.

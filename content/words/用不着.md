## Why this combination
用 "to use" plus the negative potential complement 不着 ("cannot manage
to," from 着 "to touch, to achieve contact with") gives "cannot manage to
use" — a grammatical pattern whose logic depends on knowing the potential
complement construction, not obvious from the characters alone.

## Notes
用不着 anchors 用不着担心 "no need to worry," 这东西我用不着 "I have no
use for this thing" — the negative counterpart of 用得着 "to have a use
for."

## Why this combination
"Electric vision," on the same 电 prefix as 电话, 电影, 电脑. 视 "to look at,
vision" (as in 视力 eyesight) supplies the picture-at-a-distance idea.

## Notes
看电视 "watch TV." The box itself is 电视机; the broadcast institution 电视台.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 知 "to know" + 识 "to know, to recognise, to discern." Nominalised
into "what is known" — knowledge, as a mass noun: 学知识, 知识面 (breadth of
knowledge).

## Notes
识 toneless here (*zhīshi*). The same two characters, verbal and toned, give
[[认识]]'s partner 识 "to recognise."

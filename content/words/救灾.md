## Why this combination
救 "to rescue, to save" acting on the object 灾 "disaster" gives exactly
"to rescue from disaster" — splittable, as in 救了灾.

## Notes
救灾 anchors 抗洪救灾 "flood-fighting and disaster relief," 救灾物资
"disaster relief supplies" — always about organized relief efforts after
a calamity, distinct from ordinary 救 "to rescue" someone individually.

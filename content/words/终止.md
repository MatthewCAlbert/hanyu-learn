## Why this combination
终 "to end" and 止 "to stop" are near-synonyms combined into a formal
word for bringing something to a permanent halt.

## Notes
终止 is used for formally ending an ongoing process or agreement:
终止合同 "to terminate a contract," 终止妊娠 "to terminate a pregnancy" —
a more definitive, formal stop than 停止, which can be temporary.

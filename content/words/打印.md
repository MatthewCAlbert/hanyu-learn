## Why this combination
打 as the all-purpose light verb "to do / make" (see [[打电话]], [[打开]]) + 印
"to print, to stamp; a seal, a mark" (印刷, 盖印). Originally "to affix a seal,"
now chiefly "to print out (with a printer)": 打印文件, 打印机 "printer."

## Notes
印 "print / mark" also in 印象 "impression," 脚印 "footprint." Verb-object:
打了一份, 打印出来.

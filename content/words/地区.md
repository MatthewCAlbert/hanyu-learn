## Why this combination
地 "land, earth, place" combined with 区 "district, division, zone":
land marked off as a division — a region, without necessarily implying a
formal administrative boundary.

## Notes
地区 covers both informal regions (南方地区 "the southern region") and,
after a city name, the formal prefecture-level division (地区 as an
administrative tier); 地方, by contrast, is the general word for "place,"
with no sense of a bounded area.

## Why this combination
买 "to buy" and 卖 "to sell" — an antonym pair, the same pattern as 大小
"size" (big+small) or 长短 "length" (long+short) — combine into the
general noun for commercial trade.

## Notes
买卖 is the everyday, colloquial word for business/trade (做买卖 "to do
business," 买卖人 "a merchant, tradesperson") — more casual than formal
商业 "commerce" or 生意 "business."

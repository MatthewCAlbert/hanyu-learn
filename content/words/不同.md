## Why this combination
不 "not" + 同 "same, alike." Plain negation of sameness. Adjective (不同的意见)
and noun (两者的不同 "the difference between the two").

## Notes
同 "same" also in [[同时]], [[同事]], [[同学]]. 不同 pairs with 相同 (see
[[相同]]).

## Why this combination
门 (here "a department, a clinic," a specialized-field sense as in 冷门
"an unpopular field") plus 诊 "diagnosis, medical consultation" gives
exactly "a diagnostic department" — the standard word for outpatient
care.

## Notes
门诊 anchors 门诊部 "outpatient department," 挂门诊号 "to register for an
outpatient visit" — care given without admission to the hospital, opposite
of 住院 "to be hospitalized (as an inpatient)."

## Why this combination
拍 ("to clap, to pat, and by extension to press/shoot [a shutter]," as
in 拍手, "to clap hands") takes 照 ("to shine upon, to photograph," as in
照片, "a photo") as its object: pressing the shutter to capture a
photograph.

Rated "semi" for 拍's extended "to press/trigger" sense here, distinct
from its more literal "to clap/pat" meaning.

## Notes
拍照 is the general verb "to take pictures"; 照相 is a near-synonym built
the opposite way around (照 first, 相 "appearance/image" as its object) —
both are common and largely interchangeable.

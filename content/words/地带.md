## Why this combination
地 "land, ground" and 带 (here "a belt, a zone," from its base sense "a
strip") combine into exactly "a belt of land" — a geographic zone word.

## Notes
地带 names an area defined by some shared characteristic: 危险地带
"danger zone," 沿海地带 "coastal belt" — always a stretch of land with a
particular quality, not a point location.

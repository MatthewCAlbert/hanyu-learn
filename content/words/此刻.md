## Why this combination
此 "this" is transparent; 刻 originally named a mark carved onto an
ancient water-clock to measure a quarter-hour (see its role in the older
measurement system), extended to "a moment in time" generally.

## Notes
此刻 is formal/literary, favoured in writing and speeches (此刻我很激动
"at this moment I'm very moved") over everyday 现在, which serves the
same purpose in casual conversation.

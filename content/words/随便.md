## Why this combination
随 "to follow, to go along with" + 便 "convenience, whatever suits" (see
[[方便]]) → doing whatever is handy, without particular care. "你随便" = "up to
you / help yourself"; 随便说说 "just talking"; 太随便了 "too casual / sloppy."

## Notes
随便 as "whatever" can sound dismissive if said flatly. 便 is *biàn* here. 随
"follow" also in [[随时]], [[跟随]] (not this level).

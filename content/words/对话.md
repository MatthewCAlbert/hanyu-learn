## Why this combination
对 "facing, opposite, toward" (面对面) + 话 "speech, words" → speech exchanged
face to face. A conversation (课文里的对话), and, figuratively, negotiation
between sides (南北对话, 对话代替对抗 "dialogue not confrontation").

## Notes
对话 = a two-way exchange; 讲话 / [[讲话]] = a one-way address; 谈话 = a talk /
chat (often serious). 对 "face" also in [[对面]], [[对不起]].

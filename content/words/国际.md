## Why this combination
国 "country" + 际 "the border, the space between, the juncture" (边际, 之际) →
"between countries," i.e. international. 国际关系, 国际机场, 国际化.

## Notes
际 "between / edge" also in 实际 (see [[实际]] — "on the actual edge of
things"), 人际 "interpersonal."

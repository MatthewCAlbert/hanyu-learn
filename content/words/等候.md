## Why this combination
等 "to wait" and 候 "to wait, to await" are near-synonyms combined into a
more formal word for waiting.

## Notes
等候 is a written/formal register word for waiting, often for a person or
event: 等候通知 "to wait for notification," 请在此等候 "please wait here"
— more formal than the everyday 等.

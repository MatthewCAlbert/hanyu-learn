## Why this combination
纪 "to record, a chronicle, a thread kept in order" (see also 世纪)
combined with 念 "to think of, to miss, to hold in mind" (as in 想念 "to
miss [someone]"): to record and keep in mind — to commemorate.

## Notes
纪念 works as verb ("纪念这个日子" "to commemorate this day") and noun for
a keepsake object ("留个纪念" "to keep as a memento") — 纪念日
"anniversary" and 纪念品 "souvenir" are both built directly on this word.

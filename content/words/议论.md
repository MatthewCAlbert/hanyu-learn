## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 议 ("to discuss, to deliberate," as in 建议,
"a suggestion [lit. build + discuss]") and 论 ("to discuss," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 讨论/争论/辩论) both independently name
talking something over. Pairing them gives the word for open discussion,
which often tips into gossip or criticism behind someone's back.

## Notes

引起议论 ("to spark discussion/gossip") and 背后议论 ("to talk behind
someone's back") both build on this word — 议论 carries a slight
negative shading toward idle chatter or criticism that plain 讨论 lacks.

## Why this combination
热 "hot, popular" plus 门 (here "a category, a field," extended from its
base sense "gate/door" — a specialty one enters through) gives "a
popular field/category" — the extension of "gate" to "field of interest"
is a step beyond the literal, paralleled in 冷门 "an unpopular/dark-horse
choice."

## Notes
热门 modifies topics, products, and majors: 热门话题 "hot topic," 热门专业
"popular major" — opposite of 冷门 "unpopular, little-known."

## Why this combination
鼻 "nose" (see 鼻's own etymology — a case of a pictograph, 自, rescued
from pronoun drift by adding 畀) takes the everyday noun suffix 子 to form
the standalone word for the body part.

## Notes
鼻子 is the everyday noun; 鼻 alone survives mainly in compounds (鼻涕
"nasal mucus," 鼻炎 "rhinitis," 鼻祖 "founding ancestor" — see 鼻's own
notes) rather than standing by itself.

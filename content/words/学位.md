## Why this combination
学 "study, learning" and 位 "rank, position" (see 位's own etymology)
combine into "an academic rank" — the standard word for a conferred
degree.

## Notes
学位 anchors 学位证书 "degree certificate," 硕士学位 "master's degree,"
博士学位 "doctoral degree" — always the formal credential, distinct from
学历, which is one's educational history/qualification level in general.

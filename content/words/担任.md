## Why this combination

担 ("to carry on a shoulder pole, to shoulder [a burden]," as in 担心)
plus 任 ("a post, a duty," as in 任务, "task") names shouldering a formal
post or role: to serve as, to hold (a position).

Rated "semi" for 担's figurative "to take on (a duty)" sense here.

## Notes

担任 always takes a role or title as its object (担任经理, "to serve as
manager"), unlike the more general 当 ("to act as, to become"), which can
stand more loosely.

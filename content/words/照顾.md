## Why this combination
照 "to look after, to attend to" (照料) + 顾 "to turn and look, to attend to,
to have regard for" (照顾, 顾虑). A synonym pair: tending to someone's needs
(照顾孩子, 照顾病人) and making allowances for them (照顾一下新来的).

## Notes
顾 "turn to look / attend to" also in [[顾客]] (one whom the shop attends to),
回顾 "look back."

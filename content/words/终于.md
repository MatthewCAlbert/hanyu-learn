## Why this combination
终 "the end" carries the core meaning; 于 is a classical preposition
("at, in") that here functions mainly to turn 终 into an adverb marking
that a long-awaited end point has been reached — "at [long last]."

## Notes
终于 signals that something happened after a wait or effort, often with
relief or satisfaction — 他终于到了 "he finally arrived [after being
late]" — distinct from 结束, which just names an ending without that
sense of anticipation.

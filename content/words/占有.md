## Why this combination
占 "to occupy, to take" and 有 "to have" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for holding something in one's possession.

## Notes
占有 covers physical/legal possession (占有土地 "to hold land") and
statistical share (占有率 "market share," 占有很大比例 "accounts for a
large proportion") — distinct from 占领, which implies taking by force.

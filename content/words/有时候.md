## Why this combination
有 "there are" + 时候 "moments, occasions" (see [[时候]]) → "there are times
when…". The fuller colloquial form of [[有时]]; often doubled: 有时候…有时候….

## Notes
Sits at the start of a clause or after the subject: 我有时候会忘 "I sometimes
forget."

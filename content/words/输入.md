## Why this combination
输 "to transport, to convey" (see also 运输) combined with 入 "to enter"
(see also 深入, 进入): to convey in — to import, to input.

## Notes
输入 covers importing goods (输入商品 "to import goods") and, in
computing, entering data (输入密码 "to input a password") — its opposite
is 输出 "to export, to output," built the same way with 出 "out" in
place of 入.

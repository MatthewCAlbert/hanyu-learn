## Why this combination

数 ("a number," as in 数字) plus 码 ("a code, a numeral marker," as in
号码, "a number/code") names representation by discrete numeric symbols
— extended to the modern computing sense "digital."

Rated "semi" for the extension from "numeral marker" to the specific
modern "digital (vs. analog)" sense.

## Notes

数码相机 ("digital camera") and 数码产品 ("digital products/electronics")
are the standard modern compounds — a coinage that repurposed an older
"numeral" word for the digital era.

## Why this combination
比 "to compare" plus 方 (here "a way, a method") gives exactly "a way of
comparing" — the standard word for explaining something by analogy.

## Notes
比方 works as noun ("打个比方" "to give an analogy") and, as 比方说, an
introductory phrase meaning "for example" — a spoken/colloquial register,
close to 例如 but more conversational.

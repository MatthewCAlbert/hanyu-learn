## Why this combination
物 "goods, things" plus 价 "price" gives exactly "the price of goods" —
the standard economic term for the general price level.

## Notes
物价 anchors 物价上涨 "commodity prices are rising," 物价指数 "price
index" — always the aggregate/general level of prices across goods, not
one item's specific price (that would be 价格).

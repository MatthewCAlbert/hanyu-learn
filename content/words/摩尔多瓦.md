## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Moldova. 摩, 尔, 多 and 瓦 are chosen for
sound. 瓦 is not "tile."

## Notes
尔 as a closer; 瓦 also in [[委内瑞拉]], [[萨尔瓦多]].

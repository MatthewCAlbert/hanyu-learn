## Why this combination
为 "to be" plus 期 "period, date" gives exactly "to be (set as) the
period" — a formal word stating a fixed duration or deadline.

## Notes
为期 always precedes a duration or deadline: 为期三天的会议 "a
three-day-long conference," 为期不远 "not far off (in time)" — a
written/formal register word common in schedules and announcements.

## Why this combination
当 "at, current with" and 场 "scene, place" (see 场's role in 场面) combine
directly: right at the scene, without delay.

## Notes
当场 emphasizes immediacy at a specific location: 当场被抓 "caught red-
handed on the spot," 当场道歉 "to apologize right there and then" — always
implies no time or distance separates the event from the response.

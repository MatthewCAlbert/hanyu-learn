## Why this combination

培训 ("training," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 班 ("a class,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 加班) names an organized class
for training: a training course.

## Notes

参加培训班 ("to attend a training course") and 英语培训班 ("an English
training class") both build on this word — names the organized class
itself, distinct from 培训, the general activity of training.

## Why this combination
繁 "numerous, flourishing" and 荣 "flourishing, glorious" (see 荣's own
etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for economic
or cultural prosperity.

## Notes
繁荣 anchors 经济繁荣 "economic prosperity," 文化繁荣 "cultural
flourishing," and, as a verb, "繁荣经济" "to boost the economy" — always
about visible, thriving growth.

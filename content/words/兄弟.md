## Why this combination
兄 ("older brother") and 弟 ("younger brother") name the two halves of a
sibling relationship together, the same way 夫妻 (husband+wife) or 师生
(teacher+student) name a pair by naming both roles. As a set, 兄弟 covers
"brothers" collectively; read with a neutral tone on 弟 (xiōngdi), it
narrows colloquially to "younger brother" or an affectionate "buddy."

## Notes
兄弟姐妹 ("siblings," lit. "older-brother younger-brother older-sister
younger-sister") extends the same pattern to cover all four sibling roles
at once.

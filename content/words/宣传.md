## Why this combination
宣 "to proclaim widely, to make known" (see also 宣布) combined with 传
"to pass on, to transmit" (as in 传统 "tradition," literally "handed-down
lineage"): to proclaim and pass on widely — to publicise, and, in a
political register, propaganda.

## Notes
宣传 names ordinary publicity and advertising (宣传新产品 "to publicise a
new product") as well as, in its more loaded political sense,
government propaganda (宣传部 "the Propaganda Department") — context
determines which shade applies.

## Why this combination
肌 "flesh, muscle tissue" (see 肌's own etymology — 肉也, Shuowen's plain
gloss "flesh") and 肉 "flesh, meat" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard anatomical word for muscle.

## Notes
肌肉 anchors 肌肉发达 "muscular, well-built," 锻炼肌肉 "to build muscle" —
the everyday clinical/fitness term, more precise than plain 肉, which
also covers meat as food.

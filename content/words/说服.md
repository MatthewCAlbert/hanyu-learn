## Why this combination

说 ("to speak") plus 服 ("to submit, to yield, to be won over," as in
佩服, "to admire/be won over by") names speaking until the other party
yields: to persuade.

Rated "semi" for 服's extended "to be won over" sense here, one step
from its more literal "to submit/wear [clothing]" meanings.

## Notes

说服 always names successful persuasion (说服他放弃, "persuaded him to
give up") — an unsuccessful attempt would be 劝, which describes the
act of trying without implying the outcome.

## Why this combination
网 "net / internet" (see [[网上]]) + 友 "friend." Someone you know through the
internet, or, loosely, internet users as a body: 网友们纷纷评论 "netizens
commented in droves."

## Notes
友 as a bound "-mate" morpheme: 校友 (alumnus), 书友, 病友, 网友. Distinct from
the free word [[朋友]].

## Why this combination
生 "to live, life" (see also 生产, 生命) combined with 存 "to exist, to
survive, to preserve" (as in 存在 "to exist"): to live and continue to
exist — to survive.

## Notes
生存 stresses the bare struggle to continue existing, especially under
pressure or threat (生存环境 "living conditions [for survival]," 生存
竞争 "the struggle for survival") — 生活, its close relative, is the
broader, more everyday word for "life, to live" without this survival
framing.

## Why this combination
行 (*xíng*) "to go, to act" + 动 "to move." A near-synonym pair: putting oneself
into motion. As a noun, a coordinated operation (军事行动, 采取行动); as a verb,
to move about (行动不便 "have trouble getting around").

## Notes
行动 = deliberate action / operation; [[活动]] = an activity or event; [[动作]]
= a bodily movement.

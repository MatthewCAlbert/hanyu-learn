## Why this combination

上 ("to go up, to ascend") takes 楼 ("a multi-story building, a floor,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 大楼/楼梯) as its object: going
up to a higher floor.

## Notes

上楼梯 ("to go up the stairs," built from 楼梯, covered elsewhere in this
app) and 上楼 both use 上 with a 楼-rooted object, differing in whether
the stairs themselves (梯) or the destination floor (bare 楼) is named.

## Why this combination
晚 "evening" + 餐 "a meal" (see [[早餐]], [[午餐]]). The 餐 series is the
slightly more formal register beside 早饭 / 午饭 / 晚饭 (see [[晚饭]]).

## Notes
吃晚餐. 餐 as "-meal / dining" also in [[快餐]], [[西餐]], 餐厅, 餐馆.

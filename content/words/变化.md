## Why this combination
变 "to change, to become different" and 化 "to transform, to turn into"
(as in 文化 "culture," originally "to transform through refinement") are
close synonyms, paired into the general noun and verb for change.

## Notes
变化 is intransitive and works equally as verb ("天气变化很快" "the
weather changes quickly") and noun ("发生了很大变化" "a big change has
occurred") — 改变, a near-synonym, more often takes a direct object
("改变计划" "to change the plan").

## Why this combination
比 "to compare" (see also 比较, 相比) combined with 例 "a category, an
instance, a precedent" (as in 例子 "example"): a compared instance —
proportion, ratio.

## Notes
比例 names the mathematical or comparative relationship between
quantities (比例失调 "out of proportion," 按比例 "in proportion,
proportionally") — 例子, its close relative, names a specific example
rather than a numeric ratio.

## Why this combination
回 (here "to turn away") and 避 "to avoid" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for sidestepping something.

## Notes
回避 covers avoiding a person, topic, or duty: 回避问题 "to dodge a
question," and formally, a judge or official recusing themselves
(回避制度 "recusal system") — a more deliberate, often evasive avoidance
than neutral 避免 "to prevent/avoid (an outcome)."

## Why this combination
节 "a joint, a node, a marked point in the year — a festival" (春节, 中秋节) +
日 "day" → a day that is a marked point in the calendar: 传统节日, 节日快乐.

## Notes
节 "node / festival" also in [[节目]] (segmented items), [[季节]] (not this
level), 情人节. 节日 = a festival day; 假日 / 假期 = time off (see [[假期]]).

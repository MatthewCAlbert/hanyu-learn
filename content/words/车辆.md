## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" + 辆, the measure word for vehicles (一辆车). Pairing a noun with
its own classifier makes a collective mass noun — "vehicles as a category" —
the same trick as 船只 (ships), 枪支 (firearms), 花朵 (flowers).

## Notes
车辆 is used for traffic and fleets (过往车辆 "passing traffic,"
机动车辆 "motor vehicles"), never for "a vehicle" — for that, 一辆车.

## Why this combination
艺 "skill, craft, an accomplishment" (as in 手艺 "handicraft skill")
combined with 术 "method, technique" (as in 技术 "technology, technique"):
a skill practised as a technique — art, in the sense of a crafted,
practised creative pursuit.

## Notes
艺术 covers the fine arts broadly — painting, music, theatre, film
(艺术家 "artist") — and, as a modifier, "artistic" (很艺术 "very
artistic"); 技术, built on the same 术, is reserved for technical or
scientific skill rather than creative expression.

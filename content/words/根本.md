## Why this combination
根 "root" and 本 "root, stem, origin" are close synonyms — both name
the base a plant grows from — paired for emphasis into "the very
foundation of something."

## Notes
As an adverb, 根本 takes on an emphatic negative sense — 根本不可能
"simply/absolutely not possible" — the foundational sense extending into
"not even at the most basic level."

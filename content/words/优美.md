## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 优 ("excellent, superior," as in 优秀,
"outstanding") and 美 ("beautiful," as in 美丽) both independently name
high aesthetic quality. Pairing them gives the word for graceful
beauty.

## Notes

优美的环境 ("a beautiful/pleasant environment") and 优美的舞姿 ("graceful
dance movements") both build on this word — 优美 leans toward refined,
harmonious beauty (scenery, motion, style), distinct from 漂亮, the more
everyday word for "pretty."

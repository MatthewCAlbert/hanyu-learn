## Why this combination
预 "in advance, beforehand" (see also 预防, 预计) combined with 报 "to
report, to announce" (as in 报道, 报告): to announce in advance — a
forecast.

## Notes
预报 is best known in 天气预报 "weather forecast" but applies to any
advance announcement of a coming event (地震预报 "earthquake forecast")
— 预计, its close relative, is the more general verb "to estimate,
predict," while 预报 specifically names a formal, announced forecast.

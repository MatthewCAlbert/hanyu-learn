## Why this combination
报 "to report, to submit, to declare" (see [[报纸]], [[通知]]'s cousin 报告) +
名 "name" as its object — "to hand in one's name," to put oneself down for
something: 报名参加比赛. Verb-object, so it splits: 报了名, 报个名.

## Notes
报名 = put your name down; [[名单]] = the resulting list; 名字 → 报名, but 姓名
on the form. 报 "submit" also in 报警 "call the police," 申报 "declare."

## Why this combination
首, here "head, foremost, first" (as in 首先) combined with 都 "a
capital, a great city" (as in 成都 Chengdu, "the perfected capital"):
the foremost capital city — the nation's capital.

## Notes
首都 names a country's official capital city (北京是中国的首都 "Beijing
is China's capital") — 首, alone, is productive across many "foremost"
compounds (首先 "firstly," 首次 "the first time").

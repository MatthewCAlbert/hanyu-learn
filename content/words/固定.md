## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 固 ("firm, solid," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 定 ("settled, fixed," as in 决定, 一定) both independently name
stability. Pairing them gives the standard word for fixing something
firmly in place.

## Notes
固定 covers both physically securing an object (把架子固定住, "to fix the
shelf in place") and an unchanging schedule or arrangement (固定时间,
"a fixed time") — the same "held firmly in place" idea applied literally
and figuratively.

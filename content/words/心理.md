## Why this combination
心 ("heart," standing for the mind, as in most mental-activity compounds)
plus 理 ("underlying pattern, reason, principle," as in 道理, 理论) names
the underlying patterns of the mind — psychology as a field, or an
individual's mental state in context.

Rated "semi" for 理's abstract "underlying pattern" sense, which is less
immediately obvious than its "reason" sense in everyday words like 有道理.

## Notes
Do not confuse with 心情 (mood, momentary emotional state) — 心理 is about
the structure or mechanics of the mind/a mental state generally, while 心情
is specifically how someone feels right now.

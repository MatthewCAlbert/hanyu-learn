## Why this combination
现代 "modern, modern era" (see its own entry) combined with 化 "to
transform into, -ize" (see also 文化, 变化), a productive suffix turning
a noun/adjective into the process of becoming it: to transform into the
modern — modernisation.

## Notes
现代化 names the process (and result) of becoming modern (国家现代化
"national modernisation," 现代化设备 "modernised equipment") — 化 is
highly productive as this "-ize/-ization" suffix (简化 "to simplify,"
美化 "to beautify"), directly parallel to English -ize/-ification.

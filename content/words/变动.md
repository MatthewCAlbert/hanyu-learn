## Why this combination
变 "to change" and 动 "to move" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for a shift or fluctuation.

## Notes
变动 is used for changes in figures, positions, or plans: 人事变动
"personnel changes," 价格变动 "price fluctuation" — close to 变化, though
变动 leans toward discrete, often institutional shifts (a role, a number)
rather than change in general.

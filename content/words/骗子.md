## Why this combination
骗 "to deceive, to swindle" (see 骗's own etymology) takes the agentive
noun suffix 子 to name the person who does the deceiving.

## Notes
骗子 is the everyday, blunt word for a con artist or fraudster (网络骗子
"online scammer") — carries real contempt, stronger than a milder
description of dishonesty.

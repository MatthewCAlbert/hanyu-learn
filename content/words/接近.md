## Why this combination
接 "to connect, to receive, to come into contact with" (as in 接受 "to
accept") combined with 近 "close, near": to come into contact by drawing
near — to approach.

## Notes
接近 covers both physical approach (接近终点 "approaching the finish
line") and figurative closeness (接近事实 "close to the truth," 接近成功
"nearing success") — 近, the plain adjective "near," does not function
as a verb of motion the way 接近 does.

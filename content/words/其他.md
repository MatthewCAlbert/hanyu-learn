## Why this combination
其 "that, its" (a classical pronoun) + 他 "other" (its older sense, not "he").
"The other (ones) among them" — 其他人 "other people," 其他问题 "other
questions."

## Notes
其他 for things and people; 其余 stresses "the remainder." 其 also in
[[其中]] "among them," 其实 "in fact."

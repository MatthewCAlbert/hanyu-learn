## Why this combination
损 "to harm, to diminish" (see 损's own etymology) and 害 "harm" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard formal word for causing damage.

## Notes
损害 covers physical damage (损害健康 "to harm one's health") and abstract
harm (损害名誉 "to damage one's reputation," 损害利益 "to harm [someone's]
interests") — a general-purpose word for causing detriment.

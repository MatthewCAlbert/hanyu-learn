## Why this combination
空 kòng, here "free, unoccupied [time or space]" (a distinct reading
from kōng "empty" in 空气 "air") combined with the colloquial 儿-suffix
common in Beijing-flavored speech (as in 玩儿, 一会儿): a stretch of free
time.

## Notes
空儿 names an unoccupied moment (有空儿吗？"do you have some free time?",
没空儿 "no time to spare") — 空 alone in this same kòng reading also
appears in 空闲 "free, idle," a more formal near-synonym.

## Why this combination
地 ("ground, earth") plus 下 ("below, under") names exactly what it
says: below the ground.

## Notes
地下 covers both the literal (地下室, "basement," lit. "underground room")
and the figurative "clandestine, underground" (地下活动, "underground/
covert activity") — the same "hidden beneath the surface" image applied
to secrecy.

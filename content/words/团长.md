## Why this combination
团 "group, regiment" plus 长 "chief, head" gives exactly "the head of a
group" — the same 团+长 pattern names whatever kind of 团 is meant, from
a military regiment to a modern group-buying chat group.

## Notes
团长 anchors 代表团团长 "head of a delegation," and, in recent group-
buying culture, 团长 "the organizer who runs a neighborhood group-buy" —
the newest sense (团购群的团长) has become extremely common since the
2020s.

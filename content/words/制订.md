## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 制 ("to make," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 制作/研制) and 订 ("to draw up, to decide upon," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 预订) both independently name creating
something through deliberate planning. Pairing them gives the formal
word for drafting a plan.

## Notes

制订计划 ("to draw up a plan") and 制订方案 ("to formulate a proposal")
both build on this word — 制订 (drafting a plan from scratch) is often
confused with the near-homophone 制定 (formally establishing a law or
policy), though the two overlap heavily in modern usage.

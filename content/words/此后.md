## Why this combination
此 "this" plus 后 "after" gives exactly "after this (point)" — a formal
time connective marking everything following a reference point.

## Notes
此后 is a written/formal register word: 此后再未相见 "never met again
after that" — close to 从此, though 此后 leans slightly more toward
narrating a sequence of events than 从此's sense of "from that point
onward, permanently."

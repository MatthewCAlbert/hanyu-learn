## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 排 ("to arrange in a row," as in 排球,
"volleyball [lit. row + ball]") and 列 ("a row, to arrange in order,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 列车/列入) both independently
name lining things up in order. Pairing them gives the standard word
for arranging items sequentially.

## Notes

按顺序排列 ("to arrange in order") and 排列组合 ("permutations and
combinations," a math term) both build on this word — 排列 always
implies a specific sequence or order, distinct from plain 放, "to
place," which doesn't imply any order.

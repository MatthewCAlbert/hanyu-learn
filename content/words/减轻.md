## Why this combination
减 "to reduce" and 轻 "light [in weight]" combine directly: reducing
something until it becomes light — easing a burden.

## Notes
减轻 covers physical weight (减轻负重 "to lighten a load") and figurative
burdens (减轻压力 "to relieve stress," 减轻痛苦 "to ease pain") — always
reducing an unwanted weight, literal or metaphorical.

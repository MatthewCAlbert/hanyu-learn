## Why this combination

光 (here "honor, glory," an honorific extended sense beyond its literal
"light," as in 光明) plus 临 ("to arrive, to be present," as in 面临, "to
face [a situation]") names someone's honored arrival: a polite way of
saying "you came."

Rated "semi" for 光's honorific "glory/honor" sense here, distinct from
its everyday literal "light" meaning.

## Notes

欢迎光临 ("welcome, lit. we welcome your gracious presence") is the
standard greeting heard at shops and restaurants — always addressed to
a guest, never used of oneself, since it's an honorific.

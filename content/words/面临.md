## Why this combination
面 ("face, to face") plus 临 ("to arrive at, to be confronted with,"
covered elsewhere in this app) names facing something as it arrives —
standing directly before a challenge or situation as it presents itself.

## Notes
面临 always takes a difficult or significant situation as its object
(面临挑战, "to face a challenge"; 面临危机, "to face a crisis") — it is not
used for facing an ordinary, neutral object.

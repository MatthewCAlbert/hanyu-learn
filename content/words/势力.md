## Why this combination
势 "momentum, power, tendency" and 力 "strength" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for organized power or influence.

## Notes
势力 anchors 政治势力 "political forces," 敌对势力 "hostile forces," 势力
范围 "sphere of influence" — always about organized, wielded power rather
than raw physical strength.

## Why this combination
政 is "governance,政治affairs of state," and 府 is "a mansion, an official
residence/seat of authority" (also seen in 首府 "capital," 王府 "a prince's
residence"). "The hall where governance is conducted" is a natural,
metonymic name for the institution itself — the building standing in for
the authority housed in it.

The "semi" rating is for 府: a learner meets it far less often than 政 and
has to learn its "official seat" sense specifically, rather than any of its
other, rarer uses.

## Notes
政府 refers to the executive branch/administration specifically, distinct
from 国家 (the state/country as a whole) and 政党 (a political party) — a
government changes; the country and the ruling party's existence do not
necessarily.

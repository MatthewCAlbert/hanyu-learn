## Why this combination

前 ("front, ahead," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 前途)
plus 头 (a bare directional suffix, covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 后头) names the location ahead: in front.

Rated "semi" for 头's suffix role here, distinct from its literal
"head" meaning.

## Notes

前头 pairs directly with 后头 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the
two directions built on the same 头 suffix — both colloquial cousins of
the more neutral 前面/后面.

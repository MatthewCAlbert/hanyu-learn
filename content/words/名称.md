## Why this combination
名 "name" + 称 "to call, an appellation" (称呼, 简称) → what a thing is called.
Used for objects, products, companies, places — not people: 公司名称,
商品名称.

## Notes
名称 = the name of a thing / body; [[名字]] = a person's name; [[姓名]] = a
person's full name. 称 "call / designation" also in 名称, 称号, 俗称.

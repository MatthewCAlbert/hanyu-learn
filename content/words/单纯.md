## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 单 ("single, alone," as in 单位) and 纯 ("pure,
unmixed," as in 纯粹") both independently name a lack of mixture or
complication. Pairing them gives the standard word for simplicity, and
by extension a person's guileless simplicity of character.

## Notes

单纯 describes both things (单纯的问题, "a simple problem") and people
(她很单纯, "she's naive/innocent") — as an adverb it also means "merely,
solely" (不单纯是钱的问题, "it's not merely a matter of money").

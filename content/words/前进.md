## Why this combination
前 "forward, ahead" (see also 前面, 前往) combined with 进 "to advance,
to move forward" (see also 进步, 进一步): to advance forward — to move
onward.

## Notes
前进 describes literal forward motion (向前进 "advance forward!" as a
command) and figurative progress towards a goal (为理想前进 "to advance
towards one's ideal") — it is used both concretely and as a motivational
call to keep moving ahead.

## Why this combination

名 ("name, famous," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 名人/
名片) plus 牌 ("a plate, a brand," as in 牌子, "a brand/label") plus the
retroflex diminutive suffix 儿 names a widely known brand: a name
brand.

## Notes

名牌儿产品 ("brand-name products") and 名牌儿大学 ("a prestigious
university, lit. name-brand university") both build on this word —
名牌 (without 儿) is equally common in more formal or written registers.

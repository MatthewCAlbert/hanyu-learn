## Why this combination
直 "straight, directly, without deviation" combined with 到 "to arrive
at, up to": [continuing] straight through, without break, up until a
given point — "until."

## Notes
直到 marks the point up to which something continues or is delayed (直到
现在 "up until now," 直到...才... "not until... did..."); unlike bare 到,
it stresses the unbroken continuation leading up to that point.

## Why this combination
世 "an age, a generation, the world" (as in 世界 "world") combined with
纪 "a thread [of a skein], a chronicle, a record kept in order" (as in
纪录 "record"): a chronicled span of the age — adopted in the modern era
as the word for a hundred-year century, likely modeled on the Western
calendrical concept.

## Notes
世纪 counts centuries the way 年代 counts decades (二十一世纪 "the 21st
century," 世纪末 "the end of the century") and is used almost
exclusively for this calendrical sense rather than any looser meaning of
"age."

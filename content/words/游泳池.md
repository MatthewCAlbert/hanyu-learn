## Why this combination
游泳 "to swim" (see its own entry) plus 池 "a pool, a pond" gives exactly
"a pool for swimming" — a fully compositional word.

## Notes
游泳池 anchors 室内游泳池 "indoor swimming pool," 游泳池边 "poolside" — the
standard word for a purpose-built swimming facility.

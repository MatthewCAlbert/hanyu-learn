## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" (see also 成功, 成熟) combined with 立 "to
stand, to be established" (see also 建立): to become established, to
stand — to found an organisation, or, of an argument, to stand up as
valid.

## Notes
成立 names the founding of an organisation (公司成立于2010年 "the company
was founded in 2010") and, in a separate but related sense, whether an
argument or claim "holds up, is tenable" (这个理由不成立 "this reason
doesn't hold up") — both senses share the core image of something
standing firmly established.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 诚 ("sincere," covered elsewhere in this app
as part of 诚实) and 信 ("trust, belief," as in 相信) both independently
name reliable sincerity. Pairing them gives the formal word for
integrity.

## Notes

诚信经营 ("to operate with integrity, e.g. a business") and 诚信问题
("a trust/integrity issue") both build on this word — leans formal and
institutional (business, social contracts), distinct from 诚实, which
describes an individual's honest character.

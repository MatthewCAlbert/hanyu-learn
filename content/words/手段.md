## Why this combination
手 here is not the body part but the metaphorical "hand" seen in words
like 高手 "expert" — a way of doing/handling something. 段 "a section,
stage" narrows that to one particular technique. Together: a method for
getting something done.

## Notes
手段 is neutral in 手段和方法 "methods and means" but often tips negative
in isolation: 不择手段 "by any means necessary [unscrupulously]," 耍手段
"to resort to tricks."

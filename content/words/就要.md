## Why this combination
就 "then, right away, soon" + 要 "will, be going to" → an imminent event, often
with sentence-final 了: 火车就要开了 "the train's about to leave." 就 tightens
the timing to "any moment now."

## Notes
快要…了 and 就要…了 both mark the imminent future; 就要 can also take a time
expression (明天就要走了), which 快要 cannot.

## Why this combination
公 "public, communal" (see also 公开, 公共) combined with 民 "the
people, the populace" (see also 人民): a member of the public populace
recognised under law — a citizen.

## Notes
公民 is the formal, legal term for a citizen with rights and duties
under a state (公民权利 "citizens' rights," 中国公民 "a Chinese citizen")
— 人民, its broader relative, names "the people" collectively as a
political body, not the individual legal status 公民 designates.

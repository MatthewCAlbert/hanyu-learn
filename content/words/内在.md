## Why this combination
内 "inside" plus 在 (here "existing within, present in") gives exactly
"existing on the inside" — the standard word for a quality inherent to
something rather than imposed from outside.

## Notes
内在 modifies abstract qualities: 内在原因 "underlying cause," 内在联系
"intrinsic connection" — opposite of 外在 "external, outward," and always
about what belongs to a thing's own nature.

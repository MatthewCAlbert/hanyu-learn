## Why this combination
时 "time" is transparent; 机 here carries its "critical juncture,
trigger-point" sense (as in 转机, 契机) rather than its everyday "machine"
sense — together, "the critical moment in time," i.e. the right
opportunity.

## Notes
时机 anchors 抓住时机 "to seize the opportunity," 时机成熟 "the time is
ripe," 错过时机 "to miss one's chance" — always about timing rather than
opportunity in the abstract (which 机会 covers more broadly).

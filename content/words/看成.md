## Why this combination
看 "to look, to view" plus the resultative complement 成 "to become,
resulting in" gives exactly "to view (something) as (something else)" —
the same 看+成 pattern as in 把…看成….

## Notes
看成 always takes a following complement: 把失败看成经验 "to see failure
as experience" — close to 当作 and 视为, though 看成 is the more
everyday, spoken-register choice of the three.

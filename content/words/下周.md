## Why this combination
周 "week" (see [[周末]], [[上周]]) + 下 "next" — the same "the coming one" sense
of 下 as in [[下次]], 下个月. The set is [[上周]] / 这周 / 下周; 周 and 星期 are
interchangeable, so 下周 = 下星期.

## Notes
下 here is temporal ("next"), a metaphorical use of the spatial "below."

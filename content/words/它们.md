## Why this combination
它 "it" (non-human) + the plural suffix 们 (see [[我们]]). Used for objects,
concepts and animals. Like 她, 它 is a 20th-century written invention split off
from 他.

## Notes
它 / 他 / 她 / 它们 / 他们 / 她们 are all homophones — the gender-and-animacy
split is only on the page.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 风 (here "custom, practice," an extended sense
beyond its literal "wind," as in 风景, "scenery") and 俗 ("common,
customary," as in 通俗, "popular [lit. through + common]") both
independently name shared social practice. Pairing them gives the
standard word for folk custom.

Rated "semi" for 风's extended "custom/practice" sense here, distinct
from its everyday "wind" or "scenery" meanings.

## Notes

民风民俗 ("folk customs") and 风俗习惯 ("customs and habits") both build
on this word — names traditional social practices passed down within a
community or region.

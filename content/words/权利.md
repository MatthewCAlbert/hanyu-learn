## Why this combination
权 ("power, authority," covered in this app's hanzi entry) plus 利
("benefit, profit," as in 利益, 有利) names a benefit one is empowered —
authorized — to claim: a right, in the legal/entitlement sense.

Rated "semi" because the compound leans on 权's more abstract "authority"
sense rather than the concrete "power to act" most learners meet first.

## Notes
Do not confuse with the near-homophone-in-writing 权力 (quánlì, "power,
authority" itself, as held by a government or official) — 权利 (this
entry) is a right one holds; 权力 is power one wields. Same first syllable,
different second character, genuinely different meaning.

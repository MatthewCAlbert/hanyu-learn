## Why this combination
完 "complete, finished, whole, without lack" combined with 美 "beautiful,
good": complete in its beauty/goodness, lacking nothing — perfect.

## Notes
完美 stresses flawlessness, an absence of any defect (完美的结局 "a
perfect ending") — 好 is the plain, unmarked word for "good" and makes
no claim of flawlessness the way 完美 does.

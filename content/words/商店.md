## Why this combination
商 "commerce, trade" + 店 "shop premises." A place of trade — the general word
for a retail shop, as against the bigger [[商场]] (mall) or the specific
书店 (bookshop), 饭店 (restaurant / hotel).

## Notes
商 also "merchant" (商人) and "to discuss / negotiate" (商量, 商业).

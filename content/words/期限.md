## Why this combination

期 ("a period, a term," as in 学期, "school term") plus 限 ("a limit, a
bound," as in 限制) names a bounded period of time: a deadline.

## Notes

有效期限 ("validity period, e.g. an expiration date") and 到期限
("to reach the deadline") are common frames — 期限 names the bounded
window itself, distinct from 时间, the general word for "time."

## Why this combination
各 "each, every [one individually]" combined with 种 "a kind, a type" (as
in 种类 "category"): each and every type — various, all kinds of.

## Notes
各种 sits directly before a noun, often reinforced as 各种各样 "all
sorts of, of every kind" (各种问题 "various problems," 各种各样的水果
"all kinds of fruit") — it stresses variety across categories, where
很多 stresses sheer quantity instead.

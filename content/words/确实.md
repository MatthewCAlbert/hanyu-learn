## Why this combination
确 "certain, accurate" and 实 "real, true" are close synonyms, both
about something being solidly, verifiably the case — paired for
emphasis.

## Notes
确实 works as both an adjective ("reliable, true") and, more commonly in
speech, an adverb affirming a statement ("indeed, really") — 确实很好
"[it] really is very good."

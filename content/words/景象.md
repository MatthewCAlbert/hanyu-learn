## Why this combination
景 "scenery, view" and 象 "appearance, image" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for an observed scene or state of affairs.

## Notes
景象 anchors 繁荣景象 "a scene of prosperity," 一片荒凉景象 "a scene of
desolation" — usually describes a broad, often evocative scene, not a
close-up detail.

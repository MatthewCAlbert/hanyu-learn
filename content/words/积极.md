## Why this combination
积 "to accumulate, to build up" (as in 面积 "area") combined with 极
"extremely, the utmost, a pole" (as in 极了 "extremely"): a modern
coinage, likely influenced by the physics term "positive [electric
pole/charge]" (阳极/积极 as positive pole), extended figuratively to a
person's proactive, positive attitude.

## Notes
积极 describes an active, can-do attitude and eager participation
(积极参加 "to participate actively," 积极性 "enthusiasm, initiative") —
its opposite is 消极 "passive, negative," the parallel term built on the
same physics-derived pole metaphor.

## Why this combination

健 ("healthy, strong," as in 健康) takes 身 ("body") as its object:
strengthening the body — to work out.

## Notes

健身房 ("gym, lit. keep-fit room") is the standard word for a gym built
directly on this verb; 健身 itself names the general activity, not any
one exercise.

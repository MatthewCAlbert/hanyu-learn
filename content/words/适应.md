## Why this combination
适 "to fit, to suit" (see also 适合) combined with 应 "to respond, to
correspond to" (as in 反应, 应该): to respond by fitting oneself to
[new conditions] — to adapt.

## Notes
适应 takes a new environment or circumstance as its object (适应新环境
"to adapt to a new environment," 适应能力 "adaptability") — 适合, its
close relative, describes something fitting a person or purpose rather
than a person adjusting to something.

## Why this combination
头 "head" + 发 (*fà*) "hair" → hair of the head, specifically — 发 alone is a
bound morpheme, so the body part needs 头 to stand as a word. Body hair is 毛,
not 发.

## Notes
发 is *fà* here (hair); the simplified form merges it with *fā* 發 "to send
out" (as in [[发现]]) — traditional keeps them apart (髮 vs. 發). 理发
"haircut."

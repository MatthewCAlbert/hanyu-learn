## Why this combination
[[爱沙尼亚]] + 语 "language," the same mould as 法语, 日语 and [[英文]]. The stem is
still a country or people name — usually a phonetic loan — so do not re-read
those characters as a new story.

## Notes
See [[爱沙尼亚]]. 语 leans spoken.

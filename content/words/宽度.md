## Why this combination
宽 "wide" plus 度 "degree, measure" gives exactly "the degree of
wideness" — a measurement noun, parallel to 长度 "length" and 高度
"height."

## Notes
宽度 anchors 测量宽度 "to measure the width," 道路宽度 "road width" — the
standard word for the extent of something along its shorter/lateral
dimension.

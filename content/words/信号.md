## Why this combination
信 "message, word, trust" (see [[信息]], [[相信]]) + 号 "sign, mark, number"
(号码, 记号) → a sign that carries a message. A traffic light (信号灯), a phone's
reception (手机没信号), a warning sign (危险信号).

## Notes
号 "sign / number" also in [[号码]] (not this level), [[信号]]'s neighbours
问号, 句号.

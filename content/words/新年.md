## Why this combination
新 "new" + 年 "year." Refers by default to the lunar New Year period
(过新年 = 过年), and also to 1 January (新年 vs. 元旦). 新年快乐 "Happy New
Year."

## Notes
The bigger festival name is 春节 "Spring Festival"; 新年 leans slightly toward
the calendrical turn, 过年 toward the celebration.

## Why this combination
小 "little" + 孩儿 "child" (孩 with erhua). The everyday colloquial word for a
young child; 小孩 without the 儿, and 小孩子, are the same. More casual than
[[孩子]], which can also mean "my children" of any age.

## Notes
男/女小孩儿, or just 小孩儿, for "a little boy / girl." An adult behaving
childishly: 你真是个小孩儿.

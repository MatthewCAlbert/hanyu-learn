## Why this combination
通 "to go through, to be open" + 过 "to cross, to pass." A synonym pair for
getting from one side to the other — literally (通过大门), figuratively passing a
test or a vote (法案通过了), and as a preposition "by way of" (通过努力 "through
hard work").

## Notes
The prepositional use — 通过 + means → result — is very common in written
Chinese, close to English "by / through / via."

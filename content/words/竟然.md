## Why this combination
竟 carries the sense "unexpectedly, actually, to the point of surprise"
(as in 竟, 毕竟's own different but related root sense of "finally,
after all"), and 然 is a grammaticalized adverb-forming suffix (the same
role it plays in 突然, 显然, 忽然) rather than an independently meaningful
word in the modern language.

Marked "idiom" because 然's suffix role has to be learned as a pattern —
it does not translate on its own the way 竟's "surprise" sense does.

## Notes
竟然 always frames the clause that follows as contrary to what was
expected: 他竟然通过了考试 ("he actually passed the exam" — implying doubt
that he would) — swap it out and the sentence reads as flatly neutral.

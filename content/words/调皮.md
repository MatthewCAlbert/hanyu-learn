## Why this combination

调 (here "to tease, to be mischievous," an extended sense beyond its
more familiar "to adjust/tune," as in 调整) plus 皮 (here "naughty,"
extended from its literal "skin," as in a thick-skinned resistance to
scolding) names playful mischief: naughty.

Rated "semi" for both characters' extended figurative senses here,
distinct from their more literal meanings elsewhere.

## Notes

调皮的孩子 ("a mischievous child") is the standard everyday phrase —
调皮 describes harmless, playful naughtiness, milder than words
implying real misbehavior.

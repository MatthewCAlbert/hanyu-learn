## Why this combination
暖 "warm" (see also 温暖) combined with 和 "mild, gentle, harmonious" (as
in 和平): warm and mild — pleasantly warm.

## Notes
暖和 is the everyday, colloquial word for comfortable physical warmth
(天气暖和了 "the weather has turned nice and warm," 穿得暖和点 "dress
warmly") — 温暖, its more literary relative, covers both physical
warmth and figurative emotional warmth, while 暖和 stays closer to the
concrete, comfortable-temperature sense.

## Why this combination
偶 here carries its older "carved figure, puppet/idol" sense (see 偶's
own Shuowen etymology — 桐人也, "a figure carved of paulownia wood"), not
its more common modern "by chance" sense. Combined with 像 "image,
likeness," 偶像 literally names a carved cult image — a statue worshipped
as a god — extended in modern use to a celebrity idolized by fans.

## Notes
偶像 now overwhelmingly means "celebrity idol" (偶像明星 "idol star," 偶像
剧 "idol drama," a Taiwanese TV genre) — the original religious-statue
sense survives mainly in the set phrase 偶像崇拜 "idol worship."

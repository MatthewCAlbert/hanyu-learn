## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 完 "finished, whole" + 全 "whole, entire." As an adjective,
"complete" (完全的胜利); as an adverb, "completely / entirely"
(完全同意, 完全不知道).

## Notes
As an adverb it is the go-to intensifier for totality — stronger and more
absolute than 很 or 非常.

## Why this combination
晚 "evening" + 报 "newspaper" (see [[报纸]], [[日报]]) → a paper published in the
afternoon for evening reading, typically lighter and more local than a morning
[[日报]]. Common in mastheads: 北京晚报, 羊城晚报.

## Notes
报 "newspaper" in the masthead set: [[日报]], 晚报, 早报, 周报.

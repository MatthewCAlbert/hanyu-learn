## Why this combination
旅 "a journey, to travel" (see [[旅行]], [[旅客]]) + 游 "to roam, to wander, to
tour" (游玩, 游客) → travelling for pleasure and sightseeing. 去旅游, 旅游业
"the tourism industry."

## Notes
旅游 = leisure travel / tourism; [[旅行]] stresses the journeying itself.
游 "roam" also in [[游客]], 游泳 "swim" (roam in water).

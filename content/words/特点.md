## Why this combination
特 "special, particular" (see [[特别]]) + 点 "point, spot" (see [[重点]],
[[观点]]) → a point on which something is distinctive: 这个方法的特点是….

## Notes
特点 = a distinguishing feature (neutral); 优点 / 缺点 = a merit / fault;
特色 = a distinctive character / speciality (中国特色, 特色菜).

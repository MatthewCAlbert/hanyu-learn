## Why this combination

降 ("to descend," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 降落/降温)
takes 价 ("price," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 价格/高价)
as its object: bringing the price down.

## Notes

降价促销 ("a price-cut promotion") and 大幅降价 ("a major price cut")
both build on this word — 涨价 ("to raise the price," lit. "rise +
price") is its direct opposite, built the same way with 涨 "to rise."

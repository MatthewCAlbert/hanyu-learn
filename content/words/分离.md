## Why this combination
分 "to divide" and 离 "to leave, to separate" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for separation.

## Notes
分离 covers physical/chemical separation (分离物质 "to separate
substances") and emotional parting (骨肉分离 "family members torn apart")
— more formal than everyday 分开.

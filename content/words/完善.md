## Why this combination
完 "complete, finished, without lack" (see also 完整, 完美) combined
with 善 "good, virtuous, excellent" (as in 善良 "kind-hearted"): complete
and good — comprehensive, well-developed.

## Notes
完善 describes systems and facilities as thoroughly developed (完善的
制度 "a well-developed system") and, as a verb, "to refine, to make
more complete" (完善计划 "to refine a plan") — it is used for
incremental improvement towards a well-rounded state, not a single
correction the way 修改 might be.

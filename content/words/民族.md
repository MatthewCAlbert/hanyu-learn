## Why this combination
民 "the people" (see also 人民, 农民) combined with 族 "a clan, a tribe,
a lineage group" (as in 家族 "family clan"): a people bound as one clan
— an ethnic group, a nation in the ethnic sense.

## Notes
民族 names an ethnic or national group sharing culture and descent
(少数民族 "ethnic minority," 民族文化 "ethnic/national culture") — 国家,
"country, nation-state," names the political entity, which need not
align one-to-one with a single 民族.

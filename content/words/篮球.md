## Why this combination
篮 "basket" (the original peach-basket goal) + 球 "ball" — a calque of English
"basketball," naming the game by its target. 打篮球 "play basketball,"
篮球场, [[篮球]]'s teammates 足球, 排球.

## Notes
The 球 ball-games set: 足球 "football," 排球 "volleyball," 乒乓球 "table
tennis," 羽毛球 "badminton." 篮 "basket" also in 花篮, 菜篮.

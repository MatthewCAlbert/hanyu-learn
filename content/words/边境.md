## Why this combination
边 "edge, border" and 境 "territory, boundary" (see 境's own etymology,
tied to 竟 "boundary, end") are near-synonyms combined into the standard
word for a national frontier.

## Notes
边境 anchors 边境线 "border line," 边境地区 "border region" — names the
zone along a national boundary, distinct from 边界, which is the abstract
dividing line itself.

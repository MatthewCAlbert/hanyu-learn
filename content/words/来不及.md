## Why this combination

来 ("to come") plus the potential-complement negative 不及 ("cannot
reach/catch up to," 及 as in 及时, "in time") fuses into a set phrase:
unable to arrive in time — too late.

Rated "semi": the potential-complement grammar (verb + 不 + result, "unable
to come-and-catch-up") is transparent to anyone who knows that pattern,
but not obvious without it.

## Notes

来不及 always names a missed window (来不及了, "it's too late [now]";
来不及吃饭, "no time to eat") — 赶不上 is a close near-synonym built the
same way, with 赶 "to rush/catch" instead of 来.

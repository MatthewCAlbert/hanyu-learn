## Why this combination
Not really a compound — 玩 "to play" with the erhua suffix 儿, which is felt as
part of the word in northern speech (玩儿 rather than bare 玩). Covers children
playing, adults socialising (出去玩儿 "go out and have fun"), and playing with a
thing (玩儿手机).

## Notes
Written Mandarin and the south often drop the 儿: 玩 and 玩儿 are the same word.
好玩儿 "fun."

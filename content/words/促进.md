## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 促 ("to urge, to hasten," as in 督促, "to
supervise and urge") and 进 ("to advance," as in 进步) both independently
name pushing something forward. Pairing them gives the standard word for
facilitating progress.

## Notes

促进发展 ("to promote development") and 促进消化 ("to aid digestion") both
build on this word — 促进 always takes an abstract process as its object,
distinct from 推进, which more often names moving a concrete project or
initiative forward.

## Why this combination
台 is the established name of the island; 湾 "bay / gulf" is the geography
morpheme in 臺灣. A toponym, not a phonetic of English "Taiwan." 台 is not
"platform" as an everyday noun here, though that is the character.

## Notes
Tagged as geography. Traditional 臺灣. Contrast [[香港]], another Extra place name
built from real morphemes.

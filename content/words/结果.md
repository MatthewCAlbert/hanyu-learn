## Why this combination
Literally "to bear fruit" — 结 "to tie, to form" + 果 "fruit." A plant putting
out fruit is the metaphor for a process yielding an outcome. As a noun,
"the result" (考试结果); as an adverb, "and then, as it turned out"
(结果他没来).

## Notes
The literal *jiē guǒ* "to fruit" (树结果了) keeps the first syllable in first
tone; the noun/adverb is *jié guǒ*. Same 果 = "result" in [[如果]], 后果, 效果.

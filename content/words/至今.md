## Why this combination
至 "to reach, up to, until" (as in 甚至 "even") combined with 今 "now,
the present" (as in 现今, and 今天 "today"): reaching up to now — so
far, up to the present.

## Notes
至今 marks a state or fact that has held continuously up to the present
moment (至今未婚 "unmarried to this day," 至今没有消息 "no news so far")
— it is more formal and literary than everyday 到现在为止 "up until
now."

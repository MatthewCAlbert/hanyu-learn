## Why this combination
身 stands for the self/person (as it does across many compounds, 身体,
自身), and 份 carries its "a share, a portion, one's allotted standing"
sense (also seen in 份儿, and the measure-word use of 份 for a serving or
copy). "The share/standing allotted to a person" is exactly their
identity or role.

Rated "semi" for 份's "allotted standing" sense, which is a specific,
less-common extension of its everyday "portion" meaning.

## Notes
身份证 (identity card) is by far the most common everyday compound;
以...身份 ("in the capacity of...") is a common formal frame for stating
the role someone is acting in.

## Why this combination
通 "to go through, to be current, to hold generally" (as in 通过 "to
pass through") combined with 常 "ordinary, usual, constant" (as in 常常
"often"): what holds generally as the usual case — normally, usually.

## Notes
通常 works as adjective ("通常的做法" "the usual practice") and adverb
("通常我七点起床" "I usually get up at seven") — 一般, a near-synonym,
overlaps heavily but leans slightly more towards "in general" as opposed
to strictly "as a rule."

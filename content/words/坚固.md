## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 坚 ("firm, solid," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 坚持/坚强) and 固 ("solid, fixed," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 固定) both independently name unyielding solidity.
Pairing them gives the standard word for structural sturdiness.

## Notes

坚固的桥 ("a sturdy bridge") and 坚固耐用 ("solid and durable") both
build on this word — 坚固 always describes physical structural strength,
distinct from 坚强, which describes a person's inner resilience.

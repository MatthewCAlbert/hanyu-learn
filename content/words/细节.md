## Why this combination
细 ("thin, fine," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 节 ("a joint, a
segment," as in 季节, 关节, "a joint of the body") names the finest,
smallest segments something can be broken down into: its details.

Rated "semi" for 节's "segment, a discrete unit" sense, distinct from
its more familiar role in 节日 ("festival").

## Notes
细节 names the small specifics of a larger whole (注意细节, "to pay
attention to detail") — always plural in sense, even without a plural
marker, since a single detail alone is rarely called 细节.

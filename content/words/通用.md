## Why this combination
通 (here "general, throughout") plus 用 "to use" gives exactly "used
throughout, generally applicable" — the standard word for something not
restricted to one specific case.

## Notes
通用 anchors 通用语言 "a lingua franca/common language," 通用零件
"interchangeable parts" — always about broad applicability or
interchangeability across contexts.

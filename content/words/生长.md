## Why this combination
生 "to be born, to live, to arise" (see also 生存, 生产) combined with
长 "to grow, to increase [in size]" (see also 成长, 增长): to be born and
grow — to grow, especially of living things.

## Notes
生长 is used for the biological growth of plants, animals and people
(生长发育 "growth and development," 植物生长 "plant growth") — 成长, its
close relative, is used more for a person's personal, character-related
growing up rather than pure biological growth.

## Why this combination
火 (here "fire-cured, smoked") plus 腿 "leg, thigh" gives exactly "a
smoke-cured leg (of pork)" — describing both the cut of meat and its
traditional preservation method.

## Notes
火腿 anchors 火腿肠 "ham sausage," 金华火腿 "Jinhua ham (a famous cured
variety)" — the standard word for cured pork leg, regardless of modern
curing method.

## Why this combination
投 takes its extended "to commit, to throw oneself into" sense (as covered
in this app's hanzi entry — the same sense behind 投入), and 资 ("funds,
capital," as in 资金) is its object: to throw one's capital into a
venture. As a verb-object compound it can split for emphasis: 投了不少资
("invested quite a bit of capital").

## Notes
投资 works as both verb (投资一个项目, "to invest in a project") and noun
(这是一笔好投资, "this is a good investment") without changing form.

## Why this combination
也 "also" and 好 "good, fine" combine into "that's also fine" — accepting
an option as acceptable, even if not first choice. Reduplicated (X也好，
Y也好), it extends to "whether X or Y, either way" — a grammaticalized
use not obvious from a single instance of the phrase.

## Notes
也好 alone softly accepts a suggestion (那也好 "that works too"); doubled
in X也好，Y也好, it dismisses the distinction between two options as
unimportant to the point being made.

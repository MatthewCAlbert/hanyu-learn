## Why this combination
开 "to open" (as in 开始 "to begin," literally "to open the start")
combined with 发 "to send out, to issue, to develop" (see also 发展,
发出): to open up and send out — to develop, to exploit a resource.

## Notes
开发 takes untapped resources, land or products as its object (开发
资源 "to develop resources," 软件开发 "software development") — 发展, its
close relative, is used more broadly for growth and progress in general,
not specifically the exploitation of a resource or the creation of a new
product.

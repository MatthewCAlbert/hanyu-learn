## Why this combination
甚 means "extremely, very" (surviving mostly bound, as in 甚为), and 至 is
"to arrive at, to reach (a point)" (as in 至今, 直至). Put together, "extreme
to the point of reaching [something surprising]" is exactly the rhetorical
job "even" does in English — it signals the sentence has escalated to an
extreme, unexpected case.

## Notes
甚至 typically introduces the most extreme item in a list or escalates a
claim (他什么都吃，甚至连虫子都吃 "he eats everything, even bugs") — it is an
adverb/conjunction, not a verb, and cannot take an object on its own.

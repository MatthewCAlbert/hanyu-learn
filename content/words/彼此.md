## Why this combination
彼 "that [one]" and 此 "this [one]" placed side by side give exactly
"that one and this one" — i.e., each party in a mutual relationship,
hence "each other."

## Notes
彼此 is more formal/literary than 互相 "mutually" and can stand alone as a
polite reply: 彼此彼此 "same to you" (a modest deflection of a
compliment).

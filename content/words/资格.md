## Why this combination
资 "capital, standing, resources" (see also 资金) combined with 格 "a
standard, a status, a fixed pattern" (see also 价格): the standing
[needed to meet] a standard — qualification, eligibility.

## Notes
资格 names the eligibility or standing required to do something
(有资格参加 "to be qualified to take part," 取消资格 "to disqualify") —
学历, a near-neighbour, is specifically academic credentials rather than
qualification in the broader sense 资格 covers.

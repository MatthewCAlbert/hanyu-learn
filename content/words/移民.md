## Why this combination

移 ("to move, to shift," as in 移动) takes 民 ("people," as in 人民) as
its object: moving people (from one country to settle in another).

## Notes

移民 covers both the act (移民到加拿大, "to emigrate to Canada") and the
person (他是新移民, "he's a new immigrant") — the verb-object form still
takes a noun role, much like 结婚 ("to marry") can.

## Why this combination
外 "outside, external" + 国 "country" → a country outside one's own, a foreign
nation as an entity: 外国人 "foreigner," 外国货 "imported goods." [[国外]]
reverses the parts for the adverbial "abroad."

## Notes
外 as "foreign / external" also in 外语 (foreign language), 外交 (diplomacy),
对外 (external-facing).

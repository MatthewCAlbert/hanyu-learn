## Why this combination
夏 "summer" + 天, here "season / time of year" (the same slot as in 春天, 秋天,
冬天) — not "sky" or "day." The four seasons all take this 天 suffix.

## Notes
夏 also names the legendary Xia dynasty and, in 华夏, China itself. 夏令营
"summer camp," 夏天的晚上.

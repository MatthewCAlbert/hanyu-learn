## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 语 "language, speech, to speak" (see [[汉语]], [[外语]]) + 言
"speech, words, to say" (发言, 语言). Doubled for the abstract noun "language" —
human language as a faculty or a system: 语言学 "linguistics," 身体语言 "body
language."

## Notes
语言 = language in general; a specific tongue is 汉语 / 英语 etc.; 话 is
"speech / what is said" or a dialect (北京话).

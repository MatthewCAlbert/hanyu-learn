## Why this combination

电 ("electric," as in 电脑) plus 灯 ("a lamp," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 灯光) names a lamp powered by electricity: an
electric light.

## Notes

开电灯/关电灯 ("to turn the light on/off") is the standard everyday
phrase — 电灯 names the fixture itself, distinct from 灯光, the light it
gives off.

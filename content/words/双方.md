## Why this combination
双 "pair, two [of something]" (as in 一双鞋 "a pair of shoes") combined
with 方 "side, party [to an interaction]" (see also 对方): the two sides
[of an interaction] — both parties.

## Notes
双方 names both sides in a negotiation, agreement or dispute (双方同意
"both parties agree," 双方关系 "relations between the two sides") — 两个,
"the two [things]," is far more general and lacks 双方's specific framing
of two parties in a shared interaction.

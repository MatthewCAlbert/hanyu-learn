## Why this combination
分 "to divide" plus the resultative complement 成 "to become, resulting
in" gives exactly "to divide, resulting in (parts)" — the same
verb+成 pattern as 分成两半 "split into two halves."

## Notes
分成 always takes a following complement stating the result: 分成三组
"divided into three groups" — distinct from the unrelated noun sense
"share of profit" (分成比例 "profit-sharing ratio"), which comes from the
same characters but a different construction.

## Why this combination
登 carries its "to enter into a record, to publish/note formally" sense
(covered elsewhere in this app — climbing up onto a mounting-step,
extended to entering something formally), and 记 is "to record, to note
down" (as in 记得, 日记). Entering something into a formal record twice
over is exactly "to register."

Rated "semi" for 登's "to formally enter into record" sense, a specific
extension of its more concrete "to ascend" meaning.

## Notes
登记 always implies an official record being created (户口登记,
"household registration"; 登记结婚, "to register a marriage") — a formal
administrative act, not casual note-taking.

## Why this combination
看 "to look, to view" and 待 "to treat" combine directly: viewing and
treating someone or something in a particular light.

## Notes
看待 always takes a manner adverbial: 正确看待 "to view correctly," 客观
看待问题 "to look at a problem objectively" — a formal verb for forming a
judgement or attitude toward something.

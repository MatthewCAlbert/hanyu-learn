## Why this combination
Water always finds a flat, even surface, so "the water's level" became the image
for a horizontal reference line, and from there a metaphorical "level" of
ability or quality: 汉语水平, 生活水平, 提高水平.

## Notes
Literal "horizontal": 水平线 "horizon," 水平方向. The attainment sense is by far
the commoner one. Compare 水准, a close synonym.

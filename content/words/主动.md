## Why this combination
主 "main, primary, taking the lead" (see also 主任, 主人) combined with
动 "to move, to act" (see also 自动, 运动): acting as the primary mover —
taking the initiative, acting proactively rather than being acted upon.

## Notes
主动 describes acting first, of one's own accord (主动帮忙 "to help
proactively," 主动权 "the initiative/upper hand") — its opposite is 被动
"passive," built with the same 动 but the "acted-upon" 被 in place of 主.

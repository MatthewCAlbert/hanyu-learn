## Why this combination
下 "down" and 去 "to go [away from the speaker]" combine transparently
for the literal sense of downward motion. Semi rather than transparent
because 下去'的 far more common modern job — as a complement marking that
an ongoing action continues ("keep on doing X") — is a figurative
extension: an action moving forward in time, the way motion moves
forward in space.

## Notes
As a continuation marker, 下去 always follows the verb it applies to —
说下去 "keep talking," 坚持下去 "keep persisting" — paralleling 下来's
own grammaticalised "continuing up to now" sense, but pointed toward the
future rather than the present.

## Why this combination
组 "to organise into a group, a group, a set" (as in 组成 "to make up,
to compose") combined with 合 "to combine, to close together" (see also
联合, 合理): to organise into a combined whole — a combination.

## Notes
组合 names a set or grouping formed by combining parts (音乐组合 "a music
group/band," 数字组合 "a combination of numbers") and, in mathematics,
translates "combinatorics" (组合数学 "combinatorial mathematics") — 组成,
its close relative, is more the verb "to make up, to constitute" a whole
from parts.

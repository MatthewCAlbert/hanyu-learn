## Why this combination
迷 "to be lost in, to enchant" acting on the object 人 "person" gives
"[enough to] enchant a person" — splittable, though rarely split in
practice (迷死人了 "utterly enchanting").

## Notes
迷人 describes anything captivating — scenery, personality, a voice
(迷人的微笑 "a charming smile," 迷人的风景 "enchanting scenery") — a
common, versatile compliment adjective.

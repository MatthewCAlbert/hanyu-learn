## Why this combination
南 "south" and 北 "north" are direct opposites combined into a single
word naming both directions together — the same antonym-pair pattern as
东西 "things (lit. east-west)" and 大小 "size."

## Notes
南北 anchors 南北方 "the north and the south (regions of a country),"
南北差异 "north-south differences" — always frames a country or region
along its north-south axis, often to contrast climate or culture.

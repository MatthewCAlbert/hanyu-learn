## Why this combination
分 "to divide" (see also 分别, 部分) combined with 配 "to pair, to match,
to allocate in the right proportion" (see also 配合): to divide and
allocate in proportion — to distribute.

## Notes
分配 is used for the formal distribution of resources, tasks or
outcomes among a group (分配任务 "to assign tasks," 财富分配 "wealth
distribution") — 分, alone, is the plainer everyday verb for simply
"to divide" something up.

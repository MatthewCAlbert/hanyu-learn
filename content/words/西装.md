## Why this combination
西 (short for 西方 "the West") plus 装 "attire, clothing" gives exactly
"Western-style clothing" — the standard word for the two/three-piece
suit, as distinct from traditional Chinese dress.

## Notes
西装 anchors 穿西装 "to wear a suit," 西装革履 "dressed in suit and
leather shoes" (idiom for formal Western attire) — a near-synonym of
西服, both common.

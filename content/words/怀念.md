## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 怀 ("to harbor a feeling within," covered
elsewhere in this app) and 念 ("to think of with longing," covered in
this app's entry for 想念) both independently name holding someone or
something fondly in mind. Pairing them gives a slightly more literary
synonym of 想念.

## Notes
怀念 leans more literary/formal than 想念, and can take a place or a past
time as its object as readily as a person (怀念故乡, "to long for one's
hometown"; 怀念过去, "to look back fondly on the past").

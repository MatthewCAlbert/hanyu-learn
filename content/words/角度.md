## Why this combination
角 "angle, corner, horn" + 度 "degree, measure" → an angle measured in degrees
(30度角), and figuratively the "angle" from which one views a matter:
换个角度想 "look at it another way," 从…的角度.

## Notes
度 "degree" also in [[温度]], [[态度]], 程度. 角 is *jiǎo* here (angle), not
*jué* "role."

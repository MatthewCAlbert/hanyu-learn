## Why this combination
月 "moon" plus 饼 "a flat cake" gives exactly "a moon-shaped cake" —
named for its round shape, echoing the full moon of the Mid-Autumn
Festival it is traditionally eaten during.

## Notes
月饼 anchors 中秋节吃月饼 "eating mooncakes during Mid-Autumn Festival" —
tightly tied to that specific holiday, much like how 粽子 is tied to the
Dragon Boat Festival.

## Why this combination
碰 "to bump, to touch, to knock against" + resultative 到 "reach" — making
contact, whether physically (碰到桌角) or by chance (在路上碰到老朋友,
碰到问题 "run into a problem").

## Notes
碰到 / 碰见 (see [[碰见]]) = meet by chance; 遇到 is the near-synonym; [[见到]] =
get to see (often arranged). 碰 alone: 碰运气 "try one's luck."

## Why this combination
恢 (in its "to restore [to full scope]" sense) and 复 "to return, to
repeat" both point at the same idea: bringing something back to its
original, complete state.

## Notes
恢复 covers health (恢复健康 "to recover one's health"), normalcy (恢复正常
"to return to normal"), and relations (恢复邦交 "to restore diplomatic
relations") — always a return to a prior, better state.

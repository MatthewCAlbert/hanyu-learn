## Why this combination
沙发 *shāfā* transliterates English "sofa" purely by sound — neither 沙
"sand" nor 发 "hair, to send out" contributes its ordinary meaning here;
both are chosen only for their pronunciation, the standard signature of
a phonetic loanword like 咖啡 and 巴士.

## Notes
沙发 is a pure sound loan with no semantic connection to its
components' literal meanings — in Chinese internet slang, 沙发 "sofa"
also became the term for "first reply" on a forum post (claiming the
best seat), a playful extension unrelated to the original furniture
sense.

## Why this combination
下 "to descend, to come down" + 雪 "snow" — falling weather is expressed as 下 +
the precipitation (see [[下雨]]): 下雪, 下雨, 下冰雹. 雪 is the object of 下:
下了一场大雪.

## Notes
No subject needed: 下雪了 "it's snowing." 雪 "snow" also in 雪人 "snowman,"
滑雪 "ski," 雪白 "snow-white."

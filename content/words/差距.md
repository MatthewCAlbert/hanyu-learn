## Why this combination
差 "difference" and 距 "distance" combine into exactly "the distance of
difference" — the standard word for a measurable gap between two things.

## Notes
差距 is always comparative: 缩小差距 "to narrow the gap," 存在差距 "there
is a disparity" — used for gaps in ability, quality, or development, not
literal physical distance (that would be 距离 alone).

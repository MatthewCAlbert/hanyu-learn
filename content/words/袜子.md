## Why this combination

袜 ("a sock, a stocking") plus the nominal suffix 子 names the everyday
garment: socks.

## Notes

穿袜子 ("to put on/wear socks") pairs 袜子 with 穿, the same verb used for
garments worn on the body (穿衣服, 穿裤子) — distinct from 戴, used for
accessories on the extremities like 手套 (covered elsewhere in this app).

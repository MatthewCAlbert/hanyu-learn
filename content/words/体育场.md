## Why this combination
[[体育]] "sport" + 场 (*chǎng*) "an open ground, an arena" (see [[广场]],
[[球场]], [[机场]]) → an outdoor sports ground with stands. Parse 体育 + 场.

## Notes
体育场 = outdoor (a field / stadium); [[体育馆]] = indoor (a hall). 场 is
*chǎng*.

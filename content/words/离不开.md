## Why this combination

离 ("to leave, to be apart from," as in 离开) plus the potential-
complement negative 不开 ("cannot separate/open," 开 as in 打开) fuses
into a set phrase: unable to become separated from something — cannot
do without it.

Rated "semi": the potential-complement grammar (verb + 不 + result,
"unable to leave-and-separate") is transparent to anyone who knows that
pattern, but not obvious without it.

## Notes

生活离不开水 ("life cannot do without water") and 我们离不开彼此 ("we can't
do without each other") both build on this word — always names an
essential dependency, stronger than simply "needing" something.

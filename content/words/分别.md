## Why this combination
分 "to divide, to separate" (as in 部分 "part") combined with 别 "to
distinguish, other, separate" (see also 区别, 别人): to be divided apart
— to part ways, or, as an adverb, "separately, each in turn."

## Notes
分别 covers "to part [from someone]" (我们分别了三年 "we've been apart
for three years") and, as an adverb, "individually, respectively" (他们
分别来自不同国家 "they each come from different countries") — the two
senses share the same root idea of things kept apart and distinguished.

## Why this combination
失 "to lose, to let slip" paired with 去 as a directional complement
("away [from oneself]") — something slipping out of one's grasp and
going away: to lose.

## Notes
失去 takes abstract or valued things as its object (失去信心 "to lose
confidence," 失去机会 "to lose an opportunity") more often than everyday
physical objects, where 丢 or 丢失 is more common.

## Why this combination
用 "to use" plus 于 (a classical preposition marking "at, in, for")
combines into a formal way of specifying a purpose or application — 于
here is grammatical rather than independently meaningful in modern
speech.

## Notes
用于 always introduces the purpose or domain something is applied to:
这笔钱用于教育 "this money is used for education" — a written-register
alternative to the more colloquial 用来.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 减 ("to reduce, to subtract," covered elsewhere
in this app) and 少 ("few, little") both independently name a lessening.
Pairing them gives the standard everyday verb for decreasing in
quantity.

## Notes
减少 and 增加 (to increase) form the standard antonym pair for describing
quantities going down or up — worth learning together.

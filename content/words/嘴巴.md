## Why this combination

嘴 ("mouth," the everyday word) plus 巴 (a nominal suffix that also
appears in other body-part-adjacent words, such as 尾巴, "tail") names
the mouth in casual, spoken register.

Rated "semi" for 巴's role as a bare suffix rather than a meaning-bearing
element.

## Notes

嘴巴 doubles as slang for "a slap" (打嘴巴, "to slap someone's face") —
context distinguishes the body-part sense from the idiomatic one.

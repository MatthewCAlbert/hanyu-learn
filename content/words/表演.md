## Why this combination
表 "to display, to show outwardly" (as in 表示 "to express") combined with
演 "to act out, to rehearse, to develop [a role]" (as in 演员 "actor"):
to display something acted out before an audience — to perform.

## Notes
表演 covers staged performance broadly — acting, music, acrobatics, magic
— and doubles as the noun "a performance" (精彩的表演 "a wonderful
performance") without any change in form.

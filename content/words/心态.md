## Why this combination
心 "heart, mind" plus 态 "state, condition" gives exactly "the state of
one's mind" — the standard word for a person's psychological disposition.

## Notes
心态 anchors 调整心态 "to adjust one's mindset," 平和的心态 "a calm
disposition" — always about inner outlook rather than momentary emotion
(that would be 心情).

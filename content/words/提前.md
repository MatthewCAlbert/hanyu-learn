## Why this combination
A verb-object-like compound: 提 "to raise, to lift, to bring up [in
time]" combined with 前 "ahead, forward, before": to bring [a time or
date] forward — to move something earlier than planned.

## Notes
提前 works as both verb ("提前完成" "to finish ahead of schedule") and
adverb ("提前到达" "to arrive early/ahead of time") — it always implies
moving something earlier relative to an original plan, unlike 早, the
plain adjective "early," which makes no reference to an original
schedule.

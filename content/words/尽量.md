## Why this combination
尽 "to exhaust, to use up fully" (as in 尽力 "to exert one's full
strength") combined with 量 "amount, capacity, quantity" (as in 数量
"quantity"): to exhaust the full capacity available — as much as
possible.

## Notes
尽量 sits before a verb urging maximal effort within limits (尽量早点到
"try to arrive as early as possible," 尽量少吃 "eat as little as
possible") — it asks for the best achievable outcome, not an absolute
guarantee.

## Why this combination
竞 "to compete" (see 竞's own etymology) and 赛 "to race, to compete" are
near-synonyms combined into the formal word for an organized competition.

## Notes
竞赛 favours structured, often academic or sporting contests (数学竞赛
"math competition," 军备竞赛 "arms race") — close to 比赛 but leaning
slightly more formal.

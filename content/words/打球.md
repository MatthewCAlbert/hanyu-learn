## Why this combination
球 "ball" as the object of 打 "to hit / strike" — a real 打, not the bleached
light verb. Covers hand-and-racket sports: 打篮球 basketball, 打乒乓球 table
tennis, 打网球. It splits: 打了一场球.

## Notes
Sports where you don't strike the ball use a different verb: 踢足球 "kick
football," 打 for the rest.

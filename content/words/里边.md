## Why this combination
里 "inside" + the locative suffix 边 (as in [[外边]], [[旁边]], 上边). 里边 /
外边 is the in/out pair within the same 边 series as the compass and up/down
terms.

## Notes
里面 is the exact synonym (边 vs. 面 as the suffix); both contrast with the bare
里 that suffixes onto nouns (屋里, 城里).

## Why this combination
别 "other, separate" + the particle 的, which here nominalises: "the other
one(s)." 还有别的吗 "anything else?", 别的人 = [[别人]].

## Notes
别 the adjective ("other") vs. 别 the imperative ("don't") — 别的 only uses the
first.

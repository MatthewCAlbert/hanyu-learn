## Why this combination
已 "already, to stop" + 经 "to pass through, to undergo" → "has already gone
through (it)." The 经 adds the sense of a completed passage of time, reinforcing
已. Usually paired with sentence-final 了: 已经八点了.

## Notes
已 alone is the terse literary "already" (时间已晚). 经 = "pass through" also in
[[经过]], [[经常]].

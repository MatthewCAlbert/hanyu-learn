## Why this combination
重 "heavy" in its figurative sense "weighty, serious" + 要 "essential, to
matter." Something that both carries weight and is needed — important. The
"heavy" metaphor for importance is the same one English uses.

## Notes
重 is *zhòng* here (heavy / grave), not *chóng* (again, repeat). Compare 主要
(main, principal), which ranks items rather than weighing them.

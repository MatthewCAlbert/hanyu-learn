## Why this combination
记 "to remember, to note down" (as in 记得) combined with 录 "to record,
to write down" (see 录's own entry — the simplification kept an ancient,
unrelated character 彔 rather than trimming traditional 錄): two words
for setting something down in writing, paired for the standard verb and
noun.

## Notes
记录 covers written notes (会议记录 "meeting minutes"), a formal record
(历史记录 "historical record") and, in sports, a "record" in the sense
of a best result (打破记录 "to break a record", though 纪录 is the more
formally correct written form for that specific sense).

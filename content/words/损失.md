## Why this combination
损 "to diminish, to harm" and 失 "to lose" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for loss, whether financial, material, or human.

## Notes
损失 works as both noun (经济损失 "economic loss") and verb (损失了很多钱
"lost a lot of money") — the same word covering the event and its cost.

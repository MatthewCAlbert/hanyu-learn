## Why this combination
礼 "courtesy, ritual, propriety" (礼貌, 婚礼) + 物 "thing, object" → an object
given as an act of courtesy. 送礼物 "give a gift," 生日礼物, 礼物 vs. bribe
礼 / 送礼.

## Notes
礼 "ceremony / etiquette" is a heavy Confucian word; 礼物 narrows it to the
tangible token. 物 "thing" also in [[动物]], [[食物]], [[礼物]].

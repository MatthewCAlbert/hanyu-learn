## Why this combination
战 ("to battle," covered elsewhere in this app) takes 胜 ("victory, to
win") as its result: battling one's way to victory over an opponent or
obstacle.

## Notes
战胜 covers both literal contests (战胜对手, "to defeat an opponent") and
abstract struggles (战胜困难, "to overcome difficulty"; 战胜自己, "to
overcome oneself") — the same "fighting through to victory" image
applied to both.

## Why this combination

暗 ("dark, hidden, covert," as in 黑暗) plus 示 ("to show, to indicate,"
as in 表示) names indicating something covertly rather than openly: to
hint.

## Notes

暗示 contrasts directly with 明示 ("to state explicitly, lit. bright/
clear + show") — the 暗/明 pair (dark/bright) marks the same
covert-vs-open distinction found elsewhere in the language.

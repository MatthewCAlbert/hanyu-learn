## Why this combination
相 "mutually, [towards] each other" (see also 相当, 相比) combined with
似 "to resemble, similar" (see also 类似): resembling each other —
similar, alike.

## Notes
相似 describes two or more things closely resembling each other (相似
之处 "similarities," 长得很相似 "look very alike") — 类似, its close
relative, is used more to say something is of a similar type or kind,
while 相似 stresses a closer, more direct resemblance.

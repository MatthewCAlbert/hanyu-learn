## Why this combination
办, here "to handle, to manage, to do" (as in 办公室, 办法) combined with
理 "to attend to, to arrange, to put in order" (see also 管理, 整理): to
handle and put in order — to process, to transact.

## Notes
办理 is the formal verb for processing official business (办理手续 "to
go through formalities," 办理业务 "to handle/transact business") — 办,
alone, is the everyday verb for "to handle, to do" something, used far
more broadly.

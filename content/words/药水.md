## Why this combination
药 "medicine, drug" (see [[药店]], [[药片]]) + 水 "water, liquid" → medicine in
liquid form — a syrup to drink, or drops and lotions applied externally:
滴眼药水 "put in eye drops."

## Notes
The 药 forms: 药水 (liquid), [[药片]] (tablet), 药丸 (pill), 药膏 (ointment),
药粉 (powder).

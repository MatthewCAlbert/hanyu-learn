## Why this combination

准 ("accurate, standard," as in 标准) plus 时 ("time," as in 时间) names
being exactly on the standard time: punctual.

## Notes

准时到达 ("to arrive on time") and 准时开始 ("to start on time") are
standard frames — 准时 focuses on matching a scheduled time exactly,
distinct from 及时 (covered elsewhere in this app), which means "in
time/before it's too late" more broadly.

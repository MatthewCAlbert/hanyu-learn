## Why this combination

镜 ("a mirror, a lens," as in 眼镜, "glasses") plus the nominal suffix 子
names the everyday object: a mirror.

## Notes

照镜子 ("to look in a mirror, lit. to shine [oneself in] a mirror") is
the standard verb-object phrase; 镜 alone stays productive in
compounds (望远镜, "telescope"; 显微镜, "microscope") that all share the
same lens-based root.

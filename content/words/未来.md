## Why this combination
未 ("not yet") plus 来 ("to come") reads exactly as it looks: "what has not
yet come" — the future. One of the more directly transparent compounds in
the language once 未's "not yet" sense (as opposed to plain 不, "not") is
known.

## Notes
未来 works as both a noun (未来充满希望 "the future is full of hope") and an
adjective before another noun (未来的世界 "the world of the future," 未来
五年 "the next five years").

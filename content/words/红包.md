## Why this combination

红 ("red," the color of good fortune in Chinese culture) plus 包
("a package, to wrap," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 包裹)
names money wrapped in a red envelope: a red-envelope gift.

## Notes

发红包 ("to give out red envelopes," a New Year and celebration
tradition) and 微信红包 ("a WeChat red envelope," the digital version
sent through the app) both build on this word — a custom that has moved
smoothly from paper envelopes to mobile payments.

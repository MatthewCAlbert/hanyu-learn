## Why this combination
楼 "multi-storey building / floor" + 下 "below" → a lower floor, or the ground
level relative to where you are. 楼上 is its opposite.

## Notes
楼 alone as "floor / storey" needs a number: 三楼 "third floor," 楼上 / 楼下 the
relative versions.

## Why this combination
总 "overall, general, altogether" (as in 总是 "always") combined with 结
"to tie off, to conclude" (see also 结束 "to end," 结合): to tie
everything together overall — to sum up.

## Notes
总结 works as verb ("总结经验" "to sum up lessons learned") and noun
("写总结" "to write a summary") — it names drawing conclusions from a
completed period or experience, a very common closing step in Chinese
workplace and school routines (期末总结 "end-of-term summary").

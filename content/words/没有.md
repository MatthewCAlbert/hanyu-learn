## Why this combination
没 is the negator that pairs specifically with 有 (to have, to exist), against
不 which negates everything else. So 没有 is just "not have" / "not exist,"
and by extension the marker that negates a completed action (他没有来 — he
didn't come).

## Notes
不 negates states and habits and the future; 没(有) negates possession,
existence, and past completion. Choosing between them is one of the first real
grammar decisions in Mandarin.

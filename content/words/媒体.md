## Why this combination
媒 "a go-between, a matchmaker" — extended in modern usage to "a medium,
an intermediary channel" — combined with 体 "a body, a form, a medium
[of expression]" (as in 字体 "typeface, script form"): a form that
carries and conveys [information] — a modern coinage for the mass media,
adapted from the sense of a channel that carries between two parties.

## Notes
媒体 refers collectively to news outlets and broadcasting (媒体报道
"media coverage," 社交媒体 "social media"); 媒 by itself, outside this
compound, is now mostly encountered in 媒人 "matchmaker," its older sense.

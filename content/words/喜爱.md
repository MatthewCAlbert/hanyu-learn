## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 喜 ("to be pleased with, to like," as in 喜欢)
and 爱 ("to love," as in 爱情) both independently name a positive fondness
for something. Pairing them gives a slightly more formal word for
liking/loving.

## Notes

喜爱 is more written/formal than 喜欢 and can take abstract or collective
objects more readily (深受观众喜爱, "much loved by the audience").

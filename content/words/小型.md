## Why this combination

小 ("small") plus 型 ("a type, a model," as in 类型) names the small-size
category of a type of thing: small-scale.

## Notes

小型 modifies countable classes of things with size variants (小型汽车,
"a compact car"; 小型企业, "a small business") — 大型 ("large-scale") is
its direct antonym, built on the same 型 root.

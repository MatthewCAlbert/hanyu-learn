## Why this combination

发 ("to emit, to issue," as in 发生, "to occur") takes 烧 ("to burn, a
fever," as in 燃烧) as its object: to give off feverish heat — to run a
fever.

## Notes

发烧友 ("an enthusiast, a fan," lit. "fever friend") is a playful
extension using 发烧's "burning up" image for burning enthusiasm — a
different, informal sense from the plain medical one.

## Why this combination
回 ("to return") plus 复 (here in its "to return to a former state" sense,
as in 恢复, "to recover/restore") names sending something back — a
message returned to its sender, or a state returned to how it was.

Rated "semi" for 复's "to return to a prior state" sense, distinct from
its role as a prefix meaning "again, repeated" (复习, 重复).

## Notes
回复 covers both replying to a message (回复邮件, "to reply to an email")
and recovering a former state (回复原状, "to return to the original
condition") — two branches of the same "returning" core.

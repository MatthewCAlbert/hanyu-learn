## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 降 ("to descend," covered elsewhere in this app)
and 低 ("low") both independently name downward movement. Pairing them
gives the standard word for actively lowering a rate, cost, or level.

## Notes
降低成本 ("to lower costs") and 降低标准 ("to lower a standard") both build
on this pair's core "bringing something down" sense — 降低 is an active
verb (something is lowered), distinct from 下降 (covered elsewhere in
this app), which more often describes something declining on its own.

## Why this combination
忍 "to endure, to bear" (see 忍's own etymology) with the negative
potential complement 不住 "unable to hold steady" gives "unable to hold
back one's restraint any longer" — the everyday phrase for an
irresistible urge.

## Notes
忍不住 always precedes the action one couldn't resist: 忍不住笑了 "couldn't
help laughing" — its positive counterpart 忍得住 "can manage to hold
back" is used far less often.

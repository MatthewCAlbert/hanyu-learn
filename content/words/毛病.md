## Why this combination
毛, literally "fur, hair," combined with 病 "illness, defect" (as in
生病 "to fall ill"): an illness affecting even the fur/coat — an old
expression from judging livestock by their coat, extended to any small
defect or flaw, whether in an object, a habit, or a machine.

## Notes
毛病 covers mechanical faults (车有点毛病 "the car has a bit of a
problem"), personal bad habits (他有个坏毛病 "he has a bad habit"), and
minor health complaints alike (老毛病 "an old recurring ailment") — a
broad, colloquial word for anything not quite right.

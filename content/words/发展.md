## Why this combination
发 in its extended "to emerge, to arise" sense (as in 发生 elsewhere in
this dataset) and 展 "to unfold, to spread out" combine directly:
something emerging and then unfolding further — to develop.

## Notes
发展 covers both economic/social development (经济发展) and personal
growth (个人发展) — the same "emerging and unfolding" logic scales from
nations to individuals.

## Why this combination
领 "to lead, to guide" (originally "collar/neck," extended to "to lead
by the collar/at the head") combined with 导 "to guide, to direct" (as in
指导 "to direct, to guide"): to lead by guiding — the standard verb for
leadership, and, as a noun, the leader who does it.

## Notes
领导 works as verb ("领导一个团队" "to lead a team") and, very commonly in
everyday speech, as a noun for one's boss or superior ("我们领导" "our
boss/leader") — a workplace-specific extension English "leader" makes
less automatically.

## Why this combination
郊 "the outskirts, land beyond a city's walls" plus 区 "district, area"
gives exactly "the outskirts district" — the standard word for the area
surrounding a city center.

## Notes
郊区 anchors 住在郊区 "to live in the suburbs," 城郊 "city and suburbs" —
the outer, less-developed ring around 市区 "the urban core."

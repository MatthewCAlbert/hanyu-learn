## Why this combination
真 "true" and 诚 "sincere" are near-synonyms combined into the standard
word for genuine sincerity.

## Notes
真诚 describes sincere feeling and conduct (真诚的道歉 "a sincere apology,"
真诚相待 "to treat [someone] with genuine sincerity") — the opposite of
虚伪 "hypocritical, false."

## Why this combination
女 "female" + 朋友 "friend." The romantic reading is fixed by convention: 女朋友
means a partner, not simply a friend who is female — for that you must say
女性朋友 or 女的朋友. 男朋友 works the same way.

## Notes
交女朋友 "get a girlfriend." The ambiguity trap is real: 我女朋友 is
unambiguously "my girlfriend."

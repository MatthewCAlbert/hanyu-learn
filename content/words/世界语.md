## Why this combination
世界 "world" + 语 "language": the Chinese name for Esperanto, a calque of the
idea "an international language," not a phonetic of Esperanto.

## Notes
Transparent as a compound, opaque as a pointer to Zamenhof's language — you
still have to learn that it means Esperanto.

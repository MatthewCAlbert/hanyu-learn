## Why this combination
电子 "electron, electronic" (as in 电子产品 "electronic products")
combined with 邮件 "mail" (see its own entry): electronic mail — a
calque translating "email" component by component.

## Notes
电子邮件 is the full, formal term for email (发电子邮件 "to send an
email") — in everyday speech this is almost always shortened to just
邮件 or the loanword-flavoured 伊妹儿 (a playful, now-dated
transliteration), following the same compression pattern seen in 空调,
科技 and other long compounds.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 登 ("to ascend, and by extension to register,"
as in 登记, "to register") and 录 ("to record," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 录取, "to admit/enroll") both independently name entering
something into a formal record. Pairing them gives the modern computing
word for logging in.

Rated "semi" for the shift from "formally register" to the specific
modern "log in to an account" sense.

## Notes

登录账号 ("to log in to an account") repurposed an older formal-
registration word for computing — 注册 ("to register [an account]") is
a close near-synonym for the one-time sign-up, while 登录 covers each
subsequent login.

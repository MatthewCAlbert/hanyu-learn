## Why this combination
研 literally means "to grind, to grind fine" (as in grinding ink or
pigment), extended to "to refine one's understanding of" something; 究
means "to investigate to the very end, to get to the bottom of" (as in 究竟,
追究). Grinding something down finely and investigating it to the bottom are
two complementary images of thorough intellectual work.

"Semi" because 研's "grind" origin is opaque without it being pointed out —
nothing about modern 研究 suggests physical grinding unless you know that
history.

## Notes
研究 covers both formal, academic research (科学研究) and the more everyday
sense "to look into, to think over carefully" (我们研究一下这个办法) — context
decides which register is meant.

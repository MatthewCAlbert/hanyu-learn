## Why this combination
放 "to let go, to release" and 弃 "to discard, to throw away" both push in
the same direction: letting go of something and casting it aside. Together
they give the everyday "to give up."

## Notes
放弃 takes concrete objects (放弃机会 "to give up an opportunity"), plans
(放弃计划), and even people (被父母放弃 "abandoned by one's parents") — the
same "let go and discard" logic across very different stakes.

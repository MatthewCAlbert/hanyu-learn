## Why this combination
歌 "song" and 曲 "tune, melody" (the *qǔ* reading of 曲, seen in this app's
earlier entry on that character) are near-synonyms combined into the
formal, general word for a song.

## Notes
歌曲 is the neutral, written-register word; everyday speech about a
specific song more often just says 歌 (唱歌 "to sing," 一首歌 "a song").

## Why this combination
银 "silver" (the old money metal) + 行 (*háng*) "a trade, a firm, a business
house" (as in 商行, 同行). A "silver house" — a firm dealing in money. Coined in
the 19th century for the Western bank.

## Notes
行 is *háng* here, not *xíng* "to go" — a real reading trap. 银行卡 "bank card,"
去银行 "go to the bank."

## Why this combination
蔬 "vegetable" (see 蔬's own etymology) and 菜 "vegetable, dish" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard formal word for vegetables.

## Notes
蔬菜 is the general umbrella term (新鲜蔬菜 "fresh vegetables"); 菜 alone
is more flexible in everyday speech, also covering cooked dishes
generally (做菜 "to cook a dish") in a way 蔬菜 does not.

## Why this combination
常 "often, frequently" (see [[常常]], [[经常]], [[常见]]) + 用 "to use" → used
often, hence standard / everyday: 常用词 "common words," 常用工具, 常用药.

## Notes
常用 (in regular use) ≈ [[常见]] (commonly encountered), but 常用 stresses
being *used*, 常见 being *seen*.

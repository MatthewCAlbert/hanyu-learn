## Why this combination
客 "guest" and 户 "household" together name a household or party on the
other side of a business transaction — the "guest" a business serves.

## Notes
客户 is the business/commercial register for "customer" — 客户服务
"customer service," 客户经理 "account manager" — while 顾客 is the more
general, everyday word for a shopper or patron.

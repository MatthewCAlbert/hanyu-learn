## Why this combination
星 "star" doubled. Reduplicating a small, countable noun makes it the informal,
everyday word (cf. 点点 "dots"); 星星 is the colloquial "star" beside the
bookish bare 星. Also "a speck": 一星星火光.

## Notes
星 alone in [[星期]], 明星 (see [[明星]]), 火星 "Mars." 天上的星星 "the stars in
the sky."

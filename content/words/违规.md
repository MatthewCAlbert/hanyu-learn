## Why this combination
违 "to violate, to go against" acting on the object 规 "rule" gives
exactly "to violate a rule" — splittable, as in 违了规 "broke the rules."

## Notes
违规 is the general term for rule-breaking short of a formal legal
offense: 违规操作 "improper operation," 违规停车 "illegal parking" —
milder in register than 违法 "to break the law."

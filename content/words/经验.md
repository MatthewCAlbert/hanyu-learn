## Why this combination
经 "to pass through, to undergo" (see also 经历) combined with 验 "to
test, to verify, to examine" (as in 考验 "to put to the test"): what is
verified by having gone through it — practical experience, the know-how
gained rather than the events themselves.

## Notes
经验 names accumulated practical know-how (工作经验 "work experience,"
有经验 "to be experienced"); 经历, its close relative, names the actual
events lived through rather than the skill extracted from them.

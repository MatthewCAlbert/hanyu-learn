## Why this combination
日, the clipped country morpheme of [[日本]], + 文, parallel to [[英文]]. 日- is a
productive country-prefix (日语, 日元), not a one-off clipping. The spoken
counterpart 日语 is an HSK word.

## Notes
日 here is the country-abbreviation, not "sun" as in 日子.

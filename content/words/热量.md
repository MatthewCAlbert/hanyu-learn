## Why this combination
热 "heat" plus 量 "quantity, amount" gives exactly "the amount of heat" —
the standard physics/nutrition term for measured thermal energy.

## Notes
热量 anchors 食物的热量 "the calories in food," 释放热量 "to release
heat" — the technical measurement, distinct from 热 alone, which is the
general quality "hot/heat."

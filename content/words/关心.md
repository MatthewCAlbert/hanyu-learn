## Why this combination
关 in its sense "to involve, to connect, to bear on" (关系, 相关) + 心 "heart" as
its object — "to attach one's heart (to)." To hold something or someone in one's
thoughts and wish them well: 关心国家大事, 谢谢你的关心.

## Notes
关 "shut" (关门) and 关 "concern / involve" are the same character, different
senses. Compare [[小心]] (careful) and [[放心]] (reassured) — the same 心-object
pattern.

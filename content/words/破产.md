## Why this combination

破 ("to break, to destroy," as in 破坏) takes 产 ("property, assets," as
in 财产) as its object: one's assets broken/wiped out — bankruptcy.

## Notes

破产 covers both the literal financial sense (公司破产了, "the company
went bankrupt") and a figurative one (计划破产, "the plan fell through/
collapsed"), paralleling how English "bankrupt" also extends
metaphorically.

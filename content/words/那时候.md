## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + 时候 "time, moment" (see [[时候]], [[有时候]]) → "at
that point in time," pointing back to a period already in view: 那时候还没有
手机.

## Notes
那时候 / [[那时]] / [[那会儿]] run colloquial → written → very colloquial.
Mirror of [[这时候]].

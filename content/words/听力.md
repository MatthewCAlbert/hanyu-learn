## Why this combination
听 "to listen" combined with 力 "ability, power" (see also 能力, 力量):
the power of listening — hearing ability, or listening comprehension in
language study.

## Notes
听力 names both physical hearing ability (听力下降 "hearing loss") and,
very commonly for language learners, the listening-comprehension skill
and its test section (听力考试 "listening test") — the two senses share
the same underlying "capacity to process what is heard."

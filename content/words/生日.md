## Why this combination
生 "to be born" + 日 "day" → the day one was born. Fully transparent. 过生日
"celebrate a birthday," 生日快乐 "happy birthday."

## Notes
日 "day" also in 节日 (festival), 日期 (date). The formal / literary word for
one's birthday is 诞辰, used of eminent figures.

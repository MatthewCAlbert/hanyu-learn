## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Malta (馬耳他). 马, 耳 and 他 are chosen for
sound. 马 is not "horse"; 耳 is not "ear."

## Notes
Same stem in [[马耳他语]]. 马 as a dummy also in [[马来西亚]], [[马里]].

## Why this combination
不 "not" + [[一会儿]] "a short while" → "not (even) a little while," i.e. very
soon after: 不一会儿，他就回来了 "before long, he was back."

## Notes
Always narrates a past sequence ("… and in no time …"). Related: 一会儿 "a
moment (from now / then)," 过了一会儿 "after a while."

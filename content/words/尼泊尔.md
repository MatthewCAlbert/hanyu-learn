## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Nepal (尼泊爾). 泊 is *bó* in this name,
not *pō* "lake." 尼 is not a nun.

## Notes
Same stem in [[尼泊尔语]]. Contrast 湖泊 *pō*.

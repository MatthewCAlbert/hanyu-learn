## Why this combination
震 "to shake" (see 震's own etymology) and 惊 "to startle, to be
alarmed" are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for shocking
astonishment.

## Notes
震惊 describes a stronger, more formal shock than everyday 吃惊 "to be
surprised": 震惊世界 "to shock the world," 令人震惊的消息 "shocking news."

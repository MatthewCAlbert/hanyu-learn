## Why this combination
环 "a ring, a link [in a chain]" and 节 "a joint, a segment" both name a
single connected unit within a longer structure — originally a
zoological term for one segment of a worm or centipede's body, extended
figuratively to any one stage or component in a larger process.

## Notes
环节 is the standard word for "a step/component in a process": 关键环节
"a crucial stage," 生产环节 "a stage of production" — always implies the
part belongs to a connected sequence, not an isolated piece.

## Why this combination
一 "one, whole" + 共 "together, in common, sum" (共同, 总共) → "all told as one."
Introduces a total: 一共多少钱？ 一共来了二十个人.

## Notes
一共 asks for or states a sum. 共 alone or 总共 are near-synonyms; 一起 (see
[[一起]]) is "together (in doing)," a different job for 一 + X.

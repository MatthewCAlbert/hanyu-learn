## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Czech. 捷 and 克 are chosen for sound. 捷 is
not "swift." Short for 捷克共和国.

## Notes
Same stem in [[捷克语]]. 克 as in [[乌克兰]], [[伊拉克]].

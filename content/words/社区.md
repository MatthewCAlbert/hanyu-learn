## Why this combination
社 (originally a local earth-god shrine around which a community
gathered, now generalised to "society, group") and 区 "area, district"
combine directly: the area a community is grouped around.

## Notes
社区 is the modern, formal word for a residential community (社区服务
"community service," 社区中心 "community centre") — a 20th-century
coinage translating the sociological concept.

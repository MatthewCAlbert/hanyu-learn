## Why this combination
终 "end, to the end of" applied to 身 (here "one's life") gives exactly
"to the end of one's life" — the standard word for something lasting a
lifetime.

## Notes
终身 anchors 终身职业 "a lifelong career," 终身大事 "a major lifetime
event" (idiomatically, marriage) — always about the whole span of a life,
distinct from 终生, a near-synonym with a slightly more literary register.

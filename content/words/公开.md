## Why this combination
公 "public, communal, shared" (as in 公司 "company," literally "public
management") combined with 开 "to open": opened up to the public — not
hidden or private.

## Notes
公开 works as adjective ("公开的秘密" "an open secret") and verb ("公开
道歉" "to apologise publicly," "公开信息" "to make information public")
— its opposite is 秘密 "secret" or 私下 "privately."

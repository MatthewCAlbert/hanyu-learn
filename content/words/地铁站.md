## Why this combination
[[地铁]] "underground railway" + 站 "station, stop" (see [[车站]], [[网站]]) →
a stop on the metro. Parse 地铁 + 站.

## Notes
站 "-station / stop" also in [[车站]], 火车站, 加油站, 公交站.

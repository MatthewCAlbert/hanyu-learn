## Why this combination
建 "to construct, to build" (see also 建立, 建设) combined with the
resultative complement 成 "to complete, to become" (see also 成功, 完
成): to construct to completion — to finish building.

## Notes
建成 marks that construction was completed successfully (大桥建成了
"the bridge has been completed") — the resultative 成 distinguishes a
finished result from bare 建 or 建设, which name the ongoing act of
building without confirming completion.

## Why this combination
耐 "to endure, to withstand" (see 耐's own etymology — enduring a lesser
legal penalty) and 心 "heart, mind" combine directly: a mind capable of
enduring without giving in to frustration.

## Notes
耐心 works as adjective ("有耐心" "to be patient") and noun ("patience,"
耐心等待 "to wait patiently") — the standard everyday word for patience.

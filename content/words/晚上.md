## Why this combination
晚 "late (in the day)" plus 上 used as a bare time-noun suffix, the same 上 as
in 早上 (morning) and 路上 (on the way) — it does not mean "up" here. So
"the late part of the day."

## Notes
Covers everything from dusk to bedtime. 夜里 / 夜晚 are the more literary "night";
半夜 is the middle of it.

## Why this combination
提 "to lift, to raise, to bring up" + directional 出 "out" — bringing something
out into the open for others: 提出问题, 提出建议, 提出要求.

## Notes
提 "raise / mention" also in [[提到]], [[提高]], 提醒. 出 as "out into view" also
in [[提出]]'s neighbours 拿出, [[得出]].

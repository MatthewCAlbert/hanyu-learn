## Why this combination
没 "not have" + 事儿 "matter, business, trouble." Two everyday uses: reassurance
("no harm done" — a reply to sorry or thanks, near [[没关系]]) and availability
("我今天没事儿" — I'm free today, nothing on).

## Notes
有事儿 is the flip side: "something's come up" / "I'm busy." 没事儿找事儿
"make trouble out of nothing."

## Why this combination
将 (here a future-tense adverb, "about to") and 要 (here also "going to,
will") are near-synonyms combined into a formal near-future marker.

## Notes
将要 is a written/formal adverb placed before a verb: 将要发生的变化
"the changes about to happen" — interchangeable in most contexts with the
shorter 将 or 要 alone, but common in news and formal writing for extra
weight.

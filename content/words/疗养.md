## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 疗 ("to treat, to heal," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 治疗) and 养 ("to nurture, to rest and recover,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 养成) both independently name
restoring health. Pairing them gives the standard word for extended
recuperation.

## Notes

疗养院 ("a sanatorium, a convalescent home") is the standard institution
built on this word — 疗养 implies an extended period of rest and
recovery, distinct from 治疗, the active medical treatment itself.

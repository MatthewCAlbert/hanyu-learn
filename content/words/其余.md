## Why this combination

其 ("its, that," a classical possessive/demonstrative) plus 余
("surplus, remainder," covered elsewhere in this app as one of the
characters where simplification merged two distinct traditional
characters, 余 and 餘, into one shape) names what is left over of a
group: the rest, the remainder.

Rated "semi" for 其's classical function here, less transparent to a
reader who only knows it as part of set phrases like 其他/其中.

## Notes

其余 sits alongside 其他 and 其中 as one of several "其-" words for "the
rest/others/among them" — 其余 leans toward "everything that remains" as
a leftover set, while 其他 is the more general "other(s)."

## Why this combination
般 "a sort, a kind, a manner" + 一 "one" → "of one and the same sort," hence
"general / typical" (一般来说 "generally speaking") and, of quality, "just
average / nothing special" (成绩一般). Also simile: 像…一般 "just like…".

## Notes
The dismissive "so-so" sense — 这部电影一般 — is a polite way to say "not great."

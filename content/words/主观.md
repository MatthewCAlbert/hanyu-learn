## Why this combination
主 (here "the subject, the self," as opposed to the observed object) plus
观 "view, perspective" gives exactly "the subject's own view" — the
standard philosophical term for perspective shaped by personal bias,
opposite of 客观 "objective."

## Notes
主观 anchors 主观意见 "a subjective opinion," 主观判断 "subjective
judgment" — always implies a view colored by personal feeling rather than
grounded in external, verifiable fact.

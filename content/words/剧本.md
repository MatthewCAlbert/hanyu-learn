## Why this combination
剧 "drama, play" and 本 "book, a bound text" combine directly: the
written text a drama is performed from.

## Notes
剧本 anchors 电影剧本 "film screenplay," 编剧 "screenwriter" (shares 剧) —
the standard term across theatre, film, and TV for the underlying written
script.

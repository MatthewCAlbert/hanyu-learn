## Why this combination

缩 ("to shrink, to contract," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
缩小) plus 短 ("short") names making something shorter by shrinking it.

## Notes

缩短时间 ("to shorten the time") and 缩短距离 ("to shorten the distance")
both build on this word — 缩短 always applies to length or duration,
the direct partner of 缩小 (covered elsewhere in this app), which shrinks
size or scope more generally.

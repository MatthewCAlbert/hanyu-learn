## Why this combination
试 "to try, to attempt, to test" (as in 考试 "exam," 试试 "to give [it]
a try") combined with 验 "to test, to verify" (see also 实验, 经验): to
try and verify — a trial or test.

## Notes
试验 leans towards trial-and-error testing in engineering, agriculture
or product development (试验田 "experimental field," 新药试验 "drug
trial") — 实验, its near-homophone in meaning, is reserved for formal
scientific experiments following established method.

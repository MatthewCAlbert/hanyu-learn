## Why this combination
做 "to do" + resultative 到 "reach" — the doing got there, it was pulled off.
说到做到 "do what you say," 做不到 "can't manage it," 做到最好 "do it as well as
possible."

## Notes
到 as the "success" complement also on [[想到]], [[得到]], [[找到]]. 做 alone:
[[做法]], [[做饭]], 做梦.

## Why this combination
查 "to check, to examine" is transparent. 调 here is not its common "to
adjust, to transfer" sense but a related extension, "to look into, to
inquire" — the same shift seen in 调研 "to research through field
inquiry." Together: to check into something through active inquiry.

## Notes
调查 is the general word for both formal investigation (警方调查 "police
investigation") and survey-style research (市场调查 "market research") —
the method (asking, checking) unifying otherwise different contexts.

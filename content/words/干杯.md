## Why this combination
干 (*gān*) "dry, to empty" + 杯 "cup" as its object — "empty the cup." At a
Chinese banquet 干杯 literally means draining the glass in one; 随意 "as you
please" lets you sip. Verb-object: 干了这杯.

## Notes
干 is *gān* here (dry / empty), as in [[干净]], not *gàn* "to do." 碰杯
"clink glasses"; 敬酒 "propose a toast to someone."

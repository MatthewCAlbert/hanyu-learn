## Why this combination
团 "a group, to gather, to unite" (see also 团结) combined with 体
"body, entity" (see also 具体, 身体): a united body of people — an
organised group.

## Notes
团体 names a formally organised group or association (团体活动 "group
activity," 社会团体 "social organisation") — it stresses organisation and
formal structure, distinct from 群, a mere gathering or crowd with no
implied organisation.

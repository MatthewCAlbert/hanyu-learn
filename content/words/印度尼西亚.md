## Why this combination
A phonetic rendering of Indonesia: 印度 as in [[印度]], plus 尼西亚 for -nesia. The
characters are chosen for sound; this is not "India + Buddhist nun + west +
Asia" as a semantic story.

## Notes
Everyday short form [[印尼]]. Same stem in [[印尼语]], [[印尼文]].

## Why this combination

虫 ("an insect, a worm") plus the nominal suffix 子 names the everyday
small creature: a bug.

## Notes

虫子 is the everyday casual word for any small crawling creature; more
specific words like 蚂蚁 (ant) and 蝴蝶 (butterfly) all carry the 虫-adjacent
insect radical family, though not this exact suffix pattern.

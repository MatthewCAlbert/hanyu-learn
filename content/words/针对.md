## Why this combination
针 ("needle," covered in this app's hanzi entry) plus 对 ("facing,
directed toward") pictures a needle's point aimed precisely at something —
exactly what "targeted, aimed at" means, whether the target is a problem,
a policy, or an audience.

Rated "semi" because the compound leans on 针's figurative "precise point"
sense rather than its literal sewing-needle meaning.

## Notes
针对 typically opens a clause naming the specific problem or group being
addressed (针对这个问题, "with respect to this problem") — it functions
much like a preposition, similar to 关于 and 对于 but with a sharper,
more deliberate "targeted at" sense.

## Why this combination
球 "ball, ball sport" (as in 篮球, 足球) combined with 迷 "one captivated,
enthralled, a fan" (as in 迷路 in an unrelated sense "to lose one's way,"
extended here to being "lost" in enthusiasm for something): someone
captivated by ball sports — a fan.

## Notes
球迷 names an enthusiastic fan of football, basketball or another ball
sport (足球迷 "football fan") — 迷, alone, is productive as a suffix for
"fan of X" across many domains (歌迷 "music fan," 影迷 "movie fan"), not
only sports.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 超 "to leap over, to surpass" + 过 "to pass, to exceed." Going
past a mark, a rival, or a limit: 超过一百人, 超过限速, 从右边超过 "overtake on
the right."

## Notes
超 as "surpass / super-" also in [[超市]], 超级, 超人. 过 "exceed" in [[不过]],
过分.

## Why this combination
功, here "effort, work put in" (see also 功夫, 成功) combined with 课
"class, lesson" (see also 课程, 课文): the work put into one's lessons —
schoolwork, homework.

## Notes
功课 is a somewhat dated or regional word for schoolwork/homework
(做功课 "to do homework/schoolwork") — 作业, the more standard modern
term, is used more widely for "homework" specifically; 功课 has also
picked up a figurative extension, "to do one's homework/research"
(提前做功课 "to research in advance") before an event.

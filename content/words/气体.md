## Why this combination
气 "gas, air" plus 体 (here "a form of matter," as in 固体 "solid" and
液体 "liquid") gives exactly "matter in gaseous form" — the technical
term completing the solid/liquid/gas triad.

## Notes
气体 is the scientific register word for a gas as a state of matter
(气体分子 "gas molecules"), contrasting with the everyday 空气 "air" (the
specific gas mixture we breathe).

## Why this combination
学 "study, school" + 期 "a period, a set span" (see [[星期]], [[日期]],
[[假期]]) → a division of the school year: 上学期 / 下学期, 新学期.

## Notes
期 "-period" also in [[假期]], [[时期]], 学期 vs. [[开学]] "term begins" and
放假 "term ends."

## Why this combination
衬, here "to line, to lie beneath [an outer layer]" (as in 衬衣, an
alternate name for the same garment) combined with 衫 "a shirt, an
upper garment" (whose 衤 radical marks it as clothing): the garment worn
beneath [a jacket] — a shirt.

## Notes
衬衫 is the standard word for a button-front shirt or blouse (白衬衫
"white shirt") — 衫, alone, appears in other garment compounds too
(T恤衫 "T-shirt," a partial loan-plus-native-morpheme compound).

## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Canada. 加, 拿 and 大 are chosen for
sound, not for "add + take + big."

## Notes
加 as a phonetic also writes 加纳 (Ghana) and 新加坡 (Singapore) — different
foreign names, same convenient syllable.

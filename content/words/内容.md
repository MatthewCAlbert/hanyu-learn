## Why this combination
内 "inside, inner" combined with 容 "to contain, to hold" (as in 容易 in
an unrelated sense, and 容量 "capacity"): what is contained within —
the substance of a text, speech or work, as opposed to its outward form.

## Notes
内容 pairs naturally with 形式 "form" as the classic content/form
contrast (内容和形式 "content and form"); it names what something is
about or made of, never its container or outward shape.

## Why this combination
白 "white" + the "-colour" suffix 色 (see [[颜色]], [[红色]]). 白 alone carries
extra senses — "blank / plain" (白纸), "in vain" (白跑一趟), "colloquial" (白话)
— none of which 白色 has; the compound is just the colour.

## Notes
Political 白 (白色恐怖 "White Terror") is the opposite of revolutionary 红.
The colour word itself is neutral.

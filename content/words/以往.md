## Why this combination
以 (a classical preposition, "from, by means of") and 往 "to go toward,
the past" combine into "from [here], looking back" — a formal written
equivalent of everyday 过去.

## Notes
以往 introduces a contrast with the present: 与以往不同 "different from
before," 一如以往 "just like always" — a written-register alternative to
过去 in formal contexts.

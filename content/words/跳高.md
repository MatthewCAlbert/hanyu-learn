## Why this combination
A verb-object-like compound: 跳 "to jump" (as in 跳舞) combined with 高
"high" (as in 高中, describing the result/goal of the jump): jumping for
height — the high jump.

## Notes
跳高 names the track-and-field event (练习跳高 "to practice the high
jump") and pairs with 跳远 "long jump," its close relative built the same
way with 远 "far" in place of 高 "high."

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 设 ("to set up, to establish," as in 设计, "to
design") and 置 ("to place, to set," covered elsewhere in this app) both
independently name putting something in place. Pairing them gives the
standard word for setting something up.

## Notes
设置 covers both establishing an institution (设置部门, "to set up a
department") and, in technology, configuration options (系统设置,
"system settings") — the same "putting things in their place" idea
applied to organizations and to software.

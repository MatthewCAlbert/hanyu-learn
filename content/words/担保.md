## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 担 ("to shoulder, to take on a burden," as in
担心) and 保 ("to protect, to guarantee," as in 保证) both independently
name taking responsibility for something. Pairing them gives the
standard word for a guarantee.

## Notes

担保人 ("guarantor") and 担保贷款 ("secured loan") are the standard legal/
financial compounds built on this word — 担保 always implies taking on
liability if things go wrong.

## Why this combination
合 "to match, to conform to, to fit" (see also 适合) combined with 理
"reasoning, logic, principle" (see also 理由, 理论): conforming to
reason — reasonable, rational.

## Notes
合理 judges a plan, price or explanation as sound and justifiable (合理
的价格 "a reasonable price," 合理安排时间 "to plan time sensibly") — its
opposite is 不合理 "unreasonable," and it should not be confused with
合适 "suitable, fitting," which judges fit rather than soundness of
reasoning.

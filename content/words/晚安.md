## Why this combination
晚 "evening, night" + 安 "peace, safety" → "(may your) night be at peace" — a
wish, not a description. Parallel to 早安 "good morning" and the older 请安
"pay one's respects."

## Notes
Said only on parting for the night / before sleep, unlike English "good
evening," which is also a greeting on arrival — that is 晚上好.

## Why this combination
愿 "to wish, to be willing" (as in 愿意 "to be willing") combined with
望 "to hope for, to gaze towards [in longing]" (as in 希望 "to hope"):
to wish and gaze towards — a heartfelt wish.

## Notes
愿望 names a deeply held wish or aspiration (实现愿望 "to fulfil a wish,"
美好的愿望 "a beautiful wish") — 希望, as a noun, is the more general
word for "hope" and does not always carry 愿望's sense of a specific,
personally cherished desire.

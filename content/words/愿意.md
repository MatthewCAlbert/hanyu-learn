## Why this combination
愿 "to wish, to be willing" (心愿, 情愿) + 意 "intention, mind" (意思, 意志). A
near-synonym pair naming inner assent — being of a mind to do something:
我愿意帮忙, 不愿意去.

## Notes
愿意 = willingness (a disposition); 想 = want to (an impulse); 要 = will / must.
愿 alone in 但愿, 祝愿.

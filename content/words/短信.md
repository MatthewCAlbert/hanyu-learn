## Why this combination
短 "short" + 信 "message, letter" (see [[信息]], [[信号]], [[信心]]) → a short
written message sent by phone. 发短信 "send a text," 收到一条短信.

## Notes
短信 is the SMS sense; instant-messaging texts (on WeChat etc.) are 消息 or
微信. 信 "letter" alone is a posted letter. 彩信 "MMS" (with 彩 "colour").

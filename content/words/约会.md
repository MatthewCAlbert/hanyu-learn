## Why this combination
约 carries its "to arrange, to agree upon [in advance]" sense (as in
大约, "approximately," a related but distinct extension), and 会 is "to
meet." Arranging in advance to meet is exactly an appointment — or,
colloquially, a romantic date.

Rated "semi" for 约's "to arrange/agree" sense here, one specific branch
of the character's range.

## Notes
约会 covers both a general appointment (医生约会, "a doctor's
appointment," though 预约 is more standard for this) and, very
commonly today, a romantic date — context decides which is meant.

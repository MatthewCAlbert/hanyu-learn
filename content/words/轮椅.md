## Why this combination

轮 ("a wheel," as in 轮流, "to take turns [lit. wheel + flow]") plus 椅
("a chair," as in 椅子) names a chair fitted with wheels: a wheelchair.

## Notes

坐轮椅 ("to sit in/use a wheelchair") is the standard phrase — a
straightforward descriptive compound naming the object by its two
defining parts.

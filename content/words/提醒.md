## Why this combination
提 ("to raise, to bring up," as in 提供, 提出) plus 醒 ("to wake up,"
covered in this app's hanzi entry — originally "to sober up") names
bringing something back up into someone's clouded or forgetful awareness
— waking their attention to it, exactly what "to remind" means.

## Notes
提醒 takes both the person reminded and the thing they're reminded of:
提醒我带伞 ("remind me to bring an umbrella") — no preposition is needed
between the two, unlike English "remind [someone] *about*."

## Why this combination
包 is "to wrap" (as in 包裹, 面包), and 括 is "to enclose, to bracket" (as in
括号, brackets). Wrapping something and enclosing it within a boundary is a
vivid, near-literal picture of "including" it within a larger set.

## Notes
包括 typically introduces a list that fits inside a larger stated group
(参加的人包括老师和学生 "those attending include teachers and students") — the
larger group is usually named just before it.

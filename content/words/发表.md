## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to issue, to emit" (as in 发展 "to develop," literally
"to send out and grow") combined with 表 "to display, to make known" (see
also 表达, 表明): to send something out into public display — to
publish, or to make a view publicly known.

## Notes
发表 is used for publishing written work (发表文章 "to publish an
article") and for voicing an opinion publicly (发表意见 "to voice an
opinion") — 出版, a near-synonym, is limited to the publication of books
and is not used for expressing an opinion.

## Why this combination
猜 "to guess" (see 猜's own etymology) and 测 "to measure, to estimate"
are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for informed guessing.

## Notes
猜测 favours reasoned speculation (根据线索猜测 "to conjecture based on
clues") over the playful guessing of 猜 alone in games (猜谜语 "to solve
a riddle").

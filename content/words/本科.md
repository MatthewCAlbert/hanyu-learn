## Why this combination

本 ("root, basic, main," as in 根本, "fundamental [lit. root + root]")
plus 科 ("a department, a branch of study," covered elsewhere in this
app as part of 内科) names the basic, foundational level of university
study: undergraduate.

Rated "semi" for 本's abstract "basic/foundational" contribution here.

## Notes

本科生 ("an undergraduate student") sits below 研究生 (covered elsewhere
in this app, "graduate student") in the academic hierarchy — 本科 names
the bachelor's-degree level specifically.

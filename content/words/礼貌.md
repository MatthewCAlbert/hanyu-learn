## Why this combination
礼 "ritual propriety, courtesy" plus 貌 (here "outward bearing," extended
from its base sense "appearance, face") gives "courteous outward
bearing" — the leap from "face/appearance" to "manner/demeanor" is a step
beyond the literal.

## Notes
礼貌 works as noun ("讲礼貌" "to be polite," 没礼貌 "rude") and adjective
("礼貌地问" "to ask politely") — the standard word for socially expected
courtesy in speech and behavior.

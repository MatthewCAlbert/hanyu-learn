## Why this combination

打 ("to do, to perform [an action]," a light verb as in 打电话) takes
雷 ("thunder") as its object: for thunder to occur.

## Notes

打雷了 ("it's thundering") and 打雷闪电 ("thunder and lightning," pairing
with 闪电, covered elsewhere in this app) both describe the same storm
event from its two most noticeable signs.

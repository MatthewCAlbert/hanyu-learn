## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 遇 ("to encounter," covered elsewhere in this
app) and 见 ("to see") both independently name crossing paths with
someone. Pairing them gives a common everyday word for an unplanned
meeting.

## Notes
遇见 implies a chance, unplanned encounter (在路上遇见他, "to run into him
on the road") — for a planned meeting arranged in advance, 见面 is the
more natural choice.

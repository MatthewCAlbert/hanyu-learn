## Why this combination
人 "person, human" and 间 "between, among, a space" combine into "the
space among humans" — the earthly, mortal world, traditionally
contrasted with heaven (天上) and the underworld.

## Notes
人间 carries a literary, sometimes mythic register: 天堂与人间 "heaven and
earth," 人间地狱 "hell on earth" — a poetic word for the mortal world, not
a neutral geographic term.

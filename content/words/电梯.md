## Why this combination

电 ("electric," as in 电脑) plus 梯 ("ladder, stairs," as in 楼梯,
"staircase") names a stairway driven by electricity: an elevator (or,
context permitting, an escalator).

## Notes

电梯 covers both a lift and an escalator; when the distinction matters,
自动扶梯 ("automatic supporting ladder") specifies the escalator.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 早 "early (in the day)" + 晨 "dawn, daybreak" (晨光, 清晨). The
first hours after waking — narrower than [[上午]] (which runs to noon) and
close to [[早上]].

## Notes
早晨 / [[早上]] are near-synonyms; 早晨 is a touch more written. 晨 barely occurs
alone: 清晨, 晨练 "morning exercise."

## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 得 "to obtain, to get" (as in 得到 "to obtain")
takes 分 "points, a mark" (as in 分数 "score") as its object —
literally "to obtain points" — to score.

## Notes
得分 is used in sports and exams alike (篮球得分 "to score in
basketball," 考试得分 "exam score") and, like other verb-object
compounds, splits for aspect (得了三分 "scored three points").

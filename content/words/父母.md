## Why this combination
父 "father" and 母 "mother" simply listed together — the most literal
kind of semantic compound, naming both parents as one collective unit.

## Notes
父母 is the neutral, formal collective for "parents"; 爸妈 (from 爸爸
and 妈妈) is the everyday spoken equivalent, following the same
formal/colloquial split as 父亲/爸爸 and 母亲/妈妈 individually.

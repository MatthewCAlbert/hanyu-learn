## Why this combination
对 carries its "facing, directed toward" sense (as in 对面, 对手), and 付
carries an extended "to deal with, to respond to" sense beyond its more
basic "to pay/hand over" (as in 付款). Facing something and responding to
it directly is exactly "to handle" it.

Rated "semi" for 付's extended "to respond to" sense here, one step
removed from its more concrete "to pay" meaning.

## Notes
对付 can carry a slightly negative or make-do connotation — 对付一下
("to make do, to get by") implies a less-than-ideal, stopgap response
rather than a full, satisfying solution.

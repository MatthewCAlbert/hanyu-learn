## Why this combination
平 "level, flat" and 坦 "flat, smooth" (see 坦's own etymology, "level
ground") are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for a flat,
unobstructed surface.

## Notes
平坦 anchors 平坦的道路 "a level road," 地势平坦 "the terrain is flat" —
always describes ground or terrain, distinct from smoothness of a
different kind (光滑 "slippery-smooth," describing texture).

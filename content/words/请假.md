## Why this combination
假 (*jià*) "leave, time off" as the object of 请 "to request" — to ask
permission to be absent. Splits: 请一天假 "take a day off," 请了病假 "took sick
leave."

## Notes
请假 is the individual asking; [[放假]] is the institution granting a holiday to
everyone. 假 is *jià* here, not *jiǎ* "false." 请 = "request" as in [[请问]],
请客.

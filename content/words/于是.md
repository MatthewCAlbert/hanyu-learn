## Why this combination
于 is the classical preposition "at, in" (also seen bound into 关于,
对于), and 是 here is not the modern copula "is" but its older classical
demonstrative sense, "this." "At this [point]" — right after the event
just described — is exactly the connective job "thereupon, and so" does,
linking one event to what followed from it.

Marked "idiom" because 是's demonstrative sense here is not the one modern
learners meet first, so the compound cannot be derived from 是's everyday
meaning alone.

## Notes
于是 links two sequential clauses within a narrative (他病了，于是我们去看他,
"he got sick, so we went to see him") — it marks sequence-with-consequence,
stronger than plain 然后 ("then," pure sequence with no implied causation).

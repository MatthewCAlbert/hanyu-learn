## Why this combination
城 "city [wall]" and 里 "inside" combine directly: inside the city — a
usage that goes back to when cities were literally walled enclosures.

## Notes
城里 contrasts with 乡下/农村 "the countryside" in everyday speech: 城里人
"city folk," 进城里 "to go into town" — a casual, spoken counterpart to
formal 市区 "urban area."

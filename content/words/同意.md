## Why this combination
同 "same, identical" combined with 意 "idea, intention, meaning": to hold
the same idea [as another] — to agree.

## Notes
同意 typically takes a clause or proposal as its object (我同意你的看法
"I agree with your view") and, in formal or bureaucratic use, can also
mean "to approve, to grant permission" (领导同意了 "the leader approved
it").

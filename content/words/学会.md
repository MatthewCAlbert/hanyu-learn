## Why this combination
学 "study" plus 会. With 会 "to be able to", 学会 is "to learn how": 学会游泳 "learn to swim". With 会 "association", the word is a noun for a scholarly society. Two words sharing a spelling.

## Notes
Context decides: a verb before a verb means "learn how", a noun means "society".

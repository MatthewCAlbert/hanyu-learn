## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 逐 ("one by one, in sequence," covered
elsewhere in this app — chasing down a fleeing pig, extended to
proceeding step by step) and 渐 ("gradually," also covered elsewhere —
originally a river's name, extended to slow incremental change) both
independently name a slow, step-by-step process. Pairing them gives the
standard everyday adverb for "gradually."

## Notes
逐渐 is the most common way to describe a slow, incremental process
(逐渐变化, "to change gradually") — 渐渐 (reduplicated 渐 alone) is an
equally common, slightly more colloquial alternative.

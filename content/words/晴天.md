## Why this combination
晴 "clear (of sky), fine, cloudless" + 天, here "sky / day" → a day with a
clear sky. The forecast scale runs 晴 → [[多云]] → 阴 ([[阴天]]) → 雨.
晴天霹雳 "a bolt from the blue."

## Notes
晴 "fine weather" also in 晴朗, 放晴 "clear up." Opposite 阴天 (overcast) and
雨天 (rainy day).

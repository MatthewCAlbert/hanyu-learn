## Why this combination
天 "sky, heaven" plus 文 (here "pattern, phenomena," an ancient sense as
in 天文 originally "the patterns of heaven," i.e. celestial phenomena)
gives exactly "the patterns of the sky" — the classical-origin word for
the study of celestial bodies.

## Notes
天文 anchors 天文学 "astronomy (the discipline)," 天文台 "observatory,"
天文数字 "an astronomical number (figuratively, a huge figure)" — the
standard word across scientific and everyday figurative use.

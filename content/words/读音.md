## Why this combination
读 "to read (aloud)" (see [[读书]]) + 音 "sound" (see [[声音]], [[音节]]) → how a
character sounds when read out. 这个字的读音是…, 标准读音.

## Notes
读音 = the pronunciation of a written form; 发音 = pronunciation as an act /
skill (发音准 "good pronunciation"); 拼音 = the romanised spelling of it.

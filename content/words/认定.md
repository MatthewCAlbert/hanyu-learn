## Why this combination
认 "to recognize, to acknowledge" and 定 "to settle, to fix" combine
directly: recognizing something and settling on it as fact.

## Notes
认定 covers firm personal conviction (他认定自己是对的 "he's convinced
he's right") and official determination (法院认定 "the court has
determined") — a stronger, more settled claim than plain 认为 "to think."

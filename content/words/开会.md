## Why this combination
会 "a meeting, a gathering" as the object of 开, the light verb "to hold / set
going" (cf. [[开玩笑]], 开party). Splits: 开了一上午会 "was in meetings all
morning," 开个短会.

## Notes
会 is *huì* here. The same 开 = "hold an event" in 开演唱会 "give a concert,"
开运动会 "hold a sports meet."

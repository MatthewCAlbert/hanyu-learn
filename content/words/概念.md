## Why this combination
概 "a general outline, the gist" (see also 大概) combined with 念 "to
think of, an idea held in mind" (as in 纪念, 想念): the general idea
held in mind — a concept.

## Notes
概念 names an abstract idea or category (基本概念 "basic concept," 没有
概念 "to have no idea/concept [of something]") — 想法, its neighbour, is
the lighter word for a specific, personal idea or opinion rather than a
formal conceptual category.

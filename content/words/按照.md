## Why this combination
按 "to press [down on], to follow [a plan]" combined with 照 "to shine
on, to take as a reference" (as in 照顾 "to look after," 参照 "to refer
to"): to follow, taking something as one's reference point — a
grammaticalized preposition for "according to."

## Notes
按照 introduces the rule, standard or plan being followed (按照规定
"in accordance with the regulations," 按照计划 "according to plan") and
is functionally interchangeable with bare 按 or 根据 in most contexts,
though 按照 is the fuller, more formal form.

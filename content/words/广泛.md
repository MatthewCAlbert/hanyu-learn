## Why this combination
广 "wide, broad" and 泛 (here "extensive, spread widely," related to its
base sense "to float") are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for something covering a wide range.

## Notes
广泛 modifies scope, not physical size: 广泛应用 "widely applied,"
广泛关注 "widespread attention" — always about range or reach, not the
literal width of an object (that would be 宽).

## Why this combination
明 "clear, bright, evident" (as in 显然) combined with 确 "certain, true,
definite" (as in 确实, 确保): clearly certain — explicit, unambiguous.

## Notes
明确 works as adjective ("明确的答案" "a clear-cut answer") and verb
("明确目标" "to clarify/pin down a goal") — it stresses precision and the
absence of ambiguity, stronger than plain 清楚 "clear, understandable."

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 关 ("to close," as in 关门) and 闭 ("closed,"
covered elsewhere in this app — a door with its bolt drawn) both
independently mean "to close." Pairing them intensifies plain 关 into a
more formal, thorough closing.

## Notes
关闭 covers both a literal closed door/window and, formally, shutting
down an institution or system (工厂关闭, "the factory shut down"; 关闭
账户, "to close an account") — a more decisive, complete closing than
casual 关.

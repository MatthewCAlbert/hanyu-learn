## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 没 "to not have" negates 用 "use" (as in 有用
"useful," its positive counterpart) — literally "to have no use" — of
no use, useless.

## Notes
没用 describes something (or, colloquially, someone) as of no
practical use (这个方法没用 "this method is useless," 说这些也没用
"saying this is pointless too") — its positive counterpart, replacing 没
with 有, is 有用 "useful."

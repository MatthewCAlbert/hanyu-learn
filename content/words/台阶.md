## Why this combination

台 ("a platform," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 阳台,
"balcony") plus 阶 ("a step, a stair," as in 阶段, "a stage/phase [lit.
step + section]") names the steps leading up to a platform: stairs,
steps.

## Notes

门口的台阶 ("the steps at the entrance") is the literal everyday sense;
figuratively, 给个台阶下 ("to give someone a way to save face, lit. give a
step down") uses the same image for a graceful exit from an awkward
situation.

## Why this combination
指 "to point at, to indicate" (as in 指出 "to point out") combined with
导 "to guide, to lead" (see also 领导, 导演): to point the way and guide
— to instruct or mentor.

## Notes
指导 names guidance given by someone with expertise (老师的指导
"the teacher's guidance," 指导老师 "supervising teacher/mentor") — 教,
the plain verb "to teach," does not carry the same sense of ongoing,
expert mentorship that 指导 implies.

## Why this combination
空 ("empty, hollow, the sky," as in 天空, 空气) and 间 ("a gap, a space
between," as in 房间, 之间) together name empty room between things — the
general physical concept of space.

Rated "semi" for 间's abstract "interval/gap" sense here, one step removed
from its more concrete "room" sense in 房间.

## Notes
空间 extends figuratively to "leeway, scope for action" (发展空间, "room to
grow/develop") — the same physical-room metaphor English "room" and
"space" use for non-physical possibility.

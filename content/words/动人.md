## Why this combination
A verb-object-like compound: 动 "to move, to affect [emotionally]" (see
also 感动, 自动) takes 人 "person" as its object — literally "to move a
person" — used adjectivally for anything emotionally moving.

## Notes
动人 describes a story, scene or performance as emotionally moving
(动人的故事 "a touching story," 动人的歌声 "a moving voice") — 感动, its
close relative, is the verb "to be moved" describing the person's own
reaction, while 动人 describes the moving thing itself.

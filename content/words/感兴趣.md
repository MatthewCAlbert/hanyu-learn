## Why this combination

感 ("to feel," as in 感觉) takes 兴趣 ("interest" — 兴 "excitement" plus
趣 "interest," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 乐趣) as its
object: feeling interest.

## Notes

对…感兴趣 ("to be interested in…") is the standard frame, always naming
the object of interest with 对 — 有兴趣 is a close near-synonym built
with 有 ("to have") instead of 感 ("to feel").

## Why this combination
寻 "to search for" and 求 "to seek, to request" are near-synonyms
combined into the formal word for actively seeking something.

## Notes
寻求 is formal/written, almost always paired with abstract goals: 寻求
帮助 "to seek help," 寻求解决方案 "to seek a solution" — everyday speech
prefers plain 找 for concrete searching.

## Why this combination
Same "the next" sense of 明 as in [[明天]], with the unit 年. The year row is
去年 / 今年 / 明年 (see [[今年]]) — 明 covers "next" on both the day and year
axes.

## Notes
后年 "the year after next," parallel to 后天.

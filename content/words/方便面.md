## Why this combination
[[方便]] "convenient" + 面 "noodles" (see [[面条儿]], [[面包]]) → noodles that
are convenient — pre-cooked and dried, ready after adding boiling water.
泡方便面 "make instant noodles."

## Notes
Regional names: 泡面 (Taiwan, "soak noodles"), 公仔面 / 快熟面 (Cantonese
regions), 速食面. 面 is *miàn* "noodles / flour," not "face."

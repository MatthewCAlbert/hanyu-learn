## Why this combination
只 "only" modifying 有 "to have, there is/are" — a plain adverb-plus-verb
combination that reads exactly as its parts suggest: the sole thing that
exists or holds true.

## Notes
只有...才... is a fixed correlative pattern ("only if... then...") that
learners should recognise as a set frame: 只有努力才能成功 "only through
effort can one succeed."

## Why this combination
绿 "green" + the "-colour" suffix 色 (see [[颜色]], [[红色]]). The modern
figurative use — 绿色食品 "organic food," 绿色能源 "clean energy" — borrows the
Western "green = environmental" metaphor.

## Notes
绿 alone in 绿茶 "green tea," 绿灯 "green light." 绿 is *lǜ* (some speakers
*lù*).

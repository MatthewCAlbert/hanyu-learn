## Why this combination
签 "to sign" and 订 "to conclude, to settle (an agreement)" are
near-synonyms combined into the formal word for formally signing a legal
document.

## Notes
签订 anchors 签订合同 "to sign a contract," 签订协议 "to sign an
agreement" — always a formal, often legal or diplomatic document, more
formal than plain 签.

## Why this combination
午 is noon (the 午 double-hour, 11–1). 上午 is the "upper," earlier half of the
day and 下午 the "lower," later half — 上/下 as before/after around the noon
pivot, the same axis as 上次 / 下次.

## Notes
中午 is noon itself, sitting between 上午 and 下午.

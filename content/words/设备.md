## Why this combination
设 "to set up, to establish, to arrange" (as in 建设 "to build up")
combined with 备 "to prepare, to equip, to have ready" (as in 准备 "to
prepare"): things set up and made ready [for use] — equipment,
facilities.

## Notes
设备 names installed apparatus and facilities collectively (医疗设备
"medical equipment," 安全设备 "safety equipment") rather than a single
portable tool, which would more likely be called 工具 or 器具.

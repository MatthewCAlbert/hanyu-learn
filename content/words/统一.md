## Why this combination
统 ("to unify, to govern as a single whole," covered elsewhere in this
app — the guiding main thread of a skein of silk) takes 一 ("one") as
what it brings about: governing many separate things into one. Fully
compositional once 统's "unifying" sense is known.

## Notes
统一 works as both verb ("to unify," 统一全国, "to unify the country") and
adjective ("uniform, standardized," 统一标准, "a uniform standard")
without changing form.

## Why this combination

引 ("to lead, to guide," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 引导)
plus 进 ("to advance, to enter," as in 进步) names leading something in
from outside: to introduce, to import.

## Notes

引进技术 ("to import technology") and 引进人才 ("to bring in talent") both
build on this word — 引进 names bringing in something valuable from
elsewhere, distinct from 进口, which specifically names importing goods
in trade.

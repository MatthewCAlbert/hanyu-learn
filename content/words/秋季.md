## Why this combination

秋 ("autumn," as in 秋天) plus 季 ("a season," as in 季节) names the
autumn season.

## Notes

秋季 is the formal/written register — 秋天 is the everyday spoken word
for the same season, the same pattern seen in 冬季/冬天 and 夏季/夏天, both
covered elsewhere in this app.

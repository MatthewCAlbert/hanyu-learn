## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 意 "idea, intention" + 思 "thought" (see 思想). From "what is
meant / intended" it spreads widely: 什么意思？ "what does it mean," 我的意思是…
"what I mean is," 有意思 "interesting / fun," 小意思 "just a token."

## Notes
思 toneless here (*yìsi*). The polysemy is famous — 你这是什么意思 can be a
genuine question or an accusation.

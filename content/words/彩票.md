## Why this combination
彩 here carries its extended sense "a prize won by chance" (as in 中彩
"to win a prize," related to 彩头 "good luck/omen"), not its base meaning
"color." Combined with 票 "ticket," it gives "a ticket for a chance
prize" — the logic holds once 彩's gambling-luck sense is known, but is
not obvious from "color" alone.

## Notes
彩票 anchors 买彩票 "to buy a lottery ticket," 中彩票 "to win the
lottery" — the standard word for lottery tickets and games of chance more
broadly (体育彩票 "sports lottery," 福利彩票 "welfare lottery").

## Why this combination
记 "to note, to register (in the mind)" + resultative 得 marking success — the
memory is held. Contrast [[记住]] (记 + 住 "make fast"), which is the deliberate
act "commit to memory," where 记得 is the resulting state "have in memory."

## Notes
Negative 不记得 "don't remember" ≈ 忘记 "forget." 得 is toneless here.

## Why this combination
校 (*xiào*) already means "school" on its own; 学 "to study" is added to make a
clear disyllable. A same-sense pair like [[学习]] and 明白.

## Notes
校 is *xiào* here. The same character is *jiào* "to check, to proofread" in
校对 — a genuine two-reading trap.

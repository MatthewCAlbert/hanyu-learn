## Why this combination
干 (here "to interfere with," as in 干涉) and 扰 "to disturb" (see 扰's own
etymology) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for
disruptive interference.

## Notes
干扰 spans everyday disturbance (干扰工作 "to disrupt work") and technical
interference (信号干扰 "signal interference," a physics/engineering term)
— one word for disruption in social and technical contexts alike.

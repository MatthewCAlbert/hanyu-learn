## Why this combination
共 "together, in total" plus 计 "to count, to calculate" gives exactly
"to count together" — a formal word for stating a grand total.

## Notes
共计 always precedes a total figure: 共计一百人 "one hundred people in
total" — a written/formal register word, common in reports and official
tallies.

## Why this combination
方 "direction, side" + 面 "face, surface" — a "side / face" of a thing, hence an
aspect of a matter or a party to it: 在这方面 "in this respect,"
双方面 "both sides," 一方面…另一方面… "on the one hand… on the other…".

## Notes
面 as "aspect / side" also in [[对面]], 全面, 表面.

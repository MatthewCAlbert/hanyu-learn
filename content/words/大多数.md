## Why this combination
大 "big, great" + 多数 "the majority" (多 many + 数 number) → the great
majority: 大多数人, 绝大多数 "the overwhelming majority."

## Notes
大多数 leans toward counting people / items; [[大部分]] toward a portion of a
whole. Opposite [[少数]] "the minority."

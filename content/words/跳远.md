## Why this combination
A verb-object-like compound: 跳 "to jump" (as in 跳舞, 跳高's own family)
combined with 远 "far" (as in 永远 in an unrelated sense): jumping for
distance — the long jump.

## Notes
跳远 names the track-and-field event (参加跳远比赛 "to compete in the
long jump") and forms a matched pair with 跳高 "high jump," its close
relative, sharing the verb 跳 with a different result complement.

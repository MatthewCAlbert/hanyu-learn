## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Qatar (卡塔爾). 卡, 塔 and 尔 are chosen for
sound. 卡 is not "card"; 塔 is not "pagoda."

## Notes
尔 is a favourite phonetic closer ([[爱尔兰]], [[安道尔]]).

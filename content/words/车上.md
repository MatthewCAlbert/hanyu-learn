## Why this combination
车 "vehicle" + locative 上 "on / aboard." Chinese uses 上 (not 里) for being
aboard a bus, train or car: 车上人很多 "the bus is crowded."

## Notes
上车 / 下车 (board / alight) use the same 上; 车上 is the resulting location. 上
here is a bare locative, not "up."

## Why this combination
友 "friend" and 谊 "a bond of proper, fitting relations between people"
(see 谊's own etymology) combine directly into the formal word for
friendship.

## Notes
友谊 favours formal and international contexts (中美友谊 "Sino-American
friendship," 友谊赛 "friendly match") over the everyday 友情, which fits
personal relationships more naturally.

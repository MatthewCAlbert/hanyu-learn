## Why this combination
所 (a nominalizing particle that turns a following verb into "the place/
thing where [verb] happens," seen also in 所以, 所有) plus 在 "to be
located at" gives "the place where something is located."

## Notes
所在 trails a noun to name where it is: 问题所在 "where the problem lies,"
风景优美的所在 "a place of beautiful scenery [literary]." Common in the
fixed phrase 所在地 "the location [of an office/organization]."

## Why this combination
国 "country" (as in 国家 "nation") combined with 内 "inside" (see also
内容, 内心): inside the country — domestic.

## Notes
国内 marks something as happening within the country's own borders
(国内新闻 "domestic news," 国内市场 "domestic market") — its opposite is
国外 "abroad, overseas," built the same way with 外 "outside" in place
of 内.

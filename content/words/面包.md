## Why this combination
面 "wheat flour" + 包, here "a wrapped / stuffed lump of dough" (as in 包子).
A flour lump — the term coined for Western leavened bread, which had no native
name. 面 = flour also in 面条 (noodles), 面粉.

## Notes
Shape lends the word 面包车 "bread-loaf van" (a boxy minibus). 面 is *miàn*;
the "face" sense is a separate word written the same.

## Why this combination
下 as a verbal measure word — "one stroke / go / instance" of an action — with
一 "one." After a verb it softens the action into a quick, casual one:
看一下儿 "have a quick look," 等一下儿 "hang on a sec." The non-erhua 一下 is
the same.

## Notes
Also literal "one blow" (打了一下) and, of a sudden change, 一下子 "all at
once."

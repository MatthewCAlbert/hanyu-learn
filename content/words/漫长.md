## Why this combination
漫 here carries its "spreading without limit" sense (see 漫's own
etymology) rather than a literal meaning, combined with 长 "long" —
length that seems to spread on without end.

## Notes
漫长 always describes duration, never physical distance: 漫长的等待 "an
endless wait," 漫长的岁月 "long years" — emphasizing how tedious or
extended a stretch of time feels.

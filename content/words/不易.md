## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 易 "easy" gives exactly "not easy" — a formal written
adjective, and, in a separate classical sense, 易 "to change" negated
gives "unchanging."

## Notes
不易 anchors 来之不易 "hard-won, not easily obtained," 得来不易 "not
easily come by" — a written/formal register word, more compact than
不容易.

## Why this combination
好 "good" + 事 "matter, affair, thing that happens" (see [[事情]]) → a good turn
of events, or a good deed done: 做好事 "do a good deed," 好事成双 "good things
come in pairs" (said at weddings).

## Notes
Often ironic: 你干的好事！ "a fine mess you've made." Toneless *hǎoshi* in the
sense "nosy / meddlesome" (好事之徒) is a separate reading (好 as *hào*).

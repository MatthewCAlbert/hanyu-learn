## Why this combination
沙 "sand" (as in 沙发, a phonetic loan unrelated in meaning, and 黄沙
"yellow sand") combined with the light noun suffix 子 (as in 石头 in an
unrelated suffix pattern, 桌子): the everyday concrete noun for sand.

## Notes
沙子 names sand or grit as a substance (一粒沙子 "a grain of sand," 眼睛
里有沙子 "there's grit in my eye") — 沙, alone, is the more literary form
and a productive bound component (沙漠 "desert," 沙滩 "sandy beach").

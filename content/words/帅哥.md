## Why this combination

帅 ("handsome, dashing") plus 哥 ("older brother," extended casually to
"guy, dude," as in 哥哥) names a good-looking young man.

## Notes

帅哥 pairs with 美女 ("a pretty woman") as the standard casual, often
playful terms of address for an attractive stranger — common in
everyday spoken Mandarin, not necessarily literal or formal.

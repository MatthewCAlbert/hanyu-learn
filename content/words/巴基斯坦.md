## Why this combination
A phonetic rendering of Pakistan: 巴基 for Paki- + 斯坦, the usual stand-in for
Persian/Urdu -stān "land." 坦 is not "flat."

## Notes
斯坦 also in [[哈萨克斯坦]], [[乌兹别克斯坦]], [[巴勒斯坦]]. 巴 as in [[巴西]], [[古巴]].

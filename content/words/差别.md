## Why this combination
差 "difference, discrepancy" and 别 "distinction, other" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for a difference between things.

## Notes
差别 favours measurable or notable gaps (贫富差别 "the gap between rich
and poor," 待遇差别 "difference in treatment") — close to 区别, though 差别
leans slightly more toward a gap in degree or quantity.

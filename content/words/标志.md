## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 标 ("a mark, a standard set up to be visible,"
as in 目标, 标准) and 志 ("a marker, a record of intent," as in 杂志,
"magazine," lit. "a record of miscellany") both independently name
something set up to indicate or record. Pairing them gives the standard
word for a sign or marker.

Rated "semi" because 志's "marker/record" sense is less immediately
obvious than its role in 杂志.

## Notes
标志 works as both noun ("a sign/symbol," 交通标志, "a traffic sign") and
verb ("to mark, to signify," 标志着一个新时代, "marking a new era") without
changing form.

## Why this combination
消 "to wane, to subside" and 息 "to grow, to increase" originate as a
classical cosmological pair from the Yijing — 消息 named the waxing and
waning of yin and yang, the natural ebb and flow of change itself (a
sense that survives in 消长 "rise and fall"). "The changing state of
things" generalised into "reports of what has changed": news, tidings.

Marked opaque because neither modern character meaning ("to vanish" /
"to rest, breath") points a learner toward "news" without this classical
backstory.

## Notes
息's own "news, tidings" sense (as opposed to "to rest, to breathe")
survives today almost only inside this one compound — a case where a
word's history is the only thing that makes a character's rarer sense
memorable.

## Why this combination
足 "sufficient, enough" (see also 满足) and 够 "enough, adequate" are
close synonyms, paired into a fuller, slightly more formal or emphatic
word for sufficiency.

## Notes
足够 modifies a noun ("足够的时间" "enough time") and also stands alone
as a predicate ("这些够了/足够了" "this is enough") — 够 alone is the
plainer, more colloquial word used the same way in everyday speech.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 下 ("down") and 降 ("to descend," covered
elsewhere in this app) both independently name downward movement.
Pairing them intensifies plain "down" into the standard formal verb for
decline — of a number, a temperature, or a rate.

## Notes
下降 is the formal/statistical register for decline (气温下降, "the
temperature is dropping"; 销量下降, "sales are declining") — for a
physical object simply moving downward, plain 下 or 落下 reads more
naturally.

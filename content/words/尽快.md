## Why this combination
尽 carries its "to the utmost, exhausting the full extent of" sense (as
in 尽力, "to do one's utmost"), and 快 is simply "fast." Pushing speed to
its utmost extent is exactly "as quickly as possible."

Rated "semi" for 尽's "to the utmost" sense, distinct from its other
common reading and role as "to end/exhaust" (用尽, 尽头).

## Notes
尽快 sits before a verb as an adverb (尽快完成, "finish as soon as
possible") — it does not stand alone as a full predicate the way English
"ASAP" sometimes can.

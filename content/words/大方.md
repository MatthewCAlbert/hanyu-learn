## Why this combination

大 ("big") plus 方 (here "proper, correct," an extended sense beyond its
literal "square/direction," as in 地方, "a place") names being
generously and properly at ease: poised, generous.

Rated "semi" for 方's extended "proper/correct" sense here, distinct
from its literal "square" or everyday "direction/place" meanings.

## Notes

出手大方 ("generous with money, lit. hand strikes out generously") and
落落大方 ("poised and natural in manner") both build on this word —
covering both financial generosity and social ease, unified by the
sense of not being cramped or restrained.

## Why this combination
相 "mutually, [towards] each other" combined with 当 "to correspond to,
to be equal to, to match": matching each other in degree — equivalent, and
by extension, as an adverb, "to a considerable/matching degree."

## Notes
相当 works as a verb ("to be equivalent to": 一美元相当于七元人民币 "one
dollar is equivalent to seven yuan") and, more commonly at this level, as
a degree adverb before adjectives (相当好 "quite good," stronger than 很
but weaker than 非常).

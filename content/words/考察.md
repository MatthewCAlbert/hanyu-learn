## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 考 ("to examine, to study closely," as in 考试)
and 察 ("to observe, to examine," as in 观察) both independently name
close inspection. Pairing them gives the formal word for an on-site
investigation.

## Notes

实地考察 ("on-site inspection, fieldwork") is the standard set phrase —
考察 implies going somewhere in person to examine conditions firsthand,
distinct from 研究, which can be done entirely at a desk.

## Why this combination
真 "true, real" + 的. As a comment it means "for real" (真的吗？ "really?"); as
a modifier, "genuine" (真的皮 real leather), opposed to 假的 "fake."

## Notes
Adverbially, 真 needs no 的: 真好 "really good." 真的 is the stand-alone /
predicate form.

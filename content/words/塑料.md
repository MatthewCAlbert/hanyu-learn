## Why this combination

塑 ("to mold, to shape," as in 塑造, "to sculpt/mold [a character]")
plus 料 ("material," as in 材料) names a material that can be molded
into shape: plastic.

## Notes

塑料袋 ("plastic bag") and 塑料瓶 ("plastic bottle") are everyday
compounds built on this root — 塑 elsewhere names sculpture and
figurative "molding" (as of a personality), all sharing the same
underlying image of shaping something malleable.

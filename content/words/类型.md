## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 类 ("a category, a kind," as in 种类, 分类) and 型
("a mold, a type," covered elsewhere in this app) both independently
name a grouping by shared shape or nature. Pairing them gives the
standard word for a type or category.

## Notes
类型 classifies things into kinds (电影类型, "film genre") — for a
literal physical mold or cast, plain 模 (covered elsewhere in this app)
is the more concrete word.

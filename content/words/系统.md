## Why this combination
系 is "to tie, to connect, a lineage of connected things" (as in 关系,
联系), and 统 is "to unify, to govern as one whole" (as in 统一, 传统). A
"system" is exactly that: separate connected parts (系) organized and
governed as a single whole (统).

Rated "semi" because 统's "to unify/govern as a whole" sense is less
immediately obvious to a learner than 系's more common "connect/relate"
sense — the two halves are not equally transparent.

## Notes
系统 covers both concrete systems (计算机系统, a computer system) and
abstract ones (教育系统, an education system) — broad in the same way English
"system" is.

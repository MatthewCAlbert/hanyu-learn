## Why this combination
交 "to associate with, to have dealings with, to make (friends)" (see [[交给]],
[[交通]]) + 朋友 "friend" (see [[朋友]]) as its object — to form friendships:
喜欢交朋友, 多交几个朋友. Verb-object, so it splits.

## Notes
交 "associate with" also in 交往 "have dealings / go out with," 打交道 "deal
with." In some regions 交朋友 specifically means "to start dating."

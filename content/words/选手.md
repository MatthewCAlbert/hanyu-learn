## Why this combination
选 "to select, to choose" combined with 手 in its extended "a person
skilled at [an activity]" sense (as in 高手 "an expert," 水手 "a
sailor"): someone selected [to compete] because of their skill — a
contestant or athlete.

## Notes
选手 specifically names someone competing in a contest or match (足球选手
"football player/competitor," 参赛选手 "contestant"); it is not used for
an athlete's general profession the way 运动员 is.

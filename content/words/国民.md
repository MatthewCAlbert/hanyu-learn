## Why this combination
国 "country" and 民 "people" combine into exactly "the people of a
country" — the formal word for citizens as members of a nation.

## Notes
国民 anchors 国民经济 "national economy," 国民收入 "national income" —
used as a modifier for country-wide institutions/statistics as often as it
names "citizens" directly; distinct from 公民, which emphasizes legal
citizenship and rights.

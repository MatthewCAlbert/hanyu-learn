## Why this combination
一 "one, single" + 直 "straight." Literally "in one straight line" — spatial
(一直往前走 "go straight ahead") and, by metaphor, temporal (一直很忙 "has been
busy all along"), an unbroken line through time.

## Notes
一直 = unbroken continuity; 一共 = total; 一起 = together — three different jobs
for 一 + X.

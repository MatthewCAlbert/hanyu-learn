## Why this combination
虽 "although, granted that" + 然 "so, thus, being the case" (the classical 然 of
[[然后]], 当然) → "although it is so." Concessive; correlates with 但是 / 可是 /
不过 in the main clause: 虽然贵，但是值得.

## Notes
虽 alone is the terser literary form (虽败犹荣). Word order note: 虽然 can come
before *or* after the subject — 他虽然… / 虽然他….

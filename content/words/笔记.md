## Why this combination
笔 "pen, brush; to write" + 记 "to record, to note down" (see [[记住]],
[[日记]] not this level) → what one writes down. 记笔记 / 做笔记 "take notes,"
读书笔记 "reading notes." As a literary genre, 笔记 is a classical form of
short informal sketches (笔记小说).

## Notes
笔记 + 本 = [[笔记本]] "notebook (or laptop)." 笔 "pen" also in [[铅笔]] /
钢笔 (not this level), 毛笔.

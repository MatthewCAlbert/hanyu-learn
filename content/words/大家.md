## Why this combination
Originally "a great house" — a powerful clan, then a recognised authority in a
field (书法大家 "a calligraphy master"). The "everyone" sense arose separately
from a colloquial use meaning "the whole household / group," and is now the
dominant reading: 大家好.

## Notes
The two senses are told apart by context: 大家都来了 "everyone came" vs.
文学大家 "a literary master."

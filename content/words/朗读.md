## Why this combination
朗 (here "clear, loud," as in 朗诵) plus 读 "to read" gives exactly "to
read clearly/aloud" — the standard word for reading text out loud.

## Notes
朗读 anchors 朗读课文 "to read the text aloud (in class)," 朗读比赛
"reading-aloud competition" — the standard classroom/practice term,
close to 朗诵, which leans more toward expressive, performative recitation
(often of poetry).

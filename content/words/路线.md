## Why this combination
路 "road, way, path" (as in 马路 "street") combined with 线 "line,
thread" (as in 电线 "electrical wire"): the line traced along a road —
a route.

## Notes
路线 names a planned course of travel (旅游路线 "travel route," 公交路线
"bus route") and, in a political register, a policy direction or "line"
(政治路线 "political line") — the geographic and political senses share
the same underlying image of a traced course to follow.

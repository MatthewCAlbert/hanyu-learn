## Why this combination
往 "to go towards, in the direction of" (see also 前往, 交往) reduplicated:
doubling underlines a repeated tendency — [things] repeatedly heading
the same way — grammaticalized into an adverb for frequent, typical
occurrence.

## Notes
往往 describes a pattern observed to hold generally, based on past
experience (他往往迟到 "he's usually late [based on past pattern]") —
unlike 总是 "always," it allows for exceptions, describing a strong
tendency rather than an absolute rule.

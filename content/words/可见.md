## Why this combination

可 ("can, may," as in 可能) plus 见 ("to see," as in 看见) names something
that can be seen [to be true] from the evidence: evidently, thus.

## Notes

由此可见 ("from this it can be seen that…") is the standard connector
opening a conclusion drawn from prior evidence — a written/argumentative
register word linking a piece of evidence to its inference.

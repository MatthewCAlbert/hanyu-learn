## Why this combination
楼 "multi-storey building / floor" + 上 "above" → an upper floor, or upstairs
relative to where you are. [[楼下]] is its opposite.

## Notes
Internet-forum slang: 楼上 = "the previous poster" (the thread being the 楼
"building," each reply a 层 "floor"); 楼主 = the original poster.

## Why this combination
指 "to point at, to indicate" (see also 指导) combined with the
directional complement 出 "out, to emerge": to point something out into
the open — to indicate, especially a fault or fact.

## Notes
指出 takes a fact, problem or mistake as its object (指出错误 "to point
out a mistake," 有人指出 "someone pointed out [that]") — it is used
almost exclusively for verbal or written indication, not for physically
pointing a finger, which uses bare 指 alone.

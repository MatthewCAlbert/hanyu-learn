## Why this combination
[[音乐]] "music" + 会 "a gathering, an event" (see [[开会]], [[晚会]],
运动会) → an event for hearing music. Parse 音乐 + 会. 开音乐会 "give a
concert," 去听音乐会.

## Notes
会 as "-event / gathering": 晚会, 舞会, 演唱会 "(pop) concert / gig,"
音乐会 "(classical) concert."

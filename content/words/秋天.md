## Why this combination
秋 "autumn" + 天, here "season / time of year" — the suffix shared by 春天,
[[夏天]], [[冬天]]. Not "sky" or "day."

## Notes
秋 alone in 秋季, 中秋节 "Mid-Autumn Festival," 秋收 "the autumn harvest."
一叶知秋 "one falling leaf tells of autumn."

## Why this combination
A four-syllable phonetic rendering of Armenia (亞美尼亞). 亚, 美, 尼 and 亚 are chosen
for sound. 美 is not "America" and not "beautiful."

## Notes
Same stem in [[亚美尼亚语]]. Final 亚 as in other -ia names.

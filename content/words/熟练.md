## Why this combination

熟 ("ripe, familiar, skilled through repetition," as in 熟人, "an
acquaintance [lit. familiar person]") plus 练 ("to practice, to train,"
as in 练习) names skill gained through much practice: proficient.

## Notes

熟练工人 ("a skilled worker") and 熟练掌握 ("to master proficiently") both
build on this word — 熟练 always implies skill built through repeated
practice, not innate talent.

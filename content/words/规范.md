## Why this combination
规 "a compass, a rule, a standard" (see also 规定) combined with 范 "a
model, a pattern, a mould" (see also 范围's own 范, whose simplification
reused an ancient, unrelated character "a type of grass"): a rule set as
a model — a norm, a standard.

## Notes
规范 names an established standard of correct behaviour or usage
(语言规范 "language norms," 行业规范 "industry standards") and works as a
verb too ("规范市场" "to regulate the market") — it stresses conformity
to an accepted model, distinct from 规定's "to stipulate a specific
rule."

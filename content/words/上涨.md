## Why this combination
上 "up" and 涨 "to rise, to swell (of water)" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for a measurable rise.

## Notes
上涨 is used for prices, water levels, and rates: 物价上涨 "prices are
rising," 河水上涨 "the river is rising" — a more concrete/measurable rise
than the broader 提高 or 增加.

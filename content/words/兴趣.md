## Why this combination
兴 here takes the *xìng* reading — a distinct word from the *xīng* of 兴奋
(excited), meaning "mood, inclination" — and 趣 is "flavor, appeal" (as
in 有趣, "interesting," lit. "having flavor/appeal"). A mood drawn toward
something with appeal is exactly "interest."

Rated "semi" specifically for the reading split: *xìng* here versus
*xīng* in 兴奋 — same character, different tone, different word.

## Notes
对…有兴趣 or 对…感兴趣 ("to be interested in...") is the standard frame —
兴趣 takes 对 to mark what the interest is directed at, not a bare object.

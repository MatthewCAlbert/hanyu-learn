## Why this combination

规 ("a compass, a rule/standard," as in 规定) plus 模 ("a mold, a model,"
as in 模型) both name tools for fixing a fixed measure or pattern;
compounded, they shifted to naming the measured extent of something
itself — its scale.

Rated "opaque": the modern "scale/scope" sense is a further abstraction
from "measuring tool" that isn't recoverable just from the parts.

## Notes

规模 almost always modifies or is modified by a size word (大规模, "large
scale"; 规模最大的, "the largest in scale") — it names extent, not shape
or design, despite 模's "model" root.

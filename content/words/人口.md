## Why this combination
人 "person" + 口 "mouth" — households were traditionally counted by "mouths to
feed," so 口 became a counter for people (一家五口 "a family of five"). 人口 is
the resulting collective noun: 人口众多, 人口普查 "census."

## Notes
口 as a person-counter also in 家口, 户口 "household registration." Distinct from
[[人们]] "people (in general)" and [[人数]] "headcount."

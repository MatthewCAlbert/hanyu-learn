## Why this combination
美 "beautiful, good-looking" and 丽 "fine, lovely, resplendent" (as in
美丽 itself and, historically, in place names like 丽江) are close
synonyms, paired into the standard written adjective for beauty.

## Notes
美丽 is the formal, written register for "beautiful" (美丽的风景
"beautiful scenery") and is used for sights, scenery and women, but not
for men — 好看 is the everyday, more general word for "good-looking" or
"nice to look at."

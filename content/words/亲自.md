## Why this combination
亲, here in its "in person, directly, close at hand" sense (as in 亲手
"with one's own hands") combined with 自 "self" (as in 自己): oneself,
in person — personally, without delegating to anyone else.

## Notes
亲自 sits before the verb to stress that the subject did something
without deputising anyone (亲自参加 "to attend in person," 亲自做 "to do
it oneself") — 自己, by contrast, is the reflexive pronoun "oneself" and
can serve as subject or object, not only as this adverb.

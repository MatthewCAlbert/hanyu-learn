## Why this combination
球 "ball" + 排, here in the sense "to push / strike with the flat hand"
(排击) — the ball is volleyed with open hands, not caught. A calque-style
coinage naming the game by its characteristic action, like [[篮球]] (by its
basket) and [[网球]] (by its net).

## Notes
The 球 ball-games set: [[篮球]], 排球, 足球, 乒乓球, 羽毛球, [[网球]]. 打排球.
(排 also "a row / to line up" — see [[排队]] — but that is not the sense here.)

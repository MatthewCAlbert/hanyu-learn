## Why this combination
球 "ball" + 排 "a row" — the players stand arrayed in rows on court.
汉语大词典 explains the name 因运动员按排站立,故名 "named because the
players stand in rows," and the game was renamed from 队球 for exactly this
reason. A popular retelling reads 排 as "to volley with open hands," but that
is not the attested origin. Like [[篮球]] (by its basket) and [[网球]] (by its
net), the game is named by a visible feature of play.

## Notes
The 球 ball-games set: [[篮球]], 排球, 足球, 乒乓球, 羽毛球, [[网球]]. 打排球.
排's everyday sense is "a row / to line up" (see [[排队]]) — the same sense
the name is built on.

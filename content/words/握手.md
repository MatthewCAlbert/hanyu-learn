## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 握 "to grip, to hold in the hand" (see also
把握) takes 手 "hand" as its object — literally "to grip hands" — to
shake hands.

## Notes
握手 is intransitive and splits for aspect like other verb-object
compounds (握了握手 "shook hands briefly," 跟他握手 "to shake hands with
him," marking the other party with 跟/和) — it also extends
figuratively to "to reconcile, to make peace" between parties.

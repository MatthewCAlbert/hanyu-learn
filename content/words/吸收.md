## Why this combination

吸 ("to inhale, to suck in," as in 呼吸, "to breathe") plus 收 ("to
gather in, to receive," as in 收入) names drawing something in and
taking it up: to absorb.

## Notes

吸收 covers the physical (身体吸收营养, "the body absorbs nutrients") and
the organizational (吸收新会员, "to take in new members") equally
comfortably.

## Why this combination
平 "flat, level" plus 原 "a plain, an open field" gives exactly "a flat
plain" — the standard geographic term.

## Notes
平原 anchors 华北平原 "the North China Plain," 平原地区 "plains region" —
a low, flat landform, contrasting with 山区 "mountainous area" and 草原
"grassland."

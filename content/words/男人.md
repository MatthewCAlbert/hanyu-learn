## Why this combination
男 "male" (itself 田 field + 力 strength) + 人 "person." Transparent. Register
matches [[女人]]: neutral-to-blunt; 男士 is the polite counterpart, 男生 the
young-people's default.

## Notes
Toneless *nánren* = husband (dialectal), parallel to 女人 = wife.

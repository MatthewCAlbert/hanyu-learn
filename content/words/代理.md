## Why this combination
代 "in place of, on behalf of" and 理 "to manage, to handle" combine
directly: managing affairs in someone else's place.

## Notes
代理 spans business (代理商 "agent, distributor") and computing (代理服务器
"proxy server") — always someone or something standing in to act for
another party.

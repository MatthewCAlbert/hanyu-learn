## Why this combination
似 carries its ordinary "to resemble, to seem like" sense (as in 类似,
好似), but 乎 contributes no independent meaning in modern Mandarin — it is
a classical interrogative/exclamatory particle that survives here only as a
fixed suffix, the same fossilized role it plays in 几乎. Marked "idiom"
rather than "semantic-compound" because 乎's half of the word cannot be
derived by a learner who does not already know this specific fossilized
pattern.

## Notes
似乎 always precedes what is being hedged (似乎在下雨, "it seems to be
raining") and pairs naturally with hedging adverbs like 好像, which is more
colloquial where 似乎 is more written/formal.

## Why this combination
邀 and 请 are near-synonyms, both meaning "to invite" — paired for the
standard formal word.

## Notes
邀请 is the formal register (邀请函 "written invitation," 应邀 "at
[someone's] invitation"); everyday spoken Mandarin often just uses 请
alone (请你来 "invite you to come").

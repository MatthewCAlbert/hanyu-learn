## Why this combination

下 ("down") plus 载 ("to carry, to load [cargo]," as in 记载, "to record")
names loading data down onto one's own device — a modern coinage
calquing the English computing sense of "download."

## Notes

下载 pairs with 上传 ("to upload, lit. up + transmit") as the two
directions of file transfer — both are 20th/21st-century compounds built
from older characters repurposed for computing.

## Why this combination
贷 "to lend, to borrow" acting on the object 款 "a sum of money" gives
"[to do something with] a sum of money [via lending]" — the object can be
separated in fuller phrasing (贷了一笔款 "took out a loan"), showing its
verb-object structure.

## Notes
贷款 works as a noun ("a loan," 房贷 shares 贷 for "mortgage") and a verb
("to take out a loan," 向银行贷款 "to borrow from the bank").

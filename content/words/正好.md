## Why this combination
正 "exactly, precisely" (see [[正是]], [[正确]]) + 好 "good, right, fitting" →
"exactly right." Of fit (大小正好), timing (来得正好), or coincidence
(正好碰上他 "happened to run into him").

## Notes
正好 = a happy exactness / coincidence; 刚好 is the near-synonym; [[恰好]] (not
this level) the formal one.

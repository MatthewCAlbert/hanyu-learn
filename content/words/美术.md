## Why this combination
美 "beautiful" (see also 美丽, 美好) combined with 术 "art, technique,
craft" (see also 艺术, 技术): the craft of beauty — the fine arts,
especially visual art.

## Notes
美术 names visual/fine art as a school subject and field, especially
drawing and painting (美术课 "art class," 美术馆 "art gallery, museum")
— 艺术, its broader relative, covers all the arts (music, dance,
literature included), while 美术 is narrower, focused on the visual
arts.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 成 "accomplishment, to succeed" (成功, [[完成]]) + 绩 "merit,
achievement, spun results" (功绩; 绩 originally "to spin thread"). What one has
achieved — exam marks (考试成绩), or accomplishments in general (工作成绩).

## Notes
成绩 = measured results / a grade; [[结果]] = an outcome; [[水平]] = a level of
ability. 绩 barely occurs outside 成绩, 业绩, 功绩.

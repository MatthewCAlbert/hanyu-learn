## Why this combination
共 "together, shared" and 享 "to enjoy" (see 享's own etymology) combine
directly: enjoying something together.

## Notes
共享 anchors the modern "sharing economy" vocabulary: 共享单车
"share-bike/bike-sharing," 资源共享 "resource sharing" — a more formal,
often institutional register than everyday 分享.

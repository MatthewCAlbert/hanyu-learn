## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" + 国 "country" → the country in its entirety: 全国人民,
全国各地 "all over the country," 全国第一.

## Notes
全 + noun is productive: [[全家]], [[全体]], [[全身]], [[全部]]. Contrast
[[国内]] "domestic" and [[全球]] "worldwide."

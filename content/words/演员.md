## Why this combination
演 "to act out, to perform" (see also 表演, 演出) combined with 员 "a
member, a person holding a role" (as in 成员, 人员): a person whose role
is to perform — an actor.

## Notes
演员 covers stage, film and television performers alike (电影演员 "film
actor") — 明星 is reserved for a famous, star-status performer, while
演员 is the neutral occupational term regardless of fame.

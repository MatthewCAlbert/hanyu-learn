## Why this combination
反 "to turn over, to reverse, to turn back [towards]" combined with 应
"to respond, to answer": a response that turns back towards whatever
prompted it — a reaction, whether physical, chemical, or emotional.

## Notes
反应 covers physiological (身体反应 "bodily reaction"), chemical (化学反应
"chemical reaction") and social senses (观众反应很好 "audience reaction was
good") alike; 回答, by contrast, is specifically a verbal answer to a
question.

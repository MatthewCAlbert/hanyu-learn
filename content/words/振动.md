## Why this combination
振 "to shake, to vibrate" and 动 "to move" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard physics/technical word for oscillating motion.

## Notes
振动 anchors 手机振动 "phone vibration (mode)," 振动频率 "vibration
frequency" — the technical term for regular back-and-forth motion,
distinct from irregular 摇动 "shaking."

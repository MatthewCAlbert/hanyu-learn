## Why this combination
工 (here "craft, work") and 艺 "art, skill" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for skilled making, whether artistic or
industrial.

## Notes
工艺 spans traditional handicrafts (传统工艺 "traditional craftsmanship")
and manufacturing (生产工艺 "production process/technique") — always about
the skilled method of making something, not the finished product itself.

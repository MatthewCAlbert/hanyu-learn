## Why this combination
脸 "face" and 色 "colour" combine on the observation that a face's colour
shifts with health and mood — pale with illness, flushed with anger — so
"face colour" became the word for both complexion and the emotional
expression that shows through it.

## Notes
脸色 covers health (脸色不好 "looking unwell") and mood (看他脸色行事 "to
read the room/act according to someone's mood," literally "to act
according to their face-colour") — physical and emotional readouts on one
word.

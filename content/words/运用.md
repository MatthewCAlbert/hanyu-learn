## Why this combination
运 carries an extended "to wield, to put into motion/application" sense
(a figurative stretch from its core "to move, to transport"), and 用 is
plain "to use." Together they name skillfully applying something —
knowledge, a method, a tool — to a purpose.

Rated "semi" for 运's "to wield/apply" sense, a specific figurative
extension beyond its everyday "to move" meaning.

## Notes
运用 leans toward applying something abstract skillfully (运用知识, "to
apply knowledge"; 灵活运用, "to apply flexibly") — for straightforward
physical use of an object, plain 用 is the natural word.

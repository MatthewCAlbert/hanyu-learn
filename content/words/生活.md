## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 生 "to live, to be born" + 活 "alive, to live." Covers the verb
"to live" (生活在北京), the noun "life / daily existence" (生活很忙) and
"livelihood" (维持生活).

## Notes
Broader than 命 (life as opposed to death) and 一生 (a lifetime). 生 and 活 both
recur widely: 生日, 活动.

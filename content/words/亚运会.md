## Why this combination

亚运会 abbreviates 亚洲运动会 ("Asian Sports Gathering") by taking 亚 from
亚洲 ("Asia") and keeping 运会 from 运动会 (covered elsewhere in this
app, "a sports meet") — the same abbreviation pattern that produces
奥运会 from 奥林匹克运动会 ("the Olympic Games").

## Notes

亚运会 and 奥运会 ("the Olympic Games") share the same 运会 ending,
built by the same truncation of 运动会 — only the preceding
organization/region name (亚洲 "Asia" vs. 奥林匹克 "Olympic") differs.

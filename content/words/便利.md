## Why this combination
便 "convenient" and 利 "beneficial, advantageous" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for ease and convenience.

## Notes
便利 works as adjective ("便利的交通" "convenient transport") and, less
often, as verb ("to facilitate," 便利居民生活 "to make residents' lives
more convenient") — a slightly more formal register than 方便.

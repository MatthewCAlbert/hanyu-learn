## Why this combination
不 "not" negating 耐烦 "to endure vexation/annoyance" (耐 "to endure" +
烦 "vexation") gives exactly "unable to endure being vexed" — the
standard word for running out of patience.

## Notes
不耐烦 anchors 显得不耐烦 "to appear impatient," 不耐烦地说 "to say
impatiently" — always describes visible irritation at having to wait or
repeat something.

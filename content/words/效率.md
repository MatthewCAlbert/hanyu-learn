## Why this combination

效 ("effect, efficacy," as in 效果) plus 率 ("a rate, a ratio," as in 出
勤率 no; more directly 速率, "speed rate") names the ratio of effect
produced to effort spent: efficiency.

## Notes

工作效率 ("work efficiency") and 效率高/低 ("high/low efficiency") are the
standard frames — 效率 always implies a ratio of output to input, not
just "being effective" on its own (that would be 有效).

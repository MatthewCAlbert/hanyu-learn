## Why this combination

高 ("high") plus 尚 ("esteemed, valued," an extended sense of a
character elsewhere seen meaning "still, yet," as in 尚未, "not yet")
names something held in high esteem: noble, lofty.

Rated "semi" for 尚's "esteemed" sense here, distinct from its more
common "still/yet" role in other compounds.

## Notes

高尚的品质 ("noble character") describes moral elevation, distinct from
literal physical height — the same "high = admirable" metaphor as
English "lofty ideals."

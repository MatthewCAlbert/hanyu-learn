## Why this combination
开 "to open" (see also 开放, 开发) combined with 展 "to unfold, to
display, to extend" (see also 展开, 发展): to open up and unfold — to
launch an organised activity.

## Notes
开展 takes an organised campaign or activity as its object (开展活动
"to launch an activity," 开展工作 "to carry out work") — 展开, its close
relative and near-anagram, is used the same way and largely
interchangeably in this sense.

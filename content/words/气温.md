## Why this combination
气 "air, atmosphere" (see [[空气]], [[天气]]) + 温 "temperature, warmth" (see
[[温度]]) → the temperature of the air, the weather-report figure:
今天气温多少度？ 气温下降.

## Notes
气温 = air temperature (weather); 体温 = body temperature; 水温 = water
temperature. All built on 温 "warmth / degree of heat."

## Why this combination
话 "speech, words, what is said" (as in 说话 "to speak") combined with
题 "a topic, a subject, a question" (as in 问题 "problem, question"): the
subject of what is said — a topic of conversation.

## Notes
话题 names the subject a conversation or discussion centres on (换个
话题 "to change the subject," 热门话题 "a hot topic") — 题目, its close
relative, is used instead for the title or topic of a written piece,
exam question or assignment.

## Why this combination
理 "the underlying principle, what ought to be" (see [[道理]], [[经理]]) + 想
"to think, to wish for" → an imagined best state one aspires to. Noun
(实现理想) and adjective (理想的工作 "an ideal job").

## Notes
理想 = a goal / vision worth striving for; 梦想 = a dream (often less
attainable); [[目标]] (not this level) = a concrete target.

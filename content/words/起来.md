## Why this combination
起 "to rise" + 来 "to come (toward the speaker)" = motion upward and toward you:
站起来 "stand up." From that concrete core it spread to marking an action
beginning (唱起来 break into song) and to framing a judgement (看起来不错
"looks good, from where I sit").

## Notes
One of the directional-complement set with 出来, 回来, 进来, [[出去]], [[回去]],
[[进去]]. 来 = toward speaker, 去 = away.

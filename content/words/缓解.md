## Why this combination

缓 ("slow, gentle, to slacken," as in 缓慢, "slow") plus 解 ("to release,
to loosen, to solve," as in 解决/解释) names slackening the intensity of
something: to ease, to relieve.

## Notes

缓解压力 ("to relieve stress") and 缓解疼痛 ("to ease pain") are the
standard frames — 缓解 doesn't necessarily solve the underlying problem,
just softens its intensity, distinct from 解决 ("to resolve
completely").

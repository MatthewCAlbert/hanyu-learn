## Why this combination
底 "the bottom, the base" (as in 到底 "in the end," literally "reaching
the bottom") combined with 下 "below, under": the space at the very
bottom — underneath.

## Notes
底下 names the space directly beneath something, colloquially (桌子
底下 "under the table") — 下面, its close relative, covers the same
spatial sense and is somewhat more standard/neutral in register, while
底下 leans slightly more spoken.

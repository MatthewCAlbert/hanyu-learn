## Why this combination
满 "full, filled" + 意 "wish, intention" (see [[意思]], [[愿意]]) → one's wishes
filled, wants met. 对结果很满意, 满意的答复, 令人满意.

## Notes
满 "full / to the brim" also in [[满足]] (not this level), 不满 "discontented,"
充满. Antonym 不满意.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 习 "to practise repeatedly, to become used to" (see [[学习]],
[[练习]]) + 惯 "to be accustomed, spoilt by habit." Both the noun "a habit /
custom" (好习惯, 风俗习惯) and the verb "to be used to" (习惯了这里的生活).

## Notes
惯 alone as a verb "to indulge / spoil" (惯孩子). 习惯 the verb takes a state or
activity: 习惯早起.

## Why this combination
Reduplication is the standard shape of intimate kinship terms: 妈妈, 爸爸,
哥哥, 姐姐. The doubling marks the word as an address term used within the
family. 妈 rests on the near-universal nursery syllable *ma*; the character adds
女 "female" as the semantic hint.

## Notes
Second syllable toneless. Formal / written: 母亲. Vocative 妈！on its own is
normal.

## Why this combination
大 "big" + 声 "voice, sound" (see [[声音]]) → a big voice = loudly. Adverbial:
大声说话, 大声喊. Chinese sizes voice volume as 大 / 小, exactly as [[小声]]
"quietly."

## Notes
大声点儿 "(a bit) louder"; 别大声 "keep it down." 声 "voice / sound" also in
[[大声]]'s pair [[小声]], 声调.

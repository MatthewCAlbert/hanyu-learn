## Why this combination
Literally "the saying / the words (that)…" — 的 + 话 "words" tacked onto a
clause to frame it as a supposition: 明天下雨的话，就不去了 "if it rains
tomorrow, we won't go." The "words" sense is bleached; 的话 is a pure
conditional tag.

## Notes
Often paired with 如果 / 要是 at the front (如果…的话), but works alone at the
end. It closes the *if*-clause, never the main clause.

## Why this combination
相 "mutually, one another" + 同 "same" → "the same as each other." Of two or
more things being alike: 意见相同, 相同的经历.

## Notes
相同 (predicate: A 和 B 相同) vs. [[同样]] (attributive: 同样的问题) vs.
[[一样]] (comparison frame). Opposite [[不同]].

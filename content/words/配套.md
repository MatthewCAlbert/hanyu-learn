## Why this combination
配 "to match, to pair" acting on the object 套 "a set" gives exactly "to
match into a set" — splittable, as in 配了一套.

## Notes
配套 anchors 配套设施 "supporting/matching facilities," 配套政策
"complementary policies" — always about pieces working together as a
coordinated whole, not standalone items.

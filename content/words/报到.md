## Why this combination
A verb-object-like compound: 报 "to report, to announce" (see also 报告,
报道) combined with the resultative complement 到 "to arrive at": to
report one's arrival — to check in, to register.

## Notes
报到 names checking in at a school, workplace or event to confirm one's
arrival (新生报到 "new students check in") — 报告, its neighbour, is a
formal written or spoken report on a topic, not this act of registering
one's presence.

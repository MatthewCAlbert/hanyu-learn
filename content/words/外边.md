## Why this combination
外 "outside" + the locative suffix 边 — the out half of the 里边 / 外边 pair
(see [[里边]]). Also "away from home": 在外边吃 "eat out," 外边的世界.

## Notes
外面 is the exact synonym. Both differ from bare 外 as a noun suffix (国外,
门外).

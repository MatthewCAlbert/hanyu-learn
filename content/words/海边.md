## Why this combination
海 "sea" (see [[大海]]) + 边 "side, edge" (the locative suffix of [[旁边]],
[[路边]], [[身边]]) → the edge of the sea: 去海边玩, 住在海边.

## Notes
海边 = the shore generally; 海滩 "a (sandy) beach"; 沙滩 "sands." 边 "-side" as
a bare locative also in [[路边]], [[身边]].

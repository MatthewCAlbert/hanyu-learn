## Why this combination
绝 "to cut off, absolute" and 望 "hope" combine directly: hope cut off
entirely — as transparent as a compound gets.

## Notes
绝望 is a strong, total despair (陷入绝望 "to sink into despair," 绝望的
处境 "a hopeless situation") — stronger than 失望 "disappointed," which
implies hope was merely let down, not extinguished.

## Why this combination
明 "bright" + 星 "star." Literally a bright star (the planet Venus is 启明星);
the "famous performer" sense is a 20th-century calque of English *star*, in
which a celebrity outshines those around them. 电影明星, 大明星.

## Notes
球星 "sports star," 歌星 "singing star" — 星 as a productive "-star" suffix
following this model. 星 alone in [[星期]], [[星星]].

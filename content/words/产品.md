## Why this combination
产 ("to produce," as in 生产, 产生) plus 品 ("an item, a thing of a
particular kind," as in 食品, 商品) names exactly what it looks like: an
item that has been produced.

## Notes
产品 leans toward manufactured goods (工业产品); 作品 (a work, e.g. of art or
writing) uses a different first character for creative output specifically
— the two are not interchangeable despite the shared "output" idea.

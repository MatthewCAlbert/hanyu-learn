## Why this combination
立, here in its "immediately, at once" sense (as in 立即, a close
synonym) combined with 刻 "a carved mark [on a water-clock]" (see also
时刻): at the very [next] marked instant — immediately.

## Notes
立刻 sits directly before the verb it modifies (立刻出发 "to set off
immediately") and is interchangeable with 马上 in most everyday contexts,
though 立刻 is slightly more formal in register.

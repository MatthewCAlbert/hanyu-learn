## Why this combination

松 ("pine," a character seen mainly in this role — unrelated to its
separate homographic sense "loose, relaxed," as in 放松) plus 树 ("a
tree," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 树林/树叶) names the
conifer: a pine tree.

## Notes

松树 is a common symbol of resilience and longevity in Chinese art and
literature, often depicted alongside 竹 (bamboo) and 梅 (plum blossom)
in the traditional "Three Friends of Winter" motif.

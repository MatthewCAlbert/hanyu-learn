## Why this combination
书 (here "writing," as in 书写) plus 法 "method, technique" gives exactly
"the method of writing" — the standard word for the art of brush
calligraphy.

## Notes
书法 anchors 书法家 "calligrapher," 练书法 "to practice calligraphy" — the
traditional East Asian art form, distinct from everyday 写字 "to write
(characters)."

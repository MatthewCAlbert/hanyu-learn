## Why this combination
几 here takes its classical "nearly, approximately" sense (distinct from
its everyday "how many/several" sense in 几个, 几点), while 乎 is the same
fossilized classical particle seen in 似乎, contributing no independent
meaning of its own in the modern word. Marked "idiom" because a learner
who only knows 几 as "how many" and 乎 as nothing at all cannot derive
"almost" without meeting this specific fixed pattern.

## Notes
几乎 modifies a whole following statement, often one describing something
that came close to happening but did not quite: 我几乎忘了 ("I almost
forgot") implies I did *not*, in fact, forget.

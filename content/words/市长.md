## Why this combination
市 "city, market" (see [[城市]], [[超市]]) + 长 (*zhǎng*) "head, chief" — the
same "-chief" suffix as [[校长]], [[队长]], [[院长]]. The head of a
municipality.

## Notes
The 长 title series scales up: 组长 → 队长 → 校长 → 市长 → 省长 → 部长.
市 also "market" in 市场, 上市.

## Why this combination
地 "earth, ground" + 球 "ball, sphere" → "the earth-ball," the planet as a
sphere. A term that carries the round-earth model in its name, coined when that
model reached China. 保护地球, 地球村 "global village."

## Notes
The 球 "-sphere" naming set: 月球 "the Moon," 星球 "a planet / star." 球 also
"ball (for games)" — [[打球]], [[篮球]].

## Why this combination
对 "facing, opposite" plus 立 "to stand" gives exactly "to stand facing
(each other)" — the standard word for two things or positions in direct
opposition.

## Notes
对立 anchors 对立面 "the opposing side," 意见对立 "opposing opinions" —
always implies a structural, ongoing opposition between two sides, not a
momentary disagreement.

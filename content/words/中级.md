## Why this combination
中 "middle" + 级 "grade, level, rank" (see [[高级]], [[年级]], [[班级]]) → the
middle rung of a graded series: 初级 → 中级 → [[高级]] (beginner → intermediate
→ advanced). 中级汉语, 中级职称.

## Notes
The 初 / 中 / 高 grading set applies to courses, exams and job titles alike.

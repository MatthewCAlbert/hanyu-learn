## Why this combination
正 "correct, upright" and 义 "righteousness, moral duty" combine directly
into the standard philosophical/moral word for justice.

## Notes
正义 anchors 正义感 "a sense of justice," 伸张正义 "to uphold justice" —
the abstract moral/legal concept, distinct from concrete legal fairness
covered by 公正.

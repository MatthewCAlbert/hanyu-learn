## Why this combination
起 "to rise, to get up" (see [[起来]], [[想起]]) + 飞 "to fly" → to rise into
flight. 飞机准点起飞. Figuratively, of an economy or a career suddenly surging:
经济起飞.

## Notes
起飞 (leave the ground) is the opposite of 降落 "land." Contrast [[出发]] (set
off, any transport) and [[出门]] (leave home).

## Why this combination
阿姆哈拉 is a phonetic rendering of Amharic, plus 语. 阿 is a dummy start; 姆 is
phonetic of -m-; 哈拉 finishes Amhara.

## Notes
Ethiopia is omitted (埃 is not an HSK character); the language is still listed.

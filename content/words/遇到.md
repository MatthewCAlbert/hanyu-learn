## Why this combination
遇 ("to encounter," as in 遇见, 相遇) takes 到 as a resultative complement —
到 marks that the encountering actually succeeded/landed, the same job it
does after many other verbs (找到, 看到, 听到). "To encounter, successfully"
is exactly "to run into" or "to come across."

## Notes
Same verb + 到 pattern as 找到 (to find, lit. "seek-arrive") and 看到 (to
see, lit. "look-arrive") — worth learning as a productive template rather
than memorizing each pairing separately.

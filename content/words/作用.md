## Why this combination
作 "to act, to do" + 用 "use, effect" → the effect an action or thing produces.
起作用 "take effect," 副作用 "side effect," 起到重要作用 "play an important
role."

## Notes
作用 = effect / function (what something does); [[影响]] = influence (how it
affects something else). 相互作用 "interaction" (in science).

## Why this combination
牌 "a plaque, a placard, a tablet" (as in 金牌 "gold medal") combined
with the light noun suffix 子 (as in 桌子, 房子): the everyday concrete
noun for a sign or plaque, extended to the brand name it displays.

## Notes
牌子 names a physical sign (挂牌子 "to hang up a sign") and, by
extension, a brand or trademark (什么牌子的手机 "what brand of phone") —
品牌, a more formal synonym built differently, is used in business and
marketing contexts for "brand" specifically.

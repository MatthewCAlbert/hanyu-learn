## Why this combination
中 "middle" + 心 "heart, core." The "heart of the middle" — a geometric centre
(圆中心), a focal point (中心思想 "central idea"), or an institution serving as a
hub (购物中心, 研究中心).

## Notes
心 as "core" also in 心里, 关心, 小心 — a metaphor as natural in Chinese as
"heart of the matter" is in English.

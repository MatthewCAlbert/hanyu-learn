## Why this combination
相 "mutual, each other" plus 声 "voice, sound" gives "mutual voices" — the
traditionally cited origin ties the name to the performers' skill at
imitating and mimicking others' voices and sounds; the leap from
"mutual sound" to "comic cross-talk performance" needs that background,
not obvious from the characters alone.

## Notes
相声 anchors 说相声 "to perform cross-talk," 相声演员 "cross-talk
performer" — a two-person (usually) comedic dialogue art form, one of
China's most recognizable traditional performance genres.

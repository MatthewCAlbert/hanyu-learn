## Why this combination
听 "to listen" is the activity; 到 "reach" is the resultative saying it
succeeded — listening that got there, i.e. hearing. 我听了 "I listened" does not
imply 我听到了 "I heard it."

## Notes
听见 is the equivalent with the older complement 见. 到 also carries the sense
"hear *of*": 听到消息 "hear the news."

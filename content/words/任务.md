## Why this combination
任 here carries its "duty, responsibility" sense (as in 责任) rather
than "to appoint," paired with 务 "affairs, matters that must be
attended to" — a duty that must be carried out: a task.

## Notes
任务 is a duty assigned from outside (by a job, a superior, a plan);
contrast 工作, the more general word for "work" that need not be a
discrete, assigned task.

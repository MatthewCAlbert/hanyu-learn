## Why this combination
院 "an enclosed yard, a courtyard" + the toneless noun suffix 子 (see [[孩子]],
[[桌子]]) → the everyday word for the walled open space of a house:
院子里种了树.

## Notes
院 "courtyard" is the root sense; the "institution" sense ([[医院]], [[学院]],
[[院长]]) grew from walled compounds. 四合院 "a courtyard house," 小院.

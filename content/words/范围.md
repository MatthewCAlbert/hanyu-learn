## Why this combination
范, originally "a mould, a cast" and by extension "a set limit, a model"
(see 范's own entry — the simplified character replaced 範 with an
ancient, unrelated Shuowen character 范 "a type of grass"), combined with
围 "to surround, an enclosure, a boundary" (as in 周围 "surroundings"):
the bounded area a mould or limit encloses — scope, range.

## Notes
范围 names the bounds within which something applies (考试范围 "the
scope of the exam," 在...范围内 "within the scope/range of..."); it is
abstract, unlike 周围 "the physical surroundings [of a place]."

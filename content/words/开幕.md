## Why this combination
开 "to open" acting on the object 幕 "curtain" gives exactly "to open the
curtain" — splittable, as in 开了幕, extended from the theater to any
formal event's opening.

## Notes
开幕 anchors 开幕式 "opening ceremony," 大会开幕 "the conference opens" —
the standard word for the formal start of an event, opposite of 闭幕 "to
close."

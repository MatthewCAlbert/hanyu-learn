## Why this combination
会 here in its noun sense "an assembly, a gathering" (rather than the
modal "can, to know how to") combines with 议 "to discuss" — a gathering
convened specifically to discuss something: a meeting.

## Notes
会议 is the formal word for an organised meeting (开会议/开会 "to hold a
meeting"); 见面 "to meet [someone]" describes a personal encounter and
does not imply any agenda or discussion at all.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 种 ("to plant, a seed," as in 种子) and 植 ("to
plant," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 植物, "plant [noun]")
both independently name putting something into the ground to grow.
Pairing them gives the formal agricultural word for cultivation.

## Notes

种植业 ("crop farming/planting industry") is the standard agricultural
term — 种植 leans formal and large-scale, while everyday casual planting
(a garden, a single tree) more often just uses bare 种.

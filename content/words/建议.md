## Why this combination
建 "to establish, to set up" and 议 "to discuss, to propose" (recall its
etymology: speech 讠 plus 义/義, "words spoken toward what is right")
combine directly: putting forward a proposal for discussion.

## Notes
建议 works as both verb ("I suggest that...") and noun ("a suggestion"),
and is notably milder than 要求 "to demand" — a suggestion, unlike a
demand, leaves the decision with the listener.

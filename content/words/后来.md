## Why this combination
后 "after" + 来 "come (along)" → "coming along after that." Narrates a later
turn in a past story: 我们先去了公园，后来下雨了.

## Notes
Key limit: 后来 is for the *past* only. For "later" in the future use 以后 or
将来. [[然后]] chains steps in any tense; 后来 specifically reports what happened
next.

## Why this combination
补 "to mend, to patch, to add [what is missing]" (as in 弥补 "to make
up for") combined with 充 "to fill, to be full, to be sufficient" (see
also 充满): to mend by filling in what is missing — to supplement.

## Notes
补充 takes what needs topping up as its object (补充水分 "to replenish
fluids," 补充说明 "to add a supplementary explanation") — it names
adding to something already largely complete, filling a remaining gap.

## Why this combination
用 here is the auxiliary "to need to" (a sense it has only in the negative). 不
negates it: 不用谢 "no need to thank me," 不用来了 "you don't need to come."

## Notes
The affirmative is not *用 but 要 or 得 (*děi*). Colloquial contraction: 甭
(*béng*) = 不用.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 经 "to manage, to run (a business)" (经营, 经商) + 理 "to put in
order, to manage" (管理, 处理). Originally the verb "to manage (an enterprise)";
now chiefly the noun — the person who does so: 总经理, 部门经理.

## Notes
经 "manage / pass through" also in [[已经]], [[经过]], [[经常]]. 理 "manage /
reason" in [[道理]], [[理想]].

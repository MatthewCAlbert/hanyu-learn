## Why this combination
随 "following, in accordance with" + 时 "time" → "as the time (requires),"
whenever the occasion calls for it: 随时可以来 "come any time,"
随时准备着 "be ready at all times."

## Notes
随 "in accordance with" also in [[随便]], 随手 "in passing." Near 任何时候
"any time whatsoever."

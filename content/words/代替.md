## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 代 ("to substitute for, on behalf of," as in
代表, "representative") and 替 ("to replace, in place of," as in 替换)
both independently name standing in for something else. Pairing them
gives the standard word for replacement.

## Notes

用A代替B ("to use A in place of B") is the standard frame; 代替 also works
adverbially as "instead" without an explicit substitute named.

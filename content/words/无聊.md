## Why this combination
无 ("to lack, to be without," covered in this app's hanzi entry) takes 聊
(in its "idle occupation, idle chatter" sense, covered in this app's
hanzi entry — itself descended from Shuowen's surprising original meaning
"a ringing in the ears") as its object: lacking anything to idly occupy
oneself with is exactly boredom.

## Notes
无聊 describes both a bored person's state (我很无聊, "I'm bored") and a
tedious thing or activity (这部电影很无聊, "this movie is boring") — the
same word covers both sides of the same idle, unstimulating situation.

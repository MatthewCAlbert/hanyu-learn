## Why this combination
解 "to untie, to loosen" and 除 "to remove" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for lifting or removing a constraint.

## Notes
解除 covers ending states (解除警报 "to lift an alert," 解除婚约 "to break
off an engagement") and formal removal from duty (解除职务 "to relieve
[someone] of their post") — always undoing something previously imposed.

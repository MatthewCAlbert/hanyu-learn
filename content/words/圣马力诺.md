## Why this combination
圣 translates San / Saint; 马力诺 is the phonetic rendering of Marino (聖馬力諾). Half
calque, half transcription. 圣 is not used in its Buddhist sense here.

## Notes
圣 as "saint" also in other transcribed toponyms (圣保罗). 马 as a dummy phonetic.

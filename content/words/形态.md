## Why this combination
形 "shape, form" plus 态 "state, condition" gives exactly "the state of a
shape" — the standard word for how something is structured or configured.

## Notes
形态 spans biology (生物形态 "biological morphology"), linguistics
(词的形态变化 "morphological change of a word"), and everyday description
(经济形态 "economic form") — always about structural/configurational
shape rather than surface appearance.

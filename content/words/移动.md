## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 移 ("to shift," covered in this app's hanzi
entry — grain stalks swaying against each other) and 动 ("to move") both
independently name motion. Pairing them gives the standard word for
movement or relocation from one place to another.

## Notes
移动 also names mobile technology by extension (移动电话, "mobile phone";
中国移动, China Mobile, the carrier) — something that moves rather than
staying fixed in place.

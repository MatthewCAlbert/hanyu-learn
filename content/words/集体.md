## Why this combination
集 "to gather, to assemble" (see also 集中) combined with 体 "body,
entity" (see also 团体, 具体): a gathered body — a collective, a group
acting as one.

## Notes
集体 modifies actions done together as a group (集体活动 "group
activity," 集体决定 "collective decision") and, as a noun, names the
group itself — 团体, its close relative, names a formally organised
association, while 集体 emphasises acting jointly, en masse.

## Why this combination
票 "a ticket" (see also 车票, 邮票) combined with 价 "price" (see also
价格, 价钱): the price of a ticket — fare, admission cost.

## Notes
票价 names the cost of any kind of ticket — transport, cinema, event
(电影票价 "cinema ticket price," 门票价格 "admission price") — a
straightforward, fully transparent compound.

## Why this combination
北 "north" + the locative suffix 边 — a compass member of the 边 series
(东/南/西/北 + 边). 北面 / 北部 are the 面 / 部 synonyms.

## Notes
北 originally depicted two people back-to-back — "to turn one's back" (败北
"be defeated"), the sunless side being north for a south-facing house.

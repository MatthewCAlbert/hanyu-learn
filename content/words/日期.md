## Why this combination
日 "day" + 期 "a fixed / appointed time" (the same 期 as 星期, 学期, 到期). A
specified day — the calendar date of an event: 出发日期 "departure date."

## Notes
日期 is the day on the calendar; 时间 can be the clock time; 日子 is "days" as
lived (过日子 "get by").

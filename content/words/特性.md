## Why this combination
特 "special, distinctive" plus 性 "property, nature" gives exactly "a
distinctive property" — the standard word for a defining trait.

## Notes
特性 anchors 材料的特性 "the material's properties," 民族特性 "national
character" — always about a quality that distinguishes one thing from
others, close to 特点 but leaning toward inherent, structural traits.

## Why this combination
消 "to dissolve, to melt away, to vanish" (as in 消息's own root sense of
"news of what has dissolved/changed") combined with 失 "to lose, to be
gone" (see also 失去): to vanish and be lost — to disappear.

## Notes
消失 describes something gradually fading from sight or existence (雾气
消失了 "the mist disappeared," 消失在人群中 "vanished into the crowd") —
不见了, more colloquial, is the everyday phrase for "gone, can't be found"
without the same gradual-fading nuance.

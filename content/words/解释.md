## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 解 ("to untie, to resolve," as in 解开, 了解) and
释 ("to release, to let go of," as in 释放) both picture freeing something
that was tangled or held back. Untangling a confusing situation into clear
words is exactly what "to explain" means.

## Notes
解释一下 ("explain a bit") is a common softened request form; 解释不清 ("can't
explain clearly") uses 释 as the head of a resultative-complement pattern
about the clarity of the explanation, not its existence.

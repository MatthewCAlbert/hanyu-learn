## Why this combination
带 "to carry, to take along" + directional 来 "come (toward the speaker)" —
carrying something in your direction. Literal (带来礼物) and figurative
(带来麻烦 "cause trouble," 带来希望).

## Notes
带去 is the away-from-speaker counterpart. The figurative "bring about" sense is
very common in written Chinese.

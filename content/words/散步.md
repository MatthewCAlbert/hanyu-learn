## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 散, here "to stroll, to let loose" (as in 散心
"to relax, to unwind") takes 步 "steps, pace" (see also 进步, 脚步) as
its object — literally "to scatter one's steps [idly]" — to take a
leisurely walk.

## Notes
散步 is intransitive and splits for aspect like other verb-object
compounds (散了会儿步 "took a little walk") — 走路, the general phrase
"to walk," names the physical act without 散步's leisurely, relaxed
connotation.

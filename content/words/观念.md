## Why this combination
观 "view, outlook, perspective" (see also 观察, 乐观) combined with 念
"to think of, an idea held in mind" (see also 概念, 纪念): the outlook
held in mind — a notion, a way of thinking about something.

## Notes
观念 names an established way of thinking, often deeply held (传统
观念 "traditional notions," 更新观念 "to update one's thinking") — 概念,
its close relative, names a defined conceptual category, while 观念
leans towards a broader outlook or belief that shapes how one thinks.

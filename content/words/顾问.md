## Why this combination
顾 "to look after, to attend to, to consult" and 问 "to ask" combine
directly: the person one turns to and asks for guidance.

## Notes
顾问 names a formal advisory role: 法律顾问 "legal adviser," 顾问委员会
"advisory committee" — always someone consulted for expertise, not a
decision-maker themselves.

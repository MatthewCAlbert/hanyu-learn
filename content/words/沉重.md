## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 沉 ("to sink, heavy," as in 沉默, "silent [lit.
sunk into silence]") and 重 ("heavy," as in 重要) both independently name
weight. Pairing them gives the word for heaviness, physical or
figurative.

## Notes

沉重 leans figurative more readily than plain 重 (心情沉重, "a heavy
heart"; 沉重的负担, "a heavy burden") — describing an oppressive
seriousness as much as literal physical weight.

## Why this combination
老, here an honorific prefix "venerable, respected" (as in 老师) rather
than literally "old," combined with 太太 "madam, wife" (see also 太太
in its own family-topic entry, "Mrs., wife"): a respected elderly woman
— a form of polite address or reference.

## Notes
老太太 is a respectful, affectionate way to refer to or address an
elderly woman, often used by strangers (那位老太太 "that elderly lady")
— its male counterpart is 老大爷 "elderly gentleman," built with a
different second element.

## Why this combination
姓 "surname, clan name" + 名 "given name" (see [[名字]], [[名单]]) → the two
parts of a person's full name together. On forms: 姓名, 请填写姓名.

## Notes
姓名 = the formal full name (on documents); [[名字]] = "name" in everyday speech
(叫什么名字), and can mean just the given name. 姓 as a verb: 我姓王 "my surname
is Wang."

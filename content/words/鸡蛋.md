## Why this combination
鸡 "chicken" + 蛋 "egg." Chinese names eggs by the bird: 鸡蛋 is the default,
鸭蛋 duck egg, 鹅蛋 goose egg. An unmarked "egg" is 鸡蛋.

## Notes
蛋 also a mild insult element (笨蛋 idiot, 坏蛋 bad egg). 鸡蛋 itself:
炒鸡蛋 scrambled egg, 鸡蛋里挑骨头 "nitpick" (pick bones out of an egg).

## Why this combination
造 ("to make, to create") plus 型 ("a mold, a type," covered elsewhere in
this app) names making something take on a particular shape or design —
shaping it as if pressed into a mold.

## Notes
造型 covers physical shaping (发型造型, "hairstyling") and artistic design
more broadly (人物造型, "character design," e.g. in film or animation) —
the same "shaping into a form" idea applied across visual media.

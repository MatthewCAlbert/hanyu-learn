## Why this combination

括 ("to enclose," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 概括) plus
号 ("a mark, a sign," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 符号/
型号) names the punctuation mark that encloses text: parentheses.

## Notes

打括号 ("to add parentheses") and 括号里的内容 ("the content inside the
parentheses") both build on this word — 括号 covers round, square, and
curly brackets alike, with a modifying word (圆括号, 方括号) specifying
the shape when needed.

## Why this combination
确 "certain, true, definite" (as in 确实 "indeed") combined with 保 "to
guarantee, to protect" (see also 保险, 保护): to make certainly
guaranteed — to ensure.

## Notes
确保 takes an outcome as its object in formal, often written contexts
(确保安全 "to ensure safety," 确保质量 "to guarantee quality") — 保证, a
close synonym, is used the same way and slightly more common in everyday
speech.

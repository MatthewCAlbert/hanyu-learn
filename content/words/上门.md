## Why this combination

上 ("to go to, to approach," as in 上课, "to go to class") takes 门
("door, gate") as its object: to go up to someone's door — to visit, or
to make a house call.

## Notes

上门服务 ("door-to-door service, house-call service") is the standard
compound — a repairperson, tutor, or salesperson who 上门 comes to the
customer rather than the other way around.

## Why this combination

充 ("to fill, to charge," as in 充分, "sufficient [lit. filled to the
degree]") takes 电 ("electricity") as its object: filling something with
electric charge.

## Notes

充电器 ("a charger") is the literal device; figuratively, 给自己充电 ("to
recharge oneself," e.g. through rest or further study) extends the same
"refilling energy" image from batteries to people.

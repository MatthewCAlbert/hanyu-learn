## Why this combination

善 (here "good at, skilled in," an extended sense beyond its more
familiar "good, kind," as in 善良) plus 于 (a classical preposition
meaning "at, in," fossilized in many formal compounds) names being
skilled at something.

Rated "semi" for 善's less common "adept" sense here, distinct from its
everyday "kind-hearted" meaning.

## Notes

善于 always takes a verb phrase as its object (善于沟通, "good at
communicating") — closer to a fixed formal pattern than to the
adjective 善良's everyday register.

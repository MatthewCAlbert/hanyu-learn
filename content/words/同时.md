## Why this combination
同 "same" + 时 "time" → in the same span of time. Both literal simultaneity
(同时到达) and a discourse connective adding a further point (…，同时也… "and
at the same time also…").

## Notes
与此同时 "meanwhile" (formal). 同 "same" also in [[同事]], [[同学]], [[不同]].

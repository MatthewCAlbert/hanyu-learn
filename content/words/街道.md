## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 街 ("a street," specifically one lined with
shops) and 道 ("a road, a way") both independently name a thoroughfare.
Pairing them gives the standard word for a city street.

## Notes
街道 also names an administrative subdistrict in Chinese urban planning
(街道办事处, "subdistrict office") — a formal governmental sense layered
on top of the everyday "street" meaning.

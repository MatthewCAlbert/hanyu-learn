## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" plus 世界 "world" (itself 世 "generation/era" + 界
"boundary, realm") gives exactly "the entire world" — as transparent as a
compound gets.

## Notes
全世界 emphasizes totality more strongly than plain 世界: 全世界都知道
"the whole world knows" vs. the more neutral 世界知名 "world-renowned."

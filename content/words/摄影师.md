## Why this combination
摄影 "photography" (see 摄's own etymology) plus the agentive suffix 师
"master, expert" (as in 老师, 律师) combine directly: an expert in
photography.

## Notes
摄影师 covers stills photographers and film cinematographers alike; 师 is
a productive suffix for skilled professionals across many trades (厨师
"chef," 理发师 "hairdresser").

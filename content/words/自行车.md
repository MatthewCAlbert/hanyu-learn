## Why this combination
自行 "to move by itself, of its own motion" (自 self + 行 go) + 车 "vehicle" →
a vehicle that goes by its rider's own power, without an animal or an engine.
Parse 自行 + 车. 骑自行车 "ride a bike."

## Notes
Colloquial synonyms: 单车 (southern), 脚踏车 (Taiwan). Shared bikes are
共享单车. 骑车 (see [[骑车]]) "cycle."

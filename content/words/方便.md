## Why this combination
Originally a Buddhist term — 方 "method" + 便 "expedient, suited to the moment" —
rendering *upāya*, "skilful means" adapted to circumstances. From "an expedient
way" it broadened to plain "convenient": 交通方便, 方便面 "instant noodles,"
行个方便 "do (someone) a favour."

## Notes
The toilet euphemism (去方便一下) is a jokey extension of "attend to a
convenient matter." 便 is *biàn* here, not *pián* (as in [[便宜]]).

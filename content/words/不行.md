## Why this combination
行 (*xíng*) here in its colloquial adjective sense "OK, workable, will do";
不行 negates it — "that won't do / not allowed / no good." Also an intensifier
after 得: 累得不行 "dead tired," 高兴得不行.

## Notes
行 as "OK" (行不行？ "is that all right?") is distinct from 行 "to walk / go"
and 行 (*háng*) "a row / trade." The complement use 得不行 = "…to an extreme."

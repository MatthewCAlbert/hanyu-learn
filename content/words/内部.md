## Why this combination
内 ("inside," as in 内心, 国内) plus 部 ("a section, a part," as in 部门,
部分) names the section that lies within something — its interior, as
opposed to what is visible from outside.

Rated "semi" for 部's "a bounded section/part" sense combining with 内 in
a way that is not purely additive — "内部" describes an internal domain
more than literally "an inside part."

## Notes
内部 modifies organizational or structural insides (内部会议, "an internal
meeting"; 内部结构, "internal structure") more often than it describes the
inside of a simple physical object, where 里面 or 里边 read more naturally.

## Why this combination
怎 "how" (see [[怎么]]) + 样 "manner, kind" → "in what manner." The interrogative
member of the 这样 / 那样 / 怎样 set. 该怎样做 "how should it be done."

## Notes
怎样 (a touch formal / written) ≈ [[怎么样]] (colloquial). Both ask about manner,
condition, or an opinion.

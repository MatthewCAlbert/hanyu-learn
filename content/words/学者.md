## Why this combination
学 "to study" takes the agentive suffix 者 "one who [does the preceding
verb]" — a very productive pattern also seen in 读者 "reader," 作者
"author" — giving "one who studies," i.e. a scholar.

## Notes
学者 names an academic or intellectual (知名学者 "a renowned scholar") —
distinct from 学生 "student," who is still learning rather than
contributing original research.

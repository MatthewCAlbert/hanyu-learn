## Why this combination
一点 "a bit" (一 one + 点 dot; see [[一点儿]]) with 点 doubled. Reduplicating
the 点 shrinks the amount further and adds an affectionate or diminishing tone:
就吃了一点点, 进步了一点点.

## Notes
一点点 < [[一点儿]] < 一些 on the scale of quantity. Also a well-known bubble-tea
chain name.

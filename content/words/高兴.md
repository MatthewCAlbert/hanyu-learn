## Why this combination
兴 (*xìng*) is "mood, interest, spirits" (as in 兴趣, 高兴). 高 "high" raises it:
spirits up = cheerful. The same "up = good mood" metaphor as English "high
spirits."

## Notes
兴 is *xìng* here (mood), not *xīng* "to rise, to flourish." 很高兴认识你
"pleased to meet you."

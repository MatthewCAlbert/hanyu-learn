## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 身 "body, trunk" + 体 "body, physical form." The compound covers
the physical body and, by a natural extension, one's health: 身体好 "in good
health," 注意身体 "look after yourself."

## Notes
The "health" reading is why 身体怎么样 is a normal greeting to someone older.

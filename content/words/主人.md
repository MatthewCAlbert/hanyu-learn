## Why this combination
主 "master, chief, host, main" + 人 "person" → the person in charge / in
possession: the host to a guest (主人和客人), the owner of a thing or animal
(狗的主人), one's own boss (当家做主人).

## Notes
主 "master / main" also in [[主要]], [[主人]]'s cousin 主意, 房主 "property
owner." Opposite 客人 (guest) / [[顾客]] (customer).

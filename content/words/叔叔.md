## Why this combination
Simple reduplication of 叔 ("uncle, father's younger brother," covered
elsewhere in this app). Doubling a kinship term is a common, friendly way
to form the everyday address word from the more formal single
character.

## Notes
叔叔 extends past literal kinship to a general polite address for a
younger adult man (much as 阿姨, covered elsewhere in this app, does for
women) — a stranger's child might call any adult man 叔叔 out of
politeness.

## Why this combination

牙 ("tooth," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 刷牙) plus 刷
("a brush; to brush," also covered elsewhere in this app as part of
刷牙) names the tool used to brush teeth: a toothbrush.

## Notes

牙刷 and 刷牙 share the same two characters in reverse order — 刷牙
("to brush teeth") is the verb-object action, while 牙刷 ("a
toothbrush") is the noun naming the tool used to do it.

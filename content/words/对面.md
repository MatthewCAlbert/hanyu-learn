## Why this combination
对 "facing, opposite" (see [[对话]], [[对不起]]) + 面 "side, face, surface"
(see [[方面]], [[面前]]) → the side that faces you: 马路对面, 坐在我对面.
Also adverbial, "head-on": 对面来了一辆车.

## Notes
对面 = across from / opposite; [[旁边]] = beside; [[中间]] = in between;
[[前边]] = the front side.

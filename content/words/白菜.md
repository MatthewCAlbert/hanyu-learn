## Why this combination
白 "white" combined with 菜 "vegetable, greens" (as in 蔬菜 "vegetables,"
点菜 "to order dishes"): the white-stemmed vegetable — Chinese cabbage.

## Notes
白菜 names one of the most common vegetables in Chinese cooking (大白菜
"napa cabbage") — 菜, alone, is highly productive, meaning both
"vegetable" and, more broadly, "a dish" of any kind (做菜 "to cook a
dish").

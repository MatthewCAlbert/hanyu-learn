## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + erhua 儿 as a place-maker (see [[这儿]]). The northern
colloquial counterpart of [[那里]]. With a person: 我妈那儿 "at my mum's."

## Notes
Also temporal in 打那儿以后 "after that" (colloquial). 儿 is toneless *-r*.

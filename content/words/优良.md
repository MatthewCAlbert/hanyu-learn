## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 优 ("excellent, superior," covered elsewhere
in this app as part of 优美/优秀) and 良 ("good," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 善良) both independently name high quality. Pairing
them gives the standard word for fine quality.

## Notes

优良品质 ("fine quality") and 优良传统 ("a good/fine tradition") both build
on this word — 优良 leans formal and often modifies institutional or
inherited qualities, distinct from everyday 好.

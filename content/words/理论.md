## Why this combination
理 "reasoning, principle, logic" (see also 理由, 道理) combined with 论
"to discuss, to argue, a treatise" (as in 讨论 "to discuss"): reasoning
laid out and argued — a theory, a systematic account of why something is
so.

## Notes
理论 names an organised body of ideas (科学理论 "scientific theory")
and pairs against 实践 "practice" as the classic theory/practice contrast
(理论和实践 "theory and practice"); as a verb, in informal speech, 理论
can also mean "to argue it out, to reason with [someone]" (找他理论
"to go argue it out with him").

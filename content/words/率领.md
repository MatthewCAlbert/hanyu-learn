## Why this combination
率 "to lead" and 领 "to lead" are near-synonyms combined into a formal
word for leading a group.

## Notes
率领 anchors 率领代表团 "to lead a delegation," 率领球队 "to captain a team"
— a formal, often ceremonial register word for leading people on a
specific mission or occasion, more formal than everyday 带领.

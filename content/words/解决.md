## Why this combination
解 "to take apart, to analyse" (recall its etymology: a knife dividing
an ox at the joints) and 决 "to decide, to settle" combine directly:
breaking a problem down and then settling it once and for all.

## Notes
解决 can also carry a blunter, more forceful sense in informal speech —
把他解决了 can mean "to deal with him" in a terminal sense — an
extension worth knowing about but distinct from the neutral "to solve a
problem" that dominates HSK-level usage.

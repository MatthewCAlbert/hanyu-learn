## Why this combination
总 already carries "always, invariably" on its own. 是 here functions as
an intensifying particle reinforcing that constancy, the same role it
plays in 只是 and 就是 elsewhere in this dataset, rather than contributing
its literal "to be" meaning.

## Notes
总是 and bare 总 are close to interchangeable as "always" — 总是/总
迟到 "always late" — with 总是 slightly more common in casual speech and
总 favoured in tighter, more literary phrasing.

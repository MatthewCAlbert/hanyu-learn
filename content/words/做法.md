## Why this combination
做 "to do, to make" + 法 "way, method" (see [[办法]], [[方法]], [[想法]],
[[看法]]) → the way something is done: 这种做法不对 "that's the wrong
approach," 红烧肉的做法 "how to make red-braised pork."

## Notes
The 法 "way of —ing" family attaches to many verbs: 说法, 写法, 用法, 活法.
做法 = an approach or a recipe; [[办法]] = a solution to a problem.

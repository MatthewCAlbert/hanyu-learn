## Why this combination
污 "filthy, polluted" plus 水 "water" gives exactly "filthy water" — the
standard word for used or contaminated water.

## Notes
污水 anchors 污水处理 "wastewater treatment," 污水排放 "sewage discharge"
— an environmental/industrial term, always water requiring treatment
before reuse or safe disposal.

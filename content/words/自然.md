## Why this combination
自 "self" combined with 然, the classical manner-suffix ("so, in that
way") seen elsewhere in this dataset (当然, 突然): "being so of itself,"
without outside interference — the root sense behind both "natural" and,
as a noun, "Nature" as the totality of what exists without human
intervention.

## Notes
自然 covers the physical world ("Nature," 大自然) and the manner
adjective/adverb "natural(ly)" (自然的反应 "a natural reaction") — both
senses trace back to the same "arising of itself, unforced" logic.

## Why this combination
只 "only" is straightforward; 是 here is not the copula "to be" but an
intensifying particle that reinforces 只's restrictive sense — "it is
only/merely [this]." Transparency is semi because that intensifying use
of 是 is not the sense most learners meet first.

## Notes
只是 doubles as a mild contrastive conjunction ("...but, however") when
it opens a clause that softly qualifies what came before — 我喜欢，只是
有点贵 "I like it, it's just [that it's] a bit expensive."

## Why this combination
到 "to arrive" and 来 "to come" are near-synonyms combined into the
formal, often abstract word for arrival.

## Notes
到来 favours abstract or momentous arrivals (春天的到来 "the arrival of
spring," 新时代的到来 "the dawn of a new era") over everyday physical
arrivals, where plain 到 or 来 (他到了 "he's arrived") is standard.

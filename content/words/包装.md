## Why this combination
包 "to wrap" and 装 "to install, to fit into" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for packaging.

## Notes
包装 covers literal packaging (产品包装 "product packaging") and
figurative self-presentation (包装自己 "to package/present oneself
[favourably]") — the same "wrapping to improve appearance" logic applied
to objects and people alike.

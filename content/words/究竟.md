## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 究 ("to investigate thoroughly," covered in this
app's entry for 研究) and 竟 ("to reach the end, to finish," covered in
this app's hanzi entry). Investigating something all the way to its end
is exactly what "getting to the bottom of a matter" means — and, as an
adverb, "after all, when everything is said and done."

## Notes
究竟 in a question intensifies it into a demand for the real, final
answer (你究竟想说什么? "what exactly are you trying to say?") — stronger
and more pointed than a plain question without it.

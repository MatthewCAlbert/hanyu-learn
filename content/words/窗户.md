## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 窗 ("window," the modern standalone character)
and 户 (an ancient word for a single-leaf door or household opening, as
in 户口, "household registration") both once named an opening in a wall.
Pairing them settled on the narrower modern sense of window.

Rated "semi" for 户's now-obscure "opening/door" sense here, eclipsed by
its far more common modern meaning "household."

## Notes

窗户 is the everyday word for window; bare 窗 survives mainly in compounds
(窗口, "window/counter"; 窗帘, "curtain").

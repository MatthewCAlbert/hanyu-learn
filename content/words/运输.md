## Why this combination
运 "to transport, to move, to carry" (as in 运动 "movement") and 输 "to
transport, to convey" (as in 输入 "to input, to transmit in") are close
synonyms, paired into the standard formal word for transportation.

## Notes
运输 names the movement of goods and people on a large scale (运输公司
"transportation company," 货物运输 "freight transport") — 交通, its
neighbour, names the transportation system/traffic more broadly, while
运输 stresses the act of moving cargo or passengers specifically.

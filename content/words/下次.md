## Why this combination
次 "an occurrence, a time (countable)" + 下 "next" — the same 下 = "the coming
one" as in 下周, 下个月. 上次 "last time" and 这次 "this time" complete the set.

## Notes
下 here is temporal-sequential ("next"), a metaphorical extension of the spatial
"below."

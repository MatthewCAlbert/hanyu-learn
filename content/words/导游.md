## Why this combination

导 ("to guide," as in 指导, "to guide/direct," and 导演, "a director
[lit. guide + perform]") plus 游 ("to travel, to tour," as in 旅游)
names one who guides travelers: a tour guide.

## Notes

导游带领游客 ("the tour guide leads the tourists") uses 导游 alongside
带领, another guiding verb — 导游 always names the person/role, distinct
from the verb 导, "to guide," on its own.

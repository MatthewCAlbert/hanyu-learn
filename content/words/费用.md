## Why this combination
费 "to spend, an expense" (see also 浪费) combined with 用 "to use,
usage": the cost incurred through use — an expense.

## Notes
费用 names money spent or required for a purpose (旅行费用 "travel
expenses," 医疗费用 "medical costs") — 钱, the general word for "money,"
does not carry 费用's framing of money as a required cost tied to a
specific purpose.

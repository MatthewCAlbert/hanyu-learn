## Why this combination
建 "to construct, to build" (as in 建立) combined with 设 "to establish,
to set up, to arrange" (see also 设备, 设计): to construct and
establish — to build up.

## Notes
建设 is used for large-scale, often abstract construction and
development (经济建设 "economic development," 城市建设 "urban
development") — 建立, its close relative, is used for establishing
something more specific, like a relationship or an organisation, rather
than large infrastructural or societal development.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 描 ("to trace an outline," covered elsewhere in
this app) and 述 ("to narrate, to recount," also covered elsewhere) both
independently name rendering something in careful detail. Pairing them
gives the standard word for describing something in words.

## Notes
描述 is the general word for describing anything in detail (描述一个
场景, "to describe a scene") — more neutral/technical than 描写, which
leans specifically toward literary, vivid depiction.

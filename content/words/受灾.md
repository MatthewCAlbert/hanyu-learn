## Why this combination
受 "to receive, to suffer" acting on the object 灾 "disaster" gives
exactly "to suffer a disaster" — splittable, as in 受了灾.

## Notes
受灾 anchors 受灾群众 "disaster-affected residents," 受灾面积 "the area
affected by disaster" — the standard word for being on the receiving end
of a calamity, whether natural or otherwise.

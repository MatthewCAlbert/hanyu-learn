## Why this combination

一 ("one," here functioning as an intensifying prefix) plus 再 ("again,"
as in 再见, "goodbye [lit. again + see]") names something happening
again and again: repeatedly.

## Notes

一再强调 ("to repeatedly emphasize") and 一再要求 ("to repeatedly request")
both build on this word — a formal written-register adverb, closer to
"time and again" than the more neutral 多次 (covered elsewhere in this
app).

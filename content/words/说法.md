## Why this combination
说 "to speak" and 法 "method, way" combine directly: the particular way
something is phrased or framed.

## Notes
说法 covers both neutral wording (换一种说法 "put another way") and
competing accounts of events (官方说法 "the official version," 不同的说法
"different accounts") — often implying there's more than one way to tell
the story.

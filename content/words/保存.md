## Why this combination
保 "to protect, to keep" (see also 保护, 保留) combined with 存 "to
exist, to preserve, to store" (as in 存在 "to exist"): to protect and
keep in existence — to preserve.

## Notes
保存 covers preserving physical things (保存食物 "to preserve food") and,
just as commonly today, saving digital files (保存文件 "to save a
file") — 保留, its close relative, leans more towards "to retain,
to hold back" something rather than actively keeping it from
deteriorating or being lost.

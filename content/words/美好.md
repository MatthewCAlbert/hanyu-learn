## Why this combination
美 "beautiful, good" and 好 "good" are close synonyms, paired into a
formal, written adjective praising something as fine and desirable —
mainly used of abstract things rather than physical appearance.

## Notes
美好 modifies abstract nouns like life, memories, the future (美好的
生活 "a wonderful life," 美好的回忆 "fond memories") — 美丽, its
near-homophone-in-meaning, is used for concrete visual beauty (scenery,
appearance) instead.

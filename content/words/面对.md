## Why this combination
面 "face" and 对 "to face, to be turned towards" reinforce each other:
to turn one's face directly towards something — literally a person, or
figuratively a problem, difficulty or choice one cannot avoid.

## Notes
面对 takes both concrete objects (面对面 "face to face") and abstract ones
(面对困难 "to face difficulties") — 面临, a near-synonym, is more formal
and used almost exclusively for the abstract sense.

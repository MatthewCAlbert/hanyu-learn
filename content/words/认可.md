## Why this combination
认, here "to recognise, to admit" (see also 认为, 否认) combined with 可
"can, may, permissible" (as in 可以 "may, can"): to recognise as
permissible — to approve, to acknowledge.

## Notes
认可 names formal or professional approval and recognition (得到认可
"to gain recognition/approval," 官方认可 "officially recognised") — 同意,
its neighbour, is the more everyday "to agree," while 认可 carries the
added sense of an authority validating something as legitimate.

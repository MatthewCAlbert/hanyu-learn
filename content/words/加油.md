## Why this combination
加 "to add" + 油 "oil, fuel" as its object — literally topping up the tank, and
by extension pressing the accelerator ("more fuel"). From "give it more gas"
came the cheer 加油！ — "put more effort in."

## Notes
Verb-object, so it splits: 加满油, 加把油. 加油站 "petrol station." The chant
加油 is one of the first phrases foreigners hear at any Chinese sporting event.

## Why this combination
播 "to broadcast, to sow" (see also 广播, 播放) combined with the
directional complement 出 "out, to emerge": to broadcast out [to
viewers] — to air.

## Notes
播出 is used specifically for a programme's airing (这部剧将于下周播出
"this series will air next week") — 播放, its close relative, is
broader, covering playing any media, live or recorded, not only the act
of a scheduled broadcast going out to an audience.

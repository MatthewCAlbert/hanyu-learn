## Why this combination
调 (here "to adjust, to reconcile") and 解 (here "to resolve, to
dissolve," as in 解决) are near-synonyms combined into the standard word
for settling a dispute through a third party.

## Notes
调解 anchors 调解纠纷 "to mediate a dispute," 人民调解委员会 "people's
mediation committee" — always implies a neutral party helping two sides
reach agreement, distinct from one side simply 解决 "resolving" the
matter alone.

## Why this combination
子 (here a diminutive-flavoured noun element, "a small object," as in
棋子 "chess piece") and 弹 "a pellet, a projectile" (see 弹's own
etymology — a bow-and-pellet picture) combine directly into "a small
projectile" — the modern firearm bullet.

## Notes
子弹 anchors 子弹壳 "bullet casing," 空包弹 "blank cartridge" — the
standard term for a firearm's projectile, distinct from 炮弹 "artillery
shell," a larger-calibre relative sharing the same 弹 root.

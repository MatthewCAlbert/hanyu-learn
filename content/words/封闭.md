## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 封 ("to seal," as in 信封, "an envelope") and 闭
("to close," as in 关闭) both independently name shutting something off.
Pairing them gives the standard word for sealing something closed.

## Notes

封闭 covers literal closure (道路封闭, "the road is closed off") and a
figurative isolated mindset (思想封闭, "closed-minded, insular") —
道路封闭 for construction/security closures is the most common everyday
use.

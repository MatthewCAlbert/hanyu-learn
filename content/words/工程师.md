## Why this combination
工程 "engineering, a project" (工 "work," 程 "a course, a measured
progression," see also 过程, 课程) combined with 师 "a master, a skilled
practitioner" (as in 老师 "teacher," 医师 "physician"): a master of
engineering — an engineer.

## Notes
工程师 names the professional title (软件工程师 "software engineer,"
工程师职称 "engineer rank/title") — 工程, alone, names the field or a
specific project itself, distinct from the person who practises it.

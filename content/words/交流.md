## Why this combination
交 "to exchange, to intersect, to hand over" (as in 交易 "transaction")
combined with 流 "to flow" (as in 流利 "fluent"): to flow across between
two parties — the exchange of ideas, culture or information back and
forth.

## Notes
交流 is used for the exchange of ideas, culture and information between
people or groups (文化交流 "cultural exchange," 交流经验 "to exchange
experience/know-how") — 交换, a near-synonym built on the same 交, is
used more for exchanging concrete items (交换礼物 "to exchange gifts").

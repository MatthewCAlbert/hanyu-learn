## Why this combination
后 "back, rear" + the locative suffix 边 (as in [[前边]], [[里边]]). 前边 /
后边 is the front/back pair of the 边 series. 后 doubles as "later," so 后边
can also mean "further on / afterwards" in a text or sequence.

## Notes
后面 is the exact synonym. Bare 后 as a suffix: 以后, 然后, 课后.

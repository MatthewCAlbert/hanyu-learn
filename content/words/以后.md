## Why this combination
以 "taking (a point) as the reference" + 后 "after." "Reckoned from (that point)
onward." Alone it means "in future" (以后要小心); after a phrase it means "after
X" — 下课以后, 三年以后.

## Notes
Mirror of [[以前]] "before." The 以 + locative frame also gives [[以上]],
[[以下]], [[以外]].

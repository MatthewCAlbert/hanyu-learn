## Why this combination

爱 ("to love") takes 国 ("country," as in 中国) as its object: to love
one's country.

## Notes

爱国主义 ("patriotism") and 爱国者 ("a patriot") both build on this word —
structurally parallel to other short verb-object compounds like 结婚
("to marry") and 生气 ("to get angry").

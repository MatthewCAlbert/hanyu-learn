## Why this combination
思 "to think" is transparent; 维 here carries its "to hold together, to
maintain a connecting framework" sense (as in 维持, and the "dimension"
sense in mathematics) rather than an everyday meaning — together, "the
connected framework of thinking," i.e. a way of reasoning.

## Notes
思维 names a mode or pattern of thought (逻辑思维 "logical thinking," 思维
方式 "way of thinking") — more abstract and structural than the concrete
act of thinking captured by plain 想 or 思考.

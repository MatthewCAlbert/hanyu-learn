## Why this combination
领 (here "the neck/collar area") plus 带 "a band, a strip" gives exactly
"a band (worn) at the neck" — the standard word for a necktie.

## Notes
领带 anchors 打领带 "to tie/wear a tie," 领带夹 "tie clip" — part of the
Western-suit vocabulary alongside 西装 "suit" and 衬衫 "dress shirt."

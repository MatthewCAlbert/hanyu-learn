## Why this combination
强 "force, strength" and 迫 "to press, to compel" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for coercion.

## Notes
强迫 anchors 强迫症 "obsessive-compulsive disorder" (literally "compulsion
sickness"), 强迫劳动 "forced labour" — always implies action taken against
someone's will.

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 优 ("excellent, superior," as in 优点, 优势) and 秀
("flourishing, refined, outstanding" — its original sense was a grain
plant flowering well, extended figuratively to human excellence, and it
survives today mostly in names and in words like 秀气, "refined-looking").
Two words for excellence reinforce each other.

Rated "semi" for 秀's figurative "outstanding" sense, one step removed
from most learners' first encounter with the character (usually as part
of a given name).

## Notes
优秀 describes people and their qualities (优秀学生, "an outstanding
student") — for objects or results, 优质 ("high-quality") is the more
natural choice.

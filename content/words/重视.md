## Why this combination
重 (*zhòng*) "heavy, weighty" (see [[重要]], [[重点]]) + 视 "to look at, to
regard" (see [[电视]], 视为) → to regard as weighty, to give something serious
attention: 重视教育, 不够重视.

## Notes
重 is *zhòng* here (as in [[重要]]), not *chóng* "again" ([[重新]], [[重复]]).
Opposite 忽视 / 轻视 "to overlook / belittle."

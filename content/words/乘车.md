## Why this combination
乘 "to ride, to travel by" acting on the object 车 "vehicle" gives
exactly "to ride a vehicle" — splittable, as in 乘了三次车.

## Notes
乘车 anchors 乘车须知 "notes for passengers (rules for riding)," 乘车证
"bus/transit pass" — a formal register word, more written/official than
everyday 坐车.

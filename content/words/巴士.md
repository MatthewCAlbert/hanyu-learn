## Why this combination

巴士 transliterates English "bus" using two characters chosen purely for
their sound (巴 *bā*, 士 *shì*), carrying no meaning of their own in
this word. It entered Mandarin via Cantonese/Hong Kong usage before
spreading more widely.

## Notes

大巴 ("a large bus/coach," lit. "big + bus [truncated]") and 小巴 ("a
minibus") both build on the borrowed 巴 syllable — 公共汽车 remains the
more formal mainland term for "bus," with 巴士 more common in southern
China and Hong Kong.

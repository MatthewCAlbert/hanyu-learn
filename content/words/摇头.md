## Why this combination
摇 "to shake, to wag" acting on the object 头 "head" gives exactly "to
shake one's head" — splittable, as in 摇了摇头.

## Notes
摇头 is the gesture of disagreement or refusal, opposite of 点头 "to nod"
— often used narratively to convey a character's reaction without
reported speech: 他摇头表示不同意 "he shook his head to show disagreement."

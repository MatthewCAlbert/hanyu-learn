## Why this combination
商 "merchant, trade" (as in 商人 "merchant," 商店 "shop") combined with
业 "trade, occupation, field of endeavour" (see also 职业, 专业): trade as
an entire field of activity — commerce, business as a sector.

## Notes
商业 names commerce as an institution or sector (商业区 "business
district," 商业活动 "commercial activity") — 生意, by contrast, is the
everyday word for an individual's or firm's own business dealings, not
the sector as a whole.

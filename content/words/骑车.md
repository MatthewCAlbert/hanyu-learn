## Why this combination
骑 "to ride (astride)" — of a horse, a bike, a motorbike — + 车, here a
two-wheeler ([[自行车]] or 摩托车). Verb-object: 骑了一小时车, 骑车上班.

## Notes
骑 "ride astride" (骑马, 骑车) vs. 坐 "ride sitting in" (坐车, 坐飞机). 骑 also
of straddling: 骑在墙上.

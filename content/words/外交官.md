## Why this combination

外交 ("diplomacy, foreign affairs" — 外 "outside, foreign" plus 交
"exchange," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 交际) plus 官
("an official," as in 官方) names an official who handles foreign
affairs: a diplomat.

## Notes

外交部 ("the Ministry of Foreign Affairs") and 外交政策 ("foreign
policy") both build on the same 外交 root — 外交官 adds the productive
官 "official" suffix, the same one seen in other official titles.

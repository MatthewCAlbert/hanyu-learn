## Why this combination
主 ("main, host, primary") plus 题 ("a topic, a title," as in 题目, 问题)
names exactly what it says: the primary topic something is about.

## Notes
主题 names the central topic of a work or discussion (电影的主题, "the
film's theme") — for a specific question posed for discussion, 话题
("topic of conversation") is the more natural everyday word.

## Why this combination
此 carries a classical/literary "this" sense (surviving mostly in
written compounds, as opposed to the everyday 这), and 外 is "outside,
beyond." "Beyond this [point already made]" is exactly what "besides,
in addition" signals — adding one more point past what has been said.

Rated "semi" for 此's literary "this" sense, less familiar than the
everyday 这 most learners meet first.

## Notes
此外 opens a sentence to add a further point after an initial statement
(此外，我们还需要..., "besides, we also need...") — a written-register
connector, more formal than everyday 另外.

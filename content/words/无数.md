## Why this combination
无 ("to lack, to be without," covered elsewhere in this app) takes 数
("number, a count") as its object: lacking a number is exactly "too many
to count."

## Notes
无数 always precedes the noun it modifies (无数次, "countless times";
无数人, "countless people") — a productive, everyday intensifier for
"very many."

## Why this combination

极 ("extreme, a pole/utmost point," as in 南极, "South Pole") plus 其 (a
classical pronoun/particle used here in a fossilized adverbial role)
fuses into a formal intensifying adverb: extremely.

Rated "semi" for 其's bleached grammatical role, which carries no
independent meaning in this fixed pairing.

## Notes

极其 is the formal-register cousin of everyday 非常/特别, appearing
mainly in written or serious spoken contexts (极其重要, "extremely
important").

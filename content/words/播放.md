## Why this combination
播 "to broadcast, to sow" (see also 广播, 直播) combined with 放 "to
release, to let go, to set going" (see also 开放, 放到): to release into
broadcast — to play a recording or transmit a programme.

## Notes
播放 is used for playing recorded media as well as broadcasting live
(播放音乐 "to play music," 播放节目 "to broadcast a programme") — 广播,
its neighbour, is specifically radio/PA broadcasting, while 播放 covers
any medium, live or recorded.

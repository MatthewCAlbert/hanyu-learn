## Why this combination
字 "written character" (see [[汉字]], [[名字]]) + 典 "a canonical work, a
standard authority" (经典 "classics," 词典) → the authoritative book of
characters. The most famous is the Qing 《康熙字典》.

## Notes
字典 has entries for single 字 (with pronunciation, meanings, stroke count);
词典 (see [[词典]]) has entries for 词 (words of one or more characters). 查字典
"look sth up in the dictionary."

## Why this combination
克, here in its "to overcome, to subdue" sense (as in 克制 "to restrain
oneself") combined with 服 "to submit, to yield, to be subdued" (as in
衣服 in an unrelated sense, and 说服 "to persuade," literally "to talk
[someone] into submission"): to subdue into submission — to overcome.

## Notes
克服 takes difficulties or obstacles as its object (克服困难 "to
overcome difficulties," 克服缺点 "to overcome shortcomings") — it is
used specifically for surmounting hardship, not for defeating an
opponent, which would be 打败 or 战胜 instead.

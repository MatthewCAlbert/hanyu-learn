## Why this combination
模 "a mould, a model" and 样 "shape, appearance" are near-synonyms
combined into the everyday word for how someone or something looks.

## Notes
模样 describes physical appearance (长得什么模样 "what does he/she look
like") and, loosely, an approximate age or state (五十岁模样 "looking to
be about fifty") — a casual, spoken-register synonym of 样子.

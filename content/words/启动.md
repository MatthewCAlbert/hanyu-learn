## Why this combination
启 "to open, to begin" and 动 "to move" combine directly: setting
something in motion by opening it up to start.

## Notes
启动 spans machinery (启动汽车 "to start a car") and abstract projects
(启动计划 "to launch a plan," 启动资金 "start-up capital") — the everyday
formal word for "to get something going."

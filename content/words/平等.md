## Why this combination
平 "level, even" (see [[公平]], [[水平]]) + 等 "rank, grade, class" (see
[[等于]], [[高级]]'s cousin 等级) → level in rank, no one above another:
男女平等, 平等对待, 人人平等.

## Notes
平等 = equal in rights / status (a principle); [[公平]] = fair (in treatment);
相等 (see [[相等]]'s cousin) = numerically equal. Buddhist 平等 = "impartial,
without distinction."

## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 医 ("medicine, a doctor," as in 医生, 医院) and 疗
("to treat, to heal," covered in this app's entry for 治疗) both
independently name medical care. Pairing them gives the standard noun for
healthcare/medical treatment as a system or field, distinct from a single
act of treating one illness.

## Notes
医疗 names the field/system (医疗保险, health insurance; 医疗设备, medical
equipment), while 治疗 names the specific act of treating a particular
illness — related but not interchangeable.

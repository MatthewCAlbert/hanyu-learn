## Why this combination
切 here is not "to cut" but its literary adverbial sense "entirely,
completely, without exception" (also seen in 迫切 "urgent," 密切
"close"). 一 "one" plus "entirely" gives "the one totality that covers
everything" — everything, all.

## Notes
一切 functions as both a pronoun ("everything") and, before a noun, a
determiner ("all [nouns]") — 一切困难 "all difficulties" — without
changing form between the two roles.

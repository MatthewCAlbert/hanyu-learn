## Why this combination
约 (here "an agreement, a pact," as in 约定) and 束 "to bind, to tie"
(see 束's own etymology — a bundle tied together) are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for holding behavior within limits.

## Notes
约束 works as verb ("约束自己的行为" "to restrain one's own behavior") and
noun ("受道德约束" "bound by moral constraints") — always about limits on
conduct, whether self-imposed or external.

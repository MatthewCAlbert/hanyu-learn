## Why this combination
辞 (here "words, expressions," an older/variant character for 词) plus
典 "a canon, a reference standard" gives exactly "a reference standard
for words" — the standard word for a dictionary, differing from 词典 only
in the choice of 辞 vs. 词 for "word."

## Notes
辞典 anchors 大辞典 "a comprehensive dictionary," often in the titles of
larger or more formal dictionaries — functionally identical to and
interchangeable with 词典 in modern usage.

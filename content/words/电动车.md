## Why this combination

电动 ("electrically powered" — 电 "electric" plus 动 "to move," as in
运动) plus 车 ("vehicle") names a vehicle driven by electric power: an
electric vehicle.

## Notes

电动车 covers both electric bicycles/scooters (the everyday
commuting sense in China) and, more broadly, electric cars —
context settles which is meant.

## Why this combination
信 "letter" plus 箱 "box, case" gives exactly "a box for letters" — the
standard word for a physical mailbox, later extended to email inboxes
(电子信箱).

## Notes
信箱 anchors 邮政信箱 "post office box," 电子信箱 "email inbox" — the
physical postal sense is now often paired with or replaced by the
electronic sense in everyday usage.

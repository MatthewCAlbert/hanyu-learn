## Why this combination
留 "to keep, to remain, to detain" + resultative 下, which marks something
settling into place and staying (as in 放下, 停下, [[坐下]]). 留下地址 "leave an
address," 留下来吃饭 "stay for dinner," 留下深刻印象 "leave a deep impression."

## Notes
留 alone: 留学 (see [[留学生]]), 保留. 下 as the "settle / remain" complement is
a productive pattern.

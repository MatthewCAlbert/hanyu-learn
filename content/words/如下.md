## Why this combination
如 "to be like" plus 下 "below, following" gives, literally, "(it is)
like what follows" — a fixed formal expression introducing a list or
quotation, the logic clear once explained but not guessable word-by-word.

## Notes
如下 always precedes the material it introduces: 原因如下 "the reasons
are as follows," 内容如下 "the content is as follows" — a written/formal
register marker, rare in casual speech.

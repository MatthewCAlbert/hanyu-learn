## Why this combination
处 "to handle, to deal with" plus 分 (here "to allot, to mete out")
gives exactly "to deal with by meting out (punishment)" — the standard
word for formal disciplinary action.

## Notes
处分 works as noun ("受到处分" "to receive disciplinary action") and verb
("处分违纪人员" "to discipline those who broke the rules") — a formal,
institutional word for punishment less severe than criminal penalty.

## Why this combination
生 "to give rise to, to grow, to be born" combined with 产 "to produce,
to give birth to" (see also 产生, 产品 formed on the same root): to give
rise to and produce — manufacturing, and, in its original sense, giving
birth.

## Notes
生产 covers industrial manufacturing (生产汽车 "to manufacture cars,"
生产线 "production line") and, in a more formal register, childbirth
(孕妇生产 "a pregnant woman giving birth") — 产生, its reversed twin,
is used instead for abstract results arising (see 产生's own entry).

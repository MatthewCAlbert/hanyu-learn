## Why this combination
二维 "two-dimensional" (二 "two" + 维 "dimension") plus 码 "code" gives
exactly "a two-dimensional code" — the standard technical term for a QR
code, contrasting with 一维码/条形码, the older one-dimensional barcode.

## Notes
二维码 anchors 扫二维码 "to scan a QR code," 生成二维码 "to generate a QR
code" — ubiquitous in mobile payments and information sharing in modern
China.

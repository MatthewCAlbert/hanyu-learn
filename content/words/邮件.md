## Why this combination
邮 "postal, mail" (as in 邮局 "post office") combined with 件, the
classifier-turned-noun for a discrete item (see also 文件, 事件): an item
sent by post — mail, and, by extension, email.

## Notes
邮件 covers physical mail (寄邮件 "to send mail") and, since the internet
era, electronic mail (电子邮件 "email," often shortened casually to just
邮件) — the same word spans both through the shared "sent item"
image.

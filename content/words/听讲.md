## Why this combination
听 "to listen" + 讲, here nominalised "a talk, a lecture" (讲 to expound; see
[[讲话]], [[讲课]]) as its object — to sit and listen to someone explaining.
In school: 上课要认真听讲 "pay attention in class." Verb-object.

## Notes
听讲 = listen to a lecture / talk (formal setting); [[听说]] = "hear it said"
(hearsay); 听课 = sit in on a class.

## Why this combination
消 "to eliminate, to dispel" acting on the object 毒 "poison, germs"
gives exactly "to eliminate germs" — splittable, as in 消了毒.

## Notes
消毒 anchors 消毒液 "disinfectant," 高温消毒 "heat sterilization" — the
standard word for killing harmful microorganisms, from medical equipment
to household cleaning.

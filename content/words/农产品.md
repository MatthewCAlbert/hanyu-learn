## Why this combination
农 "agriculture" plus 产品 "product" (产 "to produce" + 品 "item") gives
exactly "an agricultural product" — the standard commercial/economic
term, paralleling 水产品 "aquatic products."

## Notes
农产品 anchors 农产品市场 "agricultural produce market," 出口农产品
"export agricultural goods" — the broad economic category covering crops,
livestock products, and related goods.

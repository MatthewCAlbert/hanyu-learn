## Why this combination
听 "to listen" + 写 "to write" — writing down what you hear. A serial pair (do
one, then the other), naming the classroom exercise and, in music, transcribing
by ear. Both verb (老师听写生词) and noun (今天有听写).

## Notes
Same listen-then-act shape in 听说 "hear (it) said" and 听懂 "understand by
listening."

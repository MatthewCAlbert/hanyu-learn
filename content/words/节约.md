## Why this combination
节, here "to moderate, to restrain, to limit" (as in 节日 in an
unrelated sense "festival," originally "a joint/division of time")
combined with 约 "to economize, to restrain, a bond" (as in 预约's own
约): to restrain and economise — to conserve, to save.

## Notes
节约 takes resources like time, water or money as its object (节约用水
"to conserve water," 节约时间 "to save time") — 浪费, its direct
opposite, names wasteful spending of the same kinds of resources.

## Why this combination
西 "west" + 方 "region, quarter" (see [[方向]], [[南方]]). The compass sense
(太阳从西方落下) and, capitalised in sense, "the West" — Europe and North
America (西方国家, 西方文化). In Buddhism, 西方 also = the Western Paradise
(西方极乐世界).

## Notes
The 方 compass set: 东方, 南方, 西方, 北方. [[西边]] is the plain "west side";
西方 leans toward "the West" as a civilisation.

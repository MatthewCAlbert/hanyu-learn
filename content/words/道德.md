## Why this combination
道 "the Way, the proper path" and 德 "virtue, what one gains by walking
that path rightly" (see 德's own etymology) combine into "the proper path
and the virtue gained from it" — a philosophical pairing with roots in
classical Daoist and Confucian thought, not a simple additive compound.

## Notes
道德 anchors 道德观 "moral outlook," 不道德 "immoral," and 职业道德
"professional ethics" — the modern, secular register for "ethics," distinct
from religious or legal codes.

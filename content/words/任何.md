## Why this combination
何 "what, which" is the more transparent half. 任 here is not "to
appoint" but its extended sense "to let, to allow, no matter [how/what]"
— the same 任 seen in 任性 "wilful" (letting one's nature run free).
"No matter what" collapses into the determiner "any."

## Notes
任何 always precedes a noun (任何人 "anyone," 任何时候 "anytime") and,
unlike English "any," does not stand alone as a pronoun — that job falls
to words like 什么都 or 谁都.

## Why this combination
到 "to arrive at" and 底 "the bottom" combine literally into "to reach
the very bottom [of something]" — pursuing a matter all the way to its
end or its root cause. The figurative leap from "physical bottom" to
"the ultimate truth of a matter" is why transparency sits at semi.

## Notes
到底 often adds insistence to a question — 你到底想要什么？"What do you
actually want [when all is said and done]?" — pressing past surface
answers toward the real one.

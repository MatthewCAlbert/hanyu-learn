## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 安 "peaceful, settled, at ease" (see [[安全]], [[平安]]) + 静
"still, silent, quiet" (安静, 冷静). Free of noise and of disturbance:
安静的地方, 请安静.

## Notes
安静 = quiet and undisturbed (a place, a person settling down); 冷静 = calm
under pressure; 平静 = tranquil (a mood, a sea).

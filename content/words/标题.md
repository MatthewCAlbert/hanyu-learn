## Why this combination
标 "a marker, a sign, that which marks [a level]" (see also 标准, 目标)
combined with 题 "a topic, a title" (see also 题目, 问题): the marker
naming the topic — a title, a headline.

## Notes
标题 names the title or headline of a written piece, especially
journalistic (新闻标题 "news headline") — 题目, its close relative, is
used more broadly for the title of any work or an exam question, while
标题 leans towards headlines and section headers specifically.

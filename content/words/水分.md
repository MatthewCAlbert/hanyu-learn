## Why this combination
水 "water" plus 分 "portion, content" gives exactly "water content" — the
literal measurement sense; the figurative sense (data or claims padded
with unearned exaggeration) extends "water" diluting something real, the
same metaphor as English "watered down."

## Notes
水分 names literal moisture (水果的水分 "the moisture in fruit") and,
figuratively, inflated or false content: 数据里有水分 "there's padding in
the data" — the figurative sense is common in discussions of statistics
and reports.

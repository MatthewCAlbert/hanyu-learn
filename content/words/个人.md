## Why this combination
个 (the general classifier, here used in its bound sense "individual,
single") modifying 人 "person" — a single person considered apart from
any group: an individual.

## Notes
个人 as a modifier marks something as personal or private (个人意见
"personal opinion," 个人信息 "personal information") — the individual as
opposed to the collective, a distinction with real cultural weight in
contexts contrasting 个人 with 集体 "the collective."

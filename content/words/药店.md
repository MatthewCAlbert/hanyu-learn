## Why this combination
药 "medicine, drug" (see [[药水]], [[药片]]) + 店 "shop" (see [[商店]],
[[书店]], [[饭店]]) → a shop selling medicines. 去药店买药.

## Notes
药 "medicine" also in [[中医]]'s cousin 中药 "Chinese medicine," 西药, 吃药
"take medicine." 药房 is the near-synonym (esp. inside a hospital).

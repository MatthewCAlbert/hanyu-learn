## Why this combination
单 "single" + 位 "a place, a position, a slot" (座位, 位置) → one standard slot
taken as the basic count. A measuring unit (以米为单位), and, in PRC usage, the
"work unit" that employs and administers a person (在哪个单位工作).

## Notes
The 工作单位 sense — an employer as a social institution — was central to
20th-century PRC life. 位 as "slot / seat" also in [[座位]], [[水平]]'s cousin
地位.

## Why this combination
指 "to point at" and 责 "to blame, responsibility" combine directly:
pointing the finger of blame at someone.

## Notes
指责 is a formal, public accusation of fault (受到指责 "to come under
criticism," 相互指责 "to blame each other") — stronger and more direct
than the milder 批评 "to criticize."

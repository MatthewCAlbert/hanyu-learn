## Why this combination
起 here is the noun sense "a group, a batch" (as in 一起案件 "a case"). 一起 =
"in one group" = together. The adverb 我们一起去 "let's go together" is the
everyday use.

## Notes
Adverbial 一起 (do X together) vs. locative 一块儿 (colloquial, same meaning) vs.
和…一起 (together with…). All three overlap heavily in speech.

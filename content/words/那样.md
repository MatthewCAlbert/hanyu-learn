## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + 样 "manner, kind" (see [[这样]], [[一样]]). The
far-from-speaker member of the 这样 / 那样 / 怎样 set. Refers to a manner or
degree just mentioned: 别像他那样 "don't be like him."

## Notes
像…那样 / 那样的… "that sort of." Colloquial contraction 那么样.

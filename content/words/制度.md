## Why this combination
制 "to fashion, to control, to establish" (see also 制造, 制作) combined
with 度 "a measure, a standard" (see also 程度, 态度): a standard
established [for how things are run] — a system, an institution.

## Notes
制度 names formal, organised systems of rules — political, economic,
educational (教育制度 "education system," 法律制度 "legal system") — it
names the structure of rules itself, not a physical institution/building
the way an English "institution" sometimes can.

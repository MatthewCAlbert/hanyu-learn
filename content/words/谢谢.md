## Why this combination
Verb 谢 "to thank" doubled. Reduplicating a monosyllabic verb softens it and
makes it casual and conversational — the same move as 看看 "have a look" or
说说 "tell me a bit." 谢谢 is the everyday spoken thanks; the single 谢 survives
mainly in 感谢, 谢意.

## Notes
Second syllable is toneless (*xièxie*). More formal: 感谢, 多谢, 谢谢您.

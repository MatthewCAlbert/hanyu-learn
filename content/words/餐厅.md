## Why this combination
餐 "a meal" and 厅 "a hall, a large room" combine directly: the hall
where meals are eaten.

## Notes
餐厅 covers both a restaurant (去餐厅吃饭 "to go eat at a restaurant") and
an institutional dining hall (员工餐厅 "staff canteen") — a more formal
word than the everyday 饭馆 for a small eatery.

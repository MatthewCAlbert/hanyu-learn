## Why this combination
处 "to dwell, to be situated" and 在 "to be at" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for being situated in a state or position.

## Notes
处在 almost always takes an abstract state as its object: 处在困境中 "to
find oneself in a difficult situation," 处在发展阶段 "to be at a
developmental stage" — a state, not a physical place.

## Why this combination
回 "to return" + 来 "come (toward the speaker)": returning to where you are.
回去 is returning away from you. As a complement: 拿回来 "bring back," 买回来
"buy and bring home."

## Notes
The 回来 / 回去 choice is pure viewpoint — is the speaker at the destination or
not. Same logic across [[出来]] / [[出去]], [[进来]] / [[进去]].

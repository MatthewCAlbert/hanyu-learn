## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 性 ("nature, inherent character," as in 性格,
"personality") and 质 ("substance, quality," as in 质量) both
independently name a thing's fundamental character. Pairing them gives
the formal word for the essential nature of something.

## Notes

性质 names what kind of thing something fundamentally is (问题的性质,
"the nature of the problem"; 化学性质, "chemical properties") — 性格 is
reserved for a person's character specifically, while 性质 applies to
things, events, and issues.

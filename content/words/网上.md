## Why this combination
网 "net" — here clipped from 网络 / 互联网 "the internet" — plus locative 上
"on," treating the network as a surface you are "on." 网上购物 "shop online,"
网上聊天 "chat online."

## Notes
网 alone now routinely means "the internet": 上网 "get online," 网友 "online
friend," 网店 "web shop."

## Why this combination
招 "to recruit, to summon" acting on the object 生 "students" gives
exactly "to recruit students" — splittable, as in 招了一百个生 in some
constructions, though usually kept together.

## Notes
招生 anchors 招生简章 "admissions brochure," 招生名额 "enrollment quota" —
the standard word for a school's or program's student-recruitment
process.

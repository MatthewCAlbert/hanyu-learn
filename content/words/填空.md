## Why this combination

填 ("to fill, to fill in," as in 填写, "to fill out [a form]") takes 空
("empty, a blank," a distinct sense from its more familiar "air/sky,"
as in 空调) as its object: filling an empty space.

## Notes

填空题 ("a fill-in-the-blank question") is the standard exam-format
term — 填空 names the general task, while 填空题 names the specific
question type built on it.

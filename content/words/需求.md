## Why this combination
需 "to need, to require" (as in 需要 "to need") combined with 求 "to
seek, to demand, to request" (as in 要求 "to demand, a requirement"):
what is needed and sought — demand, requirement.

## Notes
需求 is a formal, often economic term for demand (市场需求 "market
demand," 满足需求 "to meet demand") — 需要, its everyday relative, is the
general verb "to need" and does not carry 需求's specifically economic
or formal-analytical register.

## Why this combination

改 ("to change," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 改善/改进)
plus 正 ("correct, right," as in 正确) names changing something to make
it correct: to correct, to amend.

## Notes

改正错误 ("to correct a mistake") and 及时改正 ("to correct promptly") both
build on this word — 改正 always implies fixing something specifically
identified as wrong, distinct from 改进 (covered elsewhere in this app),
which improves something not necessarily broken.

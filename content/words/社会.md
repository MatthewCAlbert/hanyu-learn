## Why this combination
社 here draws on its root sense "a local community bound by a shared
earth-altar" (recall its etymology) rather than the modern abbreviation
for "organisation." 会 supplies "a gathering, an assembly." Together:
a community, gathered — society. Marked semi because 会's contribution
requires its noun "assembly" sense rather than the more familiar modal
"can, to know how to."

## Notes
社会 is a 19th/20th-century coinage (via Japanese 社会, itself built
from these same Chinese characters) created to translate the Western
concept of "society" as an abstract collective — not a word with deep
classical roots of its own.

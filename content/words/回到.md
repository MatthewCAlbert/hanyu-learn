## Why this combination
回 "to return" + resultative 到 "reach," which licenses a destination right
after: 回到家, 回到学校. Bare 回 already takes some places (回家, 回国); 回到 is
the general form and adds the "arrived" nuance.

## Notes
Figurative too: 回到正题 "get back to the point." Compare [[来到]], [[回来]].

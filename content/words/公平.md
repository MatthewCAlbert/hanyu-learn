## Why this combination
公 "public, common, impartial" (not favouring one's own — 大公无私) + 平 "level,
even, equal" → treating all sides on a level: 公平竞争, 这样不公平.

## Notes
公 "impartial" also in [[公司]], [[公园]], [[公路]]. Near [[平等]] (equality) and
公正 (just); opposite 不公 / 偏心.

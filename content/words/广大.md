## Why this combination
广 "wide, broad" (see also 广场, 广播) combined with 大 "big, great":
wide and great — vast in extent, or, of a group of people, numerous and
inclusive.

## Notes
广大 describes vast physical areas (广大地区 "a vast area") and, very
commonly, a large body of people addressed collectively and respectfully
(广大群众 "the broad masses," 广大读者 "our many readers") — this
"numerous people" sense is a distinctive extension worth knowing beyond
the literal spatial one.

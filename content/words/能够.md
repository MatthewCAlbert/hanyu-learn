## Why this combination
能 "can, be able" + 够 "enough, to reach a threshold" → to have enough capacity
to do something. The 够 adds a sense of measuring up to what is required.

## Notes
能够 and bare 能 overlap; 能够 is a touch more formal and emphasises sufficiency.
Both differ from 会 "know how to" and 可以 "be allowed to."

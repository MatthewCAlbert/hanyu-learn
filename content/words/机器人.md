## Why this combination
机器 "machine" and 人 "person" calque the Western concept "robot" (itself
from Czech *robota*, "forced labour," via a 1920s play) — a machine built
in the shape of, and doing the work of, a person.

## Notes
机器人 anchors 工业机器人 "industrial robot," 机器人技术 "robotics" — a
direct, transparent calque, unlike the pure sound-loan 逻辑 or 幽默.

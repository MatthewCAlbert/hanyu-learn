## Why this combination
Both syllables were chosen for sound, most likely tracing an ancient loan
along Silk Road trade routes rather than being built from native Chinese
roots. Neither 玻 nor 璃 carries meaning on its own outside this word — a
textbook case of a disyllabic loanword where the characters are pure
sound-carriers.

## Notes
玻璃 is entirely opaque and must be learned as a whole unit, the way
"sofa" (沙发) is. A modern slang sense — 玻璃 for a gay man — is a much
later, unrelated coinage playing on "glass" as fragile/see-through, not
part of the word's original meaning.

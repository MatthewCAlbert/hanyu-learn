## Why this combination
关 "to shut" + 上 as a resultative complement meaning "closed to, brought into
contact" — the same 上 in 合上 (close a book), 锁上 (lock). So "shut it to."
Doors, windows, lids, and switches: 关上灯 "turn off the light."

## Notes
Opposite [[打开]]. Here 上 is not "up" and not a location — it marks the closing
as complete.

## Why this combination
中 abbreviates 中国 (see [[中国]]); 文 is "writing, text, language." Literally
"Chinese writing," now the general name for the language, leaning slightly
toward the written form. 英文, 日文, 法文 follow the same mould.

## Notes
汉语 stresses the spoken Han language; 中文 the written / national language;
普通话 specifically the standard spoken form. In practice 中文 and 汉语 overlap
heavily.

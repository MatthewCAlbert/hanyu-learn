## Why this combination
慌 "panicked, flustered" and 忙 "busy, hurried" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for acting in a hurried, flustered
manner.

## Notes
慌忙 anchors 慌忙逃跑 "to flee in a panic," 慌忙解释 "to hurriedly
explain" — always implies both haste and a degree of nervous
disorganization, more than plain 匆忙 "hurried."

## Why this combination
变 "to change" + resultative 成 "to become, to end up as" — change that results
in a new form. Always takes the result: 变成蝴蝶, 水变成冰.

## Notes
变成 stresses transformation from a prior state; [[成为]] is the neutral "come to
be (something)." 成 as "-into" complement also in 分成, 翻译成.

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 声 "sound, voice" + 音 "sound, tone, note" (see [[音乐]], 音节).
Covers any sound (听到一个声音) and the human voice (她的声音很好听).

## Notes
声 alone: [[大声]], [[小声]], 声调 "tone (of a syllable)." 音 alone: [[口音]]
(not this level), 录音.

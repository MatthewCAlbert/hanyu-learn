## Why this combination
高速 "high-speed" (see its own entry) combined with 公路 "public road,
highway" (公 "public," see also 公共, plus 路 "road," see also 路线): a
public road built for high speed — an expressway.

## Notes
高速公路 is the full, formal name for an expressway (走高速公路 "to take
the expressway") — in casual speech this is regularly shortened to just
高速 (see its own entry, "走高速" "to take the expressway"), the standard
compression pattern for long compound place-type nouns.

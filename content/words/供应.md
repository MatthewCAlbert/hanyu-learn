## Why this combination
供 ("to supply, to provide," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 应 (here
in a "to meet, to respond to [a need]" sense, as in 应付, 反应) names
meeting a demand by providing for it: to supply.

Rated "semi" for 应's "to respond to/meet a need" sense here, distinct
from its more common role in 应该 ("should").

## Notes
供应链 ("supply chain") and 供不应求 ("supply falls short of demand," a
fixed four-character idiom pairing 供 and 应 in opposite roles) both
build on this character pair's core "meeting demand with supply" sense.

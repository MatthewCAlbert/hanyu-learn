## Why this combination
觉 "to sense, to become aware" plus the resultative 得, which marks the sensing
as achieved — "to come to sense," hence "to feel" or "to reckon." Introduces a
subjective opinion: 我觉得太贵.

## Notes
Here 觉 is *jué*; the same character is *jiào* "sleep" in [[睡觉]]. Compare
认为 (to hold the view that), which is firmer and more formal than 觉得.

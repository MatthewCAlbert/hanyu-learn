## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 健 "strong, robust, vigorous" (健身, 强健) + 康 "well, at ease,
in good order" (小康, 康复). Both adjective (身体健康) and noun (注意健康).

## Notes
Figurative: 健康的心态 "a healthy attitude," 内容不健康 "unwholesome content."
Compare [[身体]], which also carries a "health" sense.

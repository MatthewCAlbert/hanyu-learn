## Why this combination
影视 shortens 电影 "film" (影 "shadow," see also 影片) and 电视
"television" to one character from each: 影 from 电影, 视 from 电视 — a
compact way to name the two media together, following the same
compression logic as 科技.

## Notes
影视 names the film and television industry collectively (影视作品
"film and TV works," 影视公司 "film and TV company") — used specifically
when both media are meant together, rather than either alone.

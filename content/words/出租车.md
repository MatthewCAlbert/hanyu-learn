## Why this combination
出租 "to rent out, to let" (出 out + 租 rent) + 车 "vehicle" → a vehicle let out
by the ride. Parse 出租 + 车. 打出租车 / 打车 (see [[打车]]) "take a taxi."

## Notes
Colloquial: 出租 (clipped), 的士 (from Cantonese, → 打的). Taiwan usage
prefers 计程車; 出租车 is still understood there as "taxi."
出租 alone also "for rent" on a sign (房屋出租).

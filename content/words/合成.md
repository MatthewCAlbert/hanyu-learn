## Why this combination
合 "to combine" plus the resultative complement 成 "to become, to form"
gives exactly "to combine into (something)."

## Notes
合成 spans chemistry (合成材料 "synthetic material") and media (照片合成
"photo compositing") — always the act of joining separate parts into one
new whole.

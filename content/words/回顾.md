## Why this combination
回 "to turn back" and 顾 "to look at, to attend to" (see 顾's role in
顾问) combine directly: turning back to look at what has passed.

## Notes
回顾 is the formal register for reviewing the past: 年终回顾 "year-end
review," 回顾历史 "to look back on history" — more formal than everyday
回忆 "to reminisce," which leans toward personal memory.

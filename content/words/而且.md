## Why this combination
而 "and, yet" (a classical connective) + 且 "moreover, furthermore." A
near-synonym pair that adds a further, often stronger point. Correlates with
不但 / 不仅: 不但便宜，而且好吃.

## Notes
而 alone is a bookish "but / and" (华而不实). 且 alone = "for the moment"
(你且等等).

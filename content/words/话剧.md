## Why this combination
话 "speech, words" (see also 话题, 电话) combined with 剧 "drama, a
play" (see also 电视剧, 京剧): drama carried by spoken words — a modern
spoken stage play, as distinct from sung opera forms.

## Notes
话剧 specifically names Western-style spoken drama (演话剧 "to perform
in a play"), a modern import distinguished from traditional sung/
musical Chinese theatre like 京剧 "Peking opera" — the "spoken" 话
marks exactly this contrast.

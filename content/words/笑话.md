## Why this combination
笑 "to laugh" + 话 "words, talk" → words that make people laugh: 讲笑话 "tell a
joke," 闹笑话 "make a fool of oneself," 看笑话 "watch someone make a fool of
themselves." As a verb, "to laugh at" (别笑话我).

## Notes
话 as "-talk / -words" also in [[说话]], [[对话]], [[讲话]], 电话. The
verb use 笑话 (mock) is toneless on the second syllable (*xiàohua*).

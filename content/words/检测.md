## Why this combination
检 carries its "to inspect, to check" sense (as in 检查), and 测 is "to
measure," covered elsewhere in this app. Inspecting something by
measurement is exactly detection or technical testing.

Rated "semi" for 检's specific "to inspect/examine" sense, distinct from
a more generic reading of the character.

## Notes
检测 leans technical (设备检测, "equipment testing"; 病毒检测, "virus
testing") — for everyday academic testing, 考试 or plain 测试 is more
natural.

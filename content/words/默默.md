## Why this combination

默 ("silent," as in 沉默, "silent," covered elsewhere in this app) doubled
into 默默 turns the idea of silence into an adverb describing a quiet,
undemonstrative manner of acting — the same reduplication pattern seen
in 渐渐 (covered elsewhere in this app).

## Notes

默默无闻 ("unknown, unsung, lit. silently without fame") and 默默付出
("to give quietly, without seeking recognition") both build on this
word — 默默 always modifies an action done without fanfare or complaint.

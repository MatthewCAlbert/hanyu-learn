## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 细 ("fine, thin, detailed," as in 细节, "a
detail") and 致 ("exquisite, fine," an extended sense distinct from its
more common "to attain/cause," as in 一致, "identical") both
independently name careful fineness. Pairing them gives the standard
word for meticulous care.

Rated "semi" for 致's less common "exquisite/fine" sense here, distinct
from its more familiar "to attain/cause" role elsewhere.

## Notes

工作细致 ("careful, meticulous work") and 细致的观察 ("careful
observation") both build on this word — describes attentiveness to
small details, whether in manner, craftsmanship, or analysis.

## Why this combination
期 "to anticipate, a period awaited" and 望 "to look toward, to hope"
are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for hopeful expectation.

## Notes
期望 is more formal and weighty than everyday 希望: 辜负期望 "to fail to
live up to expectations," 期望值 "expectation level" — often implies a
standard someone is expected to meet.

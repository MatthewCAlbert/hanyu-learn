## Why this combination
高 "high" and 度 "degree, extent, measure" combine directly: the measured
extent of highness.

## Notes
高度 works as a noun (山的高度 "the mountain's height") and, before an
adjective, as an intensifying modifier (高度重视 "to attach great
importance to," literally "to a high degree").

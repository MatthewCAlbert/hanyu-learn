## Why this combination

语 ("language, speech," as in 汉语) plus 音 ("sound," as in 声音) names
the sound of spoken language: speech sound, or by extension recorded
voice audio.

## Notes

语音消息 ("a voice message") and 语音识别 ("speech recognition") are the
standard modern tech compounds — 语音 covers both the linguistics sense
(pronunciation/phonetics) and the everyday "audio voice" sense.

## Why this combination
院 "an institution / establishment" (see [[医院]], [[学院]], 法院) + 长
(*zhǎng*) "head, chief" — the "-chief" suffix of [[校长]], [[市长]], [[队长]].
Whatever the 院, its head is the 院长: 医院院长, 学院院长, 法院院长.

## Notes
长 is *zhǎng* here. The title's exact English rendering depends on the 院 —
"dean," "hospital director," "chief judge," "premier" (行政院长 in the ROC).

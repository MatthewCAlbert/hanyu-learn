## Why this combination
合 "to conform to, to match" (see also 合理, 适合) combined with 法 "law"
(as in 法律 "law"): conforming to the law — legal, lawful.

## Notes
合法 modifies actions and status that conform to the law (合法权益
"legal rights and interests," 合法身份 "legal status") — its opposite is
非法 "illegal," and it should not be confused with 合理 "reasonable,"
which judges soundness of reasoning rather than legal conformity.

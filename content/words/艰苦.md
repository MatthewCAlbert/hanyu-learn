## Why this combination
艰 "difficult, hard" and 苦 "bitter, hardship" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for enduring difficult conditions.

## Notes
艰苦 describes prolonged hardship: 艰苦的环境 "harsh conditions," 艰苦奋斗
"to struggle arduously" — close to 困难, though 艰苦 emphasizes endured
suffering more than a solvable problem.

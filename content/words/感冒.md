## Why this combination
感 "to feel, to be affected by" (see also 感觉, 感动) combined with 冒
"to brave, to risk exposure to [wind/cold]" (as in 冒险 "to take a
risk"): to be affected by exposure [to cold/wind] — the traditional
account of how a common cold is caught, giving the word for the illness
itself.

## Notes
感冒 names the common cold (得了感冒 "to have caught a cold") and, in
casual negative use, "to have no interest in" (不感冒 "not into it,
uninterested") — the negative idiomatic use is a modern colloquial
extension, not present in the original medical sense.

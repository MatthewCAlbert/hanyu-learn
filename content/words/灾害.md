## Why this combination
灾 "disaster, calamity" and 害 "harm" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for a destructive natural or man-made event.

## Notes
灾害 anchors 自然灾害 "natural disaster," 灾害预警 "disaster warning" —
close to 灾难, though 灾害 leans toward the destructive event itself
(especially natural ones), while 灾难 can also describe the resulting
suffering more broadly.

## Why this combination
修 "to repair, to mend" and 复 "to return, to restore" (see 复's role in
恢复) are near-synonyms combined into the formal word for restoration.

## Notes
修复 covers physical restoration (修复古迹 "to restore a historic site")
and computing (修复文件 "to repair a corrupted file") — always bringing
something damaged back to a working or original state.

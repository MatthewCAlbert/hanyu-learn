## Why this combination
事实 "fact" (see its own entry) combined with the locative suffix 上 "in
regard to, on [that footing]": grounded on the facts — a discourse
adverb introducing what is really the case.

## Notes
事实上 functions the same way as 实际上, often correcting or qualifying a
prior statement (他说没关系，但事实上他很生气 "he said it was fine, but in
fact he was very angry") — the two are close to interchangeable at this
level.

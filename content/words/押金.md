## Why this combination
押 "to pledge, to hold as security" plus 金 "money" gives exactly "money
held as security" — the standard word for a refundable deposit.

## Notes
押金 anchors 交押金 "to pay a deposit," 退押金 "to refund a deposit" —
always refundable security money (rental, equipment), distinct from
定金, a non-refundable down payment toward a purchase.

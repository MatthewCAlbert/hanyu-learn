## Why this combination

召 ("to summon, to call together") plus 开 ("to open, to start," as in
开会, "to hold a meeting") names summoning people together and starting
the proceedings: to convene.

## Notes

召开 is the formal, official register for starting a meeting or
conference (召开大会, "to convene an assembly") — the plain verb for an
everyday meeting is simply 开会.

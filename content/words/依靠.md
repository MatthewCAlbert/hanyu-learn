## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 依 ("to lean on, to rely on," covered elsewhere
in this app) and 靠 ("to lean against, to depend on") both independently
name leaning on something for support. Pairing them gives the standard
everyday word for reliance.

## Notes
依靠 works as both verb ("to rely on," 依靠自己, "to rely on oneself") and
noun ("a support, a mainstay," 精神依靠, "a source of emotional support")
without changing form.

## Why this combination
早 "early" combined with 已 "already" (as in 已经 "already"): already
[the case] early on — long since, for a long time already.

## Notes
早已 emphasises that something happened well before now, further back
than plain 已经 implies (他早已离开了 "he left long ago") — it stresses
the gap in time more strongly than 已经 alone.

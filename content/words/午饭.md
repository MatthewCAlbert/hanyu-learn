## Why this combination
午 "noon" + 饭, "cooked rice" for "meal" (see [[吃饭]]). The meal named by its
time-slot, with 早饭 and [[晚饭]].

## Notes
午餐 the formal register. 吃午饭, 午饭时间 "lunchtime." A midday nap is 午睡 /
午觉.

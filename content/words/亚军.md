## Why this combination
亚 "second, sub-, next in rank" plus 军 (here "champion," from 冠军 "first
place") gives exactly "the second-place champion" — the standard word for
a sports or competition runner-up.

## Notes
亚军 anchors 获得亚军 "to win second place" — part of a ranking triad with
冠军 "champion (1st)" and 季军 "third place," all using different
head-words for each rank.

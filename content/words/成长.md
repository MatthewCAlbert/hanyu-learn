## Why this combination
成 "to become, to complete" (see also 成熟, 成功) combined with 长 "to
grow, to increase [in size]" (as in 长大 "to grow up"): to become
[mature] through growing — to grow up, to develop.

## Notes
成长 names the gradual process of growing up or developing, physically
or in character (孩子的成长 "a child's growth/development," 成长过程 "the
process of growing up") — 长大, its everyday synonym, is the more
colloquial verb for simply "to grow up" without 成长's slightly more
formal, process-oriented weight.

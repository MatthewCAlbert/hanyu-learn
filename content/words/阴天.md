## Why this combination
阴 "shady, cloudy, the yin (dark, cool) principle" (the opposite of 阳; see
[[太阳]]) + 天 "sky / day" → a day when the sky is covered and sunless. The
forecast scale: 晴 ([[晴天]]) → [[多云]] → 阴 → 雨.

## Notes
阴 "overcast / shady" also in 阴影 "shadow," 树阴 "the shade of a tree,"
阴历 "the lunar calendar."

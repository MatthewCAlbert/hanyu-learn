## Why this combination
跟 "to follow" and 随 "to follow, to accompany" are near-synonyms
combined into a formal word for following someone or something.

## Notes
跟随 is used for both physical following (跟随导游 "to follow the tour
guide") and figurative following (跟随潮流 "to follow a trend) — a more
formal register than the everyday 跟.

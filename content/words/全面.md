## Why this combination
全 "whole, entire" (see also 全球, 全国) combined with 面 in its "side,
aspect, facet" sense (as in 方面 "aspect"): covering every side/aspect —
comprehensive, all-around.

## Notes
全面 modifies plans, understanding and reforms that cover every relevant
aspect (全面了解 "comprehensive understanding," 全面发展 "all-around
development") — its opposite is 片面 "one-sided, partial," built on the
same 面.

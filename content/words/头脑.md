## Why this combination
头 "head" (as in 石头 in an unrelated grammaticalised use, and 头发
"hair") combined with 脑 "brain" (as in 电脑 "computer," literally
"electric brain"): the head and brain together — one's mind, one's
mental capacity.

## Notes
头脑 names mental capacity and clear thinking (头脑清楚 "clear-headed,"
有头脑 "sharp-minded") and, in the fixed phrase 头脑人物, "a leader,
the person in charge" — a figurative extension from "the mind [of an
operation]" to the person who supplies it.

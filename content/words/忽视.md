## Why this combination

忽 (here "to neglect, to be careless of," an extended sense beyond its
more familiar "suddenly," as in 忽然) plus 视 ("to look at, to view," as
in 电视) names looking at something without due attention: to overlook.

Rated "semi" for 忽's extended "to neglect" sense here, distinct from
its more common "suddenly" meaning.

## Notes

不容忽视 ("cannot be ignored, lit. not permitting neglect") is a common
set phrase — 忽视 implies a failure of attention, distinct from 无视
("to disregard," a more deliberate act of ignoring).

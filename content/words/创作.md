## Why this combination
创 "to start, to found, to originate" (see also 创造) combined with 作
"to make, to create" (see also 作品, 作者): to originate a creation — to
create a work of art or literature.

## Notes
创作 is used specifically for artistic and literary creation (文学创作
"literary creation," 创作歌曲 "to compose a song") — 创造, its close
relative, is broader and covers creating anything new, not only artistic
work.

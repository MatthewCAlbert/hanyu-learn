## Why this combination
香 "fragrant, tasty" plus 肠 "intestine" (used for the casing) gives
exactly "a tasty (filled) intestine" — describing the traditional method
of stuffing meat into a cleaned intestine casing.

## Notes
香肠 anchors 广式香肠 "Cantonese-style sausage," 香肠肠衣 "sausage casing"
— the everyday word for sausage, whether made with a natural or synthetic
casing today.

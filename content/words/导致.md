## Why this combination
导 is "to guide, to lead" (as in 领导, 指导), and 致 is "to bring about, to
cause to arrive at (a state)" (as in 导致's own less common but related
以致, 致命 "to bring about death"). "To guide toward bringing about" a
result is a fittingly formal, causal "to lead to."

Rated "semi" for 致's less familiar "to bring about" sense, most learners'
first real exposure to it.

## Notes
导致 almost always introduces a negative or undesirable consequence
(导致失败, 导致严重后果) — for neutral or positive outcomes, 使得 or 带来 read
more naturally.

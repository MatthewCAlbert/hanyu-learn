## Why this combination
发 "to send out, to emit, to issue" (see also 发展, 发表) combined with
the directional complement 出 "out, to emerge": to send outward — to
issue a document, or to emit a sound or light.

## Notes
发出 covers issuing formal notices (发出通知 "to issue a notice") and
emitting a sound, light or smell (发出声音 "to make a sound," 发出光芒
"to give off light") — the resultative 出 marks that whatever was sent
successfully emerged.

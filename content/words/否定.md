## Why this combination
否 "to negate, no" (see also 否认) combined with 定 "to decide, to
settle" (see also 决定, 规定): to settle as a negation — to negate, to
reject.

## Notes
否定 rejects a claim, value or plan as not valid (否定的答案 "a
negative answer," 全盘否定 "to totally reject/negate") — its opposite
is 肯定 "to affirm, certainly," built the same way with 肯 "to agree to"
in place of 否.

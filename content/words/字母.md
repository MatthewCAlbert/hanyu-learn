## Why this combination

字 ("a character, a word") plus 母 (here "base, root," an extended sense
beyond its literal "mother," as in 母亲) names the base units that
"mother" or generate written words: alphabet letters.

Rated "semi" for 母's extended "root/base unit" sense here, distinct from
its everyday "mother" meaning.

## Notes

字母表 ("the alphabet, lit. letter table") and 大写字母 ("capital letter,
lit. big-written letter") both build on this word — 字母 specifically
names alphabetic letters, distinct from 汉字, Chinese characters
themselves.

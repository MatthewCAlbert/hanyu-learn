## Why this combination
初 "beginning, initial" (see also 初步, 初中) combined with 级 "a level,
a rank, a grade" (see also 年级, 高级 "advanced/senior level"): the
beginning level — junior, elementary.

## Notes
初级 names the entry-level tier of a course, qualification or
organisation (初级中学 "junior middle school," the full form 初中
abbreviates, and 初级课程 "beginner course") — its opposite is 高级
"advanced, senior," built the same way with 高 in place of 初.

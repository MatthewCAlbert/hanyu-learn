## Why this combination

专 ("focused, specialized, exclusive," as in 专业, "a major/specialty")
takes 心 ("heart, mind") as its object: focusing one's mind.

## Notes

专心学习 ("to study attentively") and 不专心 ("not focused, distracted")
both build on this word — 专心 names an undivided mental focus, close in
spirit to 认真 ("earnest") but specifically about attention rather than
diligence.

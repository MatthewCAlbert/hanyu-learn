## Why this combination
缺 "to lack, to be short of, incomplete" (see also 缺少) combined with
点 "a point, a spot" (as in 特点 "characteristic," literally "a
special point"): a point where something is lacking — a shortcoming.

## Notes
缺点 names a specific weakness or flaw in a person or thing (性格缺点
"a character flaw," 找缺点 "to find fault") — its opposite is 优点
"strong point, merit," built the same way with 优 "excellent" in place
of 缺.

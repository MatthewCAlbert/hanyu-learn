## Why this combination

综 ("to combine threads on a loom," part of the 纟-semantic family
covered elsewhere in this app alongside 纷/络/统/维, all rooted in
weaving) plus 合 ("to close, to join, to combine," as in 合作) names
weaving separate strands into one whole: to synthesize, comprehensive.

## Notes

综合 modifies things assembled from many parts (综合能力, "comprehensive
ability"; 综合医院, "general hospital") and, as a verb, means to pull
scattered material together into one account (综合各方意见, "to
synthesize views from all sides").

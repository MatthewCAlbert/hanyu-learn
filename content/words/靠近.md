## Why this combination
靠 "to lean against, to be near" and 近 "close, near" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard verb for drawing near or being nearby.

## Notes
靠近 works for both movement (靠近窗户 "to move closer to the window") and
static proximity (靠近市中心 "close to the city centre") — a general-
purpose "near/approach" verb.

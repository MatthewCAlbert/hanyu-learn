## Why this combination
坚 "firm, unyielding" (recall its root 臤, "hard, worthy") and 持 "to
hold" combine directly: holding onto something firmly, without letting
go — to persist.

## Notes
坚持 takes a course of action, a belief, or a habit as its object
(坚持锻炼 "to keep up exercising," 坚持自己的意见 "to insist on one's own
opinion") — always about not giving up under some pressure to stop.

## Why this combination
自 "self" combines with 豪 "bold, heroic" (see 豪's own etymology — a
fierce, formidable boar) — feeling boldly confident about oneself and
one's achievements, i.e. pride.

## Notes
自豪 is a positive, healthy pride distinct from 骄傲, which can carry a
negative "arrogant" reading depending on context: 为...感到自豪 "to feel
proud of..." is always a compliment, never a criticism.

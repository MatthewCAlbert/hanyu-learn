## Why this combination
直 "straight" plus 线 "line" gives exactly "a straight line" — the
geometric term, extended adverbially to describe a sharp, undeviating
rise or fall.

## Notes
直线 names the geometric shape (画一条直线 "draw a straight line") and,
before a verb, an intense rate of change: 直线上升 "to rise sharply/
steeply," treating the trend line's steepness as literally "straight up."

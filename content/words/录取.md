## Why this combination

录 ("to record, to register," as in 记录) plus 取 ("to take, to select,"
as in 取得) names selecting and formally registering someone: to admit,
to recruit.

Rated "semi" for the shift from "recording" to the specific institutional
sense of admission.

## Notes

被…录取 ("to be admitted by…") is the standard passive frame for school
or job admissions (被大学录取, "admitted by a university") — 录取通知书
("admission letter") is the document that confirms it.

## Why this combination
感 "to feel" + resultative 到 "reach" — the feeling registers. Takes an emotion
or state directly: 感到高兴, 感到累, 感到奇怪.

## Notes
感到 (verb + object: 感到害怕) vs. [[感觉]] (noun, or verb + clause: 感觉他不高兴)
vs. 觉得 (verb + clause, opinion). 感到 is a touch more formal.

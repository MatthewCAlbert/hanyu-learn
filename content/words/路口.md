## Why this combination
路 "road" + 口 "opening, mouth" — the point where roads open into one another.
Same 口 = "opening" as in [[门口]], 出口 (exit), 入口 (entrance).

## Notes
十字路口 "crossroads" (十字 "cross-shaped"), 路口右转 "turn right at the
junction."

## Why this combination
商 "merchant, trade" (see also 商业, 商人) combined with 品 "an item, a
product" (see also 作品, 食品): an item of trade — a commodity, goods.

## Notes
商品 names anything bought and sold in commerce (商品经济 "commodity
economy," 打折商品 "discounted goods") — 产品, its close relative, names
a manufactured product regardless of whether it is yet being traded.

## Why this combination
生 "to be born, to live" and 命 "life, one's allotted span" are close
synonyms, both naming the fact of being alive, paired for a fuller,
more formal word than either alone.

## Notes
生命 names life as a biological/existential state (生命的意义 "the
meaning of life"); contrast 生活, which names the activity of living
day to day, and 命, which alone leans toward fate or a specific
lifespan.

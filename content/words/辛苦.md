## Why this combination
辛 and 苦 are near-synonyms, both meaning "bitter, laborious, hard" —
doubled up for emphasis into the everyday word for exhausting effort.

## Notes
辛苦了! is a common, warm way to acknowledge someone's hard work (said to
a colleague after a long task) — functionally closer to "thanks for your
effort" than to a literal complaint about hardship.

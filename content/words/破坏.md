## Why this combination
破 "broken, torn, damaged" (as in 打破 "to break") and 坏 "bad, to
collapse, to ruin" (as in 坏了 "broken down") are close synonyms, paired
into the standard verb for causing damage or destruction.

## Notes
破坏 takes both physical and abstract objects — 破坏环境 "to damage the
environment," 破坏关系 "to ruin a relationship" — and is stronger and
more deliberate-sounding than plain 坏, which merely describes something
as broken or bad without naming an agent.

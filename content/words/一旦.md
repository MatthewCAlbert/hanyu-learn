## Why this combination
"One day" (一 + 旦) narrows, in classical usage, to "a single, sudden
point in time" — and from there generalizes to the conditional
connective "once [X happens], then..." The literal "one dawn" is
recoverable, but only once you see the leap from "a single day" to "a
single triggering moment."

## Notes
一旦 sets up a hypothetical trigger, almost always followed by a
consequence clause: 一旦决定，就不要后悔 "once you've decided, don't look
back." Distinct from 一次 "one time," which counts occurrences rather than
framing a condition.

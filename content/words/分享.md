## Why this combination
分 "to divide" and 享 "to enjoy" combine directly: dividing up something
enjoyable so more than one person gets to enjoy it.

## Notes
分享 is reserved for good things — joy, knowledge, resources (分享经验 "to
share experience," 分享快乐 "to share happiness") — unlike neutral 分 "to
divide," which carries no such positive connotation.

## Why this combination

点 ("to point to, to select one by one," as in 一点, "a little/a point")
takes 名 ("name," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 名人/名片) as
its object: singling out names one by one — to call the roll.

## Notes

老师点名 ("the teacher calls the roll") is the standard classroom
context — 点名 also extends figuratively to "singling someone out" for
criticism or mention without a literal attendance check.

## Why this combination
出 "to come out of, to emerge from" plus the classical preposition 于
"from, at" (see 于's grammatical role in 用于) gives "to emerge from [a
motive/source]" — a formal way to state the origin of an action.

## Notes
出于 always introduces a motive or origin: 出于好奇 "out of curiosity," 出
于安全考虑 "for safety reasons" — a written-register alternative to
everyday 因为.

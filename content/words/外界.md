## Why this combination
外 "outside" and 界 "boundary, realm" (see 界's role in 世界) combine
directly: the realm beyond a given boundary — the outside world.

## Notes
外界 anchors 外界压力 "outside pressure," 与外界隔绝 "cut off from the
outside world" — always framed relative to an inside group or space it
lies beyond.

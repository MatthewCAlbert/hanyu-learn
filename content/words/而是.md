## Why this combination
而 is the classical conjunction "and yet, but" (surviving in modern
Mandarin mainly bound into words like 然而, 而且), and 是 is the copula
"is." "而是" only ever appears as the second half of the fixed frame
不是...而是... ("not X, but rather Y") — correcting a claim by naming what is
actually true instead.

Marked "idiom" because the whole meaning depends on that two-part frame,
not on 而是 as a free-standing phrase.

## Notes
不是...而是... always negates the first item explicitly with 不是 — 而是
alone, without a preceding 不是, does not carry the "rather" sense on its
own.

## Why this combination

男 ("male," as in 男人) plus 女 ("female," as in 女人) names the two sexes
together, or (as a modifier) marks a distinction between them.

## Notes

男女平等 ("gender equality"), 男女老少 ("men, women, old and young — i.e.
everyone"), and 男女朋友 ("boyfriend/girlfriend, lit. male-female
friend") all build on this basic pairing.

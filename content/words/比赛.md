## Why this combination
比 "to compare" and 赛 "to compete" reinforce each other directly: a
competition is, at its core, a structured comparison of who performs
better. Transparent once both characters are known.

## Notes
比 alone already forms comparative constructions (他比我高 "he is taller
than me"); 比赛 specialises that comparing into a formal, organised
contest rather than an everyday comparison.

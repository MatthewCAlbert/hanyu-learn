## Why this combination
官 ("an official," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 方 ("a side, a
party [to an arrangement]," as in 双方) names the official/government
side of an arrangement, as opposed to unofficial or private parties.

Rated "semi" for 方's "a side/party" sense here, distinct from its more
common "direction, method" meanings elsewhere.

## Notes
官方声明 ("an official statement") and 官方消息 ("official news/word") both
mark information as coming from a government or authoritative source,
distinct from unverified rumor.

## Why this combination
阳 "sun, the yang [bright] principle" (as in 太阳 "sun") combined with 光
"light, brightness" (as in 光线 "light ray"): the light of the sun —
sunshine, extended figuratively to an open, upbeat personality.

## Notes
阳光 names literal sunshine (阳光明媚 "bright and sunny") and, as a
modifier in modern usage, describes a person or image as open, positive
and above-board (阳光男孩 "a sunny/upbeat boy," 阳光透明 "open and
transparent [of governance]") — a figurative extension parallel to
English "sunny disposition."

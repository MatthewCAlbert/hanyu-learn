## Why this combination
池 "a pond, a pool" plus the noun suffix 子 gives exactly "a pond/pool"
— the suffix adds no meaning of its own, matching the pattern of 桌子,
椅子.

## Notes
池子 anchors 洗澡池子 "bathhouse pool," and colloquially, the dance floor
or old theater stalls — the everyday, colloquial counterpart to more
formal words like 水池 or 游泳池.

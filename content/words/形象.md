## Why this combination
形 "form, shape" (as in 形式 "form, formality") and 象 "image, appearance,
likeness" (see also 对象, 印象) are close synonyms, paired into the noun
for the overall impression or appearance something presents.

## Notes
形象 names a public image or persona (公司形象 "corporate image," 树立
形象 "to build an image") and, as an adjective, "vivid, concrete,
figurative" of language or description (形象的比喻 "a vivid metaphor") —
the adjective use praises description for painting a clear mental
picture.

## Why this combination
大 ("big") plus 型 ("a type, a category by shape/size," covered elsewhere
in this app) names the large category of size a thing belongs to: a
large-scale version of something.

## Notes
大型 always precedes a noun as an attributive modifier (大型活动, "a
large-scale event"; 大型设备, "large equipment") — it does not stand
alone as a predicate the way plain 大 can.

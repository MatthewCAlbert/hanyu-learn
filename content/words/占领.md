## Why this combination
占 "to occupy, to take" and 领 (here "to take possession of") are
near-synonyms combined into the standard military/territorial word for
seizing and holding ground.

## Notes
占领 is used for taking control by force: 占领城市 "to capture a city,"
占领市场 "to capture (dominate) a market" (figurative extension) —
distinct from 占据, which can describe occupying a position without
necessarily implying conquest.

## Why this combination
鲜 (here "fresh, vivid") and 艳 "gorgeous, brightly colored" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard word for vivid, eye-catching
color.

## Notes
鲜艳 anchors 色彩鲜艳 "vividly colored," 鲜艳的花朵 "brightly colored
flowers" — always about strong, saturated color, whether in nature,
clothing, or design.

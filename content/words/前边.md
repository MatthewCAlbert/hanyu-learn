## Why this combination
前 "front, ahead" + the locative suffix 边 — the front member of the 前边 /
[[后边]] pair. 前 also means "earlier," so 前边说过 = "mentioned earlier."

## Notes
前面 is the exact synonym. Bare 前 as a suffix: 以前, 从前, 面前.

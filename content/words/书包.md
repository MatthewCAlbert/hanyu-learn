## Why this combination
书 "book" + 包 "bag" — the bag you carry books in. 包 is the generic carry-bag
morpheme of [[钱包]], 背包, 书包.

## Notes
背书包 "wear a schoolbag." Figurative 思想包袱 uses a different 包 word for a
mental "burden."

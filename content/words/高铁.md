## Why this combination

高铁 abbreviates 高速铁路 ("high-speed railway") by taking 高 from 高速
("high-speed") and 铁 from 铁路 ("railway, lit. iron + road") — a
compact truncation of a four-character phrase into two syllables.

Rated "semi" for the abbreviation obscuring 速 "speed" and 路 "road"
along the way, leaving only "high" and "iron" visible.

## Notes

坐高铁 ("to take the high-speed rail") is now the everyday phrase for
long-distance train travel in China — 铁路 itself remains the general,
unabbreviated word for "railway."

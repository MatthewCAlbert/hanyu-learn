## Why this combination
孩 already means "child"; 子 is the unstressed noun suffix (originally "child,
small thing") that turns a bare root into a countable everyday noun — the same
子 as in 桌子, 杯子, [[儿子]]. Neutral tone on 子 marks it as the suffix.

## Notes
Covers both "a child" in general and "my kids" (我有两个孩子). For a small child
specifically, 小孩儿.

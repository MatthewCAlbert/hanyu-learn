## Why this combination
回 "to return" acting on the object 信 "letter" gives exactly "to send a
letter back" — splittable, as in 回了一封信.

## Notes
回信 works as verb ("请尽快回信" "please reply soon") and noun ("收到他的
回信" "received his reply letter") — now extended informally to any
written reply, not just postal letters.

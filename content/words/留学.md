## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 留, here "to stay, to remain [in a place]" (as
in 保留) takes 学 "to study" (see also 学习, 留学生's own logic) as its
object — literally "to remain [abroad] to study" — to study abroad.

## Notes
留学 always implies studying in a foreign country (出国留学 "to go
abroad to study," 留学生 "international/study-abroad student," see its
own entry) — 学习, the general verb "to study," carries no such
implication of studying away from home.

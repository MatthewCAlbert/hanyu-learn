## Why this combination
Built on 意思 in its sense "how one feels about doing something" (好意思 = "to
have the face to do it"). 不好意思 = "one doesn't feel good about it" — shy,
embarrassed, or apologetic: 不好意思，麻烦你了; 他不好意思拒绝.

## Notes
A softer, more social apology than [[对不起]] — for minor inconvenience or
awkwardness rather than genuine wrongdoing. Also "too embarrassed to":
不好意思开口.

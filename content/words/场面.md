## Why this combination
场 "field, scene, occasion" and 面 "face, aspect" (see 面's role in 画面)
combine directly: the visible face a particular occasion or scene
presents.

## Notes
场面 covers dramatic spectacle (壮观的场面 "a spectacular scene") and
social occasions requiring composure (撑场面 "to keep up appearances,"
场面话 "polite/formal small talk") — always about a scene's visible,
social presentation.

## Why this combination
事 "affair, matter" + 情, here "circumstance, state of affairs" (情况) rather
than "feeling." A near-synonym pair for an abstract "thing" — an event, a task,
a problem: 有事情要做, 出了点事情.

## Notes
事情 = an affair / matter; 东西 = a physical thing. 情 toneless (*shìqing*).

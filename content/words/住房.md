## Why this combination
住 "to live, to dwell" (see [[住院]], [[住院]]'s cousin 居住) + 房 "house, room"
(see [[房子]], [[房间]]) → a place to live, housing as a resource:
住房问题 "the housing problem," 住房条件.

## Notes
住房 = housing / accommodation (often as a social or economic issue); [[房子]] =
a house / flat as an object; [[房间]] = a room within one.

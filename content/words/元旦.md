## Why this combination
元 "first, primary" plus 旦 "dawn, daybreak" gives exactly "the first
dawn (of the year)" — the standard word for the first day of the solar
(Gregorian) calendar year.

## Notes
元旦 anchors 元旦假期 "New Year's Day holiday" — always the January 1st
solar-calendar holiday, distinct from 春节 "Chinese/Lunar New Year," a
separate and more culturally significant holiday weeks later.

## Why this combination

寒 ("cold," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 寒冷) plus 假
("a vacation," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 暑假) names the
vacation taken during the cold season: winter break.

## Notes

寒假 pairs directly with 暑假 (covered elsewhere in this app) as the two
main school breaks — 寒/暑 mark the season's temperature extreme, not
the literal weather during the break.

## Why this combination
沙特 is the phonetic rendering of Saudi; 阿拉伯 is the same stem as in the HSK word
阿拉伯语. Two transcriptions side by side, not a native compound.

## Notes
Everyday short form [[沙特]]. 阿拉伯 is Arabia, not "ā + pull + uncle."

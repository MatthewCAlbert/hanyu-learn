## Why this combination
赤道 "equator" is a real calque of Equatorial; [[几内亚]] is the phonetic rendering
of Guinea. Half translated, half transcribed.

## Notes
赤道 itself is 赤 "red" + 道 "path," the astronomical equator. Contrast [[厄瓜多尔]],
whose name means "equator" in Spanish but is transcribed, not translated.

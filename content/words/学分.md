## Why this combination

学 ("to study," as in 学习) plus 分 ("a point, a mark," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 学分's sibling words like 得分) names
points earned through study: academic credits.

Rated "semi" for the specific institutional "credit unit" sense, one
step from 分's more general "point/mark" meaning.

## Notes

修学分 ("to earn credits, lit. cultivate credits") and 学分不够 ("not
enough credits") both build on this word — names the unit universities
use to track progress toward a degree, distinct from 分数 (covered
elsewhere in this app), an exam score.

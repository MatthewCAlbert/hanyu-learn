## Why this combination
周 "a circuit, all the way around" (as in 一周 "a week/one full circuit")
combined with 围 "to surround, to encircle" (as in 范围): the space
running all the way around something — its surroundings.

## Notes
周围 names the physical area encircling a place or person (周围的环境
"the surrounding environment," 我周围的人 "the people around me") —
环境, its near-synonym, refers more to the qualitative conditions of a
setting than to the physical space itself.

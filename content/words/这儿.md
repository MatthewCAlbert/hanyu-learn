## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + the erhua suffix 儿, which here does the work 里 does
in [[这里]] — turning a pointing word into a place. 这儿 / 那儿 / 哪儿 is the
northern, colloquial set; 这里 / 那里 / 哪里 the neutral one.

## Notes
With a person: 我这儿 "my place / where I am." 儿 is the toneless *-r*, not
full *ér*.

## Why this combination

事 ("a matter, an affair," as in 事情) plus 先 ("before, first," as in
首先) names the time before a matter takes place: beforehand.

## Notes

事先 always precedes the verb it modifies (事先通知, "to notify in
advance") — 事后 ("after the fact") is its direct mirror-image partner,
built from the same 事 root.

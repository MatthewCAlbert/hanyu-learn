## Why this combination

热 ("hot, warm," and figuratively "enthusiastic," as in 热情) plus 闹
("noisy, bustling with activity," as in 闹钟, "an alarm clock [lit.
noise-making clock]") names a warm, buzzing commotion: lively.

Rated "semi" for 热's figurative "enthusiastic/warm" contribution here,
one step from its plainer literal "hot" sense.

## Notes

热闹 describes a positive kind of noisy energy — a festival, market, or
party (很热闹, "very lively/bustling") — distinct from plain 吵 ("noisy,"
which is usually unwelcome).

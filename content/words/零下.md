## Why this combination
零 "zero" + 下 "below" → temperatures below 0°C: 零下十度 "minus ten." The
mirror 零上 "above zero" exists but is rarely said, since positive temperatures
are just given as a number.

## Notes
下 "below" as the counterpart of 上 "above" also in [[以下]], [[楼下]],
[[地下]]. 零 "zero" also in 零钱 "small change," 零食 "snacks."

## Why this combination
月 "moon" plus 球 "sphere, ball" gives exactly "the moon (sphere)" — the
scientific/astronomical register word, parallel to 地球 "Earth" and
月亮's more everyday, poetic register.

## Notes
月球 is used in scientific and technical contexts: 月球表面 "the lunar
surface," 登陆月球 "to land on the moon" — where everyday speech about
the moon in the sky uses 月亮.

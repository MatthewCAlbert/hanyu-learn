## Why this combination
Not securely decomposable. 亮 "bright, shining" fits the sense; the first
syllable 漂 (normally *piāo* "to float" or *piǎo* "to bleach") does not
obviously contribute meaning here and its role is disputed — it may be a
phonetic writing of a colloquial word. Best learned whole.

## Notes
Second syllable toneless (*piàoliang*). Figurative: 事情办得漂亮 "handled
beautifully," 说漂亮话 "fine words (with little behind them)."

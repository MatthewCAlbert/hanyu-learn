## Why this combination

食 ("food, to eat," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 食物/粮食)
plus 堂 ("a hall," as in 课堂, "a classroom [lit. lesson + hall]") names
the hall where people eat: a cafeteria.

## Notes

学校食堂 ("school cafeteria") and 员工食堂 ("staff canteen") both build on
this word — an institutional dining hall, distinct from 饭馆/饭店, a
commercial restaurant one pays to eat at individually.

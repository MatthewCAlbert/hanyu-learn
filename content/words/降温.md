## Why this combination

降 ("to descend," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 降落) takes
温 ("temperature, warmth," as in 温暖) as its object: temperature going
down.

## Notes

天气降温了 ("the weather has turned colder") is the literal weather sense;
figuratively, 给…降温 ("to cool down [tensions/enthusiasm]") extends the
same falling-temperature image to a situation calming down.

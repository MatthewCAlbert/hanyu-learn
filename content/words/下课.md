## Why this combination
课 "lesson" as the object of 下 "to come off" — the counterpart of 上 in
[[上课]]. 下课了 "class is over," 一下课就走 "left the moment class ended."

## Notes
Figurative, of a sports coach or official: 下课 "be sacked" (chanted by fans).
The per-day pair is [[上学]] / 放学; the per-lesson pair 上课 / 下课.

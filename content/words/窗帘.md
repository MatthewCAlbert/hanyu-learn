## Why this combination
窗 "window" plus 帘 "a curtain, a screen" gives exactly "a window
curtain" — a fully compositional, everyday household word.

## Notes
窗帘 anchors 拉开窗帘 "to open the curtains," 窗帘布 "curtain fabric" — the
standard word, distinct from 门帘 "door curtain," which uses the same
帘 for a different location.

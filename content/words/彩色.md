## Why this combination
彩 "colourful, variegated" (as in 精彩 "wonderful," literally "refined
colour") combined with 色 "colour" (as in 颜色): colourful colour — full
colour, multicoloured.

## Notes
彩色 modifies things printed or displayed in full colour, as opposed to
black-and-white (彩色照片 "colour photo," 彩色电视 "colour television")
— its opposite is 黑白 "black and white."

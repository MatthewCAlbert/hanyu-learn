## Why this combination
制 (here "to control") and 约 "to restrict, to bind" (see 约束's shared
root) are near-synonyms combined into a formal word for one factor
limiting another.

## Notes
制约 anchors 相互制约 "mutual constraint," 受地形制约 "constrained by
terrain" — always describes a structural, often systemic limiting
relationship between factors, common in political and scientific writing.

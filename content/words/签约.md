## Why this combination
签 "to sign" (see 签's own etymology) acting on the object 约 "a contract,
an agreement" gives exactly "to sign a contract" — splittable, as in
签了约 "has signed a contract."

## Notes
签约 anchors 签约球员 "a contracted [sports] player," 正式签约 "to
formally sign a contract" — the standard business/sports term for
entering a binding agreement.

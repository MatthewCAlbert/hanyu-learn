## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 真 "true, real" + 正 "upright, proper, exact." Together, "the real,
proper thing" as against a nominal or partial one: 真正的朋友, 真正理解.

## Notes
真正 (attributive / emphatic) vs. 真的 (predicate: 是真的) vs. 真 (adverb before
adjectives: 真好). 正 also = "exactly" in [[正好]], [[正是]].

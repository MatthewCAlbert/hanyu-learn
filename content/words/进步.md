## Why this combination
进 "to advance, to move forward" (see also 进一步, 进展) combined with
步 "a step, pace": to advance a step — progress, improvement.

## Notes
进步 applies to a person's own skill or a society's development (你的
汉语进步了 "your Chinese has improved," 社会进步 "social progress") —
进展, its close relative, is used more for the headway made on a project
or task rather than a person's or society's own betterment.

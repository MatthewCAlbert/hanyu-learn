## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 伤 ("wound, injury," as in 受伤) and 害 ("harm,"
as in 害处, 危害) both independently name damage done to a person. Pairing
them broadens the word to cover both physical and emotional harm.

## Notes
伤害 covers emotional as well as physical injury (伤害感情, "to hurt
someone's feelings") — broader than 受伤, which stays mostly physical.

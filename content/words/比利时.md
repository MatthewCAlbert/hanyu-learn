## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of Belgique / Belgium (比利時). 比, 利 and 时
are chosen for sound. 比 is not "to compare"; 时 is not "time."

## Notes
利 as a phonetic also in [[意大利]], [[智利]] — different names, same convenient
syllable.

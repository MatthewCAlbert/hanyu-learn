## Why this combination
始 "beginning, start" (as in 开始 "to begin") combined with 终 "end"
(as in 最终 "finally, ultimately"): from beginning to end — throughout,
all along.

## Notes
始终 asserts that something held true across an entire span without
change (始终没有放弃 "never gave up, the whole way through," 始终如一
"consistent from start to finish") — it is stronger and more literary
than plain 一直 "always, continuously."

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 看 ("to look, to see") and 望 ("to look toward,
to hope," as in 希望, "to hope [lit. rare-hope + look-toward]") both
independently name directing attention toward someone. Pairing them
gives the formal word for paying a visit.

## Notes

看望病人 ("to visit a patient") and 看望父母 ("to visit one's parents")
both build on this word — 看望 always implies a caring, often formal
visit to check on someone, distinct from casual 看 or 找, "to see/find"
someone for any reason.

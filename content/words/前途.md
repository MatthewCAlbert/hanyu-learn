## Why this combination

前 ("front, ahead," as in 以前) plus 途 ("a path, a way," as in 用途,
"use/purpose") names the path lying ahead: one's future prospects.

Rated "semi" for the figurative "life's path" reading of 途, one step
removed from its literal "road" sense.

## Notes

有前途 ("to have a bright future/prospects") is the standard predicate
use — 前途 names one's prospects generally, distinct from 未来 ("the
future" as a time period).

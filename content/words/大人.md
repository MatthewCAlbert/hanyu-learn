## Why this combination
大 "big, grown" + 人 "person" → a full-grown person, an adult (as against
小孩). Historically also a term of address to a senior official (青天大人).

## Notes
大人 (adult) contrasts with [[小孩儿]] / 孩子. The honorific sense survives only
in period drama. 成人 / 成年人 is the formal "adult."

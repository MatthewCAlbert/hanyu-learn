## Why this combination
A three-syllable phonetic rendering of México. 墨, 西 and 哥 are chosen for
sound, not for "ink + west + elder brother."

## Notes
哥 as a phonetic closer also in 芝加哥 (Chicago). Contrast 墨 "ink" in 墨水.

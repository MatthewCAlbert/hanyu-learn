## Why this combination
打, here a light verb "to strike, to carry out [an action]" (as in 打
电话 "to make a phone call") combined with the resultative complement 破
"broken, torn" (see also 破坏): to strike something to the point of
breaking — to break, to smash.

## Notes
打破 takes both physical objects (打破杯子 "to break a cup") and abstract
limits (打破纪录 "to break a record," 打破沉默 "to break the silence") —
the resultative 破 always marks that the striking action succeeded in
breaking something.

## Why this combination
质 ("substance, the real nature of a thing," covered elsewhere in this
app) plus 量 ("quantity, an amount") together name the measurable amount
of true substance something has — how much genuine quality it contains.
In physics, the same logic gives 质量 its technical sense "mass": the
measurable amount of matter an object contains.

Rated "semi" because the compound covers two genuinely distinct modern
senses (everyday "quality" and physics "mass") from one shared
etymological logic.

## Notes
Context decides which sense is meant: 产品质量 ("product quality") versus
质量守恒 ("conservation of mass," a physics term) — the same two
characters, two different technical domains.

## Why this combination
虚 "empty, receptive" plus 心 "heart, mind" gives exactly "an empty
(i.e. receptive, not full of oneself) mind" — a subject-predicate
description of humility.

## Notes
虚心 anchors 虚心接受意见 "to humbly accept feedback," 虚心学习 "to learn
with an open mind" — always about being receptive to correction and
learning, opposite of 骄傲 "arrogant."

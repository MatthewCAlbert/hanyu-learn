## Why this combination
指 "to point (at), to indicate" and 标 "a mark, a sign" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for a measurable target or benchmark.

## Notes
指标 anchors 经济指标 "economic indicator," 完成指标 "to meet a quota" —
covers both planned targets (production quotas) and measured indicators
(statistics), always something quantifiable.

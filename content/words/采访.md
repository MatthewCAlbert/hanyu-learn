## Why this combination
采 carries its "to gather, to collect" sense (as in 采取, 采用), and 访 is
"to visit, to inquire of" (as in 访问, 拜访). Visiting someone to gather
information from them is exactly what a journalist does when
interviewing a subject.

Rated "semi" for 访's "to visit for the purpose of inquiry" sense, a step
more specific than a plain social visit.

## Notes
采访 is specifically journalistic (记者采访, "a reporter conducts an
interview") — for a general social visit with no information-gathering
purpose, 拜访 or plain 看望 is the natural choice instead.

## Why this combination
园 "garden" and 林 "forest, grove" are near-synonyms combined into the
standard word for a designed landscape garden.

## Notes
园林 anchors 苏州园林 "Suzhou gardens (a famous classical garden style),"
园林设计 "landscape design" — refers to a cultivated, often artistically
designed green space, more elaborate than a plain 公园 "park."

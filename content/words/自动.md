## Why this combination
自 "self" (see also 自己, 自然) combined with 动 "to move, to act" (as in
运动 "movement"): acting by itself — automatic, needing no outside
prompt, whether that "self" is a machine or a person's own initiative.

## Notes
自动 describes machines that operate without manual control (自动门
"automatic door") and, of a person, acting without being told (自动
帮忙 "to help of one's own accord") — the same word covers mechanical
automation and voluntary human initiative.

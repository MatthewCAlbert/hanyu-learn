## Why this combination
医 "medicine, to heal" + 生 in its old "person, practitioner" sense — the same
生 as in [[学生]], [[先生]]. A person of medicine.

## Notes
看医生 / 看病 "see a doctor." 大夫 (*dàifu*) is the common northern synonym;
医师 is the formal register.

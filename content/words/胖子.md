## Why this combination

胖 ("fat," as in 他很胖) plus the nominal suffix 子 names a person
characterized by that trait: a fat person.

## Notes

胖子 is casual and can read as blunt or teasing depending on context —
more formal or medical registers prefer 肥胖 ("obesity/obese") instead.

## Why this combination
定 "settled, decided" carries most of the weight; 肯 "to agree, to be
willing" adds the sense of a positive, affirming stance rather than a
neutral one. Together: something affirmed as settled — "certain,
definite," or, as a verb, "to confirm/affirm."

## Notes
肯定 works as an adverb (肯定会来 "will definitely come"), an adjective
(肯定的答复 "an affirmative reply"), and a verb (肯定他的贡献 "to
acknowledge his contribution") — three grammatical roles on one word.

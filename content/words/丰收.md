## Why this combination
丰 "abundant, plentiful" plus 收 "harvest" gives exactly "an abundant
harvest" — the standard agricultural word, extended figuratively to any
rich yield of effort.

## Notes
丰收 anchors 粮食丰收 "a bumper grain harvest," and figuratively, 丰收的
一年 "a fruitful year (of achievements)" — the metaphor extends farming
abundance to any productive outcome.

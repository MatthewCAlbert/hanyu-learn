## Why this combination
大 "big, vast" + 海 "sea" → the open sea, the ocean as a vast expanse — more
evocative than plain 海: 面对大海, 大海捞针 "look for a needle in the ocean."

## Notes
海 "sea" also in [[海边]], 上海, 海外. 大 here adds scale and a note of awe,
as in 大自然 (see [[大自然]]).

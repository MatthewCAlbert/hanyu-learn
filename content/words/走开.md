## Why this combination
走 "to walk, to leave" (see [[走路]], [[走过]], [[走进]]) + resultative 开
"away, off, aside" (the same 开 as [[分开]], [[离开]]) → to move off from a
spot: 请走开一点 "please step aside." As a command, 走开！is a curt "get lost."

## Notes
开 "away / aside" as a resultative also in 让开 "get out of the way,"
躲开 "dodge," 挪开 "shift aside."

## Why this combination
Synonym pair: 请 "to request, please" (see [[请问]], [[请假]]) + 求 "to seek, to
beg" (see [[要求]], 求助). Asking for something, politely and often earnestly:
请求帮助, 提出请求.

## Notes
请求 (polite, may be earnest) is softer than [[要求]] (a demand / stipulation)
and firmer than 请 alone. 求 "seek" also in 需求, 追求.

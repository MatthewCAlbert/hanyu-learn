## Why this combination
表, here in its "outer, outward-facing" sense (see also 表达, 表现)
combined with 面 "face, surface" (as in 上面 in an unrelated formation):
the outward-facing surface — the exterior.

## Notes
表面 names both a literal physical surface (水的表面 "the surface of the
water") and figurative outward appearance, often contrasted with inner
reality (表面上很热情，其实不是 "outwardly warm/friendly, but not really")
— 表面上 is a fixed adverbial for "on the surface, ostensibly."

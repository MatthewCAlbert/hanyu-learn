## Why this combination
热 "hot, warm" (see also 热烈, 热情) combined with 爱 "to love" (as in
爱好, 爱人): love that runs hot — ardent, passionate love.

## Notes
热爱 is used for a deep, passionate devotion to a cause, country or
pursuit (热爱祖国 "to love one's country ardently," 热爱生活 "to love life
passionately") — 喜欢, the everyday "to like," is far lighter and does
not carry 热爱's intensity or formality.

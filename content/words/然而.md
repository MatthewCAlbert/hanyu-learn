## Why this combination
然 carries a classical "so, thus, in this way" sense (the same root sense
behind its role as an adverb-forming suffix in 突然, 竟然), and 而 is the
classical conjunction "but, yet" (covered in this app's entry for 而是).
"[Being] so — but..." is exactly the pivot "however" makes.

Rated "semi" because 然's independent "so, thus" sense here is a step
removed from its far more common role as a bare grammatical suffix
elsewhere.

## Notes
然而 is the formal/written register's "however" — 但是 or 不过 read more
naturally in everyday speech, while 然而 belongs in essays, reports, and
narrative prose.

## Why this combination
大 ("big") plus 会 ("a meeting," as in 会议, 开会) names exactly what it
says: a large-scale meeting or assembly, as opposed to a small,
ordinary one.

## Notes
大会 implies scale and formality (联合国大会, "the UN General Assembly") —
for an everyday small meeting, plain 会 or 会议 is the natural word.

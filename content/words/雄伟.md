## Why this combination
雄 (here "grand, powerful," extended from its base sense "male") and 伟
"great, imposing" are near-synonyms combined into the standard word for
awe-inspiring scale.

## Notes
雄伟 anchors 雄伟的建筑 "a majestic building," 气势雄伟 "an imposing
presence" — always about grand scale inspiring awe, typically of
buildings, mountains, or monuments.

## Why this combination
幸 "good fortune, luck" (see also 幸福) combined with 运 "fate, fortune,
[the turning of] luck" (as in 运气 "luck"): good fortune in how fate
turns — lucky.

## Notes
幸运 works as adjective ("我很幸运" "I'm very lucky") and noun ("这是他的
幸运" "this is his good fortune") — 运气, its close relative, is used
more as the standalone noun "luck" (运气好 "to have good luck") and less
often as an adjective on its own.

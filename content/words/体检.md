## Why this combination

体检 abbreviates the fuller phrase 身体检查 ("body examination") by
taking one character from each half — 体 from 身体 ("body") and 检 from
检查 ("to examine") — a common Chinese abbreviation pattern of
compressing a multi-character phrase into a shorter fixed pair.

## Notes

年度体检 ("annual physical checkup") and 体检报告 ("checkup report") both
build on this shortened form, now used far more often than the full
身体检查 in everyday speech.

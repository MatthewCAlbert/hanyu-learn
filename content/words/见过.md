## Why this combination
见 "to see, to meet" (see [[看见]], [[见到]]) + the experiential aspect particle
过 "have (done) at some point" → "have seen / met before." 我见过他 "I've met
him," 没见过这么大的鱼.

## Notes
过 here is the *experiential* marker (an event in one's past experience), not
the verb 过 "to pass." Negated with 没(有): 没见过.

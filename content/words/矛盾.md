## Why this combination
矛盾 comes directly from a parable in the *Han Feizi*: a merchant claimed
his spear (矛) could pierce anything, and his shield (盾) could block
anything — a contradiction impossible to reconcile, since if the claims
were both true, what would happen when his own spear met his own shield?
The two objects together became the fixed word for "contradiction" —
unrecoverable from either character alone without knowing the story.

## Notes
矛盾 covers logical contradiction (自相矛盾 "self-contradictory," reusing
the full parable phrase), interpersonal conflict (他们之间有矛盾 "there's
friction between them"), and philosophical/dialectical "contradiction"
(a key term in Marxist dialectics, as used in Chinese political theory).

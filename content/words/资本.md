## Why this combination
资 "funds, resources" and 本 (here "root, basis," the foundation from
which something grows) combine into "the fundamental resource (for
production)" — the standard economic term for capital.

## Notes
资本 anchors 资本主义 "capitalism," 投资资本 "investment capital" — also
used figuratively for any advantageous asset: 骄傲的资本 "grounds for
being proud."

## Why this combination

操 ("to exercise, to drill," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
体操) plus 场 ("a field, a place," as in 市场) names a place for
exercising: a sports ground.

## Notes

学校操场 ("school sports ground") is the standard usage — 操场 typically
names an open outdoor field for exercise or assembly, distinct from
体育馆 (a covered gymnasium).

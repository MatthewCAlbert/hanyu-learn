## Why this combination
活 "alive, living" and 力 "strength, force" combine directly: the
strength that comes from being vibrantly alive.

## Notes
活力 describes people (充满活力 "full of energy"), organizations (企业活力
"business vitality"), and cities (城市活力 "urban vibrancy") alike — a
lively, dynamic force rather than raw physical power.

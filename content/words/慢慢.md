## Why this combination
慢 "slow" reduplicated: doubling an adjective before using it adverbially
is a regular pattern in Chinese (慢慢 "slowly," 好好 "properly," 常常
"often"), softening and drawing out the sense of the base word into a
manner adverb.

## Notes
慢慢 describes a gradual, unhurried manner (慢慢来 "take your time,"
慢慢地走 "to walk slowly") — as a standalone phrase, 慢慢来 is a common,
gentle way to tell someone not to rush.

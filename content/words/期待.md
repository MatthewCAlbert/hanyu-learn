## Why this combination
期 here carries its "to hope for, to anticipate" sense (as in 期望,
distinct from its far more common "a period of time" sense in 时期, 假期),
and 待 is "to wait" (as in 等待). Hoping for something while waiting for it
is exactly "to look forward to."

Rated "semi" for 期's less common "to hope for" sense, easy to miss under
its dominant "period" meaning.

## Notes
期待 carries a warmer, more emotionally invested tone than plain 等 (to
wait) — it implies wanting the awaited thing to happen, not just passing
time until it does.

## Why this combination
尤 "especially, outstandingly" is transparent on its own; 其 here is a
grammatical pronoun ("it, that [aforementioned thing]") rather than an
independently meaningful word, giving "especially [that one]" — a fixed
adverb rather than a freely composed phrase.

## Notes
尤其 singles out one item from a group already mentioned: 我喜欢水果，尤其
是苹果 "I like fruit, especially apples" — it needs something to single
out from, unlike standalone 特别.

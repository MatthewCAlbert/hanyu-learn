## Why this combination
广 "wide, broad" (as in 广场 "square," 广告 "advertisement") combined
with 播 "to spread out, to sow, to broadcast" (see also 播's entry in
media-and-internet, its 扌-adjacent sense of scattering seeds extended to
scattering a signal): to spread widely — to broadcast.

## Notes
广播 covers radio broadcasting specifically (广播电台 "radio station")
as well as the general verb "to broadcast, to announce over a speaker
system" (在机场广播 "to announce over the airport PA") — 直播, its
close relative, is used specifically for live broadcasting.

## Why this combination
Demonstrative 那 "that" + the unstressed suffix 么 (see 什么, [[怎么]]). Three
jobs: manner (别那么说 "don't talk like that"), degree (那么贵 "that
expensive"), and discourse connective (那么，我们开始吧 "well then, let's
begin").

## Notes
Pairs with 这么 "this way / this much." As a connective it introduces a
consequence or a next step, close to "so / then."

## Why this combination
晴 "clear, sunny (weather)" and 朗 "bright, clear" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for a clear, sunny sky.

## Notes
晴朗 anchors 晴朗的天气 "sunny weather," 天空晴朗 "the sky is clear" —
always about weather condition, opposite of 阴天 "overcast."

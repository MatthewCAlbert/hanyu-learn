## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 测 ("to measure, to gauge," as in 预测, "to
predict [lit. pre-measure]") and 量 ("to measure," a distinct verbal
reading of the same character seen as a noun in 数量, "quantity") both
independently name taking a measurement. Pairing them gives the
technical/formal word for measuring.

## Notes

测量 leans toward instruments and precision (测量温度, "to measure
temperature"; 测量师, "a surveyor") — everyday casual measuring more
often uses plain 量 alone (量体温, "to take one's temperature").

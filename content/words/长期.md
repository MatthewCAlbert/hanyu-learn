## Why this combination
长 "long [in duration]" combined with 期 "a period, a term, a fixed span
of time" (as in 星期 "week," 学期 "school term"): a long span of time —
long-term.

## Notes
长期 modifies plans and effects spanning a long stretch of time (长期
计划 "long-term plan," 长期以来 "for a long time now") — its opposite is
短期 "short-term," built the same way on 期.

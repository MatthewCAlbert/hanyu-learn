## Why this combination
水产 "aquatic production" (水 "water" + 产 "to produce") plus 品 "product,
item" gives exactly "a product from aquatic production" — the standard
commercial/culinary umbrella term.

## Notes
水产品 anchors 水产品市场 "seafood/aquatic products market" — broader than
海鲜 "seafood," since it includes freshwater as well as marine products,
and non-animal items like edible seaweed.

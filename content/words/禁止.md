## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 禁 ("to prohibit," covered elsewhere in this app
— rooted in sacred ritual taboo) and 止 ("to stop") both independently
name forbidding an action. Pairing them gives the standard formal word
for a ban.

## Notes
禁止吸烟 ("smoking prohibited") and other fixed public-sign phrasings
(禁止 + verb) are the character pair's most visible everyday use — formal
and impersonal, exactly the register of an official notice.

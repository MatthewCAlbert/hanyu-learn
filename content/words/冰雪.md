## Why this combination

冰 ("ice," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 冰箱) plus 雪
("snow," as in 下雪) names ice and snow together.

## Notes

冰雪世界 ("an ice-and-snow world/wonderland") and 冰雪运动 ("winter [ice
and snow] sports") both build on this word — a coordinate compound
naming the two forms of frozen precipitation together as a single
wintry image.

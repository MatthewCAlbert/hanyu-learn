## Why this combination
如 here keeps a literary sense "in the manner of, as" rather than its
more familiar "if" (如果). Paired with 何 "what," the combination asks
"in what manner" — a formal register synonym for 怎么样.

## Notes
如何 is noticeably more formal/written than 怎么样 — it appears in
essays, official notices and set phrases (为何 "why," 无论如何 "no
matter what") more than in casual speech.

## Why this combination
大 ("big," here marking birth order, "the eldest") plus 哥 ("older
brother") names the eldest of one's older brothers.

## Notes
大哥 extends past literal kinship to a respectful, friendly address for a
man roughly one's own age or slightly older (much like 阿姨 and 叔叔,
both covered elsewhere in this app, do for other relations) — and,
colloquially, to a gang or organization's leader.

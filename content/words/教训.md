## Why this combination
教 ("to teach") plus 训 ("to instruct, to admonish," as in 培训) names
both the act of correcting someone through instruction (the verb, "to
lecture/scold") and, as a noun, the hard lesson such correction leaves
behind.

Rated "semi" for 训's "to admonish, to correct sternly" sense, a step
sterner than plain "to teach."

## Notes
接受教训 ("to learn one's lesson," lit. "to accept the lesson") is the
common noun use; as a verb, 教训他一顿 ("to give him a talking-to") always
carries a scolding, corrective edge.

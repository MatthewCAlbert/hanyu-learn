## Why this combination
友 "friend, friendly" (see [[朋友]], [[网友]]) + 好 "good, on good terms" → on
good terms as friends. Of people (态度很友好) and, in diplomatic language, of
states (友好国家, 中日友好).

## Notes
友好 is often institutional / diplomatic; for a warm individual manner,
热情 / 亲切. 友 also in 友谊 "friendship," 友情.

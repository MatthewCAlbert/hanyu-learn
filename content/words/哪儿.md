## Why this combination
Interrogative 哪 "which" + erhua 儿 as a place-maker (see [[这儿]]) → "which
place" → where. Also indefinite (哪儿都行 "anywhere's fine") and rhetorical
(我哪儿知道 "how would I know?").

## Notes
Northern colloquial counterpart of [[哪里]]. 哪 *nǎ* vs. 那 *nà* — tone is the
only difference.

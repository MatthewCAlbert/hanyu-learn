## Why this combination
便 (here "informal, convenient") plus 条 "a slip, a strip (of paper)"
gives exactly "an informal slip (of paper)" — the standard word for a
brief, casual written note.

## Notes
便条 anchors 留个便条 "to leave a note," 便条纸 "note paper/sticky notes"
— always brief and informal, distinct from a formal letter (信) or memo
(通知).

## Why this combination
知 already means "to know." 道 here is the old sense "the way, the principle" —
so the literal reading is "to know the way of things." Classical 知道 meant
grasping the Dao; the modern word has bleached to plain factual knowing.

## Notes
Contrast [[认识]] (to know = be acquainted with a person or place). 知道 is for
facts and information: 我知道他姓王 but 我认识他.

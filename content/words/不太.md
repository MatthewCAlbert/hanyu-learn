## Why this combination
不 "not" + 太 "too, excessively; very" → "not very," a softened negative before
an adjective or verb: 不太好, 不太喜欢, 不太清楚. Gentler than a flat 不.

## Notes
不太 (mild, polite hedge) ≈ [[不大]]. 太…了 "too …" is the positive frame;
不太… drops the 了 and turns it into an understatement.

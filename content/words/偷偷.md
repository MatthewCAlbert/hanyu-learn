## Why this combination
偷 carries its "stealthy, sneaking" sense (see 偷's own notes on this
secondary meaning, alongside "to steal") doubled for adverbial rhythm —
reduplication here softens and smooths the word into a natural-sounding
adverb, the way many single adjectives double for adverbial use in
Mandarin.

## Notes
偷偷 always modifies a verb describing a covert action: 偷偷溜走 "to sneak
away," 偷偷地哭 "to cry secretly" — the doubled form is the everyday
adverb; 偷 alone as "stealthily" is rarer outside this reduplicated form.

## Why this combination
打 "to hit" takes 架 as its object — here a noun for "a quarrel/fight"
(a specialized sense distinct from 架's everyday "shelf, frame") — giving
"to engage in a fight." Splittable, as in 打了一架 "had a fight."

## Notes
打架 is the everyday, physical word for fighting (小孩打架 "kids
fighting") — distinct from 吵架 "to argue verbally," which involves no
physical contact.

## Why this combination

塑料 ("plastic," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 袋 ("a bag,"
covered elsewhere in this app as part of 口袋) names a bag made of
plastic.

## Notes

限塑令 ("plastic-bag restriction order") and 环保袋 ("an eco-friendly
bag," the alternative it's often replaced with) both relate to this
word in discussions of environmental policy.

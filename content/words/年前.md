## Why this combination
年 "year" plus 前 "before" gives exactly "before the year(-end)" — a time
phrase marking a deadline or period just ahead of a year boundary.

## Notes
年前 anchors 年前完成 "to finish before year's end," 年前置办年货 "to shop
for New Year goods before the holiday" — context (fiscal year vs. Lunar
New Year) decides which "year" boundary is meant.

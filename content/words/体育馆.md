## Why this combination
[[体育]] "sport, physical education" + 馆, the suffix for a public building of a
given function (see [[图书馆]], 博物馆, 餐馆) → an indoor sports hall. Parse
体育 + 馆.

## Notes
体育馆 = indoor (a hall); [[体育场]] = outdoor (a stadium / field). 馆 "-hall"
also in [[图书馆]], 美术馆, 大使馆 "embassy."

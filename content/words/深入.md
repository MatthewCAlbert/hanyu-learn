## Why this combination
深 "deep" (see also 深刻) combined with 入 "to enter" (as in 进入 "to
enter"): to enter deeply — to penetrate, go in-depth.

## Notes
深入 describes thorough, in-depth engagement with a subject (深入研究
"in-depth research," 深入了解 "to understand thoroughly") — 深刻, its
close relative, describes a mark or impression that goes deep, while
深入 stresses an active process of going further inward.

## Why this combination
举 "to raise, to lift up, to initiate" (举办, 举手) + 行 (*xíng*) "to carry out,
to conduct" (see [[进行]]) → to mount and run an event. 举行会议, 举行婚礼,
比赛在北京举行.

## Notes
举行 (a formal / scheduled event) vs. 开 (开会, 开party) vs. [[举办]] (not this
level — organise / host). 行 is *xíng* here.

## Why this combination

布 (here "to arrange, to lay out," an extended verbal sense beyond its
literal noun meaning "cloth") plus 置 ("to place, to set," as in 位置)
names arranging things in their places: to decorate, to lay out.

Rated "semi" for 布's extended "to arrange" sense here, distinct from its
much more common noun meaning "cloth, fabric."

## Notes

布置房间 ("to decorate/arrange a room") and 布置作业 ("to assign
homework") share the same underlying idea of setting something out in
order — physical space in one case, a task in the other.

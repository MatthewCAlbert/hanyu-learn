## Why this combination
镜 ("a polished reflective surface," covered elsewhere in this app —
originally bronze mirrors) plus 头 (here in its "the front tip/end of
something" sense, as in 车头, "the front of a vehicle") names the glass
element at the front tip of a camera: the lens.

Rated "semi" for 头's "tip/front end" sense here, distinct from its
literal "head" meaning.

## Notes
镜头 also names a single continuous shot in a film (特写镜头, "a
close-up shot") — the lens standing in for what it captures, the same
way English "shot" names both the camera action and its result.

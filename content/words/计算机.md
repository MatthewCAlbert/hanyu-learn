## Why this combination
计算 "to compute, to calculate" (计 reckon + 算 count) + 机 "machine" (see
[[手机]], [[飞机]]) → a "calculating machine." Parse 计算 + 机. The formal /
technical term; [[电脑]] "electric brain" is the everyday word.

## Notes
计算机 dominates in academic and mainland-official use; 电脑 in daily speech and
in Taiwan. 计 "reckon" also in [[计划]], 设计, 会计.

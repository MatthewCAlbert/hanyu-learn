## Why this combination

无 ("without, there is no") plus 限 ("a limit, a bound," as in 限制, and
covered in this app's entry for 有限, its direct antonym) names having no
bound: unlimited, infinite.

## Notes

无限 modifies both concrete quantities (无限流量, "unlimited data") and
abstract intensities (无限风光, "boundless scenery"; 无限的可能, "endless
possibilities") — the mirror image of 有限 ("limited"), covered
elsewhere in this app.

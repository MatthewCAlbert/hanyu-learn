## Why this combination
病 "sickness" and 毒 "poison" combine directly into "the poison that
causes sickness" — a fitting pre-microscope description of a pathogen,
later formalized as the biological term "virus."

## Notes
病毒 covers both the biological pathogen (流感病毒 "flu virus") and, since
the 1980s, computer malware (电脑病毒 "computer virus") — the same
"invisible, self-spreading harm" metaphor extended to software.

## Why this combination
律 is "statute, law" (as in 法律, 规律), and 师 here is the professional
suffix "master (of a craft or field)" seen across a whole set of job
titles — 医师 (physician), 工程师 (engineer), 教师 (teacher). "A master of
statutes" is the profession's plain description.

Rated "semi" because 师's productive suffix role is a distinct pattern
worth learning on its own, separate from its more familiar "teacher" sense
in 老师.

## Notes
Compare 法官 (judge) — a different professional title built on 法 rather
than 律, and using 官 (official) rather than the 师 suffix, since a judge
holds a state office rather than practicing a craft.

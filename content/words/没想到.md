## Why this combination

没 ("to not have") negates 想到 ("to think of, to occur to [someone],"
想 "to think" plus the verb-complement 到 "to arrive/reach") to name a
thought that never occurred to someone: unexpected.

## Notes

真没想到 ("I really didn't expect that") and 没想到会这样 ("didn't expect
it would turn out this way") both build on this word — a very common
everyday reaction expressing surprise at an unanticipated outcome.

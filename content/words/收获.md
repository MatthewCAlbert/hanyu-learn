## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 收 ("to gather in, to receive," as in 收入) and
获 ("to obtain, to harvest," covered elsewhere in this app — itself a
merger of two traditional characters, one for catching game, one for
harvesting crops) both independently name gathering in a gain. Pairing
them gives the standard word for a harvest, literal or figurative.

## Notes
收获 covers the literal agricultural harvest (收获庄稼, "to harvest
crops") and, just as commonly, a figurative one (这次旅行的收获, "what I
gained from this trip") — the same "gathering in a reward for effort"
image applied to both.

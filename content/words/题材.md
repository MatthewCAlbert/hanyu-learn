## Why this combination
题 (here "topic, subject") and 材 "material" combine into exactly "the
material of the topic" — the standard word for the subject a work of art
or writing is about.

## Notes
题材 anchors 电影题材 "film subject matter," 历史题材 "historical
subject" — always about content/theme in creative work, distinct from
主题 "theme," which is the central idea drawn from that content.

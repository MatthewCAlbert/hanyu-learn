## Why this combination
保 and 护 both independently mean "to protect" — 保 with a nuance of
"to keep safe, to guarantee," 护 with a nuance of "to shield, to guard."
Paired for emphasis, in the same near-synonym pattern as 需要 and 开始.

## Notes
护 rarely stands fully alone in modern speech outside compounds (护士
"nurse," 护照 "passport" — literally a document that "protects and
illuminates" one's travel) — 保护 is the form learners meet most often.

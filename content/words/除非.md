## Why this combination
除 "except, excluding" and 非 "not" combine into "excluding the case
where it's not so" — a grammaticalized connective whose "unless" sense
follows once you see the double-negative logic buried in the parts.

## Notes
除非 often pairs with 否则 or 才 in the full pattern: 除非你去，我才去
"I'll only go if you go [unless you go, I won't]" — the negative
condition frames what must be true for the main clause to hold.

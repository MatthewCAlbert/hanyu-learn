## Why this combination
胡子 is a fusion of 胡须 "beard/whiskers" with the noun suffix 子; 须
"whiskers" itself is well attested (a hair radical 彡/而 form), but why
胡 — whose base sense is "foreign, reckless" (胡人 "northern/western
peoples," 胡说 "nonsense") — supplies the first syllable here is not
securely explained. Not confidently attested as either a semantic or
phonetic borrowing; treat the surface "foreign-people + suffix" reading
as descriptive, not etymological.

## Notes
胡子 is the everyday word for facial hair on the chin/cheeks: 留胡子 "to
grow a beard," 刮胡子 "to shave" — 胡须 is the more formal/written
synonym.

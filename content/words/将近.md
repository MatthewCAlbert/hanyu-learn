## Why this combination
将, here a future-marking adverb "about to, going to" (see also 将来)
combined with 近 "near, close" (see also 接近, 最近): about to be near
[a figure] — almost, nearly reaching it.

## Notes
将近 precedes a number or quantity to mark it as nearly, but not quite,
reached (将近一百人 "almost a hundred people") — 几乎, its neighbour, is
the more general adverb for "almost" and is not restricted to numbers.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 趋 ("to tend toward, to hasten toward," a
character occurring mainly in this role) and 势 ("power, a tendency, a
state of affairs," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 形势,
"circumstances") both independently name a direction of movement.
Pairing them gives the formal word for a trend.

Rated "semi" for 趋's specialized "tend toward" sense, a character with
little independent everyday use outside this compound.

## Notes

发展趋势 ("developmental trend") and 上升趋势 ("upward trend") both build
on this word — closer to 潮流 (covered elsewhere in this app) in meaning
but more analytical/statistical in register, common in reports and data.

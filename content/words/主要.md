## Why this combination
主 "principal, host, chief" + 要 "essential, important" → the chief and
essential one. Attributive (主要原因 "the main reason") and adverbial
(主要是因为… "mainly because…").

## Notes
主要 ranks items by importance; [[重要]] weighs a single thing's importance.
Opposite 次要 "secondary."

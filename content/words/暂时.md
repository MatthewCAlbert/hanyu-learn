## Why this combination
暂 "temporary, brief" and 时 "time" combine directly into "a temporary
span of time" — as straightforward as a compound gets.

## Notes
暂时 works as an adverb (暂时不能来 "can't come for the time being") and
adjective (暂时的解决方法 "a temporary solution") — always flagging that
the current state is expected to change.

## Why this combination
努 "to exert, to put forth" + 力 "strength, effort" as its object — "to put out
one's strength." Verb-object, so it splits: 努了半天力 "strained for ages,"
多努点力. Also an adjective: 他很努力.

## Notes
努 barely occurs outside this word and 努嘴 "to purse the lips (as a signal)."

## Why this combination
年 "years (of age)" + 轻 "light (in weight), slight." Few years weigh little —
"light in years" = young. The same "light = a low amount" metaphor as 年轻
vs. 年老, or 分量轻.

## Notes
年轻人 "young people," 年轻时 "when (one was) young." 轻 "light" also in
[[凉快]]'s neighbour 轻松 "relaxed."

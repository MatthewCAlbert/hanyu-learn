## Why this combination
左 "left" and 右 "right" combine into the literal pair "left and right,"
and, because a number bracketed between "a bit less" and "a bit more" sits
loosely around a point the way left and right sit around a centre, the
same pair was extended to mean "approximately, or so."

## Notes
左右 as an approximator always follows the number it qualifies (三十岁
左右 "around thirty years old"), unlike 大约, which precedes it; the same
form, in formal or classical-flavoured usage, also means "to control,
to sway" (受人左右 "to be swayed by others").

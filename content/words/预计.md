## Why this combination
预 "in advance, beforehand" (as in 预习 "to preview [a lesson]," 预约
"to book in advance") combined with 计 "to calculate, to plan" (see also
计算): to calculate in advance — to forecast, to estimate.

## Notes
预计 introduces an estimated figure or outcome (预计到达时间 "estimated
time of arrival," 预计明年完成 "expected to be finished next year") —
估计, a close synonym, is used the same way and is slightly more
colloquial.

## Why this combination
A verb-object compound: 理 "to put in order, to arrange" (see also 管理,
整理) takes 发 "hair" (a separate character reading, fà, from 发展's
fā) as its object — literally "to put the hair in order" — to cut/style
hair.

## Notes
理发 names getting a haircut (理发店 "barbershop, hair salon," 理发师
"hairdresser, barber") — 发, in this word, is read fà, distinct from
its far more common fā reading in words like 发展 and 发现, a useful
reading to keep separate.

## Why this combination
原 "original, source" and 始 "beginning" are near-synonyms combined into
the standard word for the earliest, unmodified state of something.

## Notes
原始 covers documentary originality (原始文件 "original document") and
undeveloped, primitive states (原始社会 "primitive society," 原始森林
"primeval forest") — always the earliest, unaltered form.

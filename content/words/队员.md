## Why this combination
队 "a team, a line, a group" (as in 球队 "ball team") combined with 员
"a member" (see also 成员, 演员): a member of a team.

## Notes
队员 names a member of any organised team — sports, rescue, work (球队
队员 "team player," 消防队员 "firefighter," literally "fire-brigade team
member") — 成员, the more general word, applies to any group, not
specifically a 队 "team, squad."

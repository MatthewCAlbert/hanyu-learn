## Why this combination

语 ("language," as in 汉语) plus 法 ("a law, a method," as in 办法, "a
way/method [lit. handle + method]") names the governing rules of a
language: grammar.

## Notes

语法错误 ("a grammar mistake") and 语法规则 ("grammar rules") both build
on this word — 语法 names the system of rules, distinct from 词汇
(covered elsewhere in this app), the vocabulary those rules govern.

## Why this combination

影 ("a shadow, an image," as in 电影, "a movie [lit. electric shadow]")
plus the nominal suffix 子 names the everyday object: a shadow.

## Notes

影子 covers the literal cast shadow and, figuratively, a faint trace or
lingering presence (他的影子还留在这里, "his presence still lingers
here") — the same "shadow/trace" image as English.

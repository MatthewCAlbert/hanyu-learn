## Why this combination
来 "to come" acting on the object 信 "letter" gives exactly "a letter
that has come (arrived)" — splittable, as in 来了一封信, and usable as a
verb phrase, "读者来信" "readers write in."

## Notes
来信 works as noun ("收到一封来信" "received a letter") and verb-object
phrase ("欢迎来信" "letters welcome") — the mirror image of 去信 "to send a
letter (outward)," which is far less common.

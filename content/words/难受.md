## Why this combination
难 "hard, difficult" + 受 "to bear, to endure, to undergo" (see [[受到]],
[[接受]]) → "hard to bear." Covers physical discomfort (吃坏了肚子，很难受) and
emotional distress (听到这个消息心里很难受).

## Notes
难 + verb = "hard to —": [[难过]], [[难看]], [[难听]], [[难题]]. 难受 overlaps
with [[难过]]; 难受 leans more toward bodily wretchedness.

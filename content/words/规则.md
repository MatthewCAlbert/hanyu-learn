## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 规 ("a compass, a standard to measure against,"
as in 规定, 规矩) and 则 ("a rule, a fixed standard," covered in this app's
hanzi entry — cut shares divided to a fixed measure) both independently
name a governing standard. Pairing them gives the ordinary word for a
rule or regulation.

## Notes
规则 covers both formal rules (游戏规则, "the rules of a game") and
regularity as a quality (不规则, "irregular," of a shape or pattern) — the
same "fixed, governing standard" idea applied to conduct and to form.

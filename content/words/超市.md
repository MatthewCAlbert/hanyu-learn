## Why this combination
Clipped from 超级市场 "super-grade market" — 超级 "super-, top-grade" (超 exceed
+ 级 grade; see [[超过]], [[高级]]) + 市场 "market." Each half loses a syllable:
超 + 市. 去超市买东西.

## Notes
Same clipping in 网吧 (网络 + 吧), 空调 (空气 + 调节). 大型超市 "hypermarket,"
便利店 "convenience store."

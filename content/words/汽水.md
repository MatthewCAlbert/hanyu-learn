## Why this combination

汽 ("steam, gas," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 汽油) plus
水 ("water") names carbonated water — gas bubbled through water:
soda.

Rated "semi" for 汽's "carbonation/gas" contribution here, one step
from its more familiar role naming vehicle-powering steam/gas in 汽车.

## Notes

喝汽水 ("to drink soda") is the everyday phrase — 汽水 names carbonated
soft drinks generically, while brand names like 可乐 (covered elsewhere
in this app) name one specific kind.

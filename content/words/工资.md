## Why this combination
工 "work, labour" (see also 工具, 工人) combined with 资 "capital,
resources, funds" (see also 资金, 资格): the resources paid out for
labour — wages.

## Notes
工资 names regular pay for work, most often monthly (发工资 "to pay
wages," 工资低 "low pay") — 收入, its broader relative, covers all
income including wages, investment returns and other sources.

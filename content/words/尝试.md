## Why this combination
尝 "to taste, to try" and 试 "to test, to try" are near-synonyms combined
into the formal word for making an attempt.

## Notes
尝试 favours formal/written contexts (进行尝试 "to make an attempt," 大胆
尝试 "to boldly attempt") over the everyday 试试 "give it a try," which is
lighter and more casual.

## Why this combination
当 (here "the current, this very") plus 代 "era, generation" gives
exactly "the current era" — the standard word for the present historical
period.

## Notes
当代 anchors 当代文学 "contemporary literature," 当代艺术 "contemporary
art" — a period label used in scholarship, contrasting with 古代
"ancient times" and 近代 "modern (pre-contemporary) times."

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 称 ("to call, and by extension to praise," as
in 称呼, "to address/call [someone]") and 赞 ("to praise," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 赞成/赞助) both independently name
speaking well of someone. Pairing them gives the standard word for
praise.

## Notes

受到称赞 ("to receive praise") and 称赞他的努力 ("to praise his effort")
both build on this word — a formal, spoken-and-written verb for
praising, close to but slightly more measured than the everyday 表扬.

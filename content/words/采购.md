## Why this combination
采 (here "to select, to gather") and 购 "to purchase" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard word for organizational buying.

## Notes
采购 anchors 采购部门 "procurement department," 集中采购 "centralized
purchasing" — the formal, business/institutional register of buying,
distinct from everyday personal 购买 or 买.

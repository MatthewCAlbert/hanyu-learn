## Why this combination
下 "down, lower" + the locative suffix 边 — the down member of the [[上边]] /
下边 pair. Also "further down" in a text, and "the rank below."

## Notes
下面 is the exact synonym and the more common one in writing. 桌子下边 "under
the table."

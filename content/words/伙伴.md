## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 伙 ("a companion," covered elsewhere in this
app — soldiers who shared a cooking fire) and 伴 ("a companion," also
covered elsewhere — an exact-sound phonetic pairing with 半, "half," as
in "one's other half") both independently name a companion. Pairing them
gives the standard everyday word for a partner or companion.

## Notes
伙伴 covers both casual companionship (小伙伴, "buddy/pal," especially
among children) and formal partnership (合作伙伴, "a business partner") —
a broad word for anyone one works or plays alongside.

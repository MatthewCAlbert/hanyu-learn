## Why this combination
细 "fine, detailed" is transparent; 仔 here carries an older "careful,
attentive" sense rather than its more familiar "small/young" sense seen
in 仔细's cousin words — together, "fine and attentive," i.e. careful.

## Notes
仔细 is the everyday word for care and attentiveness: 仔细检查 "to check
carefully," 仔细听 "to listen carefully" — one of the most common
adverbial modifiers in spoken Mandarin.

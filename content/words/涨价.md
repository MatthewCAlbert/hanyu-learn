## Why this combination
涨 "to rise, to swell" acting on the object 价 "price" gives exactly "the
price rises" — splittable, as in 涨了三次价.

## Notes
涨价 anchors 房价涨价 "housing prices rise," 涨价通知 "price increase
notice" — the standard everyday word for a price going up, opposite of
降价 "to lower prices."

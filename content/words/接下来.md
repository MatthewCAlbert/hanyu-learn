## Why this combination
接 "to carry on, to follow on from" (see [[接着]]) + the directional complement
下来 "on down (from here to now)" — carrying straight on into what comes next.
接下来我们看第二部分 "next, let's look at part two."

## Notes
接下来 (what comes next in a sequence / presentation) ≈ [[然后]] but more
forward-pointing; 下来 as "down to the present" also in 留下来, 坚持下来.

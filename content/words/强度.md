## Why this combination
强 "strong" plus 度 "degree, measure" gives exactly "the degree of
strength" — a measurement noun, parallel to 深度 "depth" and 速度 "speed."

## Notes
强度 anchors 运动强度 "exercise intensity," 光强度 "light intensity" — a
technical/scientific measurement word, distinct from the adjective 强
alone.

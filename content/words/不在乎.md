## Why this combination

不 ("not") negates 在乎 ("to care about, to mind" — itself 在, "to be
present at, to rest on," plus 乎, a classical sentence-final/locative
particle roughly "in, at"), which together form a fixed verb "to take
something to heart." Negated, it names indifference.

Rated "opaque": 乎's classical grammatical role gives no clue toward
"to care about" without already knowing 在乎 as a set phrase.

## Notes

不在乎 and its positive counterpart 在乎 both take a direct object
(他不在乎钱, "he doesn't care about money") — close in meaning to 无所谓
but more personal/emotional in tone.

## Why this combination
相 "mutually, [towards] each other" (see also 相当, 互相) combined with
比 "to compare" (as in 比较 "to compare, relatively"): to compare
[things] against each other — to compare.

## Notes
相比 is used in the fixed pattern 与...相比 "compared with..." (与去年
相比 "compared with last year") — it functions as a formal way to
introduce a comparison, distinct from 比较, the more general adjective/
adverb for "relatively, comparatively."

## Why this combination
演 "to perform, to act out" (see also 表演, 演出) combined with 唱 "to
sing" (as in 唱歌): to perform through singing — to sing for an
audience.

## Notes
演唱 is the formal word for a public singing performance (演唱一首歌
"to perform a song") — 唱歌, the everyday phrase, is used for singing
generally, including casually and privately, without 演唱's performance
framing.

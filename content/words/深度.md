## Why this combination
深 "deep" plus 度 "degree, measure" gives exactly "the degree of
depth" — a measurement noun, parallel to 高度 "height" and 速度 "speed."

## Notes
深度 works as noun ("depth," 水的深度 "the water's depth") and, before
another noun, as an intensifying modifier: 深度报道 "in-depth reporting,"
深度睡眠 "deep sleep" — extending physical depth to thoroughness.

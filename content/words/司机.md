## Why this combination
司 "to take charge of, to manage, to operate" (司令, 公司) + 机 "machine, engine"
→ the one who operates the machine. Coined for the locomotive driver and carried
over to cars and buses. 出租车司机, 公交司机.

## Notes
司 "be in charge of" also in [[公司]], 司法. Casual: 师傅 for a driver you're
addressing. 老司机 "old hand" (lit. veteran driver).

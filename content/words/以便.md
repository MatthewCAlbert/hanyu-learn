## Why this combination
以 (classical "in order to, by means of") and 便 "convenient" combine into
"so as to make [something] convenient" — a formal connective for stating
purpose.

## Notes
以便 introduces a purpose clause in formal writing: 请提前预约，以便安排
"please book in advance so that arrangements can be made" — a written-
register alternative to everyday 为了.

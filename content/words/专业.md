## Why this combination
专 "specialised, focused exclusively [on one thing]" (see also 专家)
combined with 业 "trade, occupation, field of endeavour" (as in 职业
"occupation"): a field one has specialised in — a university major or a
professional specialty.

## Notes
专业 names a field of study or expertise (我的专业是历史 "my major is
history") and, as a modifier, "professional, expert-level" (专业水平
"professional level"); it does not mean "occupation" in general the way
职业 does.

## Why this combination

灯 ("a lamp, a light fixture") plus 光 ("light, brightness," as in 阳光)
names the light given off by a lamp: lamplight, or a place's overall
lighting.

## Notes

灯光 covers both the literal glow (灯光很暗, "the light is dim") and the
stagecraft/interior-design sense of lighting design (舞台灯光, "stage
lighting").

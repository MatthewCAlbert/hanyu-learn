## Why this combination
电影 "film" (see [[电影]]) + 院, the walled-establishment suffix of [[医院]],
[[学院]], 戏院. A hall for showing films.

## Notes
Parse 电影 + 院. Casual clipping: just 影院, or 去看电影 without naming the
venue.

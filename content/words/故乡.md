## Why this combination
故, here "old, former, of the past" (as in 故事 "story," literally "old
affairs") combined with 乡 "village, one's native countryside" (see also
家乡): the village of one's past — one's native homeland.

## Notes
故乡 is a literary, often nostalgic word for one's homeland, especially
used when far away or writing sentimentally (思念故乡 "to long for
one's homeland") — 家乡, its close, everyday synonym, is used more
casually in ordinary conversation.

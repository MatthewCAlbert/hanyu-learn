## Why this combination
微 ("faint, subtle," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 笑 ("to laugh, to
smile") names exactly what it looks like: a faint, gentle smile, as
opposed to a full laugh.

## Notes
微笑 is specifically a quiet, closed-mouth smile — for a full laugh or
loud laughter, plain 笑 or 大笑 ("to laugh heartily") is the word to use
instead.

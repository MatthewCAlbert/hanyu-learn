## Why this combination
群 "a crowd, a group" and 众 "many, a multitude" are near-synonyms
combined into the standard political/social word for the general
populace.

## Notes
群众 carries a political register in Mainland Chinese usage — 群众路线
"the mass line," 人民群众 "the people/masses" — distinct from neutral
人们 "people" in everyday conversation.

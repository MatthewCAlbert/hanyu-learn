## Why this combination
存 "to store, to save" acting on the object 款 "a sum of money" (see
款's own etymology) gives exactly "to store money" — splittable, as in
存了一笔款 "made a deposit."

## Notes
存款 works as verb ("to deposit money in a bank") and noun ("savings,"
存款账户 "savings account") — the everyday banking term, opposite of 取款
"to withdraw money."

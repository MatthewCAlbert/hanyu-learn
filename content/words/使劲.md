## Why this combination

使 ("to use, to employ") takes 劲 ("strength, energy, force," as in 干劲,
"drive/motivation [lit. work + energy]") as its object: putting one's
strength to use.

## Notes

使劲推 ("to push with all one's might") and 使劲儿 (with the retroflex
儿-suffix, common in northern spoken Mandarin) both build on this word —
names a burst of physical exertion, distinct from 努力, which covers
sustained effort of any kind.

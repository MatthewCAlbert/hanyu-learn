## Why this combination
算 "to calculate, to reckon, to figure" as the object of 打, the light verb "to
do / carry out" (see [[打开]], [[打电话]]) — "to do one's figuring," to work out
what one will do. 打算去留学, 有什么打算？

## Notes
打算 (personal intention, colloquial) vs. [[计划]] (a worked-out plan) vs.
[[准备]] (getting ready to). 算 alone: 算了 "forget it," 算账.

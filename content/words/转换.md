## Why this combination
转 "to turn, to rotate" and 换 "to exchange" are near-synonyms combined
into the standard word for converting from one state or form to another.

## Notes
转换 covers technical conversion (格式转换 "format conversion") and
abstract shifts (角色转换 "role switch," 观念转换 "shift in thinking") —
always changing from one defined state into another.

## Why this combination
主 "main" plus 体 "body" gives exactly "the main body (of something)" —
the standard word for the primary, larger component within a whole, and,
in philosophy/law, the acting subject.

## Notes
主体 anchors 建筑主体 "the main structure of a building," 权利主体 "the
subject of rights (legal term)" — spans physical structure and abstract
agency, always naming the primary/central element.

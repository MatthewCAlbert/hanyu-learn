## Why this combination
忘 "to forget" carries the whole meaning. 记 "to note, to keep in mind" is added
as a near-empty second syllable for rhythm — the compound means exactly what 忘
does, not "forget-and-remember." A common pattern where one half is bleached.

## Notes
忘 alone is still fully usable (别忘了). Contrast [[记得]] / [[记住]], where 记 is
the meaningful element.

## Why this combination
系 carries its "a connected system, a lineage" sense (covered in this
app's entry for 系统), and 列 is "a row, a list" (covered elsewhere in
this app). A connected set of items arranged in a row is exactly a
series.

## Notes
一系列 ("a whole series/string of") is the most common everyday frame —
一系列问题, "a series of problems" — always implying multiple related
items, not just one.

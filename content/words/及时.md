## Why this combination
及 "to reach, to catch up to, in time for" (as in 来得及 "there's still
time") combined with 时 "time": reaching [something] in time — timely,
prompt.

## Notes
及时 modifies both a verb and a noun (及时帮助 "timely help," 处理得很
及时 "dealt with promptly") — it praises action taken at exactly the
right moment, neither too early nor too late.

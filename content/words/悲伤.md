## Why this combination
悲 "sorrow" (see 悲's own etymology) and 伤 "injury, to be hurt" are
near-synonyms combined into the standard formal word for deep sadness —
grief felt as a kind of wound.

## Notes
悲伤 is a formal/literary register for sadness, stronger and more
sustained than everyday 难过: 悲伤的消息 "sorrowful news," 陷入悲伤 "to sink
into grief."

## Why this combination
不 "not" + 错 "wrong, mistaken, in error." Two readings from one phrase: the
literal "not wrong / correct" (你说得不错 "you're right") and the idiomatic
"quite good" (这家饭馆不错) — English "not bad" makes the same move.

## Notes
错 alone as a verb "to miss / err" (错过, 认错). The praise sense of 不错 is
mild — better than 一般, short of 很好.

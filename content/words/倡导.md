## Why this combination
倡 "to advocate, to initiate" and 导 "to lead, to guide" are
near-synonyms combined into a formal word for being the first to promote
an idea.

## Notes
倡导 anchors 倡导者 "an advocate, a proponent," 倡导环保 "to advocate for
environmental protection" — always implies leading by example in
proposing a new idea or practice, a more formal register than 提倡.

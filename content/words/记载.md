## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 记 ("to record, to remember," as in 记得) and
载 ("to carry, to record [in writing]," covered elsewhere in this app as
part of 下载, "to download") both independently name preserving
information in writing. Pairing them gives the formal word for a
written record.

## Notes

历史记载 ("historical records") and 据记载 ("according to written
records") both build on this word — leans toward formal, often
historical documentation, distinct from 日记 (covered elsewhere in this
app), a personal diary.

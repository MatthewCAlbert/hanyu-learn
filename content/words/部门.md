## Why this combination
部 "section, division, ministry" combined with 门 in its extended sense
of "category, specialty" (as in 门类 "category") rather than its literal
"door, gate": a division marked off as its own category within a larger
organisation.

## Notes
部门 names an organisational unit inside a company, government or
institution (销售部门 "sales department"); it is not used for a
free-standing organisation the way 公司 or 机构 are.

## Why this combination
出 carries its "to exceed, to go beyond (the ordinary)" sense (as in 出
众, "to stand out from the crowd"), and 色 here takes its abstract "kind,
quality, category" sense (the same sense covered in this app's entry for
角色, distinct from 色's more common "color" meaning). Exceeding the
ordinary category is exactly "outstanding."

Rated "semi" because both halves lean on secondary senses — 出's
"exceeding" rather than its plain "to go out," and 色's "quality" rather
than "color."

## Notes
出色 modifies performance and ability specifically (表现出色, "an
outstanding performance") — for a person's fixed rank or title, 优秀
reads more naturally instead.

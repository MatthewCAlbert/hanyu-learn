## Why this combination

售 ("to sell," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 出售) plus 货
("goods," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 百货) plus 员
("a member, a person [in a role]," as in 服务员, "a waiter/attendant")
names a person who sells goods: a shop assistant.

## Notes

商场售货员 ("a department-store salesperson") is the standard workplace
title — shares its 员 "person-in-role" suffix with 服务员 and other
职业 words built the same way.

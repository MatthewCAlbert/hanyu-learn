## Why this combination
Clipped from 南非洲 "South Africa" — 南 "south" + 非 from 非洲 "Africa." A
transparent geography compound; the country name 南非共和国 is built from 南非,
not the other way round. 非 here is not the negative.

## Notes
非洲 itself is 非 phonetic of "Afri-" + 洲 "continent." Same 非 in 东非, 西非.

## Why this combination
南 "south" + 非 clipped from 非洲 "Africa." A transparent geography compound,
short for 南非共和国. 非 here is not the negative.

## Notes
非洲 itself is 非 phonetic of "Afri-" + 洲 "continent." Same 非 in 东非, 西非.

## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 粮 ("grain, provisions," a character occurring
mainly in this role) and 食 ("food, to eat," as in 食物) both
independently name sustenance. Pairing them gives the standard word for
staple food, especially grain.

## Notes

粮食问题 ("food security issues") and 粮食产量 ("grain output") both build
on this word — 粮食 specifically names staple cereal foods (rice, wheat,
corn), distinct from 食物, the general word for "food" of any kind.

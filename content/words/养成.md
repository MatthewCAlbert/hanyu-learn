## Why this combination

养 ("to raise, to nurture," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
培养) plus 成 ("to become, to complete," as in 成功) names nurturing
something until it becomes established: to develop, to form.

## Notes

养成习惯 ("to form a habit") is the standard, almost fixed frame — 养成
overwhelmingly collocates with 习惯, "habit," more than any other object.

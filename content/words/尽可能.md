## Why this combination
尽 "to the fullest extent" (see 尽's role in 尽管) and 可能 "possible"
combine directly: pushing to the fullest extent of what's possible.

## Notes
尽可能 always precedes a verb it modifies: 尽可能快 "as fast as possible,"
尽可能避免 "to avoid as much as possible" — a common formal adverbial
phrase in both speech and writing.

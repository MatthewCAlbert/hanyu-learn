## Why this combination
别 in the sense "other, separate" (as in 别的, 区别) + 人 "person." Straightforward
"other people," treated as a pronoun: 别人怎么想 "what others think."

## Notes
别 has a second, unrelated life as the prohibitive "don't" (别走). Same
character, two jobs.

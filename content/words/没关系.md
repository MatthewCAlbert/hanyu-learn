## Why this combination
关系 "connection, bearing, consequence"; 没关系 = "(it) has no bearing" — no
harm done, nothing hangs on it. The standard reply to an apology, and a general
"doesn't matter": 去不去没关系.

## Notes
有关系 "it is connected / it matters." 关系 on its own is a heavyweight social
word ("connections, guanxi"); here it is just "bearing."

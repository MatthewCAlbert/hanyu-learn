## Why this combination
大 "big, main" plus 纲 (here "the main rope of a fishing net," from which
all other strands are controlled) gives exactly "the main controlling
line" — extended metaphorically to the guiding structure of a document or
plan.

## Notes
大纲 anchors 教学大纲 "teaching syllabus," 论文大纲 "essay outline" — the
structural skeleton of a longer work, laying out its main points before
the details are filled in.

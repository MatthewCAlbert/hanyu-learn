## Why this combination
美 clipped from 美利坚, the phonetic rendering of "America," + 国 "country." The
same mould as [[英国]], [[法国]], [[德国]]: a sound-clip of the foreign name plus
"state." 美 here is not "beautiful."

## Notes
美 as the country-abbreviation also in 美元 "US dollar," 中美 "China–US."

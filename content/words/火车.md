## Why this combination
"Fire vehicle" — named for the coal fire of the steam locomotive, exactly as
[[汽车]] was named for steam. The fire is long gone from the engines and stayed
in the word.

## Notes
坐火车 "go by train." 火车站 station, 火车票 ticket. High-speed rail has its own
name, 高铁.

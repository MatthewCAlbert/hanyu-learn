## Why this combination
资 carries its "resources, funds, means" sense (as in 资金, 投资), and 料 is
"material, stuff" (as in 材料, 原料). Resources in the specific form of raw
material — usually informational rather than physical — gives "data,
documents, reference material."

Rated "semi" for 资's figurative stretch from "funds" to "informational
resource," which is not the character's most common everyday sense.

## Notes
资料 and 材料 overlap heavily; 资料 leans toward informational/reference
material (研究资料, 个人资料 "personal information"), while 材料 covers
physical raw materials just as readily (建筑材料, "building materials").

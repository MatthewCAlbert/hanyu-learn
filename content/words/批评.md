## Why this combination
批 "to annotate, to comment on [a document]" (see also 批准) combined
with 评 "to judge, to critique" (see also 评价): to comment on and
judge — to criticise.

## Notes
批评 names both constructive critique and reprimand for wrongdoing
(接受批评 "to accept criticism," 批评孩子 "to scold a child") — 评价, its
close relative, is the more neutral word for "to evaluate," without
批评's inherent negative or corrective charge.

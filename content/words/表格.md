## Why this combination
表, here "a chart, a table [of data]" (as in 时间表 "timetable") combined
with 格 "a pattern, a grid, a cell" (see also 价格, 资格): a chart made
of grid cells — a form, a table.

## Notes
表格 names both a document to be filled in (填表格 "to fill out a form")
and a data table with rows and columns (制作表格 "to create a table") —
表, alone, is used the same way in many contexts but 表格 is the fuller,
more explicit form.

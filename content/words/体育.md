## Why this combination
体 "body" + 育 "to nurture, to educate" (see [[教育]]) → "education of the body,"
the school-subject sense, on the model of 德育 / 智育 / 体育 "moral / intellectual
/ physical education." Broadened to "sport" in general: 体育新闻, 体育馆.

## Notes
体育 = organised sport / PE; [[运动]] = exercise or a sport; [[比赛]] (not this
level) = a match. 体 "body" also in [[身体]], [[全体]].

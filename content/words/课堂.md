## Why this combination
课 "lesson, class" (see [[上课]], [[课本]], [[课文]]) + 堂 "a hall, a room for
a purpose" (礼堂 auditorium, 食堂 canteen) → the hall where a class happens,
and by extension the class as an event: 课堂上, 课堂纪律, 课堂气氛.

## Notes
课堂 = the class as a live setting ("in class"); [[教室]] = the physical room
whether or not a class is on; 课 = the lesson itself.

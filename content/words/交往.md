## Why this combination
交 "to exchange, to intersect, to interact" (see also 交流, 交易)
combined with 往 "to go towards" (see also 前往): to go towards and
interact — to associate with, to keep company with.

## Notes
交往 names an ongoing personal or social relationship (人际交往
"interpersonal relations," 和他交往 "to associate/be involved with him,"
sometimes specifically "to date him") — 联系, its neighbour, is the more
neutral word for simply "to be in contact with" someone, without
交往's social/relational depth.

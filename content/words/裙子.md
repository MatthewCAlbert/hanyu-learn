## Why this combination
裙, whose 衤 "clothing" radical marks it as a garment word, names a
skirt specifically, combined with the light noun suffix 子 (as in 裤子,
桌子): the everyday concrete noun for the garment.

## Notes
裙子 is the standard word for a skirt or dress-skirt (连衣裙 "a dress,"
literally "a joined-garment skirt") — 裙, alone, appears mainly as a
bound component, as in 裤子's parallel formation.

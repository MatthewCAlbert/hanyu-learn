## Why this combination
人 "person" combined with 才 "talent, ability" (as in 天才 "genius,"
literally "heaven-given talent"): a person possessed of talent — a
talented individual.

## Notes
人才 names a capable, valuable person, especially in a professional
context (培养人才 "to cultivate talent," 人才市场 "talent/job market") —
it is used for the person themselves, not merely their ability, which
would be named by 才能 or 能力.

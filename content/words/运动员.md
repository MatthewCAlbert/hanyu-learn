## Why this combination
运动 ("sport, exercise, movement") plus 员 (a productive suffix marking "a
member of, a person engaged in," as in 人员, 演员, "actor") names a person
whose occupation or activity is sport: an athlete.

Rated "semi" for 员's productive role-marking suffix pattern, worth
learning on its own rather than deriving from any single sense of 员.

## Notes
员 recurs across many role-naming words — 演员 (actor), 服务员 (waiter),
职员 (staff member) — all built the same way: activity/domain plus 员
naming the person who does it.

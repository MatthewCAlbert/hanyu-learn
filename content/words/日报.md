## Why this combination
日 "day, daily" + 报, here "newspaper" (see [[报纸]]) → a paper that comes out
every day, as against a 晚报 (evening paper, see [[晚报]]) or 周报 (weekly).
Common in mastheads: 人民日报, XX日报.

## Notes
报 "newspaper" alone in mastheads and compounds: 日报, [[晚报]], 早报, 看报.

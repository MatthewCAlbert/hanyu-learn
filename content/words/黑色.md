## Why this combination
黑 "black, dark" + the "-colour" suffix 色 (see [[颜色]], [[红色]]). 黑 alone in
黑板 "blackboard," 黑夜 "dark night," and slang 黑 "to smear / hack."

## Notes
Figurative 黑 = illicit / underworld: 黑市 "black market," 黑社会 "organised
crime." The colour word itself is neutral.

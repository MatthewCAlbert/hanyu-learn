## Why this combination

说 ("to say") plus the potential-complement negative 不定 ("cannot be
fixed/settled," 定 as in 决定, "to decide") fuses into a set phrase: "it
can't be said for certain" — used as a hedge meaning "maybe, perhaps."

Rated "semi": the potential-complement grammar (说 + 不 + 定, "unable to
say-and-settle") is transparent to anyone who knows that pattern, but not
obvious without it.

## Notes

说不定 opens a clause as a sentence adverb (说不定他已经到了, "maybe he's
already arrived") — functionally close to 也许 or 可能, but built from a
verb phrase rather than a single adverb.

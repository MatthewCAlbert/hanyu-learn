## Why this combination
指 "to point at" and 示 "to show" (see 示's role in 启's etymology) are
near-synonyms combined into the formal word for pointing out or
instructing.

## Notes
指示 covers pointing something out (指示方向 "to indicate direction") and
formal orders from authority (上级指示 "instructions from a superior") —
a more official register than everyday 告诉 "to tell."

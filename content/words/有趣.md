## Why this combination
有 ("to have") takes 趣 ("interest, fun," covered elsewhere in this app —
originally "swift," later drawn toward "the direction one hastens
toward," i.e. one's interest) as its object: having that quality of
appeal is exactly "interesting."

## Notes
有趣 describes something engaging or amusing (有趣的故事, "an interesting
story") — for a person's intellectual curiosity itself, 兴趣 (covered
elsewhere in this app) is the noun to reach for instead.

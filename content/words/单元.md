## Why this combination
单 "single, individual" (as in 单独 "alone, solitary") combined with 元
"a basic unit, an element" (as in 元素 "element"): a single basic
unit — a unit, a section.

## Notes
单元 names a self-contained unit within a larger whole, from a
textbook chapter (第一单元 "unit one") to a residential building's
staircase/entrance section (三单元 "entrance/stairwell 3") — the same
"self-contained division of a whole" logic runs through both uses.

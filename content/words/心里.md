## Why this combination
心 "heart, mind" + locative 里 "inside." Where thoughts and feelings are held —
心里很难过 "sad at heart," 心里想 "think to oneself," 心里有数 "have a clear
idea." Chinese locates both emotion and cognition in the 心.

## Notes
里 as a bare locative suffix, like 家里, 手里. Near-synonym 心中 (see [[心中]]),
a touch more literary.

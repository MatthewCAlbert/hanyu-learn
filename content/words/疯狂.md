## Why this combination
疯 "insane" and 狂 "wild, frenzied" reinforce each other, both already
carrying the "out of control" sense on their own.

## Notes
疯狂 works as an intensifying adjective well beyond literal madness:
疯狂购物 "shopping like crazy," 疯狂爱上 "to fall madly in love" — a
common intensifier in casual speech.

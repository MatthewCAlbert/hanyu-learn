## Why this combination
坚 "firm, hard, unyielding" (as in 坚持 "to persist") combined with 强
"strong" (see also 强大, 强烈): firm and strong — resilient, unyielding
in character.

## Notes
坚强 describes inner resilience and willpower, especially in the face of
hardship (坚强的意志 "a strong will," 坚强地面对困难 "to face difficulties
with resilience") — 强壮, its near-neighbour, describes physical
strength of body rather than strength of character.

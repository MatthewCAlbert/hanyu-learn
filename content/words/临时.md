## Why this combination
临 ("to arrive at, to approach," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 时
("time") names something arranged only as the moment arrives, rather
than planned ahead: temporary, ad hoc.

## Notes
临时 modifies arrangements made on short notice (临时决定, "a last-minute
decision"; 临时工, "a temporary worker") — always implying "not planned
in advance," not just "brief."

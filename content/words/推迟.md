## Why this combination

推 ("to push," as in 推广, "to promote [lit. push + broad]") plus 迟
("late," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 迟到, "to be late")
names pushing something later in time: to postpone.

## Notes

推迟会议 ("to postpone a meeting") and 推迟到下周 ("to push back to next
week") both build on this word — 推迟 always implies a deliberate choice
to delay, distinct from being simply late by accident (迟到).

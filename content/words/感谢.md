## Why this combination
感 "to feel, to be moved" + 谢 "to thank" (see [[谢谢]]) → to feel and express
gratitude. More formal and heartfelt than 谢谢: 感谢大家的支持.

## Notes
感谢 (weighty, often written) · 谢谢 (everyday) · 多谢 (casual). Same 感 = "be
affected" in [[感动]], [[感觉]].

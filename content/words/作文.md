## Why this combination
作 "to compose, to write, to make" (see [[作家]], [[作业]], [[作用]]) + 文
"writing, a piece of prose" (see [[课文]], [[中文]], [[语言]]) → to compose a
piece of writing. Chiefly the school exercise and its product: 写作文,
一篇作文, 作文比赛.

## Notes
Verb-object, so it can split: 作了一篇文. Contrast [[作业]] "homework" (any
subject) and 写作 "writing" (as an activity / craft).

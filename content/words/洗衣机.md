## Why this combination
洗衣 "to wash clothes" (洗 wash + 衣 garment; see [[洗澡]], [[衣服]]) + 机
"machine" (see [[手机]], [[飞机]], [[电视机]]) → a machine for washing clothes.
Parse 洗衣 + 机.

## Notes
The 机 "-machine" set for appliances: 洗衣机, 电视机, 收音机 "radio,"
[[计算机]] "computer," 吹风机 "hairdryer."

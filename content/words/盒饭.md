## Why this combination
盒 "box" plus 饭 "rice, a meal" gives exactly "a meal in a box" — a
straightforward, compositional word.

## Notes
盒饭 anchors 订盒饭 "to order a boxed meal," 一份盒饭 "a boxed lunch" — the
everyday word for a takeout meal packaged in a partitioned box, common for
office lunches.

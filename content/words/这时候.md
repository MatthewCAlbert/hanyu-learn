## Why this combination
Demonstrative 这 "this" + 时候 "time, moment" (see [[时候]], [[有时候]]) → "at
this point in time." Fuller and more colloquial than [[这时]]; can also mean
"this time of day" (这时候还没睡？).

## Notes
这时候 / [[这时]] / 这会儿 run colloquial → written. Mirror of 那时候 (see
[[那时]]).

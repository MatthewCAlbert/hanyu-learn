## Why this combination
去 in time expressions means "gone by, past" — the year that has departed. The
same 去 gives 过去 "the past." It breaks the neat 昨/今/明 pattern: the year row
is 去年 / 今年 / 明年, not *昨年.

## Notes
前年 "the year before last," parallel to 前天.

## Why this combination

燃 ("to burn," covered elsewhere in this app as one half of the 然/燃
pair, where 然 kept the abstract "so, thus" sense while 燃 split off to
carry the literal "to burn" meaning) plus 料 ("material," as in 材料)
names material meant for burning: fuel.

## Notes

燃料 is the general technical word for any combustible fuel (化石燃料,
"fossil fuel") — 汽油 (covered elsewhere in this app) names one specific
kind.

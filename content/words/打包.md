## Why this combination
打 (here a light verb, "to do, to make," as in many 打+noun verbs) acting
on the object 包 "a package, a bundle" gives "to make into a package" —
splittable, as in 打了个包.

## Notes
打包 anchors 把剩菜打包 "to box up leftovers (at a restaurant)," and, in
computing, "to package/archive files" — the restaurant sense is now among
its most common everyday uses.

## Why this combination
进 "to advance, to progress" and 化 "to transform, to change into"
combine into "to transform by advancing" — the standard biological term
for evolution, coined to translate the Western scientific concept.

## Notes
进化 anchors 进化论 "the theory of evolution," 生物进化 "biological
evolution" — the scientific term, distinct from 变化 "change" in
general, which carries no implication of directional development.

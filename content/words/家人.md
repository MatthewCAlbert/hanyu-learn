## Why this combination
家 "household, family" + 人 "person" → the people of one's household. Collective:
我家人 or 家里人 "my family," treated as a group.

## Notes
家 "household" also in 大家 (everyone), 一家人 ("one family," often figurative).
Old sense "servant of a house" is obsolete.

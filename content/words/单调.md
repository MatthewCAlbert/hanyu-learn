## Why this combination

单 ("single, alone," covered elsewhere in this app as part of 单纯)
plus 调 (here "a tune, a melody," a distinct sense from its "to adjust"
role in 调整 or its "naughty" role in 调皮) names having only a single
tune repeated: monotonous.

Rated "semi" for 调's specific "tune/melody" sense here, one of several
distinct senses this character carries across different compounds.

## Notes

生活单调 ("life is monotonous") and 单调的声音 ("a monotonous sound") both
build on this word — extends from music (a single repeated note) to any
repetitive, unvarying experience.

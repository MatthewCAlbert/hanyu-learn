## Why this combination
职 "post, duty" and 务 "affair, task" are near-synonyms combined into the
formal word for an official role and its associated duties.

## Notes
职务 anchors 担任职务 "to hold a post," 职务晋升 "promotion" — emphasizes
the duties attached to a position, distinct from 职位, which leans more
toward the position/rank itself.

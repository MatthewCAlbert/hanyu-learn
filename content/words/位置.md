## Why this combination
位 is "a seat, a rank, a position" (also the measure word for people, 一位
老师), and 置 is "to place, to set (something) down" (as in 布置, 设置).
"Where something has been placed/ranked" gives the general noun for
location or standing.

Rated "semi" for 置's less common standalone verb sense, which most
learners meet in compounds before meeting it alone.

## Notes
位置 covers both physical location (这个位置很好, "this spot is great") and
figurative standing (他在公司里的位置, "his position within the company") —
the same range English "position" covers.

## Why this combination

原 ("original, raw, un-processed," as in 原因, "cause [lit. original +
reason]") plus 料 ("material," as in 材料) names material in its
unprocessed, original state: raw material.

## Notes

原料 names material before manufacturing (生产原料, "production raw
materials") — 材料 is the broader word covering both raw and finished
material for building or making something.

## Why this combination
作 "to compose, to write, to create" + 家 as the "-ist / expert" suffix (画家
painter, 科学家 scientist, 音乐家 musician). One whose vocation is writing
literary works.

## Notes
作家 = a literary writer (novels, essays); 作者 = "the author" of a specific
text, whoever wrote it. 家 as "-ist" also in [[科学家]], [[画家]].

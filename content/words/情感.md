## Why this combination
情 "feeling, sentiment" (see also 感情, 心情) combined with 感 "to feel,
to be moved" (see also 感动, 感受): feeling that moves one — emotion.

## Notes
情感 is a formal, often written near-synonym of 感情, used in more
literary, analytical or clinical contexts (情感教育 "emotional
education," 人类情感 "human emotion") — 感情, the everyday word, is used
in speech, especially for the bond between people.

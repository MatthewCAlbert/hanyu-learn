## Why this combination
有 "to have" + 意思 in its sense "interest, point, savour" (see [[意思]]) →
"has something to it." Of a book, an idea, an activity: 这本书很有意思.

## Notes
有意思 can also hint at romantic interest — 他对你有意思. Opposite 没意思
"boring / pointless." The 有 + noun frame, like [[有名]], [[有用]].

## Why this combination
熟 (ripe, and by extension "familiar, well-practiced") and 悉 "to know in
detail" combine directly into "to know thoroughly and familiarly."

## Notes
熟悉 covers people (熟悉的朋友 "a close, familiar friend") and information
(熟悉情况 "to be familiar with the situation") — familiarity gained
through repeated contact or study, not first impressions.

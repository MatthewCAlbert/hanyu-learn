## Why this combination
发 carries its "to emit, to bring forth, to develop" sense (as in 发展,
发生), and 挥 is "to wield, to swing vigorously" (covered in this app's
hanzi entry). Bringing forth and vigorously wielding a talent or ability
is exactly "to display it fully, to bring it into play."

Rated "semi" for 挥's specific "to wield" sense here, distinct from its
role in 指挥 (to command) and 挥霍 (to squander).

## Notes
发挥作用 ("to play a role, to take effect," lit. "to bring forth an
effect") and 发挥水平 ("to perform up to one's [true] level/ability") are
the two most common everyday frames built on this word.

## Why this combination
突 "to rush, to burst forth suddenly" (as in 突然 "suddenly") combined
with the directional complement 出 "out, to emerge": to burst outward —
to protrude, and, figuratively, to stand out prominently.

## Notes
突出 describes something standing out physically (突出的部分 "the
protruding part") or in quality (突出的表现 "outstanding performance,"
突出问题 "a prominent/pressing problem") — the literal "protrude" and
figurative "outstanding" senses share the same core image of standing
out from a surrounding surface.

## Why this combination
动 "to move" + 物 "thing, matter, being" → "a thing that moves of itself,"
against 植物 "planted thing" = plant and 矿物 "mineral." A tidy scientific
naming set built on 物.

## Notes
动物园 "zoo," 小动物 "small creatures," 野生动物 "wildlife." 物 "thing" also in
[[礼物]], [[食物]], [[植物]] (not this level).

## Why this combination
互 "mutually, reciprocally" (see also 互相) combined with 联 "connected,
linked" (see also 联合, 联系) and 网 "a net, a network" (as in 网上, 网站):
a network of mutual connections — a calque translating English
"inter-net" component by component.

## Notes
互联网 is the formal, technical term for the Internet as global
infrastructure (互联网公司 "internet company") — 网, alone, or 网上
"online," is used far more often in everyday speech for the same
underlying concept.

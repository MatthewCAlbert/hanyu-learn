## Why this combination
京 "capital [city]" (short for 北京, as in 京城 "the capital") combined
with 剧 "opera, drama" (as in 话剧 "spoken drama," 电视剧): the opera
style that developed in the capital — Peking opera.

## Notes
京剧 names the stylized traditional opera form associated with Beijing
(看京剧 "to watch Peking opera," 京剧脸谱 "Peking opera facial makeup") —
剧 recurs productively across performance genres (京剧, 话剧, 电视剧), a
consistent "drama/opera" pattern.

## Why this combination

春 ("spring," as in 春天) plus 季 ("a season," as in 季节) names the
spring season.

## Notes

春季 is the formal/written register — 春天 is the everyday spoken word
for the same season, the same pattern seen in 冬季/冬天, 夏季/夏天, and
秋季/秋天, all covered elsewhere in this app.

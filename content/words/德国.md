## Why this combination
德 clipped from 德意志, the phonetic rendering of "Deutsch-," + 国 "country."
The same mould as [[法国]], [[美国]]. 德 here is not "virtue."

## Notes
德 as the country-abbreviation also in [[德语]], [[德文]].

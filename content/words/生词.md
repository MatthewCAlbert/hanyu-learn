## Why this combination
生 here "unfamiliar, strange, not yet worked in" (the opposite of 熟 "familiar"
— the same 生/熟 contrast as raw/cooked food) + 词 "word" (see [[词语]],
[[词典]]) → a word one hasn't met before: 这课有二十个生词, 查生词.

## Notes
生 "unfamiliar" also in 生人 "a stranger," 陌生, 面生. Opposite: 熟词 "familiar
word." 生词本 "vocabulary notebook."

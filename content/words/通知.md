## Why this combination
通 "to get through, to communicate" + 知 "to know; to make known" → to pass word
so that someone knows. Verb (通知大家) and noun (发通知, 收到通知).

## Notes
通 "communicate / get through" also in [[通过]], [[交通]], 电话打通了. Formal
register — an official 通知 is a document.

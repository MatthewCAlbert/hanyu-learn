## Why this combination
行, here "to travel, to go on a journey" (as in 旅行, 飞行) combined
with 李, originally referring to a servant or courier who carried a
traveller's belongings (a specialised, now-obsolete sense of the
character, unrelated to its common surname use): what a travelling
courier carried — luggage.

## Notes
行李 names the bags and belongings one carries while travelling (行李箱
"suitcase," 托运行李 "to check luggage") — 箱子, its neighbour, names a
box or case specifically, one common form luggage takes.

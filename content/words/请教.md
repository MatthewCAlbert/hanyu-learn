## Why this combination
请 "to request, please" (as in 请问 "may I ask") combined with 教 "to
teach" (as in 教育, 教师): to request teaching — to humbly ask for
guidance.

## Notes
请教 is a polite, respectful verb for seeking advice from someone more
knowledgeable (向老师请教 "to seek guidance from the teacher," 请教
一个问题 "to ask for advice on a question") — 问, the plain verb "to
ask," carries none of 请教's deferential, respectful tone.

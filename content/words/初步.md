## Why this combination
初 "at first, initial, the beginning" (as in 当初, 最初) combined with
步 "a step, pace" (see also 进步, 脚步): the first step [taken] — initial,
preliminary.

## Notes
初步 modifies plans, results and understanding still at an early,
unfinished stage (初步计划 "preliminary plan," 初步了解 "initial/basic
understanding") — it signals that more development is still expected.

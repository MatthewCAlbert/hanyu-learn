## Why this combination
拿 "to take, to hold" (see [[拿到]]) + directional 出 "out" — bringing something
out from where it was: 从包里拿出手机. Figuratively, "produce / muster":
拿出证据 "produce evidence," 拿出勇气 "summon up courage."

## Notes
出 "bring out / produce" as a complement also in [[提出]], [[得出]], [[找出]],
看出.

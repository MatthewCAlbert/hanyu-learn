## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 宽 ("wide, spacious," as in 宽阔, "wide and
open") and 广 ("broad, vast," covered elsewhere in this app as part of
广场/广大) both independently name expansiveness. Pairing them gives the
standard word for broad extent.

## Notes

宽广的胸怀 ("a broad-minded/generous heart") and 宽广的道路 ("a wide road")
both build on this word — covers both literal physical breadth and
figurative open-mindedness or scope.

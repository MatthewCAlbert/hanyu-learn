## Why this combination
A near-synonym compound: 祝 ("to wish, to pray for [someone's good
fortune]," as in 祝贺, "to congratulate") and 福 ("fortune, blessing," as
in 幸福) both independently name wishing good fortune upon someone.
Pairing them gives the standard word for a blessing.

## Notes
祝福 works as both verb ("to wish someone well," 祝福你, "bless you/best
wishes to you") and noun ("a blessing," 送上祝福, "to offer blessings")
without changing form.

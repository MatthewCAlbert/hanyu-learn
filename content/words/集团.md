## Why this combination
集 "to gather, to assemble" and 团 "group, cluster" reinforce each other
into the formal word for a gathered body of people or organizations.

## Notes
集团 spans politics (利益集团 "interest group") and business (企业集团
"business conglomerate") — always a formally organized body, larger and
more structured than a casual 团体.

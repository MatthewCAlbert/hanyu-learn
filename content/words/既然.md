## Why this combination
既 carries its "already, having happened" sense (covered in this app's
hanzi entry), and 然 is the same grammaticalized adverb-forming suffix
seen in 竟然, 突然, contributing no independent meaning of its own.
"Given that [something has] already [happened]" is exactly the causal
"since" this word introduces.

Marked "idiom" because 然's suffix role has to be learned as a fixed
pattern rather than derived from an independent meaning.

## Notes
既然...就... is the standard paired frame: 既然你来了，就留下吃饭吧 ("since
you're here, stay for dinner") — 既然 states the given fact, 就 draws the
conclusion from it.

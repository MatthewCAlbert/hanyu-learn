## Why this combination
土 "earth, soil" and 豆 "bean" combine into "an earth-bean" — a
bean-shaped vegetable that grows underground, distinguishing the potato
from beans that grow above ground.

## Notes
土豆 is the northern/Mainland-standard term; 马铃薯 is the more formal,
botanical name, and 洋芋 is a regional term used in parts of southwestern
China. Note also the Taiwanese usage where 土豆 can mean "peanut" instead.

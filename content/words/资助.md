## Why this combination
资 "capital, resources" and 助 "to assist" combine directly: assisting
someone with financial resources.

## Notes
资助 anchors 资助学生 "to sponsor a student," 经济资助 "financial aid" —
always material/monetary support, distinct from moral or practical
支持 "support" more generally.

## Why this combination
总 "overall, chief" and 裁 "to decide, to judge" (see 裁's own etymology —
cutting cloth to a pattern, extended to judgement) combine directly: the
person whose decisions govern the whole organization.

## Notes
总裁 names the top executive title in large companies (公司总裁 "company
CEO/chairman") — a step above 总经理 "general manager" in many corporate
hierarchies.

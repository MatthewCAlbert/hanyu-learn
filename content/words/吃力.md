## Why this combination
吃 (here "to consume, to take in") acting on the object 力 "strength"
gives exactly "to consume strength" — the standard word for something
requiring taxing effort.

## Notes
吃力 anchors 爬楼很吃力 "climbing stairs is exhausting," 说得很吃力
"speaking with great effort/strain" — always about the toll an activity
takes, whether physical or mental.

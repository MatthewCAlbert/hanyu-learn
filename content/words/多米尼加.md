## Why this combination
The usual short form of 多米尼加共和国, the Dominican Republic. A phonetic rendering
of Dominicana. Not Dominica the island state, which is 多米尼克 (克 is not in this
Extra list).

## Notes
多米- as a dummy also in other transcriptions; the last syllable is what
learners need to watch.

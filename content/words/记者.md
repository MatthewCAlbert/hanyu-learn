## Why this combination
记 "to record, to note down" with 者, a productive suffix meaning "one
who [does the preceding verb]" (the same pattern as 作者 "author," 读者
"reader"). One who records [events for publication]: a journalist.

## Notes
者 as an agent-noun suffix is worth recognising as a pattern in its own
right — it turns almost any verb into "a person who does that verb,"
far more productively than any single English suffix manages.

## Why this combination
参 "to take part in, to join" (see [[参加]]) + 观 "to view, to observe" (see
[[观点]], 参观) → to go somewhere and look around it — a museum, a factory, a
school: 参观博物馆.

## Notes
参观 = tour a place (institutions, exhibits); [[旅游]] = travel for pleasure;
[[访问]] (not this level) = visit / call on (a person or country).

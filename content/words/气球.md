## Why this combination

气 ("air, gas") plus 球 ("a ball," as in 篮球) names a ball-shaped object
filled with air: a balloon.

## Notes

吹气球 ("to blow up a balloon") and 热气球 ("a hot air balloon") both
build on this word — 气球 shares its 球 "ball" root with sports words
like 篮球/足球/排球, all naming round balls used in play.

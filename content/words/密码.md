## Why this combination
密 ("secret, hidden," covered elsewhere in this app) plus 码 ("a number,
a code," also covered elsewhere) names a secret code: a password.

## Notes
密码 covers both everyday passwords (输入密码, "enter your password") and
formal cryptographic ciphers (密码学, "cryptography") — the same "secret
code" core across casual and technical registers.

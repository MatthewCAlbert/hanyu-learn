## Why this combination

有 ("to have") plus 限 ("a limit, a bound," as in 限制) names having a
bound on something: limited, finite.

## Notes

有限公司 ("limited company," i.e. Ltd.) is the standard business-registry
term, borrowing 有限's "bounded (liability)" sense directly from its
everyday meaning.

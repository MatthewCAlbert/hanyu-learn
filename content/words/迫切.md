## Why this combination

A near-synonym compound: 迫 ("to force, urgent," covered elsewhere in
this app as part of 被迫, "to be forced") and 切 ("urgent, close," covered
elsewhere in this app as part of 亲切/密切) both independently name
pressing urgency. Pairing them gives the standard word for something
that demands immediate attention.

## Notes

迫切需要 ("urgently need") and 迫切的愿望 ("a pressing wish") both build on
this word — 迫切 describes the intensity of a need or desire, close to
but more formal than everyday 急.

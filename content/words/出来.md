## Why this combination
出 "to go out" + 来 "to come (toward the speaker)": exiting, in your direction.
出去 is the same exit going away from you. As a complement it also marks
something emerging into view or being worked out — 想出来 "figure out,"
认出来 "recognise."

## Notes
Part of the directional set: 出/进/回/上/下 for the path, 来/去 for whether it
heads toward or away from the speaker.

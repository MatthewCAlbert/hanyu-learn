## Why this combination
教 (*jiào*) "to teach" + 学 "to learn / study." The two sides of the classroom
process, together naming the enterprise of teaching: 教学质量, 教学方法,
教学相长 "teaching and learning advance each other."

## Notes
教学 = the process / practice of teaching; [[教育]] = education as a system;
[[教师]] = the person; [[教学楼]] = the building. 教 is *jiào* here.

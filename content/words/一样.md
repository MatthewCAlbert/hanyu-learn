## Why this combination
样 "kind, type, appearance"; "one kind" = of a single sort = identical. Used in
comparison frames: A 和 B 一样 "A is the same as B," or 像…一样 "just like…".

## Notes
不一样 "different." The measure-like 样 also gives 样子 (appearance) and 怎么样
(how about it).

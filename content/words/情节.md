## Why this combination
情 "situation, circumstance" and 节 "a joint, a segment" (see 节's role in
环节) combine metaphorically: the connected segments that make up an
unfolding situation — a story's sequence of linked events.

## Notes
情节 names both narrative plot (电影情节 "the film's plot") and, in legal
contexts, the specific circumstances of a case (情节严重 "the
circumstances are serious," affecting sentencing) — connected segments of
either a story or an incident.

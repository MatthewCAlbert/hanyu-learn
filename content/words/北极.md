## Why this combination
北 "north" plus 极 "pole, extreme point" gives exactly "the northern
pole" — the standard geographic term, mirroring 南极 "South Pole."

## Notes
北极 anchors 北极熊 "polar bear," 北极圈 "the Arctic Circle" — the
geographic pole and the surrounding polar region.

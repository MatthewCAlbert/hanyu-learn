## Why this combination
玩笑 "a joke, jest" (玩 play + 笑 laugh) as the object of 开, here the light verb
"to make / set going" (as in 开会, 开party). Splits: 开个玩笑 "just kidding,"
开他的玩笑 "tease him," 别拿这个开玩笑 "don't joke about this."

## Notes
跟…开玩笑 "joke with someone," 开…的玩笑 "joke at someone's expense." 开不起
玩笑 "can't take a joke."

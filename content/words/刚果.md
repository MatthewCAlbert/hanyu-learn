## Why this combination
A two-syllable phonetic rendering of Congo (剛果). 刚 is not "hard." The name is
shared by two states, distinguished in full as 刚果共和国 and 刚果民主共和国 (刚果（布） /
刚果（金）).

## Notes
果 is phonetic, not "fruit."

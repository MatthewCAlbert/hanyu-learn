## Why this combination
犹 (see 犹's own etymology — "closely resembling/following," extended
here to a wavering, uncommitted stance) and 豫 (see 豫's own etymology —
a large elephant's slow, deliberate movement, read as caution) are
near-synonyms combined into the fixed disyllabic word for hesitation —
neither character carries "hesitate" clearly on its own.

## Notes
犹豫 is one of many two-character words in Mandarin that exist only as a
pair; 犹 and 豫 essentially never appear as independent single-character
words with this meaning outside this combination.

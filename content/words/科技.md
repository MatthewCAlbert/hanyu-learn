## Why this combination
科技 shortens 科学技术 "science and technology" to its first character
from each half — a standard compression pattern for paired disyllabic
terms (compare 高铁 from 高速铁路): 科 from 科学 "science," 技 from 技术
"technology."

## Notes
科技 is used as a compact modifier in compounds (科技公司 "tech company,"
科技发展 "technological development") — the full form 科学技术 is
reserved for more formal or emphatic contexts where both halves need to
stand out separately.

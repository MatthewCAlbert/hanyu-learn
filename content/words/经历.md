## Why this combination
经 "to pass through, to go by way of" and 历 "to undergo, to pass through
[in sequence]" (as in 历史 "history," things passed through over time)
are close synonyms, paired for both the verb "to go through [an event]"
and the noun it forms, "experience."

## Notes
经历 as a noun names the sum of what someone has lived through (工作经历
"work experience," 人生经历 "life experience"); 经验, a near-homophone in
meaning, names the practical know-how gained from that experience rather
than the events themselves.
